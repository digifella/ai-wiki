# AI Generated Videos

AI-generated videos are digital [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] created using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools that convert text-based descriptions into realistic video outputs. These tools accept natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] from users describing desired video content and generate corresponding footage without requiring traditional filming equipment or extensive [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] [[concepts/expertise|expertise]]. The technology has made video production more accessible to creators with minimal technical [[concepts/skills|skills]], enabling the rapid production of content that would traditionally require professional crews and post-production work.

## How It Works

The underlying technology typically combines [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with [[concepts/image-and-video-diffusion-models|diffusion models]] or other generative techniques. Users provide text descriptions of the video scene, camera [[concepts/exercise|movement]], and [[concepts/style|style]], which the AI interprets to synthesize frames sequentially.
