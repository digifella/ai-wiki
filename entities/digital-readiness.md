# Digital Readiness

**[[concepts/digital-readiness|Digital Readiness]]** refers to the preparedness of [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] systems, students, and clinicians to effectively utilize digital tools and technologies in [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]]. It encompasses technical [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]], systemic infrastructure, and the human factors necessary for successful [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] integration.

## Key Concepts & Associations

- **Stakeholder Engagement**: Critical for identifying barriers and facilitators to digital [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] among clinicians, patients, and administrators.
- **Equity**: Ensuring digital health solutions do not exacerbate existing disparities in access or [[concepts/quality-of-care|quality of care]].
- **Allied [[concepts/health-promotion|Health Education]]**: Integration of digital literacy into training curricula for [[concepts/allied-health|allied healthcare]] students.
