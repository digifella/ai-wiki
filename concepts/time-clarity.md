# Time Clarity

The Time Clarity Interview System is a structured framework designed to help business owners identify and resolve clarity issues that manifest as time management challenges. Rather than prescribing scheduling techniques or productivity shortcuts, the system operates from the premise that most business owners struggle not with insufficient hours, but with insufficient clarity about their priorities, workflows, and decision-making processes.

## Core Function

The framework uses guided interviews as its primary mechanism. By asking strategic questions, it helps business owners surface underlying ambiguities in their business operations. This process allows individuals to distinguish between actual time constraints and confusion regarding strategic direction or operational responsibilities.

## Application

The system is applied through a series of structured conversations that encourage deep reflection on current business practices. Participants are guided to articulate their goals and obstacles, leading to a clearer understanding of where time is being lost due to indecision or misalignment. The outcome is a refined focus on high-impact activities, reducing the cognitive load associated with daily management.
