# Traditional Consulting Model

The traditional consulting model has historically relied on expert consultants delivering strategic advice and implementation services to client organizations. This approach typically involves face-to-face engagements, specialized knowledge workers, and project-based billing structures. Consultants serve as trusted advisors who diagnose business problems, develop recommendations, and guide execution.

## Impact of AI Technology

Recent advances in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] are fundamentally altering the [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] landscape. [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] can now perform certain analytical and diagnostic tasks that previously required experienced consultants, including data analysis, process optimization, and strategy formulation. This shift is creating pressure on traditional consulting firms to integrate [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into their [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]] models and to redefine the value proposition of human consultants.

## Adaptation and Evolution

Rather than replacing consulting entirely, AI is reshaping the role of consulting professionals. Firms are adapting by using AI to augment consultant productivity, automate routine tasks, and handle larger volumes of analytical work with smaller teams. This transition raises questions about skill requirements, [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] models, and the future [[concepts/structure|structure]] of the consulting industry as the sector adjusts to increasingly capable [[concepts/automations|automated systems]].
