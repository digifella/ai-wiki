# Using [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] new features

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=obtjaOy4blw>
Channel: [[entities/rob-the-ai-guy|Rob the AI guy]]

This video introduces three brand new features recently launched by [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]]:

1. **[[concepts/scheduled-actions|Scheduled Actions]]:**
	Users can now schedule tasks directly within Gemini's web app. Access this feature under "Settings & Help" in the bottom-left corner, then click "Scheduled actions." To create a scheduled action, prompt Gemini, e.g., "Please create me a scheduled action: Send me a daily [[concepts/summary|summary]] of my calendar, to-do's and important unread emails at 8 AM." Gemini will then use its `scheduler.schedule` tool to set up the recurring action. Users can pause or edit these scheduled actions, including changing the frequency (daily, weekly, monthly) and time. It's crucial to enable various [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] apps (Gmail, Calendar, Docs, [[concepts/motivation|Drive]], Keep, Tasks) and other Google services (Flights, Hotels, Maps, [[entities/youtube|YouTube]], YouTube Music, OpenStax) in Gemini's "Apps" settings to leverage its full [[concepts/automation|automation]] potential. An example mockup shows how a daily summary could include calendar events, a to-do list, and important unread emails.
	
2. **[[entities/imagen|Imagen]] 4 & AI Studio for Image Generation:**
	Google has integrated Imagen 4 (and [[entities/imagen-4-ultra|Imagen 4 Ultra]]) into its free AI Studio platform (aistudio.google.com) for image generation. To access, navigate to "Generate Media" and select "Imagen." The new Imagen 4 Ultra model is highlighted for its speed, generating hyper-realistic images significantly faster than competitors like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] (DALL-E 3). Users can describe their desired image, and Imagen will generate it, with options to download, copy, export to Drive, and adjust aspect ratios.
	
3. **[[entities/gemini-cli|Gemini CLI]] ([[concepts/cli|Command Line]] Interface):**
	This is a powerful, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] AI [[entities/agent|agent]] that allows users to interact with Gemini directly from their terminal. It's designed for developers and technical users, enabling tasks like [[concepts/writing|writing]] code, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and automating [[concepts/workflow|workflows]]. Key capabilities include: Querying and editing large codebases (with a [[concepts/1-million-token-context|1 million token context]] window). Generating new applications from PDFs or sketches using Gemini's [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]]. Automating operational tasks like querying pull requests or handling complex rebases. Connecting to other services (like Imagen, Veo, Lyra for media generation) via MCP servers. Grounding queries with the [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]] tool. Examples provided include writing a Discord bot, summarizing Git changes, exploring codebases, implementing code drafts, automating workflows, and interacting with the system (e.g., converting images, organizing PDF invoices). The video also briefly mentions new [[entities/gemma|Gemma]] [[concepts/models|models]] (Gemma 3n E2B and Gemma 3n E4B) available in AI Studio, which are [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] typically hosted locally for experimentation.
	

The speaker emphasizes the importance of embracing AI to stay competitive in the evolving job market and encourages viewers to explore his [[concepts/ai-automation|AI Automation]] School for personalized feedback and help with AI workflows.
