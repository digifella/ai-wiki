# FF(X)

**FF(X)** is the [[entities/us-navy|United States Navy]]'s next-generation frigate program, designed to complement larger surface combatants by providing cost-effective, distributed lethality and presence capabilities. It serves as a critical [[entities/nodejs|node]] in the Navy’s broader fleet expansion strategy, aiming to increase total hull count while maintaining operational flexibility.

## Strategic Context & Integration

FF(X) is central to the U.S. Navy's industrial base revitalization and force structure goals, specifically addressing the need for a versatile, exportable, and scalable surface warfare platform. The program is deeply intertwined with broader strategic planning documents and budgetary frameworks.

*   **Fleet Expansion Role**: FF(X) platforms are intended to fill the gap left by retiring Oliver Hazard Perry-class frigates and to provide additional capacity for escort duties, anti-submarine warfare, and presence missions without the high [[concepts/operational-costs|operational costs]] of destroyers.
*   **30-Year Shipbuilding Plan Alignment**: The development timeline and procurement rates of FF(X) are key variables in the US Navy's [[concepts/fy2027-30-year-shipbuilding-plan|FY2027 30-Year Shipbuilding Plan]]: [[concepts/fleet-growth|Fleet Growth]], Nuclear Battleships, [[concepts/funding-paradox|Funding Paradox]]. This plan outlines long-term fleet growth targets, where FF(X) contributes significantly to increasing the total number of ships while managing budget constraints and industrial base limitations.
*   **Complement to Large Combatants**: FF(X) operates within a combined arms surface fleet, supporting larger platforms like DDG(X) (future destroyer) and [[concepts/nuclear-battleships]] by absorbing lower-threat missions and enhancing networked sensing and firing capabilities.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Design [[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]**: Emphasizes modularity, commonality with allied navies, and ease of maintenance.
*   **Industrial Base**: Aims to utilize existing frigate/destroyer production lines to avoid new tooling costs and [[concepts/speed|speed]] up delivery.
*   **Operational Flexibility**: Designed for both high-intensity [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] and lower-end contingency operations.
