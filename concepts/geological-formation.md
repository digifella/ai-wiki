# Geological Formation

A geological formation is a significant body of rock or unconsolidated material with recognizable characteristics that distinguish it from other units and enables it to be mapped. These formations are crucial for understanding the history and processes of [[entities/earth|Earth]], including weathering, erosion, deposition, and [[concepts/crustal-movement|tectonic activity]].

- **[[entities/coober-pedy|Coober Pedy]]** - A unique subterranean town in [[entities/south-australia|South Australia]], where residents live in homes carved directly into the ground to protect against extreme surface temperatures.
  - climate: Harsh desert conditions with intense heat during the day and cold at night influence living patterns underground.
  - [[concepts/geology]]: Presence of soft clay-like rock called tafoni rock.
  - **Tectonic Context:** The broader geological history of the region is influenced by major tectonic events, such as the [[concepts/moyston-fault|Moyston Fault: The Tectonic Collision That Created Victoria]], which describes a crucial boundary that divides regions in [[concepts/western-victoria|Western Victoria]].
