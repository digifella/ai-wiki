# Advanced Plan

The [[concepts/advanced-plan|Advanced Plan]] is a [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] tier offered by [[entities/evernote|Evernote]] as part of its subscription service offerings. The plan sits within Evernote's broader [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]] and serves users with more demanding note-taking and organizational needs compared to the service's basic tier options.

## Context and User Response

The Advanced Plan became a subject of discussion among Evernote users in April 2026 following announced changes to the platform's pricing [[concepts/structure|structure]]. Users reacted to the modifications with mixed sentiment, with some evaluating whether the plan's features and [[concepts/cost|cost]] justified continued use of the service. These discussions prompted guidance on account migration for users considering alternatives to Evernote's paid offerings.
