# Lebanon

Lebanon is a country located on the eastern Mediterranean coast in the Middle East, bordered by [[entities/syria|Syria]] to the north and east and [[entities/israel|Israel]] to the south. The country occupies a strategic position within the Levant region, serving as a crossroads between the Mediterranean world and the interior of the Middle East. Its capital and largest city is Beirut.

## Geography and Strategic Position

The country's geography is defined by the Mediterranean coastline to the west and the Anti-Lebanon Mountains to the east, with the fertile Bekaa Valley lying between them. Lebanon's proximity to the [[concepts/golan-heights|Golan Heights]] and its position within the broader Israeli-Syrian-Iranian geopolitical triangle have made it a focal point of regional [[concepts/power|power]] dynamics. The country serves as a critical [[entities/nodejs|node]] in regional [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]] and strategic corridors affecting multiple neighboring states.

## Political and Security Environment

Lebanon's political system is characterized by a confessional arrangement reflecting its diverse religious communities, including Maronite Christians, Shia Muslims, Sunni Muslims, and Druze populations. The country's [[concepts/security|security]] landscape is shaped by the presence of multiple state and non-state actors, including Palestinian refugee populations and various militias. These factors have historically created complex internal dynamics influenced by external regional powers, particularly Syria, Israel, and [[concepts/iran|Iran]].
