# Policies

This page documents policy considerations and [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks relevant to the development and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/copilot-chat|CoPilot]] [[concepts/agents|Agents]], informed by work conducted by [[entities/georgia-engel|Georgia Engel]] and [[entities/dan-polifiori|Dan Polifiori]]. The policies addressed here reflect practical requirements for implementing autonomous agents in enterprise environments where security, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and operational oversight are essential.

## Enterprise AI Deployment Security

Policy frameworks for CoPilot [[concepts/agent-deployment|Agent deployment]] emphasize [[concepts/secure-enterprise-ai|secure enterprise AI]] practices. Key considerations include [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] mechanisms, data handling protocols, and audit trails for agent actions. These policies are informed by industry approaches to mitigating vulnerabilities in [[concepts/ai-agent|autonomous agent]] systems and ensuring that agents operate within defined operational boundaries.

## Governance and Agent Control

Effective governance of CoPilot Agents requires clear policies around agent permissions, decision-making authority, and human oversight. Policies address how agents interact with enterprise systems, what actions they can autonomously perform, and under what conditions human intervention is required. These frameworks help organizations maintain control over agent behavior while enabling the efficiency benefits of [[concepts/automation|automation]].
