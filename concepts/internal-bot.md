# Internal Bot

An Internal Bot is a [[concepts/custom-ai-agent|custom AI agent]] built on the [[concepts/microsoft-applications|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] platform, designed to automate workflows and tasks specific to an organization's needs. Unlike [[concepts/general-purpose-chatbots|general-purpose chatbots]] intended for broad public use, internal bots operate within an organization's existing [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], integrating directly with deployed systems, data sources, and established business processes. They function as [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] tailored to particular departments or workflows rather than serving generic [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

These agents leverage an organization's existing Microsoft 365 ecosystem to access relevant data securely while maintaining [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with internal [[concepts/governance|governance]] [[concepts/policies|policies]]. By connecting to [[concepts/business-applications|enterprise applications]] such as SharePoint, Teams, and Outlook, internal bots can retrieve information, execute [[concepts/commands|commands]], and generate content based on [[concepts/user-permissions|user permissions]] and organizational context. This integration allows them to act as specialized assistants that understand the specific [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], structures, and procedures unique to the company.

The development of internal bots typically involves configuring [[entities/copilot-studio|Copilot Studio]] or similar low-code tools to define specific triggers, actions, and [[concepts/data-connectors|data connectors]]. This approach enables IT administrators and business analysts to create solutions that address precise [[concepts/operational-challenges|operational challenges]] without requiring extensive custom [[concepts/coding|coding]]. As a result, organizations can [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] scalable AI capabilities that enhance [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and streamline complex processes while ensuring that data remains within the [[concepts/secure|secure]] boundaries of the corporate environment.
