# Rubik's Cube Analogy

## Overview
The Rubik's Cube serves as a powerful analogy for understanding complex systems and problems in [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and computer science. Just as solving a scrambled Rubik's Cube requires strategic [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] and algorithmic approaches, certain computational challenges can be analyzed through the lens of this tangible puzzle.

### Key Concepts
- **Complexity [[concepts/theory|Theory]]:** The study of how much of a resource (time, [[concepts/memory|memory]]) is needed to solve [[concepts/computational-problems|computational problems]].
- **[[concepts/np-completeness|NP-Completeness]]:** A classification for problems where any given [[concepts/solution|solution]] can be verified quickly, but finding such a [[concepts/solution|solution]] may take an impractically long time.
