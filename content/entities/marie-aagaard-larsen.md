Marie Aagaard Larsen is a Danish amateur archaeologist and metal detectorist known for her participation in Denmark's government-deputized detectorist program.

*   Featured alongside [[entities/kristen-nedergaard-dreiøe|Kristen Nedergaard Dreiøe]] in the Denmark Let Amateurs Dig for Treasure—And It Paid Off [[entities/scientific-american|Scientific American]] article ([[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]]), documenting Denmark's program deputizing private detectorists to unearth artifacts in farm fields.
*   Conducts fieldwork using [[concepts/metal-detectors|metal detectors]] on grain fields in southern Denmark, contributing to detailed revelations of Denmark's historical landscape.
*   Her findings exemplify how amateur detectorists are revealing Denmark's past in extraordinary detail through systematic excavation of buried artifacts.
