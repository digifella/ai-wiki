# Third-party applications

2026 04 14 Chat [[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] update Channel [[entities/rick-mulready|Rob the AI Guy]]

Third-party applications are software [[concepts/software|programs]] developed by [[concepts/nodes|entities]] other than the original manufacturer of a platform or system. They extend the functionality of the primary system, often integrating with it to provide additional features or services.

## Key Characteristics
- **Integration**: Designed to work alongside existing systems, often through [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] or other interfaces.
- **Enhanced Functionality**: Provide capabilities not available in the original system.
- **Independence**: Developed and maintained by external developers or companies.

## Examples
- **Browser Extensions**: Tools like ad blockers or password managers that enhance web browsing.
- **Mobile [[concepts/apps|Apps]]**: Applications available on app stores that interact with a device's operating system.
- **[[concepts/ai-tools|AI Tools]]**: Applications that integrate with [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI platforms]] to provide specialized services.

## Use Cases
- **[[concepts/productivity|Productivity]]**: Tools that streamline workflows or automate tasks.
- **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Applications that allow users to tailor their [[concepts/experience|experience]].
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Software that makes systems more accessible to users with disabilities.
