# AI Interaction Design

The discipline of structuring communication, [[concepts/workflow|workflows]], and [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] to optimize the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and utility of [[concepts/large-language-models]].

## Core Paradigms
- **Beyond [[concepts/basic-prompting|Basic Prompting]]**: Shifting from simple, transactional prompt-and-response interactions toward complex, multi-turn [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] partnerships.
- **Cognitive Partnership**: Implementing strategies to transform [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude AI]] into a "genius-level [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] partner" (Ref: 2026 04 24 Strategies to Transform [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] into a Genius Level Thinking Partner).

## Key Strategies
- **Advanced Capability Unlocking**: Utilizing specific interaction techniques to move [[entities/anthropic-claude|Claude AI]] beyond basic [[concepts/responses|responses]] and into high-level reasoning modes (Source: [[entities/sabrina-ramonov|Sabrina Ramonov]]).
- **Iterative Engagement**: Moving away from one-shot queries in favor of structured, multi-step [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] to maximize model intelligence.
