# Peer-to-Peer Network

A decentralized architecture where participants (peers) act as both clients and servers, sharing resources directly without centralized coordination.

## Key Characteristics
- **Decentralization**: No single point of failure or control.
- **Resource Sharing**: Direct exchange of bandwidth, storage, or compute power.
- **Resilience**: Network remains functional despite individual node failures.

## Applications & Implementations
- **Distributed [[concepts/computation|Computing]]**: Aggregating idle hardware for complex tasks.
- **Decentralized AI**: Running [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] models across consumer devices.
- **File Sharing**: Protocols like BitTorrent.
