# Cell Growth

**Cell growth** refers to the increase in cell mass and volume, a prerequisite for Cell Division and Replication. It involves the synthesis of macromolecules (proteins, lipids, nucleic acids) and the expansion of cellular structures. In natural systems, this is regulated by nutrient availability, signaling pathways, and the Cell Cycle.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Biosynthesis**: Upregulation of metabolic pathways to produce cellular components.
- **Membrane Expansion**: Incorporation of new lipids to maintain surface-area-to-volume ratios.
- **Checkpoints**: Regulatory [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] ensuring growth precedes division.
