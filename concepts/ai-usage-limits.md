# Ai Usage Limits

AI usage limits are restrictions implemented by service providers to control the frequency and extent of user access to their models. These constraints typically define boundaries on request rates and token consumption within specified time periods, with different thresholds applied across various subscription tiers. By establishing these limits, providers manage infrastructure costs, prevent system abuse, and ensure equitable strategic resource allocation among their user base.

Anthropic has recently updated its Claude AI subscription model to enforce stricter usage limits. This adjustment aims to balance system stability with user demand, ensuring that high-volume users do not disproportionately impact service availability for others. The changes reflect a broader industry trend toward more granular control over computational resources as demand for large language models continues to grow.

In addition to modifying access thresholds, Anthropic has introduced specific prohibitions on certain third-party tools, such as OpenClaw. This ban is part of a broader effort to maintain security and integrity within the Claude ecosystem. By restricting unauthorized access methods, the company seeks to protect its intellectual property and ensure that all interactions comply with its terms of service.
