# Skin Tone Adjustment

Skin tone adjustment in Adobe Lightroom refers to the process of refining how human skin appears in photographs through targeted color corrections. This technique is particularly important in portrait and lifestyle photography, where natural-looking [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] are essential to the final image quality. The [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration Panel]] in Lightroom provides the primary tool for making these [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] in a non-destructive manner, meaning the original image data remains unchanged while edits are applied as [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] [[concepts/instructions|instructions]].

## The Calibration Panel Approach

The Calibration Panel allows photographers to adjust the foundational colors of an image by modifying how specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] are rendered. Rather than applying blanket color adjustments across the entire photograph, this method targets particular hues—particularly those in the red and orange ranges that correspond to human skin tones. By [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] these color channels at the source level, adjustments feel more natural and integrated with the overall image rather than appearing as localized corrections.

## Cinematic Styling

Skin tone adjustments form a key component of achieving cinematic styling in photography. Professional cinematography and high-end photography often employ consistent [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] strategies that shape how skin tones interact with the rest of the color palette. By using Lightroom's calibration tools to establish a cohesive color foundation, photographers can create [[concepts/images|images]] with a polished, film-like quality where skin tones complement rather than compete with the surrounding environment.
