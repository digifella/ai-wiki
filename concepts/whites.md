# whites

## Overview
In digital [[concepts/image-processing|image processing]] and [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]], "whites" refers to the brightest highlights in an image, often managed through tonal controls to prevent clipping or to enhance [[concepts/contrast|contrast]].

## Key Concepts

### Light Adjustment Layer
The introduction of the dedicated **Light** adjustment layer integrates [[entities/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] tonal controls directly into the layer stack, offering non-destructive editing capabilities.

- **Functionality**: Provides granular control over highlights, whites, and [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] without affecting the underlying pixel data.
- **Tonal Controls**: Allows for precise adjustment of the white point, enabling users to recover detail in overexposed areas or crush [[concepts/blacks|blacks]] for dramatic effect.
- **"Pause" Feature**: The layer includes a "Pause" demystification feature, allowing users to temporarily bypass the adjustment to compare before/after states or troubleshoot unexpected tonal shifts.
- **[[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-Destructive Workflow]]**: Unlike direct histogram adjustments, this layer can be toggled, masked, or removed without altering the original image.
