# Whites Adjustment

The Whites Adjustment is a tonal [[concepts/power|control]] feature in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that modifies the brightness of the lighter midtones and near-white areas of an image. Located in the [[concepts/tone|Tone]] Curve or Basic panel, this slider allows photographers to selectively brighten or darken the [[concepts/whites|whites]] without affecting the deepest [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], providing fine-grained control over image [[concepts/tonal-music|tonality]] during post-processing.

## Application in Landscape Photography

Landscape photographers frequently use the Whites Adjustment to enhance sky detail, recover blown [[concepts/highlights|highlights]], or add dimension to light-toned elements like clouds and water reflections. The adjustment works non-destructively within Lightroom's processing pipeline, meaning changes can be modified or reversed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] any point without affecting the original image file.

## Related Workflow

The Whites Adjustment typically works alongside other tonal controls such as [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Shadows, and [[concepts/blacks-sliders|Blacks sliders]] to achieve cohesive [[concepts/photo-enhancement|image enhancement]]. Many photographers incorporate it into broader processing strategies, including mood-based [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] for dramatic or moody aesthetic effects.
