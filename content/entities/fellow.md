# Fellow

A Fellow is a senior member or title holder within an academic or professional college or institution. Fellows typically hold advanced qualifications and established expertise in their field, and may have responsibilities for [[concepts/governance|governance]], mentoring, or academic [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] within their [[concepts/organization|organization]].

## Governance and Communications

In February 2026, the Board of a college issued a communication to Fellows and trainees regarding allegations of governance matters that had been circulated to college members. The Board expressed concern that Fellow [[entities/email|email]] addresses had been used to distribute communications about these allegations without [[concepts/authorization|authorization]], raising questions about the appropriate use of institutional communication channels and member [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
