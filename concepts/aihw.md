# Aihw

Aihw is a survey component designed to measure respondent familiarity with the term "digital health." It functions as a baseline assessment tool within broader digital health surveys, establishing the knowledge level of participants before they encounter more detailed questions about digital health practices, technologies, or services. This initial measurement helps contextualize subsequent data by accounting for varying levels of prior exposure to the concept.

The component employs a four-point Likert scale to capture self-assessed familiarity with the term. This standardized measurement allows researchers to quantify subjective understanding, providing a consistent metric for comparing familiarity across different demographic groups or survey iterations. By establishing this baseline, Aihw ensures that subsequent analysis of digital health engagement is interpreted with an accurate understanding of the respondents' starting point.
