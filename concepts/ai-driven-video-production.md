# Ai Driven Video Production

Ai Driven Video Production is a workflow that automates the conversion of text-based source materials into finished video content through the sequential application of AI tools. This process combines slide generation and video synthesis to reduce manual effort in content creation, enabling faster production of video resources from written materials such as research documents, long-form articles, or structured notes.

## Slide Generation

The typical workflow begins with the use of NotebookLM to generate slide decks from source documents. NotebookLM analyzes the provided text to extract key points and structure them into a coherent presentation format. This step transforms unstructured or semi-structured text into a visual outline, serving as the foundational asset for the subsequent video synthesis phase.

## Video Synthesis

The generated slides are then imported into Google Vids to create the final video output. Google Vids utilizes the slide content to automatically assemble video clips, add voiceovers, and apply visual transitions. This integration allows for the rapid production of unwatermarked videos directly from the NotebookLM output, streamlining the end-to-end creation process without requiring manual editing of individual frames or audio tracks.
