# Manual masking

Techniques for isolating subjects from backgrounds, focusing on high-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] edge control for complex boundaries.

## Core Methodologies
- **Hybrid Workflow**: Achieving professional-grade [[concepts/hair-masking]] requires a combination of [[concepts/ai-masking]] (utilizing [[concepts/select-subject]]) and traditional manual refinement techniques.
- **Optimization**: Specific [[concepts/photoshop]] configurations can be leveraged to enhance the accuracy of automated [[concepts/subject-selection|subject selection]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]].
- **[[concepts/problem-solving|Problem-Solving]]**: Successful [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] relies on integrating automated tools with manual pixel-level [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to resolve complex edge artifacts.
- **AI-Driven [[concepts/image-inpainting|Inpainting]]**: Modern workflows utilize [[concepts/comfyui]] with [[concepts/sam-segmentation|SAM]] for automatic, high-fidelity masking to enable [[concepts/targeted-image-editing|targeted image editing]] and inpainting, reducing manual effort while maintaining precision.
