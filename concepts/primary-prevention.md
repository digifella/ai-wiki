## Primary Prevention

Primary prevention refers to interventions designed to prevent the onset of disease or injury before they occur. This includes strategies such as [[concepts/health-promotion|health education]], lifestyle modifications, and environmental changes.

### Content Integration from Community Health: Prevention Levels & Determinants of Health (2026-04-11)
- **[[concepts/levels-of-prevention|Prevention levels]]** in [[concepts/community-health|community health]] are crucial for understanding the [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]] of interventions.
  - Primary prevention aims to stop diseases before they start through [[concepts/public-health|public health]] initiatives and individual lifestyle changes.
  - Secondary prevention involves [[concepts/secondary-prevention|early detection and treatment]] of disease to prevent progression.
  - Tertiary prevention focuses on reducing the negative impact of a disease that has already occurred.
- **[[concepts/determinants-of-health|Determinants of Health]]** include social, economic, and environmental factors that influence an individual’s [[concepts/health|health]] status. These determinants play a critical role in primary prevention efforts by addressing root [[concepts/causes|causes]] rather than symptoms.
