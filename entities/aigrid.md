# Aigrid

Aigrid is a YouTube channel dedicated to the analysis of artificial intelligence research, with a specific emphasis on computational architectures and methodologies that extend beyond large language models. The channel provides educational content regarding the evolving landscape of AI technology, examining emerging frameworks in machine learning and reasoning.

The primary focus of the channel's coverage is Meta's VL-JEPA (Vision-Language Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture). In addition to this specific model, Aigrid explores other non-LLM reasoning architectures, offering detailed examinations of alternative approaches to AI development and implementation.
