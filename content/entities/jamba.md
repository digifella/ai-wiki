# Jamba

Jamba is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) developed by [[entities/ai21-labs]], featuring a **[[concepts/hybrid-ssm-transformer|hybrid SSM-Transformer]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]]** and a **256k [[concepts/context-window|context window]]**. Released in [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]], it represents a new [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation model]] optimized for long-context processing.

* **Release:** Newly released by AI21 [[entities/labs|Labs]] (2026).
* **Flavors:** [[entities/jamba-mini-17|Jamba Mini 1.7]], [[entities/jamba-large-17|Jamba Large 1.7]].
* **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]:** [[concepts/hybrid-ssm-transformer|Hybrid SSM-Transformer]] foundation model (combining [[concepts/state-space-model]] and Transformer ([[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]])).
* **Key Feature:** 256k [[concepts/context-window|context window]] enables processing of extremely long inputs.
