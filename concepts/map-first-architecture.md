## Map-First Architecture

### Overview
The Map-First Architecture is an innovative approach to structuring data and context for [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], addressing the limitations of traditional [[concepts/traditional-rag|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) systems. This method emphasizes a hierarchical, [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]] that enhances AI's ability to process and utilize information effectively.

### Key Concepts
- **Structured Context:** A more organized way to feed [[concepts/contextual-information|contextual information]] into [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] compared to raw data dumps.
- **Hierarchical Data System:** Utilizes a tree-like structure to manage and present data for better comprehension by the AI, enabling it to navigate through complex information with [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].
- **Beyond [[concepts/rag-limitations|RAG Limitations]]:** Addresses issues such as context fragmentation and lack of coherent [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] in traditional retrieval-based systems.
