# AI Generated Documentary Production

AI Generated Documentary Production is a workflow for creating documentary content that uses advanced language models and [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] tools to automate key production stages. The process begins with source material collection—research documents, articles, interviews, and reference notes—which are then processed through AI systems like [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]. These tools synthesize the source materials into structured scripts and narrative frameworks, reducing the time required for traditional scriptwriting and research synthesis phases.

## Workflow Components

The typical workflow involves several distinct stages. Initial source materials are uploaded to language models capable of analyzing and extracting key information. The AI then generates documentary scripts that maintain fidelity to the source material while organizing content into narrative structures. Video generation tools subsequently create visual content to accompany the generated narration, either through [[concepts/text-to-video|text-to-video]] systems or by organizing existing footage. The final output combines the AI-generated script, narration, and visual elements into a completed documentary.

## Production Considerations

This approach offers faster turnaround times compared to conventional documentary production by automating research synthesis and initial scriptwriting. However, the quality and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of outputs depend significantly on the source materials provided and the specificity of the prompts used to guide the AI systems. Human oversight remains necessary for fact-checking, editorial decisions, and ensuring the final product meets intended [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and ethical requirements. The workflow is particularly suited to projects where source materials are well-documented and narrative structures are relatively straightforward.
