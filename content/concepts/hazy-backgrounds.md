# Hazy Backgrounds

Hazy backgrounds are a common challenge in [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]], where atmospheric conditions, lens flare, or moisture create a washed-out, low-[[concepts/contrast|contrast]] appearance in background elements. This loss of clarity and detail can make [[concepts/images|images]] appear flat and reduce overall visual impact, particularly in outdoor photography or shots taken through environmental haze.

## Dehazing with Photoshop 2026

Photoshop 2026 introduced an enhanced Object Selection tool that enables precise dehazing of backgrounds without affecting foreground subjects. This tool allows editors to isolate hazy areas and apply targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], restoring contrast and clarity while maintaining natural-looking results. The Object Selection tool's improved [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] reduces the need for manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]], making the dehazing process more efficient.

A [[entities/phlearn|PHLEARN]] [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] demonstrates the practical application of this technique, showing how to use the Object Selection tool to select hazy background areas and apply dehazing adjustments. The 6-minute tutorial covers the workflow for fixing flat colors and restoring depth to images affected by atmospheric haze, providing step-by-step guidance for photographers and image editors seeking to improve [[concepts/sharpness|image clarity]].
