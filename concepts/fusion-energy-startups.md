# Fusion Energy Startups

## Overview
Private sector investment in [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]] has surged, with numerous startups aiming to commercialize [[concepts/limitless-energy|fusion power]]. However, significant technical and timeline challenges persist, leading to critical scrutiny from the [[concepts/physics|physics]] community regarding the feasibility of current approaches.

## Key Critiques & Challenges

### Helion Energy
Despite substantial funding, [[entities/helion-energy|Helion Energy]] [[concepts/faces|faces]] intense skepticism regarding its technical roadmap and timeline.

*   **Physicist Critique:** Prominent physicists have argued that Helion's proposed $15 billion fusion machine is fundamentally unworkable [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-Physicists-Critique-Helion-Energys-15-Billion-Nuclear-Fu|Physicists' Critique: Helion Energy's $15 Billion Nuclear Fusion Machine Cannot Work]].
*   **Timeline Delays:** Startups like Helion have repeatedly pushed back completion dates, raising questions about the realism of their commercialization goals.
*   **Technical Feasibility:** The critique highlights unresolved plasma confinement and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] hurdles specific to Helion's magnetic confinement approach.
