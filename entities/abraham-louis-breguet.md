# Abraham-Louis Breguet

**[[concepts/abraham-louis-breguet|Abraham-Louis Breguet]]** (1747–1823) was a Swiss watchmaker, inventor, and entrepreneur who is widely regarded as one of the most important figures in the history of horology. He founded the Breguet brand and is credited with numerous innovations that defined modern watchmaking.

## Key Innovations
*   **The Tourbillon**: Invented in 1795 and patented in 1801, this mechanism was designed to counteract the effects of gravity on the accuracy of pocket watches. By mounting the escapement and balance wheel in a rotating cage, it averages out positional errors caused by gravity.
*   **Breguet Overcoil**: A modification to the balance spring that improves isochronism.
*   **Parachute Shock Protection**: An early system to protect the balance staff from shocks.
*   **Skeleton Dials**: Popularized the aesthetic of visible movement components.

*   **Gravity Compensation**: The rotating cage continuously changes the position of the balance wheel and escapement, ensuring that gravitational errors are averaged out over time.

## Legacy
Breguet's work established standards for precision and elegance that persist in high-end horology. His influence extends beyond mechanics to the aesthetic language of luxury watches, including the signature Breguet hands and guilloché dials.
