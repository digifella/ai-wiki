# Invisible Universe

The Invisible Universe encompasses the vast, unseen components of the cosmos that dictate its structure, evolution, and expansion. At the core of this concept lies the mystery of [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark matter and dark energy]], which constitute the majority of the mass and [[concepts/calorie-count|energy content]] of the universe, yet remain largely undetectable by conventional means.

## Dark Matter and Cosmic Mysteries

Dark matter is a hypothetical form of matter that interacts gravitationally but does not emit, absorb, or reflect [[concepts/light|light]], making it "invisible." Understanding its role is crucial to understanding the large-scale structure of the universe.
