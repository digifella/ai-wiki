# Majorana 1

**Majorana 1** is the inaugural quantum [[concepts/cpu|processor]] developed by [[entities/microsoft]] as part of its Quantum Development Kit initiative, designed to demonstrate the viability of topological [[concepts/qubits|qubits]]. Unlike superconducting or trapped-ion systems, Majorana 1 relies on Majorana [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] modes to encode quantum information, [[entities/theoretically-media|theoretically]] offering inherent [[concepts/secure|protection]] against decoherence and local noise.
