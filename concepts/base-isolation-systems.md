# Base Isolation Systems

[[concepts/earthquake-bearings|Base isolation systems]] are a critical component in [[concepts/earthquake-resistant-design|earthquake-resistant design]] that aims to protect buildings and other structures from the damaging effects of seismic activity. These innovative [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] solutions help mitigate structural damage by decoupling a structure from its foundation, allowing it to move independently during an earthquake.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/earthquake-base-isolation|Seismic Isolation]]**: A method used to reduce building movements caused by [[concepts/earthquakes|earthquakes]].
- **Bearings**: Critical components in base isolation systems that allow relative [[concepts/exercise|movement]] between the superstructure and substructure of buildings.
- **Critical Infrastructure [[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]**: The ability of essential services to maintain operations during and after a disaster.
