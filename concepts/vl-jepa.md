# VL-JEPA

**Backlinks:**

## Overview
**[[concepts/cause-and-effect|VL-JEPA]]** (Vision-Language [[concepts/joint-embedding-predictive-architecture|Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture]]) is a research-driven approach to AGI that prioritizes vision-based modeling over purely linguistic [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]].

## Key Principles
- **Research Origin:** Developed by [[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] and the [[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] [[entities/meta-fair-lab|FAIR]] Lab.
- **Architectural Shift:** Represents a strategic departure from current [[concepts/generative-ai]] paradigms and [[concepts/llm]]-centric architectures (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt]]).
- **Core Thesis:** Operates on the philosophical and technical premise that "**Language is not Intelligence**," suggesting that intelligence requires a model of the [[entities/earth|world]] that transcends text-based generation.
