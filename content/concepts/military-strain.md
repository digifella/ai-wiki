# Military Strain

## Definition
**Military Strain** refers to the cumulative degradation of a military force's combat effectiveness, logistical capacity, and operational readiness caused by sustained [[concepts/conflict|conflict]], attrition, resource depletion, or systemic inefficiencies. Manifestations include equipment shortages, personnel exhaustion, logistical bottlenecks, mobilization acceleration, and reduced capacity for ceremonial or symbolic displays of [[concepts/power|power]].

## Key Indicators
- **Material Depletion:** High rates of equipment loss outpacing production or replacement [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
- **Logistical [[concepts/stress|Stress]]:** Inability to sustain forward operations due to supply chain fractures or transport limitations.
- **Personnel Fatigue:** Degradation of unit [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] and morale due to prolonged [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] or insufficient rotation.
- **Symbolic Retraction:** Downsizing of public military parades or displays, often signaling priority shifts toward combat necessities.
