# Poaching Eggs

Poaching is a cooking method in which eggs are gently simmered in water without their shells, typically until the whites are set while the yolk remains soft. The technique is valued in cooking for producing eggs with a delicate texture and is commonly served on toast, salads, or as part of dishes like eggs Benedict.

## The Vinegar Pre-Soak Method

A technique demonstrated by cooking educator PapaMau involves pre-soaking eggs in vinegar before poaching. The acidity of the vinegar is thought to help the egg whites coagulate more rapidly upon contact with the hot water, resulting in a tighter, more compact shape with less wispy strands. This method aims to reduce the need for excessive swirling in the water or the use of additional vinegar in the poaching liquid itself, offering an alternative approach to achieving a neat presentation.
