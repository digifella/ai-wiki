# YKK

YKK is a Japanese company that has become synonymous with [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] [[concepts/buttons|zippers]], buckles, and other fastening solutions used in clothing, luggage, and various industrial applications.

## History
- Founded by Tadao Yoshida in 1934.
- Initially named "[[concepts/ykk|Yoshida Kogyo Kabushikigaisha]]," it later became known as YKK after the company's trademarked brand name.
- Known for its commitment to [[concepts/vertical-integration|vertical integration]] and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]], ensuring every part of a [[concepts/zipper-history|zipper]] is manufactured under strict standards.

## Products
- [[concepts/zippers|Zippers]] (standard and custom designs)
- Buttons

## Philosophy
- The "Cycle of Goodness" [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] emphasizes mutual benefit for all stakeholders, reflecting a holistic approach to business [[concepts/ethics|ethics]].

## Engineering Innovations
- Development of the zipper pull and slider that can smoothly engage and disengage zipper teeth.
- Continuous research in materials [[concepts/science|science]] to create more durable and aesthetically pleasing [[concepts/fastening-devices|fasteners]].
