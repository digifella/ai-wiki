# Google Search Ai Mode

[[concepts/google-searchs-ai-mode|Google Search AI]] Mode is a feature that integrates [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities into [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s search platform and related products. The mode enhances the search experience by incorporating [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] across Google's ecosystem, enabling users to access advanced capabilities without leaving the search interface.

## Core Capabilities

The feature encompasses multiple AI-powered functionalities including [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]], note-taking tools, and other productivity features. These capabilities allow users to generate content, organize information, and interact with search results in more dynamic ways than traditional search interfaces provide.

## Integration Across Google Products

Google Search AI Mode is designed to work across multiple Google products and services, creating a unified experience for users. This [[concepts/integration|integration]] reflects Google's broader strategy of embedding [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] throughout its suite of [[concepts/software|applications]] rather than maintaining them as separate, isolated tools.
