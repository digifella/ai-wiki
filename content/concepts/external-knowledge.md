# External Knowledge

External knowledge refers to information and context sourced from materials outside of a system's [[concepts/training-data|training data]]. This encompasses documents, databases, APIs, and other external sources that augment an AI system's [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] runtime. By incorporating external knowledge, [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] can access up-to-date information, [[concepts/domain-specific-data|domain-specific data]], and proprietary materials that would otherwise be unavailable or outdated.

## Integration with AI Agents

In the context of [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]], external knowledge is typically integrated through [[concepts/context-engineering|context engineering]] techniques. This involves retrieving relevant information from external sources and providing it to the [[entities/agent|agent]] within its input context, allowing the system to reference and reason over current, accurate, or specialized information during task execution. This approach enables [[concepts/agents|agents]] to perform tasks that require knowledge beyond their [[concepts/language-data|training data]] while maintaining [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and relevance.

## Practical Applications

[[concepts/external-knowledge-integration|External knowledge integration]] has become central to building functional AI systems for specialized domains. [[concepts/software|Applications]] [[concepts/range|range]] from marketing [[concepts/automation|automation]] teams that leverage external brand guidelines and market data, to [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] systems that integrate with personal information repositories, to AI co-workers that access organizational documentation and real-time data sources. The effectiveness of these systems depends on both the quality of the external knowledge source and the efficiency of the retrieval and integration mechanism.
