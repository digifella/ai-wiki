# Gen Alpha

**Generation Alpha** refers to the demographic cohort succeeding Generation Z, typically defined as individuals born between **2010 and 2024**. This group is characterized by being the first to be born entirely in the 21st century, growing up with ubiquitous access to smartphones, tablets, and AI-driven media from infancy.

## Key Characteristics
*   **Digital Natives:** First generation to interact with touchscreens and voice assistants before learning to write.
*   **Educational Shifts:** Increasing reliance on digital learning tools and adaptive software in primary education.
*   **Cognitive Trends:** Emerging data suggests shifts in [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] spans, reading comprehension, and problem-solving methodologies compared to previous cohorts.

## Literacy and Cognitive Impact
Recent analysis highlights a concerning trend in foundational skills among this cohort, particularly regarding traditional literacy and [[concepts/numeracy|numeracy]].

*   **Declining Basic Skills:** Evidence points to a widespread decline in basic literacy, numeracy, and [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving skills]] among Gen Alpha.
*   **Technological Influence:** The pervasive integration of technology in early childhood development is cited as a primary driver for these cognitive shifts.
*   **[[concepts/educational-crisis|Educational Crisis]]:** The education system is struggling to adapt to the changing cognitive profiles of students born after 2010.
