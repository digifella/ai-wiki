# 3d Character

A 3D character is a digital model of a human, animal, or creature constructed using [[concepts/computer-graphics|computer graphics]] software. Unlike 2D illustrations, 3D characters exist as three-dimensional data structures composed of [[concepts/nodes|vertices]], edges, and polygons that form a mesh. This geometric foundation allows the character to be viewed and manipulated from any angle within digital space, making it suitable for animation, interactive media, and real-time visualization.

Creating a 3D character typically involves several sequential stages. Modeling establishes the basic geometry of the figure, followed by rigging, which adds an internal skeleton to enable [[concepts/exercise|movement]]. Texturing and shading then apply surface details and colors, while lighting and [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]] finalize the visual output for specific contexts such as film, video games, or [[concepts/virtual-reality|virtual reality]] environments.

Recent advancements have integrated AI-assisted tools into the workflow to streamline processes like mesh generation, [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] synthesis, and motion capture data cleanup. These technologies reduce the manual labor required for high-fidelity assets, allowing creators to focus on artistic direction and narrative integration. The resulting characters serve as central elements in interactive [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]], [[concepts/simulation|simulation]], and immersive digital experiences.
