[[concepts/copilot-chat|Copilot]] is an [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] within [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] 365 enabling natural language [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]].

- **Agent Function**: Agents look up [[concepts/policies|policies]] and respond to user queries (e.g., [[entities/microsoft-365]] support).
- **Preview Status**: [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] are currently in preview mode.
- **Example Agent**: [[entities/georgia-engel|Georgia Engel]]'s Microsoft 365 training agent (see 2026 04 14 Creating [[concepts/copilot-agents|CoPilot Agents]]):
  - Answers frequently asked questions
  - Guides through common tasks and issues
  - Uses friendly, helpful, collaborative [[concepts/tone|tone]]
- **[[concepts/local-ai-model|Local AI]] & [[concepts/hybrid-intelligence|Hybrid Intelligence]]**: Microsoft is shifting towards "[[concepts/local-ai-processing|Hybrid Intelligence]]," empowering users with powerful AI capabilities directly on [[entities/windows|Windows]] devices to reduce cloud reliance.
  - Focuses on [[concepts/local-execution|on-device processing]] for [[entities/github-copilot|Copilot]].
