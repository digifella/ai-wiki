# Meta

- Data centers are pivotal infrastructures for the internet and [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]], hosting servers that store, manage, and process vast amounts of data.
- The growing demand for data [[entities/storage|storage]] and processing [[concepts/power|power]] has led to an increase in the number and size of data centers globally.
- With the rise of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] (AI), [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], and [[concepts/big-data|big data]] analytics, the computational demands on data centers have significantly increased.
- **Generative Advancements**: [[concepts/models|Models]] such as [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s [[concepts/gemini-2.5-flash-lite|Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite]] are demonstrating high [[concepts/speed|speed]] and efficiency in generating UI [[concepts/code|code]], [[concepts/virtual-operating-systems|virtual operating systems]], and functional application prototypes (e.g., "Travel" apps) from context.
- These technological advancements are driving up energy consumption rates [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] unprecedented levels, leading to higher carbon footprints unless offset by renewable energy sources.
- Meta has pioneered [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] development, exemplified by founding engineers (e.g., [[entities/harper|Harper]], a [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] AI expert and former Meta engineer) who built foundational [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]] now adopted industry-wide.
- **Non-[[concepts/llm-models|LLM Architectures]]**: Through the [[entities/meta-fair-lab|Meta FAIR Lab]], researchers such as [[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] are developing [[concepts/vl-jepa|VL-JEPA]] ([[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]-Language Joint Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]), a shift away from [[concepts/llm|LLM]]-centric [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] toward vision-based [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] for [[concepts/agi|AGI]], based on the thesis that "language is not intelligence."
