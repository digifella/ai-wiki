# Stellar Kinematics

**Stellar kinematics** is the branch of [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]] that studies the motions of [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] and other celestial bodies within galaxies, focusing on their positions, velocities, and accelerations. It serves as a primary tool for mapping the [[concepts/gravitational-potential|gravitational potential]] of galaxies and inferring the presence of unseen mass.

## Core Concepts

*   **Rotation [[concepts/curves|Curves]]:** The plot of orbital [[concepts/velocity|velocity]] of visible stars/gas versus their radial distance from the [[concepts/galactic-center|galactic center]]. In the absence of dark matter, velocities should decline following Keplerian dynamics ($v \propto r^{-1/2}$) beyond the visible disk.
*   **Mass Discrepancy:** The difference between the dynamical mass (inferred from kinematics) and the luminous mass (inferred from [[concepts/light|light]]). This discrepancy is the primary evidence for [[concepts/dark-matter]].
*   **Virial Theorem:** Used to estimate the total mass of [[concepts/galaxy-clusters|galaxy clusters]] based on the velocity dispersion of member galaxies.

## Theoretical Frameworks

### Dark Matter Hypothesis
*   Posits that the majority of mass in the universe is non-baryonic and does not interact electromagnetically.
*   Explains flat rotation curves by proposing a massive, extended dark matter halo that dominates the gravitational potential at large radii.
*   [[concepts/standard-model-of-particle-physics|Standard model]]: $\Lambda$CDM.

### Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND)
*   Proposes that Newton's laws of motion require modification at very low accelerations ($a < a_0$).
*   Predicts flat rotation curves without invoking dark matter.
*   Challenges arise in explaining cluster dynamics and the [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] power spectrum.

## Recent Developments & Challenges

*   **[[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]] Rotation Data:** Recent analyses of Milky Way kinematics have presented tensions for both standard dark matter models and [[concepts/modified-newtonian-dynamics-mond|MOND]].
    *   New data suggests rotation profiles that defy simple explanation by either paradigm.
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Milky-Way-Rotation-Data-Challenges-Dark-Matter-and-Modif|Milky Way Rotation Data Challenges Dark Matter and Modified Gravity]] for detailed clip [[concepts/notes|notes]].
*   **Implications:**
    *   May require more complex dark matter distributions (e.g., baryon [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] effects).
    *   May necessitate refinements to MOND or alternative [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] theories.
    *   Highlights the need for high-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] astrometry (e.g., from *[[entities/earth|Gaia]]* mission) to constrain galactic mass profiles.
