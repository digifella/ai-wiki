# Camera Raw Team

The **[[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] Team** is the internal group at [[entities/adobe]] responsible for the development, maintenance, and enhancement of [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] (ACR) and [[concepts/lightroom]] raw processing engines.
