# Primordial Universe

The primordial universe refers to the earliest stages of the cosmos before the formation of galaxies and [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]]. This era was characterized by extreme conditions including high temperatures and [[concepts/density|density]], leading to the development of the first matter from particles.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/big-bang|Big Bang]]**: The initial [[concepts/intelligence-explosion|singularity]] marking the beginning of time and space.
- **[[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background|Cosmic Microwave Background]] (CMB)**: Radiation left over from an early stage in the universe's expansion.
- **Dark Ages**: Period following the recombination era when the universe was dark due to a lack of luminous sources.

### Recent Developments
- On 2026-04-12, [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]] discussed groundbreaking evidence for [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|Population III stars]] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGekUw84lxQ)). These theoretical stars are believed to be the first generation of stars in the universe.
  - The [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] ([[entities/jwst|JWST]]) detected signs of [[concepts/population-iii-stars|Population III stars]] in [[entities/gn-z11|GN-z11]], a galaxy at redshift z=11.09 ([source](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGekUw84lxQ)).
  - This discovery marks a significant milestone in understanding the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] and its evolution.
