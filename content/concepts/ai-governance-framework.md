# Ai Governance Framework

## Source Notes
- 2026-04-23: Objective New [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] are being rapidly adopted by [[concepts/health|healthcare]] professionals, yet existing [[concepts/organisational-governance|organisational governance]] often lacks the processes necessary to oversee their safe and responsible use. Previous [[concepts/ai-governance|AI governance frameworks]] have largely focused on high-level AI [[concepts/ethics|ethics]] ([[entities/bmj-review|BMJ Review]])
- 2026-04-14: Previous AI governance frameworks have largely focused on high-level AI [[concepts/ethics|ethics]] principles, leaving [[concepts/healthcare-organisations|healthcare organisations]] struggling to translate these principles into practice, assess risk, and embed [[concepts/ai-oversight|AI oversight]] into existing processes. This study aimed to develop and validate a practice-oriented AI governance framework.
