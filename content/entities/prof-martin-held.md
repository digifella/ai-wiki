# Prof Martin Held

Prof Martin Held is an academic affiliated with both the University of Copenhagen and [[entities/deakin-university|Deakin University]]. He is known for his work in digital [[concepts/health|health]] interventions, particularly in the development of the [[concepts/ses-family|SES Family]] modules, a suite of [[concepts/software|applications]] designed to support [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] and [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] resolution.

## SES Family Modules

The SES Family project, developed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the University of Copenhagen, focuses on addressing the "[[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]"—the tension between the desire for [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] solutions and resistance to their actual use. The modules include interventions based on Jungian grief [[concepts/models|models]] and address topics such as conflict and resolution. The accompanying platform, [[entities/sesfamilycom|SESFamily.com]], operates with a team of approximately 20 employees and features an interactive app with a simple, user-focused layout.
