# Surya

Surya (Sanskrit: सूर्य, IAST: Sūrya) is the Hindu god of the sun, depicted as a charioteer driving a seven-horse chariot across the sky. Revered as the source of life, energy, and cosmic order, Surya is central to Vedic hymns and solar worship in [[concepts/hinduism]].
