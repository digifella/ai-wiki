# Master Of Editing

Master Of Editing is a course taught by Will that focuses on the [[concepts/catalog|Lightroom catalog]] system and [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] techniques. The course covers practical approaches to organizing and managing photo libraries within Lightroom's catalog framework, alongside editing workflows for processing [[concepts/images|images]].

## Course Content

The curriculum includes instruction on [[concepts/dark-and-moody-photo-processing|dark and moody photo processing]] methods, with emphasis on techniques for dramatic photo enhancement. The course teaches how to use Lightroom's tools and systems to achieve specific aesthetic outcomes in post-production work.
