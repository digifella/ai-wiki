# Mixed Reality

Mixed Reality (MR) is a technology that blends digital content with the physical world, enabling interactive experiences where virtual and real elements coexist and interact in real time. Unlike Virtual Reality, which creates entirely digital environments, or Augmented Reality, which overlays digital elements on a camera feed, Mixed Reality anchors digital objects to physical spaces with spatial awareness and persistent interaction capabilities. This positioning allows users to place, manipulate, and interact with virtual objects as though they exist in the real environment.

The technology relies on advanced spatial mapping and sensor fusion to understand the geometry and lighting of the surrounding environment. By registering virtual objects to specific physical coordinates, MR ensures that digital content remains stable and responsive to user movement and environmental changes. This level of integration distinguishes it from simpler overlay techniques, allowing for occlusion, realistic lighting interactions, and physical collision detection between virtual and real-world objects.

Within the context of Microsoft Studio 2, Mixed Reality serves as a foundational layer for immersive development tools and collaborative platforms. It enables the creation of applications that leverage the full depth of the user's physical space, supporting complex workflows that require the simultaneous presence of digital assets and physical context. This integration facilitates new modes of interaction and productivity by removing the boundary between the digital interface and the physical workspace.
