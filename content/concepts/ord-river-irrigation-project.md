# Ord River Irrigation Project

The Ord River Irrigation Project is a significant agricultural and infrastructure project in Australia's Northern Territory. Initiated in the 1960s with the ambitious goal of transforming vast tropical lands into productive farmland, it has faced numerous economic challenges over its decades-long history.

- **Investment**: Multi-billion dollar investment aimed at developing [[concepts/northern-australia|Northern Australia]].
- **Agricultural Potential**: Aims to convert sparsely populated regions into agricultural hubs.
- **Environmental Impact**: Struggles with unforeseen environmental challenges affecting productivity and sustainability.
- **Economic Failure**: Despite substantial funding, the project has faced economic setbacks and criticism for its lack of returns.
