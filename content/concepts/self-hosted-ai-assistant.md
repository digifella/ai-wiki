# Self Hosted AI Assistant

A self-hosted [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] is a personal [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] system that runs on your own [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] or infrastructure rather than relying on [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]]. This approach gives users direct [[concepts/power|control]] over their data, [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and the [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] dedicated to the assistant. Self-hosted solutions are typically built on [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] frameworks and [[concepts/models|models]], allowing for [[concepts/customization|customization]] and transparency in how the system operates.

## Clawdbot

[[concepts/remote-chat|Clawdbot]] is an example of an open-source, self-hosted [[concepts/personal-ai-assistant|personal AI assistant]]. It functions as a local alternative to [[concepts/cloud-ai|cloud-based AI]] assistants, enabling users to deploy and run their own instance without depending on external services. By hosting the assistant locally, users maintain ownership of their conversations and data while avoiding potential privacy concerns associated with [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud platforms]].

## Key Benefits

[[concepts/self-hosted-ai|Self-hosted AI]] assistants offer several practical advantages. They reduce reliance on commercial AI services and their associated subscription costs or usage limitations. Users can modify the underlying models and [[concepts/code|code]] to suit specific needs, and they retain complete control over when and how their data is processed. For those with technical capability, self-hosting also provides the opportunity to experiment with different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and implementations.
