# Kagra

KAGRA (Kamioka Gravitational Wave Detector) is a ground-based gravitational wave observatory located in the Kamioka mine in Hida, Gifu Prefecture, [[entities/japan|Japan]]. Operated by the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan (NAOJ) in collaboration with international partners, it is one of the [[entities/earth|world]]'s major laser interferometers designed to detect ripples in spacetime caused by violent cosmic events, such as colliding [[concepts/black-holes|black holes]] and [[concepts/neutron-stars|neutron stars]]. The facility is distinguished by its underground location, which reduces seismic noise, and its use of cryogenic sapphire mirrors to minimize thermal noise, enhancing its sensitivity to high-frequency [[concepts/gravitational-waves|gravitational waves]].

The detector operates as part of a global network alongside the [[concepts/ligo|LIGO]] and [[concepts/virgo|Virgo]] collaborations. This international partnership allows for the triangulation of gravitational wave sources, improving the [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] of sky [[concepts/multi-language-support|localization]] and confirming detections through independent [[concepts/verification|verification]]. KAGRA's unique technical specifications, including its 3-kilometer arm length and advanced cryogenic technology, complement the capabilities of other detectors, particularly in the mid-to-high frequency range of the gravitational wave spectrum.

Recent scientific discussions involving KAGRA have focused on analyzing data for evidence of sub-solar mass [[concepts/primordial-black-hole|primordial black holes]]. These theoretical objects, formed in the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] rather than through [[concepts/gravitational-collapse|stellar collapse]], represent a significant area of interest in [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]]. The integration of KAGRA's data with that of other detectors provides critical constraints on the abundance and properties of such exotic matter, contributing to broader efforts to understand the [[concepts/writing|composition]] and evolution of the cosmos.
