# Credibility Assessment

**Credibility assessment** is the systematic process of evaluating the trustworthiness, [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and authority of information sources. It involves analyzing [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], authorship, publication venues, and content signals to assign a credibility tier.

## Key Dimensions
- **Source [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: [[concepts/verification|Verification]] of origin and lack of tampering.
- **Publication Venue**: Evaluation of [[concepts/document-review|peer-review]] status and journal rankings.
- **Authorship**: [[concepts/expertise|Expertise]] and affiliation of creators.
- **Content Signals**: [[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]], citation practices, and [[concepts/tone|tone]].
