# Turkish Government

The Republic of Turkey operates as a presidential republic with a unicameral legislature. The President serves as both head of state and head of government, wielding executive authority over the country's administration. The Grand National Assembly (Türkiye Büyük Millet Meclisi) functions as the sole legislative body, comprising 600 members responsible for enacting laws and representing the Turkish population. An independent judiciary forms the third branch, interpreting laws and administering justice according to the Turkish legal system.

## Administrative Structure

The administrative framework of the Turkish government is centralized, with the executive branch holding significant influence over policy implementation and regulatory enforcement. This structure impacts various state services, including immigration and border control procedures. The Ministry of Foreign Affairs and the Ministry of Interior are key entities responsible for managing international relations and domestic security, respectively.

## Visa and Entry Regulations

The Turkish government manages entry requirements for foreign nationals through its diplomatic missions and online portals. The e-visa system allows eligible travelers to obtain authorization for short-term visits without visiting an embassy. This digital process is administered by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs and is subject to specific eligibility criteria based on nationality and purpose of travel. Regulations regarding visa exemptions, extensions, and entry conditions are periodically updated by relevant government decrees.
