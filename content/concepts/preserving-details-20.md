# Preserving Details 2.0

Preserving Details 2.0 refers to a comparison of three AI-driven [[concepts/image-size|upscaling tools]] available in [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]], as documented in a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] by [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]] from [[entities/photoshopcafe|PhotoshopCAFE]]. These tools represent [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s generative upscaling [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], which use machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] to enlarge [[concepts/images|images]] while attempting to maintain or reconstruct fine details that would otherwise be lost in traditional upscaling methods.

## Overview of AI Upscaling in Photoshop

The three upscaling tools compared in the [[entities/adobe-photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta version]] each employ different AI approaches to image enlargement. Rather than simply interpolating pixels, these generative methods analyze image content and intelligently fill in detail information, making them suitable for various types of source material including photographs and artwork. The comparison allows users to understand the strengths and limitations of each approach for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Practical Application

For photographers and digital artists, these upscaling tools address a common [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] challenge: increasing image dimensions without visible quality loss. By leveraging AI trained on large image datasets, Photoshop's [[concepts/ai-powered-upscaling|generative upscale]] features can produce results that preserve recognizable details and textures better than traditional [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] methods. The availability of multiple tools within the same [[concepts/software|software]] allows users to experiment and select the most appropriate option for their specific project requirements.
