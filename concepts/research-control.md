# Research Control

Research Control is a feature within the ChatGPT Deep Research update that grants users granular authority over the information sources utilized during the research process. This functionality enables individuals to specify and manage the origin of data, allowing for more customized and targeted information gathering that aligns with specific requirements and preferences.

Through this control mechanism, users can influence the selection and prioritization of sources that inform the final research output. By managing which materials are included or excluded, the system tailors results to meet defined quality standards and contextual needs, ensuring that the generated insights are derived from trusted or relevant domains as determined by the user.
