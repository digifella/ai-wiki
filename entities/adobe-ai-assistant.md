# Adobe Ai Assistant

The Adobe Ai Assistant is a feature integrated into Adobe Photoshop that leverages artificial intelligence to streamline design workflows and reduce repetitive tasks. The tool is designed to help designers and image editors work more efficiently by automating common operations across projects of varying complexity.

## Automated Layer Renaming

One of the primary capabilities of the Ai Assistant is automated layer renaming. This function analyzes the content of individual layers and assigns descriptive names based on the visual elements present, such as "Background," "Text," or "Shape." This automation helps maintain organized file structures, particularly in complex documents with numerous layers, facilitating easier navigation and collaboration.

## Generative Markup Tools

The assistant also includes generative markup tools that assist in the structural organization of design files. These tools utilize AI to interpret layer relationships and suggest logical groupings or naming conventions. By providing these suggestions, the feature aims to standardize file management practices and reduce the manual effort required to prepare files for handoff or further editing.
