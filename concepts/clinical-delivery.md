# Clinical Delivery

**Clinical delivery** encompasses the [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], frameworks, and practices through which [[concepts/health|healthcare]] services are provided to patients. This includes traditional in-person care models as well as emerging remote care modalities.

## Key Components

- **Service Modalities**: Includes face-to-face consultations, [[concepts/telehealth]] services, and hybrid care models.
- **Workforce Integration**: Involves [[entities/allied-health]] professionals working alongside medical practitioners to ensure comprehensive [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]].
- **Regulatory & Safety Frameworks**: Guided by health authorities to ensure quality and [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]] are met.
