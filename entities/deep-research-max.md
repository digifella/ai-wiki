# Deep Research Max

Deep Research Max is an AI-powered research tool developed by Google that utilizes the Gemini 2.5 Flash API to enhance research capabilities. The platform combines large language model processing with access to proprietary data sources and automated visualization generation, enabling users to conduct comprehensive research across multiple domains.

The tool leverages the Gemini 2.5 Flash API to process and analyze information, integrating both public and proprietary datasets to provide research insights. A key feature is its ability to automatically generate visualizations from the analyzed data, allowing for more intuitive interpretation of complex research findings. This integration aims to streamline the workflow for users requiring detailed, data-driven analysis.
