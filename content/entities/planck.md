# Planck

## Overview
The **Planck mission** is a space-based observatory designed to map the [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] (CMB) with high precision, providing critical data for the Lambda-CDM model, [[concepts/dark-matter]], and Dark Energy research.

## Key Research & Observations
- **Curvature Tension:** Recent analysis of Planck data identifies a significant unresolved problem regarding the universe's geometry, suggesting the universe may not be perfectly flat.
- **Impact on [[concepts/cosmology|Cosmology]]:** This tension challenges the fundamental assumptions of Inflation [[concepts/theory|Theory]] and necessitates a re-evaluation of the universe's large-scale [[concepts/structure|structure]].
- **Reference:** Universe Curvature Tension: Planck Data Challenges Flatness, Impacts Inflation Theory
