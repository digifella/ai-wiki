# Bill Leung

## Overview
Chef and culinary educator associated with [[entities/the-woks-of-life|The Woks of Life]], specializing in technique-driven Asian cuisine and reproducible home-cooking methodologies. Focuses on authentic flavor development, high-heat wok techniques, and accessible ingredient optimization.

## Key Works & Demonstrations
- [[concepts/singapore-noodles]] [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] guide: Breaks down the iconic Chinese stir-fry featuring [[concepts/rice-noodles]], prawns, and a balanced curry-ketchup sauce base, prioritizing [[concepts/texture|texture]] [[concepts/power|control]] and sauce [[concepts/integration|integration]] over commercial shortcuts [[lab-notes/2026-05-16-Woks-of-Lifes-Bill-Leung-Mastering-Superior-Homemade-Sin|Woks of Life's Bill Leung: Mastering Superior Homemade Singapore Noodles]].
- Emphasizes wok hei generation through proper [[concepts/temperature-management|heat management]], ingredient staging, and continuous motion stir-frying.
- Demonstrates sauce emulsification and seasoning layering techniques to replicate restaurant-quality profiles in residential kitchens.
