# Computer Vision

Computer vision is a field of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] concerned with enabling computers to interpret and understand visual information from images and video. It involves processing, analyzing, and extracting meaningful data from visual inputs, allowing AI systems to perform tasks that typically require human [[concepts/visual-perception|visual perception]]. The discipline bridges computer [[concepts/science|science]], [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], and cognitive science to develop [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] and systems that can perceive and [[concepts/purpose|reason]] about the visual [[entities/earth|world]].

## Core Approaches

Computer vision systems typically use combinations of [[concepts/image-input-processing|image processing]], [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], and [[concepts/vanishing-gradient-problem|deep learning]] techniques. Traditional approaches involve feature detection, edge recognition, and geometric analysis. Modern systems increasingly rely on convolutional [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]] and other deep learning architectures.

Recent advancements focus on generative capabilities, particularly through [[concepts/diffusion-models|diffusion models]] for high-fidelity image and video synthesis. Key insights from industry research include:

*   **Large-Scale Diffusion Architectures:** Research by [[entities/sander-dieleman|Sander Dieleman]] at [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] highlights the technical challenges and solutions in building large-scale [[concepts/image-and-video-diffusion-models|diffusion models]] for both image and [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
*   **[[concepts/ai-generated-videos|Generative Video]]:** Moving beyond static images, modern computer vision systems are increasingly capable of generating coherent video sequences, requiring advanced temporal [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
*   **Technical Implementation:** Effective large-scale generation requires careful balancing of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]], [[concepts/large-language-model-scaling|model scaling laws]], and intuitive design choices to maintain quality and coherence.
