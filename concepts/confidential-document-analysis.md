# Confidential Document Analysis

## Overview
The practice of examining restricted or proprietary information to extract insights while maintaining strict [[concepts/privacy-focused-computing|data sovereignty]] and preventing unauthorized disclosure.

## Key Strategies for Sensitive Data
- **Local Processing**: Utilizing on-device or [[concepts/on-premise-deployment|private infrastructure]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to process confidential [[concepts/document-processing|document analysis]] without transmitting data to external cloud [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]].
- **Leak [[concepts/preventive-care|Prevention]]**: Implementing [[concepts/hard-constraints|strict boundaries]] to ensure sensitive company data never leaves the [[concepts/secure|secure]] environment, addressing critical challenges in [[concepts/data-management|data governance]].
- **Offline [[concepts/ai-integration|AI Integration]]**: Leveraging AI capabilities to read and analyze files that cannot be uploaded to [[entities/public-works|public services]] due to [[concepts/security|security]] [[concepts/policies|policies]].
