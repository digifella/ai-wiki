# Business Growth: Strategies and Concepts

Business growth refers to the expansion of a company's market presence, revenue, profit margins, or any other critical success factors. It involves various strategies such as market penetration, product development, market development, and diversification.

### Key Concepts

- **Market Penetration**: Increasing sales of existing products in current markets.
- **Product Development**: Introducing new products to the existing market.
- **Market Development**: Entering new markets with existing products.
- **Diversification**: Entering new markets with new products.
