# RHINs

**[[concepts/rural-and-remote-health|Regional Health]] Information Networks** (RHINs) are integrated [[concepts/healthcare-informatization|health information systems]] designed to facilitate the exchange of health data among [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] providers, patients, and [[concepts/public-health|public health]] agencies within a specific geographic region. They aim to improve [[concepts/coordination|care coordination]], reduce costs, and enhance [[concepts/population-health|population health]] management through interoperable data sharing.

## Core Components & Challenges

*   **Interoperability:** Standardizing data formats and exchange protocols across disparate EHR systems.
*   **[[concepts/governance|Governance]]:** Establishing legal, ethical, and technical frameworks for [[concepts/data-ownership|data ownership]], [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and [[concepts/security|security]].
*   **[[concepts/big-data|Big Data]] Integration:** Managing the volume, velocity, and variety of health data to support [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
