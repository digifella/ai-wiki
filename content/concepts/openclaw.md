## OpenClaw Overview
[[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] project by [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]] designed to provide a [[concepts/secure|secure]] framework for deploying and managing [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] within enterprises. It aims to address the challenges of integrating AI into traditional business environments while ensuring robust [[concepts/security|security]] measures are in place.

### Key Features
- **Security:** Implements advanced [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] protocols tailored for [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI applications]].
- **Scalability:** Supports large-scale [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] across multiple enterprise systems.
- **Flexibility:** Offers [[concepts/ai-workflow|customization]] options to fit various organizational needs and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements.
