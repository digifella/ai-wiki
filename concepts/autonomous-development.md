# Autonomous Development

**Autonomous Development** refers to the capacity of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to iteratively design, test, and improve their own architectures and capabilities without direct human intervention. This concept is central to discussions on [[concepts/self-improvement]] and the trajectory toward [[concepts/agi]].

## Key Dynamics

*   **Iterative Enhancement**: Systems utilize output from previous iterations as input for subsequent training or architectural [[concepts/software-updates|updates]].
*   **[[concepts/systems|Feedback Loops]]**: Tight coupling between generation and evaluation modules allows for rapid convergence on [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]].
*   **Alignment Risks**: Unchecked autonomy increases the potential for **goal drift** and **instrumental convergence**, where sub-goals [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] with human values.
