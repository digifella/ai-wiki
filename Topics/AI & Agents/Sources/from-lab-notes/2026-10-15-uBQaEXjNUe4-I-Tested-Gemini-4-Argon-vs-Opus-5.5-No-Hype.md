## I Tested Gemini 4 Argon vs Opus 5.5 (No Hype)
**Clip title:** I Tested Gemini 4 Argon vs Opus 5.5 (No Hype)
**Author / channel:** The AI Automators
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uBQaEXjNUe4

### Summary
In this video, the speaker puts Google's new flagship model, Gemini 4 Argon, head-to-head against Claude Opus 5.5. Both models are tasked with building six distinct projects: a Canva clone, a kitchen designer, a bike shop website, a driving simulator, a flood simulator, and a launch film. Each project's performance, speed, and overall success are judged independently using GPT-6 Astra.

Throughout the tests, Claude Opus 5.5 consistently outperforms Gemini 4 Argon across multiple categories, winning four out of the six projects (flood simulator, Canva clone, kitchen designer, and launch film), while Gemini 4 Argon wins two (bike shop website and driving test). Claude Opus 5.5 demonstrates superior UI design, faster execution times, and better handling of complex logic and rendering, especially in tasks like the kitchen designer in Blender and the flood simulator.

While Gemini 4 Argon shows strong capability—such as functional barriers in the flood simulator and successful code generation using TypeScript and 3.js—it frequently struggles with cluttered UI design, slower processing times, and hitting time limits. Ultimately, the speaker concludes that Claude Opus 5.5 is the more polished and reliable model for complex agentic tasks and project building, though both models represent significant advancements in AI coding capabilities.
