# Planck satellite data

High-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] mission mapping the [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] (CMB) to constrain fundamental cosmological parameters and the evolution of the universe.

## Scientific Significance & Current Tensions
- Primary observational foundation for the Lambda-CDM model.
- Provides critical empirical tests for Inflation [[concepts/theory|Theory]].
- Recent findings contribute to the [[concepts/universe-curvature-tension|Universe Curvature Tension]]: [[entities/planck|Planck]] Data Challenges Flatness, Impacts Inflation Theory, which identifies:
    - A significant unresolved "curvature tension" in modern [[concepts/cosmology]].
    - Potential challenges to the long-standing assumption of a [[concepts/flatness-of-the-universe|Flat Universe]].
    - Theoretical implications that may require revisions to standard models of [[concepts/cosmic-expansion|cosmic expansion]].
