# Physical Embodiment

**Physical embodiment** refers to the integration of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems with [[concepts/hardware|physical hardware]], enabling agents to perceive, interact with, and manipulate the real [[entities/earth|world]]. This concept bridges the gap between abstract computational models and tangible action, relying on multimodal inputs to ground AI in [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]].

## Key Developments

Recent advancements highlight the shift from rigid, pre-programmed robotic behaviors to adaptable, general-purpose systems driven by large [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal models]].

- **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Integration]]**: Modern [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] [[entities/labs|labs]] are increasingly integrating [[concepts/multimodal-language-models|multimodal AI]] (such as [[entities/gemini-ai]]) to allow robots to process visual, auditory, and tactile data simultaneously, enhancing their ability to navigate unstructured environments.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]**: Moving beyond rigid pre-programming, new architectures enable robots to adapt to novel tasks and physical constraints in real-time, a critical step toward true general-purpose robotics [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-Google-DeepMind-Robotics-Gemini-AI-for-Adaptable-General|Google DeepMind Robotics: Gemini AI for Adaptable, General-Purpose Robots]].
- **Research Focus**: Initiatives like those at [[entities/google-deepmind]] emphasize the [[concepts/value|importance]] of physical embodiment in developing AI that can generalize across diverse physical tasks rather than excelling only in simulated or narrow domains.
