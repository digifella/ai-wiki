# Clear Goals

## Definition
Clear Goals refer to specific, measurable, and shared objectives that provide direction and alignment for individuals or teams. They serve as the primary mechanism for translating Strategy into actionable Execution.

## Core Principles
- **Specificity**: Goals must be unambiguous to prevent interpretation variance.
- **Measurability**: Progress must be quantifiable to allow for [[concepts/purpose|objective]] assessment.
- **Alignment**: Individual goals must support broader [[concepts/organizational-structure]] objectives.
- **Visibility**: Goals must be transparent to all relevant stakeholders to ensure coordinated effort.

## Relation to Team Effectiveness
Clear goals are a foundational element of effective collaboration. Without them, teams suffer from Decision Paralysis and misaligned incentives.

- **[[entities/hackman|Hackman]]’s Framework**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Hackman-Five-Conditions-for-Team-Effectiveness-Teams| Teams]] identifies a "compelling direction" (clear, challenging goals) as one of the five necessary conditions for team [[concepts/success|success]].
- **Integration**:
    - Provides the "direction" component in Hackman Five Conditions for [[concepts/team-effectiveness|Team Effectiveness]] .
    - Reduces [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]], allowing the team to focus on Process and Resources rather than debating intent.
    - Enables the evaluation of Performance against a fixed standard.

## Implementation
- **SMART Criteria**: Utilize SMART Goals framework (Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound).
- **OKRs**: Integrate with Objectives and Key Results (OKRs).
- **Review Cycles**: Establish regular check-ins to adjust goals based on [[concepts/feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]].
