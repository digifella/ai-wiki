# Lightroom Classic v15
[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] Classic version 15 introduces a suite of new features and significant enhancements designed to streamline [[concepts/workflow|workflows]], expand creative control, and improve overall performance for photographers. The updates focus heavily on AI-powered content removal, refined [[concepts/color-and-masking-adjustments|color and masking adjustments]], and better [[concepts/integration|integration]] with other Adobe products.

- **Clip title:** 15 New Features in Lightroom Classic (v15)
- **Author / channel:** [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]]
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKXqg50v1sA

### Summary
- AI-powered content removal tools for automatic [[concepts/object-detection|object detection]] and [[concepts/masking|masking]].
- Enhanced [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] capabilities with refined control over [[concepts/hue|hue]], saturation, and luminance [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
- Improved [[concepts/mask-refinement|mask refinement]] options to fine-tune selections more precisely.
- Faster performance across the board including quicker loading times and non-destructive editing operations.
- Better integration with Adobe's ecosystem for seamless [[concepts/asset-management|asset management]] and collaboration.
