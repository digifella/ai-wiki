# Four Year Experiment

The "Four Year Experiment" was a manufacturing initiative undertaken by Destin Sandlin, the creator of the YouTube channel *Smarter Every Day*. Motivated by the severe safety hazards associated with traditional wire bristle grill brushes—specifically the risk of detached bristles causing fatal internal injuries—Sandlin aimed to produce a durable, American-made alternative. The project was deeply influenced by his parents' background as union autoworkers, which instilled in him a respect for domestic manufacturing and labor standards.

## Motivation and Context

The primary driver for the project was the well-documented danger of wire bristle brushes, which have been linked to numerous hospitalizations and deaths when bristles detach and are ingested. Sandlin sought to address this public health issue by developing a grill cleaning tool that eliminated the risk of loose metal fragments. His decision to manufacture the product in the United States was a direct reflection of his family's history in the automotive union, emphasizing a commitment to quality control and fair labor practices over lower-cost overseas production.

## Execution and Outcome

Over the course of four years, Sandlin navigated the complexities of industrial design, supply chain management, and mass production to bring the grill brush to market. The process involved rigorous testing to ensure the new design met safety standards while maintaining cleaning efficacy. The initiative served as a practical application of engineering principles and business strategy, demonstrating how a content creator could leverage their platform and personal values to influence physical product development and domestic manufacturing.
