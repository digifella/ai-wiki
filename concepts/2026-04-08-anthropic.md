# 2026 04 08 Anthropic

Anthropic is an artificial intelligence safety company founded in 2021 by former OpenAI researchers, including Dario and Daniela Amodei. The organization was established with a primary mission to develop advanced AI systems that are robust, interpretable, and aligned with human values. Unlike competitors that may prioritize raw capability or speed, Anthropic’s core focus remains on understanding and mitigating the risks associated with powerful machine learning models.

The company is best known for developing Claude, a large language model built using constitutional AI methods. This approach emphasizes safety and interpretability, aiming to create systems that can be more easily understood and controlled by humans. The development of Claude represents a significant effort to balance the capabilities of large language models with rigorous safety standards.

Anthropic's work continues to influence the broader AI landscape by promoting research into alignment and safety. The company maintains that its technologies are designed to be beneficial and reliable, addressing concerns about the potential negative impacts of advanced AI. This focus on safety distinguishes Anthropic from other entities in the field, as it prioritizes the long-term stability and trustworthiness of its systems.
