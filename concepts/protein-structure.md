# Protein Structure

Hierarchical organization of amino acid chains into functional three-dimensional conformations stabilized by non-covalent interactions and covalent bonds. Structure dictates biological activity, solubility, and mechanical properties.

## Hierarchy
*   **Primary:** Linear sequence linked by peptide bonds; determines folding potential.
*   **Secondary:** Local folding via backbone [[concepts/hydrogen-bonding|hydrogen bonding]]: alpha-helix, beta-sheet, turns.
*   **Tertiary:** Global 3D fold driven by side-chain interactions: hydrophobic effect, ionic bonds, disulfide bridges, van der Waals forces.
*   **Quaternary:** Assembly of multiple polypeptide subunits via interface complementarity.

## Stability & Dynamics
*   Conformational energy landscape minimized at native state; folding guided by thermodynamic [[concepts/ambition|drive]] to bury hydrophobic residues.
*   Chaperones assist folding and prevent aggregation of exposed hydrophobic patches.
*   Environmental perturbations (heat, pH shift, chaotropes) disrupt stabilizing forces causing [[concepts/denaturation|Denaturation]] and loss of function.
*   Aggregation of misfolded proteins associated with Amyloid formation and neurodegenerative pathologies.

## Applications & Observations
*   **Culinary Matrix & Coagulation:** Thermal denaturation of Gluten and egg proteins during baking induces coagulation and network setting; precise [[concepts/thermal-regulation|temperature control]] balances gas [[concepts/storing|retention]] for volume against excessive protein tightening that compromises [[concepts/texture|texture]] [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-Americas-Test-Kitchen-Science-and-Techniques-for-Fluffy|America's Test Kitchen: Science and Techniques for Fluffy Pancakes]].
*   **High-Protein Modulations:** Addition of cottage cheese introduces casein micelles and whey proteins that compete for water binding and interact with flour matrices, requiring adjustment of leavening ratios to maintain Secondary structure expansion capability and prevent dense [[concepts/cake-structure|crumb formation]] [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-Americas-Test-Kitchen-Science-and-Techniques-for-Fluffy|America's Test Kitchen: Science and Techniques for Fluffy Pancakes]].
*   **Batter Resting Dynamics:** Allowing batter rest permits full hydration of Primary structure components and relaxation of viscous networks, optimizing gas bubble entrapment and uniform protein distribution prior to thermal setting [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-Americas-Test-Kitchen-Science-and-Techniques-for-Fluffy|America's Test Kitchen: Science and Techniques for Fluffy Pancakes]].
