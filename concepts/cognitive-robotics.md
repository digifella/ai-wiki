# Cognitive Robotics

**Cognitive robotics** is an interdisciplinary field focusing on the development of robots that possess [[concepts/cognitive-abilities|cognitive abilities]], such as perception, [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/learning|learning]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], to operate autonomously in complex, unstructured environments. It bridges [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], [[concepts/robotics]], and cognitive-[[concepts/science|science]].

## Key Research Directions
- **Embodied [[concepts/cognition|Cognition]]:** Investigating how physical interaction with the environment shapes [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]].
- **Symbolic-Subsymbolic Integration:** Combining [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]] with [[concepts/symbolic-logic|symbolic reasoning]] for [[concepts/true-intelligence|robust AI]].
- **Human-Robot Interaction (HRI):** Enhancing natural, intuitive communication between humans and [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]].

## Theoretical Foundations
- **[[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]] of AI:** Examining the nature of [[concepts/ai-consciousness|machine consciousness]] and reasoning.
- **[[concepts/cognitive-architectures|Cognitive Architectures]]:** Designing frameworks that mimic human mental structures (e.g., SOAR, ACT-R).
- **[[concepts/responsible-ai-use|Ethical AI]]:** Addressing bias, safety, and alignment in [[concepts/autonomous-operation|autonomous systems]].
