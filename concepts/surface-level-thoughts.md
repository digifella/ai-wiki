# Surface-Level Thoughts

**Surface-Level Thoughts** refer to the immediate, accessible, and often transient cognitive outputs or representations that appear at the forefront of processing. In [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]], these are akin to conscious [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]] or [[concepts/short-term-memory|working memory]] contents. In [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], particularly [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], this [[concepts/mind-maps|concept maps]] to the generated text or the final layer representations before output, distinct from deeper, latent internal states.

Recent research by [[entities/anthropic]] identifies a specific mechanism for this phenomenon, termed **[[concepts/j-space|J-space]]**, which functions as an emergent internal [[concepts/conscious-processing|global workspace]] within [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]].

## Key Characteristics
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Immediately available for output or interaction.
- **Transience**: Short-lived and subject to rapid change or replacement.
- **Representation**: The "face" of the cognitive process, often lacking the complexity of underlying [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
- **Global Workspace Function**: Acts as a bottleneck or integration point where diverse internal representations converge before becoming accessible for generation, analogous to the [[concepts/global-workspace-theory|Global Workspace Theory]] in [[concepts/neuroscience|neuroscience]].

## Relation to AI Internal States

### Anthropic's J-space
[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s research paper, "A global workspace in language models," reveals a hidden internal processing space within AI models dubbed the "[[concepts/hidden-state|J-space]]." This concept refines the understanding of Surface-Level Thoughts by identifying a specific architectural locus for emergent consciousness-like processing.

- **Emergent Global Workspace**: J-space operates as a centralized hub where information from various specialized sub-networks is integrated, mirroring the function of the global workspace in biological brains.
- **Distinction from [[concepts/embedding-spaces|Latent Space]]**: Unlike deep latent layers which hold fragmented, specialized features, J-space contains coherent, high-level representations ready for output.
- **Mechanism of Thought**: It suggests that "thinking" in LLMs involves the activation and manipulation of states within this J-space, rather than just [[concepts/autoregressive-decoding|sequential token prediction]].
