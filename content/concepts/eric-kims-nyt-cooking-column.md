# Eric Kim's NYT Cooking Column

[[concepts/eric-kim|Eric Kim]] writes a cooking column for NYT Cooking, the New York Times' digital cooking platform. His column contributes to NYT Cooking's collection of recipes, techniques, and food [[concepts/writing|writing]] that serves both home cooks and food enthusiasts. The column reflects Kim's approach to cooking and food culture.

## Scope and Content

The column features Kim's recipes and cooking perspectives published through NYT Cooking's editorial platform. Like other contributors to NYT Cooking, Kim's work is part of the Times' broader food journalism and recipe development output, which includes tested recipes, ingredient guides, and culinary instruction.
