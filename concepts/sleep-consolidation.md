# Sleep Consolidation

**[[concepts/sleep|Sleep]] [[concepts/consolidation|Consolidation]]** refers to the process by which the brain stabilizes and integrates memories acquired during wakefulness. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], this concept is being adapted to simulate [[concepts/autonomous-memory-distillation|autonomous memory distillation]] to enhance [[concepts/model-intelligence|model intelligence]].
