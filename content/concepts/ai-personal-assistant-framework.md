# AI personal assistant framework

A structural [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] designed to serve as a centralized "brain," integrating [[concepts/local-llm]]s, personal data, and [[concepts/automation|Automation]] Pipelines to orchestrate daily digital [[concepts/workflow|workflows]].

## Core Architectures

### openclaw
An [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], personal-first framework focused on [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] and high autonomy.
- **[[concepts/deployment|Deployment]]**: Optimized for local [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] (e.g., [[entities/macbook|MacBook]]).
- **[[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**:
    - Comprehensive Automation Pipelines for end-to-end task execution.
    - Multi-platform [[concepts/integration|integration]] with communication ecosystems, including [[entities/telegram]], [[entities/slack]], and WhatsApp.
    - Centralized orchestration of various daily [[concepts/software|applications]] and workflows.

## Key Components
- **[[concepts/inference|Inference]] Engine**: Local or cloud-based [[concepts/models|models]].
- **Interface Layer**: Integration with messaging and productivity apps.
- **Action Layer**: Automated [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] and [[concepts/tool-calling|tool-use]] capabilities.
