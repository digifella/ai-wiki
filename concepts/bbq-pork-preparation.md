# BBQ Pork Preparation

## Overview
BBQ Pork [[concepts/preparation|Preparation]] encompasses various methods for roasting or [[concepts/bbq|grilling]] pork to achieve a caramelized exterior and tender interior. A prominent variation is the Cantonese [[concepts/style|style]], known as Cha Siu.

## Cantonese Cha Siu Method
*   **Authentic Profile**: Sweet and savory roasted meat dish central to [[concepts/cantonese-cuisine]].
*   **Preparation Key**: Focuses on selecting the correct cut of pork for optimal [[concepts/texture|texture]].
*   **Coloration**: Traditional methods may use artificial food coloring; however, authentic preparation can achieve the signature red hue without artificial additives through specific marinade ingredients (e.g., fermented bean curd, honey, maltose).
*   **Cooking Technique**: Typically oven-roasted to ensure even cooking and [[concepts/glazing|glaze]] formation.
*   **Source Reference**: Detailed procedural guide available in [[lab-notes/2026-06-13-Cantonese-Cha-Siu-BBQ-Pork-Preparation-Guide|Cantonese Cha Siu BBQ Pork Preparation Guide]].

## General Considerations
*   **Meat Selection**: Cuts vary by region; pork belly or shoulder are common.
*   **[[concepts/marinating|Marination]]**: Essential for flavor penetration and tenderness.
*   **[[concepts/thermal-regulation|Temperature Control]]**: High heat is often required to char the exterior without drying the interior.
