# Camera App Zoom

Adobe Lightroom's beta camera application, available for iPhone 13 Pro and later, integrates computational photography features directly into the camera capture interface. Rather than requiring post-processing adjustments in editing software, the app applies image processing at the moment of capture, leveraging the advanced processing capabilities built into recent iPhone models.

## Key Features

The app includes three primary computational photography tools. Zoom functionality allows users to capture images at various focal lengths with computational enhancement. Night mode enables improved low-light photography through advanced sensor processing. Reflection removal uses computational techniques to reduce or eliminate unwanted reflections in captured images, such as glass reflections or water surfaces.

## Technical Implementation

These features operate by processing raw sensor data in real-time on the device, utilizing the hardware capabilities of iPhone 13 Pro and later models. The computational approach allows for image enhancement during capture rather than relying solely on traditional post-processing workflows, streamlining the photography workflow for Lightroom users.
