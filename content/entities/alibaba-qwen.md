## Alibaba Qwen

[[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]] Cloud's [[entities/qwen|Qwen]] is a series of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] designed to provide comprehensive and efficient support for various tasks. It includes specialized versions like **[[entities/qwen3-coder|Qwen3-Coder]]** which focuses on [[concepts/coding|coding]] assistance, making it an attractive option for developers seeking cost-effective [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] solutions.
