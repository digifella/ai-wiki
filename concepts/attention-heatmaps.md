# Attention Heatmaps

Attention heatmaps are visualization tools that display how attention mechanisms in neural networks allocate computational focus across input data. In multimodal AI systems, these heatmaps represent the relative weights or importance scores assigned by attention layers to different input elements, such as image regions, text tokens, or video frames. By rendering these weights as color-coded visualizations, developers can interpret which parts of the input most significantly influenced the model's output during processing.

This technique is particularly relevant in the context of Google Stitch 2.0, where it serves as a diagnostic interface for understanding agent behavior. The visualization allows users to trace the decision-making path of the AI agent, highlighting specific data points that triggered particular responses or actions. This transparency aids in debugging and refining the agent's performance by making the abstract concept of "attention" concrete and observable.

The primary utility of attention heatmaps lies in their ability to provide interpretability for complex models. By identifying which inputs receive the highest attention scores, developers can verify that the model is focusing on relevant features rather than noise or artifacts. This insight is crucial for ensuring that AI agents operate reliably and align with intended design goals, particularly in applications requiring high precision and accountability.
