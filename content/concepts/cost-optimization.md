# Cost Optimization in AI Development

**[[concepts/llms|Definition]]:**
Cost optimization refers to strategies and techniques employed by developers and organizations to minimize expenses while maintaining or improving the performance of their [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. This includes optimizing resource usage, reducing waste, and leveraging [[concepts/advanced-tools|advanced tools]] designed for efficient development.

## Key Concepts
- **Resource Allocation**: Efficient use of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] such as GPUs and CPUs.
- **Monitoring Tools**: Utilities that track system performance in real-time to identify inefficiencies.
- **Managed Services**: [[concepts/cloud-based-services|Cloud-based services]] providing pre-configured environments tailored for [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]], reducing [[concepts/setup|setup]] time and costs.
- **Local [[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] Model [[concepts/deployment|Deployment]]**: [[concepts/running|Running]] AI models locally on consumer-grade [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] (e.g., NVIDIA RTX 30/40 series GPUs) to avoid $10k+/month cloud costs while improving data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], often implemented through [[concepts/hybrid-cloud|hybrid cloud]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].
