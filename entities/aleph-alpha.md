# Aleph Alpha

**Aleph Alpha** is a German [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] company specializing in [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] with a strong emphasis on data sovereignty and multilingual capabilities, particularly for German and English.

## Key Developments

### Kolibri-1
*   **Overview:** Introduction of Kolibri-1: Aleph Alpha's Sovereign AI Model and Advanced Generation Capabilities, a new sovereign open-weight language model.
*   **Architecture:** Utilizes a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) reasoning framework.
*   **Specifications:**
