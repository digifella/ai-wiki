# Move Tool

The Move Tool is a fundamental feature in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] that allows users to reposition elements within a document. Available in both the standard and beta versions of Photoshop, it enables precise control over the placement of layers, selections, and objects on the [[entities/canvas|canvas]]. The tool is one of the most frequently used utilities in the application, serving as a basic requirement for most editing and composition workflows.

## Harmonize Feature

The Harmonize feature, introduced in Photoshop Beta, extends the capabilities of the Move Tool by providing intelligent repositioning assistance. This feature helps users align and arrange content more efficiently by analyzing spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and suggesting optimal placements. The tool includes certain limitations in its current beta implementation, with workarounds available for users seeking to achieve specific compositional results beyond the feature's default behavior.
