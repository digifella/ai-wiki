# Million-Dollar Prize Concept Overview

## Definition
The term "million-dollar prize" refers to a monetary reward offered for solving complex and significant scientific or [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]].

## Notable Examples
- **[[concepts/p-vs-np-problem|P vs NP Problem]]**: This problem, central in [[concepts/computational-complexity|computational complexity]] [[concepts/theory|theory]], carries a million-dollar prize as part of the [Clay Mathematics Institute's](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clay_Mathematics_Institute) [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems|Millennium Prize Problems]].
  - **Implications and [[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]**
    - The P vs. NP question challenges whether every problem whose [[concepts/solution|solution]] can be quickly verified by a computer can also be quickly solved by one, potentially impacting fields such as [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]], algorithm [[concepts/design|design]], and computational biology.
    - Solving this problem could unlock advancements in data [[concepts/security|security]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]].
