# Cisco

Cisco Systems is a multinational technology company headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984, the organization designs and manufactures networking hardware that serves as core infrastructure for enterprise and service provider networks globally. Its primary product lines include routers, switches, and related equipment essential for data transmission and network connectivity across organizations of varying sizes.

Beyond physical networking hardware, Cisco develops comprehensive software solutions focused on security, collaboration, and network management. These offerings support the operational needs of modern digital environments, enabling secure communication and efficient data flow for businesses and institutions worldwide.
