# Google Ai Overviews

[[entities/google-ai|Google AI]] Overviews is a feature that uses [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to provide synthesized answers directly within [[concepts/google-search|Google search]] results. Rather than simply listing links, the system generates concise summaries by drawing from multiple sources to address user queries comprehensively.

## Generative Engine Optimization

As AI-powered search becomes more prevalent, website optimization strategies have evolved beyond [[concepts/traditional-seo|traditional SEO]]. [[concepts/generative-engine-optimization|Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)]] refers to the practice of structuring website content to be more effectively processed and cited by [[concepts/ai-search|AI search]] systems. This involves ensuring content is clear, well-organized, and contextually rich enough for language [[concepts/models|models]] to extract and synthesize accurate information.

## Technical Implementation

Developers and content creators can leverage tools like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] to audit and adapt their websites for AI search compatibility. This includes examining content [[concepts/structure|structure]], ensuring [[concepts/factual-accuracy|factual accuracy]], and optimizing for the types of queries that trigger AI-generated overviews. The approach complements rather than replaces traditional SEO, as websites must remain discoverable through conventional search mechanisms while also being suitable sources for AI synthesis.
