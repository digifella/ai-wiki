# Adaptive Strategy

[[concepts/robust-planning|Adaptive strategy]] is a planning and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] framework that prioritizes flexibility and responsiveness to dynamic conditions. Unlike traditional approaches that rely on fixed, predetermined plans, this methodology emphasizes [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]] of the environment and real-time adjustment of tactics based on new information. It is designed to handle high uncertainty and unpredictability by treating plans as iterative hypotheses rather than static [[concepts/recommendations|directives]].

The core mechanism involves a continuous [[concepts/loop|loop]] of evaluation and adjustment. Agents or systems assess current outcomes against expected goals, identify deviations, and modify their behavior accordingly. This process allows for the incorporation of shifting environmental factors and emerging data, ensuring that actions remain relevant and effective despite changing circumstances.

This approach is particularly valuable in complex domains where static planning fails to account for rapid changes. By decoupling execution from rigid initial assumptions, adaptive strategy enables more robust performance in volatile contexts. It supports [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] by allowing for course corrections at any stage, reducing the risk of catastrophic failure due to unforeseen events.
