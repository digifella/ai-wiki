# Financial Performance

**Financial performance** refers to the extent to which a firm achieves its primary financial objectives. It is a measure of the effectiveness with which a company uses its resources to generate profit, grow assets, and create value for shareholders. Key metrics include revenue growth, profit margins, return on equity (ROE), and earnings per share (EPS).

## Key Dimensions

- **Profitability:** The ability to generate earnings relative to revenue, total assets, or shareholders' equity.
- **Liquidity:** The ability to meet short-term debt obligations with short-term assets.
- **Solvency:** The ability to meet long-term debt obligations.
- **Efficiency:** How well a company uses its assets to generate revenue.
