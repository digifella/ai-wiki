# Longitudinal Health Monitoring

**Longitudinal [[concepts/health|Health]] Monitoring** refers to the continuous or repeated observation of [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]] over extended periods to detect trends, early pathologies, and physiological changes. Unlike cross-sectional snapshots, this approach emphasizes temporal data [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] for [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|predictive analytics]] and [[concepts/personalized-medicine|personalized medicine]].

## Core Principles
- **Temporal Continuity:** Data collection at regular intervals to establish baselines and detect deviations.
- **Non-Invasive Tracking:** Preference for methods that allow frequent monitoring without patient burden.
- **Predictive Analytics:** Using historical data to forecast health outcomes and intervene preemptively.
