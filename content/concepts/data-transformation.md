# Data Transformation

The process of converting data from one format or [[concepts/structure|structure]] into another to ensure compatibility, usability, or to enable complex downstream processing.

## Frameworks and Implementations
- **Cocoindex**: A data transformation framework designed for [[concepts/llm-rag|LLM RAG]] to construct [[concepts/real-time-knowledge-graph|real-time knowledge graphs]].
    - Utilizes [[concepts/large-language-models|LLMs]] to process collections of [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] documents.
    - Employs [[entities/neo4j|Neo4j]] as the underlying [[concepts/graph-database|graph database]].
