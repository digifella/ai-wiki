# Derek Parfit

Derek Parfit was a British philosopher known for his influential contributions to [[concepts/ethics|ethics]], metaphysics, and the [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] of personal identity. His work has been widely cited in academic discussions of [[concepts/health|wellbeing]] and what makes life valuable.

## Philosophical Work on Wellbeing

Parfit's theories concerning wellbeing and ethics have been referenced in contemporary academic discourse on how wellbeing should be defined and understood. His philosophical accounts have informed debates about the [[entities/nature|nature]] of human flourishing and the foundations of ethical [[concepts/theory|theory]], with particular relevance to discussions that distinguish between different conceptions of wellbeing in both theoretical and practical contexts.
