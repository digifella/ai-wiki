# Louise Schaper

[[entities/louise|Louise]] Schaper is experimenting with automation solutions to manage podcast consumption. Facing time constraints with an expanding list of [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]] she wants to follow, Schaper has begun testing an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]]-based approach to summarize her most valued podcasts and deliver summaries directly to her inbox.

## Podcast Summarization Project

Schaper's workflow uses [[entities/kortexai|Kortex]].ai to build an [[concepts/automated-agent|automated agent]] capable of summarizing podcast content. This approach was inspired by content from [[entities/rick-mulready|Rob the AI Guy]], a channel focused on [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] and workflows. The automated [[concepts/automated-workflow|podcast summary agent]] represents an attempt to reclaim time by reducing the need to listen to full episodes while still staying informed about valued content.
