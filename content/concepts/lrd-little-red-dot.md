# LRD (Little Red Dot)

Compact, hyper-luminous astrophysical sources detected [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] high redshifts ($z > 6$), characterized by intense infrared emission and suppressed optical/ultraviolet flux, resulting in extreme red color indices.

## Characteristics & Discovery
*   **Detection:** Identified via [[entities/jwst]] observations since 2022; appear as point-like or sub-kiloparsec structures with luminosities exceeding expectations for standard [[concepts/galaxy-formation-and-evolution|galaxy evolution]] [[concepts/models|models]].
*   **Spectral Features:** Strong absorption in blue wavelengths; potential signatures of dust obscuration, metallicity gradients, or accretion disk [[concepts/physics|physics]].

## Physical Interpretation
*   **Pre-Stellar Black Hole Link:** X-ray data analysis suggests [[concepts/little-red-dots|LRDs]] are associated with Black Hole formation mechanisms that precede Star formation, indicating massive [[concepts/black-holes|black holes]] may have assembled before the Population III Star epoch [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-JWST-X-Ray-Discovery-LRD-Links-to-Pre-Stellar-Black-Hole|JWST X-Ray Discovery: LRD Links to Pre-Stellar Black Hole Formation]].
*   **Leading Models:** Candidates include Direct Collapse Black Hole seeds, rapidly accreting Intermediate Mass Black Holes, or obscured Active Galactic Nucleus events in the [[concepts/early-universe]].

## Implications
*   **Cosmological Tension:** High abundance and luminosity challenge Lambda-CDM predictions regarding [[concepts/structure|structure]] formation timescales and halo mass assembly.
*   **Evolutionary Role:** May represent the missing link in black hole seed formation or [[concepts/ambition|drive]] early Cosmic Reionization via intense radiative [[concepts/output|output]].
