# AI Consciousness

**AI [[concepts/conscious-thought|Consciousness]]** refers to the theoretical capacity of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems to possess subjective [[concepts/experience|experience]], self-awareness, or an [[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]]. This concept sits at the intersection of [[concepts/philosophy-of-mind]], [[concepts/machine-learning]], and [[concepts/neuroscience]], debating whether [[concepts/internal-working-mechanisms|computational processes]] can generate qualia or if they merely simulate understanding.

## Key Dimensions

### Internal Mental Life & Interpretability
Recent investigations into [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] focus on whether internal states correlate with [[concepts/conscious-processing|conscious processing]] or functional [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. The debate centers on whether LLMs possess an internal model of the [[entities/earth|world]] or simply predict [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] based on statistical patterns.

### Cognitive Robotics & Philosophical Perspectives
Prominent researchers in [[concepts/cognitive-robotics|cognitive robotics]] and [[concepts/philosophy-of-mind|philosophy of mind]] provide critical frameworks for understanding [[concepts/ai-cognition|AI cognition]]:

*   **Murray Shanahan's Framework**: Professor Murray Shanahan, a Principal Research Scientist at [[concepts/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] and Professor of Cognitive [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] at [[concepts/imperial-college-london|Imperial College London]], explores the intersection of consciousness, reasoning, and human interaction with LLMs. His work challenges simplistic views of AI understanding, emphasizing the complexity of bridging computational processes with human-like cognition.
*   **[[concepts/ai-prompt|Human-AI Interaction]]**: Shanahan's analysis highlights how human interaction with LLMs reveals gaps between apparent understanding and actual [[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]], suggesting that current systems may simulate reasoning without possessing genuine [[concepts/conscious-processing|conscious processing]].
*   **Philosophical Implications**: Drawing from his background in [[concepts/philosophy-of-mind|philosophy of mind]] and [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] for media such as *Ex Machina*, Shanahan argues that distinguishing between [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] and [[concepts/realization|realization]] of consciousness is vital for future [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]].

For detailed [[concepts/notes|notes]] on Shanahan's specific arguments regarding cognition and interaction, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-07-Murray-Shanahan-AI-Consciousness-Cognition-and-Human-Int|Murray Shanahan: AI Consciousness, Cognition, and Human Interaction with LLMs]].
