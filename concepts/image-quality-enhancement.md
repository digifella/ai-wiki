# Image quality enhancement

The process of improving the visual fidelity, [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]], and [[concepts/color-vibrancy|color vibrancy]] of [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]] through various digital manipulation techniques.

## Key Methodologies
- [[concepts/non-destructive-editing]]: Utilizing [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] (integrated within [[concepts/photoshop]] or [[concepts/lightroom]]) to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] without permanently altering the original source data.
- [[concepts/color-exaggeration|Color Enhancement]]: Implementing specific workflows and "tricks" to make colors "pop" and increase visual impact.
- Image Optimization: Enhancing overall image quality by [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], and [[concepts/color-saturation|color saturation]].
