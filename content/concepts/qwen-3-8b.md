# Qwen 3 8B
## Overview
[[entities/qwen-3-8b|Qwen 3 8B]] is an architectural framework designed for [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] aiming to reduce the computational footprint while maintaining high intelligence and functionality. This model serves as the foundation for several advanced LLM implementations.
