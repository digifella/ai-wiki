# Rapid Inference

**Rapid [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]]** refers to the capability of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to process inputs and generate outputs with minimal latency, often prioritizing structured [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] over generative text production. This approach is critical for real-time applications requiring immediate, calibrated responses.

## Key Characteristics
- **Low Latency:** Optimized for [[concepts/speed|speed]] rather than creative generation.
- **[[concepts/structured-output|Structured Output]]:** Returns specific data types (e.g., probabilities, JSON) rather than free-form text.
- **[[concepts/multimodal-input|Multimodal Input]]:** Capable of processing diverse data streams simultaneously.
- **Dynamic [[concepts/instruction-following|Instruction Following]]:** Ability to adapt to changing constraints in real-time without retraining.

### Jev: Frontier Model for Rapid, Structured Decision-Making
**[[lab-notes/2026-10-13-Jev-Frontier-Model-for-Rapid-Structured-Decision-Making|Jev: Frontier Model for Rapid, Structured Decision-Making]]** represents a refinement of existing classification and decision models rather than a new category. Key attributes include:
- **Frontier Performance:** Designed for fast, structured decision-making in software applications.
- **Dynamic [[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]:** Enhances the ability to follow dynamic [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] compared to previous iterations.
- **Classification Focus:** Extends capabilities in precise classification tasks while maintaining low latency.
