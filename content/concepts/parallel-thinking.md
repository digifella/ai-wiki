# Parallel thinking

A cognitive technique for concurrent [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] across multiple perspectives, enhancing creativity and [[concepts/robustness|robustness]] in complex [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. Contrasts with [[concepts/linear-thinking|linear thinking]] by exploring diverse pathways simultaneously rather than sequentially.

## AI implementation

The [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Pro model's **[[concepts/deep-think|Deep Think]]** mode (launched 2026-04-14) employs parallel thinking to improve [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] on highly complex problems requiring simultaneous analysis of multiple factors.

- Leverages [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s research in [[concepts/reasoning]] and [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] for efficient multi-path exploration
- Detailed in [[entities/gemini-pro|Gemini Pro]] [[concepts/deep-think-mode|Deep Think]] launch (2026-04-14)
- Designed to handle problems beyond [[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|sequential reasoning]] limits
