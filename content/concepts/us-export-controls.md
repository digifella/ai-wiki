# US export controls

[[concepts/regulatory-framework|Regulatory framework]] implemented by the [[entities/united-states|United States]] to restrict the transfer of advanced semiconductor technology and AI [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] to strategic competitors, specifically targeting China.

## Key Impacts
- **Impact on LLM Development**:
    - Delays in [[entities/deepseek]] R2 development attributed to hardware-related performance issues and supply constraints.
    - Scarcity of [[entities/nvidia]] [[entities/h20-chips|H20 chips]] directly linked to US export controls.
- **[[concepts/hardware-limitations|Hardware Limitations]]**:
    - Restrictions on high-end GPU availability are creating bottlenecks in the [[concepts/training|training]] of large-scale models within restricted jurisdictions.
