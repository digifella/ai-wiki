# Rakime Elmir

Rakime Elmir is a researcher and scholar specializing in the study of paternal experiences during childbirth, with a particular focus on cases involving traumatic or difficult labour and delivery. Her work examines the psychological and emotional impacts on fathers who are present during these critical perinatal moments, contributing to a deeper understanding of how men experience and are affected by childbirth events.

## Research Focus

Elmir's research centers on fathers' perspectives within perinatal studies, an area that has historically received less attention than maternal health outcomes. By analyzing the specific challenges faced by men during traumatic births, her scholarship aims to highlight the often-overlooked emotional toll on partners and family members. This focus seeks to bridge gaps in existing literature regarding the holistic impact of childbirth on the entire family unit.

## Service Delivery Enhancement

A key component of Elmir's work involves the enhancement of service delivery for fathers. She advocates for improved support systems and clinical practices that address the needs of men attending births, particularly in high-stress or traumatic scenarios. Her contributions emphasize the importance of integrating paternal well-being into standard perinatal care protocols to ensure comprehensive support for all parties involved in the birthing process.
