# DSLR photography

Digital Single-Lens Reflex (DSLR) photography utilizes a mechanical mirror system to reflect [[concepts/light|light]] from the lens into an optical viewfinder, allowing for real-time light inspection before the sensor captures the image.

## Core Technical Strengths
- High [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
- Superior [[concepts/low-light-performance|low light]] performance.
- Advanced video capabilities.
- Precision through interchangeable [[concepts/optics|optics]].
