# Michael Faraday

**Michael Faraday** (1791–1867) was an English scientist who contributed to the study of electromagnetism and electrochemistry. His early work on electromagnetic induction, diamagnetism, and electrolysis laid the foundations for electric motor and electric generator technology.

## Key Contributions
*   Discovery of electromagnetic induction (1831)
*   Laws of electrolysis
*   Discovery of benzene
*   Development of the Faraday cage
*   Invention of the Dish of Electricity

## Legacy
Faraday's conceptual framework for fields revolutionized physics, influencing James Clerk Maxwell's equations. His work remains central to modern energy storage and materials science.
