# Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

**Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory ([[entities/llnl|LLNL]])** is a federally funded research and development center located in Livermore, California. Established in 1952, LLNL is managed by [[entities/uc-berkeley|University of California]] for the U.S. Department of Energy's National Nuclear [[concepts/security|Security]] Administration. Its primary mission focuses on [[concepts/export-control|national security]], specifically the maintenance of the [[entities/united-states|United States]] nuclear stockpile and the development of advanced technologies for defense and energy.

## Key Facilities and Infrastructure

LLNL hosts several world-class scientific facilities, including:

*   **National Ignition Facility (NIF):** A laser fusion facility that achieved the first ignition of a fusion target in 2022.
*   **[[concepts/el-capitan-supercomputer|El Capitan Supercomputer]]:** One of the [[entities/earth|world]]'s most powerful supercomputers, designed for [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/computation|computing]] (HPC) tasks related to [[concepts/nuclear-weapons|nuclear weapons]] stewardship and [[concepts/scientific-simulation|scientific simulation]].
    *   **Scale & [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]:** El Capitan represents a significant leap in computational power, utilizing advanced AMD Instinct accelerators and HPE Cray architecture to handle extreme-scale simulations.
    *   **Mission:** It supports critical national security missions, including stockpile stewardship, [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]], and climate modeling.
    *   **Documentation:** For detailed technical insights and engineering context, see [[lab-notes/2026-09-27-El-Capitan-Supercomputer-Scale-Engineering-and-Mission-a|El Capitan Supercomputer: Scale, Engineering, and Mission at LLNL]].

## Research Areas

*   **Nuclear Weapons [[concepts/science|Science]]:** Ensuring the safety, security, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of the U.S. nuclear arsenal without nuclear testing.
*   **High-Energy [[concepts/density|Density]] [[concepts/physics|Physics]]:** Studying matter under extreme conditions similar to those found in [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] and planetary cores.
*   **Energy Security:** Developing technologies for [[concepts/fusion-power|fusion energy]], nuclear fission, and advanced energy [[entities/storage|storage]].
*   **Nonproliferation:** Monitoring and verifying global nuclear threats through advanced sensing and [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] technologies.
