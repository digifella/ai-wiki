# Label Removal

**Label removal** refers to the process of detaching adhesive labels and clearing residual glue from surfaces to prepare them for reuse, recycling, or repurposing. This is a critical step in upcycling and proper waste management protocols for [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]].

## Methods and Techniques

*   **Surface Compatibility**: Techniques vary based on the substrate material, primarily distinguishing between glass and [[concepts/plastic-containers|plastic containers]] due to differences in heat tolerance and chemical resistance.
*   **Adhesive Dissolution**: Effective removal often requires solvents or heat to break down the adhesive bond without damaging the container material.
*   **Residue Cleanup**: After label detachment, mechanical or chemical methods are used to remove sticky residue to ensure the surface is clean for subsequent use.
