Iterative [[concepts/learning|learning]] is a method of enhancing performance through repeated cycles of practice, analysis, and adjustment. This process often involves setting specific goals and using [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to refine strategies or processes. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]], this extends to the continuous updating of [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] and [[concepts/active-parameters|model parameters]].

## Key Concepts
- **Feedback [[concepts/loop|Loop]]:** A continuous process where outcomes are analyzed, and [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are made based on that analysis.
- **Incremental Improvement:** Small changes made in each [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] aimed at achieving cumulative improvement over time.
- **Data-Driven [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]:** Utilizing data to inform decisions and guide the [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative process]].
- **[[concepts/knowledge-standardization|Knowledge Standardization]]:** The move toward [[concepts/open-source|open standards]] for AI knowledge representation, such as [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/okf|Open Knowledge Format (OKF)]], which standardizes personal knowledge [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] for AI interoperability, evolving from concepts like [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s "[[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]]" [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Googles-OKF-Standardizing-Karpathys-LLM-Wiki-for-AI-Inte|Google's OKF: Standardizing Karpathy's LLM Wiki for AI Interoperability]].

## Applications
- **[[concepts/coding|Software Development]]:** Iterative learning can be applied to software [[concepts/development-methodologies|development methodologies]] such as Agile, where iterations are used to deliver functional pieces of a product incrementally.
- **[[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]] Knowledge Bases:** Applying [[concepts/iterative-research|iterative refinement]] to [[concepts/knowledge-base|agent memory]] and [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]], ensuring that knowledge [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] are structured and interoperable via formats like OKF.
