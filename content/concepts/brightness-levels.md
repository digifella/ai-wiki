# Brightness Levels

Brightness in image editing refers to the overall lightness or darkness of an image. Adjusting brightness can alter the mood and tone of a photograph, making it appear more vibrant or subdued. This concept is crucial for achieving visual balance and enhancing aesthetic appeal.

- [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]

### Applications

Adjusting brightness levels impacts the overall appearance of [[concepts/images|images]] in various [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]:

- **Color correction**: Enhancing colors by adjusting brightness.
- **Mood setting**: Altering the emotional tone through lightness or darkness [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

### Tools and Techniques
- **[[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s Brightness/[[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] Tool**:
  - Allows direct adjustment of brightness without affecting other aspects of an image, contributing to precise [[concepts/lightroom|color grading]] and editing workflows.

- **[[concepts/blend-if-sliders|Blend If Sliders]] ([[concepts/advanced-blending|Advanced Blending]])**:
  - Found within Layer Style options, these sliders enable [[concepts/pixel-transparency|pixel-perfect transparency]] adjustments based on brightness levels.
