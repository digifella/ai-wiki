# Human Intelligence

## Overview
**Human Intelligence** refers to the cognitive capabilities inherent in humans, including [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]], perception, and [[concepts/learning|learning]]. It serves as the primary benchmark and analogy for understanding and developing **[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]**.
