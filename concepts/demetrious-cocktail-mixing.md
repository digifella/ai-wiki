# Demetrious Cocktail Mixing

Demetrious Cocktail Mixing is a bartending operation based in Heraklion, Crete, specializing in cocktail preparation and craft beverage development. The business provides mixology services and custom drink creation within the local food and beverage sector, serving clients who require professional cocktail preparation for events and establishments.

## Founder Background

The venture was founded by Helen, a former pharmacist who transitioned into cocktail mixing and mixology. Her background in pharmacy informed her approach to mixology, bringing precision and systematic thinking to beverage formulation and ingredient understanding. This professional pivot represents a shift from pharmaceutical compounding to the hospitality industry.

## Operations

Operating in Heraklion's hospitality sector, the business focuses on craft beverage development and professional bartending services. The operation serves the local market through mixology expertise and the creation of specialized drink recipes tailored to client preferences.
