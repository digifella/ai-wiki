# Foundry Ai

Foundry Ai is an Azure-based AI service designed to facilitate the implementation of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. RAG is an approach that combines [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with [[concepts/external-data|external data]] retrieval, allowing AI systems to reference and incorporate relevant information from specified sources rather than relying solely on [[concepts/training-data|training data]].

## Purpose and Function

The service enables developers and organizations to build [[concepts/software|applications]] that ground AI [[concepts/responses|responses]] in specific documents, databases, or knowledge bases. By retrieving relevant context before generating responses, Foundry Ai helps improve [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], reduce hallucinations, and allow the system to work with proprietary or up-to-date information that may not be in the model's training data.

## Integration with Azure

As an Azure service, Foundry Ai integrates with Microsoft's broader cloud infrastructure and AI offerings. This positioning allows it to work alongside other Azure services and tools commonly used in enterprise environments.
