# Historical revelation

The uncovering of historical truths through evidence-based methods, often revealing previously unknown cultural, social, or political contexts.

* **Denmark's Citizen-Science Archaeology**: [[entities/danish-government|Danish government]] deputized amateur detectorists to search farm fields, yielding unprecedented historical insights through citizen-science. Key finds include artifacts revealing daily life in medieval Denmark, exemplified by [[entities/kristen-nedergaard-dreiøe|Kristen Nedergaard Dreiøe]] and [[entities/marie-aagaard-larsen|Marie Aagaard Larsen]]'s work (documented in [[entities/scientific-american|Scientific American]]).
