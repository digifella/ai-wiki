# Landscape Masks

Landscape masks are a selective adjustment feature available in Adobe Lightroom and the Camera Raw Filter that automatically identify and isolate distinct regions within landscape photographs. Rather than requiring manual geometric selections or brush work, landscape masks use algorithms to detect common landscape elements such as the sky, foreground, and mid-tone areas. This automated detection enables photographers to apply independent adjustments to different parts of an image without affecting the entire photograph.

## How They Work

The feature analyzes the tonal and spatial characteristics of an image to distinguish between landscape components. Once detected, these regions become selectable masks that photographers can refine or adjust. The masks typically recognize the sky as a separate element from the ground and other landscape features, allowing for targeted modifications to exposure, color, and contrast in each area independently.

## Practical Applications

Landscape masks are particularly useful for common editing tasks such as darkening overexposed skies, enhancing foreground details, or adjusting the overall tone of mid-range elements. Because the detection is automated, photographers can apply these adjustments more quickly than with manual selection tools, while still maintaining the ability to refine or exclude specific areas if needed. This makes the feature accessible to both beginners and experienced photographers working with landscape imagery.
