# Slide Deck Repurposing

Slide Deck Repurposing is a workflow technique that converts presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process begins with NotebookLM's ability to automatically create structured slide decks from source documents—including articles, PDFs, and other text materials. These slides are then imported into Google Vids, where they serve as the foundation for video production, extending the utility of the original content across multiple formats.

## Workflow and Process

The technique involves uploading NotebookLM-generated slides to Google Vids, which can process them alongside audio, text, and visual elements to produce finished videos. A key advantage of this approach is that videos created through Google Vids output content without the watermarks that may appear on slides generated directly from NotebookLM, providing cleaner final deliverables suitable for publication or distribution.

## Practical Applications

This method is useful for content creators, educators, and professionals who need to repurpose research materials or documentation into multiple formats. By automating the initial slide generation through NotebookLM and then converting to video through Google Vids, users can efficiently create presentation-based video content from source materials without manually designing slides or recording separate video content from scratch.
