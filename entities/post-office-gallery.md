# Post Office Gallery

Post Office Gallery is a contemporary art venue dedicated to exhibitions featuring innovative artistic practices and socially relevant themes. The gallery operates as a platform for diverse perspectives within the visual arts, prioritizing work that engages with meaningful cultural and social topics.

## Visualising the Invisible

The gallery hosted "Visualising the Invisible," an exhibition by artist Kim Percy that examined the relationship between dyslexic cognition and artistic creativity. The exhibition explored how dyslexic ways of thinking and perceiving can constitute distinct strengths within artistic practice, challenging conventional understandings of neurodiversity in creative fields.
