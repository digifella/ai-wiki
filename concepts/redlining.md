# Redlining

Redlining is a process in which changes or proposed edits are marked on a document to indicate revisions from an earlier [[concepts/draft|draft]]. Commonly used in legal and contract review processes, it involves highlighting additions, deletions, or modifications for thorough analysis and negotiation.
