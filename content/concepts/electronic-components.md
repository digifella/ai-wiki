# Electronic Components

Electronic components are discrete physical devices used in electrical circuits to [[concepts/power|control]], direct, and modify the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of electric current. These fundamental building blocks serve functions such as storing energy, restricting current flow, amplifying signals, or switching circuits on and off. Common examples include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors. Components are typically categorized as either passive (requiring no external power source) or active (requiring power to operate).

## Open-Source Hardware in Electronics

The field of electronics has increasingly embraced [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] methodologies, making designs and specifications freely available for study, modification, and reproduction. This approach democratizes access to electronic [[concepts/design|design]] and enables collaborative development across distributed communities. Open-source electronics projects [[concepts/range|range]] from simple educational kits to complex computing platforms, fostering [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and reducing barriers to entry for hobbyists, students, and researchers.

The [[entities/nano-banana|Nano-banana]] project represents an example of [[concepts/open-source-hardware|open-source hardware]] development within the broader electronics ecosystem. Such projects demonstrate how community-driven design can contribute to accessible electronic innovation.
