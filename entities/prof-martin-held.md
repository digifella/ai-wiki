# Prof Martin Held

Prof Martin Held is an academic researcher affiliated with the University of Copenhagen and Deakin University. His scholarly work primarily focuses on digital health interventions, with specific emphasis on applications for mental health support and conflict resolution.

A significant component of his research involves the development of the SES Family modules. This collection of software applications is designed to enhance mental health outcomes and promote family wellbeing. Held’s contributions in this area highlight the intersection of digital technology and psychological intervention, particularly in addressing complex family dynamics.
