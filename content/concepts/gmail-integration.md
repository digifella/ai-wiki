# Gmail Integration

[[entities/gmail|Gmail]] [[concepts/integration|integration]] refers to the process of connecting Gmail accounts to external systems, [[concepts/software|applications]], and AI assistants to enable automated [[entities/email|email]] handling and streamlined workflows across multiple services. Rather than manually checking and responding to emails within Gmail itself, integration allows emails to trigger actions in other platforms, and conversely, allows those systems to send communications through Gmail. This approach is particularly useful for organizations and individuals managing complex task workflows that span multiple tools.

## Common Integration Approaches

Integration can be achieved through several methods, including official APIs provided by [[concepts/google-search|Google]], third-party [[concepts/automation|automation]] platforms like [[entities/zapier|Zapier]] or [[entities/make|Make]], and custom-built solutions. Some AI assistants are designed specifically to read emails, extract actionable information, and execute tasks in connected services such as project management tools, communication platforms, or note-taking applications. These integrations typically require [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] through OAuth or similar [[concepts/secure|secure]] protocols to grant the external system appropriate access to Gmail accounts.

## Practical Applications

A typical use case involves an [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] monitoring an inbox for specific types of emails, extracting relevant information, and automatically creating corresponding tasks in project management systems or sending notifications to team communication channels. This reduces manual data entry, speeds up response times, and helps ensure that important emails do not slip through unnoticed. Integration can also facilitate logging important information back to Gmail, creating a unified record of communications and their outcomes across different platforms.
