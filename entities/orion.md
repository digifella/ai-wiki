# Orion

Orion is a crewed spacecraft system developed by NASA for the Artemis program, designed to transport astronauts to the Moon and facilitate deep space exploration missions. The spacecraft consists of three primary components: a crew module that accommodates up to six astronauts, a service module that provides propulsion and life support systems, and a launch abort system engineered to ensure crew safety during launch and ascent phases.

## Design and Capabilities

The Orion crew module is a cone-shaped capsule capable of operating beyond low Earth orbit, distinguishing it from earlier spacecraft designed primarily for orbital flight. This design allows the vehicle to withstand the thermal and radiation environments of deep space while supporting the crew during transit to lunar orbit and back. The service module, built by the European Space Agency, supplies electrical power, thermal control, and propulsion for course corrections and orbital maneuvers.

## Mission Context

Orion serves as the central element of the Artemis II mission, which aims to send astronauts around the Moon and return them safely to Earth. Reports on the mission focus on crew operations and the performance of in-flight systems, validating the spacecraft's readiness for future lunar landings. The system is intended to be reusable, allowing for multiple missions with minimal refurbishment between flights, thereby supporting sustained human presence in deep space.
