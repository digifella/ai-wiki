# Places

Places encompasses significant geographic locations of geopolitical importance, with particular focus on [[entities/united-states|United States]] military installations and detention facilities that have shaped international relations and policy debates. These locations serve as sites of strategic military operations, diplomatic disputes, and in some cases, controversial detention practices that have generated substantial legal and ethical scrutiny.

## Military Installations

The United States maintains numerous military [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] worldwide that function as critical infrastructure for defense strategy and force projection. Among these, certain installations located on foreign territory or in disputed zones have become focal points for diplomatic negotiations and ongoing international tensions. These bases often represent long-standing agreements between nations and continue to play roles in contemporary geopolitical considerations.

## Detention Facilities

Several US-controlled locations have been designated as detention facilities housing individuals classified as [[concepts/security|security]] threats or suspected combatants. [[concepts/guantanamo-bay|Guantanamo Bay]], established on a naval base in southeastern [[entities/cuba|Cuba]], exemplifies the intersection of military operations and detention policy. The facility has been subject to sustained international criticism regarding detention practices, legal proceedings, and allegations of abuse, while remaining a contentious issue in both US domestic politics and bilateral relations with Cuba.
