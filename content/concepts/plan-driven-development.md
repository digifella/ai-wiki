# plan-driven development

Plan-driven development (also known as waterfall methodology) is a [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]] approach emphasizing comprehensive upfront planning, sequential phase execution, and detailed documentation. It prioritizes predictability and control over adaptability, with each phase (requirements, [[concepts/design|design]], implementation, [[concepts/testing|testing]]) completed before progressing. This contrasts with agile development's iterative flexibility.
