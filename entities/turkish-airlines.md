# Turkish Airlines

Turkish Airlines (Türk Hava Yolları, THY) serves as the national flag carrier and the largest airline in Turkey. Established in 1933, the company operates a comprehensive network of scheduled domestic and international flights. Its operations connect destinations across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas, positioning it as a major player in global aviation.

Istanbul Airport functions as the airline's primary hub, facilitating significant transcontinental travel between Europe and Asia. This strategic location allows Turkish Airlines to maintain an extensive route network that spans multiple continents. The airline provides service to major cities worldwide, leveraging its hub to offer efficient connections for passengers traveling between distant regions.
