# AI Social Media Consultancy

AI Social Media Consultancy refers to the strategic use of artificial intelligence tools and autonomous agents to support marketing, content creation, and video production workflows. Rather than replacing creative professionals, this approach leverages AI to handle routine operational tasks such as market research, data analysis, content scheduling, and performance measurement. This division of labor enables creative teams to concentrate on strategic planning and conceptual work while AI handles time-consuming administrative and analytical components of campaigns.

## Workflow Integration

The practical application of AI consultancy involves integrating AI agents into existing creative processes. Tools like Perplexity Labs—an AI agent product—can assist with research synthesis, competitive analysis, and content recommendations. These systems process large volumes of information quickly, providing creative teams with data-driven insights that inform campaign decisions. Video production workflows similarly benefit from AI handling preliminary tasks like shot organization, transcription, and metadata generation, freeing producers to focus on editorial choices and creative direction.

## Current Implementation

Greg Isenberg has demonstrated the application of these tools in real-world marketing and video production contexts, showing how AI agents can integrate into professional creative work. The approach emphasizes complementary use, where AI handles structured, data-intensive work while humans retain control over creative judgment, brand voice, and strategic decisions. This model reflects a broader shift toward human-AI collaboration in creative industries rather than full automation of creative processes.
