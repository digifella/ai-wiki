# Smart Model

Smart Model refers to the integration of advanced language models into Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio, representing an evolution in the capabilities available to enterprise users. This integration leverages improved versions of large language models to enhance the performance of AI agents operating within Microsoft's productivity ecosystem. The approach enables these systems to process information more rapidly while maintaining higher standards of accuracy in their outputs.

## Performance Improvements

The Smart Model integration focuses on two primary technical improvements: processing speed and reasoning capability. Faster processing reduces latency in user interactions with Copilot features, while enhanced reasoning allows these AI agents to work through more complex logical problems and multi-step tasks. These improvements make it practical for organizations to deploy AI assistance across a wider range of business processes that previously required human intervention.

## Enterprise Applications

Within enterprise environments, Smart Models enable Copilot Studio users to build more sophisticated AI agents capable of handling nuanced business logic and context-dependent decision-making. The integration supports the automation of knowledge work across Microsoft 365 applications, allowing organizations to increase productivity without requiring correspondingly larger technical teams to manage the underlying systems.
