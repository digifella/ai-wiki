# Hubjam10

Hubjam10 is a content creation workflow that transforms text-based outputs from NotebookLM into video content by combining AI-generated audio with personalized video elements. The method leverages NotebookLM's audio synthesis capabilities, which can generate podcast-style narration from document collections, and pairs this with the creator's own facial presence through video recording or facial animation technology.

## Process and Components

The technique operates by first using NotebookLM to process source documents and generate spoken audio content. This audio output is then synchronized with video footage of the creator or an animated representation of their likeness, producing a finished video product. This approach allows creators to repurpose written research, notes, or documentation into multimedia content without manually recording separate narration.

## Applications

Hubjam10 is primarily used in educational and explanatory content creation, where the combination of synthesized audio analysis with creator presence can enhance viewer engagement. The method is particularly suited for creators who want to scale video production from existing written or documented research materials.
