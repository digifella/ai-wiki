# Methods

## Overview
Methods refer to systematic procedures, techniques, or approaches used to achieve a goal or solve a problem. In the context of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]], methods encompass [[concepts/training|training]] algorithms, [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] techniques, and other processes that enable the functioning and improvement of these [[concepts/models|models]].

## Key Aspects
- **[[concepts/training|Training]] Methods**: Techniques used to train LLMs, including [[concepts/transformers|transformer architectures]] and various [[concepts/constrained-optimization|optimization algorithms]].
- **Interpretability Methods**: Approaches to understand and explain the inner workings of LLMs, such as [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]] and feature visualization.
- **Evaluation Methods**: Metrics and benchmarks used to assess the performance and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of LLMs.
