# Online Entertainment Platforms

Online entertainment platforms are digital services that deliver movies and television shows to viewers over the internet. These platforms have fundamentally changed how audiences access audiovisual content by replacing traditional broadcast schedules with on-demand streaming. Users can watch content at their own pace across multiple devices including computers, smartphones, tablets, and smart televisions, with the ability to pause, resume, and rewatch material as needed.

## Business Models and Access

Online entertainment platforms operate under various business models to provide access to content. While many major services require subscription fees, a significant subset of these platforms offers movies and television shows to viewers for free. These free services typically rely on advertising revenue or other monetization strategies to sustain operations, allowing users to consume media without direct financial cost. This model distinguishes them from premium subscription-based services, though the core functionality of on-demand streaming remains consistent across both types.
