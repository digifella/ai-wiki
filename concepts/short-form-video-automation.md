# Short Form Video Automation

Short Form Video Automation is a workflow that combines AI-assisted coding with content distribution tools to streamline the creation and posting of short-form video content across multiple social media platforms. The system integrates Claude Code—which provides programmatic assistance for video processing, editing, and generation tasks—with Blotato, a platform designed to manage and automate content distribution across networks. This combination reduces manual effort in both production and publishing phases.

## Workflow Components

The automation process typically involves using Claude Code to handle video processing tasks such as editing, adding captions, applying effects, or generating video from templates and assets. Once videos are prepared, Blotato manages the scheduling and simultaneous posting across platforms like TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, and other short-form video services. This eliminates the need to manually upload and configure each video on individual platforms.

## Practical Applications

The system is useful for content creators, marketing teams, and developers who need to maintain a consistent posting schedule across multiple channels. By automating repetitive technical tasks, creators can focus on conceptual work—developing video ideas, sourcing content, and refining messaging—while the system handles execution. The approach is particularly effective for content that follows established templates or patterns.
