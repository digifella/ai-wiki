# Eric Kim

Eric Kim is a food writer and culinary professional known for his expertise in [[concepts/korean-cuisine|Korean cuisine]] and [[concepts/bbq|barbecue]] [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking techniques]]. His work focuses on documenting and interpreting Korean food traditions, with particular emphasis on Korean barbecue ([[concepts/korean-bbq|Korean BBQ]]) practices and their cultural significance.

## Culinary Focus

Kim's professional activities center on the intersection of traditional Korean cooking methods and contemporary food [[concepts/writing|writing]]. His coverage of Korean barbecue encompasses both the technical aspects of grilling and the social dimensions of the cooking style, which emphasizes communal dining and table-side [[concepts/preparation|preparation]].

## Professional Practice

As a culinary professional, Kim engages with food writing, recipe development, and cultural documentation related to Korean food traditions. His work contributes to broader conversations about Asian cuisines in English-language food media and the [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] of traditional cooking knowledge.
