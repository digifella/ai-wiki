# Critical Infrastructure Resilience
Critical infrastructure [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] involves designing systems and networks to withstand and recover from disruptions caused by [[concepts/severe-weather-events|natural disasters]], such as [[concepts/earthquakes|earthquakes]]. This includes both physical assets like buildings and transportation systems, as well as technological infrastructure like power [[concepts/grids|grids]] and communication networks.
