# GPT-3

**[[concepts/neural-networks|GPT-3]]** ([[entities/gpt-3|Generative Pre-trained Transformer 3]]) is a large-scale [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[entities/openai]]. It represents a significant leap in [[concepts/code-size|model size]] and capability compared to its predecessor, [[concepts/gpt-2|GPT-2]], leveraging unprecedented [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] to exhibit emergent language model capabilities.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Massive Scale:** GPT-3 is characterized by its enormous [[concepts/parameter-count|parameter count]], which allows it to perform complex [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]] tasks with minimal or no [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]].
*   **Emergent Abilities:** The model demonstrates capabilities that were not explicitly programmed but emerged from the scale of [[concepts/training-data|training data]] and [[concepts/model-architecture|model architecture]].
*   **Foundation for Successors:** Builds upon foundational insights gained from [[entities/gpt-2|GPT-2]], serving as a critical milestone in the evolution of transformer-based language models.
