# Ai Driven Notebooks

AI Driven [[concepts/notebook-tools|Notebooks]] refers to [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s integrated [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] feature within the [[concepts/google-ai|Gemini]] platform, established following the [[concepts/consolidation|consolidation]] of [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into the main [[concepts/gemini|Gemini ecosystem]]. This structural merger combined NotebookLM’s specialized [[concepts/legal-document-review|document analysis]] capabilities with Gemini’s broader [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] interface, creating a unified environment for research and content organization.

The feature allows users to upload and manage source documents directly within the [[concepts/chat-application|chat interface]], enabling the AI to reference specific materials during interactions. By integrating these distinct tools, Google aims to streamline workflows that require both deep [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] and general-purpose generative assistance, reducing the need to switch between separate applications for research tasks.

This consolidation represents a [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] in Google's AI product architecture, prioritizing a single entry point for users who require both conversational querying and structured [[concepts/document-management|document management]]. The resulting toolset supports continuous research workflows where users can iteratively refine queries based on uploaded sources without leaving the Gemini environment.
