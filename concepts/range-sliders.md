# Range Sliders

Range sliders are interface controls within Adobe Camera Raw that allow users to define specific tonal or color boundaries for adjustments made through the Point Color feature. By establishing upper and lower limits, these controls isolate particular segments of an image's tonal or chromatic spectrum. This mechanism ensures that edits are applied selectively to the targeted areas rather than affecting the entire image uniformly.

In Point Color editing workflows, range sliders function in conjunction with the primary adjustment parameters to refine the scope of influence. They enable precise masking of color ranges, allowing editors to modify specific hues without altering adjacent colors. This granular control is essential for correcting color casts, enhancing saturation in specific regions, or isolating subjects based on their color properties.

The utility of range sliders extends to complex color grading tasks where global adjustments would introduce unwanted artifacts. By narrowing the active range, users can target subtle variations in lightness or chroma that might otherwise be missed. This capability supports non-destructive editing practices by providing a reversible method to constrain the impact of color corrections to defined spatial or spectral coordinates within the image data.
