# Email Automation

Email automation refers to the use of software tools and platforms to automatically send, schedule, and manage email communications without requiring manual intervention for each message. This functionality enables users to create workflows that trigger emails based on specific conditions, send messages at predetermined times, or execute repetitive email tasks efficiently. Email automation is widely integrated into customer relationship management (CRM) systems, marketing platforms, and general communication infrastructure, reducing the operational burden on teams managing high volumes of correspondence.

## Common Use Cases

Email automation serves multiple business functions. Marketing teams use it to send campaign messages to segmented audiences, while customer support systems deploy automated responses to common inquiries. Sales workflows often include automated follow-up messages triggered by customer actions, and transactional systems send automated notifications for events such as order confirmations or password resets. Organizations also use email automation for routine administrative communications, scheduling regular reports, or managing subscription-based messaging.

## Platform Integration

Most modern email automation tools function as standalone platforms or as integrated features within larger business software suites. CRM platforms typically include native email automation capabilities, while dedicated marketing automation platforms offer sophisticated segmentation and personalization features. Some communication platforms, such as Google Gemini, provide scheduled action features that allow users to automate and schedule tasks within their interface, extending automation capabilities to conversational or collaborative contexts.
