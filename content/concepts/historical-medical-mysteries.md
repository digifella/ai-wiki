# Historical Medical Mysteries

## Kuru Disease
- **Discovery**: 1950s, [[entities/fore-tribe|Fore tribe]] in [[entities/papua-new-guinea|Papua New Guinea]]
- **Transmission**: Cannibalistic funeral ritual
- **Symptoms**: Laughing fits, loss of coordination, death
- **Breakthrough**: Identified as first human prion disease (1980s)
- **Legacy**: Key evidence for prion [[concepts/theory|theory]]

## Mad Cow Disease (BSE)
- **Emergence**: 1980s UK
- **Link to Humans**: vCJD (variant Creutzfeldt-Jakob disease)
- **Controversy**: Banning of beef exports

## Prion Protein Hypothesis
- **[[concepts/slms|Definition]]**: Misfolded proteins causing neurodegenerative diseases
- **Nobel Prize**: Stanley B. Prusiner (1997)
- **Mechanism**: Conversion of normal prion protein (PrP^C) to infectious form (PrP^Sc)
