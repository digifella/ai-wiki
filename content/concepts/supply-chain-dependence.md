# Supply chain dependence

Reliance on specific suppliers, regions, or transportation networks for goods [[concepts/flow|flow]], creating [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to disruptions. Key manifestations include:

- **Single-source dependencies** (e.g., critical components from one country)
- **Geopolitical risks** (e.g., trade conflicts, sanctions)
- **[[concepts/cost|Cost]] inefficiencies** from lack of alternatives
- **Reduced crisis [[concepts/resilience|resilience]]** (e.g., pandemic, [[concepts/severe-weather-events|natural disasters]])

**Case study: "[[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]" [[concepts/domestic-manufacturing|domestic manufacturing]] experiment**
- [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] (host of [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]]) documented a four-year effort to manufacture the [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] (safer grill brush) entirely within [[concepts/american-manufacturing]].
- Motivated by personal history with [[entities/union-autoworkers|Union autoworkers]] in [[concepts/american-manufacturing]].
- Revealed challenges of domestic supply chain competitiveness vs. global networks.
- Highlighted complexities in sourcing materials, assembly, and cost parity without overseas dependencies.
