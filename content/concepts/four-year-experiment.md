# Four Year Experiment

A four-year experiment is an extended research, development, or manufacturing project that requires sustained iterative work to validate and refine a complex process or product. Such projects are characterized by long timelines, repeated cycles of [[concepts/testing|testing]] and improvement, and significant resource commitment before achieving market viability or [[concepts/proof|proof]] of concept. The extended duration allows for thorough validation of assumptions, identification of unforeseen challenges, and refinement of processes that cannot be accelerated without compromising quality or understanding.

## The Smarter Scrubber Case Study

[[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]], host of the [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]], undertook a four-year experiment to develop and manufacture an American-made grill brush as a [[concepts/safety-alternative|safer alternative]] to traditional wire bristle brushes. The project was motivated by his parents' background as [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]] and reflected a broader interest in domestic manufacturing. Over the course of the project, Sandlin documented the challenges of bringing a consumer product to market, including material selection, manufacturing [[concepts/workflow-enhancements|process optimization]], safety testing, and [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] production while maintaining quality [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]] practices.

The experiment demonstrated the practical complexities involved in small-scale manufacturing, revealing why many products are manufactured overseas and why [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic production]] requires careful planning and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]]. Through multiple [[concepts/design|design]] revisions and manufacturing approaches, Sandlin worked to create a brush that addressed genuine [[concepts/safety-concerns|safety concerns]] associated with wire bristles while remaining economically viable for consumers.
