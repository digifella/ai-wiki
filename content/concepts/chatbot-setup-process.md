# Chatbot Setup Process

Setting up [[concepts/ai-chatbots|AI chatbots]] for [[concepts/legal-work|legal work]] requires configuring [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] that align with professional [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and task requirements. The three primary platforms—[[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]—each offer [[concepts/customization|customization]] features that allow users to establish baseline [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] for how the AI responds to legal queries and documentation tasks. These [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] typically define [[concepts/output|output]] format, [[concepts/tone|tone]], and specific competencies needed for legal [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Configuration Essentials

The [[concepts/setup-process|setup process]] involves specifying professional conventions relevant to legal work, such as citation formatting, confidentiality handling, and appropriate disclaimers about AI limitations in legal contexts. Users should define the scope of what the chatbot should assist with—whether drafting, research, analysis, or review—and establish clear boundaries around legal advice versus informational support. Each platform handles custom instructions differently, with ChatGPT using a dedicated settings panel, [[concepts/claude|Claude]] incorporating [[concepts/system-prompts|system prompts]], and [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]] employing similar instruction-based parameters.

## Implementation for Legal Optimization

Effective configuration requires matching the chatbot's output to specific legal workflows. This includes setting expectations for document [[concepts/structure|structure]], maintaining [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] in [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], and ensuring [[concepts/responses|responses]] include appropriate qualifications and disclaimers. Users should test the configuration with sample tasks relevant to their practice area before relying on the system for routine work, as custom instructions directly [[concepts/power|influence]] the [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and usability of the chatbot's output in professional contexts.
