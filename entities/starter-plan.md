# Starter Plan

The Starter Plan is an entry-level subscription tier offered by Evernote, designed for individual users who require basic note-taking and organizational capabilities without access to advanced features. Positioned as the most accessible paid option, it serves as a bridge for users transitioning from free accounts, providing core Evernote functionality at a lower cost than premium subscription levels.

## Features and Functionality

This tier includes essential tools such as text formatting and basic organization through notebooks. It ensures synchronization across devices, allowing users to access their notes seamlessly on mobile, desktop, and web platforms. The plan supports a limited number of notebooks and stacks, catering to personal use cases that do not demand complex project management structures.

## Pricing and Migration Context

Evernote has adjusted its pricing structure over time, often repositioning the Starter Plan to compete with other productivity applications. The tier is frequently discussed in the context of user migration guidance, helping individuals move from the free tier to a paid subscription while retaining access to critical data. This transition path is intended to retain users who need more storage or synchronization limits than the free version provides but do not require the full suite of premium features.
