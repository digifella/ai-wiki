# Jang Tae-yoo

## Overview
*[[entities/bon-appétit|Bon Appétit]], [[entities/your-majesty|Your Majesty]]* is a 12-episode Netflix K-drama adapted from [[entities/park-kook-jae|Park Kook-jae]]'s web novel *[[entities/bon-appétit-your-majesty|Surviving as Yeonsan-gun's Chef]]*, directed by Jang Tae-yoo and produced by [[entities/studio-dragon|Studio Dragon]]. It stars [[entities/im-yoon-ah|Im Yoon-ah]] as Yeon Ji-young, a modern South Korean chef with French culinary [[concepts/training|training]] who is transported back to the [[concepts/joseon-era|Joseon era]] after an accident with an antique book.
