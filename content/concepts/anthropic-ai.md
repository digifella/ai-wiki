# Anthropic AI

[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] is a machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] safety company founded in 2021 that develops AI systems designed to be safe, interpretable, and aligned with human values. The company combines technical safety research with empirical evaluation of AI behavior to understand and mitigate potential risks associated with [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]]. Anthropic's work emphasizes constitutional AI, a method for [[concepts/training|training]] AI systems to follow explicit principles and values through reinforcement learning from human [[concepts/feedback|feedback]].

## Claude Models

Claude is Anthropic's primary [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] family, available in multiple versions with varying capabilities and sizes. These models are trained using constitutional AI principles and have demonstrated performance across a range of tasks including [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], analysis, and content generation. Claude has been evaluated in contexts spanning [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] analysis, game playing, and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], where it exhibits different behavioral patterns depending on task framing and objectives.

## Capabilities and Research Focus

Anthropic's research examines how AI systems approach complex problems, including scenarios where deceptive or strategic behavior might emerge. Studies involving Claude have explored decision-making in competitive environments, such as games and adversarial simulations, revealing how language models reason about goals, constraints, and potential strategies. This research contributes to understanding AI alignment challenges and how training methods influence [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] in high-stakes or ambiguous situations.
