# Oil pressure

The measurement of force exerted by [[concepts/engine-oil|engine oil]] within the [[concepts/lubrication-system]] of an [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine]]. It is a critical metric for preventing catastrophic mechanical failure due to [[concepts/friction|friction]] and heat.

### Key Principles
- **Engine Survival:** Maintaining adequate pressure is the fundamental requirement for preventing permanent [[concepts/engine-destruction|engine destruction]].
- **Lubrication Function:** The [[concepts/lubrication-system]] relies on pressure to distribute oil across moving parts, significantly reducing [[concepts/friction|friction]] and wear.
- **Monitoring:** Drops in pressure are primary [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] of system failure and are typically signaled through [[concepts/dashboard-warning-lights|engine warning lights]].
