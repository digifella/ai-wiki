# Biosphere 2

Biosphere 2 is a large-scale Earth science research facility located in Oracle, Arizona. Constructed during the 1980s and completed in 1991, the facility comprises a series of connected domes and enclosed glass structures designed to simulate and study Earth's ecosystems. Spanning approximately 3.15 acres, Biosphere 2 contains distinct biomes including a rainforest, ocean, marsh, savanna, and desert, along with agricultural and human habitat areas.

## Design and Research Purpose

The structure was built as a closed ecological system to investigate how natural and human-made ecosystems interact. The primary goal was to test the feasibility of creating self-sustaining habitats for potential space colonization and to better understand the Earth's biosphere, known as Biosphere 1. By isolating the internal environment from the outside world, researchers aimed to monitor the complex exchanges of water, gases, and nutrients within a controlled setting.

## Operational History

The facility hosted two major eight-year manned missions in the 1990s, during which crews lived entirely within the sealed environment. These experiments revealed significant challenges in maintaining stable atmospheric conditions and food production, leading to scientific debates regarding the viability of closed ecological systems for long-term human habitation. Subsequent years saw the facility used for various biological and climate change studies, with the structure being opened to the atmosphere in 2011 to allow for more flexible research applications.
