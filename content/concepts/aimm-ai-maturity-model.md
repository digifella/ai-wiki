# Aimm Ai Maturity Model

The Aimm (Ai Maturity Model) is a framework for evaluating and developing personal AI infrastructure, presented by [[entities/daniel-miessler|Daniel Miessler]]. The model provides a structured approach for individuals to systematically build and improve their use of [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] and systems over time. Rather than treating AI [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] as an all-or-nothing transition, the framework conceptualizes AI capability development as a progression through defined maturity stages, allowing people to incrementally advance their relationship with AI technology.

## Purpose and Application

The model addresses how individuals can move beyond casual or ad-hoc AI tool use toward more intentional and integrated personal AI systems. By organizing AI adoption into stages, the framework helps people assess their current position, identify gaps, and plan concrete steps for development. This staged approach acknowledges that building effective personal AI infrastructure requires deliberate effort and [[concepts/learning|learning]] across multiple dimensions, from [[concepts/tool-selection|tool selection]] to workflow integration to skill development.

## Context

The Aimm model contributes to broader conversations about personal AI infrastructure—the systems, practices, and tools individuals establish to leverage [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] effectively in their work and lives. Rather than prescribing a single optimal path, the framework recognizes that maturity develops through repeated cycles of adoption, experimentation, and refinement as individuals discover which [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] and integrations deliver value within their specific contexts.
