# Shark Evolution

The study of shark evolution offers a glimpse into the fascinating history of marine life. Sharks have existed for over 450 million years, and their lineage has been marked by numerous [[concepts/adaptations|adaptations]] and speciations. They are [[concepts/cartilaginous-fish|cartilaginous fish]] that belong to the class Chondrichthyes, which includes [[concepts/rays|rays]], skates, and [[concepts/chimaeras|chimaeras]].

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/golden-age-of-sharks|Golden Age of Sharks]]**: A period characterized by an incredible diversity in [[concepts/spines|shark morphology]] and adaptation.
  - Began before the dominance of modern predators like the Great White
  - Included ancestors of modern sharks, rays, and chimaeras like the [[concepts/ratfish|ratfish]]

- **Adaptive Radiation**: The rapid diversification of organisms into a variety of ecological niches.
