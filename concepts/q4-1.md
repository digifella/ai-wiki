# Q4_1

**Q4_1** refers to a specific [[concepts/reduced-precision|4-bit quantization]] format used in [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Model (LLM)]] [[concepts/inference|inference]], particularly within the context of [[concepts/ternary-bonsai-27b]] architectures. It serves as a [[concepts/draft|draft]] [[concepts/model-configuration|model configuration]] to optimize performance and reduce [[concepts/4gb-memory|memory footprint]] compared to higher-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] formats.

## Key Characteristics

- **[[concepts/parameter-reduction|Quantization]] Level**: [[concepts/4bit-quantisation|4-bit precision]], significantly reducing [[concepts/vram|VRAM]] requirements while maintaining acceptable accuracy for drafting tasks.
- **Primary Use Case**: Employed as a drafter in [[concepts/llm-inference-acceleration|speculative decoding]] setups to accelerate token generation.
- **Comparison Context**: Evaluated against [[concepts/qwen-36-27b-mtp]] and [[concepts/bf16|BF16]] 16-bit variants to assess trade-offs between [[concepts/speed|speed]] and fidelity.
