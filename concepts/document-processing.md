[[concepts/pdf-manipulation|Document processing]] involves automated extraction, structuring, and analysis of information from unstructured documents to enable efficient [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]].

**Key Applications**:
- Entity and [[concepts/relationship-extraction|relationship extraction]] using LLM for semantic understanding
- [[concepts/vector-store|Knowledge Graph]] construction from document collections
- Enhanced [[concepts/rag]] ([[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]]) systems via graph-based [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]]
- [[concepts/real-time-knowledge-graph|Real-time knowledge graph]] [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] from document streams
- [[concepts/agentic-rag|Agentic RAG]] systems enhanced with direct [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] capabilities for [[concepts/rpa|Robotic Process Automation]]

**Advancements in RPA and Decision Making**:
- Integration of [[concepts/image-decision-models|Image Decision Models]] allows RPA tools to move beyond repetitive step automation (clicking/copying) to handle complex "decision-making" workflows involving forms, scans, and screenshots [[lab-notes/2026-10-03-Jev-Image-Decision-Models-for-RPA-Direct-Unstructured-Da|Jev Image Decision Models for RPA: Direct Unstructured Data Decisions]].
- Enables direct [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] decisions, bridging the gap between traditional structured automation and complex [[concepts/screenshot-analysis|visual data interpretation]].
