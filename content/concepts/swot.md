# SWOT

[[concepts/strategic-analysis|Strategic analysis]] framework identifying **Strengths**, **Weaknesses**, **Opportunities**, and **Threats** for business planning.

- **Strengths**: Internal advantages (e.g., skilled team, strong brand)
- **Weaknesses**: Internal limitations (e.g., high costs, limited tech)
- **Opportunities**: External advantages (e.g., market trends, new tech)
- **Threats**: External challenges (e.g., competition, economic shifts)
