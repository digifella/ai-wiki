# Performance Degradation

**Performance Degradation** encompasses the decline in physiological, cognitive, and psychomotor capabilities of individuals, or processing efficiency in artificial systems, particularly under constrained or high-[[concepts/stress|stress]] environments such as spaceflight or [[concepts/long-document-processing|long-document processing]]. It is determined by the interaction between human/algorithmic factors, environmental/data stressors, and operational demands.

## Key Dimensions

*   **Physiological [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Maintenance of homeostasis and [[concepts/health|health]] status, directly impacting energy levels and reaction times.
*   **Cognitive Function**: [[concepts/decision-making|Decision-making]], situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]], and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] efficiency.
*   **Psychomotor [[concepts/skills|Skills]]**: [[concepts/coordination|Coordination]] and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] in manual tasks.
*   **[[concepts/algorithm-optimization|Algorithmic Efficiency]]**: In [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], the ability to maintain [[concepts/speed|processing speed]] and accuracy without degradation when handling extended inputs, such as long documents.

## Environmental Stressors & Risks

Performance degradation is often a secondary effect of prolonged [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to stressors.

*   **Human Factors**: In spaceflight, degradation results from microgravity, radiation, and [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], impacting crew safety and mission [[concepts/success|success]].
*   **AI/Data Factors**: In [[concepts/vision-language-models|Vision Language Models]] (VLMs), degradation occurs when processing long documents, leading to loss of context or increased latency. Recent developments, such as those discussed in [[lab-notes/2026-07-01-Baidu-Unlimited-OCR-Enhancing-DeepSeek-OCR-for-Long-Docu|Baidu Unlimited-OCR: Enhancing DeepSeek-OCR for Long Document Processing]], highlight efforts to mitigate this through [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] like [[entities/baidu|Baidu]]'s Unlimited-OCR, which aims for efficient, continuous processing without performance loss.
