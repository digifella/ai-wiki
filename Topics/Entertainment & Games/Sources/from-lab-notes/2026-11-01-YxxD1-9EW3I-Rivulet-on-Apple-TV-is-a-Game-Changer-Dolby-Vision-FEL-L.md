## Rivulet on Apple TV is a Game Changer! Dolby Vision FEL, Lossless Audio
**Clip title:** Rivulet on Apple TV is a Game Changer! Dolby Vision FEL, Lossless Audio
**Author / channel:** Lon.TV
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YxxD1-9EW3I

### Summary
In the video, Lon Seiben introduces a free Apple TV app called Rivulet, which is designed to enhance the Plex server experience. Seiben explains that while the Nvidia Shield has traditionally been the go-to standard for high-end media playback on Plex due to its support for lossless audio and various HDR formats, Apple TV has lagged behind in this regard. Rivulet aims to bridge this gap by supporting lossless audio formats, Dolby Vision, and the full enhancement layer (FEL) on Apple TV.

Rivulet integrates smoothly with the Plex API, automatically syncing data and maintaining bookmarks across devices. Seiben highlights Rivulet's ability to handle Dolby Vision Profile 7 FEL by pre-processing the FEL layer using the Apple TV's processor, a CPU-intensive task that the app manages effectively. The app also supports lossless audio decoding, routing the audio to connected speakers while maintaining height channels for Dolby Atmos setups. Additionally, Rivulet features a robust live TV integration, allowing users to connect to Plex server live TV features, including over-the-air antennas and multi-view capabilities.

Seiben concludes that Rivulet is a valuable application for Apple TV users looking to get more out of their Plex server, noting its mature feel despite its recent release. He recommends checking out the app, which is available for free on the App Store with open-source code.
