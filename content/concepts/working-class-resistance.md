# Working Class Resistance

Collective action by laborers opposing exploitation, [[concepts/automation]]-induced displacement, wage suppression, and unsafe conditions within Capitalist modes of production. Strategies include strikes, sabotage, unionization, and political agitation.

## Historical Instances

- Luddites: Early 19th-century British [[concepts/exercise|movement]] where textile workers destroyed machinery to defend skilled labor [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and wages; driven by acute [[concepts/socio-economic-grievances]] and livelihood threats rather than inherent anti-technology ideology [[lab-notes/2026-05-16-Luddites-Socio-Economic-Grievances-Driving-19th-Century|Luddites: Socio-Economic Grievances Driving 19th Century Industrial Resistance]].
- Peterloo Massacre: 1819 suppression of pro-democracy rally highlighting state violence against labor assembly.
- Haymarket Affair: 1886 Chicago incident catalyzing international Labor Day observance and radical unionism.

## Structural Drivers

- Mechanization eroding craft monopolies and wage floors.
- Capital concentration creating asymmetrical bargaining [[concepts/power|power]].
- Legal frameworks prioritizing property rights over worker safety and collective bargaining rights.
- Precarity and lack of social safety nets amplifying [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to market shocks.
