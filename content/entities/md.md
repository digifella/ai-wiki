# Md

Md is an abbreviation commonly used in medical and [[concepts/health|healthcare]] contexts, most frequently standing for "Medical Doctor" or "Doctor of Medicine." It represents the primary professional degree awarded to physicians in many English-speaking countries, including the [[entities/united-states|United States]], Canada, and the [[entities/uk|United Kingdom]].

## Usage and Context

The MD credential indicates that a healthcare professional has completed medical school and the necessary [[concepts/training|training]] to practice medicine. It is typically used as a post-nominal designation following a physician's name. The term is distinct from other medical doctorates such as DO (Doctor of Osteopathic Medicine) in the United States or equivalent qualifications in other countries.

In academic and professional settings, the MD designation carries significant weight in establishing medical credibility and is often required for licensure and practice privileges [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] hospitals and medical facilities.
