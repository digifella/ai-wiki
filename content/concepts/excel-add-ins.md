# Excel Add-ins

Extensions and integrations designed to augment the core functionality of Microsoft Excel through custom user interfaces, specialized toolsets, or automated workflows.

## AI-Powered Add-ins
The landscape is shifting toward the integration of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] to automate complex logic, formula generation, and data manipulation.

- **[[entities/claude-ai|Claude AI]] Excel Add-in**:
    - Highly effective for [[concepts/financial-modeling]] workflows.
    - Demonstrated capability in providing significant value to finance professionals, particularly where native tools like [[entities/microsoft-copilot]] have historically struggled to provide deep utility in Office applications.
    - Reference: 2026 04 10 Claude AI Excel Add in for Financial Modeling Overview and Tutorial

## Key Categories
- **[[concepts/automation|Automation]]**: VBA, Office Scripts, and Power Automate.
- **Data Analysis**: Tools for [[concepts/power-query|Power Query]], Power Pivot, and statistical modeling.
- **External Integrations**: API-driven add-ins for Data Science and real-time financial data feeds.
