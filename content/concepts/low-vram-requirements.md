# Low Vram Requirements

Low [[concepts/vram|VRAM]] requirements refer to the ability of [[concepts/ai-driven-video-production|AI video generation]] [[concepts/models|models]] to operate efficiently on consumer-grade [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] with limited video [[concepts/memory|memory]]. This has become an increasingly important characteristic for [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/mobile-ai|local AI models]], as it directly impacts [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and practical [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] outside of well-resourced research environments.

## Significance for Local AI Video Models

Traditional video generation models often demand substantial VRAM allocations, restricting their use to high-end GPUs or cloud infrastructure. Models like [[concepts/ltx-2|LTX-2]] have been designed with reduced memory footprints, enabling operation on more widely available hardware while maintaining quality [[concepts/output|output]]. This reduction in requirements makes local video synthesis feasible for individual developers, small studios, and researchers without access to enterprise-level computing resources.

## Practical Impact

Lower VRAM thresholds reduce both the financial and environmental costs of [[concepts/running|running]] AI video generation locally. They also provide greater [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and [[concepts/power|control]], as users can process video generation tasks entirely on personal hardware without relying on [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]]. The efficiency gained through optimized model architectures and [[concepts/inference|inference]] methods contributes to broader [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[entities/watch-open-source|open-source video]] [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] and supports the development of creative [[concepts/software|applications]] with tangible resource constraints.
