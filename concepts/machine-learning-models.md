# Machine Learning Models

[[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|Machine learning models]] are computational systems that learn patterns from data rather than following explicitly programmed [[concepts/instructions|instructions]]. During training, these models adjust internal parameters to minimize [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] errors or achieve specified objectives. Once trained on a dataset, they can generate predictions or make decisions when presented with new, unseen data.

## Training and Deployment

The [[concepts/training-process|training process]] involves exposing a model to labeled examples or unlabeled data, depending on the [[concepts/learning|learning]] approach. The model iteratively refines its parameters through [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] like gradient descent until it reaches acceptable performance on a validation set. After training, the model can be deployed to make inferences on fresh data without additional training.

## Common Model Types

[[concepts/machine-learning|Machine learning]] models span several categories, including supervised models for regression and classification tasks, unsupervised models for clustering and dimensionality reduction, and [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]] models that learn through reward signals. [[concepts/ai-models|Neural networks]], decision trees, support vector machines, and transformer-based architectures represent distinct approaches suited to different problem domains.

## Access and Customization

Platforms like [[concepts/open-source-machine-learning|Hugging Face]] provide centralized access to [[concepts/pre-trained-models|pre-trained models]] across [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], and [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] tasks. Users can leverage these [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] directly or fine-tune them on [[concepts/custom-dataset|domain-specific data]], reducing the computational and [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] barriers to deploying [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]].
