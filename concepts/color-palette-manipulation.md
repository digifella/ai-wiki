# Color Palette Manipulation

Color Palette Manipulation is a technique in Lightroom Classic that utilizes the Color Mixer tool to perform targeted adjustments on specific color ranges within an image. This process relies on HSL (Hue, Saturation, and Luminance) controls, which allow photographers to isolate individual color channels rather than applying broad adjustments across the entire photograph. The tool typically provides independent sliders for reds, oranges, yellows, greens, cyans, blues, and magentas. By isolating these specific channels, users can modify the hue, saturation, and luminance of each color independently to correct color casts, enhance vibrancy, or create specific artistic effects without affecting other parts of the image.

## HSL Adjustments

The HSL panel serves as the primary interface for this manipulation, offering three distinct types of controls for each of the eight primary color channels. Hue adjustments shift a color toward its neighbors on the color wheel, such as turning reds toward orange or blues toward purple. Saturation controls increase or decrease the intensity of a specific color, allowing for the desaturation of distracting elements or the enhancement of key subjects. Luminance adjustments alter the brightness of a specific color channel, which can be used to lighten skin tones in the orange range or darken skies in the blue range without impacting the overall exposure of the image.

## Practical Applications

This technique is particularly valuable for correcting white balance issues that are isolated to specific colors, such as removing unwanted green tints from foliage or reducing orange skin tones. It also enables precise control over color grading, where photographers can shift the mood of an image by altering the relationship between complementary colors. For instance, pushing blues toward teal and oranges toward red can create a cinematic look, while desaturating background colors can help draw attention to the main subject. The granularity of these controls allows for nuanced edits that global adjustment tools cannot achieve.
