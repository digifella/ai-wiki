# Graphiti

[[concepts/graphiti|Graphiti]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] platform that combines [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval augmented generation]] (RAG) with [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] to improve [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] in [[concepts/dynamic-data-environments|dynamic data environments]]. [[concepts/traditional-rag|Traditional RAG]] systems face challenges when working with data that changes frequently or grows substantially over time, as they struggle to maintain coherent context and manage [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between information sources. Graphiti addresses these limitations by leveraging [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]] structures to better organize, represent, and retrieve interconnected data.

## Purpose and Application

The platform is designed to enhance how language [[concepts/models|models]] access and utilize external information by structuring data as networks of relationships rather than isolated documents or chunks. This approach allows systems using Graphiti to maintain stronger [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]] when dealing with evolving datasets, where the relationships between data points may be as important as the data itself. The [[concepts/vector-store|knowledge graph]] foundation enables more sophisticated querying and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] across complex information landscapes.
