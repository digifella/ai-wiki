# DeepSeek AI

**[[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] AI** refers to the series of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and multimodal systems developed by [[entities/deepseek-ai|DeepSeek]], a leading Chinese [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] laboratory. The organization is known for [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] and efficient training methodologies, competing with major players like [[entities/openai]], [[entities/meta-ai]], and [[entities/google-deepmind]].

## Core Competencies & Developments

- **Language Modeling**: Development of [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] LLMs optimized for [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|mathematical reasoning]], and [[concepts/multilingual-support|multilingual support]].
- **Efficiency**: Focus on training efficiency and [[concepts/speed|inference speed]], often utilizing hybrid [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]].
- **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Integration]]**: Recent advancements in integrating [[concepts/vision-capabilities|visual processing]] with textual [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].

## Multimodal Reasoning: Visual Primitives (2026)

Recent developments highlight a shift towards more structured visual processing:

- **[[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]] with [[concepts/visual-primitives|Visual Primitives]]**: A novel approach where the model processes images not just as raw pixels or generic [[concepts/dense-vectors|embeddings]], but by decomposing them into discrete "visual primitives." This allows for more precise alignment between visual elements and [[concepts/reasoning-skills|logical reasoning]] steps.
- **Precise [[concepts/multimodal-reasoning|Multimodal Reasoning]]**: This method aims to reduce [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] in visual tasks by grounding [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] in identifiable visual components rather than holistic [[concepts/visual-perception|image interpretation]].
- **Source Reference**: Detailed analysis available in [[lab-notes/2026-05-22-DeepSeeks-AI-Thinking-with-Visual-Primitives-for-Precise|DeepSeek's AI: Thinking with Visual Primitives for Precise Multimodal Reasoning]].
