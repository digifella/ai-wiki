# Autonomous Customization

**Autonomous [[concepts/customization|customization]]** refers to the capability of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to independently modify their own functionality, structure, or behavior to better suit specific tasks or user needs without direct human intervention. This concept is central to the evolution of [[concepts/self-extending-ai]] agents.

## Core Principles
- **Self-Modification:** The system can generate, test, and [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] new code or configurations autonomously.
- **Contextual Adaptation:** Customization is driven by real-time interaction data and specific use-case requirements.
- **Open Ecosystems:** Reliance on [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] frameworks to allow transparent and extensible architectures.

## Key Developments

### DeepSeek Harness
A significant implementation of autonomous customization is the **[[concepts/deepseek-harness|DeepSeek Harness]]**, a self-extending [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] designed to empower agents with dynamic functionality.

- **Functionality:** Enables [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to be "self-extending," allowing them to grow and adapt their capabilities over time.
- **Architecture:** Free and open-source, promoting community-driven improvements and [[concepts/opacity|transparency]].
- **Significance:** Represents a shift from static models to dynamic, evolving systems that can address novel problems autonomously.
