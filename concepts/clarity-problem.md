# Clarity Problem

- **Core [[concepts/slms|Definition]]**: The fundamental issue for most business owners is not a lack of time, but a lack of [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]].
- **The "Treadmill" Symptom**: Operating at high velocity/effort without a specific direction or destination.
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]] Framework**: The [[concepts/time-clarity-interview-system|Time Clarity Interview System]].
	- **Mechanism**: Reverses the standard [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] interaction paradigm.
	- **Process**: Instead of the user [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] the AI, the AI conducts an interview with the user to extract, refine, and establish direction.
