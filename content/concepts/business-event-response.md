# Business event response

The strategic framework for executing predefined actions and [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] in response to specific triggers within an [[concepts/organization|organization]]'s operational ecosystem.

## Automation Paradigms

- **[[concepts/time-based-automation|Time-based Automation]]**: Traditional [[concepts/automation|automation]] functioning on fixed schedules (analogous to an alarm clock) that triggers at specific intervals regardless of environmental changes.
- **[[concepts/action-based-automation|Action-based Automation]]**: [[concepts/event-driven-architecture]] where workflows are triggered by specific occurrences or state changes within a system.

## Recent Developments

- **[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] Routines**: A significant evolution in [[concepts/ai-automation]] introduced by [[entities/anthropic]].
	- Marks a shift from time-based scheduling to action-based execution.
	- Enables more reactive and context-aware business event [[concepts/responses|responses]].
