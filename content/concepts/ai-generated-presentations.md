# AI Generated Presentations

[[concepts/llm-driven-slide-generation|AI-generated presentations]] refer to the automated creation of presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] through [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems. This approach combines AI language models that generate content with specialized formatting tools to produce slideshows. Users typically provide input through simplified text formats or structured prompts, which AI systems then expand into full presentation content and convert into visual slides. This method reduces the manual effort required for presentation design and formatting, allowing creators to focus on content [[concepts/organization|organization]] and messaging.

## Technical Implementation

The process generally involves two main components: content generation and slide formatting. AI language models handle the [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] phase, producing text, bullet points, [[entities/speaker|speaker]] notes, and structural organization based on user input. [[concepts/specialized-tools|Specialized tools]] like Marp convert this content into formatted slides using [[concepts/markdown|Markdown]] or similar markup languages. This [[concepts/separation-of-concerns|separation of concerns]] allows users to work with familiar text-based formats while the system handles the visual presentation layer automatically.

## Practical Applications

AI-generated presentations are used across various contexts including business meetings, educational settings, and conference talks. The approach is particularly useful for creating initial drafts, generating multiple presentation variations quickly, or producing presentations in volume. Users retain the ability to edit and refine both the content and visual styling after generation, making the AI system a starting point rather than a final output tool.
