# Tone

Tone refers to the stylistic and emotional quality of communication, particularly when prompting AI models like Claude. In the context of interacting with Claude, tone encompasses the manner in which instructions are conveyed, the formality level employed, and the implicit expectations set through language choice. The tone of a prompt influences how the model interprets the request, the depth of its response, and the style in which it presents information.

## Impact on Model

The selected tone acts as a contextual signal that guides the model’s behavior and output characteristics. Formal tones typically elicit more structured, precise, and professional responses, suitable for technical documentation or business communication. Conversely, casual or conversational tones encourage a more relaxed, creative, and approachable style, which can be beneficial for brainstorming or informal dialogue.

Adjusting tone also affects the perceived authority and nuance of the interaction. Direct, imperative tones may result in concise, action-oriented outputs, while collaborative or exploratory tones can foster deeper reasoning and iterative refinement. Understanding these dynamics allows users to align the model’s output with specific project requirements, ensuring the generated content matches the intended audience and purpose.
