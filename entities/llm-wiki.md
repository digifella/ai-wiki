## Recent Developments

### Persistent Memory Systems
Current research and implementations are shifting from static [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]] to dynamic, persistent [[concepts/memory-structures|memory structures]] for [[concepts/local-ai-agents|local AI agents]].

- **Librarian System Architecture**: A new approach to providing local AI with evolving, [[concepts/persistent-memory|persistent memory]].
	- **Source**: [[lab-notes/2026-07-13-Developing-Persistent-Intelligent-Memory-for-Local-AI-wi|Developing Persistent, Intelligent Memory for Local AI with a Librarian System]]
	- **Key Concept**: Moves beyond simple [[concepts/context-injection|context injection]] by implementing a structured "librarian" system that manages [[concepts/retrieving|memory retrieval]] and [[entities/storage|storage]], allowing the AI to maintain state across sessions without relying solely on repeated prompt feeding.
