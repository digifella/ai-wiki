# Tint Adjustment

Tint adjustment is a color grading feature introduced in Photoshop 2025 that enables users to modify the color temperature and hue balance of digital images. This functionality allows photographers and designers to systematically shift colors toward warmer or cooler tones, either across the entire image or within specific selected areas. As part of Adobe's broader color correction capabilities, the tool provides precise control over the overall color cast of a photograph.

The feature is integrated into the updated Color and Vibrance tool, which consolidates several color management functions into a single interface. This integration streamlines the workflow for adjusting color balance and vibrancy simultaneously. The tool is detailed in tutorials by Blake Rudis, who highlights its role in modernizing color grading processes within the software.
