# 2026 Campaign Season

The **2026 Campaign Season** marks a distinct [[concepts/phase|phase]] in the War in [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]], characterized by a shift from high-intensity maneuver warfare to a protracted [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] defined by logistical attrition and fortified defensive lines.

## Strategic Dynamics

*   **Shift in Offensive Tempo**: [[concepts/russian-offensive|Russian offensive]] efforts have continued but at a significantly slowed pace compared to previous years, indicating a transition to grinding, resource-heavy operations.
*   **Ukrainian Defensive Posture**: Ukraine has consolidated its positions into a "[[concepts/fortress-belt|Fortress Belt]]," emphasizing deep defense and [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] against sustained pressure.
*   **Logistical Warfare**: The campaign is increasingly defined by strikes on [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] rather than front-line breakthroughs. Key targets span from [[entities/crimea|Crimea]] to Kostiantynivka, disrupting Russian material [[concepts/flow|flow]].

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/logistics-strikes|Logistics Strikes]]**: Significant focus on interdicting supply routes connecting occupied territories to active front lines. See [[lab-notes/2026-06-29-Crimea-to-Kostiantynivka-2026-Logistics-Strikes-and-Ukra|Crimea to Kostiantynivka: 2026 Logistics Strikes and Ukraine's Fortress Belt]] for detailed analysis of these operations.
*   **[[concepts/defensive-fortifications|Fortress Belt]] Construction**: Ukraine has reinforced defensive infrastructure, creating a layered defense system designed to absorb and blunt Russian advances while inflicting maximum casualties.
