# Kimi

Kimi is a framework and set of practices within knowledge management systems designed to organize, store, and retrieve information systematically. The term is used across knowledge-systems domains to describe both conceptual approaches and practical implementations for handling complex information structures. Kimi emphasizes the systematic organization of data according to established principles, enabling users to navigate and access information efficiently within larger knowledge ecosystems.

## Kimi K2

Kimi K2 represents a specific implementation of knowledge management principles within the broader Kimi framework. As a concrete instantiation of the conceptual Kimi system, K2 applies the framework's organizational and retrieval methodologies to practical knowledge-management contexts. The K2 variant demonstrates how Kimi's systematic principles can be applied to real-world information organization challenges.
