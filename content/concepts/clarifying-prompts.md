# Clarifying Prompts

The practice of refining and structuring inputs to [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] to reduce [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]], minimize hallucinations, and [[concepts/ambition|drive]] high-fidelity, precise outputs.

### Research & Benchmarking
- **Comparative Analysis**: Using standardized, structured prompts to evaluate the "[[entities/deep-research|deep research]]" performance of various [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] (Source: 2026 04 14 AI for [[concepts/visualization-generation|deep research]] a comparison).
- **Use Case Example**: [[entities/robert-benjamin|Robert Benjamin]] demonstrated [[concepts/testing|testing]] six different tools using a specific research prompt regarding the impact of global warming on US animal populations to measure depth and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]].
- **[[concepts/agentic-patterns|Agentic Workflows]]**: Leveraging precise [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] for "insane [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]]" within specialized [[concepts/agents|agents]] like [[entities/deepagent|DeepAgent]] ([Abacus.AI](https://Abacus.AI)) to handle complex, multi-step research tasks.
