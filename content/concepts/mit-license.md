# MIT License Overview

The [[entities/mit-license|MIT License]] is a permissive [[concepts/photo-editing|free software]] [[concepts/license|license]] originating from the [[concepts/massachusetts-institute-of-technology-entitiesmitmit|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] (MIT). It grants users broad permissions to use, modify, and distribute the licensed [[concepts/software|software]] while providing legal protection for the copyright holder.

### Key Features
- **Permissive [[concepts/licensing|Licensing]]:** Allows redistribution, commercialization, and modification with minimal restrictions.
- **Simplified Terms:** Contains a brief set of conditions that are easy for developers and users to understand and comply with.
- **No Warranty or Liability:** The license does not provide any warranties or take liability for the use of the software.
