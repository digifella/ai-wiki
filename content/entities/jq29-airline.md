# Jq29 Airline

JQ29 is a flight service operating between [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] and Bangkok with a scheduled overnight stopover in Istanbul. The route connects Australia's second-largest city to Thailand's capital via [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]], making it a multi-leg international journey rather than a direct service.

## Route and Schedule

The flight departs Melbourne at 13:20 and arrives in Istanbul at 05:15 the following morning. Passengers remain in Istanbul overnight before continuing to Bangkok, allowing for rest and potential city exploration during the layover.

## Booking Reference

Flight JQ29 is identified by booking reference U4PKH2 for ticketing and passenger information purposes. The service includes accommodation arrangements during the Istanbul stopover, with affiliated hotel partnerships such as Old Alessia City Hotel available to passengers.
