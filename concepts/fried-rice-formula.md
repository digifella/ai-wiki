# Fried Rice Formula

Fried rice is a versatile dish whose success depends primarily on two critical factors: proper rice preparation and thoughtful ingredient selection. This formula-based approach allows cooks to produce successful fried rice consistently, regardless of which specific ingredients are available or preferred. The foundation of quality fried rice relies on using the correct type of cooked rice and preparing it correctly before frying.

## Rice Treatment

The most important step in making fried rice is ensuring the rice is dry and cool. Freshly cooked rice contains too much moisture, which leads to steaming rather than frying, resulting in a mushy texture. Ideally, day-old rice that has been refrigerated is used, as the cooling process retrogrades the starches, making the grains firmer and less sticky. If time is short, freshly cooked rice can be spread out on a baking sheet to cool and evaporate excess steam before being refrigerated for a short period.

## Ingredient Selection

The choice of mix-ins should complement the savory base without overwhelming the rice. Common proteins include diced chicken, shrimp, pork, or tofu, which are typically cooked separately before being added to the wok. Vegetables such as peas, carrots, and scallions provide texture and color, while aromatics like garlic and ginger build the foundational flavor profile. The seasoning is usually simple, relying on soy sauce for saltiness and umami, and sometimes sesame oil for aroma, ensuring the rice remains the dominant element of the dish.
