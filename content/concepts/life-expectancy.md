# Life Expectancy
Life expectancy is a measure of the average number of years a person is expected to live based on current [[concepts/health|health]] conditions and trends. It's influenced by various factors including [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]], [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]], education, environmental quality, socioeconomic status, and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] initiatives.

### Key Points:
- Between 1900 and 1999, life expectancy in the [[entities/united-states|United States]] increased by over 30 years.
- Approximately 25 of those additional years are attributed to advancements in public health efforts ([[concepts/public-health]]]).
- Public health interventions such as sanitation improvements, [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]] [[concepts/software|programs]], and clean water initiatives have significantly contributed to this increase.
