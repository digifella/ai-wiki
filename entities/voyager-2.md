# Voyager 2

**[[concepts/voyager-2|Voyager 2]]** is a robotic space probe launched by [[entities/nasa]] on August 20, 1977, to study the outer planets. It is the only spacecraft to have visited all four giant planets: Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune.

## Key Discoveries

### Saturn
*   First probe to provide detailed imagery of Saturn's rings and moons.
*   Discovered the **hexagonal cloud pattern** at Saturn's north pole in 1981 [[lab-notes/2026-07-18-Saturns-Hexagonal-North-Pole-Mystery-and-Explanations|Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole: Mystery and Explanations]].
*   Identified new moons and rings, including the F ring and moons like Epimetheus and Janus.

### Uranus
*   First and only spacecraft to visit Uranus (1986).
*   Discovered 10 new moons and 2 new rings.
*   Revealed the planet's extreme axial tilt and cold [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]].

### Neptune
*   First and only spacecraft to visit Neptune (1989).
*   Discovered the Great Dark Spot and observed active [[concepts/weather-patterns|weather patterns]].
*   Confirmed the existence of rings and discovered 6 new moons.

## Current Status
*   Launched into interstellar space in 1989.
*   Entered the Heliopause in 2018, becoming the second human-made object to do so (after Voyager 1).
*   Continues to transmit data to [[entities/earth|Earth]] via the [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|Deep Space]] Network, though signal strength is diminishing.
