### Summary

In this podcast episode, Professor Hannah Fry sits down with Demis Hassabis, the CEO and Co-Founder of Google DeepMind, to discuss the past year in AI and explore where the field is heading next.

They delve into several key topics, including:
- **Major shifts in AI:** The shift from large language models to agentic AI, the acceleration of drug discovery, and the integration of multimodal models into robotics and driverless cars.
- **The future of AI:** The potential for AI to tackle complex problems in science and medicine, such as room-temperature superconductors and nuclear fusion, and the importance of balancing AI development with rigorous scientific research and international collaboration.
- **The ethical and societal implications of AI:** The potential for AI to create echo chambers, the risk of AI-generated misinformation, and the need for new economic and social models to adapt to a post-AGI world.

Demis Hassabis shares his insights and predictions, expressing optimism about AI's potential to benefit humanity while acknowledging the challenges and responsibilities that come with it. He emphasizes the importance of responsible AI development and the need for ongoing dialogue between researchers, policymakers, and the public.
