# Nuclear Fusion

The process of combining [[concepts/light|light]] atomic nuclei to [[concepts/deployment|release]] massive amounts of energy, fundamentally mimicking the [[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]] occurring within a Star.

## Core Concepts
- Aims to replicate the colossal energy output of the Sun to create a sustainable power source for [[entities/earth|Earth]].
- Relies on the fusion of elements such as Hydrogen and Helium.
- Focuses on the scientific quest to [[concepts/harness|harness]] the power of a Star via terrestrial replication.

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/wendelstein-7-x|Wendelstein 7-X]]**: [[entities/germany|Germany]]'s stellarator reactor has set [[concepts/new-world|new world]] records, demonstrating significant progress in steady-state plasma confinement and validating the non-axisymmetric design.

## Historical Context & Anomalies
- **[[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Project-Huemul-Argentinas-Controversial-Nuclear-Fusion-E|Project Huemul: Argentina's Controversial Nuclear Fusion Experiment and Fraud]]**: A mid-20th-century Argentina [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] led by César Lattes that claimed to achieve [[concepts/fusion-power|nuclear fusion]] but was later exposed as a scientific fraud, highlighting the risks of premature claims in energy research.
