# Disco

Disco is a feature introduced in [[concepts/cc|Google Gemini updates]], also known by the alternative name GenTabs. The feature was demonstrated in a Google Gemini updates video published in April 2026.

## Functionality

Disco enables users to organize and manage multiple related tasks or information streams simultaneously within the [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] interface. The feature appears designed to improve workflow efficiency by allowing parallel engagement with different topics or query threads without losing context or requiring repeated setup.
