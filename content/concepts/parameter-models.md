# Parameter Models

Parameter Models refers to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] whose behavior and capabilities are defined by their learned [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]—the numerical [[concepts/weights|weights]] and biases that are optimized during [[concepts/training|training]]. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and generative systems, parameter models encompass a range of architectures from [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] to multimodal systems, with significant variation in how they are distributed and deployed.

## Open Weights Models

[[entities/openai|OpenAI]] released open weights versions of its GPT models, making the parameter data publicly available for [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]]. This represents a shift in [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] for developers and researchers who previously relied on API-based access, enabling more control over [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] and integration into custom workflows.

## Multimodal Text-to-Video and Image-to-Video Models

Text-to-video and [[concepts/image-to-video-model|image-to-video generation]] models like [[concepts/14b-parameter-model|Wan 2.2]] can be run locally using frameworks such as [[concepts/comfyui|ComfyUI]], a node-based interface that manages [[concepts/active-parameters|model parameters]] and processing pipelines. These models operate on similar parameter-based principles as language models but extend the concept to handle temporal sequences and visual transformations, allowing users to generate video content without [[concepts/cloud-dependencies|cloud dependencies]].
