## Nvidia CUDA GPU Parallel Computing for AI Advancement

This video provides a concise yet comprehensive introduction to [[concepts/compute-unified-device-architecture|CUDA]] ([[concepts/compute|Compute]] Unified Device [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]), a [[concepts/general-purpose-computing|parallel computing]] platform developed by [[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]].
