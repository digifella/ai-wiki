# Gemini Text Models

Gemini Text Models constitute Google’s family of large language models (LLMs) designed for the processing and generation of natural language. As a foundational component of the Google AI ecosystem, these models provide the underlying infrastructure for a wide array of Google products and services. They enable core capabilities such as content creation, information retrieval, and complex conversational interactions by leveraging advanced transformer-based architectures.

The architecture is designed to support multimodal inputs, allowing the models to understand and generate text alongside other data types. This versatility positions the Gemini family as a central element in Google’s strategy for AI-native applications, including design tools like Google Stitch. By integrating these models into its broader suite, Google aims to enhance user productivity and streamline workflows through intelligent automation and contextual understanding.
