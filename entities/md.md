# Md

The abbreviation "Md" is commonly used in medical and healthcare contexts to stand for "Medical Doctor" or "Doctor of Medicine." It represents the primary professional degree awarded to physicians in many English-speaking countries, including the United States, Canada, and the United Kingdom. The credential signifies that the holder has completed the necessary academic and clinical training to practice medicine.

To obtain an MD degree, individuals typically complete four years of medical school following an undergraduate degree. The MD credential indicates that the physician has met the rigorous educational standards required for medical licensure and professional practice.

In a separate context regarding regulatory technology, the Food and Drug Administration (FDA) has begun using agentic AI for in-house operations. This initiative marks a shift in how the agency manages internal workflows and processes, distinct from the clinical definition of the MD credential.
