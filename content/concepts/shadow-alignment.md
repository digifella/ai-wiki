# Shadow Alignment

Shadow Alignment refers to a feature within [[concepts/ai-powered-image-manipulation|AI-powered image manipulation]] tools, particularly in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]], that automatically adjusts and aligns [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] in [[concepts/images|images]] during editing. This functionality leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to analyze existing shadow patterns and apply consistent [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] across image elements, streamlining the manual process of shadow correction and matching.

## Application in Image Editing

In practical use, Shadow Alignment helps maintain visual coherence when manipulating images with multiple elements or layers. By automatically detecting shadow directions, intensity, and positioning, the tool can apply proportional adjustments when elements are moved, resized, or composited together. This reduces the need for manual shadow painting and allows for faster, more consistent results in professional image editing workflows.

The feature represents part of a broader trend in Adobe Photoshop toward AI-assisted editing tools that automate repetitive technical tasks, allowing editors to focus on creative decisions rather than granular adjustments.
