# 16-bit depth

A measurement of the precision of color and luminance information stored per pixel. Increasing bit depth expands the available palette, significantly reducing Color Banding (posterization) during heavy [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] and Compositing.

## Technical Significance
- **Luminosity Levels**: Provides $2^{16}$ (65,536) discrete levels per color channel, compared to only 256 levels in 8-bit depth.
- **Gradient [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Essential for maintaining smooth transitions in high-[[concepts/contrast|contrast]] Digital Imaging workflows.

## Practical Application
Derived from 2026 04 14 [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]] Compositing in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]:
- **Professional [[concepts/setup|Setup]]**: Recommended for [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] compositing environments (e.g., 300 ppi, [[concepts/adobe-rgb]] color space).
- **Workflow Integration**: Critical for preserving fidelity when importing high-[[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic-range]] assets from [[concepts/lightroom]].
