# External Data

External data refers to information and resources that exist outside an AI model's static training corpus or built-in [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]. This category encompasses files, databases, [[concepts/application-programming-interfaces-apis|Application Programming Interfaces (APIs)]], real-time information streams, and other external systems that [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can access during operation. By connecting to these sources, [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] can utilize current information, domain-specific datasets, and proprietary data that would otherwise be unavailable to them.

The integration of external data is fundamental to transforming static language models into practical, real-[[entities/earth|world]] applications. Without this capability, AI systems would remain limited to the [[concepts/knowledge-cutoff|knowledge cutoff]] of their [[concepts/custom-dataset|training data]], unable to perform tasks requiring up-to-date context or specialized [[concepts/experience|domain expertise]]. Accessing external resources allows models to retrieve precise, verified information and interact with [[concepts/production-environments|live environments]], thereby increasing their utility and accuracy in dynamic [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].

The [[concepts/external-tools|Model Context Protocol]] (MCP) serves as a standardized framework to facilitate this connectivity. [[concepts/acting|Acting]] as a universal interface, MCP allows [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] to securely and efficiently connect to various external data sources. This standardization enables models to be transformed into functional agents capable of executing [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]] by leveraging the full breadth of available [[concepts/digital-infrastructure|digital infrastructure]] rather than relying solely on internal parameters.
