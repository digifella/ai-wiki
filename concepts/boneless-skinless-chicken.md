# Boneless Skinless Chicken

**Boneless skinless chicken** refers to poultry meat, typically Chicken breast or Chicken thigh, from which the bones and skin have been removed. This cut is widely used for its versatility, [[concepts/lean|lean]] profile, and [[concepts/quick-cooking|quick cooking]] time. Common [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] methods include [[concepts/bbq|grilling]], baking, stir-frying, and deep-frying.

## Culinary Applications

### Japanese Cuisine: Karaage
A prominent application of this cut is in **[[concepts/japanese-fried-chicken|Karaage]]**, a popular Japanese cuisine dish featuring crispy fried chicken pieces. The technique often involves marinating the meat in soy sauce, ginger, and garlic before coating it with starch to achieve a crisp [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] without traditional breading.

## Preparation Notes
- **[[concepts/texture|Texture]]:** Without skin, achieving crispiness requires techniques such as cornstarch coating or double-frying.
- **[[concepts/marinating|Marination]]:** Essential for flavor infusion since the fat and collagen found in skin/bones are absent.
- **[[concepts/cooking-time|Cooking Time]]:** Significantly faster than bone-in cuts; prone to drying out if overcooked.
