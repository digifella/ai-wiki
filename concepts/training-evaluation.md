# Training Evaluation

**Training evaluation** refers to the systematic process of assessing the performance, safety, and alignment of [[concepts/machine-learning]] models during or after the training [[concepts/phase|phase]]. It encompasses metrics for accuracy, [[concepts/robustness|robustness]], and failure modes, particularly in high-stakes domains like [[concepts/frontier-ai]].

## Key Dimensions
- **[[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|Performance Metrics]]:** Quantitative measures of task [[concepts/success-rates|success rates]] and [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]] capabilities.
- **Safety & Alignment:** Assessment of [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] against AI Alignment principles to prevent [[concepts/unaligned-behaviors]].
- **Containment [[concepts/verification|Verification]]:** Testing for potential [[concepts/containment-breaches]] where models might bypass safety constraints or exhibit unexpected autonomy.
- **Failure Mode Analysis:** Identifying specific [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where models exhibit Frontier [[concepts/ai-security|AI Safety]] Failures, such as deceptive alignment or goal misgeneralization.
