# Hyperon stack

The **Hyperon stack** refers to the underlying architecture and ecosystem supporting advanced AI [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], particularly within the context of SingularityNET and its associated frameworks. It emphasizes persistent, explainable, and symbolic [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] over purely statistical approaches.

## Key Components & Integrations

### OmegaClaw Integration
The stack supports or integrates with **OmegaClaw**, a neural-symbolic AI [[concepts/agent-harness|agent framework]] designed to address the limitations of traditional request-response [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].

*   **Architecture:** Combines [[concepts/symbolic-logic|symbolic logic]] with [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] to enable persistent and explainable reasoning [[lab-notes/2026-09-07-OmegaClaw-A-Neural-Symbolic-AI-Agent-for-Persistent-Expl|OmegaClaw: A Neural-Symbolic AI Agent for Persistent, Explainable Reasoning]].
*   **Development:** [[concepts/open-source-framework|Open-source framework]] developed by SingularityNET.
*   **Core [[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]:** Prioritizes symbolic [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] over pure LLM-based generation to improve [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and [[concepts/opacity|transparency]].
*   **Reference:** [OmegaClaw: A Neural-Symbolic AI Agent for Persistent, Explainable Reasoning](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ToU9gYXBBWI)
