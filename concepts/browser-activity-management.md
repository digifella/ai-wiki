Browser Activity Management is the practice of overseeing and controlling all activities performed within a web browser to enhance [[concepts/productivity|productivity]], [[concepts/security|security]], and efficiency. This includes monitoring browsing history, managing bookmarks, setting up [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] controls, and using extensions or add-ons to streamline tasks.

### Key Concepts
- **Browsing History:** Recording and categorizing visited websites for reference.
- **Bookmarks & [[concepts/notes|Notes]]:** Saving important sites and taking notes directly in the browser.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy Settings]]:** Adjusting settings to protect personal information.
- **Extensions/Add-Ons:** Utilizing third-party tools to enhance functionality.
