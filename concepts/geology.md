# Geology

Geology is a branch of [[entities/earth|Earth]] sciences that studies the solid features of Earth and other terrestrial planets and moons. It encompasses processes such as [[concepts/tectonic-plate-shifts|plate tectonics]], [[concepts/geological-formation|rock formation]], [[concepts/volcanic-eruption|volcanic activity]], sedimentation, and erosion, which shape planetary surfaces over time.

## Mars Geological Findings
- [[concepts/ancient-rainforests|Ancient rainforests]] on [[concepts/mars|Mars]]: Evidence suggesting past [[concepts/habitability|habitability]] through fossilized remains or isotopic signatures.
- [[concepts/signs-of-life|Signs of life]]: Potential biosignatures indicating microbial activity in the Martian subsurface.
- Lightning phenomena: Recent observations indicate electrical discharges similar to those occurring on Earth, impacting atmospheric [[concepts/weathering|weathering]] and surface chemistry.

## Volcanic Structures and Rock Formation
- [[lab-notes/2026-10-06-Basalt-Column-Formation-Lava-Cooling-Contraction-and-Hex|Basalt Column Formation: Lava Cooling, Contraction, and Hexagonal Cracking]]: Regular polygonal rock formations (e.g., in Iceland and [[entities/united-kingdom|Northern Ireland]]) resulting from the cooling and contraction of [[concepts/basalt|basaltic]] [[concepts/lava-flows|lava]].
- [[concepts/volcanic-eruption|Volcanic activity]]: Key driver of [[concepts/basalt|basalt]] production and subsequent structural cooling patterns.
- [[concepts/geological-formation|Rock formation]]: Includes both igneous processes (like [[concepts/basalt-column|basalt column]] formation) and sedimentary processes.
