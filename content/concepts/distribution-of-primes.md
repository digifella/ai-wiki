# Distribution of Primes

[[concepts/prime-numbers|Prime numbers]] are integers greater than 1 that have no positive divisors other than 1 and themselves. The [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of [[concepts/prime-numbers|prime numbers]] among the integers is a fundamental topic in [[concepts/number-theory|number theory]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/prime-counting-function|Prime-counting function (π(n))]]**: The function π(n) gives the number of primes less than or equal to n.
- **[[concepts/prime-number-theorem|Prime Number Theorem]]**: As n approaches infinity, π(n) is approximately equal to n / log(n).
- **[[concepts/twin-prime-conjecture|Twin Primes]] Conjecture**: There are infinitely many pairs of prime numbers that differ by 2 (e.g., 3 and 5, 11 and 13).
