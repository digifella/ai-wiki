# SATA drive bays

Physical compartments or slots within a chassis (such as a NAS, [[concepts/home-server]], or PC) designed to accommodate SATA (Serial ATA) hard drives or SSDs. These bays are fundamental to [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] scalability and [[entities/storage|storage]] density.

## Hardware Implementations
* **[[concepts/m2-storage|TerraMaster F4-425 Plus]]**: An example of a [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] NAS and [[concepts/home-server|home server]] application utilizing drive bays for storage flexibility and virtualization.
	* **Processor**: [[concepts/intel-n150|Intel N150]] [[concepts/cpu|CPU]] (Performance-efficient, low power).
	* **[[concepts/memory|Memory]]**: 16G [[concepts/ram|RAM]].
