## Claude AI Productivity: Seven Secret Prompts Summary Report
**Clip title:** 7 Secret Prompts That Make Claude 10x Better
**Author / channel:** Sabrina Ramonov 🍄
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rabGqnyd_Zw

### Summary
This video introduces seven "secret codes" or advanced [[concepts/commands|commands]] for [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]], designed to significantly enhance user productivity. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], an experienced AI educator, claims these methods, tested across thousands of prompts and unknown to 99% of users, can make users ten times more productive in half the time. The video aims to provide a free "swipe file" of these codes and live demonstrations to help users bypass trial-and-error.

The first three codes focus on optimizing Claude's [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] process and resource usage. `/ultrathink` compels Claude to exert maximum [[concepts/reasoning-effort|reasoning effort]] for a specific prompt, overriding its default reasoning level. This is ideal for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] but should be used selectively to conserve "[[concepts/tokens|tokens]]" (AI processing units). `/caveman` is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] skill that dramatically reduces output tokens, making it highly beneficial for users on lower-tier plans who need to manage [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]]. Lastly, `/insights` allows Claude to analyze your last 30 days of sessions, generating a detailed report on your usage patterns, identifying strengths, bottlenecks, and suggesting personalized [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] for improvement based on your actual work.

The next set of codes revolves around automation and multitasking. `/loop` enables users to schedule [[concepts/recurring-tasks|recurring tasks]] within their local Claude [[concepts/session|session]]. These tasks can repeat at intervals as short as one minute but require the user's computer and Claude session to remain active and will expire after three days. A related, more advanced feature is `/schedule`, which runs recurring tasks independently in the cloud. This offers greater flexibility as it doesn't require the user's computer to be on or the session to be active, making it suitable for daily standups or recurring reports, though it has a minimum interval of one hour, no local file access, and daily run caps depending on the user's plan. For seamless multitasking, `/btw` (by the way) allows users to ask ancillary questions or run minor prompts without interrupting a primary, long-running task in the same session, preserving the main task's context.

Finally, `/clear` addresses context management, a crucial aspect of efficient AI interaction. This command purges the current session's context. Its utility lies in preventing AI [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] from degrading due to an overload of irrelevant previous conversations and in saving tokens by giving Claude a fresh slate for new tasks. The speaker strongly recommends using `/clear` when switching between distinct tasks to maintain optimal performance and manage [[concepts/token-consumption|token consumption]]. These seven codes collectively offer advanced strategies for leveraging Claude more effectively, transforming how users interact with the AI for improved efficiency and output quality.
