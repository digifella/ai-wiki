The methodology of constructing AI-driven [[concepts/software|applications]] and [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] through [[concepts/natural-language-processing|Natural Language processing]] and visual orchestration, bypassing traditional [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]] and manual [[concepts/coding|coding]].

    - Developed by [[entities/google-labs]] to enable the creation, sharing, and democratization of [[concepts/ai-mini-apps]] and software creation.
    - **Functionality**: Uses natural language descriptions and visual editing to chain together prompts, [[concepts/models]], and tools.
