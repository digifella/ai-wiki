# Benchmark testing

The process of evaluating the performance, capability, or efficiency of a system ([[concepts/software|software]], [[concepts/hardware|hardware]], or [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]) against standardized or custom metrics.

### Methodologies
- **Standardized Benchmarks**: Use of established datasets and metrics to measure specific [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] (e.g., [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], or linguistic [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]]).
- **Complex/Custom Benchmarks**: High-fidelity tests designed to simulate real-world, multi-step workflows.
    - **[[concepts/one-shot-build|One-Shot Build]]**: A benchmark measuring an [[entities/agent|agent]]'s ability to execute a complete, complex project from a single prompt.
        - *Case Study*: Evaluating [[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus 4.5]] vs [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT 5.2]] by utilizing a massive, complex **[[concepts/prd|PRD]]** as the core [[concepts/testing|testing]] framework ([[entities/matt-maher|Matt Maher]]).
