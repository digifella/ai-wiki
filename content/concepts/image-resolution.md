# Image Resolution

Image resolution refers to the level of detail in a digital image, typically measured in [[concepts/pixels-per-inch|pixels per inch]] (PPI) or megapixels. Increasing resolution after an image has been captured presents technical challenges, as enlarging an image through simple [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] methods produces blurry or pixelated results. Traditional [[concepts/image-upscaling|upscaling techniques]] like interpolation attempt to estimate missing pixel data, with varying degrees of success depending on the enlargement factor and image content.

## Generative Upscaling Methods

Recent advances in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] have introduced generative approaches to image upscaling. These methods use [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] trained on large datasets to predict plausible high-resolution detail when enlarging [[concepts/images|images]]. Rather than simply interpolating between existing pixels, generative upscaling can infer textures, edges, and fine details that align with the visual content of the original image. This approach often produces more natural results than traditional interpolation, though computational requirements are higher.

## Professional Tools and Integration

Image upscaling capabilities have been integrated into professional [[concepts/software|software]] like [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]], which now offers generative upscaling features. These tools typically allow users to enlarge images while maintaining visual quality, sometimes with options to control the upscaling process. The implementation of AI-based upscaling in mainstream creative software has made the technique more accessible to photographers, designers, and content creators who previously relied on specialized upscaling applications.
