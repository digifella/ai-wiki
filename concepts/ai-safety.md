# Guardrails
**Guardrails** refer to [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and [[concepts/policies|policies]] for ensuring safe and [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|ethical AI]] use.

## Current Landscape
*   **Open-Source & Frameworks:** [[concepts/unsloth-optimization|Nvidia]] provides open-source frameworks for safety, while [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]] offers consulting services. The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] like [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-OSS]] under the [[concepts/apache-license|Apache License]] expands access to safety tools.
*   **Monitoring & [[concepts/governance|Governance]]:** [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] has introduced [[concepts/autonomous-ai-agents|Observer Agents]] for real-time AI monitoring. Demis Hassabis has proposed new institutions for [[concepts/agi|AGI]] governance to address future risks.
*   **Regulatory Concerns:** There is ongoing discourse regarding the potential negative impact of government regulation on [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], economic growth, and global competitive standing.
