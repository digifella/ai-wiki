# V03

V03 is a guide documenting techniques for using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to generate realistic video content featuring oneself as a talking head. The guide addresses both personal and professional [[concepts/software|applications]], providing practical instruction on how to create convincing synthetic videos using available AI platforms and tools.

## Core Methodology

The guide emphasizes the technical process of inputting [[concepts/text|text]] or scripts alongside facial imagery to produce video [[concepts/output|output]] where an AI-generated version of a person appears to speak the provided content. This approach enables individuals to create video content without requiring traditional filming, editing equipment, or extensive time commitment. The methodology prioritizes achieving [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] in character representation across generated videos.

## Applications

V03 identifies both personal projects and professional uses as viable applications for this technology. Professional contexts include advertising and promotional materials, while personal applications span creative projects and communications where video content featuring oneself would be beneficial but traditional video production is impractical.
