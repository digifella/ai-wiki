# William George Armstrong

**Sir William [[entities/george|George]] Armstrong, 1st Baron Armstrong** (1810–1900) was a prominent English engineer, industrialist, and philanthropist. He is best known for founding Armstrong Whitworth and pioneering the use of hydraulic power in industry. His personal residence, Cragside, served as a laboratory for his experimental innovations in domestic automation and renewable energy.

## Key Innovations & Legacy

*   **Cragside as the First Smart Home:** Armstrong transformed his Northumberland estate into a prototype for modern smart home technology, decades before the digital age.
    *   Integrated [[concepts/automations|automated systems]] for lighting, heating, and [[concepts/water-management|water management]].
    *   Utilized [[concepts/hydroelectric-power|hydroelectric power]] generated on-site to power these systems.
    *   Featured early implementations of [[concepts/climate-control|climate control]] and [[concepts/remote-monitoring|remote monitoring]] concepts.
