# Emily Pulse It

Emily Pulse It is an initiative of the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI) dedicated to exploring the intersection of art and science. The project creates platforms for dialogue and engagement between artistic and scientific communities, working to bridge traditionally separate disciplinary boundaries through collaborative conversation and shared inquiry.

The initiative examines how artistic works and masterpieces can be engaged with in scientific contexts, and conversely, how scientific practices inform artistic thinking. By bringing together practitioners from both fields, the program facilitates a mutual exchange of perspectives that challenges conventional modes of understanding within each discipline.

Through these structured interactions, Emily Pulse It aims to foster a deeper appreciation for the creative processes inherent in scientific research while highlighting the analytical rigor present in artistic practice. This approach seeks to enrich both communities by encouraging them to view their respective methodologies through a new, interdisciplinary lens.
