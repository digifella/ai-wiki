# Shaking Technique

A method of cocktail [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] involving rapid agitation of ingredients within a vessel containing ice to achieve specific chemical and physical changes.

## Core Mechanics
- **Aeration**: Introduces air bubbles into the liquid, resulting in a cloudy [[concepts/presence|appearance]] and a frothy, textured mouthfeel.
- **Dilution**: Facilitates the controlled melting of ice to balance high-[[concepts/proof|proof]] spirits and temper acidity.
- **Temperature**: Rapidly lowers the temperature of the mixture toward the freezing point.
- **Comparison**: Unlike the [[concepts/stirring-technique]], which prioritizes [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] and minimal dilution, shaking focuses on [[concepts/texture|texture]] and vigorous integration.
