# Black level adjustment

The process of defining the darkest point in an image to establish a specific [[concepts/black-point-adjustment|Black point]], which directly influences [[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] and the [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of the Histogram.

## Workflow Context
- In advanced Landscape processing in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] - [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]], adjusting the [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] slider is a vital component of Initial [[concepts/image-preparation|Image Preparation]].
- This adjustment is typically performed alongside foundational tweaks to [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Highlights, [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], and [[concepts/whites|Whites]].
- Establishing proper black levels is a prerequisite before applying complex [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] techniques, such as the "Landscape" mask available in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] Classic, [[entities/lightroom-cc|Lightroom CC]], and [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]].
