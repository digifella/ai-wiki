# Context-aware retrieval

Context-aware retrieval is a method aimed at improving efficiency in [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] by focusing on context-specific information rather than relying solely on vast general knowledge. This approach allows LLMs to provide more accurate and relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]] with less computational overhead, addressing the inefficiency often associated with current LLMs.

- **[[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] [[entities/deepseek-engram|Engram]]**: A recent development discussed in a video by [[entities/two-minute-papers|Two Minute Papers]] [[entities/two-minute-papers]]. DeepSeek introduces an innovative [[concepts/solution|solution]] that addresses the inefficiencies inherent in [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]. The method utilizes context-aware retrieval to enhance performance.
