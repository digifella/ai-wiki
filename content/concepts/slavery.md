# Slavery

Slavery is a practice where individuals are owned by others and are forced to work without payment or freedom of [[concepts/exercise|movement]]. Historically, slavery has been employed across various societies for economic gain and social [[concepts/power|control]].

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/colonialism]]
- indentured servitude
- forced labor

### Historical Context

- Early forms of slavery were prevalent in ancient civilizations such as Mesopotamia, Egypt, Greece, and Rome.
- The transatlantic slave trade from the 15th to the 19th century involved the forced migration and exploitation of millions of Africans.

### Economic Impact
- Slavery was integral to colonial economies, particularly in the production of cash crops like [[concepts/sugar|sugar]], tobacco, and cotton.
