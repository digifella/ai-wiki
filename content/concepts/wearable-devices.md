# Wearable Devices

[[entities/iot-devices|Wearable devices]] are electronic instruments designed to be worn directly on the body, typically on the wrist, chest, or other accessible areas. These devices are equipped with [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] that continuously collect data about the wearer's physical state and environment. In the [[concepts/security|security]] and infrastructure domain, wearable devices serve critical functions for monitoring, communication, and situational awareness among personnel working in high-risk or demanding environments.

## Applications in Allied Health

Wearable devices have become increasingly important in [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] settings, where they monitor patient vital signs, mobility, and recovery progress. Physiotherapists, occupational therapists, and other [[concepts/health|health]] professionals use data from these devices to track patient outcomes, adjust treatment protocols, and provide objective evidence of functional improvement. The continuous data collection enables real-time assessment that would be impractical through periodic clinical examinations alone.

## Technical Capabilities

The effectiveness of wearable devices depends on their embedded sensor systems, which may include accelerometers, heart rate monitors, temperature sensors, and GPS units. These sensors feed data to processing units that either store information locally or transmit it wirelessly to external platforms for analysis. The combination of multiple sensor types allows comprehensive monitoring of various [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] simultaneously, making [[concepts/internet-of-things|wearables]] versatile tools across different operational and clinical contexts.
