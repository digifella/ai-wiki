# Atmospheric Variables

Core physical quantities describing the state of the [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]], essential for meteorology, [[concepts/climate-science|climate science]], and [[concepts/weather-forecasting|weather forecasting]].

## Key Variables
- **Temperature**: Measure of thermal energy in the air.
- **[[concepts/pressure|Pressure]]**: Force exerted by the weight of the air column.
- **Humidity**: Amount of water vapor present (e.g., specific humidity, relative humidity).
- **Wind**: [[concepts/velocity|Velocity]] vector ([[concepts/speed|speed]] and direction) of air [[concepts/exercise|movement]].
- **Precipitation**: Water falling from the atmosphere (rain, snow, etc.).

## Computational Forecasting
Modern atmospheric modeling increasingly relies on [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] to solve complex [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid dynamics]] equations more efficiently than traditional Numerical Weather [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|Prediction]] (NWP) models.

- **[[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[concepts/weathernext-3|WeatherNext 3]]**: A recent advancement in AI-driven forecasting, offering high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] global predictions.
	- Focuses on accuracy, timeliness, and local granularity.
	- Overcomes limitations of traditional models through [[concepts/vanishing-gradient-problem|deep learning]] architectures.
	- See [[lab-notes/2026-09-04-DeepMinds-WeatherNext-3-AI-Driven-High-Resolution-Global|DeepMind's WeatherNext 3: AI-Driven High-Resolution Global Weather Forecasting]] for technical details.
