# Corporate AI environment

The ecosystem of AI systems, tools, and practices deployed within an [[concepts/organization|organization]], including sanctioned and unsanctioned implementations. A critical challenge is **[[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]**—unauthorized [[concepts/ai-projects|AI projects]] operating without IT or [[concepts/security|security]] oversight.

- **Shadow AI definition**: Unauthorized or undiscovered AI instances within corporate networks, as defined by IBM's [[entities/jeff-crume|Jeff Crume]] (2026), posing significant security and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] risks [[concepts/shadow-ai]]
- **Key risk**: Employees using [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] for tasks without [[concepts/governance|governance]], leading to [[concepts/data-leakage|data leakage]], regulatory non-compliance, and uncontrolled model [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] [[concepts/ai-governance|AI Governance]]
- **IBM's management strategy**: Proactive visibility into all AI instances required to mitigate risks, emphasizing organizational awareness beyond formal IT channels AI Governance
- **Source**: [IBM Shadow AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YBE6hq-OTFI) ([[entities/jeff-crume|Jeff Crume]], [[entities/ibm-technology|IBM Technology]], 2026)
