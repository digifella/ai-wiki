# Broad Institute

The Broad Institute is a [[concepts/health-research|biomedical research]] center that serves as a hub for genomics and [[concepts/gene-editing|gene editing]] research. It is particularly known for its work in [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR technology]] and related genetic [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] applications.

## Notable Research and Researchers

[[entities/mit|The institute]] has been the location of significant developments in gene editing technology. [[entities/feng-zhang|Dr. Feng Zhang]], a prominent [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] at the Broad Institute, has contributed to the evolution and advancement of CRISPR gene editing techniques. The institute continues to explore new approaches and discoveries in genetic modification beyond the original CRISPR framework.

## Public Engagement

The Broad Institute has participated in [[concepts/science|science]] education and outreach initiatives, including hosting visits from educational media organizations such as [[entities/scishow-channel|SciShow]]. These visits have provided opportunities to discuss the current state and future directions of gene editing research with broader audiences.
