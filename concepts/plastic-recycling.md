# Plastic Recycling

## Biological Remediation
Emerging strategies focus on utilizing Living Organisms and specific Enzymes to break down plastic polymers, offering a sustainable alternative to traditional mechanical or chemical recycling methods.

### Key Developments
- Recent investigations highlight the potential of biological agents to address the global [[concepts/plastic-pollution|plastic pollution]] [[concepts/crisis|crisis]], noting that since the 1950s, humanity has produced approximately 8.3 billion tons of plastic.
- Researchers are developing enzymatic approaches to remediate waste, targeting the degradation of persistent polymers that resist conventional disposal.
