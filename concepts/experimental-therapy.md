# Experimental Therapy

Experimental therapy refers to medical treatments that are currently under investigation or in clinical trials, not yet fully approved for standard clinical use. These approaches often target specific molecular pathways or utilize novel biological agents to treat diseases where conventional therapies have failed or are unavailable.

## Key Modalities
- **[[concepts/oncolytic-virotherapy|Oncolytic Virotherapy]]**: Uses genetically modified viruses to selectively infect and kill cancer cells while sparing normal tissue.
- **Immunotherapy**: Harnesses the patient's immune system to fight cancer (e.g., checkpoint inhibitors, CAR-T cells).
- **Gene Therapy**: Introduces, removes, or changes genetic material within a person's cells to treat disease.
- **Targeted Therapy**: Drugs that target specific molecules involved in the growth and progression of cancer.
