# Automated email workflows

Systems and processes designed to automate the handling, processing, and responding to electronic correspondence.

## AI-Driven Implementation
- **[[concepts/no-code-ai-agents|No-Code AI Agents]]**: Utilizing [[entities/notebooklm]] and [[entities/gemini]] to create safe, accessible alternatives to complex [[concepts/coding|coding]] frameworks like [[entities/openclaw]].
- **Rule-Based [[concepts/response-generation|Response Generation]]**: Configuring [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] to ingest specific Business Rules to draft contextually accurate Email [[concepts/responses|Responses]].
- **Low-[[concepts/code|Code]] Architectures**: Leveraging grounded [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to reduce the need for heavy [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]] in Business Process [[concepts/automation|Automation]].
