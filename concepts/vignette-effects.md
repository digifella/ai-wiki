# Vignette effects

A visual technique where the periphery of an image is progressively darkened or lightened to draw the viewer's [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] toward the central subject and enhance [[concepts/writing|Composition]].

### Core Characteristics
- Primarily utilized during Post-processing to manipulate focal points.
- Often paired with [[concepts/film-grain|Grain]] effects to simulate Analog photography aesthetics and filmic [[concepts/texture|texture]].

### Implementation in lightroom
- Features three distinct methods for applying vignettes, allowing for varying levels of [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and control over edge intensity.
