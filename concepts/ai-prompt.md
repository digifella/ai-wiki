# AI Prompt

An **AI Prompt** is a [[concepts/chat-prompt|structured input]] provided to an [[concepts/large-language-model]] (LLM) or [[concepts/generative-ai]] system to elicit specific outputs, behaviors, or [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] patterns. It serves as the primary interface for Human-AI Interaction, defining context, constraints, roles, and desired formats.

## Core Components

- **Context**: Background information necessary for the model to understand the task.
- **Instruction**: Explicit [[concepts/commands|commands]] detailing what action the model should perform.
- **Constraints**: Limitations on format, [[concepts/tone|tone]], length, or scope.
- **Examples**: Few-shot or zero-shot demonstrations of desired [[concepts/training-data|input-output pairs]].

## Strategic Evolution

Modern [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] has evolved from simple query-response patterns to complex system-level architectures. Recent developments highlight the shift towards [[concepts/organizational-intelligence|organizational intelligence]] and critical safety implications in high-stakes environments.

### Case Study: Aviation and Cognitive Responsibility
The integration of AI in critical infrastructure, such as aviation, necessitates a re-evaluation of Human-AI Interaction beyond mere prompting. The [[lab-notes/2026-07-01-Air-India-Crash-Human-Responsibility-and-Evolving-AI-Cog|Air India Crash: Human Responsibility and Evolving AI Cognition in Aviation]] incident underscores several key dimensions:

- **[[concepts/accountability|Responsibility]] Allocation**: The boundary between human oversight and AI autonomy is not static; it requires dynamic definition within the interaction protocol.
- **Cognitive Symbiosis**: Effective interaction depends on aligning [[concepts/ai-cognition|AI cognition]] with human [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/loops|loops]], preventing [[concepts/algorithmic-decision-making|automation bias]] or over-reliance.
- **[[concepts/near-extinction|Systemic Failure]] Modes**: Errors often arise not from the AI's output quality alone, but from the breakdown in the human-AI communication [[concepts/loop|loop]] regarding intent and constraint interpretation.
