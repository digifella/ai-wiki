# LLM training

[[concepts/training|Training]] [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] requires substantial [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] and [[concepts/cost|cost]], with recent estimates highlighting extreme expenses. Key developments include:

- **Cost**: [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] reported [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] Ultra (2023) training cost ~$191M and [[entities/gpt-4|GPT-4]] (2023) ~$78M (Altman claimed higher); 2025 estimates continue to reflect prohibitive costs.
- **4-bit training**: Shift towards [[concepts/floating-point-numbers|4-bit floating-point]] (FP4) training to reduce [[concepts/memory|memory]] and [[concepts/compute|compute]] demands, as detailed in How does [[concepts/4bit-quantisation|4bit quantisation]] work.
