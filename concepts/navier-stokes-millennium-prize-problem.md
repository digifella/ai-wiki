# Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize Problem

The [[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes equations]] describe the motion of viscous fluid substances. The [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems|Millennium Prize Problems]] are seven significant unsolved problems in mathematics, each carrying a $1 million prize. The Navier-Stokes [[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|existence and smoothness]] problem asks whether solutions to these equations always exist and are smooth (non-singular) in three dimensions.
