# Framework Era

The "Framework Era" refers to a period in the development and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] where various frameworks provided a structured approach for integrating [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI capabilities]] into systems. This era saw significant advancements through tools like [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval Augmented Generation]] (RAG), which combined retrieval mechanisms with generative [[concepts/models|models]], enhancing the ability of AI systems to produce contextually relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]].

- [[concepts/information-provision|Retrieval Augmented Generation]]
- [[entities/llamaindex]]

### Key Concepts
- **Frameworks**: Structured toolkits for developing and deploying [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] applications.
- **RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**: A method that combines retrieval from a corpus of documents with generation to improve the relevance and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of responses.

### Evolution and Shifts

The framework era has seen shifts towards more [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] and methodologies. Recent developments point towards an increasing focus on **[[concepts/numerical-data-extraction|Agentic Document Processing]]**, which aims to enhance how AI systems interact with and process [[concepts/structured-output|structured data]], moving beyond mere retrieval mechanisms.
