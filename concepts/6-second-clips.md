# 6 Second Clips

6 Second Clips is a short-form video generation feature developed by Google that enables users to create brief video content, typically around six seconds in length, using AI-assisted tools. The feature leverages generative AI to automate aspects of video production, allowing users to generate video assets without requiring extensive video editing expertise. This tool reflects a broader trend in creative technology to lower the barrier to entry for digital content creation by integrating powerful generative models directly into accessible platforms.

The functionality is part of Google's recent updates to its creative ecosystem, which includes enhancements to Gemini, NotebookLM, and Google Vids. By utilizing these underlying technologies, the feature allows for the rapid synthesis of visual media from text prompts or existing assets. This integration supports creators in producing quick, high-quality clips suitable for social media and other short-form video contexts, streamlining the workflow from concept to final output.
