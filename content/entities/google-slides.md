# Google Slides

Google Slides is a cloud-based presentation [[concepts/software|software]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] as part of [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]]. It allows users to create, edit, and collaborate on [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] online, with documents stored in Google Drive and accessible from any device with internet connectivity. The platform supports real-time collaboration, enabling multiple users to work on the same presentation simultaneously with built-in commenting and sharing features.

## Gemini Integration

As of 2026, Google Slides has integrated Gemini, Google's [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]], to enhance presentation creation and editing capabilities. The integration enables automated [[concepts/slide-deck-generation|slide deck generation]], design [[concepts/customization|customization]], and content suggestions. This positions Slides alongside other Workspace applications including Docs and Sheets, which have received similar AI-powered updates to streamline document and spreadsheet creation.

Google Slides competes in the presentation software market alongside traditional tools like Microsoft PowerPoint and other cloud-based alternatives. The addition of generative [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] represents an effort to differentiate the platform by reducing manual design and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] work.
