# Google AI

[[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s research and development division for [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], focusing on advanced models, tools, and [[concepts/developer|developer]] resources.

## Key Features

- **[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] API**: Access to [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] model family (including 2.5 series) for [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and creative tasks.
- **[[concepts/lyria-realtime|Lyria RealTime]] Music Generation**: Generate music via [[concepts/gemini-api|Gemini API]] using [[entities/lyria-realtime]] model (see [Music generation documentation](https://ai.google.dev/gemini-api/docs/music-generation)).
- **Model Updates**: Recent enhancements to 2.5 family of [[concepts/thinking-models|thinking models]] for improved [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and creativity.
