# Tedxlse

TEDxLSE is a local TEDx event hosted [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the London School of Economics and Political [[concepts/science|Science]]. As part of the broader [[entities/ted|TED]] conference ecosystem, TEDxLSE features talks by speakers sharing [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] worth spreading on topics relevant to the LSE community and beyond.

## Kate Laffan's Talk on Wellbeing

In April 2026, philosopher [[entities/kate-laffan|Kate Laffan]] delivered a TEDxLSE talk titled "What is [[concepts/health|Wellbeing]]?" The presentation examined various [[concepts/accounts-of-how-you-feel|philosophical accounts of wellbeing]] while addressing their practical [[concepts/software|applications]] and limitations. Laffan explored how different philosophical frameworks for understanding wellbeing—such as hedonistic, preference satisfaction, and objective list theories—face challenges when applied to real-world contexts. Her talk bridged abstract philosophical inquiry with concrete questions about how societies measure and pursue human flourishing.
