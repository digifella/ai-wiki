## Whisper Transcription

A key component of OpenAI's ecosystem is **[[concepts/whisper-ai|Whisper]] Transcription**, which utilizes the [[entities/whisper-ai]] model for converting [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] to text. Whisper is a [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] system trained on 680,000 hours of multilingual audio data collected from the web. The model is designed to handle diverse real-[[entities/earth|world]] audio conditions, including varying quality levels, accents, and background noise.
