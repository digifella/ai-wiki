# Understanding The Physical World

[[concepts/cause-and-effect|VL-JEPA]] ([[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]-Language Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]) is [[entities/meta|Meta]]'s architectural approach to machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] that diverges from the dominant [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] paradigm. Rather than [[concepts/training|training]] [[concepts/models|models]] to generate [[concepts/text|text]] or [[concepts/images|images]] token-by-token, [[concepts/vl-jepa|VL-JEPA]] operates on the principle of predictive learning in joint embedding spaces. The architecture learns representations of the physical world by predicting abstract features of unseen parts of images or unobserved future states, without requiring explicit generation of pixel-level or token-level outputs.

## Distinction from Generative Models

[[concepts/generative-ai-models|Generative AI models]], including [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs), typically operate through next-token prediction or pixel generation, optimizing for [[concepts/output|output]] fidelity rather than world understanding. VL-JEPA instead focuses on learning compressed, semantic representations that capture the underlying [[concepts/structure|structure]] of visual and conceptual information. This approach is [[entities/theoretically-media|theoretically]] motivated by the hypothesis that genuine physical understanding emerges from predicting abstract features rather than surface-level details, potentially requiring less computational overhead than generative approaches while developing more generalizable models of how the world works.

## Implications for Reasoning

The architectural differences between VL-JEPA and LLM-based [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] systems reflect different assumptions about how machines should process information. While [[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM reasoning]] models chain together language [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] to simulate step-by-step [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], VL-JEPA's embedding-space predictions may offer an alternative framework for understanding causality and physical dynamics. Neither approach has definitively proven superior for all tasks, and the relative strengths of predictive embedding architectures versus autoregressive language models remain an active area of investigation in [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] research.
