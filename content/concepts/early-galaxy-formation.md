# Early Galaxy Formation

Early [[concepts/galaxy-formation|galaxy formation]] refers to the processes by which galaxies assembled during the universe's first billion years, a period critical to understanding cosmic [[concepts/structure|structure]]. Theoretical [[concepts/models|models]] developed over recent decades predicted that galaxies would grow gradually through mergers and accretion, with the most [[concepts/massive-galaxies|massive galaxies]] forming relatively late in cosmic history. These models were based on our understanding of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]], [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]], and the [[concepts/physics|physics]] of star formation operating within the expanding universe.

## JWST Observations and the Challenge

Recent observations from the [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] have revealed galaxies in the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] that appear significantly more massive and more structurally mature than existing models predict they should be [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] their observed ages. Spectroscopic analysis suggests these galaxies formed [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] and assembled their bulk material earlier than [[concepts/theory|theory]] suggests was possible. The discrepancy is not marginal—some observed galaxies appear to be several billion solar masses at epochs when models expected objects perhaps one-tenth that mass.

## Implications for Galaxy Formation Theory

These observations have prompted reassessment of fundamental assumptions about early galaxy formation. Possible [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] under investigation include revision of star formation efficiency in the early universe, modification of assumptions about dark matter [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] and behavior, or changes to our understanding of how rapidly primordial gas could cool and condense into stars. The tension between observation and theory remains unresolved, with implications extending to models of chemical enrichment, [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] mechanisms from [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]] and [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|active galactic nuclei]], and the overall timeline of cosmic structure assembly.
