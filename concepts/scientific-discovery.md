# Scientific Discovery

The systematic process of expanding human knowledge through observation, experimentation, and the validation of new phenomena.

## Core Framework
- Hypothesis generation and testing
- [[concepts/empirical-evidence|Empirical evidence]] acquisition
- [[concepts/document-review|Peer review]] and reproducibility
- Falsifiability

### Feynman's Three-Step Method
[[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] introduced a three-step process for [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific inquiry]]:
- **Guess:** Formulate a hypothesis or new law.
- **[[concepts/compute|Compute]]:** Derive the logical consequences of the guess.
- **[[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|Compare]]:** Compare consequences with nature/experiment to check for agreement.

## Recent Advances & Applications

### AI-Augmented Discovery
- [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|Autonomous AI agents]], such as [[entities/deepmind-aletheia|DeepMind Aletheia]], are increasingly integrated into the discovery [[concepts/loop|loop]], accelerating hypothesis generation and validation.
- Recent breakthroughs include the confirmation of [[concepts/liquid-water|liquid water]]'s dual identity, highlighting the role of AI in resolving complex physical anomalies and [[concepts/phase-transitions|phase transitions]].

### Decoding Biological Complexity
- [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Decoding-Biologys-Language-AI-CRISPR-and-Single-Cell-Seq|Decoding Biology's Language: AI, CRISPR, and Single-Cell Sequencing for Disease]]
- [[entities/silvana-konermann|Silvana Konermann]] advocates for integrating AI with [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR]] and [[concepts/single-cell-sequencing|single-cell sequencing]] to decode the wild complexity of the human cell.
- This approach aims to tackle persistent [[concepts/health|health]] challenges by translating biological data into [[concepts/actionable-insights|actionable insights]], effectively treating biology as a language to be deciphered.
