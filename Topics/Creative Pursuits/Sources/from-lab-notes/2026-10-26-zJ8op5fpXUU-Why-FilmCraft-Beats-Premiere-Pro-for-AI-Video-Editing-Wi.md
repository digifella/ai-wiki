The video explores the integration of AI with FilmCraft, a video editing software, using the Model Context Protocol (MCP). The presenter tests whether an AI agent, specifically Codex, can autonomously edit a complete video using raw assets like a voiceover, script, music, and travel footage. 

Codex successfully installs and configures the FilmCraft MCP, gaining access to various editing tools. #### Description
Could FilmCraft be a better choice than Adobe Premiere Pro for AI-powered video editing?

In this episode of Applorable, I put FilmCraft, the open-source video editor from ArtCraft, to the test using OpenAI Codex and the Model Context Protocol (MCP).

Rather than manually editing footage or using AI computer control to navigate the interface, MCP allows Codex to communicate directly with FilmCraft and perform editing tasks.

I give Codex a voiceover, a folder containing 58 travel stock footage clips, a music track and a simple prompt to create a professional travel agency advertisement.

The results genuinely surprised me!

In this video:
- What FilmCraft is and how it compares with Adobe Premiere Pro
- How to download and install FilmCraft
- What MCP (Model Context Protocol) does
- Connecting OpenAI Codex to FilmCraft through MCP
- Using AI to select and edit stock footage automatically
- Adding transitions, subtitles, music and motion graphics
- Creating a complete travel advertisement with a single AI prompt
- Reviewing the finished commercial and editable FilmCraft timeline
- Why MCP-powered video editing could transform content creation

Could AI-connected video editing software change the way we create YouTube videos, advertisements and professional content?
