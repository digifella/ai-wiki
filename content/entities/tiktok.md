# Tiktok

TikTok is a short-form video social media platform that enables users to create, share, and discover video content typically ranging from 15 seconds to several minutes in length. The platform uses algorithmic recommendation systems to surface content to users based on engagement patterns and viewing history, making it one of the most widely used social media [[concepts/software|applications]] globally.

## Content and Features

The platform supports a wide variety of content formats, including music clips, dance videos, comedy sketches, educational material, and trending challenges. TikTok provides built-in editing tools, filters, effects, and access to a library of licensed music and sounds that creators can incorporate into their videos. The platform also increasingly incorporates AI-[[concepts/automated-content-generation|automated content generation]] tools, allowing creators to produce videos with reduced manual effort.

## User Base and Impact

TikTok has accumulated a substantial user base across multiple demographic groups and geographic regions. The platform's [[concepts/power|influence]] extends beyond entertainment to include marketing, education, and news [[concepts/distribution|distribution]]. Its algorithmic approach to content discovery has made it a significant driver of viral trends and cultural phenomena.
