# Architectural Improvements

Architectural improvements in artificial intelligence refer to enhancements made to the underlying design and structure of machine learning models, particularly within video generation systems. These refinements focus on how models process information, organize computational layers, and manage sequential data generation. Such structural changes directly influence the quality, efficiency, and practical capabilities of model outputs by optimizing the flow of data through the network.

In the context of the Wan 2.2 models, these improvements manifest in the integration of advanced attention mechanisms and optimized transformer blocks. These structural adjustments allow for more effective handling of temporal consistency in video frames and spatial coherence in image generation. The modifications reduce computational overhead while maintaining high-fidelity output, enabling smoother transitions and more accurate adherence to textual prompts.

For users installing Wan 2.2 locally via ComfyUI, understanding these architectural shifts is essential for proper workflow configuration. The updated model architecture requires specific node setups to leverage its enhanced processing capabilities effectively. Proper alignment between the ComfyUI environment and the model's structural requirements ensures stable inference and maximizes the benefits of the underlying design enhancements.
