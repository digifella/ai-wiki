# Simon Sinek

Simon Sinek is a popular motivational [[entities/speaker|speaker]] and [[entities/tasia-custode|author]] known for his insights on [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], specifically the concept of starting with "why." He explores why some individuals and organizations achieve enduring [[concepts/success|success]] while others struggle to inspire action. His work encourages leaders to focus on their purpose and [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] as the driving force behind effective communication and inspiration.
