# Well Being

Well-being is defined as a state of physical, mental, and emotional health characterized by life satisfaction, psychological functioning, and overall quality of life. It encompasses both subjective experiences of happiness and contentment, as well as objective measures of health and social functioning. The concept is increasingly recognized as a multidimensional construct that extends beyond the mere absence of illness to include positive psychological and social dimensions.

The construct typically includes several interconnected components, primarily physical health, mental stability, and emotional regulation. These elements work together to determine an individual's overall sense of well-being, which is influenced by both internal psychological states and external social environments.

Research in this domain, such as the work presented by Laurie Santos, focuses on behavioral habits and healthy practices that contribute to well-being, particularly in adolescents. This research highlights how specific daily behaviors and cognitive patterns can significantly impact the long-term mental and emotional health of younger populations, offering evidence-based strategies for improvement.
