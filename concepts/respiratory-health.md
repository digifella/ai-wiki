# Respiratory Health

Respiratory health encompasses the function and wellbeing of the lungs, airways, and oxygen exchange systems in the body. The respiratory system continuously supplies oxygen to the bloodstream while removing carbon dioxide waste, a process essential for cellular function and physical performance. When functioning properly, these systems adapt efficiently to varying activity levels and environmental conditions, maintaining adequate oxygen saturation during rest and exertion alike.

## Key Components

The system relies on the coordinated action of the upper and lower respiratory tracts. The upper tract, including the nose and mouth, filters, warms, and humidifies incoming air. The lower tract consists of the trachea, bronchi, and alveoli, where gas exchange occurs. The diaphragm and intercostal muscles facilitate breathing by changing the volume of the thoracic cavity, creating pressure gradients that draw air in and push it out.

## Factors Influencing Health

Respiratory health is influenced by genetic predisposition, environmental exposures, and lifestyle choices. Chronic conditions such as asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and pneumonia can impair lung function and reduce oxygen uptake. Exposure to pollutants, tobacco smoke, and allergens can cause inflammation and long-term damage to airway tissues. Maintaining respiratory health involves avoiding harmful substances, managing underlying conditions, and engaging in regular physical activity to strengthen respiratory muscles.
