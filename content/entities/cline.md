The tool positions itself within the growing market of [[concepts/development-speed|AI-assisted development]] environments, competing alongside other AI code editors and completion tools. It incorporates features intended to streamline [[concepts/coding|software development]] workflows and improve [[concepts/developer-productivity|developer productivity]].
