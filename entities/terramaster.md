# Terramaster

TerraMaster is a manufacturer of network-attached storage (NAS) devices designed for small office and home environments. The company produces a range of NAS solutions that function as centralized storage systems, allowing multiple users and devices to access files simultaneously over a network connection. TerraMaster devices are typically configured with multiple drive bays and run dedicated NAS operating systems to manage file sharing, backup, and data security.

## F4-425 Plus

The TerraMaster F4-425 Plus is a four-bay NAS device intended for small business and prosumer use. It provides local and remote file access, backup capabilities, and media serving functionality. The device supports various RAID configurations to protect against drive failure and offers compatibility with common storage protocols used in networked environments.
