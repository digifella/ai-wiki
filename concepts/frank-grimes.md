# Frank Grimes

**Frank Grimes** is a fictional character from *[[concepts/the-simpsons|The Simpsons]]*, appearing primarily in the Season 9 episode [[entities/homers-enemy|Homer's Enemy]]. He serves as a [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] foil to [[concepts/homer-simpson]], representing the archetype of the hardworking, rule-abiding citizen who is systematically crushed by the incompetence and luck of others.

## Character Profile
- **Role:** Employee at Simpsons Power Plant (Sector 7G).
- **Personality:** Meticulous, ambitious, cynical, and deeply resentful of perceived unfairness.
- **Narrative Function:** Demonstrates the show's critique of meritocracy and the absurdity of bureaucratic systems.
