# Table-to-text extraction

The process of converting [[concepts/structured-output|structured data]] from tables into text formats to facilitate [[concepts/rag]] (Retrieable-Augmented Generation) and [[concepts/nlp]] workflows.

## Models & Trends
- **[[entities/nanonets-ocr-small|Nanonets OCR Small]]**: A powerful, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] OCR model featuring 3B [[concepts/parameters|parameters]].
- **Efficiency Trend**: A shift toward smaller, highly efficient models (e.g., 3B parameter [[concepts/range|range]]) optimized for specific extraction tasks, moving away from larger architectures like [[entities/llama|Llama]] OCR and [[entities/mistral|Mistral]] OCR.
- **Application**: Targeted at improving the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of [[concepts/rag]] pipelines by converting complex table structures into machine-readable text.
