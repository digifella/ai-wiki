# Prof Martin Held

Prof Martin Held is an academic researcher affiliated with both the University of Copenhagen and Deakin University. His research centers on digital health interventions, with particular focus on mental health and conflict resolution applications.

## SES Family Modules

Held is recognized for his work developing the SES Family modules, a collection of software applications designed to support mental health and family wellbeing. This work represents a significant contribution to the intersection of digital technology and psychological intervention, particularly in addressing conflict within family systems.
