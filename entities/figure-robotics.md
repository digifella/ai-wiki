# Figure Robotics

**Figure [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]]** is a technology company specializing in the development of general-purpose [[concepts/humanoid-robots|humanoid robots]]. The organization has gained significant public [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] for its Figure Robotics [[entities/f02|F.02]] Humanoid Robot Terminator-Style [[concepts/decommissioning|Decommissioning]] Event, a highly theatrical decommissioning of its F.02 units.

## Key Events

### 2026-10-03: F.02 Decommissioning
On October 3, 2026, Figure Robotics conducted a public decommissioning of its F.02 humanoid robots. The event was notable for its cinematic presentation, deliberately mimicking the iconic molten [[concepts/metal|metal]] destruction scene from *Terminator 2: Judgment Day*.
