# Tint Adjustment

Tint adjustment is a [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] feature available in [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]] that allows users to modify the [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and [[concepts/hue|hue]] balance of [[concepts/images|images]]. This tool is part of [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s expanded color correction [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], enabling photographers and designers to shift colors toward warmer or cooler tones systematically across an image or selected areas.

## Integration with Color and Vibrance Tool

In [[entities/photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]], tint adjustment operates as a component of the redesigned [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]]. [[entities/blake-rudis|Blake Rudis]]'s [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] on this feature demonstrates how tint [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] work alongside vibrance controls to achieve cohesive color grading results. The feature supports [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]], allowing users to modify tint values without permanently altering the underlying image data.

## Relationship to Other Color Tools

Tint adjustment complements existing color correction workflows in Adobe's ecosystem, including similar capabilities found in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]'s [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration Panel]] and Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]]. These tools work together to provide comprehensive color management from capture through final [[concepts/output|output]], supporting [[concepts/foundational-color|foundational color]] adjustments, skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] corrections, and stylistic color grading approaches.
