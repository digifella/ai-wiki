# Shadows Adjustment

Shadows Adjustment is a post-processing technique in Lightroom Classic that targets the tonal values in the darker areas of an image during editing. Rather than applying adjustments globally across an entire photograph, this approach uses Lightroom's masking features to selectively modify only the shadow regions. This selective adjustment allows photographers to enhance detail and tonality in darker areas while preserving the tonal characteristics of midtones and highlights.

## Application in Landscape Photography

In landscape photography, shadows adjustment is particularly useful for recovering detail in deep shadows caused by high-contrast lighting conditions, such as midday sun or dense forest canopies. By using tools like the Masking panel to create a Shadow Mask, editors can increase exposure, clarity, and texture specifically within these dark zones without overexposing the brighter parts of the scene. This method helps balance the dynamic range of the image, ensuring that foreground elements remain visible and detailed.

The technique relies on the precision of Lightroom’s algorithmic masking, which distinguishes between shadow, midtone, and highlight values based on the image histogram. Adjustments made to the shadow mask can include lifting black points, reducing contrast, or adding warmth to cool shadow areas. This localized control prevents the "flat" look often associated with global shadow recovery, maintaining the natural depth and three-dimensionality of the landscape while revealing hidden details in the darker portions of the composition.
