# Publication Quality Diagrams

Publication Quality Diagrams refers to automated tools and frameworks designed to generate technical illustrations suitable for academic papers. These systems address the practical challenge of producing figures that meet publication standards—including clarity, logical consistency, and accurate representation of complex concepts—while reducing the manual effort required from researchers.

A prominent example of this domain is PaperBanana, a framework developed by researchers at Google and Peking University. It focuses on automated academic illustration specifically tailored for AI scientists, aiming to streamline the creation of high-quality visual content for scientific publications.
