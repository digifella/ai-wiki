# mHealth

**Mobile [[concepts/health|health]]** ([[concepts/mobile-health|mHealth]]) refers to the practice of medicine and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] supported by [[concepts/portable-devices|mobile devices]], including mobile phones, patient monitoring devices, pagers, and personal digital assistants (PDAs). It encompasses [[concepts/digital-health]], [[concepts/telehealth|telemedicine]], and [[concepts/apps|mobile applications]] used for health delivery, education, and data collection.

## Key Concepts & Literature

- digital health (ehealth)
- evidence-based & research methodology
- [[concepts/pediatric-care|pediatrics]] - children
- [[concepts/health-system|health system]] - organization and administration
- mobile application (app)
