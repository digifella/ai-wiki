- **Latency:** Time taken to generate a response.
- **Throughput:** [[concepts/tokens|Tokens]] processed per second.
- **Accuracy:** [[concepts/accuracy|Correctness]] of outputs against ground truth.
- **Cost per Token:** Financial efficiency of [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]].
