# Range Sliders

Range sliders are interface controls within Adobe Camera Raw that allow users to define specific tonal or color boundaries for adjustments made through the Point Color feature. By establishing upper and lower limits, these controls isolate particular segments of an image's tonal or chromatic spectrum. This mechanism ensures that edits are applied selectively to the targeted areas rather than affecting the entire image uniformly.

In Point Color editing workflows, range sliders function in conjunction with color selection tools to refine the scope of adjustments. Users can manipulate these boundaries to include or exclude specific hues and brightness levels, enabling precise control over how color corrections interact with different parts of the photograph. This granular approach supports complex color grading tasks by preventing unwanted shifts in areas that should remain unchanged.
