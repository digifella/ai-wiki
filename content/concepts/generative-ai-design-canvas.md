# Generative AI Design Canvas

A generative [[concepts/design-iteration|AI design canvas]] represents a new class of design tools that integrate [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] directly into the creative [[concepts/workflow|workflow]], rather than treating it as a separate feature. [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|Google Stitch]] exemplifies this approach as an [[concepts/generative-uiux|AI-native design]] platform, where generative [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] are fundamental to how the tool operates rather than supplementary functions. This [[concepts/integration|integration]] changes how designers approach layout, composition, and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].

## Core Workflow Changes

Traditional design tools like [[entities/figma|Figma]] operate with AI as an additive layer. [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] design canvases, by [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], embed AI throughout the entire creative process—from initial concept generation to refinement and [[concepts/digital-asset-creation|asset creation]]. Designers using these platforms can leverage generative capabilities for [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]], content suggestions, and design variations without context-switching between [[concepts/software|applications]]. The [[entities/canvas|canvas]] itself becomes a collaborative space between human intention and algorithmic possibility.

## Accessibility and Adoption

The availability of free or accessible generative [[concepts/front-end-components|AI design tools]] has lowered barriers to entry for design work. [[concepts/automated-interface-design|Google Stitch]]'s positioning as a free alternative to commercial platforms democratizes access to [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] capabilities, making sophisticated [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]] available to users who might otherwise rely on expensive proprietary software. This broader [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] shapes how design professionals and amateurs alike approach creative [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].
