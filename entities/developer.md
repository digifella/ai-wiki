# Developer

A developer is a professional who uses Claude AI and structured documentation to streamline product development. The core practice involves creating Markdown-based Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) that translate product specifications into concrete, actionable tasks. This approach systematizes the conversion of abstract requirements into executable work items, reducing friction in the development process.

## Workflow and Tools

The developer's workflow centers on generating task managers and specialized sub-agents from well-structured PRDs. Claude AI processes these documents to create organized task hierarchies and deploy focused agents for particular aspects of a product build. This automation reduces manual coordination overhead and allows developers to maintain consistency across complex projects.

## Benefits and Application

By standardizing documentation format and leveraging AI-assisted task generation, developers can allocate more time to technical work and problem-solving rather than administrative coordination. The methodology scales effectively across projects of varying complexity, as the Markdown-based PRD format remains adaptable to different product domains and team structures.
