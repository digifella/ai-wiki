# Conversation Flow

Conversation [[concepts/flow|Flow]] is a structured prompt template designed to enhance interactions between users and [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] within business advisory contexts. It functions as a metacognitive framework that guides AI assistants to clarify and refine user inputs before generating substantive responses. By introducing a mandatory clarification step, the template addresses the common limitation of ambiguous or incomplete requests, which often result in outputs misaligned with user intentions or actual information needs.

The process operates by directing the AI to analyze the initial query for missing context, conflicting constraints, or vague objectives. Instead of immediately producing a [[concepts/solution|solution]], the system identifies potential response constraints and asks targeted follow-up questions. This [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative refinement]] ensures that the subsequent advice is grounded in accurate data and specific business requirements, thereby reducing the risk of generic or irrelevant [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]].

Implementing this flow optimizes the efficiency of AI-assisted work by shifting the burden of [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] from the user to the interaction protocol. It standardizes the intake [[concepts/phase|phase]] of business advisory tasks, ensuring that critical details regarding scope, timeline, and resources are established early. This approach improves the [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of AI outputs in professional settings where precision and [[concepts/contextual-accuracy|contextual accuracy]] are paramount.
