# Source Grounded Analysis

Source Grounded Analysis is a research methodology that requires conclusions and claims to be directly supported by documented sources. Rather than making assertions based on general knowledge or [[concepts/inference|inference]], this approach maintains explicit connections between findings and their [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]] throughout the analytical process. This practice increases [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], enables [[concepts/verification|verification]], and reduces unsupported speculation in research and [[concepts/writing|writing]].

## Core Principles

The methodology prioritizes traceability, ensuring that each claim can be traced back to its originating document or reference. This differs from traditional analysis that may synthesize information into general statements without preserving source attribution. By maintaining these connections, researchers can demonstrate the evidentiary basis for their conclusions and allow others to evaluate the quality and relevance of supporting materials.

## Tools and Implementation

Digital tools facilitate source grounded analysis by organizing [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]] alongside analytical [[concepts/notes|notes]] and findings. Platforms like [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] enable researchers to upload documents, synthesize information, and generate analyses while maintaining links to the underlying sources. Such tools can highlight which sources support specific conclusions and identify gaps where claims lack documented support, making the analytical process more rigorous and transparent.

## Applications in AI and Research

Source grounded analysis is particularly relevant for [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] conducting research, where maintaining provenance of information is critical for [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and trustworthiness. The methodology supports more accurate [[concepts/automated-synthesis|knowledge synthesis]], reduces [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] or unsupported claims, and provides clear audit trails for complex analytical work across domains including [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] research, policy analysis, and [[concepts/technical-documentation|technical documentation]].
