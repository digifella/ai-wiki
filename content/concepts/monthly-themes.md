# Monthly Themes

Monthly Themes is a structured approach to [[concepts/personal-development|personal development]] introduced by [[concepts/happiness|happiness]] researcher [[entities/gretchen-rubin|Gretchen Rubin]] as part of her Happiness Project framework. The method involves selecting a specific area of life or [[concepts/health|wellbeing]] to focus on each month, then identifying and practicing concrete [[concepts/daily-routines|daily habits]] that support improvement in that domain. Rather than attempting broad life changes all at once, this incremental approach distributes behavioral goals across the year, making them more manageable and sustainable.

## Implementation

The process typically involves choosing a theme relevant to one's values or problem areas—such as sleep, [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], or [[concepts/organization|organization]]—and then determining which daily habits would meaningfully contribute to that theme. Practitioners track their adherence to these [[concepts/habits|habits]] throughout the month, building [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] before moving to a new focus area. This cyclical [[concepts/structure|structure]] provides natural checkpoints for reflection and adjustment.

## Underlying Principles

The concept works on the assumption that sustained wellbeing improves through deliberate practice of specific behaviors rather than reliance on willpower alone. By concentrating [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] on one area monthly, individuals can develop deeper awareness of their patterns and make meaningful progress without the overwhelm of simultaneous major changes. The method has been adopted by many people seeking to systematically address multiple aspects of their health and happiness over time.
