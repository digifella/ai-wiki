# Oil pressure warning light

An indicator on a vehicle's dashboard signaling a drop in [[concepts/oil-pressure]] within an [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine]].

### Critical Functions
- **Engine Survival:** Acts as a primary alert for the [[concepts/health|health]] of the [[concepts/lubrication-system]]; [[concepts/oil-pressure|oil pressure]] is the fundamental mechanism preventing catastrophic mechanical failure.
- **Lubrication [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]:** Monitors the presence of oil required to maintain a protective barrier between moving parts.
- **[[concepts/risk-mitigation|Risk Mitigation]]:** Signals an immediate need for intervention to prevent metal-on-metal [[entities/contact|contact]] and subsequent [[concepts/engine-destruction|engine destruction]].
