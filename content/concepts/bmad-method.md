# BMAD method

- Addresses critical gap: AI enables rapid app/website creation but bypasses proper [[concepts/software-development-process|software development process]] (e.g., [[concepts/testing|testing]], version [[concepts/power|control]]), resulting in non-production code
- Evolved from simple [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|AI coding]] to sophisticated [[concepts/context-engineering|context engineering]] (tools: [[entities/cursor|Cursor]], [[entities/claude-code]])
- Streamlines entire development [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] through [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] [[concepts/integration|integration]]
