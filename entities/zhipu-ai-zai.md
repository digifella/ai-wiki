# Zhipu Ai Zai

Zhipu AI, also known as Z.Ai, is a Chinese artificial intelligence company specializing in the development of large language models and AI systems. The organization focuses on creating practical AI solutions tailored for business automation, software development, and complex reasoning tasks.

The company's primary product is the GLM-4.7 open-source large language model. This model is specifically designed to address use cases in coding, business automation, and complex reasoning. It represents Zhipu AI's effort to provide accessible AI tools for developers and organizations seeking alternatives to proprietary language models.
