# Relationship Extraction

The process of identifying and characterizing the semantic links and predicates between entities within [[concepts/unstructured-text|unstructured text]].

## Applications in Knowledge Engineering
- **[[concepts/knowledge-graph|Knowledge Graph]] Construction**: A core component in building [[concepts/real-time-knowledge-graph|real-time knowledge graphs]] from large document collections.
- **[[concepts/llm-rag|LLM RAG]] Pipelines**: Used to enhance [[concepts/llm-rag|LLM RAG]] by providing structured context via [[concepts/graph-database|graph databases]] like [[entities/neo4j|Neo4j]].
- **Automated Transformation**:
    - Leverages [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] for automated extraction.
    - Utilizes frameworks like Cocoindex to transform [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] and other [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] into structured [[concepts/relationships|relationships]].
