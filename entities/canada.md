# Canada

**Canada** is a country in North [[entities/america|America]] comprising ten provinces and three territories. It is the [[entities/earth|world]]'s second-largest country by total area, bordered by the [[entities/united-states|United States]] to the south and northwest, and by the Atlantic, Pacific, and Arctic Oceans.
