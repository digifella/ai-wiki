# 3D Spatial Intelligence

**3D Spatial Intelligence** refers to the computational capability to perceive, understand, and interact with three-dimensional environments. It involves processing spatial data to enable accurate mapping, object recognition, and navigation within physical or virtual spaces.

## Core Concepts

*   **Spatial Awareness:** The ability to determine the position and orientation of objects relative to the observer and each other.
*   **Environmental Mapping:** Constructing digital representations of physical spaces for analysis or simulation.
*   **Interactive Simulation:** Using simulated environments to test algorithms, train models, or visualize spatial data before real-world deployment.

## Key Tools & Simulators

### God's Eye View (GEV)

**[[entities/gods-eye-view-gev|God's Eye View (GEV)]]** is a prominent open-source 3D spatial intelligence simulator that has gained significant traction for its ability to provide comprehensive environmental context.
