# Multimodal AI

[[concepts/multimodal-ai]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] models capable of ingesting and/or generating data across various [[concepts/data-management|Data-Modalities]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/modality|Modality]]**: A specific data type or format used as input or output.
- **Common Modalities**: Includes Text, [[concepts/images|Images]], Audio, [[concepts/lidar|Lidar]], and Thermal-Imaging.
- **Processing Capabilities**: Models are distinguished by their ability to integrate and reason across these different data streams simultaneously.
