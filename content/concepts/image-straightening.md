# Image straightening

The process of adjusting the rotation of an image to ensure the Horizon line is level and vertical elements are perpendicular to the frame.

### Workflow Integration
- Integral component of Initial [[concepts/image-preparation|Image Preparation]] in [[concepts/landscape-photography|Landscape photography]] workflows.
- Typically performed alongside basic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Highlights, [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], [[concepts/whites|Whites]], and [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] within Adobe [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] or [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]].
- Should be completed prior to executing advanced [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] techniques (e.g., "Landscape" masking).
