## Social Welfare Initiatives
Social welfare initiatives are [[concepts/policies|policies]] or [[concepts/software|programs]] established by governments or non-governmental organizations to enhance the social [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] of individuals and communities. These can include education, [[concepts/health|healthcare]], housing assistance, food [[concepts/security|security measures]], poverty alleviation efforts, and support for marginalized groups.

- [[concepts/emperor-ashoka|Emperor Ashoka's reign]] in the [[concepts/mauryan-empire|Mauryan Empire]] marked a significant shift towards social welfare and humanitarian values after his brutal conquest of [[entities/kalinga|Kalinga]] (261 BCE).
- The establishment of Buddhist principles emphasizing non-violence, [[concepts/compassion|compassion]], and ethical [[concepts/governance|governance]].
- Promotion of [[concepts/religious-tolerance|religious tolerance]], [[concepts/public-health|public health]] measures, and support for education during Ashoka's rule.
