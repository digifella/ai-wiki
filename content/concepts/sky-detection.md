# Sky Detection

Sky detection is an [[concepts/automated-masking|automated masking]] technique used in [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] [[concepts/software|software]] such as [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] to isolate and selectively edit the sky portion of an image. Rather than manually painting masks or using selection tools, the feature uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automatically identify sky areas based on tonal and color characteristics, enabling photographers to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]—such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], saturation, or [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]]—exclusively to that region without affecting the landscape below.

## Workflow and Application

The sky detection masks in modern versions of [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] and [[entities/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw]] are accessed through single-click preset options in the [[concepts/mask-creation|mask creation]] interface. Once activated, the software analyzes the image and generates a mask boundary along the horizon line. This approach is particularly valuable in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], where skies often require different exposure or color treatment than foreground elements. Photographers can then fine-tune the mask edges if needed before applying [[concepts/local-adjustments|local adjustments]].

## Technical Basis

Sky detection relies on [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] trained to recognize sky patterns, including clear skies, clouds, and varying [[concepts/light|light]] conditions. The [[concepts/automation|automation]] reduces the time spent on manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] while maintaining reasonable [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] across diverse lighting and weather conditions. However, like all automated detection, it may require manual refinement in [[concepts/images|images]] with complex horizons or unusual sky conditions.
