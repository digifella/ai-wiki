# Steve Mould

Steve Mould is a British [[concepts/science|science]] communicator and YouTuber known for his engaging and insightful videos on scientific phenomena. His channel focuses on demonstrating principles of [[concepts/physics|physics]], chemistry, and engineering in an accessible manner.

## Notable Works
- **[[concepts/wirtz-pump|Wirtz Pump]] Mechanics: Overcoming [[concepts/airlock|Airlock]] and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|Hydrostatic Pressure]] Challenges** - In this video (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=wCxRHueX6jQ), Steve Mould explores the Wirtz pump, a spiral-shaped device designed to lift water. He [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] how the initial demonstration of the pump fails due to airlock and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] issues ([airlock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Airlock_(fluid)), [hydrostatic pressure](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hydrostatics)). Steve's explanation delves into [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]], explaining why a small Wirtz pump cannot lift water significantly higher than its own height of 20cm.
