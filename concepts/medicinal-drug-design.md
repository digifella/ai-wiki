# Medicinal Drug Design

**Medicinal drug design** is the interdisciplinary process of discovering and designing novel compounds with therapeutic properties. It integrates principles from chemistry, biology, pharmacology, and increasingly, [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to optimize efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetic profiles.

## Key Approaches

- **Structure-Based Drug Design (SBDD):** Utilizes 3D structural data of target proteins to design ligands that fit specific binding pockets.
- **Ligand-Based Drug Design (LBDD):** Relies on knowledge of existing active molecules to infer pharmacophores and design new analogs.
- **AI-Driven Discovery:** Leverages [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to predict molecular properties, generate novel structures, and accelerate the hit-to-lead process.

## AI in Drug Design

The integration of AI has transformed traditional workflows, particularly in predicting protein structures and molecular interactions.

- **AlphaFold Integration:** [[concepts/deep-learning-models|Deep learning models]] like AlphaFold have revolutionized the understanding of [[concepts/protein-folding|protein folding]], providing critical structural data for Structure-Based Drug Design.
- **[[entities/isomorphic-labs|Isomorphic Labs]]:** A spin-off from [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] focusing on applying AI to [[concepts/health-challenges|biological challenges]]. Key figures include [[entities/rebecca-paul|Rebecca Paul]] (Head of Medicinal Drug Design) and [[entities/max-jaderberg|Max Jaderberg]] (Chief AI Officer).
- **Impact:** AI accelerates the identification of potential drug candidates by simulating molecular interactions and predicting ADMET (Absorption, Distribution, [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|Metabolism]], Excretion, and Toxicity) properties earlier in the pipeline.
