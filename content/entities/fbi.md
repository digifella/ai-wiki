# FBI

The **Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI)** is the domestic intelligence and [[concepts/secure|security]] service of the [[entities/america|United States]] and its principal federal law enforcement agency. Its mission focuses on protecting the [[entities/united-states|United States]] from terrorist attacks, large-scale fraud, and significant threats to the nation's economic, industrial, and technological infrastructure.

## Core Mandates
- Counterterrorism
- Counterintelligence
- Cybercrime investigation and mitigation.
- Protection of National [[concepts/security-protocol|Security]] and critical infrastructure.

## Emerging Threat Landscape
- Monitoring advancements in AI-driven [[concepts/cybersecurity]] vulnerabilities, specifically regarding the potential for automated [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] discovery via [[concepts/agents|agents]] like [[concepts/mythos|Claude Mythos]] AI.
- Assessing the proliferation of high-risk [[entities/ai]] technologies that may facilitate large-scale cyberattacks or the discovery of zero-day exploits.
- Tracking industry-identified risks in automated [[concepts/security|security]] intelligence as highlighted by recent developments in [[entities/ibm-technology]] research.
