# The Bitters

Bitters are concentrated flavoring compounds utilized in cocktail preparation, produced by steeping herbs, spices, roots, and other botanical materials in alcohol. Applied sparingly, typically in quantities of a few dashes per drink, they serve to enhance flavor complexity, balance sweetness, and add aromatic depth to mixed beverages. The most widely recognized commercial varieties include Angostura, Peychaud's, and orange bitters, each possessing distinct flavor profiles suited to specific cocktail styles.

The concept of bitters traces its origins to the 19th century, where they were initially created and sold by pharmacists as medicinal tonics. This historical lineage is reflected in modern interpretations, such as the work of a former pharmacist in Heraklion who applies pharmaceutical precision to cocktail mixing. This approach highlights the intersection of medicinal heritage and contemporary mixology, emphasizing the botanical origins and precise application of these flavoring agents.
