### Summary
The speaker introduces a new, free app called "pal2tech Fujifilm Companion," designed for iOS and Android devices, with no ads or data collection. The app serves as a centralized hub containing over 600 of the speaker's Fujifilm videos, complete with listings, search capabilities, and filtering options by topics like Instax or ISO.

In addition to videos, the app features guides on camera settings, firmware updates, and official Fujifilm manuals. It includes a dedicated section for film simulations with details, recipes, and links to external resources like Fuji X Weekly. Unique features include an "Aim & Recommend" tool that suggests film simulations based on live scenes, field tools for astrophotography and depth of field, and a gear section for tracking owned cameras and accessing product pages.

The standout feature, "Camera X-Ray," allows users to connect their Fujifilm camera via USB to read settings, view shutter counts and firmware versions, and understand why certain menu items are greyed out by linking directly to official manuals and related videos. The speaker emphasizes that Fujifilm is not affiliated with the app and that all development, opinions, and potential bugs are the sole responsibility of the speaker.
