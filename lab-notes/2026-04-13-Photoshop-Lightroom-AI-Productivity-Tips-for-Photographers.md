## Photoshop & Lightroom AI Productivity Tips for Photographers
**Clip title:** [[concepts/photoshop-ai-tips|Photoshop AI Tips]] Every Photographer Should Know from Adobe’s [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]]
**Author / channel:** Professional Photographers of [[entities/america|America]] (PPA)
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TCV8KiZxWNM

### Summary
This episode of the Professional Photographer Podcast, hosted by Pat Miller and featuring Adobe's Principal Digital Imaging Evangelist [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]], delves into five power tips designed to enhance productivity and streamline workflows within [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]. Recorded live from [[entities/imaging-usa-2026|Imaging USA 2026]], the discussion aims to help photographers harness the full potential of these powerful tools, enabling them to work faster, produce better results, and reduce [[concepts/stress|stress]] in their post-production process.

The initial tips focus on optimizing the [[concepts/lightroom-workflow|Lightroom workflow]], beginning with fundamental [[concepts/organization|organization]]. Julianne emphasizes the importance of a consistent system for importing and backing up [[concepts/images|images]] to prevent data loss or disorganization. She introduces a new early-access feature in Lightroom: [[concepts/assisted-culling|Assisted Culling]]. This AI-powered tool automatically helps photographers identify and tag "selects" or "rejects" during import by analyzing factors like open eyes and focus, significantly speeding up the initial review process for large volumes of photos, particularly for event and portrait photographers.

Moving into the editing and organizational aspects of Lightroom, Julianne highlights the often-underutilized features of Collections and Smart Collections. Regular collections allow photographers to curate and reorder images for specific projects without altering their physical file locations. Smart Collections, on the other hand, automatically populate with images that meet predefined criteria (e.g., all five-star landscape photos from the last year), providing instant access to categorized content for marketing or social media. A crucial time-saving strategy shared is to complete all culling before beginning any extensive editing in the [[concepts/develop-module|Develop module]], preventing wasted effort on images that may ultimately be discarded. Additionally, customizing [Raw Defaults](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_Defaults) and utilizing AI-powered [[concepts/masking|masking]] presets can automate initial [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] and targeted local edits, further enhancing efficiency.

The conversation then transitions to Photoshop, focusing on the transformative capabilities of [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]]. Tools like Generative Fill and Harmonize are discussed for their ability to non-destructively generate new content, seamlessly extend images, or adjust lighting and [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] to integrate elements realistically. Julianne explains how these features allow artists to rapidly prototype [[concepts/creative-ideas|creative ideas]], add new objects (like a scarf on a giraffe), or effortlessly remove unwanted elements such as distracting people or wires from photographs. This not only saves immense time but also fosters greater creative freedom by reducing the logistical constraints of capturing perfect shots in-camera.

In conclusion, while embracing the rapid advancements in [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]], Julianne underscores that these technologies serve as powerful assistants, augmenting a photographer's abilities rather than replacing human creativity and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. The most critical overarching takeaway for any photographer, regardless of their proficiency or genre, is the perennial importance of robust backup strategies. Continuously backing up work to modern [[entities/storage|storage]] solutions is essential to preserve valuable images and ensure they remain accessible as technology evolves.
