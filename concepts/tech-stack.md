# Tech Stack

A tech stack refers to the combination of programming languages, frameworks, tools, and platforms that developers use to build applications. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]], tech stacks have become increasingly important as developers integrate [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]], [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], and specialized libraries into their workflows. Understanding which tools and technologies are most commonly adopted provides insight into industry trends and [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]].

## Survey Methodology

The Pragmatic Engineer 2025 [[concepts/survey|survey]] and the [[entities/artificial-analysis|Artificial Analysis]] survey both assessed [[concepts/developer|developer]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] and technologies, though they differed in scope and focus. These surveys capture snapshots of the tools developers actively use when building AI-powered systems, revealing shifts in preference toward models with higher [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]] and lower latency.

## Emerging Model Landscape: Gemini 4 Argon

Recent developments in the competitive [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]] highlight Google's resurgence with the announcement of **[[concepts/large-language-model|Gemini 4 Argon]]**. Key insights from recent analysis include:

*   **[[concepts/performance-benchmarks|Performance Benchmarks]]:** Initial [[entities/artificial-analysis|Artificial Analysis]] benchmarks place Gemini 4 Argon's intelligence on par with OpenAI's [[concepts/gpt-6-astra|GPT-6 Astra]], scoring 53 on the [[concepts/intelligence-index|intelligence index]].
*   **Context Capacity:** The model supports a [[concepts/1-million-token-context-window|1 million token context window]], significantly impacting how developers manage long-form [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and complex codebases.
*   **Adoption Implications:** As this model moves from testing to public release, it is expected to influence [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tool]] adoption metrics, particularly for enterprises requiring extensive context retention.
