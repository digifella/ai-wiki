# Residence Visa For Tourism

A residence visa for tourism in Turkey is a long-term permit category that allows foreign nationals to reside in the country for extended periods while engaging in leisure activities. This visa type differs significantly from standard tourist visas, which typically restrict stays to a maximum of 90 days within a 180-day period. It is specifically designed for individuals who wish to maintain a residential presence in Turkey beyond the conventional limits of short-term tourism.

Applicants must meet specific criteria established by Turkish authorities to qualify for this status. The application process generally requires proof of sufficient financial means to support oneself without working, valid health insurance coverage, and a clean criminal record. Additionally, applicants must demonstrate a legitimate reason for their extended stay, such as property ownership or family ties, although the primary purpose remains tourism and leisure rather than employment or business operations.

The duration of the initial residence permit varies based on individual circumstances and the discretion of the local immigration office, often ranging from one to two years. Holders of this visa are required to renew their permits periodically before expiration to maintain legal status. Failure to comply with the conditions of stay, such as engaging in unauthorized work or exceeding the allowed absence periods, can result in the revocation of the visa and potential entry bans.
