# Role Ambiguity

**Role [[concepts/ambiguity|Ambiguity]]** is the uncertainty an employee experiences regarding their job responsibilities, performance expectations, or the power and authority associated with their position. It is a significant source of Role [[concepts/stress|Stress]] and is negatively correlated with Job Satisfaction, Organizational Commitment, and Performance.

## Core Characteristics
- **Unclear Expectations:** Lack of information about what is expected in terms of duties, goals, or procedures.
- **Undefined Authority:** Uncertainty regarding [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] power or reporting lines.
- **[[concepts/feedback|Feedback]] Vacuum:** Absence of clear feedback [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] to validate whether tasks are being performed correctly.

## Consequences
- Increased [[concepts/anxiety]] and Burnout.
- Reduced Proactive Behavior and [[concepts/innovation|innovation]].
- Higher turnover intent.
- [[concepts/conflict|Conflict]] with colleagues due to overlapping or undefined responsibilities.

## Contextual Factors & Models
- Often stems from poor [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] Communication or rapid [[concepts/organizational-change|organizational change]].
- Related to Role Conflict where expectations are contradictory, whereas ambiguity is about the *absence* of clear expectations.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Hackman-model-of-team-effectiveness-explained-Five-facto| Five factors for great teamwork — BiteSize Learning]] highlights that clear roles and boundaries are a foundational condition for [[concepts/team-effectiveness|team effectiveness]], directly countering role ambiguity.

## Mitigation Strategies
- **Job Design:** Clearly define role descriptions and key performance [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] (KPIs).
- **Communication:** Regular [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]] and check-ins.
- **Structure:** Establish clear reporting lines and decision-making authorities.
