# Food Science

Food science is an interdisciplinary field dedicated to the study of the physical, chemical, and biological properties of food. It applies principles from chemistry, biology, and engineering to understand how food behaves during processing, storage, and preparation. The discipline focuses on ensuring food safety, improving nutritional value, and developing new food products that meet consumer demands for quality and convenience.

A significant area of application within this domain involves the analysis and optimization of meat products. Current research and industrial practices in this sector concentrate on the specific characteristics of ground meat, examining factors such as texture, microbial stability, and flavor retention. This specialized focus aims to enhance the safety and sensory attributes of processed meat items through rigorous scientific evaluation.
