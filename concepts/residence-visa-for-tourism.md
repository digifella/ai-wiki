# Residence Visa For Tourism

A residence visa for tourism in Turkey is a long-term visa category that permits foreign nationals to reside in the country for extended periods while engaged in tourism-related activities or leisure pursuits. This visa type differs from standard tourist visas, which typically allow stays of up to 90 days, and is designed for individuals seeking to maintain a residential presence in Turkey beyond conventional tourism duration limits.

## Application Requirements

Foreign nationals applying for a residence visa for tourism must meet specific criteria established by Turkish immigration authorities. Applicants typically need to demonstrate financial stability, provide proof of accommodation in Turkey, and submit standard documentation including a valid passport, completed application forms, and evidence of health insurance. The visa is generally available to citizens of most countries, though specific requirements may vary depending on the applicant's nationality.

## Duration and Conditions

The residence visa for tourism allows holders to stay in Turkey for renewable periods, commonly issued for one year at a time. Unlike work visas or residence permits tied to employment or investment, this category is specifically intended for individuals who do not plan to engage in employment within Turkey. Holders may reside in the country, travel within its borders, and engage in personal leisure activities while maintaining their visa status through periodic renewal processes.
