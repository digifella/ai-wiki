# Insect Intelligence

The study of [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]], [[concepts/learning|learning]] mechanisms, and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] within Insecta. Research focuses on how high-order complexity emerges from minimal neural architectures.

## Key Research & Findings

- [[concepts/bumblebees|Bumblebees]]:
    - Demonstrated advanced [[concepts/cognition|cognition]] and the presence of [[concepts/cumulative-culture]] (intergenerational transmission of learned behaviors).
    - Exhibit specialized flood [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] strategies.
    - Challenges long-held biological assumptions regarding the correlation between brain size (comparable to a poppy seed) and [[concepts/cognitive-complexity|cognitive complexity]].
