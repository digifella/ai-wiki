The execution of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/models|models]], specifically [[concepts/large-language-models]], directly on mobile [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] (e.g., [[entities/iphone|iPhone]], [[entities/ipad]]) rather than via cloud-based [[concepts/application-programming-interfaces-apis|APIs]].
