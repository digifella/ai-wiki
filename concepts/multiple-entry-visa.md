# Multiple Entry Visa

A multiple entry visa is a travel document that permits the holder to enter and exit a country on more than one occasion during the visa's validity period. Unlike single entry visas, which become invalid after one border crossing, multiple entry visas allow repeated journeys into and out of the country without requiring a new visa application for each trip. This [[concepts/type-of-visa|type of visa]] is commonly issued to frequent travelers, business professionals, and individuals who plan several visits within a designated timeframe.

## Duration and Conditions

The validity period of a multiple entry visa varies significantly depending on the issuing country and the specific visa category. Holders are typically subject to a maximum duration of stay per visit, which may be shorter than the total validity of the visa itself. For example, a visa valid for one year might only allow stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period. Travelers must ensure they comply with these limits to avoid overstaying, which can result in fines, deportation, or future entry bans.

## Eligibility and Application

Eligibility for a multiple entry visa often depends on the applicant's travel history, purpose of visit, and financial stability. Business professionals and tourists with a proven record of compliance with immigration laws are frequently prioritized. The application process generally requires submitting standard documentation, such as a valid passport, proof of accommodation, and evidence of sufficient funds, along with a specific request for multiple entry privileges. Approval is at the discretion of the immigration authorities, who assess the necessity and legitimacy of repeated travel.
