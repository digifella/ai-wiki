# Slide Deck Transformation

Slide Deck Transformation refers to the process of converting presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], with specific [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to removing or avoiding watermarks in the final output. NotebookLM, [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s AI-powered [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] tool, can automatically generate [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source documents and notes. These slides can then be imported into Google Vids, Google's [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] platform, to produce multimedia presentations.

## Process Overview

The transformation workflow typically involves exporting slides from NotebookLM and importing them into Google Vids, where they can be enhanced with voiceover, transitions, and additional media elements. The resulting videos are generated in a format that may or may not include platform watermarks depending on the tool settings and export options used. Users seeking unwatermarked output need to identify and configure appropriate export [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] within the Google Vids interface.

## Design Customization

NotebookLM provides options for customizing slide design before or during the transformation process. These customizations can be made either directly within NotebookLM or through integration with Google's design tools like [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], allowing users to refine the visual appearance and professional quality of presentations before they are converted to video format.
