# Chemical Engineering

Application of physical sciences, [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], and economics to transform raw materials into valuable products via [[concepts/food-chemistry]] and physical unit operations. Focuses on [[concepts/design|design]], analysis, optimization, and [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] of processes.

## Fundamental Principles
- Chemical thermodynamics: Energy balances, [[concepts/phase|phase]] equilibria, and process feasibility.
- Transport phenomena: Conservation of mass, momentum, and heat transfer.
- Reaction kinetics: Rate laws, mechanisms, and reactor design.
- Process [[concepts/power|control]]: [[concepts/automation|Automation]], [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]], and system stability.
- Catalysis: Acceleration of reaction rates and selectivity using catalysts.

## Key Applications & Systems
- Petrochemicals: Refining, cracking, polymerization, and fuel synthesis.
- Pharmaceuticals: Bioprocessing, fermentation, and drug synthesis.
- Environmental engineering: [[concepts/emissions-reduction|Emissions control]], wastewater treatment, and remediation.
- Materials [[concepts/science|science]]: Development of functional materials and [[concepts/nanotechnology|nanotechnology]].
- Automotive emissions management:
  - [[concepts/catalytic-converter]] technology represents a critical [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of heterogeneous catalysis to reduce toxic vehicular exhaust.
  - Utilizes three-way catalyst mechanisms to simultaneously oxidize carbon monoxide and unburnt hydrocarbons while reducing nitrogen oxides.
  - System [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] employs ceramic honeycomb or metal foam substrates coated with high-surface-area washcoats and active precious metal nanoparticles (Platinum, Palladium, Rhodium).
  - High loading of rhodium and platinum group metals creates significant material value density, driving specific theft dynamics and supply chain vulnerabilities; see [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-Catalytic-Converter-Report-History-Emissions-Role-and-Th|Catalytic Converter Report: History, Emissions Role, and Theft Dynamics]].
