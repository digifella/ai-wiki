# Stakeholder Profiles

Stakeholder Profiles function as intelligence status cards that display the information maturity level of individual stakeholder records within a business strategy context. Each profile card provides visibility into the current state of intelligence associated with a particular stakeholder, helping teams quickly identify which stakeholders have adequate supporting data and which may require additional research or focused attention.

The display mechanism utilizes standardized intelligence status indicators that reflect the completeness and reliability of the gathered data. These indicators allow users to assess the quality of information at a glance, ensuring that strategic decisions are based on verified and sufficient intelligence rather than assumptions.

A key feature of the Stakeholder Profiles interface is the ability to filter profiles with no matched intel. This filtering capability enables teams to isolate stakeholders lacking sufficient data, facilitating targeted data collection efforts and ensuring that no critical stakeholder is overlooked due to information gaps.
