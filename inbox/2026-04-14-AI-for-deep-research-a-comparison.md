# AI for [[entities/deep-research|deep research]] - a comparison

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=meg8PU7qnz4>
The video, presented by **[[entities/robert-benjamin|Robert Benjamin]]**, compares various [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] for "deep research" and then outlines several "insane [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]]" for these capabilities, focusing on the [[entities/deepagent|DeepAgent]] tool available through [Abacus.AI](https://Abacus.AI).
**AI Tool Comparison for Deep Research:**
Robert tested six [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] with the prompt: "Please craft a research report about how global warming is affecting certain animal populations in the [[entities/united-states|United States]]. Please include plenty of graphs and photos that support what you find."

* **[[concepts/methodology|Methodology]]:**
	* Some tools ([[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], DeepAgent) asked [[concepts/clarifying-questions|clarifying questions]] before proceeding, which Robert found beneficial for tailoring the research.
	* Others ([[entities/grok|Grok]], Genspark, [[concepts/manus|Manus]], Perplexity) immediately started the research without further input.
* **Results:**
	* **[[entities/gemini|Gemini]]:** Researched 169 websites but ultimately failed to fulfill the request, stating, "I'm having a hard time fulfilling your request." Robert found this "incredibly disappointing."
	* **ChatGPT:** Took 7 minutes, 33 sources, and 90 searches. It produced a text-heavy report with very few and small images/graphs, not fully meeting the visual requirements. Robert was "disappointed."
	* **[[concepts/grok|Grok]]:** Did not provide any graphs or photos, only a small table, despite the explicit request in the prompt. Robert was "even more disappointed."
	* **Genspark:** Created a "PowerPoint" [[concepts/style|style]] output with graphs and photos, but lacked substantial text, resembling a website or presentation more than a research report.
	* **Manus:** Generated a lengthy 44-page PDF report. However, it completely missed the requirement for graphs and photos. Robert felt this would be "screwed" for real-world application due to the missing visuals.
	* **Perplexity:** Produced a "pretty short" report (48 sources) with some graphs and photos. It was decent but lacked depth.
	* **DeepAgent (Winner):** This tool "incredibly impressed" Robert. It produced a comprehensive 23-page PDF report. Crucially, it successfully included a wealth of relevant graphs (e.g., temperature changes, habitat loss, [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]] maps) and descriptive photos (e.g., drought, coral bleaching, polar bears), directly fulfilling the prompt's visual requirements.

**Robert's Ranking:**

1. **DeepAgent**
2. Perplexity
3. Manus (due to report length, despite missing visuals)

**Five Insane Use Cases for DeepAgent's Research Capabilities:**
Robert [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] DeepAgent's strength in combining deep research with other AI [[entities/agent|agent]] features for powerful applications:

1. **Market Research:**
	* **Prompt Example:** "Do an in-depth research about market growth and future opportunities in the fitness and [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]] industry globally. Use bullets, tables and charts to make the report clearer."
	* **DeepAgent Output:** A comprehensive PDF report with market sizing, growth projections, regional analysis, investment opportunities, and relevant charts and tables.
	* **Use Case:** Create detailed market research reports for personal interest, business strategy, or even as lead magnets to attract clients by providing valuable industry insights.
2. **Travel [[concepts/itinerary-planning|Itinerary Planning]]:**
	* **Prompt Example:** "I am planning a budget solo backpacking trip to Tokyo. Please create a 5 day itinerary."
	* **DeepAgent Output:** Interactively asks clarifying questions (budget [[concepts/range|range]], interests, time of year, accommodation preference, specific areas). Then generates a detailed PDF itinerary covering best neighborhoods, budget accommodation recommendations, transportation costs, daily activities, food guides, safety tips, and includes maps and images.
	* **Use Case:** Generate personalized and detailed travel plans for any destination, complete with practical information and visuals, saving immense time on manual research.
3. **In-depth Competitive/Industry Analysis:**
	* **Prompt Example:** "Do an in-depth research around EV battery manufacturers globally, and provide a competitive study around them. Cite source of information for all the information you use. The report should have one section to rate the manufacturers across various relevant [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. Provide the report in pdf format."
	* **DeepAgent Output:** Asks clarifying questions (focus on global/local players, specific battery technologies, key comparison parameters, future outlook, geographic regions). Produces a 53-page PDF report detailing market share, production capacity, technology, R&D, partnerships, and manufacturing facilities for major global EV battery manufacturers, including visual charts and cited sources.
	* **Use Case:** Conduct highly detailed analyses for academic pursuits ([[entities/phd|PhD]], Master's), professional work, or gaining expertise in complex industries, providing comprehensive overviews faster than traditional methods.
4. **Social Media Trend Reports:**
	* **Prompt Example:** "Generate a report summarizing the top trending topics on social media and across the internet over the past 1 week. For each trending topic, the agenda of this report is know the topics to post about on my socials to increase engagement. please include the following: Topic Name..., Platform(s)..., General Sentiment..., Quanlitative Metrics..., Engagement levels..., Regional/geographic popularity..., [[concepts/summary|Summary]] of Discussion..."
	* **DeepAgent Output:** A detailed report categorizing trending topics, identifying platforms, [[concepts/sentiment-analysis|sentiment analysis]], quantitative metrics (posts/mentions, engagement levels, growth rate), geographic [[concepts/distribution|distribution]], and a discussion summary.
	* **Use Case:** Inform social media content strategy, identify emerging trends, and understand audience sentiment for specific niches to increase engagement and relevance.
