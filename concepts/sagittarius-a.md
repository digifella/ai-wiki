# Sagittarius A*

Sgr A* is the supermassive [[concepts/black-holes|black hole]] (SMBH) residing at the dynamical center of the [[concepts/milky-way]], defining the gravitational potential of the [[concepts/galactic-center|Galactic Center]].

## Key Properties
- **Mass:** ~4.1–4.3 million solar masses (M_sun).
- **Distance:** ~26,670 light-years from Solar System.
- **Location:** Bright radio source in constellation Sagittarius; coincides with infrared source [[entities/sgra|Sagittarius A*]].
- **Imaging:** Shadow and photon ring resolved by [[concepts/event-horizon|Event Horizon]] Telescope collaboration (2022).

## Surrounding Environment
- **Stellar Cluster:** Dense concentration of young [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], including S-stars on highly eccentric, short-period orbits probing the black hole's [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]].
- **G-Objects:** Transient, dust-enshrouded [[concepts/nodes|entities]] exhibiting properties intermediate between stars and gas clouds.
- **G-Object Origin:** [[lab-notes/2026-05-17-Resolved-G-Objects-Near-SgrA-Originate-from-IRS-16SW-Bin|Resolved: G-Objects Near SgrA* Originate from IRS 16SW Binary Stars]] confirms G-objects result from tidal disruption and interaction of [[entities/irs-16sw|IRS 16SW]] [[concepts/binary-stars|binary star systems]] near the galactic center.
