# Alchemy

Medieval practice of transforming base metals into gold, seeking the philosopher's stone, and exploring the fundamental [[entities/nature|nature]] of matter. Evolved into modern Chemistry and influenced Hermeticism.

## Key Concepts
- Philosopher's Stone: Legendary substance enabling transmutation and immortality.
- Emerald Tablet: Foundational text of Hermetic [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] attributed to [[entities/hermes|Hermes]] Trismegistus.
- Magnum Opus: The alchemical process of spiritual and material transformation.

## Modern Context
- Metaphorical use in fields like data science ("data alchemy").
- Historical precursor to Chemistry and Pharmacology.
- Alchemy's symbolic framework still influences Jungian psychology.
