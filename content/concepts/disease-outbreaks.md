# Disease Outbreaks

Disease outbreaks can be exacerbated by various factors including environmental changes and human activities. [[concepts/climate-change|Climate change]] is one of the significant contributors to the increase in disease outbreaks around the world.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/climate-change]]
- [[concepts/vulnerable-populations|vulnerable-populations]]

### Summary
The concept of disease outbreaks encompasses a wide range of [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where diseases spread rapidly within populations, often due to new or re-emerging pathogens. Environmental changes such as [[concepts/climate-change|climate change]] can significantly influence the patterns and intensity of disease transmission, affecting both human [[concepts/health|health]] and wildlife.

### Climate Change Impact on Disease Outbreaks

- **[[concepts/extreme-weather-events|Extreme Weather Events]]**: More frequent and severe storms, floods, droughts, and heatwaves disrupt ecosystems and create conditions favorable for the spread of diseases.
- **Air Quality Degradation**: Increased air pollution can lead to respiratory issues and weaken immune systems, making individuals more susceptible to infectious diseases.
- **Vector-borne Diseases**: Warmer temperatures extend the range and seasonality of disease vectors like mosquitoes and ticks, increasing the risk of outbreaks.
