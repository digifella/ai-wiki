# Iron-60

**Iron-60** ($^{60}\text{Fe}$) is a long-lived [[concepts/radioactive-isotope|radioactive isotope]] of iron with a half-life of approximately 2.6 million years. It is primarily produced in massive [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] during [[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]] and expelled via [[concepts/supernovae]] or Asymptotic Giant Branch star winds.

## Detection and Significance
*   **Paleo-record Proxy:** $^{60}\text{Fe}$ serves as a key tracer for recent near-[[entities/earth|Earth]] supernovae events, as it is not produced significantly by [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] in [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]].
*   **Ice Core Analysis:** Recent studies of Antarctic Ice cores have detected anomalies in $^{60}\text{Fe}$ concentrations.
    *   See analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-06-02-Antarctic-Ice-Fe-60-Solar-Systems-Entry-into-Local-Inter|Antarctic Ice Fe-60: Solar System's Entry into Local Interstellar Cloud]]
    *   Evidence suggests a correlation between $^{60}\text{Fe}$ deposition and the Solar System's traversal into the Local Interstellar Cloud.

## Astrophysical Context
*   **Galactic Origin:** $^{60}\text{Fe}$ indicates interaction with the Interstellar [[entities/medium|Medium]] (ISM), specifically dust [[concepts/granular-materials|grains]] from nearby [[concepts/compact-stellar-objects|stellar remnants]].
*   **Timeline:** Elevated levels observed in deep-sea sediments and ice cores typically date back to the last few million years, aligning with models of the Solar System's exit from the Local Bubble and entry into denser interstellar clouds.
*   **Isotope Ratio:** Combined analysis with $^{26}\text{Al}$ helps distinguish between [[concepts/supernova|supernova]] injection and spallation background.
