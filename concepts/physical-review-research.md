# Physical Review Research

*Physical Review Research* is an open-access, general-interest journal published by the American Physical Society (APS). It publishes [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] research across all areas of [[concepts/physics|physics]], emphasizing broad interest and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]].

## Key Studies and Topics

- **[[concepts/quantum-gravity|Quantum Gravity]] & Time [[concepts/accuracy|Precision]]**: Recent investigations into the [[concepts/fundamental-limits|fundamental limits]] of temporal measurement suggest intrinsic uncertainties imposed by quantum [[concepts/gravitational-effects|gravitational effects]].
	- See detailed analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-05-30-Quantum-Gravity-Limits-on-Time-Measurement-Precision|Quantum Gravity Limits on Time Measurement Precision]]
	- 2025 studies indicate a hard limit on time precision, potentially linking the [[entities/planck|Planck]] scale to observational constraints.

## Characteristics

- **Scope**: General physics, interdisciplinary topics, and pedagogical insights.
- **Access**: Open access, promoting wider dissemination of scientific findings.
- **Focus**: Emphasis on novel results that may not fit into specialized *Physical Review* letters or rapid communications but warrant full publication.
