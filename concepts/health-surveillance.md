# Health Surveillance

**[[concepts/health|Health]] Surveillance** refers to the ongoing, systematic collection, analysis, and interpretation of health-related data essential to the planning, implementation, and evaluation of [[concepts/population-health|public health]] practice. In the context of modern [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]], this increasingly involves automated, [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]] via [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]], [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|remote patient monitoring]] systems, and emerging AI-driven diagnostic tools.

## Key Components
- **Continuous Data Collection**: Utilization of [[concepts/internet-of-things|IoT devices]] and [[concepts/wearable-device-technology|wearables]] to track vital signs.
- **Automated Analysis**: Use of [[concepts/machine-learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to detect anomalies in real-time.
- **Longitudinal Tracking**: Monitoring [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]] over extended periods to identify trends and predict outcomes.
