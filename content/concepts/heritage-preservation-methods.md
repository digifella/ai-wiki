# Heritage Preservation Methods

[[concepts/cultural-heritage|Heritage]] [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] encompasses the strategies and techniques used to maintain, protect, and document culturally significant spaces, objects, and practices. These methods ensure that important historical elements remain accessible to future generations while respecting their original context and [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].

## Physical Spaces and Fixtures

One documented example of heritage preservation involves the retention of original architectural and design elements within heritage-listed establishments. The [[entities/south-seas-cafe|South Seas Cafe]] demonstrates preservation practices applied to interior features, including mosaic tile work and specialized flooring materials such as [[concepts/dunlop-rubber-floors|Dunlop rubber floors]]. These structural elements represent both the aesthetic choices and manufacturing techniques characteristic of their period, making their preservation valuable for understanding historical design practices and material culture.

## Documentation and Cultural Context

Effective heritage preservation extends beyond physical conservation to include the documentation of associated cultural practices and spatial uses. Understanding how spaces functioned—including details about their original purposes, distinctive features, and the roles they served in community life—contributes to more complete preservation. This [[concepts/contextual-information|contextual information]] is essential for interpreting heritage sites and maintaining their cultural significance even as their original functions may change over time.
