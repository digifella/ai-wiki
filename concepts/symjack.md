# SymJack

**SymJack** represents the intersection of symbolic analysis and automated [[concepts/vulnerability-exploitation|vulnerability exploitation]] in [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] ecosystems. It focuses on leveraging AI-driven insights to identify, patch, and [[concepts/secure|secure]] supply chain dependencies before they become critical failure points.

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/automated-vulnerability-detection|Automated Vulnerability Detection]]**: Utilization of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to scan code repositories for known CVEs and zero-day potential.
- **Supply Chain [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Ensuring dependency graphs remain uncompromised through [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]].
- **Open Source Collaboration**: Integrating [[concepts/security|security]] protocols directly into the [[concepts/software-development-process|development lifecycle]] of community-driven projects.
