# Phase 3 trial

A clinical trial phase designed to evaluate the efficacy and safety of a new treatment in a large group of people, typically comparing it to standard treatments or placebos. Positive results in this phase are critical for regulatory approval.

## Recent Developments

### Moderna and Merck mRNA Vaccine
Significant progress has been made in the [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]] for [[concepts/resected-melanoma]] using personalized mRNA technology.

- **Trial Outcome**: Positive Phase 3 trial results announced for the combination of Moderna/Merck's personalized [[concepts/mrna-cancer-vaccine|mRNA cancer vaccine]] ([[entities/intismeran-autogene|Intismeran Autogene]]) and [[entities/merck|Merck]]'s Keytruda ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]).
- **Indication**: Adjuvant therapy for patients with resected melanoma.
- **Significance**: Represents a major advancement in personalized cancer vaccines, potentially setting a new standard for post-surgical treatment to prevent recurrence.
