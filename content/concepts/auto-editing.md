# Auto Editing

Auto editing refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automatically refine, correct, and improve text generated through voice dictation. Rather than requiring manual correction after transcription, auto editing systems process dictated speech in real-time or immediately after capture, addressing [[concepts/grammar|grammar]], punctuation, clarity, and stylistic issues automatically. This approach aims to reduce the [[concepts/friction|friction]] between spoken composition and written output, making [[concepts/ai-powered-dictation|voice-to-text]] workflows more efficient for writers, content creators, and professionals who prefer dictation.

## Applications and Use Cases

Auto editing technology is particularly useful for scenarios where manual post-processing creates workflow bottlenecks. Journalists, novelists, academics, and business professionals using voice dictation benefit from reduced editing time, while [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] applications enable users with mobility or visual impairments to compose text more independently. The technology can also support multiple languages and adapt to individual speaking patterns and domain-specific [[concepts/terminology|terminology]] over time.

## Notable Implementations

Wispr Flow is a notable example of an AI-powered dictation app that incorporates auto editing capabilities. The company raised $30 million in funding from [[entities/menlo-ventures|Menlo Ventures]], reflecting investor confidence in the voice-to-text market. Other implementations of auto editing exist across various dictation and transcription platforms, though the specific feature set and effectiveness varies by application.
