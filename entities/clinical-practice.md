# Clinical Practice

**Clinical Practice** refers to the delivery of healthcare services, integrating clinical guidelines, patient engagement strategies, and [[concepts/health-information-technology|health information technology]] to optimize outcomes. Modern frameworks emphasize the shift from paternalistic models to [[concepts/person-centred-care|collaborative care]] ecosystems.

## Key Concepts & Interventions

*   **Coproduction in Care Delivery**: The active involvement of patients as partners in designing and delivering care, moving beyond mere consultation.
*   **Point-of-Care Technology**: Utilization of real-time data tools to support [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]] and patient interaction during visits.
*   **Dashboard Integration**: Use of structured visual data representations to align provider and patient perspectives on health status and goals.
