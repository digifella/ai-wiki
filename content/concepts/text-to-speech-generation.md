# Text To Speech Generation

Text-to-speech (TTS) generation is an AI capability that converts written [[concepts/text|text]] into spoken [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] [[concepts/output|output]]. Modern TTS systems use [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] to synthesize natural-sounding speech, moving beyond earlier rule-based and concatenative synthesis methods. The technology has [[concepts/software|applications]] in [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], [[concepts/voice-assistants|virtual assistants]], audiobook production, and interactive systems.

## Qwen3-TTS Family

The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family, released as [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]], represents an advancement in neural [[concepts/text-to-speech-synthesis|text-to-speech synthesis]]. These [[concepts/models|models]] support multiple features including basic text-to-speech conversion, [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] (allowing [[concepts/customization|customization]] of speech characteristics), and [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]] (enabling synthesis in specific [[entities/speaker|speaker]] voices).

## Key Capabilities

[[concepts/ai-cloning|Voice cloning]] functionality enables the model to generate speech that matches the characteristics of a reference speaker, while voice [[concepts/design|design]] allows users to control acoustic properties of the generated speech. These features [[entities/make|make]] the technology useful for creating personalized audio experiences and adapting output to specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] without requiring extensive retraining.
