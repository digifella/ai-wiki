Generative markup tools leverage [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automate and enhance the creation of content and design elements. These tools are increasingly being integrated into popular [[concepts/design|graphic design]] applications like [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] and [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop for the Web]].
