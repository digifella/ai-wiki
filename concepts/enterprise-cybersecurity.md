# Enterprise Cybersecurity

**Enterprise [[concepts/cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]]** encompasses the strategies, technologies, and practices used to protect an organization's networks, systems, and data from cyber threats. In the modern landscape, it increasingly involves managing supply chain risks, securing [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud infrastructure]], and leveraging [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] for both defense and offense mitigation.

## Key Trends & Initiatives

- **AI-Driven [[concepts/security|Security]] Operations**: Integration of AI/ML models for real-time threat detection, [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]] identification, and automated response.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open Source]] Security**: Securing the software supply chain, particularly critical dependencies and containerized applications.
- **[[concepts/zero-trust|Zero Trust]] Architecture**: Moving beyond perimeter-based security to identity-centric access control.
