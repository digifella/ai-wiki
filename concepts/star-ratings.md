# Star Ratings

Star ratings in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] serve as a primary organizational mechanism for managing and prioritizing images within a [[concepts/catalog|catalog]]. This system allows photographers to assign a [[concepts/visual-quality-assessment|visual quality assessment]] ranging from one to five [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], facilitating efficient sorting and filtering during the culling and editing phases. By utilizing this method, users can establish consistent judgments about image quality without the need to manually reorganize files into separate folders or create complex folder structures.

## Implementation and Workflow

The rating system is typically applied during the initial culling process, where photographers quickly evaluate raw captures to identify keepers and rejects. A common workflow involves assigning one star to images that meet basic technical criteria, three stars to strong candidates worthy of further review, and five stars to final selections intended for delivery or publication. This hierarchical approach enables rapid filtering, allowing editors to isolate specific subsets of images for detailed post-processing or batch [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
