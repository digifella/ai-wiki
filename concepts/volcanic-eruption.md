## Volcanic Eruption

**Definition:** A volcanic eruption is the expulsion of rock, lava, ash, and gases from a volcano. These events range in scale from small to catastrophic.

### Overview
[[concepts/volcanic-eruptions|Volcanic eruptions]] are significant geological phenomena that can drastically alter landscapes, ecosystems, and human populations. They occur when magma rises through conduits and vents to the surface due to tectonic forces or internal pressure changes within the [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s crust.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/magma-chamber|Magma Chamber]]:** A reservoir beneath a volcano where magma accumulates before an eruption.
- **Vent/Nozzle:** An opening at the Earth’s surface that allows magma, ash, and gases to escape during an eruption.
- **Tephra:** Material thrown out of a volcanic vent during an eruption including fragments of rock, lava, and glass.

### Notable Eruptions
- **Cerberean [[concepts/supervolcano|Supervolcano]] Discovery [[concepts/geology|Geology]] and [[concepts/explosive-history|Explosive History]] Northeast [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] (2026)**
  - **Location:** Northeast of Melbourne, Australia
  - **Era:** Devonian
  - **Features:**
    - Spanning approximately 30km in width with a collapsed [[concepts/caldera|caldera]] about 27 kilometers wide.
    - One of the largest known eruptions in Australian geological history.
    - Characterized by extensive [[concepts/lava-flows|lava flows]] and ash deposits indicative of supervolcanic activity.
