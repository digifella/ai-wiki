# Gravity Compensation

**[[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]] Compensation** refers to [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] designed to counteract the effects of gravity on [[concepts/timekeeping-accuracy|timekeeping accuracy]]. In mechanical watches, gravity causes positional errors as the balance wheel and escapement change orientation relative to the [[entities/earth|earth]]'s gravitational field.

## Key Mechanisms

### Tourbillon
The Tourbillon is a prominent gravity compensation device invented by [[concepts/abraham-louis-breguet|Abraham-Louis Breguet]]. It rotates the escapement and [[concepts/watch-mechanism|balance wheel assembly]] to average out positional errors.

- **Purpose**: To mitigate the influence of gravity on the rate of the watch by continuously changing the position of the balance wheel.
- **[[concepts/gyro-tourbillon|Gyro Tourbillon]]**: A complex variant that serves as the "heart of a watch," responsible for keeping accurate time through intricate rotation.
- **Visualization**: Complex mechanisms like the Gyro Tourbillon can be studied via 3D-printed models to understand their [[concepts/internal-geometry|internal geometry]] and function.
