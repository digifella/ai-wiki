# Non-linear Video Editor

A non-linear video editor ([[concepts/non-linear-video-editing|NLE]]) enables [[concepts/non-linear-editing|random-access editing]] of video clips stored digitally, decoupling editing operations from the physical sequence of source media. NLEs utilize a timeline-based interface with independent tracks for video, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and effects, allowing precise manipulation, transitions, and post-production without degradation of original assets.

## Core Features
- Random access to digital media [[concepts/files|files]].
- Multitrack composition and layering.
- Real-time preview and non-destructive effect application.
- Support for diverse codecs and resolution workflows.

## Recent Developments & AI Integration
- **[[concepts/ltx-desktop|LTX Desktop]]**: Free, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] local NLE embedding [[entities/ltx-23]] [[concepts/ai-driven-video-production|AI video generation]] within the editing environment, facilitating [[concepts/mobile-ai|on-device AI]] assistance and privacy-preserving workflows [[lab-notes/2026-05-13-LTX-Desktop-Groundbreaking-Free-Open-Source-Local-AI-Vid|LTX Desktop: Groundbreaking Free, Open-Source Local AI Video Editor with LTX 2.3]].
- Trend toward hybrid NLEs combining traditional editing tools with generative [[concepts/models|models]] for [[concepts/digital-asset-creation|asset creation]] and automated editing tasks.
