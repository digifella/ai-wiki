# Meat cutting techniques

Methods of partitioning animal protein to manipulate [[concepts/texture]], Heat Transfer, and [[concepts/food-chemistry]].

## Fundamental Principles
- **[[concepts/film-grain|Grain]] Direction**: Cutting perpendicular to muscle fibers (against the grain) shortens fibers to increase tenderness.
- **Surface Area**: Influences the scale of the [[concepts/maillard-reaction]] and the efficiency of Marinade penetration.
- **Thickness Uniformity**: Essential for consistent Heat Transfer and preventing uneven cooking across the piece.

## Integrated Culinary Applications
- **[[concepts/bbq|Grilling]] Optimization**: Precise Cut is a critical variable in the triad of Marinade, Cut, and Cook Techniques used to maximize Juiciness and prevent Dry Meat (Ref: 2026 04 27 [[concepts/grilling-chicken|Grilled Chicken Juiciness]] Marinade Cut and Cook Techniqu).
- **Pitfall Mitigation**: Utilizing specific cutting techniques is fundamental to avoiding common errors that lead to Dry Meat during high-heat cooking.
