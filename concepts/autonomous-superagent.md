# Autonomous Superagent

An advanced [[concepts/ai-system|AI architecture]] capable of independent goal formulation, execution, and adaptation without continuous human intervention. This concept marks a shift from reactive [[concepts/large-language-model]]s to proactive, self-directed agents.

## Core Characteristics
- **Autonomy:** Operates without explicit step-by-step [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], determining its own sub-goals and execution paths.
- **Superagent Capabilities:** Demonstrates [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]], [[concepts/acting|tool use]], and multi-step planning beyond standard prompt-response patterns.
- **AGI Threshold:** Represents a claimed milestone in achieving [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|Artificial General Intelligence]] through self-directed [[concepts/learning|learning]] and adaptation.
