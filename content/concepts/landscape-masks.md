# Landscape Masks

[[concepts/masking-tool|Landscape Masks]] are a [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] feature available in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]] that enable photographers to selectively apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific areas of an image based on landscape elements. This tool is particularly useful for [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], where photographers often need to adjust sky, foreground, and mid-[[concepts/tone|tone]] areas independently to achieve balanced exposures and enhanced visual impact.

## Usage in Lightroom and Camera Raw

The Landscape Masks feature allows users to automatically detect and isolate common landscape elements such as skies, terrain, and water. Once a landscape element is selected through the mask, adjustments to [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, saturation, clarity, and other [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] can be applied only to that masked area, leaving the rest of the image unaffected. This selective approach provides greater [[concepts/power|control]] compared to traditional global adjustments.
