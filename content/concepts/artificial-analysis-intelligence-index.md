# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is a framework for evaluating and comparing [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and AI systems based on standardized performance metrics. It assesses models across multiple dimensions including latency, throughput, cost per token, and [[concepts/inference|inference]] efficiency. The index enables developers, researchers, and organizations to understand the practical trade-offs between different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] when selecting solutions for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Evaluation Framework

The index measures performance across both speed and efficiency metrics. By providing standardized benchmarks, it allows for objective comparison of how different models perform under similar conditions. This includes measuring response time, the number of [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] processed per unit time, and the financial cost associated with generating tokens. Such metrics are particularly relevant for organizations evaluating [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] options, as they reflect real-world operational considerations beyond raw model capability.

## Practical Applications

The framework serves developers and researchers seeking to match AI models to specific requirements. Different applications have different priorities—some may prioritize speed for real-time interactions, while others optimize for cost-efficiency or throughput in batch processing scenarios. By making these performance characteristics transparent and comparable, the index helps stakeholders make informed decisions about which models best suit their technical and budgetary constraints.
