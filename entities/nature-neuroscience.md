# Nature Neuroscience

**[[concepts/nature-neuroscience|Nature Neuroscience]]** is a leading peer-reviewed scientific journal focusing on the nervous system. It publishes high-impact research across molecular, cellular, systems, and computational [[concepts/neuroscience|neuroscience]].

## Key Research & Discoveries

### Dual Embryonic Origins of Human Brain Development
Recent findings published in this journal challenge the traditional monolithic view of brain ontogeny.

- **Core Discovery:** [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]] developmental [[concepts/biology-researchers|biologists]] have identified that the human brain arises from two distinct pools of [[concepts/neural-progenitor-cells|neural progenitor cells]] ([[concepts/npcs|NPCs]]), effectively functioning as two separate organs during embryonic development [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Dual-Embryonic-Origins-of-Human-Brain-Development|Dual Embryonic Origins of Human Brain Development]].
- **[[concepts/mindset-shift|Paradigm Shift]]:** This contradicts the centuries-old belief that the brain emerges from a single, unified pool of NPCs that later differentiate uniformly.
- **Implications:** Suggests complex, dual-[[concepts/pathway-platform|pathway]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] in human brain evolution and development, potentially explaining unique aspects of [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]] and susceptibility to neurodevelopmental disorders.
