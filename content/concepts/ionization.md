# Ionization

Ionization is the process by which an atom or a molecule acquires a positive or negative charge through gaining or losing one or more electrons. In the context of [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]], high-energy particles such as [[concepts/protons|protons]] and other atomic nuclei enter Earth's atmosphere and collide with air molecules, leading to a cascade of ionizing events.

### Summary
- Ionization is triggered when energetic particles from space interact with the Earth’s atmosphere.
- The process leads to the formation of charged particles that can damage electronics and satellites in orbit around the Earth.
