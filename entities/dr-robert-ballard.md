# Dr Robert Ballard

Dr. Robert Ballard is an American oceanographer and underwater explorer best known for discovering the wreck of the RMS Titanic in 1985. His work advanced deep-sea exploration technology and methodology, establishing techniques that became foundational for subsequent underwater archaeological expeditions.

## The Titanic Discovery

In 1985, Ballard led a joint French-American expedition to locate the Titanic in the North Atlantic Ocean. Operating at depths exceeding 12,000 feet, his team employed remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) and sonar technology to map and document the site. This mission was originally part of a classified US Navy effort to locate two lost nuclear submarines, the USS Thresher and USS Scorpion, but Ballard successfully diverted resources to find the famous liner.

## Scientific and Technical Contributions

Ballard’s approach to deep-sea exploration emphasized the use of autonomous underwater vehicles and real-time imaging, which allowed for more efficient surveying of large seabed areas. His methods revolutionized maritime archaeology by enabling the detailed study of shipwrecks without the need for immediate human submersion. These innovations have since been adopted by numerous scientific institutions for oceanographic research and resource exploration.
