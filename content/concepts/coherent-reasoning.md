# Coherent Reasoning

Coherent [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] is the ability to maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]] and contextual relevance across multiple sequential [[concepts/inference|inference]] steps, enabling complex [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] without contradiction or drift.

- **[[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]] [[concepts/multimodal-reasoning|multimodal reasoning]]**: [[entities/gemini-ai|Google's Gemini]] 3 executes 10-15 steps of [[concepts/coherent-reasoning|coherent reasoning]] simultaneously across video, [[concepts/images|images]], and [[concepts/code|code]] (see 2026 04 14 8 [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]).
- **Automated wiki updates**: Directly applies to [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] by preserving existing content while integrating new information via WikiLink-based [[concepts/cross-references|cross-references]].
- **[[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] [[concepts/integration|integration]]**: Uses coherent reasoning to execute multi-step tasks across workspace tools without manual intervention.
