# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

The Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index is a benchmarking framework developed by the research firm Artificial Analysis. It is designed to evaluate and compare large language models and broader AI systems using standardized performance metrics. The framework aims to provide independent assessments across multiple operational dimensions, allowing developers and organizations to make data-driven decisions regarding model selection and deployment.

The index focuses on quantifying key attributes such as inference speed, cost efficiency, and accuracy. By establishing a consistent methodology for measuring these factors, the framework enables objective comparisons between different model architectures and providers. This approach helps mitigate the variability often found in proprietary benchmarks, offering a clearer view of real-world performance characteristics.

The framework covers several critical operational dimensions essential for modern AI integration. These include latency and throughput for speed-sensitive applications, token pricing for cost analysis, and task-specific accuracy for quality assurance. The data is regularly updated to reflect the rapid evolution of the AI landscape, ensuring that assessments remain relevant as new models are released.

Recent developments in the field, such as the launch of Google's Gemini 3 Flash, highlight the importance of such indices. Models optimized for speed, efficiency, and low cost are increasingly prioritized by enterprises seeking scalable solutions. The Intelligence Index provides the necessary data to evaluate how these new offerings perform relative to existing standards, facilitating informed technical and financial planning.
