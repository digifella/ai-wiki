# Cross Doc Classification

Cross Doc Classification is a feature within the Portal Intelligence Layer designed to enable the systematic categorization of documents across multiple sources. The system analyzes document content and relationships to establish appropriate categories, allowing users to apply consistent classification schemes regardless of the document's origin or format. This capability facilitates better organization and retrieval of information from diverse document sets by standardizing how disparate data is grouped and indexed.

The implementation involved the integration of ten distinct tasks within the Portal Intelligence Layer. These tasks were merged to ensure the robust functioning of the classification engine, addressing the complexities of processing heterogeneous data inputs. The resulting infrastructure supports the automated assignment of labels based on semantic analysis rather than manual intervention.

By standardizing classification across the platform, the feature reduces fragmentation in document management. Users can now rely on a unified taxonomy that applies uniformly to all ingested materials, improving the accuracy of search results and the overall coherence of the knowledge base.
