# Bec

Bec conducted a comprehensive review of the AI masterplan, focusing on organizational readiness and implementation challenges. The review identified several critical barriers to effective AI [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], including [[concepts/infrastructure-limitations|infrastructure limitations]] that constrain system capabilities and [[concepts/tool-issues|tool issues]] that impede workflow efficiency.

## Key Findings

The review highlighted concerns regarding [[concepts/co-pilot-use|ungoverned AI solutions]] and the risks associated with [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]—informal, unmanaged AI implementations operating outside official channels. Bec recommended feeding these findings into Board discussions, emphasizing the need for faster decision-making around implementation proposals.

## Challenges Identified

Specific pain points emerged during the assessment, including difficulties with the [[concepts/proposal-system|proposal system]] and operational constraints posed by SharePoint systems. Bec emphasized the importance of clarifying [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and exploring opportunities to accelerate analysis speed and reduce manual review workload through AI-assisted processes.
