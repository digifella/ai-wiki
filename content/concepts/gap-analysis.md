# Gap analysis

A strategic methodology used to compare actual performance against potential or desired performance. The goal is to identify the "gap" between the current state and the target state to facilitate targeted improvements.

## Core Methodology
- **Current State Assessment**: Analyzing existing metrics, processes, and resource capabilities.
- **Future State [[concepts/llms|Definition]]**: Establishing the desired objectives and [[concepts/performance-benchmarks|performance benchmarks]].
- **Gap Identification**: Pinpointing the specific discrepancies between current and target states.
- **Action Planning**: Developing specific interventions and resource allocations to bridge the identified gaps.

## AI-Augmented Discovery
Leveraging [[entities/notebooklm]] can automate and enhance the research phases of a gap analysis:
- **[[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|Deep Research]]**: Utilizing deep-dive synthesis to identify complex discrepancies within large-scale [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
- **[[concepts/source-discovery|Source Discovery]]**: Utilizing automated discovery to find external benchmarks and industry [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] when internal documentation is insufficient.
- **[[concepts/fast-research|Fast Research]]**: Rapidly scanning existing internal documents to establish the "Current State" baseline.
- **Visual Reporting**: Using [[entities/nano-banana-pro]] to generate visual representations of identified gaps for stakeholder communication.
