# Global Cuisine Style

Global Cuisine [[concepts/style|Style]] refers to cooking approaches and techniques that draw from culinary traditions across different cultures and regions. Rather than adhering to a single culinary tradition, this style emphasizes adaptable methods and foundational formulas that can be applied across various ingredients and cultural contexts. It prioritizes understanding the underlying principles of [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking techniques]] over rigid recipe adherence, enabling cooks to create dishes that respect traditional methods while remaining flexible to available ingredients.

## Fried Rice as a Case Study

Fried rice exemplifies the global cuisine style approach, as it represents a technique found in multiple culinary traditions with regional variations. The core formula for fried rice relies on essential techniques for rice [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] and the strategic selection of mix-ins rather than specific ingredient requirements. By mastering rice treatment—such as using day-old rice and achieving proper moisture [[concepts/power|control]]—cooks can produce quality fried rice regardless of the specific proteins, vegetables, or seasonings they incorporate.

## Application and Principles

This style of cooking empowers home cooks and chefs to move beyond recipe dependency by understanding transferable techniques. The global cuisine style encourages experimentation within a [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]], allowing practitioners to honor culinary traditions while creating dishes suited to their available resources and cultural context. This approach has gained recognition through educational cooking content that breaks down traditional dishes into their fundamental components and techniques.
