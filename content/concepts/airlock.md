# Airlock Concept

Airlocks are mechanisms designed to maintain a pressure barrier between environments of different pressures or atmospheres. In the context of [[concepts/hardware|mechanical systems]] and pumps, an airlock can refer to a small pocket of air trapped within a fluid system that obstructs normal [[concepts/flow|flow]].

### Summary Points:
- An airlock in [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]] refers to a barrier formed by air or gas bubbles within liquid columns, which impedes the continuous [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of liquids.
- The [[concepts/wirtz-pump|Wirtz pump]] is an innovative [[concepts/spiral-pump|spiral pump]] designed for lifting water without relying on suction. However, it [[concepts/faces|faces]] challenges due to airlocks and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]].
