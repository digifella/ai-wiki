# Kardashev Scale

The Kardashev Scale is a [[concepts/theory|theoretical framework]] for classifying civilizations based on their capacity to [[concepts/harness|harness]] and utilize energy. Proposed by Soviet astrophysicist Nikolai Kardashev in 1964, the scale provides a quantitative method for conceptualizing the relationship between technological advancement and [[concepts/energy-consumption|energy consumption]]. It remains influential in discussions of long-term civilizational development and the [[concepts/seti|search for extraterrestrial intelligence]] (SETI), offering a systematic approach to understanding what observable signatures advanced civilizations might produce.

## Classification Types

The original scale defines three primary types based on energy sources. A Type I civilization, also known as a planetary civilization, can utilize all the energy available on its home planet. A [[concepts/kardashev-type-ii-civilization|Type II civilization]], or stellar civilization, can harness the total energy output of its host star, often conceptualized through structures like a [[concepts/dyson-sphere|Dyson sphere]]. A Type III civilization, or galactic civilization, has the capability to control energy on the scale of its entire galaxy.

## Modern Extensions and Applications

Subsequent researchers have extended the scale to include sub-types and higher levels. Type 0 represents pre-industrial or early industrial societies, such as current human civilization, which relies on fossil fuels and has not yet achieved full planetary energy control. Extensions beyond Type III include Type IV, which would control energy on the scale of a galaxy cluster, and Type V, which would utilize energy from the entire universe. These theoretical bounds help frame discussions on the feasibility of large-scale space [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], such as [[concepts/orbital-computing|space-based AI data centers]], by establishing energy availability thresholds for such technologies.

## Observational Signatures

In the context of SETI, the scale suggests that advanced civilizations would emit distinct thermal or electromagnetic signatures due to their massive energy usage. Researchers analyze these potential signatures to distinguish between natural astrophysical phenomena and artificial technological activity. The framework thus serves as a heuristic tool for evaluating the likelihood and detectability of extraterrestrial intelligence based on the physical constraints of [[concepts/energy-capture|energy harvesting]] and dissipation.
