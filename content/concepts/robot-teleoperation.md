# Robot teleoperation

The remote [[concepts/power|control]] of Robots by a human operator, often involving Human-Robot Interaction (HRI) via Immersive Technology or Haptic [[concepts/feedback|Feedback]] to translate human intent into precise machine [[concepts/exercise|movement]].

## Core Components
- Latency Management: Minimizing delay between operator input and robot execution.
- Degrees of Freedom (DoF): Mapping human joint movement to robotic actuators.
- Sensory [[concepts/feedback|Feedback]]: Providing the operator with visual and tactile data from the robot's environment.
