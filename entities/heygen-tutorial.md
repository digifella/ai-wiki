# Heygen Tutorial

Heygen is an AI-powered video generation platform designed to enable users to create realistic videos without the need for traditional filming equipment or advanced technical skills. The platform automates the video production process by generating complete videos from text descriptions, significantly reducing the time and resources typically required for video creation.

The core feature of Heygen is its Video Agent Tool, which generates AI-created videos based on user input. This tool synthesizes visual content and narration to produce finished videos that can be customized to meet specific requirements.
