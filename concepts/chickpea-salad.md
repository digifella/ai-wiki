# Chickpea Salad

Chickpea salad is a cold dish that combines cooked chickpeas (also known as garbanzo beans) with fresh vegetables and a [[concepts/light|light]] dressing. Typically served as a side dish or a light main course, the [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] involves mixing the legumes with ingredients such as tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions. The mixture is usually dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, or vinegar, and seasoned with herbs and spices like parsley, cumin, or garlic.

Chickpeas are legumes native to the Mediterranean and Middle East regions, though they are now cultivated globally. They are nutritionally dense, providing a significant source of plant-based protein and dietary fiber. This high fiber content contributes to prolonged [[concepts/satiety|satiety]], making chickpea salad a popular choice for individuals focusing on [[concepts/weight-loss|weight management]] and appetite control.

The nutritional profile of chickpeas includes essential vitamins and minerals such as folate, iron, and magnesium. When paired with fresh vegetables and healthy fats from olive oil, the salad offers a balanced macronutrient [[concepts/writing|composition]]. The combination of complex carbohydrates, protein, and fiber helps stabilize blood [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] levels, supporting sustained energy [[concepts/deployment|release]] without the rapid spikes associated with refined carbohydrates.
