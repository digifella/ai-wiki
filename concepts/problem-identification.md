# Problem Identification

Problem identification serves as the foundational step in developing a strategic approach to integrating artificial intelligence into business operations. This phase involves systematically discovering, analyzing, and documenting specific challenges, inefficiencies, and opportunities within existing processes that could benefit from AI-driven solutions. It ensures that technical implementations are grounded in actual business needs rather than technological trends.

The process requires mapping current workflows to pinpoint bottlenecks and areas where automation or predictive capabilities can add value. By clearly defining these issues, organizations can align their AI initiatives with broader organizational objectives. This alignment is critical for preventing the deployment of tools that fail to address core operational gaps or create misalignment between technical capabilities and strategic goals.

Without rigorous problem identification, companies risk investing in AI solutions that do not solve meaningful problems or that disrupt existing workflows without delivering measurable returns. The scope of this stage extends beyond immediate technical fixes to include evaluating the readiness of data infrastructure and the potential impact on workforce dynamics. Establishing a clear problem statement early in the lifecycle helps prioritize initiatives that offer the highest strategic value and feasibility.
