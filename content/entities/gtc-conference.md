# Gtc Conference

[[entities/gtc|GTC]] Conference is a technology conference that features presentations and discussions on [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]'s [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|AI agent frameworks]], particularly focusing on [[concepts/secure|secure]] enterprise [[concepts/software|applications]]. The conference has served as a venue for comparing and evaluating different approaches to implementing [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] in business environments.

## Focus Areas

The conference has highlighted NVIDIA's development of [[concepts/secure-ai-agent|secure AI agent]] solutions, including [[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]] and [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], which are designed for enterprise [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]. These platforms represent different approaches to building and deploying [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] that can operate reliably within organizational contexts.

The conference discussions have examined how these [[concepts/autonomous-agent-system|AI agent frameworks]] [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] in terms of functionality, [[concepts/security|security]] features, and suitability for various enterprise [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. Topics have included the technical [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of different agent architectures and their potential applications across business operations.
