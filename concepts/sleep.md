# Sleep

Sleep is a fundamental biological process essential to physical health, cognitive function, and emotional well-being. During sleep, the body undergoes critical restorative processes including memory consolidation, hormonal regulation, and immune system maintenance. These physiological functions are necessary for learning, growth, and disease prevention across all age groups.

## Sleep and Adolescent Development

Adolescents require 8-10 hours of sleep per night according to sleep science research, yet many teens experience insufficient sleep due to biological shifts in circadian rhythms. Dr. Laurie Santos highlights sleep as a science-backed behavior supporting teen well-being, emphasizing its role in mitigating stress and improving mental health outcomes. Prioritizing adequate rest helps counteract the natural delay in sleep onset common during puberty, ensuring better academic performance and emotional stability.
