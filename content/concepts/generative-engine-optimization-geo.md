# Generative Engine Optimization Geo

**[[concepts/ai-overviews|Generative Engine Optimization]]** ([[concepts/geo|GEO]]) is a digital marketing and content strategy designed to help websites perform well in AI-powered search engines and [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs), which differ fundamentally from traditional keyword-based search algorithms. As [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] systems like [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s AI Overview and [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] increasingly serve as primary interfaces for information discovery, GEO addresses the challenge of making content discoverable and useful to AI systems that synthesize information into natural language [[concepts/responses|responses]] rather than ranking discrete web pages.

## Core Principles

GEO strategies typically focus on creating content that AI systems can easily extract, understand, and incorporate into generated answers. This includes structuring information clearly, providing authoritative sourcing, and presenting factual content in formats that LLMs can reliably process. Unlike [[concepts/traditional-seo|traditional SEO]], which optimizes for algorithmic ranking factors and click-through rates, GEO prioritizes being selected as source material for AI-generated responses. Organizations implementing GEO often emphasize topical expertise, comprehensive coverage of subjects, and transparent attribution of information.

## Business Implications

As search behavior gradually shifts toward [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] interfaces, GEO has become relevant for publishers, e-commerce sites, and information providers seeking visibility in these emerging discovery channels. The strategy represents a recognition that search engine optimization itself is evolving, requiring businesses to adapt their content strategies to remain discoverable as the mechanisms through which users find information continue to change.
