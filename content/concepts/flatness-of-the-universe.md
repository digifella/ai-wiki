# Flatness of the Universe

The geometric state of the universe where the total density parameter $\Omega$ is equal to 1, resulting in Euclidean (flat) space.

### Fundamental Concepts
- **Geometry**: Governed by the Friedmann-Lemaître-Robertson-Walker (FLRW) metric within the framework of General Relativity.
- **Inflation**: The Inflationary Universe [[concepts/theory|theory]] posits that a period of exponential expansion drove the universe toward flatness, resolving the "flatness problem."
- **Density**: Determined by the relationship between the actual energy density and the critical density required to maintain a flat geometry.

### Current Research & Tensions
- [[concepts/universe-curvature-tension|Universe Curvature Tension]]: [[entities/planck|Planck]] Data Challenges Flatness, Impacts Inflation Theory
- **Curvature Tension**: An emerging, significant unresolved problem in [[concepts/cosmology]] regarding the universe's true geometry.
- **Data Discrepancies**: Recent analysis of [[concepts/planck-satellite-data|Planck Mission]] data challenges the long-standing assumption of a flat universe.
- **Theoretical Impact**: These challenges to flatness may necessitate significant revisions to the standard Inflationary Universe model.
