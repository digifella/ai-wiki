# Meat Juiciness

Meat juiciness refers to the perceived moisture and tenderness of cooked meat, which is influenced by how well the meat retains its natural liquids during cooking. This quality is important for both palatability and the overall eating experience, as juicy meat is generally perceived as more flavorful and tender than dry meat.

## Baking Soda and Ground Meat

Baking soda is an effective ingredient for improving the juiciness and [[concepts/texture|texture]] of ground meat. When applied to ground meat before cooking, baking soda raises the pH of the meat's surface, which allows it to retain more moisture during the cooking process. This chemical change also promotes better browning through the [[concepts/maillard-reaction|Maillard reaction]], creating a more flavorful crust while keeping the interior moist. The effect is particularly noticeable in ground meat preparations, where the increased surface area allows the baking soda to work more efficiently.

## Other Techniques for Poultry

Beyond baking soda, juiciness in meat like chicken can be enhanced through several [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]]. These include using marinades to add moisture and flavor, selecting appropriate cuts that naturally retain more liquid, and employing proper [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking techniques]] that avoid overcooking. Temperature control and resting meat after cooking are also important factors in preserving juiciness.
