# Deep Research Agent

The [[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] [[entities/agent|Agent]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], configurable agent built on the [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph framework]] using [[entities/langchain|LangChain]]. It is designed to perform automated research tasks by leveraging language [[concepts/models|models]] and structured workflows. The agent can be customized to suit different research requirements and integrates with LangChain's broader ecosystem of tools and components.

## Architecture and Framework

The agent is constructed using [[concepts/langgraph|LangGraph]], which provides a graph-based approach to orchestrating multi-step [[concepts/multi-agent-workflows|agent workflows]]. This [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] allows for [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] chains where the agent can decompose research tasks, execute searches or retrievals, and synthesize findings iteratively. LangChain [[concepts/integration|integration]] enables the agent to connect with various data sources, language models, and [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]].

## Functionality

The Deep Research Agent is capable of conducting systematic research by breaking down complex queries into manageable subtasks, gathering information from multiple sources, and refining results through iterative processing. Its configurable [[entities/nature|nature]] means users can adjust [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], define [[concepts/custom-tools|custom tools]], and tailor the research process to specific domains or [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
