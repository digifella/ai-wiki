# Gtc Conference

The GTC Conference is an annual technology event organized by NVIDIA that convenes industry professionals, researchers, and developers to examine advancements in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computing infrastructure. The gathering typically features keynote presentations, technical workshops, and panel discussions designed to address the needs of both enterprise and research computing environments.

In recent years, the conference has placed increasing emphasis on the development and deployment of AI agents within enterprise applications. Recent iterations have highlighted NVIDIA's NemoClaw and OpenClaw as key examples of secure AI agent frameworks intended for business use. These sessions focus on how such tools can be integrated into existing enterprise workflows to enhance security and operational efficiency.

The event serves as a primary platform for NVIDIA to showcase its latest hardware and software innovations, particularly those related to accelerated computing and generative AI. Attendees engage with technical content that bridges the gap between theoretical research and practical implementation in large-scale computing environments.
