# Nordvpn

NordVPN is a [[concepts/vpn|virtual private network]] (VPN) service provider that offers encrypted internet connections to its users. The service allows individuals to browse the web with increased [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] by routing their traffic through NordVPN's servers, [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] their IP address and encrypting their data.

## Dedicated VPN Service

NordVPN operates a dedicated VPN infrastructure designed to provide [[concepts/secure|secure]] connections across multiple server locations. The service is available as a subscription offering, with users able to connect through various global server endpoints to encrypt their internet activity.
