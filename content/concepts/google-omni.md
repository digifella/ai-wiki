# Google Omni

**[[concepts/google-search|Google]] Omni** is a unified [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] developed by Google, designed to process and generate content across [[concepts/text|text]], image, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and video modalities within a single model framework. It represents a shift from specialized unimodal [[concepts/models|models]] to a cohesive system capable of cross-modal understanding and generation.

## Core Capabilities

- **Unified Architecture**: Consolidates multiple [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into one model, reducing latency and context switching between different [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]].
- **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Understanding]]**: Simultaneously analyzes text, visual, and auditory inputs to derive contextual meaning.
- **Generative [[concepts/output|Output]]**: Capable of producing high-fidelity video, audio, and text [[concepts/responses|responses]] based on mixed-mode prompts.

## "Nanobanana" Video AI Module

Recent developments have highlighted specific capabilities within the Omni framework, particularly regarding [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]], internally or colloquially referred to as "Nanobanana."

- **Reference Analysis**: A detailed review of the "Nanobanana" capabilities was conducted by [[entities/theoretically-media|Theoretically Media]], providing insights beyond official keynote presentations. See: [[lab-notes/2026-05-21-Google-Omni-Reviewing-the-Nanobanana-Multimodal-Video-AI|Google Omni: Reviewing the "Nanobanana" Multimodal Video AI Capabilities]]
- **Key Findings from Review**:
    - **Unified Video Generation**: The model treats video generation as a native [[concepts/modality|modality]] rather than a post-processing step, ensuring higher coherence in motion and temporal [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
    - **Early Access [[concepts/testing|Testing]]**: Independent testing revealed capabilities that exceed initial public demonstrations, particularly in handling complex scene transitions and multi-character interactions.
    - **[[concepts/contextual-awareness|Contextual Awareness]]**: The "Nanobanana" component demonstrates improved ability to adhere to long-form narrative prompts without drifting from the original intent.
