# Clinical Informatics

**[[concepts/medical-informatics|Clinical informatics]]** is the interdisciplinary field that uses information technology, biomedical knowledge, and cognitive [[concepts/science|science]] to improve [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]], [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]], and the efficiency of clinical workflows. It sits at the intersection of Clinical Informatics, [[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] IT, [[entities/national-academies|and Medicine]].

## Core Domains
- **[[concepts/data-management|Data Management]]**: Structuring and managing patient [[concepts/health|health]] records (EHR) for [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and interoperability.
- **Decision Support**: [[concepts/algorithms|Algorithms]] and tools that assist clinicians in diagnosis and treatment planning (CDSS).
- **[[concepts/user-interface|User Interface]] Design**: [[concepts/workflow-optimization|Optimizing workflows]] to reduce [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] and prevent errors.

## Barriers to Implementation
The successful adoption of clinical informatics tools is often hindered by human factors rather than technical limitations. Key issues include:
- **Digital Literacy**: Variability in [[entities/employees|staff]] [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] with technology.
- **Workflow Integration**: Poor alignment between [[concepts/software-engineering|software design]] and clinical routines.
- **Training Deficits**: Lack of ongoing education for new systems.

## Recent Research & Integrative Reviews
Recent literature highlights the critical role of nursing competency in digital [[concepts/adoption|technology adoption]]. A significant [[concepts/integrative-review|integrative review]] identifies specific issues affecting nurses' capability to utilize digital tools at work, emphasizing the need for targeted training and systemic support [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Journal-of-Clinical-Nursing---2020---Brown---Issues-affe|Journal of Clinical Nursing - 2020 - Brown - Issues affecting nurses  capability to use digital technology at work  An]].

### Key Findings from Brown et al. (2020)
- **[[concepts/skill-gaps|Capability Gaps]]**: Nurses often face challenges in translating technical [[concepts/skills|skills]] into effective [[entities/clinical-practice|clinical practice]].
- **Systemic Barriers**: Institutional support and adequate time for training are frequently insufficient.
- **Literacy Dimensions**: Digital literacy encompasses not just tool usage, but also [[concepts/scientific-skepticism|critical evaluation]] of [[concepts/digital-information|digital information]] sources.
