# Dreamdojo AI

Dreamdojo AI is an artificial intelligence system developed by NVIDIA designed to address the simulation-to-reality gap in robotic task execution. This gap represents a fundamental challenge in robotics: AI models trained in simulated environments often fail to perform reliably when deployed on physical robots. Dreamdojo AI aims to bridge this divide by enabling robots to execute complex tasks more effectively in real-world conditions despite being trained primarily in simulation.

## Technical Approach

The system works by training AI models in simulated environments while incorporating techniques to improve their transfer to physical robotics. This addresses the problem that simulated physics, sensor characteristics, and environmental conditions often differ significantly from real-world conditions, causing models to struggle with generalization.

## Applications

Dreamdojo AI is intended to support robotic automation across various domains where robots need to perform intricate manipulation and execution tasks. By improving the reliability of sim-to-real transfer, the technology aims to reduce the need for extensive real-world training data and physical prototyping during robot development.
