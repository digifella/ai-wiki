# Mit

MIT is an abbreviation most commonly referring to the Massachusetts Institute of Technology, a research university located in Cambridge, Massachusetts, founded in 1861. The institution is internationally recognized for its programs in engineering, science, technology, and management, and has played a significant role in academic research and technological innovation.

## MIT Sloan Management Review

MIT Sloan Management Review is a peer-reviewed publication associated with the MIT Sloan School of Management. The journal publishes research and articles on business strategy, management practice, and organizational innovation, drawing on both academic research and practitioner insights. It serves as a channel for disseminating knowledge to business leaders, managers, and academics interested in contemporary management challenges and solutions.
