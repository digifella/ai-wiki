# [[entities/rob-the-ai-guy|Rob the ai guy]]

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rY1dws9cOXk>
The video [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] five brand new changes and features in [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]] designed to boost productivity and blow your mind.
Here's a breakdown:

1. **Gemini in [[entities/google-meet|Google Meet]]:**
	You can now pull Gemini directly into your Google Meet calls. It can **catch you up** on what you missed, **summarize discussions**, and **list key takeaways**. Crucially, Gemini can **access your [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]]** (sheets, documents, etc.) and search through them simultaneously during the meeting, answering questions based on your files and even relevant emails.
	
2. **Advanced Image and [[concepts/video-generation|Video Generation]] ([[entities/imagen-4-ultra|Imagen 4 Ultra]]):**
	[[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]] now features **Imagen 4 Ultra (Preview)**, their most up-to-date [[concepts/image-generation-model|image generation model]]. This model produces **higher-quality images and videos**. It offers **better [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in changing text** within images compared to other [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]. Users can also **control the aspect ratio** of generated images.
	
3. **Automating Tasks with [[concepts/scheduled-actions|Scheduled Actions]] & Expanded App [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:**
	Gemini can now **automate tasks for you**. By going into `gemini.google.com` and navigating to "Settings & Help" -> "Scheduled actions," you can set up [[concepts/recurring-tasks|recurring tasks]]. For example, you can schedule Gemini to "Send me a daily [[concepts/summary|summary]] of my calendar, to-dos and important unread emails" at a specific time (daily, weekly, or monthly). This is possible because Gemini is deeply integrated with various Google apps (accessible via "Settings & Help" -> "Apps"), including **Gmail, [[entities/google-calendar|Google Calendar]], [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]], Google Drive, Google Keep, Google Tasks, Google Flights, Google Hotels, [[entities/google-maps|Google Maps]], [[entities/youtube|YouTube]], YouTube Music, and OpenStax**. This allows Gemini to interact with and automate actions across your entire [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]]. Your custom-built "Gems" (personalized AI agents/projects) are also visible and usable across your Google Workspace.
	
4. **Gemini 2.5 Flash Model:**
	Google has released a new, significantly **faster model called 2.5 Flash**. It's available both directly in `gemini.google.com` and within Google AI Studio. For most general tasks, **2.5 Flash is quicker** than the 2.5 Pro model and doesn't sacrifice quality. Users can use AI Studio's "Compare mode" to test prompts across different models (e.g., 2.5 Pro vs. 2.5 Flash) to see the difference in speed and output. The presenter suggests using 2.5 Flash for most tasks unless [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]], math, or code is required, for which 2.5 Pro is still recommended.
	
5. **URL Context Tool for Research:**
	Within Google AI Studio, a new **"URL context tool"** allows Gemini to **fetch and analyze information directly from specific web links** provided in your prompt. This enables Gemini to generate [[concepts/responses|responses]], summaries, or comparison tables based _only_ on the information from those URLs, rather than performing its own broad web search. Alternatively, the "Grounding with [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]]" option can be toggled on to allow Gemini to perform its own web searches for information.
