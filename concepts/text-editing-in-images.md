# Text Editing in Images

The process of modifying embedded text within static image formats or [[concepts/ai-generated-infographics]] to rectify spelling errors, typos, or factual inaccuracies.

## Techniques & Toolsets
- **[[entities/canva]] '[[concepts/canva-grab-text-feature|Grab Text]]'**: A feature used to extract and enable direct editing of text layers within a graphic.
- **[[entities/adobe-acrobat|Adobe Acrobat]]**: A method for manipulating text within PDF-based visual assets.

## Key Applications
- **[[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-generated content]] Refinement**: Addressing common inaccuracies and "hallucinated" text artifacts produced by models such as [[entities/gemini]].
- **[[concepts/infographic]] Correction**: [[concepts/video-editing|Post-production]] editing of complex visual data presentations to ensure professional accuracy.
