# Shadow AI

**Definition**: Unauthorized or undiscovered [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] projects operating within corporate environments without formal approval or oversight, often leveraging generative [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] for business tasks.

**Risks**:
- [[concepts/security|Security]] vulnerabilities from [[concepts/unauthorized-ai-deployments|unsanctioned AI]] tool usage
- [[concepts/data-leakage|Data leakage]] and [[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|intellectual property]] [[concepts/exposure|exposure]]
- [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]] failures due to unmonitored AI deployment
- Inconsistent AI model quality and [[concepts/ethics|ethics]]
- Long-term [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]] threats as current [[concepts/encryption-standards|encryption standards]] become vulnerable to [[concepts/quantum-computing|quantum computing]] attacks

**Sector-Specific Implications**:
- **[[concepts/health|Healthcare]]**: Rapid, unsanctioned integration of AI and [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] in [[entities/clinical-practice|clinical practice]] outpaces regulatory frameworks, creating a gap between diagnostic benefits and systemic oversight [[lab-notes/2026-07-17-Shadow-AI-Rapid-Integration-in-Healthcare-Diagnostic-Ben|Shadow AI: Rapid Integration in Healthcare, Diagnostic Benefits, and System Challenges]].
