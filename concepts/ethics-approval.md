# Ethics Approval

**[[concepts/ethics|Ethics]] approval** is the formal review and [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] granted by an Institutional Review Board (IRB) or Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) to ensure that research involving humans or sensitive data adheres to ethical standards, legal requirements, and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] regulations.

## Core Principles

- **Informed Consent**: Ensuring participants understand risks and benefits.
- **Privacy & Confidentiality**: Protecting identifiable information, especially in [[concepts/health|Health]] Data contexts.
- **Risk-Benefit Analysis**: Weighing potential harms against societal or scientific gains.
- **Justice**: [[entities/meta-fair-lab|Fair]] selection of subjects and equitable distribution of benefits.

## Contexts and Applications

### Research Involving Humans
Traditional biomedical and social [[concepts/science|science]] research requires rigorous assessment of protocol design, participant recruitment, and data handling.

### Secondary Use of Health Data
The reuse of existing clinical or administrative data for research or [[concepts/population-health|public health]] purposes presents unique ethical challenges regarding consent and privacy.

- **[[entities/my-health-record|My Health Record]] (Australia)**: The national [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] system allows for [[concepts/secondary-use|secondary use]] under strict [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks.
- **Benefits**: Enhances [[concepts/public-health]] surveillance, facilitates [[concepts/healthcare-improvement]] strategies, and accelerates medical research without new patient burden.
- **Governance**: Balances [[concepts/ai-security]] with [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]; requires specific ethics approval pathways that may differ from primary research consent models.
- **Key Reference**: See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-ADHA---How-secondary-use-of-My-Health-Record-data-can-im|ADHA - How secondary use of My Health Record data can improve health outcomes]] for analysis on how secondary use of [[concepts/my-health-record-data|My Health Record data]] drives health outcomes in Australia.

## Regulatory Frameworks

- **Belmont Report**: Foundational US guidelines emphasizing [[concepts/respect|respect]] for persons, beneficence, and justice.
- **Declaration of Helsinki**: International [[concepts/core-principles-of-ethics|ethical principles]] for medical research.
- **National Health and Medical Research Council (NHMRC)**: Australian guidelines for research involving humans, including specific provisions for Health Data reuse.
- **GDPR/HIPAA**: Regional regulations impacting data privacy and ethical [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] in research.

## Workflow

1. **Protocol Development**: Define research question, methodology, and data sources.
2. **[[concepts/risk-assessment|Risk Assessment]]**: Identify potential ethical breaches or privacy violations.
3. **Submission**: Submit to HREC/IRB with documentation on consent processes (or waivers for de-identified secondary data).
4. **Review**: Committee evaluates compliance with ethical standards and local laws.
5. **Approval/Modification**: Grant of approval with conditions or request for revisions.
6. **Monitoring**: Ongoing oversight for ongoing studies.
