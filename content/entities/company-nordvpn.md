# Company Nordvpn

[[concepts/nordvpn|Nordvpn]] is a [[concepts/vpn|virtual private network]] (VPN) service provider that offers VPN solutions to individual users and organizations. The company develops and maintains VPN infrastructure designed to encrypt user internet traffic and mask IP addresses.

## Service Offerings

[[entities/nordvpn|Nordvpn]] provides [[concepts/dedicated-vpn|dedicated VPN]] services with a focus on [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and [[concepts/security|security]]. The platform operates servers across multiple countries, allowing users to route their internet connections through various geographic locations. The service includes features such as encryption protocols, [[concepts/dns|DNS]] leak protection, and kill switch functionality.

## Operations

The company maintains a network of VPN servers and related infrastructure to support its user base. Like other VPN providers, Nordvpn's business model centers on subscription-based access to its VPN services, with users paying recurring fees for [[concepts/connection|connection]] privileges.
