# Active Tabs

Active Tabs is an educational video by Kole Jain that introduces foundational principles of user interface and user experience (UI/UX) design. The content is structured to serve beginners seeking foundational knowledge while remaining accessible as a reference for designers with existing experience. Rather than exploring specialized or advanced techniques, the video emphasizes practical, universally-applicable design principles that form the basis of effective digital design.

## Core Design Principles

The video covers several fundamental UI/UX concepts essential to digital design. Affordances are explained as the ways design elements communicate their function and usability to users. Hierarchy refers to the visual organization of information to guide user attention and establish relationships between elements. The video also addresses grids as structural tools for creating organized, coherent layouts, and typography as a means of establishing visual communication and readability. These principles work together to create interfaces that are both functional and intuitive.

## Educational Approach

The video takes a practical approach to design education, focusing on concepts that apply across different design contexts and mediums. By concentrating on foundational principles rather than tool-specific techniques, the content remains relevant regardless of the design software or platform being used. This makes Active Tabs a useful resource for designers beginning their practice or those seeking to reinforce their understanding of core design concepts.
