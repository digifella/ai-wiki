# Orion

Orion is a crewed spacecraft system developed by NASA for the Artemis program, designed to transport astronauts to the Moon and facilitate deep space exploration missions. The spacecraft consists of three primary components: a crew module that accommodates up to six astronauts, a service module that provides propulsion and life support systems, and a launch abort system engineered to ensure crew safety during launch and ascent phases.

## Design and Capabilities

The Orion crew module is a cone-shaped capsule capable of operating beyond low Earth orbit, distinguishing it from earlier spacecraft designs. It is equipped with advanced avionics, navigation systems, and life support infrastructure to sustain crews during multi-day missions. The service module, derived from European Space Agency technology, carries consumables and propellant necessary for orbital maneuvering and return trajectories.

## Artemis II Mission

Orion is central to the Artemis II lunar mission, which represents a crewed test flight of the integrated spacecraft system. The mission provides operational data on crew systems performance, in-flight operations procedures, and spacecraft behavior during lunar transit and return, establishing a foundation for subsequent crewed lunar landing missions under the Artemis program.
