# Demetrious Cocktail Mixing

Demetrious Cocktail Mixing is a bartending operation based in Heraklion, founded by Helen, a former pharmacist who transitioned into mixology. The venture represents a career shift from pharmaceutical work to craft cocktail [[concepts/preparation|preparation]], blending technical knowledge with creative beverage creation.

## Background

Helen's background in pharmacy informed her approach to cocktail mixing, as both fields involve precise measurement, understanding of chemical compounds, and knowledge of how different ingredients interact. This foundation proved applicable to the craft of bartending, where ingredient combinations and proportions are similarly critical.
