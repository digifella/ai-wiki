# Social Media Carousels

Social media carousels are a multi-slide content format available on major platforms including Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok. Users create carousels by uploading a series of images or slides that viewers navigate sequentially. Each carousel typically contains between 2 and 10 slides, though specific platform limits vary. The format allows creators to present multiple related images or pieces of information within a single post.

## Navigation and Platform Variations

On mobile devices, viewers advance through carousel slides by swiping horizontally, while desktop users often click arrows or scroll to move between frames. Platform implementations differ slightly; for instance, Instagram allows up to 10 images or videos per carousel, while LinkedIn supports up to 10 images in a document post. TikTok introduced carousels to allow users to share multiple photos in a single video-style feed entry, expanding the format beyond traditional static image sharing.

## Content Strategy and Utility

Creators utilize carousels to break down complex topics, tell sequential stories, or provide step-by-step tutorials that require more space than a single image permits. The format encourages higher engagement rates because users must interact with the post to view the full content, increasing dwell time. It is particularly effective for educational content, product showcases, and visual narratives where context is built progressively across frames.
