# LHAASO

[[concepts/lhaaso|LHAASO]] (Large High Altitude Air Shower Observatory) is an observatory located in the Haizi Mountain on the Tibetan Plateau. It aims to study high-energy [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] and their sources.

## Key Facts
- **Location:** Haizi Mountain, Tibetan Plateau
- **[[concepts/purpose|Purpose]]:** To detect and analyze ultra-high energy cosmic [[concepts/rays|rays]].
- **Operational Since:** 2019
