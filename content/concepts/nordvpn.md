# Nordvpn

NordVPN is a commercial [[concepts/vpn|virtual private network]] (VPN) service that provides encrypted internet connectivity and IP address [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] for users. The service operates across a global network of servers and is designed to enhance user [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and security on public and untrusted networks.

## Core Service Offering

NordVPN offers subscription-based VPN access with servers distributed across multiple countries. The service encrypts user traffic between their device and NordVPN's servers, obscuring browsing activity and location from internet service providers and network operators. Users can select which server location to route traffic through, allowing them to appear as though they are accessing the internet from different geographic regions.

## Dedicated VPN Infrastructure

NordVPN includes dedicated VPN infrastructure as part of its service [[concepts/architecture|architecture]]. This specialized infrastructure supports the routing and encryption of user traffic across its network, enabling the core functionality of VPN connectivity for its subscriber base.
