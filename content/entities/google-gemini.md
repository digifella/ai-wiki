# Google Gemini

## Overview
[[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] is a powerful [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tool designed to enhance various aspects of [[concepts/app-creation|software development]] and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]. It offers advanced capabilities in [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]], allowing developers to create sophisticated interactive [[concepts/software|applications]].
