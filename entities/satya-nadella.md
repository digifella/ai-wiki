# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is an Indian-American business executive and the Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft, a position he has held since February 2014. Under his leadership, Microsoft has undergone a significant strategic transformation, shifting focus toward cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and subscription-based services. This repositioning has helped revitalize the company's market position and relevance in emerging technology sectors.

## Career and Background

Nadella joined Microsoft in 1992 and spent over two decades working in various technical and leadership roles before his appointment as CEO. His career at the company included significant responsibilities in the Cloud and Enterprise division, where he oversaw the development of Azure and other cloud services. His promotion to CEO marked a departure from the previous leadership era and introduced a more collaborative corporate culture focused on innovation and digital transformation.

## Leadership Philosophy

Since becoming CEO, Nadella has emphasized cloud-first and mobile-first strategies, transforming Microsoft's business model and product offerings. He has also championed the integration of artificial intelligence technologies across Microsoft's platform and services. His approach has been credited with stabilizing Microsoft's stock performance and expanding the company's influence in enterprise and consumer technology markets.
