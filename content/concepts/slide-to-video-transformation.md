# Slide To Video Transformation

Slide to Video Transformation is a workflow for converting presentations generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video format while removing watermarks using [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s suite of tools. NotebookLM, Google's AI-powered [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] application, can automatically generate [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source documents. However, these [[entities/google-slides|slides]] may include watermarks or other branding elements. This transformation process leverages [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], Google's [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool, to convert the slides into polished video content without such markers.

## Process Overview

The transformation workflow involves exporting slides from NotebookLM and importing them into Google Vids for video conversion. During this process, users can customize the presentation design and styling through [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], Google's [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]], to ensure the final video meets professional [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]. This approach allows creators to maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across their presentation materials while producing video-ready content suitable for various platforms and audiences.

## Design Customization

A key aspect of the transformation is the ability to customize slide design elements before or during the video creation process. By integrating Gemini's capabilities, users can adjust visual styling, [[concepts/typography|typography]], color schemes, and layout to align with specific branding guidelines or aesthetic preferences. This customization step helps ensure that the resulting video maintains a professional appearance while reflecting the creator's intended design direction.
