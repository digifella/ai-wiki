# Moderna

**Moderna, Inc.** is a biotechnology company pioneering the development of messenger RNA (mRNA) therapeutics and vaccines.

## Key Developments & Partnerships

### Moderna-Merck Collaboration
*   **Focus:** Development of personalized mRNA cancer vaccines for [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]] in [[concepts/resected-melanoma|resected melanoma]].
*   **Trial Status:** Positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results announced for the combination of Moderna's mRNA vaccine ([[entities/intismeran-autogene|Intismeran Autogene]]) and [[entities/merck|Merck]]'s Keytruda ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]).
*   **Significance:** Represents a major advancement in personalized cancer immunotherapy, leveraging mRNA technology to target individual tumor mutations.
*   **Related Analysis:** Moderna Merck mRNA Cancer Vaccine and Keytruda for Resected Melanoma Adjuvant Therapy

## Core Technologies
*   **mRNA Platform:** Utilizes lipid nanoparticle (LNP) delivery systems to encode genetic instructions for protein production within host cells.
*   **[[concepts/personalized-medicine|Personalized Medicine]]:** Adapts vaccine formulations to specific patient tumor profiles.
