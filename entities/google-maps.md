# Google Maps

Google Maps is a web-based mapping and navigation service provided by Google. It offers users access to satellite imagery, street maps, 360° panoramic street-level imagery through Street View, real-time traffic conditions, and route planning for various modes of transportation including driving, public transit, walking, and cycling. The service is available through web browsers and mobile applications across multiple platforms including iOS and Android.

## Data Access and APIs

Google Maps provides official data access through its Maps API, which allows developers to embed mapping functionality into their own applications. This suite of APIs enables programmatic interaction with map data, including geocoding, directions, and place details. While the API offers a legitimate and supported method for integrating Google Maps data, it operates under strict usage limits and pricing structures designed to prevent unauthorized bulk extraction.

## Web Scraping and Automation Context

In the context of web scraping and automation, Google Maps is a frequent target for data extraction due to the richness of its geographic and business information. Automated tools often attempt to bypass Google's anti-scraping measures, such as CAPTCHAs and rate limiting, to harvest data at scale. However, scraping Google Maps directly violates its Terms of Service, and Google actively employs technical countermeasures to detect and block non-compliant automated traffic. Developers are generally advised to utilize the official APIs for any programmatic access to ensure compliance and data reliability.
