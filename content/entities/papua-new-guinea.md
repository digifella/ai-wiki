# Papua New Guinea

Papua New Guinea is a country located on the eastern half of the island of New Guinea in Oceania, alongside Indonesia. It is notable in medical and anthropological history as the location where the disease kuru was first identified and studied in the mid-20th century.

## Kuru and the Fore Tribe

Kuru is a fatal neurodegenerative disease that affected the Fore people, an indigenous group in Papua New Guinea's Eastern Highlands. The disease emerged as a significant medical mystery in the 1950s and 1960s, causing progressive dementia, loss of motor control, and death. Research into kuru proved instrumental in establishing the existence of [[concepts/infectious-proteins|prions]]—infectious [[concepts/agents|agents]] composed solely of misfolded protein—and challenged the conventional understanding of infectious disease at the time. This discovery fundamentally altered biomedical science and has implications for understanding the origins and [[entities/nature|nature]] of life itself, as explored in discussions of the [[concepts/protein-world-hypothesis|Protein World Hypothesis]].
