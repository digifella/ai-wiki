# BRDs

Business Requirements Documents (BRDs) formally define business needs, objectives, and constraints for a project or product. They serve as the foundation for development, aligning stakeholders and guiding [[concepts/solution|solution]] [[concepts/design|design]].

## Key Components
- Business objectives
- Functional requirements
- Non-functional requirements
- Constraints
- Success metrics
- Stakeholder analysis
