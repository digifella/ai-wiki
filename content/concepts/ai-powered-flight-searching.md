# AI Powered Flight Searching

AI-powered flight searching uses [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to help identify inexpensive flight options by processing travel preferences against available market data. Rather than manually visiting multiple travel booking websites, users provide structured information through natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]]—including destinations, travel dates, budget constraints, and flexibility [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. The AI then analyzes this input and suggests relevant deals, potentially surfacing options that standard search interfaces might not prominently display.

## How It Works

Users interact with [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] by crafting detailed prompts that specify their travel requirements. Effective prompts typically include origin and destination cities, preferred travel dates or date ranges, budget limits, cabin class preferences, and any flexibility regarding departure times or layover durations. The AI processes this information against its [[concepts/training-data|training data]] and knowledge of flight [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] patterns to generate recommendations, compare options across different [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], and explain trade-offs between price, duration, and convenience.

## Practical Applications

This approach is particularly useful for identifying less obvious routes or connecting flights that may be cheaper than direct options, understanding seasonal price variations, and exploring alternative airports near intended destinations. The conversational [[entities/nature|nature]] of AI interaction allows users to refine their search iteratively—asking follow-up questions, adjusting parameters, or exploring hypothetical scenarios—without needing to navigate multiple separate booking platforms. However, users should verify final prices and availability directly on airline or booking websites, as the AI's information may not reflect real-time inventory or the most current fares.
