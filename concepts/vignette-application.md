# Vignette Application

Vignette application is a post-processing technique that darkens or lightens the edges of a photograph while maintaining a brighter center, thereby directing viewer attention toward the main subject. This effect is commonly used in portrait, landscape, and product photography to create compositional emphasis and add subtle depth. The technique can be applied either during shooting through lens characteristics or, more commonly, during post-processing where it offers greater control and flexibility.

## Implementation in Lightroom Classic

In Lightroom Classic, vignette effects are primarily managed through the Effects panel within the Develop module. Users can adjust the Amount slider to control the intensity of the darkening or lightening, while the Midpoint slider determines the radius of the effect, influencing how far the vignette extends toward the center of the image. The Style dropdown allows for the selection between Paint Overlay, which blends the effect with existing tones, or Color Priority, which preserves the original colors of the vignette area.

Additionally, the post-crop vignette feature enables photographers to apply vignetting specifically to the cropped area of the image, ensuring the effect aligns with the final composition. This method provides precise control over the transition between the focused center and the peripheral edges, allowing for nuanced adjustments that enhance the overall visual impact without altering the core subject matter.
