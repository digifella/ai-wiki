## Siri

**Summary:** Siri is an intelligent [[concepts/personal-assistant|personal assistant]] introduced by Apple as part of their iOS ecosystem. It uses voice recognition and [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]] to interact with users and perform a wide variety of tasks such as setting reminders, making calls, sending messages, and more.

- [[concepts/voice-assistants]]

### Key Features
- Voice Recognition: Siri can understand and respond to spoken [[concepts/commands|commands]].
- Integration: Seamlessly integrates with other Apple products and services for a cohesive [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]].
- Third-party Services: Can connect to various third-party apps and services through API integrations.

### Security Considerations
- [[concepts/ai-security|Data Privacy]]: Ensures user data is stored securely and only on the device or locally, unless explicitly shared with another app or service.
- [[concepts/authentication|Authentication]]: Requires passcode or [[concepts/biometric-authentication|biometric authentication]] for activation in some [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
- Transparency: Offers detailed information about what Siri can access and how it operates.
