# Discussion Tab

The Discussion Tab is a collaborative feature integrated into Microsoft 365 Copilot that enables users to engage in threaded conversations and share feedback directly within documents and insights. Announced in April 2026 via the Microsoft 365 Copilot Connection channel, the feature was demonstrated by Nick Harris, a Senior Copilot Cloud Solution Architect at Microsoft, as part of a broader platform update rollout.

## Functionality

The feature allows users to maintain context-specific dialogue without leaving their current workspace. By embedding conversation threads directly into the interface, it facilitates real-time collaboration and review processes. This integration ensures that discussions remain linked to the specific content or insights being analyzed, reducing the need to switch between applications and helping to preserve the continuity of the workflow.

## Availability

The Discussion Tab was introduced as part of a wider update to the Microsoft 365 Copilot ecosystem. Its deployment was highlighted during a demonstration by Microsoft engineering leadership, signaling its importance in enhancing the platform's collaborative capabilities. The feature is designed to support enterprise users who require structured communication channels within their daily document management and data analysis tasks.
