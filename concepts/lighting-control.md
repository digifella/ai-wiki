# Lighting Control

Lighting Control is an AI image editing technique that uses JSON-structured prompts to achieve precise manipulation of lighting elements in images generated by Google's Gemini model. Rather than relying solely on natural language descriptions, this method encodes detailed lighting specifications in a machine-readable format, allowing creators to define parameters such as light intensity, direction, and color temperature with greater accuracy than conventional text-based prompts alone.

The technique involves formatting lighting instructions as JSON data within the prompt structure, which the model parses to apply specific visual attributes. This approach enables granular control over the mood and realism of the output, addressing the ambiguity often found in natural language requests. By standardizing the input format, users can consistently reproduce desired lighting conditions across multiple generations.

Demonstrated in a renderdrop video, this method highlights the utility of structured data in creative workflows. It serves as a bridge between technical precision and artistic intent, allowing for repeatable results in digital rendering and image synthesis. The technique is particularly relevant for users requiring specific atmospheric effects or consistent lighting setups in their generated content.
