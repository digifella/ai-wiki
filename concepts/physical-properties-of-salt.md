# Physical properties of salt

## Core Properties
- **Chemical [[concepts/writing|Composition]]**: Primarily Sodium Chloride (NaCl).
- **Crystal Structure**: Cubic lattice.
- **Solubility**: High solubility in polar solvents such as water.

## Culinary Properties & Application
- **[[concepts/salt-types|Salt Types]] and [[concepts/density|Density]]**:
    - Variations in [[concepts/salt-types]] significantly influence seasoning efficacy and flavor delivery.
    - [[concepts/film-grain|Grain]] Density and surface area-to-volume ratios affect the rate of dissolution.
- **Application Techniques**:
    - Effective [[concepts/cooking-techniques]] require matching specific salt grain sizes to the food being prepared to ensure consistent seasoning.
