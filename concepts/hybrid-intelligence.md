# Hybrid Intelligence

**Hybrid Intelligence** refers to an architectural paradigm where [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities are distributed across both cloud-based servers and local edge devices. This approach aims to balance the scalability and vast knowledge of cloud models with the [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], latency, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] benefits of [[concepts/local-execution|on-device processing]].

## Core Principles
- **Distributed Processing:** Tasks are dynamically routed between local hardware and [[concepts/cloud-based-computer|cloud infrastructure]] based on complexity, privacy requirements, and connectivity status.
- **Privacy by Design:** Sensitive data processing occurs locally, reducing [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to external servers.
- **[[concepts/space-based-data-centers|Latency Reduction]]:** [[concepts/edge-devices|On-device inference]] enables real-time responses for critical functions without network dependency.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]:** Systems remain functional during network outages by leveraging [[concepts/local-models|local models]].

## Strategic Implementation: Microsoft's Approach
[[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] has recently accelerated its [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of this paradigm through its [[concepts/microsoft-windows|Windows]] and [[entities/surface|Surface]] ecosystem, emphasizing [[concepts/local-ai-processing|local AI processing]] to empower users and reduce cloud reliance.

### Key Developments
- **[[concepts/strategic-pivot|Strategic Shift]]:** Microsoft is prioritizing "Hybrid Intelligence" to deliver powerful AI capabilities directly on devices Microsoft's [[concepts/local-ai-model|Local AI]] Push: Hybrid Intelligence, HydraFusion, and [[concepts/copilot-chat|Copilot]]'s On-Device Capabilities.
- **On-Device Copilot:** Enhancements to [[entities/copilot]] include significant on-device capabilities, allowing for faster, more private interactions without constant [[concepts/cloud-integration|cloud connectivity]].
- **HydraFusion:** Introduction of HydraFusion technology to optimize the integration of local and cloud resources for seamless performance.
- **Hardware Integration:** Tight coupling of AI features with new Windows and Surface hardware to maximize [[concepts/local-processing|local processing]] efficiency.
