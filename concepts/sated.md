# SATED

**SATED** ([[concepts/safe-appliance-tidy-efficient-and-delicious|Safe Appliance Tidy Efficient and Delicious]]) is a specialized cooking device designed for zero-[[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] environments, specifically the International [[concepts/space-station|Space Station]] (ISS). Developed by engineer **[[entities/jim-sears|Jim Sears]]**, it addresses the challenges of food [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] in microgravity.

## Overview
- **Purpose**: Revolutionize [[concepts/water-bath-method|food preparation]] for astronauts, moving beyond standard rehydrated meals to more complex cooking.
- **Key Features**:
  - Designed for safe operation in zero-g conditions.
  - Focuses on tidiness and efficiency in resource-constrained environments.
  - Enables "delicious" outcomes via controlled heating [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] suitable for space.
