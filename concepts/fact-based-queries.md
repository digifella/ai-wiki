# Fact Based Queries

Fact-based queries constitute a fundamental capability of the [[concepts/nemotron-family|Nemotron]] [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]], designed to address direct user inquiries across diverse subject matter. This functionality enables the system to retrieve, process, and communicate specific factual information rather than engaging in open-ended conversational exchanges. The primary [[concepts/purpose|objective]] is to deliver accurate and verifiable data that directly satisfies the user's immediate informational needs.

The processing workflow begins with the analysis of the user's input to identify the core information request. Nemotron then retrieves relevant data from its [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] and synthesizes a response that prioritizes [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]]. By focusing on [[concepts/factual-accuracy|factual accuracy]], the system distinguishes itself from general [[concepts/conversational-ai|conversational agents]], ensuring that outputs are grounded in verifiable evidence rather than speculative or creative generation.
