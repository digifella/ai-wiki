# Blacks Adjustment

The [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] adjustment is a tonal control slider in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that modifies the darkest values in an image during post-processing. Located in the [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]]'s [[concepts/tone|Tone]] Curve panel, this slider specifically targets the [[concepts/black-point-adjustment|black point]]—the deepest shadow areas of a photograph. Unlike the [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]] slider, which affects a broader range of mid-dark tones, the Blacks adjustment operates on a narrower tonal range at the extreme end of the histogram, making it a precise tool for controlling only the deepest shadows.

## Adjustment Direction and Effects

Moving the Blacks slider to the left darkens the deepest shadows, increasing [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] and defining the black point. This action can make an image appear more vibrant by removing gray cast from shadows, but excessive adjustment may cause clipping, where detail is lost in the darkest areas. Conversely, moving the slider to the right lifts the black point, reducing contrast and creating a faded or matte look. This technique is often used to simulate film characteristics or to recover detail in overexposed highlights by balancing the overall tonal curve.

## Application in Landscape Processing

In [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], the Blacks adjustment is frequently used to establish a solid foundation for the tonal range. By ensuring the histogram touches the left edge without significant clipping, photographers can prevent images from appearing flat or washed out. It is particularly effective for enhancing depth in scenes with strong foreground elements or dark silhouettes. Careful monitoring of the histogram and highlight warnings is recommended to maintain detail [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] while achieving the desired aesthetic impact.
