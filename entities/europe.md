# Europe

Europe is a continent comprising numerous countries across Western, Central, Eastern, and Northern regions. The continent has historically developed distinct regulatory frameworks for emerging technologies, with individual nations often serving as testing grounds for EU-wide policy adoption. As of 2026, Europe has begun formal approval processes for advanced autonomous driving systems, with individual member states establishing precedents that inform broader European Union regulatory direction.

## Autonomous Vehicle Regulation

European regulatory bodies have undertaken systematic evaluations of advanced driver-assistance systems, leading to the approval of Tesla's Full Self-Driving technology. The Netherlands has established a significant precedent in this domain by permitting the deployment of these systems, a move that is expected to influence the regulatory roadmap for the wider European Union. This development marks a shift toward standardized acceptance of high-level automation across member states.
