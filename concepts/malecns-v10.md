# MaleCNS v1.0

**[[entities/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]]** is the complete [[concepts/neural-wiring-diagram|connectome]] of a male fruit fly (*Drosophila melanogaster*) brain and nerve cord, open-sourced by [[entities/google|Google]] and the [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus. It represents a full "wiring diagram" of the insect's central nervous system, providing a foundational dataset for understanding neural circuitry and behavior.

## Key Details
- **Scope**: Complete mapping of the male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord.
- **Contributors**: Google and Janelia Research Campus.
- **Significance**: First open-source, whole-brain connectome for a fruit fly, enabling large-scale computational neuroscience and behavioral modeling.
- **Status**: Open-source dataset available for public research.
