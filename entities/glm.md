# Glm

GLM (General Language Model) is an open-source large language model developed by researchers at Tsinghua University. It was designed to provide an accessible alternative to proprietary language models, offering advanced natural language processing capabilities for research, commercial, and community applications. By releasing its technology under an open license, the project aims to democratize access to state-of-the-art language technology and foster broader participation in the development and application of large language models.

## Architecture and Training

GLM distinguishes itself through a unique pre-training framework that combines masked language modeling with a generative mask mechanism. This approach allows the model to capture both contextual understanding and generative capabilities simultaneously. The training process involves a bidirectional context encoding for the masked tokens, enabling the model to learn rich semantic representations from large-scale unlabeled text corpora.

## Development and Impact

The GLM series has evolved through multiple iterations, with subsequent versions expanding parameter scales and improving performance on various natural language understanding and generation benchmarks. The open-source nature of the project has encouraged widespread adoption in the academic and developer communities, facilitating further research into efficient training methods and specialized applications for Chinese and multilingual contexts.
