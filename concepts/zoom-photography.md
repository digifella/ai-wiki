# Zoom Photography

Zoom photography is the technique of using optical or digital zoom to capture subjects at a distance, magnifying the subject within the frame without physically moving closer. This approach is particularly valuable in [[concepts/wildlife-photography]], sports photography, and [[concepts/portrait-photography]], where proximity to the subject may be impractical, unsafe, or unwanted.

## Optical Versus Digital Zoom

[[concepts/optical-zoom|Optical zoom]] uses the camera's lens elements to magnify the subject, preserving image quality and detail. Digital zoom, by contrast, crops and enlarges the existing image through software processing, which typically results in a loss of [[concepts/solution|resolution]] and increased noise. While optical zoom maintains the integrity of the original sensor data, digital zoom relies on [[concepts/algorithms|computational algorithms]] to simulate magnification, making it a less desirable option for high-fidelity imaging.
