# Europe

Europe is a continent comprising numerous countries across Western, Central, Eastern, and Northern regions. As of 2026, a significant regulatory development has occurred regarding autonomous vehicle technology, with the approval of Tesla's [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving]] (FSD) system marking a notable milestone in the continent's approach to advanced driver assistance systems.

## Autonomous Vehicle Regulation

In April 2026, Tesla's Full Self-Driving technology received approval for use in Europe, with the Netherlands establishing a regulatory precedent for broader [[entities/eu|European Union]] implementation. This approval represents an important step in the EU's framework for evaluating and certifying autonomous driving capabilities across member states.
