# SANS Institute

The SANS Institute is a leading cybersecurity training and education organization that specializes in developing technical documentation and training materials focused on information security and defensive computing practices. Founded in 1989, SANS has established itself as a prominent provider of hands-on security training courses and certifications for information technology professionals and security practitioners.

The organization produces comprehensive educational resources addressing practical approaches to securing computer systems, networks, and infrastructure. SANS is particularly known for developing security hardening guidelines and best practices documentation, including materials relevant to critical infrastructure protection such as military and government installations. Their work emphasizes implementable security measures grounded in real-world defensive strategies.

## Educational Focus

SANS offers instructor-led and online training courses covering topics including penetration testing, network security, incident handling, and system administration security. The Institute also administers professional certification programs recognized across the cybersecurity industry. Additionally, SANS publishes the SANS Top 25 Software Errors and maintains various open-source security tools and resources made available to the broader security community.
