## Optimizing Codex AI: Advanced Features, Model Selection, and Productivity Tips
**Clip title:** You aren't using [[concepts/codex|Codex]] like me...
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=etduwo9Lu3M

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive guide to maximizing [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and ensuring safety when using [[concepts/codex|Codex]], an [[entities/ai-coding-assistant|AI coding assistant]], particularly focusing on its [[concepts/advanced-features|advanced features]] after the [[entities/chatgpt-56|GPT 5.6]] update. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], with over a thousand hours of [[concepts/experience|experience]] with the tool, aims to share lesser-known tips and tricks for both new and experienced users to enhance their AI-powered [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]]. The main topics covered range from optimizing AI model selection to advanced automation, integration with [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]], and critical safety measures.

A significant portion of the video is dedicated to understanding and selecting the right AI model and [[concepts/effort-levels|effort levels]]. Codex offers various [[entities/chatgpt-56|GPT 5.6]] models (Sol, [[entities/terra|Terra]], [[entities/luna|Luna]]) and [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] efforts ([[concepts/light|Light]] to Ultra). The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] advises using the "Sol" model with "High" [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] effort for the most challenging problems, while "Luna" with "High" or "Extra High" effort is recommended for easier tasks due to its better cost-to-performance ratio. Users are cautioned against using the "Fast" [[concepts/speed|speed]] setting unless absolutely necessary, as it is 2.5 times more expensive for only a 1.5x [[concepts/speed|speed]] increase. Furthermore, the video introduces advanced concepts like "Thread Handoffs," which allow an initial prompt to automatically spin up a new, specialized thread within Codex using a different model and thinking effort to handle specific tasks, like deployment, allowing for more complex and delegated workflows.

The video also highlights how to extend Codex's capabilities through [[concepts/plugins|plugins]], its integrated browser, and custom [[concepts/skills|skills]]. The Zapier Multi-[[concepts/connection|Connection]] Platform (MCP) is presented as a powerful plugin that connects Codex to over 9,000 [[concepts/third-party-applications|external applications]], enabling seamless automation across various platforms like Gmail, Trello, and [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Docs. The built-in browser is lauded as an "incredible" feature, not just for testing but for performing general web tasks, with the ability to import cookies and passwords for a fully integrated experience. Additionally, the speaker emphasizes the underappreciated value of "Skills" (also known as "[[concepts/loops|Loops]]" or "Goals"), which allow users to define overarching objectives for the AI, enabling it to continuously work towards a [[concepts/solution|solution]] for hours or even days, iteratively refining its approach without constant human [[concepts/prompting|prompting]]. Resources like a "[[concepts/loop|Loop]] Library" are provided to help users explore and implement effective automation routines.

Finally, the video addresses crucial [[concepts/security|security]] and safety considerations, especially given a recent incident where an AI model accidentally deleted user files. The speaker introduces "[[concepts/hooks|Hooks]]" – custom rules that run before a command executes – to prevent potentially destructive actions like deleting the root filesystem or entire home directories. Users can configure these hooks to act as safeguards, ensuring that even if the AI generates unsafe code, it won't be executed without intervention. Another vital safety feature is setting "Approvals" to "Approve for me," which means the AI model itself determines if an action is potentially unsafe and prompts the user for approval before proceeding, striking a balance between automation and control. These safety measures, along with the ability to remotely control Codex from a mobile device, offer users robust tools for efficient and [[concepts/secure|secure]] [[concepts/application-development-automation|AI-driven development]].
