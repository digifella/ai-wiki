# Scishow Channel

[[entities/scishow|SciShow]] is an educational [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel dedicated to presenting [[concepts/science|science]] content to a general audience. The channel produces videos covering a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of scientific topics, combining clear [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] with engaging presentation styles to [[entities/make|make]] complex subjects accessible to viewers of all backgrounds.

## Content and Coverage

The channel's content spans multiple areas of scientific research and discovery. Notable episodes include coverage of [[concepts/gene-editing|gene editing]] technologies and their development, with particular [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to breakthrough research in the field. The channel has produced field trip content visiting major research institutions, including visits to scientists [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] leading research centers studying cutting-edge genetic technologies.

## Notable Features

One significant episode featured a field trip to the [[entities/broad-institute|Broad Institute]], where the channel documented research into gene editing and interviewed prominent researchers in the field. This type of on-location content allows viewers to see scientific research in its actual institutional context and learn directly from the scientists conducting the work.
