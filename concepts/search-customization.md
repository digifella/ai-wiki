# Search Customization

Search customization involves tailoring the parameters and settings of AI-powered search engines to align with specific user requirements and preferences. Within the context of Perplexity AI, this process enables users to refine how the system retrieves, processes, and displays information, thereby enhancing its utility for distinct use cases. The primary goal is to transform the search engine into a more precise tool by allowing adjustments to search scope, result filtering, response formatting, and source selection.

Key customization features in Perplexity AI allow users to control the depth and breadth of their queries. Users can adjust the search scope to focus on specific domains or timeframes, ensuring that results are relevant to the immediate context. Additionally, the platform supports result filtering to exclude irrelevant content and source preferences to prioritize certain types of references, such as academic papers or news outlets. These settings collectively help in managing the volume and quality of information returned by the engine.

The customization options extend to the presentation of information, where users can dictate the response format to suit their workflow. This includes selecting between concise answers, detailed explanations, or structured data outputs. By configuring these elements, users can optimize the search experience for tasks ranging from quick fact-checking to in-depth research, ensuring that the AI agent delivers information in the most efficient and accessible manner for the intended purpose.
