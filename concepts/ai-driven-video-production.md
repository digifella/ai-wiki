# Ai Driven Video Production

AI-driven video production is a workflow that automates the conversion of text-based source materials into finished video content through the sequential application of AI tools. This process combines slide generation and video synthesis to reduce manual effort in content creation, enabling faster production of video resources from written materials such as research documents, long-form articles, or structured notes.

The typical workflow begins with the use of NotebookLM to generate slide decks from source documents. These generated slides are then processed using Google Vids to create unwatermarked videos. By leveraging these specific tools in sequence, the method streamlines the transition from static text to dynamic visual media, minimizing the need for manual editing or design work.
