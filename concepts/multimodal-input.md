# Multimodal Input

**Multimodal Input** refers to the capability of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to process and integrate multiple types of data streams simultaneously, such as text, images, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and video. This integration allows for more nuanced understanding and generation of content compared to unimodal systems.

## Key Developments: Mistral Large 4

Recent advancements in [[concepts/european-ai-sovereignty|European AI sovereignty]] highlight the evolution of multimodal architectures through [[lab-notes/2026-10-07-European-AI-Sovereignty-Mistral-Large-4-Capabilities-Per|European AI Sovereignty: Mistral Large 4 Capabilities, Performance, Challenges]].

*   **Model Overview:** [[concepts/mistral-large-4|Mistral Large 4]] (internally dubbed "[[entities/le-chonk|Le Chonk]]") is a [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]] developed entirely in [[entities/europe|Europe]] from scratch.
*   **Architecture:**
    *   **Parameters:** 1 trillion [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]] with 49 billion [[concepts/activated-parameters|active parameters]].
    *   **Structure:** Utilizes a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE) architecture for efficiency.
    *   **[[concepts/licensing|Licensing]]:** [[concepts/open-weight|Open-weight]] and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]].
*   **[[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|Multimodal Capabilities]]:**
    *   Unifies [[concepts/instruction-following|instruction following]] across modalities.
    *   Enhances performance in [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks involving mixed input types.
*   **Strategic Context:** Represents a significant step toward reducing dependency on non-European [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]], emphasizing sovereignty in [[concepts/knowledge-acquisition|model development]].
