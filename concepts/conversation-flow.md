# Conversation Flow

Conversation Flow is a structured prompt template designed to enhance interactions between users and AI systems in business advisory contexts. The template functions as a metacognitive framework that guides AI assistants to clarify and refine user inputs before generating responses. By introducing a clarification step into the interaction process, it addresses a fundamental limitation in AI-assisted work: ambiguous or incomplete requests that frequently produce outputs misaligned with user intentions or actual information needs.

## How It Works

The template operates by directing the AI to identify potential constraints and gaps in a user's initial request rather than immediately proceeding to answer. When a user submits a query, the AI first evaluates whether sufficient context exists, whether the question contains hidden assumptions, or whether alternative interpretations are possible. The AI then presents these observations to the user, allowing them to refine their request before a full response is generated. This approach reduces iteration cycles and helps ensure that subsequent outputs address the user's actual problem rather than a misinterpreted version of it.

## Application and Limitations

Conversation Flow is most effective in complex advisory scenarios where precision matters—such as business strategy, risk assessment, or technical problem-solving. However, the template's value depends on user willingness to engage in the clarification dialogue; users seeking immediate answers may find the extra step burdensome. The approach also assumes that users can articulate constraints or gaps once they are identified, which may not always be feasible in novel or poorly understood domains.
