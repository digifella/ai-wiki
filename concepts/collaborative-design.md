# Collaborative Design

Collaborative design is an approach to creative [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]] that emphasizes teamwork, shared [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], and integrated [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] throughout the [[concepts/design-thinking|design process]]. Rather than following traditional linear workflows where designers hand off completed work to stakeholders, collaborative design embeds input from multiple perspectives—including designers, developers, product managers, and end users—at each stage of development. This integration aims to identify problems earlier, reduce rework, and produce solutions that better address user needs and technical constraints.

## Evolution of Design Workflows

The shift toward collaborative design is driven by the need for greater agility and alignment in [[concepts/product-design|product development]]. Traditional siloed processes often result in miscommunication and late-stage discoveries that are costly to fix. By involving cross-functional teams from the outset, organizations can ensure that technical feasibility and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] are balanced continuously. This method fosters a [[concepts/collective-awareness|shared understanding]] of project goals, reducing the [[concepts/friction|friction]] typically associated with handoffs between distinct departments.

## Role of AI-Native Tools

Modern collaborative design is increasingly supported by AI-native platforms designed to streamline these complex interactions. Tools such as [[concepts/ai-design-agent|Google Stitch]] exemplify this evolution by providing an [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|AI-native design canvas]] that evolves [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]] and prototyping capabilities. These platforms facilitate real-time co-creation, allowing stakeholders to interact with prototypes and provide immediate feedback. The integration of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] helps automate routine tasks, enabling designers to focus on high-level creative decisions while maintaining [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] across the team.
