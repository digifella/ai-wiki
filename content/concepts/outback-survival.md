# Outback Survival

Surviving in Australia's harsh [[concepts/australian-outback|outback]] requires innovative strategies to adapt to extreme temperatures and environmental challenges. One such example is the subterranean town of [[entities/coober-pedy|Coober Pedy]].

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/climate-adaptation|Climate Adaptation]]**: Utilizing [[concepts/underground-living|underground living]] spaces to mitigate temperature extremes.
- **[[concepts/geology|Geology]]**: Leveraging natural formations for shelter, construction, and resource extraction.
- **Resource Management**: Efficient use of water and other resources in a desert environment.
- **Community Living**: Building a sustainable community with shared infrastructure and resources.

## New Note Integration
- Coober Pedy residents carve their homes, businesses, and even churches directly into the earth.
- These underground living spaces are known as "[[concepts/dugouts|dugouts]]" providing complete living quarters including bedrooms, living rooms, and kitchens.
- The subterranean lifestyle is driven by harsh climatic conditions and unique geological features.
