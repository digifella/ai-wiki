# Cardiovascular Health

Cardiovascular health refers to the functional state of the heart and blood vessels, encompassing the heart's ability to pump blood effectively and the circulatory system's capacity to deliver oxygen and nutrients throughout the body. This physiological condition is assessed through measurable indicators including blood pressure, resting heart rate, cholesterol levels, and arterial elasticity. Good cardiovascular health reduces the risk of heart disease, stroke, and other circulatory conditions, which remain among the leading causes of mortality globally.

## Physical Activity and Exercise

Regular physical activity is one of the primary modifiable factors influencing cardiovascular health. Exercise strengthens the heart muscle, improves blood vessel function, and helps maintain healthy weight and blood pressure. Both aerobic activities (such as walking, running, and swimming) and resistance training contribute to cardiovascular improvements, though aerobic exercise has the most direct impact on heart and lung function. Even moderate activity spread throughout the week provides measurable cardiovascular benefits.

## Related Factors

Beyond exercise, cardiovascular health is influenced by diet, stress management, sleep quality, and genetics. Diets rich in fiber, omega-3 fatty acids, and limited in saturated fat support circulatory function. Smoking cessation, alcohol moderation, and management of chronic conditions like diabetes significantly improve cardiovascular outcomes. These factors interact with physical activity to determine overall heart and circulatory system function.
