# Strait Of Hormuz

The Strait of Hormuz is a narrow waterway situated between Iran and Oman, serving as the primary maritime link between the Persian Gulf and the Gulf of Oman, which opens into the Arabian Sea. At its narrowest point, the strait spans approximately 54 nautical miles (100 kilometers). Its geographic position makes it one of the world's most critical maritime chokepoints, facilitating the movement of global energy resources.

Approximately one-third of all globally traded seaborne crude oil passes through this corridor, alongside significant volumes of liquefied natural gas. This high concentration of energy traffic renders the strait vital to international energy security. Consequently, any disruption to navigation or conflict involving regional actors, particularly Iran, has the potential to cause immediate and substantial volatility in global oil market prices.
