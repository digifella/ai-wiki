# Geology

Geology is a branch of [[entities/earth|Earth]] sciences that studies the solid features of Earth and other terrestrial planets and moons. It encompasses processes such as [[concepts/tectonic-plate-shifts|plate tectonics]], [[concepts/geological-formation|rock formation]], [[concepts/volcanic-eruption|volcanic activity]], sedimentation, and erosion, which shape planetary surfaces over time.

## Mars Geological Findings
- [[concepts/ancient-rainforests|Ancient rainforests]] on [[concepts/mars|Mars]]: Evidence suggesting past [[concepts/habitability|habitability]] through fossilized remains or isotopic signatures.
- [[concepts/signs-of-life|Signs of life]]: Potential biosignatures indicating microbial activity in the Martian subsurface.
- Lightning phenomena: Recent observations indicate electrical discharges similar to those occurring on Earth, impacting atmospheric [[concepts/writing|composition]].
