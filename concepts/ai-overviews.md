# Ai Overviews

AI Overviews are synthesized summaries generated by [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems in response to search queries, displayed prominently at the top of search results pages. [[concepts/google-search|Google]] introduced this feature as part of its Search Generative [[concepts/experience|Experience]] (SGE) to consolidate information from multiple sources into direct answers. This approach aims to reduce the need for users to visit individual websites by providing immediate, aggregated responses to user intent.

The underlying mechanism relies on [[concepts/generative-engine-optimization|Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)]] to adapt website content for AI consumption. Rather than ranking individual web pages based on traditional metrics, the system generates new text that attempts to answer queries directly. This shift requires content creators to optimize their sites not just for keyword relevance, but for [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] and structure that [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] can effectively parse and summarize.

Adapting to this landscape involves using tools like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] to refine content strategies. The goal is to ensure that website data is accessible and interpretable by [[concepts/generative-ai|generative models]], thereby maintaining visibility within the AI-generated summary. This represents a fundamental change in [[concepts/seo-optimization|search engine optimization]], moving from driving traffic to specific URLs to influencing the AI's synthesis of information.
