# Planck

## Overview
The **[[concepts/planck-satellite-data|Planck mission]]** is a space-based observatory designed to map the [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] (CMB) with high [[concepts/accuracy|precision]], providing critical data for the Lambda-CDM model, [[concepts/dark-matter]], and [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|Dark Energy]] research.

## Key Research & Observations
- **Curvature Tension:** Recent analysis of Planck data identifies a significant unresolved problem regarding the universe's geometry, suggesting the universe may not be perfectly flat.
- **Impact on [[concepts/cosmology|Cosmology]]:** This tension challenges the fundamental assumptions of Inflation [[concepts/theory|Theory]] and necessitates a re-evaluation of the universe's large-scale structure.
