# Custom Voice Generation

**Custom [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|Voice Generation]]** refers to the process of creating synthetic speech that mimics specific vocal characteristics, [[concepts/timbre|timbre]], or [[concepts/style|style]], often using [[concepts/neural-networks|deep learning models]]. This technology enables personalized [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] output for applications ranging from [[concepts/assistive-devices|accessibility tools]] to [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]].

## Key Technologies & Models

### Gemini Audio
[[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s **[[entities/google-gemini|Gemini]] Audio** represents a significant advancement in AI [[concepts/text-to-speech|Text-to-Speech]] capabilities, specifically focusing on custom voice generation and control.

- **Core Functionality**: Generates diverse and expressive voice content from text inputs.
- **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Allows users to create and utilize their own custom voices, enhancing [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] and brand [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
- **Control**: Offers [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over the generated audio output, enabling precise [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to tone, pacing, and emotion.
