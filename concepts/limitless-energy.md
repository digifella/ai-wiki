# Limitless Energy

**Definition:** The theoretical or practical generation of abundant, low-cost, and essentially inexhaustible energy sources. Often associated with [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]], [[concepts/cold-fusion]], and advanced renewable technologies.

## Scientific Realities
- **[[concepts/nuclear-fusion]]**: The process powering [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]]; considered the holy grail for clean, limitless power. Requires extreme temperatures/pressures (e.g., Tokamak, Laser Inertial Confinement).
- **Thermodynamics**: Strictly bound by First Law of Thermodynamics ([[concepts/preservation|conservation]]) and Second Law of Thermodynamics (entropy). "Free energy" devices violating these are scientifically impossible.

## Historical Controversies & Frauds
The quest for limitless energy has attracted charlatans exploiting public hope and scientific ignorance. Notable cases include:

- **[[entities/project-huemul|Project Huemul]]: Argentina's Controversial [[concepts/fusion-power|Nuclear-Fusion]] [[concepts/scientific-experiment|Experiment]] and Fraud**: A mid-20th century Argentine project led by Manuel Sandoval Valenzuela. Claimed to achieve [[concepts/hydrogen-fusion|nuclear fusion]] at room temperature using heavy water and specific catalysts.
    - Exposed as a massive fraud; no energy was generated.
    - Involved high-level government complicity and deception before being debunked internationally.
- **[[concepts/cold-fusion]]**: The 1989 Pons-Fleischmann announcement of excess heat in electrolytic cells. Widely discredited due to lack of reproducibility and theoretical inconsistencies.

## Current State
- **ITER** and private ventures (e.g., Commonwealth Fusion Systems) aim for net-positive fusion energy by the late 2030s/2040s.
- No verified technology currently provides "limitless" energy without significant resource input or environmental cost.
