# 2026 04 11 How Climate Affects Community Health

Climate conditions shape [[concepts/community-health|community health]] outcomes through multiple interconnected pathways. [[concepts/weather-patterns|Weather patterns]], temperature extremes, and atmospheric [[concepts/writing|composition]] directly influence disease transmission, air quality, water availability, and food [[concepts/security|security]] at the population level. These effects are not uniform across communities; [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to climate-related [[concepts/health|health]] impacts varies based on geography, infrastructure, economic resources, and existing health disparities.

## Air Quality and Respiratory Health

Climate conditions significantly affect air quality, which in turn influences respiratory and [[concepts/cardiovascular-health|cardiovascular health]]. Higher temperatures can increase the formation of ground-level ozone and exacerbate [[concepts/granular-matter|particulate matter]] concentration in the [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]]. Wildfire seasons that intensify with changing climate patterns [[concepts/deployment|release]] smoke affecting air quality across large regions. Communities with limited air filtration infrastructure or higher baseline rates of respiratory illness [[concepts/experience|experience]] disproportionate health burdens from degraded air quality.

## Temperature-Related Illness and Vector-Borne Disease

Extreme heat and cold events pose direct health risks, particularly for [[concepts/vulnerable-populations|vulnerable populations]] including older adults, young children, and those with chronic conditions. Changes in temperature and [[concepts/precipitation-patterns|precipitation patterns]] also alter the geographic range and seasonal activity of disease vectors such as mosquitoes and ticks, potentially expanding the prevalence of diseases like dengue fever, Lyme disease, and malaria in previously unaffected regions.

## Water, Food Systems, and Nutrition

Climate variability affects freshwater availability and quality while disrupting agricultural [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and food [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]]. Flooding, drought, and temperature shifts impact crop yields and livestock health, with cascading effects on [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]] and food security for communities dependent on local or regional food systems. Water contamination from [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|extreme weather events]] creates additional health risks through waterborne disease transmission.
