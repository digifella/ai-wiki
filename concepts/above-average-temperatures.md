# Above-Average Temperatures

**Above-average temperatures** refer to climatic conditions where observed or predicted thermal values exceed the long-term historical median for a specific region and time period. This metric is critical for assessing [[concepts/heat-stress]], drought risk, and water [[concepts/security|security]].

## Key Indicators & Impacts
*   **Thermal [[concepts/anomaly|Anomaly]]:** Deviation from the baseline mean temperature, often used in [[concepts/climate-change]] modeling.
*   **Regional Variance:** [[concepts/temperature-trends|Temperature trends]] are rarely uniform; local geography and oceanic interactions (e.g., ENSO) [[concepts/ambition|drive]] significant spatial differences.
*   **Seasonal Outlooks:** Long-range forecasts provide probabilistic assessments of temperature likelihoods (e.g., "more likely than not" to be above average).
