# Deployment Management
Deployment management encompasses strategies and methodologies for the effective release, installation, upgrade, and maintenance of [[concepts/software|software]] or systems. It aims to ensure that changes are implemented smoothly with minimal disruption and maximum efficiency. Key aspects include [[concepts/automation|automation]] tools, [[concepts/cli-tools|configuration]] management, monitoring, [[concepts/rollback-procedures|rollback procedures]], and continuous integration/continuous [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] (CI/CD) pipelines.
