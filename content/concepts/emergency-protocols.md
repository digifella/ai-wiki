# Emergency Protocols

Emergency protocols are detailed procedures established to ensure a coordinated response in the event of a disaster or crisis. These plans help mitigate risks and provide guidelines for decision-making during emergencies.

## Key Components
- **Incident Command System (ICS)**: A standardized approach to command, control, and coordinate emergency response.
- **Communication Plans**: Ensuring clear lines of communication between all stakeholders involved in the emergency response.
- **Resource Management**: Identifying, mobilizing, allocating, and managing resources effectively during emergencies.

The [[concepts/winter-storm|winter storm]] that hit Texas in February 2021 caused widespread [[concepts/power-outages|power outages]] due to unprecedented low temperatures. This event underscores the importance of robust emergency protocols and highlights areas for improvement.
