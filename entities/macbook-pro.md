# MacBook Pro

The MacBook Pro is a line of laptop computers manufactured by Apple that runs macOS. Introduced in 2006, it represents Apple's premium portable computing offering, positioned above the standard MacBook in terms of performance, build quality, and price. The product line targets professional users and power consumers who require sustained computational performance in a portable form factor.

## Hardware and Performance

MacBook Pro models have evolved significantly since their introduction, particularly following Apple's transition to in-house silicon chips beginning in 2020. Modern MacBook Pro computers feature Apple's custom processors (M-series chips), which deliver high computational performance relative to their thermal and power constraints. This efficiency enables the machines to handle demanding professional workflows including video editing, software development, and 3D rendering while maintaining reasonable battery life.

## Local AI Capability

The processing power and memory configurations of contemporary MacBook Pro models make them capable of running large language models locally using software such as LM Studio. This allows users to operate AI models on their machine without relying on cloud-based services, providing privacy and offline access to language model functionality. The capability varies depending on the specific MacBook Pro configuration and model selected.
