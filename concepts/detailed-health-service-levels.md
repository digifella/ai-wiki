# Detailed Health Service Levels

The [[concepts/proposal-assembler|Proposal Assembler]] uses a [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]] to categorise [[concepts/health|health]] services according to their complexity and scope. This classification system helps health organizations organize interventions in a consistent manner, enabling clearer communication about what different services entail and how they relate to one another across the [[concepts/health-system|health system]].

## Purpose and Application

The service level framework supports several key functions within health system planning. It provides a common language for discussing the depth and breadth of health interventions, from basic preventive services to specialized [[concepts/tertiary-prevention|tertiary care]]. By establishing clear distinctions between service levels, organizations can better allocate resources, identify gaps in [[concepts/service-delivery|service provision]], and understand how individual services contribute to overall health system capacity.

## Service Level Categories

[[concepts/health-care|Health services]] within the framework are typically organized along a spectrum ranging from primary (foundational, community-based services) through secondary (district or regional services) to tertiary (specialized, hospital-based services). Each level represents increasing complexity, requiring different infrastructure, staffing, and technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]]. This hierarchical structure reflects how most health systems function and allows proposals to be positioned appropriately within the broader service delivery architecture.

## Implementation Value

The detailed classification enables health organizations to develop proposals that are contextualized within their system's existing structure. Rather than proposing services in [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], planners can understand how new interventions align with or complement existing service levels, facilitating more strategic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] about [[concepts/health-system-strengthening|health system strengthening]] and service expansion.
