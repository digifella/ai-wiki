# Gemini 3 Flash

* **Positioning**: Google's newest model, positioned as a [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] "workhorse" or daily driver for developers. Focused on speed, efficiency, and low cost.
* **Performance**: Significantly outperforms [[entities/gemini-25-flash]] predecessor and often rivals/beats [[entities/gemini-25-pro]] and [[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]] Pro in [[concepts/coding|coding]] and [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]]. Achieved **78% on [[concepts/swe-bench-verified|SWE-bench Verified]]**, beating [[entities/claude-sonnet-45|Claude Sonnet 4.5]].
* **Cost**: $0.50 per million input [[concepts/tokens|tokens]].
* **Model Type**: Despite being a "Flash" (lightweight) model, delivers [[entities/high-performance|high performance]] comparable to heavier variants.
