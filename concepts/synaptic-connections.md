# Synaptic Connections

**Synaptic connections** are the junctions between neurons where signals are transmitted via chemical or electrical means. They form the structural and functional basis of neural circuits and are critical for neuroplasticity, learning, and [[concepts/memory|memory]].

## Key Developments in Connectomics

Recent advancements in mapping synaptic networks have shifted from theoretical models to high-resolution empirical data.

- **[[concepts/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]] Release**: [[entities/google|Google]] and [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus have open-sourced the complete [[concepts/neural-wiring-diagram|connectome]] of a male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord. This dataset provides a full "wiring diagram" of the organism's central nervous system.
- **Structural Implications**: The open-sourcing allows for unprecedented analysis of synaptic density, topology, and circuit logic in a whole-brain context.
