## WeatherNext 3

**[[entities/weathernext-3|WeatherNext 3]]** is a high-resolution [[concepts/global-weather-forecasting|global weather forecasting]] model developed by [[entities/google-deepmind]]. It utilizes [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] to overcome the computational and accuracy limitations inherent in traditional numerical weather prediction methods.

### Key Features
- **AI-Driven Architecture:** Leverages deep learning to process vast atmospheric datasets more efficiently than conventional physics-based models.
- **High Resolution:** Provides localized and timely forecasts with greater granularity than previous iterations.
- **Global Scope:** Capable of predicting weather patterns across the entire globe simultaneously.
- **Accuracy Improvements:** Demonstrates significant gains in forecast precision, particularly for extreme weather events.
