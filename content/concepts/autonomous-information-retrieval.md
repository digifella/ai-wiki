# Autonomous information retrieval

The capability of AI systems to independently identify, navigate, and extract relevant data from disparate sources to satisfy complex research objectives without manual step-by-step instruction.

## Core Mechanisms
- **[[entities/deep-research]]**: Iterative, multi-step querying processes designed to uncover non-obvious connections and deeper layers of information.
- **[[concepts/source-discovery]]**: The ability of an [[entities/agent|agent]] to autonomously expand its [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] by locating new documents or entities to supplement existing [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Updates (2026-04-14)**:
    - [[concepts/integration|Integration]] of [[entities/deep-research]] for advanced, autonomous investigation.
    - Implementation of [[entities/nano-banana-pro]] for enhanced visual-centric retrieval and processing.
    - Enhanced **Source Discovery** capabilities:
        - **[[concepts/fast-research|Fast Research]]**: Rapid identification of immediate or proximal sources.
        - Autonomous Discovery: Capability to find and integrate new sources internally when user-provided documents are unavailable.
