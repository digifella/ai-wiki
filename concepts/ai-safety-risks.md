# AI safety risks

## Overview
[[concepts/model-safety|AI safety]] risks encompass the potential for [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] systems to cause harm through unintended behaviors, misuse, or systemic failures. Key areas include alignment problem, [[concepts/existential-risk]], model collapse, and dual-use dilemma concerns.

## Current Landscape & Pacing
The rapid [[concepts/deployment|release]] of advanced models creates challenges in monitoring safety benchmarks and regulatory [[concepts/compliance|compliance]]. Recent developments highlight the tension between [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/risk-mitigation|risk mitigation]].

- **[[entities/ibm-granite-42|IBM Granite 4.2]] & [[entities/meta|Meta]] Muse**: Recent discussions on IBM [[concepts/granite-42|Granite 4.2]] and [[entities/meta-muse|Meta Muse]] focus on the implications of "[[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]]" architectures and the pacing of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]].
- **Pacing Risks**: Accelerated deployment cycles may outpace the development of robust safety protocols and [[concepts/ai-interpretability|interpretability]] tools.
- **Industry Response**: Major tech firms are increasingly engaging in public discourse on [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|responsible AI]], as seen in recent industry [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]] and whitepapers.

## Key Risk Categories
- **Misuse**: Potential for AI-generated disinformation, [[concepts/cybersecurity-threats|cybersecurity threats]], and autonomous weapons.
- **Technical Failures**: [[concepts/data-hallucination|Hallucination]], adversarial attacks, and distribution shift.
- **[[concepts/societal-impact|Societal Impact]]**: Bias and fairness, job displacement, and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] erosion.
