# Leverage AI

Leverage AI is a pragmatic approach to integrating [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] technologies into [[concepts/business-workflows|organizational workflows]] by identifying and prioritizing [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] that deliver measurable value. Rather than implementing [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] broadly or experimentally, this methodology focuses on specific business problems where automation or enhanced [[concepts/speed|speed]] can directly improve outcomes. The approach balances the potential benefits of [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] against their actual capabilities, costs, and integration requirements within existing infrastructure.

## Assessment and Implementation

The core of leveraging AI involves systematic evaluation of potential applications before deployment. Organizations assess which tasks are repetitive enough to automate, which decisions benefit from AI analysis, and where speed improvements provide [[concepts/unique-value-proposition|competitive advantage]]. This evaluation stage considers both the technical feasibility of available tools and the practical constraints of existing systems, avoiding implementations that create more complexity than they resolve.

## Infrastructure and Tool Limitations

Effective AI leverage requires realistic understanding of constraints. [[concepts/infrastructure-limitations|Infrastructure limitations]]—such as [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]], data availability, and integration complexity—often determine what implementations are viable. Similarly, current [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] have specific capabilities and limitations that must align with actual use case requirements. Organizations adopting this approach explicitly account for these boundaries rather than expecting tools to solve problems beyond their current scope.

The practical goal of leveraging AI is incremental operational improvement through targeted deployment, where the cost and effort of implementation are justified by concrete gains in efficiency or output quality.
