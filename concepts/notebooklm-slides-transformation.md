# Notebooklm Slides Transformation

NotebookLM Slides Transformation is a workflow that converts slide decks generated by Google's NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process leverages NotebookLM's automatic slide generation feature, which creates structured presentations from source documents, research materials, or notes. Users then export these slides and import them into Google Vids, where the presentation format can be enhanced with video production features including narration, transitions, and multimedia elements.

## Workflow and Technical Process

The transformation begins with the ingestion of source materials into NotebookLM, which analyzes the content and generates a corresponding slide deck. Once the slides are finalized, they are exported in a compatible format, typically PowerPoint or PDF. This exported file is then uploaded to Google Vids, which interprets the slide structure to create a timeline-based video project.

Within Google Vids, the imported slides serve as the visual foundation for the video. The platform allows users to add voiceovers, background music, and visual effects to enhance the presentation. While the summary notes mention removing watermarks, the core technical capability focuses on the integration of static slide content into a dynamic video format, enabling users to produce shareable video summaries directly from their source documents without manual reformatting.
