# Gravitational Effects

Gravitational effects refer to the phenomena and consequences resulting from the gravitational force exerted by celestial bodies or masses on Earth. This includes various observable behaviors in materials such as [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]], planetary motion, tides, and the behavior of [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]]**: The fundamental force attracting two masses towards each other.
- **Weight**: Mass multiplied by gravity.
- **Orbital Mechanics**: How objects move under the [[concepts/power|influence]] of gravitational forces.
- **Tidal Forces**: Gravitational effects causing tides on Earth due to Moon and Sun.
