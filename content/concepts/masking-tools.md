# Masking tools

Localized adjustment features used to isolate specific regions of an image for [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]]. Primary implementations are found in Adobe [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], [[entities/lightroom-cc|Lightroom CC]], and [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]].

## Key Features & Implementation
- **[[concepts/landscape-masking|Landscape Masking]]**: A specialized feature designed for terrain-based enhancement.
    - **[[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] Dependency**: Successful application relies on initial Image [[concepts/preparation|Preparation]], including lens straightening and global [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to the Basic Panel (specifically [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Highlights, [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], [[concepts/whites|Whites]], and [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]]).
    - **Source Reference**: 2026 04 14 Landscape processing in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] Classic [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]]
- **[[concepts/intersect-masking|Intersect Masking]]**: A precision tool introduced in late 2023 for highly specific area [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]].
    - **Functionality**: Complements standard "Add" and "Subtract" masking functions by identifying overlapping regions.
    - **Source Reference**: Andy Hutchinson (2026-04-21)
