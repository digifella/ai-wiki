# Science

Science is a systematic method of inquiry and knowledge production that seeks to understand the natural world through observation, experimentation, and logical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. It encompasses a broad range of disciplines united by common methodological principles: the formulation of testable hypotheses, empirical investigation, peer review, and the [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of understanding based on evidence. Science operates as both a body of accumulated knowledge and an ongoing process of discovery.

## Physics and Scientific Modeling

Physics represents one of the fundamental natural sciences, concerned with understanding matter, energy, space, and time at all [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]]—from [[concepts/subatomic-particles|subatomic particles]] to cosmic structures. Scientific modeling is a central practice within physics and across scientific disciplines, involving the creation of simplified representations of complex systems that can be tested, refined, and used to make predictions. Models range from mathematical equations to computational simulations and [[concepts/conceptual-frameworks|conceptual frameworks]], serving as bridges between [[concepts/theory|theory]] and observation.

The systematic [[entities/nature|nature]] of science distinguishes it from other forms of inquiry through its emphasis on reproducibility, measurability, and the willingness to revise conclusions when new evidence emerges. This iterative approach has enabled the development of increasingly precise and comprehensive understandings of natural phenomena, from fundamental forces to the [[concepts/structure|structure]] of materials and biological systems.
