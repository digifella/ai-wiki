## Chef John's Korean Street Toast: Preparation and Assembly
**Clip title:** Korean Street Toast: How to Make This Delicious Breakfast Sandwich | Food Wishes
**Author / channel:** Food Wishes
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Og1cdmqs9ko

### Summary
This video from [[entities/food-wishes|Food Wishes]] by [[entities/chef-john|Chef John]] demonstrates how to create a "[[concepts/korean-street-toast|Korean Street Toast]]," a popular and surprisingly delicious sweet and savory [[concepts/breakfast-sandwich|breakfast sandwich]]. Chef John introduces the dish with his signature playful humor, emphasizing that despite its simple name, the flavor combination is extraordinary and highly addictive. He sets the stage for a unique culinary experience, encouraging viewers not to judge a recipe by its title.

The [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] begins with [julienning](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Julienning) fresh vegetables: carrots, cabbage, and green onions. These are seasoned with freshly ground black pepper, a few shakes of cayenne or Korean chili flakes, and two pinches of salt. Chef John then instructs to mix and mash these vegetables by hand for about 30 seconds, softening them to become more compliant. Two eggs are cracked into the mixture and thoroughly whisked, forming the savory-sweet base of the sandwich.

Next, the bread is prepared by melting about two tablespoons of butter in a large non-stick skillet over medium-high heat. Two slices of thick white bread are toasted until beautifully golden brown and crispy on both sides. After removing the toast, another chunk of butter is melted in the same pan, and the egg and vegetable mixture is poured in. Using a spatula, the mixture is spread and shaped into a rectangle, cooked, and then cut in half before being flipped to cook evenly until golden brown and tender. Two thin slices of smoked ham are then fried in the same pan, topped with a slice of American or mild cheddar cheese, and cooked until the cheese melts.

For the final assembly, the cooked vegetable egg patties are stacked onto one slice of the buttered toast, followed by the melted ham and cheese. The sandwich is then topped with a crucial and surprisingly effective element: a sprinkle of white [[concepts/sugar|sugar]], which Chef John notes perfectly balances the savory flavors. Finally, generous drizzles of mayonnaise and ketchup are added, and the second slice of toast completes the sandwich. Cut diagonally, the Korean Street Toast reveals a stunning cross-section of crispy bread, tender vegetable-egg, smoky ham, melted cheese, and the vibrant sauces.

Chef John concludes by passionately recommending the full-sized, two-egg version of this sandwich, highlighting the extraordinary blend of tastes and textures—the crispy, buttery bread, the slightly sweet and savory egg patty, the smoky ham, and the unique combination of [condiments](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Condiment) and [[concepts/sugar|sugar]]. He asserts that this breakfast sandwich is so incredibly satisfying, it might "ruin" you for any other kind. He encourages viewers to try it soon for a truly memorable culinary experience.
