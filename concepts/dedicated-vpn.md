# Dedicated Vpn

A dedicated VPN is a virtual private network service that assigns a static IP address to a single user or organization, rather than sharing rotating addresses among multiple users. This architecture contrasts with standard shared VPN services, where multiple subscribers route traffic through the same pool of IP addresses. By isolating the IP address, the service ensures that the digital footprint remains consistent and exclusive to the subscriber.

NordVPN offers dedicated VPN as a premium tier service, maintaining a distinct infrastructure for these connections. This setup provides enhanced privacy and security for users who require a fixed identity for online activities, such as accessing remote servers or maintaining consistent whitelisted status with external services. The exclusive nature of the IP address prevents the "bad neighbor" effect common in shared pools, where one user's malicious activity could lead to the entire IP block being blacklisted.

The service is particularly beneficial for businesses and power users who need reliable connectivity for VoIP, gaming, or remote desktop applications. Because the IP address does not change, it simplifies network configuration and reduces the risk of connection drops associated with dynamic IP rotation. While more expensive than standard plans, the dedicated VPN offers a higher level of control and predictability for critical digital operations.
