# Performance Efficiency

**Performance Efficiency** quantifies the ratio of computational [[concepts/output|output]] to resource consumption. In [[concepts/large-language-model]] systems, this involves optimizing [[concepts/inference-optimization]], memory-utilization, energy-consumption, and [[concepts/cost|cost]] per token to maximize capability density relative to [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] expenditure.

- [[lab-notes/2026-05-07-DeepSeek-V4-Unprecedented-1M-Token-Context-Open-Source-L|DeepSeek V4: Unprecedented 1M Token Context Open-Source LLM Performance and Efficiency]] redefines efficiency [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]:
  - Delivers a **1 million token [[concepts/context-window]]** in an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] format, decoupling long-context [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] from proprietary infrastructure costs.
  - Outperforms billion-dollar closed systems, demonstrating breakthrough gains in [[concepts/inference-optimization]] and [[concepts/model-architecture]].
  - Validates strategies for achieving enterprise-grade performance with optimized resource allocation, challenging traditional [[concepts/scaling-laws]].
