# Clean Beauty

Clean Beauty refers to cosmetic and personal care products formulated without certain controversial or potentially harmful ingredients. Products marketed as "clean beauty" typically exclude synthetic chemicals, parabens, sulfates, phthalates, and other additives that consumers or manufacturers consider unsafe or environmentally damaging. The [[concepts/exercise|movement]] emphasizes transparency in ingredient sourcing and manufacturing processes.

The clean beauty category has grown significantly as consumer awareness of ingredient safety has increased. Brands within this space often highlight their commitment to natural or minimally processed formulations, though the term "clean" itself lacks regulatory definition. This has led to varying [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] across the industry, with some companies applying stricter criteria than others.

The market encompasses skincare, haircare, makeup, and fragrance products, ranging from premium brands to affordable options. Clean beauty overlaps with related movements such as natural and organic beauty, though these categories are distinct—a product may be natural without being clean by certain standards, or vice versa. The sector continues to evolve as scientific research informs ingredient safety discussions and consumer preferences shape product development.
