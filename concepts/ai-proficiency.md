# AI Proficiency

AI proficiency refers to the capability to work effectively with artificial intelligence systems as they become integrated into workplace processes. Unlike specialized technical skills limited to machine learning engineers or data scientists, AI proficiency encompasses a broader set of competencies relevant across industries and job functions. This includes understanding what AI systems can and cannot do, recognizing appropriate use cases, and adapting workflows to leverage AI tools effectively.

## Core Competencies

Developing AI proficiency requires familiarity with how AI systems operate, their limitations, and their practical applications within specific domains. This involves learning to use common AI tools and platforms, understanding the types of tasks AI can assist with, and recognizing when human judgment remains essential. Workers benefit from developing literacy around AI capabilities—such as language models, image generation, and data analysis—without necessarily understanding the underlying mathematics or engineering required to build these systems.

## Workplace Integration

As organizations adopt AI tools across functions from customer service to content creation to analytical work, employees who can effectively collaborate with these systems become increasingly valuable. Proficiency in this context means knowing how to prompt AI systems appropriately, evaluate their outputs critically, and integrate AI-assisted work into existing processes. This skill set varies by industry and role but generally includes the ability to identify opportunities for AI application and understand associated risks such as accuracy limitations and bias.
