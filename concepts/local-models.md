# Local Models

**Local Models** refer to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems that are deployed and executed on local hardware (e.g., personal computers, servers) rather than relying on remote cloud-based APIs. This approach prioritizes data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], offline accessibility, and reduced latency.

## Key Concepts

*   **Privacy & Security**: Data remains on-device, mitigating risks associated with third-party data processing.
*   **Autonomy**: Functionality persists without internet connectivity.
*   **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Allows for fine-tuning and modification of model weights and parameters.
