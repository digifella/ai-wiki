# Anthropic Claude

[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]. The model has gained [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] in professional and creative fields for its [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] in processing complex prompts and generating detailed [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Prompting Techniques

Effective use of [[concepts/claude|Claude]] and similar [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] requires more sophisticated [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] approaches than simple queries. [[entities/kevin-patrick-robbins|Kevin Patrick Robbins]], an advertising, editorial, and commercial photographer, has discussed how most users achieve only [[concepts/superficial-results|superficial results]] because they rely on overly simplistic prompts. Moving beyond basic input-[[concepts/output|output]] interactions allows users to extract more nuanced and business-relevant outputs from the model.

The quality of results from Claude depends significantly on how users [[concepts/structure|structure]] their requests, including context, specificity, and [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of prompts. This principle applies across professional [[concepts/software|applications]], from creative work to business analysis.
