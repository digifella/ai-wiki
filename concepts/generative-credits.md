# Generative Credits

**Generative Credits** refers to the usage-based accounting system for AI-driven features within [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s ecosystem, particularly in [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]]. This model tracks and limits the consumption of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] for high-demand operations like [[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]] and advanced [[concepts/ai-masking|AI masking]] tools.

## Integration: Photoshop Beta 27.11.0

Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] have introduced complex AI features that interact with the Generative Credits system. Key developments include:

- **[[concepts/enhance-edge|Enhance Edge]] AI**: A new feature in [[concepts/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] 27.11.0 designed to improve selection accuracy, specifically for complex edges like hair and fur.
- **[[concepts/hair-masking|Hair Masking]] & [[concepts/halos|Halos]]**: The AI specifically targets the removal of halos and refinement of fine details, reducing manual cleanup time.
- **Credit Consumption**: Usage of these advanced [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] deducts from the user's Generative Credits balance, reflecting the high computational cost of real-time [[concepts/on-device-processing|edge processing]].
- **Availability**: Feature introduced in Beta 27.11.0; subject to change as [[concepts/adobe|Adobe]] refines the credit allocation model for beta users.
