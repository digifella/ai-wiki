## iOS 27 Photos App: New Features for Enhanced Organization and Editing
**Clip title:** 10 [[concepts/new-features|New Features]] Of the Photos App In iOS 27 To Check Out
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** macmostvideo
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cMKAjdaG3Tk

### Summary
The [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] video from [[entities/macmostcom|MacMost.com]] highlights a comprehensive set of [[concepts/new-features|new features]] introduced to the Photos app in [[concepts/ios-27|iOS 27]], designed to enhance organization, editing, and overall [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]]. The presenter demonstrates ten key additions, ranging from granular [[concepts/photo-management|photo management]] options to advanced [[concepts/ai-powered-editing|AI-powered editing]] capabilities, along with a bonus wallpaper feature.

Among the new organizational tools, iOS 27 introduces **[[concepts/star-ratings|Star Ratings]]**, allowing users to assign a 1-5 star rating to individual photos and videos after enabling the feature in settings. This enables powerful filtering options to quickly find highly-rated content. Furthermore, the long-awaited ability to **Add Keyword Tags** directly on the [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] Photos app is now available, with these tags seamlessly syncing to the Mac Photos app for consistent organization across devices. Users can also filter their library to show only photos **Captured By Me**, effectively recreating the functionality of the old Camera Roll album. For managing large selections, a **View Selected** option now provides a dedicated screen to review and refine chosen photos before sharing or editing. Lastly, a **Sync Immediately** toggle in [[entities/icloud|iCloud]] settings allows users to prioritize the immediate upload of new photos to iCloud, which is particularly beneficial for travelers or those needing quick backups.

The Photos app also receives significant enhancements in its creative and AI-driven editing suite. Users can now easily extract a single frame from a video and **Save Video Frame To Photo**, though the presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] that video [[concepts/solution|resolution]] [[entities/will|will]] be lower than a still photo. More impressively, new [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], compatible with [[entities/apple|Apple]] Intelligence-enabled iPhones, revolutionize [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]]. The **AI Clean Up** feature builds upon the existing retouch tool, offering superior [[concepts/object-removal|object removal]] with options for "Fast" or "High Quality" processing. **AI Extend** intelligently fills in areas beyond a photo's original borders, useful for adjusting [[concepts/writing|composition]] or aspect ratios. Finally, **AI Reframe** analyzes a photo's depth and perspective, allowing users to drag and adjust the [[concepts/writing|composition]], with the AI filling in any newly exposed areas. All these editing tools maintain [[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|non-destructive editing]], preserving the original photo.

As a bonus, the video showcases an **Extend Wallpaper** feature, found outside the Photos app but leveraging photo capabilities. When setting a wallpaper, users can choose one of their photos, and the AI [[entities/will|will]] intelligently extend the image to better fit the iPhone's screen, especially useful for vertical orientations. Collectively, these new features make the Photos app in iOS 27 a more robust and intelligent tool for managing, organizing, and creatively enhancing personal media on Apple devices.
