# Dr Karoly Zsolnai Fehér

Dr Karoly Zsolnai Fehér is a researcher at DeepMind, Google's artificial intelligence research laboratory. His work centers on the development of artificial intelligence systems designed to support scientific discovery and enhance the reliability of computational research methods.

A primary focus of his research is Aletheia, a self-correcting AI system aimed at improving the accuracy and robustness of scientific inquiry. This system addresses specific challenges in automated scientific discovery by incorporating mechanisms to identify and correct errors within computational processes.
