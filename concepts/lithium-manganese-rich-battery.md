# Lithium Manganese Rich battery

**Lithium Manganese Rich (LMR)** batteries are a class of lithium-ion battery cathodes characterized by high specific capacity and energy density, leveraging manganese-rich structures to reduce cost and reliance on cobalt.

## Key Developments (2026)

*   **[[entities/lg-energy-solution|LG Energy Solution]] Breakthrough:** Significant progress announced in LG LMR Breakthrough: Cheaper, Longer-Range EV Batteries Surpassing LFP regarding commercial viability.
    *   **Cost:** Achieved production costs lower than LFP ([[concepts/lfp-battery|Lithium Iron Phosphate]]) batteries.
    *   **Performance:** Surpassed LFP in energy density, enabling longer vehicle range.
    *   **Implications:** Positions LMR as a competitive alternative to both NMC (for cost/weight) and LFP (for range/cost balance).
    *   **Source:** [LG LMR Breakthrough: Cheaper, Longer-Range EV Batteries Surpassing LFP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ve5UitAyP0M)

## Technical Context

*   **Composition:** High manganese content reduces material costs compared to Cobalt-heavy chemistries.
*   **Advantages:**
    *   High specific capacity (>250 mAh/g theoretical).
    *   Lower raw material cost.
    *   Improved thermal stability compared to some NMC variants.
*   **Challenges:**
    *   Voltage fade over cycling.
    *   Initial activation energy requirements.
    *   Electrolyte decomposition management.
