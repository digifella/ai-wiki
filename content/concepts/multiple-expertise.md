# Multiple Expertise

Multiple Expertise is an approach in [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] that leverages multiple [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) to both retrieve and verify knowledge. Rather than relying on a single model for information gathering, this method uses one LLM to retrieve candidate knowledge while another acts as an expert reviewer to validate [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and relevance. This dual-[[concepts/architecturetechnique|model architecture]] aims to improve [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] by introducing cross-verification between independent systems.

## Application in Health Services

The concept has been applied practically to the development of an [[concepts/aimm-ai-maturity-model|AI maturity model]] for health services. In this context, multiple LLMs work together to gather and assess information relevant to measuring organizational readiness and capability in healthcare [[concepts/adoption|AI implementation]]. The approach addresses the particular challenge of ensuring that guidance on healthcare technology adoption is both comprehensive and accurate, given the critical [[entities/nature|nature]] of health service [[concepts/software|applications]].
