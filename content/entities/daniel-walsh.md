# Daniel Walsh

Daniel Walsh is a content creator known for educational videos about [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]]. He has produced technical explainer content focused on emerging AI architectures and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tools.

## GraphRAG and LightRAG

Walsh authored a video explaining GraphRAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation enhanced with [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]]), which demonstrates how structured knowledge representations can improve AI system performance. In this work, he showcased LightRAG, an open-source system designed to enhance agent [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] through the use of knowledge graphs structured as [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]]. This video was published in April 2026.
