# Travel Research Curation

Systematic [[concepts/organization|organization]] of travel research to transform overwhelming planning into actionable, shareable experiences. Reduces post-booking chaos by structuring information around practical needs.

## Core Workflow
- **Curate trusted sources** in [[entities/notebooklm]] (e.g., guidebooks, local blogs) to build a structured [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]
- **Enhance with [[entities/gemini]]** to generate context-aware itineraries (restaurant suggestions, schedule checks, town exploration routes)
- **Share interactive outputs** as collaborative planning resources (video demonstration: [Just Booked It - tips](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lzi0JTtjuAs))

## Problem Addressed
- Overwhelming post-flight-booking tasks: exploring towns, finding restaurants, verifying schedules (0:00-0:19)

## Integration Points
- Feeds into [[concepts/travel-planning|Travel Planning]] process as pre-departure research phase
- Complements AI-Powered [[concepts/research-tools|Research tools]] for efficiency
- Prevents information fragmentation across notes/apps
