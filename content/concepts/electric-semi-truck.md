# Electric Semi-Truck Overview

An electric semi-truck is a large truck designed to operate on battery power instead of conventional fossil fuels. These vehicles are part of the broader shift towards electrification in the transportation sector, aimed at reducing emissions and improving efficiency.

## Key Features
- **Battery Technology:** Utilizes advanced lithium-ion batteries or other next-generation technologies for energy [[entities/storage|storage]].
- **[[concepts/charging-infrastructure|Charging Infrastructure]]:** Requires a robust network of high-speed charging stations capable of rapid refueling.
- **Range and Efficiency:** Designed to match the range and performance capabilities of diesel trucks while offering lower operating costs.
- **Environmental Impact:** Significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions, contributing to sustainability goals.
