# Color Contrast

Color contrast refers to the visual difference between colors in an image, determined by their positions on the color wheel and their relative brightness or saturation levels. Strong color contrast draws the viewer's eye and creates visual interest, while subtle contrast produces [[concepts/musical-harmony|harmony]] and [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]]. Photographers and digital artists use color contrast as a fundamental tool for [[concepts/writing|composition]] and emphasis.

## Technical Adjustment

Modern [[concepts/image-processing-software|image processing software]] provides tools to adjust color contrast independently of [[concepts/contrast|tonal contrast]]. [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]], a widely used raw image processing [[concepts/engine|engine]], includes specific controls for this purpose. [[entities/glyn-dewis|Glyn Dewis]] has discussed the [[concepts/color-variance-control|Variance slider]] feature within this software, which allows users to manipulate color contrast dynamics without altering the overall luminance structure of the photograph. This capability enables precise control over the vibrancy and separation of hues, facilitating more nuanced post-processing workflows.
