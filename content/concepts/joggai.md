# Joggai

[[entities/joggai|Joggai]] is an automated tool designed to convert audio-based [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]] into video content with minimal manual intervention. The platform addresses a common challenge in podcast [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] by enabling creators to repurpose [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] content for video platforms without requiring traditional [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] [[concepts/skills|skills]] or workflows.

## Functionality

The tool accepts AI-generated or human-produced podcast audio as input and automatically generates corresponding video episodes. This process eliminates the need for manual editing, allowing creators to publish video versions of their podcasts more efficiently than through traditional production methods.

## Context and Usage

Joggai emerged as part of broader trends in content [[concepts/automation|automation]] and cross-platform distribution. Podcast creators and producers use the tool to expand their reach by publishing content simultaneously across audio and video platforms, adapting to changing consumption patterns where video content [[concepts/commands|commands]] greater engagement on many social media channels.
