# Exposure adjustment

The process of modifying the perceived brightness of an image by adjusting the [[concepts/light|light]] levels across various tonal ranges.

## Core Parameters
- [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]: The primary control for overall image luminosity.
- Highlights: Control over the brightest areas of the image.
- [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]]: Control over the darkest areas of the image.
- [[concepts/whites|Whites]]: Definition of the brightest point in the tonal range.
- [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]]: Definition of the darkest point in the tonal range.

## Implementation & Workflow
- **Localized [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]**: Utilizing [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] features—such as the "Landscape" mask in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], [[entities/lightroom-cc|Lightroom CC]], and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]]—to apply luminosity changes to specific segments of an image.
- **Pre-processing**: Effective [[concepts/exposure-manipulation|exposure manipulation]] often follows initial steps like [[concepts/straightening|Straightening]] and basic panel adjustments to Highlights, Shadows, Whites, and Blacks.
