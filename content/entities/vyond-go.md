# Vyond Go

Vyond Go is an AI-assisted [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] tool developed by Vyond that streamlines the [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] process through [[concepts/automation|automation]] and template-based workflows. Users can generate videos by providing scripts—either by creating new ones directly within the platform or uploading existing scripts—and selecting from a library of [[concepts/stock-characters|stock characters]] to populate their videos.

## Workflow

The tool is designed to reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]] in video production by allowing users to customize [[concepts/character-generation|character selection]] before the AI generates the final video. This approach combines user input on narrative content and character choice with automated video assembly, making video creation more accessible to users without specialized animation [[concepts/skills|skills]].

## Features

Vyond Go includes AI-assisted capabilities to help guide users through the video creation process, supported by [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]] such as [[entities/bootcamp-sessions|bootcamp sessions]] available through Vyond's help center.
