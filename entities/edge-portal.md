# Edge Portal

Edge Portal is a web-based service provided by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) that allows users to access company information and related services. The portal serves as a centralized platform for retrieving details about registered Australian companies and managing interactions with ASIC's corporate registry functions.

Users can access Edge Portal by navigating to the ASIC website and searching for relevant company details, typically using the Australian Company Number (ACN) or company name. The portal provides access to company registration data, financial reports, and other regulatory documents, facilitating transparency and compliance for businesses and the public.
