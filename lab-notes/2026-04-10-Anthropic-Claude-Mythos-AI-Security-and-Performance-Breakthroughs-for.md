## Anthropic Claude Mythos: AI Security and Performance Breakthroughs for
Critical Software
**Clip title:** [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] Built an AI So Dangerous They Won’t Release It
([[entities/claude|Claude]] [[concepts/mythos|Mythos]])
**Author / channel:** [[entities/the-ai-advantage|The AI Advantage]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=NOR4NHL-SiI

### Summary
The video discusses the recent preview of [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s new [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]]
model, "[[entities/claude-mythos|Claude Mythos]]," and its profound implications for the [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]]
and various industries. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] highlights [[entities/mythos|Mythos]]'s groundbreaking
performance across several benchmarks, particularly in [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]]
and [[concepts/multimodal-tasks|multimodal tasks]], where it significantly outperforms existing models
like [[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus]] 4.6, [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]].4, and [[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]].1 Pro. This unreleased model is
currently being utilized in "[[entities/project-glasswing|Project Glasswing]]," an initiative with major
tech companies like [[entities/amazon-web-services|AWS]], Apple, Google, and Microsoft, to enhance the
security of critical software by autonomously identifying and exploiting
vulnerabilities.

The first key insight presented is that the recent effectiveness of
"agentic" [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] (like [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]], which the speaker uses for [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]) isn't primarily due to sophisticated toolchain engineering, but
rather the dramatic improvement in the underlying [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]]
themselves. Earlier agentic projects such as BabyAGI and AgentGPT struggled
because previous models lacked sufficient "[prompt adherence](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Prompt_adherence)" and "context
retention" over long interactions. The speaker credits [[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus]] 4.5 and
4.6 as the "tipping point," enabling [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to maintain complex
[[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and [[concepts/memory|memory]], thereby becoming genuinely useful tools that can
perform multi-step tasks reliably without losing track of their objectives.

The second crucial insight revolves around the strategic timing of
Anthropic's Mythos announcement and its competitive implications. Mythos's
benchmark scores are exceptionally high, with a verified software
engineering benchmark ([[concepts/SWE-bench|SWE-bench]] Verified) of 93.9%, a substantial leap
from [[entities/claude|Claude]] [[entities/opus|Opus]] 4.6's 80.8%. This powerful, unreleased model was unveiled
shortly after the release of GLM-5.1, an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] model that briefly
claimed top performance in [coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Coding). Anthropic's swift reveal of Mythos,
significantly surpassing GLM-5.1, is seen as a move to defend its business
model and [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] position, effectively setting a new bar for [[concepts/ai-capabilities|AI capabilities]] and forcing competitors like [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] and Google to accelerate
their own advanced [[concepts/knowledge-acquisition|model development]].

The third takeaway emphasizes the accelerating impact of these highly
capable [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and the widening "gap" it creates across industries. The
increasing [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and effectiveness of agentic tools, powered by
models like Mythos, will [[entities/make|make]] them indispensable for a growing [[concepts/range|range]] of
tasks, from coding and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] to finance. While "physical
businesses" might be less immediately affected, industries heavily reliant
on software and digital processes will see profound changes. The speaker
predicts a "cataclysmic shift" in how work is done, creating an enormous
divide between individuals and organizations that effectively leverage
these advanced [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] and those that do not, underscoring the urgency for
everyone to understand and engage with these rapidly evolving capabilities.
