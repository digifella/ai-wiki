One documented technique addresses the removal of chain-link fences from photographs. [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]]'s [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] demonstrates two modern methods for accomplishing this task, providing practical guidance for photographers and image editors working with obstructed scenes. These approaches leverage Photoshop's tools to eliminate unwanted fence elements while maintaining the [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] of the background imagery.

The methods shown in Morganti's tutorial represent contemporary approaches to this editing problem, suitable for photographers who need to clean up images taken through or behind fencing without relying on manual [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] alone.
