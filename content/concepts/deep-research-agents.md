# Deep Research Agents

Deep Research Agents are [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]] built on the [[entities/langchain|LangChain]] framework designed to conduct extended research tasks with minimal human intervention. These [[concepts/agents|agents]] utilize language models to decompose complex research questions into manageable subtasks, recursively gather information, and synthesize findings into coherent outputs. The LangChain implementation provides an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] foundation that developers can configure and extend for domain-specific research [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Architecture and Functionality

The [[concepts/deep-research-agent|Deep Research Agent]] operates through iterative cycles of planning, querying, and synthesis. Rather than returning immediate [[concepts/responses|responses]], these agents maintain research context across multiple steps, allowing them to pursue deeper lines of inquiry. They can autonomously decide when sufficient information has been gathered and how to organize findings hierarchically. The configurable [[entities/nature|nature]] of the LangChain implementation enables [[concepts/customization|customization]] of search strategies, output formats, and integration with various data sources.

## Use Cases and Applications

These agents are particularly suited for tasks requiring comprehensive [[concepts/information-synthesis|information synthesis]], such as literature reviews, competitive analysis, [[concepts/technical-documentation|technical documentation]] generation, and exploratory research in unfamiliar domains. By automating the [[concepts/iterative-research|iterative research]] process, Deep Research Agents reduce the time required for knowledge workers to develop well-researched summaries and can help identify patterns or connections that might be missed in manual research workflows.
