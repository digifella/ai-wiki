# Tesla FSD Supervised

Tesla's Full Self-Driving Supervised (FSD Supervised) is an advanced driver assistance system that automates vehicle control while requiring continuous driver monitoring. The system operates at Level 2 automation, positioning itself between conventional driver assistance features and fully autonomous driving capabilities. Unlike fully autonomous systems, FSD Supervised requires the operator to remain actively engaged and prepared to intervene at any moment, maintaining responsibility for vehicle safety and control decisions.

## European Regulatory Status

In April 2026, Tesla obtained regulatory approval for FSD Supervised in the Netherlands, marking a significant development in European autonomous vehicle policy. This approval potentially establishes a precedent for broader European Union adoption, as regulatory frameworks across EU member states have historically varied in their approach to advanced driver assistance systems. The Dutch approval demonstrates one regulatory pathway for deploying Level 2 automation technology while maintaining driver accountability requirements.

The system's approval in the Netherlands may influence how other European regulators evaluate similar technologies, though widespread EU adoption would require navigating diverse national regulations and harmonized standards under the framework of EU automotive legislation.
