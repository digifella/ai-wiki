# Circumference Calculation

The calculation of a circle's [[concepts/circumference|circumference]] is fundamentally linked to its diameter and the mathematical constant Pi (π). The formula for finding the circumference \( C \) of a circle given its diameter \( d \) or radius \( r \) is:

\[ C = πd \]
or
\[ C = 2πr \]

### Key Concepts

- **Pi (π)**: An [[concepts/irrational_number|irrational number]] representing the ratio of a circle's circumference to its diameter, approximately equal to 3.14159.
- **Radius**: The distance from the center of a circle to any point on its edge.
- **Diameter**: A straight line passing through the center of a circle, connecting two points on the edge (twice the radius).
