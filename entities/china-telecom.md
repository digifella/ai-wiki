# China Telecom

**China Telecom** is a major state-owned telecommunications operator in China. Beyond traditional connectivity services, the organization actively invests in [[concepts/ai-research|artificial intelligence research]] and development through its dedicated AI research groups.

## AI Research & Development

China Telecom's AI initiatives focus on developing [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] for enterprise and consumer applications, particularly in [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] and computer vision.

### Key Projects

*   **TeleOCR**: A 1.2 billion-parameter document parser designed to extract structured data from various document types.
    *   **Specialization**: Optimized for "camera-captured" documents, addressing challenges such as image distortions, shadows, and non-standard angles that typically hinder traditional parsers.
    *   **Efficiency**: Capable of running on [[concepts/consumer-grade-hardware|consumer-grade hardware]] with as little as 8GB of GPU memory, making it accessible for [[concepts/local-control|local deployment]].
    *   **Performance**: Reported to outperform larger commercial models in specific [[concepts/content-extraction|document parsing]] tasks.
