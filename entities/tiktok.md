# Tiktok

TikTok is a short-form video social media platform that allows users to create, share, and discover video content typically ranging from 15 seconds to 10 minutes in length. Launched internationally in 2018, the application evolved from Douyin, a Chinese video platform developed by ByteDance. It has rapidly expanded to become one of the most widely used social media applications globally, boasting hundreds of millions of monthly active users.

The platform hosts primarily user-generated content spanning entertainment, education, music, comedy, and lifestyle categories. TikTok utilizes an AI-driven recommendation algorithm to curate a personalized "For You" feed for each user, which significantly contributes to its rapid content discovery and user engagement. This algorithmic approach allows content to gain viral traction regardless of the creator's follower count, distinguishing it from traditional social networks based on social graphs.

Beyond user-generated videos, TikTok incorporates AI-automated content features and editing tools that lower the barrier to entry for creators. The platform supports various formats, including live streaming, duets, and stitched videos, fostering a collaborative environment. Its global expansion has led to localized versions that adhere to regional regulations while maintaining the core functionality of short-form video sharing.
