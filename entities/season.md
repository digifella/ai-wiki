# Season

**Season** is a Swiss company specializing in long-term [[concepts/renewable-energy-storage|renewable energy storage]] solutions. The entity focuses on addressing the intermittency of renewable sources by developing technologies capable of [[concepts/storing|storing]] [[concepts/surplus-energy|surplus energy]] for extended periods with minimal loss.

## Key Technologies & Developments

- **[[concepts/thermochemical-heat-storage|Thermochemical Heat Storage]]**: Season has developed an innovative system designed to store surplus renewable energy for months. This technology aims to provide a more efficient alternative to traditional methods like [[concepts/heat-pumps|Heat Pumps]] for long-duration [[entities/storage|storage]].
- **Low-Loss Storage**: The core [[concepts/purpose|objective]] of their technology is to minimize energy degradation over time, ensuring that stored energy remains viable for seasonal use.
