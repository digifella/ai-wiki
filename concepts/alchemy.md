# Alchemy

Medieval practice of transforming base [[concepts/metal|metals]] into [[concepts/major-gold-deposit|gold]], seeking the philosopher's stone, and exploring the fundamental nature of matter. Evolved into modern Chemistry and influenced Hermeticism.

## Key Concepts
- Philosopher's Stone: Legendary substance enabling transmutation and immortality.
- Emerald Tablet: Foundational text of Hermetic [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] attributed to [[entities/hermes|Hermes]] Trismegistus.
- Magnum [[concepts/opus|Opus]]: The alchemical process of spiritual and material transformation.

## Modern Context
- Metaphorical use in fields like data [[concepts/science|science]] ("data alchemy").
- Historical precursor to Chemistry and Pharmacology.
- Alchemy's symbolic framework still influences Jungian [[concepts/psychology|psychology]].
- Cultural Application: The term "Magnum [[entities/opus|Opus]]" is applied to significant creative works, such as the analysis of *The Simpsons* episode "Homer's Enemy" as a pivotal turning point in the series [[lab-notes/2026-07-12-Analysis-of-The-Simpsons-Homers-Enemy-as-Pivotal-Profoun|Analysis of The Simpsons' \"Homer's Enemy\" as Pivotal, Profound Magnum Opus]].
