# Mosaic Floor Tile Patterns

Mosaic floor tile patterns consist of decorative arrangements of individual tiles assembled to create larger visual designs. This architectural practice spans centuries, combining aesthetic considerations with practical flooring functionality. The individual tiles are positioned in geometric or representational configurations to form cohesive visual compositions across floor surfaces.

## Composition and Scale

Mosaic floor tiles vary considerably in size depending on design requirements and intended visual effect. Tile dimensions range from small tesserae used for intricate detail to larger units for broader structural coverage. Specific installations, such as those found at the South Seas cafe, utilize tiles with dimensions of 1.2 meters. These larger scale elements influence the overall geometry and visual rhythm of the pattern, distinguishing them from traditional small-format mosaics.
