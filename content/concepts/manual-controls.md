# Manual Controls

Manual controls in [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]] refer to the user's ability to directly adjust [[concepts/camera-settings|camera settings]] such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], focus, white balance, and ISO on a smartphone device, rather than relying solely on automatic modes. These controls enable photographers to have greater creative [[concepts/power|control]] over their [[concepts/images|images]] and adapt to challenging lighting conditions that automatic systems may struggle with.

## Adobe Project Indigo

[[concepts/camera-app-design|Adobe Project Indigo]] is a camera application developed by Adobe that emphasizes manual controls for advanced mobile photography. Introduced by [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]], founder of [[entities/photoshopcafe|PhotoshopCAFE]], the app addresses the limitations of standard smartphone camera interfaces by providing photographers with professional-grade manual adjustment options. This approach bridges the gap between the convenience of mobile devices and the creative control typically associated with dedicated camera equipment like DSLRs and mirrorless cameras.

The [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] of comprehensive manual controls in mobile camera apps represents a shift toward empowering users with greater technical agency. Rather than treating smartphones as point-and-shoot devices, [[concepts/software|applications]] like Adobe Project Indigo recognize that many mobile photographers seek deeper creative input over their image capture process, making manual controls an essential feature for serious mobile photography work.
