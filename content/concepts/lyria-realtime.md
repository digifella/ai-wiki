# Lyria Realtime

[[entities/lyria-realtime|Lyria Realtime]] is a music generation tool developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] as part of their [[concepts/gemini-api|Gemini API]] ecosystem. It enables real-time generation of musical content through API access, allowing developers to integrate music generation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] into their [[concepts/software|applications]] and creative tools.

## Functionality

The system generates music based on user inputs and [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], supporting creative workflows that require dynamic [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] production. Lyria Realtime operates within [[entities/google|Google]]'s broader suite of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and tools, which includes their [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/thinking-models|thinking models]] family.

## Integration

Lyria Realtime is accessible through the [[entities/gemini-api|Gemini API]], making it available to developers building applications in the creative-pursuits domain. The tool represents Google's expansion into AI-assisted music creation alongside their existing [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] offerings.
