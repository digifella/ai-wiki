# Leverage AI

Leverage AI refers to the strategic assessment and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to enhance operational [[concepts/speed|speed]] and automate routine tasks within organizations. This approach focuses on identifying high-impact [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] where AI can deliver meaningful improvements while accounting for real-world constraints. The goal is to move beyond theoretical [[concepts/software|applications]] toward practical implementations that address specific bottlenecks and pain points in existing workflows.

## Use Cases and Application Areas

Determining appropriate AI [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]] requires evaluating where [[concepts/automation|automation]] provides the greatest benefit relative to [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] complexity. Common areas include accelerating repetitive analysis work, automating grunt work that would otherwise consume significant human time, and streamlining [[concepts/document-management|document management]] and proposal processes. The effectiveness of any implementation depends on matching [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to genuine [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] problems rather than applying technology broadly.

## Managing Constraints

Organizations implementing AI must contend with [[concepts/infrastructure-limitations|infrastructure limitations]] and tool-specific issues that affect deployment and performance. Sharepoint [[concepts/integration|integration]], [[concepts/data-pipeline|data pipeline]] constraints, and the varying capabilities of different AI platforms all [[concepts/power|influence]] what's practically achievable. Successfully leveraging AI requires acknowledging these constraints upfront and working within them rather than treating them as obstacles to overcome later, which can derail otherwise promising initiatives.
