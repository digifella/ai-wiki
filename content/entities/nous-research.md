# Nous Research

Nous Research is an [[concepts/organization|organization]] involved in the development of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems, most notably [[concepts/autonomous-workflow-automation|Hermes Agent]]. The organization focuses on creating AI solutions designed to improve through self-improvement mechanisms and [[concepts/adaptive-learning|adaptive learning]] capabilities.

## Hermes Agent

Hermes Agent is a self-improving AI system developed by Nous Research. The system is designed to learn adaptively based on user interactions, with capabilities in autonomous operation and dynamic [[concepts/learning|learning]]. The project has garnered [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] within [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] communities, with documentation and discussions available through various online platforms.
