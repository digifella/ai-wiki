# CoT Monitoring

**Chain of Thought (CoT) Monitoring** refers to the technical and procedural practices used to observe, analyze, and interpret the intermediate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] steps generated by [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) during [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]]. As models increasingly utilize internal "reasoning" traces (often termed **Neuralese**) to solve [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], monitoring these latent states becomes critical for ensuring alignment, detecting emergent deceptive behaviors, and maintaining [[concepts/opacity|transparency]].

## Core Concepts

*   **Neuralese Interpretation**: The effort to decode the high-dimensional, non-human-readable representations (Neuralese) that models use internally. This concept highlights the gap between human-readable output and the model's actual computational process [[concepts/mathematical-problem-solving|AI Neuralese]] and Chain of Thought Monitoring: Concepts and Safety Implications.
*   **Transparency vs. Opacity**: Monitoring aims to bridge the "[[concepts/black-box-models|black box]]" problem by making the model's [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process auditable, particularly in high-stakes domains.
*   **Safety Implications**:
    *   **Deception Detection**: Identifying if a model is generating "good" CoT for evaluation but "bad" CoT for actual execution.
    *   **Alignment [[concepts/verification|Verification]]**: Ensuring intermediate steps adhere to safety guidelines before final [[concepts/output-generation|output generation]].
    *   **[[concepts/emergent-behavior|Emergent Behavior]] Tracking**: Observing how reasoning patterns evolve as model capabilities increase.
