# Infrastructure Limitations

The organization's current [[concepts/technical-infrastructure|technical infrastructure]] presents operational challenges that constrain efficiency and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. The [[concepts/proposal-system|proposal system]] and SharePoint platform have emerged as primary sources of [[concepts/friction|friction]], creating delays and complications in how [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] are evaluated, managed, and advanced through organizational processes.

## Proposal System Constraints

The proposal system lacks the flexibility and usability needed to support effective idea management and evaluation workflows. Users encounter difficulties submitting, tracking, and processing proposals, which slows the pace of decision-making and creates barriers to participation from team members seeking to contribute new initiatives or improvements.

## Information Management and Sharing

SharePoint's limitations as a centralized information platform restrict the organization's ability to effectively store, organize, and distribute knowledge across teams. These constraints compound communication challenges and reduce access to institutional information when needed for decision-making or collaborative work.

## Potential for Improvement

Emerging technologies, particularly AI-assisted review tools, offer potential to accelerate proposal analysis and reduce manual processing burdens. Implementation of more capable analysis systems could help mitigate some current infrastructure limitations, though broader platform modernization remains necessary to fully address organizational needs.
