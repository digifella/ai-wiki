# Ingredient Selection

Ingredient selection is the foundational step in culinary [[concepts/preparation|preparation]], determining the quality, flavor profile, and structural [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of the final dish. It involves evaluating freshness, seasonality, origin, and specific varietal characteristics.

## Key Principles
- **Freshness & Quality**: Prioritize ingredients with optimal [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], aroma, and visual appeal.
- **Seasonality**: Use produce at its peak for maximum flavor and nutritional value.
- **Source [[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]**: Verify supplier [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] and ethical sourcing practices.
