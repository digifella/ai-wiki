# Gemini 2.5 Pro

[[entities/google|Google]]'s [[entities/gemini-30-pro|advanced AI model]] featuring enhanced [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capabilities through [[concepts/deep-think]] mode, a [[concepts/1-million-token-context|1 million token context]] window (planned to expand to 2 million), and praised as fast, smart, and powerful. Successor to [[entities/nano-banana|Gemini]] 2.0, optimized for [[concepts/complex-problem-solving|complex problem-solving]].

## Deep Think Mode
- New mode leveraging [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s cutting-edge research in **[[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]**, including [[concepts/parallel-thinking|parallel thinking]] techniques
- Specifically designed to tackle highly complex problems requiring [[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]]
- Achieved [[concepts/gold-medal-standard|gold-medal standard]] at the [[concepts/international-mathematical-olympiad|International Mathematical Olympiad]] (IMO) using [[concepts/reasoning|mathematical reasoning]], demonstrating exceptional [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] capabilities (see [video](https://youtu.be/Nmv4YxpbhU8))
- [Video demonstration](https://youtu.be/-1moHqPBpjc)
