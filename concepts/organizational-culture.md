# Organizational Culture

**Organizational culture** refers to the [[concepts/cultural-ethos|shared values]], beliefs, norms, and practices that shape the behavior and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes within an organization. It influences how teams prioritize tasks, manage risk, and interact with external stakeholders.

## Key Dimensions
- **[[concepts/speed|Speed]] vs. Caution:** The tension between rapid deployment ("shipping") and rigorous safety/ethical review.
- **Decision-Making:** Centralized vs. decentralized authority structures.
- **Risk Tolerance:** Willingness to [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] with unproven methodologies.

## Case Studies in AI Development

### OpenAI vs. DeepMind
Recent analyses highlight distinct cultural divergences between major [[entities/ai-labs|AI labs]], particularly regarding product [[concepts/deployment|release]] strategies and internal hesitation.

- **[[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/shipping-culture|Shipping Culture]]:** Characterized by a "move fast" mentality, prioritizing rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] and public deployment of models to maintain [[concepts/unique-value-proposition|competitive advantage]] [[lab-notes/2026-08-25-Tibo-OpenAIs-Shipping-Culture-vs.-DeepMinds-Hesitation-o|Tibo: OpenAI's Shipping Culture vs. DeepMind's Hesitation on LMchat]].
- **[[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s Hesitation:** Historically more cautious, with a focus on research [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] and safety, leading to slower productization cycles compared to peers.
- **Impact on [[entities/lmchat|LMchat]]:** The [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] in these cultures has been cited as a factor in the differing approaches to launching [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] [[concepts/chat-interfaces|chat interfaces]], with [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]] leveraging its shipping culture for faster market entry.
