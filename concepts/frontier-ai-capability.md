# Frontier AI Capability

**[[concepts/frontier-ai|Frontier AI]] Capability** refers to the leading edge of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] performance, characterized by systems that demonstrate [[concepts/jacks-of-all-trades|generalist]] [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/complex-orchestration|complex task orchestration]], and emergent behaviors surpassing previous benchmarks. These capabilities often emerge from large-scale [[concepts/large-language-model]]s, [[concepts/multi-agent-systems]], and advanced AI Alignment techniques.

## Key Developments

### Multi-Agent Orchestration
Recent advancements highlight the shift from single-[[concepts/inference|model inference]] to coordinated [[concepts/multi-agent-frameworks|multi-agent frameworks]], which enhance reasoning depth and task completion rates.

- **[[entities/sakana-fugu|Sakana Fugu]] System**: A [[concepts/desktop-ai-platform|multi-agent orchestration system]] developed by [[entities/sakana-ai|Sakana AI]] (Japanese lab).
	- **Performance Benchmark**: The **[[concepts/fugu-ultra|Fugu Ultra]]** model variant demonstrates [[concepts/performance-matching|performance parity]] with **[[concepts/claude-fable-5|Fable 5]]** in specific intelligence tasks, achieving comparable results without relying on the [[concepts/fable-5-model|Fable 5]] architecture.
	- **Significance**: Represents a breakthrough in efficient [[concepts/ai-orchestration|multi-agent coordination]], suggesting that orchestration layers can match or exceed specialized single-model capabilities in certain domains.
	- **Source Analysis**: Detailed breakdown available in [[lab-notes/2026-06-23-Sakana-Fugu-Multi-Agent-AI-Matching-Fable-5-Performance|Sakana Fugu: Multi-Agent AI Matching Fable 5 Performance]].
