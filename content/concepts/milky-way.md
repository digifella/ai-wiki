# Milky Way

The Milky Way is a barred spiral galaxy containing our solar system. It is composed of stars, gas, dust, and a significant concentration of [[concepts/dark-matter]], which is estimated to be six times more abundant than regular [[concepts/baryonic-matter|baryonic matter]].
