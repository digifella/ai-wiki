# Baking Soda for Ground Meat

## Introduction
[[concepts/baking-soda|Baking soda]] is a common household ingredient with surprising culinary [[concepts/software|applications]]. When used in the right quantities and methods, it can significantly enhance the [[concepts/texture|texture]], juiciness, and browning of [[concepts/ground-meat|ground meat]].

## Key Benefits
- **Texture:** Improves the chewability and reduces toughness.
- **Juiciness:** Increases moisture retention during cooking.
- **Browning:** Enhances surface color for an appetizing appearance.

## Methods
1. Mix a small amount (1/4 teaspoon per pound) of baking soda with ground meat before forming into patties or adding to recipes.
2. Allow the mixture to sit for [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] least 15 minutes but not more than an hour before cooking.
3. Rinse thoroughly under cold water and pat dry before cooking.
