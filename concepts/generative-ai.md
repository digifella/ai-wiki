# Generative AI

Generative AI refers to [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] technologies that can produce new and original content autonomously, such as images, text, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], video, and more. This concept encompasses a wide range of applications from creative industries like design, marketing, and entertainment to technical fields including [[concepts/coding|software development]] and scientific research.

- **[[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]]** and **[[concepts/diffusion-models|Diffusion Models]]** are core architectures enabling text, image, and [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
- **Applications** span [[concepts/coding|coding assistants]], [[concepts/video-avatar|video avatar cloning]], and [[concepts/spatial-intelligence|spatial intelligence]] for [[concepts/robotics|robotics]].
- **[[concepts/trust|Trust]] & [[concepts/verification|Verification]]**: As generative capabilities advance, distinguishing [[concepts/ai-content-creation|synthetic content]] from human-made or natural data becomes critical. This includes:
    - Digital media verification (text, image, video).
    - [[concepts/biological-sequence-analysis|Biological sequence analysis]] (distinguishing AI-generated DNA/protein sequences from natural ones).
    - See [[lab-notes/2026-10-02-AI-Watermarking-Distinguishing-AI-Generated-Content-and|AI Watermarking: Distinguishing AI-Generated Content and Biological Sequences]] for detailed methodologies on [[concepts/deepfake-detection|deepfake detection]] and bio-sequence watermarking.
