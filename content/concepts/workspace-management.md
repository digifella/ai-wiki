# Workspace Management

Workspace management refers to the organizational features and practices used to [[concepts/structure|structure]] and organize work within [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]] like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]]. It encompasses the tools and approaches that help users keep projects, files, and tasks organized in a way that supports productivity and clarity, particularly for those new to [[concepts/coding|coding]] or development work.

In the context of Claude Code, workspace management becomes especially important for non-technical users who may be unfamiliar with traditional [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]]. A well-organized workspace allows users to understand what files exist in their project, how they relate to one another, and what work has been completed or remains to be done. This includes managing the visibility and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of different files and projects within the system.

Effective workspace management helps reduce cognitive load by presenting information in a structured, understandable way. Rather than overwhelming users with technical details, good workspace organization surfaces the elements most relevant to the task at hand, making it easier for non-technical users to focus on their work and follow along with what Claude Code is doing on their behalf.
