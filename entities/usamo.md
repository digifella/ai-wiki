# USAMO

The [[entities/united-states|United States of America]] Mathematical Olympiad (USAMO) is an annual high school [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] competition administered by the Mathematical Association of [[entities/america|America]] (MAA). It serves as the final stage of the AMC series, following the AMC 10/12 and AIME, and selects the top students for the USA Mathematical Olympiad team that competes in the [[concepts/international-mathematical-olympiad|International Mathematical Olympiad]] (IMO). The competition emphasizes rigorous proof-based [[concepts/problem-solving|problem solving]] in algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and [[concepts/number-theory|number theory]].
