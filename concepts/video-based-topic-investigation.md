# Video Based Topic Investigation

Video Based Topic Investigation is a multimodal research tool designed to extract and analyze information directly from video content. By processing visual and textual data simultaneously, the system captures insights from visual context, spoken dialogue, and on-screen text. This approach enables a more comprehensive analysis than traditional text-only research methods, allowing for the interpretation of complex video formats that rely heavily on non-verbal cues.

The architecture is built using LangGraph and leverages Google's Gemini 2.5 models to handle the computational requirements of multimodal processing. This combination allows the tool to maintain state across complex workflows, ensuring that contextual relationships between visual elements and audio transcripts are preserved during analysis. The system is demonstrated through a specific implementation that highlights its capability to navigate and interpret dynamic video data.

In the domain of creative pursuits, this tool facilitates deeper engagement with video-based media by providing structured data derived from unstructured visual inputs. It supports researchers and creators in identifying patterns, themes, and key moments that might be overlooked in manual review. The technology serves as a bridge between raw video files and actionable insights, enhancing the efficiency of content analysis workflows.
