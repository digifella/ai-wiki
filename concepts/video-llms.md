# Video LLMs

[[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) extended to process and [[concepts/purpose|reason]] about [[concepts/video-resource|video content]], enabling [[concepts/spatial-temporal-object-understanding|spatial-temporal object understanding]] and fine-grained video comprehension.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/spatial-temporal-object-understanding|Spatial-temporal object understanding]]**: Tracking objects across video frames with temporal context
- **Video grounding**: Linking language queries to specific objects in video sequences
- **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal integration]]**: Combining visual, temporal, and linguistic data streams
