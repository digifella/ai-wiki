# Musical harmony

The simultaneous sounding of different notes to create Chords and provide vertical [[concepts/structure|structure]] to a [[concepts/melody|Melody]].

### Core Principles
- Chords serve as the foundational building blocks of harmonic [[concepts/structure|structure]].
- Harmonic progression provides emotional depth and character to a [[concepts/melody|Melody]].
- Harmony is fundamentally shaped by the structural [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between Chords and [[concepts/musical-scales|Scales]].
