# Danish Government

The Danish Government functions as the executive branch of Denmark's constitutional [[concepts/monarchy|monarchy]], led by the [[concepts/prime-lens|Prime]] Minister and composed of various ministries responsible for implementing state policy and administering public affairs. These ministries oversee critical sectors such as finance, defense, [[concepts/health|health]], education, and the environment, ensuring the operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of the state within its established legal framework.

In the realm of [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]] management, the government has implemented specific [[concepts/policies|policies]] regarding archaeological [[concepts/preservation|preservation]]. Notably, it has authorized private [[concepts/metal|metal]] detectorists to search farm fields for archaeological artifacts, balancing the [[concepts/secure|protection]] of historical sites with the interests of amateur archaeologists. This approach aims to document and preserve Denmark's material history while managing the impact of unauthorized digging.
