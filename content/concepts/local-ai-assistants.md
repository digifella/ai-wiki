# Local AI Assistants

Local AI assistants are [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] designed to run on personal devices rather than relying solely on cloud-based servers. These systems prioritize user [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], offline functionality, and reduced latency by processing data locally. Local AI assistants can perform tasks such as answering questions, managing schedules, controlling smart home devices, and providing [[concepts/writing|writing]] assistance without requiring constant internet connectivity.

## OpenClaw

OpenClaw is a [[concepts/remote-chat|local AI assistant]] that was previously known as ClawdBot or MoltBot. The project represents an effort to provide users with an alternative to cloud-dependent AI systems, emphasizing [[concepts/local-computation|local computation]] and data retention on the user's own [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. Information about OpenClaw's capabilities and development has been documented in video analysis and technical discussions within the AI community.
