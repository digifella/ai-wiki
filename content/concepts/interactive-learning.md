# Interactive Learning

Interactive learning is an educational approach that transforms static information sources into dynamic, engaging [[concepts/learning|learning]] experiences. In the context of digital education, this involves converting research [[concepts/notes|notes]] and reference materials into structured [[concepts/online-courses|online courses]] where learners can actively participate, ask questions, and build understanding through exploration rather than passive consumption.

## Integration with NotebookLM and Google Classroom

[[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] notebooks can serve as the foundational content layer for interactive online courses when combined with [[entities/google-classroom|Google Classroom]]. A fully populated NotebookLM [[concepts/notebook|notebook]]—containing organized sources, generated study guides, and synthesized notes—provides the instructional material that educators can then [[concepts/structure|structure]] into a pedagogically sound course format. Google Classroom acts as the delivery and management platform, allowing instructors to sequence content, add assignments, facilitate discussion, and track student progress.

## Implementation Process

The transformation from notebook to course requires [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] of the source material, strategic [[concepts/organization|organization]] of content into learning modules, and thoughtful [[concepts/design|design]] of interactive elements such as quizzes, discussion prompts, and collaborative activities. This approach leverages the AI-assisted note synthesis [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of NotebookLM to reduce instructor preparation time while maintaining the flexibility and [[concepts/customization|customization]] that Google Classroom provides for different learning groups and pedagogical goals.
