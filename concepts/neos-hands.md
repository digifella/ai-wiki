# NEO's Hands

**[[entities/neos-hands|NEO's Hands]]** refers to the advanced robotic hand platform developed by [[entities/1x-technologies|1X Technologies]], designed to replicate human dexterity through biomimetic [[entities/national-academies|engineering]]. The system emphasizes natural [[concepts/exercise|movement]] patterns and tactile [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], distinguishing itself from traditional rigid robotic manipulators.

## Technical Design & Biomimicry
*   **Biomimetic Architecture:** The design prioritizes biological accuracy, mimicking the tendon-driven structure of the human hand rather than relying solely on motorized joints at each knuckle.
*   **Dexterity Focus:** Engineered to handle complex, fine-motor tasks that require adaptive grip and precise force modulation.
*   **Integration:** Part of a broader ecosystem of robotic limbs aimed at restoring or enhancing human physical capabilities.
