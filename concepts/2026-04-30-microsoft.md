# 2026 04 30 Microsoft

[[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] is a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington, founded in 1975. As of April 2026, the company operates across multiple business segments including cloud computing through [[entities/azure|Azure]], productivity software via [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]], gaming through Xbox, professional networking via LinkedIn, and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] services.

## Strategic Priorities

Microsoft's business strategy as of 2026 centers on three interconnected domains. Cloud infrastructure remains foundational to the company's growth trajectory, with Azure serving enterprise customers and supporting the deployment of AI workloads at scale. Artificial intelligence integration across product lines represents a major strategic initiative, including deployment of language models and generative AI capabilities within enterprise software and consumer applications. Quantum computing research constitutes a longer-term strategic investment, with Microsoft pursuing quantum development through its Quantum division alongside academic and industry partnerships.

## Market Position

The company maintains significant competitive positions across its operating segments, competing with other major technology firms in cloud services, enterprise software, and emerging technology domains. Microsoft's business model emphasizes recurring revenue through subscription services, particularly through Azure and Microsoft 365, while also deriving income from licensing, gaming, and advertising platforms.
