# Anthropic Claude

Anthropic Claude is a large language model developed by Anthropic, an artificial intelligence company that prioritizes safety and alignment in its research. Designed as a conversational AI assistant, the model is engineered to handle a diverse array of tasks, including writing, data analysis, coding, research, and creative projects.
