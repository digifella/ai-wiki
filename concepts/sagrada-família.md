# Sagrada Família

**Sagrada Família** (Full Name: *Basílica de la Sagrada Família*) is a large unfinished minor basilica in the Eixample district of Barcelona, Spain. Designed by Catalan architect [[concepts/antoni-gaudí|Antoni Gaudí]] (1852–1926), it is his magnum opus and a UNESCO World Heritage Site.

## Key Characteristics
- **Architectural Style:** Synthesis of Gothic architecture and Art Nouveau, characterized by organic forms and complex geometry.
- **Status:** Construction began in 1882; as of 2026, it remains unfinished, with completion projected for the centenary of Gaudí's death (2026–2030).
- **Funding:** Relies entirely on private donations and ticket sales; no public funds are used.
