# Joggai

[[entities/joggai|Joggai]] is an [[concepts/ai-bots|automated software]] tool designed to convert [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]] into video episodes. The platform addresses a workflow challenge common in digital media production, where creators maintain separate distribution channels for audio and video formats of the same content. By automating the technical conversion process, Joggai reduces the manual labor traditionally required to repurpose podcast audio into video-ready content.

## Functionality

The tool streamlines the conversion of audio podcast files into video formats suitable for platforms that prioritize or require visual content. This automation allows creators to maintain a consistent presence across multiple media types without the resource-intensive process of manually generating video assets for each audio [[concepts/deployment|release]]. The system focuses on [[concepts/technical-efficiency|technical efficiency]], enabling the rapid transformation of existing audio libraries into video episodes for broader distribution.
