# Sunshine Superhub

The **Sunshine Superhub** is a major multimodal transport interchange in [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]], Victoria, serving as a critical [[entities/nodejs|node]] in the Melbourne Rail Network. It integrates heavy rail, tram, bus, and future [[concepts/melbourne-airport-rail-link]] services, positioning Sunshine, Victoria as a key western [[concepts/gateway|gateway]].

## Melbourne Airport Rail Link Integration

The completion of the [[concepts/melbourne-airport-rail-link]] is central to the Superhub's evolution into a true airport gateway. Recent developments highlight the following:

- **Status Update:** As of August 2026, progress on the link continues, with significant focus on resolving Airport [[concepts/connection|Connection]] Challenges related to integration with the existing network at Sunshine.
- **Strategic Context:** Melbourne remains an outlier among major Australian capital cities for lacking a direct rail link to its airport, a gap this project aims to close.
