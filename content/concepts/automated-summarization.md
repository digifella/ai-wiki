# Automated Summarization

Automated [[concepts/summarization|Summarization]] is a subfield of [[concepts/natural-language-processing]] ([[concepts/nlp|NLP]]) that involves condensing long texts into shorter versions while retaining key information. It is widely used in [[concepts/knowledge-bases|Information Retrieval]], [[concepts/content-creation]], and [[concepts/knowledge-management]].

## Types of Automated Summarization

- **Extractive Summarization**: Selects and combines sentences or phrases from the original [[concepts/text|text]].
- **Abstractive Summarization**: Generates new sentences that capture the essence of the original text.

## Applications

- **News Summarization**: Condensing news articles into brief summaries.
- **[[concepts/document-summarization|Document Summarization]]**: Summarizing research papers, reports, and other long documents.
- **[[concepts/podcast-summarization|Podcast Summarization]]**: Automatically generating summaries of podcast episodes.

## Challenges

- **Context [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Ensuring the [[concepts/summary|summary]] retains the context and meaning of the original text.
- **Bias and Fairness**: Avoiding [[concepts/biases|biases]] in the summarization process.
- **Multilingual Summarization**: Handling summarization across different languages.
