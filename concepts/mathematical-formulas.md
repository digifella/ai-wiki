# Mathematical Conjectures & Formulas

Mathematical formulas serve as precise symbolic representations of [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between quantities and concepts. They form the foundation of formal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] across pure [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], applied sciences, and computational disciplines. In fields like [[concepts/cryptography]] and [[concepts/number-theory]], formulas become essential tools for understanding the underlying structures that [[concepts/secure|secure]] modern communication and protect sensitive information.

## Role in Mathematics and Logic

Formulas translate abstract mathematical [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] into a standardized notation that enables rigorous [[concepts/proof|proof]] and [[concepts/verification|verification]]. They allow mathematicians to express complex relationships compactly and manipulate them according to logical rules. This symbolic language has proven invaluable for establishing theorems, solving equations, and exploring mathematical structures with [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]].

## Notable Examples

The [[concepts/riemann-hypothesis]] stands as one of mathematics' most significant [[concepts/unsolved-problems|unsolved problems]]. Proposed by [[entities/bernhard-riemann|Bernhard Riemann]] in 1859, it concerns the distribution of [[concepts/prime-numbers]] and the non-trivial zeros of the [[concepts/riemann-zeta-function|Riemann zeta function]].
