# Prosperity

State of flourishing, material abundance, and high well-being; encompasses economic stability, infrastructure quality, social vitality, and [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] against systemic shocks.

## Core Dimensions
- **Economic:** Sustainable growth, asset accumulation, diversified trade, income [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].
- **Infrastructure:** Modern utilities, transportation networks, architectural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], [[concepts/urban-planning|urban planning]].
- **Social:** [[concepts/health|Health]] outcomes, educational access, cultural vibrancy, political stability.

## Historical Dynamics & Vulnerability
- Prosperity is non-linear; subject to reversal via geopolitical shifts, policy failures, or resource mismanagement.
- Correlation exists between institutional stability and long-term wealth [[concepts/preservation|preservation]].

## Case Study: Havana
- **Pre-1959 Era:** [[entities/havana|Havana]], [[entities/cuba|Cuba]] demonstrated significant prosperity, characterized by rapid modernization and urban sophistication, earning the moniker "Paris of the Caribbean".
- **Disruption:** [[concepts/revolution|Revolution]] in 1959 precipitated structural upheaval, severing existing economic patterns.
- **Decline:** Post-revolution trajectory marked by severe Economic Decline, infrastructure decay, and loss of prior prosperity metrics.
