# Zs300tz300

The [[entities/zs200|Panasonic ZS300]] (known as TZ300 in some markets) is a compact [[concepts/point-and-shoot-camera|superzoom camera]] that received examination in a PetaPixel video review. The review, titled "The SUPERZOOM Point and Shoot is Back... but at What Cost?", analyzes the camera's position in the market roughly eight years after the previous generation's release.

## Upgrade Assessment

According to the review, the ZS300 represents a modest generational update with minimal advancements in superzoom capabilities relative to its predecessor. The camera maintains the superzoom point-and-shoot formula but comes at a higher price point than previous models, raising questions about value proposition for potential buyers in an increasingly crowded compact camera market.
