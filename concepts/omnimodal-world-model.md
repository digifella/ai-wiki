# Omnimodal World Model

## Overview
An **Omnimodal [[concepts/joint-embedding-predictive-architecture-jepa|World Model]]** is a foundational [[concepts/ai-system|AI architecture]] capable of processing, understanding, and generating data across multiple modalities ([[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], language, [[concepts/physics|physics]], control signals) to simulate and predict physical [[entities/earth|world]] dynamics. Unlike unimodal models, these systems unify perception and action, serving as the core cognitive [[concepts/engine|engine]] for **[[concepts/physical-ai]]** and advanced [[concepts/robotics|robotics]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Multimodal Unification**: Simultaneous ingestion of visual, textual, proprioceptive, and force-[[concepts/feedback|feedback]] data.
- **Generative [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]]**: Ability to predict future states or generate synthetic trajectories for planning.
- **[[concepts/abstraction|Generalization]]**: [[concepts/transfer-learning|Transfer learning]] capabilities across diverse physical environments and robot morphologies.
- **Embodied [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]**: Grounding abstract concepts in physical constraints and interactions.
