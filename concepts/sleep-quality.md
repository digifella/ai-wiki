# Sleep Quality

[[concepts/sleep|Sleep]] quality is a critical aspect of overall [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]], affecting cognitive function, mood, [[concepts/physical-health|physical health]], and emotional stability. It encompasses the depth and restfulness of sleep rather than just the duration.

## Key Points

- Adequate sleep is essential for optimal brain development during adolescence.
- Poor sleep can lead to decreased mental [[concepts/lightroom|clarity]], mood swings, and reduced [[concepts/immune-system-function|immune system function]].
- Regular [[concepts/exercise|exercise]] promotes better sleep quality by helping regulate [[concepts/circadian-rhythms|circadian rhythms]].
- Establishing a consistent bedtime routine enhances sleep efficiency.
- Cognitive behavioral therapy for insomnia (CBT-I) is an effective treatment for chronic sleep problems.
