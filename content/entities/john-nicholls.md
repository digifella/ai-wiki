# John Nicholls

John Nicholls is a key figure in the development of Nvidia [[concepts/cuda|CUDA]] ([[concepts/compute|Compute]] Unified Device [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]), a platform that transformed [[concepts/graphics-processing-units-gpus|Graphics Processing Units (GPUs)]] into powerful tools for general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] computing. Along with [[entities/ian-buck|Ian Buck]], he contributed significantly to this groundbreaking technology.

## Contributions
- **CUDA Development:** Critical work on CUDA, launched by Nvidia in 2007.
- **[[concepts/general-purpose-computing|Parallel Computing]] Revolution:** Enabled GPUs to perform non-graphical tasks efficiently and effectively.
