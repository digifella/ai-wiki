# healthcare sector

The [[concepts/health|healthcare]] sector encompasses the provision of [[concepts/health-care|health services]] to individuals, including diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]]. It is characterized by complex stakeholder interactions, regulatory constraints, and rapid technological integration.

## Key Sub-sectors and Dynamics

- **[[concepts/clinical-care|Clinical Services]]**: Direct [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]] delivered by physicians, nurses, and [[concepts/ahps|allied health professionals]].
- **[[concepts/digital-innovations|Digital Transformation]]**: The integration of electronic [[concepts/patient-information|health records]] (EHR), [[concepts/telehealth|telehealth]], and interoperable data systems to improve efficiency and outcomes.
- **[[concepts/allied-health|Allied Health]] Infrastructure**: Support services such as physiotherapy, radiology, and laboratory services, which are critical for holistic patient management.

## Recent Developments and Research

- **[[concepts/digital-readiness|Digital Readiness]] Challenges**: Recent analysis highlights significant gaps in [[entities/digital-readiness|digital readiness]] within the allied [[concepts/health-sector|health sector]]. Key issues include:
    - Variability in access to centralized systems like [[entities/my-health-record]] among healthcare providers.
    - Inconsistent consumer uptake and engagement with [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] records.
    - Technical and procedural barriers to seamless data exchange between primary care and [[concepts/allied-health-services|allied health disciplines]].
