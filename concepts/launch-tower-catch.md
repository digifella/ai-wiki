# Launch Tower Catch

The **launch tower catch** refers to the mechanical system designed to arrest the ascent of a [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] Starship booster during its return to the launch site, enabling rapid reusability. This mechanism is critical for the operational viability of the Starship program, allowing the Super Heavy booster to be caught by the "Mechazilla" tower arms immediately after engine cutoff.

## Key Developments

- **Flight 14 Milestone**: The concept is directly tied to the operational readiness demonstrated in [[concepts/starship-flight-14|SpaceX Starship Flight 14]]: First Orbital Mission, Starlink V3 Deployment, which marked the first full orbital mission.
- **Mission Context**: Flight 14 targeted a launch date of September 22, 2026, pending regulatory approval, aiming to deploy [[concepts/starlink-v3]] satellites and achieve full orbital [[concepts/velocity|velocity
- **Orbital Insertion**: Flight 14 achieved the vehicle's first successful orbital insertion, validating the Super Heavy booster's performance envelope prior to the catch maneuver.
- **Starlink Deployment**: The mission successfully deployed 26 [[concepts/starlink-v3|Starlink V3]] satellites, confirming the payload integration and deployment mechanisms.
- **Controlled Splashdowns**: Post-catch procedures included controlled splashdowns for booster components, providing critical data on reentry dynamics and landing accuracy.
