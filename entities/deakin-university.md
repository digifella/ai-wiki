# Deakin University

Deakin University is an Australian public research university based in Victoria. The institution operates multiple campuses across the state and is recognized as a comprehensive university offering undergraduate and postgraduate programs across a wide range of disciplines.

## Research Focus

Deakin University maintains a significant research focus on digital health and mental health research. The university has developed particular expertise in examining the intersection of digital technologies with psychological wellbeing and health outcomes. This research orientation reflects institutional priorities in understanding how technology adoption influences individual and population health.

## Academic Profile

The university is associated with researchers and academics working across health sciences, psychology, and technology-related fields. Faculty members contribute to investigations into the application of digital tools in healthcare delivery and mental health support services.
