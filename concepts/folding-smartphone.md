# Folding Smartphone

A mobile device form factor that utilizes a flexible display to fold into a more compact size. While historically dominated by [[entities/android|Android]] manufacturers, the market landscape shifted significantly in 2026 with the entry of major traditional smartphone vendors.

## Key Developments

- **[[entities/apple|Apple]]'s Entry into Foldables:** The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of the [[concepts/iphone-duo]] marked Apple's first foray into the folding smartphone market, announced during the [[entities/iphone-18-pro|iPhone 18 Pro]] and Duo: [[concepts/apple-fall-event|Apple Fall Event]] Recap and Impressions in September 2026.
- **Market Impact:** Apple's introduction of the [[entities/iphone-duo|iPhone Duo]] alongside the [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] 18 Pro signaled a major validation of the folding form factor for mainstream consumers, previously considered a niche segment.
- **Technical Challenges:** Folding screens continue to address issues regarding hinge durability, crease visibility, and software adaptation for dynamic aspect ratios.
