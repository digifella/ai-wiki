# Colonialism

[[concepts/colonialism]] is the practice of a power extending its control over foreign territories and resources to expand economic and political [[concepts/power|influence]].

## Commodity-Driven Expansion
- The evolution of [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] from its ancient origins into a ubiquitous [[concepts/global-commodity]] serves as a primary example of imperial trade expansion.
- The development of sugar as a central [[concepts/colonial-commodity|colonial commodity]] was inextricably linked to the establishment of plantation economies and the history of enslavement.
