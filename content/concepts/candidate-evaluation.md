# Candidate Evaluation

Candidate evaluation is a critical process in human resources that involves assessing prospective employees to determine their suitability for a position within an [[concepts/organization|organization]]. With the advent of [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] (GenAI), traditional methods of evaluating candidates have become increasingly complex, as GenAI can assist candidates in crafting polished and seemingly perfect [[concepts/responses|responses]] during interviews.

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/interview-techniques|Interview Techniques]]:** Effective strategies to evaluate a candidate's true [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
- **GenAI [[concepts/power|Influence]]:** The impact of Generative AI on [[concepts/interview-preparation|interview preparation]] and performance.
- **Genuine Expertise Identification:** Methods for discerning actual [[concepts/skill|skill]] levels from AI-assisted perfection.

### Summary
The following are essential points derived from the research and discussions within the community:

- Candidates using GenAI for [[concepts/interview-preparation|interview preparation]] often receive higher ratings due to their polished [[concepts/responses|responses]].
- Hiring managers face a challenge in distinguishing between genuine expertise and superficially "perfect" answers generated with AI assistance.
- Effective questioning can reveal a candidate's true understanding and experience, beyond what AI can simulate.
