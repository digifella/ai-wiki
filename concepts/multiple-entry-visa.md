# Multiple Entry Visa

A multiple entry visa is a travel document that permits the holder to enter and exit a country on more than one occasion during the visa's validity period. Unlike single entry visas, which become invalid after one border crossing, multiple entry visas allow repeated journeys into and out of the country without requiring a new visa application for each trip. This type of visa is commonly issued to frequent travelers, business professionals, and individuals who plan several visits within a designated timeframe.

## Duration and Conditions

The validity period of a multiple entry visa varies by country and visa category. Some visas remain valid for a fixed duration—typically ranging from several months to multiple years—during which an unlimited number of entries are permitted. Others may specify a maximum number of entries within the validity period, such as double or triple entry visas. Each entry usually grants a specific duration of stay, which may be the same or different from the visa's overall validity period.

## Application and Requirements

Multiple entry visas typically require applicants to demonstrate a genuine need for multiple visits, such as employment contracts, business correspondence, or family ties. Documentation standards and approval criteria vary significantly between countries. Processing times and fees are generally similar to those for single entry visas, though some countries charge higher fees for multiple entry privileges. Applicants should verify current requirements with the relevant embassy or consulate, as policies change regularly.
