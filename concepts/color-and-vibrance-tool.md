# Color And Vibrance Tool

The Color and Vibrance tool is a non-destructive adjustment layer introduced in Photoshop 2025 that provides refined control over color saturation and tonal intensity. As an adjustment layer, modifications made through this tool do not permanently alter the underlying image data, preserving the original pixel information and enabling flexible editing workflows. This approach allows users to revisit and modify color adjustments at any point during the post-production process without degrading image quality.

## Saturation vs. Vibrance

The tool distinguishes between global saturation and selective vibrance to offer more nuanced color management. While traditional saturation adjustments affect all colors in an image uniformly, often leading to oversaturation in already vibrant areas, the vibrance function intelligently boosts less saturated colors while protecting skin tones and existing vivid hues. This selective approach helps maintain natural-looking results and prevents the clipping of highlights or shadows that can occur with aggressive global saturation increases.

## Workflow Integration

As part of the 2025 update, this tool integrates into the standard adjustment layer stack, allowing it to be stacked with other corrections such as curves or levels. Users can toggle the visibility of the layer to compare adjustments against the original image and use layer masks to restrict the effect to specific regions. This non-destructive nature ensures that creative decisions remain reversible, supporting iterative editing processes common in professional photo retouching and digital art.
