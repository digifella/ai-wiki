🗂️ AI & Agents · View mindmap

What you’ll now see:

  • Every profile card shows either “N intel” (green) or ”⚠ no intel” (amber) — automatically updated after every save, create, or CSV import
  • A ”⚠ No Intel (N)” filter tab appears in the profile type bar, showing exactly how many profiles have no matched intel — click it to see just those profiles
  • No button needed — the count is live every time profiles load

On linking: the match is still name-based (shared_intel.stakeholder_name = stakeholder_profiles.canonical_name, case-insensitive). If a profile’s name doesn’t exactly match the intel entry’s name, it’ll show “no intel” even if you have related entries. You can fix mismatches by editing either the profile’s canonical name or the intel entry’s stakeholder name.
