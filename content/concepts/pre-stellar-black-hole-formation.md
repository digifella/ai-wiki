# Pre-Stellar Black Hole Formation

Formation of [[concepts/black-holes|Black Holes]] via mechanisms independent of standard [[concepts/stellar-evolution|stellar evolution]], enabling existence prior to or concurrent with the earliest Stellar Populations in the [[concepts/early-universe]]. Resolves tension between observed high-redshift supermassive black holes and insufficient [[concepts/cosmic-time|cosmic time]] for seed growth from [[concepts/compact-stellar-objects|stellar remnants]].

## Formation Channels
- Direct Collapse Black Hole (DCBH): Monolithic collapse of metal-free gas clouds into $\sim 10^4-10^5 M_{\odot}$ seeds, bypassing fragmentation.
- [[concepts/primordial-black-hole]] (PBH): Collapse of primordial density fluctuations; candidates for [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]] or early seeds.
- Runaway collisions in dense Globular Cluster cores or proto-[[concepts/dense-gas-clouds|star-forming regions]].

## JWST Observational Constraints
- [[concepts/little-red-dots]] ([[concepts/lrds|LRDs]]) detected since 2022 exhibit compact morphology and high luminosity inconsistent with star formation alone.
- X-ray emission analysis confirms hard spectra from [[concepts/lrd-little-red-dot|LRDs]], indicative of accretion onto compact objects formed before widespread stellar ignition [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-JWST-X-Ray-Discovery-LRD-Links-to-Pre-Stellar-Black-Hole|JWST X-Ray Discovery: LRD Links to Pre-Stellar Black Hole Formation]].
- Spectral energy [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] and variability support pre-stellar origin over heavily obscured [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|AGN]] or stellar bursts.
- [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]] analysis (2026) synthesizes X-ray data to demonstrate black hole precedence over [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|first stars]].

## Implications
- Necessitates non-stellar seed formation to explain $z > 6$ supermassive black holes.
- Constrains reionization history and early [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] mechanisms.
