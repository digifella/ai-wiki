# Core Purpose

Core purpose is defined as the fundamental [[concepts/purpose|reason]] an individual or organization exists, extending beyond the [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of profit or the [[concepts/success|achievement]] of specific, finite goals. It represents the underlying "why" that drives [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], motivates action, and provides long-term direction. In the context of business strategy, understanding this core purpose is considered essential for sustaining [[concepts/motivation|motivation]] and ensuring that daily activities align with deeper values.

The concept is often explored through [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] frameworks, such as [[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]’s [[concepts/story-method|Story Method]], which is highlighted in discussions by [[entities/high-performance|High Performance]]. This approach suggests that uncovering core purpose requires examining the narrative underlying why an entity exists. By analyzing this story, individuals and organizations can identify the consistent themes and motivations that define their identity, thereby creating a clearer path for strategic alignment and meaningful work.
