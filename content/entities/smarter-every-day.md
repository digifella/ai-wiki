Smarter Every Day is a YouTube channel created by [[entities/destin-sandlin]], known for scientific [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] and hands-on experiments exploring everyday phenomena.

- [[entities/destin-sandlin]] conducted a [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] (2022-[[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]]) to manufacture the [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] (a safer, more durable grill brush) entirely within the [[entities/united-states|United States]], documented in the video [Destin - manufacturing in America](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ZTGwcHQfLY) (2026-04-14). The project aimed to prove American-made products can compete in the market, motivated by his parents' history as [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]].
- The experiment covered sourcing materials, production challenges, and market competition, highlighting the complexities of [[concepts/domestic-manufacturing|domestic manufacturing]].
