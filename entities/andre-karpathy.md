# Andre Karpathy

Andre Karpathy is a computer scientist and AI researcher known for his work in deep learning, computer vision, and neural networks. He has held significant research and engineering positions at major technology organizations, including OpenAI and Tesla, where he focused on visual perception systems and large-scale AI model development.

## Research and Contributions

Karpathy has made notable contributions to the field of computer vision and deep learning. His work has encompassed neural network architectures, training methodologies, and practical applications of machine learning systems. He has been involved in developing systems for visual recognition and perception tasks that operate at scale.

## Architectural Concepts

Karpathy has advocated for conceptual frameworks in AI systems design, including the idea of AI-maintained knowledge bases and layered AI context architectures. These proposals address challenges in organizing and retrieving information within large language models and AI systems, suggesting approaches to improve how AI systems manage and utilize contextual information.
