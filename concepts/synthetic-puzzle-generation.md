# Synthetic Data

**Synthetic Data** refers to the automated creation of logical, spatial, or pattern-based problems designed to evaluate or train [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], particularly in the context of **Fluid Intelligence** and **General [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AGI)** benchmarks. Unlike static datasets, synthetic puzzles allow for infinite variation and specific testing of [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] rather than memorization.

## Key Applications & Benchmarks

*   **ARC-AGI Challenge**: A primary benchmark for testing fluid intelligence by requiring models to generalize from [[concepts/few-shot-examples|few-shot examples]] to novel tasks.
*   **[[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] & [[concepts/spatial-intelligence|Spatial Intelligence]]**: Synthetic environments are critical for solving data [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] in robotics. By generating vast amounts of simulated spatial interactions, AI systems can develop robust physical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and manipulation [[concepts/skills|skills]] without the high costs and risks of [[concepts/real-world-data|real-world data]] collection. This approach is central to [[concepts/world-ai|World AI]]'s strategy following their acquisition of Scenix, focusing on bridging the gap between digital [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] and physical execution [[lab-notes/2026-08-02-Fei-Fei-Li-Spatial-Intelligence-Solves-Robotics-Data-Sca|Fei-Fei Li: Spatial Intelligence Solves Robotics Data Scarcity]].

## Strategic Shifts

*   **Curated Real-World Data**: Recent [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier models]] demonstrate a divergence from pure synthetic generation towards curated real-world data and [[concepts/hill-climbing-optimization|hill-climbing optimization]].
*   **Hybrid Approaches**: The integration of synthetic spatial data with real-world robotics applications suggests a [[concepts/hybrid-model|hybrid model]] where simulation accelerates initial [[concepts/learning|learning]], while real-world interaction refines fine-grained physical understanding.
