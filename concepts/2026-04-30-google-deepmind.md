The consolidation aimed to streamline research pipelines and accelerate the deployment of advanced models across Alphabet's product ecosystem. By combining DeepMind's expertise in [[concepts/algorithm-optimization|algorithmic efficiency]] and [[concepts/true-intelligence|general intelligence]] with Google's [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and data scale, the unified entity seeks to bridge the gap between theoretical breakthroughs and practical applications.
