# Interactive Travel Content

Digital resources enabling dynamic engagement with travel information beyond static guides, allowing real-time [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] and actionable [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] during trip planning.

Key implementation methods:
- [[entities/notebooklm]] for curating trusted research (articles, PDFs, websites) into structured destination [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]]
- [[entities/gemini]] for generating interactive content (personalized itineraries, restaurant recommendations, schedule checks) from curated research
- Combines to transform research into shareable, actionable experiences addressing post-booking overwhelm (e.g., exploring towns, finding eateries, verifying schedules)

Solves core problem: Overwhelming detailed planning after flight booking (0:00-0:19 in [video demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Lzi0JTtjuAs))
