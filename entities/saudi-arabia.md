## Saudi Arabia

### Overview
Saudi Arabia is a country located in Western Asia on the Arabian Peninsula. It borders [[entities/jordan|Jordan]], Iraq, [[entities/kuwait|Kuwait]], Qatar, Bahrain, the [[entities/uae|United Arab Emirates]], Oman, and Yemen.

### Economy
The [[concepts/economic-system|economy]] of Saudi Arabia is largely based on oil production and exportation. The government has initiated several economic reform [[concepts/software|programs]] aimed at diversifying the nation's economy beyond petroleum.

### Water Scarcity and Desalination
Saudi Arabia [[concepts/faces|faces]] severe [[concepts/water-scarcity|water scarcity]] due to its [[concepts/arid-climate|arid climate]] and limited [[concepts/renewable-freshwater-resources|renewable freshwater resources]]. As a result, it heavily relies on [[concepts/desalination-technology|desalination]] plants for fresh water supply.
