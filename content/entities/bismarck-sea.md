# Bismarck Sea

The Bismarck Sea is a body of water in the southwestern Pacific Ocean, located in Oceania between [[entities/papua-new-guinea|Papua New Guinea]] and the Bismarck Archipelago. The sea is bounded by several major islands and island groups, including New Britain and New Ireland, and connects to the Solomon Sea to the east.

## 1888 Volcanic Event

The Bismarck Sea is historically notable as the site of a catastrophic volcanic event in 1888, when [[entities/ritter-island|Ritter Island]] experienced a major [[concepts/landmass-collapse|flank collapse]]. This collapse triggered a mega-[[concepts/tsunami|tsunami]] with waves estimated to reach approximately 100 metres in height. The tsunami caused significant devastation to nearby coastal areas and islands in the region, marking one of the most severe volcanic-induced tsunamis recorded in the Pacific.

## Modern Significance

Today, the Bismarck Sea remains an important body of water for regional shipping and maritime activity in Oceania. The sea's history of volcanic and seismic activity continues to be studied by geologists and oceanographers as a significant case study in understanding volcanic hazards and tsunami generation in the Pacific region.
