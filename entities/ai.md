# Ai

[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) refers to computer systems designed to perform tasks that typically require [[concepts/human-intelligence|human intelligence]]. These tasks include [[concepts/learning|learning]] from [[concepts/experience|experience]], recognizing patterns, understanding language, and making decisions. [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] range from narrow applications focused on specific tasks to broader systems with more general capabilities.

## Large Language Models and Knowledge Graphs

[[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are a significant class of AI systems trained on vast amounts of text data. Recent developments have focused on integrating LLMs with [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]] technologies to enhance [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. The [[concepts/etl-framework|Cocoindex framework]] represents one approach to building real-time [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] from document collections, using LLMs to extract and structure information.

## AI Agents

Beyond static [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] and reasoning, modern AI evolves into **[[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]** capable of autonomous action. Key developments include:

- **Distinction from [[concepts/ai-bots|Chatbots]]**: Unlike traditional chatbots that primarily generate responses, agents are distinguished by their ability to [[concepts/purpose|reason]], take actions, and adapt dynamically to environments.
- **[[concepts/react-framework|ReAct Framework]]**: A foundational pattern for agent behavior that combines reasoning ([[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]]) with [[concepts/acting|acting]] (tool usage or external queries), enabling more robust [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] workflows. See detailed [[concepts/notes|notes]] in [[lab-notes/2026-06-13-AI-Agents-Explained-ReAct-Framework-Behavioral-Types-and|AI Agents Explained: ReAct Framework, Behavioral Types, and Google ADK]].
- **[[concepts/behavioral-types|Behavioral Types]]**: Categorization of agent behaviors based on their interaction patterns and autonomy levels.
- **[[concepts/google-search|Google]] ADK**: Development kits provided by platforms like [Google Cloud Tech](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Zqno_vux6d8) to facilitate the rapid building and deployment of these agents.
