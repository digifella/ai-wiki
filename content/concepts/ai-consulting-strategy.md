# AI Consulting Strategy

[[concepts/ai-consultant|AI consulting]] strategy refers to the approaches companies take in advising clients on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] implementation, risk management, and [[concepts/governance|governance]]. A notable [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] exists between different organizational [[concepts/models|models]] in this emerging field, particularly between proprietary [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] services and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] safety frameworks.

## Proprietary Consulting vs. Open-Source Approaches

[[entities/openai|OpenAI]] has developed consulting services around [[concepts/ai-agent-implementation|AI agent deployment]] and implementation, positioning specialized advisory as a value-added service for enterprise clients navigating AI [[concepts/adoption|adoption]]. This model generates revenue through expert consultation while creating demand for OpenAI's core products.

In contrast, [[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]] has released open-source [[concepts/ai-guardrails|AI guardrails]] and safety frameworks, making comparable safety and governance tools freely available to the broader [[concepts/developer|developer]] and enterprise community. This approach prioritizes ecosystem adoption and standardization over direct consulting revenue, allowing organizations to implement safeguards independently.

## Strategic Implications

The choice between proprietary consultation and open-source frameworks reflects broader business [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] differences. Open-source [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]] reduce barriers to [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|responsible AI]] implementation but may limit consulting service revenues. Proprietary approaches create dependency on expert services but potentially exclude cost-conscious organizations from safety [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]]. As [[concepts/ai-governance|AI governance]] becomes increasingly important, these divergent strategies [[entities/will|will]] likely shape how organizations approach risk management and regulatory [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] in AI systems.
