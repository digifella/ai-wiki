# Smart Cities

## Concept & Development
*   **Definition:** Urban areas that use different types of electronic methods and sensors to collect specific data.
*   **Core Mechanism:** Integration of Internet of Things (IoT) [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], data analytics, and real-time monitoring to optimize urban services and resource management.
*   **Historical Context:** Evolution from traditional urban planning to data-driven governance, aiming for increased efficiency, sustainability, and quality of life.

## Implementation Challenges
*   **Technical Complexity:** High costs of infrastructure deployment and maintenance.
*   **Data [[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]:** Significant concerns regarding citizen surveillance and data ownership.
*   **Public Trust:** Growing skepticism regarding the efficacy and ethical implications of smart city deployments.

## Failures & Critiques
*   **Critical Analysis:** Recent assessments suggest that many smart city initiatives are failing to meet their stated goals.
*   **Key Concerns:**
    *   Over-reliance on technology without addressing social equity.
    *   Lack of transparency in data usage.
    *   Disconnection between technological solutions and actual urban problems.
