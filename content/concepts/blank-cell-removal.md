# blank cell removal

The process of identifying and stripping empty or null values from Data Ranges to optimize [[concepts/data-cleaning|Data Cleaning]] and [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] reporting [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]].

## Techniques
- **[[concepts/trimrange|TRIMRANGE]] ([[entities/excel|Excel]] "[[concepts/trim-references|Trim References]]"):** A feature used to dynamically remove empty cells from the boundaries of a [[concepts/range|range]].
    - Significantly simplifies the management of Dynamic Arrays.
    - Eliminates unwanted empty cells from Excel reports.
