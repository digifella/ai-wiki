# Color Tint

[[concepts/hue|Color Tint]] is a feature within [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]'s Color and Vibrance adjustment tool that enables users to modify the color cast and overall hue balance of [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]]. Operating as a non-destructive adjustment layer, it preserves the original image data while allowing changes to color temperature and tonal qualities without permanent alteration to the underlying pixels.

## Corrective and Creative Applications

The feature supports both corrective and creative workflows. Photographers use Color Tint to remove unwanted [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] caused by different lighting conditions, such as mixed indoor and outdoor [[concepts/light|light]] sources or incorrect white balance settings. By isolating specific tonal ranges, users can neutralize green or magenta tints that often appear in shadow or highlight areas, ensuring accurate color representation.

In creative contexts, the tool allows for the intentional shifting of color temperature to establish a specific mood or aesthetic. Users can apply warm or cool tints to enhance the emotional impact of an image, such as adding a golden hour [[entities/glow|glow]] to a [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]] or a cool blue tint to a landscape. The adjustment layer format ensures that these creative decisions remain editable, allowing for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] of the intensity and range of the tint without committing to irreversible changes.
