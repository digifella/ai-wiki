# Whiteboard

A whiteboard video is an animated video format that simulates the process of drawing and [[concepts/writing|writing]] on a whiteboard in real time. This style of video presentation combines visual elements with narration to explain concepts, tell stories, or present information in an engaging and easy-to-follow manner. Whiteboard videos are commonly used for educational content, marketing [[concepts/explanations|explanations]], and instructional tutorials.

## Creating Whiteboard Videos

Several tools enable the creation of animated whiteboard videos with varying workflows. [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], a [[concepts/google-search|Google]]-based platform, can be used as a starting point for generating content and [[concepts/structure|structure]]. [[entities/google-nano-banana-pro|Nano Banana Pro]] serves as an intermediate tool in the production pipeline, while [[concepts/pictory|Pictory]] specializes in converting text, scripts, or source material into animated video content with whiteboard-style visuals. These tools can be used in sequence to streamline the [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] process from initial content generation through final animation.

The typical workflow involves preparing or generating your source material, organizing it into a script or outline, and then using these platforms to automate the animation and [[concepts/visual-rendering|visual rendering]]. This approach reduces the manual work required to create frame-by-frame animations while maintaining the distinctive whiteboard aesthetic that makes these videos effective for explanation and [[concepts/learning|learning]].
