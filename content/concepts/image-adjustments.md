# Image Adjustments

Image [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] refer to digital modifications made to photographs to enhance their visual quality, correct technical issues, or achieve a desired aesthetic. These techniques are commonly applied using [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] [[concepts/software|software]], with [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] being one of the most widely used tools for this [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]. Adjustments can [[concepts/range|range]] from corrective measures that address [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] or color problems to creative enhancements that alter the overall look and mood of an image.

## Common Adjustment Techniques

Basic image adjustments typically include exposure correction, which brightens or darkens an image to achieve proper lighting. Color balance adjustments address unwanted [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] caused by different lighting conditions. [[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] adjustment increases or decreases the difference between [[concepts/light|light]] and dark areas, affecting the overall punch and definition of an image. Saturation and vibrancy controls allow editors to intensify or mute colors. [[concepts/sharpness|Sharpness]] adjustments enhance or soften fine details, and clarity controls affect mid-[[concepts/tone|tone]] contrast to add definition or smoothness.

These fundamental techniques form the foundation of [[concepts/photo-enhancement|photo enhancement]] workflows and can often produce significant visible improvements with relatively straightforward adjustments. Mastering these core methods allows photographers and designers to quickly address common image issues and establish a professional baseline before applying more specialized editing techniques.
