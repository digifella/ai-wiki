# FSD Deployment

[[entities/tesla|Tesla]]'s [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving]] ([[entities/full-self-driving-fsd|FSD]]) Supervised system received [[concepts/regulatory-approval|regulatory approval]] in the Netherlands in April 2026, marking a significant milestone for autonomous vehicle [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] in [[entities/europe|Europe]]. This approval established the first precedent for FSD operation within the [[entities/eu|European Union]], following the system's earlier deployment in North [[entities/america|America]]. The Netherlands' [[concepts/regulatory-framework|regulatory framework]] and assessment process informed the path forward for broader European [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].

## Regulatory Framework

The Netherlands approval demonstrated the feasibility of meeting [[entities/european-union|EU]] safety and liability [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for [[concepts/supervised-autonomy|supervised autonomous driving]] systems. Rather than requiring complete autonomous capability, the [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd-supervised|FSD Supervised]] model maintains human oversight and intervention requirements, aligning with existing European regulatory expectations around driver responsibility. This intermediate classification between traditional driver assistance and full autonomy provided a regulatory template that other EU member states could evaluate and potentially adopt.

## EU Deployment Implications

The Dutch precedent facilitated discussion of standardized deployment criteria across EU jurisdictions. Different member states maintained independent regulatory [[concepts/power|authority]], but the Netherlands case study provided documented evidence of technical [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], safety metrics, and operational [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] that informed subsequent approval considerations in other regions. The approval process documented requirements for infrastructure compatibility, insurance frameworks, and driver [[concepts/training|training]] protocols relevant to European deployment conditions.
