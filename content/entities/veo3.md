# Veo3

Veo3 is an [[concepts/ai-driven-video-production|AI video generation]] tool developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]]. It is part of Google's broader suite of AI products and services, positioned alongside other generative [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] including [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], [[entities/google-search-ai-mode|Search AI Mode]], and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]].

The tool represents Google's entry into the AI video generation market, which has become an increasingly competitive space as various technology companies develop capabilities for automated video creation and editing.
