# Asic Company

The ASIC Company Portal is an online service provided by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) that enables users to search and retrieve information about registered Australian companies. Accessible through ASIC's official website, the portal requires users to enter a company's Australian Company Number (ACN) to access business data and regulatory information relevant to that entity.
