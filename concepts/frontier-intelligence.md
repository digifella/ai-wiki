# Frontier Intelligence

**Frontier Intelligence** refers to the class of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems that represent the state-of-the-art in capability, particularly in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]], and [[concepts/complex-task-execution|complex task execution]]. These models define the current boundary of what is computationally feasible for [[concepts/large-language-model]]s and [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence]] research.

## Key Characteristics
- **Scalability**: Performance improves predictably with increased [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] and data scale.
- **Reasoning Depth**: Ability to handle multi-step logical deduction and abstract [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].
- **[[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|Cost-Efficiency]]**: Optimization of [[concepts/inference|inference]] costs relative to [[concepts/output-quality|output quality]].
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]] Spectrum**: Ranging from closed proprietary systems to [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-weight]] models that democratize access to frontier capabilities.
