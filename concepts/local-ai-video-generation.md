# Local AI video generation

The practice of executing [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|generative video]] models on local hardware to bypass cloud-based constraints.

## Key Benefits
- **[[concepts/privacy]]**: Local processing ensures data [[concepts/secure|security]] and data residency.
- **[[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|Cost-Efficiency]]**: Eliminates the need for recurring [[concepts/subscription-models|Subscription models]].
- **No Constraints**: Removes [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]], rate-limiting, and cloud-side censorship.

## Tooling & Models
- [[entities/pinokio|Pinokio]]: An automated installer and browser used to simplify the deployment of [[concepts/open-source|Open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] on personal computers.
- [[entities/comfyui|ComfyUI]]: A node-based graphical interface for Stable Diffusion and other AI models, enabling complex workflow [[concepts/customization|customization]] for [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
- Supported Models: [[entities/ltx-2|LTX-2]], Wan.

## Hardware Requirements
- **GPU**: A dedicated, [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] processing unit is essential due to the resource-intensive nature of video synthesis.
