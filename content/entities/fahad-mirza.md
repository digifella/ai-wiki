# Fahad Mirza

Fahad Mirza is a technical [[entities/speaker|speaker]] and [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] who produces educational videos on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] tools. He specializes in explaining the installation and practical usage of [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|generative video]] [[concepts/models|models]] within accessible [[concepts/software|software]] frameworks.

## Work with WAN 2.2

Mirza is known for creating instructional content on the [[concepts/14b-parameter-model|WAN 2.2]] [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] models, specifically covering both [[concepts/text-to-video|text-to-video]] and [[concepts/image-to-video|image-to-video]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. His guides focus on implementing these models locally using [[concepts/comfyui|ComfyUI]], an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] node-based interface for [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]]. In these tutorials, he provides step-by-step installation [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and demonstrates the technical [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] and operational features of the models, making complex generative [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] accessible to users interested in [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]].
