# Neuralink Technology

Neuralink is a neurotechnology company developing brain-computer interface (BCI) systems designed to establish direct communication pathways between the human brain and external devices. The technology utilizes surgically implanted electrode arrays to record neural signals, which are subsequently decoded and translated into commands for computers or assistive devices. This invasive approach distinguishes itself from non-invasive BCI methods by offering higher signal fidelity and more granular control through direct cortical access.

The implementation of Neuralink’s technology involves specialized infrastructure and transdural surgical procedures. These procedures are engineered to minimize tissue damage while ensuring the secure placement of the implant within the brain. The hardware consists of flexible electrode threads that interface with neural tissue, allowing for the high-bandwidth data transfer necessary for complex signal processing.

As of 2026, the technology has progressed from experimental stages to documented clinical application. Early patient outcomes have been recorded, providing empirical data on the safety and efficacy of the implantation procedures. These documented impacts serve as a critical foundation for ongoing research into the long-term stability of the device and its utility in restoring function for individuals with neurological conditions.
