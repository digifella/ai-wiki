# Silhouette Reduction

Silhouette Reduction is a post-processing technique used in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] to refine and clean up the edges of masks. When creating masks during editing—whether for selective [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], [[concepts/object-removal|object removal]], or localized corrections—the boundary between masked and unmasked areas can appear harsh or imperfect. Silhouette Reduction smooths and feathers these edges to create more natural transitions.

## How It Works

The feature operates by softening the perimeter of a mask, reducing visible artifacts and creating a gentler blend between the adjusted and unadjusted portions of an image. This is particularly useful when working with complex shapes or when [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] edge work would be time-consuming. By reducing the abruptness of the mask boundary, the technique helps prevent obvious editing lines that can detract from image quality.

## Practical Applications

Silhouette Reduction is commonly applied when sharpening specific subjects, correcting [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] in isolated areas, or making color adjustments to particular elements of a [[concepts/writing|composition]]. It is especially valuable in [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]] and product photography, where clean edges are important but the subject outline may be irregular or difficult to trace precisely. The technique helps achieve a more polished final result with less manual [[concepts/edge-refinement|edge refinement]] required.
