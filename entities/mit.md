### Summary
The CLM represents a significant evolution beyond traditional [[concepts/llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs). While conventional LLMs operate as "append-only" systems where conversational history continuously stacks in the [[concepts/context-length|context window]], CLMs aim to address fundamental limitations in how [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agents]] manage information. This breakthrough, highlighted by [[entities/superintelligence-labs|Superintelligence Labs]] and MIT, focuses on optimizing context window efficiency and dynamic [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]].
