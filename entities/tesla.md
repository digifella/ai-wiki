# Tesla

## Overview
Tesla is an American multinational automotive and [[concepts/clean-power|clean energy]] company headquartered in Austin, [[entities/texas|Texas]]. It was founded by [[entities/elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and others in July 2003 with the goal of accelerating the [[entities/earth|world]]'s transition to sustainable energy.
