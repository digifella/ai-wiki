# Dynamic Videos

Dynamic videos refer to a category of interactive or adaptive [[concepts/video-creation|video formats]] that can change their content based on user interaction, context, or data inputs. These are often created and managed through advanced [[concepts/software|software]] tools like [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] and can be deployed across various platforms such as [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]].
