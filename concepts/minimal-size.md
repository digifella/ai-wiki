# Minimal Size

Minimal Size is a research domain focused on the benchmarking and evaluation of small language models (SLMs) designed to operate within strict computational constraints, typically limited to 4GB of memory or less. This area addresses the practical necessity for capable AI systems that can function on consumer-grade hardware and edge devices, eliminating the dependency on enterprise-level computational infrastructure. By establishing standardized benchmarks for models at this scale, researchers aim to identify which architectures and training methodologies deliver the strongest general problem-solving capabilities under these resource limitations.

## Evaluation Criteria

The primary objective of Minimal Size is to determine the upper bounds of performance for models fitting within the 4GB threshold. Evaluation metrics prioritize general problem-solving proficiency, reasoning accuracy, and instruction following, rather than raw parameter count or training data volume. This approach seeks to distinguish between models that merely fit the size constraint and those that effectively utilize their limited capacity to maintain high utility across diverse tasks.

## Hardware Implications

By targeting the 4GB memory limit, this concept directly influences the deployment landscape of artificial intelligence. It enables the integration of advanced language processing into devices with restricted resources, such as smartphones, IoT sensors, and older personal computers. This democratization of AI capabilities reduces latency and privacy concerns associated with cloud-based processing, allowing for more immediate and localized decision-making in end-user applications.
