# Prime Lenses

Prime lenses are fixed focal length lenses that cannot zoom. Unlike zoom lenses, which offer variable focal lengths, prime lenses have a single, unchanging focal length such as 35mm, 50mm, or 85mm. This fixed perspective requires photographers to physically move closer to or farther from their subject to frame an image, rather than adjusting the lens itself.

## Optical Quality

Prime lenses typically offer superior optical quality compared to zoom lenses at equivalent focal lengths. Because the optical design addresses a single focal length rather than a range, manufacturers can optimize the lens elements more effectively, resulting in sharper images, better color rendition, and reduced optical aberrations. This optical advantage is one of the primary reasons professional photographers choose primes despite their lack of zoom flexibility.

## Practical Considerations

Prime lenses generally feature wider maximum apertures than comparable zoom lenses, allowing more light to reach the camera sensor. This characteristic makes them particularly useful in low-light situations and provides greater control over depth of field. However, the fixed focal length means photographers must change lenses or reposition themselves to adjust framing, which can be less convenient than zooming but encourages more intentional compositional decisions.
