# Zs200

The Panasonic ZS200 is a superzoom compact camera belonging to the company's ZS series of point-and-shoot devices. It was engineered to provide extended optical zoom capabilities within a portable form factor, targeting photographers who require significant zoom range without the size and complexity of interchangeable lens systems.

Within the ZS lineup, the ZS200 occupies a middle position in the product hierarchy. It served as a predecessor to the later ZS300 model. Industry analysis, such as that provided by PetaPixel, has noted that the subsequent ZS300 release offered only minimal upgrades over the ZS200's superzoom specifications while introducing a higher price point.
