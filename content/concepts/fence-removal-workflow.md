# Fence Removal Workflow

A streamlined process for eliminating chain-link fences from photographs using [[concepts/modern-photoshop-tools|modern Photoshop tools]], replacing labor-intensive traditional methods.

- **[[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]]'s Method 1**: Utilizes the *[[concepts/remove-tool|Remove Tool]]* (for wires/cables) to quickly remove fences, as demonstrated in his [video tutorial](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1C4-CIEFTcU) for zoo photography [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
- **Modern Approach**: Leverages new [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] features to significantly reduce editing time compared to manual [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] or healing techniques.
- **Context**: Specifically optimized for chain-link fences in complex backgrounds (e.g., zoo enclosures), where traditional methods required extensive [[concepts/layer-masking|layer masking]].
