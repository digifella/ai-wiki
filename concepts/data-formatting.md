# Data formatting

The process of transforming raw data into a structured, standardized, and usable format to facilitate data analysis, [[concepts/data-cleaning|data cleaning]], and [[concepts/automation]].

### Key Methods & Tools
- **[[concepts/regular-expressions]] (REGEX)**: A powerful method for [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] used to automate complex [[concepts/data-extraction]] and data cleaning tasks.
    - **[[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] REGEX Functions**:
        - `REGEXEXTRACT`: Enables precise extraction of specific substrings based on defined patterns.
        - `REGEXREPLACE`: Facilitates cleaning and standardization by replacing or removing text via pattern matching.
    - *Reference*: 2026 04 22 Excels REGEX Functions Efficient [[concepts/scraping|Data Extraction]] Cleaning and Formatting
- **Data Cleaning**: The removal of errors, inconsistencies, and noise to ensure dataset [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
- **[[concepts/data-extraction]]**: The [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] of specific, relevant information from larger, unstructured [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
