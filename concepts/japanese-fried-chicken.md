# Japanese fried chicken (Karaage)

**Japanese fried chicken**, commonly known as **Karaage**, is a [[concepts/style|style]] of frying food originating from [[entities/japan|Japan]]. It typically consists of bite-sized pieces of meat or seafood coated in potato starch and marinated before frying, resulting in a crispy exterior and juicy interior distinct from Western-style fried chicken which often uses flour-based batters.

## Characteristics
- **[[concepts/marinating|Marination]]**: Ingredients are heavily marinated, often using soy sauce, ginger, and garlic, allowing the flavor to penetrate the meat before cooking.
- **Coating**: Utilizes potato starch (or cornstarch) rather than flour, creating a lighter, crunchier crust that does not absorb as much oil.
- **Cooking Method**: Usually fried in batches at controlled temperatures to ensure even cooking without [[concepts/burning|burning]] the exterior.
