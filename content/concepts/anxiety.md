# Anxiety

Anxiety is a complex emotional state characterized by feelings of worry, nervousness, or unease about something with an uncertain outcome. It can manifest in various forms such as generalized anxiety disorder (GAD), social anxiety disorder, panic disorder, and specific phobias.

### Key Concepts
- **Causes:** Anxiety can stem from numerous factors including [[concepts/stress|stress]], genetics, brain chemistry, environmental factors, and life experiences.
- **Symptoms:** Physical symptoms include heart palpitations, sweating, trembling, shortness of breath, fatigue, digestive issues. Psychological symptoms include restlessness, difficulty concentrating, irritability, muscle tension, and sleep disturbances.
- **Treatment:** Common approaches to managing anxiety involve therapy (e.g., cognitive-behavioral therapy), medication, lifestyle changes, and support groups.
