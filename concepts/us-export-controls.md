# US export controls

[[concepts/regulatory-framework|Regulatory framework]] implemented by the [[entities/united-states|United States]] to restrict the transfer of advanced semiconductor technology and [[concepts/ai-hardware|AI hardware]] to strategic competitors, specifically targeting [[entities/china|China]].

## Key Impacts
- **Impact on LLM Development**:
    - Delays in [[entities/deepseek]] R2 development attributed to hardware-related performance issues and supply constraints.
    - [[concepts/limited-resources|Scarcity]] of [[entities/nvidia]] [[entities/h20-chips|H20 chips]] directly linked to US export controls.
- **[[concepts/hardware-limitations|Hardware Limitations]]**:
    - Restrictions on high-end GPU availability are creating bottlenecks in the training of large-scale models within restricted jurisdictions.
- **[[concepts/frontier-ai|Frontier AI]] Sovereignty & Deployment Restrictions**:
    - Constraints on hardware access are driving alternative architectural approaches to maintain [[concepts/performance-matching|performance parity]] without restricted chips.
    - [[lab-notes/2026-06-23-Sakana-Fugu-Multi-Agent-AI-Matching-Fable-5-Performance|Sakana Fugu: Multi-Agent AI Matching Fable 5 Performance]] demonstrates how [[concepts/ai-agent-coordination|multi-agent orchestration]] systems can match high-end performance (e.g., [[concepts/claude-fable-5|Fable 5]]) despite hardware limitations, highlighting a shift towards software-level efficiency over raw [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power.
