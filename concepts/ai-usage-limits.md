# Ai Usage Limits

AI [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] are restrictions implemented by service providers to control the frequency and extent of user access to their models. These constraints typically define boundaries on request rates and [[concepts/token-consumption|token consumption]] within specified time periods, with different thresholds applied across various [[concepts/pricing-tiers|subscription tiers]]. By establishing these limits, providers manage [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] costs, prevent system abuse, and ensure equitable [[concepts/strategic-resource-allocation|resource distribution]] among the user base.

Recent [[concepts/policy-updates|policy updates]] by [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] regarding its [[concepts/2026-04-08-anthropic|Claude AI]] [[concepts/subscription-access|subscription model]] illustrate the practical application of these limits. The company has introduced stricter usage caps to manage demand and maintain service stability. Additionally, Anthropic has enforced bans on specific third-party tools, such as [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], to prevent circumvention of these [[concepts/usage-limits|usage restrictions]] and ensure [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with their terms of service.
