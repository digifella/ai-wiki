# Frontier AI Standards

**[[concepts/frontier-ai|Frontier AI]] Standards** refer to the technical, safety, and [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks designed to regulate the development and deployment of [[concepts/agi|Artificial General Intelligence]] (AGI) and other [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]]. These standards aim to mitigate existential risks, ensure alignment with human values, and establish institutional oversight for high-capability models.

## Key Developments & Actors

### Demis Hassabis & Institutional Governance
Recent proposals emphasize the need for new institutional structures to manage AGI risks. [[lab-notes/2026-07-18-Demis-Hassabis-Building-New-Institutions-for-AGI-Governa|Demis Hassabis: Building New Institutions for AGI Governance and Frontier AI Standards]] highlights the following points:

*   **Framework Proposal:** [[entities/demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], CEO of [[entities/deepmind]] and Nobel laureate, has introduced "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age."
*   **Institutional Shift:** The proposal advocates for building new governance institutions specifically tailored to the unique challenges of AGI, moving beyond traditional regulatory models.
*   **Paradigm Change:** This initiative marks a significant shift in the AI debate, focusing on proactive institutional design rather than [[concepts/reactive-policy|reactive policy]].

## Core Principles

*   **Safety First:** Prioritizing robust safety testing and alignment [[concepts/verification|verification]] before deployment.
*   **Global [[concepts/coordination|Coordination]]:** Establishing international standards to prevent regulatory arbitrage.
*   **[[concepts/opacity|Transparency]]:** Mandating clear reporting of capabilities and risks for [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier models]].
