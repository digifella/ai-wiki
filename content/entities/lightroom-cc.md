# Lightroom CC

Adobe's cloud-based [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] application, sharing core feature sets with [[concepts/lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]].

## Editing Capabilities
- **[[concepts/landscape-masking]]**: Specialized [[concepts/masking-tools|masking tools]] for terrain-specific [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
- **Standard [[concepts/workflow|Workflow]]**:
    - **Initial Image Preparation**:
        - Straightening
        - Basic panel adjustments: [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Highlights, [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], [[concepts/whites|Whites]], and [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]].
