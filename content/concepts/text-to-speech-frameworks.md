# Text To Speech Frameworks

Text-to-speech (TTS) frameworks are [[concepts/software|software]] systems designed to convert written [[concepts/text|text]] into spoken [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] [[concepts/output|output]]. These frameworks vary in [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], computational requirements, and quality characteristics. They form an important component of accessible applications, [[concepts/voice-assistants|voice assistants]], and automated narration systems.

## Kitten TTS

[[concepts/cpu-optimized-tts|Kitten TTS]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/text-to-speech-framework|text-to-speech framework]] developed by [[entities/kitten-ml|Kitten ML]]. It is specifically optimized for [[concepts/cpu-based-inference|CPU-based inference]], distinguishing it from many contemporary TTS systems that rely on [[concepts/gpu-acceleration|GPU acceleration]]. This [[concepts/cpu-optimization|CPU optimization]] makes Kitten TTS suitable for [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where GPU resources are limited or unavailable, including edge devices and resource-constrained environments.

As an open-source project, Kitten TTS is available for modification and [[concepts/integration|integration]] into custom applications. The framework represents an approach to making TTS technology more accessible by reducing [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] dependencies while maintaining functional [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
