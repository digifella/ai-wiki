# Greg Rosenberg

Greg Rosenberg is a content creator who produces educational material about AI [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] techniques and [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]]. He maintains a YouTube channel focused on practical guidance for working with [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] models.

## Prompting Techniques Content

Rosenberg has created instructional content summarizing ten techniques for effectively prompting [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] and [[concepts/claude-opus-45|Claude Opus 4.5]]. This material draws on [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s documented best practices and provides guidance on optimizing interactions with these [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. His work covers [[concepts/api-cost-optimization|API cost optimization]] and practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of Claude's capabilities across different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
