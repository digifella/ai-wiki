# Modality

In the context of artificial intelligence, modality refers to a distinct type or channel of data that a system processes. These modalities represent information encoded in specific formats, such as text, images, audio, or video, each requiring specialized computational methods for analysis and interpretation. The concept also extends to other data types, including sensor readings and structured tabular data, defining the fundamental input channels through which AI models perceive their environment.

Traditional AI systems are typically unimodal, meaning they are designed to process only a single type of data. For example, a text classification model operates exclusively on linguistic inputs. In contrast, multimodal systems are engineered to integrate and process multiple data types simultaneously. This integration allows for more complex reasoning and richer contextual understanding, as the model can correlate information across different sensory channels, such as matching visual content with corresponding audio or textual descriptions.
