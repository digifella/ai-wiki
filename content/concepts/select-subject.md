# Select Subject

An [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] feature designed to automatically isolate subjects or people from their backgrounds.

## Methodologies
- **AI-Powered Selection**: Utilizes [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] for rapid, automated edge detection and object [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]].
- **Manual Techniques**: Traditional selection [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] used for high-precision requirements where [[concepts/automation|automation]] fails.
- **Hybrid Workflow**: The most effective approach for complex edges, combining [[concepts/ai-automation|AI automation]] with manual [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].

## Precision Masking & Optimization
- **Enhanced AI Processing**: Configuring [[concepts/photoshop]] settings specifically to optimize the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and performance of the Select Subject algorithm.
