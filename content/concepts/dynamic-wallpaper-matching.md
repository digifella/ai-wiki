# Dynamic Wallpaper Matching

Dynamic Wallpaper Matching is a feature introduced in [[entities/android|Android]] 16 that intelligently adjusts device wallpapers and visual elements based on user preferences and system settings. The feature is part of a broader suite of [[concepts/design|design]] refinements that accompanied the stable release of [[entities/android-16|Android 16]] in 2025, alongside the [[entities/june-2025-pixel-drop|June 2025 Pixel Drop]].

## Implementation on Pixel Devices

The feature was demonstrated on the [[entities/pixel-9-pro-xl|Pixel 9 Pro XL]], where it dynamically adapts wallpaper presentation to complement the device's interface and user-selected themes. This capability reflects [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s continued emphasis on [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] within Android's design language, allowing users to maintain visual coherence across their device without manual adjustment of background [[concepts/images|images]].

## Context

Dynamic Wallpaper Matching represents one of several design tweaks introduced in the Android 16 stable release. The feature works in conjunction with other [[concepts/customization|customization]] options to provide a more seamless and integrated visual experience across the Pixel ecosystem.
