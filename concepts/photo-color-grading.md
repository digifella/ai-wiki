# Photo Color Grading

Photo color grading is the creative process of adjusting colors, tones, and color casts in photographs during post-processing to establish a specific visual mood or aesthetic. Unlike basic color correction, which aims to restore accurate colors, color grading is an intentional artistic choice that shapes how viewers perceive and feel about an image. This technique is fundamental to contemporary photography, allowing creators to unify the appearance of multiple images or establish a cohesive visual style across a body of work.

## Tools and Software

Adobe Lightroom is a primary tool for this process, offering robust features for adjusting hues, saturation, and luminance. Users often employ the HSL/Color panel to isolate specific color ranges, while the Tone Curve allows for precise control over contrast and tonal distribution. The split-toning feature enables the application of different color casts to highlights and shadows, facilitating the creation of dark and moody aesthetics that provide dramatic visual impact.

## Aesthetic Application

The technique is frequently used to evoke specific emotional responses through deliberate manipulation of color temperature and contrast. By shifting tones toward cooler palettes or deepening shadows, photographers can create a somber or intense atmosphere. This approach differs from standard correction by prioritizing artistic expression over technical accuracy, resulting in images that convey a distinct narrative or stylistic identity.
