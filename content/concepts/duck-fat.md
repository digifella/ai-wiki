# Duck Fat

---
type: concept
tags: duck fat, cooking, fat, ingredients
updated: 2026-05-04

Duck fat is rendered fat obtained from duck, prized for its rich, distinctive flavor, particularly in savory cooking. It is often used to impart depth and richness to dishes, offering a superior taste compared to standard animal fats.

### Uses and Properties

*   **Flavor Profile:** Provides a rich, gamey, and distinctive flavor to cooked foods.
*   **Culinary Application:** Highly recommended for roasting, frying, and baking where flavor is a primary goal.
*   **Sourcing:** Typically sourced from duck fat rendered during the cooking process.
*   **[[entities/storage|Storage]]:** Should be stored appropriately to maintain quality.
