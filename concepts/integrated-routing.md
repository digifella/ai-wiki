# Integrated Routing

Integrated Routing is a beta feature of Cloudflare's [[entities/email|email]] service that combines email sending, routing, and [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI workflow]] capabilities within a single platform. The service enables users to manage email delivery and processing through Cloudflare's infrastructure while incorporating AI-powered automation for enhanced email handling and intelligence.

## Core Functionality

The service allows organizations to handle email operations—including message delivery, recipient routing, and processing rules—through Cloudflare's [[concepts/unified-interface|unified interface]]. By integrating these functions together, users can manage email workflows without relying on separate [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]].

## AI and Automation Integration

Integrated Routing incorporates [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI processing]] capabilities, enabling [[concepts/automated-content-creation|automated workflows]] that can analyze, filter, or respond to emails based on configurable rules and intelligent criteria. This automation layer extends beyond simple routing to include intelligent email handling and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].

## Platform Integration

As part of Cloudflare's broader infrastructure platform, Integrated Routing connects with the company's existing [[concepts/developer|developer]] tools and services, allowing teams to incorporate email workflows into their wider application ecosystems and automation pipelines.
