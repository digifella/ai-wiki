# Developer platforms

Systems and environments designed to facilitate the creation, [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], and management of [[concepts/software|software]] and AI-driven [[concepts/software|applications]].

## AI Interface Comparison
* **[[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]]**: A powerful and versatile platform providing advanced [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] tailored for [[concepts/power-users|power users]] and professionals.
* **[[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]] Web App**: A consumer-friendly [[concepts/chat-application|chat interface]] (similar to [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]) designed for general [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].
