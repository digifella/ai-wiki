# 2026 04 10 Evernote New Pricing Structure User Reactions And Migration Guidance

In April 2026, Evernote announced significant changes to its subscription pricing model. The restructuring affected the company's free tier and paid plan offerings, prompting widespread discussion among its user base regarding cost, feature access, and value proposition. The announcement came as the note-taking platform continued to compete in an increasingly crowded productivity software market.

## User Response

The pricing changes generated notable reactions across Evernote's user community. Many long-term users expressed concern about increased costs or reduced functionality at existing price points. The announcement sparked discussions on user forums, social media, and technology communities about the sustainability of the service and comparisons with competing platforms such as OneNote, Notion, and Obsidian. Some users indicated plans to migrate their data to alternative services, while others evaluated whether the revised pricing aligned with their usage patterns and budget constraints.

## Migration Context

Following the announcement, guidance and documentation emerged addressing user concerns about data portability and transition processes. Users exploring alternatives sought clarity on exporting their notes, managing multiple services, and evaluating which platform best served their needs at the new price points. The period represented a significant moment of customer evaluation for the company, with retention depending partly on how effectively the platform communicated the value of its updated offerings relative to the new cost structure.
