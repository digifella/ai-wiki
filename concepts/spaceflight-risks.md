# Spaceflight Risks

Spaceflight risks encompass the spectrum of potential adverse events, [[concepts/health|health]] hazards, and mission-critical failures associated with human and robotic operations beyond [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]]. These risks are categorized into environmental, physiological, operational, and psychological domains.

## Physiological & Health Risks

[[concepts/human-spaceflight|Human spaceflight]] exposes astronauts to unique stressors that compromise health and cognitive performance. Key areas of concern include:

*   **[[concepts/in-flight-medical-conditions|In-Flight Medical Conditions]]:** The [[concepts/probability|probability]] of acute medical events occurring during missions necessitates robust countermeasures. As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Review-of-NASAs-Evidence-Reports-on-Human-Health-Risks-2|Review of NASA's Evidence Reports on Human Health Risks: 2017 Letter Report (2018)]], there is a recognized possibility of [[concepts/adverse-health-outcomes|adverse health outcomes]] and significant decrements in performance resulting from in-flight medical conditions.
*   **Radiation [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]:** Galactic [[concepts/cosmic-rays|Cosmic Rays]] (GCR) and Solar Particle Events (SPE) pose long-term cancer risks and acute radiation sickness threats. See Radiation Hazards in Space.
*   **Microgravity Effects:** Fluid shift, bone [[concepts/density|density]] loss, and muscle atrophy degrade physical capabilities. See Microgravity Physiology.
*   **Spaceflight Associated Neuro-ocular Syndrome ([[entities/sans-institute|SANS]]):** Visual impairment intracranial pressure syndrome linked to prolonged exposure to microgravity.

## Operational & Environmental Risks

*   **Launch/Entry Failure:** Catastrophic failure during atmospheric transit remains a primary probability distribution concern. See Launch [[concepts/vehicle-reliability|Vehicle Reliability]].
*   **Life Support System Failure:** Critical dependencies on Closed-[[concepts/loop|Loop]] Environmental Control and Life Support Systems ([[concepts/orion-systems|ECLSS]]).
*   **Micrometeoroid and Orbital Debris (MMOD):** High-velocity impacts threaten hull [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] and critical subsystems.

## Mitigation Strategies

[[concepts/risk-mitigation|Risk mitigation]] relies on redundant systems, rigorous pre-flight screening, real-time [[concepts/health-surveillance|health monitoring]], and emergency abort capabilities. Continuous review of evidence reports, such as those by the [[entities/national-academies|National Academies]], informs evolving [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]] and operational protocols to minimize human error and biological [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]].
