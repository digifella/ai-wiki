# Hydrogen fusion

The nuclear process in which atomic nuclei of Hydrogen combine to form a heavier nucleus, releasing massive amounts of Nuclear Energy.

## Key Principles & Research
- Occurs naturally in stellar bodies like the Sun, which acts as a massive reservoir of Hydrogen and [[concepts/helium-fusion|Helium fusion]].
- Current scientific efforts focus on replicating this stellar power on Earth to create a sustainable energy future.
- See: Nuclear Fusion: Replicating Stellar Power for Earth's Energy Future
