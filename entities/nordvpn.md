# Nordvpn

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012. The company operates a global network of servers that allow users to encrypt their internet connections and route traffic through its infrastructure. This process masks the user's IP address and geographic location from internet service providers, network administrators, and other entities that monitor network activity.

The service enables users to conceal their online activity and location while browsing the internet. By connecting to servers in various countries, users can bypass geographic restrictions and maintain privacy. The platform offers dedicated applications for multiple operating systems and devices, ensuring consistent security protocols across different platforms.

NordVPN has expanded its offerings to include additional privacy tools, such as threat protection features that block malicious websites and ads. The company emphasizes its commitment to user privacy through a strict no-logs policy, which asserts that it does not store records of user activity or connection data.
