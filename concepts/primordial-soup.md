# Primordial Soup

The **primordial soup** theory posits that life on Earth originated from a mixture of organic compounds in early oceans, energized by lightning, UV radiation, or volcanic activity. Recent research challenges this monolithic view, suggesting a more complex, dual-origin scenario.

## Current Scientific Consensus

*   **Hydrothermal Vent Hypothesis:** New evidence supports the idea that life may have originated in deep-sea hydrothermal vents, where mineral-rich fluids provide energy and catalytic surfaces [[concepts/hydrothermal-vents]].
*   **[[concepts/dual-origins-of-life|Dual Origins of Life]]:** Recent studies suggest life on Earth may have had two separate origins, rather than a single event in a primordial soup [[lab-notes/2026-08-28-Rethinking-LUCA-Evidence-for-Dual-Origins-of-Life-in-Hyd|Rethinking LUCA: Evidence for Dual Origins of Life in Hydrothermal Vents]].
*   **[[concepts/luca|LUCA]] ([[concepts/last-universal-common-ancestor|Last Universal Common Ancestor]]):** The concept of LUCA is being re-evaluated in light of these dual-origin theories, implying a more complex network of early life forms rather than a single progenitor.
