# Color sampling

The process of selecting and isolating specific color values within a digital image to apply targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

### Features & Tools
* **[[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] [[concepts/variance-filter|Variance Filter]]**: An "early access" feature located within the [[concepts/point-color]] section of the [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]].
    * The **Variance** slider allows users to [[concepts/power|control]] the specific [[concepts/range|range]] of colors affected after a color sample has been taken.
    * Enables precise manipulation of the color breadth impacted by a [[concepts/point-color]] adjustment.
