# Active Tabs

Active Tabs is an educational video produced by Kole Jain that outlines fundamental principles of user interface and user experience design. The content is structured to provide beginners with foundational knowledge while remaining accessible as a reference for experienced designers. Rather than focusing on specialized or advanced techniques, the video emphasizes practical, universally applicable design principles that form the basis of effective digital design.

The presentation covers core concepts such as affordances, visual hierarchy, grid systems, and typography. These topics are presented as essential components for creating intuitive and functional digital products. By breaking down these elements, the video aims to clarify how individual design choices contribute to the overall usability and aesthetic coherence of an interface.

The material serves as a general overview rather than a deep dive into specific methodologies. It is intended to establish a common vocabulary and understanding of design fundamentals, allowing viewers to recognize how these basic rules interact within larger design systems.
