# Statistical Computing

**Statistical computing** refers to the use of computer [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] and software to perform statistical analysis, model estimation, and [[concepts/simulation|simulation]]. It bridges the gap between theoretical **statistics** and practical application through implementation in languages such as R programming, [[concepts/python|Python]] (programming language), or specialized systems like SAS.

## Core Components

- **Computational Statistics**: [[concepts/numerical-analysis|Numerical methods]] for solving problems that lack closed-form solutions, including numerical integration, optimization, and root-finding.
- **Statistical Software**: Environments designed for data manipulation, visualization, and [[concepts/inference|inference]].
	- **[[concepts/r-programming-language|R Language]]**: A dominant [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] language specifically designed for statistical computing and [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]].
	- **[[entities/python|Python]]**: Widely used via libraries like pandas, [[entities/numpy]], and SciPy.
- **Simulation Methods**: Monte Carlo methods, bootstrapping, and resampling techniques to approximate complex distributions.
