# Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory

**Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory ([[entities/llnl|LLNL]])** is a federally funded research and development center located in Livermore, California. Established in 1952, LLNL is managed by University of California for the U.S. Department of Energy's National Nuclear Security Administration. Its primary mission focuses on national security, specifically the maintenance of the United States nuclear stockpile and the development of advanced technologies for defense and energy.

## Key Facilities and Infrastructure

LLNL hosts several world-class scientific facilities, including:

*   **National Ignition Facility (NIF):** A laser fusion facility that achieved the first ignition of a fusion target in 2022.
*   **[[concepts/el-capitan-supercomputer|El Capitan Supercomputer]]:** One of the world's most powerful supercomputers, designed for high-performance [[concepts/computation|computing]] (HPC) tasks related to nuclear weapons stewardship and [[concepts/scientific-simulation|scientific simulation]].
    *   **Scale & Engineering:** El Capitan represents a significant leap in computational power, utilizing advanced AMD Instinct accelerators and HPE Cray architecture to handle extreme-scale simulations.
    *   **Mission:** It supports critical national security missions, including stockpile stewardship, astrophysics, and climate modeling.
    *   **Documentation:** For detailed technical insights and engineering context, see [[lab-notes/2026-09-27-El-Capitan-Supercomputer-Scale-Engineering-and-Mission-a|El Capitan Supercomputer: Scale, Engineering, and Mission at LLNL]].

## Research Areas

*   **Nuclear Weapons Science:** Ensuring the safety, security, and reliability of the U.S. nuclear arsenal without nuclear testing.
*   **High-Energy Density Physics:** Studying matter under extreme conditions similar to those found in stars and planetary cores.
*   **Energy Security:** Developing technologies for fusion energy, nuclear fission, and advanced energy storage.
*   **Nonproliferation:** Monitoring and verifying global nuclear threats through advanced sensing and simulation technologies.
