# Janelia

**Janelia** is a research campus of the Howard Hughes Medical Institute (HHMI) located in Ashburn, Virginia. It is renowned for its work in neuroscience, particularly in mapping neural circuits and developing advanced imaging technologies.

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]]**: In September 2026, [[entities/google|Google]] and Janelia collaborated to open-source the complete [[concepts/connectome|connectome]] of a male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord Google's Open-Sourced [[concepts/fruit-fly-brain|Fruit Fly Brain]] [[concepts/neural-wiring-diagram|Connectome]]: Structure and Implications.
*   **Open Science**: This release represents a significant milestone in open-source neuroscience, providing a full "wiring diagram" of the *Drosophila* nervous system for global research.
*   **Impact**: The dataset facilitates new models of neural [[concepts/computation|computation]] and behavior, bridging the gap between structural anatomy and functional dynamics.
