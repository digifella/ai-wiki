# Mark Denney

Mark Denney is a landscape photographer known for his educational content on photographic techniques. He has produced instructional material focused on solving common challenges in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], particularly those related to achieving sharp, detailed [[concepts/images|images]] across entire compositions.

## Focus Stacking Technique

Denney is recognized for explaining the [[concepts/blur-correction|focus stacking]] technique, a method used to enhance [[concepts/image-detail|photo sharpness]] in landscape photography. Focus stacking addresses a fundamental challenge in landscape photography: achieving [[concepts/sharpness|sharp focus]] across both foreground and background elements when the depth of field of a camera lens is insufficient. The technique involves capturing multiple photographs [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] different focus distances and then combining them in post-processing to create a final image with greater overall sharpness and detail retention.

His educational videos on this subject provide practical instruction for photographers seeking to improve image quality in landscape work, particularly in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where maintaining focus across varying distances would otherwise be difficult or impossible with a single [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].
