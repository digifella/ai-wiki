# Enhanced Reasoning

**Enhanced [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]** refers to the advanced cognitive capabilities of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to perform complex logical deduction, multi-step [[concepts/problem-solving|problem solving]], and self-correction. It moves beyond simple [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] to include [[concepts/reasoning-skills|structured thought processes]], [[concepts/verification|verification]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], and improved judgment in [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] contexts.

## Key Characteristics

- **[[concepts/advanced-reasoning|Multi-Step Deduction]]**: Ability to break down complex queries into sequential [[concepts/logical-steps|logical steps]].
- **Self-Verification**: Internal checks to validate outputs against constraints or ground truth before finalizing responses.
- **Judgment & [[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]**: Improved accuracy in distinguishing between plausible but incorrect answers and factually correct ones.
- **[[concepts/self-improvement|Self-Improvement]]**: Mechanisms allowing the model to refine its reasoning paths based on [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] or error analysis.
