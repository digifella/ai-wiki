# Qwen Tts Model

The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] collection of text-to-speech [[concepts/models|models]] developed by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]]. These models are designed to convert written [[concepts/text|text]] into spoken [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] with support for multiple advanced features, making them suitable for various [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]] [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Key Features

The [[concepts/instruction-control|Qwen3-TTS]] models include [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]], [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]], and standard [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|text-to-speech generation]]. [[concepts/tone|Voice]] [[concepts/design|design]] allows users to customize acoustic properties of generated speech, while [[concepts/ai-cloning|voice cloning]] enables the models to replicate specific [[entities/speaker|speaker]] characteristics from audio samples. These features provide flexibility for applications ranging from [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] to [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] tools.

## Availability

As open-source software, the Qwen3-TTS models are publicly available for research and commercial use, allowing developers and researchers to integrate the technology into their own projects and applications.
