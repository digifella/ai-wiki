# Human Perception

Active process of selecting, organizing, and interpreting sensory inputs to construct functional internal representations of the environment. Perception relies on predictive mechanisms and embodied experience rather than passive data recording.

## Characteristics
- **World Modeling:** Humans maintain dynamic [[concepts/world-models]] [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] physical laws, causal [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and spatial geometry, enabling [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] and prediction of future states.
- **Sensory Grounding:** Perceptual priors are derived from multisensory interaction with the physical environment, anchoring abstract concepts to motor and sensory [[concepts/feedback|feedback]].
- **Active [[concepts/inference|Inference]]:** Perception minimizes prediction error by updating internal [[concepts/models|models]] based on sensory evidence; related to Predictive [[concepts/coding|Coding]] frameworks.

## Comparison with AI Systems
Analysis [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] structural divergences between human perception and [[concepts/large-language-models]] regarding [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]] understanding:
- LLMs primarily operate on statistical token correlations, often lacking grounded semantics or causal understanding of physical dynamics.
- Humans generalize effectively to novel physical [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] via embodied priors; LLMs exhibit hallucinations and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] failures in out-of-[[concepts/distribution|distribution]] physical contexts.
- **World Models** are investigated as a technical bridge to impart AI systems with simulation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and physical reasoning comparable to human internal modeling [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-World-Models-Bridging-Human-AI-Understanding-of-Physical|World Models: Bridging Human-AI Understanding of Physical Reality]].
