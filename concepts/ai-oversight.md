# AI Oversight

Systematic monitoring and [[concepts/governance|governance]] of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and deployment to ensure safety, [[concepts/ethics|ethics]], and alignment with organizational values.

- **[[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] gap**: Rapid [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] by healthcare professionals occurs without adequate governance structures, creating safety risks [[concepts/healthcare-policy|Healthcare governance]].
- **Framework limitation**: Existing approaches prioritize high-level AI [[concepts/integrity|ethics]] principles over actionable implementation, leaving organizations unable to translate principles into practice [[concepts/risk-assessment|Risk assessment]].
- **[[entities/bmj-review|BMJ Review]]**
- **Observer Agent Integration**: Emerging technical solutions like [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Anthropic-Observer-Agents-AI-Monitoring-for-Reliability|Anthropic Observer Agents: AI Monitoring for Reliability and Ethics]] introduce specialized [[concepts/subagents|subagents]] designed to monitor and evaluate the actions of primary [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] in real-time. This addresses critical [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] gaps by providing an automated layer of ethical and operational oversight, moving beyond static policy frameworks to dynamic, continuous [[concepts/verification|verification]] of AI behavior.
