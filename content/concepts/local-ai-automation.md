# Local AI Automation

Local AI automation refers to the [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] and execution of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems within an [[concepts/organization|organization]]'s own infrastructure rather than relying on external cloud-based services. This approach prioritizes data security, operational control, and independence from third-party service providers.

## Enterprise Implementation

Organizations implementing local AI automation typically use enterprise platforms designed to manage [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]—autonomous systems that perform specific tasks with minimal human intervention. These platforms must balance [[concepts/automation|automation]] capabilities with security requirements, as [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] means the organization assumes full responsibility for system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] and data protection.

## Security Considerations

Local deployment addresses vulnerabilities inherent in distributed or cloud-dependent AI systems. By keeping AI operations on-premises, enterprises can implement stricter access controls, maintain data residency [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and reduce [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to external security risks. This is particularly important for organizations handling sensitive information or operating in regulated industries.

## Platform Solutions

Modern local AI automation platforms provide frameworks for building, deploying, and managing AI agents within [[concepts/secure|secure]] enterprise environments. These solutions typically include [[concepts/agent-toolkits|agent toolkits]], [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]] capabilities, and monitoring systems designed specifically for organizations requiring self-hosted infrastructure rather than [[concepts/software|Software]]-as-a-Service models.
