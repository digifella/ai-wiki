# Patient Generated Health Data (PGHD)

Patient Generated [[concepts/health|Health]] Data refers to health and [[concepts/wellness|wellness]] information collected directly by patients or gathered through devices they use, rather than through traditional clinical settings. This data is typically captured continuously or at regular intervals using [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]] and [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] that monitor various physiological parameters such as heart rate, activity levels, [[concepts/sleep|sleep]] patterns, and other vital signs relevant to individual health conditions.

## Collection and Technology

PGHD is primarily collected through [[entities/iot-devices|wearable devices]]—electronic instruments worn on the body that incorporate embedded sensors to perform real-time monitoring. These devices range from smartwatches and fitness trackers to specialized medical [[concepts/internet-of-things|wearables]] designed for specific health conditions. The continuous nature of data collection provides a more comprehensive picture of a patient's health status compared to periodic clinical assessments, particularly valuable in [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] settings where ongoing monitoring supports treatment and rehabilitation.

## Clinical Applications

In [[entities/allied-health|allied health]] contexts, PGHD enables [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] providers to access [[concepts/purpose|objective]], longitudinal data about patient behavior and physiological responses outside clinical environments. This information supports more informed [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]], allows for earlier detection of changes in patient status, and facilitates more personalized treatment approaches. The data can also increase patient engagement by providing individuals with direct [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] about their [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]].

## Considerations

Effective implementation of PGHD systems requires careful [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to data [[concepts/security|security]], [[concepts/privacy-protection|privacy protection]], and interoperability with existing [[concepts/healthcare-informatization|health information systems]]. The accuracy and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of data collected by consumer-grade devices may vary, and integration of PGHD into clinical workflows requires clear protocols and validation standards.
