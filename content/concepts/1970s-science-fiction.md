# 1970s science fiction

The 1970s era of [[concepts/science|science]] fiction is characterized by a pivot toward speculative fiction exploring themes of [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]], environmentalism, and [[concepts/scifi-movies|scifi movies]].

## Key Thematic Pillars
* **[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]**: Exploration of prescient, often unsettling, developments in [[concepts/machine-intelligence|machine intelligence]].
* **Environmentalism**: Cinematic narratives centered on ecological crisis and planetary [[concepts/preservation|preservation]].
* **[[concepts/scifi-movies|scifi movies]]**: Projections regarding shifting social structures and political decay.
