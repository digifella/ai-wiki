## Sage‑A1.5: Agentic Orchestration, Verification, and Open Scientific Discovery
**Clip title:** Sage‑A1.5: Agentic Orchestration, Verification, and Open Scientific Discovery
**Author / channel:** Fahd Mirza
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhMWVQ6ZXbc

### Summary
The video features a discussion on Sage-A1.5, a 35 billion parameter mixture of expert models built on Qwen 3.6. Sage-A1.5 is trained for long, multi-step work, allowing it to split tasks across sub-agents, check evidence, reason, and change course. 

The speaker tests the model using a real task in Hermes agent, involving a spacecraft gravity assist around Jupiter. Sage-A1.5 successfully simulates real physics, checks numbers against theory, and fixes itself. The speaker also tests the model's ability to act as a forensic physicist, auditing a seatbelt with real physics, working out the time, season, and region from a photo, and ranking hypotheses with probabilities. Sage-A1.5 performs well in both tests, demonstrating strong problem-solving and scientific reasoning capabilities.

Overall, the video highlights the advanced capabilities of Sage-A1.5, showcasing its potential for complex tasks in scientific research and engineering.
