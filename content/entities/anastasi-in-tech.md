# Anastasi In Tech

Anastasi In Tech is a [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel that produces video content analyzing technological developments and corporate strategies in the semiconductor and space industries. The channel examines large-scale industrial projects and their implications for technology sectors.

## Tesla-SpaceX Terafab Analysis

The channel's video "This New Chip Factory Could Save [[entities/america|America]]" examines [[entities/tesla|Tesla]]'s [[concepts/vertical-integration|vertical integration]] strategy for semiconductor manufacturing, specifically focusing on the development of 2nm [[concepts/ai-chips|AI chips]] through the [[concepts/terafab|Tesla-SpaceX Terafab]] initiative. The video explores how this collaboration between Tesla and [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] represents an attempt to establish domestic chip manufacturing [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and reduce reliance on external semiconductor suppliers.

The analysis discusses the technical and industrial significance of advancing chip manufacturing to 2nm processes while maintaining in-house production capabilities. The content positions this development within broader conversations about technological sovereignty and industrial capacity in the [[entities/united-states|United States]].
