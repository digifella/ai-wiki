# Autonomous Driving Technology

Autonomous driving technology encompasses a range of advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) and full autonomy solutions designed to reduce the need for human intervention in vehicle operation. Key players include [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] with its [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving]] (FSD) system, Waymo's autonomous ride-hailing service, and various automotive manufacturers implementing SAE Levels 2-5 technologies.
