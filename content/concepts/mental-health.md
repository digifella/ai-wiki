## Mental Health Overview
Mental health encompasses our emotional, psychological, and social [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]]. It affects how we think, feel, and act; it determines how we handle [[concepts/stress|stress]], relate to others, and make choices.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/exercise|Exercise]]**: Regular physical activity is crucial for mental health, reducing symptoms of [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]] and [[concepts/depression|depression]] while improving mood.
- **Sleep**: Adequate sleep helps maintain cognitive functions and emotional stability. Lack of sleep can exacerbate mental health issues such as [[concepts/depression|depression]] and bipolar disorder.
