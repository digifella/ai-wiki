For those using [[entities/adobe-lightroom|LR]] and running iPhones - this is for YOU. Works on iphone 13pro and later.

LR has released an incredibly powerful camera app (in beta) that [[entities/will|will]] do amazing zooms, night shots, remove reflections from [[entities/windows|windows]] and glasses etc. It initially overheated the phones but that has been fixed.  here is the overview from one of the guys I like (forget his weird accent - he's a kiwi who has lived all over the world and his accept is a mess he says)
