# Information And Communications Technology

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) encompasses the tools, systems, and infrastructure used to process, transmit, and manage information electronically. In health and [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] contexts, ICT includes [[concepts/corporate-platforms|digital platforms]], software applications, telecommunications networks, and devices that support [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]], patient communication, and health [[concepts/data-management|information management]].

## Applications in Healthcare

ICT enables fundamental functions across [[concepts/health|healthcare]] systems, including the collection, [[entities/storage|storage]], and analysis of health data. [[concepts/electronic-health-records|Electronic health records]] (EHRs), [[concepts/telehealth|telemedicine]] platforms, [[concepts/medical-imaging-technology|diagnostic imaging]] systems, and patient management software represent key applications. These technologies facilitate communication between healthcare providers, support [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]], and enable [[concepts/remote-monitoring|remote monitoring]] of patient health status.

## Measurement and Assessment

ICT familiarity is often assessed through [[concepts/survey|survey]] instruments using Likert [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]], which measure respondents' self-reported comfort and [[concepts/experience|experience]] with [[concepts/digital-interventions|digital health technologies]]. Such assessments help identify gaps in digital literacy among patients and healthcare [[entities/employees|workers]], informing training needs and technology implementation strategies.

## Broader Impact

The integration of ICT into health systems affects [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], efficiency, and [[concepts/quality-of-care|quality of care]]. However, successful implementation depends on adequate infrastructure, digital literacy, user-centered design, and consideration of equity issues to ensure technologies benefit diverse populations.
