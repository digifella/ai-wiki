# Error Correction

**[[concepts/bug-fixing|Error correction]]** refers to the set of techniques used to mitigate the effects of noise and decoherence in computational systems, ensuring [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]] and reliable operation. In the context of [[concepts/quantum-computing]], it is the primary bottleneck preventing scalable, fault-tolerant systems.

## Core Challenges
*   **Decoherence:** Quantum states are fragile and interact with their environment, causing loss of information.
*   **Noise:** Physical [[concepts/qubits|qubits]] suffer from high error rates, necessitating complex correction protocols.
*   **Resource Overhead:** Implementing [[concepts/quantum-error-correction|Quantum Error Correction]] (QEC) requires a massive ratio of physical qubits to logical qubits.

Recent critical assessments highlight significant gaps between theoretical promises and practical realities:
