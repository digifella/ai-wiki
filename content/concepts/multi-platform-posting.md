# Multi Platform Posting

Multi-platform posting refers to the simultaneous or coordinated [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of content across multiple social media channels and formats. Rather than creating separate versions of content for each platform, this approach streamlines production by automating the adaptation and publishing process.

## Automation with Claude Code and Blotato

Recent developments in [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|AI-assisted coding]] have made multi-platform posting more accessible to creators. [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]], an [[entities/ai-coding-assistant|AI coding assistant]], can be paired with [[entities/blotato|Blotato]], a content management tool, to automate the creation and distribution of short-form video content. This combination reduces the manual work required to format, edit, and post content to different platforms, potentially completing the entire [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] in minutes rather than hours.

The workflow typically involves generating or preparing source content once, then using [[concepts/automation|automation]] to adapt it to the specifications of different platforms—such as aspect ratios, duration limits, and format requirements—before publishing. This approach is particularly useful for creators managing multiple channels simultaneously, as it minimizes repetitive formatting tasks while maintaining [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across platforms.
