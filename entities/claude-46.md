# Claude 46

[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] 46 represents a conceptual shift in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] development practices observed around 2026. Rather than focusing primarily on selecting between different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] or refining prompts to elicit better outputs, the emerging paradigm emphasizes the infrastructure and systems that orchestrate AI execution. This reflects a maturation in how organizations [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] and utilize [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] in production environments.

## Harness Engineering

The core concept underlying [[concepts/claude|Claude]] 46 is that effective [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] performance depends less on the underlying model itself and more on the "[[concepts/harness|harness]]"—the [[concepts/execution-orchestration|execution framework]] that manages how the model operates. [[concepts/execution-failures|Harness engineering]] encompasses the architecture, orchestration [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], and operational systems that guide [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] and integrate it into broader workflows. This approach suggests that [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] effort should shift from [[concepts/ai-prompt-engineering|prompt optimization]] toward building robust systems for coordinating AI tasks.

## Execution Orchestration

Closely related to [[concepts/harness-engineering|harness engineering]] is [[concepts/model-configuration|execution orchestration]], which refers to the management of complex, [[concepts/multi-step-ai-operations|multi-step AI operations]]. Rather than treating an AI model as an isolated component, orchestration involves coordinating sequences of model calls, managing context, handling failures, and integrating outputs with other systems. This reflects a practical recognition that real-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] require sophisticated [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] layers beyond the model itself.
