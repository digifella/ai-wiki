# Meeting Prep Strategist

A Meeting Prep Strategist is a specialized AI assistant created through Google Gemini's Gems feature. Rather than utilizing Gemini's general-purpose interface, this custom assistant is configured with specific instructions, knowledge bases, and behavioral parameters designed to support professional meeting preparation. This specialization allows the assistant to provide focused guidance tailored to specific contexts, such as client negotiations, team stand-ups, or executive briefings.

The configuration process involves defining the assistant's role and tone within the Gem settings, enabling it to adopt a consistent persona relevant to the user's industry or specific meeting type. By leveraging the Gems framework, users can create reusable agents that maintain context across multiple sessions, ensuring that the advice remains aligned with established professional standards and organizational goals.

These customized agents function by applying predefined logic to incoming queries, filtering general AI capabilities through the lens of the specified meeting objective. This approach reduces the cognitive load on the user by automating the structuring of agendas, drafting talking points, and anticipating potential questions based on the configured knowledge base. The result is a streamlined preparation workflow that adapts to the unique requirements of each professional interaction.
