# [[entities/eliot-prince|Eliot Prince]] - power user of Perplexity

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eJ0FczURNNI>

The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] in the video [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] Perplexity as the _one_ essential AI tool, claiming it can replace the need for separate subscriptions to platforms like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[entities/claude|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] due to its comprehensive features.
Here's a detailed breakdown of the features and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] discussed:

1. **Integrated AI Model Selection (0:35):**
	* Perplexity allows users to choose from a variety of top [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] within its single interface. This includes:
		* Perplexity's own **[[concepts/sonar|Sonar]]** (fast model)
		* [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s **[[entities/claude-4|Claude 4]].0 Sonnet** (advanced model)
		* [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s **[[entities/gpt-4|GPT-4]].1** (advanced model)
		* [[entities/google|Google]]'s **[[entities/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Pro** (latest model)
		* [[concepts/xai|xAI]]'s **[[entities/grok|Grok]] 3 Beta** (latest model)
		* Specialized "[[concepts/reasoning-models|reasoning models]]" like **Reason1.lng**, **R1 1776**, and **o3**.
	* A "Best" option automatically selects the most suitable model for each query (e.g., Sonar for research, Claude for [[concepts/coding|coding]]). This eliminates the need to subscribe to multiple AI services.
2. **Advanced Source Filtering (2:06):**
	* Users can precisely control where Perplexity pulls its information from by toggling specific sources on or off. Options include:
		* **Web:** Searches across the entire internet.
		* **Academic:** Searches academic papers.
		* **Social:** Focuses on discussions and opinions (e.g., Reddit, forums).
		* **Finance:** Searches SEC filings for financial data.
	* An example showed searching "what is the best AI model right now?" with only the "Social" source enabled, resulting in an overview and comparative table based on Reddit community consensus.
3. **[[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] & [[entities/labs|Labs]] Functionality (3:30):**
	* Perplexity is presented as a purpose-built research tool that goes beyond simple queries.
	* **Researching Pain Points (3:30):** The speaker demonstrates a query: "I want to come up with proven micro [[concepts/saas|saas]] [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] and tools I can build. This requires finding peoples pain points and problems which they are struggling with. Then we know from the start that the tool/saas [[entities/will|will]] solve a specific problem. It's pre-validated. Can you research Reddit for problems and pain points that could be solved with a micro saas or tool. I want to focus on the parenting niche."
	* **Output and Validation (5:06):** Perplexity performs a deep search across 90 Reddit sources and threads, identifying real pain points and potential SaaS solutions. Examples include "Sleep [[concepts/training|Training]] Support Tool" and "Family Calendar Coordination Platform," with validation pulled directly from user discussions.
	* **Project Creation with Labs (5:57):** The "Labs" feature allows Perplexity to act as an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] to build out projects based on research. From the parenting pain point example, the speaker prompts Perplexity to "start bringing to life with a [[concepts/business-plan|business plan]], brand mood board, and mvp features to get us going."
	* **Comprehensive Business Plan (6:34):** The Labs feature generates a detailed output including:
		* A project name ("FamilyFlow")
		* Market Opportunity (TAM, SAM, SOM with estimated annual values)
		* Competitive [[concepts/gap-analysis|Gap analysis]]
		* Core Value Propositions
		* MVP Feature Set (with detailed functions and validation metrics)
		* Brand Identity & Mood Board (including color palette and image suggestions)
		* Technology Stack for building the app
		* Roadmap beyond MVP
		* Conservative Financial Projections
		* Key Risks & Mitigations
	* **App Mockup Generation (7:39):** Labs can also create visual mockups of applications and dashboards. An example shows a "cool modern techy glowing vivid magenta and vivid green 'SEO competitor' dashboard" complete with interactive charts and key metrics.
4. **Image Generation (8:00):**
	* Perplexity can generate images directly from text prompts, either integrated into Labs projects or as standalone creations in the chat. The speaker demonstrates creating [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] thumbnails and even a "professionally lit and edited photograph" of himself, showcasing its ability to combine user photos with generated graphics.
5. **Spaces (8:54):**
	* Perplexity offers "Spaces," which are akin to [[concepts/custom-gpts|custom GPTs]]. Users can:
		* Define [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] and roles for the AI (e.g., "Brainstorm Buddy").
		* Upload local [[concepts/files|files]] or add URLs to provide specific context for the AI's [[concepts/responses|responses]].
		* Select any of the available [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] ([[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]].1, Claude, Gemini, etc.) within that specific space.
		* Choose specific sources (Web, Academic, Social, Finance) for the AI to consult within the space.
		* Mention and link other "Spaces" together for complex, interconnected projects.
6. **Automated Tasks (10:00):**
	* Users can set up [[concepts/recurring-tasks|recurring tasks]] to automate research and content delivery. The speaker shows a weekly task to research "most important AI technology trends, product launches, and innovations," delivering the curated information automatically.
7. **Voice Input (11:45):**
	* Perplexity supports voice [[concepts/prompting|prompting]], allowing users to interact with the AI hands-free.

In conclusion, Perplexity is presented as a powerful, all-encompassing AI platform that centralizes various advanced AI [[concepts/models|models]], offers detailed source control, enables [[entities/deep-research|deep research]] for business development (including comprehensive business plans and app mockups), generates images, allows for custom AI "spaces," and supports automated tasks and voice input.
