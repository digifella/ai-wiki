# Perun

Perun is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] and military analyst focused on defense strategy, geopolitical implications of military technology, and the intersection of warfare with economic systems. The channel examines how modern conflicts interact with [[concepts/global-trade|global trade]] networks and [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], analyzing [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where military action directly disrupts economic activity.

## Airbase Hardening and A2/AD

Perun's analysis addresses the [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] of air force infrastructure to modern [[concepts/a2ad|anti-access/area denial]] (A2/AD) systems, particularly ballistic and cruise missiles. The content examines strategies for hardening military [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] against these threats, including dispersion, concealment, and redundancy measures. This reflects concerns about how peer competitors can project [[concepts/power|power]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] extended ranges, potentially neutralizing traditional air power advantages.
