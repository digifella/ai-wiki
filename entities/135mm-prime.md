# 135mm Prime

A 135mm prime lens is a fixed focal length optic categorized as a short telephoto lens. It provides moderate subject magnification and pronounced background compression, making it distinct from wider angle lenses. As a prime lens, it offers a single, non-variable focal length, which typically results in a wider maximum aperture and sharper optical performance compared to zoom lenses of equivalent range. The lens is commonly used in portrait and telephoto photography due to its ability to isolate subjects from their backgrounds.

The compression effect flattens perspective, reducing the apparent distance between foreground and background elements. This characteristic allows photographers to achieve wide-angle compositions through panorama stitching techniques, leveraging the long lens compression to create seamless, expansive images that maintain consistent depth and detail across the frame.
