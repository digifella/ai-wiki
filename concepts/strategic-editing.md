# Strategic Editing

**Strategic editing** refers to the intentional, goal-oriented approach to post-production where technical [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are subservient to the desired emotional impact or [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] of the final image. It moves beyond generic presets or isolated corrections to a holistic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process regarding how an image should "feel."

## Core Principles
- **Emotion First:** Define the target mood before applying any tools.
- **Intentionality:** Every adjustment must serve the narrative or aesthetic goal.
- **Depth & Impact:** Use techniques to add dimensionality rather than just correcting errors.

## Case Study: Two-Color Grading
A practical application of strategic editing is the use of limited color palettes to shift mood.
