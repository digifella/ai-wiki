# Company Nordvpn

[[entities/nordvpn|NordVPN]] is a virtual [[concepts/internal-networking|private network]] (VPN) service provider founded in 2012 and headquartered in Panama. The company operates a global network of servers distributed across numerous countries, enabling users to encrypt their internet traffic and obscure their IP addresses. NordVPN provides services to both individual consumers and organizations seeking [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and [[concepts/security|security]] for their online activities.

## Core Operations

The company's primary function is routing user internet connections through encrypted VPN tunnels, allowing customers to browse the web with their actual location hidden. This [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] supports anonymity and bypasses geographic restrictions on content. The service is designed to protect data from interception by third parties, including internet service providers and hackers.
