# Dr Feng Zhang

[[entities/feng-zhang|Dr. Feng Zhang]] is a researcher at the [[entities/broad-institute|Broad Institute]] known for his work in [[concepts/gene-editing|gene editing]] technology. He has been featured in educational media discussing advances in the field, including appearances on the [[entities/scishow-channel|SciShow channel]]'s Field Trip series.

## Gene Editing Research

Zhang's research focuses on the development and application of gene editing tools. He has been involved in work related to advances beyond [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR]], contributing to discussions about the future directions of gene editing technology and its potential [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Public Engagement

Dr. Zhang has participated in [[concepts/research-communications|science communication]] efforts, including a SciShow Field Trip episode that explored his work at the Broad Institute and provided insights into the evolving landscape of gene editing research.
