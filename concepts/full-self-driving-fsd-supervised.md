# Full Self Driving Fsd Supervised

[[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self Driving (FSD)]] Supervised is an advanced driver assistance system developed by [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] that operates at SAE Level 3 autonomy. The system manages both longitudinal and lateral vehicle control under specified operating conditions, such as highway driving, while requiring the driver to remain attentive and capable of resuming manual control when necessary. This positions FSD Supervised as an intermediate stage between conventional Level 2 advanced driver assistance systems and full autonomous driving.

The regulatory landscape for this technology is evolving, with recent approvals in specific jurisdictions setting important precedents. The approval of [[concepts/tesla-fsd-supervised|Tesla FSD Supervised]] in the Netherlands marks a significant step, potentially paving the way for its broader rollout across the [[concepts/european-union|European Union]]. This development highlights the increasing scrutiny and gradual acceptance of Level 3 systems within European regulatory frameworks.

From a scientific and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] perspective, the transition to Level 3 autonomy requires robust sensor fusion and real-time [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] capable of handling complex driving [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] without immediate human intervention. However, the mandatory requirement for driver readiness distinguishes it from higher levels of automation, maintaining a critical human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] [[concepts/safety-protocol|safety protocol]].
