# Artificial Intelligence

**[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI)** refers to the [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] of [[concepts/human-intelligence|human intelligence]] processes by computer systems, including [[concepts/learning|learning]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and self-correction. In materials [[concepts/science|science]] and energy [[entities/storage|storage]], AI is accelerating discovery through high-throughput screening and [[concepts/machine-learning|predictive modeling]].

## Applications in Energy Storage

AI is currently revolutionizing [[concepts/battery-technology]] and [[concepts/materials-design]] by enabling the invention of materials that do not yet exist in nature.

## Evolution of Scientific Discovery

Recent advancements mark a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in how scientific problems are approached:

- **[[concepts/mathematical-breakthroughs|Mathematical Breakthroughs]]:** [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s recent developments in [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|mathematical reasoning]] capabilities have expanded the scope of AI-assisted discovery [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-OpenAI-AI-Redefines-Scientific-Discovery-Mathematical-Br|OpenAI AI Redefines Scientific Discovery: Mathematical Breakthroughs and Factory Intelligence]].
- **[[concepts/factory-intelligence|Factory Intelligence]]:** The field is transitioning from "[[concepts/artisanal-intelligence|artisanal intelligence]]" (manual, expert-driven processes) to "factory intelligence" (automated, scalable, and systematic discovery pipelines).
- **Redefining Rules:** These breakthroughs suggest that AI is no longer just a tool for optimization but a driver of fundamental scientific rules and methodologies.
