# Wagering on War

Structural phenomenon where financial speculation on [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] outcomes generates Perverted Incentives that undermine military objectives, accelerate [[entities/war-profiteering|War Profiteering]], and risk strategic destabilization.

## Mechanisms
- **[[concepts/insider-trading|Insider Trading]]:** Exploitation of non-public military or intelligence data for financial gain compromises operational [[concepts/security|security]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
- **[[concepts/prediction-markets|Prediction Markets]]:** Betting platforms on geopolitical events may distort public perception and incentivize actors to prolong crises for profit.
- **[[concepts/military-corruption|Military Corruption]]:** Financial entanglement between defense sectors, political elites, and speculative capital fosters Military-Industrial Complex abuses and resource misallocation.

## Impact
- Destruction of army [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] and effectiveness due to profit-driven interference in command structures.
- Exacerbation of global conflicts as financial returns outweigh humanitarian or diplomatic resolutions.
- Erosion of institutional [[concepts/trust|trust]] through revealed conflicts of interest in [[concepts/crisis|crisis]] management.
