# Hubspot

Hubspot is a customer relationship management (CRM) platform designed to assist sales, marketing, and customer service teams in managing customer interactions and business processes. The system centralizes customer data, communication history, and activity tracking within a single interface, enabling organizations to coordinate efforts across departments throughout the entire customer lifecycle.

The platform offers an integrated suite of applications that facilitate the tracking of leads, management of sales pipelines, and execution of marketing campaigns. By automating routine tasks and providing unified visibility into customer data, Hubspot aims to streamline workflows and improve operational efficiency for businesses of varying sizes.
