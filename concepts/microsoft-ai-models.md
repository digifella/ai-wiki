# Microsoft AI Models

[[entities/microsoft]]'s suite of [[concepts/ai-models|artificial intelligence models]] and services, leveraging foundational architectures (often in partnership with [[entities/openai]]) to power enterprise solutions, search integration, and consumer-facing bots. Key developments include the deployment of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] into [[entities/microsoft-365]], [[entities/azure]], and Bing/Copilot ecosystems.

*   [[entities/azure-ai|Azure AI Services]]: Cloud-based infrastructure allowing developers to [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] [[concepts/custom-models|custom models]] or use pre-built [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], speech, and [[concepts/language-capabilities|language capabilities]].
