# Personal AI Framework

A structured approach to building, deploying, and managing [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] agents tailored to individual user needs, emphasizing [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], [[concepts/local-installation|local execution]], and modularity.

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/cloud-independence|Local-First Architecture]]**: Prioritizes [[concepts/local-execution|on-device processing]] to ensure data privacy and reduce latency.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open Source]]**: Relies on transparent, community-driven codebases for auditability and [[concepts/customization|customization]].
- **Modularity**: Allows users to swap components (LLMs, [[concepts/memory|memory]] stores, tools) based on specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Notable Implementations

### OpenJarvis
A prominent example of a local-first personal AI framework developed by [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford University]]'s [[entities/hazy-research|Hazy Research]] and [[entities/scaling-intelligence-labs|Scaling Intelligence Labs]].

- **[[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]**: Enables running powerful [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] directly on personal devices, prioritizing [[concepts/user-control|user control]] and privacy over cloud reliance.
- **Integration**: Utilizes [[concepts/ollama|Ollama]] for efficient [[concepts/local-model|local model]] management.
- **Performance Focus**: Designed to track and optimize [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] (wattage) during operation.
- **Documentation**: See [[lab-notes/2026-06-27-OpenJarvis-Stanfords-Local-First-Personal-AI-Framework-w|OpenJarvis: Stanford's Local-First Personal AI Framework with Ollama]] for detailed analysis.
