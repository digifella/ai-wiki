# French Culinary Techniques

French [[concepts/cooking-techniques|culinary techniques]] represent a formalized approach to cooking that emphasizes precision, [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], and classical methods. These techniques form the foundation of professional chef [[concepts/training|training]] worldwide and are characterized by standardized knife [[concepts/skills|skills]], sauce [[concepts/preparation|preparation]], [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]], and plating conventions developed over centuries of European culinary tradition.

## Representation in Korean Drama

French culinary training appears as a narrative device in Korean dramas, most notably in *[[entities/your-majesty|Your Majesty]]*, where a contemporary South Korean chef applies French cooking methods within a historical [[concepts/joseon-era|Joseon-era]] setting. This juxtaposition creates dramatic tension between modern culinary knowledge and the ingredients and cultural context of a different time period. The series uses French technique as a marker of the protagonist's professional expertise and as a vehicle for introducing viewers to both classical cooking methods and their practical [[concepts/software|applications]].

The presence of French culinary elements in K-drama reflects broader global interest in French gastronomy as a prestigious and recognizable culinary standard, while simultaneously exploring how specialized knowledge functions as a plot device in cross-temporal narratives.
