# Partner Level Work Product

Partner level work product refers to AI-generated content configured to meet professional [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] required in legal practice and similar high-stakes fields. This involves customizing [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] with specific [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] designed to produce output suitable for client deliverables, court filings, and other professional contexts where quality, [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] are essential.

## Configuration and Customization

Effective partner level work product requires systematic setup of each platform's available customization features. This includes establishing [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] that define tone, citation requirements, formatting standards, and domain-specific language conventions. Users configure these tools to operate within defined parameters that align with professional regulatory requirements and institutional standards, rather than relying on default configurations designed for general use.

## Key Considerations

Implementing partner level work product necessitates understanding each platform's capabilities, limitations, and appropriate [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] within professional practice. Different LLMs have varying strengths in legal analysis, document generation, and research support. Practitioners must establish verification protocols to ensure AI-generated content meets accuracy standards before submission, maintain clear records of AI involvement in work product creation, and remain aware of evolving professional responsibility rules regarding AI use in legal practice.
