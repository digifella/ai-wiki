# 30-second ad

A concise advertisement format delivering core messaging within 30 seconds, optimized for brand awareness, product promotion, or call-to-action campaigns.

## Modern Creation Method

- **Source Material**: Extract business [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] from [[entities/notebooklm]] [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] content
- **[[concepts/script-building|Script Building]]**: Generate script using [[entities/gemini]]'s "[[concepts/canvas|Canvas]]" feature (Advanced/Pro tier)
- **[[concepts/video-generation|Video Generation]]**: Create cinematic output via Google [[concepts/veo|Veo]] (integrated in [[concepts/google-vids]])
- **Output**: Ready-to-distribute 30-second ad with minimal manual editing
