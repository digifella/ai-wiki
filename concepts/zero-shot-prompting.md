# Zero-Shot Prompting

**Zero-Shot Prompting** is a technique where the model performs a task without prior examples or fine-tuning, relying solely on the instruction provided in the prompt. While traditionally associated with explicit step-by-step instructions, the paradigm is shifting with the advent of advanced [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] models.

## Evolution in Advanced Models

With the release of [[entities/claude|Claude]] 5 (specifically [[entities/fable-5|Fable 5]] and [[entities/opus-5|Opus 5]]) and [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/gpt-6-astra|GPT-6 Astra]], the necessity for rigid, explicit step-by-step prompting is diminishing.

## Industry Trends: Efficiency and Specialization

The broader AI landscape is moving away from monolithic scaling toward specialized, efficient architectures. This shift is evident in recent developments involving major industry players and specialized agents like [[entities/typesafe-ai|TypeSafe AI]].
