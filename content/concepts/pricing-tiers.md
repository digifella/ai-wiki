# Pricing Tiers

## Overview
Pricing tiers are structured plans offered by [[concepts/software|software]] or service providers to accommodate various customer needs and budgets. Each tier typically includes specific features and limitations designed for different levels of usage.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/frontier-models|Frontier Models]]:** Leading-[[concepts/edge-ai|edge AI]] models with advanced capabilities.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open Source]] Alternatives:** Free or low-cost AI solutions that offer flexibility and community support.
- **Specialized Generative AIs:** Tools tailored for specific industries or [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], often offering unique features not found in general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] models.
