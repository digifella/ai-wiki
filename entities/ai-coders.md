# AI Coders

## Definition and Role
**AI Coders** refers to developers or [[concepts/automations|automated systems]] leveraging [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] to generate, refactor, and maintain code. Unlike basic usage, expert-level application involves integrating [[concepts/advanced-tools|advanced tools]] into a cohesive workflow to maximize efficiency and reduce manual overhead.
