# Psychological Nightmares

**Psychological Nightmares** refer to a subgenre of horror and thriller media where terror is derived from mental instability, Reality Distortion, and existential dread rather than physical threats. These narratives often exploit the fear of losing one's grip on sanity, utilizing unreliable narrators and surreal imagery to induce [[concepts/cognitive-dissonance|cognitive dissonance]] in the audience.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Reality Fracturing**: The breakdown of logical [[concepts/cause-and-effect|cause-and-effect]], often manifesting as dream [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] or [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] Hypothesis-like glitches.
- **Internal Horror**: Threats originating from the protagonist’s own psyche, trauma, or repressed memories.
- **Ambiguous Endings**: Refusal to provide concrete resolutions, leaving the viewer in a state of prolonged uncertainty.
