# AI Consultant

An AI consultant is a professional service model that leverages artificial intelligence tools to scale expertise-based advice and solutions beyond traditional one-to-one consulting. Rather than relying exclusively on direct client engagement, this approach transforms accumulated professional knowledge, documentation, and personal notes into a system capable of serving multiple users simultaneously. This enables practitioners to extend the reach of their expertise while reducing the time investment required per interaction.

## Implementation Method

The typical workflow involves using tools like NotebookLM to process existing expertise materials—including research notes, case studies, documentation, and professional insights—and then publishing the resulting AI model as a service through Gemini Gems or similar platforms. This approach converts passive knowledge repositories into interactive consultation tools that can handle common queries, provide contextual advice, and maintain consistency with the consultant's methodology and standards.

## Advantages and Considerations

This model allows subject matter experts to monetize their knowledge more efficiently than traditional consulting, as the initial knowledge encoding effort supports unlimited subsequent client interactions. However, the quality and scope of the AI consultant depends significantly on the comprehensiveness and accuracy of the source materials used to train it. Regular updates and refinement of the underlying knowledge base remain necessary to maintain relevance and address evolving client needs.
