# Battery Reliability

Battery [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] represents a critical evaluation metric for electric vehicles, encompassing the ability of battery systems to maintain performance, retain capacity, and operate safely throughout their intended lifespan. Lithium-ion batteries, the dominant technology in modern EVs, undergo chemical degradation through repeated [[concepts/charging-cycle-life|charge-discharge cycles]], [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to temperature extremes, and manufacturing inconsistencies. The rate and extent of this degradation directly impacts vehicle value, operational [[concepts/cost|cost]], and consumer confidence in the technology.

## Performance Degradation and Capacity Retention

Most EV batteries experience measurable [[concepts/battery-degradation-rate|capacity loss]] within the first few years of operation, typically declining 2-3 percent annually under normal conditions. However, certain vehicle [[concepts/models|models]] have demonstrated accelerated degradation patterns, losing 10-15 percent capacity within 5-7 years. Factors contributing to premature failure include inadequate thermal management systems, battery chemistry choices that prioritize cost over longevity, and [[concepts/design|design]] flaws that expose cells to excessive [[concepts/stress|stress]]. Real-world data from fleet operators and long-term owner surveys provides the most reliable indicator of which models maintain usable capacity over extended periods.

## Manufacturing and Design Defects

Some electric vehicles have exhibited systematic battery defects traceable to manufacturing processes or component selection. Issues ranging from internal short circuits to separator degradation have led to premature cell failure, warranty claims, and in rare cases, safety incidents. Manufacturers with comprehensive [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]] protocols and robust thermal management systems consistently achieve better reliability outcomes than competitors relying on rushed production timelines or untested [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]].
