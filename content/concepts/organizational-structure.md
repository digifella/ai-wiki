# Organizational Structure

Organizational [[concepts/structure|structure]] refers to the formal system of task and reporting [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within an [[concepts/organization|organization]] that defines how activities are divided, grouped, and coordinated. A well-defined organizational structure enables effective communication, [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], coordination, and [[concepts/power|control]] in [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of organizational goals.

### Key Points
- Defines [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]] and roles
- Facilitates communication and information [[concepts/flow|flow]]
- Supports goal alignment and resource allocation
