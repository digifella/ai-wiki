# Portrait Masking

Portrait Masking is an automated masking feature in Lightroom Classic that utilizes artificial intelligence to detect and isolate portrait subjects within photographs. By analyzing facial features and subject boundaries, the tool generates precise selection masks with a single click. This functionality allows photographers to apply localized edits to specific areas of an image without the need for manual selection work, significantly reducing the time spent on repetitive masking tasks.

The feature extends beyond simple subject isolation by offering distinct masks for key elements such as the sky, background, teeth, and eyes. This granular control enables targeted adjustments to lighting, color, and sharpness for individual components of a portrait. The AI-driven approach ensures that boundaries are respected, facilitating complex edits that would traditionally require extensive manual effort.

By streamlining the selection process, Portrait Masking integrates into the broader editing workflow to enhance efficiency. It allows users to quickly refine portraits and landscapes by applying adjustments to specific regions, thereby maintaining image quality while accelerating the post-processing phase. The tool is designed to handle varying lighting conditions and subject positions, providing consistent results across different photographic scenarios.
