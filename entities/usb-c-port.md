# Usb C Port

A USB-C port is a standardized connector interface used for charging, data transfer, and video output on modern electronic devices. The physical connector features a small, oval-shaped design that is fully reversible, allowing it to be inserted in either orientation. This reversibility eliminates the directional constraints of earlier USB standards, such as USB Type-A and Type-B, providing a more user-friendly experience by removing the need to identify the correct insertion direction.

The port supports a wide range of communication protocols and power delivery standards, making it versatile for various applications. It can handle high-speed data transfer, display video signals, and deliver significant amounts of power to charge devices or even power larger peripherals. This flexibility has led to its adoption as a universal standard across smartphones, laptops, monitors, and other consumer electronics, replacing older proprietary and legacy connector types.
