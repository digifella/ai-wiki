# Adobe

**[[concepts/adobe|Adobe]]** is a multinational computer software company known for its multimedia and creativity software products. It is the primary [[concepts/developer|developer]] of [[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]], a plugin for [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] that processes raw image data.
