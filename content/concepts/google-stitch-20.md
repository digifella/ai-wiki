# Google Stitch 2.0

[[concepts/clickable-prototyping|Google Stitch 2.0]] is an [[concepts/ai-design-agent|AI design agent]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] that automates the transformation of design concepts into visual outputs. Users can input design [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] in the form of [[concepts/text|text]] descriptions or hand-drawn sketches, and the system generates corresponding design assets. This tool is designed to accelerate the [[concepts/visual-strategy-development|design workflow]] by reducing the time required to move from initial concept to finished work.

## Functionality and Use Cases

The platform operates as a [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] system that interprets user input—whether textual briefs or rough sketches—and produces design materials accordingly. This capability makes it applicable across various design disciplines, from graphic design to interface design. By processing both written specifications and visual references, Stitch 2.0 bridges different methods of design communication and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].

## Context in Design Tools

Stitch 2.0 represents [[entities/google|Google]]'s expansion into [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] tooling. As part of a broader trend of generative [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] into creative [[concepts/software|software]], it positions itself alongside similar design acceleration tools while leveraging Google's machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] infrastructure.
