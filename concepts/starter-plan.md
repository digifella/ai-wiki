# Starter Plan

The Starter Plan functions as the entry-level tier within Evernote’s restructured pricing model, which was implemented in 2026. This tier represents a strategic shift toward a more segmented subscription framework, designed to accommodate users with basic note-taking requirements and limited feature needs. By positioning the Starter Plan below mid-tier and premium options, Evernote aims to address a wider spectrum of customer usage patterns and budget constraints. The introduction of this tier coincided with significant adjustments to the overall pricing structure, resulting in a general increase in costs for existing subscribers who were migrated to the new system.

## Pricing and Feature Set

The Starter Plan is priced at a lower monthly or annual rate compared to the Plus and Professional tiers, offering a reduced feature set tailored for individual users. Key limitations include a cap on the number of devices that can sync simultaneously and a restriction on monthly upload allowances. Unlike higher tiers, it does not include advanced organizational tools such as custom tags, offline access to notebooks, or priority customer support. These constraints are intended to incentivize users with more complex needs to upgrade while providing a functional baseline for casual note-taking.

## User Reception

The implementation of the Starter Plan and the accompanying price hikes for other tiers generated mixed reactions from the user community. Long-time users expressed frustration regarding the loss of legacy features and the perceived reduction in value for money, leading to increased scrutiny of Evernote’s business practices. Conversely, some users acknowledged the plan as a necessary option for those who only require basic functionality without the complexity of enterprise-grade tools. The restructuring marked a definitive move away from Evernote’s previous freemium model toward a more rigidly paid subscription ecosystem.
