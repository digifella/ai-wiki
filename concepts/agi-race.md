# AGI Race

The competitive dynamic among [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research [[concepts/nodes|entities]] striving to achieve [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence]] (AGI). This landscape is characterized by rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycles, significant capital allocation, and intense competition for top-tier talent.

## Current State & Dynamics

- **Competitive Pressure**: The race is defined by the [[concepts/speed|speed]] of model iterations and the strategic acquisition of key researchers who influence architectural paradigms.
- **Talent Migration**: High-profile moves between major [[entities/labs|labs]] (e.g., [[entities/openai]], [[entities/google-deepmind]], [[entities/anthropic]]) significantly shift perceived trajectories and technical capabilities.
