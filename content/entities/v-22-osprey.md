# V-22 Osprey

The [[concepts/vertical-takeoff-and-landing|V-22 Osprey]] is a multi-mission [[concepts/tiltrotor-technology|tiltrotor]] aircraft engineered to operate in complex [[concepts/dual-flight-modes|dual flight modes]], transitioning between VTOL ([[concepts/vertical-takeoff-and-landing-vtol|Vertical Takeoff and Landing]]) and fixed-wing flight.

## Engineering & Operational Profile
- Utilizes advanced engineering to manage the mechanical transition between vertical and horizontal flight regimes.
- Capable of performing high-stakes operations, such as night rescue missions in the Libyan desert.
