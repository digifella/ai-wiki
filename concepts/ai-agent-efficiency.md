# AI Agent Efficiency

**AI [[concepts/agent-efficiency|Agent Efficiency]]** refers to the optimization of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]], latency, and reliability within iterative AI Agent Loop. Key strategies include [[concepts/structured-decision|structured decision]] modeling, reduced [[concepts/token-consumption|token consumption]], and enhanced error handling to prevent [[concepts/infinite-loops|infinite loops]] or [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] drift.

## Core Principles

*   **Structured Decision Models:** Utilizing [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] to guide [[concepts/reasoning-steps|agent reasoning]], improving reliability and reducing unnecessary processing steps.
*   **Iterative Optimization:** Continuous refinement of agent actions within the loop to minimize resource waste.
*   **Reliability:** Ensuring consistent [[concepts/output-quality|output quality]] despite complex or ambiguous inputs.
