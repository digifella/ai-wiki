## Meta's New Models & Agent
**Clip title:** Meta's New Models & Agent
**Author / channel:** Sam Witteveen
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aKVfjG7_R4g

### Summary
Here's a structured summary of the video content:

### Main Topic
The video explores the recent release of Meta's "Muse Spark Open," an 816-billion-parameter open-weights AI model, discussing its capabilities, licensing, and performance in comparison to other models like Reflection's Beam and Meta's own closed models.

### Key Points Discussed
- **Meta's Recent Releases:** Meta released the Muse Spark Open model, marketed as the most capable US open-weight model to date. The speaker notes that Meta has been actively releasing open models, such as Muse Glimmer and Muse Spark Open, alongside closed frontier models like Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full.
- **Model Specifications:** Muse Spark Open is a mixture-of-experts model with 816 billion total parameters and 42 billion active parameters per token. It features a 1-million token context window and is natively multimodal (text, image, video, and audio).
- **Performance and Benchmarks:** While Muse Spark Open is solid, it ranks lower on the Artificial Analysis intelligence index compared to top Chinese open models (like MiMo-V2.6-Pro and GLM-5.3). However, it outperforms Beam in several benchmarks, though Beam is smaller and offers a cleaner Apache 2.0 license.
- **Licensing and Usage:** Muse Spark Open operates under Meta's Muse model license, which requires displaying "Built with Muse Spark Open" and negotiating a separate agreement for commercial operations exceeding $20 million in revenue.
- **Demos & Observations:** The speaker runs demos comparing Muse Spark Open and Muse Wonder, noting that Muse Wonder uses fewer thinking tokens and produces consistent results, while Muse Spark Open uses significantly more tokens for reasoning.

### Conclusions and Takeaways
- **Meta's Strategic Shift:** Meta's return to releasing open-weight models is seen as a positive development for the AI community, providing viable alternatives to closed-source systems, particularly for users seeking plausible deniability regarding data usage.
- **Open vs. Closed Models:** Muse Spark Open is a powerful, highly capable model, but closed models like Muse Wonder generally offer better performance and efficiency for specific tasks, albeit with stricter access and licensing terms.
