# AI Augmented Workflows

AI Augmented Workflows describe a working methodology that integrates [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with persistent note-taking systems to enhance [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]]. By connecting AI assistants like [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] with tools such as [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]], users create environments where their [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] have contextual access to personal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]]. This integration allows the AI to reference existing [[concepts/notes|notes]], understand project history, and contribute directly to an evolving body of work—rather than operating in isolated conversation sessions.

## Core Architecture

The fundamental structure combines a note-taking application that maintains documents and links with an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] capable of reading and [[concepts/writing|writing]] to that collection. [[concepts/claude|Claude]] or similar models can be queried with context from relevant notes, retrieve information from a user's existing [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]], and generate content that integrates with the broader system. This creates a [[concepts/user-feedback-loop|feedback loop]] where the AI's outputs become part of the persistent record, available for future reference and building.

## Practical Applications

This approach suits various [[concepts/personal-knowledge-management-pkm|knowledge work]] tasks including research synthesis, project management, writing assistance, and decision documentation. The persistent nature allows users to maintain [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] across work sessions—an AI can [[concepts/recall|recall]] previous discussions, reference decisions already made, and build incrementally on past analysis rather than starting fresh in each conversation.

## Limitations and Considerations

While AI-augmented workflows offer efficiency gains, they depend on meaningful [[concepts/note-organization|note organization]] and require users to actively manage what information the AI can access. The quality of outputs remains bounded by the underlying model's capabilities and the [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] of context provided, and such systems introduce questions about data [[entities/storage|storage]], [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and vendor dependency that merit careful consideration.
