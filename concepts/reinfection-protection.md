# Reinfection protection

The biological or medical capacity to prevent a subsequent infection by a pathogen following an initial [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]], or therapeutic intervention.

- [[concepts/malaria-vaccination|Malaria vaccination]] via mosquitos: Experimental delivery via insect vectors has demonstrated lifelong [[concepts/secure|protection]] in animals, notably despite the extremely short [[concepts/drug-half-life]] of the delivered agent.
