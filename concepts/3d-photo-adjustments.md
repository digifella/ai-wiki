# 3D Photo Adjustments

Techniques used to manipulate the perceived spatial dimensions, depth, and plane-specific characteristics of a 2D image.

## Core Concepts
- [[concepts/depth-map]]: A grayscale image where luminosity values represent distance from the camera (typically white for foreground, black for background).
- [[concepts/lens-blur]]: A feature in [[concepts/camera-raw]] and [[concepts/lightroom]] designed to simulate optical bokeh and depth-of-field.
- [[concepts/depth-range-masking|Depth Masking]]: The process of using spatial data to isolate specific planes within an image for localized editing.

## Advanced Techniques
- **Precise Plane Manipulation**: Leveraging [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/depth-map|depth map]] feature to execute more advanced, realistic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] than standard automated tools allow.
- **Granular Adjustments**: Utilizing depth data to target specific layers of an image for complex [[concepts/light|light]] or color manipulation without affecting the entire frame.
