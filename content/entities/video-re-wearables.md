# Video Re Wearables

[[concepts/wearable-devices|Wearable devices]] represent a significant technological advancement in [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] practice. These are electronic instruments designed to be worn directly on the body, equipped with [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] that continuously collect biometric and physiological data. Beyond their consumer [[concepts/software|applications]] as fitness trackers, [[concepts/internet-of-things|wearables]] in [[entities/allied-health|allied health]] contexts serve diagnostic and monitoring functions that support [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]] and patient management.

The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of embedded sensor technology allows [[entities/iot-devices|wearable devices]] to track a [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]] relevant to allied health professionals, including [[concepts/exercise|movement]] patterns, heart rate variability, muscle activation, and other real-time physiological [[concepts/indicators|indicators]]. This continuous data collection enables practitioners in fields such as physiotherapy, occupational therapy, and sports medicine to objectively assess patient progress and adjust interventions based on quantifiable evidence rather than periodic clinical assessments alone.

The [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[entities/wearable-devices|wearable technology]] in allied health represents a shift toward more remote and continuous patient monitoring. This capability allows [[concepts/health|healthcare]] providers to extend their oversight beyond traditional clinic-based appointments, supporting better outcomes through real-time [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] and earlier detection of complications or changes in patient status.
