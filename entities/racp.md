# Racp

The Royal Australasian College of Physicians (RACP) is a professional medical organisation representing physicians across Australia and New Zealand. Established as the primary certifying and standard-setting body for internal medicine and related specialties in the Australasian region, the College maintains a fellowship programme and trains medical professionals through various educational initiatives.

## Governance

In February 2026, the RACP Board issued a statement to Fellows and trainees addressing governance matters within the organisation. The College Board expressed concern regarding allegations related to governance, communicating these issues directly to the fellowship and trainee communities as part of its commitment to transparency regarding internal organisational affairs.
