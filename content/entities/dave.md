# Dave
## Overview
Dave is an individual who has garnered [[concepts/attention|attention]] for his unique approach to explaining complex concepts in technology and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. He is known for breaking down barriers between technical jargon and layman's understanding, often using unconventional methods.

### Projects & Contributions
- **[[concepts/transformer-training|Demystifying AI]]: Transformer [[concepts/training|Training]] on a [[entities/pdp-1144|1979 PDP-11]]**
    - On [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|April 13]], 2026, Dave presented an eye-opening video titled "EXPOSED: The Dirty Little Secret of AI (On a 1979 PDP-11)" on his channel, [[entities/daves-garage|Dave's Garage]].
    - In this video, he demonstrates the [[concepts/training-process|training process]] of a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] using a transformer model on a vintage 1979 [[concepts/pdp-1144|PDP-11/44]] computer. This system operates with limited [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] resources: a single 6MHz [[concepts/cpu|CPU]] and initially 64KB of [[concepts/ram|RAM]] (later upgraded to 4MB).
    - The core message from Dave is that the magic behind AI isn't new or mysterious; it's about understanding how algorithms can be adapted to work within constraints.
