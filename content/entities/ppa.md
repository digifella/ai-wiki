# Ppa

Ppa refers to a collection of AI productivity resources focused on assisting photographers with [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s creative [[concepts/software|software]] suite, particularly [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]. These resources concentrate on practical techniques for leveraging [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] features to streamline photography workflows and enhance editing efficiency.

## Adobe Integration

The materials address how photographers can implement [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] within Adobe's ecosystem to automate repetitive tasks and improve editing [[concepts/speed|speed]]. This includes features available in both Photoshop and Lightroom that utilize [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] to assist with image processing, selection, and adjustment tasks.

## Practical Application

The resources are designed for working photographers seeking to integrate [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into their existing creative processes rather than replace traditional [[concepts/skills|skills]]. The focus remains on productivity enhancement through software features rather than fundamental changes to photographic practice.
