# External Ai Harnesses

External AI harnesses refer to third-party tools, integrations, and platforms that extend or modify the functionality of commercial AI systems. This concept gained particular relevance in April 2026 following [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]] regarding how its [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] service could be accessed and used through [[concepts/third-party-applications|external applications]] and interfaces.

## Anthropic's Policy Changes (April 2026)

In April 2026, Anthropic announced significant changes to its Claude AI subscription [[concepts/policies|policies]], including the banning of [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], a third-party tool or integration that had previously allowed alternative access methods to the [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] service. These [[concepts/policy-updates|policy updates]] also introduced new [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] for subscription tiers. The changes reflected Anthropic's efforts to standardize how Claude is accessed and to establish clearer boundaries around authorized [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and integration points.

## Scope and Implementation

The policy modifications affected users relying on external harnesses to interact with Claude, requiring migration to official Anthropic channels or approved alternatives. The changes included financial implications for existing users and prompted discussions about the relationship between AI providers and the broader ecosystem of third-party developers building on top of commercial AI services.
