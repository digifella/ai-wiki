# Medgemma 27b

MedGemma 27B is a 27 billion parameter large language model developed by Google, specifically engineered for medical applications. It is designed to process and comprehend both text and images within clinical and healthcare contexts, enabling the handling of multimodal tasks that involve medical documentation, diagnostic imagery, and health data analysis.

The model combines text and image understanding to support medical professionals in clinical workflows. Its architecture allows it to interpret medical images while simultaneously processing healthcare-related information, facilitating more accurate analysis and integration of diverse medical data sources.
