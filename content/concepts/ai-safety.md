# Guardrails
**Guardrails** refer to mechanisms and [[concepts/policies|policies]] designed to ensure the safe and ethical use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] (AI) technologies. This concept has gained prominence as AI's capabilities expand, leading tech companies like Nvidia and [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] to develop their own strategies for deploying robust guardrail systems.

## Summary
- **[[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]]**: Recently open-sourced a guardrails framework aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] making AI [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] safer and more accessible.
- **[[entities/openai|OpenAI]]**: Tends to offer [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] services rather than an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] approach for [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI applications]]; however, has recently released **[[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-OSS]]**, a series of [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/models|models]] under the [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/license|license]] to offer customizable and low-[[concepts/cost|cost]] [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]].
