# Data Management

Data management encompasses a [[concepts/range|range]] of practices aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] organizing and managing information to ensure its [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], [[concepts/security|security]], and usability. It involves various activities such as data [[entities/storage|storage]], backup strategies, the transition between different platforms or services, and full-stack [[concepts/web-application|web application]] development.

## Key Concepts
- **[[entities/storage|Storage]] Solutions:** Options for storing and retrieving your data efficiently.
  - cloud-storage
  - [[concepts/local-storage|local-storage]]
- **Backup Strategies:** [[concepts/methods|Methods]] to ensure that your information is protected against loss or corruption.
  - backup-strategies
- **[[concepts/data-management|Data Migration]]:** Processes involved in transferring your data from one system to another, such as moving from [[entities/evernote|Evernote]] to other platforms.
  - data-migration
- **Full-Stack [[concepts/web-application|Web Application]] Development:** Building functional [[concepts/web-applications|web applications]] with [[concepts/user-accounts|user accounts]], [[concepts/database-storage|database storage]], and [[concepts/file-uploads|file uploads]] using frameworks like [[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]] and Firebase.
  - [[concepts/full-stack-web-app|full-stack-web-app]]
  - firebase
  - [[concepts/google-search|google]]-[[entities/ai-studio|ai-studio]]

## Recent Developments
- New [[concepts/pricing|pricing structures]] introduced by service providers can affect user strategies and decisions regarding their data management.
- The shift towards full-stack web [[concepts/software|applications]] with integrated database and file storage solutions.
- Comprehensive guides available for transforming static websites into dynamic web applications using [[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]] and Firebase.
