# Compression in Local Large Language Models (LLMs)

[[concepts/file-size-reduction|Compression techniques]] are essential for optimizing the performance and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. They reduce [[concepts/code-size|model size]] and computational requirements while preserving or enhancing functionality.

### Key Points:
- **[[concepts/model-pruning|Model Size Reduction]]**: Techniques like [[concepts/model-compression]] and [[concepts/model-compression]] reduce the [[entities/storage|storage]] footprint of LLMs.
- **[[concepts/computational-efficiency|Computational Efficiency]]**: [[concepts/compression-algorithm|Compression methods]] improve [[concepts/computational-efficiency]] by lowering [[concepts/memory|memory]] and processing demands.
- **Context [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Ensuring that compressed models retain critical [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] for specialized tasks.
- **Hardware Feasibility**: [[concepts/parameter-reduction|Quantization]] enables complex models to run on consumer hardware by fitting within available [[concepts/memory|RAM]], as demonstrated in recent analyses of local [[concepts/coding|coding]] LLMs.
- **[[concepts/scenarios|Use Cases]]**: Critical for [[concepts/local-coding-assistants|local coding assistants]] where latency, [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and offline availability are prioritized over cloud-based [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]].
