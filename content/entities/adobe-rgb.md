# Adobe RGB

[[concepts/adobe-rgb|Adobe RGB]] is a color space designed for use in digital imaging and professional color work. It was developed by Adobe Systems and offers a larger gamut than the standard sRGB color space, making it particularly useful for print production and professional photography workflows. The color space is characterized by its ability to represent a wider [[concepts/range|range]] of colors, especially in the cyan-green range, which makes it valuable for [[concepts/software|applications]] requiring precise color [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]].

## Use in Professional Workflows

Adobe RGB is commonly used in digital photography, graphic [[concepts/design|design]], and color-critical [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]]. Many professional photographers and designers work in Adobe RGB color space when preparing [[concepts/images|images]] for print, as it provides better color fidelity for CMYK conversion and print outputs. Software like [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] support Adobe RGB as a working color space, allowing users to maintain color accuracy throughout their editing processes.

## Considerations and Limitations

While Adobe RGB offers advantages for professional work, it is not widely supported for web display, as most monitors and web browsers are optimized for sRGB. This means that images created in Adobe RGB may display differently when viewed online unless they are converted to sRGB. Photographers and designers must therefore consider their final [[concepts/output|output]] [[entities/medium|medium]] when choosing whether to work in Adobe RGB or sRGB.
