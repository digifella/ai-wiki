# Skill Leap Ai

Skill Leap Ai is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] focused on practical guides for deploying and [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] on personal computers. The channel provides step-by-step tutorials designed to [[entities/make|make]] advanced [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] accessible to individual users without requiring [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]] or external APIs.

## Content Focus

The channel's primary emphasis is on [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] of [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]], particularly [[entities/meta|Meta]]'s [[entities/llama|Llama]] series. Notable content includes guides for running [[entities/llama-31|Llama 3.1]] with a stated [[concepts/setup|setup]] time of approximately 10 minutes, aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] users seeking private, on-device [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] [[concepts/inference|inference]]. The channel also covers related topics such as [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] and [[concepts/browser-control|browser control]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
