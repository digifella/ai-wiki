# Pixel Integrity Preservation

Principle of digital [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] focused on maintaining original image quality while removing unwanted elements without introducing artifacts or distortion.

- [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]]'s [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] demonstrates using [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s **[[concepts/remove-tool|Remove Tool]]** (Wires and Cables) for efficient [[concepts/chain-link-fence-removal|chain-link fence removal]] in zoo photography, preserving [[concepts/texture|texture]] and context (see 2026 04 14 [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]] remove chain link fence from photos).
- Relies on modern AI-assisted tools to replace obstructive elements with contextually accurate pixels, avoiding manual [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] or blurring.
- Core application in [[concepts/photo-editing|Image Retouching]] workflows where [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|Non-Destructive Editing]] techniques prevent irreversible quality loss.
- Requires careful [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] to maintain edge [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] and avoid "halo" effects common in traditional removal methods.
