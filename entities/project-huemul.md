# Project Huemul

**Project Huemul** was a fraudulent [[concepts/fusion-power|nuclear fusion]] [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] conducted in Argentina during the mid-20th century, orchestrated by physicist **Román Battista**. The project claimed to have achieved [[concepts/cold-fusion|cold fusion]] or significant energy generation using lithium and heavy water but is widely regarded as one of history's most elaborate scientific hoaxes.
