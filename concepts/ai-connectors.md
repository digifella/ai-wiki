# AI Connectors

AI connectors are integrations and [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] that link [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and language models—such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and similar systems—to existing applications, workflows, and data sources. These connectors enable [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to interact across multiple platforms and services, allowing them to access information, execute [[concepts/commands|commands]], and trigger actions in [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]. By bridging AI capabilities with enterprise software, databases, and specialized applications, connectors extend the functionality of standalone AI models into broader operational ecosystems.

## Purpose and Function

Connectors serve as intermediaries that standardize communication between AI systems and [[concepts/third-party-applications|third-party applications]]. Rather than requiring custom development for each integration, connectors provide pre-built pathways for AI agents to query databases, retrieve files, send messages, process payments, or update records in external systems. This reduces implementation complexity and allows organizations to automate multi-step workflows that span multiple tools and platforms.

## Common Use Cases

AI connectors enable practical applications across business and creative domains. They facilitate customer service automation by connecting chatbots to CRM systems, support [[concepts/document-interaction|document retrieval]] workflows by linking language models to file [[entities/storage|storage]] services, and streamline business processes by allowing agents to interact with accounting software, [[concepts/issue-trackers|project management tools]], or communication platforms. In creative contexts, connectors help [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] access design assets, content libraries, or collaboration environments.

## Technical Implementation

Connectors are typically implemented through REST APIs, webhooks, or standardized frameworks that define how AI systems request and receive data. Major [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI platforms]] and [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]] increasingly offer connector marketplaces or [[concepts/native-integration|native integration]] options to simplify setup. The effectiveness of a connector depends on clear API documentation, appropriate [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] protocols, and well-defined data formats that allow reliable bidirectional communication between the [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] and target application.
