# Turkish Airlines

Turkish Airlines (Türk Hava Yolları, THY) is the national flag carrier and largest airline of [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]]. Founded in 1933, it operates scheduled domestic and international flights from its main hub [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Istanbul Airport, serving destinations across [[entities/europe|Europe]], Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

## E-Visa Documentation

Turkey offers an electronic visa ([[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-Visa]]) system for eligible travelers, allowing citizens of many countries to obtain travel [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] online prior to arrival. Documentation for Turkish e-Visas can be obtained through the official [[entities/turkish-government|Turkish government]] portal. Applicants should verify their eligibility and specific requirements based on their nationality before applying.

Travelers using Turkish Airlines or other carriers should ensure they have the necessary travel documentation, including valid passports and appropriate visas or e-Visas, well in advance of their departure date.
