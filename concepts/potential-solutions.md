# Potential Solutions

Potential Solutions refers to the identification and evaluation of candidate approaches to address specific problems or challenges within business and organizational contexts. In strategy development, these solutions emerge from rigorous problem analysis and represent the range of viable options available before final decisions are made. This concept is particularly relevant in domains requiring systematic evaluation of alternatives, such as process improvement, technology adoption, and operational optimization.

## Identification and Evaluation

The process of surfacing potential solutions involves generating a broad set of alternatives through brainstorming, benchmarking, and analysis of existing constraints. Each candidate is then assessed against predefined criteria, including feasibility, cost, risk, and alignment with strategic goals. This evaluation phase ensures that only the most promising options proceed to detailed planning and implementation, reducing the likelihood of resource waste on unviable paths.
