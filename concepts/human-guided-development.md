# Human-guided development

A development paradigm where human oversight and direction remain central to iterative AI and software enhancement, ensuring alignment with human values and goals through continuous [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]].

## Key developments
- [[concepts/ai-agent-evolution|AI Recursive Improvement]] has entered a [[concepts/phase|phase]] of [[concepts/self-improvement]], where [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] autonomously enhance their own capabilities (per [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]]'s video "[[concepts/hard-takeoff|Hard Takeoff]] has started"), potentially accelerating toward an [[concepts/intelligence-explosion]] and challenging traditional Human-guided development models.
- This marks a critical inflection point where AI-driven self-improvement may outpace human-guided [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycles.
