# Vintage Car Restoration

**Vintage Car [[concepts/preservation|Restoration]]** involves the process of returning a classic vehicle to its original factory condition or enhancing it with modern technology while preserving its aesthetic [[concepts/honesty|integrity]]. Key disciplines include Bodywork, [[concepts/engine|Engine]] Rebuilding, Interior Upholstery, and Automotive Electronics.

## Modern Integration Challenges
Integrating modern safety and convenience features into pre-1980s vehicles requires careful planning to maintain period-correct aesthetics while improving functionality. Common modifications include:
- Hidden LED Lighting systems for improved visibility without altering exterior lines.
- Modern Stereo Systems disguised behind vintage dashboards.
- Upgraded Braking Systems with hidden ABS modules.

## Best Practices
- **Wiring:** Use Loom Wrapping to protect modern wiring from heat and abrasion in vintage engine bays.
- **Power Management:** Ensure Voltage [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]] is stable to prevent damaging sensitive LED components in 12V systems.
- **Aesthetics:** Prioritize [[concepts/stealth|Stealth]] Modifications that do not compromise the vehicle's historical value or visual authenticity.
