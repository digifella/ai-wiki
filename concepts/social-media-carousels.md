# Social Media Carousels

Social media carousels are a multi-slide content format available on major platforms including Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok. Users create carousels by uploading a series of images or slides that viewers navigate sequentially. Each carousel typically contains between two and ten slides, though specific platform limits vary. The format allows creators to present multiple related images or pieces of information within a single post, effectively combining the static nature of photography with the narrative flow of video.

## Navigation and Platform Variations

On Instagram, carousels are primarily accessed by swiping horizontally, with the platform displaying page indicators to show progress through the sequence. LinkedIn supports document uploads that function as carousels, allowing users to share PDFs or presentations directly in the feed for professional or educational purposes. Facebook integrates carousels into its photo albums and post structures, while TikTok has introduced carousel features to allow users to share multiple images alongside video content.

## Content Strategy and Utility

Creators utilize carousels to break down complex topics, tell step-by-step stories, or showcase product variations without requiring video production. This format encourages higher engagement rates as users spend more time interacting with the post to view all slides. It serves as a versatile tool for educators, marketers, and artists to deliver structured information or visual narratives in a digestible, single-unit format.
