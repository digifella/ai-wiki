## Overview
Donald Macaskill is the Chief Executive of Scottish Care, a key [[concepts/tone|voice]] in shaping policy for social care in [[entities/united-kingdom|Scotland]]. He advocates for [[concepts/person-centred-care|person-centered care]] models that prioritize human dignity and flourishing, particularly regarding the integration of emerging technologies like [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) into aged care systems.

## Key Positions & Advocacy

*   **Humanity-Centric Technology:** Advocates for "holding fast" to core human values while adopting new tools, ensuring that efficiency gains do not come at the cost of [[concepts/empathy|empathy]] or [[concepts/personalized-care|individualized care]] plans.
