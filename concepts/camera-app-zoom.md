# Camera App Zoom

Camera App Zoom is a beta feature within the Adobe Lightroom mobile application that integrates computational photography tools directly into the capture interface. This functionality allows users to apply image processing at the moment of capture rather than relying solely on post-processing adjustments. By leveraging the advanced neural engines and hardware capabilities of supported devices, the app enables real-time optimization of photographic elements during the shooting process.

The feature is available for iPhone 13 Pro and later models, utilizing the specific hardware sensors and processors found in these devices to enhance image quality. Key capabilities include support for zoom functionality, which aids in composition and framing, as well as night shots that improve visibility and detail in low-light conditions. Additionally, the app offers reflection removal tools to address common issues with glass surfaces and other reflective materials.

This integration represents a shift in mobile photography workflows by bringing professional-grade processing power to the point of capture. It allows photographers to achieve desired aesthetic results immediately, reducing the need for extensive editing later. The feature remains in beta, indicating that its functionality and performance may evolve as Adobe continues to refine the computational photography algorithms.
