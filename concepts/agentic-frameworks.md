# Agentic Frameworks

Agentic frameworks are software tools and libraries designed to facilitate the development of autonomous AI agents. These systems enable agents to perceive their environment, make decisions, and execute actions with minimal human intervention. By providing standardized abstractions for agent architecture, these frameworks simplify the complex process of creating intelligent systems that can operate independently. Key components typically included in these frameworks are decision-making loops, planning mechanisms, and tool integration protocols.

## Core Architecture

Most agentic frameworks rely on a core loop that allows the agent to observe its state, reason about potential actions, and interact with external tools or APIs. This architecture often incorporates memory systems to retain context across interactions and planning modules to break down complex goals into manageable steps. The integration of large language models serves as the cognitive engine, interpreting inputs and generating structured outputs that drive the agent's behavior.

## Governance and Security Risks

The rise of agentic frameworks has introduced new governance and cyber risks, particularly regarding ungoverned implementations within organizations. Autonomous agents may execute actions with unintended consequences if their decision-making boundaries are not strictly defined or monitored. Organizations face challenges in ensuring that these agents adhere to security policies, data privacy regulations, and operational constraints, necessitating robust oversight mechanisms to prevent unauthorized access or data leakage.
