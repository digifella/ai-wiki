# Google Gemma

Google Gemma is a family of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]]. The models are released under the [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0 license]], making them freely available for research and commercial use. Gemma models are designed to be efficient and deployable across various [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] configurations, from edge devices to standard servers.

## Model Variants and Capabilities

The Gemma series includes multiple model sizes and variants, with the [[concepts/23b-parameter-models|Gemma 4]] representing a recent [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] offering advanced capabilities. These models are engineered for efficient [[concepts/inference|inference]] and have been optimized for tasks including general [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], and [[concepts/instruction-following|instruction following]]. The smaller variants are particularly suited for resource-constrained environments where [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] is a priority.

## Deployment and Fine-tuning

Gemma models support fine-tuning on custom datasets, enabling adaptation to specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and domains. Tools and frameworks have been developed to facilitate local fine-tuning workflows, allowing practitioners to customize models without requiring extensive [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]]. The open-source [[entities/nature|nature]] of Gemma encourages integration with existing machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] workflows and notebooks.
