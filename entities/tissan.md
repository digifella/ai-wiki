# Tissan

Tissan is a review initiative conducted by Bec that evaluates an organization's artificial intelligence strategy and operational readiness. The review examined the organization's AI masterplan and identified significant gaps between the strategic objectives outlined in planning documents and the actual capabilities available to implement them.

## Infrastructure and Systems Assessment

The Tissan review revealed substantial constraints in the existing infrastructure supporting AI implementation across the organization. The assessment identified critical issues in the technical systems and tooling required to support the proposed AI workflows. These limitations hindered the ability to execute the masterplan effectively, highlighting a disconnect between high-level strategic goals and the underlying technical foundation.

## Operational Challenges

Beyond infrastructure, the review highlighted challenges with the proposal and tool systems used within the organization. These systemic inefficiencies contributed to the broader gap between planned AI capabilities and realized operational outcomes. The findings underscore the need for alignment between strategic planning, technical infrastructure, and the tools available to staff.
