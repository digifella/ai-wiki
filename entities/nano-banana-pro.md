# Nano Banana Pro

## Overview
[[entities/nano-banana-pro|Nano Banana Pro]] is an advanced [[concepts/ai-image-editing|AI image editing]] model by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], designed for precise and consistent control over image modifications. It builds upon the capabilities of its predecessor, [[entities/nano-banana-2]], enhancing the ability to manipulate images through JSON [[concepts/data-extraction|data extraction]] and manipulation.
