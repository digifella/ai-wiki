# Exoplanet Discovery

The systematic detection and characterization of planets outside the Solar System. This field relies on indirect detection methods, [[concepts/direct-imaging|direct imaging]], and future mission technologies to identify habitable zones and atmospheric compositions.

## Recent Developments

*   **Mission [[concepts/software-updates|Updates]] & Technological Advancements:** Recent weekly roundups highlight significant progress in [[concepts/space-exploration]] missions, including updates on the [[concepts/dragonfly-mission|Dragonfly mission]] to [[concepts/oceangate-titan|Titan]] and the Hera mission to the Didymos system.
*   **Celestial Phenomena:** New insights into historical astronomical records, such as those regarding [[concepts/theta-eridani]], continue to inform modern understanding of stellar environments and potential planetary systems.
*   **Key Resource:** For a comprehensive summary of these developments, see [[lab-notes/2026-07-19-Dragonfly-Hera-and-Theta-Eridani-Recent-Space-Discoverie|Dragonfly, Hera, and Theta Eridani: Recent Space Discoveries]].

## Detection Methods

*   **Transit [[concepts/photometry|Photometry]]:** Monitoring brightness dips as planets cross their host [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]].
*   **Radial Velocity:** Measuring stellar wobble caused by planetary [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravitational pull]].
*   **Direct Imaging:** Capturing [[concepts/light|light]] directly from the planet, often requiring advanced coronagraphs.
