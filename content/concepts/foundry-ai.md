# Foundry Ai

[[entities/foundry|Foundry Ai]] is an Azure-based AI service designed to facilitate the [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] of [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) systems. RAG is an approach that combines [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with [[concepts/external-data|external data]] retrieval, allowing AI systems to reference and incorporate relevant information from specified sources rather than relying solely on [[concepts/training-data|training data]].

## Purpose and Function

The service enables developers and organizations to build [[concepts/software|applications]] that ground AI [[concepts/responses|responses]] in specific documents, databases, or [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]]. By retrieving relevant context before generating responses, Foundry Ai helps improve [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], reduce hallucinations, and allow the system to work with proprietary or up-to-date information that may not be in the model's [[concepts/language-data|training data]].

## Integration with Azure

As an [[entities/azure|Azure]] service, Foundry Ai integrates with [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]]'s broader cloud infrastructure and AI offerings. This positioning allows it to work alongside other Azure services and tools commonly used in enterprise environments.
