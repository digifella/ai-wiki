# Scaling

**[[concepts/computational-scaling|Scaling]]** refers to techniques and methods for efficiently managing the increase in computational requirements as [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models grow larger. This can involve everything from parallelization strategies on modern hardware like GPUs and [[entities/tpus|TPUs]] to innovative approaches that leverage older or less powerful systems.
