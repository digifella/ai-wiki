# Your [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI Workflow]] -[[concepts/deep-think-mode|deep Think Mode]]

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MBW24z4yxPY>
This video provides a detailed overview of [[entities/google|Google]]'s latest AI updates, focusing on new features and enhancements across various platforms like [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]], and [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], including a brief mention of an external AI tool called Proactor.
Here's a breakdown of the key points:

1. **[[entities/gemini-2-5-pro|Gemini 2.5 Pro]] with Deep Think Mode (0:09)**:
	Deep Think mode, accessible with a [[entities/google-ai|Google AI]] Ultra subscription, allows Gemini 2.5 Pro to process questions more thoroughly, leading to more detailed and accurate responses. It uses "[[concepts/parallel-thinking|parallel thinking]]" to explore multiple paths and [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] simultaneously, resulting in more creative and well-thought-out answers. It also utilizes advanced reinforcement [[concepts/learning|learning]], meaning it improves over time with more usage. The speaker demonstrates its [[concepts/coding|coding]] capabilities by asking it to create an interactive fluid [[concepts/simulation-technology|simulation]] using HTML, Canvas, and JavaScript. While the non-Deep Think version failed, the Deep Think version provided a functional and visually superior simulation on the first try. Another coding test involved generating ambient music with an interactive starfield visualizer. The non-Deep Think version struggled with functionality and visual [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], while the Deep Think version, after one round of changes, produced a much more realistic and interactive experience. The speaker [[concepts/notes|notes]] that Deep Think mode has a usage limit (8 prompts in this case), and while powerful for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] (math, [[concepts/code|code]], business strategies), its cost might not be justified for simpler queries or if not paired with other Google AI Ultra features like Veo 3 and Flow.
	
2. **Google Search AI Mode Updates (0:30, 7:15)**:
	AI Mode in Google Search has been transformed, now allowing users to upload images and PDFs to provide more context to their questions. It can summarize content from uploaded files (e.g., a psychology PDF). It can generate interactive study guides from questions, integrating information and offering follow-up questions. Google is rolling out "Search Live," allowing users to open their camera and ask AI questions about what they see in real-time, receiving instant answers and relevant links (demonstrated with an "elephant toothpaste experiment").
	
3. **NotebookLM Enhancements (1:02, 9:09)**:
	NotebookLM has introduced "Video Overview," a new feature that can turn hundreds of uploaded sources into a concise video [[concepts/summary|summary]]. It also allows for generating multiple "Audio Overviews" within the same notebook, which can then be shared with colleagues who can listen in their native language, eliminating the need to create separate notebooks or delete previous audio summaries. It can also generate study guides and briefing documents directly from sources.
	
4. **Proactor (External AI Tool) (6:09)**:
	Proactor is introduced as an all-in-one AI tool designed to boost productivity. It listens to and transcribes meetings, calls, [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] videos, and voice notes in real-time. It then provides a detailed timeline, smart summary, [[concepts/highlights|key takeaways]], to-do lists, and AI suggestions. The speaker highlights its utility for professionals who need to be present in conversations (podcasting, interviewing, learning, collaborating) without worrying about note-taking or forgetting details.
	

The video concludes by emphasizing Google's rapid [[concepts/integration|integration]] of AI into its platforms, predicting that the Google search experience will be completely replaced by AI in just a year. The speaker invites viewers to suggest other [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] for future reviews.
