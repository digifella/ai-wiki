## BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)

BDNF is a protein that supports the survival and differentiation of existing neurons, as well as the growth and development of new neurons. It is particularly important in areas of the brain involved with [[concepts/learning|learning]], [[concepts/memory|memory]], and higher [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]].

### Key Points
- **Function**: BDNF plays a crucial role in neuroplasticity, enhancing cognitive functions such as memory and learning.
- **[[concepts/chronic-pain|Chronic Pain]] Impact**: Research indicates that [[concepts/chronic-pain|chronic pain]] can lead to reduced levels of BDNF, which may exacerbate the perception of pain and hinder recovery processes.
