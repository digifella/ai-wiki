# Openai Models

OpenAI has released a series of open-weights models collectively referred to as GPT-OSS (Open Source Series). This initiative represents a strategic shift in the company's distribution model, moving away from exclusive reliance on paid API endpoints to making model weights publicly available. By providing access to the underlying parameters, OpenAI enables the broader developer and research communities to inspect, modify, and redistribute the models.

The release of these weights allows for greater transparency and flexibility in how AI systems are deployed. Developers can fine-tune the models for specific use cases, run them on local hardware, or integrate them into private infrastructure without depending on external service providers. This approach aims to foster innovation and accessibility within the artificial intelligence ecosystem.
