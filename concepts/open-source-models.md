# Open-Source Models

**Open-source models** refer to large language models (LLMs) and other AI architectures whose weights, code, and [[concepts/training-data|training data]] are publicly available for inspection, modification, and redistribution. This ecosystem contrasts with proprietary closed-source models, fostering transparency, community-driven innovation, and customizable deployment.
