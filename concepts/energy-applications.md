# Energy Applications

## Overview
**Energy Applications** encompasses the conversion, [[entities/storage|storage]], and utilization of energy through chemical and physical processes. Key domains include electrochemical systems, material synthesis, and resource recovery.

## Electrochemical Processes
*   **Electrolysis**: The use of electric current to [[concepts/ambition|drive]] non-spontaneous redox reactions. Contrary to common simplification, electricity does not merely "split" H₂O; it drives specific ion migrations and electrode reactions depending on electrolyte [[concepts/writing|composition]] and membrane properties.
    *   See: [[lab-notes/2026-06-09-Electrolysis-and-DIY-Ion-Exchange-Metal-Refining-and-Ene|Electrolysis and DIY Ion-Exchange: Metal Refining and Energy Applications]] for detailed breakdowns of ion-exchange [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and practical implementations.
*   **[[concepts/metal-refining|Metal Refining]]**: Utilizes electrochemical deposition to purify [[concepts/metal|metals]] or recover valuable elements from waste streams.
    *   Relates to Resource Recovery and Waste Management.

## Key Concepts
*   **Ion-Exchange Membranes**: Critical for separating reaction chambers, allowing selective ion transport while preventing product mixing. DIY approaches can leverage accessible materials for prototyping.
*   **Redox Reactions**: Fundamental electron transfer processes enabling energy conversion in batteries, fuel cells, and electrolyzers.
