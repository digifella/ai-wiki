# Yann LeCun

## Profile
Chief AI Scientist at [[entities/meta]] and Professor at New York University. A foundational figure in Deep [[concepts/learning|Learning]] and Convolutional [[concepts/neural-networks|Neural Networks]].

## Research Philosophy
- **[[concepts/world-models|World Models]]**: Advocates for the development of architectures that can predict and model the physical [[entities/earth|world]], enabling true adaptive AI. See detailed analysis in [[lab-notes/2026-06-14-Yann-LeCuns-Argument-World-Models-for-True-Adaptive-AI-B|Yann LeCun's Argument: World Models for True, Adaptive AI Beyond LLMs]].
  - Argues that current systems lack the ability to build internal models of reality necessary for [[concepts/true-intelligence|general intelligence]].
- **Anti-LLM Paradigm**: Maintains that "Language is not Intelligence"; argues that [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) are fundamentally limited because they lack the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and planning capabilities required for AGI.
  - Critiques the reliance on next-token [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] as insufficient for understanding causal [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] in the world.
- **Self-Supervised [[concepts/learning|Learning]]**: Focuses on enabling models to learn directly from sensory input without massive human-labeled [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].

## Key Research & Architectures
- **[[concepts/jepa|JEPA]] ([[concepts/joint-embedding-predictive-architecture-jepa|Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture]])**: A non-generative approach to [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]].
  - Proposed as a strategic path beyond [[concepts/large-language-models]], aiming to separate representation learning from reconstruction.
  - Designed to work in conjunction with [[concepts/interactive-environments|World Models]] to enable efficient planning and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
