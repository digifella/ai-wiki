# Latte Art Physics

The study of [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid dynamics]], surface tension, and [[concepts/viscosity|viscosity]] interactions involved in creating patterns in espresso-based beverages.

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/density|Density]] Differential:** The [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] between the lighter milk foam and the darker espresso crema relies on precise temperature and aeration control.
- **Surface Tension:** The interface between the milk and coffee allows for the formation of distinct shapes before [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] [[concepts/causes|causes]] them to sink or blend.
- **Viscosity Control:** Milk [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] (microfoam) must be balanced to allow for [[concepts/flow|flow]] control without breaking the surface tension prematurely.
