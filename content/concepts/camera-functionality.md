# Camera Functionality

[[entities/snapseed|Snapseed]], a free [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] application, has expanded its feature set to include integrated camera functionality alongside its established professional editing tools. This addition allows users to capture [[concepts/images|images]] directly within the app before applying editing effects, streamlining the workflow from capture to post-processing.

## Integration with Editing Workflow

The camera feature is designed to work seamlessly with Snapseed's existing photo editing capabilities. Users can take photos using the in-app camera and immediately access the app's suite of editing tools without needing to switch between separate [[concepts/software|applications]]. This integration positions Snapseed as a more comprehensive [[concepts/solution|solution]] for [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]].

## Professional-Grade Features

Alongside the camera functionality, Snapseed maintains its position as a free tool offering professional-level editing features. The combination of capture and editing capabilities in a single application provides both amateur and experienced photographers with accessible tools for image creation and refinement.
