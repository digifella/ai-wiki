# Large Language Models

[[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are foundational [[concepts/neural-networks]] architectures, primarily based on the [[concepts/transformer-models]] design, capable of [[concepts/text-generation]] and complex [[concepts/natural-language-processing]] tasks. They rely on [[concepts/attention-mechanisms]] and residual-connections to process sequential data, with performance scaling closely tied to [[concepts/model-parameters]] and [[concepts/long-running-sessions|context-window-management]].

## Core Architecture & Training
- **Foundation**: Built on [[concepts/transformer-models]] utilizing [[concepts/attention-mechanisms]] for [[concepts/global-context-awareness|global context awareness]].
- **Optimization**: Techniques include [[concepts/fine-tuning]], lora, and [[concepts/ai-model-fine-tuning|parameter-efficient-fine-tuning]] for efficient adaptation. [[entities/unsloth]] is a key open-source-tools for post-training-optimization.
- **Inference**: Optimized via [[concepts/speculative-decoding]], multi-token-prediction, and parallel-decoding to enhance gpu-throughput. [[concepts/inference-optimization]] and [[concepts/prompt-caching]] are critical for [[concepts/memory-management]] and latency reduction.
- **Efficiency**: [[concepts/model-compression]] and [[concepts/edge-ai]] strategies address hardware-trade-offs and [[concepts/cost-optimization]]. [[concepts/energy-based-models]] and [[concepts/logical-consistency|constraint-satisfaction]] approaches are explored for robustness.

## Ecosystem & Models
- **Frontier Models**: Includes [[entities/openai]]'s [[entities/gpt-5]] and [[concepts/gpt-56-sol]], [[entities/anthropic-claude]], and [[entities/mistral-ai]] variants.
- **Qwen Family**: [[entities/qwen]] models are significant for their open-source availability and coding capabilities.
  - Qwen 3.8-Max: Autonomous Coding, Debugging, and Open-Source Qwen 3.8-27B represents a milestone in autonomous coding and debugging, with the open-source [[concepts/qwen-38-max]] variant (27B parameters) enabling local inference and [[concepts/agentic-ai]] workflows.
- **Other Notable Models**: [[entities/deepseek]], [[concepts/minimax-m3]], and [[entities/ornith-10]].

## Applications & Patterns
- **Agentic AI**: [[concepts/agentic-ai]] systems use [[concepts/multi-agent-systems]] and orchestration-patterns for complex tasks. github-ai-agents and [[concepts/ai-coding-agents]] leverage LLMs for software development.
- **RAG**: [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval-augmented-generation]] and rag-pipelines integrate external data via [[concepts/pdf-parsing]] and data-ingestion to mitigate hallucinations.
- **Structured Output**: schema-constrained-generation and [[concepts/structured-data-extraction]] ensure reliable data formats.
- **Reasoning**: [[concepts/test-time-compute]] and [[concepts/inference-time-reasoning]] enhance problem-solving via self-scaffolding and [[concepts/evolution-strategies]].

## Infrastructure & Tools
- **Local Inference**: [[concepts/local-inference]] tools support [[concepts/privacy-preserving-ai]] and [[concepts/edge-ai]] deployment.
- **Verification**: formal-verification and lean-4 are used for correctness in critical applications.
- **Monitoring**: [[concepts/harness-design]] and stanford-ai-index provide benchmarks and evaluation frameworks.
- **Routing**: model-routing optimizes cost and performance across [[concepts/frontier-models]].
