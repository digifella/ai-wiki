# Photo Color Grading

Photo color grading is the creative process of adjusting colors, tones, and color casts in photographs during post-processing to establish a specific visual mood or aesthetic. Unlike basic color correction, which aims to restore accurate colors, color grading is an intentional artistic choice that shapes how viewers perceive and feel about an image. This technique is fundamental to contemporary photography, allowing creators to unify the appearance of multiple images or establish a cohesive visual style across a body of work.

## Tools and Software

Adobe Lightroom is one of the most widely used platforms for color grading, offering intuitive controls for adjusting exposure, contrast, saturation, and individual color channels. Other common tools include Capture One, DaVinci Resolve, and various plugins that provide specialized grading capabilities. Most color grading workflows involve adjusting the highlights, midtones, and shadows separately to create depth and visual interest.

## Common Applications

Color grading is applied across many photographic genres to achieve specific aesthetic goals. Dark and moody aesthetics, popular in portrait and landscape photography, are created by reducing overall brightness, increasing contrast, and shifting colors toward cooler tones or desaturated hues. Other approaches might emphasize warm tones for nostalgic effects or boost specific color channels to create distinctive looks. Consistency in grading across image series is particularly important for commercial work, editorial photography, and social media portfolios where a recognizable visual identity enhances viewer engagement.
