# Explanations

[[entities/ai-assistant|Nemotron]] is a text-based [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] designed to provide a [[concepts/range|range]] of support across different domains and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. The system is built to handle diverse requests while maintaining clarity and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in its [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Core Capabilities

The primary capability of [[entities/nemotron|Nemotron]] is answering questions across a broad spectrum of topics. This foundational function enables users to seek information, clarification, and explanations on subjects ranging from technical domains to general knowledge. The system processes [[concepts/natural-language-search|natural language queries]] and generates contextually relevant responses based on its [[concepts/training-data|training data]].

Beyond [[concepts/fact-based-queries|question-answering]], Nemotron supports various text-based interactions typical of modern AI assistants, including assistance with [[concepts/writing|writing]], analysis, and [[concepts/information-synthesis|information synthesis]]. The system operates as a [[concepts/conversational-interface|conversational interface]], allowing users to engage in multi-turn [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] to refine their queries and obtain more targeted information.
