# Im Yoon-ah

## Overview
Im Yoon-ah is a South Korean actress known for her versatile performances in various TV dramas and films. She has gained widespread recognition for her roles in popular K-dramas such as "The Queen's Gambit" and "Hotel Del Luna."

## Projects

### Bon Appétit, Your Majesty (K-Drama)
* **Description:** A 12-episode Netflix K-drama adapted from [[entities/park-kook-jae|Park Kook-jae]]'s web novel *[[entities/bon-appétit-your-majesty|Surviving as Yeonsan-gun's Chef]]*, directed by [[entities/jang-tae-yoo|Jang Tae-yoo]] and produced by [[entities/studio-dragon|Studio Dragon]].
* **Role:** Im Yoon-ah stars as Yeon Ji-young, a modern South Korean chef with French culinary [[concepts/training|training]] who is transported back to the [[concepts/joseon-era|Joseon era]] after an accident with an antique boo.
