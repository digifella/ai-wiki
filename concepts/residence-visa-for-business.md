# Residence Visa For Business

A residence visa for business in Turkey is a long-term permit category designed for foreign nationals who intend to establish or manage commercial operations within the country. This visa allows holders to reside in Turkey while conducting legitimate business activities, serving as a distinct alternative to standard tourist or short-term visit visas. It is specifically tailored for entrepreneurs, investors, and executives who require legal residency to oversee their enterprises effectively.

Eligibility for this visa requires applicants to meet specific financial and business criteria established by Turkish immigration authorities. Prospective applicants must demonstrate sufficient financial resources to support themselves and their dependents without relying on public assistance. Additionally, they are typically required to provide documentation proving the existence of a registered business entity, such as a company registration certificate, tax number, and proof of capital investment or employment generation.

The application process involves submitting detailed documentation to the local Provincial Directorate of Migration Management in Turkey. Requirements often include a valid passport, health insurance coverage, proof of accommodation, and a clean criminal record from the applicant's country of origin. Approval grants the holder the right to live in Turkey for a specified period, usually renewable, and provides the legal framework necessary to operate a business, hire local staff, and manage corporate affairs without the restrictions faced by short-term visa holders.
