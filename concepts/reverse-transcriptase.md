# Reverse Transcriptase

**Reverse transcriptase (RT)** is an enzyme that catalyzes the formation of DNA from an RNA template. It plays a critical role in the life cycles of retroviruses and is a fundamental tool in molecular biology.

## Recent Developments

*   **Discovery of [[concepts/array-associated-reverse-transcriptase|Array-associated Reverse Transcriptase (ART)]]:** In September 2026, [[entities/claude-ai]] identified a novel [[concepts/crispr-like-system|CRISPR-like system]] involving a previously unknown class of reverse transcriptases [[lab-notes/2026-09-26-Claude-AI-Discovers-Novel-CRISPR-like-System-Array-assoc|Claude AI Discovers Novel CRISPR-like System, Array-associated Reverse Transcriptase (ART)]].
*   **Implications:** This discovery suggests a broader diversity of RT functions in prokaryotic immune systems than previously understood, potentially expanding the toolkit for gene-editing technologies.
