[[concepts/pdf-manipulation|Document processing]] involves automated extraction, structuring, and analysis of information from unstructured documents to enable efficient [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]].

**Key Applications**:
- Entity and [[concepts/relationship-extraction|relationship extraction]] using LLM for semantic understanding
- [[concepts/vector-store|Knowledge Graph]] construction from document collections
- Enhanced [[concepts/rag]] ([[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]]) systems via graph-based [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]]
- [[concepts/real-time-knowledge-graph|Real-time knowledge graph]] [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] from document streams
- [[concepts/agentic-rag|Agentic RAG]] systems enhanced with [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] for dynamic [[concepts/reasoning-steps|agent reasoning

**Emerging Agentic Capabilities**:
- Recent advancements in open-weight models like [[lab-notes/2026-07-30-Solar-Open-2-LLM-Agentic-Capabilities-for-Productivity-a|Solar Open 2 LLM: Agentic Capabilities for Productivity and Coding Demonstrations]] demonstrate improved [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] in complex [[concepts/office-[[concepts/productivity|productivity]]|office productivity]] tasks and [[concepts/coding|coding]] workflows.
- These agentic models enhance document processing pipelines by enabling [[concepts/autonomous-[[concepts/acting|[[concepts/tool-use-automation|[[concepts/vanishing-gradient-problem|tool-use]]]]]]|autonomous tool use]] and [[concepts/deep-reasoning|[[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]]]] during [[concepts/entity-extraction|entity extraction]] and [[concepts/relationship-extraction|relationship extraction]] phases.
- Integration of such models supports more robust [[concepts/agentic-rag|Agentic RAG]] architectures, allowing for dynamic adaptation during [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] operations.
