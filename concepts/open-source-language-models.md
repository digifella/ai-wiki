# Open-source language models

[[concepts/large-language-models]] characterized by publicly accessible [[concepts/weights|weights]], training methodologies, and architectures, typically distributed under permissive licenses like [[entities/apache-20]].

## Key Characteristics
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Enables [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]], [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]], and inspection of [[concepts/machine-learning]] [[concepts/weights|weights]].
- **Efficiency**: Increasing focus on [[entities/bitnet|Edge AI]] and Parameter-efficient tuning to reduce computational overhead.
- **Capabilities**: Evolution from text-only to [[concepts/multi-modal-input|Multimodal processing]] (text, image, etc.).

## Notable Developments
- **[[concepts/gemma-4|Google Gemma]] 4** (2026-04-22):
    - A new family of models optimized for [[concepts/edge-deployment|Edge AI]] via "edge versions" (E2B and E4B).
    - Features a 2.3B parameter multimodal architecture designed to approximate the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capabilities of 70B [[concepts/parameter-models|parameter models]].
    - Released under the [[entities/apache-20]] [[concepts/license|license]].
