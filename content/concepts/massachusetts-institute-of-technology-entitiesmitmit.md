# Massachusetts Institute Of Technology

The Massachusetts Institute of Technology ([[entities/mit|MIT]]) is a major research university located in Cambridge, Massachusetts. Founded in 1861, MIT has become one of the world's leading institutions for education and research in [[concepts/science|science]], engineering, and technology. The university's campus sits along the Charles River, adjacent to Boston, and serves approximately 1,000 faculty members and 1,100 students across its graduate and undergraduate [[concepts/software|programs]].

## Education and Research

MIT operates five schools and one college offering degree programs in engineering, science, [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] and planning, humanities and arts, sloan school of management, and Schwarzman college of computing. The institute is particularly recognized for pioneering research in fields including aeronautics, materials science, nuclear science, and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. MIT's laboratories and research centers conduct sponsored research projects funded by government agencies, private foundations, and industry partners.

## Institutional Impact

Beyond its academic programs, MIT has significantly influenced technology development and entrepreneurship. The institute is closely associated with the development of the [[concepts/modified-conceptsmit-licensemit-license|MIT License]], a permissive [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] software [[concepts/license|license]] widely adopted in the technology industry. MIT alumni have founded numerous technology companies and contributed substantially to [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] across multiple sectors, establishing the university's role as a central institution in the American research and development ecosystem.
