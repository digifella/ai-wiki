# Fujifilm X-T5

The [[entities/fujifilm-x-t5|Fujifilm X-T5]] is a high-end mirrorless digital [[concepts/camera-settings|camera]] released in 2023. It features a 40-megapixel APS-C X-Trans CMOS 5 HR sensor, advanced [[concepts/camera-settings|autofocus]] system with Eye Detection AF, and a [[concepts/range|range]] of professional-grade imaging functions.

## Key Features
- **Sensor:** 40MP BSI X-Trans CMOS 5 HR Sensor
- **Processor:** X-Processor 5
- **Autofocus System:** Advanced Hybrid Autofocus (up to 187 phase-detection points)
- **ISO Range:** Auto, 160 - 12800 (expandable up to ISO 409600)
- **Shutter Speeds:** Electronic front curtain shutter (30 min - 1/180 sec) and mechanical shutter (1/4000 sec - 30 min)

## Lock Feature
The Fujifilm X-T5 incorporates a robust "Lock" feature designed to prevent [[concepts/accidental-changes|accidental changes]] to [[concepts/camera-settings|camera settings]], ensuring stability during critical shooting sessions. This feature can be activated through specific menu options or by pressing dedicated [[concepts/buttons|buttons]].
