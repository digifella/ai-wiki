# Structureux

Structureux is a systematic process for conducting rigorous critique of responses, outputs, or creative work. Rather than accepting work at surface value, the method treats critique as an essential refinement step designed to improve quality and reliability. The process involves deliberate examination of content against multiple evaluation criteria to identify gaps, errors, inconsistencies, and areas requiring strengthening.

## Core Practice

The Structureux approach examines work across several dimensions simultaneously. A structured critique typically assesses accuracy of factual claims, logical coherence of arguments, completeness relative to the stated scope, and alignment with intended purpose. The method encourages the reviewer to ask specific diagnostic questions rather than offer vague impressions, making weaknesses explicit and actionable. This systematic examination reveals both obvious flaws and subtler problems that surface-level reading might miss.

## Application

Structureux is particularly useful in contexts where output quality directly affects downstream work or decisions. Creative writers, researchers, technical teams, and educators can apply the method to their own work or to collaborative outputs. The process complements other quality assurance approaches by focusing on thoughtful analysis rather than mechanical checking. By treating critique as a structured discipline rather than an informal judgment, Structureux helps creators move beyond initial drafts toward more refined and reliable work.
