# total parameters

The sum of all trainable [[concepts/weights|weights]] in a [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] model's [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], representing its theoretical capacity to store information. This metric differs from *[[concepts/activated-parameters|activated parameters]]* (the subset actively used during [[concepts/inference|inference]]).
