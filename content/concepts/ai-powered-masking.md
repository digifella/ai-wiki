# AI-powered masking

The application of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] algorithms to automate the identification and [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] of complex boundaries within an image.

## Techniques & Methodologies
- **Hybrid Approach**: Achieving high-precision results, particularly for complex textures like [[concepts/hair-masking]], requires combining [[concepts/ai-powered-subject-selection|AI-powered masking]] with traditional manual refinement techniques.
- **Automated Selection**: Utilizing [[concepts/photoshop]] features such as **[[concepts/select-subject|Select Subject]]** to generate initial boundaries via automated algorithms.
- **Optimization**: Adjusting [[concepts/photoshop]] configurations and [[concepts/setup|setup]] specifically to enhance the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of AI processing during the selection process.
