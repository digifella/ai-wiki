# Intelligent Tools

Intelligent Tools refers to a suite of AI-powered [[concepts/software|applications]] and features integrated across [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s product ecosystem. These tools leverage advanced generative [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to enhance productivity, [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and information processing across different domains. Recent updates have expanded AI functionality across multiple [[entities/google|Google]] platforms, making AI assistance more accessible to users across various workflows.

## Key Products

[[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]] is a [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool that uses AI to automate aspects of video production, reducing the time and technical [[concepts/skills|skills]] required to generate polished video content. [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] functions as an [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] assistant that helps users organize, analyze, and synthesize information from documents and sources. [[entities/ai-studio|Google AI Studio]] provides developers and creators with access to [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and tools for building custom applications and experimenting with AI capabilities.

## Integration and Accessibility

These tools represent Google's broader strategy of embedding AI capabilities directly into consumer and professional products rather than offering them as standalone services. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of AI features across [[entities/google-search|Google Search]], productivity applications, and development platforms aims to [[entities/make|make]] advanced AI functionality available to users with varying levels of technical expertise. This approach reflects a shift toward AI becoming a standard component of digital tools rather than a specialized technology.
