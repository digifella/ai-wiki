# Chassy

Chassy is a scholar known for [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical analysis]] of the [[entities/dreyfus-and-dreyfus|Dreyfus and Dreyfus]] model of skill acquisition and its [[concepts/software|applications]] to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/training|training]]. Chassy's work has focused on identifying limitations and challenges in the approaches proposed by the Dreyfus brothers regarding how models and [[concepts/expert-systems|expert systems]] should be trained.

## Critique of Dreyfus and Dreyfus

Chassy has contributed to a body of critique that examines the theoretical foundations and practical implications of the [[concepts/dreyfus-model|Dreyfus model]] for machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] and expert systems development. This critical perspective, shared with other scholars including Gobel, highlights gaps between the [[entities/dreyfus|Dreyfus proposal]] and the actual capabilities and requirements of expert systems in practice.
