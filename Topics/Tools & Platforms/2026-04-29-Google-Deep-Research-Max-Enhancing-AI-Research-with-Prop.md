## Google Deep Research Max: Enhancing AI Research with Proprietary Data & Visuals
**Clip title:** Google's NEW Deep Research Max [[entities/agent|Agent]] Is INSANE
**Author / channel:** AI with [[entities/surya|Surya]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FVU4qLjy2jE

### Summary
The video introduces Google's new [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/deep-research-agents|Deep Research Agents]], designed to address persistent limitations of existing AI [[concepts/research-tools|research tools]]. Traditionally, [[concepts/ai-research-agents|AI research agents]] primarily query the open web, leading to two main issues: a lack of access to private, proprietary company data, and outputs that are overwhelmingly text-heavy, requiring significant manual effort to transform into presentation-ready visuals.

Google's [[entities/deep-research-max|Deep Research Max]] [[entities/agent|agent]] aims to resolve these challenges. Its first major enhancement is the ability to plug directly into an organization's actual work data through various integrations (referred to as [[concepts/mcps|MCPs]]), including financial platforms like FactSet and Bloomberg, as well as internal documents such as [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]] and spreadsheets. This means the agent can conduct comprehensive research by combining publicly available information with a company's confidential or specialized datasets, offering a much richer and more relevant output for serious professional work.

Furthermore, Deep Research Max distinguishes itself by generating genuinely presentation-grade reports. Unlike previous agents that deliver walls of [[concepts/text|text]], this new [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] can produce integrated visuals such as world maps, timelines, donut charts, bar charts, and [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]] directly within the report. The video demonstrates this by [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] the agent to research AI chip export controls and semiconductor [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], explicitly requesting various charts and maps, which the agent successfully generates in a polished, cited format. Two versions of the agent are available: Deep Research Preview, optimized for [[concepts/speed|speed]] and efficiency, and Deep Research Max, designed for maximum search exhaustiveness and report comprehensiveness, though it may consume more [[concepts/tokens|tokens]].

In conclusion, Google's Deep Research Max represents a significant leap forward in [[concepts/ai-driven-research|AI-powered research]]. By seamlessly integrating private enterprise data with open-web content and delivering visually compelling, presentation-ready reports, it streamlines the entire research [[concepts/workflow|workflow]]. This empowers users to quickly obtain comprehensive, insightful, and actionable intelligence, drastically reducing the time and effort traditionally spent on [[concepts/data-synthesis|data synthesis]] and visualization for [[concepts/strategic-analysis|strategic analysis]] and presentations.
