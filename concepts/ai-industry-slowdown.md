# AI Industry Slowdown

The **[[concepts/ai-industry|AI industry]] slowdown** refers to the growing consensus among key industry leaders and researchers advocating for a deliberate deceleration in the pace of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] development. This [[concepts/exercise|movement]] prioritizes safety, alignment, and regulatory oversight over rapid deployment and [[concepts/unique-value-proposition|competitive advantage]].

## Key Drivers

*   **Existential Risk Concerns:** Prominent figures argue that current trajectories pose unacceptable risks of catastrophic outcomes if safety measures do not keep pace with capability gains.
*   **Power Concentration:** There is increasing concern that unchecked acceleration leads to excessive centralization of power within a few unaccountable [[concepts/nodes|entities]].
*   **Pacing Advocacy:** Leaders are calling for coordinated "pacing" [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] to ensure responsible development.
