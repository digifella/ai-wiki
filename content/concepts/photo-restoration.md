# Photo Restoration

Photo restoration refers to the digital process of improving, repairing, and enhancing degraded or damaged photographs. This can include removing scratches, dust, and stains; correcting color fading; reconstructing missing portions; and improving overall image quality. Traditionally performed by skilled manual editors using specialized [[concepts/software|software]], photo restoration has increasingly been augmented by [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools that automate and accelerate various restoration tasks.

## AI-Assisted Restoration

Modern [[concepts/ai-image-editing|AI image editing]] models have begun to support photo restoration workflows through instruction-based interfaces. HiDream-E1.1 is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] model designed for this [[concepts/motivation|purpose]], allowing users to specify restoration tasks through natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] rather than manual editing techniques. The model supports [[concepts/dynamic-resolution|dynamic resolution]], enabling it to work with [[concepts/images|images]] of varying sizes, and integrates local language models to interpret restoration [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], reducing dependency on external APIs and improving [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
