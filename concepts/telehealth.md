# Telehealth

**Telehealth** refers to the remote delivery of [[concepts/health|healthcare]] services and health information via telecommunications technology. It encompasses telemedicine, [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|remote patient monitoring]], and [[concepts/health-promotion|health education]], aiming to improve [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and efficiency in [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] systems.

## Key Dimensions

- **[[concepts/clinical-delivery|Clinical Delivery]]**: Remote consultations, diagnostics, and [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]] across various care settings.
- **[[concepts/information-visualization|Data Visualization]]**: Critical for monitoring [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]] and system performance; rapid development of dashboards is essential for operational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
- **Integration**: Requires seamless interoperability with existing hospital [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] systems.
- **AI and [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] Integration**: The accelerating [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence]] and robotics in [[entities/clinical-practice|clinical practice]] introduces diagnostic benefits but creates systemic challenges regarding [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/coordination|coordination]]. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-17-Shadow-AI-Rapid-Integration-in-Healthcare-Diagnostic-Ben|Shadow AI: Rapid Integration in Healthcare, Diagnostic Benefits, and System Challenges]] for details on how doctors, patients, and robots are moving faster than the underlying system infrastructure.
