# Reasoning Capabilities

[[concepts/thinking-and-reasoning-capabilities|Reasoning capabilities]] in [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] refer to the ability of language models to break down complex problems, apply [[concepts/logical-steps|logical steps]], and arrive at justified conclusions. These capabilities enable models to move beyond [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] to perform more structured [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] tasks. Both [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/small-language-models-slms|small language models (SLMs)]] demonstrate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] abilities, though they differ significantly in their scale, computational requirements, and performance on reasoning-focused benchmarks.

## Large Language Models

[[concepts/large-language-models|Large language models]] such as [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]] and [[concepts/3d-game-development|GPT-5]] have demonstrated increasingly sophisticated reasoning across diverse domains, including [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], and logical [[concepts/inference|inference]]. These models benefit from extensive [[concepts/language-data|training data]] and parameters, which enhance their ability to recognize and apply complex patterns. Recent integration efforts, such as [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]] with [[entities/microsoft-copilot|Microsoft Copilot]], have focused on making [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|advanced reasoning]] capabilities accessible through commercial AI assistants, enabling users to leverage these abilities for professional and creative tasks.

## Small Language Models

[[concepts/compact-language-model|Small language models]] represent a distinct category optimized for efficiency rather than raw capability. [[concepts/slms|SLMs]] are designed to run on [[concepts/edge-devices|edge devices]] and resource-constrained environments while maintaining practical reasoning performance. [[concepts/benchmark-testing|Benchmarking]] of [[concepts/small-language-models|SLMs]] has become increasingly important as organizations seek to balance reasoning quality with deployment feasibility, cost, and latency. These models typically show reduced reasoning capacity compared to their larger counterparts but can be sufficient for specific, well-defined tasks.

## Current Developments

Research continues to focus on understanding the [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] underlying reasoning in both model categories, with particular [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to how training methodologies, [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], and architectural choices influence problem-solving performance. The field remains an active area of investigation as practitioners work to improve reasoning [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and develop better evaluation frameworks for assessing reasoning quality across different model [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]].
