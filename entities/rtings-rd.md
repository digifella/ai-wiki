# RTINGS R&D

**RTINGS R&D** is the research and development division responsible for consumer electronics testing, data collection, and analytical content production.

## Key Initiatives & Research

- **[[concepts/smart-tv|Smart TV]] Data Collection and Shifting Business Models**
  - Investigated the correlation between declining hardware costs and the rise of data-driven revenue streams in the TV industry [[lab-notes/2026-09-13-Smart-TV-Data-Collection-and-Shifting-Business-Models|Smart TV Data Collection and Shifting Business Models]].
  - Analyzed how modern smart TVs leverage user data to subsidize hardware prices, contrasting with the traditional high-margin hardware sales model of 15 years ago.
  - Examined industry-wide practices beyond [[entities/lg]], noting that multiple manufacturers are adopting similar surveillance-based business models.
