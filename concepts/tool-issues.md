# Tool Issues

The organization experiences significant operational friction due to limitations within its current tooling infrastructure. The proposal system has become a primary source of workflow bottlenecks, directly impeding decision-making processes and slowing overall project progression. These inefficiencies create delays that extend review cycles and hinder timely project advancement.

SharePoint, serving as the primary content management platform, further constrains operational efficiency through its rigid structure and cumbersome navigation patterns. The platform’s design limits user experience and complicates information retrieval, leading to duplicate efforts and difficulty in locating necessary documents. These structural issues exacerbate the time required for routine tasks and reduce overall productivity.

To address these challenges, the organization proposes leveraging artificial intelligence to accelerate analysis and automate manual tasks. Implementing AI-driven solutions aims to streamline workflows, reduce the burden of repetitive processes, and improve the speed and accuracy of information processing. This strategic shift seeks to mitigate the current bottlenecks and enhance the overall efficiency of the tools platform infrastructure.
