# Critical Materials

**Critical materials** are raw materials essential to the economic and [[concepts/export-control|national security]] of a country, where the supply chain is vulnerable to disruption due to geopolitical concentration, extraction challenges, or processing bottlenecks. Rare [[entities/earth|Earth]] Elements are the most prominent subset, heavily dominated by single-source processing.

## Strategic Context
- **[[concepts/geopolitical-vulnerability|Geopolitical Vulnerability]]:** High dependence on specific regions (e.g., [[entities/china|China]] for rare earth processing) creates strategic risks for defense, energy, and tech sectors.
- **Substitution Imperative:** Reducing reliance on critical materials drives [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in material [[concepts/science|science]], focusing on abundance, recyclability, and [[concepts/performance-matching|performance parity]].
- **[[concepts/supply-chain-resilience|Supply Chain Resilience]]:** Diversification of sources and development of alternative materials are key strategies for mitigating supply shocks.

## AI-Driven Material Discovery
[[concepts/artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence]] is accelerating the identification and validation of substitutes for critical materials, reducing the traditional R&D timeline from years to months.

- **[[entities/altrove|Altrove]] Initiative:** A Paris-based startup leveraging AI and [[concepts/lab-automation|lab automation]] to [[concepts/design-variations|design alternatives]] to critical materials, specifically addressing rare earth geopolitical [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]].
- **Methodology:** Combines high-throughput experimentation with [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to predict [[concepts/material-properties|material properties]] and optimize synthesis pathways.
- **Key Focus:** Targeting Rare Earth Elements used in magnets, batteries, and electronics to decouple [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]] from monopolistic sources.
- **Recent Development:** As of August 2026, Altrove has demonstrated significant progress in identifying viable substitutes through its AI-powered discovery pipeline [[lab-notes/2026-08-25-Altrove-AI-Powered-Material-Discovery-to-Address-Rare-Ea|Altrove: AI-Powered Material Discovery to Address Rare Earth Geopolitical Vulnerability]].
- **Impact:** Potential to lower costs, increase supply security, and reduce environmental impact associated with rare earth mining and processing.
