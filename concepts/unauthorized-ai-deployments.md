# Unauthorized AI deployments

[[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] or tools deployed within an organization without formal [[concepts/authorization|authorization]], oversight, or [[concepts/governance|governance]], often bypassing IT, [[concepts/security|security]], and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] protocols.

- **[[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]**: A specific form of unauthorized deployment where [[entities/employees|employees]] use [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] tools (e.g., for document generation, data analysis) without [[concepts/collective-awareness|organizational awareness]]. Risks include [[concepts/data-leakage|data leakage]], compliance violations, and lack of model governance. (Source: IBM [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow ai]])
- **Root cause**: Employees circumventing official channels due to perceived inefficiencies in sanctioned AI workflows.
- **Key risk**: [[concepts/provide-alternative|Undiscovered AI]] instances create blind spots in [[concepts/secure|security]] posture and regulatory compliance.
- **Mitigation strategy**: Proactive discovery of [[concepts/shadow-it|shadow AI]] instances, followed by governance integration (e.g., IBM's framework for managing unsanctioned AI).
