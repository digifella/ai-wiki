# Nemotron 3 Super

[[entities/nemotron|Nemotron]] 3 Super is a 30-billion-parameter [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]. It is part of NVIDIA's [[entities/ai-assistant|Nemotron]] family of language [[concepts/models|models]], which are designed for various [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]] tasks.

The model received coverage on the [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]] [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel, where it was reviewed and discussed. This review helped bring [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to the model within the tech enthusiast community.
