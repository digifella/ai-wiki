# Pilot Training

**Pilot Training** is the systematic process of educating and certifying individuals to operate aircraft safely. It encompasses theoretical knowledge, simulator [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]], and actual flight hours, governed by regulatory bodies like the FAA or EASA. Modern training paradigms increasingly integrate Human Factors [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] and Automation Dependency management.

## Core Competencies
- **Flight Mechanics**: [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|Aerodynamics]], performance, and systems management.
- **Procedural Adherence**: Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) and checklists.
- **[[concepts/crew-resource-management-crm|Crew Resource Management (CRM)]]**: Communication, [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] within the cockpit.
- **Emergency Response**: Handling system failures, weather deviations, and medical emergencies.

## Evolving Challenges: AI and Human Responsibility
Recent incidents highlight the [[concepts/friction|friction]] between [[concepts/automations|automated systems]] and human oversight. The [[lab-notes/2026-07-01-Air-India-Crash-Human-Responsibility-and-Evolving-AI-Cog|Air India Crash: Human Responsibility and Evolving AI Cognition in Aviation]] serves as a critical case study for modern training curricula.

Key insights from this event include:
- **Automation Complacency**: Pilots may disengage cognitively when AI handles routine tasks, leading to degraded situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]] during anomalies.
- **[[concepts/accountability|Responsibility]] Boundaries**: [[concepts/ambiguity|Ambiguity]] in where [[concepts/human-responsibility|human responsibility]] ends and [[concepts/ai-cognition|AI cognition]] begins can cause fatal delays in manual intervention.
- **Training Gaps**: Current simulators may not adequately stress-test pilot reactions to AI "hallucinations" or unexpected autonomous behaviors.

## Training Methodologies
- **Simulator-Based Training**: High-fidelity replication of rare failure modes.
- **Scenario-Based Training (SBT)**: Dynamic, unscripted events to test adaptive decision-making.
- **Continuous Assessment**: Data-driven evaluation of pilot [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]] post-certification.
