# Portrait Retouching

Portrait retouching refers to the post-processing techniques used to enhance and improve portrait photographs in Adobe Photoshop. These techniques focus on refining skin appearance, adjusting facial features, correcting lighting and color issues, and overall image quality while maintaining a natural appearance. Retouching is a standard practice in professional portrait photography, fashion photography, and headshot production.

## Core Techniques

Common portrait retouching approaches include skin smoothing and blemish removal, which can be accomplished through clone stamping, healing brushes, or frequency separation methods. Color correction addresses uneven skin tones and color casts, while exposure and contrast adjustments balance lighting issues. Eye enhancement, teeth whitening, and subtle reshaping of facial features are also frequently applied. The goal throughout these adjustments is to preserve natural detail and avoid an over-processed appearance.

## Workflow Considerations

Effective retouching typically involves non-destructive editing methods, such as working with adjustment layers or duplicate layers, to allow flexibility and reversibility during the editing process. Professional retouchers often work at high zoom levels to address specific areas precisely, and frequently step back to assess the overall impact of their changes on the portrait's appearance and authenticity.
