## PepsiCo

**Overview**
PepsiCo is a multinational food, snack and beverage corporation headquartered in Purchase, New York. It operates in over 200 countries and produces a wide range of products including soft drinks (such as Pepsi-Cola), snacks (like Lay's chips and Doritos), and other beverages.

### Key Products
- **Soft Drinks:** Pepsi-Cola, Mountain Dew, Mirinda, Aquafina
- **Snacks:** Lay’s, Cheetos, Fritos, Ruffles, Tostitos, Walkers Crisps
- **Bottled Water & Beverages:** Gatorade, Lipton Tea, Pure Leaf Iced Teas

### Corporate History
PepsiCo was founded in 1965 as a result of the merger between Pepsi-Cola Company and Frito-Lay [[entities/inc|Inc.]] It has since grown to become one of the [[entities/earth|world]]'s largest food and beverage companies through strategic [[concepts/acquisitions|acquisitions]] and organic growth.
