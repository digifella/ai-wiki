# 135mm Prime

A 135mm [[concepts/prime-number|prime]] lens is a [[concepts/prime-lens|fixed focal length lens]] commonly used in [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]] and [[concepts/zoom-photography|telephoto photography]]. As a prime lens, it offers a single, non-variable [[concepts/focal-length|focal length]], which typically results in a wider maximum aperture and sharper optical performance compared to zoom lenses of equivalent range. The 135mm focal length falls into the short [[concepts/telephoto-lens|telephoto]] category, providing moderate subject magnification and pronounced background compression.

## Optical Characteristics

The 135mm focal length produces significant [[concepts/lens-compression|lens compression]], which flatters facial features and creates pleasing [[concepts/lens-blur|bokeh]] separation between subject and background. This compression effect also enables creative compositional techniques, such as [[concepts/panorama-stitching|panorama stitching]], which can achieve wide-angle compositions by combining multiple overlapping frames. The fixed focal length design allows manufacturers to optimize optical elements for this specific distance, often resulting in superior [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]] and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] compared to variable zoom ranges.

## Applications

135mm [[concepts/prime-numbers|primes]] are particularly valued for portrait work, where the focal length provides adequate working distance while maintaining intimate framing. They are also used in telephoto and [[concepts/wildlife-photography|wildlife photography]] where their compact size and optical quality offer practical advantages. The moderate magnification and field of view make them suitable for both studio and location-based photography.
