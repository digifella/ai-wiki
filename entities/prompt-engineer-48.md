**Prompt Engineer 48** is a content creator and AI researcher specializing in [[concepts/desktop-based-llms|local large language models]] and [[concepts/content-extraction|document parsing]] technologies.

## Key Contributions & Research

### TeleOCR
*   Developed/Featured **TeleOCR**, a 1.2 billion-parameter document parser created by China Telecom's AI research group.
*   Optimized for "camera-captured" documents, addressing distortions, shadows, and angles that traditional parsers struggle with.
