# Machine Learning Models

[[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|Machine learning models]] are computational systems designed to learn patterns from data rather than following explicitly programmed [[concepts/instructions|instructions]]. These [[concepts/models|models]] are trained on datasets, adjusting their internal [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] to minimize prediction errors or achieve specified objectives. Once trained, they can [[entities/make|make]] predictions or decisions on new, unseen data. The quality and relevance of [[concepts/training-data|training data]] significantly influences model performance and generalization [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].

## Platforms and Access

[[concepts/open-source-machine-learning|Hugging Face]] provides an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] platform that democratizes access to pre-built [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models across various domains including [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], and [[concepts/audio-processing|audio processing]]. Rather than [[concepts/training|training]] models from scratch, practitioners can download and implement existing models, reducing computational costs and development time. The platform supports [[concepts/model-customization|model customization]], allowing users to fine-tune [[concepts/pre-trained-models|pre-trained models]] on their own datasets to adapt them for specific [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Applications in AI Agents

Machine learning models form the foundation of [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]], enabling autonomous [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and task execution. Models [[concepts/power|power]] perception tasks, allowing [[concepts/agents|agents]] to process environmental inputs, as well as [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and action selection. The ability to customize and adapt models makes them particularly valuable in developing [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] for domains ranging from content generation to [[concepts/ai-recursive-self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]] systems.
