# AI video workflow

A collection of methodologies and tool-chains used to automate and enhance [[concepts/video-creation|video production]] through [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]], focusing on high-fidelity output.

## Methodologies

### Admin Privileges Workflow
A specialized approach for creating high-retention, broadcast-quality documentary videos. This workflow prioritizes motion, high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] assets, and voice-matching to avoid the low-quality "slideshow" aesthetic often associated with automated content.
