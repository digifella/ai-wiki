# Conversational UI/UX

Conversational UI/UX refers to [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience design]] built around [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]]-based interactions between users and digital systems. Rather than traditional graphical interfaces with [[concepts/buttons|buttons]] and menus, [[concepts/conversational-interfaces|conversational interfaces]] rely on natural language exchanges—through [[concepts/text|text]] or [[concepts/tone|voice]]—to enable users to accomplish tasks and access information. This [[concepts/design|design]] paradigm mirrors human communication patterns, potentially making digital interactions more intuitive and accessible.

## Google Stitch

[[concepts/collaborative-design|Google Stitch]] is an [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|AI-native design canvas]] specifically developed for creating conversational UI/UX experiences. As an AI-assisted prototyping tool, Stitch integrates [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] into the [[concepts/visual-strategy-development|design workflow]] itself, allowing designers to build and iterate on conversational interfaces more efficiently. The platform bridges the gap between design conception and [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] by leveraging [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to support the unique challenges of conversational design, such as dialogue [[concepts/flow-management|flow management]] and natural language handling.

The rise of conversational interfaces reflects broader shifts in how people interact with technology, driven by advances in [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]] and the increasing prevalence of [[concepts/voice-assistants|voice assistants]] and chatbots. Specialized design tools like Google Stitch acknowledge that conversational UI/UX requires different [[concepts/design-thinking|design thinking]] and workflows compared to traditional graphical interface design.
