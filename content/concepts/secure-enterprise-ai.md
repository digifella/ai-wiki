# Secure Enterprise AI

Secure Enterprise AI refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems and [[concepts/agents|agents]] designed specifically for [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] within enterprise environments where security, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] are critical requirements. These platforms address the growing need for AI solutions that can operate on sensitive business data while maintaining strict [[concepts/governance|governance]] [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and protecting against vulnerabilities that might exist in less security-focused AI systems.

## NVIDIA NemoClaw

NVIDIA NemoClaw is a [[concepts/enterprise-ai-agent-platform|secure enterprise AI agent platform]] that was developed to address specific vulnerabilities identified in [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], an earlier automated information pipeline system. NemoClaw is designed to provide enterprises with a more robust framework for deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] that can handle complex business workflows while maintaining enhanced security protocols and data protection measures.

The platform emerged from recognized limitations in existing [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] architectures, particularly around data handling and system vulnerabilities. By building security considerations into its core design rather than as an afterthought, NemoClaw aims to serve organizations that require both [[concepts/ai-automation-agents|AI automation]] capabilities and the assurance that their systems meet enterprise-grade security standards.
