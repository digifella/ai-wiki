title: "krafton"

KRAFTON is a leading global game [[concepts/developer|developer]] known for creating popular battle royale games such as **PUBG** and **The Firmament**. The company also invests heavily in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research, pushing the boundaries of what's possible with AI technologies.

### Recent Developments
- KRAFTON has collaborated with [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] University and [[entities/mit|MIT]] on a project called [[concepts/ai-model-harness|Meta-Harness]].
  - Meta-Harness aims to optimize AI model harnesses for [[concepts/autonomous-learning|autonomous learning]] and evolution.
  - This system enables end-to-end [[concepts/ai-workflow|optimization]] of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] by improving the code that governs their operation, including [[concepts/memory|memory]] management, text search capabilities, and [[concepts/ai-coding|code generation]].
