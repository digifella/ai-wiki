# Storytelling Techniques

[[concepts/storytelling|Storytelling techniques]] are the structural and stylistic methods employed by creators to construct engaging narratives across various media, including literature, film, games, and [[concepts/interactive-experiences|interactive experiences]]. These techniques form the foundation of how stories are conveyed to audiences, determining how information is revealed, how characters develop, and how emotional responses are generated throughout a narrative.

## Core Elements

Fundamental storytelling techniques include establishing narrative structure through exposition, rising action, climax, and [[concepts/solution|resolution]]. Point of view selection—whether first-person, third-person, or omniscient—shapes how audiences [[concepts/experience|experience]] events and access character interiority. Pacing controls the rhythm of revelation and action, while [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] serves multiple functions including [[concepts/character-development|character development]], exposition, and [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] generation.
