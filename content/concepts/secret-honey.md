# Secret Honey
## Definition & Function
Culinary designation for the deliberate, uncredited incorporation of Honey into savory preparations to modulate acidity, suppress capsaicin perception, and accelerate [[concepts/maillard-reaction]] pathways. Functions as a Flavor Balancing modifier rather than a primary component, typically introduced during liquid reduction or final simmering to preserve volatile aromatic compounds and prevent bitter pyrolysis.

## Documented Applications
- Featured as the undisclosed sweetening [[entities/agent|agent]] in My Aussie Garden & Kitchen's stovetop protocol for mild Curried Sausages, where it counters heat intensity without overwhelming the spice profile.
- Demonstrates [[entities/daz|Daz (Chef)]]'s technique for beginner-friendly [[concepts/one-pot-cooking|one-pot cooking]], utilizing honey to [[concepts/rounding|round]] out tomato-based sauce acidity and accelerate glaze formation.
- Full breakdown and replication [[concepts/notes|notes]]: [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-One-Pot-Mild-Curried-Sausages-Dazs-Stovetop-Recipe-with|One-Pot Mild Curried Sausages: Daz's Stovetop Recipe with Secret Honey]]

## Technical Parameters
- **Dosage:** ½–1 tsp per liter of cooking liquid; adjusted post-tasting to maintain savory dominance.
- **Addition Window:** Mid-reduction (60–70°C) to prevent enzymatic breakdown and ensure uniform [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].
- **Substitutes:** Maple Syrup, Agave, or Brown [[concepts/sugar|Sugar]] (note: alters Umami Enhancement profile and pH buffering capacity).
