# Color viewing modes

Methods and parameters for interpreting and manipulating color information within digital imaging and post-processing workflows.

## Color Manipulation Parameters
- **HSL (Hue, Saturation, Luminance)**: A primary framework for targeted [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]].
	- **Hue**: The specific shade or chromatic position of a color.
	- **Saturation**: The intensity or purity of a color.
	- **Luminance**: The perceived brightness of specific color channels.

## Software Implementation
- [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]]: Utilizes the **[[concepts/color-mixer]]** panel to perform localized [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific color channels via HSL parameters.
