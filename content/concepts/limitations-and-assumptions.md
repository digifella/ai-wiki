# Limitations And Assumptions

The Limitations and Assumptions ([[entities/los-angeles|LA]]) approach is a methodology for enhancing the quality and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of [[concepts/ai-summarization|AI-generated notes]]. When applied in conjunction with the QEC (Question, Evidence, Conclusion) framework, LA helps users critically evaluate the boundaries and foundational premises of AI-generated content. This dual-framework approach addresses a common challenge with AI-generated materials: the need to identify what claims are based on solid evidence versus what relies on unstated or questionable assumptions.

## Integration with QEC Framework

The [[concepts/la-approach|LA approach]] complements the [[concepts/qec-framework|QEC framework]] by adding an explicit evaluation layer. While QEC structures [[concepts/notes|notes]] around questions, supporting evidence, and resulting conclusions, LA prompts users to document the limitations of those conclusions and the assumptions underlying them. This creates a more complete picture of an AI-generated note's reliability and applicability. Users can identify gaps in evidence, acknowledge the scope constraints of conclusions, and [[entities/make|make]] explicit any assumptions the AI model may have implicitly incorporated.

## Practical Application

In practice, applying LA to [[concepts/ai-generated-notes|AI-generated notes]] involves documenting what the note does not cover, what conditions must be true for the conclusions to hold, and what unstated premises the analysis depends on. This is particularly valuable in domains like [[concepts/financial-modeling|financial modeling]] or evidence-based analysis, where understanding the boundaries of a conclusion is as important as the conclusion itself.
