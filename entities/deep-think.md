# Deep Think

Deep Think is DeepMind's designation for Aletheia, an artificial intelligence system developed to support scientific discovery. The system was designed with self-correction capabilities, allowing it to identify and address errors in its own outputs during the research process. This approach aims to improve the reliability and accuracy of AI-generated results when applied to scientific research contexts.

## Capabilities

The core distinguishing feature of Deep Think is its self-correcting architecture. Rather than presenting outputs as fixed conclusions, the system is engineered to review its own reasoning, detect inconsistencies or errors, and refine its results iteratively. This capability is intended to address fundamental challenges in using AI for scientific work, where accuracy and reproducibility are essential.

## Application

Deep Think represents an effort to apply advanced AI techniques to accelerated scientific research workflows. By automating aspects of the discovery process while incorporating mechanisms for error detection and correction, the system is positioned as a tool to augment human researchers rather than replace them entirely.
