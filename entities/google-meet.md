# Google Meet

Google Meet is a video conferencing platform developed by Google that enables real-time communication and collaboration between users. Launched as part of Google's integrated suite of productivity applications, the service allows participants to join meetings through web browsers or dedicated mobile applications. The platform supports features including screen sharing, real-time captions, and recording capabilities, making it suitable for both professional and educational contexts.

## Core Features

The platform provides standard video conferencing functionality with support for multiple participants. Users can share their screens during calls, which is useful for presentations and collaborative work. Real-time caption generation assists with accessibility and language comprehension. Meetings can be recorded and saved for later review, and participants can be invited via email or direct links.

## Integration with Google Services

Google Meet integrates with other Google applications including Google Calendar, Gmail, and Google Workspace, allowing users to schedule and launch meetings directly from these services. This integration streamlines workflow for organizations already using Google's productivity ecosystem.

## Recent Updates

Google has begun integrating its AI assistant Gemini into Google Meet to enhance productivity features. These updates aim to provide additional capabilities for meeting participants, though the specific implementations and rollout timeline continue to develop.
