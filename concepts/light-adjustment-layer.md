# Light Adjustment Layer

The **[[concepts/adjustment-layers|Light Adjustment Layer]]** is a [[concepts/non-linear-editing|non-destructive editing]] tool in [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] (Beta v27.9+) designed for global and local luminance control. Unlike traditional Curves or Levels [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], it offers specialized controls for simulating [[concepts/light|light]] sources and managing illumination intensity without altering color balance directly.

## Key Features & Functionality
*   **[[concepts/non-destructive-editing|Non-Destructive Workflow]]**: Applies lighting changes as a separate layer, preserving original [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]].
*   **Luminance Control**: Adjusts brightness and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] with specific [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] optimized for light [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] rather than general tonal mapping.
*   **Integration**: Available in [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]] (v27.9+); likely to merge into stable releases in subsequent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]].
*   **[[concepts/scenarios|Use Cases]]**: Ideal for correcting [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], adding [[concepts/dramatic-lighting|dramatic lighting]] effects, or balancing high-contrast scenes where standard [[Brightness/Contrast]] tools lack [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].
