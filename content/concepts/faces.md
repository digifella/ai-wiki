# Faces

## Overview
Faces are a fundamental aspect of human identity and communication, often used in various [[concepts/software|applications]] including [[concepts/ai-image-generation|AI image generation]].

## Applications in AI
- Used in [[concepts/ai-image-generation|AI image generation]] to create realistic or stylized facial representations.
- Key component in [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] like [[entities/flux1|FLUX.1]] for generating [[concepts/high-quality-images|high-quality images]].
