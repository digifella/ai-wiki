# Iterative reasoning

A computational process where an agent executes repeated cycles of research, evaluation, and refinement to arrive at a high-fidelity conclusion or complex plan.

## Key Implementations
- [[concepts/deep-research-agents|Langchain DeepResearch]]: A highly configurable, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] agent built using the [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph framework]].
    - Demonstrates advanced Multi-step Planning capabilities, such as orchestrating complex, multi-destination travel logistics.
    - Utilizes [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to perform deep, [[concepts/iterative-research|iterative research]] and decision-making.
