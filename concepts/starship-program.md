# Starship Program

Fully reusable super heavy-[[entities/lift|lift]] launch vehicle developed by [[entities/spacex]] for Orbital Launch, [[concepts/mars|Mars]] Colonization, and Point-to-Point Transport. System architecture comprises two stages: Super Heavy booster and [[concepts/starship]] spacecraft. Engineered for [[concepts/reusable-rocket-technology|rapid reusability]], high [[concepts/engine-thrust|thrust]] via Raptor [[concepts/engine|Engine]] array, and in-orbit propellant transfer capability.
