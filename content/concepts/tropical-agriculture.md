# Tropical Agriculture

Tropical [[concepts/agriculture|agriculture]] refers to farming systems and practices developed in tropical regions, characterized by high rainfall, consistent temperatures, and diverse ecosystems. These agricultural approaches have evolved over centuries to address the unique environmental conditions of tropical zones, including dealing with intense seasonal rains, nutrient-poor soils in some areas, and the prevalence of tropical pests and diseases. Traditional tropical agriculture often relies on intercropping, agroforestry, and crop rotation to maintain soil [[concepts/health|health]] and productivity.

## Large-Scale Irrigation and Development

Modern tropical agriculture has increasingly incorporated [[concepts/large-scale-irrigation|large-scale irrigation]] infrastructure as governments and development agencies have sought to expand agricultural [[concepts/output|output]] and support growing populations. These projects represent significant capital investments intended to transform marginal or seasonal agricultural lands into productive year-[[concepts/rounding|round]] farming regions. However, such schemes have frequently encountered substantial challenges in tropical environments, including difficulties managing water [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] systems in remote areas, soil salinity issues, and unanticipated ecological impacts.

## Case Study: The Ord River Scheme

Australia's [[concepts/ord-river-irrigation-project|Ord River irrigation project]] in Western Australia exemplifies the economic and operational difficulties that can arise in tropical [[concepts/agricultural-development|agricultural development]]. Initiated in the mid-twentieth century with the goal of establishing large-scale irrigated farming in the Kimberley region, the scheme faced persistent economic challenges and operational setbacks. Issues including high infrastructure maintenance costs, lower-than-expected agricultural yields, and difficulties adapting farming practices to the tropical climate contributed to the project underperforming relative to initial projections, demonstrating the complexities of applying large-scale irrigation [[concepts/models|models]] in tropical regions.
