# Phlearn

Phlearn is a YouTube channel dedicated to providing video tutorials on [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] techniques, with a primary focus on [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]. The channel produces educational content designed to teach viewers practical [[concepts/skills|skills]] for manipulating and enhancing photographs.

## Content and Tutorials

The channel covers a range of Photoshop features and editing techniques, including advanced topics such as object selection, [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], and image correction methods. [[concepts/tutorial|Tutorial]] videos typically range from several minutes to longer-form educational content, with titles often highlighting specific tools or features within Photoshop's latest versions. Recent tutorials have featured demonstrations of newer Photoshop features, such as techniques for fixing color issues and dehazing [[concepts/images|images]].

## Audience and Scope

Phlearn serves photographers, digital artists, and other creative professionals seeking to improve their post-processing skills through step-by-step visual instruction. The channel's content appeals to both beginners [[concepts/learning|learning]] fundamental editing concepts and experienced users exploring advanced Photoshop capabilities.
