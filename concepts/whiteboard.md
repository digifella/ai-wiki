# Whiteboard

A whiteboard video is an animated format that simulates the real-time process of drawing and writing on a whiteboard. This style combines visual elements with narration to explain concepts, tell stories, or present information in an engaging manner. The format is commonly used for educational content, marketing explanations, and instructional tutorials.

Whiteboard videos appeal to viewers by breaking down complex information into digestible visual steps. The continuous visual progression of drawings helps maintain sustained attention, making it an effective tool for clarifying difficult topics or guiding audiences through logical sequences.

Modern production workflows often leverage specific tools to streamline the creation of these animations. Guides for this domain frequently reference platforms such as NotebookLM, Nano Banana Pro, and Pictory to assist in generating the necessary assets and animations efficiently.
