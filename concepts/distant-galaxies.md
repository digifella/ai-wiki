# Distant galaxies

**Distant galaxies** are celestial structures located at significant cosmological distances from [[entities/earth|Earth]], often observed as they appeared in the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] due to the [[concepts/finite-speed-of-light|finite speed of light]]. Studying these objects provides critical insights into [[concepts/galaxy-formation-and-evolution|galaxy evolution]], [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]], and the reionization epoch.

## Key Observations & Research

*   **James [[entities/jwst|Webb]]'s 100-Hour Deep Field**: Recent intensive observation campaigns have targeted seemingly "empty" regions of the sky to unveil hidden distant structures.
