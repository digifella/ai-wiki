# Non-Destructive Workflow

A methodology in digital editing where original data remains intact, allowing for reversible changes and iterative refinement.

## Core Principles
- **Reversibility:** All modifications can be undone or adjusted without loss of original quality.
- **Layer-Based Architecture:** Utilizes Adjustment Layers to apply effects globally or locally without altering the base pixel data.
- **Tonal Control:** Precise manipulation of light and color via dedicated tools.

## Implementation in Photoshop
- **[[concepts/light-adjustment-layer|Light Adjustment Layer]]:** Integrates [[entities/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] tonal controls into a non-destructive layer stack.
- **Functionality:** Provides advanced Tonal Controls for light management, demystifying complex exposure adjustments.
- **Pause Feature:** Allows for temporary suspension of processing to evaluate intermediate states without committing changes.
- **Context:** Introduced in the 2026 update to address user confusion regarding its functionality and behavior.
