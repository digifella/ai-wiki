## Digital Imaging Workflow

A comprehensive process used by photographers to manage and enhance their images from capture through [[concepts/video-editing|post-production]]. This workflow often integrates tools like [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] for organizing, cataloging, and basic editing, alongside [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] for advanced retouching and [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]].

### Key Elements
- **Image Capture:** [[concepts/camera-raw|Raw format]] files are preferred due to their superior quality and flexibility.
- **Organizing:** Use [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], [[concepts/keywords|keywords]], and collections to effectively manage a large image library in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]].
- **Editing & Enhancement:** Leverage [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]] features for efficient [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] without compromising original data. [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]] Compositing in [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] demonstrates non-destructive compositing using layers and masks in Photoshop.
- **Export & Delivery:** Prepare images for [[concepts/various-outputs|various outputs]] such as web galleries, print media, or social networks.
