# Italy

The Italy detector is a particle physics instrument situated deep underground within the Stawell Gold Mine in Victoria, Australia. Its location at significant depth is critical for shielding sensitive equipment from cosmic radiation, thereby establishing the low-background environment required to detect rare particle interactions.

The detector operates in conjunction with the SABER detector as part of a collaborative effort to search for weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs). WIMPs are theoretical candidates for dark matter, which is hypothesized to constitute a large portion of the universe's mass. This joint experimental approach aims to enhance the sensitivity and reliability of dark matter detection efforts in the region.
