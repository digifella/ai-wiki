# Microsoft Studios

Microsoft Studios is the video game development and publishing division of Microsoft. The entity oversees the creation and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of games across Microsoft's platforms, including Xbox consoles, PC, and cloud [[concepts/gaming|gaming]] services through Xbox Game Pass.

## History and Structure

Microsoft Studios was established as the company's gaming division to develop first-party titles and acquire established game development studios. Through [[concepts/acquisitions|acquisitions]] and internal development, the division has built a portfolio of game franchises and development talent under the Microsoft umbrella.

## Role and Operations

The division functions as both a [[concepts/developer|developer]] and publisher, creating original games and maintaining established franchises. Microsoft Studios games are typically made available across multiple platforms within the Microsoft ecosystem, with many titles included in the Xbox Game Pass subscription service as part of Microsoft's broader gaming strategy.
