# Weatherization
Weatherization involves making buildings and infrastructure more resistant to [[concepts/severe-weather-events|extreme weather]] conditions through various measures such as insulation, sealing air leaks, and installing efficient heating and [[concepts/cooling-systems|cooling systems]]. Proper weatherization can help reduce [[concepts/energy-consumption|energy consumption]] and mitigate the impacts of [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|severe weather events]].
