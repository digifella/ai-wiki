# Flags

In [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]], flags serve as a binary marking system that categorizes images into two distinct states: flagged and unflagged. This simple two-option structure allows photographers to efficiently manage large volumes of data during the initial review [[concepts/phase|phase]]. By avoiding the complexity of multi-level rating systems or [[concepts/color-labels|color labels]], flags provide a rapid method for separating images [[concepts/value|worth]] retaining from those that should be discarded.

The primary utility of flags lies in their role as a first-pass tool within the [[concepts/creative-workflow|editing workflow]]. During the culling stage, photographers use them to quickly isolate potential candidates for further processing. This streamlined approach facilitates a faster [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process, ensuring that only the most promising images advance to subsequent stages of development and organization.

While basic in nature, the flagging mechanism is integral to maintaining an organized digital asset library. It acts as a foundational filter that simplifies the transition from raw capture to final edit. By clearly delineating between kept and rejected images, flags help photographers maintain focus and efficiency throughout the creative process.
