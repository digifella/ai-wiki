# Safe AI Use

## Core Concept
Ensuring AI systems are deployed and operated in ways that minimize harm and maximize benefit to users and society.

## Key Aspects
- **[[concepts/risk-assessment|Risk Assessment]]**: Systematic evaluation of potential AI-related risks.
- **Ethical Alignment**: Ensuring AI systems adhere to ethical principles.
- **Regulatory [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]]**: Adherence to legal [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for AI use.
- **Transparency**: Clear communication about AI system [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and limitations.
- **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]]**: Defining responsibility for AI outcomes.

## Applications
- **[[concepts/health|Healthcare]]**: Safe [[concepts/integration|integration]] of AI in medical diagnostics and treatment.
- **Finance**: Risk management in AI-driven financial systems.
- **Autonomous Systems**: Safety protocols for self-driving vehicles.

## Challenges
- Balancing [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] with safety constraints.
- Ensuring cross-industry standardization of safety protocols.
