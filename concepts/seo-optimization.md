# SEO Optimization

The process of enhancing a website's visibility in search [[concepts/engine|engine]] results through organic (non-paid) means by optimizing content, technical structure, and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]].

**Core components:**
- On-page SEO: Content quality, [[entities/meta-ai|meta]] tags, header [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]], [[concepts/image-quality-enhancement|image optimization]]
- Technical SEO: Site [[concepts/speed|speed]], mobile responsiveness, crawlability, [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]]
- Off-page SEO: Backlink acquisition, brand mentions, social signals
