# Solar Energy

**Solar energy** is radiant [[concepts/light|light]] and heat from the Sun that is harnessed using a range of technologies such as solar heating, photovoltaics, solar thermal energy, solar architecture, and biomass. It is a key component of renewable energy systems, offering a sustainable alternative to fossil fuels.

## Key Characteristics
- **Source**: [[concepts/fusion-power|Nuclear fusion]] reactions in the Sun.
- **Intermittency**: Availability depends on time of day, weather, and [[entities/season|season]], necessitating energy [[entities/storage|storage]] solutions.
- **Applications**: Electricity generation, water heating, space heating, and fuel production.

## Storage and Integration Challenges
The intermittent nature of solar power requires robust storage [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] to ensure grid stability and continuous supply. Traditional methods include battery storage (e.g., lithium-ion) and pumped hydro storage. However, long-duration storage remains a critical bottleneck for seasonal balancing.

### Recent Developments in Long-Term Storage
- **Thermochemical Storage**: Emerging technologies focus on [[concepts/storing|storing]] energy chemically to overcome seasonal gaps.
- **Season's Technology**: A Swiss company, Season, has developed a [[concepts/thermochemical-heat-storage|thermochemical heat storage]] [[concepts/solution|solution]] capable of storing surplus renewable energy for months with minimal loss. This technology aims to provide a more efficient alternative to traditional [[concepts/heat-pumps|heat pumps]] for long-term storage.
