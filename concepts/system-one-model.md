# System One model

**[[concepts/system-one-intelligence|System One]] model** refers to a specific architectural approach to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]], notably championed by [[entities/typesafe-ai]] through their model **Jev**. This paradigm positions itself as a fundamental departure from traditional [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs), emphasizing reliability, type safety, and [[concepts/reliable-automation|deterministic automation]] over probabilistic text generation.

## Key Characteristics
*   **Non-probabilistic Core**: Unlike standard LLMs, Jev operates as a [[concepts/decision-model|decision model]] prioritizing deterministic outcomes and type safety.
*   **Performance Profile**: Designed for high speed, reliability, and [[concepts/cost-efficiency|cost-efficiency]] in automated workflows.
*   **Architectural Class**: Described as a "completely different class of [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]]" distinct from traditional transformer-based generation.
