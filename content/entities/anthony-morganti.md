# Anthony Morganti

Anthony Morganti is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] specializing in tutorials for [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] software. His work focuses primarily on [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]], two widely-used [[concepts/software|applications]] in digital photography and image post-processing.

## Content and Focus

Morganti produces instructional videos covering various [[concepts/photo-editing-techniques|photo editing techniques]] and features. His tutorials include practical demonstrations such as removing unwanted elements from photographs—for example, techniques for [[concepts/photoshop-techniques|removing chain-link fences]] using Photoshop's editing tools. He also covers updates and new features in Adobe's software, including analysis of [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] updates and Adobe's Firefly [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] for photographers.
