# Color mixer

A tool within [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] used for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]].

### Recent Updates
#### New Camera Raw Variance filter
* **Location**: Found within the **[[concepts/point-color]]** section of the Color Mixer.
* **Functionality**: The **Variance** slider controls the [[concepts/range|range]] of colors affected by a [[concepts/point-color|Point Color]] adjustment after a specific color has been sampled.
* **Source**: [[entities/glyn-dewis|Glyn Dewis]] ([Video Reference](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mEgFQC430k0))

#### Targeted HSL Adjustments
* **Functionality**: Utilizing the [[concepts/hslcolor-mixer|HSL/Color Mixer]] panel to manipulate **[[concepts/hue|Hue]]**, **Saturation**, and **Luminance**.
* **Platform**: [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]].
* **Source**: [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]] ([Video Reference](https://youtu.be/LzqlkBUkINg))
