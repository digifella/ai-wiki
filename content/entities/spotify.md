# Spotify

Spotify is a digital music streaming service that operates on a subscription-based model, offering users access to millions of songs, [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]], and other [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] content. The platform has become a primary target for users seeking to reduce or eliminate recurring subscription costs through alternative solutions.

## Self-Hosted Alternatives

There is growing interest in building personal media servers as alternatives to subscription services like Spotify. These projects typically run on affordable [[concepts/hardware|hardware]], such as [[entities/ubuntu|Ubuntu]]-based systems, and can be configured for under $100. Such servers allow users to host and stream their own media collections without ongoing subscription fees, though they require initial [[concepts/setup|setup]] and maintenance.

## AI-Powered Podcast Summarization

Recent developments explore using [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to automatically summarize podcast content, addressing the challenge of consuming large volumes of audio material. This approach can be integrated into [[concepts/subscription-based-services|self-hosted media server]] solutions to enhance their functionality and provide value comparable to commercial [[concepts/movie-streaming|streaming platforms]]' features.
