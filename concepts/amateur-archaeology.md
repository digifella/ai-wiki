# Amateur archaeology

Non-professional participation in archaeological research, often through [[concepts/metal-detecting|metal detecting]] or community-based projects. Key examples include:

- **Denmark's government-authorized program**: Deputizing private detectorists to [[concepts/survey|survey]] farm fields, yielding detailed historical insights (e.g., artifacts found by [[entities/kristen-nedergaard-dreiøe|Kristen Nedergaard Dreiøe]] and [[entities/marie-aagaard-larsen|Marie Aagaard Larsen]] in southern Denmark)
- **Impact**: Reveals national history through systematic amateur contributions, combining public engagement with [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]]
- **Model**: Demonstrates how structured amateur involvement (e.g., official permits, field [[concepts/coordination|coordination]]) can produce significant archaeological knowledge
