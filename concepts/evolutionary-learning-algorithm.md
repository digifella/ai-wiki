# Evolutionary Learning Algorithm

**Evolutionary [[concepts/learning|Learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|Algorithms]]** are [[concepts/calculation-methods|computational methods]] inspired by biological evolution, utilizing [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] such as mutation, crossover, and selection to optimize solutions for complex problems. Unlike traditional gradient-based learning, these algorithms explore [[concepts/solution|solution]] spaces through iterative population-based search.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Population Initialization**: Generation of a diverse set of candidate solutions.
- **Fitness Evaluation**: Assessment of each candidate against [[concepts/loss-functions|objective functions]].
- **Selection**: Preferential choice of high-fitness individuals for reproduction.
- **Genetic Operators**: Application of Mutation and Crossover to generate new generations.
- **Termination**: Halting criteria based on convergence or computational budget.

## Narrative Integration in AI
Recent [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]] suggest that standard evolutionary models lack contextual depth, leading to brittle [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]]. A proposed solution involves integrating **[[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] structures** as a guiding mechanism for evolutionary pressure.

- **[[concepts/human-stories|Human Stories]] as Evolutionary Pressure**: 
  - [[concepts/dr-know-it-all-knows-it-all|Dr. Know-it-all]] argues that "stories" provide the missing semantic context required for robust [[concepts/ai-advancement|AI advancement]] [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Decoding-AIs-Problem-Human-Stories-as-an-Evolutionary-Le|Decoding AI's Problem: Human Stories as an Evolutionary Learning Algorithm]].
  - Stories act as high-level fitness functions, [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] cultural, ethical, and causal constraints that raw data lacks.
  - This approach frames narrative coherence as a survival trait in the evolutionary [[concepts/loop|loop]], allowing AI to learn not just *what* works, but *why* it matters within a human context.
