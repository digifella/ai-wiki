# Operational Order

The Operational Order is a comprehensive guide from [[entities/america|America]]'s Test Kitchen that establishes a systematic approach to making fried rice. Presented by [[entities/lan-lam|Lan Lam]] in the video "The Formula to Great Fried Rice with Any Ingredients," the guide breaks down fried rice [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] into reproducible techniques that can be applied regardless of specific ingredient choices.

## Rice Treatment

The foundation of successful fried rice begins with proper rice preparation. The guide emphasizes that rice quality and [[concepts/texture|texture]] directly impact the final dish, making treatment methods essential to master before adding other components.

## Ingredient Mix-ins

Beyond the base rice, the Operational Order addresses how to effectively incorporate mix-in ingredients. By establishing a structured approach to ingredient selection and [[concepts/integration|integration]], the guide enables cooks to adapt the formula to available ingredients while maintaining consistent results.
