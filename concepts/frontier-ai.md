# Frontier AI

Frontier AI is [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s research initiative focused on developing and advancing large-scale [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems. The program encompasses work on state-of-the-art [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], foundational technologies, and the practical considerations involved in deploying these systems at scale. As a research effort, Frontier AI represents [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of progress in AI capabilities while addressing both technical challenges and responsible development practices.

## Core Research Areas

The initiative covers multiple technical domains central to modern [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]]. This includes research into advanced language models such as [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], as well as work on [[concepts/dense-vectors|embeddings]] technology that enables more efficient representation and processing of information. Beyond [[concepts/knowledge-acquisition|model development]], Frontier AI addresses the infrastructure and methodological requirements for [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] these systems responsibly.
