# Electronic Visa System E Visa

The Electronic Visa System, commonly referred to as the E Visa, is the official digital platform operated by the Republic of Turkey for processing visa applications. Launched to modernize and streamline entry procedures, the system allows eligible foreign nationals to apply for Turkish visas entirely online. This digital infrastructure eliminates the need for applicants to visit physical embassies or consulates, providing a centralized web-based interface for managing the entire application lifecycle.

## Eligibility and Application Process

The system is available to citizens of designated countries who meet specific entry requirements, such as holding valid passports from eligible nations and possessing necessary travel documents like return tickets and proof of accommodation. Applicants must complete an online form, pay the requisite fees via credit or debit card, and receive their visa approval electronically. The process is designed to be completed within a short timeframe, typically resulting in the issuance of the visa via email shortly after approval.

## Visa Validity and Usage

The E Visa is generally issued as a single-entry or multiple-entry visa valid for a specific duration, often allowing stays of up to 90 days within a 180-day period, depending on the applicant's nationality and the type of visa granted. It is strictly for tourism, business, or transit purposes and does not permit the holder to work or study in Turkey. Travelers must ensure their passport remains valid for at least six months beyond their entry date and must present the printed or digital copy of the E Visa upon arrival at Turkish border control points.
