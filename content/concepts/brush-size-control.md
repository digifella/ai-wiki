# Brush Size Control

[[concepts/brush-size|Brush size]] [[concepts/power|control]] in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] refers to the ability to adjust brush dimensions during active painting and editing work. Rather than accessing brush settings through menus or panels, Photoshop provides keyboard shortcuts that allow artists to modify brush properties in real time without interrupting their [[concepts/workflow|workflow]]. This method of control is particularly useful when working on detailed digital [[concepts/art|art]], [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]], or illustration projects where frequent size [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are necessary.

## Size Adjustment

The left and right bracket keys (`[` and `]`) on the keyboard control brush size in Photoshop. Pressing the left bracket decreases brush size incrementally, while the right bracket increases it. This allows for quick [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] between strokes or during continuous painting work. The amount of size change per keystroke can vary depending on the brush and zoom level, but typically shifts the diameter by a small percentage, enabling both major and fine-tuned adjustments.

## Hardness Adjustment

[[concepts/brush-hardness|Brush hardness]]—the softness or crispness of the brush edges—can be similarly controlled using Shift combined with the bracket keys. Shift+left bracket reduces hardness (softening edges), while Shift+right bracket increases hardness (creating sharper edges). This paired system allows artists to fine-tune both brush dimensions and edge quality without reaching for dialog boxes or brush panels, maintaining continuous focus on their artwork.
