# Bmj

The British Medical Journal (BMJ) is a peer-reviewed medical journal founded in 1840 and published by BMJ Publishing Group. It is one of the most widely read medical journals in the world, with readers including healthcare professionals, researchers, policymakers, and medical students. The journal maintains editorial independence and operates under both subscription and open-access publishing models to distribute research findings to diverse audiences.

## Content and Publishing

BMJ publishes original research, systematic reviews, editorials, and analysis across clinical medicine, public health, and healthcare policy. The journal covers topics ranging from evidence-based practice to health policy commentary and medical education. Its content is peer-reviewed to maintain academic standards, and the journal has established itself as a significant source for evidence-based medical information and professional debate within the healthcare sector.

## Accessibility and Reach

The journal's dual publishing approach—combining subscription-based and open-access content—reflects a commitment to making research available beyond institutional subscriptions. This model has contributed to BMJ's prominence in both academic medicine and public health discourse, allowing its research and analysis to influence clinical practice and healthcare policy internationally.
