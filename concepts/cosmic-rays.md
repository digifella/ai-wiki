# Cosmic Rays

Cosmic [[concepts/rays|rays]] are energetic charged particles, primarily [[concepts/protons|protons]] and atomic nuclei, originating from outer space that impact the [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]]. They present a constant stream of radiation to our planet.
