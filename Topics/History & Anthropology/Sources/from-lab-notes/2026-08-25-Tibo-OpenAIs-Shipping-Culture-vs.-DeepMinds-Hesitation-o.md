## Tibo: OpenAI's Shipping Culture vs. DeepMind's Hesitation on LMchat
**Clip title:** Tibo: Ultrafast Mode, The Reset Button, OpenAI vs Anthropic, RSI, and more!
**Author / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4qjEgPojjzM

### Summary
The video features an interview with [[entities/tibo|Tibo]], a developer at [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], who discusses his past experiences at [[entities/google|Google]]'s DeepMind and contrasts it with the current culture and future vision at OpenAI. Tibo recounts how Google's DeepMind team had developed a chatbot similar to ChatGPT (dubbed [[entities/lmchat|LMchat]]) a year prior to its public release by OpenAI, but Google was hesitant to launch it due to internal concerns about disrupting their existing products. This experience highlighted for Tibo the challenges of balancing groundbreaking research with product development within a large, established company, particularly the friction between various departments and a lack of "bias to ship" potentially disruptive innovations.

In contrast, Tibo emphasizes OpenAI's unique culture, which he describes as empowering, bottom-up, and strongly biased towards shipping products and making them broadly accessible. At OpenAI, research and product teams collaborate closely, fostering an environment where ideas can quickly transition from concept to implementation with minimal internal resistance or "stop energy." The company prioritizes simplicity, performance, and efficiency in its products, citing the ChatGPT iOS app as an example of high-quality design. This drive stems from a forward-looking philosophy and a willingness to disrupt their own offerings with new research and ideas, reallocating resources as needed to pursue cutting-edge advancements.

Tibo delves into the future of AI models and interfaces, anticipating a shift from current "clunky" AI tools to a "perfect little partner" that deeply understands user goals, is both proactive and reactive, and integrates seamlessly into daily life. He highlights the impact of innovations like UltraFast speeds and advanced voice capabilities, which dramatically change user workflows by enabling real-time interaction, rapid prototyping, and efficient [[concepts/multitasking|multitasking]] without cognitive overload. The merger of Codex and ChatGPT is presented as a natural progression, reflecting the multimodal and versatile nature of increasingly capable models. This unified agent is designed to tailor itself to individual user needs, transcending traditional categories like "coder" or "non-technical user," thereby offering a truly personalized experience.

Ultimately, OpenAI's mission is rooted in making powerful [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] broadly accessible and beneficial for humanity. Tibo explains that continuous improvements in [[concepts/computational-efficiency|model efficiency]] and cost reduction, exemplified by projects like Luna, are crucial for achieving this widespread utility. The company also invests heavily in safety and alignment research, viewing it as paramount as AI capabilities advance. By focusing on models that understand natural language and human nuances, OpenAI aims to create technology that feels like a natural extension of human communication and capabilities, rather than a foreign tool that users must adapt to. The goal is for AI to provide profound personal support, whether for writing, giving advice, or assisting with health and finance, ensuring that the benefits of AI are shared globally and responsibly.
