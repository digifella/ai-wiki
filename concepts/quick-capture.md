# Quick Capture

**Quick Capture** refers to the practice of rapidly recording thoughts, ideas, or data with minimal friction to prevent loss of context. In the context of Obsidian, this often involves leveraging external tools or native features to bypass the full application interface for faster input.

## Workflow Optimization

Recent developments in external tooling aim to reduce the latency between thought and capture. A notable example is the integration of specialized launchers designed to streamline the interaction between the operating system and note-taking applications.

### Lean Launcher Integration

The introduction of **[[concepts/lean-launcher|Lean Launcher]]** addresses common pain points in Windows workflows, specifically regarding context switching and application startup latency.

*   **Purpose:** Streamlines the process of initiating Obsidian for quick capture tasks.
*   **Key Benefit:** Reduces the friction of opening the full Obsidian interface when only a quick note is required.
*   **Context:** Designed to mitigate the frustration of breaking flow during the capture phase.
