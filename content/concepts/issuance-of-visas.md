# Issuance Of Visas

[[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] offers an electronic visa ([[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-visa]]) system for eligible foreign nationals seeking entry to the country. The e-visa is a digital travel [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] that allows visitors to enter Turkey for tourism, business, and other short-term purposes without obtaining a traditional paper visa from a [[entities/turkish-embassy|Turkish embassy]] or consulate.

## Application Process

The e-visa application is completed online through Turkey's official e-visa portal. Applicants must provide personal information, passport details, and travel plans. Processing is typically completed within minutes to hours of submission, after which the authorization is sent via [[entities/email|email]]. Applicants must print the e-visa or have it available on their device [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the time of travel.

## Eligibility and Validity

The e-visa is available to nationals of most countries and is valid for a single entry or multiple entries depending on the type issued. The visa itself is typically valid for 90 days from the date of issuance, and visitors are generally permitted to stay in Turkey for up to 90 days within a 180-day period from the date of entry. The specific terms and eligible nationalities are determined by Turkish immigration regulations.
