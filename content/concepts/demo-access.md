# Demo Access

Demo Access refers to the grant of experimental access to a [[concepts/software|software]] tool or service, typically communicated via [[entities/email|email]] notification. In this case, it specifically denotes access granted to the [[concepts/gemini-diffusion-model|Gemini Diffusion]] [[concepts/experimental-model|experimental model]] from [[concepts/google-search|Google]], which was confirmed via notification email sent on June 12, 2025 to [[entities/paul|paul]]@project-143.com.

The notification came from [[entities/google|Google]]'s automated notification system and indicated that the recipient's access to the [[entities/gemini-diffusion|Gemini Diffusion]] tool had been activated and was ready for use. Such demo access typically allows users to test experimental or beta features before they are made available to the general public, enabling [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] and evaluation of the tool's functionality.

Gemini Diffusion appears to be part of Google's broader suite of AI and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] tools. Demo access programs serve as a way for organizations to [[concepts/power|control]] rollout of new [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], gather [[concepts/user-feedback|user feedback]], and identify issues before wider [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
