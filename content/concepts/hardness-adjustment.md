# Hardness Adjustment

Hardness adjustment refers to modifying the softness or crispness of a brush edge in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]. A brush with low hardness produces soft, feathered strokes, while high hardness creates sharp, defined edges. This setting is essential for controlling the precision and blending quality of painting and digital artwork.

## Bracket Key Method

The quickest way to adjust [[concepts/brush-hardness|brush hardness]] in Photoshop is using the bracket keys on the keyboard. The left bracket key `[` decreases hardness for softer edges, while the right bracket key `]` increases hardness for sharper edges. This method allows real-time adjustment without accessing menus, making it ideal for rapid [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] changes during painting sessions.

## Alternative Adjustment Methods

Beyond bracket keys, hardness can be adjusted through the Brush Settings panel, the Tool Options bar, or by right-clicking with the brush tool selected to open the brush picker. These methods provide more precise [[concepts/power|control]] when specific hardness values are needed, though they require more steps than the keyboard shortcut approach.
