# Instruction-Based Editing

A technique where user-provided natural language [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] guide content modification (e.g., image or [[concepts/text|text]]) without manual pixel-level editing. Enables precise, context-aware transformations through direct textual prompts.

**Key [[concepts/software|Applications]]**:
- [[concepts/image-editing|Image Editing]]: [[entities/hidream-e11|HiDream-E1.1]] ([[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]]) supports instruction-based editing with [[concepts/dynamic-resolution|dynamic resolution]], [[concepts/detail-removal|detail removal]] (e.g., raindrops from street scenes while preserving background [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]]), and improved quality/[[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] over OmniGen2 and [[entities/flux-kontext-dev|Flux Kontext Dev]].
