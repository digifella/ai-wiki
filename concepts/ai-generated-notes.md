# AI Generated Notes

[[concepts/ai-summarization|AI-generated notes]] refer to summaries, analyses, and documentation created through [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems rather than manual human composition. These [[concepts/notes|notes]] are produced by language models and [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI platforms]] that can process [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]]—articles, videos, documents, or recordings—and extract key information in condensed formats. Common tools for generating such notes include [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], GPT-based systems, and other AI [[concepts/writing|writing]] assistants that have become increasingly integrated into [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] and research workflows.

## Improving Utility with Structured Frameworks

The practical value of AI-generated notes can be significantly enhanced through the application of structured [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] frameworks. Two approaches have proven particularly effective: the QEC (Question, Evidence, Conclusion) framework and the [[concepts/limitations-and-assumptions|Limitations and Assumptions approach]].

The [[concepts/qec-framework|QEC framework]] organizes information by explicitly stating the question being addressed, the evidence supporting an [[concepts/solution|answer]], and the resulting conclusion. This method forces [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] around what problem the notes are meant to solve and ensures that claims are grounded in cited evidence rather than [[concepts/inference|inference]]. When applied to [[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-generated content]], QEC helps identify gaps where the model may have made unsupported leaps or where additional research is needed.

The [[concepts/la-approach|Limitations and Assumptions approach]] involves explicitly documenting what constraints apply to the notes and what underlying assumptions the source material or [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] has made. This includes noting the date of [[concepts/training-data|training data]], potential [[concepts/biases|biases]] in source material, scope boundaries, and any uncertainties. By surfacing these dimensions, users develop a more calibrated understanding of what the notes reliably convey and where they should be treated with skepticism or supplemented with additional [[concepts/verification|verification]].
