# Post Office Gallery

Post Office Gallery is a venue that has hosted contemporary [[concepts/art|art]] exhibitions. The gallery gained recognition for presenting "[[concepts/visual-art|Visualising the Invisible]]," an exhibition by artist [[concepts/visual-arts|Kim Percy]] that explored connections between artistic practice and dyslexia.

## The Visualising the Invisible Exhibition

[[entities/kim-percy|Kim Percy]]'s exhibition [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Post Office Gallery examined how dyslexic [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] patterns can constitute creative strengths rather than limitations. The show presented visual work that engaged with themes of perception, [[concepts/cognition|cognition]], and neurodiversity in artistic practice. The exhibition was documented and noted in April 2026.
