# Practice-Oriented Framework

A framework designed to translate high-level principles into actionable practices, particularly in complex domains like [[concepts/ai-oversight|AI oversight]] and [[concepts/ethics|ethics]].

## Key Characteristics
- Focuses on practical implementation over theoretical principles
- Often used in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] and technology [[concepts/governance|governance]]
- Aims to bridge gaps between policy and execution
