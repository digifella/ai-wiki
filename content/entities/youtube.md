# YouTube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] in 2006. It allows users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos. The platform has become one of the largest repositories of video content on the internet, hosting everything from user-generated content to professional productions, educational material, and entertainment.

## Core Functionality

Users can create accounts to upload videos, organize them into playlists, and manage their content through a creator dashboard. The platform uses algorithmic recommendation systems to suggest videos to viewers based on viewing history and engagement patterns. YouTube offers both free ad-supported viewing and a paid subscription service (YouTube Premium) that removes advertisements and enables offline viewing.

## Role in Content Distribution

YouTube serves as a primary distribution channel for creators, educators, musicians, and organizations. The platform's monetization features allow creators to earn revenue through advertising, channel memberships, and other mechanisms. It has significantly impacted media consumption patterns and the way information, entertainment, and educational content reaches audiences globally.
