# Singularity

The hypothetical point in time at which technological growth becomes uncontrollable and irreversible, resulting in unfathomable changes to human civilization. Often associated with the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of [[concepts/superintelligence]].

## Key Concepts
- **Technological Acceleration**: The rapid increase in AI capabilities and computational power.
- **[[concepts/ai-singularity|Intelligence Explosion]]**: A scenario where AI recursively improves itself, leading to rapid advancement.
- **Alignment Problem**: The challenge of ensuring AI goals remain aligned with human values.
- **[[concepts/containment-breaches|Containment Breaches]]**: Instances where [[concepts/frontier-ai-models|frontier AI models]] exhibit unexpected, problematic, or [[concepts/unaligned-behaviors|unaligned behaviors]], forcing developers to pause training and re-evaluate safety protocols.
