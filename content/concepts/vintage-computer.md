# Vintage Computer
A term referring to older computing systems that predate modern digital computers but still retain operational significance and interest in the context of technological history. Key features include limited processing [[concepts/power|power]] and [[concepts/memory|memory]] compared to contemporary [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].

## Demystifying AI Transformer Training on a 1979 PDP-11
The video, presented by [[entities/dave|Dave]], aims to demystify the [[concepts/training-process|training process]] of a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] by [[concepts/running|running]] a transformer on a vintage 1979 [[concepts/pdp-1144|PDP-11/44]] computer. This system operates with a single 6MHz [[concepts/cpu|CPU]] and initially has only 64KB of [[concepts/ram|RAM]] (later upgraded to 4MB). The core idea, Dave argues, is not magical or new; rather it [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the fundamental principles of AI [[concepts/training|training]] regardless of [[concepts/hardware-limitations|hardware limitations]].
