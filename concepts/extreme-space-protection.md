# Extreme Space Protection

**Extreme Space [[concepts/secure|Protection]]** refers to the integrated systems, materials, and technologies designed to shield humans and equipment from the hostile environment of Outer Space. This includes protection against vacuum, extreme temperatures, radiation, micrometeoroids, and debris.

## Core Threats
- **Vacuum:** Rapid decompression, ebullism, and hypoxia.
- **Thermal Extremes:** Fluctuations between ~120°C (sunlight) and -150°C (shadow).
- **Radiation:** [[concepts/cosmic-rays|Galactic Cosmic Rays]] (GCRs) and Solar Particle Events (SPEs).
- **Impact Hazards:** Micrometeoroids and Orbital Debris (MMOD).

## Technological Implementation

### Space Suits
[[concepts/space-suits|Space suits]] function as miniature spacecraft, providing a pressurized, breathable environment. Key components include:

- **Multi-Layer Construction:** Utilizes specialized fabrics to balance flexibility with pressure [[concepts/storing|retention]] and [[concepts/temperature-management|thermal control]].
- **Material [[concepts/science|Science]]:** Recent advancements focus on lightweight, high-tensile strength materials that resist puncture and abrasion while allowing mobility.
- **Life Support Integration:** Closed-[[concepts/loop|loop]] systems for oxygen supply, carbon dioxide scrubbing, and temperature [[concepts/regulation|regulation]].
