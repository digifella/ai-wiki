# Author Channel New Scientist

[[entities/new-scientist|New Scientist]] is a weekly [[concepts/science|science]] and technology magazine founded in 1956. It functions as a major publishing channel for [[concepts/research-communications|science communication]], translating complex scientific research and technological developments into accessible content. The publication serves both specialist researchers and general audiences, establishing itself as a significant [[concepts/tone|voice]] in science journalism since its inception.

The channel reaches millions of readers globally through various media formats. While it maintains a strong presence in print editions, it has expanded its distribution to include [[concepts/corporate-platforms|digital platforms]] and other media channels. This multi-platform approach ensures that its coverage of emerging scientific trends and technological advancements remains widely available to an international audience.
