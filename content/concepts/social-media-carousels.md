# Social Media Carousels

Social media carousels are a content format available on major platforms including Instagram, LinkedIn, and Facebook, where users can upload multiple [[concepts/images|images]] or [[entities/google-slides|slides]] that viewers navigate sequentially. Each carousel typically contains between 2 and 10 slides, though platform limits vary. Viewers swipe or click through the slides, with each one potentially featuring different images, text, or calls-to-action.

## Format and Design Considerations

The carousel format encourages deeper engagement than single-image posts, as users must actively interact with the content to see all slides. This extended interaction time can improve platform algorithmic performance. Creators typically use carousels to tell a story across multiple slides, present step-by-step [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], showcase product variations, or deliver educational content in digestible segments. Design [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across slides—such as matching fonts, color schemes, and aspect ratios—is important for a polished presentation.

## Strategic Applications

Marketing teams and content creators use carousels to present complex information in an accessible format while capturing user [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]]. The format works particularly well for tutorials, before-and-after comparisons, list-based content, and narrative-driven posts. Analytics on carousel performance often show higher completion rates and engagement metrics compared to single-slide alternatives, making them a useful tool for content strategy planning.
