# Fossil Evidence

Fossil evidence is crucial for understanding prehistoric life and evolutionary history. It includes preserved remains or traces of organisms from past geological periods. This evidence helps scientists piece together the timeline of Earth's [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]].

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/paleontology|Paleontology]]**: The study of ancient life through [[concepts/fossils|fossils]].
- **Stratigraphy**: The branch of [[concepts/geology|geology]] concerned with rock layers and their chronological succession.
- **[[concepts/evolutionary-biology|Evolutionary Biology]]**: Studies the processes that have brought about the diversity of life on Earth as inferred through paleontological records.

## Notable Examples

- Dinosaurs
- [[concepts/mammalian-evolution|Mammalian Evolution]]
- Coral Reefs Through Time

### Recent Insights (2026)

- The "[[concepts/golden-age-of-sharks|Golden Age of Sharks]]" refers to a period characterized by incredibly diverse and often bizarre [[concepts/cartilaginous-fish|cartilaginous fish]], including the ancestors of modern sharks, rays, and [[concepts/chimaeras|chimaeras]] like the [[concepts/ratfish|ratfish]].
- This era preceded the dominance of iconic predators such as the Great White Shark.
- Tracing this history is challenging but provides insights into [[concepts/shark-evolution|shark evolution]] and adaptation.
