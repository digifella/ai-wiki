# Infrastructure Development

Infrastructure Development refers to the planning, construction, and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of physical and [[concepts/organization|organizational systems]] necessary to support [[concepts/space-exploration|space exploration]] activities. In the context of deep space missions, this encompasses launch facilities, [[concepts/spacecraft-systems|spacecraft systems]], ground control networks, and the logistical frameworks required to sustain human presence beyond Earth orbit.

## Deep Space Launch and Crewed Mission Developments

[[entities/scott-manley|Scott Manley]]'s March-April 2024 update addressed recent progress in deep space infrastructure capabilities. The update focused on technological and organizational developments enabling [[concepts/crewed-missions|crewed missions]] to operate at greater distances from Earth, representing a significant evolution in humanity's capacity for sustained deep space operations. This represented an assessment of the current state of infrastructure supporting deep space exploration during that period.
