# Decision Automation

**Decision automation** refers to the use of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] and algorithmic systems to make or recommend choices without human intervention, typically based on predefined rules, [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]] inputs, or learned patterns.

## Core Mechanisms
- Relies on **[[concepts/structured-inputs|structured inputs]]** to reduce [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] and improve model accuracy.
- Utilizes Generative [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] to process [[concepts/zeta-function|complex variables]] and output actionable decisions.
- Often integrated into [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|Workflow Automation]] pipelines to streamline operations.
