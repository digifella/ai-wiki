# AI chatbots

Interactive systems powered by [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] designed for [[concepts/conversational-interfaces|conversational interfaces]] and task execution.

## Evolution & Capabilities

- **[[entities/agent|Agent]] Transformation**: Platforms like [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] enable the conversion of standard chatbots into autonomous [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].
- **[[concepts/external-tools|Model Context Protocol]] (MCP)**: [[concepts/model-context-protocol|Model Context Protocol (MCP)]] functions as a universal interface allowing [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to connect directly to [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources and tools, bypassing limitations of chat-restricted uploads.
- **Resource**: <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p0pR_zq-85M> demonstrates leveraging MCP to enhance [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]].
