# Strategic Questions

Strategic questions are a fundamental framework for structuring business presentations and narratives using [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]]. Rather than relying on AI to generate complete presentations automatically, this approach involves using AI as a research and synthesis partner while maintaining human judgment in organizing ideas. The method emphasizes asking targeted questions that clarify the core message, audience needs, and logical flow before or during slide creation.

## Structuring Presentations with AI

The process begins by identifying the key strategic question that the presentation answers. This question becomes the spine around which all content organizes, ensuring coherence and relevance. AI tools like [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] can synthesize research materials and sources into structured outlines, which presenters then shape into slide formats. The presenter maintains editorial control over argument [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]], supporting evidence, and visual presentation rather than accepting AI-generated defaults.

## Editing and Refinement

Effective AI-assisted presentation creation requires iterative editing. Rather than treating AI output as final, presenters should use it as a first draft to be critically reviewed, restructured, and refined. This involves eliminating redundancies, strengthening weak arguments, and ensuring each slide advances the central thesis. Human-led editing ensures the presentation reflects accurate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and appropriate emphasis on business objectives rather than superficial polish that AI tools might prioritize.
