# TRIMRANGE

A feature in [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] known as "[[concepts/trim-references|Trim References]]" (or "Trim Refs") designed to dynamically remove empty cells from the edges of a data range.

### Key Capabilities
- **[[concepts/dynamic-range|Dynamic Range]] Management**: Simplifies the handling of [[concepts/dynamic-data-ranges|Dynamic Data Ranges]] by automatically stripping leading and trailing blanks.
- **Clean Reporting**: Eliminates unwanted empty cells to ensure cleaner, more professional [[entities/excel|Excel]] Reports.
- **Formula Simplification**: Reduces the complexity of Excel Formulas previously required to manually trim blank boundaries from [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
