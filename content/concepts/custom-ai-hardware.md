# custom AI hardware

Specialized semiconductor architectures and computing systems optimized for accelerating [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] workloads, prioritizing high-throughput tensor operations and [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] over the general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] versatility of standard GPUs.
