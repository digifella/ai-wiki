# Interactive Travel Content

Digital resources enabling dynamic engagement with travel information beyond static guides, allowing real-time [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] and actionable [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] during trip planning.

Key implementation methods:
- [[entities/notebooklm]] for curating trusted research (articles, [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], websites) into structured destination [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]]
- [[entities/gemini]] for generating interactive content (personalized itineraries, restaurant [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]], schedule checks) from curated research
