[[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]] [[entities/nvidia-rtx-gpus|RTX GPUs]] are consumer-grade graphics cards featuring real-time ray tracing and AI acceleration [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], primarily designed for [[concepts/gaming|gaming]] but increasingly adopted for AI workloads. Key features include:

- **AI Acceleration**: Dedicated Tensor Core [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] enables efficient execution of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] locally, reducing reliance on expensive [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud services]].
- **[[concepts/cost-optimization|Cost Optimization]]**: [[concepts/local-deployment|Local deployment]] using RTX GPUs (including NVIDIA GeForce 30-series and NVIDIA GeForce 40-series) can cut monthly AI costs from $10,000+ (cloud) to near-[[concepts/zero|zero]] operational expenses.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] Enhancement**: Data processing occurs entirely on user-owned [[concepts/hardware|hardware]], eliminating cloud data transmission risks.
- **Hybrid [[concepts/cloud-integration|Cloud Integration]]**: Combines local RTX GPU processing with [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]] for non-sensitive tasks, balancing [[concepts/cost|cost]], [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and scalability.
