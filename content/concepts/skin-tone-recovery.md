# Skin Tone Recovery

Skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] recovery in concert photography refers to the process of correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] and lighting irregularities that distort how skin appears in [[concepts/images|images]] taken in challenging stage environments. Concert venues typically feature colored gels, spotlights, and inconsistent lighting that can render [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] with unwanted color tints—such as excessive blue, red, or green hues. Recovery techniques aim to restore natural, neutral skin tones while preserving the overall mood and character of the photograph.

## Adobe Lightroom Approach

[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] provides several tools for addressing skin tone issues in concert photos. The White Balance adjustment can be used to counteract overall color casts by shifting temperature and tint values. The HSL ([[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, Luminance) panel allows targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]], enabling photographers to desaturate or shift unwanted hues in skin tones without affecting other colors in the image. The [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration panel]] offers additional [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] of color rendering across [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], midtones, and [[concepts/highlights|highlights]].

For more precise work, [[concepts/local-adjustments|local adjustments]] using the Adjustment Brush or Graduated Filter can be applied directly to skin areas, allowing color and tone corrections to be isolated from the background. [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] by color [[concepts/range|range]] further enables selective adjustments to skin tones independent of other elements in the frame. These non-destructive edits preserve the original image data while providing flexibility for [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]].
