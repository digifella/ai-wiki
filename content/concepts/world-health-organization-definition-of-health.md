# World Health Organization definition of health

The World [[concepts/health|Health]] [[concepts/organization|Organization]] (WHO) defines health as a state of complete physical, mental and social [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] and not merely the absence of disease or infirmity. This concept underscores that health is more than just the lack of illness; it encompasses an individual's overall [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].

- **Comprehensive Approach**: Emphasizes total well-being.
- **Negative Definition Critique**: Challenges traditional views by stating that health goes beyond mere non-disease states.
