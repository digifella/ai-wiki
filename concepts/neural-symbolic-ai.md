# Neural-Symbolic AI

**Neural-symbolic AI** refers to hybrid [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] architectures that combine the [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] and [[concepts/learning|learning]] capabilities of [[concepts/neural-networks]] with the logical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], knowledge representation, and explainability of symbolic AI. This approach aims to overcome the "[[concepts/black-box-models|black box]]" nature of pure [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] while addressing the brittleness of traditional symbolic systems.

## Key Characteristics
- **Explainability:** Leverages [[concepts/symbolic-logic|symbolic logic]] to provide transparent reasoning paths, unlike opaque neural [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]].
- **[[concepts/persistent-reasoning|Persistent Reasoning]]:** Maintains state and context over long horizons, enabling complex multi-step [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem solving]].
- **Hybrid Architecture:** Integrates LLM capabilities for perception and language with symbolic engines for [[concepts/verification|verification]] and [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]].

## Notable Implementations

### OmegaClaw
A prominent [[concepts/open-source-framework|open-source framework]] demonstrating these principles is **OmegaClaw**, developed by SingularityNET. It is designed to function as a persistent, explainable reasoning agent rather than a simple request-response model.

- **Core [[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]:** Built on symbolic logic to ensure [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], moving beyond pure LLM dependency.
- **Capabilities:** Focuses on persistent [[concepts/memory|memory]] and [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]] in [[concepts/agent-deployment|agent operations]].
