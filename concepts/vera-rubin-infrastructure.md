# Vera Rubin Infrastructure

**[[entities/vera-rubin-modules|Vera Rubin]] [[concepts/infrastructure|Infrastructure]]** refers to the next-generation [[concepts/computing-architecture|computing architecture]] unveiled by [[concepts/unsloth-optimization|NVIDIA]], designed to support the demands of **[[concepts/agentic-ai]]** and sovereign AI clusters. It represents a holistic shift from [[concepts/discrete-gpu|discrete GPU]] servers to fully integrated, liquid-cooled superchip systems.

## Core Components
- **Vera Rubin Superchip**: Integrated system combining multiple [[concepts/gpu-clusters|GPU clusters]] and networking components into a single unit.
- **Blackwell Ultra GPUs**: [[concepts/high-performance-ai|High-performance AI]] processing units forming the computational core.

## Sovereign AI Ecosystem Integration
The infrastructure supports diverse sovereign AI workloads, including [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] designed for [[concepts/data-sovereignty|data sovereignty]] and regional compliance.

- **Kolibri-1**: Aleph Alpha's sovereign open-weight [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]], optimized for German and English contexts.
  - Architecture: [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) [[concepts/reasoning-model|reasoning model]].
  - Scale: 78 billion [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]], with ~3.5 billion active per token.
