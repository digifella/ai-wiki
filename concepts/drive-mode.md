# Drive Mode

[[concepts/ambition|Drive]] Mode is a control-locking feature available on [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] designed to prevent accidental [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to critical [[concepts/camera-settings|camera settings]] during operation. When activated, the interface restricts access to specified parameters, allowing photographers to maintain consistent [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], focus, and other configurations without the risk of unintended changes. This functionality ensures that the camera remains in a predetermined state, which is essential for maintaining technical [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] throughout a shooting [[concepts/session|session]].

The feature is particularly valuable during fast-paced shooting situations where the camera might be jostled or bumped, potentially altering settings mid-session. By locking the controls, photographers can focus on [[concepts/writing|composition]] and timing without worrying about the camera's [[concepts/hidden-state|internal state]] shifting due to physical [[entities/contact|contact]]. This is especially useful in dynamic environments where rapid adjustments to the camera body are inevitable.

Practical applications of Drive Mode include [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] requiring strict adherence to specific technical parameters, such as studio work, sports photography, or any situation where consistent image quality is prioritized over immediate [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]]. It serves as a safeguard against user error, ensuring that the camera operates exactly as configured until the mode is explicitly deactivated.
