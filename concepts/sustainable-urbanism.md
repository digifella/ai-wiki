# Sustainable Urbanism

**Sustainable Urbanism** is an [[concepts/city-planning|urban planning]] paradigm focused on creating environmentally, economically, and socially sustainable cities. It emphasizes compact, walkable, mixed-use development, efficient public transportation, and green [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] to reduce ecological footprints while enhancing [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/density|Density]] & Mixed-Use:** Maximizing land efficiency through high-density, mixed-use developments to reduce sprawl.
- **Walkability & Transit:** Prioritizing pedestrian infrastructure and public transit over private vehicle dependency.
- **Ecological Integration:** Incorporating green spaces, [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]] corridors, and sustainable resource management.
- **Social Equity:** Ensuring accessible housing, [[entities/public-works|public services]], and community engagement.

## Emerging Paradigms: Mindful Development
Recent initiatives explore integrating [[concepts/mental-health|psychological well-being]] and spiritual values into urban design, moving beyond purely metric-based sustainability.

- **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]] (GMC):** [[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]] is developing a new city in the southern lowlands that explicitly addresses the modern existential-[[concepts/crisis|crisis]] through "[[concepts/mindful-development|mindful development]]."
  - Conceptualized as an "anti-Dubai," focusing on Gross National [[concepts/happiness|Happiness]] (GNH) rather than pure GDP.
  - Integrates [[concepts/mindfulness|mindfulness]] practices into urban planning, architecture, and [[concepts/governance|governance]].
  - Aims to balance economic growth with environmental [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] and [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]].
  - See: [[lab-notes/2026-08-07-Bhutans-Gelephu-Mindfulness-City-Addressing-Existential|Bhutan's Gelephu Mindfulness City: Addressing Existential Crisis with Mindful Development]]
