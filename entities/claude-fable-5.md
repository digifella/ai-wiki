# Claude Fable 5

**[[concepts/claude-fable-5|Claude Fable 5]]** is an advanced [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] released by [[entities/anthropic]], distinguished by its focus on organizational intelligence and complex strategic [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. The model's behavior is defined by a massive, leaked governing prompt (~120,000 characters), which has become a subject of intense analysis regarding AI alignment and operational strategy.

## Key Characteristics & Strategy

*   **Governing [[concepts/ai-prompt|Prompt Architecture]]**: The model utilizes a highly detailed [[concepts/system-prompt|system prompt]] that explicitly defines its behavioral boundaries and strategic objectives. *   **[[concepts/cost-optimization|Cost Optimization]]**: Strategies include tiered [[concepts/effort-levels|effort levels]], wargaming techniques, and multi-agent advisor/orchestrator patterns to preserve planning capabilities while reducing token expenditure.
