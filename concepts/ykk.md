YKK is a global manufacturer of fastening solutions, particularly known for its extensive production of [[concepts/buttons|zippers]]. The company's name is an acronym derived from [[entities/ykk|Yoshida Kogyo Kabushikigaisha]] (literally "Yoshida Industrial Co., [[entities/studio-dragon|Ltd.]]" in Japanese). Established in 1934 by Tadao Yoshida, YKK has grown to become one of the largest [[concepts/zipper-history|zipper]] manufacturers worldwide, offering a wide range of products including sliders, coils, and various types of interlocking [[concepts/fastening-devices|fasteners]].
