# Brainstorming

Brainstorming is a collaborative ideation technique designed to generate a large volume of [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] or solutions for a given problem or topic. In traditional settings, participants contribute thoughts freely with minimal initial judgment, creating an environment where creative [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] is encouraged. The method operates on the principle that quantity of ideas can increase the likelihood of discovering novel or effective solutions, as the variety of perspectives raises the chances of identifying viable approaches.

## AI-Assisted Brainstorming

AI assistants can support brainstorming processes by rapidly generating multiple candidate ideas, exploring different conceptual angles, and synthesizing varied perspectives on a topic. Unlike human participants who may experience [[concepts/cognitive-biases|cognitive biases]] or social constraints, AI systems can systematically explore [[concepts/solution|solution]] spaces and present options without fatigue or conformity pressure. This capability makes AI useful for expanding the initial idea pool before human evaluation and refinement.

## Practical Applications

In practice, AI brainstorming assistance works best when combined with human judgment. Users can prompt an AI system with a problem statement, desired constraints, or specific domains, and receive a [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/potential-solutions|potential solutions]] to evaluate and build upon. The AI can also reframe problems, suggest analogies from different fields, or iterate on promising directions identified by human participants, creating a collaborative ideation [[concepts/loop|loop]] rather than replacing human creativity.
