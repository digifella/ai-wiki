# Digit

Digit is a humanoid robot engineered for mobile manipulation tasks in industrial and logistics environments. The robot combines bipedal locomotion with articulated upper-body systems to perform complex handling operations in spaces designed for human workers. Its design enables navigation through varied warehouse environments while performing precise manipulation tasks, making it suitable for applications including package handling, sorting, and other logistics operations.

## Design and Capabilities

The robot integrates mobility with dexterous manipulation capabilities, featuring a bipedal stance that allows it to traverse standard doorways and navigate stairs. It is equipped with a torso and arms that provide a wide range of motion, enabling it to interact with objects at various heights and orientations. The system utilizes advanced sensors and control algorithms to maintain balance and stability while carrying loads, allowing it to operate safely alongside human colleagues in dynamic settings.

## Applications

Digit is primarily deployed in logistics and warehousing sectors to automate repetitive and physically demanding tasks. It is capable of picking, placing, and sorting packages, as well as restocking shelves and managing inventory. By performing these duties, the robot aims to reduce the physical strain on human workers and increase operational efficiency in high-volume distribution centers. Its versatility allows it to be integrated into existing workflows without requiring significant infrastructure modifications.
