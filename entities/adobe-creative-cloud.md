# Adobe Creative Cloud

**[[concepts/adobe|Adobe Creative Cloud]]** (often referred to as **CC** or **[[entities/adobe|Adobe]] CC**) is a collection of desktop, mobile, and online [[concepts/apps|apps]] developed by Adobe [[entities/amd|Inc.]] for fields including photography, design, video, web, and UX.

## Overview
The suite is primarily distributed via [[concepts/subscription-access|subscription model]], providing access to applications such as:
- [[concepts/photoshop]] (Raster [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]])
- Illustrator (Vector graphics)
- [[entities/premiere-pro]] ([[concepts/video-editing|Video editing]])
- After Effects ([[concepts/remotion-plugin|Motion graphics]])
- InDesign (Page layout)

## Ecosystem & Integration
- **Cloud [[entities/storage|Storage]]:** Integrated with [[entities/creative-cloud-app|Adobe Creative Cloud]] Files for asset synchronization.
- **Libraries:** Shared assets across applications via Adobe Creative Cloud Libraries.
- **Marketplace:** Access to third-party [[concepts/plugins|plugins]], fonts, and [[concepts/templates|templates]] via the Adobe Exchange.
