# Mobile Photography

Mobile photography refers to the practice of capturing [[concepts/images|images]] using smartphones and mobile devices. While smartphones have become increasingly capable in recent years, they have traditionally occupied a different niche than dedicated cameras like DSLRs and mirrorless systems. Dedicated cameras continue to excel in specialized areas such as video production, low-light performance, and dynamic range handling. However, smartphones have progressively closed the gap in overall image quality and have become the primary camera for many users due to their convenience and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

## Adobe Project Indigo

Adobe Project Indigo is a camera application developed to address the specific needs of advanced mobile photography. Introduced by [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]], founder of PhotoshopCAFE, the app represents an effort to provide professional-grade tools within a mobile environment. Rather than attempting to replicate desktop photography workflows, the app is designed with mobile-specific constraints and capabilities in mind, offering photographers a more sophisticated approach to image capture on their devices.
