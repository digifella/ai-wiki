# Reasoning

**Reasoning** refers to the cognitive process of drawing conclusions, making inferences, or forming judgments based on evidence, logic, and prior knowledge. In the context of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], it encompasses the ability of models to process complex inputs, such as visual data, and derive coherent, logical outputs.

## Multimodal Reasoning

Modern reasoning capabilities extend beyond text to include Vision-Language Models (VLMs) that process images alongside textual prompts. This integration allows for more nuanced understanding of context, spatial relationships, and visual semantics.

### Case Study: DeepSeek V4-Flash

Recent evaluations highlight significant advancements in multimodal reasoning through the [[concepts/deepseek-v4-flash|DeepSeek V4-Flash]] [[concepts/vision-model|Vision Model]] Evaluation: Capabilities, Limitations, and Reasoning. Key insights from this evaluation include:

- **Multimodal Processing**: The model demonstrates enhanced ability to process images alongside text, bridging the gap between visual perception and logical deduction.
- **Capability Expansion**: Highlights include improved accuracy in interpreting complex visual scenarios compared to previous iterations.
- **Limitations**: Despite advancements, specific constraints in reasoning depth and edge-case handling remain areas for development.

## Core Components

1. **Input Encoding**: Transformation of raw data (text, images, audio) into latent representations.
2. **Logical [[concepts/model-inference|Inference]]**: Application of rules or learned patterns to derive new information.
3. **Output Generation**: Formulation of coherent responses or decisions based on inferred conclusions.
