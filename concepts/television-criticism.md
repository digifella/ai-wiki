# Television Criticism

**Television Criticism** is the practice of analyzing, interpreting, and evaluating television [[concepts/software|programs]], series, and the [[entities/medium|medium]] itself. It encompasses [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] scholarship, journalistic review, and fan-based discourse, focusing on narrative structure, [[concepts/character-development|character development]], socio-political commentary, and production values.

## Key Areas of Analysis

- **Narrative & Structure**: Examination of episodic vs. serialized [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]], pacing, and plot coherence.
- **Character Studies**: Analysis of character arcs, archetypes, and audience identification.
- **Socio-Political Commentary**: How shows reflect, critique, or reinforce cultural norms, politics, and social issues.
- **Genre Conventions**: Study of specific genres (e.g., sitcom, drama, sci-fi) and their evolution.
