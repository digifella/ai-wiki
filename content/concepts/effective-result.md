# Effective Result

Effective Result is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] technique for [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|AI-assisted coding]] that takes its name from [[entities/ralph-wiggum|Ralph Wiggum]], a character from the animated television series *The Simpsons*. The technique was popularized through educational content created by [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]], a technology educator and YouTuber known for explaining programming concepts and software development practices.

## Core Approach

The technique leverages [[concepts/ai-coding-workflows|AI coding tools]] by adopting a simplified, sometimes naive approach to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]—mirroring Ralph's characteristic lack of sophistication. Rather than attempting to construct complex solutions upfront, developers using this method prompt AI assistants with straightforward, elementary descriptions of what they want to achieve. This counterintuitive strategy often results in functional code because the AI generates solutions based on clear, unambiguous requests, potentially reducing overthinking and technical overcomplexity.

## Practical Application

The Effective Result method is positioned as a practical approach within the broader landscape of [[concepts/development-speed|AI-assisted development]] workflows. It suggests that developers can achieve working solutions by maintaining clarity and simplicity in their interactions with [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]], even when their initial problem statements lack technical sophistication. This reflects a growing recognition that effective [[concepts/augmentation-of-human-capabilities|human-AI collaboration]] in software development sometimes benefits from directness over elaborate specification.
