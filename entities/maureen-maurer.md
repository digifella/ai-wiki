# Maureen Maurer

**Maureen Maurer** is a [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] and [[entities/tasia-custode|author]] specializing in [[concepts/healthcare-policy|health policy]] and patient engagement frameworks. She is a co-author of seminal work defining elements of patient and family engagement in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] systems.
