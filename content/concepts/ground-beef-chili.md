# Ground Beef Chili

Ground beef chili is a hearty dish that combines ground beef with various spices and vegetables to create a flavorful stew-like meal. It's popular for its simplicity and flexibility in ingredient combinations.

## Enhancing Ground Meat Baking Soda's Effect on Texture, Juiciness, and Browning
- [[entities/americas-test-kitchen|America's Test Kitchen]] presents the use of [[concepts/baking-soda|baking soda]] as an effective technique to improve [[concepts/meat-texture|ground meat texture]], juiciness, and browning.
- The video explains how baking soda interacts with proteins in ground beef to enhance moisture retention and color development during cooking.
- Recommended ratio: 1/4 teaspoon of baking soda per pound of ground beef.
