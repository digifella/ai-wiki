# Selective Adjustment

**Selective Adjustment** refers to the technique of modifying specific regions of an image while leaving others unchanged, allowing for precise control over [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, and detail. This is a core workflow in [[concepts/smartphone-camera-apps|digital Photography]] and Post-Processing.

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/disconnection|Isolation]]**: Targeting specific subjects, skies, [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], or highlights.
- **[[concepts/non-destructive-editing|Non-Destructive Editing]]**: Applying changes via masks or [[concepts/adjustment-layers|adjustment layers]] rather than altering [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]] directly.
- **[[concepts/image-contrast|Local Contrast]] & Color**: Enhancing depth and mood by adjusting parameters locally rather than globally.

## Tools & Techniques
- **Brush Tools**: Manual painting of masks for [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]].
- **[[concepts/gradient-tools|Gradient Filters]]**: Linear or radial [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] for skies and vignettes.
- **Range [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]]**: Selecting pixels based on luminance, color, or depth data.
- **AI-Powered Selection**: Automated subject, sky, or [[concepts/object-detection|object detection]] for rapid [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]].
