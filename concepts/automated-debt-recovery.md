# Automated debt recovery

The application of [[concepts/automations|automated systems]], [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], and software workflows to identify, notify, and collect outstanding debts from individuals or [[concepts/nodes|entities]].

## Critical Risks and Case Studies

- **Algorithmic [[concepts/accountability|accountability]]**: The systemic danger posed when automated [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes lack human oversight, [[concepts/verification|verification]], or [[concepts/bug-fixing|error-correction]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
- **[[concepts/robodebt-scheme]] (Australia)**
    - An implementation of Automated debt recovery introduced in 2016 by the Australian Government.
    - Utilized an unlawful [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]] to identify and recover alleged overpayments of [[concepts/welfare-benefits|welfare benefits]].
    - Resulted in catastrophic failures, significant humanitarian harm, and documented fatalities.
