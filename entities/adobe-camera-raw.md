# Adobe Camera Raw

**[[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR)** is a raw [[concepts/image-processing|image processing]] plugin and standalone application developed by [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]. It serves as the [[concepts/engine|engine]] behind the raw development capabilities in [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]]. ACR allows photographers to adjust [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], color balance, and detail in raw files before importing them into other [[concepts/adobe|Adobe]] applications.

## Key Features
- **Raw Processing:** [[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-destructive editing]] of raw image data.
- **[[concepts/gradient-tools|Gradient Filters]]:** Includes linear, radial, and Gradient Mask tools for [[concepts/masking-tools|localized adjustments]].
- **[[concepts/bidirectional-gradient|Bidirectional Gradient]]:** A newer feature allowing gradients to expand from both ends of a line simultaneously, offering more precise control over tonal transitions.
- **[[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-Powered Tools]]:** Includes [[concepts/select-subject-masking|Select Subject]], Select Sky, and other AI-driven masking capabilities.
- **Strategic [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]]:** [[concepts/advanced-tools|Advanced tools]] for evoking emotion and adding depth through intentional color choices.
