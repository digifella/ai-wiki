# Sky Detection

Sky detection is an automated masking technique employed in modern photo editing software to isolate sky regions within landscape and outdoor photographs. Primarily available in Adobe Lightroom and Adobe Camera Raw, the feature utilizes artificial intelligence to identify and select sky areas based on their tonal, color, and textural characteristics. This automation allows photographers to apply targeted adjustments to the sky without manually using brushes or lasso tools, ensuring that edits do not inadvertently affect the foreground or other elements of the composition.

The process relies on machine learning models trained on vast datasets of images to distinguish the sky from clouds, atmospheric haze, and terrestrial objects. When activated, the software generates a precise mask that adapts to complex boundaries, such as tree lines or architectural silhouettes. Users can refine this initial selection using adjustment sliders that control the mask's edge softness and feathering, ensuring seamless transitions between the edited sky and the rest of the image.

This functionality significantly streamlines the post-processing workflow by reducing the time required for precise selections. It enables non-destructive editing, allowing photographers to modify exposure, contrast, color temperature, and saturation specifically within the sky region. The technique is particularly valuable for correcting overexposed horizons, enhancing cloud detail, or replacing dull skies with more dynamic alternatives while maintaining natural-looking edges.
