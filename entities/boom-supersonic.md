# Boom Supersonic

**Boom Supersonic** is an aerospace manufacturer focused on developing commercial supersonic passenger aircraft. The company's flagship project is the [[concepts/overture-airliner]] airliner, designed to restore viable [[concepts/supersonic-travel|supersonic travel]] for the public.

## Key Developments

*   **Engine Technology**: The Overture relies on the **Symphony** engine, a collaborative development between Boom Supersonic and **GE Aerospace**. This engine is critical to achieving the aircraft's efficiency and supersonic capabilities.
*   **Recent Analysis**: Recent technical breakdowns highlight the viability of the Symphony engine design, marking a significant milestone in the project's engineering phase [[lab-notes/2026-09-05-Boom-Supersonic-Overture-Symphony-Engine-Development-and|Boom Supersonic Overture: Symphony Engine Development and Commercial Supersonic Flight Ambitions]].
*   **Commercial Ambitions**: The company aims to reduce transcontinental flight times significantly, targeting routes such as New York to London or Los Angeles to Las Vegas.
