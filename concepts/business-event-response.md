# Business event response

The [[concepts/swot|strategic framework]] for executing predefined actions and workflows in response to specific triggers within an organization's operational ecosystem.

## Automation Paradigms

- **[[concepts/time-based-automation|Time-based Automation]]**: Traditional automation functioning on fixed schedules (analogous to an alarm clock) that triggers at specific intervals regardless of environmental changes.
- **[[concepts/action-based-automation|Action-based Automation]]**: [[concepts/event-driven-architecture]] where workflows are triggered by specific occurrences or state changes within a system.

## Recent Developments

- **[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] Routines**: A significant evolution in [[concepts/ai-automation]] introduced by [[entities/anthropic]].
	- Marks a shift from time-based scheduling to action-based execution.
	- Enables more reactive and context-aware business event responses.
