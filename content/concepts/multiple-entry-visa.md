# Multiple Entry Visa

A multiple entry visa is a travel document that permits the holder to enter and exit a country on more than one occasion during the visa's validity period. Unlike single entry visas, which are exhausted after one crossing of the border, multiple entry visas allow repeated journeys without requiring a new visa application for each trip. This [[concepts/type-of-visa|type of visa]] is particularly useful for frequent travelers, business professionals, and individuals who plan multiple visits within a set timeframe.

## Turkey Multiple Entry Visa

[[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] offers multiple entry [[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-visa]] options for eligible travelers. The Turkish e-visa system provides digital visa [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] that can be obtained online without visiting an embassy or consulate. Multiple entry variants allow visitors to cross Turkey's borders several times during the visa's validity period, typically spanning several months or up to one year depending on the specific visa category and the traveler's nationality. This makes it convenient for those planning business trips, family visits, or tourism across multiple journeys to the country.

The multiple entry e-visa application process is streamlined and can be completed entirely through the official online portal. Applicants need to provide standard documentation including a valid passport, travel details, and supporting information as required. Once approved, the e-visa is sent digitally and does not require a physical stamp in the passport, though travelers must carry [[concepts/proof|proof]] of authorization when crossing Turkish borders.
