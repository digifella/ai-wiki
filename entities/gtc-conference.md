# GTC Conference

GTC Conference is an annual technology conference organized by NVIDIA that brings together industry professionals, researchers, and developers to discuss advances in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computing technologies. The event features keynote presentations, technical workshops, and panel discussions covering topics relevant to enterprise and research computing environments.

## Focus Areas

The conference has increasingly focused on AI agents and enterprise applications in recent years. Notable topics include NVIDIA's development of secure AI agent frameworks designed for enterprise use, including technologies for building and deploying autonomous systems in business-critical contexts. These discussions address practical implementation challenges, security considerations, and architectural approaches for integrating AI agents into existing enterprise infrastructure.

The conference serves as a venue for NVIDIA to showcase emerging technologies, gather feedback from the developer and enterprise communities, and provide technical education on GPU computing, AI model optimization, and large-scale system deployment. Attendees typically include software engineers, data scientists, research institutions, and technology leaders seeking to understand the latest developments in accelerated computing and artificial intelligence.
