# Anti Gravity Ai

Anti Gravity Ai is an artificial intelligence agent system designed with a primary emphasis on user control and data ownership. The platform prioritizes transparency regarding how information is handled and stored, ensuring that users maintain direct access to the data generated or processed through the agent's operations. This approach distinguishes the system by focusing on the preservation of user rights over their digital assets rather than treating data as a proprietary asset of the service provider.

The architecture of Anti Gravity Ai integrates two core technical capabilities: data export and GitHub synchronization. The data export functionality allows users to retrieve their information in portable formats, facilitating easy migration or backup of their work. Concurrently, GitHub synchronization enables version control and collaborative management of agent-related workflows, allowing users to leverage standard development tools for tracking changes and managing the evolution of their AI configurations.
