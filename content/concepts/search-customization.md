# Search Customization

Search Customization refers to the process of tailoring search [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and settings within AI-powered search engines to better suit individual needs and preferences. In the context of [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity AI]], [[concepts/customization|customization]] allows users to refine how the search engine retrieves, processes, and presents information, making it a more effective tool for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Perplexity AI Customization

Perplexity AI is an AI-powered search engine that enables users to customize their search experience through various configuration options. According to a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] by [[entities/enovair|Enovair]], Perplexity serves as a highly versatile search tool that many users adopt as their primary [[concepts/ai-search|AI search]] [[concepts/solution|solution]]. The customization features allow users to adjust how the engine searches and presents results, enabling more targeted and relevant [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]].

## Practical Application

Implementing search customization in Perplexity involves adjusting settings and parameters to align with specific research goals, information needs, or workflows. This approach transforms the search engine from a generic tool into one tailored to individual or organizational requirements, improving both efficiency and result quality.
