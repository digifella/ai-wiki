# Coldfusion

Coldfusion is a [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] entity that produces analysis and commentary on developments in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], with particular focus on [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] and their implications for technology strategy and [[concepts/security|security]]. The entity's work examines the technical [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], vulnerabilities, and market dynamics surrounding increasingly autonomous systems.

## Autonomous AI Agents and Security

A central theme in Coldfusion's coverage is the rise of autonomous AI agents and the security vulnerabilities that accompany them. This includes examination of systems designed to operate with minimal human oversight and the potential risks they introduce across various domains.

## Economic and Strategic Implications

Coldfusion also tracks how [[concepts/compute-costs|compute costs]] [[concepts/power|influence]] strategic decisions within major AI organizations. This includes analysis of how infrastructure expenses reshape product development priorities and market positioning, as evidenced in coverage of major companies' pivots in their [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] strategies.
