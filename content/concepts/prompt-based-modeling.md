# Prompt Based Modeling

Prompt based modeling is a technique for designing and structuring prompts to guide AI language models in completing specific tasks or automating workflows. Rather than relying on fixed outputs or traditional programming logic, this approach leverages natural language [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to shape [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]], allowing practitioners to adapt system [[concepts/responses|responses]] without modifying underlying code. The effectiveness of prompt based modeling depends on clarity of instruction, appropriate context provision, and [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] based on model outputs.

## Core Applications

Prompt based modeling is commonly applied in [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] design, where [[concepts/agents|agents]] need to decompose complex problems, make decisions, and coordinate multiple steps. It enables [[concepts/automation|automation]] of workflows that would otherwise require manual intervention or custom development. [[concepts/scenarios|Use cases]] range from customer service automation and content generation to data analysis and process orchestration.

## Practical Considerations

The cost and efficiency of prompt based modeling implementations vary depending on the underlying model and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] context. Factors such as prompt length, model complexity, and API call frequency directly impact operational expenses. As with other AI-driven approaches, designing effective prompts requires understanding both the model's capabilities and the specific requirements of the task at hand, often benefiting from iterative [[concepts/testing|testing]] and refinement cycles.
