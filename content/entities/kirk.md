# Kirk

Kirk is a YouTube content creator who produces educational videos on cloud technologies and [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] tools. His channel focuses on practical demonstrations and tutorials for developers working with enterprise platforms.

## Azure AI and RAG Content

Kirk has created content exploring Azure AI capabilities in the context of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) workflows. In a video published in April 2026, he demonstrated how to implement [[concepts/contextualized-language-understanding|RAG systems]] using Azure AI in conjunction with [[concepts/rich-tooling|Foundry]], a data and AI platform. This video serves as a resource for developers seeking to understand the integration of these technologies in practical [[concepts/software|applications]].
