# Scenarios

Scenarios are structured descriptions of situations, processes, or [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]] that illustrate how concepts, tools, or techniques are applied in practice.

## Key Aspects

- **Goal-Oriented**: Scenarios typically focus on achieving specific objectives or solving particular problems.
- **Contextual**: They provide context by detailing the environment, constraints, and participants involved.
- **Process-Driven**: Scenarios often outline the steps, methods, or workflows used to accomplish the goal.
- **Results-Focused**: They highlight the outcomes, insights, or lessons learned from the scenario.
