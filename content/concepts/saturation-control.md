# Saturation Control

Saturation control refers to the ability to adjust the intensity and vividness of colors in a digital image. In [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]], this functionality is primarily managed through the [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]], which allows users to modify how saturated or muted colors appear across an image or within specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]].

## Color and Vibrance Tool

The Color and Vibrance tool in Photoshop 2025 provides a dedicated interface for saturation [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. This tool enables photographers and designers to enhance or reduce [[concepts/color-saturation|color intensity]] with precision, distinguishing between overall saturation changes and more nuanced vibrance adjustments that prioritize already-vibrant colors while protecting [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] and neutral areas.

## Practical Applications

Saturation control is essential for color correction, creative enhancement, and stylistic choices in image editing. Whether recovering detail in oversaturated photographs, adding punch to flat [[concepts/images|images]], or achieving specific aesthetic goals, effective saturation management contributes significantly to the final visual quality of edited work.
