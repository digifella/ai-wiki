# Opal Labs

[[entities/opal|Opal]] [[entities/labs|Labs]] is an experimental [[concepts/workflow-creation|no-code AI tool]] introduced by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Labs that enables users to create and share [[concepts/ai-mini-applications|AI mini-applications]] through [[concepts/natural-language-descriptions|natural language descriptions]]. The platform is designed to lower the barrier to entry for [[concepts/ai-application-development|AI application development]] by allowing users to build functional [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] without requiring traditional programming knowledge.

The tool reflects a broader trend in [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] toward [[concepts/democratization-of-software-creation|democratization of software creation]], making AI [[concepts/app-creation|application development]] more accessible to non-technical users. By combining natural language interfaces with [[concepts/no-code|no-code]] development principles, Opal Labs aims to streamline the process of conceptualizing, building, and distributing small-scale [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]].
