# Electronic Components

Electronic components are discrete physical devices used in electrical circuits to control, direct, and modify the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of electric current. These fundamental building blocks serve essential functions such as [[concepts/storing|storing]] energy, restricting current flow, amplifying signals, or switching circuits on and off. Common examples include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors. Every modern electronic device, from simple LED flashlights to complex computers, relies on networks of these components working together.

## Passive Components

Passive components do not require an external power source to operate and cannot amplify signals. Resistors limit current flow and dissipate energy as heat. Capacitors store electrical energy in an electric field and are used for filtering and timing applications. Inductors store energy in a magnetic field and are commonly found in power supplies and radio frequency circuits. These components form the foundation of most electronic designs.

## Active Components

Active components require external power to function and can amplify or switch signals. Transistors are semiconductor devices that act as electronic switches or amplifiers and are the primary building blocks of modern integrated circuits. Diodes allow current to flow in one direction and are essential for rectification and signal detection. Integrated circuits (ICs) combine thousands or millions of transistors on a single chip to perform complex functions.

## Open-Source Development

The [[entities/nano-banana|Nano-banana]] project represents efforts within the electronics community to develop and document [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] electronic components and designs, promoting [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and collaborative [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in electronics research and development.
