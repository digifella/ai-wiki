# Qwen 3.6-Plus

A [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/alibaba]], designed to advance the transition from static LLMs to [[concepts/agentic-ai|Real-World Agents]].

### Core Capabilities
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai]]**: Enhanced autonomous capabilities for complex programming tasks.
- **[[concepts/multimodal-ai]]**: Advanced [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] across diverse input modalities.

### Key Updates
- Represents a significant architectural upgrade over previous Qwen iterations.
- Specifically engineered to facilitate the development of functional, [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous agents]].
- **Performance Claims**: Benchmarks indicate it outperforms [[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus]] 4.5 and [[entities/gemini-3]].
- **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]**: Focused on increasing reliability and frontier-level [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic capabilities]].
