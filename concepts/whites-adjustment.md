# Whites Adjustment

The Whites Adjustment is a tonal control slider in Adobe Lightroom Classic's Develop module designed to selectively brighten or darken the lighter midtones and near-white areas of a photograph. Located in the Basic panel alongside related tools such as Exposure and Highlights, it operates independently of shadow adjustments. This separation allows photographers to fine-tune the brightness of highlights without altering the overall contrast or affecting the darker tones of an image.

The slider functions by targeting a specific tonal range, primarily focusing on brighter midtones and near-white pixels. This selective targeting enables precise adjustments to the upper end of the tonal spectrum, helping to define the brightest parts of a scene. By isolating these specific values, the tool helps prevent clipping in the highlights while allowing for greater control over the image's luminance distribution.

In the context of landscape photography, this feature is frequently utilized to enhance the clarity and impact of bright elements such as skies, clouds, or reflective surfaces. Demonstrated in tutorials by photography educator Anthony Morganti, the Whites Adjustment serves as a critical component in the post-processing workflow for landscape images. It allows for the refinement of tonal balance, ensuring that bright areas retain detail and contribute effectively to the overall composition without overwhelming the viewer.
