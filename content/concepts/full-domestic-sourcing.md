# Full domestic sourcing

Sourcing all raw materials, components, and manufacturing processes within a single country, typically the country of final sale, to eliminate foreign dependencies and support local economies.

**Examples**:
- Destin - manufacturing in [[entities/america|America]]: 4-year experiment by [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] ([[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]]) to manufacture the "[[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]" (safer grill brush) entirely in the US, motivated by parents' union auto work background, aiming for [[concepts/market-competitiveness|market competitiveness]].
