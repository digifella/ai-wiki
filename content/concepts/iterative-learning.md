# Iterative Learning

Iterative learning is a method of enhancing performance through repeated cycles of practice, analysis, and adjustment. This process often involves setting specific goals and using [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to refine strategies or processes.

## Key Concepts
- **Feedback Loop:** A continuous process where outcomes are analyzed, and [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are made based on that analysis.
- **Incremental Improvement:** Small changes made in each [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] aimed at achieving cumulative improvement over time.
- **Data-Driven [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]:** Utilizing data to inform decisions and guide the [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative process]].

## Applications
- **[[concepts/coding|Software Development]]:** Iterative learning can be applied to software development methodologies such as Agile, where iterations are used to deliver functional pieces of a product incrementally.
- **AI & [[concepts/machine-learning|Machine Learning]]:** [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] use iterative processes like [[concepts/training|training]] cycles to improve their performance over time by adjusting [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and algorithms based on data feedback.

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/software-algorithms|Auto-research]]** is an emerging methodology in the field of software [[concepts/ai-workflow|optimization]] that leverages AI-driven approaches for optimizing algorithms using iterative learning and [[concepts/defined-metrics|defined metrics]]. Key points include:
  - Demonstrated through a restaurant [[concepts/inventory-simulation|inventory simulation]], where an initial naive algorithm led to over 50% failed orders.
  - The application of Auto-research improved [[concepts/inventory-management|inventory management]] significantly by [[concepts/learning|learning]] from past mistakes and adapting strategies.
