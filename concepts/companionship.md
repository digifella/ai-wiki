# Companionship

**Companionship** refers to the state of being accompanied by or associated with others, providing emotional support, [[concepts/social-interaction|social interaction]], and a sense of belonging. It is a fundamental human need that contributes to [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]], [[concepts/well-being|well-being]], and longevity.

## Key Dimensions
- **[[concepts/human-connection|Social Connection]]:** The presence of others reduces feelings of [[concepts/disconnection|Isolation]] and [[concepts/loneliness]].
- **Emotional Support:** Shared experiences and empathy buffer against [[concepts/stress|stress]] and [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]].
- **Reciprocity:** Healthy companionship involves mutual exchange of care and [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]].

## Technological Interfaces: Robots as Companions
The integration of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] into caregiving contexts has raised questions about the authenticity of Human-Robot Interaction.
- **Target Demographic:** Older Adults and individuals with limited mobility or cognitive decline.
- **Purpose:** To mitigate social isolation and provide routine interaction.
- **[[concepts/ethical-considerations|Ethical Considerations]]:** Concerns regarding deception, dependency, and the replacement of human [[entities/contact|contact]].
