**[[entities/qwen3-coder|Qwen3-Coder]]**

[[concepts/terminal-coders|Qwen3-Coder]] is a specialized [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] model designed for [[concepts/ai-coding|coding tasks]]. It aims to provide a cost-effective alternative to proprietary [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud-based solutions]] like [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/claude|Claude]], and [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/models|models]].

### Key Features
- **[[concepts/coding|Coding]] [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**: [[concepts/terminal-coding-agents|Qwen3-Coder]] excels in handling various programming tasks efficiently.
- **[[concepts/local-llm|Local Processing]]**: Utilizes local resources for processing, ensuring [[concepts/ai-security|data privacy]] and reducing costs associated with [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]].
- **[[concepts/customization|Customizability]]**: Users can tailor the model to their specific coding environments and preferences.

### Development History
- Initial tests of a general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] [[concepts/offline-ai|local AI]] model ([[entities/qwen|Qwen]] 3.5) by [[concepts/developer|developer]] Nick showed promising results in coding capabilities but lacked [[concepts/specialization|specialization]] for programming tasks.
- The development team worked on refining Qwen3-Coder, enhancing its performance specifically for coding-related queries and tasks.
