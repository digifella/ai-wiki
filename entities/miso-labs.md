# Miso Labs

Miso [[entities/labs|Labs]] is an [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] and development organization focused on advanced [[concepts/unified-multimodal-models|multimodal models]], particularly in the domain of [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] synthesis and [[concepts/text-to-speech-model|Text-to-Speech]]. The organization is recognized for releasing high-fidelity, [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] or accessible proprietary models that prioritize emotional nuance and realistic prosody.

## Key Developments
