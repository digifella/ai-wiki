# Pathway platform

**[[entities/pathway|Pathway]]** is a platform designed to advance AI capabilities beyond the limitations of current Large Language Models (LLMs). It focuses on architectural innovations to handle complex, real-time data processing and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks more effectively than traditional static models.

## Key Developments

- **[[concepts/bdh-architecture|BDH Architecture]]**: Pathway is implementing a new architecture referred to as BDH, aimed at overcoming specific bottlenecks in current AI systems [[lab-notes/2026-08-19-Pathways-BDH-Architecture-Advancing-AI-Beyond-Current-LL|Pathway's BDH Architecture: Advancing AI Beyond Current LLM Limitations]].
- **Leadership Insight**: [[entities/zuzanna-stamirowska|Zuzanna Stamirowska]], CEO and co-founder of Pathway, has discussed the future of AI and the necessity of moving beyond LLM-centric approaches in recent analyses [[Pathway's BDH Architecture: Advancing AI Beyond Current LLM Limitations](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fjB6sEPC4CE)].
- **Strategic Focus**: The platform emphasizes dynamic data handling and real-time [[concepts/model-inference|inference]], positioning itself as a critical [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] layer for next-generation AI applications.
