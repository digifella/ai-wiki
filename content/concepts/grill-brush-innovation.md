# Grill brush innovation

Key advancements addressing safety risks (bristle shedding) and durability:

- **[[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]**: Developed by [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] ([[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]]) as part of a [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] to manufacture entirely in [[entities/america|America]]. Uses non-wire bristles to replace hazardous wire bristle brushes. Destin - manufacturing in America
  - Video documentation: [Smarter Every Day: Destin - manufacturing in America](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ZTGwcHQfLY)
  - [[concepts/motivation|Motivation]]: Sandlin's parents were [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]], driving interest in [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]] viability.

- **Non-wire materials**: Nylon, ceramic, and stainless steel bristles eliminate shedding risks compared to traditional wire brushes.

- **Modular [[concepts/design|design]]**: Replaceable heads extend [[concepts/product-durability|product lifespan]] and reduce waste.
