# General Purpose Ai Agent

A [[concepts/chatgpt-agent|General Purpose AI Agent]] is a feature within [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] designed to automate complex, [[concepts/multi-step-tasks|multi-step tasks]] by combining [[concepts/browser-control|browser control]] capabilities with [[concepts/deep-research-function|deep research]] functions. Unlike traditional chatbots that respond to individual queries, these agents execute sequences of actions—such as navigating websites, gathering information, and analyzing data—to complete comprehensive tasks with minimal human intervention.

## Capabilities

General Purpose [[concepts/ai-agents|AI Agents]] operate by breaking down larger objectives into actionable steps. They can navigate web interfaces, extract information from various sources, and synthesize findings to provide detailed answers or complete specific workflows. This process allows the agent to handle tasks that require iterative searching and data aggregation, which are typically beyond the scope of standard conversational models.

## Operational Context

The [[concepts/ai-agent-skills|agent functions]] primarily within the ChatGPT environment, leveraging integrated tools to interact with the live web. By automating the navigation and data collection phases, it reduces the manual effort required for research-intensive projects. The system aims to deliver finished results rather than just intermediate links, effectively [[concepts/acting|acting]] as an autonomous assistant for complex [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] and processing.
