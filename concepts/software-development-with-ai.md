## Overview
The integration of AI into Software [[concepts/development-workflows|Development workflows]] involves leveraging [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]], [[concepts/edge-deployment|local inference]] models, and [[concepts/algorithm-optimization|optimization techniques]] to enhance [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and reduce latency. ## Hermes Agent
The [[entities/hermes-agent]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], [[concepts/self-evolution|self-improving AI]] agent designed for local application and optimization. It serves as a foundational tool for developers seeking to implement [[concepts/autonomous-coding|autonomous coding]] assistants without relying solely on cloud-based [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/self-improvement|Self-Improvement]]**: The agent is designed to iteratively refine its own performance and [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] capabilities.
- **Local Application**: Emphasizes running [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] locally to ensure [[concepts/privacy|data privacy]] and reduce dependency on external services.
- **Optimization**: Focuses on setup and tuning for maximum efficiency in [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]].
