# mRNA cancer vaccine

An emerging class of immunotherapeutics designed to stimulate the immune system to recognize and attack cancer cells by delivering mRNA encoding tumor-associated antigens.

## Key Developments

- **[[entities/moderna|Moderna]] & [[entities/merck|Merck]] Collaboration**: Positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results announced for a personalized mRNA cancer vaccine targeting [[concepts/resected-melanoma|resected melanoma]].
  - The vaccine candidate is known as **Intismeran** (also referred to as **Autogene**).
  - It is administered as **[[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]]** in combination with **Keytruda** ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]).
  - Moderna Merck mRNA Cancer Vaccine and Keytruda for Resected Melanoma Adjuvant Therapy
- **Significance**: Represents a major advancement in personalized cancer treatment, leveraging the speed of mRNA technology for patient-specific neoantigen targeting.
