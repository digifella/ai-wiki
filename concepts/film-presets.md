# Film Presets

Film Presets are a collection of [[entities/camera-raw-filter|camera raw]] processing presets introduced in [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] 18.3 and [[entities/lightroom-classic-v15|Lightroom Classic 15]].3 that emulate the visual characteristics of traditional film stocks. These presets allow photographers to apply film-inspired color grading and [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|tonal adjustments]] to [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]] by simulating the [[concepts/presence|appearance]] of various analog film types. The presets operate within Adobe's raw processing engine, enabling adjustment and [[concepts/customization|customization]] beyond their default application.

## Function and Application

The presets work by applying predetermined combinations of color profiles, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] curves, and grain structures to mimic specific film emulsions. Users can select from a variety of simulated film stocks, each offering distinct aesthetic qualities such as saturation levels, highlight roll-off, and [[concepts/shadows|shadow detail]]. Because these presets are applied within the raw processing pipeline, they remain non-destructive and can be fine-tuned using standard [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] and color tools after application.

## Integration with Other Features

The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of Film Presets coincides with broader [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to Adobe's editing ecosystem, including enhancements to [[concepts/depth-range-masking|depth range masking]] and [[concepts/lens-correction|lens correction]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]]. These improvements allow for more precise [[concepts/masking-tools|localized adjustments]] when using [[concepts/film-simulations|film simulations]], ensuring that the aesthetic effect integrates seamlessly with technical corrections. Additionally, the update leverages Firefly [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] and optimized background processing to maintain performance stability while applying complex [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]] tasks.
