# Gemini 30 Pro

[[entities/gemini-30-pro|Gemini 30 Pro]] is an [[concepts/ai-design-agent|AI design agent]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] that automates the creation of digital designs from multiple [[concepts/pointing-mechanisms|input modalities]]. The system accepts both textual descriptions and hand-drawn sketches as input, processing them through [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to generate design outputs. This dual-input approach allows users to specify design intent either through [[concepts/human-readable-instructions|natural language instructions]] or preliminary visual sketches, accommodating different communication preferences in the [[concepts/visual-strategy-development|design workflow]].

## Functionality

The agent processes user inputs—whether written specifications or visual rough drafts—through integrated machine learning architectures to produce coherent design artifacts. By interpreting semantic meaning from text and spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] from sketches, the system bridges the gap between abstract intent and concrete [[concepts/visual-representation|visual representation]]. This capability enables designers to iterate rapidly, refining concepts by switching between verbal and visual input methods without losing contextual [[concepts/continuity|continuity]].

## Integration and Workflow

Designed to fit into existing digital design pipelines, Gemini 30 Pro facilitates a more flexible creative process. Users can begin with a rough [[concepts/sketch|sketch]] to establish layout and [[concepts/writing|composition]], then use text to define specific attributes, colors, or structural details. Conversely, a detailed textual prompt can be supplemented with sketches to clarify ambiguous elements. This flexibility supports a non-linear design workflow, allowing professionals to explore variations and refine outputs based on their preferred mode of expression.
