# ASR Models

Architectures for [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition]] converting acoustic signals to [[concepts/text|text]] sequences. Ranges from hybrid HMM-DNN systems to end-to-end Transformer and Conformer networks. Includes streaming, non-streaming, and multimodal variants integrating [[concepts/computer-vision]] or [[concepts/large-language-model]] context.

## Notable Models & Updates

- **[[entities/ibm|IBM]] [[entities/granite|Granite]] Speech 4.1**: Open ASR model within the Granite 4.1 family spanning language, vision, speech, and embeddings; emphasized for [[concepts/speed|inference speed]] and enterprise applicability.
- **Analysis Reference**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-08-IBM-Granite-Speech-4.1-ASR-Models-Features-Accuracy-and|IBM Granite Speech 4.1 ASR Models: Features, Accuracy, and Enterprise Applications]] covers features, [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] benchmarks, and speed evaluation ([[entities/sam-witteveen|Sam Witteveen]], 2026-05-08).
- **Key [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**: [[concepts/open-weight|Open-weight]] availability; optimized for low-latency transcription; part of broader multimodal foundation suite.
