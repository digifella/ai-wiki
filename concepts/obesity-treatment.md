# Obesity Treatment

**Obesity** is a chronic multifactorial disease characterized by excessive adipose tissue accumulation. Treatment paradigms have shifted from behavioral modification alone to a combination of lifestyle, pharmacological, and surgical interventions.

## Overview
- **Multimodal Approach**: Effective management requires addressing caloric balance, metabolic health, and psychological factors.
- **Criteria for Intervention**: Typically initiated for BMI ≥30 or BMI ≥27 with comorbidities (e.g., Type 2 Diabetes, hypertension).

## Pharmacological Interventions
Recent advancements focus on incretin mimetics that enhance [[concepts/satiety|satiety]] and regulate glucose [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|metabolism]].

- **[[concepts/glp-1-agonists|GLP-1 Receptor Agonists]]**:
    - Drugs like **Ozempic** ([[entities/ozempic|semaglutide]]) and **Mounjaro** (tirzepatide) are revolutionizing treatment by targeting glucagon-like peptide-1 receptors.
    - **Mechanism**: Slows gastric emptying, increases insulin secretion, and suppresses appetite via central nervous system signaling.
    - **Impact**: Considered a potential medical [[concepts/revolution|revolution]] for achieving significant and sustained [[concepts/weight-loss|weight loss]].
    - **Challenges**: Issues regarding long-term sustainability, side effects (nausea, GI distress), and [[concepts/health|healthcare]] [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] are prevalent. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-20-Ozempic-GLP-1-Agonists-Revolutionizing-Obesity-Treatment|Ozempic & GLP-1 Agonists: Revolutionizing Obesity Treatment and Challenges]] for detailed analysis of these challenges and the "uncomfortable truths" surrounding widespread [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].

## Other Modalities
- **Surgical Options**: Bariatric surgery (e.g., gastric bypass) remains the most effective option for severe obesity but carries higher risks.
- **Lifestyle Modification**: Dietary changes and [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]] remain foundational but often insufficient as standalone treatments for severe cases.
