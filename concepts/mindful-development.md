# Mindful Development

**Mindful Development** is an urban and economic planning paradigm that prioritizes [[concepts/mental-health|psychological well-being]], spiritual [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]], and [[concepts/ecological-balance|ecological balance]] over purely metric-driven growth. It seeks to address modern existential-[[concepts/crisis|crisis]] by integrating [[concepts/gross-national-happiness]] principles into [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and policy.

## Core Principles
- Prioritizes mental health and spiritual [[concepts/happiness|fulfillment]] alongside economic stability.
- Integrates traditional cultural values with modern sustainable technology.
- Views urban spaces as environments for contemplation and community [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] rather than mere consumption hubs.

## Case Study: Gelephu Mindfulness City
[[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]] is pioneering this concept through the development of the **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]]**, a project designed to counter the hyper-commercialized model of cities like Dubai.

- **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]:** To create a "Anti-Dubai" that addresses the global existential-crisis through [[concepts/sustainable-urbanism|mindful urbanism]].
- **[[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]:** Rooted in Bhutan's national philosophy of [[concepts/gross-national-happiness]] and its spiritual heritage as the "Land of the Thunder Dragon."
- **Integration:** Combines high-[[concepts/computing-infrastructure|tech infrastructure]] with deep [[concepts/mindfulness|mindfulness]] practices to foster holistic well-being.
