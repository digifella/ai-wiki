# Naval Operations

Coordinated military activities executed by naval forces across maritime domains, integrating strategy, logistics, infrastructure, and force projection.

## Strategic Infrastructure & Bases
Naval operations rely on a network of ports and forward operating [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] to sustain fleet readiness and regional [[concepts/power|control]].

- **Forward Operating Bases:** Critical [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] for resupply, repair, and power projection.
  - [[concepts/guantanamo-bay]]: Primary US naval station in the Western Hemisphere.
    - Established via 1903 treaty granting perpetual lease rights; Cuban sovereignty claims remain contested.
    - Serves as a major logistics hub for Caribbean and Atlantic operations.
    - Hosts the Guantanamo Bay Detention Camp, creating intersection between naval [[concepts/security|security]] and International Human Rights Law.
    - Ongoing debates regarding closure, legal status, and diplomatic [[concepts/friction|friction]].
    - Detailed acquisition and controversy analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-Guantanamo-Bay-US-Acquisition-Military-Base-and-Detentio|Guantanamo Bay: US Acquisition, Military Base, and Detention Center Controversies]]

## Operational Domains
- **Amphibious Warfare:** [[concepts/integration|Integration]] of naval firepower with ground force [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
- **Maritime Logistics:** Sustainment of naval assets through underway replenishment and base support.
- **Maritime Domain Awareness:** Surveillance and control of sea lanes.
