# Russia

## Overview
Transcontinental state spanning Eastern [[entities/europe|Europe]] and Northern Asia. Permanent UN [[concepts/security|Security]] Council member, major energy exporter, and nuclear-armed [[concepts/power|power]]. [[concepts/governance|Governance]] under Vladimir Putin prioritizes strategic autonomy, centralized military-industrial mobilization, and economic realignment toward BRICS and Global South partnerships.

## Conflict & Military Posture
- Launched full-scale invasion of [[entities/ukraine]] in February 2022; [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] devolved into protracted attrition warfare across Donbas, Kharkiv, and southern operational corridors.
- Force [[concepts/structure|structure]] adapted to high-intensity drone/artillery exchanges, layered minefields, and rotational conscription cycles to sustain manpower losses.
- Wartime economy restructured for sanctions [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], utilizing shadow shipping networks, alternative clearing systems, and defense-sector production caps.

## 2026 Battlefield & Strategic Assessments
- May 2026 operational reviews document measurable degradation in Russian troop readiness and materiel reserves, visibly reflected in the downscaled 2026 Moscow Victory Day Parade.
- Frontline narratives remain highly volatile, with analytical consensus oscillating between imminent collapse [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] and stabilized trench warfare as tactical [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] continue.
- Detailed breakdown of force allocation, logistical strain, and parade symbolism: [[lab-notes/2026-05-18-2026-Ukraine-Battlefield-Russias-Military-Strain-Evident|2026 Ukraine Battlefield: Russia's Military Strain Evident in Downscaled Parade]]
- Continued dependency on foreign munitions [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]] (primarily North Korea and [[entities/iran]]) to offset [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic production]] bottlenecks and attrition rates.
