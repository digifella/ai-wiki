# Gmail

A core [[entities/email|email]] service provided by [[entities/google]] as a fundamental component of [[entities/google-workspace]].

## AI Integration
- **[[entities/gemini]] [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**:
    - Features [[concepts/native-integration|native integration]] within the [[entities/google-workspace]] ecosystem.
    - Utilizes `@` mentions to interface directly with [[entities/google-workspace]] data.
    - Leverages [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|advanced reasoning]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for end-to-end functionality.
- **[[concepts/openclaw|Clawdbot (OpenClaw)]] Integration**:
    - Connects to Gmail to allow the [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] to execute [[concepts/real-world-tasks|real-world tasks]].
- **[[concepts/no-code|No-Code]] [[concepts/automation|Automation]]**:
    - Utilizes [[entities/notebooklm]] and [[entities/gemini]] to create safe, [[concepts/no-code-ai-agents|no-code AI agents]] that draft automated email [[concepts/responses|responses]] based on specific business rules.
