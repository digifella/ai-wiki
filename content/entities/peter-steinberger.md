# Peter Steinberger
Peter Steinberger is a [[concepts/developer|developer]] known for his contributions to [[concepts/open-source|open-source projects]] in the realm of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/automation|automation]].

## OpenClaw Project
[[entities/openclaw|OpenClaw]], an [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI]] agent developed by Peter Steinberger, is designed to operate continuously on personal computers or [[concepts/virtual-private-servers|Virtual Private Servers]] ([[concepts/vps|VPS]]). It is written in [[concepts/typescript|TypeScript]] and officially supported as an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] project by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]. The primary goal of [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] is to automate tasks such as task management and project tracking.

### Features
- **[[concepts/247-operation|24/7 Operation]]**: Capable of [[concepts/running|running]] non-stop, ensuring [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]] and execution.
- **[[concepts/automation|Task Automation]]**: Automates various tasks to improve efficiency and productivity.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open Source]]**: Developed in [[concepts/typescript|TypeScript]] and openly available for modification and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].
