# Adobe Acrobat

## Overview
A professional [[concepts/software|software]] suite used for viewing, creating, and managing PDF documents and digital [[concepts/workflow|workflows]].

## AI & Visual Content Workflow
- Utilized for directly editing text within AI [[concepts/infographic-design|Infographics]] and [[concepts/images|images]] to correct spelling mistakes or inaccuracies resulting from [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI]] tools like [[entities/gemini]].
- Functions as a primary alternative to [[entities/canva]]'s 'Grab Text' feature for extracting and modifying text within visual assets.
