Their long-standing corporate culture of rapid iteration, aggressive market penetration, and efficient supply chain management, honed through years in hardware, provides a unique operational advantage. Coupled with strategic talent acquisition and a focus on innovative, efficient AI architectures and training methodologies (such as their hybrid attention and multi-teacher distillation), Xiaomi MiMo has delivered high-performing models at significantly lower costs. The commitment to open-sourcing aspects of their research, including the MiMo-V2.5 series under an MIT license, further positions them as a formidable and disruptive force in the global AI landscape.
