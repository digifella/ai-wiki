# Victor Hess

Victor Franz Hess was an Austrian physicist who discovered that [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] originate from outside of Earth's atmosphere in 1912. His work earned him the Nobel Prize in [[concepts/physics|Physics]] in 1936.

## Key Contributions
- Discovery of cosmic radiation, also known as cosmic rays.
- Proved that these high-energy particles emanate from space and not atmospheric interactions.
