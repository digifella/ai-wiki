# Majorana particle

A theoretical particle that functions as its own antiparticle. In condensed matter [[concepts/physics|physics]], Majorana fermions emerge as quasiparticles within specific topological phases of matter.

### Quantum Computing Applications
The exploitation of Majorana [[concepts/zero|zero]] modes is a cornerstone of Topological [[entities/quantum-computing|Quantum Computing]], aimed at creating [[concepts/qubits|qubits]] that are natively protected from decoherence.

* **Scalability Roadmap:** [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] is actively pursuing a path toward Million Qubit Topological [[entities/quantum-computing|Quantum Computers]] to enable the modeling of complex natural laws.
* **[[entities/majorana-particle|Majorana 1]]:** A specific development milestone/approach focused on achieving large-scale, fault-tolerant quantum architectures.
