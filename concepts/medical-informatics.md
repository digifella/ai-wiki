# Medical Informatics

Medical Informatics is the interdisciplinary field that uses information technology to solve [[concepts/health|healthcare]] problems and improve [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]]. It encompasses the collection, [[entities/storage|storage]], [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and optimal use of medical information for [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].

## Core Domains

- **Biomedical Informatics**: Application of information [[concepts/science|science]] to healthcare [[entities/national-academies|and medicine]].
- **[[concepts/clinical-informatics|Clinical Informatics]]**: Focuses on [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]], including [[concepts/electronic-health-records|electronic health records]] (EHRs), clinical decision support systems, and patient monitoring.
- **[[concepts/population-health|Public Health]] Informatics**: Uses information technology and analytic methods to improve [[concepts/public-health|public health]] and [[concepts/health-care|health care]].
- **Consumer [[concepts/health-information-technology|Health Informatics]]**: Provides consumers with access to personal health information and health knowledge.

## Key Technologies & Concepts

- **Electronic [[concepts/patient-information|Health Records]] (EHR)**: Digital versions of patients' paper charts.
- **Interoperability**: The ability of different [[concepts/knowledge-management|information systems]], devices, and applications to access, exchange, integrate, and cooperatively use data in a coordinated manner.
- **[[concepts/telehealth|Telemedicine]]**: Remote [[concepts/clinical-care|clinical services]] delivered via telecommunications technologies.
- **Data Analytics**: Statistical analysis of [[entities/big-data|large datasets]] to identify patterns and trends in health outcomes.

Recent literature highlights the evolution of [[concepts/digital-interventions|digital health tools]] and their impact on care delivery [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future|The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future]]. - **National Strategy**: Emphasis on integrating [[concepts/digital-health]] initiatives with national health progress metrics.
- **Stakeholder Collaboration**: Increased involvement of [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] institutions (e.g., National Academy of Medicine), industry leaders (e.g., [[entities/epic-systems|Epic Systems]], [[entities/verily|Verily]]), and policy experts in shaping future standards.
