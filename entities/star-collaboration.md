# STAR Collaboration

The **STAR Collaboration** (Solenoidal Tracker at [[entities/rhic|RHIC]]) is an international experimental research effort located at the Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) at [[entities/brookhaven-lab|Brookhaven National Laboratory]]. The collaboration focuses on studying the properties of Quark-Gluon Plasma and the fundamental characteristics of the Strong Interaction.

## Core Research Areas
- **QCD [[concepts/phase|Phase]] Diagram:** Investigating the phase transition of nuclear matter under extreme temperatures and baryon densities.
- **Heavy-[[concepts/heavy-ion-collisions|Ion Collisions]]:** Utilizing high-energy collisions to recreate conditions of the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] to study Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD).
- **Nuclear Matter Properties:** Studying the properties of Quark-Gluon Plasma and the transition from hadronic to partonic matter.
