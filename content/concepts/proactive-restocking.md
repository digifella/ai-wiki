# Proactive Restocking

Proactive restocking is an [[concepts/inventory-management|inventory management]] approach that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/software-algorithms|algorithmic optimization]] to [[entities/make|make]] restocking decisions before stock depletion occurs. Rather than responding to low inventory levels reactively, this method anticipates demand and adjusts supply preemptively based on predictive analysis and historical patterns.

## Algorithmic Foundation

The core mechanism relies on [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative learning]] systems that continuously refine restocking [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. These algorithms process inventory data, demand signals, and supply chain variables to optimize timing and quantity decisions. The system establishes defined [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]]—such as stock turnover rates, carrying costs, and service levels—against which decisions are evaluated and improved over successive cycles.

## Application and Scope

Proactive restocking addresses the tension between maintaining adequate inventory and minimizing excess stock. It applies across retail, manufacturing, and logistics contexts where demand forecasting and [[concepts/cost|cost]] efficiency are significant operational concerns. The approach reduces both stockout risk and the working capital tied up in excess inventory, though effectiveness depends on [[concepts/data-conceptsintegrityintegrity|data quality]] and the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of underlying demand [[concepts/models|models]].
