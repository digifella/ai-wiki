# Post Corporate World

Post Corporate World is a conceptual framework describing a shift toward individual autonomy in digital infrastructure. It proposes that people manage their own data, communication, and digital services independently, rather than relying on centralized corporate platforms such as Google, Meta, or Microsoft. This approach emphasizes personal control over digital assets and interactions, marking a fundamental change in how individuals engage with technology.

The concept was detailed in a presentation by Daniel Miessler on Personal AI Infrastructure, specifically focusing on his tool Kai. Hosted by Clint Gibler, the discussion explored how individuals can build and maintain their own digital ecosystems. Miessler outlined methods for developing personal AI agents that operate outside traditional corporate silos, allowing users to retain ownership of their information and computational resources.

This framework challenges the prevailing model of data outsourcing by advocating for decentralized personal infrastructure. It suggests that by leveraging tools like Kai, individuals can create secure, private, and customizable digital environments. The goal is to reduce dependency on large tech corporations while enhancing privacy and control over personal digital life.
