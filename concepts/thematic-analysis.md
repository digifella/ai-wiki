# Thematic Analysis

[[concepts/flow-management|Thematic analysis]] is a [[concepts/qualitative-research|qualitative research]] method used to identify, analyze, and report patterns (themes) within data. Researchers systematically code and categorize textual or visual data—such as interview transcripts, [[concepts/survey|survey]] responses, or [[entities/focus-group|focus group]] discussions—to uncover recurring concepts, meanings, and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]]. The method is flexible and can be applied across various research disciplines, from [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] to [[concepts/sociology|sociology]].

## Methodological Scope

*   **Qualitative Frameworks**: Primarily associated with [[concepts/grounded-theory|grounded theory]] and thematic [[concepts/coding|coding]], focusing on interpretive analysis of human behavior and social phenomena.
*   **Quantitative & Observational Methods**: Extends to [[concepts/signal-processing|signal processing]] and [[concepts/spatial-analysis|spatial analysis]] for detecting anomalies in large-scale datasets, including [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]] [[concepts/empirical-evidence|observational data]] and telecommunications interference.
*   **[[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|AI Performance Evaluation]]**: Encompasses the analysis of [[concepts/ai-performance|AI model]] metrics, specifically [[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|cost-efficiency]], integration hurdles, and pattern recognition in [[concepts/automations|automated systems]].

Emerging [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] are transforming data analysis by moving beyond simple [[concepts/summarization|summarization]] to active file generation and deep analytical processing.
