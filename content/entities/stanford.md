## Stanford

### Overview
[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford University]] is a private research university located in Stanford, California. It is one of the leading institutions for technological and scientific advancements.

### Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/ai-model-harness|Meta-Harness]]**: A groundbreaking system developed by teams at [[entities/stanford]], [[entities/mit|MIT]], and [[entities/krafton|KRAFTON]] aimed at optimizing AI model harnesses.
  - **[[concepts/slms|Definition]]**: A "harness" refers to the traditional [[concepts/code|code]] wrapped around a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) that dictates its operation, such as storing memories, searching text, [[concepts/writing|writing]] code. Meta-Harness focuses on [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous optimization]] of these LLM operations.
- **AI Fundamentals and [[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]**: [[entities/harper|Harper]], an AI expert with 10 years of experience in AI and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], holds two degrees from [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] in Computer Science, focusing on AI. She previously worked at [[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] (formerly Facebook) for four years as a founding engineer, building [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]], and was acquired by NVIDIA. She now teaches AI and related topics.
