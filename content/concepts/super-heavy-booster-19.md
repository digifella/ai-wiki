# Super Heavy Booster 19

Super Heavy Booster 19 is a booster [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]]'s Super Heavy launch vehicle, designed and prepared for use in [[concepts/starship|Starship]] Flight Test 12. As part of SpaceX's iterative [[concepts/testing|testing]] program, this booster represents a specific configuration within the [[concepts/starship-development|Starship development]] sequence, paired with the Starship [[concepts/version-3|V3 upper stage]] for this particular flight test.

## Flight Configuration

The booster is designated to fly as the first stage of the Starship Flight Test 12 stack. Its pairing with the [[concepts/starship-version-3|Starship V3]] upper stage reflects SpaceX's approach of testing successive [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] iterations to validate [[concepts/design|design]] improvements and system performance. The specific technical specifications and modifications incorporated into Booster 19 relative to earlier iterations form part of the ongoing development cycle.

## Development Context

Booster 19's [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] and the broader Flight Test 12 mission occur within the context of SpaceX's accelerated Starship test cadence, which has been shaped by industry developments and regulatory progress. Details regarding the booster's construction, ground preparations, and technical readiness are documented as part of the mission planning for Flight Test 12.
