# Electronic Visa System E Visa

The Electronic Visa System, commonly referred to as the E Visa, is the official digital platform operated by the [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Republic of Turkey]] for processing visa applications. Launched to modernize and streamline entry procedures, the system allows eligible foreign nationals to apply for Turkish visas entirely online. This [[concepts/digital-infrastructure|digital infrastructure]] eliminates the need for applicants to visit physical embassies or consulates, providing a centralized web-based interface for managing the entire application lifecycle.

The platform enables users to complete application forms, upload necessary documentation, and receive visa decisions from any location with an internet [[concepts/connection|connection]]. By digitizing the [[concepts/verification|verification]] and approval processes, the system aims to reduce processing times and administrative bottlenecks associated with traditional paper-based methods. Eligibility for the E Visa is determined by the applicant's nationality, with specific countries designated as eligible for this expedited digital entry process.

## Application Process

Applicants must access the official government portal to initiate their visa request. The process typically involves filling out personal details, passport information, and travel plans, followed by the payment of the required visa fee via [[concepts/secure|secure]] online methods. Once submitted, the application undergoes automated and manual review by Turkish authorities. Approved visas are issued electronically and linked to the applicant's passport number, requiring travelers to print a copy or carry a digital version for presentation upon arrival in Turkey.
