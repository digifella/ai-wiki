# Operational Order

The Operational Order is a systematic framework developed by America's Test Kitchen to standardize the preparation of fried rice. Presented by Lan Lam in the video "The Formula to Great Fried Rice with Any Ingredients," this guide separates core technical procedures from specific ingredient selections. By establishing reproducible techniques, the framework allows cooks to apply consistent principles across various ingredient combinations rather than relying on rigid, fixed recipes.

The foundation of the Operational Order lies in proper rice selection and treatment. The guide emphasizes that the quality and texture of the final dish depend heavily on the initial state of the rice, advocating for specific methods to ensure grains remain distinct and do not clump during the cooking process. This step is critical for achieving the desired mouthfeel and structural integrity of the fried rice.

Following rice preparation, the framework outlines essential techniques for ingredient mix-ins and cooking execution. It provides a logical sequence for incorporating proteins, vegetables, and seasonings to ensure even distribution and optimal flavor integration. This systematic approach ensures that the cooking process remains controlled and efficient, resulting in a consistent outcome regardless of the specific ingredients used.
