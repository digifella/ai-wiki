# Real-world task evaluation

Evaluation of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and models against practical, operational benchmarks rather than synthetic or [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] datasets. Focuses on efficacy, latency, cost, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] in [[concepts/business-workflows]].

## Key Evaluation Dimensions
- **Task Fidelity:** Accuracy of output in unstructured, real-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
- **Integration Capability:** Ease of embedding into existing tech stacks (e.g., e-commerce, CRM).
- **Cost-Benefit Ratio:** Computational cost vs. value generated per task.
- **Reliability:** [[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]] of performance under varying loads and edge cases.
