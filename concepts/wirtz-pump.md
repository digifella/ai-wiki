# Wirtz Pump Mechanics Overcoming Airlock and Hydrostatic Pressure Challenges

The [[entities/wirtz-pumps|Wirtz pump]] is a type of [[concepts/spiral-pump|spiral pump]] designed to lift water effectively. It stands out for its innovative mechanism that tackles common challenges like airlocks and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]].

## Key Concepts
- **Airlocks:** Blockages caused by trapped air within the system, preventing fluid [[concepts/flow|flow]].
- **Hydrostatic Pressure:** The pressure exerted by a column of liquid at equilibrium due to [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]], affecting the pump's ability to lift water efficiently.
