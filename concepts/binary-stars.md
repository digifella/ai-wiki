# Mass Transfer

- **Astrophysical Context: Binary Stars**
  - **Definition:** Gravitational systems comprising two [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] bound to a common Barycenter, governing ~50-60% of solar-type stellar populations and >80% of massive stars.
  - **Classification & Detection:**
    - **Visual/Resolved:** [[concepts/direct-imaging|Direct imaging]] via adaptive optics/interferometry; yields Orbital Elements, Eccentricity, and inclination.
    - **Spectroscopic:** Doppler Effect modulation of spectral lines; provides mass function and minimum [[concepts/companion|companion]] mass.
    - **Eclipsing:** Photometric light-curve modulation; delivers precise Stellar Radius and Surface [[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]] via Limb Darkening modeling.
    - **Astrometric:** Primary star's positional wobble; critical for non-luminous companions (Brown Dwarf, Compact Object).
  - **Formation & Dynamics:**
    - Originate via Star Formation or Stellar

- **[[concepts/chemical-engineering|Chemical Engineering]] Context: [[concepts/beverage-manufacturing|Beverage Processing]]**
  - **Overview:** Mass transfer techniques applied to remove specific solutes (caffeine, ethanol) from liquid matrices while preserving flavor profiles.
  - **Key Processes:**
    - **[[concepts/caffeine-removal|Decaffeination]]:** Removal of caffeine from coffee/tea using [[concepts/solvent-extraction|solvent extraction]] or [[concepts/supercritical-fluid-extraction|supercritical fluid extraction]].
    - **[[concepts/ethanol-removal|De-alcoholization]]:** Removal of ethanol from alcoholic beverages via [[concepts/vacuum-distillation|vacuum distillation]] or [[concepts/desalination-technology|reverse osmosis]].
  - **Source Integration:** See [[lab-notes/2026-06-24-Decaffeination-and-De-alcoholization-Processes-History-a|Decaffeination and De-alcoholization Processes: History and Scientific Methods]] for detailed historical and methodological breakdown.
