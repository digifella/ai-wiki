# SpaceX

**SpaceX** (Space Exploration Technologies Corp.) is a private aerospace manufacturer and space transportation company founded by [[entities/elon-musk]].

## Starship Program

The Starship super heavy-lift launch vehicle is central to SpaceX's long-term goals, including Mars colonization and Starlink deployment.

### Recent Developments

*   **Flight 14 Milestone:** Scheduled for September 22, 2026, pending regulatory approval, this mission marks the first orbital test flight of the Starship vehicle [[lab-notes/2026-09-17-SpaceX-Starship-Flight-14-First-Orbital-Mission-Starlink|SpaceX Starship Flight 14: First Orbital Mission, Starlink V3 Deployment]].
*   **Mission Duration:** The flight is expected to last approximately 10 hours, significantly longer than previous suborbital tests.
*   **Payload:** The mission includes the deployment of [[concepts/starlink-v3|Starlink V3]] satellites, advancing the constellation's next-generation [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].
*   **Flight 14 Outcomes:** The mission achieved the vehicle's first [[concepts/orbital-insertion|orbital insertion]], successfully deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites, and executed controlled splashdowns.
