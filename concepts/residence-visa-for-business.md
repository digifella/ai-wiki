# Residence Visa For Business

A residence visa for business in Turkey is a long-term visa category designed for foreign nationals who wish to establish or manage business operations within the country. This visa permits individuals to reside in Turkey while conducting legitimate business activities, distinguishing it from standard tourist or short-term visit visas. The residence visa requires applicants to meet specific financial and business criteria set by Turkish immigration authorities.

## Eligibility Requirements

Applicants must demonstrate sufficient financial resources to support themselves and their business operations in Turkey. This typically includes proof of capital investment, business registration documentation, or evidence of ongoing business income. Foreign nationals applying for this visa category must also provide documentation establishing the legitimate nature of their business activities and their intention to maintain a residence in Turkey for business purposes.

## Application Process

The application procedure involves submitting required documentation to Turkish immigration authorities, including business registration certificates, financial statements, proof of residence, and personal identification documents. Processing times and specific requirements may vary depending on the applicant's nationality and the complexity of their business structure. Once approved, the residence visa is typically issued for one year and may be renewed subject to continued compliance with business and financial requirements.
