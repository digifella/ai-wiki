# Instruct Models

Instruct models are language models fine-tuned to follow user [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and perform specific tasks effectively. Unlike [[concepts/base-models|base models]] trained primarily on next-token prediction, instruct models undergo additional [[concepts/training|training]] to improve their ability to understand and execute complex directives, making them more suitable for practical [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Performance Comparison

A notable comparison examined several leading instruct models on [[concepts/coding-benchmarks|coding benchmarks]], including Qwen3, [[concepts/kimi-k2|Kimi K2]], [[entities/claude-opus-4|Claude Opus 4]], and Deepseek-V3-0324. These models were evaluated across various [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks to assess their capabilities in software development, algorithm implementation, and code generation. The comparative analysis provides insight into how different model architectures and training approaches perform on technical [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] tasks.

## Significance

Instruct models have become the standard interface for most [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]], replacing base models in user-facing systems. Their development represents a shift in how [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] are optimized—moving from general language modeling toward goal-oriented performance on tasks users actually need to accomplish. Evaluating instruct models on concrete benchmarks like coding tasks helps determine their practical utility and relative strengths across different application domains.
