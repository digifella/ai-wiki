# Economic Impact

Assessment of quantitative and qualitative changes to economic [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] resulting from interventions, shocks, or projects. Encompasses GDP shifts, Employment generation, Income [[concepts/distribution|Distribution]] effects, and Welfare [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. Analysis distinguishes Direct Effects, Indirect Effects, and Induced Effects, utilizing Input-[[concepts/output|Output]] [[concepts/models|Models]], CGE Models, or [[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis]].

## Evaluation Frameworks
- **[[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis]]**: Requires Shadow [[concepts/pricing|Pricing]] of Externalities; vulnerable to Discount Rate sensitivity and Valuation [[concepts/biases|biases]].
- **Fiscal Multiplier**: Measures expenditure propagation; efficacy varies by Economic Cycle and Leakage.
- **Distributional Impact**: Assesses regressive/progressive effects; links to Inequality metrics.

## Infrastructure Investment
Large-scale Public Goods projects assert benefits via Agglomeration Economies, congestion mitigation, and long-term Productivity gains. Scrutiny applies to Opportunity [[concepts/cost|Cost]], Crowding Out, and alignment with Net Present Value.

- [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] [[concepts/suburban-rail-loop|Suburban Rail Loop]] (SRL) East involves a $32 billion AUD capital commitment subject to [[concepts/diagnostic-audit|rigorous critique]] regarding its stated rationale.
- Public narratives emphasize transit efficiency and economic stimulation; however, independent analysis suggests the "true justification" may diverge significantly from these claims.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-12-Melbourne-Suburban-Rail-Loop-Justification-Critiques-and|Melbourne Suburban Rail Loop: Justification, Critiques, and True Rationale]] documents the discrepancy between public debate and underlying project drivers, raising concerns about Political Economy motivations overriding Economic Efficiency.
- Case [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the necessity of validating Justification against actual Return on Investment and alternative Fiscal Policy allocations.
