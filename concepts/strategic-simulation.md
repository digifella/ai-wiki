# Strategic Simulation

**Strategic [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]]** refers to the use of computational models, Wargaming, and [[concepts/adversarial-simulations|adversarial testing]] environments to evaluate, refine, and preserve complex [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] capabilities, particularly within [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] systems. It serves as a critical mechanism for [[concepts/performance-testing|stress-testing]] planning [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] against edge cases and dynamic constraints.

## Core Principles

- **Adversarial Validation**: Utilizing opposing agents or [[concepts/environment-simulation|simulated environments]] to identify failure modes in planning [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]].
- **Capability [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Extracting and [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] high-level [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] patterns from transient or deprecated models before access is lost.
- **[[concepts/robustness|Robustness]] [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]**: Ensuring that [[concepts/ai-assisted-planning|AI planning]] remains stable under varying conditions, cost constraints, or API changes.
