# Long-range forecast

A **long-range forecast** refers to meteorological or climatological predictions extending beyond the typical short-term window, often covering seasonal [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] (e.g., months). These forecasts are critical for water resource management, [[concepts/agriculture|agriculture]], and disaster preparedness.

## Key Characteristics
- **Temporal Scope:** Typically covers 1–6 months ahead.
- **Uncertainty:** Higher uncertainty than short-range forecasts due to chaotic [[concepts/atmospheric-dynamics|atmospheric dynamics]].
- **[[concepts/causes|Drivers]]:** Influenced by large-scale climate phenomena such as El Niño–Southern Oscillation (ENSO), Indian Ocean Dipole (IOD), and Southern Oscillation Index (SOI).
- **Application:** Used for strategic planning rather than immediate operational decisions.
