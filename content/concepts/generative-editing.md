# Generative Editing

Generative Editing refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] within [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] version 27.6 that enable users to automatically generate, modify, and enhance image content. These features leverage [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to perform tasks that traditionally required manual pixel-level work or advanced technical [[concepts/skills|skills]]. The tools are designed to integrate seamlessly into Photoshop's existing [[concepts/workflow|workflow]], allowing both professional designers and casual users to accomplish complex edits more efficiently.

## Core Capabilities

Version 27.6 includes several generative functions built into the core editing interface. Users can generate new content to fill selected areas, remove or replace objects intelligently, and extend [[concepts/images|images]] beyond their original boundaries. These operations analyze surrounding pixels and image context to produce results that blend naturally with existing content. The system also offers variations and refinement options, allowing editors to iterate on generated results until they achieve their desired outcome.

## Integration and Workflow

The generative features are accessible through standard Photoshop tools and selection methods, minimizing the learning curve for existing users. Rather than replacing traditional editing techniques, these tools function as complements to conventional layer-based editing, brushes, and adjustment controls. Users retain full [[concepts/power|control]] over their edits and can undo or modify generative outputs [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] any point in their workflow.
