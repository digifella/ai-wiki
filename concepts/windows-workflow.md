# Windows Workflow

## Overview
A structured approach to managing digital tasks, notes, and applications within the Windows environment, with a specific focus on minimizing context switching and optimizing integration with knowledge management tools like Obsidian.

## Key Components

### Lean Launcher Integration
Recent developments in [[concepts/workflow-optimization|workflow optimization]] highlight the importance of efficient application launching. The introduction of **Lean Launcher** addresses common frustrations regarding context switching for Obsidian users.

*   **Purpose:** Streamlines the transition between capturing ideas and organizing them in Obsidian.
*   **Core Benefit:** Reduces friction in the "[[concepts/quick-capture|Quick Capture]]" phase of the workflow.
*   **Source Analysis:** Based on insights from LeanProductivity regarding the "Obsidian Quick Capture Just Became Real" update.
*   **Reference:** [[lab-notes/2026-09-24-Lean-Launcher-Streamlining-Obsidian-Quick-Capture-and-Wi|Lean Launcher: Streamlining Obsidian Quick Capture and Windows Workflow]]

### Obsidian Quick Capture
A critical component of the modern Windows workflow, allowing for rapid ingestion of information before it is processed and linked within the vault.

*   **Workflow Step:** Capture → Process → Link → Archive.
*   **Optimization:** Utilizing tools like [[concepts/lean-launcher]] ensures that the "Capture" step does not become a bottleneck.
