# AI-powered compositing

The integration of [[concepts/generative-ai]]-driven features with traditional Digital Imaging manipulation techniques to streamline complex image assembly, element synthesis, and [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]].

## Technical Implementation
* **Hybrid Workflows**: Combining traditional [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] tools with new AI-powered features to automate complex compositing tasks.
* **High-Fidelity [[concepts/open-standards|Standards]]**:
    * Resolution & Depth: 300 ppi, 16-bit.
    * Color Space: [[concepts/adobe-rgb]].
* **Asset Pipeline**: Initial image selection and importing via [[concepts/lightroom]].
