# Health Metrics

Health metrics are quantifiable measurements of physiological and behavioral [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] used to assess and monitor an individual's [[concepts/health|health]] status. These metrics form the foundation of both clinical medicine and personal health management, providing objective data about bodily functions, activity levels, and overall wellness. Common health metrics include heart rate, blood pressure, body temperature, [[concepts/sleep|sleep]] duration, [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]] levels, and various blood chemistry values.

## Collection and Monitoring

Health metrics are collected through diverse methods ranging from clinical instruments in medical settings to personal monitoring devices. [[concepts/wearable-devices|Wearable devices]]—electronic sensors worn on the body or embedded in clothing—have become increasingly prevalent for continuous health data collection. These devices can track metrics such as heart rate variability, steps taken, calories burned, and sleep patterns throughout daily life. Traditional clinical measurements remain essential for comprehensive [[concepts/conducting-health-screenings|health assessment]] and often serve as validation points for consumer-grade monitoring devices.

## Applications

In clinical settings, health metrics guide diagnosis, treatment planning, and [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|disease management]]. Physicians use standardized metrics to assess patient conditions and track [[concepts/responses|responses]] to interventions. For personal health management, individuals increasingly use metrics to establish baselines, set wellness goals, and identify trends in their health status. The [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of [[entities/wearable-devices|wearable technology]] has expanded self-monitoring [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], though clinical-grade [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and interpretation by healthcare professionals remain important for medical [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
