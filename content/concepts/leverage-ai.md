# Leverage AI

Leverage AI refers to the strategic assessment and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to enhance operational speed and automate routine tasks within organizations. This approach focuses on identifying high-impact [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] where AI can deliver meaningful improvements while accounting for real-world constraints. The goal is to move beyond theoretical [[concepts/software|applications]] toward practical implementations that address specific bottlenecks and pain points in existing workflows.

## Use Cases and Application Areas

Determining appropriate AI use cases requires evaluating where [[concepts/automation|automation]] provides the greatest benefit relative to implementation complexity. Common areas include accelerating repetitive analysis work, automating grunt work that would otherwise consume significant human time, and streamlining [[concepts/document-management|document management]] and proposal processes. The effectiveness of any implementation depends on matching [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to genuine workflow problems rather than applying technology broadly.

## Managing Constraints

Organizations implementing AI must contend with [[concepts/infrastructure-limitations|infrastructure limitations]] and tool-specific issues that affect deployment and performance. Sharepoint integration, [[concepts/data-pipeline|data pipeline]] constraints, and the varying capabilities of different AI platforms all influence what's practically achievable. Successfully leveraging AI requires acknowledging these constraints upfront and working within them rather than treating them as obstacles to overcome later, which can derail otherwise promising initiatives.
