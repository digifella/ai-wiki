## My Lightroom edits sucked... Until I started doing this
**Clip title:** My Lightroom edits sucked... Until I started doing this
**Author / channel:** Zac Watson
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gs0x0-0GeFE
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Zac Watson: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
**Main Topic:** The video explores five masking techniques in Lightroom that can significantly elevate photo editing results.

**Key Points:**
- **Mask 1: How to brighten subjects correctly:** Avoid unnatural results by balancing exposure, highlights, and contrast instead of simply cranking up exposure on a subject mask.
- **Mask 2: How to enhance light in a shot:** Use a radial gradient with careful placement and subtraction to create realistic, atmospheric light spilling into the frame.
- **Mask 3: Custom Vignette:** Create a custom vignette using radial gradients and the brush tool to selectively darken edges rather than relying on standard vignette sliders.
- **Mask 4: Color range:** Isolate and adjust specific color ranges within masks (like greens or oranges) to blend tones seamlessly and remove unwanted color casts.
- **Mask 5: Background:** Use background masks and slight reductions in clarity and contrast to create a dreamy, blurred background that makes subjects stand out.

**Conclusions and Takeaways:** 
The speaker emphasizes that mastering these five masking techniques provides immense control over photo editing, leading to more natural, professional, and complete-looking final images. He recommends implementing these masks across portrait and landscape work for superior results.
