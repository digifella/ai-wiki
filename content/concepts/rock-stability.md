# Rock Stability

Rock stability refers to the ability of rocks and rock formations to withstand forces such as [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]], water pressure, and geological stresses without failing or moving. This concept is critical in understanding natural hazards like landslides and slope failures, and it plays a significant role in engineering projects including mining, tunneling, and construction.

- Rock stability is influenced by factors such as:
  - Geological [[concepts/structure|structure]]
  - Soil composition
  - Climate conditions
  - Water presence and [[concepts/exercise|movement]]
