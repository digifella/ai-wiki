# Instruct Model

An instruct model is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) that has been fine-tuned to follow explicit [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and respond to user queries in a structured manner. These [[concepts/models|models]] are trained on [[concepts/instruction-following|instruction-following]] datasets where the model learns to interpret user intent and generate appropriate [[concepts/responses|responses]]. [[concepts/instruct-models|Instruct models]] represent a significant evolution from [[concepts/base-models|base models]], which are trained primarily on next-token prediction without specific instruction-following optimization.

## Performance and Benchmarking

Instruct models are commonly evaluated across standardized benchmarks and practical tasks to assess their [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. Recent comparisons have included models such as [[concepts/qwen3-model|Qwen3]], [[concepts/kimi-k2|Kimi K2]], [[entities/claude-opus-4|Claude Opus 4]], and [[concepts/deepseek-v3|Deepseek-V3]], which are tested on various [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]] and real-world [[concepts/software|applications]] including [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks. These benchmarks help establish relative performance characteristics and suitability for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Applications in AI Agents

Instruct models form the foundation for many [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]], as their instruction-following capabilities enable them to interpret high-level directives and execute [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]]. The alignment between model outputs and user intent, which instruct tuning provides, makes these models particularly suitable for [[concepts/agentic-applications|agentic applications]] where reliable behavior and task completion are essential. [[concepts/open-source|Open-source]] instruct models have also expanded the [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of capable models for developers building AI systems.
