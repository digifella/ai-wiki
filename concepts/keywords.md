# Keywords

Keywords in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] are [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] tags assigned to images to facilitate efficient organization, searchability, and [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] within a [[concepts/catalog|catalog]]. They function as descriptive labels applied individually or in hierarchical structures, allowing photographers and editors to categorize images by subject matter, location, person, technical attributes, or custom classification systems. This metadata becomes embedded in image files, making it portable across different systems and compatible with industry-standard formats.

Within Lightroom's interface, keywords can be managed through the Keywording panel in the [[concepts/library-module|Library module]]. Users can add new keywords manually, create hierarchical keyword lists for complex taxonomies, or apply existing keywords to multiple selected images simultaneously. The software also supports the import of keyword presets, enabling consistent tagging workflows across different projects or clients.

The primary utility of keywords lies in their ability to streamline the [[concepts/editing-workflow-optimization|editing process]]. By tagging images with relevant terms, users can quickly filter the catalog to view only specific subsets of photos, such as all images taken at a particular location or featuring a specific subject. This reduces the time spent searching through large collections and ensures that assets are easily retrievable for future use or archival purposes.
