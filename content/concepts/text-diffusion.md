# Text Diffusion

Text Diffusion refers to a class of [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] that produce text by iteratively refining outputs through a diffusion process, analogous to how diffusion models work in image generation. Rather than generating text sequentially token-by-token like traditional language models, diffusion-based [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] starts with noise and progressively denoises it into coherent text across multiple steps.

## Gemini Diffusion

[[concepts/google-search|Google]] has developed an experimental text diffusion model called Gemini Diffusion, which became available to selected users in mid-2025. This model represents Google's exploration of diffusion-based approaches as an alternative to conventional transformer-based text generation architectures. Access to Gemini Diffusion has been extended through Google's experimental program to test the approach's practical [[concepts/software|applications]] and performance characteristics.

The potential advantages of text diffusion models include different trade-offs in generation speed, quality, and controllability compared to autoregressive models. However, diffusion-based text generation remains an active area of research, and the practical benefits and limitations of this approach continue to be evaluated by researchers and early-access users.
