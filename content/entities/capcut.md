# Capcut

Capcut is a [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] application that supports AI-assisted workflows for creating documentary-[[concepts/style|style]] content. The platform enables creators to produce broadcast-quality videos with features designed to maintain high viewer retention through motion graphics and [[concepts/synchronized-audio|synchronized audio]]-visual elements.

## AI Video Workflow

Capcut's [[concepts/ai-video-animation|AI video workflow]] facilitates the creation of documentary content by automating certain aspects of the editing process. The workflow integrates [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to handle tasks such as [[concepts/tone|voice]] matching and visual synchronization, reducing manual editing time while maintaining production quality. This approach allows creators to work more efficiently when producing longer-form documentary content.

While Capcut provides accessible [[concepts/ai-assisted-editing|AI editing]] features, creators should evaluate the cost-benefit of different tools within their workflow.
