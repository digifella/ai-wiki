# Notebooklm Slide Transformation

[[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Slide Transformation is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] that converts presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] generated by [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], with the capability to remove or bypass watermarking. [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] generates AI-powered [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source documents, while [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] is a [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool that can incorporate these [[concepts/google-slides|slides]] into video presentations. This transformation process bridges document analysis and multimedia [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]].

## Process Overview

The transformation leverages Google Vids' [[concepts/integration|integration]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to import NotebookLM-generated slides and convert them into video format. The technique addresses a limitation in NotebookLM's native [[concepts/output|output]] by producing videos without the platform's standard watermark, making the resulting content suitable for professional [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] or publication where watermarks would be undesirable.

## Use Cases

This approach is relevant for content creators, educators, and professionals who want to transform research documents or study materials into polished video presentations. By combining NotebookLM's [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] with Google Vids' video capabilities, users can create multimedia content that maintains the analytical [[concepts/structure|structure]] of the original slides while presenting them in video format.
