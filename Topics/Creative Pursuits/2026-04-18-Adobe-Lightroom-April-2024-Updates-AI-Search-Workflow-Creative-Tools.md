## Adobe Lightroom April 2024 Updates: AI Search, Workflow & Creative Tools
**Clip title:** 12 NEW Lightroom features, just released
**Author / channel:** photoshopCAFE
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=AMRmW7BicMk

### Summary
The video provides a comprehensive overview of the significant April 2024 updates for both Adobe Lightroom (desktop) and [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], highlighting new AI-powered functionalities, workflow improvements, and creative tools. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], Colin Smith from PhotoshopCAFE, systematically demonstrates features unique to each platform, as well as those shared across both, emphasizing how these enhancements streamline the photographic editing and organizational process.

For Lightroom (desktop), the most impactful update is the new AI-powered natural language search. This feature allows users to find specific images by describing their content using everyday language (e.g., "boats sunset coast," "neon signs"), without requiring pre-existing [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] or keywords. This leverages AI to understand image content, drastically improving [[concepts/asset-management|asset management]]. Additionally, Lightroom desktop now offers more robust [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] [[concepts/integration|integration]], allowing users to control file format, color space (including HDR), bit depth, and [[concepts/solution|resolution]] when sending photos to Photoshop for further editing. Users can also now pan and zoom directly within the crop tool, providing finer control over composition during cropping.

Lightroom Classic also received notable updates focused on performance and efficiency. AI mask synchronization can now occur in the background, meaning users can apply complex masks to multiple images and continue working without interruption while the processing completes. Similar background processing enhancements apply to other computationally intensive tasks like AI Denoise, Raw Details, and Super Resolution. Adobe has also improved overall performance for interactive slider adjustments, [[concepts/memory|memory]] utilization in full-screen mode, and cloud sync download speeds in Lightroom Classic.

Several key features were updated in both Lightroom Classic and Lightroom (desktop). [[concepts/assisted-culling|Assisted Culling]] received improvements, making its reject filter more accurate at identifying images with blurry subjects (even those with intentional shallow depth of field) and [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] issues. A new slider allows users to control the strictness of exposure flagging. Both versions now support Photoshop Big (PSB) [[concepts/files|files]], a welcome addition for those working with extremely large Photoshop documents. Lastly, Adobe introduced new "Film-Inspired" presets, offering a [[concepts/range|range]] of creative looks with adjustable intensity, and integrated "Moodboards" via Firefly.adobe.com, enabling users to effortlessly create visual collages from selected photos for creative conceptualization.

In conclusion, the April 2024 Lightroom updates demonstrate a strong push towards leveraging AI for smarter image management and more efficient workflows. The natural language search and enhanced background processing are significant time-savers, while improved culling, Photoshop integration, and creative presets offer more control and artistic possibilities. These updates collectively aim to provide a more fluid and powerful experience for photographers across both Lightroom platforms.
