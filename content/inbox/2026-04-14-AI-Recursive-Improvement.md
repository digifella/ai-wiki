## AI [[concepts/self-improvement|Recursive Self-Improvement]]: The Dawn of [[concepts/intelligence-explosion|Intelligence Explosion]]  
**Clip title:** [[concepts/hard-takeoff|Hard Takeoff]] has started  
**Author / channel:** [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]]  
**URL:** [https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhoFqhLXc3g](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhoFqhLXc3g)  
  
### [[concepts/summary|Summary]]  
The video discusses the groundbreaking shift in Artificial Intelligence (AI) development, asserting that we have officially entered the "[[concepts/self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]]" phase. This critical juncture means that AI [[concepts/agents|agents]] are now capable of contributing significantly to their own evolution and enhancement, dramatically accelerating the pace of technological progress. The presenter [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] that human researchers are increasingly moving from being the primary developers to more of a [[concepts/guiding-role|guiding role]], with computational power becoming the main limiting factor in [[concepts/ai-advancement|AI advancement]]. This transition is predicted to lead to an "[[concepts/intelligence-explosion|intelligence explosion]]."  
  
Multiple examples are provided to illustrate this phenomenon. [[entities/minimax|MiniMax]] M2.7, a model from a Chinese frontier [[entities/google-deepmind|AI lab]], explicitly states its capability for "[[concepts/self-evolution|self-evolution]]," updating its own [[concepts/memory|memory]], developing complex [[concepts/skills|skills]] through reinforcement [[concepts/learning|learning]], and improving its learning process based on experimental results. This process allows the AI to handle a substantial portion (30-50%) of the research [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] autonomously. Similarly, [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[entities/codex|GPT-5.3 Codex]] demonstrated its self-creative abilities by [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] its own [[concepts/training|training]], managing its deployment, and diagnosing test results, a feat that "blew away" the [[concepts/codex|Codex]] team. [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] CEO [[entities/sam-altman|Sam Altman]]'s earlier stated goals for having automated [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] interns by September 2026 and true automated AI researchers by March 2028 appear to be well within reach, if not already partially surpassed.  
  
[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] is also presented as an AI [[concepts/solution|solution]] that has evolved beyond a mere [[concepts/coding|coding]] tool. It is now used internally for [[entities/deep-research|deep research]], [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]], note-taking, and powering major "[[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/loops|loops]]," enabling autonomous prototyping and [[concepts/feature-development|feature development]] by [[concepts/writing|writing]] [[concepts/code|code]], [[concepts/running|running]] tests, and iterating solutions with minimal human oversight. Furthermore, [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] "[[concepts/autoresearch|AutoResearch]]" project allows individual developers, even those without extensive [[concepts/machine-learning|Machine Learning]] backgrounds, to leverage powerful frontier [[concepts/models|models]] to autonomously [[concepts/design|design]] experiments, train [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], and continuously optimize their [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] for faster research. The presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] his own use of such systems, underscoring the [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and impact of these self-improving agents.  
  
The video concludes that this era of recursive self-improvement in AI is not a distant future but a present reality. Across major frontier [[entities/labs|labs]] and even among independent researchers, AI models are demonstrating the ability to improve themselves, leading to a relentless and accelerating cycle of [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]. This fundamental shift implies that the [[concepts/speed|speed]] of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] [[entities/will|will]] increasingly be dictated by the AI systems themselves, rather than solely by human input, promising an unprecedented and exciting period of technological advancement.
