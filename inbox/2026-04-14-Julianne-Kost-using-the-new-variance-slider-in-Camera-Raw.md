# [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]] - using the new variance slider in [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]]

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FSk--Gi-PeA>
The video demonstrates how to use the "[[concepts/color-variance-control|Color Variance Control]]" in [[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]]'s "[[concepts/point-color|Point Color]]" feature. The presenter, [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]], uses an image of a landscape with reflections to showcase the functionality. Here's a breakdown of the key actions:

1. **Selecting a Color:** The presenter uses the [[concepts/eyedropper-tool|eyedropper tool]] to select a specific color in the image (green from the trees).
2. **Adjusting [[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, and Luminance:** The presenter then manipulates the Hue, Saturation, and Luminance sliders within the color swatch to modify the selected color.
3. **Using the [[concepts/range|Range]] Sliders:** The video [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the "Range" sliders, which allow the user to control the extent to which the [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] affect similar colors.
	* Increasing the "Range" slider includes more colors in the adjustment.
	* Decreasing it narrows the scope to fewer similar colors.
	* The "Hue Range," "Saturation Range," and "Luminance Range" sliders offer more [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over the color adjustments.
4. **Resetting and Undoing:** The presenter shows how to reset or delete a color swatch to start over.
5. **Applying to Portrait Retouching:** The video then transitions to a portrait image to demonstrate how the "Color Variance Control" can be used for skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] adjustments.
	* The presenter creates a mask for "Facial Skin" and "Body Skin."
	* Using the eyedropper on the skin, they adjust the Hue, Saturation, and Luminance sliders to even out [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]].
	* The Variance slider is used to either accentuate or reduce [[concepts/color-contrast|color contrast]] within the selected skin areas.
	* Finally, the presenter shows how to toggle the visibility of the mask to see the "before" and "after" effect of the adjustments.
