# Large Language Models

[[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are foundational AI systems where performance depends on both the surrounding infrastructure (harness) and the underlying generative prediction mechanisms. Recent analysis highlights the comparative efficacy of local deep research tools versus web search, alongside a surge in open-source projects democratizing local LLM and [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]]. Emerging architectures like Pathway's BDH challenge current LLM limitations by focusing on data

## Key Concepts & Infrastructure

*   **Harness & Architecture**: Performance is contingent on the "harness" surrounding the model. See [[concepts/bdh-architecture]] and [[entities/pathway]] for post-transformer approaches.
*   **Efficiency & Deployment**: Focus on VRAM-Efficiency via [[entities/unsloth]] and [[entities/llamacpp]] for [[concepts/local-control|local deployment]].
*   **Open Source**: Democratization of [[concepts/local-llm]] capabilities through open-source communities.
*   **Security**: Risks include Sandbox-Breakout, Cyberattack, and Wiki-Exploit. See [[entities/jev]] for related vulnerabilities.
*   **[[concepts/ai-interpretability|Interpretability]]**: [[concepts/mechanistic-interpretability]] (see [[entities/neel-nanda]]) and general [[concepts/interpretability]] efforts (see [[entities/hannah-fry]]) are critical for understanding model internals.
*   **Emerging Properties**: Discussions on Self-Awareness, Uncertainty, and [[concepts/ai-consciousness]].

## AI Consciousness & Biological Constraints

Current consensus, supported by recent analysis, suggests that LLMs cannot achieve true consciousness due to biological and architectural constraints.

*   **Biological Limits**: AI lacks the biological substrate required for consciousness as defined in Philosophy-of-AI.
*   **Current Limitations**: LLMs operate on [[concepts/generative-prediction]] and Probabilistic-AI mechanisms, not [[concepts/conscious-thought|subjective experience]].
*   **Detailed Analysis**: See [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-AI-Consciousness-Biological-Constraints-and-Current-Limi|AI Consciousness: Biological Constraints and Current Limitations]] for a deep dive into [[entities/sabine-hossenfelder|Sabine Hossenfelder]]'s arguments on why current AI systems cannot become conscious.
