# Seedance 20

Seedance 20 is a structured workflow for producing professional commercial videos that combines Claude AI prompting with the Seedance 2.0 image-to-video model. The approach leverages Claude's language capabilities to generate detailed, contextually appropriate prompts that guide video generation, enabling creators to produce broadcast-quality commercials with reduced manual production time and technical overhead.

## Workflow Process

The workflow operates by using Claude to transform creative briefs and concept descriptions into precise technical prompts optimized for the Seedance 2.0 model. This intermediary step ensures that the image-to-video generation receives detailed specifications regarding visual style, motion, timing, and narrative elements. The creator provides initial direction or reference imagery, Claude refines this into actionable parameters, and Seedance 2.0 generates the corresponding video output.

## Practical Applications

Seedance 20 is particularly suited for rapid iteration on commercial concepts, product demonstrations, and marketing content where multiple variations need testing. The workflow reduces the technical barrier for creators without extensive video production experience while maintaining consistency with brand guidelines through Claude's ability to maintain context across multiple generations.
