# Healthcare Workforce Development

**[[concepts/health|Healthcare]] Workforce Development** encompasses the systematic strategies for recruiting, training, retaining, and optimizing the performance of [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] professionals to ensure [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]] and [[concepts/robustness|system resilience]]. Key dimensions include clinical competency, [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] capacity, [[concepts/interdisciplinary-collaboration|interdisciplinary collaboration]], and effective communication [[concepts/skills|skills]].

## Core Components

- **Education & Training**: Integration of evidence-based practices into curricula, simulation-based [[concepts/learning|learning]], and continuous professional development (CPD).
- **Competency Frameworks**: Defining core skills across domains including clinical technical skills, patient safety, and [[concepts/personalised-care|Patient-Centered Care]].
- **Policy & [[concepts/governance|Governance]]**: Regulatory standards, accreditation requirements, and national health workforce plans.
- **Equity & Diversity**: Addressing disparities in workforce [[concepts/writing|composition]] and cultural safety, particularly in [[concepts/indigenous-health|Indigenous health]] contexts.
