# Reality Twists

**Reality Twists** refer to [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] devices in fiction, particularly in [[concepts/science|Science]] Fiction and Thriller genres, where the established ontological framework of the story is subverted. These twists challenge the viewer's or reader's perception of truth, often revealing that the initial premise was a [[concepts/simulation|simulation]], [[concepts/memory|memory]] fabrication, or alternate dimension.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Unreliable Narrator**: The protagonist’s perception is compromised by [[concepts/depression|mental illness]], intoxication, or deception.
- **Simulation Hypothesis**: The [[entities/earth|world]] is revealed to be artificial (e.g., Matrix-[[concepts/style|style]] constructs).
- **Time Loop/Non-Linearity**: Causality is disrupted, forcing re-evaluation of event chronology.
- **Identity Displacement**: Characters discover they are not who they believe (clones, aliens, projections).
