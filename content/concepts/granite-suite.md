# Granite Suite

[[entities/ibm]]-developed ecosystem of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation models]] optimized for enterprise workflows, [[concepts/security|security]], and multi-modal [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].

## Overview
- **[[concepts/open-weight|Open Weights]]:** Fully open [[concepts/models|models]] available for community and enterprise use.
- **Multi-Modal:** Spans language, [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], speech, and embedding domains.
- **Enterprise Focus:** Designed for [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and flexible [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] (cloud/on-prem/edge).

## Capabilities & Families
- **Language:** IBM [[entities/granite|Granite]] LLMs for [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].
- **Vision:** IBM Granite Vision for [[concepts/image-analysis|image analysis]] and generation.
- **Speech:** IBM Granite Speech including [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|ASR]] and text-to-speech; details in [[lab-notes/2026-05-08-IBM-Granite-Speech-4.1-ASR-Models-Features-Accuracy-and|IBM Granite Speech 4.1 ASR Models: Features, Accuracy, and Enterprise Applications]].
- **Embeddings:** IBM Granite Embeddings for [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]] and retrieval.
