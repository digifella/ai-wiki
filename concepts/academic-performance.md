# Academic Performance

[[entities/tomasz-janowski|Academic]] performance encompasses a student's ability to achieve success in educational settings through various measures such as grades, test scores, and overall [[concepts/learning|learning]] outcomes. This concept is multifaceted and influenced by numerous factors including [[concepts/cognitive-abilities|cognitive abilities]], [[concepts/motivation|motivation]], [[concepts/study-habits|study habits]], [[concepts/social-emotional-learning|social support systems]], and [[concepts/physical-health|physical health]].

## Influential Factors

- **Cognitive Abilities:** Cognitive [[concepts/skills|skills]] such as [[concepts/memory|memory]], [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]], [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical thinking]] significantly impact academic performance.
- **[[concepts/motivation|Motivation]]:** [[concepts/motivation|Internal and external motivations]] [[concepts/ambition|drive]] students to engage with their coursework and strive for success.
- **Study [[concepts/habits|Habits]]:** Effective study habits, including time management, note-taking, and active learning strategies, contribute positively to academic [[concepts/success|achievement]].
- **[[concepts/social-support-systems-family-peers-teachers-and-mentors-provide-emotional|Social Support Systems:** Family, peers, teachers, and mentors provide emotional support and guidance that enhance a student's educational experience.]]
