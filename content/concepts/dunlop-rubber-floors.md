# Dunlop Rubber Floors

Dunlop rubber floors represent a distinctive choice in mid-twentieth-century commercial interior design, combining durability with aesthetic possibility. The material, manufactured by the Dunlop company, offered practical advantages for high-traffic public spaces, particularly hospitality venues where both functionality and visual appeal were desired.

## South Seas Cafe Installation

The South Seas Cafe features a notable example of Dunlop rubber flooring, with 1.2-meter tiles arranged in a mosaic pattern. This installation reflects the design choices of the period, when rubber flooring was valued for its [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] and ease of maintenance in busy commercial environments. The mosaic arrangement demonstrates how the material could be configured to create visual interest while serving functional purposes.
