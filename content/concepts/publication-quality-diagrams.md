# Publication Quality Diagrams

Publication Quality Diagrams refers to a class of tools and frameworks designed to automate the creation of technical illustrations for academic papers, with particular focus on research in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]]. These systems aim to address the time-consuming process of manually designing figures that meet publication [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] while accurately representing complex concepts and data.

## PaperBanana Framework

[[concepts/automated-academic-illustration|PaperBanana]] is a framework developed through collaboration between researchers [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] [[concepts/google-search|Google]] and [[entities/peking-university|Peking University]] specifically designed to assist AI scientists in generating publication-ready illustrations. The framework automates aspects of academic diagram creation, reducing the manual effort required to produce figures suitable for peer-reviewed venues while maintaining visual clarity and technical [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]].

The [[concepts/motivation|motivation]] behind such tools stems from the recognition that creating polished [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]] for academic papers is a significant bottleneck in the research publication process. By automating [[concepts/diagram-generation|diagram generation]], researchers can allocate more time to experimental work and analysis rather than illustration [[concepts/design|design]] and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].
