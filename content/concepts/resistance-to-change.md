# Resistance to Change

**Definition:** Resistance to change refers to individuals' or groups' reluctance to accept new ideas, behaviors, technologies, or [[concepts/policies|policies]]. This can manifest as opposition, skepticism, or simply inaction.

## Key Points
- **Origin and Scope**: The concept of resistance to change spans various fields including psychology, sociology, management science, and organizational behavior.
- **Root Causes**: Common reasons for resistance include fear of the unknown, perceived threat to job [[concepts/security|security]], lack of trust in [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], or simple inertia and habit.
- **Strategies to Overcome Resistance**:
  - Education and communication
  - Involvement and participation
  - Negotiation and agreement
  - Manipulation (e.g., coercive tactics)
  - Explicit and implicit coercion
