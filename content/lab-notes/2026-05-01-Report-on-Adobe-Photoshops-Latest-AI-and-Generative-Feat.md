### Summary
The video "Ten Photoshop Updates That You May Have Missed" provides a comprehensive overview of recent enhancements to [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]], largely focusing on the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and generative capabilities to streamline workflows and offer more creative control. Presented by [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]], the summary highlights a [[concepts/range|range]] of features designed to improve efficiency, precision, and the overall [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] for designers and photographers alike.

Several updates aim to enhance fundamental editing tasks. The "Updates to Dynamic [[concepts/text|Text]]" feature allows users to place [[concepts/text|text]] along curved paths (like circles or arcs) with full editability, repositioning, and transformation options. For image clean-up, the "General Distractions Removal" tool intelligently identifies and removes unwanted elements such as people, wires, poles, and general visual clutter, offering [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]] and categorized distraction overlays. The "Rotate Object" feature significantly aids compositing by automatically creating a new layer for an object, allowing for intuitive [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]], tilting, and rotating while Photoshop generates and fills unseen areas, along with a "Harmonize" option to match lighting and [[concepts/tone|tone]]. Additionally, "Layer Clean-up" introduces automated renaming and deletion of empty layers, promoting a more organized Layers panel, though certain layer types (like backgrounds or manually renamed layers) are exempt from auto-renaming.

The video further delves into advanced AI functionalities and cross-[[concepts/application-integration|application integration]]. "Round-trip [Cloud Documents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_Documents)" enables seamless opening and saving of Firefly-generated content directly within Photoshop, with linked cloud documents ensuring changes reflect across platforms. The "[[concepts/reflection-removal|Reflection Removal]]" tool offers a powerful way to eliminate reflections and lens flares from [[concepts/images|images]], providing options to separate reflections onto their own layers for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]]. A notable advancement is "Reference Image Support for Partner Models" (e.g., [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] and Flux), which allows users to upload reference [[concepts/images|images]] to guide [[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]], ensuring that AI-generated content matches specific identities, styles, and structures for more consistent and controlled outcomes. This is complemented by the "Generative Credits Usage" icon, providing transparent tracking of credit consumption for AI-powered features.

Finally, the "Instruct Edit [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] with Firefly Image Model 5" showcases Adobe's most advanced generative model. This allows users to employ natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] for complex edits, such as removing multiple elements from a scene while maintaining visual quality and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across various dimensions (no longer restricted to square blocks like previous models). The modernized "Generate Image Experience" ties these features together with a redesigned UI, an expanding [[concepts/contextual-taskbar|contextual taskbar]] for prompts, and the ability to add [[concepts/style|style]] and composition references. Furthermore, the "Modernized Actions Panel" now boasts full parity with classic actions, offering enhanced search, custom icons, descriptions, and improved organizational tools, solidifying Photoshop's commitment to delivering a powerful, intuitive, and future-ready creative environment.
