# Brush Size Control

Brush size control in [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] refers to the ability to adjust brush dimensions during active painting and editing work. Rather than accessing brush settings through menus or panels, Photoshop provides keyboard shortcuts that allow artists to modify brush properties in real time without interrupting their workflow. This method of control is particularly useful when working on detailed digital art, [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]], or illustration projects where frequent size [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are necessary.

## Keyboard Shortcuts

The primary method for controlling brush size involves the square bracket keys. Pressing the right bracket key (]) increases the [[concepts/brush-size|brush diameter]], while pressing the left bracket key ([) decreases it. Each press typically adjusts the size by a standard increment, allowing for rapid [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] during active strokes.

## Hardness Adjustment

In addition to size, the same bracket keys control [[concepts/brush-hardness|brush hardness]] when combined with the Shift modifier. Holding Shift while pressing the right bracket key increases hardness, making the brush edge sharper and more defined. Conversely, holding Shift and pressing the left bracket key decreases hardness, resulting in softer, more diffused edges. This dual functionality enables artists to fine-tune both the scale and [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] of their strokes efficiently.
