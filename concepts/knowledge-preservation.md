# Knowledge Preservation

**Knowledge [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]** refers to the systematic strategies, technologies, and practices employed to maintain the [[concepts/honesty|integrity]], [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and utility of information over time. In the context of [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]], this extends beyond [[concepts/dead-files|static data]] [[entities/storage|storage]] to include the preservation of dynamic capabilities, such as the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] patterns and planning heuristics of advanced [[concepts/large-language-model]]s.

## Core Principles

- **[[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Ensuring data remains unaltered and authentic.
- **Accessibility**: Maintaining formats and interfaces that allow future [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] and use.
- **Contextual [[concepts/storing|Retention]]**: Preserving the [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] and relational structures that give data meaning.

## Strategic Approaches

### 1. Static Archiving
Traditional methods involving bit-level copying, format migration, and redundant storage systems to prevent data loss due to hardware failure or obsolescence.

### 2. Dynamic Capability Preservation
Emerging strategies focused on capturing the "intelligence" or behavioral patterns of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] before they are deprecated or altered. This involves extracting reasoning traces, planning structures, and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] heuristics.

- **Wargaming for Robust [[concepts/ai-assisted-planning|AI Planning]]**: A specific technique proposed to extract and preserve the unique planning intelligence of models like [[entities/claude-fable-5]]. This method involves simulating adversarial or complex [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] to force the model to reveal its underlying strategic [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], which can then be codified or archived. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-06-Preserving-Claude-Fable-5-Intelligence-Wargaming-for-Rob|Preserving Claude Fable 5 Intelligence: Wargaming for Robust AI Planning]] for detailed implementation [[concepts/notes|notes]].

### 3. Format Agnosticism
Converting proprietary or ephemeral formats into open, standardized schemas (e.g., [[concepts/markdown]], [[concepts/json]], XML) to ensure long-term readability independent of specific software platforms.

## Challenges

- **[[concepts/vendor-lock-in|Proprietary Lock-in]]**: Dependence on specific platforms (e.g., [[entities/anthropic]], [[entities/openai]]) that may change access terms or model architectures.
- **Ephemeral Interfaces**: The loss of interactive capabilities when [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] are sunsetted.
- **Cost Prohibitions**: Rising costs associated with maintaining access to high-[[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] resources for preservation efforts.
