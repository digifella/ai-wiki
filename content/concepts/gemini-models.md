# Gemini models

[[entities/google|Google]]'s series of large language and [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] [[concepts/models|models]], designed for [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], [[concepts/code|code]] [[concepts/writing|writing]], [[concepts/image-analysis|image analysis]], and complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks. Key variants include [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] 1.0, [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 1.5, and [[entities/gemini-pro|Gemini Pro]].

**Key interfaces comparison**
* **[[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]] [[concepts/web-application|Web App]]** (gemini.[[concepts/google-search|google]].com): Consumer-focused [[concepts/chat-application|chat interface]] for everyday queries, quick summaries, and general assistance. Optimized for simplicity but limited in [[concepts/granular-control|advanced customization]].
* **[[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]]** (aistudio.google.com): Professional-grade platform for developers and [[concepts/power-users|power users]], enabling model [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]], [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]], [[concepts/integration|API integration]], and enterprise workflows. Offers superior versatility over the standard web app.

For a detailed comparison of interfaces and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], see: [AI studio and Gemini use case Grace Leung](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cgZYpwM-Tzg)
