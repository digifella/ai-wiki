# Survival Threshold

A survival threshold is the minimum set of conditions or capabilities required for an entity to persist and function within a given environment. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai]] [[concepts/simulation|simulation]], survival thresholds define the computational and performance boundaries that determine whether an agent remains active or is removed from a system. These thresholds establish the baseline requirements an agent must meet across multiple dimensions, including resource consumption, task completion rates, and [[concepts/acting|adaptive behavior]].

## Application in Digital Ecosystems

[[entities/sakana-ai|Sakana AI]]'s [[entities/digital-ecosystems]] research uses survival thresholds to study how simulated [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] compete and coexist within constrained computational environments. Agents that fail to meet performance requirements or exceed resource allocations may be eliminated, while those that satisfy threshold conditions continue operating.
