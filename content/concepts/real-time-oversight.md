# Real-Time Oversight in AI Development

Real-time oversight is a critical aspect of managing and deploying intelligent [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] at scale. It involves [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]] and analysis to ensure optimal performance, [[concepts/cost-optimization|cost efficiency]], and adherence to specified guidelines or objectives.

### Key Concepts
- **Continuous Monitoring:** The ongoing observation and evaluation of agent activities.
- **[[concepts/cost-optimization|Cost Optimization]]:** Techniques aimed at reducing expenses while maintaining high-quality outputs.
- **Performance Metrics:** Quantifiable [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] that measure the effectiveness and efficiency of [[concepts/agents|agents]].
- **Strategic Guidance:** Advice tailored to enhance operational strategies based on real-time data.
