# Proposal Assembler

A Proposal Assembler is an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] designed to construct comprehensive proposals by integrating information from multiple sources and formatting it according to specified requirements. Rather than generating content from scratch, the agent functions as an orchestrator that collects, synthesizes, and organizes existing materials into cohesive proposal documents.

## Core Operation

The Proposal Assembler retrieves relevant data from various repositories including databases, documents, [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]], and external systems. It identifies pertinent information based on proposal parameters, then organizes and formats this material according to templates or specifications provided by the user. This approach reduces redundant content creation and ensures consistency with existing organizational knowledge and standards.

## Key Characteristics

The agent typically operates within defined constraints such as structure requirements, compliance rules, and content guidelines. It may incorporate quality checks to verify completeness and coherence before delivery. By automating the assembly process, the Proposal Assembler reduces the time required to produce proposals while maintaining factual accuracy based on source materials rather than generated content.
