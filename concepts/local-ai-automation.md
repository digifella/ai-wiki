# Local AI Automation

Local [[concepts/ai-automation-agents|AI automation]] refers to the deployment and execution of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems within an organization's own infrastructure rather than relying on external [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]]. This approach prioritizes data [[concepts/security|security]], operational control, and independence from third-party service providers.

## Enterprise Implementation

Organizations implementing [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] automation typically use enterprise platforms designed to manage [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]—[[concepts/voice-assistants|autonomous systems]] that perform specific tasks with minimal human intervention. These platforms must balance automation capabilities with security requirements, as [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] means the organization assumes full [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] for system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] and [[concepts/internet-security|data protection]].

## Security Considerations

[[concepts/on-premise-deployment|Local deployment]] addresses vulnerabilities inherent in distributed or cloud-dependent [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. By keeping AI operations on-premises, enterprises can implement stricter access controls, maintain data residency [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and reduce [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to external [[concepts/security-concersns|security risks]]. This is particularly important for organizations handling sensitive information or operating in regulated industries.

## Platform Solutions

Modern local [[concepts/ai-automation-workflows|AI automation]] platforms provide frameworks for building, deploying, and managing [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] within [[concepts/secure|secure]] enterprise environments. These solutions typically include [[concepts/agent-toolkits|agent toolkits]], [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]] capabilities, and [[concepts/monitoring-systems|monitoring systems]] designed specifically for organizations requiring self-hosted infrastructure rather than [[concepts/saas|Software-as-a-Service]] models.
