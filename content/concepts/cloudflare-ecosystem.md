# Cloudflare Ecosystem

Cloudflare is a global content delivery network and cloud infrastructure platform that provides services for website performance, [[concepts/security|security]], and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]]. The company operates a broad ecosystem of interconnected tools designed to address web performance, DDoS protection, [[concepts/dns|DNS]] management, and application security. These services operate across Cloudflare's distributed network of data centers worldwide.

## Email Service Beta

Cloudflare's [[concepts/email-service-beta|Email Service Beta]] represents an expansion into email infrastructure and delivery. The service integrates email sending, routing, and [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. It incorporates AI-driven functionality to assist with [[concepts/email-management|email management]] and processing tasks. The beta was announced in April 2026 as part of Cloudflare's broader platform expansion.

The Email Service integrates with Cloudflare's existing infrastructure, allowing users to manage email delivery through the same platform used for other web services. This positions email handling as a complementary service within the larger Cloudflare ecosystem rather than as a standalone offering.
