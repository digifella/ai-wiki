## Lean Launcher: Streamlining Obsidian Quick Capture and Windows Workflow
**Clip title:** Obsidian Quick Capture Just Became Real
**Author / channel:** LeanProductivity - Sascha D. Kasper
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zlFTFUIkKU8

### Summary
This video introduces "[[concepts/lean-launcher|Lean Launcher]]," a new Windows app launcher designed to streamline workflow, particularly for Obsidian users. It addresses the common frustration of context switching and breaking focus when attempting to capture spontaneous ideas, tasks, or log entries, or when trying to quickly find notes. Lean Launcher aims to provide a fast, [[concepts/keyboard-centric-interface|keyboard-centric interface]] that integrates deeply with Obsidian while also offering general system utilities, minimizing disruption and enhancing productivity.

Lean Launcher's primary strength lies in its [[concepts/quick-capture|quick capture]] capabilities for Obsidian. Users can instantly add tasks (using a `t` prefix), general notes (with an `a` prefix), or timestamped log entries (via an `l` prefix) to their Obsidian vault's daily note. This process is remarkably seamless, functioning even when Obsidian is minimized or completely closed, and automatically creating daily notes if they don't already exist, all while respecting Obsidian's configured date and folder formats. The logging feature is particularly highlighted as a swift alternative to traditional journaling, allowing for rapid record-keeping that can later be expanded into more detailed journal entries.

Beyond its robust Obsidian integration, Lean Launcher also functions as a versatile system utility. It enables users to quickly launch any installed application, perform fuzzy searches for files across their computer, conduct web searches using a configurable default search engine, and even execute quick calculations directly from its minimalist interface. The developer emphasizes Lean Launcher's "lean" philosophy, distinguishing it from other feature-rich but potentially resource-heavy launchers. By focusing on essential functionalities, it remains light on system resources and prioritizes a keyboard-driven user experience.

The application is highly customizable, allowing users to define their own global hotkeys to summon the launcher, personalize search prefixes for different categories (files, web, apps), and adjust Obsidian-specific settings, including the target vault and specific daily note formatting overrides. Installation is notably simple, requiring only a direct download of the executable file without any complex setup procedures, sign-ups, or account registrations. This emphasis on [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], with all operations remaining local to the user's machine, combined with its compact size (approximately 500KB executable) and low RAM usage (less than 10MB when running), positions Lean Launcher as an efficient and secure tool. The video also briefly introduces "Lean Terminal," an Obsidian plugin that further enhances workflow by integrating a native terminal directly within Obsidian, eliminating the need to alt-tab for command-line operations.
