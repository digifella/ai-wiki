# Eukaryotic cells

Cells characterized by a membrane-bound nucleus and specialized organelles, distinguishing them from Prokaryote.

## Evolutionary Origins
- The [[concepts/endosymbiosis-hypothesis|Endosymbiosis hypothesis]] proposes that the formation of complex eukaryotes resulted from the symbiotic integration of distinct cellular lineages.
- Recent breakthroughs highlight the role of [[concepts/asgard-archaea]] in the evolutionary transition toward Complex Life.
- Research is currently focused on recreating the "first [[entities/contact|contact]]" events between archaeal and bacterial lineages that drove this complexity.
