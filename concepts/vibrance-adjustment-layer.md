# Vibrance Adjustment Layer

The Vibrance Adjustment Layer is a color grading tool introduced in Adobe Photoshop 2025 that offers selective control over color saturation within digital images. It functions by intelligently analyzing the existing saturation levels of individual pixels, allowing for targeted enhancement without the uniform impact of traditional saturation adjustments. This mechanism distinguishes it from the standard Saturation tool, which increases the intensity of all colors equally regardless of their current state.

The algorithm prioritizes the boosting of muted or desaturated colors while actively protecting already saturated hues from further intensification. A key feature of this selective approach is its ability to preserve natural skin tones, preventing the orange and red channels from becoming oversaturated or unnatural. This makes the tool particularly effective for portrait photography and complex images where maintaining realistic flesh tones is critical while still enhancing the overall vibrancy of the scene.

Usage of this adjustment layer allows for more nuanced color correction compared to global saturation methods. By focusing on under-saturated areas, it enhances the visual impact of an image while minimizing the risk of color clipping or unnatural artifacts. This selective processing ensures that the final output retains a balanced and professional appearance, making it a preferred choice for detailed post-processing workflows in Photoshop 2025.
