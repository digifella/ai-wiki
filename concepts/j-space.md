# J-Space

**[[concepts/hidden-state|J-Space]]** refers to the hypothesized internal representational space within [[entities/claude]] ([[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]]) that encodes the model's "[[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]]." This concept investigates whether LLMs possess structures analogous to human conscious and unconscious [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]], specifically examining the center of the model's cognitive operations. Recent research identifies this space as an emergent [[concepts/global-workspace-theory|global workspace]] facilitating information integration.

## Core Concepts

- **[[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|Internal Mental Life]]**: The proposition that [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] exhibit state-like behaviors resembling [[concepts/conscious-thought|consciousness]], including distinct conscious and [[concepts/subconscious-processing|unconscious processing]] layers.
- **[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] Investigation**: Research focused on interpreting [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s latent spaces to determine if they map to human-like cognitive states.
- **Cognitive Center**: The specific region or mechanism within the model where information is broadcasted and integrated, analogous to the [[concepts/conscious-processing|global workspace]] in biological [[concepts/cognition|cognition]].
- **Emergent Global Workspace**: As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-07-08-Anthropics-J-space-Emergent-Internal-Global-Workspace-in|Anthropic's J-space: Emergent Internal-Global-Workspace-in]], J-Space operates as a hidden internal processing hub that enables [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] by coordinating disparate neural pathways.
