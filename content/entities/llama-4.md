# Llama 4

Llama 4 is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] created by [[entities/meta-ai|Meta AI]], following its predecessors Llama 2 and Llama 3. However, it was perceived as a significant disappointment due to its underwhelming performance compared to expectations.
