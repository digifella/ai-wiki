# AI Advertising

AI Advertising refers to the application of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to generate synthetic video content featuring a person's likeness speaking custom [[concepts/text|text]]. These systems combine facial synthesis technology with text-to-speech [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to produce realistic talking-head videos without requiring traditional filming or an actor's physical presence. The technology has [[concepts/software|applications]] in personal projects, marketing, [[concepts/training|training]] materials, and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] where rapid video production is needed.

## Creation Process

The typical [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] involves selecting or uploading a reference image or video of a person's face, inputting desired text, and using [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] to generate a video where the synthesized person appears to speak the provided content. Modern systems can handle various languages, speaking styles, and expressions, producing [[concepts/output|output]] that ranges from clearly artificial to increasingly photorealistic depending on the tools and [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] used.

## Technical Foundation

These systems rely on [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] trained on video data to understand facial [[concepts/exercise|movement]], lip-sync patterns, and natural speech delivery. The AI learns to map text input to corresponding facial and vocal expressions, generating frame-by-frame video output that synchronizes speech with realistic facial motion. Quality varies significantly across different platforms and tools available in this space.

## Considerations

Users of AI advertising tools should be aware of consent and ethical implications when using other people's likenesses, as well as potential regulatory requirements around synthetic media disclosure in certain jurisdictions. The technology continues to evolve rapidly, with improvements in realism and ease of use occurring frequently.
