# Matthew Be

Matthew Be is a [[concepts/developer|developer]] and [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] associated with [[concepts/local-ai-assistants|Clawdbot]], an [[concepts/self-hosted-ai-assistant|open-source personal AI assistant]] platform. He has created educational content demonstrating the [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and setup of the system, including video tutorials that explain how [[concepts/remote-chat|Clawdbot]] functions as a self-hosted alternative to commercial AI assistants.

## Clawdbot

Clawdbot is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], self-hosted personal [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] that Be has promoted as a practical alternative to proprietary solutions. The platform is designed to run on users' own infrastructure, giving them [[concepts/power|control]] over their data and the underlying AI system. Be's content focuses on making the technical aspects of deploying and using such systems accessible to a broader audience.
