# AI Powered Communication

AI-powered communication refers to the integration of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] technologies into communication platforms and workflows, particularly through the use of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]. [[entities/salesforce|Salesforce]] CEO [[entities/marc-benioff|Marc Benioff]] has positioned this approach as central to the company's strategy for transforming how organizations conduct business. The strategy centers on connecting [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] with communication infrastructure, notably through [[entities/slack|Slack]], to enable more efficient and intelligent interactions between systems, teams, and external parties.

## Integration with Slack and Agents

Salesforce's approach involves deploying [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]] within Slack, its popular enterprise communication platform. This integration allows organizations to embed [[concepts/ai-in-robotics|intelligent automation]] directly into their existing communication workflows rather than requiring separate, disconnected tools. The agents can handle routine tasks, provide information, and facilitate collaboration within the communication environment where teams already work.

## Implications for Work Transformation

The widespread [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of AI-powered communication is expected to significantly transform how work is performed across organizations. By automating communication-related tasks and enabling intelligent agents to participate in workplace interactions, companies can potentially increase [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and shift human [[entities/employees|workers]] toward higher-value activities. However, this transformation also raises questions about the appropriate role of AI in workplace [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and the need for [[concepts/organizational-adaptation|organizational adaptation]] to new hybrid human-AI workflows.

## Regulatory Considerations

As AI-powered communication systems become more prevalent, questions of [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] and oversight have become increasingly important. Industry leaders like Benioff have engaged with discussions about appropriate [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks, including concerns around [[concepts/ai-scapegoating|AI scapegoating]] and the need for balanced regulatory approaches that neither stifle [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] nor leave legitimate risks unaddressed.
