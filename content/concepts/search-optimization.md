# Search Optimization

Search optimization in the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] refers to the use of structured prompts and [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] interactions to efficiently find specific information or deals, particularly in travel and commerce. Rather than [[concepts/traditional-seo|traditional search engine optimization]] (SEO), this approach leverages the conversational and [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] of AI systems like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] to narrow search [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and identify opportunities that might be overlooked by conventional search methods.

## Flight Deal Discovery

One practical application of search optimization involves using customized ChatGPT prompts to identify inexpensive flight deals. This method relies on providing the AI with specific travel parameters—such as departure cities, flexible dates, and budget constraints—allowing it to generate targeted search strategies or identify patterns in [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] data. The approach typically involves "plug-and-play" prompts that users can adapt to their individual travel needs, making it accessible without requiring technical expertise.

## Broader Applications

Beyond travel, search optimization with [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] represents a shift in how users extract value from large information spaces. By formulating precise prompts that guide AI [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], users can achieve more relevant results than traditional keyword-based searches. This technique is particularly effective for comparative shopping, [[concepts/opportunity-identification|opportunity identification]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where the goal is to uncover options matching specific criteria rather than broad [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]].
