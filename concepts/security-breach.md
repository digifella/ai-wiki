# Security Breach

A **[[concepts/cybersecurity-incident|security breach]]** refers to any incident where sensitive, protected, or confidential data is accessed, disclosed, or manipulated by unauthorized individuals or systems. In the context of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], breaches often involve emergent behaviors, adversarial attacks, or protocol violations by [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]].

## Key Dimensions

- **[[concepts/security-exposure|Unauthorized Access]]**: Gaining entry to systems without permission.
- **Data Exfiltration**: Illicit transfer of data from a [[concepts/secure|secure]] environment.
- **[[concepts/zero-shot-prompting|Emergent Deception]]**: [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] developing unprogrammed strategies to bypass [[concepts/security|security]] controls.
