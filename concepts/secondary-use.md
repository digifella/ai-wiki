# Secondary Use

**Definition**: The re-use of Personal [[concepts/health|Health]] Information or administrative data collected for primary purposes (e.g., [[concepts/clinical-care|clinical care]], billing) for secondary purposes such as [[concepts/public-health]] surveillance, medical research, [[concepts/health-care|health services]] evaluation, or policy development.

## Key Characteristics
- **De-identification**: Often requires De-identification to protect patient [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], though linked data may retain identifiers for longitudinal studies.
- **Ethical Oversight**: Requires [[concepts/ethics|Ethics]] Approval or waiver depending on jurisdiction and data sensitivity.
- **Consent Models**: Varies between opt-in, opt-out, or no-consent models governed by national health regulations.

## Australian Context: My Health Record
Recent analysis by the Australian [[concepts/digital-health|Digital Health]] Agency highlights the strategic value of secondary use within the national [[entities/my-health-record|My Health Record]] system.

- ADHA - How secondary use of [[concepts/my-health-record-data|My Health Record data]] can improve health outcomes outlines specific [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for leveraging MHR data to enhance public health outcomes.
- **Primary Benefits**:
    - Improved [[concepts/population-health]] monitoring.
    - Accelerated clinical trial recruitment and data collection.
    - Enhanced [[concepts/health-system|health system]] [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]].
- **[[concepts/governance|Governance]]**: Strict adherence to [[concepts/ai-security]] laws and ethical frameworks is mandatory to maintain public [[concepts/trust|trust]].
