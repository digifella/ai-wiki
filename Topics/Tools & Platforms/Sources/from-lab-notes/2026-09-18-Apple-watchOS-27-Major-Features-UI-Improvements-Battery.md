## Apple watchOS 27: Major Features, UI Improvements, Battery Optimizations
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OFq4gA_xPMc

### Summary
[[entities/apple|Apple]] has released [[concepts/watchos-27|watchOS 27]], a significant software update for compatible Apple Watch models, including the [[entities/apple-watch-se-3|Apple Watch SE 3]], Series 9-12, and Ultra 2-4. Older models are no longer supported. The update, which can be installed via the iPhone's Watch app or directly on the watch, aims to enhance overall user experience, performance, and battery life through various improvements and new features.

A notable visual enhancement is the "[[concepts/liquid-glass|Liquid Glass]]" update, which brings improved legibility and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] to the display. To optimize battery life, "Proactive Battery Notifications" intelligently disable rarely used features like certain gestures or Siri's "raise to speak" when not actively in use. The update introduces a new "Siri Modular" watch face, offering extensive [[concepts/customization|customization]] options for colors, styles, fonts, and complications. Furthermore, the App Grid has been redesigned to prioritize recently used applications, and "Smart Stacks" now feature a broader range of intelligent widgets, such as Siri App suggestions, Parked Car information, Birthday messages, and Transit cards, all accessible with a new double-tap gesture.

Communication and utility functions also receive substantial upgrades. Notifications now offer more granular control, allowing users to mute alerts for specific durations or disable time-sensitive notifications. Phone calls on the watch benefit from contextual information, like displaying airline ticket details or Apple order information when calling relevant services. The "Find My" application has been consolidated into a single, map-centric interface, simplifying the process of locating people, devices, and items. A major overhaul to Siri's AI provides broader world knowledge, improved contextual understanding, and the ability to perform actions within various apps, with conversations syncing across all Apple devices (though initially unavailable in the EU/China). The Health app expands its tracking capabilities with new support for perimenopause and menopause stages. However, the Walkie-Talkie app has been removed from watchOS 27.

For families, watchOS 27 introduces "Apple Watch for Kids," replacing the previous Family Setup with enhanced child accounts and robust parental controls. These controls allow parents to manage contacts, set app usage limits, and restrict web content to only approved websites, with a dedicated Apple website detailing these safety features. The Wallet app gains a "Create a Pass" feature, enabling users to generate custom digital tickets, membership cards, and event passes with QR codes. General performance enhancements include faster music playback on the Apple Watch and improved synchronization of fitness data with the Health app, which itself now supports a landscape view on the iPhone.

In conclusion, watchOS 27 is a comprehensive update that significantly refines the Apple Watch experience. By focusing on both prominent new features and crucial background improvements, Apple enhances the device's usability, performance, and battery efficiency. The update reinforces the Apple Watch's role as an intelligent, secure, and health-focused companion, deeply integrated into the wider Apple ecosystem.
