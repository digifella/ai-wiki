Strategy: Open Source, Governance, and Physical AI

Summary

  • Nvidia adopts open-source strategies for LLMs like Nemotron-3 Ultra and Nemotron 3.5 ASR.
  • Focus on Physical AI through Cosmos 3 and enterprise agents via NHDemoClaw.
  • Strategic partnership with Apple for on-device AI capabilities.
  • Engagement with HIL: Hybrid AI for Adaptive Human-like Dynamic Athletic Control to address limitations in dynamic athletic control (e.g., parkour) where simple imitation fails.

Recent Developments & Research

  • Computex 2026: Jensen Huang unveiled Vera Rubin infrastructure and declared the era of agentic AI.
  • HIL Research: Analysis of HIL: Hybrid AI for Adaptive Human-like Dynamic Athletic Control highlights the need for adaptive control in dynamic athletic tasks, moving beyond static human imitation.
  • Geopolitics: Monitoring competition from China’s Nvidia-free large models and managing supply chain volatility.
  • Technical Exploration: Investigating diffusion models for text generation via the TwoTower architecture and developing Audex-2B for compact audio-text integration.
