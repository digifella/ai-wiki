### Summary
Sure! Here's a summary of the video content:

Loop engineering, a new evolution of prompt engineering, designs systems that perform prompting tasks for the user. It involves creating a loop consisting of a goal, action, observation, and adjustment. The goal should be specific with an end condition. A schedule or hook can trigger the loop.

Context engineering involves choosing what passes the loop can see, such as through compaction or the progressive disclosure of agent skills. A spine stores persistent state, and agents use tools to execute tasks. Subagents act as checkers and co-workers.

While loop engineering requires minimal human involvement, humans are still needed to write goals and intent, and read output. The four pitfalls of loop engineering are unverified code, comprehension debt, intent debt, and cognitive surrender. Loop engineering works best with a human in the loop.
