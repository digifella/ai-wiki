# Dark Energy Survey

The **[[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|Dark Energy]] [[concepts/survey|Survey]] (DES)** is a collaborative effort to map the southern sky to understand the nature of dark energy and the [[concepts/accelerating-expansion-of-the-universe|accelerating expansion of the universe]]. Recent data from DES, alongside findings from the **Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI)**, has challenged the standard cosmological model by suggesting that dark energy may not be a constant but rather evolves over time.

## Key Findings & Recent Updates

- **Reassessment of Expansion:** New analyses indicate the universe's expansion history may differ from previous assumptions, [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] a re-evaluation of the Lambda-CDM model.
- **DES/DESI Synergy:** Combined data from DES and DESI provides tighter constraints on the equation of state of dark energy ($w$), hinting at potential deviations from a [[concepts/type-ia-supernovae|cosmological constant]].
