# Base model weights

The [[concepts/model-weights|learned parameters]] (numerical tensors) within a [[concepts/neural-network]] or [[concepts/large-language-model]] (LLM) that are optimized during the Pre-[[concepts/training-process|training process]]. These [[concepts/weights|weights]] encode the patterns, linguistic structures, and factual information captured from the training corpus.

## Core Principles
- **Knowledge [[entities/storage|Storage]]**: The [[concepts/weights|weights]] act as the foundational repository of learned information within the [[concepts/architecturetechnique|Model Architecture]].
- **[[concepts/inference-engine|Inference Engine]]**: During [[concepts/inference]], input vectors are transformed through matrix multiplications with these [[concepts/parameters|weights]] to produce outputs.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]**: Base weights serve as the starting point for [[concepts/fine-tuning]], [[concepts/instruction-tuning]], and Parameter-Efficient [[concepts/fine-tuning|Fine-Tuning]] ([[concepts/parameter-efficient-adaptation|PEFT]]) techniques.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: The availability of [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] weights, such as those in the [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] V4 suite, drives [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in the [[concepts/open-source]] ecosystem.

## Recent Developments
