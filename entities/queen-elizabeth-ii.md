# Queen Elizabeth II

**Elizabeth II** (Elizabeth Alexandra Mary; 21 April 1926 – 8 September 2022) was Queen of the United Kingdom and the other Commonwealth realms from 6 February 1952 until her death in 2022. She was the longest-reigning and oldest-ever monarch in UK history.

## Key Context: Infrastructure & Connectivity
While her reign focused heavily on constitutional stability and diplomatic relations, the era saw significant infrastructural developments connecting the UK to continental Europe, impacting trade and travel.

*   The [[concepts/channel-tunnel]] (often referred to as the "Chunnel") was a major [[concepts/engineering-marvel|engineering project]] completed during her reign, facilitating rail links between the UK and France.
