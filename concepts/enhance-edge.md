# Enhance Edge

**Enhance Edge** is an AI-powered feature in [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] designed to refine selection boundaries, specifically targeting complex textures like hair and fur. It addresses common issues such as [[concepts/halos|halos]] and jagged edges, improving the realism of composites and masks.

## Key Developments

- **Feature Introduction**: Debuted in [[concepts/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] 27.11.0, this tool leverages [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to interpret edge details more accurately than traditional [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] techniques.
- **[[concepts/hair-masking|Hair Masking]]**: Specifically optimized for fine strands, solving long-standing difficulties in isolating hair against complex backgrounds.
- **Halo Removal**: Automatically detects and eliminates the white or colored fringing often seen around subject edges after masking.
- **[[concepts/generative-credits|Generative Credits]]**: Usage of this AI capability consumes Generative Credits, impacting the user's monthly allowance.
