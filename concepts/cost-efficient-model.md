# cost-efficient model

A cost-efficient model refers to an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] architecture or deployment strategy that maximizes [[concepts/output-quality|output quality]] and utility while minimizing computational, financial, and operational expenses. In the context of the 2026 [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]], efficiency is no longer just about [[concepts/inference-speed|inference speed]] but also about proactive integration and reduced latency.

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/gpt-6-astra|GPT-6 Astra]] Advancements**: The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[entities/gpt-6]] marked a significant shift toward ultrafast [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] capabilities, directly impacting the cost-per-token metric for high-volume applications.
*   **Proactive [[concepts/ai-integration|AI Integration]]**: [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s 2026 Dev Day highlighted "[[concepts/ai-workflow|Proactive AI]]" agents (referred to as "[[concepts/dots-agent|Dots]]") that reduce user overhead by anticipating needs, effectively lowering the human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] cost.
*   **Workflow Integration**: Emphasis on deep integration into personal and professional workflows ensures that the model's utility justifies its operational cost through automation of [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]].
