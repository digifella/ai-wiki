# Dr David Sinclair

Dr. [[entities/david|David]] Sinclair is a researcher who has contributed to discussions on aging and longevity. He has examined the relationship between psychological attitudes toward aging and [[concepts/health|health]] outcomes in older adults.

## Ageism and Longevity

Sinclair's work [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] how societal attitudes toward aging may [[concepts/power|influence]] individual health and lifespan. He has argued that ageism—discrimination based on age—can have measurable negative effects on older people's [[concepts/physical-health|physical health]] and mortality rates. His research suggests that challenging ageist stereotypes and beliefs about aging may contribute to better health outcomes and longer lives.

## Research Focus

His work emphasizes that aging itself is not inherently destructive, but rather how people perceive and respond to aging can significantly impact their [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] and longevity. This perspective bridges psychological and biological approaches to understanding human aging.
