## DeepMind’s AI Just Did The Impossible
**Clip title:** DeepMind’s AI Just Did The Impossible
**Author / channel:** Two Minute Papers
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gPNiuyYirfw
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Two Minute Papers: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video discusses a new research paper from DeepMind called "AlphaProof Code" that used AI to prove that the messaging protocol 'libsignal' is secure. The speaker explains that while the mathematical algorithms behind messaging apps like Signal and WhatsApp can be proven, the code itself often cannot. 

To address this, DeepMind used the Lean programming language and a team of AI agents to generate 6.5 million lines of mathematical proofs. The AI successfully proved that the software encrypts and decrypts messages correctly, that compromised encryption keys do not reveal older messages, and that the system can recover its security after compromises. 

The video notes that while the work has limitations—such as not covering group chats or low-level cryptography—it represents a major step forward for AI in verifying code security. The speaker concludes that having verifiably secure computer code is a "game changer" for the future.
