# Unsolved Problems

## Overview
Unsolved problems are questions that have not yet been answered or resolved by the scientific community despite significant effort. They often represent critical challenges across various fields of study, including [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/physics|physics]], and computer science.

### Summary
The [[concepts/p-vs-np-problem|P vs NP problem]] is one of the most significant unsolved questions in [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and computer science, carrying a [[concepts/million-dollar-prize|million-dollar prize]] for its [[concepts/solution|solution]]. It fundamentally asks whether problems whose solutions can be quickly verified can also be quickly solved.

- **Key Concepts:**
  - P (complexity)
  - NP (complexity)
  - computational-complexity-[[concepts/theory|theory]]

- **Implications:** A definitive answer could revolutionize fields from medicine to [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]].

- **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]:** First proposed in the early days of computational [[concepts/theory|theory]], it remains a cornerstone unsolved problem.
