# Mens Health

Men's health encompasses the physical, mental, and social [[concepts/health|wellbeing]] of male populations across the lifespan. Key health concerns for men often include cardiovascular disease, [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] disorders, and [[concepts/preventive-care|preventive care]] uptake, which tends to be lower among men compared to women. Health services and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] initiatives increasingly recognize the need to address gender-specific health needs and remove barriers to men's engagement with healthcare systems.

## Paternal Health and Birth Experiences

Research indicates that approximately 30% of men attend births, yet their experiences and wellbeing during this significant event remain under-addressed in many healthcare settings. Traumatic birth experiences can affect fathers' mental health and their adjustment to parenthood. Enhancing [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]] for fathers—including psychological support, education, and inclusion in perinatal care—has been identified as an important area for improvement in maternity services. Recognizing fathers as participants rather than passive observers can improve outcomes for both parents and infants.
