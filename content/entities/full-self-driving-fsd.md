# Full Self Driving (FSD)

Full Self Driving (FSD) is Tesla's advanced driver assistance system that aims to enable autonomous vehicle operation. The system relies on [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] and [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] to perceive the vehicle's environment and make driving decisions across various road conditions and [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].

## Computational Architecture

A key [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in FSD's development involves [[concepts/algorithm-optimization|optimization techniques]] for [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] processing. Tesla has patented computational methods that enhance the efficiency of [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] calculations by converting multiplication operations into addition operations through the use of logarithms. This approach reduces computational overhead and allows for faster processing of the large datasets required for real-time autonomous driving decisions.

The optimization techniques are particularly important given the complexity of the visual and sensory processing tasks that FSD must perform, from [[concepts/object-detection|object detection]] to trajectory planning. By streamlining these mathematical operations, Tesla aims to improve both the speed and [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] of its autonomous systems.
