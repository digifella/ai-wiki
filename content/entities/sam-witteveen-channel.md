# Sam Witteveen Channel

The Sam Witteveen Channel is a YouTube channel dedicated to covering developments in [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|artificial intelligence models]] and tools. The channel produces technical content focused on practical [[concepts/software|applications]] and demonstrations of emerging [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]].

## Qwen3-TTS Coverage

The channel has featured detailed information about the Qwen3-TTS family of models, an open-source text-to-speech system released by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]]. Content on the channel explores the capabilities and features of these [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|TTS models]], including [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] and [[concepts/customization|customization]] options available within the framework.
