# Unified Multimodal Models

Architectures designed to process, interpret, and generate multiple data modalities within a single, cohesive framework, enabling seamless cross-modal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Modal [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Unification of disparate data streams, including [[concepts/text]], [[concepts/images]], and [[concepts/audio]], into a shared latent space.
- **Cross-modal [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]**: The ability to perform complex [[concepts/inference|inference]] and derive semantic [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] across different input types.
- **Agentic Foundation**: Providing the cognitive engine for [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to perceive and interact with multi-sensory environments.

## Recent Developments
- NVIDIA [[entities/nemotron-3-nano-omni|Nemotron 3 Nano Omni]]: Unified [[concepts/multimodal-ai|Multimodal AI]] [[entities/agent|Agent]] Model Overview: A transformative "all-in-one" model specifically engineered for [[concepts/agentic-ai]], unifying [[concepts/text]], [[concepts/images]], and [[concepts/audio]] modalities into a single [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].
