# Sam Altman

Sam Altman is the Chief Executive Officer of OpenAI, an artificial intelligence research company. He assumed the role of CEO in 2019 and has led the organization through a period of significant growth and public visibility in the generative AI field. Under his leadership, OpenAI has developed and deployed major AI systems, including ChatGPT and DALL-E, which have become widely used applications in the AI landscape.

## Career and Background

Before becoming CEO of OpenAI, Altman was known for his work in the technology startup ecosystem. He served as president of Y Combinator, the influential startup accelerator, where he oversaw investments in hundreds of technology companies. His experience in identifying and supporting emerging technology ventures informed his approach to leading OpenAI during its expansion.

## Leadership at OpenAI

As CEO, Altman has shaped OpenAI's strategy regarding AI development, deployment, and business models. He has been a public spokesperson for the company, commenting on developments in generative AI technology and the company's approach to AI safety and responsible deployment. His tenure has coincided with OpenAI's transition from a research-focused organization to one with significant commercial products and partnerships.
