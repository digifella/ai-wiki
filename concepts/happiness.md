# Happiness

**Definition:** Happiness is a subjective emotional state characterized by feelings of joy, satisfaction, contentment, and fulfillment. It can be influenced by various factors such as personal achievements, [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]], and life circumstances.
