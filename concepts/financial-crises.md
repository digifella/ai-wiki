# Financial Crises

## Overview
Financial crises are severe disturbances in [[entities/wall-street|financial markets]] that disrupt normal economic activities and can lead to widespread negative effects on the [[concepts/economic-system|economy]], including business [[concepts/fastening-devices|closures]], high unemployment rates, and reductions in trade and credit availability.

### Key Concepts
- **Banking Failures:** Occurrences when banks cannot meet their obligations due to insolvency or a run on deposits.
- **Stock Market Crashes:** A sudden and significant decline in stock prices over a period of days or weeks.
- **Recessions:** Periods where economic activity is reduced, characterized by higher unemployment rates and falling incomes.

### Historical Examples
- 1929 Great [[concepts/depression|Depression]]
- 2008 Global Financial [[concepts/crisis|Crisis]]
