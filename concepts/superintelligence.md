# Superintelligence

## Definition
A hypothetical [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] with cognitive capabilities far surpassing the brightest human minds in virtually every field, including scientific creativity, general wisdom, and social [[concepts/skills|skills]].

## Key Dynamics in Superintelligence Development

### Compute Access and Economic Reality
The path to superintelligence is heavily constrained by the economics of [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] and hardware access. Recent discourse highlights a tension between open empowerment and centralized control:

- **Zuckerberg's [[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]**: [[entities/mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] advocates for a future where [[concepts/ai-advancement|AI advancement]] is democratized, arguing that the benefits of superintelligence should be accessible to everyone rather than monopolized by a few [[concepts/nodes|entities]] [[lab-notes/2026-08-12-Zuckerbergs-AI-Vision-Empowerment-vs.-Economic-Reality-o|Zuckerberg's AI Vision: Empowerment vs. Economic Reality of Compute Access]].
- **Critique of Centralization**: This vision directly challenges the current trajectory of major [[entities/labs|labs]] like [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] and Dario AI, which rely on massive, closed compute clusters. Critics argue that the economic reality of [[concepts/compute-scarcity|compute scarcity]] favors incumbents, potentially stifling the very "empowerment" Zuckerberg claims to support.
- **Implications for AGI/Superintelligence**: If compute remains a bottleneck controlled by a few corporations, the development of superintelligence may not lead to broad societal benefit but rather to increased concentration of power.
