# Tabby's Star

**[[concepts/tabbys-star|Tabby's Star]]** ([[concepts/kic-8462852|KIC 8462852]]) is a main-sequence star in the constellation Cygnus, famous for its irregular and significant dimming events that have puzzled astronomers for over a decade.

*   **[[concepts/irregular-dimming|Irregular Dimming]]:** Exhibits non-periodic, deep dips in brightness that do not fit standard planetary transit models.
