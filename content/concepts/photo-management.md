# Photo Management

Photo management is a system for organizing, cataloging, and processing digital photographs efficiently. Adobe Lightroom, particularly Lightroom Classic, serves as the primary tool for implementing a structured photo management workflow. A [[concepts/catalog|Lightroom catalog]] functions as a centralized database that tracks all photos, their [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], and editing history, allowing photographers to maintain control over large image libraries without moving or duplicating files on disk.

## Workflow and Organization

Effective photo management in Lightroom involves establishing consistent cataloging practices and utilizing the [[concepts/software|software]]'s organizational features. The application enables photographers to apply ratings, flags, and keyword metadata to [[concepts/images|images]], making it possible to sort and retrieve specific photos quickly. This systematic approach is particularly valuable when working with large volumes of images from multiple shoots or projects.

## Modern Features and Enhancement Tools

Contemporary versions of Lightroom Classic include AI-powered features that streamline both the organizational and editing aspects of photo management. [[concepts/portrait-masking|Automated mask presets]], powered by machine [[concepts/learning|learning]], can identify and select specific elements like landscapes or people with minimal user input. Additional capabilities such as background processing and AI-assisted enhancements allow photographers to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] efficiently across multiple images while maintaining consistent creative control over their edits.
