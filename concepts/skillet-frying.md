# Skillet Frying
Skillet frying is a common method of cooking food in a pan with oil or fat over direct heat. It's versatile and can be used for various ingredients like meat, vegetables, and even breakfast items such as bacon.

## Key Techniques
- **[[concepts/thermal-regulation|Temperature Control]]:** Essential for achieving the desired [[concepts/texture|texture]] and flavor.
- **[[concepts/oil-types|Oil Selection]]:** The type of oil used affects both taste and cooking performance.
- **Pan Material:** Different materials (e.g., cast iron, stainless steel) influence heat distribution and [[concepts/storing|retention]].
- **Seasoning:** Enhances flavors during cooking.
