## Diffusion of Innovations

**Definition:** The process by which an [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] is communicated through certain channels over time among the members of a social system.

### Key Concepts
- **Innovation Thresholds**: The stages individuals go through as they adopt new [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] ([[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]], interest, evaluation, trial, [[concepts/adoption|adoption]]).
- **Perceived Attributes**: Relative advantage, compatibility, complexity, trialability, observability.
- **Communication Channels**: Personal communication, mass media, social networks.
- **Social Systems**: Rural, urban, and organizational contexts.
