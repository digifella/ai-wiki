# Subscription Based Services

Subscription-based services have become a dominant business model in digital media and [[concepts/software|software]], with companies like Netflix, [[concepts/movie-streaming|streaming platforms]], and cloud software providers generating recurring revenue from users paying monthly or annual fees. This model provides predictable income for service providers while allowing consumers to access content or tools without large upfront purchases.

## Self-Hosting as an Alternative

An emerging counter-trend involves users building their own media servers using affordable [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] to replace commercial subscription services. A [[entities/ubuntu|Ubuntu]]-based [[concepts/media-server|media server]] setup can be constructed for under $100, enabling individuals to host and stream their own content libraries locally. This approach eliminates monthly subscription costs for services like Netflix while providing greater control over content availability and playback.

## Practical Considerations

Self-hosted solutions require technical knowledge for installation and maintenance, including server setup, network configuration, and ongoing updates. The initial hardware investment is low, but users must account for electricity costs and the time investment in managing the system. For users with modest streaming needs and basic technical competency, local media servers can offer significant long-term savings compared to maintaining multiple subscription services.
