# Smart TV

## Overview
A television set fitted with an integrated operating system and connectivity features, allowing users to access internet-based content, applications, and streaming services.

## Business Model Evolution
Modern Smart TV hardware economics have shifted significantly from traditional manufacturing margins to data-driven revenue streams.

- **Subsidized Hardware**: The affordability of modern smart TVs is not driven by hardware innovation, but by a fundamental shift in business models Smart TV Data Collection and Shifting Business Models.
- **Data as Currency**: Manufacturers attribute low purchase prices to the collection of user viewing data, which is monetized through advertising and analytics Smart TV Data Collection and Shifting Business Models.
- **Historical Context**: Unlike 15 years ago when TVs were high-margin luxury items costing thousands, today's devices are often sold at or near cost to establish a persistent data collection endpoint in the home.
