# Simon d'Entremont

**Simon d'Entremont** is a professional photographer and content creator known for challenging conventional photography workflows. He advocates for the strategic use of [[concepts/semi-automatic-camera-modes|semi-automatic camera modes]] over exclusive reliance on manual settings, arguing that this approach better serves professional efficiency and creative intent.

## Key Contributions & Philosophy

*   **Strategic Semi-Automatic Modes:** Promotes the use of semi-automatic modes (such as [[concepts/aperture|Aperture]] Priority or Shutter Priority) as the primary workflow for 95% of professional scenarios, rather than full manual mode.
*   **Efficiency over Rigidity:** Argues that mastering manual settings is important for foundational knowledge but often inefficient for real-world shooting conditions where speed and adaptability are critical.
*   **Educational Content:** Produces video content aimed at demystifying camera settings and optimizing professional workflows.
