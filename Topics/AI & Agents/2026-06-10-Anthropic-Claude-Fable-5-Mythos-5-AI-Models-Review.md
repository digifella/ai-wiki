## Anthropic Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 AI Models Review
**Clip title:** [[entities/mythos|MYTHOS]] MYTHOS MYTHOS
**Author / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ou-0vjl6FZo

### Summary
The video provides a detailed breakdown and review of [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s newly released [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], [[entities/fable-5|Claude Fable 5]] and [[concepts/ai-benchmarks|Claude Mythos]] 5. The presenter, who had early access, introduces Fable 5 as a "mythos-class" model made safe for general use, while [[entities/mythos-5|Mythos 5]] is its uncensored counterpart given to the [[concepts/security|security]] community for hardening [[concepts/software|software]]. He asserts that this new generation of AI is "absolutely a new class of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]," performing exceptionally well across various domains.

The discussion highlights Fable 5's impressive performance on benchmarks, significantly outperforming [[concepts/qwen-36-35b-a3b|Claude Opus 4.8]] and [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT 5]].5 in areas like [[concepts/autonomous-ai-coding-agent|agentic coding]] (SweBench Pro, FrontierCode Diamond), [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]] (GDP Val), and spatial [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. Despite these high benchmark scores, the presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] that his [[concepts/personal-experience|personal experience]] sometimes contradicted the benchmarks, especially concerning GPT 5.5's perceived capabilities. He emphasizes that Fable 5 excels [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] complex, long-horizon tasks, capable of "massive explorations" of entire codebases, a capability demonstrated by Stripe's reported success in migrating a 50-million-line Ruby [[concepts/code|codebase]] in one day.

However, the presenter also points out several "quirks" in Fable 5's behavior. The model is incredibly verbose, providing deep, technical explanations that often require simplification. It exhibits high "information [[concepts/density|density]]," meaning more information is conveyed per token, which makes it token-efficient but sometimes challenging for human readability. A notable frustration was the model's tendency to ask numerous clarifying and confirming questions for even simple tasks. The presenter discusses the concept of "[[concepts/loops|loops]]" and "workflows" (agentic delegation) as critical features that unlock Fable 5's full potential for complex, parallel task execution, leading to speculation that future AI models might develop their own hyper-dense languages.

Regarding pricing, Fable 5 and Mythos 5 are offered at $10 per million input [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] and $50 per million output tokens, which the presenter deems expensive. He advises strategic usage, reserving these high-[[concepts/pricing|cost models]] for the most difficult problems while utilizing cheaper alternatives for simpler tasks. He suggests that the delayed public release of Mythos 5 was intentional, allowing Anthropic to maintain a competitive lead by using the model for its internal R&D. Ultimately, the presenter concludes that Fable 5 is an incredibly capable model, and its true power is unlocked when combined with workflows and loops, creating a [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] for highly autonomous "software factories" that even top [[entities/ai-labs|AI labs]] may not yet be fully leveraging.
