# Slide To Video Transformation

Slide to Video Transformation is a workflow for converting presentations generated by NotebookLM into video format. Google's NotebookLM application can automatically generate slide decks from source documents such as research papers, articles, or notes. Users may wish to convert these presentations into video files for distribution, archival, or presentation purposes across different platforms.

## Process Overview

The transformation process typically involves exporting the generated slide deck from NotebookLM in a standard format, such as PDF or PowerPoint. Since NotebookLM does not natively export video files, users must utilize external tools or Google Workspace integrations to animate the static slides. Common methods include importing the exported deck into Google Slides, adding transitions and speaker notes, and then using the "Film" feature or third-party screen recording software to capture the presentation as a video file.

## Technical Considerations

A key aspect of this workflow is ensuring the final output is free of watermarks, which often occur when using free tiers of external video editing software. By leveraging native Google tools or open-source alternatives, creators can maintain a clean visual presentation. The resulting video file preserves the textual and visual information of the original slides while adding temporal context through audio narration or timed visual changes, making it suitable for asynchronous learning or broad distribution.
