# Self-Evolution

[[concepts/self-evolutionary-development|Self-evolution]] in the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] refers to an emerging trend where [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] are designed with the capability for [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous optimization]]. This involves features such as self-[[concepts/debugging|debugging]] during [[concepts/training|training]] phases, managing their [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] autonomously, and diagnosing test results independently.

### Key Concepts
- **Autonomous [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]]:** The ability of AI systems to improve themselves without external human intervention.
- **Iterative Harnessing:** A process by which an AI system can iteratively refine its operations and [[concepts/learning|learning]] [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] through repeated cycles of execution and assessment.

### Pioneering Examples
- [[concepts/3d-game-development|GPT-5]].3 [[entities/codex|Codex]]: Demonstrates self-evolution [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], such as [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] its own training processes and managing deployment autonomously.
