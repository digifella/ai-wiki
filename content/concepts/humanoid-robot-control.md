# Humanoid robot control

The field of engineering and [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] dedicated to managing the locomotion, manipulation, and stability of Humanoid [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] platforms.

## Control Paradigms
- Teleoperation: Remote manipulation of robots by human operators to facilitate complex or unstructured tasks.
- Autonomous control: [[concepts/autonomous-operations|Self-governing systems]] using [[entities/ai]] and sensorimotor [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to navigate and interact with environments.

## Recent Advancements
- **[[concepts/nvidia-sonic|NVIDIA Sonic]]** (2026-04-26):
    - A groundbreaking [[entities/ai]] system developed by [[entities/nvidia]] designed for nuanced Teleoperation.
    - Enables humanoid robots, such as [[entities/digit|Digit]] (robot), to execute a wide array of complex and fine-grained tasks.
    - Focuses on bridging the gap between human intent and robotic dexterity.
