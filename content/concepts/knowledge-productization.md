# Knowledge Productization

The strategic process of converting individual expertise, unstructured intellectual property, and proprietary insights into scalable, repeatable, and monetizable assets.

## 🚀 AI-Driven Implementation
Modern [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] utilize [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] to "productize" human [[concepts/cognition|cognition]] by transforming raw data into autonomous [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]].

- **Core Objective**: Transform personal expertise and Messy Notes into professional, Monetizable AI Consultants.
- **Toolstack**: [[entities/notebooklm]], [[concepts/google-gemini-gems|Gemini Gems]].
- **Methodology**: Synthesizing proprietary knowledge into structured **[[concepts/logic-manuals]]** that serve as the operational foundation for [[concepts/custom-ai-assistants|custom AI assistants]].
- **Workflow Stages**:
	- **Phase 1: [[concepts/knowledge-capture|Knowledge Capture]] & [[concepts/organization|Organization]]**: The fundamental stage of aggregating and structuring raw expertise before synthesis.
