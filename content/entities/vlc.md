# Vlc

VLC is a free and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] media player that supports a wide range of audio and video formats. Beyond standard playback, it includes functionality for DVD handling, including the ability to open [[concepts/dvd-iso|DVD ISO]] image files and perform [[concepts/dvd-ripping|DVD ripping]] when the optional [[entities/libdvdcss|libdvdcss library]] is installed.

## DVD Functionality

VLC can open DVD ISO files, though they must be loaded via drag-and-drop rather than through the standard file open menu. For DVD ripping tasks, VLC requires the libdvdcss library to be installed separately. Users experiencing limitations with VLC's ripping capabilities may consider alternative tools such as Handbrake or [[entities/makemkv|MakeMKV]] as alternatives in a ripping workflow.
