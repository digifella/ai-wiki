# Gemma 4

**[[concepts/23b-parameter-models|Gemma 4]]** is a compact, [[concepts/unified-ai|unified AI]] model developed by [[entities/google|Google]] [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]. It represents a shift toward efficient, direct [[concepts/multi-modal-perception|multi-modal perception]], addressing the computational inefficiencies of previous large-scale models.

## Key Characteristics
- **Compact Architecture**: Designed for efficiency, reducing the computational overhead typical of massive models.
- **Unified Multi-Modal Perception**: Capable of direct processing of multiple data types (e.g., [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] and language) without separate, disjointed pipelines.
- **[[concepts/direct-perception|Direct Perception]]**: Moves away from indirect feature extraction, allowing for more immediate and accurate interpretation of complex inputs.
