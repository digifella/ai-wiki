# Qwen Team

The Qwen Team is responsible for developing the Qwen series of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and related [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]]. The team operates under [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]]'s broader AI initiatives and focuses on creating [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] and commercially available models for various [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Qwen3-TTS

In April 2026, the Qwen Team open-sourced the Qwen3-TTS family of models, a text-to-speech system designed for natural [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]]. The Qwen3-TTS models support features including [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] and [[concepts/instruction-control|voice control]] capabilities, enabling users to customize speech output characteristics. The open-source release made these models accessible to developers and researchers for integration into their own applications.
