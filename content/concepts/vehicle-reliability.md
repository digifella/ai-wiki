# Vehicle reliability

The ability of a vehicle to perform its intended functions without failure throughout its Vehicle lifecycle under specified operating conditions.

## Key Determinants
- **Mechanical [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Durability of the powertrain, drivetrain, and structural components.
- **[[concepts/software|Software]] [[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]**: Stability of OTA updates, ADAS, and infotainment ecosystems.
- **Battery [[concepts/health|Health]]**: Resistance to degradation and efficiency of [[concepts/cooling-systems|Thermal management]] in Electric Vehicles.
- **Manufacturing Quality**: [[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]] in build tolerances and material [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] to prevent early-stage failures.

## Recent Observations
- Certain Electric Vehicles (EVs) are identified as high-risk/low-value assets that should be avoided, even when available at significant discounts.
