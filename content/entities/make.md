# Make

Make is a business [[concepts/automation|automation]] platform that integrates with [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/lightweight-automation-platforms|ChatGPT Workspace Agents]] to streamline repetitive tasks for teams. The service allows organizations to deploy AI-powered [[concepts/agents|agents]] that can handle routine business processes without manual intervention, though access is currently limited to certain team configurations.

## Functionality and Availability

[[entities/wokato|ChatGPT Workspace Agents]] automate various business workflows by performing tasks that would otherwise require human [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]]. The automation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] are available to teams meeting specific criteria, though OpenAI has not publicly detailed all eligibility requirements. The service operates within established limitations that affect scope and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] options.

## Adoption Context

As a business automation tool, Make positions itself alongside other [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]] platforms. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] with [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] represents an effort to make AI-assisted [[concepts/recurring-actions|task automation]] more accessible to organizations of varying sizes, though the current restrictions on availability suggest a phased rollout approach.
