# Dynamic Video Content

Dynamic Video Content is a video production methodology that leverages artificial intelligence image generation models to create video frames through systematic iterative refinement. Rather than relying exclusively on traditional cinematography or pre-rendered digital assets, this approach generates individual frames using AI models such as GPT Image 2. The producer functions as a director of the generative process, crafting and refining prompts to guide the visual output toward a specific aesthetic or narrative goal.

## Methodology and Workflow

The core of this technique involves a cyclical workflow where static images are generated, evaluated, and modified based on specific criteria. Producers utilize iterative prompt refinement to adjust lighting, composition, and subject details across successive generations. This process allows for precise control over visual consistency and narrative progression, treating the AI model as a tool for frame-by-frame construction rather than a source of random output.

## Role of the Producer

In this framework, the human operator shifts from a traditional director or camera operator to a curator of generative parameters. Success depends on the ability to articulate visual requirements through text and to critically assess the output for coherence. The producer must manage the tension between creative intent and the stochastic nature of the underlying models, ensuring that the final sequence maintains logical continuity despite being assembled from independently generated elements.
