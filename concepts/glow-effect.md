# Glow effect

The **[[entities/glow|Glow]] effect** refers to a photographic and digital editing technique that creates a soft, luminous diffusion around highlights, enhancing mood and [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]]. It is distinct from simple brightness [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], focusing on the quality of [[concepts/light|light]] spread.
