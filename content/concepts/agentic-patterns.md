# Agentic Patterns

Agentic patterns are recurring architectural and behavioral structures that emerge in [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]]. These patterns represent standardized solutions to common problems in [[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/design|design]], such as how [[concepts/agents|agents]] coordinate with one another, process information, execute tasks, and manage state across operations. Rather than prescriptive frameworks, agentic patterns describe observed configurations that have proven effective for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and constraints.

## Multi-Agent Coordination

One significant class of agentic patterns involves the interaction and coordination between multiple agents. These patterns address challenges such as [[concepts/leadership|task delegation]], consensus-building, and sequential or parallel execution of agent-driven workflows. Multi-[[concepts/multi-agent-orchestration|agent coordination]] patterns enable complex [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] by allowing [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] to operate on different aspects of a problem while maintaining coherent overall behavior.

## Document and Information Processing

Another common pattern class involves agents working with documents, [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]], and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]]. These patterns typically combine input processing, semantic analysis, and [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] to extract or transform information. [[concepts/document-processing|Document processing]] patterns are widely used in research workflows, content generation, and knowledge extraction tasks.

## Code Execution and Development Workflows

Agentic patterns also encompass structured approaches to [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], [[concepts/testing|testing]], and execution. These patterns define how agents can safely interact with [[concepts/code-execution|code execution]] environments, validate outputs, and iteratively improve code-based solutions. Such patterns are central to agent-assisted [[concepts/coding|software development]] and technical problem-solving workflows.
