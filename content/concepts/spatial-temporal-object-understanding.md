# Spatial-temporal object understanding

The ability of AI systems to identify, track, and reason about objects across both spatial (location) and temporal (time) dimensions within video sequences.

## Key implementations

- **[[concepts/videorefer-suite]]**: [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]]'s [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] ([[concepts/apache-2-license|Apache 2 license]]) model suite that enhances [[concepts/large-language-model]] capabilities for fine-grained spatial-temporal object understanding in video. Enables LLMs to track specific objects throughout video content (e.g., "track the red car from frame 10 to 30").
- **[[concepts/local-deployment|Local deployment]]**: Full installation guide and demonstration available at [[entities/fahd-mirza|Fahd Mirza]] - Videorefer model [[concepts/running|running]] locally (video: [Alibaba VideoRefer Suite overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OZnfsJjLH5k)).
