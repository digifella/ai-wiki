# Meal Prep

Meal prep is a [[concepts/water-bath-method|food preparation]] technique that applies professional [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]] to home kitchens, enabling efficient advance [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] of meals and meal components. The method typically involves preparing vegetables and proteins several days in advance, then [[concepts/storing|storing]] them for quick assembly throughout the week. This approach reduces daily [[concepts/cooking-time|cooking time]] and labor while maintaining food quality through proper technique and [[entities/storage|storage]] practices.

## Core Techniques

The foundation of meal prep relies on restaurant-[[concepts/style|style]] [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking methods]] adapted for batch preparation. Blanching and steaming are primary techniques used to partially cook vegetables while preserving [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], color, and nutritional content. Blanching involves briefly boiling vegetables then immediately cooling them in ice water to stop the cooking process. Steaming similarly cooks vegetables through moist heat but offers more precise control over doneness. Both methods allow vegetables to be prepared several days ahead without significant quality degradation.

Proteins such as chicken, fish, and legumes are typically cooked in bulk using simple methods like [[concepts/seafood-poaching|poaching]], baking, or steaming. These components are then portioned and stored separately from other ingredients, allowing for flexible meal assembly throughout the week. By preparing components individually rather than complete meals, meal prep maintains versatility while minimizing preparation time when eating.

## Storage and Planning

Proper storage is essential to meal prep [[concepts/success|success]]. Prepared components are typically refrigerated in airtight [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]] and consumed within three to five days, depending on the ingredient type. This timeframe represents a practical balance between preparation efficiency and food safety. Effective meal prep requires basic planning to identify which vegetables, proteins, and [[concepts/granular-materials|grains]] [[entities/will|will]] be prepared, ensuring that chosen ingredients maintain quality through the intended storage period.
