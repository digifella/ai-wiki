# Dr. John Knowlton

**[[entities/dr-john|Dr. John]] Knowlton** is a key figure in the field of robotics and biomimetic design, notably associated with **[[entities/1x-technologies|1X Technologies]]**. His work focuses on the technical analysis and development of advanced [[concepts/robotics|robotic systems]], particularly regarding dexterous manipulation and hand mechanics.

## Key Contributions & Analysis

*   **[[concepts/neos-hands|NEO's Hands]] Development**: Knowlton provided critical insights into the design of **[[entities/neos-hands|NEO's Hands]]**, an advanced robotic hand developed by 1X Technologies.
*   **Biomimicry Focus**: His analysis emphasizes the integration of biological principles into technical design, correcting common misconceptions about robotic hand mechanics.
*   **[[concepts/collaborative-research|Collaborative Research]]**: Works closely with experts such as [[entities/dr-scott|Dr. Scott]] Walter to refine technical specifications and functional capabilities of robotic limbs.
