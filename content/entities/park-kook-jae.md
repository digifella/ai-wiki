# Park Kook-jae

## Overview
Park Kook-jae is a notable author known for his works that blend historical fiction with culinary themes. His most celebrated work is the web novel *[[entities/bon-appétit-your-majesty|Surviving as Yeonsan-gun's Chef]]*, which has been adapted into the Netflix K-drama **[[entities/bon-appétit|Bon Appétit]], [[entities/your-majesty|Your Majesty]]**.

## Bon Appétit, Your Majesty
- **Adaptation**: Based on Park Kook-jae's web novel.
- **Director**: [[entities/jang-tae-yoo|Jang Tae-yoo]]
- **Producer**: [[entities/studio-dragon|Studio Dragon]]
- **Main Actor/Actress**: [[entities/im-yoon-ah|Im Yoon-ah]] (as Yeon Ji-young)
- **Series Details**: 12-episode Netflix K-drama.
- **Premise**: Modern South Korean chef Yeon Ji-young, trained in French [[concepts/culinary-arts|culinary arts]], is transported back to the [[concepts/joseon-era|Joseon era]] following an accident with an antique book.
