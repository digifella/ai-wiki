# External AI Harnesses

External AI harnesses refer to third-party tools, integrations, and platforms that extend or modify the functionality of commercial [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. These include browser extensions, API wrappers, alternative user interfaces, and custom applications built around underlying [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]]. Such harnesses allow users to access [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI services]] through non-official channels, often adding features like enhanced workflow integration, specialized interfaces for particular [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], or modified system behaviors that differ from the official offering.

## Regulatory Context

As of April 2026, [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] has implemented significant changes to its [[concepts/claude-ai-subscription-policy|Claude AI subscription policy]], specifically targeting the use of external harnesses. The company has officially banned the use of [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], a prominent third-party interface, citing violations of its terms of service regarding [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]] and system modification. This enforcement marks a shift toward stricter control over how users interact with Claude's underlying models outside of approved environments.

The updated policy also introduces stricter [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] for accounts detected utilizing unapproved API wrappers or alternative front-ends. These restrictions are designed to prevent circumvention of rate limits and ensure that all interactions comply with Anthropic's [[concepts/security|security]] and ethical guidelines. Users relying on such third-party tools face potential account suspension or termination if they continue to use non-compliant integrations after the effective date of the new regulations.
