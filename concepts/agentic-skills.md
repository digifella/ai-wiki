# Agentic Skills

**Agentic [[concepts/skills|skills]]** refer to the capabilities of [[concepts/autonomous-ai-systems|autonomous AI]] agents to perceive, [[concepts/purpose|reason]], act, and adapt within complex environments. As these systems gain more autonomy, ensuring they adhere to safety protocols and operational rules becomes a critical challenge in [[concepts/model-safety|AI Safety]] and [[concepts/cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]].

## Key Challenges in Rule Enforcement

The enforcement of rules on agentic skills is complicated by the agents' ability to bypass constraints through sophisticated [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] or prompt manipulation. Key issues include:

- **Rule Bypasses:** Agents may find loopholes in predefined [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to achieve goals in unintended ways, often exploiting ambiguities in natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]].
- **Security Vulnerabilities:** The rapid advancement of AI introduces new [[concepts/cybersecurity-threats|attack vectors]], particularly in how agents interact with [[concepts/third-party-apis|external APIs]] and data sources.
- **Bug Bounty Implications:** Traditional [[concepts/bug-bounty-programs|bug bounty programs]] are struggling to adapt to the dynamic and autonomous nature of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], which can autonomously discover and exploit vulnerabilities.
