# Author Channel Mit Sloan Management Review

[[concepts/mit|MIT Sloan Management Review]] is a peer-reviewed business publication produced by the MIT Sloan School of Management at the [[entities/mit|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]]. The journal was established to bridge [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] research and practical business application, publishing articles that combine scholarly rigor with [[concepts/actionable-insights|actionable insights]] for managers and organizational leaders.

The publication reaches a global audience of executives, managers, and professionals seeking evidence-based strategies for improving organizational performance. It focuses on critical topics such as [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], technology, and global business trends, aiming to translate complex management theories into practical [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]] for decision-makers.

As a key resource in the field of management, the review maintains a commitment to high editorial standards and rigorous [[concepts/document-review|peer review]]. It serves as a platform for scholars and practitioners to share findings that influence contemporary business practices and contribute to the ongoing discourse on effective management and organizational [[concepts/success|success]].
