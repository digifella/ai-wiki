# Fruit Fly Brain

The central nervous system of *Drosophila melanogaster*, serving as a [[concepts/target-model|primary model]] organism for [[concepts/neuroscience|neuroscience]] and connectomics. Recent advancements in mapping its complete wiring diagram have significantly accelerated research into neural circuitry and behavior.

## Key Developments

- **[[concepts/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]] [[concepts/deployment|Release]]**: [[entities/google|Google]] and [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus have open-sourced the complete [[concepts/connectome|connectome]] of a male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord.
- **Dataset Scope**: Represents a full "wiring diagram" of the central nervous system, enabling detailed analysis of neural connectivity.
- **Impact**: Facilitates open-access research into neural [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], behavior, and computational modeling of insect brains.
