# Drive Mode

[[concepts/ambition|Drive]] Mode is a camera [[concepts/power|control]] feature found on [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] that allows photographers to lock specific settings to prevent accidental [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] during shooting. This feature is particularly useful in fast-paced photography situations where inadvertent changes to camera [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] could compromise image capture. By engaging Drive Mode, users can protect critical settings from being unintentionally modified while the camera is in use.

The feature works by restricting access to selected control settings, effectively freezing their current values until the lock is deliberately disabled. This is especially valuable when working with a fully configured camera [[concepts/setup|setup]], as it eliminates the risk of bumping dials or pressing [[concepts/buttons|buttons]] that might alter [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], autofocus behavior, or other important parameters. Different Fujifilm camera [[concepts/models|models]] may offer varying degrees of [[concepts/customization|customization]] regarding which settings can be locked.

Drive Mode represents a practical ergonomic [[concepts/solution|solution]] for professional and serious amateur photographers who need reliable, consistent camera behavior throughout a shoot. Rather than requiring constant monitoring of settings or relying on muscle [[concepts/memory|memory]] to avoid [[concepts/accidental-changes|accidental changes]], photographers can configure their preferred setup once and maintain it with confidence.
