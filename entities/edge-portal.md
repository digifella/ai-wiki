# Edge Portal

Edge Portal is a web-based service provided by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC) that allows users to access company information and related services. The portal serves as a centralized platform for retrieving details about registered Australian companies and managing interactions with ASIC's corporate registry functions.

## Functionality

Users can access Edge Portal by navigating to the ASIC website and searching for relevant company details, typically using the Australian Company Number (ACN) or company name. The portal provides access to company registration information, financial documents, and other corporate records maintained in ASIC's registry. It enables both the public and registered users to view company details and, for authorized users, to lodge documents and manage company filings with ASIC.

## Purpose

Edge Portal forms part of ASIC's digital infrastructure for corporate governance and transparency in Australia. By providing online access to company information, the portal supports compliance with regulatory requirements and enables stakeholders—including investors, creditors, and regulatory bodies—to access publicly available corporate information efficiently.
