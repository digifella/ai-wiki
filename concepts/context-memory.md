# Context Memory

The ability of an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] to retain and utilize past interactions, [[concepts/user-control|user preferences]], and contextual data to enhance subsequent responses and [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]] without repetitive input. Enables [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] in [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]] and personalized assistance.

**Core [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]]**:
- [[concepts/persistent-state|Persistent state]] management across sessions
- Dynamic [[concepts/context-window|context window]] adaptation
- User behavior [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]]

**Applications**:
- [[concepts/custom-ai-assistants|Custom AI assistants]] via [[entities/gemini]]'s "[[entities/google-gemini-gems|Gems]]" feature:
  - *[[concepts/meeting-prep-strategist|Meeting Prep Strategist]]*: Retains historical meeting data, participant roles, and agenda context for automated [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]
  - *Automated Weekly Business Report*: Leverages past reports and metrics to generate context-aware summaries
- Reduced [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] through contextual [[concepts/recall|recall]] (e.g., remembering project specifics without re-explanation)
