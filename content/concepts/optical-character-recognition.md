# Optical Character Recognition

Optical Character Recognition ([[concepts/optical-character-recognition-ocr|OCR]]) is the automated process of converting [[concepts/images|images]] of [[concepts/text|text]]—such as scanned documents or photos—into machine-encoded, editable, and searchable data.

## Specialized Models & Emerging Trends
- **[[entities/nanonets-ocr-small|Nanonets OCR Small]]**: A newly introduced, highly efficient model featuring 3B [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], specifically optimized for converting tables into text to support [[concepts/traditional-rag|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) [[concepts/workflow|workflows]].
- **Shift Toward Efficiency**: There is a growing industry trend toward smaller, specialized, and [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/models|models]], contrasting with larger-scale architectures such as [[entities/llama|Llama]] OCR and [[entities/mistral|Mistral]] OCR.
- **[[concepts/infographic|Infographic]] Text Correction**: Utilizing [[entities/adobe-acrobat|Adobe Acrobat]] and [[entities/canva|Canva]] ('Grab Text') to identify and correct spelling inaccuracies or errors within text extracted from [[entities/gemini|Gemini]]-generated [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]].
