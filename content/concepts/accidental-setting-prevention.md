# Accidental Setting Prevention

Accidental Setting Prevention is a feature found on Fujifilm digital cameras designed to protect camera settings from unintended changes during operation. The feature allows photographers to lock specific controls and parameters, preventing accidental adjustments that could compromise image capture or disrupt workflow. This is particularly useful in professional or fast-paced shooting environments where maintaining consistent settings is critical.

## Functionality

The feature typically works by locking access to designated dials, buttons, or menu items through a dedicated lock mechanism or menu setting. Once activated, locked controls cannot be adjusted without first disengaging the prevention feature, requiring an intentional action from the user. This prevents the common problem of bumping a dial or pressing a button during handheld shooting that could inadvertently change exposure, aperture, shutter speed, or other important parameters.

## Practical Applications

Photographers benefit from Accidental Setting Prevention in situations where settings need to remain consistent across multiple shots, such as during event coverage, studio work, or when using specific manual configurations. By locking non-essential controls while shooting, users can focus on composition and timing without worry that their chosen settings will be altered by accidental contact with the camera body.
