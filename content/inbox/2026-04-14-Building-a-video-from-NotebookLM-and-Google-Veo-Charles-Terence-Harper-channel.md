# Building a video from [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] and [[entities/google-veo|Google Veo]] - Charles Terence [[entities/harper|Harper]] channel

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=g6w4Pz_urDg>
Here is a step-by-step guide on how to turn a [[concepts/notebooklm-notebook|NotebookLM notebook]] into a 30-second [[concepts/video-commercial|video commercial]] using [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]].

### **Overview**

* **Goal:** Create a cinematic [[concepts/30-second-ad|30-second ad]] based on [[concepts/business-philosophy|business philosophy]] stored in NotebookLM.
* **Tools Used:** NotebookLM, [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] (Advanced/Pro), [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], Google [[concepts/motivation|Drive]].
* **Key Feature:** Using "[[concepts/canvas|Canvas]]" in Gemini for [[concepts/script-building|script building]] and "[[concepts/veo|Veo]]" in Google Vids for [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].

* * *

### **Step 1: [[concepts/preparation|Preparation]] in Gemini**

1. **Open Gemini:** Start a new chat in Gemini.
2. **Add Source:** Click the `+` icon to add a source and select the **NotebookLM notebook** that contains your business [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] or [[concepts/marketing-rules|marketing rules]].
3. **[[concepts/prompt-engineering|Prompt Engineering]]:**
	* Paste a "Master Prompt" that instructs Gemini to act as a Video Director.
	* **Customize the Prompt:** Insert your specific business information and adjust visual styles (e.g., specify "No [[concepts/faces|faces]]" or "Cinematic [[concepts/style|style]]").
	* **Open Canvas:** Once the prompt is ready, open the **Canvas** view in Gemini to better manage the output.
4. **Generate Script:** Submit the prompt. Gemini [[entities/will|will]] analyze the notebook and generate a scene-by-scene script with visual descriptions.

### **Step 2: Video Generation in Google Vids**

1. **Open Vids:** Go to `vids.google.com` and start a new video.
2. **Select Tool:** Choose **Veo** (specifically Veo 3.1) to generate video from text.
3. **Generate Scene 1:**
	* Copy the visual description for Scene 1 from the [[concepts/gemini-canvas|Gemini Canvas]].
	* Paste it into the Veo prompt box in Vids.
	* Click **Generate**. Drag the result to the timeline.
4. **The [[concepts/continuity|Continuity]] Trick (Crucial Step):**
	* Take a **screenshot of the last frame** of Scene 1.
	* Save it to your computer.
	* For **Scene 2**, click "Add Ingredients" in the Veo tool and upload that screenshot.
	* Paste the text prompt for Scene 2.
	* _Why?_ Uploading the previous frame ensures visual continuity and smooth transitions between clips.
5. **Repeat:** Continue this process for all scenes (Screenshot last frame $\\rightarrow$ Upload as ingredient $\\rightarrow$ Prompt next scene).

### **Step 3: Creating the Soundtrack**

1. **Back to Gemini:** Ask Gemini to write a prompt for the background music based on the video's mood and the NotebookLM content.
2. **Generate Music:**
	* Change the Gemini tool to **Create Music**.
	* Paste the music prompt.
	* _Note:_ Ensure you specify **"Instrumental"** if you do not want lyrics.
3. **Import Audio:**
	* Download the generated MP3.
	* Upload it to [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]].
	* In Google Vids, go to **Uploads**, select the track, and drag it to the timeline.

### **Step 4: Voiceover and Scripting**

1. **Generate Voiceover Script:** In the Gemini Canvas, ask it to write a 30-second voiceover script based on the scenes generated.
2. **Add Voiceover in Vids:**
	* Click the **Voiceover** tool in Vids.
	* Select **AI Voiceover**.
	* Copy the script for Scene 1 from Gemini and paste it into Vids.
	* Choose a voice avatar and click **Insert**.
3. **Repeat:** Do this for every scene to match the visual timing.

### **Step 5: Editing and Mixing**

1. **Adjust Timing:** Manually resize the video clips on the timeline (e.g., trim them to 5 seconds each) so the visuals align with the music length (30 seconds).
2. **Audio Mixing:**
	* Go to the **Sound** settings.
	* **Mute the Video Tracks:** The AI-generated video clips often contain unwanted noise. Mute the video track volume for _all_ scenes so only the Music and Voiceover are audible.
	* Ensure the background music volume balances well with the voiceover.

### **Step 6: Export**

1. **Review:** Watch the full video to check transitions and audio levels.
2. **Share/Export:** Click **Share**. You can:
	* Export to Google Drive.
	* Download as an **MP4**.
	* Download as a **GIF**.
