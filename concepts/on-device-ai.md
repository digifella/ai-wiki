The execution of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models directly on local hardware (e.g., [[concepts/edge-computing|Edge Computing]], smartphones, IoT) to minimize latency, reduce [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] dependency, and enhance [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] by avoiding cloud-based [[concepts/gpu-clusters|GPU clusters]].

### Key Advancements & Trends
- [[concepts/model-efficiency]] (specifically [[entities/bitnet]] and [[entities/bonsai]]) are driving a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in [[concepts/model-efficiency|model efficiency]].
    - Enables massive models (e.g., 27B parameters) to run on mobile-class hardware.
    - Reduces file size and [[concepts/4gb-memory|memory footprint]] for [[concepts/edge-deployment|local inference]].
- **Microsoft's Hybrid Intelligence Strategy**:
    - Shift towards [[concepts/local-ai-processing|local AI processing]] on Windows PCs to empower users with powerful AI capabilities directly on devices.
    - Reduces reliance on cloud-based [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] for standard Copilot tasks.
    - Introduces [[concepts/hybrid-intelligence]] frameworks to balance local inference with cloud scalability.
    - See [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-Microsofts-Local-AI-Push-Hybrid-Intelligence-HydraFusion|Microsoft's Local AI Push: Hybrid Intelligence, HydraFusion, and Copilot's On-Device Capabilities]] for detailed analysis of HydraFusion and Copilot's on-device capabilities.
