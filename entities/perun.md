# Perun

Perun is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] and military [[entities/zed-francis|analyst]] focused on defense strategy, geopolitical implications of military technology, and the intersection of warfare with economic systems. The channel examines how modern conflicts interact with [[concepts/global-trade|global trade]] networks and [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], analyzing [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where military action directly disrupts economic activity.

The content examines strategies for hardening military [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] against these threats.
