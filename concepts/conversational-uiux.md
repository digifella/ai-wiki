Conversational UI/UX refers to [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience design]] built around dialogue-based interactions between users and digital systems. Rather than relying on traditional graphical elements like [[concepts/buttons|buttons]] and menus, [[concepts/conversational-interfaces|conversational interfaces]] enable users to accomplish tasks and access information through natural language exchanges—either text-based or voice-based. This approach aims to make digital interactions feel more intuitive and human-like by mimicking how people naturally communicate.

## Core Characteristics

Conversational interfaces are characterized by their reliance on [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]] and [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] [[concepts/flow|flow]]. Users input requests or questions in their own words, and the system responds contextually, ideally maintaining a coherent conversation rather than isolated [[concepts/commands|commands]]. These interfaces can take various forms, including [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]], [[concepts/voice-assistants|voice assistants]], and messaging-based applications, each optimized for different contexts and user needs.

## Design Considerations

Designing effective conversational UI/UX requires [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] structure, language [[concepts/tone|tone]], error handling, and context [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]]. Designers must anticipate user intents, manage [[concepts/conversation-flow|conversation flow]] across multiple turns, and gracefully handle misunderstandings. The [[concepts/experience|experience]] should feel natural without requiring users to learn specific syntax or commands, while maintaining [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] about what the system can and cannot accomplish.

## Applications and Tools

Conversational interfaces have become increasingly common across customer service, [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]], and [[concepts/recurring-actions|task automation]]. Design platforms and frameworks have emerged to support their creation, allowing designers to prototype and iterate on dialogue flows before implementation. As [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]] technology continues to advance, conversational UI/UX remains an evolving field with expanding possibilities for human-computer interaction.
