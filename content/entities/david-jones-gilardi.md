# David Jones Gilardi

David Jones Gilardi is a [[concepts/developer|Developer]] Relations Engineer at IBM. He specializes in presenting on [[entities/openrag|OpenRAG]] and [[concepts/agentic-rag-systems|Agentic RAG]] (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) systems, contributing to IBM's efforts in advancing generative [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] and developer education.

## Work and Contributions

Gilardi has been active in IBM's technical content strategy, particularly through the IBM Channel on YouTube. He has created educational content explaining OpenRAG and its [[concepts/software|applications]] in [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]], helping developers understand how RAG architectures can be implemented and optimized.
