# Video Editing & AI Decision Models

## Overview
The landscape of video editing is undergoing a paradigm shift driven by Generative AI. Traditional workflows relying on manual timeline manipulation and complex software stacks are increasingly being augmented or replaced by prompt-driven generative capabilities. Concurrently, the broader AI ecosystem is evolving with specialized decision models for operational tasks such as customer service assessment.

## AI-Driven Workflow Integration
Recent advancements highlight the convergence of photo and video editing through [[concepts/unified-ai|unified AI]] interfaces. Key developments include:

*   **Prompt-Driven Generation**: Tools like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and [[entities/gemini]] allow users to transform complex editing tasks into natural language prompts, reducing the need for manual skill or software like [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]].
*   **Unified Ecosystems**: Platforms such as [[entities/canva|Canva]] and [[entities/google|Google]] Flow are integrating generative models to streamline both static and dynamic media creation.
*   **Emerging Decision Models**: Beyond creative generation, new categories of [[concepts/ai-decision-models|AI decision models]] are emerging for operational efficiency. The "[[concepts/decision-model|Decision Model]] Showdown 2026" compares five models—CLM, Laya, OpenJev, Kev, and Jev—specifically for assessing [[concepts/customer-service-urgency|customer service urgency]] and frustration [[lab-notes/2026-09-27-AI-Decision-Model-Evaluation-Customer-Service-Urgency-Fr|AI Decision Model Evaluation: Customer Service Urgency & Frustration Assessment]].
