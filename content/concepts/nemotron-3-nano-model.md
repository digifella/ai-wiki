# Nemotron 3 Nano Model

[[entities/nemotron-3-nano|Nemotron 3 Nano]] is a 30-billion-parameter [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by NVIDIA. It is part of NVIDIA's broader [[concepts/nemotron-3-family|Nemotron-3 family]] of models, which was released to provide accessible [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] for various [[concepts/software|applications]]. As an open-source release, the model is available for public use, modification, and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] across different platforms and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Model Specifications and Design

The model's 30-billion-parameter [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] positions it as a mid-sized language model, balancing [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] with performance capabilities. This scale makes it suitable for deployment on consumer and enterprise [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] without requiring extremely high-end [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]]. The model is designed to handle [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] and instruction-following tasks as part of the broader [[entities/ai-assistant|Nemotron]] family's focus on [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] applications.

## Release and Availability

NVIDIA released Nemotron 3 Nano as part of its strategy to democratize access to capable [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. The open-source [[entities/nature|nature]] of the release allows researchers, developers, and organizations to integrate the model into their systems, fine-tune it for specific applications, or use it as a baseline for further development.
