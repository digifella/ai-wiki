# NotebookLM Slides Transformation

NotebookLM Slides Transformation is a workflow that converts slide decks generated by Google's NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process leverages NotebookLM's automatic slide generation feature, which creates structured presentations from source documents, research materials, or notes. Users then export these slides and import them into Google Vids, where the presentation format can be enhanced with video production features including narration, transitions, and multimedia elements.

## Workflow and Technical Process

The transformation begins when NotebookLM generates slides from uploaded source material. These slides are exported in a standard format compatible with Google Vids. Once imported into Google Vids, the slides serve as a foundation for video creation, where users can add voiceovers, background music, and visual effects. The method allows creators to convert text-based research or notes into multimedia presentations without building the slide structure from scratch.

## Watermark Removal

A notable aspect of this workflow involves handling watermarks that may appear on NotebookLM-generated slides. Users have documented methods for removing these watermarks during the transformation process, typically through Google Vids' editing capabilities or by exporting slides in formats that allow for post-processing before video generation. The specific techniques vary depending on the output format and platform updates.
