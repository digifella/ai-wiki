# Residence Visa For Tourism

A residence visa for tourism in Turkey is a long-term permit category that allows foreign nationals to reside in the country for extended periods while engaging in leisure activities. This visa type differs significantly from standard tourist visas, which typically restrict stays to a maximum of 90 days within a 180-day period. It is specifically designed for individuals who wish to maintain a residential presence in Turkey beyond the conventional limits of short-term tourism.

## Eligibility and Application Criteria

Applicants must meet specific criteria established by Turkish immigration authorities, which generally include proof of sufficient financial means to support themselves without working, valid health insurance coverage, and a clean criminal record. The application process requires submitting detailed documentation to the local Provincial Directorate of Migration Management in Turkey, rather than through embassies abroad.

## Duration and Renewal

This permit is typically issued for one year at a time and must be renewed annually as long as the holder continues to meet the initial eligibility requirements. Failure to renew the visa before its expiration can result in legal penalties and difficulties in future entry. Holders are prohibited from engaging in paid employment or business activities during the validity of the visa, maintaining its status strictly as a tool for residential tourism and leisure.
