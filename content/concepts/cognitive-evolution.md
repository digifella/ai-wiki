# Cognitive evolution

The evolutionary trajectory and development of complex mental processes, information processing, and the emergence of subjective experience within biological organisms.

- **[[concepts/inner-space|Inner Space]]**: The concept of the mind as a private, "inner universe" unique to each individual.
- **Foundations of Subjectivity**: The mind functions as the primary seat for Consciousness, [[concepts/memory|memory]], and introspection.
- **Biological Divergence**: The investigation of the cognitive gap and fundamental qualitative differences between humans and animals.
