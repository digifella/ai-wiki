# Calorie Count

**Calorie count** refers to the measurement of energy content in food and drink, typically expressed in kilocalories (kcal) or Calories. It represents the potential energy available to the body for metabolic processes, [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], and heat production.

## Core Concepts

- **Energy [[concepts/density|Density]]**: The amount of energy per unit of weight or volume. High-energy-density foods (fats, oils) provide more calories per gram than low-energy-density foods (vegetables, water-rich fruits).
- **Metabolic Cost**: Not all consumed calories are fully absorbed. Factors such as food processing, fiber content, and the thermic effect of food (TEF) influence net caloric availability.
- **Energy Balance**: The relationship between calories consumed (intake) and calories expended (basal metabolic rate, physical activity, TEF). Positive balance leads to weight gain; negative balance leads to [[concepts/weight-loss|weight loss]].

## Measurement & Estimation

- **Atwater System**: Standard method for calculating food energy using average conversion factors: 4 kcal/g for protein and carbohydrates, 9 kcal/g for fat, and 7 kcal/g for alcohol.
- **Labeling Variance**: Commercial labels estimate calories based on average [[concepts/writing|composition]], not specific batch variations.
- **Sweeteners & [[concepts/sugar|Sugar]] Alcohols**:
	- **[[concepts/sugar-alcohol|Sugar Alcohols]]**: Compounds like [[entities/xylitol|Xylitol]] provide fewer calories than [[concepts/sucrose|table sugar]] (~2.4 kcal/g vs 4 kcal/g) due to incomplete absorption and [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|metabolism]] in the gut.
	- **Specific [[concepts/notes|Notes]]**: See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Xylitol-Human-Health-Benefits-and-Canine-Toxicity|Xylitol: Human Health Benefits and Canine Toxicity]] for details on xylitol's caloric profile relative to sucrose and its metabolic impact.

## Contextual Factors

- **Glycemic Index (GI)**: Measures the [[concepts/speed|speed]] at which carbohydrates raise blood glucose levels, influencing insulin response and [[concepts/satiety|satiety]], independent of total caloric count.
- **Bioavailability**: Micronutrient density and macro-nutrient quality affect how efficiently calories are utilized for cellular repair versus [[entities/storage|storage]].
