# Claude Opus 47

[[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus]] 47 is an AI model released by [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] that builds upon previous iterations in the Opus series. The model features improvements in overall [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]] and expanded [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] in [[concepts/visual-understanding|visual understanding]], enabling it to process and analyze [[concepts/images|images]] with greater [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and sophistication than earlier versions.

## Pricing and Availability

The release of Claude [[entities/gpt-54|Opus 47]] included [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to the model's [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]]. These changes reflect [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s approach to balancing model capability improvements with [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] for various user tiers and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Performance Characteristics

[[concepts/testing|Testing]] of Claude Opus 47 demonstrates measurable improvements across standard benchmarks and real-world [[concepts/software|applications]]. The model maintains the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and [[concepts/language-capabilities|language capabilities]] expected of the Opus tier while enhancing its multimodal abilities to work effectively with both [[concepts/text|text]] and visual inputs.
