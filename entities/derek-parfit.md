# Derek Parfit

Derek Parfit (1942–2017) was a British philosopher best known for his influential work on ethics, metaphysics, and personal identity. He spent most of his academic career at Oxford University, where he became one of the most significant moral philosophers of the late twentieth century. His approach was characterized by rigorous analytical reasoning and novel arguments that significantly shaped contemporary philosophical debate.

## Ethics and Wellbeing

Parfit's contributions to ethical theory centered on consequentialism and theories of wellbeing. His work explored how we understand what makes life go well for individuals and how this relates to moral obligations. His ideas on wellbeing have been widely cited in contemporary discussions of human flourishing and continue to inform debates about how societies measure and pursue the conditions for good lives.

## Personal Identity and Metaphysics

Beyond ethics, Parfit made substantial contributions to understanding personal identity, arguing against traditional views that the self is a separately existing entity. His work in metaphysics questioned fundamental assumptions about the nature of persons and identity over time, challenging how philosophers conceptualize the continuity of human existence.
