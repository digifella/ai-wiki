# Gema 3 Model

The Gema 3 Model is a conceptual framework within the domain of AI agents that redefines the interaction with Large Language Models (LLMs). It proposes treating these models as structured, queryable systems rather than opaque text processors. This approach challenges conventional interaction patterns by advocating for systematic access to and modification of internal representations and learned knowledge through structured operations.

Central to this framework is the integration of Larql, a method designed for querying and modifying the internal database structures of LLMs. By enabling direct interaction with the underlying data architecture, Larql allows for precise retrieval and alteration of model parameters and embeddings. This capability shifts the paradigm from purely generative text output to a more deterministic management of internal model states.

The framework emphasizes the potential for fine-grained control over model behavior and knowledge retention. By exposing internal structures to structured queries, developers can theoretically optimize performance, correct specific factual errors, or update knowledge bases without full retraining. This represents a significant departure from traditional black-box interactions, offering a pathway for more transparent and manageable AI agent systems.
