# Meteorites

Meteorites are extraterrestrial materials that survive the entry through Earth's atmosphere and reach the surface as meteorite fragments. They provide invaluable insights into the formation of our solar system, planetary evolution, and the potential for [[concepts/extraterrestrial-life|life beyond Earth]].

## Notable Discoveries and Updates

- The discovery of organic compounds in Martian meteorites suggests the presence of past or present microbial life.
- Mars missions have returned samples that indicate ancient water flows and potentially habitable environments on the planet's surface.
