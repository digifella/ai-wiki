# Notebooklm Slides Transformation

Notebooklm Slides Transformation is a workflow that converts slide decks generated by Google's NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process leverages NotebookLM's automatic slide generation feature, which creates structured presentations from source documents, research materials, or notes. Users export these slides and import them into Google Vids to initiate the conversion.

The transformation relies on the compatibility between the exported slide format and Google Vids' import capabilities. Once the deck is uploaded, Google Vids analyzes the visual structure and text content of the slides to automatically generate a video timeline. This integration allows users to transform static presentations into dynamic video narratives without manually scripting or editing individual frames.

The workflow is designed to streamline content repurposing by reducing the manual effort typically required for video production. By utilizing the native connection between NotebookLM and Google Vids, users can maintain consistency between the original source material and the final video output. This method is particularly useful for educators, marketers, and professionals who need to quickly distribute presentation content in video format.
