# Apple Watch Ultra 3

The **[[entities/apple|Apple]] Watch Ultra 3** is a rugged, high-performance smartwatch designed for extreme environments and professional use. It serves as the flagship model in the Apple Watch lineup, offering enhanced durability, extended battery life, and advanced health and fitness tracking capabilities.

## Compatibility & Software

The Apple Watch Ultra 3 is optimized for the latest software ecosystem, ensuring seamless integration with iOS and watchOS features.

- **Supported OS:** Fully compatible with Apple [[concepts/watchos-27|watchOS 27]], which introduces significant UI improvements and battery optimizations tailored for the Ultra's hardware capabilities.
- **Legacy Support:** While older models like the Ultra 2 are supported by [[entities/watchos-27|watchOS 27]], the Ultra 3 is the primary target for new feature rollouts.
- **Installation:** Updates can be installed via the iPhone's Watch app or directly on the device.

## Key Features

- **Durability:** Titanium case with sapphire crystal display, rated for extreme conditions.
- **Battery Life:** Enhanced power management features in watchOS 27 extend operational time during GPS-intensive activities.
- **Health Monitoring:** Advanced sensors for heart rate, blood oxygen, and temperature sensing.
- **Connectivity:** Dual-frequency GPS and cellular options for global coverage.
