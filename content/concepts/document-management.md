# Document Management

Document management refers to the systems and practices used to organize, store, retrieve, and maintain digital [[concepts/files|files]] and records within an infrastructure. In the context of [[concepts/home-lab|home lab]] environments, effective document management often relies on containerized solutions that provide scalability, [[concepts/security|security]], and ease of [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] without requiring extensive [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] resources.

## Docker-Based Solutions

Several underrated [[concepts/docker-containers|Docker containers]] offer practical document management [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for home [[entities/labs|labs]]. These containers typically provide features such as [[concepts/full-text-search|full-text search]], version [[concepts/power|control]], access permissions, and web-based interfaces. Solutions in this category include specialized document servers, note-taking [[concepts/software|applications]], and file [[concepts/organization|organization]] tools that can be deployed quickly using [[concepts/docker|Docker]]'s [[concepts/containerization|containerization]] approach.

## Implementation Considerations

When selecting document management containers for a home lab, administrators should consider factors such as resource requirements, ease of backup and restoration, [[concepts/integration|integration]] with existing services, and the specific use case—whether for personal archiving, collaborative note-taking, or formal document retention. Many underrated options offer comparable functionality to enterprise solutions while remaining lightweight enough for modest hardware setups.
