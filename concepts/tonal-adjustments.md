# Tonal Adjustments

The process of modifying the luminance and color distribution of an image to enhance [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], depth, and mood.

## Key Elements
- [[concepts/exposure]]: The fundamental adjustment of [[concepts/light|light]] intensity.
- [[concepts/contrast]]: The range and scale of difference between light and dark values.
- highlights & [[concepts/shadows]]: [[concepts/targeted-image-editing|Targeted manipulation]] of the brightest and darkest regions of the tonal range.
- Midtones: Adjustment of the intermediate [[concepts/brightness-levels|luminance levels]] that define core [[concepts/texture|texture]] and color.
- [[concepts/lightroom]]: The application of hue and saturation shifts to establish an aesthetic or emotional [[concepts/tone|tone]].
