# Claude Ai Subscription Policy

As of April 2026, [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] implemented significant changes to its subscription framework affecting how users interact with the platform and the terms under which they can access [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s capabilities.

## Usage Limits and Access Changes

The [[concepts/policy-updates|policy updates]] introduced modifications to [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] for [[entities/claude-ai|Claude AI]] subscribers. These changes adjusted the rate limits and request volumes permitted under different subscription tiers, affecting both individual users and organizations relying on [[concepts/claudemd|Claude]] for regular tasks.

## OpenClaw Ban

A notable component of the subscription policy update was the prohibition on [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], an automated information pipeline tool that had previously been used to automate interactions with [[concepts/2026-04-08-anthropic|Claude AI]]. This ban reflects Anthropic's effort to regulate how the service is consumed and to prevent certain categories of automated usage patterns.

## Financial and Pricing Implications

The [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]] carried financial implications for existing and prospective users, requiring [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to subscription costs and fee structures.
