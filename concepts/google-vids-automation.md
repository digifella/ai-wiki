# Google Vids Automation

Google Vids Automation is a two-stage workflow that converts text-based source material into finished video files by combining NotebookLM's slide generation with Google Vids' video production capabilities. The process begins with NotebookLM, an AI-integrated notebook tool that analyzes raw text input and automatically generates presentation slides in Google Slides format. These generated slides then serve as the foundation for video creation in Google Vids.

## Workflow and Process

The automation workflow leverages the output structure of NotebookLM to streamline video production. Once slides are generated, users import them into Google Vids, which uses the slide content—including text, structure, and visual layout—to guide automated video generation. Google Vids applies its own AI capabilities to add narration, transitions, and visual elements, producing a complete video file without requiring manual editing or additional design work.

## Output and Distribution

A key advantage of this workflow is the production of unwatermarked video files, making the output suitable for direct distribution or publication. The final videos can be downloaded and shared across various platforms without the branding artifacts that often accompany cloud-based video tools. This makes Google Vids Automation particularly useful for content creators, educators, and professionals who need to convert structured information into polished video content efficiently.
