# Organizational Structure

Organizational structure refers to the formal system of task and reporting [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within an [[concepts/organization|organization]] that defines how activities are divided, grouped, and coordinated. A well-defined organizational structure enables effective communication, decision-making, coordination, and control in pursuit of organizational goals.

### Key Points
- Defines [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]] and roles
- Facilitates communication and information flow
- Supports goal alignment and resource allocation
