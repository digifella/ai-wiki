# Otway Ranges

The Otway Ranges are a significant geological feature in Victoria, Australia, known for their unique geological history and the associated mineral deposits.

## Overview
The Otway Ranges encompass a region known for its diverse topography and underlying [[concepts/geology|geology]], which has historically made it an area of interest for exploration and mining. These ranges are closely tied to the broader geological [[concepts/structure|structure]] of Victoria and [[concepts/power|influence]] the [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of various mineral resources.

## Geological Significance and Anomalies
The area presents complex geological formations, often leading to anomalies that distinguish it from other Victorian goldfields.

*   **Wangerrip [[concepts/gold-deposit|Gold Deposit]]:** A key area of study within the ranges is the Wangerrip gold deposit. This site is highlighted as an anomalous geological mystery in Victoria, contrasting with the numerous other well-known goldfields in the region.
