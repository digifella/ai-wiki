# Perplexity Labs

[[concepts/ai-copywriting|Perplexity Labs]] is an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] product developed by [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], the AI-powered search engine company. The product is designed to automate tasks in marketing and video production workflows, positioning itself as a tool for content creators and marketing professionals who need to streamline repetitive processes.

## Capabilities and Use Cases

The agent has been demonstrated performing tasks relevant to marketing and [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]], though its exact feature set and limitations remain under ongoing evaluation. Early reviews have compared its performance against other prominent [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] and [[concepts/agents|agents]] in the market, including [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], to assess its competitive positioning and practical utility for professional workflows.
