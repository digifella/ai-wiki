# Mindset Shift

A fundamental change in how individuals or groups perceive situations, problems, or themselves, enabling new approaches to challenges and improved performance. Often involves moving from a fixed to a Growth [[concepts/mindset|Mindset]], or from siloed [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] to Systems Thinking.

## Key Dimensions
- **Individual**: Transitioning from [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] to abundance thinking; embracing failure as data.
- **Collective**: Shifting from competition to collaboration; prioritizing shared goals over individual metrics.

## Evidence & Applications
- **[[concepts/project-aristotle|Project Aristotle]]**: Research indicates that [[concepts/psychological-safety]] is the primary factor distinguishing high-performing teams, outweighing individual talent or IQ. This represents a shift from viewing [[concepts/team-composition|team composition]] as the key driver to viewing [[concepts/group-dynamics|team dynamics]] and trust as the foundation for [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]. See [[entities/project-aristotle|Project Aristotle]]: [[concepts/google-search|Google]]’s Data-Driven Insights on High-Performing Teams. for detailed findings on dependency management, structure [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]], and meaning as secondary but critical factors.
- **[[concepts/leadership|Leadership]]**: Moving from directive management to facilitative leadership encourages autonomy and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]].
