# Integrated Model Selection

Integrated model selection is a methodology that allows users to access and switch between multiple [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] through a single interface, rather than maintaining separate subscriptions and accounts with different providers. This approach reduces operational [[concepts/friction|friction]] and [[concepts/cost|cost]] by consolidating access to various [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] under one platform.

## Implementation and Benefits

Platforms implementing this methodology provide users with a unified selection interface where they can choose which underlying model to use for each query or task. By enabling direct comparison and switching between models like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] without leaving the platform, users can optimize for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]—whether prioritizing [[concepts/speed|speed]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capability, or specialized knowledge—without the friction of context-switching between separate [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Economic Impact

A primary advantage of integrated model selection is cost reduction. Users no longer need to maintain multiple concurrent subscriptions to access different models. Instead, a single subscription to the aggregating platform may provide access to several third-party models, or the platform may use its own models alongside partner offerings, providing flexibility [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] lower total cost of ownership.
