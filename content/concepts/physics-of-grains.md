# Physics of Grains
The study of grain mechanics and behavior is central to understanding the [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]] of [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] such as sand, wheat, or other small particles. Key concepts include:

- **[[concepts/angle-of-repose|Angle of Repose]]**: The maximum slope angle at which a pile of material can stand without sliding down.
- **[[concepts/self-sorting|Self-Sorting]]**: Process where different sizes or types of grains separate and organize themselves within a mixture due to mechanical actions like pouring or shaking.
- **[[concepts/vertical-stacking|Vertical Stacking]]**: Unusual phenomenon where [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] form vertical columns when certain conditions are met, breaking the typical angle of repose.
