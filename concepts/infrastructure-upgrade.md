# Infrastructure Upgrade

## Overview
Infrastructure upgrades refer to the process of enhancing or replacing existing systems, tools, or platforms to improve performance, scalability, [[concepts/security|security]], or functionality.

## Key Aspects
- **Performance:** Enhancements to [[concepts/speed|speed]], efficiency, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].
- **Scalability:** Ability to handle increased load or growth.
- **Security:** Upgrades to protect against vulnerabilities and threats.
- **Functionality:** [[concepts/new-features|New features]] or capabilities introduced.
