# Medical Paradox

Systemic contradictions and counterintuitive outcomes in [[concepts/health|healthcare]], particularly concerning [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], clinical [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] frameworks, and evidence dissemination.

## Characteristics
- **Performance vs. Utility Gap:** [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] demonstrate superior benchmark performance while introducing [[concepts/friction|friction]] in clinical workflows, liability allocation, or trust calibration.
- **Evidence Access Divergence:** Global evidence aggregation tools face regional exits or restrictions, creating information asymmetries despite digital connectivity.
- **[[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] [[concepts/opacity|Opacity]]:** Transition from assistive tools to autonomous reasoning systems raises ethical and [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] concerns without commensurate gains in transparency.
