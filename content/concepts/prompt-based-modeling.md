# Prompt Based Modeling

Prompt based modeling is a technique for designing and structuring prompts to guide AI language [[concepts/models|models]] in completing specific tasks or automating workflows. Rather than relying on fixed outputs or traditional programming logic, this approach leverages natural language [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to shape [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]], allowing practitioners to adapt system [[concepts/responses|responses]] without modifying underlying [[concepts/code|code]]. The effectiveness of prompt based modeling depends on clarity of instruction, appropriate context provision, and [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] based on model outputs.

## Core Applications

Prompt based modeling is commonly applied in [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] [[concepts/design|design]], where [[concepts/agents|agents]] need to decompose complex problems, [[entities/make|make]] decisions, and coordinate multiple steps. It enables [[concepts/automation|automation]] of workflows that would otherwise require manual intervention or custom development. [[concepts/scenarios|Use cases]] [[concepts/range|range]] from customer service automation and content generation to data analysis and process orchestration.

## Practical Considerations

The [[concepts/cost|cost]] and efficiency of prompt based modeling implementations vary depending on the underlying model and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] context. Factors such as prompt length, model complexity, and API call frequency directly impact operational expenses. As with other AI-driven approaches, designing effective prompts requires understanding both the model's [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and the specific requirements of the task [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] hand, often benefiting from iterative [[concepts/testing|testing]] and refinement cycles.
