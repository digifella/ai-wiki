# Hubble Space Telescope

The Hubble Space Telescope (HST) is a space-based observatory launched into [[concepts/low-earth-orbit-leo|low Earth orbit]] in 1990. It remains one of the most important tools in modern [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]], providing high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] images across the ultraviolet, visible, and near-infrared spectra.

## Deep Field Observations

Hubble's legacy is heavily defined by its "[[concepts/deep-field|Deep Field]]" [[concepts/software|programs]], which involve staring at seemingly empty patches of sky for extended periods to capture light from the most [[concepts/distant-galaxies|distant galaxies]].

- **[[concepts/hubble-deep-field|Hubble Deep Field]] (HDF):** First major deep field observation that revealed thousands of previously unseen galaxies, fundamentally changing our understanding of the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]].
- **Hubble Ultra Deep Field (HUDF):** An even deeper [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] that pushed the limits of [[concepts/light|visible light]] astronomy, showing galaxies formed just a few hundred million years after the [[concepts/big-bang|Big Bang]].
- **James Webb's 100-Hour Deep Field:** Recent observations by the [[concepts/james-webb-space-telescope]] ([[concepts/jwst|JWST]]) in the [[concepts/fornax-constellation|Fornax constellation]] have built upon Hubble's work. These 100-hour exposures unveil distant galaxies in regions previously thought to be empty, providing complementary data to Hubble's visible-light findings. See [[lab-notes/2026-09-16-James-Webbs-100-Hour-Deep-Field-Unveiling-Distant-Galaxi|James Webb's 100-Hour Deep Field: Unveiling Distant Galaxies in Fornax]] for detailed analysis of these recent discoveries.

## Key Contributions

- **Hubble Constant:** Helped refine the rate of [[concepts/expansion-of-the-universe|expansion of the universe]].
- **Exoplanet Atmospheres:** First to analyze the atmospheres of planets outside our solar system.
- **[[concepts/galaxy-formation-and-evolution|Galaxy Evolution]]:** Provided evidence for the co-evolution of galaxies and their central supermassive [[concepts/black-holes|black holes]].
