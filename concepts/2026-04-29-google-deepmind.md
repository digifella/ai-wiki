# 2026 04 29 Google Deepmind

Google DeepMind is the [[concepts/ai-research|artificial intelligence research]] division of Google, formed through the 2023 merger of Google Brain and DeepMind Technologies. Google Brain was Google's internal machine learning research team, while DeepMind Technologies was an independent AI research company that Google acquired in 2014. The consolidation unified both organizations' research operations and leadership, bringing together their respective teams and expertise in machine learning, [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]], and neural network development.

## Research Areas

The division conducts research across foundational machine learning, reinforcement learning algorithms, [[concepts/deep-learning-models|neural network architectures]], and [[concepts/multimodal-ai-agents|multimodal AI systems]]. Its work encompasses both theoretical research and applied applications, spanning areas such as [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]], computer vision, robotics, and [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]]. DeepMind's established research programs include:

*   **Generative Models & Diffusion:** Recent insights from Research Scientist Sander Dieleman highlight the development of large-scale diffusion models for image and video generation, focusing on technical intuition and scalability [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Dielemans-DeepMind-Insights-Building-Large-Scale-Diffusi|Dieleman's DeepMind Insights: Building Large-Scale Diffusion Models for Image and Video]].
*   **[[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]]:** Continued advancement in LLM architectures and capabilities.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** Applications in protein folding (e.g., AlphaFold) and other biological sciences.
