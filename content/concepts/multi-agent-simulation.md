# Multi Agent Simulation

Multi-[[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] refers to computational environments where multiple [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] interact, compete, and coexist according to defined rules and objectives. These simulations model complex system dynamics by allowing individual agents to [[entities/make|make]] independent decisions based on their local observations and programmed behaviors, with emergent patterns arising from their collective interactions.

[[entities/sakana-ai|Sakana AI]] has developed [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]] as a practical application of multi-agent simulation, creating environments where different AI species compete for resources and adapt over time. These simulations explore how artificial agents can evolve strategies, form cooperative [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and maintain population dynamics similar to biological ecosystems. The approach enables researchers to study emergent behaviors and test theories about agent interaction without requiring real-world [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

The research value of such simulations lies in understanding how simple individual rules can generate complex collective phenomena. By observing which strategies succeed or fail within constrained environments, researchers can gain insights into agent [[concepts/design|design]], resource allocation, and the conditions under which different AI systems can coexist productively. These digital ecosystems serve as experimental testbeds for developing more robust and adaptive AI architectures.
