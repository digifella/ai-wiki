# Generative AI Design Tool

A [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] [[concepts/design|design]] tool is a software application that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to assist designers in creating and iterating on visual assets. These tools leverage [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to automate or accelerate aspects of the [[concepts/design-thinking|design process]], including [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] (UI) design, graphic creation, and layout composition. By processing user inputs—whether [[concepts/text|text]] descriptions, sketches, or existing design elements—these [[concepts/software|applications]] can generate design suggestions, variations, or complete assets that designers can then refine and adapt.

## Capabilities and Applications

Generative [[concepts/front-end-components|AI design tools]] typically offer features such as automated layout suggestions, element generation based on textual prompts, [[concepts/style-transfer|style transfer]], and [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]]. They are used across contexts including web and app design, marketing materials, and [[concepts/product-design|product design]]. Rather than replacing designers, these tools function as assistants that handle routine generation tasks and exploration of design alternatives, allowing human designers to focus on higher-level creative decisions and refinement.

## Market Development

The category has grown as major technology companies and startups have entered the space. Tools positioning themselves as [[concepts/generative-uiux|AI-native design]] platforms represent an evolution in how design software integrates generative [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] from the ground up, rather than adding them as secondary features. This shift reflects broader changes in [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]] as teams increasingly incorporate AI-assisted generation into their creative processes.
