# Prediction Markets

[[entities/wall-street|Financial markets]] where participants trade securities based on future event outcomes; prices function as probabilistic forecasts via Information Aggregation.

## Core Mechanics
- **Price as [[concepts/probability|Probability]]:** Market price approximates the aggregate belief of event likelihood; efficient [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] requires Liquidity and diverse participation.
- **[[concepts/motivation|Incentive]] Alignment:** Financial stakes reward accurate Forecasting, reducing bias compared to unstructured polls or expert consensus.
- **[[concepts/solution|Resolution]]:** Contracts settle based on verified outcomes, enforcing [[concepts/honesty|Truthfulness]] through economic penalties for misprediction.

## Risks & Market Failures
- **[[concepts/insider-trading]]:** [[concepts/informational-asymmetry|Asymmetric information]] advantages distort prices, invalidating forecasts and raising ethical concerns regarding fairness and manipulation.
