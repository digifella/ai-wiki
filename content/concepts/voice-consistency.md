# Voice Consistency

Uniformity of vocal characteristics ([[entities/pitch|pitch]], tone, pacing) across AI-generated [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] or video outputs, critical for maintaining authenticity in synthetic media. Ensures seamless [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] and brand recognition.

## Key Principles
- Requires synchronized [[concepts/text-to-speech-synthesis|text-to-speech]] (TTS) modeling and audio parameter alignment
- Prevents [[concepts/cognitive-dissonance|cognitive dissonance]] in narrative [[concepts/continuity|continuity]]
- Essential for professional [[concepts/software|applications]] (e.g., marketing, eLearning)

## Implementation Tools
- Create Ai video with your face and any [[concepts/text|text]] demonstrates [[concepts/tone|voice]] [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] through:
  - [[concepts/character-consistency|Character consistency]] (visual and vocal) across scenes
  - [[concepts/custom-voice-narration|Custom voice narration]] for any text input
- Leverages tools like [Runway ML](https://runwayml.com) and [HeyGen](https://www.heygen.com) for cohesive [[concepts/output|output]]
