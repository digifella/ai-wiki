# Gemini 30 Pro

[[entities/gemini-30-pro|Gemini 30 Pro]] is an [[concepts/google-stitch-20|AI design agent]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] that automates the conversion of [[concepts/design|design]] concepts into functional outputs. The system accepts input in multiple formats, including [[concepts/text|text]] descriptions and hand-drawn sketches, processing these into design artifacts through [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] trained on design principles and patterns.

## Functionality and Use Cases

The [[entities/agent|agent]] operates as a productivity tool for designers and teams working under time constraints. By accepting natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] or visual sketches, it generates design outputs that can serve as starting points for further refinement. This approach reduces the initial labor-intensive [[concepts/phase|phase]] of design conceptualization, allowing human designers to focus on [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]] rather than generating first drafts from scratch.

## Technical Approach

The system leverages [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to interpret user input across different modalities. The underlying [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] enables cross-modal understanding, where [[concepts/sketch|sketch]]-based inputs and textual descriptions are processed through a unified framework to produce coherent design outputs that maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] with the original intent.
