# Color Masking

[[concepts/color-range-control|Color masking]] is a [[concepts/masking-technique|selective editing]] technique that allows photographers and digital artists to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific areas of an image based on color information. Rather than modifying an entire photograph uniformly, color masking isolates regions according to [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]], tonal values, or spatial boundaries. This approach provides precise control over which parts of an image receive particular edits, making it especially useful for targeted adjustments to specific elements like skies, foliage, or [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] without affecting the rest of the [[concepts/writing|composition]].

## AI-Powered Implementation

In modern workflows, particularly within [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]], color masking has been significantly enhanced by [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. Traditional methods required manual refinement of selection edges, which could be time-consuming and prone to inaccuracies. [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] now automatically detect and separate subjects, backgrounds, and distinct color zones with high [[concepts/accuracy|precision]]. This automation reduces the manual labor involved in creating complex masks, allowing for more efficient and consistent results across large batches of images.

## Practical Applications

The primary utility of color masking lies in its ability to isolate specific hues for independent adjustment. Users can target the blue channel to enhance skies without altering the overall [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], or isolate skin tones to correct color balance while preserving the [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of the background. This selective control is critical for maintaining natural-looking edits, as it prevents unwanted shifts in color or [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] in areas that should remain unchanged. By focusing adjustments on relevant data, the technique supports more nuanced and professional-grade post-processing outcomes.
