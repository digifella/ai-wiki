# Mosaic Floor Tile Patterns

Mosaic floor tile patterns are decorative arrangements of individual tiles composed to create larger visual designs. These patterns have been used in architectural design for centuries, combining aesthetic appeal with functional flooring solutions. The tiles are typically arranged in geometric or representational configurations to form cohesive visual compositions across floor surfaces.

## Physical Properties and Installation

Mosaic floor tiles vary in size depending on design requirements and aesthetic intent. Tiles of substantial dimensions, such as the 1.2-meter tiles documented at the [[entities/south-seas-cafe|South Seas cafe]], create bold visual statements with fewer individual units needed to cover a given area. The arrangement of such large tiles requires careful planning to ensure proper alignment and structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], particularly in high-traffic commercial environments like cafes and restaurants.

## Commercial Applications

Mosaic flooring has been adopted in hospitality settings as part of interior design schemes. The South Seas cafe, which features 1.2-meter mosaic floor tiles, exemplifies the use of this flooring approach in creating distinctive dining environments. Such installations contribute to the overall atmosphere and character of commercial spaces while maintaining practical durability for frequent foot traffic.
