# Gtc Conference

GTC Conference is a technology conference that features presentations and discussions on NVIDIA's [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|AI agent frameworks]], particularly focusing on [[concepts/secure|secure]] enterprise [[concepts/software|applications]]. The conference has served as a venue for comparing and evaluating different approaches to implementing [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] in business environments.

## Focus Areas

The conference has highlighted NVIDIA's development of [[concepts/secure-ai-agent|secure AI agent]] solutions, including [[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]] and [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], which are designed for enterprise [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]. These platforms represent different approaches to building and deploying AI agents that can operate reliably within organizational contexts.

The conference discussions have examined how these AI agent frameworks compare in terms of functionality, security features, and suitability for various enterprise [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. Topics have included the technical capabilities of different agent architectures and their potential applications across business operations.
