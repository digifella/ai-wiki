# Issuance Of Visas

[[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] offers an electronic visa ([[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-visa]]) system for eligible foreign nationals seeking entry to the country. The e-visa is a digital travel [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] that allows visitors to enter Turkey for tourism, business, and other short-term purposes without obtaining a traditional paper visa from a [[entities/turkish-embassy|Turkish embassy]] or consulate. This system streamlines the visa application process by eliminating the need for in-person visits to diplomatic missions.

## Application Process

Applicants complete their e-visa request through Turkey's official online portal, providing personal information, passport details, and travel plans. The process typically requires submission of a valid passport, proof of sufficient funds, and in some cases, supporting documentation relevant to the purpose of visit. Most applications are processed within hours or days, with approval notifications sent via email.

## Eligibility and Validity

Not all nationalities are eligible for Turkey's e-visa system, and requirements vary by country of citizenship. Those granted an e-visa receive a digital document linked to their passport number, which is presented upon arrival at Turkish border control. E-visas are typically valid for a specified duration from the date of issuance and permit stays of defined length, generally allowing multiple entries within the validity period for eligible visitors.
