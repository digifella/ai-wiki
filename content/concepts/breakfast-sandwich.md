# Breakfast Sandwich

A breakfast sandwich is a prepared food item consisting of a filling placed between slices of bread, typically served as a morning meal. The sandwich format allows for quick assembly and consumption, making it a popular choice for breakfast across many cultures. [[concepts/breakfast-sandwiches|Breakfast sandwiches]] commonly feature proteins such as eggs, bacon, sausage, or ham, along with cheese and vegetables, though regional and cultural variations exist in both ingredients and [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] methods.

## Korean Street Toast

[[concepts/korean-street-toast|Korean street toast]], known locally as *toast* or *토스트*, represents a distinctive regional interpretation of the breakfast sandwich. These sandwiches are commonly prepared and sold by street vendors and small establishments throughout Korea, often served as quick, affordable meals. Korean street toast typically features a filling of eggs, ham or processed meat, vegetables such as lettuce or tomato, and condiments like mayonnaise or ketchup, all contained within sliced bread that is often buttered and lightly toasted on the exterior.

The preparation of Korean street toast emphasizes efficiency and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], with vendors developing standardized methods for assembling multiple sandwiches in succession. The bread is typically buttered before toasting to create a crispy exterior while maintaining a soft interior. Assembly usually follows a logical sequence, with proteins and vegetables layered systematically before the sandwich is cut diagonally and wrapped for serving, a presentation [[concepts/style|style]] characteristic of [[concepts/korean-street-food|Korean street food]] culture.
