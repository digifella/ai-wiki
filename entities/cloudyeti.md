# Cloudyeti

CloudYeti is a YouTube channel dedicated to the practical implementation of local artificial intelligence systems. The content targets developers and technology practitioners who seek to deploy AI models on personal hardware rather than relying exclusively on cloud-based services. The channel emphasizes self-hosted solutions, providing technical guidance on managing and optimizing AI infrastructure within private environments.

It explores the technical aspects of running code generation models, such as Qwen Code, using tools like Ollama. In addition to specific tooling tutorials, the channel addresses broader discussions regarding the frameworks and utilities necessary for effective local AI management.
