# Spacetime geometry

The mathematical description of the four-dimensional manifold within General Relativity, defining the metric, Curvature, and causal structure of the universe.

## Core Components
- **Metric Tensor**: Defines the interval between events and the local geometry of the manifold.
- **Curvature**: Characterized by the [[entities/bernhard-riemann|Riemann]] Curvature Tensor; determines the deviation of Geodesics from Euclidean straight lines.
- **Topology**: The global structure, connectivity, and shape of the manifold.

## Cosmological Implications
- **Flatness Problem**: The extreme [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] required for the universe's [[concepts/density|density]] to remain near the critical density, implying a near-[[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] 

## Quantum Gravity and Repulsive Mechanisms
Recent [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]] investigate non-classical [[concepts/gravitational-effects|gravitational effects]], specifically the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of repulsive forces through quantum mechanical principles.
- **[[concepts/antigravity-technology|Repulsive Gravity]] via Quantum Superposition**: Theoretical proposals suggest that "antigravity" or repulsive gravitational forces can emerge from the realm of [[concepts/quantum-mechanics|quantum mechanics]], challenging traditional classical interpretations of gravity.
- **Destructive Interference**: [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]] involving [[concepts/quantum-superposition|quantum superposition]] and [[concepts/destructive-interference|destructive interference]] are posited to generate these repulsive effects.
- **Theoretical Context**: This area of study intersects with [[concepts/quantum-gravity|quantum gravity]] and attempts to reconcile General Relativity with [[concepts/quantum-field-theory|quantum field theory]].
