# Engine failure

The cessation of normal operation in an engine, typically resulting from mechanical, thermal, or fluid-dynamic breakdown.

## Critical Failure Mechanisms
* **[[concepts/lubrication-system]] failure**: The primary driver of catastrophic engine death; the system is essential for preventing metal-on-metal [[entities/contact|contact]].
* **Loss of [[concepts/oil-pressure]]**: A critical state where the engine's ability to survive is immediately compromised.
* **Thermal/Frictional breakdown**: In an [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine]], the absence of continuous oil [[concepts/flow|flow]] leads to rapid component degradation and seizure.

## Monitoring and Early Warning
* **Warning Lights**: Essential real-time [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] of system instability.
* **[[concepts/oil-pressure-warning-light]]**: A high-priority signal that the lubrication-driven survival mechanism has failed.
