# Affect Theory

Affect theory is a branch of interdisciplinary studies that explores emotional [[concepts/responses|responses]] and their impact on human behavior, [[concepts/cognition|cognition]], and social interactions. It draws from various fields including psychology, sociology, and [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] to understand the dynamics of feelings and affects.

- Slavoj Žižek - influential philosopher known for his contributions to psychoanalytic theory.
- Judith Butler - prominent feminist theorist whose work often intersects with affect theory.
