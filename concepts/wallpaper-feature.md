# Wallpaper Feature

## Overview
The **wallpaper feature** allows users to customize the [[concepts/gui-interface|visual interface]] of their device. In the context of **[[concepts/ios-27]]**, this functionality is deeply integrated with the **Photos App**, leveraging new organizational and editing tools to streamline the selection and application of personal imagery.

## iOS 27 Integration
With the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of **[[concepts/ubuntu|iOS 27]]**, the workflow for setting wallpapers has been enhanced through [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to the **Photos App**. Key improvements include:

- **Granular [[concepts/photo-management|Photo Management]]**: New tools allow for precise selection and curation of images specifically for wallpaper use, reducing clutter in the selection process.
- **Advanced [[concepts/ai-powered-editing|AI-Powered Editing]]**: AI capabilities enable automatic background separation, [[concepts/automated-color-harmonization|color matching]], and enhancement, ensuring the selected image fits the device's aesthetic and lighting conditions.
- **Enhanced Organization**: Improved tagging and album structures help users quickly locate high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] or previously edited images suitable for wallpapers.
