# Vostok Research Station

**Vostok Station** (also known as Mirnyy-Vostok) is a Russian Antarctic research station located on the southeastern shore of [[entities/lake-vostok|Lake Vostok]], near the geographic South Pole. It sits atop the East Antarctic Ice Sheet, one of the highest and coldest [[entities/places|places]] on [[entities/earth|Earth]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Location:** Eastern Antarctica, approx. 1,200 km from the South Pole.
- **Elevation:** ~3,488 meters (11,444 ft) above sea level.
- **Climate:** One of the coldest inhabited places on Earth; recorded minimum temperature of -89.2°C (-128.6°F).
- **Operation:** Year-[[concepts/rounding|round]] scientific outpost focused on atmospheric, astrophysical, and geological research.

## Subglacial Environment & Lake Vostok
The station is situated directly above [[concepts/subglacial-lake|Lake Vostok]], the largest of Antarctica's subglacial lakes.
- **Depth:** The lake lies beneath approximately 4 kilometers (2.5 miles) of ice.
- **[[concepts/disconnection|Isolation]]:** Sealed off from the surface environment for millions of years, creating a unique isolated ecosystem.
- **Discovery & Study:** Detailed exploration has been facilitated by Russian and international teams operating from Vostok Station.
	- See: [[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Lake-Vostok-Antarcticas-Isolated-Subglacial-World-Discov|Lake Vostok: Antarctica's Isolated Subglacial World, Discovered Beneath Four Kilometers of Ice]] for detailed analysis of the lake's isolation and discovery context.

## Scientific Significance
- **Paleoclimate Data:** Ice cores drilled at Vostok provide some of the oldest and most complete records of Earth's climate history (up to 420,000 years old).
- **[[concepts/astrophysics|Astrophysics]]:** Home to the BICEP array and other experiments studying the [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] and [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]].
- **Subglacial Exploration:** Primary access point for studying subglacial hydrology and potential extremophile life in Lake Vostok.
