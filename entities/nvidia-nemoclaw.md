# Nvidia Nemoclaw

NVIDIA NemoClaw is a secure enterprise AI agent platform designed to facilitate the safe deployment and operation of autonomous artificial intelligence agents within business environments. The system was developed specifically to address security vulnerabilities identified in OpenClaw, an earlier automated information processing system. By focusing on robust security protocols, NemoClaw aims to provide a reliable infrastructure for organizations looking to integrate autonomous AI capabilities into their operations without compromising data integrity or system safety.

Security serves as a central design principle in the platform's architecture and operational framework. This emphasis on protection allows enterprises to deploy AI agents that can perform complex, autonomous tasks while maintaining strict governance standards. The platform is intended for organizations that require high levels of control and oversight, ensuring that automated processes adhere to established security policies and regulatory requirements.
