# Youtube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] in 2006. It functions as one of the internet's largest repositories of [[concepts/video-resource|video content]], allowing users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos. The service hosts a diverse range of material, including user-generated content, professional productions, [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]], and entertainment media.

## Core Features

Users create accounts to upload and manage videos through a [[concepts/creator|creator]] dashboard. The platform provides tools for [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]], monetization, and audience analytics. Viewers can search for content, subscribe to channels, and interact with videos through likes and comments. The algorithmic recommendation system plays a significant role in content discovery, tailoring suggestions based on viewing history and engagement patterns.

## Business Model

YouTube operates primarily on a [[concepts/freemium-model|freemium model]], offering free access to content supported by advertisements. Through the YouTube Partner Program, eligible creators can earn revenue from ads displayed on their videos. The platform also offers YouTube Premium, a [[concepts/paid-subscriptions|subscription service]] that removes ads and provides access to exclusive content and background playback. Additionally, YouTube Music and YouTube TV offer specialized [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] and live television services, respectively.
