# Gtc

Gtc is NVIDIA's enterprise-focused secure AI agent technology platform designed to enable organizations to deploy AI agents for business applications while maintaining security as a foundational architectural component. The platform provides tools and frameworks that address enterprise demand for controlled deployment of autonomous AI systems while maintaining compliance with organizational and regulatory standards.

## Platform Variants

The Gtc platform includes multiple variants tailored to different deployment scenarios. NemoClaw and OpenClaw represent distinct approaches within the platform, each designed to serve specific use cases and organizational requirements for secure agent deployment in production environments.

## Enterprise Focus

As an enterprise solution, Gtc emphasizes the balance between operational capability and security governance. The platform integrates security controls throughout the agent development and deployment lifecycle, enabling organizations to leverage autonomous AI systems while maintaining oversight and compliance requirements appropriate for mission-critical business applications.
