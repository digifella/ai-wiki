# Simon Sinek

Simon Sinek is a popular motivational [[entities/speaker|speaker]] and author known for his insights on [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], specifically the concept of starting with "why." He explores why some individuals and organizations achieve enduring success while others struggle to inspire action. His work encourages leaders to focus on their [[concepts/purpose|purpose]] and [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] as the driving force behind effective communication and inspiration.

- **Key Concepts:**
  - The [[concepts/golden-circle|Golden Circle]]
  - [[concepts/start-with-why|Start With Why]]
