# Opal Labs

Opal Labs is an experimental no-code AI tool developed by Google Labs that enables users to create and share AI mini-applications without requiring programming knowledge. The platform is designed to lower the barrier to entry for AI application development by allowing users to build functional AI tools through natural language descriptions of their requirements.

## Functionality

The platform allows users to construct AI-powered applications by describing their needs in natural language rather than writing traditional code. This approach democratizes AI tool creation, making it accessible to users without technical backgrounds who want to develop custom AI applications for specific purposes.

## Distribution

Once created, users are able to share their AI mini-applications built on Opal Labs with others, facilitating the distribution and reuse of AI tools across different use cases and communities.
