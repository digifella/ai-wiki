# Notebooklm Workflow

[[concepts/ai-powered-workflow|NotebookLM Workflow]] is a process for converting presentation content generated by [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] into video formats suitable for distribution without platform [[concepts/watermarks|watermarks]]. NotebookLM is an [[concepts/ai-driven-research|AI-powered research]] assistant that synthesizes information from user-uploaded documents and sources to produce [[concepts/structured-outputs|structured outputs]], including [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] with [[entities/speaker|speaker]] [[concepts/notes|notes]]. The workflow leverages Google's integrated toolset to move content from the research [[concepts/phase|phase]] through to final video production.

## Content Generation and Export

NotebookLM generates slide presentations based on [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]] provided by users. These [[concepts/google-slides|slides]] include speaker notes that outline talking points for each section. The initial output can be exported from NotebookLM in standard formats compatible with presentation software. This export step is the foundation for downstream processing and allows content to be repurposed across multiple distribution channels.

## Video Production

The exported slides and notes are then processed through Google's [[concepts/video-creation-tools|video creation tools]] to produce unwatermarked [[concepts/video-resource|video content]]. This step typically involves converting the slide deck into a video format, often incorporating [[concepts/audio-production|text-to-speech]] narration derived from the speaker notes or creating a framework for manual recording. The resulting video files can be distributed across platforms without the watermarks that would normally appear if content were created directly through platform-specific recording features.

## Use Cases and Distribution

This workflow enables researchers, educators, and content creators to efficiently transform research documents into polished video presentations for sharing on social media, [[concepts/learning|learning]] management systems, or other distribution channels. By automating the synthesis and initial presentation phases through NotebookLM, creators can focus effort on video production quality rather than manual slide creation.
