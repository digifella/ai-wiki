# Video codecs

Algorithms that compress and decompress digital video, enabling efficient [[entities/storage|storage]] and transmission. Key types include lossy (e.g., H.264, H.265) and lossless (e.g., FFV1) formats.

## Compression Tools
- Handbrake: [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tool for [[concepts/transcoding|transcoding]], converting, and resizing video files across [[entities/windows|Windows]], Mac, and Linux. Originally designed for [[concepts/dvd-ripping|DVD ripping]], now supports diverse codecs for [[concepts/video-compression]] without significant quality loss.
