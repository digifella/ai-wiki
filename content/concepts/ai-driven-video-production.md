# AI Driven Video Production

AI-driven video production combines [[concepts/automated-slide-generation|automated slide generation]] with video synthesis to streamline [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]. This workflow leverages [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] to generate structured [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source materials, then converts them into finished videos using [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]]. The approach reduces manual [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] work while maintaining quality output suitable for educational, marketing, or documentary content.

## Workflow Process

The process begins with [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], which analyzes input documents and automatically generates presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] with [[entities/speaker|speaker]] notes. These slides are then imported into [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], which synthesizes video content from the slide deck. The integration allows creators to move from written source material to video format without manually recording or editing footage frame-by-frame.

## Watermark Removal

A key consideration in this workflow is the removal of watermarks from the final video output. Google Vids may apply watermarks to free-tier videos, which can be eliminated through specific techniques or account settings. This enables creators to produce unwatermarked content suitable for professional [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] or publication on various platforms without visible branding artifacts.
