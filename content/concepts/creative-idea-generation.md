# Creative Idea Generation

Creative Idea Generation refers to the process of using [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to assist in developing, planning, and conceptualizing new ideas across various domains. Within [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s Codex framework, this capability has been integrated into [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] designed to support creative workflows and ideation processes.

## ChatGPT 5.1 and Creative Applications

ChatGPT 5.1 is a model introduced as part of OpenAI's [[concepts/creative-ideas|Codex updates]] that specifically focuses on planning and generating creative ideas. The model demonstrates particular effectiveness in [[concepts/brainstorming|brainstorming]], conceptualization, and idea development tasks, making it applicable to fields ranging from [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] to product design and strategic planning.
