# Perspective Adjustment

Perspective adjustment is a technique in digital photography and [[concepts/design|graphic design]] used to correct or alter the apparent perspective of an object within an image. This can involve transforming skewed objects into straightened shapes or changing the viewpoint of an image, making it appear as though the viewer has changed their position relative to the subject.

- **Definition**: Perspective adjustment involves manipulating [[concepts/images|images]] to change the perceived angles and distances between objects.
- **[[concepts/software|Applications]]**:
  - [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]] and real estate
  - Product photography
  - Fine [[concepts/art|art]] and graphic [[concepts/design|design]]
