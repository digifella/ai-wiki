# Alastair Philip Wiper

Alastair Philip Wiper is a journalist and writer known for reporting on cultural and scientific topics. He has contributed to major publications covering subjects ranging from archaeology to [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and social change.

## Denmark's Archaeology Initiative

Wiper reported on Denmark's distinctive approach to archaeological discovery, which involves authorizing private metal detectorists to search for artifacts on farmland. Rather than restricting artifact hunting to credentialed archaeologists, the [[entities/danish-government|Danish government]] integrated amateur detectorists into the archaeological process, creating a collaborative framework for uncovering historical objects. This decentralized model has yielded significant finds that have enhanced understanding of Danish history and settlement patterns across different periods.

The initiative demonstrates how institutional archaeology can adapt to include public participation while maintaining documentation and knowledge [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]. The artifacts recovered through this program—including metals, tools, and other objects—have provided new insights into Denmark's past that might have otherwise remained undiscovered.
