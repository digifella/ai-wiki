# Insight Presentation

Insight Presentation is an approach to creating [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] using [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] that emphasizes proper [[concepts/structure|structure]] and editing rather than relying on automated generation alone. Based on guidance from content creator [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]], the methodology addresses a common limitation of AI presentation platforms: they often produce visually polished but structurally weak presentations that fail to communicate ideas effectively.

## The Core Challenge

Many [[concepts/consumer-demand-drivers|AI presentation tools]] prioritize aesthetic output over logical flow and message clarity. These platforms can quickly generate [[entities/google-slides|slides]] with professional formatting and design, but the underlying content structure—how ideas connect, build upon each other, and support a central argument—often remains poorly organized. This results in presentations that look good but don't persuade or inform the audience effectively.

## Structuring for Impact

The Insight Presentation approach involves using AI as a tool within a deliberate workflow rather than as an autonomous generator. This means beginning with a clear outline of your argument or narrative, using AI to generate initial content and design suggestions, then critically editing the structure to ensure logical progression and coherence. The presenter maintains control over the skeleton of the presentation while leveraging AI for content drafting and visual styling.

## Application

The methodology is particularly relevant for professionals who need to create presentations quickly but cannot compromise on effectiveness. By combining human judgment about structure with AI's speed in content generation and design, creators can produce presentations that are both time-efficient and substantively sound.
