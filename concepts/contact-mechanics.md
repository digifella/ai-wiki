# Contact Mechanics

**[[entities/contact|Contact]] mechanics** is the study of the deformation of solids that touch each other at one or more points. It bridges Continuum Mechanics, Tribology, and Material [[concepts/science|Science]] to analyze [[concepts/stress|stress]], strain, and energy transfer during interaction.

## Core Principles
- **Hertzian Contact [[concepts/theory|Theory]]**: Describes elastic contact between curved surfaces, predicting elliptical contact areas and pressure distributions.
- **[[concepts/energy-dissipation|Energy Dissipation]]**: Real-[[entities/earth|world]] contacts involve losses via hysteresis, plastic deformation, and heat generation, reducing the [[concepts/coefficient-of-restitution]].
- **[[concepts/friction|Friction]] & Adhesion**: Surface interactions governed by Coulomb Friction and intermolecular forces (e.g., van der Waals) significantly alter load transfer.

## Phenomena & Paradoxes
- **The Paradox of [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|Zero]] Bounce from Similarly Elastic Objects**: Contrary to intuition, combining two highly elastic objects does not guarantee optimal rebound. Specific configurations can result in near-[[concepts/zero|zero]] bounce due to complex energy partitioning and [[concepts/phase|phase]] cancellation during impact [[lab-notes/2026-06-26-The-Paradox-of-Zero-Bounce-from-Similarly-Elastic-Object|The Paradox of Zero Bounce from Similarly Elastic Objects]].
  - Demonstrated by [[entities/steve-mould|Steve Mould]] using "MegaBounce" balls, showing that high individual elasticity does not linearly translate to system-level rebound efficiency.

## Applications
- **[[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]**: Bearing design, gear tooth interaction, and tire-road contact.
- **Biomechanics**: Joint loading and foot-ground interaction during gait.
- **Micro/Nano-scale**: AFM tip-sample interactions and MEMS device [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].
