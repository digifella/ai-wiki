# Professional Commercial Production

Professional Commercial Production refers to workflows that leverage advanced [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]]—specifically Seedance 2.0 for [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]], [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] for creative direction and scripting, and [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]] for [[concepts/visual-asset-creation|visual asset creation]]—to streamline the creation of commercial content [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] professional quality [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]. This approach combines generative [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] with [[concepts/claude-prompting|structured prompting]] techniques to produce cohesive, broadcast-ready advertisements with reduced production timelines and costs.

## Workflow Integration

The core [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] integrates multiple AI systems in sequence. Claude AI functions as the creative lead, generating detailed scripts, shot descriptions, and visual direction based on commercial briefs. These outputs then inform prompts for Seedance 2.0, which generates video sequences, and GPT Image 2, which produces supporting visual assets. This layered approach ensures thematic [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] and narrative coherence across generated content.

## Practical Applications

Organizations use this workflow to produce commercials for digital platforms, social media campaigns, and broadcast contexts. The combination of video generation with AI-assisted scripting enables rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] on creative concepts and multiple variations from a single initial brief, supporting A/B [[concepts/testing|testing]] and audience-specific [[concepts/customization|customization]] with minimal additional production overhead.
