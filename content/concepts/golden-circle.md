# Golden Circle

The **Golden Circle** is a conceptual model developed by [[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]] that explains how great leaders and organizations inspire others to act through the power of their "why." It consists of three concentric circles:
- **Why**: The [[concepts/purpose|purpose]] or cause.
- **How**: The unique strengths or values used to fulfill [[concepts/core-purpose|the why]].
- **What**: The products, services, and tangible actions.

### Key Concepts
- **Inspiring Leadership**: Leaders who communicate from the inside out (starting with "why") inspire others more effectively than those who focus solely on what they do.
- **[[concepts/motivation|Purpose]]-Driven Communication**: Communicating one's purpose first creates a deeper emotional [[concepts/connection|connection]].
- **Golden Circle Model**: Helps individuals and organizations clarify their mission, differentiate themselves in crowded markets, and foster loyalty.

### Summary
[[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]’s TEDx talk, "[[concepts/start-with-why|Start With Why]]: How Great Leaders Inspire Action," explores the fundamental question of why some individuals and organizations achieve enduring success and inspire action, while others with seemingly equal resources and talent falter. Sinek begins by highlighting iconic examples such as Apple to illustrate how these successful entities articulate their [[concepts/purpose|purpose]] first.
