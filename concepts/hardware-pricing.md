# Hardware Pricing

## Core Dynamics
*   **Decoupling of Cost and Price:** Modern hardware [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] often diverges from manufacturing costs due to shifting [[concepts/revenue-streams|revenue streams]].
*   **Data as Currency:** Low upfront hardware costs are frequently subsidized by long-term data collection and monetization strategies.
*   **[[concepts/business-model|Business Model]] Shift:** Transition from one-time hardware sales to recurring revenue models via services and data.

## Case Study: Smart TVs
*   **Affordability Driver:** The surprising affordability of modern [[concepts/smart-tv]]s is not due to hardware [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], but a fundamental shift in business models [[lab-notes/2026-09-13-Smart-TV-Data-Collection-and-Shifting-Business-Models|Smart TV Data Collection and Shifting Business Models]].
*   **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]:** Unlike 15 years ago when TVs cost thousands, today they can be purchased at low margins because the value proposition includes user data.
*   **Surveillance Capitalism:** Manufacturers collect extensive user data, influencing the pricing strategy to prioritize [[concepts/global-market-share|market penetration]] over unit profit.
