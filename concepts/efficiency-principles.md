# Efficiency Principles

Optimization frameworks minimizing resource expenditure (time, [[concepts/compute|compute]], energy, [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]]) relative to outcome quality. Focuses on eliminating waste, reducing latency, and maximizing throughput in cognitive and operational systems.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Resource Allocation:** Directing limited capacity toward high-leverage activities via Pareto Principle analysis.
- **Automation:** Offloading repetitive execution to systems to preserve cognitive [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
- **[[concepts/iterative-refinement|Iterative Refinement]]:** Continuous [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] to reduce delta between current performance and optimal bounds.

## AI & Computational Efficiency
- **[[concepts/skills-based-interaction|Skills-Based Interaction]] Paradigm:** Transition from transient, one-off prompts to persistent, reusable "[[concepts/skills|skills]]" reduces [[concepts/context-window|context window]] overhead and enforces standardized [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]]. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-17-Anthropic-Engineers-Claude-Prompting-Skills-Based-AI-Int|Anthropic Engineers' Claude Prompting: Skills-Based-AI-Interaction Principles]].
- **[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]] Practices:** [[entities/claude-projects|Claude workflows]] utilize structured [[concepts/skill|skill]] definitions to encapsulate [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] chains, improving reproducibility and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] in [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] tasks.
- **Modular [[concepts/ai-prompt|Prompt Architecture]]:** Breaking monolithic [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] into composable units lowers error rates and facilitates targeted [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], aligning with [[concepts/software-engineering|Software Engineering]] Principles of modularity.
- **[[concepts/token-optimization|Context Optimization]]:** Pre-loading relevant skill definitions minimizes redundant token usage, directly improving Token Efficiency and [[concepts/speed|inference speed]].
- **Standardization:** Defined skills create a shared vocabulary for AI interactions, reducing interpretation variance and alignment [[concepts/friction|friction]].
