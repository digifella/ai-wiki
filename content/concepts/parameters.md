# Parameters

Parameters in [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and AI are essential elements that define the model's [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], [[concepts/power|influence]] its [[concepts/training-process|training process]], and affect its performance. These settings can include hyperparameters (like [[concepts/learning|learning]] rate, batch size) and parameters learned during [[concepts/training|training]] ([[concepts/weights|weights]] and [[concepts/biases|biases]]). Adjusting these values optimizes the model for specific tasks or datasets.
