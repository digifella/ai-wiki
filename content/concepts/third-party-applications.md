# Third-party applications

2026 04 14 Chat [[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] update Channel [[entities/rob|Rob]] the AI Guy

Third-party applications are [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/software|programs]] developed by entities other than the original manufacturer of a platform or system. They extend the functionality of the primary system, often integrating with it to provide additional features or services.

## Key Characteristics
- **[[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Designed to work alongside existing systems, often through APIs or other interfaces.
- **Enhanced Functionality**: Provide [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] not available in the original system.
- **Independence**: Developed and maintained by external developers or companies.

## Examples
- **Browser Extensions**: Tools like ad blockers or password managers that enhance web browsing.
- **Mobile Apps**: Applications available on app stores that interact with a device's operating system.
- **[[concepts/ai-tools|AI Tools]]**: Applications that integrate with AI platforms to provide specialized services.

## Use Cases
- **Productivity**: Tools that streamline workflows or automate tasks.
- **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Applications that allow users to tailor their experience.
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Software that makes systems more accessible to users with disabilities.
