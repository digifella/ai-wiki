# AI Ecosystem

[[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s AI ecosystem as of April 2026 centers on two primary products: [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]. These tools represent the company's approach to making [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] accessible across different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], from general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] to document-focused research and analysis.

## Gemini

Gemini functions as Google's [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] assistant, capable of processing text, [[concepts/images|images]], and other input formats. The platform has undergone continued development and refinement, with updates to its capabilities and integration points within Google's broader service offerings.

## NotebookLM

NotebookLM extends AI capabilities into research and document analysis workflows. The product allows users to input various source materials and [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] to generate insights, summaries, and interactive analysis. Recent updates to NotebookLM have expanded its feature set and deepened its integration with complementary [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]].

## Integration and Development

The evolution of both products reflects Google's investment in AI integration across its ecosystem. These tools are designed to work within existing user workflows while introducing new possibilities for how AI can augment research, [[concepts/writing|writing]], and analysis tasks. The continued updates to both platforms suggest an ongoing cycle of [[concepts/feature-development|feature development]] and user capability expansion.
