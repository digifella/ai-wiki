# Scaling Bottlenecks

Constraints that impede the expansion of system performance, capacity, or intelligence in large-scale computational models.

## Primary Vectors
- [[concepts/compute|Compute]]-availability: Hardware shortages or inaccuracies in provisioning GPU/TPU clusters.
- Data-[[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]]: The "data wall" caused by the exhaustion of [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]], human-generated training [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
- [[concepts/memory|Memory]]-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]]: The physical limitation of data transfer speeds between [[concepts/memory|memory]] and [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]].
- Infrastructure-limits: Constraints involving [[concepts/electricity-grid|power grid]] capacity, cooling, and network latency.
