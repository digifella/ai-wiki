## Summary Report: IBM's Five AI Risks & Career-Ending Data Leakage
**[[concepts/clip-title|Clip title]]:** Five AI Risks That Can Get You Fired—And How to Avoid Them

### Summary
The video by [[entities/martin-keen|Martin Keen]] from IBM outlines five significant ways [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) can lead to career-ending consequences for individuals and substantial risks for organizations. The central theme emphasizes that while AI can boost [[concepts/productivity|productivity]], its unsupervised or unregulated use poses critical threats, often because employees unknowingly create vulnerabilities. Keen stresses that each of these [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] has already resulted in career terminations and millions in organizational losses, highlighting the urgent need for caution and robust [[concepts/governance|governance]].

The first two risks are closely intertwined with data [[concepts/security|security]]. "[[concepts/shadow-it|Shadow AI]]" refers to employees using unapproved [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], such as personal [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] accounts for work or unauthorized browser [[concepts/plugins|plugins]]. This unapproved use frequently leads to "[[concepts/data-leakage|data leakage]]," where sensitive company data (like proprietary [[concepts/code|code]] or customer records) is pasted into these third-party [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. Once this data is submitted, it's often used to train the AI's [[concepts/models|models]], becoming irreversibly "baked in" and beyond the organization's [[concepts/power|control]]. Keen cites an IBM report indicating that 20% of data breaches are caused by [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]], emphasizing that such actions can trigger a "career-ending conversation" for the employee involved and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] issues for IT leaders who fail to implement effective [[concepts/ai-policy|AI governance]].

The next two dangers revolve around the [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] of information and system [[concepts/power|control]]. "[[concepts/data-hallucination|Hallucination]] Laundering" occurs when AI generates plausible but incorrect content (hallucinations), which an employee then copies and presents as their own verified work or reports. This can significantly damage credibility, with examples ranging from lawyers submitting court filings with fabricated case citations to executives making major business decisions based on unverified AI [[concepts/output|output]]. "Prompt Injection" describes how attackers manipulate AI by crafting inputs that override the system's original [[concepts/instructions|instructions]]. This can be "direct" (an explicit malicious command) or, more dangerously, "indirect," where malicious [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] are hidden within data (documents, emails, web pages) that the AI is prompted to retrieve and process, making detection difficult and potentially leading to the AI revealing confidential information.

Finally, the fifth risk is "Unauthorized [[concepts/agentic-ai|Agentic AI]]." This refers to [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] that perform tasks without continuous human oversight, including interacting with databases, making [[entities/api-calls|API calls]], executing/writing code, and sending messages. The particular threat highlighted is the "Zombie [[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]]" – an agent initially deployed for a legitimate [[concepts/motivation|purpose]], like a [[concepts/proof|proof]] of concept, but left [[concepts/running|running]] and authenticated after the project concludes. These forgotten agents become unmonitored backdoors into organizational systems, capable of inadvertently deleting production [[concepts/files|files]], sending unauthorized emails, or performing actions that violate [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] regulations, again posing severe accountability challenges for both the individual who created it and the IT department.

In conclusion, Martin Keen underscores that using AI without proper governance, clear [[concepts/policies|policies]], and stringent [[concepts/verification|verification]] processes is a recipe for disaster. The video serves as a critical warning that while AI offers immense potential for productivity, unchecked [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] and user behavior can lead to significant financial losses, legal repercussions, and ultimately, job loss. The key takeaway is to embrace AI responsibly, with strong governance frameworks and a culture of verification, to mitigate these critical risks.
