# Nature Neuroscience

**Nature [[concepts/neuroscience|Neuroscience]]** is a leading peer-reviewed scientific journal focusing on the nervous system. It publishes high-impact research across molecular, cellular, systems, and computational neuroscience.

## Key Concepts & Discoveries

### Dual Embryonic Origins of Human Brain Development
Recent research challenges the traditional view of a unified brain origin.

- **Core Finding:** The human brain develops from two distinct pools of [[concepts/neural-progenitor-cells|neural progenitor cells]] ([[concepts/npcs|NPCs]]) rather than a single unified pool.
- **Implication:** This suggests the brain may functionally and developmentally operate as two separate organs during embryogenesis.
- **Source:** [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Dual-Embryonic-Origins-of-Human-Brain-Development|Dual Embryonic Origins of Human Brain Development]]
- **Context:** The discovery by [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]] developmental [[concepts/biology-researchers|biologists]] highlights the complexity of human-specific brain evolution.
