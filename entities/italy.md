# Italy

The Italy detector is a particle physics instrument located deep underground in the Stawell gold mine in Victoria, Australia. It is positioned at significant depth to shield its sensitive equipment from cosmic radiation, creating an environment suitable for detecting rare particle interactions. The detector operates in conjunction with the SABER detector as part of a collaborative effort to search for weakly interacting massive particles (WIMPs).

WIMPs are theoretical candidates for dark matter, which is hypothesized to constitute a large portion of the universe's mass but does not emit or interact with electromagnetic radiation. By operating in a low-background environment, the Italy detector aims to identify the faint signals produced by WIMP interactions with atomic nuclei, contributing to the broader scientific understanding of dark matter composition and properties.
