# European Union

The **[[entities/eu|European Union]]** (EU) is a political and economic union of 27 member states located primarily in [[entities/europe]]. It has developed a single market through a standardised system of laws that applies in all member states, ensuring the free [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of goods, capital, services, and people.

## Key Characteristics

- **Supranational [[concepts/governance|Governance]]**: Operates via institutions including the European Commission, European Parliament, and Council of the [[entities/european-union|European Union]].
- **Economic Integration**: Uses the Euro as the official currency for 20 member states participating in the Eurozone.
- **Legal Framework**: Based on treaties, most notably the Treaty of Rome and the Treaty on European Union.
