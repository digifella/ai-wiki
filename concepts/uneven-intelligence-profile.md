# Uneven Intelligence Profile

**Uneven Intelligence Profile** refers to a cognitive architecture or capability distribution where specific domains exhibit high [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] while others remain significantly underdeveloped, contrasting with the uniform [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]] expected in [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence]].

## Core Concepts

*   **Domain-Specific vs. General Capabilities:** Highlights the gap between narrow [[concepts/ai-specialization|AI specialization]] and the broad [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]] required for true [[concepts/true-intelligence|general intelligence]].
*   **Alignment Risks:** Uneven profiles may lead to [[concepts/ai-safety]] challenges if high-capability domains are optimized without corresponding ethical or [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]] in other areas.
*   **Human Analogy:** Often compared to savant syndrome or specific cognitive deficits in humans, where exceptional [[concepts/skill|skill]] coexists with significant functional limitations.

## AGI Trajectory & Definitions

The concept of uneven intelligence is critical when evaluating the path to [[concepts/agi]]. Key insights from leading researchers include:

*   **[[concepts/minimal-agi|Minimal AGI]] Definition:** [[lab-notes/2026-10-13-DeepMind-Co-founder-Shane-Legg-AGI-Definition-Trajectory|DeepMind Co-founder Shane Legg: AGI Definition, Trajectory, and Superintelligence]] defines "minimal AGI" as an artificial agent capable of performing [[concepts/cognitive-tasks|cognitive tasks]] across a wide range of domains, not just excelling in one.
*   **Trajectory Analysis:** Legg discusses the societal implications and current state of AGI, emphasizing that achieving uniform cognitive competence is a distinct milestone from achieving [[entities/high-performance|high performance]] in isolated tasks.
*   **[[concepts/superintelligence|Superintelligence]] Implications:** The transition from uneven profiles to superintelligence requires not just raw computational power but the integration of diverse cognitive functions.
