# Prof Alex Usher

Prof Alex Usher is a German academic who has contributed expertise in the evaluation of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs). His work has involved providing [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to Lemai on the capabilities and performance of multiple LLM systems.

## Involvement with Lemai's AI Maturity Model

Usher has participated in collaborative efforts around the development of an AI maturity model for [[concepts/health|health]] services. This work emerged through an approach by [[entities/sandeep|Sandeep]] to Lemai proposing joint development of the framework, with Usher contributing specialist knowledge on LLM assessment and functionality to inform the model's design.
