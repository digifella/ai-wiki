# IBM SkillsBuild

IBM SkillsBuild is an educational resource platform that provides [[concepts/training|training]] and [[concepts/learning|learning]] materials on enterprise technology topics. The platform offers structured content designed to help individuals develop [[concepts/skills|skills]] in emerging areas of technology and business practice.

## RAG Systems Training

One notable offering within IBM SkillsBuild covers the development of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) systems. This educational content, featuring instructor [[entities/muhammad-farooq|Muhammad Farooq]], addresses practical approaches to building and implementing RAG architectures. The resource is designed for professionals seeking to understand how to integrate retrieval mechanisms with generative AI systems.
