# Racp

The Royal Australasian College of Physicians (RACP) is a professional medical organization representing physicians across Australia and New Zealand. Established as the primary certifying and standard-setting body for internal medicine and related specialties in the Australasian region, the College maintains a fellowship program and trains medical professionals through various educational initiatives.

In February 2026, the RACP Board issued a statement to Fellows and trainees addressing governance matters within the organization. The College Board expressed concern regarding allegations about governance matters communicated to Fellows and trainees.
