# The Cockpit Rule

The Cockpit Rule is a framework for professional skill development in artificial intelligence, introduced by content creator Jeff Su in his video "4 Skills to Get Ahead (Beyond Basic Prompts)." The concept addresses the shifting landscape of AI competency within the job market, arguing that as foundational tools become ubiquitous, the nature of required expertise must evolve. It posits that the ability to operate basic AI interfaces is no longer a unique advantage but rather a standard expectation for modern professionals.

The core principle of the rule draws an analogy to aviation, where pilots do not manually control every mechanical component of a plane but instead manage the system from the cockpit. Similarly, the framework suggests that career advancement in the age of AI depends less on the mechanical act of prompting and more on the strategic oversight of AI outputs. Proficiency in basic prompting is treated as a baseline requirement, meaning that individuals who rely solely on this skill lack a competitive differentiator in the professional environment.

Consequently, the rule emphasizes the development of higher-order skills that allow professionals to direct, evaluate, and integrate AI assistance effectively. This involves understanding the limitations of AI models, ensuring output quality, and applying critical thinking to the results generated by automated systems. By focusing on these meta-skills, individuals can leverage AI as a force multiplier for their work rather than viewing it as a standalone task completion tool.
