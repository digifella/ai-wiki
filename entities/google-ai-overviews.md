# Google Ai Overviews

Google AI Overviews is a feature within Google Search that utilizes generative artificial intelligence to synthesize and display direct answers to user queries at the top of search results. Rather than presenting traditional ranked links, the system generates concise summaries by aggregating information from multiple sources. This represents a significant shift in search result formatting, as AI-generated overviews appear prominently before traditional organic search listings.

When a user enters a query, Google's AI system analyzes relevant web pages and generative models to construct a comprehensive response. This process involves identifying key information across various domains and combining it into a coherent narrative. The goal is to provide immediate value to the user by answering their question directly, reducing the need to click through multiple links to find relevant data.

The introduction of this feature has prompted a new approach to search engine optimization known as Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). Website owners and content creators are adapting their strategies to ensure their content is effectively cited and utilized by these AI systems. Tools such as Claude Code are increasingly being used to help developers and marketers understand how to structure content for better visibility within these AI-generated summaries.
