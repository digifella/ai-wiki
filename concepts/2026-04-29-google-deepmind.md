# 2026 04 29 Google Deepmind

[[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]] is the [[concepts/ai-research|artificial intelligence research]] division of Google, formed through the 2023 merger of Google Brain and [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind Technologies]]. Google Brain was Google's internal [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] research team, while DeepMind Technologies was an independent AI research company that Google acquired in 2014. The [[concepts/consolidation|consolidation]] unified both organizations' research operations and [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], bringing together their respective teams and [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] in machine learning, [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]], and [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] development.

## Research Areas

The division conducts research across foundational machine learning, reinforcement learning [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], [[concepts/deep-learning-models|neural network architectures]], and [[concepts/multimodal-ai-agents|multimodal AI systems]]. Its work encompasses both theoretical research and applied applications, spanning areas such as [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], and [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]]. DeepMind's established research [[concepts/software|programs]] include:

*   **[[concepts/generative-ai|Generative Models]] & Diffusion:** Recent insights from Research Scientist [[entities/sander-dieleman|Sander Dieleman]] highlight the development of large-scale [[concepts/image-and-video-diffusion-models|diffusion models]] for image and [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]], focusing on technical intuition and scalability [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Dielemans-DeepMind-Insights-Building-Large-Scale-Diffusi|Dieleman's DeepMind Insights: Building Large-Scale Diffusion Models for Image and Video]].
*   **[[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]]:** Continued advancement in [[concepts/llm-models|LLM architectures]] and capabilities.
*   **Scientific Discovery:** Applications in [[concepts/protein-folding|protein folding]] (e.g., AlphaFold) and other biological sciences.
