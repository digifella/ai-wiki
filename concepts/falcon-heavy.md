# Roman Space Telescope

The **[[concepts/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope|Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope]]** (formerly WFIRST) is a [[entities/nasa|NASA]] observatory designed to study [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark energy]], exoplanets, and infrared [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]]. It serves as a complementary instrument to the [[concepts/james-webb-space-telescope]] ([[concepts/jwst|JWST]]), offering a wider field of view for surveying large areas of the sky.

## Mission Overview
*   **Primary Goals:** Investigate the nature of dark energy through [[concepts/supernova|supernova]] surveys and weak gravitational lensing; conduct a wide-field infrared [[concepts/survey|survey]] to discover thousands of exoplanets via microlensing.
*   **Instrumentation:** Features a 2.4-meter primary mirror (shared [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]] with JWST) and a Wide Field Instrument (WFI) with a field of view 100 times larger than that of the [[concepts/hubble-space-telescope|Hubble Space Telescope]].
*   **Coronagraph:** Includes a high-[[concepts/contrast|contrast]] coronagraphic instrument for [[concepts/direct-imaging|direct imaging]] of exoplanets and protoplanetary disks.

## Recent Developments (2026)
*   **Initial Successes:** Following its simulated launch [[concepts/phase|phase]], the telescope has demonstrated early operational [[concepts/success|success]] in calibration and initial imaging tests [[lab-notes/2026-09-18-Roman-Space-Telescope-Initial-Successes-and-Projected-22|Roman Space Telescope: Initial Successes and Projected 22-Year Lifespan]].
*   **Projected Lifespan:** Current projections indicate a mission duration of approximately 22 years, significantly extending the era of infrared [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|space exploration]].
*   **Key [[concepts/software-updates|Updates]]:** Initial test images have confirmed the instrument's sensitivity and stability, validating the optical alignment and [[concepts/temperature-management|thermal control]] systems.
