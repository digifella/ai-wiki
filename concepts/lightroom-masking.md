# Lightroom Masking

[[concepts/local-adjustments|Lightroom masking]] enables photographers to apply selective [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific areas of an image without altering the entire photo. Masks create defined regions where [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]], and other parameters can be adjusted independently. This non-destructive approach allows for precise control over how edits affect different parts of a photograph, from skies and foregrounds to subject-specific areas.

## Automated AI Masks

Modern versions of [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] and [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] incorporate AI-powered mask generation that automatically detects common photographic subjects and regions. These tools can isolate landscapes, people, skies, and other elements with a single click, significantly reducing manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] time. The automated presets adapt to varied compositions and lighting conditions, making them useful for both [[concepts/batch-processing|batch processing]] and individual image refinement.

## Manual Masking and Edge Refinement

Beyond automated options, [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Lightroom]] provides tools for creating and [[concepts/mask-refinement|refining masks]] manually. Users can adjust mask edges, feather boundaries, and paint masks directly onto images for precise control. Edge adjustment methods allow for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] where masks meet, preventing visible halos or unnatural transitions between masked and unmasked areas—particularly important in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] where sky-to-land transitions require careful handling.

## Integration with Camera Raw

[[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] offers comparable [[concepts/masking|masking]] functionality integrated into its workflow, allowing photographers to apply similar [[concepts/color-and-masking-adjustments|selective adjustments]] during the raw processing stage. The non-destructive nature of both Lightroom and [[entities/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw]] masks means adjustments can be modified or removed at any point without degrading image quality, making these tools essential for iterative [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]].
