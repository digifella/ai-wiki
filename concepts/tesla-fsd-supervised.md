# Tesla Fsd Supervised

Tesla Full Self-Driving Supervised (FSD Supervised) is an advanced driver assistance system classified as Level 2 automation. The technology automates steering, acceleration, and braking functions while requiring the human operator to maintain continuous monitoring of the driving environment. Unlike higher levels of autonomy, the system does not assume responsibility for safety; the driver must remain actively engaged and prepared to intervene at any moment.

## Regulatory Status

Regulatory developments in the Netherlands have marked a significant milestone for the system, receiving official approval for use on public roads. This decision establishes a precedent for broader European Union adoption, as member states often look to Dutch regulatory frameworks when evaluating new automotive technologies. The approval signifies that the system meets specific safety and operational standards required for supervised autonomous driving within the region.

## Technical Classification

As a Level 2 system, FSD Supervised operates under the SAE International classification of driving automation. It provides combined lateral and longitudinal control but mandates that the driver retain ultimate responsibility for the vehicle's operation. This classification distinguishes it from Level 3 and above systems, where the automated driving system is responsible for monitoring the environment and can disengage the driver under certain conditions.
