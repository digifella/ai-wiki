# Symbolic Logic

**Symbolic logic** is the formal system of [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] based on the manipulation of symbols according to defined rules, distinct from statistical or probabilistic approaches. In modern [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], it serves as the foundation for [[concepts/neural-symbolic-ai]], aiming to combine the learning capabilities of [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] with the precision, transparency, and [[concepts/persistent-reasoning|persistent reasoning]] of symbolic systems.

## Key Characteristics
- **Explainability:** Unlike [[concepts/black-box-models|black-box]] neural models, symbolic systems provide traceable reasoning paths.
- **Persistence:** Maintains state and knowledge across interactions, enabling long-term reasoning.
- **Precision:** Adheres to strict logical constraints, reducing hallucination and ambiguity.

## Applications in AI Agents
Recent developments in [[concepts/ai-agent]] architecture leverage symbolic logic to overcome the limitations of traditional request-response models.

- **OmegaClaw Framework:** An open-source neural-symbolic agent developed by SingularityNET that integrates symbolic logic for persistent, explainable reasoning rather than relying solely on LLM capabilities.
  - Focuses on overcoming the transient nature of standard AI interactions.
  - Provides a structured approach to complex [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]].
