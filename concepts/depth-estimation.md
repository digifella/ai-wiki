# Depth Estimation

**Depth estimation** is the computational process of determining the distance of points in a scene from the camera viewpoint. It is a foundational task in [[concepts/computer-vision|computer-vision]] and [[concepts/3d-reconstruction]], enabling systems to understand spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], scale, and geometry from 2D inputs.

## Core Concepts

*   **[[concepts/depth-information-extraction|Monocular Depth Estimation]]:** Inferring depth from a single image, often relying on learned priors about object sizes and perspective cues.
*   **Stereo Depth Estimation:** Utilizing disparity between two or more camera views to calculate depth via triangulation.
*   **[[concepts/3d-spatial-intelligence|3D Spatial Intelligence]]:** The broader capability of systems to perceive, [[concepts/purpose|reason]] about, and interact with the 3D [[entities/earth|world]], of which depth estimation is a critical component.
