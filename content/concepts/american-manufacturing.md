# American manufacturing

A sector of the U.S. economy focused on [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic production]] of goods, emphasizing skilled labor, supply chain [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and competitive [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]. Challenges include global competition, offshoring trends, and modernization needs.

## Notable Examples

- [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]]'s four-year [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] experiment to manufacture the [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] (a safer, more durable grill brush alternative to wire bristle brushes) entirely in [[entities/america|America]], competing in the marketplace while drawing from his parents' history as [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]].
