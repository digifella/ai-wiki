# Fda

The Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is an agency of the [[entities/united-states|United States]] Department of [[concepts/health|Health]] and Human Services responsible for protecting [[concepts/population-health|public health]] through [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] of food, drugs, medical devices, cosmetics, and other products. The FDA oversees [[concepts/product-safety|product safety]], efficacy, and labeling for a wide range of consumer goods and pharmaceutical products.

## AI Implementation

As of December 2025, the FDA has begun implementing [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] for internal use. These systems represent the agency's adoption of advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] technologies to support its operations and regulatory functions.
