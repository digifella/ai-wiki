# Physics Simulation

[[concepts/simulation-technology|Physics simulation]] refers to the computational modeling and prediction of physical systems and phenomena using mathematical equations and [[concepts/numerical-analysis|numerical methods]]. These simulations solve differential equations governing motion, forces, and energy transfer to approximate real-world behavior in digital environments. [[concepts/software|Applications]] [[concepts/range|range]] from engineering [[concepts/design|design]] and materials [[concepts/science|science]] to visual effects and [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], where simulating physical behavior before real-world [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] reduces costs and risks.

## Computational Methods

[[concepts/physics|Physics]] simulations employ various numerical techniques depending on the system's complexity and required [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]]. Common approaches include finite element analysis (FEA) for structural mechanics, computational [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]] (CFD) for fluid [[concepts/flow|flow]], and particle-based methods for dynamics. The choice of method involves tradeoffs between computational [[concepts/cost|cost]], precision, and the specific phenomena being modeled, such as rigid body dynamics, soft body deformation, or collision detection.

## Recent Developments

Recent advances in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] have introduced new [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to physics simulation. AI systems are increasingly used to accelerate simulations through learned approximations, predict outcomes more rapidly than traditional solvers, and automate aspects of model [[concepts/setup|setup]] and [[concepts/active-parameters|parameter optimization]]. Additionally, [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|AI-assisted coding]] tools are enabling faster development of [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] software, while robotics research continues to bridge the gap between simulated environments and real-world physical systems through improved [[concepts/advanced-ai-techniques-that-improve-generalization-from-simulated-data|sim-to-real transfer]] techniques.
