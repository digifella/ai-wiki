# Augmented Reality

**Augmented Reality (AR)** is a technology that overlays [[concepts/digital-information|digital information]]—such as images, sound, or text—onto the user's view of the real [[entities/earth|world]]. It enhances the physical environment rather than replacing it entirely (as in [[concepts/virtual-reality|Virtual Reality]]).

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/spatial-computing|Spatial Computing]]:** The intersection of physical and digital worlds, requiring precise understanding of 3D space.
- **Real-time Interaction:** AR systems must process environmental data and render overlays with low latency.
- **Registration:** The alignment of virtual objects with real-world coordinates.
