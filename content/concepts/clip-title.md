# Clip Title

A **clip title** is the semantic identifier assigned to a media clip, video segment, or recording. It provides human-readable context distinct from technical filenames or [[concepts/intrusion-detection-system|IDs]], facilitating efficient indexing, searchability, and presentation within a [[concepts/knowledge-base]].

## Attributes
- **Primary Label:** Descriptive name used for retrieval and display.
- **[[concepts/metadata|Metadata]] Key:** Often the central field in video-metadata extraction workflows.
- **Differentiation:** Separates content identity from [[entities/storage|storage]] [[concepts/adoption|implementation]].
