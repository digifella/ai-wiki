# AI model deployment

The operational process of transitioning AI model architectures from training stages to production environments, focusing on [[concepts/inference]] optimization, [[concepts/infrastructure-scalability|infrastructure scalability]], and resource management.

## Core Elements
- **[[concepts/compute]] Provisioning:** Managing the allocation of hardware resources (GPUs/TPUs) to meet workload requirements.
- **Scalability & Demand:** [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]] systems to handle fluctuating user traffic and prevent service degradation.
- **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]:** Ensuring high availability to mitigate the risk of "[[concepts/compute|compute]] crunches" and service outages.
