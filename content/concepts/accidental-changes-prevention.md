## Accidental Changes Prevention

### Overview
Accidental changes [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] refers to methods and features designed to protect user settings on devices, particularly in the context of photography where accidental alterations can significantly affect image quality and [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]]. Techniques include firmware updates with enhanced safeguards, dedicated [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] or [[concepts/software|software]] locks, and educational guides for users.

- **[[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/camera-settings|Camera]] [[concepts/lock-feature|Lock Feature]]:** A specific feature implemented by [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] to prevent unintentional changes to [[concepts/camera-settings|camera settings]], crucial for maintaining [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] in photography.
