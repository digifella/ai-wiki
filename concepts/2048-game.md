# 2048 Game

2048 is a sliding tile puzzle video game released in 2014 where players manipulate numbered tiles on a 4x4 grid by swiping in cardinal directions. When two tiles of equal value move adjacent to each other, they merge into a single tile with their combined value. The [[concepts/purpose|objective]] is to create a tile valued at 2048, though play can continue beyond this point. The game ends when no further valid moves are possible on the grid.

## Game Mechanics

The game's relatively simple ruleset and finite action space—four possible moves per turn—create a well-defined environment suitable for [[concepts/numerical-analysis|computational analysis]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] training. This structure allows for precise evaluation of [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], making it a common benchmark for testing [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]] models. The deterministic nature of tile merging and the probabilistic spawning of new tiles provide a balanced challenge for both human players and automated agents.

## AI Implementation

Recent developments in artificial intelligence have focused on training models to play 2048 locally using reinforcement learning techniques. Notable implementations, such as those demonstrated by [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]], utilize frameworks like [[concepts/unsloth-optimization|Nvidia]] and [[concepts/unsloth-studio|Unsloth]] to optimize training processes. These approaches leverage the game's clear state representation to teach [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] how to maximize scores through strategic tile management, demonstrating the practical application of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] in entertainment domains.
