# Loop Engineering

**[[concepts/loop|Loop]] [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]** is a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in [[concepts/ai-agent]] development that moves beyond static [[entities/prompt-engineering]] to focus on the iterative loops and systemic behaviors of [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]]. Unlike traditional [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], which optimizes single-turn [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], [[concepts/prompt-engineering|loop engineering]] designs the [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], state management, and execution cycles that allow agents to operate autonomously over time.

## Core Concepts

- **Beyond Single Instructions**: Traditional approaches rely on crafting perfect one-off prompts. [[concepts/autonomous-ai-agent-design|Loop engineering]] focuses on the architecture of interaction loops, where the agent’s output becomes input for subsequent steps, enabling complex task [[concepts/solution|resolution]] through [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].
- **Autonomy & Iteration**: Agents are designed to self-correct, refine outputs, and manage state across multiple turns without constant human intervention. This requires robust error handling and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] within the loop.
- **Systemic Design**: The focus shifts from linguistic tweaking to structural design—defining how agents perceive, act, and evaluate their own progress in a closed or open loop.
