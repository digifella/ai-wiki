# Ballistic Missiles

Self-propelled projectiles following a suborbital trajectory, governed by [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] and [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|aerodynamics]] following an initial powered boost [[concepts/phase|phase]].

## Technical Characteristics
- **Trajectory:** Parabolic flight path through the upper [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]] or space.
- **Payloads:** Conventional, [[concepts/nuclear-weapons|Nuclear Weapons]], or chemical/biological agents.
- **Range Classifications:** SRBM, MRBM, IRBM, and ICBM.
- **[[concepts/recommendations|Guidance]] Systems:** Inertial navigation, GPS, or celestial guidance.

## Strategic Doctrine
- **AD ([[concepts/a2ad|Anti-Access/Area Denial]]):** Use of missile [[concepts/density|density]] to restrict adversary [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and maneuverability within a contested theater.
- **Nuclear Deterrence:** Central role in maintaining second-strike capability and strategic stability.
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] Strike:** Capability to target high-value, hardened assets such as [[concepts/bunkers|Bunkers]] and command-and-control [[concepts/nodes|nodes]].

## Recent Combat Observations
- **[[concepts/conflict|Conflict]] Application:** Identified as a critical instrument in recent [[concepts/iran|Iran]] War hostilities.
- **[[concepts/economic-impact|Economic Impact]]:** Integral to the execution of Blockades and the resulting global [[concepts/trade-disruption]].
- **Theater Control:** Essential component in modern AD frameworks used to complicate maritime and aerial access.
