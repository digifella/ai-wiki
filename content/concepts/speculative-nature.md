# Speculative Nature

The **speculative [[entities/nature|nature]]** of an asset or event refers to the degree to which its value is driven by anticipated future outcomes rather than current intrinsic fundamentals. High speculation implies elevated risk, volatility, and sensitivity to sentiment, political shifts, or technological breakthroughs.

## Key Characteristics
- **Decoupling from Fundamentals:** Price movements may diverge significantly from immediate cash flows or earnings.
- **Narrative-Driven:** Valuation relies heavily on market stories, expectations, and macro-political contexts.
- **High Volatility:** Susceptibility to rapid price swings based on news cycles or regulatory changes.
