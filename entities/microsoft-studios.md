# Microsoft Studios

Microsoft Studios is the video game development and publishing division of Microsoft, responsible for creating and distributing games across the company's platforms, including Xbox consoles, personal computers, and cloud gaming services. The division serves as Microsoft's primary vehicle for first-party game development and operates a portfolio of both internally developed studios and acquired development companies that produce content for the Xbox ecosystem and other Microsoft gaming platforms.

## Development Strategy

The division has expanded its gaming portfolio through strategic acquisitions of established development studios. This approach allows Microsoft to access established franchises, talented development teams, and diverse gaming genres. Studios under the Microsoft umbrella develop titles ranging from exclusive console games to cloud-based gaming experiences, supporting Microsoft's broader gaming strategy that emphasizes accessibility across multiple devices and subscription services like Xbox Game Pass.
