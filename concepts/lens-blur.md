# Lens blur

An optical effect where objects outside the focal plane are rendered with reduced [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]], simulating [[concepts/camera-settings]]. The aesthetic quality of this blur is termed bokeh.

## Key Concepts
- **Depth of Field (DoF)**: The range of distance within an image that appears acceptably sharp.
- **Focal Plane**: The specific plane where [[concepts/light|light]] is focused.
- **Physical [[concepts/causes|Drivers]]**: Blur intensity is primarily controlled via aperture, [[concepts/focal-length|focal length]], and subject distance.

## Digital Simulation & Manipulation
- **Automated Tools**: [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] and [[concepts/lightroom]] feature automated Lens Blur tools that estimate depth.
