# AI Decision Models

**[[concepts/video-editing|AI Decision Models]]** refer to computational frameworks used by [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems to evaluate inputs, weigh options, and select optimal actions based on specific criteria. In the context of Customer Service, these models are increasingly critical for assessing real-time metrics such as Urgency and Frustration to prioritize tickets and route interactions appropriately.
