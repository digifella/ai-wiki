# Fraser Cain

**Fraser Cain** is a prominent [[entities/canada|Canadian]] astronomer, [[entities/tasia-custode|author]], and media personality known for making space [[concepts/science|science]] accessible to the public. He is best known as the founder and host of Universe Today and co-host of the Space Time Podcast.
