# Real-Time Observation

**Real-Time Observation** refers to the immediate acquisition, processing, and interpretation of data from a physical phenomenon as it occurs, minimizing latency between event and insight. In scientific and industrial contexts, this enables dynamic response [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]], and high-fidelity situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Low Latency:** Data streams are processed with minimal delay, allowing for instant [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].
- **High Frequency:** Continuous or near-continuous sampling to capture transient events.
- **Integration:** Often coupled with [[concepts/decision-making|Automated Decision Making]] or human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] systems for immediate action.
