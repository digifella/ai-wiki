## Nvidia CUDA GPU Parallel Computing for AI Advancement

This video provides a concise yet comprehensive introduction to CUDA ([[concepts/compute|Compute]] Unified Device [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]), a [[concepts/general-purpose-computing|parallel computing]] platform developed by Nvidia. Launched in 2007 and based on prior work by [[entities/ian-buck|Ian Buck]] and [[entities/john-nicholls|John Nicholls]], CUDA revolutionized computing by allowing [[concepts/graphics-processing-units-gpus|Graphics Processing Units (GPUs)]] to be utilized for general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] computation, extending their functionality far beyond just graphics processing.
