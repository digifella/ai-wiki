# Dermatology Images

Dermatology images refer to the application of multimodal artificial intelligence to analyze and interpret photographs of skin conditions alongside associated clinical text. These systems integrate computer vision with natural language processing to process visual data from medical imagery and corresponding textual information simultaneously. This combined approach allows for the extraction of diagnostic insights and clinical details that are often difficult to obtain when relying on unimodal analysis alone.

The development of such capabilities is exemplified by models like MedGemma 27B from Google, which demonstrates proficiency in both medical text and image comprehension. By leveraging these advanced AI agents, dermatology workflows can enhance the accuracy of condition identification and support clinical decision-making through the synthesis of visual and textual data.
