# Transcendental numbers

A real number that is not **algebraic**; it cannot be the root of a non-[[concepts/zero|zero]] polynomial equation with rational coefficients.

## Key Examples
- [[concepts/pi]]
- e

- **Literature**: In [[entities/carl-sagan]]'s novel *[[entities/contact|Contact]]*:

    - The [[concepts/base-11-representation|base 11 representation]] of [[concepts/pi]] contains a sequence of 1s and 0s that, when aligned, produce a message.
