# Internal combustion engine

An Internal combustion engine is a type of heat engine where the combustion of a fuel-air [[concepts/solution|mixture]] occurs within a combustion chamber, converting chemical energy into mechanical work through the expansion of high-pressure gases.

### Critical Systems & Maintenance
- [[concepts/lubrication-system|Lubrication system]]: Essential for reducing [[concepts/friction]] and managing thermal loads within the engine.
- [[concepts/oil-pressure|Oil pressure]]: A critical metric for engine survival; maintaining adequate pressure is vital to prevent catastrophic [[concepts/engine-failure|Engine failure]].
- Warning lights: Serve as the primary [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|real-time monitoring]] interface for detecting system failures and preventing damage.
