# Sciatic Nerve

The **sciatic nerve** is the longest and widest single nerve in the human body, running from the lower back, through the hips and buttocks, and down each leg. Irritation or compression of this nerve typically results in **[[concepts/sciatica|sciatica]]**, characterized by pain, tingling, or numbness along its path.

## Pathophysiology
*   **Inflammation and Irritation:** Sciatica is fundamentally an inflammatory response to [[concepts/nerve-irritation|nerve irritation]], often caused by a **herniated disc** or **spinal stenosis**.
*   **Misconception of Tightness:** Common instinct is to stretch the buttocks and legs to relieve pain; however, nerves do not benefit from static [[concepts/stretching|stretching]] in an inflamed state.
*   **De-Irritation Strategy:** Effective relief often requires **spinal extension** exercises to centralize pain and reduce nerve root compression, rather than peripheral stretching.

## Management and Rehabilitation
*   **Spinal Extension:** Prioritize extension-based movements to alleviate [[concepts/nerve-de-irritation|nerve de-irritation]].
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Sciatica-Relief-Spinal-Extension-Exercise-for-Nerve-De|Sciatica Relief: Spinal Extension Exercise for Nerve De-Irritation]] for specific protocols.
*   **Avoid Aggressive Stretching:** Stretching tight muscles in the glutes or hamstrings can exacerbate nerve inflammation and backfire.
*   **Professional [[concepts/recommendations|Guidance]]:** Consult physical therapists for personalized **nerve gliding** techniques and core stabilization.
