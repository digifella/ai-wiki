# NotebookLM Customization

[[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] customization refers to the ability to modify [[concepts/design|design]] elements and visual presentation within [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] platform, powered by [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. This functionality allows users to tailor the appearance and formatting of generated content—including [[entities/google-slides|slides]], mindmaps, and [[concepts/interactive-visuals|interactive visuals]]—to suit professional or branded presentation requirements. The [[concepts/customization|customization]] tools leverage [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]]'s generative capabilities to produce styled outputs that can be adapted for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Design and Visual Elements

Users can adjust design properties of generated materials, including mindmaps and presentation [[concepts/google-slides|slides]] created from source documents. The platform enables modification of visual styling to match organizational [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] or personal preferences. This represents a shift in [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s functionality toward competing with traditional design [[concepts/software|software]], expanding its role beyond document analysis and into presentation creation.

## Output Formats and Export

NotebookLM customization extends to various [[concepts/output|output]] formats. Users can transform generated slides into video content while managing watermarking and attribution options. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] with Gemini allows for more sophisticated generation and customization of interactive visuals and multimedia presentations derived from uploaded source materials.
