# Higgs boson

The **Higgs boson** is an elementary particle in the Standard Model of particle physics, produced by the quantum excitation of the Higgs field. Its discovery confirmed the mechanism by which other fundamental particles acquire mass.

## Key Concepts

*   **Mass Generation**: The Higgs field permeates the universe; particles acquire mass through their interaction with this field.
*   **Energy-to-Mass Conversion**: The creation of massive particles from high-energy collisions is the inverse process of mass-energy release in nuclear reactions.
*   **LHC Discovery**: The particle was discovered at [[entities/cern|CERN]]'s [[entities/large-hadron-collider]] (LHC) through proton-proton collisions, effectively creating mass from energy.
