# Local AI hosting

The practice of executing [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/agentic-ai]] on personal or private hardware rather than via [[concepts/cloud-computing]]-based [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]].

### Privacy and Security Implications
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] Misconception**: Local hosting does not inherently guarantee privacy; the absence of a third-party provider does not eliminate all data risks.
- **Risk Analogy**: While [[concepts/cloud-ai|Cloud AI]] is comparable to renting an apartment where the landlord holds a copy of your keys, local hosting shifts the [[concepts/security|security]] burden to the local environment's [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
- **Security Vectors**: Local environments remain susceptible to threats such as insecure [[concepts/model-weights|model weights]], malware, and unauthorized data exfiltration.
