# Smart Objects

Smart Objects are specialized layer containers in Adobe Photoshop that preserve original source content while enabling non-destructive editing workflows. When a layer is converted to a Smart Object, Photoshop embeds or links the source material within the layer, keeping the underlying data intact regardless of transformations applied. This allows editors to scale, rotate, warp, and otherwise modify images multiple times without degrading image quality or losing the ability to return to the original source data.

## Core Functionality

The primary advantage of Smart Objects is their ability to sustain repeated editing operations without cumulative quality loss. Because the original source material remains embedded or referenced, adjustments to color grading, filters, and image enhancement can be modified or removed at any point in the workflow. This is particularly valuable when experimenting with different aesthetic approaches or when client feedback requires reverting to earlier states. Smart Objects also enable linked files, which automatically update when the source document changes.

## Common Applications

Smart Objects are frequently used in professional image editing for applying non-destructive filters, layer effects, and transformations. They prove especially useful when scaling images multiple times, applying smart filters that can be adjusted retroactively, or managing complex compositions where elements may need revision. In addition to photography and graphic design, Smart Objects support video layers and other dynamic content within Photoshop's extended workflow.
