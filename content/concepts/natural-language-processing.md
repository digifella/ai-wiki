# Natural Language Processing (NLP)

[[concepts/nlp|Natural language processing]] is a branch of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] that focuses on enabling computers to understand, interpret and generate human language. It involves the development of algorithms and models to process natural language data for tasks such as [[concepts/sentiment-analysis|sentiment analysis]], machine translation, text [[concepts/summarization|summarization]], and [[concepts/fact-based-queries|question answering]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[entities/aletheia|Aletheia]]**: A [[concepts/self-improvement|self-correcting AI]] developed by [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]], which has contributed significantly to [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]].
- Recent advancements in [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] have seen the development of sophisticated models like [[entities/aletheia|Aletheia]].
- **Automated [[concepts/podcast-summarization|Podcast Summarization]]**: NLP [[concepts/agents|agents]] can now automatically summarize [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]], potentially saving time for users.

### Applications
- **[[concepts/sentiment-analysis|Sentiment Analysis]]**: Evaluating attitudes and opinions expressed through language data.
- **Machine Translation**: Translating text between languages.
- **Text [[concepts/summarization|Summarization]]**: Condensing large texts into shorter summaries.
- **Question Answering**: Providing direct answers to questions posed in natural language.
- **Automated [[concepts/podcast-summarization|Podcast Summarization]]**: Extracting key points from podcasts to create concise summaries.
