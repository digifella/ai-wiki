# Business Consulting

Business consulting is a professional service in which external advisors provide expert analysis, strategic recommendations, and implementation support to organizations. Consultants work across diverse industries and functional areas—including operations, strategy, finance, marketing, and organizational development—leveraging specialized knowledge and objective perspectives that internal teams may lack. The core value of consulting lies in bringing external expertise, proven methodologies, and unbiased analysis to complex organizational challenges.

## Scope and Application

Consulting engagements vary widely in scope and duration, from short-term advisory projects addressing specific problems to multi-year transformational initiatives. Consultants may focus on discrete functional areas such as process improvement or cost reduction, or take a holistic approach to organizational strategy and change management. The role typically involves diagnosis of current conditions, identification of opportunities or problems, development of solutions, and guidance on implementation.

## Consulting Models

Consulting can be delivered through large global firms offering integrated services across multiple disciplines, boutique firms specializing in particular industries or functions, or independent consultants. Organizations may engage consultants on a project basis, retain them for ongoing advisory relationships, or bring them in during periods of significant change or uncertainty. The consultant's role is fundamentally advisory—recommendations must ultimately be evaluated and implemented by the client organization itself.
