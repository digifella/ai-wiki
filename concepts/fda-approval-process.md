# Fda Approval Process

The FDA [[concepts/regulatory-approval|approval process]] constitutes the regulatory [[concepts/pathway-platform|pathway]] through which new drugs, biologics, and medical devices must be evaluated for safety and efficacy before receiving market [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] in the [[entities/united-states|United States]]. Manufacturers are required to demonstrate through rigorous testing and comprehensive documentation that their products are safe for human use and effective for their intended purposes. This multi-[[concepts/phase|phase]] evaluation system is designed to protect [[concepts/population-health|public health]] by establishing a standardized framework for assessing novel therapeutic interventions prior to consumer access.

Historically, this process has been characterized by extensive manual review and sequential data submission. However, the landscape is currently undergoing modernization through the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] tools for in-house operations. These technologies are being integrated to streamline internal workflows, enhance data analysis capabilities, and improve the efficiency of regulatory submissions. The shift toward automated, AI-driven processes aims to reduce administrative burdens and accelerate the timeline from development to approval without compromising the rigorous standards required for patient safety.
