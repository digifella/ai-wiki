# Google Maps

Google Maps is a web-based mapping and navigation service provided by [[concepts/google-search|Google]]. It offers users access to satellite imagery, street maps, 360° panoramic street-level imagery (Street View), real-time traffic conditions, and route planning for various modes of transportation. The service is available through web browsers and mobile [[concepts/software|applications]] across multiple platforms.

## Data Access and Automation

Google Maps data can be accessed through official APIs that allow developers to integrate mapping functionality into applications. However, the service has also been a subject of interest for [[concepts/web-crawling|web scraping]] and [[concepts/automation-tools|automation tools]], as users and developers seek to extract or automate interactions with map data. These activities exist in a complex legal landscape, as automated access may violate Google's terms of service depending on the method and extent of use.

## Related Tools and Integration

Google Maps integrates with other Google services and third-party tools for enhanced functionality. Users can create custom maps using [[entities/google-my-maps|Google My Maps]], which supports [[concepts/travel-itinerary-automation|automated itinerary generation]] when combined with [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]]. The platform also supports export and manipulation of geographic data, including depth mapping and spatial visualization features used in various applications.
