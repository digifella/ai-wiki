# Reusable rocket technology

Technology enabling rockets to be recovered, refurbished, and reflown, drastically reducing launch costs. Pioneered by [[entities/spacex]] with Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy, and central to the [[concepts/starship]] program.

## Impact and Applications
- Starlink satellite constellation deployment relies on reusable rockets, democratizing global internet access and potentially becoming [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s default [[concepts/internet-layer|internet layer]] (per [[entities/dr-know-it-all|Dr. Know-it-all]] Knows it all's analysis).
- Enables cost-effective mass deployment of satellite constellations, driving [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in space-based services.
- Reduces orbital launch costs by ~90% compared to traditional expendable rockets.
