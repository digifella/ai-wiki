# Self-Extending AI

**[[concepts/autonomous-customization|Self-extending AI]]** refers to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems capable of autonomously modifying their own code, architecture, or functionality to improve performance or adapt to new tasks without direct human intervention. This concept moves beyond static models toward dynamic, evolving agents.

## Core Concepts

- **Autonomous [[concepts/customization|Customization]]:** The ability of an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] to generate, test, and deploy new capabilities or tools based on real-time needs.
- **Functionality Expansion:** Unlike traditional AI which is limited by its initial [[concepts/training-data|training data]] and architecture, self-extending systems can grow their operational scope.
- **Open-Source Ecosystems:** Many implementations, such as [[concepts/deepseek-harness]], leverage open-source principles to allow community verification and collaborative improvement of self-modifying code.

## Key Implementations

### DeepSeek Harness
A notable example of this paradigm is the **[[concepts/file-readedit|DeepSeek Harness]]** system, which enables AI agents to be "self-extending."
