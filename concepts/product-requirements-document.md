# Product Requirements Document

Formal blueprint detailing a product's purpose, features, target audience, [[concepts/success|success]] metrics, and technical constraints. Aligns stakeholders and guides development.

## Key Components
- **Purpose**: Business goal and problem statement
- **Features**: Prioritized functionality list with acceptance criteria
- **User Stories**: Contextual usage [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]
- **Success Metrics**: Quantifiable KPIs (e.g., engagement, conversion)
- **Constraints**: Technical, timeline, or resource limitations
