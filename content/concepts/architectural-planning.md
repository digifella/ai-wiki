# Architectural Planning

Architectural Planning is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] process that transforms research outputs from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s [[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] feature into structured website content without requiring [[concepts/coding|coding]] knowledge. This approach enables users to convert detailed research materials generated by AI systems into professional web presentations through [[concepts/no-code|no-code]] tools and platforms, bridging the gap between research generation and web publication.

## The Workflow

The process begins with NotebookLM's Deep Research functionality, which generates comprehensive research documents, summaries, and structured outlines based on user-provided source materials. These outputs are then organized and adapted for web presentation using no-code website builders, content management systems, or [[concepts/static-website|static site]] generators that provide [[concepts/visual-editing|visual editing]] interfaces. The researcher acts as curator and architect, determining information [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]], visual layout, and navigation [[concepts/structure|structure]] without [[concepts/writing|writing]] underlying code.

## Applications and Benefits

This approach is particularly useful for individuals and small teams who need to publish [[concepts/flow-management|research findings]], curated [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]], or educational materials online. By removing the technical barrier of coding, Architectural Planning allows subject matter experts to focus on content quality and presentation strategy rather than [[concepts/web-development|web development]]. The workflow also accommodates [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]], as changes to structure and layout can be made through intuitive interfaces rather than requiring [[concepts/developer|developer]] intervention.
