# Voice Assistants & Autonomous Systems

[[concepts/tone|Voice]] assistants are software applications designed to recognize and respond to spoken [[concepts/commands|commands]] from users. They process natural language input through [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] technology and execute tasks or provide information based on the user's request. Common examples include [[entities/amazon|Amazon]]'s Alexa, [[entities/apple|Apple]]'s [[concepts/siri|Siri]], [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Assistant, and [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]]'s Cortana. These systems typically rely on cloud-based processing to interpret complex queries and access relevant data or services.
