# Architectural Planning

Architectural Planning is a workflow that converts research outputs from NotebookLM's Deep Research feature into structured website content using no-code tools and platforms. This process enables users without coding expertise to transform comprehensive, sourced research documents into functional websites ready for publication. The approach bridges the gap between AI-assisted research generation and professional web deployment.

## The Workflow Process

The workflow begins with deep research conducted through NotebookLM, which generates comprehensive, cited research documents. These outputs are then organized and structured into website-ready content through no-code website builders and content management systems. Users can map research sections to web pages, organize hierarchies, and apply templates without writing code, making the publication process accessible to researchers and subject matter experts.

## Practical Application

Architectural Planning is particularly useful for knowledge workers, researchers, and educators who need to publish research findings as accessible web content. By leveraging existing no-code platforms alongside NotebookLM's research capabilities, users can maintain research quality and sourcing while distributing findings through professional-looking websites. This approach reduces the traditional barrier of requiring web development skills or hiring developers to publish research online.
