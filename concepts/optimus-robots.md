# Optimus Robots

[[entities/optimus|Optimus]] is [[entities/tesla|Tesla]]'s humanoid robot project, initiated in the early 2020s as the company expanded beyond automotive manufacturing into [[concepts/robotics|robotics]]. The robot is designed as a general-purpose bipedal system intended to perform physical tasks in industrial and domestic environments. Development combines Tesla's [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities with mechanical [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] to create an autonomous platform capable of [[concepts/learning|learning]] and adapting to different environments and tasks.

## Technical Approach

Tesla's approach to Optimus development emphasizes practical utility and scalability. The system relies on a combination of custom hardware and advanced software architectures to enable mobility and manipulation. Key components include actuators designed for high torque and efficiency, as well as sensor suites that provide environmental [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]]. The underlying software utilizes [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]] trained on vast datasets to process visual information and execute complex motor control [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]].

## AI and Compute Infrastructure

A critical aspect of the Optimus project is the integration of Tesla's proprietary artificial intelligence stack. The robots utilize Tesla's Dojo supercomputing [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and [[concepts/custom-ai-hardware|custom silicon]], such as the [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving (FSD)]] computer, to process real-time data. This [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power allows the robots to navigate dynamic environments, recognize objects, and plan movements without pre-programmed paths. The [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] are continuously updated through over-the-air [[concepts/app-updates|software updates]], enabling the robots to improve their performance over time.

## Development and Deployment

Initial prototypes of Optimus were demonstrated in 2022, showcasing basic walking and object manipulation capabilities. Subsequent iterations have focused on improving balance, dexterity, and [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]]. Tesla has stated that the long-term goal is to produce millions of units for use in factories and homes. The company plans to leverage its existing manufacturing [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] to scale production, aiming to reduce the cost of the robot to a level comparable with a standard automobile.
