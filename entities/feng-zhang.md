# Feng Zhang

Dr. Feng Zhang is a molecular biologist and associate professor at the Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard. His primary research focuses on the development and application of gene-editing technologies, with a specific emphasis on CRISPR-based molecular systems. His work has significantly contributed to the understanding of how molecular tools can be engineered and adapted for therapeutic purposes.

Zhang’s laboratory investigates the natural diversity and evolution of CRISPR systems found across various organisms. By exploring these biological mechanisms, his research aims to advance the engineering of molecular tools for precise genetic modification. This focus on the evolution of CRISPR systems underpins his broader efforts to expand the capabilities and applications of gene-editing technologies in scientific and medical contexts.
