## Google NotebookLM's Latest Features: Enhanced Infographics, AI Videos,
and [[concepts/slide-decks|Slide Decks]]
**Clip title:** Google’s [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Released New Features That Are Insane
🤯 (FREE UPDATES)
**Author / channel:** [[entities/rob|Rob]] The AI Guy
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=E71M74FIDHc

### Summary
The video provides a detailed overview of several recent, unannounced
updates to Google's NotebookLM, an AI-powered research and content
generation tool. The main topic revolves around enhanced [[concepts/customization|customization]],
creation capabilities, and improved user interaction within the platform,
making it a more versatile tool for various applications from [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] to academic study.

One significant upgrade introduces highly customizable [[concepts/infographic|infographic]]
generation. Users can now select from a diverse [[concepts/range|range]] of visual styles,
including [[concepts/sketch|Sketch]], Kawaii, Professional, Scientific, Anime, Clay, Editorial,
Instructional, Bento Grid, and Bricks. Additionally, infographics can be
tailored in orientation (landscape, portrait, square) and level of detail
(concise, standard, detailed). This allows for outputs that are not only
informative but also visually aligned with specific branding or aesthetic
preferences, making them suitable for social media content or blog posts.
The presenter demonstrates creating an "Exotic Car Hacker's Roadmap"
infographic that intelligently interprets the source material to create
relevant visuals and actionable steps.

Further enhancements include new options for [[concepts/ai-generated-video|AI-generated video]] overviews
and editable slide decks. [[concepts/video-overviews|Video overviews]] can now be produced in either an
"explainer" (comprehensive) or "brief" (high-level) format, with various
visual styles like Classic, [[concepts/whiteboard|Whiteboard]], Kawaii, Anime, and Custom options
where users can describe a specific aesthetic. This enables users to
quickly generate visual summaries of their research. For slide decks, the
tool now allows for direct revisions and custom prompts on individual
[[entities/google-slides|slides]], meaning users can request specific changes like adjusting colors or
shortening titles without regenerating the entire presentation. NotebookLM
saves the original and new revised versions, offering flexibility.

Other notable updates focus on learning and research efficiency. Flashcard
and quiz generation now come with customizable options for the number of
cards/questions and difficulty levels (easy, [[entities/medium|medium]], hard), and can be
tailored to specific sources or topics for targeted study. The chat
functionality itself has been improved, allowing users to define the AI's
conversational goal, [[concepts/style|style]], or role (e.g., "learning guide," "[[entities/phd|PhD]] student
level," or a custom persona) and choose the response length. Finally,
source [[concepts/integration|integration]] has been expanded to include [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]] content,
alongside web searches, with options for "[[concepts/fast-research|Fast Research]]" for quick results
or "[[entities/deep-research|Deep Research]]" for more in-depth reports.

In conclusion, NotebookLM is rapidly evolving into a powerful [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]]
that significantly streamlines research, content creation, and learning
processes. The new features emphasize customization and intelligent
adaptation, providing users with more control over the output's style,
detail, and format. This makes the tool increasingly valuable for students,
educators, and professionals seeking to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] for efficiency and
creative content generation. The video implicitly underscores the broader
trend of [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] becoming more sophisticated and user-friendly,
highlighting the importance of adopting these technologies to stay
productive and competitive in a fast-changing digital landscape.
