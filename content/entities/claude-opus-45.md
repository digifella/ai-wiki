# Claude Opus 4.5

[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/claude-opus]] series represents [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] language [[concepts/models|models]] focused on complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and long-context tasks. [[concepts/claude-opus-45|Claude Opus 4.5]] is the latest [[concepts/iteration|iteration]], emphasizing [[concepts/robustness|robustness]] in enterprise-grade [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Benchmark: "One-Shot Build" for "Showbiz" App

Based on [[entities/matt-maher]]'s comparison video [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] 04 14 [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|Compare]] of [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] [[entities/opus-45|Opus 45]] vs [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 52 [[entities/matt-maher|Matt Maher]], a unique benchmark tested against [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]].2 using:

- **Task**: Generate a complete [[concepts/product-requirements-document|Product Requirements Document]] ([[concepts/prd|PRD]]) for "[[entities/showbiz|Showbiz]]" (movie/TV [[concepts/companion|companion]] app) from a single prompt
- **Input**: Massive documentation folder containing:
  - Technical [[concepts/technical-specs|specifications]]
  - [[concepts/design|Design]] [[concepts/tokens|tokens]]
  - Personality guidelines
  - Additional contextual artifacts
- **Benchmark Type**: "[[concepts/one-shot-build|One-Shot Build]]" — designed to be "impossible" for standard models to handle without [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]]
- **[[concepts/purpose|Purpose]]**: Evaluated real-world ability to synthesize multi-faceted documentation in a single [[concepts/context-window|context window]]

This approach bypassed traditional metrics to assess practical application of complex documentation [[concepts/integration|integration]].
