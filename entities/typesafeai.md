# TypeSafe.ai

**[[concepts/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]]** is an AI development company focused on creating reliable, decision-oriented models with strict adherence to factual accuracy.

## Key Entities & Products

### Jev
**Jev** is TypeSafe.ai's flagship decision AI model, designed to eliminate hallucinations in high-stakes decision-making contexts.

- **Developer:** [[entities/diogo-almeida|Diogo Almeida]] (co-inventor of [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]])
- **Core [[concepts/business-model|Value Proposition]]:** Fast, reliable decision support with [[concepts/zero-hallucinations|zero hallucinations]].
- **Differentiation:** Explicitly designed as a "decision" model rather than a general-purpose [[concepts/large-language-model]], contrasting with traditional generative approaches like ChatGPT.
- **Context:** Introduced in the video clip "We need to talk about Jev..." by [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]].
