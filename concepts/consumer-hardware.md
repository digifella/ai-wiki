# Consumer Hardware

## Definition
[[concepts/hardware|Hardware components]] designed for end-user interaction, increasingly capable of running complex computational workloads locally without reliance on cloud [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].

## Key Trends
- **[[concepts/local-ai|Local AI]] [[concepts/model-inference|Inference]]**: Shift towards running [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] on-premise to reduce latency and preserve [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] Ecosystems**: Growing support for [[concepts/open-weight-models|open-weight models]] that optimize performance on standard consumer GPUs and CPUs.
- **Hardware Efficiency**: Demand for models that balance [[concepts/parameter-count|parameter count]] with [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] to fit within consumer thermal and power constraints.
