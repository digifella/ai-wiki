# Botany

**Botany** is the scientific study of plants, including their structure, properties, and biochemical processes. It encompasses Plant Physiology, Taxonomy, Ecology, and Ethnobotany.

## Key Concepts
- **Plant Physiology**: Study of physical and biochemical functions of plants.
- **Taxonomy**: Classification and naming of plant species.
- **Ecology**: Interaction of plants with their environment and other organisms.
- **[[concepts/invasive-species|Invasive Species]] Management**: Strategies to control non-native plants that disrupt local ecosystems.
