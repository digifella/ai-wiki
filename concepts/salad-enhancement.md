# Salad enhancement

## Core Principles
Enhancing Salad quality involves optimizing [[concepts/texture|texture]], flavor balance, and structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] through specific [[concepts/cooking-techniques|culinary techniques]]. Key factors include dressing viscosity, leaf surface area, and ingredient [[concepts/thermal-regulation|temperature control]].

## Techniques & Methods
*   **Restaurant-Grade Elevation**: Insights from [[entities/americas-test-kitchen]] and chef [[entities/lan-lam|Lan Lam]] highlight that restaurant [[concepts/excellence|superiority]] stems from subtle technical [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] rather than complex ingredients. Key areas include:
    *   Dressing emulsion stability and application timing.
    *   Leaf treatment to maximize dressing adhesion without wilting.
    *   *See detailed analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-06-03-Restaurant-Salad-Techniques-Elevated-Homemade-Green-Sala|Restaurant Salad Techniques: Elevated Homemade Green Salads Summary Report]]*
*   **Dressing Dynamics**:
    *   Use Acid (vinegar/citrus) to brighten flavor and balance fats.
    *   Ensure proper [[concepts/food-emulsification|emulsification]] of Oil to coat leaves evenly.
    *   Salt early to draw out moisture and enhance flavor penetration.
*   **[[concepts/texture-slider|Texture]] Management**:
    *   Avoid overdressing, which leads to sogginess.
    *   Combine tender leaves with crunch elements (nuts, seeds, raw vegetables) for [[concepts/contrast|contrast]].
    *   Chill greens before tossing to maintain [[concepts/crispy-coating|crispness]].
