# AI Consultant

An AI consultant is a professional service model that leverages artificial intelligence to scale expertise-based advice beyond traditional one-to-one consulting. Rather than limiting service delivery to direct client interactions, this approach transforms accumulated professional knowledge, documentation, and personal notes into systems capable of serving multiple users simultaneously. This model enables practitioners to extend the reach of their expertise while reducing the time required per engagement.

## Implementation

The typical implementation uses NotebookLM, a tool that converts documents and notes into an AI knowledge base, combined with Gemini Gems—customizable AI assistants built on Google's Gemini model. A consultant uploads their professional materials, case studies, frameworks, and accumulated expertise into NotebookLM, which processes this information to create a searchable knowledge system. Gemini Gems then allows the creation of specialized AI agents that can answer client questions, provide analysis, and deliver advice aligned with the consultant's methodology and perspective.

## Applications and Benefits

This workflow applies to various consulting domains including business strategy, technical architecture, marketing, and specialized fields where documented expertise can be systematized. The primary benefits include increased client capacity without proportional increases in time investment, consistency in advice delivery across multiple clients, and the ability to serve clients asynchronously rather than through scheduled meetings. The consultant retains control over the knowledge base and can continuously update it as their expertise evolves.
