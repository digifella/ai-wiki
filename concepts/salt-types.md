# Salt types

## Overview
Salt varieties differ by [[concepts/film-grain|grain]] size, [[concepts/density|density]], and mineral [[concepts/writing|composition]], all of which dictate how they interact with food during cooking and seasoning.

## Common Varieties
* **Kosher Salt**: Large, irregular [[concepts/granular-materials|grains]]; preferred for controlled seasoning and brining.
* **Table Salt**: Fine, uniform crystals; high density; requires precise measurement to prevent over-salting.
* **Sea Salt**: Produced through evaporation; contains trace minerals; [[concepts/texture|texture]] varies by source.
* **Flaky Salt**: Large, thin crystals; used primarily as a finishing salt for [[concepts/texture|texture]] and immediate flavor impact.
