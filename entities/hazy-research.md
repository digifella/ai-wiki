# Hazy Research

**Hazy Research** is a research group at [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford University]] focused on [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] intelligence and developing efficient, [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-source AI]] systems. It collaborates closely with [[concepts/scaling|Scaling]] Intelligence [[entities/labs|Labs]] to advance the state of personal AI and large-scale [[concepts/training-process|model training]].
