# YouTube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by Google in 2006. The service allows users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos, functioning as one of the internet's largest repositories of video content. The platform hosts material ranging from user-generated content to professional productions, educational resources, and entertainment media.

## Core Features

Users create accounts to upload and manage videos through a creator dashboard. The platform provides tools for video editing, monetization, and audience analytics. Viewers can search for content, subscribe to channels, create playlists, and interact with creators through comments and likes. YouTube's recommendation algorithm suggests videos based on viewing history and user preferences.

## Content and Users

The platform serves billions of monthly users worldwide and hosts content across virtually all categories, including music, tutorials, vlogs, news, gaming, and documentaries. Content creators range from individuals producing hobbyist material to established media companies and corporations. YouTube has become a significant platform for influencers and content creators to build audiences and generate revenue through advertising and sponsorships.
