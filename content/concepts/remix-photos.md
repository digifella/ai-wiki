# Remix Photos

The practice of transforming existing photographs through digital manipulation, combination, or reinterpretation to create new visual works. Key techniques include [[concepts/photo-editing]], collage, and [[concepts/video-creation]] from still [[concepts/images|images]].

Recent developments:
- [[entities/google-photos|Google Photos]] now offers "[[concepts/image-to-video|Image to Video]]" feature (2026-04-14), converting static photos into short animated clips for dynamic remixes.
