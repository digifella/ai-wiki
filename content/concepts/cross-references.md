# Cross-references

Cross-referencing in a wiki context involves linking related content within the same or different documents to enhance navigation and understanding. It's particularly useful for maintaining coherent, interconnected [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]].

## Karpathy's LLM Wiki Pattern
- Introduced by [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]] as an alternative to traditional [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval Augmented Generation]] (RAG) [[concepts/models|models]].
- Emphasizes a persistent, compounding wiki maintained entirely by a [[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]] (LLM).
- Addresses the challenge of effectively managing and growing personal knowledge bases over time.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/information-provision|Retrieval Augmented Generation]] (RAG)**: A technique that combines vector-based [[concepts/document-retrieval|document retrieval]] with language generation.
- **[[concepts/persistent-memory|Persistent Knowledge]] Base**: A system that accumulates information continuously, allowing for the gradual refinement and expansion of data.
- **[[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Model (LLM)]]**: Advanced [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] trained on vast amounts of [[concepts/text|text]] data to generate human-like [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Advantages
- Dynamic updating without manual intervention.
- Continuous compounding of knowledge through interactions with users.
- Enhanced [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and depth as the model learns from its own outputs.
