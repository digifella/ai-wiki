# Production Software

Software deployed in a live environment to serve end-users or other systems. Requires rigorous testing, monitoring, and maintenance to ensure [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], [[concepts/security|security]], and performance.

## Critical Risks

- **[[concepts/dark-code|Dark Code]]**: AI-generated software running in production without full comprehension by any on payroll (engineers, service owners, CTOs), creating untraceable risks and comprehension gaps. [[concepts/dark-code|Dark Code]] AI-Generated Softwares Comprehension Gap and Untraceable Risks
- **[[concepts/ai-generated-code|AI-Generated Code]] Dependency**: Rapid [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] for [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] leads to opaque systems where root [[concepts/causes|causes]] of failures cannot be traced or resolved.
- **Operational Blind Spots**: Teams lack visibility into AI-generated components, increasing [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to undetected failures and security breaches.
