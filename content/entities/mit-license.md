# MIT License

The [[concepts/modified-conceptsmit-licensemit-license|MIT License]] is a permissive [[concepts/photo-editing|free software]] [[concepts/license|license]] originating [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the [[concepts/massachusetts-institute-of-technology-entitiesmitmit|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] (MIT). It grants permissions for redistribution and use in source and binary forms, with conditions regarding copyright notices.

### Key Points
- **Permissive License**: Allows usage, modification, [[concepts/distribution|distribution]], and even inclusion in proprietary [[concepts/software|software]].
- **Simplicity**: The license [[concepts/text|text]] is short and easy to understand.
- **No Warranty**: Provides no warranties or guarantees about the quality of the [[concepts/software|software]].
- **Common Usage**: Widely used for [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] projects.
