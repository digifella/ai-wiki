# Online Courses

Online courses represent a structured approach to delivering educational content and [[concepts/learning|learning]] experiences through digital platforms. In the context of business strategy, creating and deploying online courses has become an essential capability for organizations seeking to scale [[concepts/training|training]], build expertise, and engage learners across distributed audiences.

## Converting NotebookLM to Interactive Courses

[[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] notebooks can be transformed into interactive online courses by integrating them with learning management systems such as [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Classroom. This process involves leveraging the organized source materials and generated insights within a NotebookLM [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] as the foundational content, then structuring them into course modules, assignments, and assessments within a classroom environment. The combination allows educators and instructional designers to maintain rich source documentation while providing learners with interactive engagement features.

## Implementation Considerations

Successfully converting notebook-based content into online courses requires careful planning around course [[concepts/structure|structure]], learner objectives, and assessment methods. Organizations should consider how their source materials map to learning outcomes, how to break content into digestible modules, and what types of interactive elements [[entities/will|will]] best support learner engagement and knowledge retention. The choice of platform, whether [[entities/google-classroom|Google Classroom]] or alternatives, should align with organizational infrastructure and learner [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] requirements.
