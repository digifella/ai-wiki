# Jewelry Heists

Jewelry heists represent a significant category of historical crime, combining theft with often elaborate planning and execution. These incidents have frequently intersected with other criminal activities, including murder, creating complex cases that engaged law enforcement and captured public [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] across multiple time periods and locations.
