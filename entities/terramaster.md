# Terramaster

Terramaster is a manufacturer of network-attached storage (NAS) devices primarily designed for small office and home office (SOHO) environments. The company produces a range of NAS solutions that function as centralized storage systems, enabling multiple users and devices to access files simultaneously over a network connection. These devices are typically configured with multiple drive bays and run dedicated NAS operating systems to manage file sharing, backup procedures, and data security.

## F4-425 Plus

The TerraMaster F4-425 Plus is a four-bay NAS device intended for small business and professional users requiring robust data management capabilities. It supports various RAID configurations to ensure data redundancy and performance optimization. The unit is equipped with high-speed network interfaces to facilitate efficient data transfer rates across the local network.
