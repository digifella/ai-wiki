# Enhanced Masking

**Enhanced [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]]** refers to advanced selection and [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] techniques in digital [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], specifically within [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom Classic]], allowing for precise [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific areas of an image without affecting the rest. Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] have significantly improved the accuracy and [[concepts/speed|speed]] of these tools through AI-driven assistance.

## Key Developments

### Lightroom Classic 15.4 Integration
The 15.4 update introduced significant improvements to [[concepts/masking|masking]] capabilities, streamlining the workflow for complex edits.

- **Improved Selection Accuracy**: Enhanced [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] for subject, sky, and [[concepts/object-detection|object detection]], reducing the need for manual brush refinement.
- **[[concepts/assisted-culling|Assisted Culling]] Synergy**: [[concepts/masking-tools|Masking tools]] now integrate more seamlessly with culling workflows, allowing for faster identification and adjustment of key images in large batches.
- **[[concepts/software-performance|Performance Optimization]]**: Reduced latency when applying masks to high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] RAW files, improving real-time preview responsiveness.

For detailed breakdown of these features, see [[lab-notes/2026-07-05-Lightroom-Classic-15.4-Update-Enhanced-Masking-and-Assis|Lightroom Classic 15.4 Update: Enhanced Masking and Assisted Culling Features]].
