# Video Review: Taking ChatGPT's New Image Model for a Spin

**Presenter:** Greg Isenberg **Objective:** To test OpenAI's newly launched image model, compare it to his current "daily driver" (referred to as [[entities/google|Google]]'s _Nano Banana Pro_), and see if the output is high-quality enough for [[concepts/professional-use|professional use]].

* * *

## 🧪 The Experiments

The presenter walks through four distinct tests using the preset styles available in the ChatGPT image interface.

### 1\. The "Plushie" Test

* **Subject:** A photo of [[entities/sam-altman|Sam Altman]].
* **Process:** Selected the "Plushie" [[concepts/style|style]] from the carousel and uploaded the photo.
* **Observation:** The model automatically generates an "optimized prompt" describing the image in detail (similar to how the app _Glif_ works).
* **Result:** The output was described as "super cute" with impressive [[concepts/attention|attention]] to detail—specifically noting that the plushie retained the specific wave and style of Sam Altman's hair.
* **Business Insight:** He [[concepts/notes|notes]] a massive opportunity for creating CPG (Consumer Packaged Goods) brands and physical toys using these [[concepts/models|models]].

### 2\. The "[[concepts/sketch|Sketch]]" Test (Graphite Pencil)

* **Subject:** A photo of the presenter drinking a martini.
* **Initial Result:** A detailed 3D graphite sketch.
* **Critique:** While the drawing was amazing, it included a hand drawing the sketch which looked "AI-y" and unnatural.
* **Refinement:** He asked ChatGPT to **remove the hand and the [[concepts/notebook|notebook]]** and just show the drawing on a piece of paper.
* **Result:** The model successfully followed the negative constraints. The presenter noted that the new model handles [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] much better than previous versions.

### 3\. The Diagram Transformation

* **Subject:** A digital chart/diagram about "putting [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] on the internet" that he previously posted on X (Twitter).
* **Goal:** Convert a clean digital graph into a "casual, natural hand-drawn" style.
* **Instruction:** "Make this hand-drawn... a little more casual... remove weird pencil sharpening stuff."
* **Result:** "Wow." The model produced a clean, legible, hand-drawn version of the chart.
* **Takeaway:** This is highly useful for content creators. Hand-drawn diagrams often perform better on social media than polished digital graphics.

### 4\. The "Bobblehead" Test

* **Subject:** A selfie of the presenter.
* **Instruction:** Create a bobblehead, but **not** in a baseball uniform. Instead, style it as a "Tech YouTuber."
* **Result:** The model nailed the outfit, capturing his specific long-sleeve sweater and adding a camera accessory.
* **Business Insight:** Another potential use case for custom manufacturing and [[entities/shopify|Shopify]] stores.

* * *

## 📝 Analysis of OpenAI's Announcement

The presenter reviews the official blog post to highlight specific technical improvements:

* **Editing [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]:** The model excels at adding, subtracting, and blending elements (proven by the sketch test where he removed the hand).
* **Creative Transformations:** It can change [[concepts/texture|texture]] and layout while preserving the original subject's details.
* **[[concepts/instruction-following|Instruction Following]]:** The model is more reliable at following complex grid [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] (e.g., "Draw a 6x6 grid with specific items") compared to previous versions.
* **Text Rendering:** Significant improvements in spelling and [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] within images.

* * *

## 🏆 Final Verdict

* **Expectations:** The model "beat his expectations."
* **Comparison:** He considers it **as good as, if not better than**, his previous favorite tool (_Nano Banana Pro_).
* **Conclusion:** The outputs are high-quality enough to be used for real [[concepts/business-applications|business applications]], social media content, and [[concepts/product-design|product design]]. The ability to iterate and refine images via chat is a standout feature.
