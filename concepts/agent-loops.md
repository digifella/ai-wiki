# Agent Loops

**Agent [[concepts/loops|Loops]]** refer to the iterative cycles in which [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] perceive, [[concepts/purpose|reason]], act, and observe to achieve complex goals. A critical challenge in these architectures is maintaining efficiency and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] during the [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/phase|phase]] of the [[concepts/loop|loop]].

## Core Challenges
- **Traditional Architectures:** Standard agent designs often struggle with latency and error propagation during [[concepts/iterative-reasoning|iterative reasoning]].
- **Decision Bottlenecks:** Unstructured decision processes can lead to redundant actions or logical drift within the loop.

## Enhancements: Jev & OpenJev
To address these inefficiencies, specialized decision models like **Jev** and **[[entities/openjev|OpenJev]]** have been introduced. These models focus on structuring the decision process to improve agent performance.

- **Purpose:** Enhance efficiency and reliability of [[concepts/ai-bots|AI agents]] within their iterative loops [[lab-notes/2026-09-30-Jev-Enhancing-AI-Agent-Efficiency-with-Structured-Decisi|Jev: Enhancing AI Agent Efficiency with Structured Decision Models]].
- **Mechanism:** Utilizes [[concepts/structured-decision-models|structured decision models]] to optimize the "[[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]" step of the [[concepts/agentic-harness|agent harness]].
- **Context:** Part of the broader effort to refine [[concepts/agent-harness]] components for better scalability.
