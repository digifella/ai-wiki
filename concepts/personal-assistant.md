# Personal Assistant

**Definition:** A digital assistant designed to help users manage their tasks, schedule appointments, and provide information.

## Overview
A personal assistant is an AI-driven tool that helps users with day-to-day activities. It can be integrated into various devices and platforms for seamless interaction.

### Features:
- Task management
- [[concepts/knowledge-bases|Information retrieval]]
- [[concepts/automation|Scheduling]] assistance
