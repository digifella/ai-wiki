# AI Hallucinations

A phenomenon in [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) where [[concepts/models|models]] generate plausible but factually incorrect information, often appearing coherent and reasonable to users.

## Key Points
- Not a minor bug but a widespread issue affecting most LLMs during generation
- Occurs when models produce outputs that are contextually consistent but factually inaccurate
- Root cause lies in probabilistic [[entities/nature|nature]] of [[concepts/training-data|training data]] and lack of real-world grounding
- Common in complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], historical facts, and technical domains
- Recent analysis (via [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Flash [[concepts/summary|summary]]) emphasizes the pervasive [[entities/nature|nature]] of these errors in [[concepts/large-language-models]]
