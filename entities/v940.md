# V940

**V940** refers to the personalized [[concepts/mrna-cancer-vaccine|mRNA cancer vaccine]] candidate developed by [[entities/moderna]] and [[entities/merck|Merck]] & Co. (Keytruda manufacturer), specifically targeting [[concepts/resected-melanoma|resected melanoma]] in an adjuvant setting.

## Key Developments

- **Clinical Trial Results**: Positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results announced for the vaccine ([[entities/intismeran-autogene|Intismeran Autogene]]).
- **Therapeutic Context**: Evaluated as an [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]] for patients with resected melanoma.
- **Mechanism**: Utilizes mRNA technology to stimulate an immune response against tumor-specific antigens.
- **Combination Therapy**: Investigated in conjunction with Keytruda ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]) to enhance anti-tumor efficacy.
