# tool-use automation

The capability of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to interact with external [[concepts/software|software]], datasets, and environments to execute autonomous tasks and workflows.

## Key Mechanisms
- [[concepts/model-context-protocol|Model Context Protocol (MCP)]]: A [[concepts/universal-plug|universal plug]]-and-play technology that enables [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to connect directly to [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources and tools, overcoming the limitations of traditional [[concepts/chat-interfaces|chat interfaces]] restricted to uploaded [[concepts/files|files]].

## Agentic Implementations
- [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]]: Uses powerful [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and MCP to transform standard chatbots into [[concepts/agents|agents]] capable of interacting with external systems.
