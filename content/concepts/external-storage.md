# external storage

[[concepts/hardware|Hardware]] used to expand the capacity or functional utility of a host device.

## Integration & Connectivity
*   Utilizes physical interfaces, such as USB-C, to attach peripherals and expand device capabilities.
*   On mobile [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] like the [[entities/ipad|iPad]], the [[entities/usb-c-port|USB-C port]] serves as a critical [[concepts/gateway|gateway]] for connecting external [[entities/storage|storage]] to facilitate advanced productivity and creative [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] beyond standard charging functions.
