# Geopolitical Tensions

## Overview
Geopolitical tensions refer to the political hostility, rivalry, or [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] between different countries or regions, often driven by competition for resources, power, or technological supremacy. In the contemporary landscape, these tensions are increasingly mediated by advancements in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence]] and the dissemination of information.

## AI and Information Warfare
The intersection of AI technology and international relations has created new vectors for geopolitical [[concepts/friction|friction]]. Key dynamics include:

- **Misconceptions in [[concepts/model-copying|Model Copying]]**: Public discourse often conflates legitimate AI techniques with malicious espionage or theft. [[entities/prompt-engineering|AI Distillation]]: [[entities/i|Unpacking Misconceptions in Model Copying and Geopolitical Tensions]] clarifies that "distillation" is a standard technical process for [[concepts/llm-optimization-techniques|model optimization]], not inherently a tool for geopolitical sabotage.
- **[[concepts/storytelling|Narrative]] Control**: Allegations regarding AI model copying are frequently leveraged to justify trade restrictions or sanctions, exacerbating tensions between major tech powers.
- **Technical Literacy Gap**: The lack of public understanding regarding [[concepts/ai-distillation]] allows for the weaponization of technical terms, leading to heightened suspicion and diplomatic strain.
