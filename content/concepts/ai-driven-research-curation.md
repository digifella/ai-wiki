# AI-Driven Research Curation

AI-driven research curation refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to organize, synthesize, and present information from multiple sources in a structured format. Rather than manually compiling research materials, users [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] [[concepts/agents|agents]] and language models to process documents, identify key information, and generate summaries or insights. This approach reduces the time spent on information gathering and [[concepts/organization|organization]], particularly for complex research tasks where relevant data is dispersed across various formats and sources.

## Tools and Implementation

[[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] are examples of platforms that enable research curation workflows. These tools can ingest documents, PDFs, web content, and other reference materials, then process them to extract relevant information and generate structured outputs. Users can interact with the AI to ask questions about source materials, request summaries of specific topics, or synthesize findings across multiple documents. The systems maintain context across conversations, allowing for [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of research questions and outputs.

## Practical Applications

Research curation is applicable across domains where users need to synthesize information quickly. Common [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] include academic research, competitive analysis, [[concepts/travel-planning|travel planning]], and project [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. For example, when planning complex activities like booking trips, AI curation tools can process hotel reviews, flight information, travel guides, and itinerary suggestions to help users make informed decisions faster. The [[concepts/structured-output|structured output]] generated by these systems can serve as a foundation for further decision-making or as a reference document for future consultation.
