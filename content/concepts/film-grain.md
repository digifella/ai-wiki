# Film grain

The visible [[concepts/texture|texture]] or "noise" in analog photography produced by the random [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of light-sensitive silver halide crystals within the film emulsion.

## Characteristics
- Influences perceived texture and [[concepts/sharpness|image sharpness]].
- A fundamental element in film-emulation [[concepts/workflow|workflows]].
- Distinct from Digital noise, though often synthetically replicated in digital photography.

## Digital Application & Post-Processing
- Grain is frequently added during post-processing to provide organic [[concepts/integrity|character]] to digital [[concepts/files|files]].
- Often paired with [[concepts/vignette-effects|Vignette effects]] to simulate the aesthetic of vintage [[concepts/optics|optics]].
- **Techniques in [[concepts/lightroom]]:**
    - Grain and Vignette can be applied to simulate filmic qualities.
    - There are three distinct methods available for creating Vignette effects within the [[concepts/software|software]].
