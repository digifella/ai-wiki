# AI accuracy enhancement

Core strategies and methodologies for improving the [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] systems, particularly in complex domain-specific tasks.

## Key Methodologies

### Structured Data Integration
- Utilizing structured databases to ground AI processing, reducing [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] in complex inputs.
- Implementing [[lab-notes/2026-07-20-AI-Workflow-for-Construction-Drawing-Processing-Structur|AI Workflow for Construction Drawing Processing: Structured Database and Concept Wiki]] to demonstrate how structured context improves [[concepts/vllm|model performance]].

### Token Efficiency
- Optimizing input formats to reduce [[concepts/token-consumption|token consumption]] while maintaining or improving accuracy.
- Techniques include:
  - Pre-processing raw data into concise, structured formats.
  - Filtering irrelevant information before model ingestion.
  - Using [[concepts/summarization-levels|summary modes]] to condense [[entities/big-data|large datasets]].

### Domain-Specific Workflow Optimization
- Tailoring AI workflows to specific industries (e.g., construction, legal, medical) to enhance [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]].
- Example: Processing construction drawings requires specific parsing [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] to interpret spatial and technical data accurately.
