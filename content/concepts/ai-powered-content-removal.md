# AI Powered Content Removal

AI-powered content removal in [[concepts/camera-system-integration|Lightroom Classic v15]] is a non-destructive editing feature that uses machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] to automatically detect and remove unwanted elements from photographs. The tool identifies objects, people, or artifacts within an image and removes them while preserving the surrounding context and composition. This capability represents an advancement in automated editing, building on Adobe's computational photography research.

## Technical Approach

The feature operates non-destructively, meaning original image data remains intact and edits can be modified or reverted at any time. Rather than permanently altering pixels, the tool creates [[concepts/adjustment-layers|adjustment layers]] that can be adjusted independently of the base image. Users can refine removal results by adjusting masks, feathering edges, or reprocessing specific areas to achieve desired outcomes.

## Workflow Integration

Within Lightroom Classic, the content removal tool integrates into the standard editing interface, allowing photographers to apply the feature during their existing post-processing workflows. The tool is designed to handle common removal [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]—such as removing unwanted people from backgrounds, eliminating distracting objects, or cleaning up dust and artifacts—while maintaining realistic blending with surrounding areas.
