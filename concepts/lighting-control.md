# Lighting Control

Lighting Control is an AI image editing technique that uses JSON-structured prompts to achieve precise manipulation of lighting elements in images generated by Google's Gemini model. Rather than relying solely on natural language descriptions, this method encodes detailed lighting specifications in machine-readable format, allowing creators to define parameters such as light intensity, direction, and color temperature with greater accuracy than conventional text-based prompts alone.

## How It Works

The technique involves formatting lighting instructions as JSON data within prompts sent to Gemini. This structured approach enables users to specify multiple lighting properties simultaneously—such as key light angle, fill light intensity, shadow depth, and ambient color—in a way that the model can more reliably interpret and apply. The JSON format reduces ambiguity inherent in natural language and provides a systematic way to iterate on lighting adjustments.

## Application and Demonstration

The method has been demonstrated in Renderdrop video content, which showcases practical workflows for implementing JSON-based lighting control in Gemini-powered image generation. This approach is particularly useful for creators seeking consistent lighting setups across multiple images or precise control over how light interacts with scene elements, making it valuable for professional creative work and iterative design processes.
