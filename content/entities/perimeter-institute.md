# Perimeter Institute

A leading research center for [[concepts/theoretical-physics|theoretical physics]] in Waterloo, Ontario, Canada, focused on foundational questions in [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]], [[concepts/quantum-mechanics|quantum mechanics]], and [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]].

- Each person can invite six collaborators annually, with two fully funded.
- Using the [[entities/saber-detector|Saber detector]] at the [[entities/stawell-gold-mine|Stawell gold mine]] in Victoria to search for WIMPs, in collaboration with an Italian detector.
