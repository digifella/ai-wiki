# Finding Purpose

Finding purpose refers to the process of identifying personal meaning and direction in life. Purpose functions as a foundational element of wellbeing, providing motivation, resilience, and a sense of alignment between values and actions. The concept extends beyond career or achievement to encompass broader life direction and what individuals consider worth dedicating their effort toward. Research in psychology and health studies consistently links a strong sense of purpose to improved mental health, longevity, and overall life satisfaction.

## Identifying Personal 'Why'

A prominent method for uncovering purpose involves Simon Sinek’s story-based approach to identifying one’s "why." This framework suggests that individuals can discover their core motivation by examining their personal history, specifically looking for recurring patterns of passion, fulfillment, and natural strengths. By analyzing past experiences where one felt most engaged and alive, individuals can trace a narrative thread that reveals their fundamental drive. This reflective process helps distinguish between external expectations and internal desires, allowing for a more authentic definition of purpose that guides decision-making and behavior.
