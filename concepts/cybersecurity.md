## Cybersecurity
[[concepts/internet-security|Cybersecurity]] involves protecting internet-connected systems, including hardware, software, and data, from cyber threats. It encompasses a broad range of strategies to prevent [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]], misuse, disclosure, disruption, modification, or destruction of information. As technology advances, so do the methods employed by attackers, necessitating constant vigilance and [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in [[concepts/security|security]] measures.
### Key Concepts
- **Vulnerabilities**: Weaknesses within software that can be exploited by an attacker.
- **Threats**: Potential dangers to a system's security.
- **Risk Management**: Evaluating and mitigating the likelihood of threats exploiting vulnerabilities.
- **[[concepts/ai-governance|AI Governance]] Framework**: [[concepts/practice-oriented-framework|Practice-oriented framework]] translating AI [[concepts/ethics|ethics]] principles into actionable [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]] and oversight within [[concepts/health|healthcare]] organizations ([[entities/bmj-review|BMJ Review]], 2026).

### Related Technologies
- Encryption: Techniques for securing data.
- Firewalls: [[concepts/vpn|Network security]] systems that monitor and control incoming and outgoing network traffic.
