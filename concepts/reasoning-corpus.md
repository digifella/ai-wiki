# Reasoning Corpus

The **[[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] Corpus** represents the aggregate body of knowledge, patterns, and logical structures used to evaluate, train, or benchmark the deductive and inductive capabilities of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] systems. It distinguishes between static [[concepts/factual-recall|factual recall]] and dynamic [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] abilities, serving as a critical metric for assessing true [[concepts/true-intelligence|General Intelligence]] versus [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]].

## Core Components

*   **Fluid Intelligence Assessment**: The capacity to solve novel problems, identify patterns, and apply [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] without relying on prior specific [[concepts/language-data|training data]]. This is contrasted with Crystallized Intelligence, which relies on [[concepts/experience|accumulated knowledge]].
*   **[[concepts/synthetic-puzzle-generation|Synthetic Puzzle Generation]]**: The use of algorithmic methods to create infinite variants of logic puzzles to prevent overfitting and test [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]] capabilities.
*   **[[concepts/benchmark-testing|Benchmarking]] Frameworks**: Standardized tests designed to measure reasoning depth, including visual, textual, and multi-modal reasoning tasks.

## Key Benchmarks and Challenges

*   **[[concepts/arc-agi-2-challenge|ARC AGI 2 Challenge]]**: A primary benchmark for measuring fluid intelligence in AI. Unlike standard language benchmarks, ARC ([[concepts/abstraction-layer|Abstraction]] and Reasoning Corpus) requires models to interpret visual [[concepts/grids|grids]] and infer transformation rules from [[concepts/few-shot-examples|few-shot examples]].
    *   Recent analyses question whether current [[concepts/large-language-model]]s possess genuine fluid intelligence or merely exploit statistical correlations in the training distribution.
    *   See also: [[lab-notes/2026-06-06-LLM-Fluid-Intelligence-ARC-AGI-2-Challenge-and-Synthetic|LLM Fluid Intelligence: ARC AGI 2 Challenge and Synthetic Puzzle Generation]]

## Methodologies

*   **Systematic Evaluation**: Testing models on out-of-distribution samples to verify [[concepts/robustness|robustness]].
*   **Rule Extraction**: Attempting to reverse-engineer the [[concepts/logical-steps|logical steps]] a model takes to arrive at a [[concepts/solution|solution]], ensuring [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] in reasoning processes.
