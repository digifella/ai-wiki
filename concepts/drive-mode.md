# Drive Mode

Drive Mode is a control-locking feature found on Fujifilm digital cameras designed to prevent accidental adjustments to critical settings during operation. When activated, the camera interface restricts access to specified parameters, effectively locking the current configuration. This mechanism allows photographers to maintain consistent exposure, focus, and other technical settings without the risk of unintended changes caused by accidental button presses or dial rotations.

The functionality ensures that the camera remains in a predetermined state, which is particularly useful for workflows requiring strict consistency or when operating the device in challenging environments. By securing specific controls, users can focus on composition and timing without worrying about the camera's internal settings shifting unexpectedly. This feature supports a more stable shooting experience, especially in situations where rapid adjustments are not desired or where the camera is mounted or handled in ways that increase the likelihood of accidental input.
