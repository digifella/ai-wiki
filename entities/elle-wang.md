# Elle Wang

[[entities/dr-elle-yuan-wang|Elle Wang]]'s note-taking methodology focuses on structuring AI-generated information using critical frameworks. Core principles:

- Applies [[concepts/qec-framework]] (Question, Evidence, Conclusion) to transform raw information into [[concepts/actionable-insights|actionable insights]] by explicitly framing questions, sourcing evidence, and deriving conclusions.
