# experience

**Definition**: The accumulation of knowledge, [[concepts/skills|skills]], and understanding derived from direct involvement in or [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to events and activities over time. It serves as the foundational substrate for intuition, [[concepts/expertise|expertise]], and effective judgment.

## Core Characteristics
- **Cumulative Nature**: Built through repeated engagement, [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]], and reflection on past outcomes.
- **Contextual Specificity**: Highly domain-specific; transferability depends on [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] capabilities.
- **Tacit vs. Explicit**: Much of experience is tacit (unarticulated [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|procedural knowledge]]), difficult to codify but critical for rapid [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] in ambiguous situations.

## Relationship to Judgment and Leadership
Experience is the primary input for forming [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] judgment, particularly in situations where data is incomplete or contradictory.

- **Synthesis of Inputs**: Judgment is defined as the ability to combine [[concepts/personal-qualities|personal qualities]] with [[concepts/relevant-knowledge|relevant knowledge]] and **experience** to form [[concepts/opinions|opinions]] and make decisions [[lab-notes/2026-05-28-The-Elements-of-Good-Judgment|The Elements of Good Judgment]].
- **[[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] Core**: In the absence of clear evidence supporting a specific option, leaders rely on experience-driven judgment to interpret evidence and point toward the right choice.
- **[[concepts/exemplary-leadership|Exemplary Leadership]]**: [[concepts/sound-choice|Good judgment]] is considered the core of exemplary leadership, [[concepts/acting|acting]] as the bridge between factual assembly and decisive action.

## Mechanisms of Acquisition
- **Deliberate Practice**: Focused repetition with [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to refine mental models.
- **Reflection**: Analyzing past decisions to update heuristics and reduce cognitive bias.
- **Diverse Exposure**: Engaging with varied [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] to broaden pattern recognition libraries.
