# Images

Images are a primary [[concepts/data-modality|data modality]] processed by [[concepts/multimodal-ai-agents|multimodal AI systems]], alongside text and other input types. In the context of modern [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], images serve as visual information that [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] can analyze, interpret, and generate. Multimodal AI systems are designed to accept images as input—whether photographs, [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]], screenshots, or other visual content—and produce outputs such as descriptions, answers to questions about image content, or modified versions of those images.

## Processing in AI Systems

[[concepts/multimodal-ai|Multimodal AI]] models like [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] and [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] process images through specialized visual [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] mechanisms that convert visual data into numerical representations the model can reason about. This allows the system to understand spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], objects, text within images, colors, and composition. The encoded image information is then integrated with [[concepts/text-modality|text processing]] capabilities, enabling the model to answer questions about images, summarize visual content, or combine visual and textual [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] in a single task.

## Applications

The ability to process images alongside text enables a range of practical applications in creative work, professional analysis, and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]]. Users can upload screenshots for UI/UX [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], architectural diagrams for technical discussion, photographs for identification or analysis, or design mockups for collaborative refinement. This multimodal capability reduces the [[concepts/friction|friction]] of conveying visual information and allows for more natural, efficient communication between humans and AI systems.
