# Attention Heads

## Definition
Sub-components of the Multi-Head [[concepts/attention|Attention]] mechanism within [[concepts/transformer-architectures|Transformer architectures]]. They enable the model to simultaneously attend to information from different representation subspaces [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] different positions.

## Mechanism
- Each head performs independent Scaled Dot-Product [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|Attention]] using partitioned $Q$ (Query), $K$ (Key), and $V$ (Value) projections.
- The outputs of all heads are concatenated and linearly transformed to produce the final [[concepts/output|output]] of the attention layer.

## Inference and Performance Context
- **[[concepts/inference|Inference]] Complexity:** The computational workload of processing multiple heads is a primary driver of the complexity found in modern [[concepts/llm-inference|LLM Inference engines]].
- **[[concepts/memory|Memory]] and Throughput:** Efficient management of the projections generated by these heads is critical to [[concepts/software-performance|Performance Optimization]], particularly regarding the [[concepts/memory|memory]] bandwidth bottlenecks addressed by advanced [[concepts/memory-mapping|Memory Mapping]] techniques.
- **Optimization Strategies:** [[concepts/scaling|Scaling]] the number of heads and their dimensions requires specialized [[concepts/inference-engines|Inference Engines]] to handle the high-throughput requirements of concurrent head computations.
