# Privacy

Privacy refers to the state or condition of being free from unwanted intrusion or [[concepts/attention|attention]]. In the digital age, privacy encompasses protection against unauthorized surveillance and data collection, particularly concerning personal information.

## Encryption Strategies

- **Encrypted [[concepts/dns|DNS]] (DoH/DoT)**: Prevents ISPs from tracking visited domains by encrypting DNS queries, unlike unencrypted DNS. As shown in [[entities/daves-garage|Dave's Garage]]'s video, it's a free, effective alternative to [[concepts/vpn|VPNs]] for stopping ISPs from selling browsing data ("being the product"). Requires no paid service and blocks ISP-level tracking without sacrificing [[concepts/speed|speed]] or convenience.
