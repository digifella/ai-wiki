# GPT-3.5

[[concepts/large-language-model|Large language model]] series developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], serving as a successor to GPT-3. Powers [[concepts/software|applications]] like chatbots and content generation. Prone to [[concepts/ai-hallucinations]]—generating plausible but factually incorrect outputs.

## Key Characteristics
- [[concepts/ai-hallucinations]]: Widespread issue in LLMs, not a minor bug (per [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] [[concepts/summary|Summary]] Report: [[concepts/ai-hallucinations|AI Hallucinations]] 2026-03-06)
- Generates factually incorrect but coherent [[concepts/text|text]], especially in ambiguous contexts
- Requires careful validation in production deployments
