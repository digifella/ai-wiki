# Trainee

A trainee is a person undergoing structured [[concepts/training|training]] or education within an institutional setting, typically working toward professional qualification or [[concepts/skill|skill]] development. In academic and professional contexts, trainees often work alongside more experienced practitioners while completing formal requirements for their field.

## Role in Institutional Governance

Trainees form part of the broader institutional community and may be included in communications regarding organizational matters. In some cases, trainees' [[entities/contact|contact]] information may be used by institutional [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] to disseminate information about [[concepts/governance|governance]] issues, [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]], or other matters of collective concern. The inclusion of trainees in such communications reflects their status as stakeholders within the institution, though their role in governance [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] may differ from that of full members or senior staff.
