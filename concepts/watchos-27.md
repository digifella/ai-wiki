# watchOS 27

**[[entities/watchos-27|watchOS 27]]** is a major software update for the [[entities/apple|Apple]] Watch ecosystem, focusing on significant UI improvements, battery optimizations, and expanded hardware compatibility.

## Key Features & Changes

*   **Hardware Compatibility**:
    *   Supports [[entities/apple-watch-se-3|Apple Watch SE 3]], Apple Watch Series 9, Apple Watch Series 10, Apple Watch Series 11, Apple Watch Series 12, Apple Watch Ultra 2, [[entities/apple-watch-ultra-3|Apple Watch Ultra 3]], and Apple Watch Ultra 4.
    *   **Deprecation**: Older models are no longer supported.
*   **User Interface**: Major visual and interaction improvements across the OS.
*   **Performance**: Enhanced battery life optimizations.
*   **Installation**: Available via the Watch app on iPhone or directly on the device.
