# Automated information extraction

The use of AI and [[concepts/large-language-models]] to automatically identify, retrieve, and structure data from unstructured sources such as text, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and images.

### Notable Implementations
- **[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 [[concepts/multi-modal-researcher|Multi-modal Researcher]]**:
    - Built using [[entities/langchain|LangChain]] and [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph]].
    - Leverages [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 native multi-modal capabilities to perform [[concepts/comprehensive-investigations|comprehensive investigations]] and generate diverse outputs.
    - **Workflow**: Operates based on user-defined research topics (e.g., investigating the concept of LLMs as new operating systems).
