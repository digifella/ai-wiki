# Pembrolizumab

**Pembrolizumab** (brand name Keytruda) is a monoclonal-antibody targeting the PD-1 receptor, used in immunotherapy for various cancers.

## Recent Developments in Adjuvant Melanoma Therapy

Significant advancements in the adjuvant treatment of [[concepts/resected-melanoma|resected melanoma]] involve the combination of pembrolizumab with emerging mRNA technologies.

- **[[entities/moderna|Moderna]] [[entities/merck|Merck]] Collaboration**: Positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results have been announced for a personalized [[concepts/mrna-cancer-vaccine|mRNA cancer vaccine]] ([[entities/intismeran-autogene|Intismeran Autogene]]) developed by Moderna and Merck.
- **Combination Therapy**: Research indicates the potential efficacy of combining this mRNA vaccine with Keytruda for patients with resected melanoma.
- **Clinical Significance**: This approach represents a major shift in [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]], aiming to reduce recurrence rates through personalized neoantigen targeting alongside PD-1 inhibition.
- **Source Analysis**: Detailed breakdown of the clinical impact and trial data is available in [[lab-notes/2026-08-29-Moderna-Merck-mRNA-Cancer-Vaccine-and-Keytruda-for-Resec|Moderna Merck mRNA Cancer Vaccine and Keytruda for Resected Melanoma Adjuvant Therapy]].
