# Macbook Pro

The MacBook Pro is a line of laptop computers manufactured by Apple that runs macOS. Introduced in 2006, it represents Apple's premium portable computing offering, positioned above the standard MacBook in terms of performance, build quality, and price. The product line targets professional users and power consumers who require sustained computational performance in a portable form factor.

MacBook Pro models have evolved significantly since their introduction, particularly following Apple's transition to in-house silicon chips beginning in 2020. This shift to Apple Silicon, including the M1, M2, and M3 series processors, has dramatically improved energy efficiency and thermal management, allowing for high-performance tasks such as video editing, software development, and running local language models via applications like LM Studio without the need for active cooling fans in lower-tier configurations.

The device series is characterized by its Retina display, Touch Bar (on models prior to 2021), and a range of port options including Thunderbolt 4/USB-C and HDMI. Modern iterations feature the Liquid Retina XDR display for high dynamic range content creation and support for advanced connectivity standards, maintaining its position as a primary tool for creative professionals and developers who demand portability alongside desktop-class capabilities.
