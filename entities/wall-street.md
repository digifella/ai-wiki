# Wall Street

Wall Street is a narrow street located in the Financial District of Lower Manhattan, New York City. Historically, it served as the center of banking and securities trading, giving rise to its status as a synonym for the American financial services industry. The term is now used broadly to describe the financial markets, major financial institutions, and the professionals who operate within them, including stock exchanges, investment banks, and brokerage firms.

The area functions as the primary hub for equity trading and investment activities in the United States. It hosts the headquarters of major financial entities, including the New York Stock Exchange and NASDAQ. The concentration of these institutions has made Wall Street a global symbol of capitalism and financial power.

Recent market dynamics have seen Wall Street analysts focusing on the valuation of potential initial public offerings from major technology companies, such as SpaceX's Starlink. This attention highlights the street's ongoing role in assessing and facilitating the integration of new financial instruments and corporate entities into the broader market ecosystem.
