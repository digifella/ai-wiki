## Overview
Architecture encompasses the art and [[concepts/science|science]] of designing and constructing buildings and other physical structures. It involves the planning, functional design, and technical details of building projects.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/urban-planning|Urban Planning]]**: The process of [[concepts/land-use|land use]] allocation with the goal of guiding urban growth.
- **Sustainability**: Practices that enhance environmental quality and reduce resource depletion.
- **Historic [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Efforts to protect significant buildings, districts, objects, or landscapes from deterioration or destruction.
- **[[concepts/light-rag|Light RAG]] System**: A [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval-augmented generation]] system that involves [[concepts/chunking-documents|chunking documents]], extracting [[concepts/nodes-and-relationships|nodes and relationships]], and [[concepts/storing|storing]] them in both a [[concepts/vector-store|vector store]] and a [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]].
- **[[concepts/graph-rag|Graph RAG]] System**: A variant of RAG that emphasizes the use of graph structures for knowledge representation.
