# Coherence

**Coherence** refers to the [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]], [[concepts/payload-structure|structural integrity]], and semantic alignment within a system or [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]. In the context of [[entities/ai]] and Knowledge Representation, coherence ensures that disparate pieces of information do not contradict each other and that the system operates with a unified understanding of reality.

## Key Dimensions

*   **Logical Consistency:** Absence of internal contradictions within the data or [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes.
*   **Semantic Alignment:** Concepts and [[concepts/nodes|entities]] are defined and related in a way that preserves meaning across different contexts.
*   **Structural [[concepts/honesty|Integrity]]:** The underlying framework (e.g., graph, ontology) supports the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of information without fragmentation.

## Role in Agentic Systems

For [[concepts/background-agents|autonomous agents]] to function reliably, they require a stable foundation to interpret [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and execute actions. Without coherence, agents may suffer from [[concepts/hallucination]], [[concepts/context-drift]], or Goal-[[concepts/safety-concerns|Alignment failures]].

*   **[[concepts/ai-safety|Guardrails]] for [[concepts/trust|Trust]]:** Ontologies act as essential guardrails, constraining the agent's reasoning space to predefined, verified [[concepts/relationships|relationships]]. This reduces the risk of generating nonsensical or harmful outputs.
*   **Neurosymbolic Integration:** Coherence bridges the gap between [[concepts/neural-networks]] ([[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]]) and Symbolic AI ([[concepts/reasoning-skills|logical reasoning]]). By grounding neural outputs in symbolic ontologies, systems achieve both flexibility and rigor.
*   **Error Reduction:** Explicit ontological structures help detect and correct inconsistencies in real-time, enhancing the overall trustworthiness of the system.
