# Falcon 9

**Falcon 9** is a partially reusable two-stage-to-orbit medium-[[entities/lift|lift]] launch vehicle designed and manufactured by [[entities/spacex]] in Hawthorne, California. It is the [[entities/earth|world]]'s first orbital-class reusable rocket, featuring a first stage capable of autonomous landing and rapid reuse.

## Overview
- **Manufacturer:** [[entities/spacex]]
- **Status:** Operational
- **Stages:** 2 (First stage: Merlin engines; Second stage: Merlin Vacuum)
- **Payload Capacity:** ~22,800 kg to LEO (reusable), ~22,800+ kg to LEO (expendable)
- **Key Features:** Autonomous landing legs, grid fins, cold gas thrusters for orientation.
