# Digital Ecosystems

[[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|Digital Ecosystems]] is a simulation platform developed by [[entities/sakana-ai|Sakana AI]] that [[concepts/models|models]] the interactions, survival, and coexistence of multiple [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/agents|agents]] within a controlled computational environment. The system is designed to study how different AI species with varying [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and objectives compete for resources, cooperate, and evolve over time.

## Purpose and Approach

The simulation functions as a research tool to investigate emergent behaviors in [[concepts/expertise-based-ai-assistants|multi-agent systems]]. By creating [[concepts/virtual-environments|virtual environments]] where [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] must navigate resource constraints, interact with other agents, and adapt to changing conditions, researchers can observe patterns of coexistence, competition, and [[concepts/specialization|specialization]] that might inform understanding of both biological ecosystems and artificial agent systems.

## Applications

The work has potential implications for studying AI alignment, cooperative behavior among autonomous systems, and the dynamics of complex adaptive systems. It may also provide insights into how diverse AI systems can be deployed together in shared environments while maintaining stability and mutual benefit.
