# Legal AI Configuration

[[concepts/ai-setup|Legal AI configuration]] involves customizing [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] to support [[concepts/legal-work|legal work]] while maintaining professional standards and protecting sensitive client information. Proper configuration enables these tools to assist with legal research, document drafting, contract analysis, and case [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. The [[concepts/installation|setup process]] requires establishing clear [[concepts/system-instructions|system instructions]] that define the AI's role, scope, and ethical boundaries within legal practice.

## Core Configuration Elements

Effective legal AI configuration begins with [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] that specify the AI should not provide formal legal advice, clarify that outputs require attorney review before use, and establish protocols for handling confidential information. Configuration should define the specific legal domains or tasks the AI [[entities/will|will]] support—such as [[concepts/legal-document-review|contract review]], legal research assistance, or document template creation—rather than attempting general-purpose legal work. [[concepts/security|Security]] considerations are critical, including [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]] on what types of client data can be shared with the [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] and encryption practices for sensitive documents.

## Implementation Across Platforms

Each [[entities/ai-news-strategy-daily|AI platform]] offers different configuration methods. ChatGPT's custom [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] feature allows detailed prompts about the AI's function and output preferences. [[concepts/claude|Claude]] provides [[concepts/system-prompts|system prompts]] and can be configured with specific response frameworks. [[concepts/google-ai|Gemini]] offers similar [[concepts/customization|customization]] through its [[concepts/setup-process|setup process]]. Regardless of platform, configuration should include instructions on citation practices, legal citation format preferences, and requirements for flagging areas requiring human attorney judgment.

## Professional Practice Considerations

[[concepts/legal-professionals|Legal professionals]] using configured [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] remain responsible for all outputs and client advice. Configuration should reinforce that AI-generated documents serve as drafts requiring substantial attorney revision and that the system cannot replace professional legal judgment. Ongoing review of AI outputs against actual legal requirements and case law ensures configuration remains effective and compliant with jurisdictional rules governing legal practice.
