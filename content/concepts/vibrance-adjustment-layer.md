# Vibrance Adjustment Layer

The Vibrance Adjustment Layer is a [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] tool in [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Adobe Photoshop 2025]] that provides refined control over [[concepts/color-saturation|color saturation]] and intensity in digital [[concepts/images|images]]. Unlike the standard Saturation adjustment, the Vibrance tool applies saturation selectively, protecting [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] and preventing oversaturation of already-saturated colors while boosting muted tones. This selective approach makes it particularly useful for portrait and [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] where maintaining natural color balance is important.

## Key Functionality

The [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]] in Photoshop 2025 allows users to adjust color intensity across the image with greater precision than previous iterations. The tool includes controls for overall vibrance adjustment as well as individual color channel manipulation, enabling targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]]. This flexibility supports both subtle refinements and dramatic color grading effects depending on the creative intent.

## Practical Application

As an adjustment layer, the Vibrance tool integrates non-destructively into [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s workflow, allowing users to modify or remove adjustments at any stage of editing. This makes it suitable for both quick color corrections during standard editing and complex color grading workflows where multiple adjustment layers are combined to achieve specific visual styles.
