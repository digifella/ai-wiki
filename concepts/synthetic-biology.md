# Synthetic Biology

**Synthetic biology** is an interdisciplinary field of science that combines biology and engineering to design and construct novel biological parts, devices, and systems, or to redesign existing natural biological systems for useful purposes. It relies heavily on advances in gene editing, genomics, and computational biology.

## Core Concepts

*   **Gene Editing:** The modification of a genome using [[concepts/crispr]], TALENs, or ZFNs.
*   **Bio-bricks:** Standardized biological parts that can be assembled into genetic circuits.
*   **Genetic Circuits:** Engineered networks of genes that perform specific functions, analogous to electronic circuits.
*   **Metabolic Engineering:** The optimization of genetic profiles for biofuel production, pharmaceuticals, and industrial chemicals.
