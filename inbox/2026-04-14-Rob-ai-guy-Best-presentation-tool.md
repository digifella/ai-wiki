# [[entities/rob-ai-guy|Rob ai guy]]. Best [[concepts/presentation-tool|presentation tool]]

---
---
https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cCmedpEpoMw

A detailed [[concepts/summary|summary]] of the video is as follows:
This video presents a [[concepts/head-to-head-comparison|head-to-head comparison]] of three [[concepts/ai-powered-presentation-creation-tools|AI-powered presentation creation tools]]: [[concepts/skywork|Skywork]], [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], and [[concepts/manus|Manus]]. The goal is to determine which tool can create the best and fastest presentations. The presenter conducts a test by giving each tool the same detailed prompt to create a five-slide [[concepts/investor-deck|investor deck]] on the [[concepts/direct-to-consumer-dtc-skincare-sector|direct-to-consumer (DTC) skincare sector]].
The prompt specifies the content for each of the five slides:

* **Slide 1:** Hard numbers, including [[concepts/market-size|market size]], [[concepts/online-share|online share]], [[concepts/dtc-penetration|DTC penetration]], and a five-year [[concepts/compound-annual-growth-rate-cagr|compound annual growth rate (CAGR)]], all sourced from reputable reports.
* **Slide 2:** Core demand drivers, such as [[concepts/gen-z|Gen Z]] and [[concepts/millennial-spending-habits|Millennial spending habits]], the shift towards clean and clinical products, influencer discovery, and margin hotspots.
* **Slide 3:** A competitive map of legacy giants versus funded indie brands, focusing on [[concepts/pricing|pricing]], channel, and differentiation.
* **Slide 4:** Highest-growth white spaces, including microbiome, AI skin analysis, devices, and refillables, backed by real funding and exit data.
* **Slide 5:** Key risks, a compelling "why now" call to action, and next diligence steps, with an emphasis on sourcing every statistic, keeping visuals clean, and minimizing text.

The video [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] that Skywork is the first to complete the task, emphasizing its [[concepts/speed|speed]]. Manus follows, while ChatGPT is shown to be significantly slower, still in the research phase long after the others have finished.
In addition to speed, the video also assesses the quality and features of the presentations generated by each tool. Skywork's output is praised for its clean [[concepts/design|design]], dynamic elements, and the inclusion of sources and report dates. It also offers easy editing and regeneration of slides, as well as the ability to download the presentation in various formats, including [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]] and PowerPoint.
The presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] that Skywork also has additional features beyond slide creation, such as [[concepts/agents|agents]] for documents, sheets, webpages, and [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]], and allows for the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of professional data sources and a [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]].
Ultimately, the video concludes that Skywork is the superior tool for creating presentations due to its speed, the high quality of its output, its user-friendly editing [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], and its extensive additional features.
