# Engine nacelle rotation

The mechanical process of pivoting engine housings (nacelles) around a fixed axis to alter the orientation of [[concepts/engine-thrust|thrust]] relative to the airframe, enabling the transition between disparate flight regimes.

## V-22 Osprey Implementation
- Fundamental mechanism for achieving **[[concepts/dual-flight-modes|dual flight modes]]** in [[concepts/tiltrotor-technology|Tiltrotor]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].
- **[[concepts/vertical-takeoff-and-landing-vtol|VTOL]] ([[concepts/vertical-take-off-and-landing|Vertical Take-Off and Landing]])**: Rotating nacelles to a vertical orientation to facilitate thrust-borne flight.
- **Fixed-wing flight**: Rotating nacelles forward to transition to wing-borne flight via Aerodynamics.
- Requires complex engineering to manage the structural loads and aerodynamic shifts during the conversion process.
