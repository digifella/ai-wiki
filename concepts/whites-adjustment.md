# Whites Adjustment

The Whites Adjustment is a tonal control slider in Adobe Lightroom Classic's Develop module that selectively brightens or darkens the lighter midtones and near-white areas of a photograph. Located in the Basic panel alongside related tools like Exposure and Highlights, it operates independently of shadow adjustments, making it useful for fine-tuning the brightness of highlights without affecting the overall contrast or darker tones of an image.

## Function and Application

The slider works by targeting a specific tonal range—primarily the brighter midtones and near-white pixels—rather than affecting the entire image uniformly. This targeted approach allows photographers to recover blown-out highlights, add punch to bright areas, or reduce glare in landscape and portrait photography. The adjustment is particularly valuable in post-processing workflows where precise control over highlight detail is needed without reshaping the image's shadow structure.

## Usage in Practice

Photographers typically use the Whites Adjustment as part of a broader tonal editing strategy, often in combination with the Exposure, Highlights, and Shadows sliders. Moving the slider to the right brightens the lighter tones, while moving it left darkens them. The adjustment has been featured in photography education materials, including landscape processing tutorials that demonstrate its practical application alongside other Lightroom tools.
