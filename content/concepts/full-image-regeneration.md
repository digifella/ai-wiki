# Full-image regeneration

The process of generating a complete new image from scratch or re-rendering an entire image based on a prompt, distinct from partial edits or localized modifications.

- [[entities/adobe-firefly|Adobe Firefly]]'s "[[concepts/layer-control|Prompt-to-Edit]]" enables text-based [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] for materials, textures, and mood, supporting [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] (similar to [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]]) but with distinct core functionality.
- Both tools [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] principles for image editing, though they cater to different workflows (full-image regeneration vs. localized fill).
