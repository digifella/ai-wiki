# Residence Visa For Business

A residence visa for business in [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] is a long-term visa category designed for foreign nationals who wish to establish or manage business operations within the country. This visa type permits individuals to reside in Turkey while conducting business activities, distinguishing it from standard tourist or short-term visit visas. The residence visa requires applicants to meet specific financial and business criteria set by Turkish immigration authorities.

## Application Requirements

Applicants for a business residence visa must typically demonstrate [[concepts/proof|proof]] of business registration in Turkey, sufficient financial means to support themselves and their business operations, and a clean criminal record. The exact documentation requirements may vary based on the applicant's nationality and the [[entities/nature|nature]] of the proposed business. Processing times and specific conditions are determined by Turkish immigration officials during the application review.

## Duration and Renewal

A business residence visa is generally issued for a specified period, after which renewal may be possible if the visa holder continues to meet the required conditions. The visa allows the holder to reside in Turkey and conduct their registered business activities, though it does not automatically confer citizenship or permanent residency status.
