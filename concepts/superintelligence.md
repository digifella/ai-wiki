# Superintelligence

## Definition
A hypothetical [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] with cognitive capabilities far surpassing the brightest human minds in virtually every field, including scientific creativity, general wisdom, and social skills.

## Key Dynamics in Superintelligence Development

### Compute Access and Economic Reality
The path to superintelligence is heavily constrained by the economics of compute and hardware access. Recent discourse highlights a tension between open empowerment and centralized control:

- **Zuckerberg's Vision**: [[entities/mark-zuckerberg|Mark Zuckerberg]] advocates for a future where AI advancement is democratized, arguing that the benefits of superintelligence should be accessible to everyone rather than monopolized by a few entities [[lab-notes/2026-08-12-Zuckerbergs-AI-Vision-Empowerment-vs.-Economic-Reality-o|Zuckerberg's AI Vision: Empowerment vs. Economic Reality of Compute Access]].
- **Critique of Centralization**: This vision directly challenges the current trajectory of major labs like Anthropic and Dario AI, which rely on massive, closed compute clusters. Critics argue that the economic reality of compute scarcity favors incumbents, potentially stifling the very "empowerment" Zuckerberg claims to support.
- **Implications for AGI/Superintelligence**: If compute remains a bottleneck controlled by a few corporations, the development of superintelligence may not lead to broad societal benefit but rather to increased concentration of power.
