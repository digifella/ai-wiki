# Kiewa Hydroelectric Scheme

The **Kiewa Hydroelectric Scheme** is a major water [[entities/storage|storage]] and power generation project located in the Victorian Alps of Australia. It utilizes the Kiewa River to generate [[concepts/hydroelectric-power|hydroelectric power]] for the Victoria (state).
