# Heat Shield Tiles

Thermal [[concepts/secure|protection]] system (TPS) components designed to withstand extreme aerodynamic heating during atmospheric re-entry. Critical for [[concepts/reusable-rocket-technology|reusable launch vehicles]] to ensure [[concepts/payload-structure|structural integrity]] and payload safety.

## Key Developments

- **[[concepts/starship-flight-14|SpaceX Starship Flight 14]] (2026-09-29):**
  - First successful [[concepts/orbital-insertion]] of the full-stack vehicle.
  - Demonstrated TPS performance during re-entry prior to [[concepts/controlled-splashdown]].
  - Vehicle successfully deployed 26 [[concepts/starlink-v3]] satellites, validating the launch vehicle's operational capability alongside thermal management systems.
  - Detailed telemetry and visual analysis of tile integrity available in [[lab-notes/2026-09-29-SpaceX-Starship-Flight-14-Orbital-Insertion-Starlink-Dep|SpaceX Starship Flight 14: Orbital Insertion, Starlink Deployment, Controlled Splashdowns]].
