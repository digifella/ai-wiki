# Metal Personality

The [[concepts/gold-metal-personality|Metal personality]] is one of the five elemental profiles in ancient Chinese [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] and psychology. In this system, each element represents a distinct set of traits, behaviors, and influences on interactions with others.

### Key Characteristics:
- **Practical and Organized:** Individuals with a strong metal personality are known for their meticulous planning and [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to detail.
- **Decisive and Confident:** They make clear decisions swiftly and are not easily swayed by external opinions or emotions.
- **Disciplined and Responsible:** Metal personalities often take on leadership roles due to their ability to set strict boundaries and adhere to rules.
