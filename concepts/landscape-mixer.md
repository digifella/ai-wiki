# Landscape Mixer

A feature within [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/neural-filters]] suite used for transferring the stylistic and atmospheric elements of one photograph onto another.

### Key Capabilities
- **[[concepts/style|Style]] Transfer:** Facilitates the transfer of color, lighting, and overall aesthetic from a reference image to a target image.
- **Environmental Transformation:** Enables specific environmental shifts, such as transforming a standard Seascape into a Sunset Photo style.
- **Aesthetic Replication:** Allows users to "copy and paste" the entire "look" of a source photograph onto different compositions.
