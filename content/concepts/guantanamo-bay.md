# Guantanamo Bay

Strategic Naval Base and Detention Center located on the southeastern coast of [[entities/cuba|Cuba]], leased by the [[entities/united-states]] since 1903. Site of significant geopolitical, legal, and ethical controversies.

## Overview
- **Acquisition & Sovereignty:** Perpetual lease established via 1903 treaty; US exercises jurisdiction and [[concepts/power|control]] while Cuba retains nominal sovereignty and disputes the lease's validity; unique extraterritorial legal status.
- **Military Function:** Historic coaling station evolved into key strategic Naval Base supporting US Caribbean operations, logistics, and intelligence activities.
- **[[concepts/detention-center|Detention Facility]]:** Guantanamo Bay Detention Camp inaugurated 2002 post-War on Terror; designed for indefinite detention of "enemy combatants"; location selected to circumvent US Constitutional protections and apply alternative legal frameworks.
- **Controversies & Debates:** Persistent allegations of Torture and enhanced interrogation techniques; international condemnation regarding indefinite detention without trial; lack of due process; recurring US executive directives for closure opposed by legislative action; diplomatic [[concepts/friction|friction]] with Cuba.
