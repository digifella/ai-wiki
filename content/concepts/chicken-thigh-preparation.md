# Chicken Thigh Preparation

Strategies for maximizing moisture retention and flavor in poultry through controlled [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] and [[concepts/cooling-systems|thermal management]].

## Core Determinants of Juiciness
- **Marinade**: Essential for flavor infusion and moisture retention.
- **Cut**: The specific selection and preparation of the meat affects heat [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] and moisture loss.
- **Cook Techniques**: Proper execution of methods, specifically [[concepts/bbq|Grilling]], is critical to avoiding common pitfalls.

## Key Pitfalls
- Improperly managed preparation leads to the common issue of "Dry Grilled Chicken."
