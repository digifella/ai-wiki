# Google Vids

Google Vids is an artificial intelligence-powered video creation tool developed by Google as part of its Workspace productivity suite. The application is designed to streamline video production by automating key aspects of the creation process, enabling users to produce polished videos without requiring extensive technical expertise or manual editing work.

## Functionality

The tool leverages generative AI to assist with multiple stages of video creation. Users can input text, scripts, or prompts, which the system then transforms into video content by generating visuals, selecting appropriate music, and arranging clips. Google Vids integrates with other Google Workspace applications, allowing users to incorporate existing documents, presentations, and media assets into their videos.

## Purpose and Access

Google Vids is intended for professionals, educators, and organizations seeking to create marketing content, training materials, presentations, and other video-based communications more efficiently. As part of Google Workspace, the tool is available to subscribers of the productivity suite, positioning it alongside other collaborative applications like Google Docs, Sheets, and Meet.
