# Electric Semi-Truck Overview

An electric semi-truck is a large truck designed to operate on battery [[concepts/power|power]] instead of conventional fossil fuels. These vehicles are part of the broader shift towards electrification in the transportation sector, aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] reducing emissions and improving efficiency.

## Key Features
- **Battery Technology:** Utilizes advanced lithium-ion batteries or other next-generation technologies for energy [[entities/storage|storage]].
- **[[concepts/charging-infrastructure|Charging Infrastructure]]:** Requires a robust network of high-[[concepts/speed|speed]] charging stations capable of rapid refueling.
- **[[concepts/range|Range]] and Efficiency:** Designed to match the range and performance [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of diesel trucks while offering lower operating costs.
- **Environmental Impact:** Significantly reduces greenhouse gas emissions, contributing to sustainability goals.
