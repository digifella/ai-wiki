Chronic pain is persistent or recurrent pain that lasts for more than three months and can significantly impact a person's [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]]. It often involves complex interactions between physiological, psychological, and [[concepts/social-psychological-factors|social factors]]. Here are some key aspects and related concepts:

- **Physiological Factors**:
  - Nerve damage (neuropathy) WikiLink
  - Inflammation
  - Neuroplastic changes in the brain

- **Psychological Factors**:
  - [[concepts/depression|Depression]] and [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]]
  - Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)
  - [[concepts/mindfulness|Mindfulness]]-based [[concepts/stress|stress]] reduction (MBSR)

- **Social Factors**:
  - Support networks
  - Work-life balance
  - [[concepts/chronic-pain-management|Pain management]] education
