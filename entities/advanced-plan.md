# Advanced Plan

The [[concepts/advanced-plan|Advanced Plan]] is a mid-tier subscription offering from [[entities/evernote|Evernote]] designed for users with substantial note-taking and organizational requirements. It provides enhanced features and [[entities/storage|storage]] capacity beyond Evernote's basic free and starter tiers, positioning it as a bridge between entry-level users and enterprise customers.

## Features and Positioning

The [[concepts/advanced-plan|Advanced Plan]] sits within [[entities/evernote|Evernote]]'s tiered [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]], offering increased monthly note creation limits and expanded [[entities/storage|storage]] compared to lower-tier options. This plan targets users whose note-taking demands exceed what free and basic plans accommodate, while remaining more affordable than enterprise solutions.
