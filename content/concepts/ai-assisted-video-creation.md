# AI Assisted Video Creation

AI-assisted video creation refers to the use of artificial intelligence tools to automate and streamline the video production process. These platforms reduce technical and creative barriers by providing pre-built assets, templates, and automated generation capabilities. Rather than requiring specialized skills in cinematography, animation, or editing, users can create videos by selecting from available components and customizing them for their needs.

## How It Works

Platforms like Vyond exemplify this approach by offering libraries of stock characters, scenes, and animations that users can combine to construct videos. Users typically work through a graphical interface where they select characters, define movements, add voiceovers, and arrange sequences. The AI handles rendering and technical production details, allowing creators to focus on narrative and messaging rather than software proficiency.

## Applications and Impact

AI-assisted video creation has expanded accessibility to video production across business, education, and marketing contexts. Organizations use these tools for training videos, explainer content, and promotional materials without maintaining in-house production teams. The technology trades creative control and uniqueness for speed and cost efficiency, making video a more feasible medium for teams with limited budgets or technical expertise.
