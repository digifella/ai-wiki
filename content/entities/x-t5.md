# X-T5
A digital mirrorless [[concepts/camera-settings|camera]] produced by [[entities/fujifilm]].

## Features & Functionality
- **[[concepts/lock-feature|Lock Feature]]**: A mechanism designed to prevent [[concepts/accidental-changes|accidental changes]] to [[concepts/camera-settings]].
    - Principles are applicable across various [[entities/fujifilm]] models, including the [[entities/x-s20|X-S20]].
    - Can be used to restrict access to a wide range of controls (up to 53 individual [[concepts/camera-settings|camera controls]]).
