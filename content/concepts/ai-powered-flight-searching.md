# AI Powered Flight Searching

AI-powered flight searching uses large language models like ChatGPT to help identify inexpensive flight options by processing travel preferences against available market data. Rather than manually visiting multiple travel booking websites, users provide structured information through natural language prompts—including destinations, travel dates, budget constraints, and flexibility parameters. The AI then analyzes this input and suggests relevant options based on its training data and reasoning capabilities.

## How It Works

Users interact with AI systems by crafting detailed prompts that specify their travel requirements. Effective prompts typically include departure and arrival cities, preferred travel dates or date ranges, budget limits, and any flexibility around timing or routing. The AI processes this information and can recommend flight combinations, suggest alternative dates that may offer lower fares, or identify potential layover strategies that reduce costs. The system may also consider factors like flight duration, number of stops, and airline preferences if mentioned.

## Limitations and Considerations

While AI flight searching offers convenience, it operates within important constraints. Large language models do not have real-time access to current flight prices or availability—their training data has a knowledge cutoff date, meaning recommendations may not reflect actual current fares. Users should treat AI suggestions as starting points rather than definitive bookings, and verify all prices and availability on actual booking platforms before purchasing. Additionally, the AI cannot complete transactions or access exclusive deals that may be available through dedicated travel booking websites or airline loyalty programs.
