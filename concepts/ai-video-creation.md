# AI Video Creation

## Overview

[[concepts/ai-advertising|AI video creation]] is the process of generating [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. This can involve everything from scriptwriting and storyboard generation to actual video [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]] and editing. With advancements in [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|AI-generated videos]] are becoming more sophisticated and are being used across various platforms including social media, [[entities/youtube|YouTube]], and beyond.

## Tool Spotlight: Google Vids

[[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] represents a significant evolution in AI video tools, integrating directly into [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]]. It functions similarly to [[concepts/google-docs|Google Docs]] but for video, allowing users to generate videos from [[concepts/text-prompts|text prompts]].

- **Core Functionality:** Transforms text documents into video presentations with AI-generated visuals, voiceovers, and music.
- **Integration:** Seamlessly pulls assets from [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]] and other Workspace [[concepts/apps|apps]].
