# Robodebt scheme

An [[concepts/automated-debt-recovery|automated debt recovery]] system implemented by the Australian government in 2016.

### Overview
- Designed to identify and recover alleged overpayments of [[concepts/welfare-benefits|welfare benefits]] using an automated algorithm.
- Deemed an unlawful mechanism due to its [[concepts/calculation-methods|calculation methods]].
- Linked to catastrophic human consequences, including documented fatalities.
