# Color Palette Manipulation

The [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]] in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] enables precise, targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] within an image through HSL ([[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, Luminance) controls. Rather than applying broad adjustments to an entire photograph, this tool allows photographers to isolate and modify individual color channels—such as reds, oranges, yellows, greens, cyans, blues, magentas, and neutrals—independently of one another.

## HSL Adjustment Capabilities

Each color [[concepts/range|range]] can be adjusted across three dimensions. Hue shifts allow rotation of a color's position on the color wheel, saturation controls the intensity or vibrancy of the color, and luminance adjusts the brightness of that specific color. This three-axis [[concepts/power|control]] system makes it possible to warm cool tones, desaturate distracting colors, or selectively brighten or darken particular hues without affecting the rest of the image.

## Practical Applications

Color Palette Manipulation is commonly used for tasks such as enhancing [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] while preserving background colors, correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] in specific regions, improving the appeal of foliage or skies, and creating consistent [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] across multiple [[concepts/images|images]]. The selective [[entities/nature|nature]] of HSL adjustments makes them particularly valuable in portrait and [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] where different color elements require distinct treatments.
