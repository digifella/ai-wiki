# Tonal Adjustments

The process of modifying the luminance and color [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of an image to enhance [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], depth, and mood.

## Key Elements
- [[concepts/exposure]]: The fundamental adjustment of [[concepts/light|light]] intensity.
- [[concepts/contrast]]: The [[concepts/range|range]] and scale of difference between light and dark values.
- [[concepts/highlights]] & [[concepts/shadows]]: Targeted manipulation of the brightest and darkest regions of the tonal range.
- Midtones: Adjustment of the intermediate luminance levels that define core [[concepts/texture|texture]] and color.
- [[concepts/lightroom]]: The application of [[concepts/hue|hue]] and saturation shifts to establish an aesthetic or emotional [[concepts/tone|tone]].

## Tools & Workflows
- [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] workflows:
    - Photoshop Camera Raw Filter: Color Grading for Photo Tonal Adjustments:
        - Utilizes the **Color Grading tab** within the [[entities/camera-raw-filter]] for advanced color manipulation.
        - Leverages color wheels to apply specific color shifts to [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], midtones, and highlights.
