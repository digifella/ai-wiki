# Invisible Universe

The Invisible Universe encompasses the vast, unseen components of the cosmos that dictate its [[concepts/structure|structure]], evolution, and expansion. [[concepts/assistive-technology|At]] the core of this concept lies the mystery of dark matter and dark energy, which constitute the majority of the mass and energy content of the universe, yet remain largely undetectable by conventional means.

## Dark Matter and Cosmic Mysteries

Dark matter is a hypothetical form of matter that interacts gravitationally but does not emit, absorb, or reflect [[concepts/light|light]], making it "invisible." Understanding its role is crucial to understanding the large-scale structure of the universe.
