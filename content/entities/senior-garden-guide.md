# Senior Garden Guide

Senior Garden Guide is a YouTube channel focused on providing horticultural education, with a particular emphasis on hydroponic and [[concepts/plant-tolerance|water-based plant cultivation]] methods. The channel produces instructional content designed to help viewers grow indoor plants using water as the growing [[entities/medium|medium]] rather than traditional soil-based systems.

## Content Focus

The channel specializes in identifying and explaining which indoor plant species can successfully thrive in water-only environments. Notable video content includes guides such as "7 Indoor Plants That Thrive in JUST Water (No Soil Needed!)," which demonstrates practical techniques for soilless [[concepts/indoor-gardening|indoor gardening]]. This approach appeals to viewers interested in alternative gardening methods, space-efficient [[concepts/plant-domestication|plant cultivation]], or simplified plant care routines.
