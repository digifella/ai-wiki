# Personal Knowledge Management (PKM)

Personal [[concepts/knowledge-management|Knowledge Management]] (PKM) is a system or strategy that individuals use to capture, organize, store, and retrieve information relevant to their personal or professional lives. It often involves the use of digital tools like [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]].

- Focuses on automating data ingestion, [[concepts/organization|organization]], [[concepts/summarization|summarization]], and retrieval processes.
- Useful for project management and other [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] requiring efficient information handling.
