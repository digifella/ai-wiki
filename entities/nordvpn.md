# NordVPN

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012. The company operates a global network of VPN servers that allow users to encrypt their internet connections and route their traffic through NordVPN's infrastructure, thereby masking their IP address and location from internet service providers, network administrators, and other parties that might monitor network activity.

## Service Features

The service enables users to conceal their online activity and location while browsing the internet. By connecting to one of NordVPN's servers in various countries, users can appear to be accessing the internet from a different geographical location. The company offers encrypted connections across multiple protocols and maintains a no-logs policy regarding user activity.

## Operations

NordVPN operates servers in numerous countries worldwide and serves a global user base. The company generates revenue through subscription-based models, offering various subscription tiers and billing periods. As a VPN provider, NordVPN competes in the broader cybersecurity and privacy-focused software market.
