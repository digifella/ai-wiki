# Human Stories

**Human Stories** are [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] structures that encode complex [[concepts/social-dynamics|social dynamics]], causal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and evolutionary heuristics. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], they serve as a potential framework for bridging the gap between [[concepts/data-formatting|raw data processing]] and [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]].

## Core Concepts

- **Narrative as [[concepts/data-structure|Data Structure]]**: Stories are not merely entertainment but compressed information packets containing [[concepts/cause-and-effect|cause-and-effect]] [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], moral frameworks, and predictive models of human behavior.
- **Evolutionary [[concepts/learning|Learning]]**: [[concepts/human-cognition|Human cognition]] evolved to process information through narrative arcs, allowing for rapid [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]] from limited examples. [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] lacking this narrative layer struggle with nuance and context.
