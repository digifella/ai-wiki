# Public Health

[[concepts/population-health|Public health]] is a field dedicated to improving the overall [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] of populations through various strategies such as education, [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]], research, and community development. It focuses on preventing disease and injury by addressing environmental hazards, reducing unhealthful behaviors, and teaching about hygiene practices.

## Key Points
- **Definition:** Public Health involves promoting and protecting health and preventing illness, disability, and death at the population level.
- **Role:** Improves [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] through [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] and control of infectious diseases; reduction of environmental risks; education on healthy lifestyles; ensuring a safe food supply; monitoring [[concepts/disease-outbreaks|disease outbreaks]]; and advocating for [[concepts/policies|policies]] to improve public welfare.

## Social Determinants of Health
Social determinants are the conditions in which people are born, grow, live, work, and age. These factors significantly influence health outcomes and include:

- Economic stability: Employment opportunities, income level.
- Education access and quality: Literacy rates, school funding.
- Healthcare systems: Availability of medical care services.
- Neighborhood and physical environment: Housing conditions, water quality.
- Social context: Community support networks, social inclusion.
