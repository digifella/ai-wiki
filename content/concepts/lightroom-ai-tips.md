# Lightroom AI Tips
## Overview
[[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Lightroom]] has been a cornerstone in digital photography for years. With the introduction of AI and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] features, photographers can achieve more with less effort, making editing and managing [[concepts/images|images]] faster and more intuitive.

## Tips Overview
- Utilize AI-driven tools like [AI Sky Replacement](https://helpx.adobe.com/lightroom/using/image-editing-tools.html) to quickly transform sky elements in photos.
- Master the **Adobe Sensei** features for intelligent [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] and management.
- Implement [[concepts/automation|automation]] with Lightroom presets and profiles to streamline repetitive tasks.
- Leverage the power of AI to improve batch processing efficiency.
- Explore advanced techniques with [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]], such as [AI-based masking](https://helpx.adobe.com/photoshop/using/workflows-mask.html) for complex selections.
