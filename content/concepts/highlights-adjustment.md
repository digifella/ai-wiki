# Highlights Adjustment

Highlights adjustment refers to the selective editing of bright areas in a photograph to balance [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] and recover detail. In Lightroom Classic, this process is streamlined through the use of [[concepts/single-click-editing|automated AI mask presets]] designed for [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]]. These masks allow photographers to target and modify highlights independently from other tonal ranges, preventing blown-out skies and overexposed regions while maintaining natural-looking results.

## Landscape Masking in Lightroom Classic

Lightroom Classic's single-click automated AI mask presets for landscape photography enable users to isolate sky and bright areas with minimal manual intervention. Once a landscape mask is applied, the highlights adjustment slider becomes a precise tool for reducing overexposure in specific regions. This approach is more efficient than traditional manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] and reduces the [[concepts/learning|learning]] curve for photographers new to advanced editing techniques.

## Alternative Approaches

Free alternatives like [[entities/snapseed|Snapseed]] offer highlights adjustment capabilities through their camera and pro features, though with less specialized [[concepts/automation|automation]] for landscape work. More [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] exist for specific highlight challenges, such as dehazing [[concepts/hazy-backgrounds|hazy backgrounds]], which address both highlight recovery and atmospheric clarity simultaneously. The choice of tool depends on workflow preferences, budget constraints, and the specific characteristics of the image being edited.
