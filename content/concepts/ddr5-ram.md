# DDR5 RAM

DDR5 (Double Data Rate 5) is the fifth generation of SDRAM, succeeding DDR4 with significant advancements in bandwidth, density, and power efficiency.

## Key Technical Features
- **Increased Bandwidth:** Higher data transfer rates and improved burst length.
- **Power Management:** Integration of a Power Management Integrated Circuit (PMIC) directly on the module for better Power Management.
- **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]:** On-die Error Correction Code (ECC) to enhance Signal [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]] and stability.
- **Efficiency:** Improved voltage [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] and higher density capabilities.
