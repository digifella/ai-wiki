# Emergent Behavior

**Emergent behavior** refers to the phenomenon where complex systems and patterns arise out of the interaction of relatively simple components. The resulting behavior is not explicitly programmed or directed by any single component but arises from local interactions, leading to macroscopic properties that differ from those of the constituent parts.

## Core Principles

- **Non-linearity**: Small changes in initial conditions or interactions can lead to disproportionately large effects.
- **Self-organization**: Structure arises without external control; order is generated from within the system.
- **Decentralization**: No central authority dictates the global behavior; decisions are made locally.
- **[[concepts/feedback|Feedback]] [[concepts/loops|Loops]]**: Positive feedback amplifies deviations, while negative feedback stabilizes the system.
