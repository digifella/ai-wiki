# Generative Apps

Generative Apps represent [[concepts/software|applications]] built on or enhanced by [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]], particularly [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and multimodal systems. These applications leverage generative [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to create content, automate workflows, or provide intelligent assistance across various domains. [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] serves as a significant platform in this space, offering both API access and integrated tools for developers building generative applications.

## Google Gemini Integration

[[entities/google-ai-ultra|Google Gemini]] has become a central component in the generative apps ecosystem, with regular updates expanding its capabilities and [[concepts/integration|integration]] options. These updates introduce new features and tools designed to improve the functionality and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of generative applications built on the platform. The evolution of [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] reflects broader trends in making [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] more practical and efficient for production use.

## Market Considerations

The generative apps market involves trade-offs between different platforms and solutions. [[concepts/cost|Cost]] remains a significant factor for developers and organizations implementing these systems, with various providers offering different [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] [[concepts/models|models]] and performance characteristics. As the market matures, multiple competing platforms continue to develop their offerings, each with distinct advantages and limitations for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
