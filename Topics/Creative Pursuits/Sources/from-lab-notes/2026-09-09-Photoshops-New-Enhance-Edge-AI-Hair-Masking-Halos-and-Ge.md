## Photoshop's New Enhance Edge AI: Hair Masking, Halos, and Generative Credits
**Clip title:** Did [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] Finally Solve Hair Masking? Insane New Feature!
**Author / channel:** PiXimperfect
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DPWm5wGvTu0

### Summary
This video introduces and thoroughly reviews [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]]'s new AI-powered "[[concepts/enhance-edge|Enhance Edge]]" feature, found in [[concepts/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] 27.11.0, designed to improve selections, particularly for complex elements like hair. The presenter demonstrates how basic "[[concepts/remove-background|Remove Background]]" functions often leave noticeable [[concepts/halos|halos]], especially when the subject's hair is against a contrasting background. Enhance Edge offers an automatic solution, available either via the contextual taskbar or within the Layer Mask properties panel, effectively minimizing these unwanted background remnants around the subject.

The core mechanism behind Enhance Edge is its use of AI to generate and expand the edges of the subject without altering the original mask. While the mask itself remains untouched, the feature effectively "fills in" the areas that would typically appear as halos, creating a seamless blend with new backgrounds. The presenter highlights an interesting unintended benefit: by utilizing the enhanced layer, users can also visually increase hair volume in their images, demonstrating a before-and-after comparison that shows a fuller appearance.

A significant point of discussion is the cost associated with this AI feature. Each application of "Enhance Edge" consumes [[concepts/generative-credits|generative credits]], with the demonstration showing an expenditure of 20 credits per use. This contrasts with traditional, non-AI Photoshop methods like "Decontaminate Colors" found in the "Select and Mask" workspace, which does not incur credit costs. While "Decontaminate Colors" can also help remove halos, the video showcases Enhance Edge's superior ability to generate texture and detail for individual hair strands, offering a more natural and refined result compared to the flatter, less dimensional output of the non-AI alternative.

However, the "Enhance Edge" feature is not without limitations. The presenter reveals that for highly intricate details, such as complex braids or very fine, wispy hair, the AI may encounter a resolution limit. In such cases, the tool can over-process, turning delicate strands into "mush" or causing a loss of fine detail. For these challenging scenarios, traditional Photoshop techniques, such as applying a "Minimum" filter to the mask, are presented as a more effective alternative to maintain detail and quality. Ultimately, "Enhance Edge" is a powerful addition for everyday selections, particularly in resolving halo issues, but users should be mindful of its generative credit cost and potential limitations when working with extremely high-resolution or highly detailed subject matter.
