# Vulnerable Populations

Vulnerable populations are groups of people who may [[concepts/experience|experience]] greater impacts from environmental changes due to factors such as age, socioeconomic status, and pre-existing medical conditions. These individuals often lack the resources and support needed to adapt effectively to new challenges.

## Key Points
- **Definition**: Groups that are particularly susceptible to adverse [[concepts/health|health]] outcomes resulting from environmental stressors.
- **Examples**:
  - Elderly people with [[concepts/chronic-diseases|chronic diseases]]
  - Children and infants
  - Low-income communities
  - Immigrant populations, especially those lacking legal documentation or language [[concepts/skills|skills]]

### Climate Change Impact
- Increased frequency of heatwaves leading to higher mortality rates among the elderly and individuals with cardiovascular conditions.
- Air pollution exacerbating respiratory illnesses in children and other sensitive groups.
- [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|Extreme weather events]] disrupting [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]] services and essential supplies.
