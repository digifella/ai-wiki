# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is an Indian-American business executive who serves as the Chief Executive Officer of [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]], a position he has held since February 2014. Under his [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], Microsoft has undergone significant strategic shifts, including a renewed focus on [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]] through [[entities/azure|Azure]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/integration|integration]] across its product portfolio.

Before becoming CEO, Nadella spent over two decades [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Microsoft in various engineering and leadership roles. He joined the company in 1992 and held positions including Vice President of Search and Advertising, and President of Cloud and Enterprise. His technical background in computer [[concepts/science|science]] and experience in cloud infrastructure proved formative to his strategic direction of the company.

Nadella's tenure has been marked by a cultural transformation at Microsoft toward greater openness, including partnerships with competitors like [[entities/apple|Apple]] and [[concepts/google-search|Google]]. He has emphasized growth [[concepts/mindset|mindset]] principles and has overseen major initiatives such as the development of cloud services partnerships and investments in AI technologies, positioning Microsoft as a significant player in enterprise cloud and AI markets.
