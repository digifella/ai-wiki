# Danish Government

The Danish Government serves as the executive branch of Denmark's system of government, operating within a constitutional monarchy framework. It is headed by the Prime Minister and consists of various ministries tasked with implementing policy and administering state affairs. These ministries oversee critical sectors including finance, defense, health, education, and the environment.

## Archaeological Policy

In a notable deviation from standard heritage management, the Danish government has authorized private metal detectorists to conduct systematic searches of farm fields for archaeological artifacts. This specific policy permits qualified individuals to search for and report discoveries, aiming to facilitate the recovery of historical items that might otherwise remain hidden in agricultural land.
