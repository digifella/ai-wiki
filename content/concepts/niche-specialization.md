# Niche Specialization

A [[concepts/strategic-approach|strategic approach]] where an entity focuses on a specific, narrow segment of a market or domain to achieve competitive advantage through deep expertise and tailored solutions.

* As of mid-2025, the AI model landscape has evolved from [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]-4 dominance to competitive parity among top models, per [[entities/dave|Dave]] Plummer's review of [[entities/chatgpt]], [[entities/claude]], [[entities/gemini]], and [[entities/grok]] (v3)
* Dave Plummer (retired Microsoft [[concepts/software|software]] engineer) notes that heavy real-world usage across these models reveals nuanced [[concepts/specialization|specialization]] in different task domains
* Evolution of [[concepts/large-language-models]] demonstrates how niche specialization can emerge even within broad AI categories
