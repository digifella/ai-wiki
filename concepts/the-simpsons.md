# The Simpsons

**[[entities/the-simpsons|The Simpsons]]** is an American animated sitcom created by Matt Groening for the Fox Broadcasting Company. It is the longest-running American primetime television series and the longest-running animated series in American television history. The show provides a satirical reflection of American culture, family life, and societal norms.

## Overview
- **Premiere:** December 17, 1989
- **[[concepts/creator|Creator]]:** Matt Groening
- **Studio:** Gracie Films, 20th Television
- **Genre:** Sitcom, [[concepts/satire]], Surreal humor

## Critical Reception & Analysis
The series is widely regarded as a cultural phenomenon, with early seasons (particularly Seasons 3–8) often cited as its "Golden Age." Recent critical discourse has highlighted specific episodes as pivotal in defining the show's thematic depth.

- **"[[entities/homers-enemy|Homer's Enemy]]" (Season 8, Episode 17):**
	- Often analyzed as a deconstruction of the show's own mythology and a critique of corporate bureaucracy and anti-establishment sentiment.

## Cultural Impact
- **Language:** Introduced numerous phrases into common vernacular (e.g., "D'oh!", "Excellent!").
- **Parody:** Frequently parodies Hollywood, politics, and media.
- **Merchandising:** One of the most heavily merchandised franchises in history.
