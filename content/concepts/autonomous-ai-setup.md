# Autonomous AI Setup

Autonomous [[concepts/ai-setup|AI setup]] refers to the process of configuring, securing, and deploying [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems with minimal human intervention. This encompasses the automated selection of [[concepts/models|models]], infrastructure provisioning, [[concepts/security|security]] configuration, and ongoing monitoring. The [[concepts/autonomous-workflow-automation|Hermes Agent]] facilitates this process by generating comprehensive guides that address the technical and operational requirements for deploying AI systems independently.

## Security Considerations

Security is a critical component of autonomous AI [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]. This includes implementing [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] and [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] mechanisms, encrypting data in transit and [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] rest, managing [[concepts/api-keys|API keys]] and credentials, and establishing audit trails for system actions. Autonomous setups must incorporate security validation at multiple stages—during model selection, infrastructure configuration, and runtime operation—to prevent unauthorized access and data [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].

## Deployment and Operations

Deployment of [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]] requires selecting appropriate infrastructure, configuring [[concepts/containerization|containerization]] or serverless environments, and establishing monitoring systems. The process typically involves version [[concepts/power|control]] for models and [[concepts/code|code]], [[concepts/automated-software-testing|automated testing]] pipelines, and rollback mechanisms. Documentation and [[concepts/configuration-management|configuration management]] ensure that deployments remain reproducible and maintainable across different environments and time periods.
