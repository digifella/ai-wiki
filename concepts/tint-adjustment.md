# Tint Adjustment

Tint adjustment is a color grading feature in Photoshop 2025 that allows users to modify the color temperature and hue balance of images. This tool enables photographers and designers to shift colors toward warmer or cooler tones across an image or in selected areas. As part of Adobe's color correction capabilities, tint adjustment provides systematic control over the overall color cast of a photograph.

## Integration with Color and Vibrance Tool

The tint adjustment feature is integrated into Photoshop 2025's updated Color and Vibrance tool, which consolidates several color modification controls in a single interface. This integration streamlines the workflow for users performing color grading tasks, allowing tint adjustments to be applied alongside vibrance and saturation modifications. The tool is designed to work with both global adjustments across entire images and localized corrections in specific areas.

## Practical Application

Tint adjustment is commonly used to correct color casts caused by different lighting conditions, such as the blue tones of daylight or the yellow-orange tones of incandescent lighting. Users can also employ tint adjustment creatively to establish a consistent color mood across a series of photographs or to achieve specific aesthetic effects in their work.
