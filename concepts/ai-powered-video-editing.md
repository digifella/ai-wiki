# AI-Powered Video Editing

**AI-Powered Video Editing** refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] and [[concepts/large-language-models]] to automate, assist, or entirely replace traditional timeline-based video [[concepts/video-editing|post-production]] workflows. This [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] moves from manual clip manipulation to intent-driven editing via natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]].

## Core Concepts

- **[[concepts/prompt-driven-editing|Prompt-Driven Editing]]**: Users describe desired edits in natural language rather than manually adjusting keyframes or cutting clips.
- **Automation of Repetitive Tasks**: AI handles trimming, color correction, captioning, and asset organization.
- **Semantic Understanding**: Models interpret context, mood, and [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] structure to suggest or execute edits.

## Key Tools & Implementations

- **Video-Use**: A notable [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] implementation that integrates [[entities/claude-code]] to create a text-based, prompt-driven video editor.
	- Eliminates traditional timeline interfaces.
	- Free and 100% open-source.
	- See detailed analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Video-Use-AI-Powered-Text-Based-Prompt-Driven-Video-Edit|Video-Use: AI-Powered, Text-Based, Prompt-Driven Video Editor]]

## Workflow Implications

- **Reduced Barrier to Entry**: Non-experts can produce complex edits using descriptive text.
- **[[concepts/speed|Speed]]**: Drastic reduction in time spent on mechanical editing tasks.
- **[[concepts/iterative-learning|Iterative Refinement]]**: Edits can be adjusted through conversational [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]] with the AI model.
