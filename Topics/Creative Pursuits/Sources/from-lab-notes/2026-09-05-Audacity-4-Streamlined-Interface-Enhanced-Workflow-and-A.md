## Audacity 4: Streamlined Interface, Enhanced Workflow, and Advanced Audio Editing Features
**Clip title:** Introducing Audacity 4: New tools & a new look
**Author / channel:** Audacity
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BTQymidLYIM

### Summary
The video provides a comprehensive overview of Audacity Version 4, highlighting significant improvements aimed at making the popular [[concepts/audio-editing|audio editing]] software more powerful, user-friendly, and efficient. The core philosophy behind this update was to streamline the interface and enhance workflow, ensuring that Audacity remains accessible while introducing advanced capabilities. Users can now customize the interface with different themes like "Classic" or "Modern" to suit their preferences.

A major focus of Version 4 is the overhauled audio editing workflow. Clips can now overlap freely and editing is non-destructive, allowing users to easily trim and restore original audio. New features include multi-selecting clips with intuitive handles for quick trimming and stretching, and the ability to group clips for more organized and efficient complex projects. The clip envelope tool has been redesigned for easier volume shaping, and a new split tool enables quick segmentation of clips, even across multiple tracks simultaneously using a Shift-key shortcut. Sample-level editing is also streamlined, allowing direct manipulation of individual samples by simply zooming in.

Further enhancements improve the user experience and expand functionality. [[concepts/audacity-4|Audacity 4]] introduces more powerful metering, with each track now featuring its own meter to easily identify clipping, which also functions as an input monitor during recording. The main playback meter offers both horizontal and vertical views. "Ripple editing" shortcuts (Shift + Delete or Alt/Command + Delete) provide flexible ways to delete clips and automatically adjust subsequent audio, a significant improvement over previous versions. Effects have been redesigned for clarity and ease of use, and real-time support for VST3, Audio Unit, and LV2 plugins has been improved. The playhead and looping features are also modernized, with the loop region now sitting above the playhead for easier manipulation and the added benefit of exporting audio specifically within this loop. Labels have been made more intuitive, allowing users to add, name, resize, and multi-select them.

Finally, Audacity 4 introduces "Workspaces," a versatile feature enabling users to customize and save different UI layouts optimized for specific tasks such as music production, podcasting, or advanced audio editing. The video emphasizes that this extensive update was made possible by the dedicated Audacity core team, engineers from Muse Group Studio, and crucially, the global community through bug reports, translations, pull requests, and invaluable feedback. Version 4 lays a robust foundational framework for even faster future development and major additions, while steadfastly remaining free and open-source forever. Users can download or update to Audacity 4 via the official website or Muse Hub.
