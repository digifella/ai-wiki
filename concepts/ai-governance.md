[[concepts/ai-policy|AI Governance]] refers to the frameworks, [[concepts/policies|policies]], and [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] through which [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems are managed, controlled, and held accountable within organizational and knowledge system contexts. It encompasses the rules, standards, and oversight structures that guide how [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] operate, make decisions, and interact with human users and other systems. Effective [[concepts/ai-security|AI governance]] establishes clear boundaries for AI behavior, defines [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] chains, and ensures alignment between [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] outputs and organizational values or societal norms.

## Core Components

[[concepts/governing-framework|AI governance]] typically addresses several interconnected areas: technical safety and performance standards, [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] and explainability, and robust oversight mechanisms. Recent discourse challenges traditional oversight assumptions, particularly the efficacy of "Human in the [[concepts/loop|Loop]]" (HITL) models in [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic AI]] contexts.

### Critique of Human-in-the-Loop Oversight

Traditional HITL models are increasingly scrutinized for their adequacy in complex [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic systems]]. Key insights from recent analysis include:

*   **Superficial Oversight:** HITL often reduces human involvement to mere review of AI outputs without deep understanding of the underlying [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process, failing to provide genuine [[concepts/governance|governance]].
*   **[[concepts/trust|Trust]] Frameworks:** There is a shift toward more nuanced trust frameworks that account for the autonomy and opacity of modern [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], rather than relying on binary human intervention.
*   **Accountability Gaps:** When humans only review outputs, accountability for errors or [[concepts/biases|biases]] may be diluted, as the human reviewer lacks the context to fully validate the AI's [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].

For a detailed breakdown of these critiques and proposed alternatives, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Critiquing-Human-in-the-Loop-A-Trust-Framework-for-AI-Go|Critiquing \"Human in the Loop\": A Trust Framework for AI Governance]].
