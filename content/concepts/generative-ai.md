# Generative AI

Generative AI refers to [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] technologies that can produce new and original content autonomously, such as [[concepts/images|images]], text, audio, video, and more. This concept encompasses a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/software|applications]] from creative industries like [[concepts/design|design]], marketing, and entertainment to technical fields including [[concepts/software|software]] development and scientific research.

- **[[concepts/llms|Definition]]**: Generative models are trained on large [[concepts/training-data|datasets]] to learn patterns and structures that allow them to generate new data similar to the [[concepts/training|training]] set.
- **Applications**:
  - **[[concepts/text-generation|Text Generation]]**: Creating coherent sentences or paragraphs using AI (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt]])
  - **Image Creation**: Generating images from textual descriptions (e.g., DALL-E, Midjourney)
  - **Design**: [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] and prototyping
  - **Software [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Integration of Adobe Firefly AI into [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]]
- **Recent Ecosystem Updates**:
  - Google AI updates across [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] (specifically [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]]), [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]], [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], and [[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]].
