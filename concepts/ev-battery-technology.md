# EV battery technology

## Overview
[[concepts/battery-technology|Electrochemical energy storage]] systems designed for Electric vehicles, focusing on energy [[concepts/density|density]], cost, safety, and cycle life. Key chemistries include Lithium-ion, LFP, and emerging Lithium Manganese Rich (LMR) variants.

## Key Developments (2026)
- **LG LMR Breakthrough**: [[entities/lg-energy-solution|LG Energy Solution]] announced a significant advancement in Lithium Manganese Rich (LMR) technology, claiming performance that surpasses LFP batteries in range while maintaining lower costs.
  - [[lab-notes/2026-09-12-LG-LMR-Breakthrough-Cheaper-Longer-Range-EV-Batteries-Su|LG LMR Breakthrough: Cheaper, Longer-Range EV Batteries Surpassing LFP]]
  - Potential implications for major OEMs like [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] and General Motors.
  - Source: [LG LMR Breakthrough: Cheaper, Longer-Range EV Batteries Surpassing LFP](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ve5UitAyP0M)

## Major Chemistries
- **LFP ([[concepts/lfp-battery|Lithium Iron Phosphate]])**: High safety, long cycle life, lower energy density, lower cost.
- **NMC/NCA (Nickel Manganese Cobalt / Nickel Cobalt Aluminum)**: High energy density, higher cost, thermal management challenges.
- **LMR (Lithium Manganese Rich)**: Emerging [[concepts/hybrid-approach|hybrid approach]] aiming to combine high energy density with [[concepts/cost-efficiency|cost efficiency]].
