# compute crunch

A state of insufficiency where the demand for [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] (GPUs, [[entities/tpus|TPUs]], and cluster capacity) exceeds the available supply, preventing the [[concepts/scaling|scaling]] of [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) or the stable delivery of [[concepts/inference|inference]] services.

### Key Developments
- **[[entities/anthropic]] Demand Miscalculation (April 2026):**
    - Failure to accurately forecast [[entities/claude]] demand resulted in a localized [[concepts/compute|compute]] crunch.
    - The shortage caused significant public relations challenges for [[entities/anthropic]].
    - Competitors, notably [[entities/openai]], are actively leveraging this resource instability to gain strategic market advantage.
