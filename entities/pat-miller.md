# Pat Miller

Pat Miller is a photographer and content creator affiliated with the Professional Photographers of America (PPA). He specializes in digital photography workflows and the application of modern software tools to contemporary photographic practice.

## Professional Work

Miller has developed recognized expertise in Adobe's creative software suite, particularly Photoshop and Lightroom. His work focuses on demonstrating how photographers can integrate these tools into their professional practices, with particular emphasis on productivity features and artificial intelligence capabilities within these applications. He creates educational content aimed at helping photographers streamline their post-processing workflows and adopt new technologies in their practice.
