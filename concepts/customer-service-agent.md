# Customer Service Agent

A role or system providing assistance to customers through communication channels, resolving inquiries, and supporting product/service engagement.

## Key Concepts

- **AI-Powered Implementation**: Building custom [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] (e.g., PoochPal) using [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]] [[entities/copilot|Copilot]] without [[concepts/coding|coding]], as demonstrated in [[entities/teachers-tech|Teachers Tech]]'s [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]].
- **[[entities/gurru-tech-solutions|No-Code Development]]**: Leveraging [[entities/microsoft-365-copilot|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] to create internal customer service bots for businesses like "[[entities/pooch-haven-spa|Pooch Haven Spa]]" (fictional example).
- **Target Audience**: Organizations requiring scalable, automated customer support solutions with minimal technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]].
- **Core Function**: Handling routine inquiries (e.g., booking, FAQs), escalating complex issues, and maintaining service [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
