[[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] is a suite of cloud-based [[concepts/apps|apps]] and services designed to help teams collaborate more effectively, manage [[entities/email|email]] and video meetings, create documents and spreadsheets online, and much more. It includes applications like [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], Calendar, [[concepts/ambition|Drive]], [[entities/google-docs|Docs]], Sheets, [[entities/google-slides|Slides]], Meet, Chat, and Sites.

## Key Features
- **Collaboration:** Enables real-time editing of documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
- **[[entities/storage|Storage]] & Sync:** Provides cloud storage with [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/motivation|Drive]] for file management and sharing.
- **Communication:** Supports email communication through Gmail and video conferencing via [[entities/google-meet|Google Meet]].
- **[[concepts/security|Security]] & Management:** Offers advanced security features such as data loss [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] and device management.
