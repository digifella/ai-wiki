Your screenshot confirms a **frustrating limitation** in the ASIC Connect system:

* The **email address (username)** is _fixed_ and used as your login.
* There’s no field or function on the “myAccount” page to change your email address (username).

This is by [[concepts/design|design]]: **ASIC Connect uses the email address as the user ID**, and they don’t allow users to change it themselves.

* * *

## ASIC Official Position (and your options)

### 1\. **You cannot change your ASIC Connect username (email) yourself**

> “Your ASIC Connect username is the email address you registered with. If you need to change your email address, you must create a new ASIC Connect account with the new email address.” (ASIC Connect FAQ)

**You must:**

* **Register a new ASIC Connect account** using your new email address.
* **Link your business(es) or company(ies) to the new account** using your ASIC key.

### 2\. **What about old lodgements and records?**

* All business names and company registrations are attached to the _account_ (email address) they were linked with.
* Once you create a new ASIC Connect account, you must “link” each entity using the **ASIC key** for that entity.
* You can request the ASIC key if you don’t have it (here).
