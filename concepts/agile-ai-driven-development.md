# Agile AI Driven Development

Agile AI Driven Development is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] methodology that integrates [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and assistants into established agile practices rather than replacing them. The approach treats [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] as enhancements to existing workflows—such as sprint planning, code review, testing, and documentation—rather than as standalone solutions. This framework emphasizes that successful [[concepts/development-speed|AI-assisted development]] depends on deliberate process design and organizational discipline, not merely adopting AI technology.

## Core Principles

The methodology maintains traditional agile values while leveraging AI capabilities at specific points in the [[concepts/software-sprint|development cycle]]. [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] may assist with [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], [[concepts/automated-software-testing|automated testing]], documentation drafting, and requirement analysis, but human developers retain [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] authority and responsibility for [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]]. The framework recognizes that [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] work best when integrated into clearly defined workflows with appropriate oversight [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].

## Process Integration

Implementation requires explicit [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to how AI agents fit into sprint cycles, team communication, and [[concepts/quality-gates|quality gates]]. Rather than treating AI as a replacement for human [[concepts/expertise|expertise]], organizations using this approach define which tasks benefit from AI assistance and establish [[concepts/verification|verification]] processes to validate AI-generated outputs. This includes determining when AI [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]] should be accepted, modified, or rejected based on project context and team judgment.

## Practical Outcomes

Organizations adopting Agile AI Driven Development aim to increase development velocity while maintaining code quality and team [[concepts/accountability|accountability]]. [[concepts/success|Success]] depends on training teams to effectively collaborate with AI tools, establishing clear handoff points between automated and human work, and continuously evaluating whether [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] delivers expected improvements to development processes.
