# Step Audio

Step [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]] is an AI [[concepts/audio|audio]] synthesis model developed by Stepfun, capable of generating high-fidelity audio from text prompts. It specializes in creating realistic soundscapes, music, and [[concepts/tone|voice]] effects with precise control over [[concepts/timbre|timbre]] and spatial properties.
