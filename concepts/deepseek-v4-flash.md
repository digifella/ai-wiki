# DeepSeek V4 Flash

**[[entities/deepseek-ai|DeepSeek]] V4 Flash** is a foundational [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[concepts/deepseek-ai|DeepSeek]]. It serves as the baseline architecture for subsequent iterations in the V4 series, including the [[concepts/deepseek-v4-pro]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Architecture:** [[concepts/open-weight|Open-weight]] design, allowing for public inspection and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]].
- **Performance:** Established a new benchmark for efficiency and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capabilities in its class.
- **Market Impact:** Pioneered a cost-effective alternative to closed-source competitors, influencing industry [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] strategies.

## Evolution and Successors
The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[entities/whale-wokeup|DeepSeek V4 Flash]] set the stage for more advanced variants. The most notable successor is [[concepts/deepseek-v4-pro]], which builds upon this foundation with significant performance improvements.

- **DeepSeek V4 Pro:** An [[concepts/open-weight-model|open-weight model]] that outperforms both its predecessor and competitors like [[concepts/gemini-37-flash|Gemini 3.7 Flash]] and [[entities/muse-spark|Muse Spark]] 1.
- **Comparison:** V4 Pro addresses limitations in V4 Flash, offering superior reasoning and [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]] while maintaining [[concepts/competitive-pricing|competitive pricing]] against price hikes from other providers.
