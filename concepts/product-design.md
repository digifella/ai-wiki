# Product design

The process of creating products that solve user problems through functional, desirable, and manufacturable solutions. Key principles include user-centered [[concepts/iteration|iteration]], cost-quality balance, and cross-functional collaboration.

## Case study: American manufacturing viability

- [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]: Grill brush designed for safety/durability (replacing wire bristle brushes), manufactured entirely in [[entities/america|America]]
- [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] host [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] conducted a 4-year [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] to prove competitive U.S. manufacturing
- [[concepts/motivation|Motivation]]: Personal history with [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]] (parents were [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]])
- Documented in [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] video: [Manufacturing in America Experiment](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ZTGwcHQfLY)
- Key insight: Demonstrated that [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic manufacturing]] can compete on quality and cost when prioritizing design efficiency
