## Hermes Agent & Obsidian: AI System for Dynamic Knowledge Management
**Clip title:** [[concepts/autonomous-workflow-automation|Hermes Agent]] + [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]] = The Ultimate [[concepts/personal-knowledge-management-pkm|Second Brain]]
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** [[entities/david|David]] Ondrej
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Q0HTefP9DFU

### Summary
This video presents a comprehensive guide to building what the presenter calls the "best [[concepts/ai-assisted-organization|second brain setup]] possible in 2026," integrating [[concepts/agentic-ai|Hermes Agent]] with Obsidian, deployed on a Virtual Private Server (VPS). The core concept revolves around transforming stagnant [[concepts/digital-information|digital information]], labeled as "dead files" (like those in [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Docs or [[concepts/local-storage|local storage]]), into "[[concepts/living-files|living files]]" that [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can actively read, write, reference, and use as dynamic context. This integration aims to overcome the limitations of traditional [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] setups, which often lack [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], [[concepts/customization|customizability]], and robust knowledge compounding.

The combination of Hermes Agent and Obsidian offers several key benefits. Firstly, it provides unparalleled visibility and control for the user, allowing them to visually observe the AI's actions and knowledge through Obsidian's graph view and organized [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] files. Customization of [[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI skills]] and behaviors becomes intuitive, as changes can be made directly within Obsidian [[concepts/notes|notes]] without complex [[concepts/coding|coding]] or [[concepts/cli|terminal]] interactions, with [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] reflected instantly. Moreover, this setup facilitates "knowledge compounding," meaning every piece of information stored in Obsidian becomes a searchable and actionable resource for the AI, eliminating the need for repetitive explanations. The system also supports multi-device synchronization across laptops, phones, and the VPS, ensuring [[concepts/data-persistence|data persistence]] and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], even if a physical device is lost or damaged.

From a technical standpoint, the video demonstrates setting up Hermes Agent on a Virtual Private Server (VPS), with a strong recommendation for Hostinger due to its ease of deployment. The presenter shows how to establish SSH access to the VPS using `cmux`, a [[entities/tmux|terminal multiplexer]] that enhances workflow by allowing simultaneous views and interactions. A crucial aspect of Hermes Agent's functionality is its intelligent [[concepts/memory|memory]] system, which maintains a small, persistent "soul.md" file for core identity and preferences, while dynamically pulling in other notes and skills on-demand to optimize [[concepts/context-window|context window]] usage and [[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|cost efficiency]]. This allows the AI to manage server-side tasks, installations, and configurations through natural language [[concepts/commands|commands]], significantly reducing the user's need for deep technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] in areas like DevOps.

The demonstration culminates in the AI agent autonomously managing its own setup, connecting to the Obsidian vault, and even performing [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] like [[concepts/scraping|scraping]] YouTube for video transcripts and saving them as markdown files within Obsidian. The overarching takeaway is that this integrated Hermes Agent and Obsidian setup creates a powerful "agentic second brain" that provides a significant [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] cheat code for 2026. By offloading tedious organizational and technical work to an AI agent, users are empowered to focus on generating [[concepts/ideas|ideas]], [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical thinking]], and other high-value, creative tasks, thereby gaining a substantial advantage in the evolving landscape of [[concepts/ai-driven-productivity|AI-driven productivity]].
