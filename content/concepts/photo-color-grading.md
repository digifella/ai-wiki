# Photo Color Grading

Photo color grading is the process of adjusting the colors, tones, and overall color cast of a photograph to achieve a specific aesthetic or mood. This technique is commonly performed in post-processing [[concepts/software|software]] such as [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], where photographers manipulate individual color channels, saturation levels, and tonal ranges to enhance or transform the original image. [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color grading]] extends beyond simple color correction—it is a creative choice that shapes how viewers perceive and respond to a photograph.

## Dark and Moody Aesthetics

One popular application of color grading is creating [[concepts/dark-and-moody-photo-processing|dark and moody]] visual styles characterized by reduced brightness, increased [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], and cooler or desaturated color palettes. This approach is achieved through tools like the [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration Panel]] in Lightroom, which allows adjustment of foundational colors and specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] such as [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]]. By strategically darkening [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], lifting or crushing [[concepts/blacks|blacks]], and shifting [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]], photographers can create dramatic visual impact and evoke emotional [[concepts/responses|responses]] in their work.

## Tools and Software

[[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]'s [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] include the [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration Panel]] for [[concepts/foundational-color|foundational color]] work, curves [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] for tonal [[concepts/power|control]], and HSL ([[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, Luminance) sliders for targeted color manipulation. [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] offers additional advanced options through the [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]] and [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] like [[concepts/neural-filters|Neural Filters]] and [[concepts/depth-maps|Depth Maps]], which enable more precise adjustments and stylistic transformations such as converting landscapes to sunset photography or applying cinematic effects. These tools provide photographers with varying levels of [[concepts/power|control]] depending on their [[concepts/skill|skill]] level and creative intent.
