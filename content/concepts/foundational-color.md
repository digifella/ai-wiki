# Foundational Color

Foundational Color is a [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] technique used in Adobe Lightroom that establishes a baseline color profile for an image before applying additional [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. It works through Lightroom's [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration panel]], where users can set color [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] that serve as the foundation for all subsequent edits. This approach helps ensure [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] and improves overall color quality by establishing neutral, accurate color rendering from the start.

The technique is particularly useful when working with specific subjects like [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], where color [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] is critical. By establishing a foundational color profile tailored to the content being edited, photographers can achieve more natural and pleasing results in their final [[concepts/images|images]]. The method integrates with Lightroom's non-destructive [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]], allowing adjustments to be modified or refined without affecting the original image data.

Foundational Color operates as part of Adobe's broader color and [[concepts/image-quality-enhancement|image quality enhancement]] tools, working alongside features in both Lightroom and Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]]. The approach emphasizes establishing correct color [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] early in the editing process rather than attempting to correct color issues through individual slider adjustments alone.
