# Jebel Ali Power and Desalination Complex

## Overview
The Jebel Ali [[concepts/power|Power]] and [[concepts/desalination|Desalination]] Complex is one of the largest integrated facilities in the world, combining power generation with desalination to meet the energy and water demands of Dubai and the broader [[entities/uae|UAE]]. It plays a pivotal role in ensuring a steady supply of clean water for domestic use and industrial needs.

## Key Features
- **Desalination:** Utilizes [[concepts/msf-technology|Multi-Stage Flash]] ([[concepts/multi-stage-flash-msf-technology|MSF]]) technology for high efficiency.
- **Power Generation:** Produces substantial electricity to support its operations and contribute to the national grid.
- **Infrastructure:** Extensive pipelines and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] networks connected to major cities in Dubai.
