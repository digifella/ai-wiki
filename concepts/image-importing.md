# Image importing

The process of bringing external visual assets into a digital workspace, such as [[entities/adobe-photoshop]], to facilitate editing or [[concepts/digital-compositing|Compositing]].

## Workflow Integrations
- **[[concepts/lightroom]] Integration**: Utilize the "Edit" command by right-clicking selected images in [[concepts/lightroom]] to automate the transfer of assets into the active [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] environment.
- **High-Fidelity Setup**: For advanced Compositing workflows, assets should be imported into documents configured for maximum data [[concepts/storing|retention]]:
    - **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
    - **Color Space**: [[concepts/adobe-rgb]]
    - **[[concepts/solution|Resolution]]**: 300 ppi
