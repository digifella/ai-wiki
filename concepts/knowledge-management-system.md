# Knowledge Management System

A **[[concepts/knowledge-management|Knowledge Management]] System (KMS)** is a [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]] for capturing, organizing, [[concepts/storing|storing]], and [[concepts/retrieving|retrieving]] information to facilitate individual or organizational [[concepts/learning|learning]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. In the context of personal [[concepts/productivity|productivity]], it often manifests as a [[concepts/note-management|Personal Knowledge Base]] or digital garden, leveraging tools like [[entities/obsidian]], [[entities/notion]], or Roam Research.

## Core Principles
- **Atomicity**: Breaking down information into small, self-contained units ([[concepts/notes|notes]]) to maximize reusability.
- **Linking**: Creating bidirectional connections between concepts to form a networked thought structure rather than a rigid [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]].
- **[[concepts/document-retrieval|Retrieval]]**: Prioritizing ease of access through tagging, searching, and graph visualization.

## Modern Implementations & AI Integration
Recent developments emphasize privacy-preserving, local-first architectures augmented by [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automate organization and synthesis.

- **Local AI-Powered Note Management**: Emerging workflows integrate [[entities/obsidian]] with [[concepts/desktop-based-llms|local Large Language Models]] (LLMs) via runtimes like [[entities/ollama]]. This setup allows for private, hands-free note processing without cloud dependency.
  - Specific implementations include the use of agents such as [[concepts/agentic-ai|Hermes Agent]] to bridge the gap between raw notes and AI [[concepts/inference|inference]] capabilities.
