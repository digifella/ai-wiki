# AI-driven scientific discovery

**AI-driven [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]]** refers to the [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in research methodologies where [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems autonomously or semi-autonomously generate hypotheses, perform calculations, and validate theories. This approach contrasts with traditional methods by [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] the rate of [[concepts/insight-generation|insight generation]].

## Key Developments

*   **Paradigm Shift:** Transition from "[[concepts/artisanal-intelligence|artisanal intelligence]]" (manual, slow, human-centric) to "[[concepts/factory-intelligence|factory intelligence]]" (automated, high-throughput, scalable) [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-OpenAI-AI-Redefines-Scientific-Discovery-Mathematical-Br|OpenAI AI Redefines Scientific Discovery: Mathematical Breakthroughs and Factory Intelligence]].
*   **[[concepts/mathematical-breakthroughs|Mathematical Breakthroughs]]:** Recent advancements by [[entities/openai]] have demonstrated significant capabilities in solving complex [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]], signaling a new era of computational rigor in discovery.
*   **Factory Intelligence:** The integration of AI into scientific workflows allows for the systematic processing of vast datasets and the rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of experimental designs, effectively industrializing the discovery process.
