# Applied Ai Team

The Applied AI Team is a specialized group within Anthropic dedicated to helping developers and organizations effectively implement Claude across real-world applications. The team serves as a bridge between Claude's technical capabilities and practical deployment scenarios by producing documentation, guides, and educational materials tailored to different use cases and industries.

## Research and Resources

The team conducts applied research to identify best practices for integrating large language models into software development workflows. This work includes the development of tools and frameworks that streamline the adoption of AI agents, with a particular focus on optimizing the use of Claude Code. By analyzing real-world implementation challenges, the group provides actionable insights that help users maximize efficiency and reliability in their AI-driven projects.
