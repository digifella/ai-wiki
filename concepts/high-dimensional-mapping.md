# High-Dimensional Mapping

## Definition
A computational technique that projects data into a space with more dimensions than the original dataset, enabling the separation of complex patterns that are non-linearly separable in lower-dimensional spaces.

## Key Applications
- **Genomics & Pathogen [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|Prediction]]**:
  - Addresses limitations of traditional Phylogenetic Tree approaches, which fail to account for mutations shared across different evolutionary branches.
  - [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] map genomic data into high-dimensional spaces to create accurate mutational "fingerprints," improving the prediction of future pathogen strains for timely [[concepts/vaccine-production|vaccine production]].
