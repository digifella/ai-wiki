# HSL (Hue)

Hue represents the fundamental color identity or "color" itself, defined by its position (angle) on a color wheel.

## The HSL Model
The HSL model is defined by three primary dimensions:
- Saturation: The intensity, purity, or vividness of a color.
- Luminance: The brightness or [[concepts/light|light]] intensity of a color.

## Implementation in Adobe Lightroom Classic
Using the **[[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]]** panel, users can perform targeted color [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]:
- Allows for independent manipulation of [[concepts/hue]], Saturation, and Luminance for specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]].
- Enables precise control over color shifts without affecting the entire image spectrum.
