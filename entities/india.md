# India

**India**, officially the **Republic of India** (*Bhārat*), is a country in South Asia. It is the seventh-largest country by area, the second-most populous country, and the most populous democracy in the [[entities/earth|world]]. Bounded by the **Indian Ocean** on the south, the **Arabian Sea** on the south-west, and the **Bay of Bengal** on the south-east, it shares land borders with **Pakistan** to the west; **Nepal**, **Bhutan** and **[[entities/china|China]]** to the north; and **Myanmar** and **Bangladesh** to the east. In the Indian Ocean, India is in proximity to **[[entities/sri-lanka|Sri Lanka]]**, **Maldives**, **Indonesia**, and **Thailand**.
