# Conversational AI

[[concepts/ai-chatbots|Conversational AI]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems designed to engage in natural language [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] with users. These systems process user input, understand intent and context, and generate relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]] in a conversational manner. Conversational AI powers various [[concepts/software|applications]] including chatbots, [[concepts/voice-assistants|virtual assistants]], and web-based interfaces that enable human-computer interaction through [[concepts/text|text]] or [[concepts/tone|voice]].

## Google Gemini Web App vs. Google AI Studio

[[concepts/google-search|Google]] offers two distinct platforms for accessing its conversational [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]], each serving different user needs. The [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] web app provides a general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] interface for casual users and typical conversational tasks, with a straightforward interface optimized for ease of use. [[entities/ai-studio|Google AI Studio]], by [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], is a specialized platform designed for [[concepts/power-users|power users]], developers, and professionals who require deeper [[concepts/customization|customization]] and [[concepts/power|control]] over AI interactions.

Google AI Studio distinguishes itself through advanced features including [[concepts/system-prompt|system prompt]] customization, multi-turn conversation management, and flexible parameter tuning that allow professionals to tailor AI behavior to specific requirements. These capabilities [[entities/make|make]] AI Studio particularly suitable for building specialized applications, conducting complex research, and implementing custom workflows that the standard [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] interface cannot accommodate. For users primarily seeking straightforward conversational interactions, the Gemini web app remains the more accessible option.
