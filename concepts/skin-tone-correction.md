# Skin Tone Correction

**Skin Tone Correction** refers to the technical process of adjusting color balance, saturation, and luminance to achieve natural, consistent, and aesthetically pleasing skin representation in digital imagery. It is a critical component of Portrait Photography, Color Grading, and Photo Retouching.

## Core Principles
- **Naturalism vs. Stylization**: Balancing anatomical accuracy with artistic intent.
- **Uniformity**: Ensuring consistent tone across the subject.
- **Tooling**: Utilizing VectorScope, [[entities/camera-raw]], and [[concepts/ai-masking]] for precision.
