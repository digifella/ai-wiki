# Yann LeCun

**Yann LeCun** is a French-American [[concepts/phil-moriarty|computer scientist]] known for his pioneering work in [[concepts/vanishing-gradient-problem|Deep Learning]], particularly in the development of Convolutional [[concepts/neural-networks|Neural Networks]] (CNNs).
