# Rakime Elmir

Rakime Elmir is a researcher and scholar specializing in the study of paternal experiences during childbirth, with a particular focus on cases involving traumatic or difficult labour and delivery. Her work examines the psychological and emotional impacts on fathers who are present during these critical perinatal moments, contributing to a deeper understanding of how men experience and are affected by childbirth events.

## Research Focus

Elmir's research centers on fathers' perspectives within perinatal studies, an area that has historically received less attention than maternal experiences. By investigating the context of men attending traumatic births, her work aims to enhance service delivery for fathers. This approach seeks to address the specific needs of male partners during high-stress perinatal events, thereby improving the overall support system for families navigating difficult childbirth experiences.
