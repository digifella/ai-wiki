# NASA
## Overview
NASA ([[concepts/nasa|National Aeronautics and Space Administration]]) is an independent agency of the [[entities/united-states|United States]] Federal Government responsible for the civilian space program as well as aeronautics and aerospace research.

### Major Projects
- **[[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis Program]]**: [[concepts/human-spaceflight|Human exploration]] of the Moon, with plans to establish sustainable [[concepts/lunar-return|lunar missions]].
- **[[entities/apollo-program|Apollo Program]]**: Historic [[concepts/lunar-landing|lunar landing]] missions featuring significant [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] achievements such as the [[concepts/lunar-roving-vehicle|Lunar Roving Vehicle]] (LRV).
- **[[concepts/gemini-program|Project Gemini]]**: The crucial intermediary program that bridged the technological gap between the initial [[concepts/mercury-program|Mercury program]] and the ambitious Apollo goals.
- **[[concepts/dragonfly-mission|Dragonfly]]**: A rotorcraft lander mission to Saturn's moon [[concepts/oceangate-titan|Titan]], focusing on prebiotic chemistry and [[concepts/habitability|habitability]].
- **[[concepts/hera-mission|Hera]]**: An ESA-led mission with NASA contributions to study the aftermath of the DART impact on the asteroid Dimorphos.

### Recent Discoveries & Updates
- Analysis of celestial phenomena including [[concepts/theta-eridani|Theta Eridani]], noting historical astronomical alignments.
- Integration of data from recent [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep-space]] probes to refine models of [[concepts/asteroid|asteroid]] dynamics and [[concepts/exoplanet|exoplanet]] atmospheres.
