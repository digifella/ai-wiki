# AI-native communication

**AI-native communication** refers to interaction paradigms where [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] is not merely a tool but the foundational layer of data exchange, [[concepts/context-management|context management]], and semantic understanding. This concept is increasingly supported by the democratization of powerful [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], particularly through [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] initiatives that enable [[concepts/local-processing|local processing]] and agent-based workflows.

## Key Drivers

*   **[[concepts/democratization-of-ai|Democratization of AI]] Tools:** The shift towards accessible, local-first AI [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] allows for private, low-latency, and customizable communication models AI-native communication.
*   **[[concepts/local-ai-model|Local LLM]] Management:** Running [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] locally enables [[concepts/secure|secure]], context-aware communication without reliance on [[concepts/third-party-apis|external APIs]], fostering [[concepts/trust|trust]] and [[concepts/privacy|data sovereignty]].
*   **[[concepts/ai-agents|AI Agents]]:** [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] facilitate dynamic, multi-step communication flows, bridging the gap between [[concepts/dead-files|static data]] and interactive semantic understanding.
