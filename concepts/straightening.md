# Straightening

Straightening is a post-processing technique in Lightroom Classic designed to correct the alignment of landscape photographs. It addresses common compositional issues such as tilted horizons or skewed framing that frequently occur during image capture. By restoring horizontal and vertical lines to their proper orientation, this process ensures that the final image reflects the intended perspective and stability of the original scene.

The workflow leverages Lightroom's landscape masking feature to automate horizon detection and correction. Instead of relying on manual rotation tools or grid overlays, the software analyzes the image data to identify the boundary between the sky and the land. This automated detection allows for precise adjustments that maintain the integrity of the image while correcting geometric distortions.

Once the horizon is identified, the tool applies a rotation and crop to level the image. This method is particularly effective for complex landscapes where manual alignment might be difficult due to irregular terrain or obstructions. The result is a balanced composition that enhances the visual impact of the photograph without introducing artificial artifacts or significant loss of resolution.
