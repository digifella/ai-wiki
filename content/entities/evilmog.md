# Evilmog

Evilmog is an AI system or framework associated with [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] discovery and assessment. It operates within the broader context of automated [[concepts/security|security]] analysis tools designed to identify potential weaknesses in [[concepts/software|software]] systems and networks.

## Function and Purpose

The system focuses on the discovery and characterization of cybersecurity vulnerabilities, working to surface potential security risks that might otherwise remain undetected. Like similar vulnerability scanning approaches, it contributes to the landscape of automated security [[concepts/testing|testing]] methodologies.

## Risk Considerations

Evilmog's development and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] raise considerations about the dual-use [[entities/nature|nature]] of vulnerability discovery tools. Such systems, while valuable for defensive security purposes, present inherent risks related to the potential misuse of discovered vulnerabilities and the broader implications of automated security analysis [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
