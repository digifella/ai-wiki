# Price-to-range ratio

A valuation metric used to assess the economic utility of an Electric Vehicle (EV) by comparing the purchase price to the vehicle's maximum driving distance on a single charge.

## Analysis & Application
- Serves as a critical metric for identifying "worst EVs" that offer poor performance-to-value correlation.
- High ratios indicate significant value deficits, even in instances where vehicles are heavily discounted.
- Key indicator used to distinguish high-utility Electric Vehicle investments from low-value assets.
