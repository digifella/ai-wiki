# Critical Infrastructure Resilience
Critical infrastructure [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] involves designing systems and networks to withstand and recover from disruptions caused by [[concepts/severe-weather-events|natural disasters]], such as [[concepts/earthquakes|earthquakes]]. This includes both physical assets like buildings and transportation systems, as well as technological infrastructure like [[concepts/power|power]] [[concepts/grids|grids]] and communication networks.

## Earthquake Base Isolation Systems: Functionality and Critical Infrastructure Resilience
- The video How [[concepts/earthquake-base-isolation|Earthquake Bearings]] Work from Practical Engineering, presented by [[entities/grady|Grady]], delves into the critical role of earthquake-resistant [[concepts/design|design]], particularly focusing on [[concepts/base-isolation-systems|base isolation systems]].
- It starts with an overview of the [[concepts/1994-northridge-earthquake|1994 Northridge earthquake]] in [[entities/los-angeles|Los Angeles]], which resulted in significant damage and loss.
- Base isolation technology is discussed as a key strategy to enhance resilience against [[concepts/earthquakes|seismic events]].
