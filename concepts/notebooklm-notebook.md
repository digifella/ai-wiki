## Creating an Online Course from NotebookLM

Turn a populated [[entities/notebooklm]] notebook into an interactive [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Classroom course:

- **Prerequisites**:
  - Fully populated [[entities/notebooklm]] notebook (sources + generated assets)
  - Google account
- **Setup**:
  1. Go to [classroom.google.com](https://classroom.google.com)
  2. Click "Create Class"
