# Before & After view

A visual comparison mechanism used in [[concepts/digital-image-processing|digital image processing]] to evaluate the impact of [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] made to an original file.

### Key Functions in Adobe Lightroom Classic
- **[[concepts/non-destructive-editing]]**: Used within the [[concepts/develop-module]] to assess the efficacy of [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] without altering the original raw data.
- **[[concepts/integration|Integration]] with [[concepts/history-panel]]**: Works in tandem with the history log to track, compare, and revert specific steps in the editing [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].
- **Visual [[concepts/verification|Verification]]**: Essential for auditing changes in [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, and detail against the unedited state.
