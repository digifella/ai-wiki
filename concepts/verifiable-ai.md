# Verifiable AI

Verifiable AI refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems whose outputs, decisions, and behaviors can be objectively validated against specified constraints, safety requirements, or ethical guidelines through formal-methods, audits, or [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].

## IBM AI Trend Predictions (2026)

Based on IBM's forecast:

- **[[concepts/ai-orchestration|Multi-agent Orchestration]]**: Collaborative agent teams (Planner, Worker, Critic) coordinated by an "Orchestrator" layer for complex [[concepts/task-decomposition|task decomposition]].
- **[[concepts/digital-labor-workforce|Digital Labor Workforce]]**: [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] executing workflows with human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] oversight, integrating with enterprise systems.
- **[[concepts/physical-ai|Physical AI]]**: [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] applications using "[[concepts/world-foundation-models|World Foundation Models]]" to interact with physical environments.
