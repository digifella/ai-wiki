# Evidence-Based Practice & Positive Psychology

[[concepts/evidence-based-decision-making|Evidence-based practice]], particularly within the lens of [[concepts/psychology|positive psychology]], emphasizes the study of human strengths, virtues, and optimal functioning rather than focusing primarily on [[concepts/depression|mental illness]] and psychological disorders. Emerging as a distinct field in the late 1990s, it represents a significant shift in psychological research and practice toward understanding what enables people to flourish and live meaningful lives. Rather than asking "what is wrong?", this approach asks "what is right?" and "how can we build on existing strengths?"

## Core Focus Areas

The field examines constructs such as [[concepts/happiness|happiness]], [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], character strengths, meaningful [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and [[concepts/quality-of-life|life satisfaction]]. Practitioners and researchers investigate factors that contribute to psychological [[concepts/health|wellbeing]], including [[concepts/motivation|motivation]] and purpose.

## Digital Health and Methodological Rigor

In the context of [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] and [[concepts/health-information-technology|health informatics]], evidence-based practice requires rigorous evaluation frameworks for [[concepts/apps|mobile applications]] and eHealth interventions. Recent research highlights the necessity of structured methodologies to determine the [[concepts/success|success]] and sustainability of digital tools in clinical and pediatric settings.

Key insights from contemporary literature include:

*   **Structured Evaluation Frameworks**: Successful digital health interventions require standardized assessment criteria beyond simple user engagement, focusing on [[concepts/efficacy-evaluation|clinical efficacy]] and [[concepts/integration|system integration]] [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Hourani---What-Makes-for-a-Successful-Digital|Hourani - What Makes for a Successful Digital]].
*   **[[concepts/interdisciplinary-collaboration|Interdisciplinary Collaboration]]**: Effective development involves collaboration between [[concepts/clinical-informatics|health informatics]] specialists, clinicians, and administrators to align digital tools with organizational goals and [[concepts/patient-needs|patient needs]].
*   **Pediatric and Systemic Impact**: Research indicates specific challenges in deploying eHealth solutions for [[concepts/pediatric-care|pediatrics]], requiring tailored approaches to ensure safety, efficacy, and adherence within broader [[concepts/health-system|health system]] administration.
