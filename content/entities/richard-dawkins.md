# Richard Dawkins

Richard Dawkins is a British ethologist, evolutionary biologist and author who holds the Charles Simonyi Chair for the Public Understanding of Science at Oxford University. He is renowned for his contributions to popularizing evolution and criticizing religious beliefs.

## Notable Works

- *The God Delusion*
