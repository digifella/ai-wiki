# Structureux

Structureux is a systematic process for evaluating [[concepts/responses|responses]], outputs, or creative work through [[concepts/diagnostic-audit|rigorous critique]]. The method focuses on identifying inaccuracies, logical weaknesses, unclear [[concepts/explanations|explanations]], incomplete coverage, hidden [[concepts/biases|biases]], and practical limitations. Rather than accepting work [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] face value, Structureux treats critique as an essential refinement step that improves quality and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].

## Core Approach

The process involves reviewing a completed response or deliverable against multiple evaluation criteria. Practitioners examine [[concepts/factual-accuracy|factual accuracy]], the strength of supporting evidence, clarity of communication, completeness relative to the scope, potential biases in framing, and feasibility of any recommendations. This structured examination reveals gaps and problems that may not be immediately apparent.

## Output and Iteration

After identifying issues through critique, Structureux proceeds to propose concrete alternatives and improvements. The outcome is typically a corrected or enhanced version that addresses the weaknesses found during analysis. This iterative approach transforms critique from a passive assessment into an active tool for improvement, making it particularly valuable in creative, analytical, and technical domains where precision and thoroughness matter.
