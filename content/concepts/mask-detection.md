# Mask Detection

Mask Detection is an automated feature in Lightroom Classic that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to generate selection masks with a single click. Rather than manually drawing or painting selections, photographers can apply pre-configured AI mask presets that automatically identify and isolate specific elements within an image, streamlining the [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]].

## Landscape and People Photography

The feature includes specialized presets tailored to common photography subjects. Landscape presets can automatically detect and mask elements like skies, water, or foreground areas, while people-focused presets identify faces, [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], or entire figures. This allows photographers to apply targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to these regions without requiring precise manual selection work.

## Practical Application

By condensing what traditionally requires multiple steps into a single action, Mask Detection accelerates the editing process. Photographers can quickly apply consistent adjustments across similar image regions and then refine the masks further if needed. The presets serve as starting points that reduce repetitive [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] tasks, particularly useful when processing large batches of similar [[concepts/images|images]].
