# Breakfast Sandwich

A breakfast sandwich is a prepared food item consisting of fillings placed between slices of bread, typically served as a morning meal. The sandwich format provides convenient assembly and portability, making it a practical choice for breakfast across many cultures. Common fillings include proteins such as eggs, bacon, sausage, or ham, frequently combined with cheese and vegetables like tomatoes, lettuce, or onions.

The bread base may range from standard sliced bread to English muffins, bagels, croissants, or flatbreads depending on regional preferences and availability. In the context of Korean street food, this concept often relates to the preparation and assembly of Korean street toast, which adapts the traditional sandwich structure to local tastes and ingredients. This variation emphasizes specific textural contrasts and flavor profiles distinct from Western counterparts.

Regional variations reflect local culinary traditions and ingredient accessibility. While some cultures prioritize sweet elements like jam or honey, others focus on savory combinations involving cured meats and aged cheeses. The structural integrity of the bread is crucial to prevent sogginess from hot fillings, often leading to toasting or grilling techniques that enhance both flavor and durability during transport.
