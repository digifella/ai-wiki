# Green Salad Construction

## Core Principles
Green salad construction is not merely mixing ingredients; it is a structural assembly of textures and flavors designed to maximize surface area for dressing adherence and balance acidity, salt, and fat. Key components include the Base Greens, Aromatics, Cheese, Croutons, and the Dressing.

## Technique & Assembly
Proper construction ensures greens remain crisp and evenly coated without wilting prematurely.

- **[[concepts/preparation|Preparation]]**: Wash greens thoroughly and dry completely. Moisture dilutes dressing and [[concepts/causes|causes]] sogginess. Use a Salad Spinner for efficiency.
- **Dressing Integration**: Apply dressing to leaves in a large bowl using a [[concepts/light|light]] tossing motion. Avoid pouring dressing directly on top of a plated salad, which leads to uneven distribution and soggy leaves.
- **Layering**: Add delicate ingredients (e.g., microgreens, fragile herbs) last to prevent bruising.
- **Acid Balance**: Fresh greens require high acidity. If greens are bitter (e.g., arugula, radicchio), balance with sweeter elements or neutral oils.
