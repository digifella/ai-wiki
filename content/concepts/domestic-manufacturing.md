# Domestic Manufacturing

Domestic Manufacturing is a four-year experimental investigation conducted by [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]], host of the YouTube channel [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]], examining manufacturing practices and production capabilities within the [[entities/united-states|United States]]. The project began in 2026 and documents Sandlin's exploration of how goods are manufactured domestically, what challenges exist in [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]], and how manufacturing practices have evolved.

The experiment takes a systematic approach to understanding contemporary manufacturing in [[entities/america|America]], moving beyond theoretical discussion to observe real production processes and industrial practices firsthand. Through this extended study, Sandlin documents various aspects of domestic manufacturing, from industrial facilities to supply chain considerations, creating educational content that explores the practical realities of producing goods in the United States.
