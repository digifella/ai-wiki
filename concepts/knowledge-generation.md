# Knowledge Generation

## Definition
The process by which new insights, theorems, and [[concepts/scientific-theories|scientific models]] are produced, increasingly driven by [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] rather than solely [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]].

## Evolution of Paradigms
*   **From Artisanal to [[concepts/factory-intelligence|Factory Intelligence]]:** A shift from individual, slow-paced human discovery ("artisanal") to scalable, automated production of knowledge ("factory intelligence") [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-OpenAI-AI-Redefines-Scientific-Discovery-Mathematical-Br|OpenAI AI Redefines Scientific Discovery: Mathematical Breakthroughs and Factory Intelligence]].
*   **[[concepts/mathematical-breakthroughs|Mathematical Breakthroughs]]:** Recent advancements by [[entities/openai]] indicate AI's capability to solve complex [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]] previously thought to require human intuition.
*   **Redefining Discovery:** AI is no longer just a tool for analysis but an active agent in generating novel scientific hypotheses and proofs.

## Key Drivers
*   Advanced [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) with [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].
*   Integration of [[concepts/symbolic-logic|symbolic reasoning]] with [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]].
*   Automated [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|theorem proving]] and [[concepts/verification|verification]] systems.
