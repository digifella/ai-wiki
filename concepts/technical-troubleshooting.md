# Technical Troubleshooting

Technical troubleshooting is the systematic process of identifying, analyzing, and resolving problems within technical systems, software, or hardware. It involves [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|diagnostic reasoning]], [[concepts/experimental-validation|hypothesis testing]], and iterative [[concepts/verification|verification]] to restore functionality.

## Core Methodology
- **[[concepts/issue-identification|Problem Identification]]**: Define the scope, symptoms, and impact of the issue.
- **Information Gathering**: Collect logs, error codes, user reports, and system states.
- **Hypothesis Generation**: Formulate potential [[concepts/causes|causes]] based on known failure modes.
- **Testing & [[concepts/disconnection|Isolation]]**: Reproduce the issue in a controlled environment to isolate variables.
- **[[concepts/solution|Resolution]] & Verification**: Apply fixes and confirm system stability.
- **Documentation**: Record findings to prevent recurrence and aid future diagnostics.
