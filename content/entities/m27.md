# M27

M27 refers to MiniMax M2.7, an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by MiniMax. The model is designed with agentic capabilities, enabling it to perform tasks that require planning and tool use beyond standard language generation.

## Capabilities and Positioning

M2.7 has been positioned by some in the AI community as comparable to [[entities/claude-opus-46|Claude Opus 4.6]] in terms of performance and functionality. The model's agentic capabilities distinguish it from simpler language models, allowing it to function as an [[concepts/ai-agent|autonomous agent]] capable of breaking down [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] and executing them systematically.

## Open Source Status

As an open-source model, M27 is publicly available for research and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], contrasting with many proprietary [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. This [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] makes it relevant for developers and organizations seeking alternatives to closed-source systems.
