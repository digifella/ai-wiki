# Artifact Excavation

Artifact excavation is the systematic recovery of material remains from archaeological sites to study past human activity and culture. Professional archaeologists employ standardized excavation methods following strict methodological standards to ensure proper documentation, [[concepts/preservation|preservation]], and interpretation of findings. These practices maintain the [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of archaeological contexts, which is essential for accurate dating and understanding of historical sequences.

## Danish Metal Detection Program

Denmark has implemented a unique initiative where the government deputizes private [[concepts/metal|metal]] detectorists to search farm fields for artifacts. This program leverages the widespread hobby of [[concepts/metal-detecting|metal detecting]] to uncover historical details that might otherwise remain hidden. By integrating private citizens into the official archaeological process, the state aims to maximize the recovery of [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]] while maintaining [[concepts/regulation|legal oversight]] of the discoveries.
