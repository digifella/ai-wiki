# AI Connectors

AI connectors are integrations and APIs that link AI agents and language models—such as ChatGPT, Claude, and similar systems—to existing applications, workflows, and data sources. These connectors enable AI systems to interact across multiple platforms and services, allowing them to access information, execute commands, and trigger actions in external tools. By connecting AI capabilities directly to business software and data repositories, connectors extend what language models can accomplish beyond text generation alone.

## Function and Purpose

AI connectors serve as bridges between AI systems and the wider software ecosystem. They allow language models to retrieve real-time information from databases, call APIs, modify documents, send communications, or initiate processes in connected applications. This capability transforms AI from a conversational tool into an agent capable of performing multi-step workflows autonomously. For example, a connected AI system might retrieve customer data, draft a response, and send it through an email service—all within a single automated sequence.

## Common Applications

Organizations use AI connectors to automate routine tasks across marketing, customer service, content management, and data analysis. Creative workflows benefit from connectors that integrate AI writing and generation tools with design platforms, publishing systems, and asset management tools. Development teams use connectors to link AI models with code repositories, testing frameworks, and deployment pipelines.
