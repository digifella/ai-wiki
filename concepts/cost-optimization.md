# Cost Optimization in AI Development

**[[concepts/llms|Definition]]:**
Cost optimization refers to strategies and techniques employed by developers and organizations to minimize expenses while maintaining or improving the performance of their [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. This includes optimizing resource usage, reducing waste, and leveraging [[concepts/advanced-tools|advanced tools]] designed for efficient development.

## Key Concepts
- **Resource Allocation**: Efficient use of [[concepts/compute-resources|compute resources]] and [[concepts/memory-management|memory]] to prevent bottlenecks and reduce idle time.
- **Model Selection**: Choosing between high-cost, [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] models (e.g., [[concepts/claude-opus-5|Claude Opus 5]]) for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] and smaller, cheaper models for routine queries.
- **Orchestration**: Using [[concepts/multi-agent-systems|multi-agent systems]] to route tasks to the most cost-effective model, preserving proprietary capabilities via [[concepts/wargaming|wargaming]] simulations.
- **[[concepts/local-control|Local Deployment]]**: Leveraging [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-source models]] for [[concepts/inference|inference]] to eliminate per-token API costs.

Recent evaluations of [[concepts/claude-opus-5|Claude Opus 5]] indicate significant advancements in creative and technical prompts, particularly in 3D content generation. While the model demonstrates superior capability, its high inference cost necessitates careful cost-effectiveness analysis.
