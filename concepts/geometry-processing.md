# Geometry Processing

**Geometry processing** refers to the computational study of [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] for analyzing and manipulating geometric data, typically in the form of meshes, point clouds, or volumetric data. It serves as the foundational layer for [[concepts/computer-graphics]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], and [[concepts/robotics|robotics]].

## Core Concepts
- **Mesh Manipulation**: Operations such as smoothing, simplification, and parameterization.
- **Shape Analysis**: Extracting features like curvature, normals, and topology.
- **Spatial [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]**: Understanding 3D structure and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within a scene.
