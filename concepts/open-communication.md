# Open Communication

**Open Communication** refers to an organizational or team environment where information flows freely, transparently, and bidirectionally among all members. It minimizes [[concepts/algorithmic-filtering|information asymmetry]], encourages psychological safety, and facilitates rapid [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/opacity|Transparency]]:** Visible access to [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes and [[concepts/clear-goals|strategic goals]].
- **Psychological Safety:** A shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking [[concepts/psychological-safety]]. This is actively cultivated through [[concepts/inclusive-leadership|Inclusive Leadership]], which models [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] and validates diverse perspectives [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-How-to-Drive-Psychological-Safety-Through-Inclusive-Lead|How to Drive Psychological Safety Through Inclusive Leadership]]. Key insights on the definition and measurement of this [[concepts/safety-culture|safety climate]] are detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Psychological-Safety-Amy-C.-Edmondson|Psychological Safety – Amy C. Edmondson]].
- **[[concepts/active-listening|Active Listening]]:** Engagement in [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] where [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] is received without defensiveness.
- **Information [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]:** Removal of bureaucratic barriers to knowledge sharing.
