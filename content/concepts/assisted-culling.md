# Assisted Culling

Assisted Culling refers to a feature in [[concepts/software|software]] designed to help photographers more efficiently manage their photo collections. By utilizing advanced algorithms and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], the process of selecting high-quality [[concepts/images|images]] from large datasets becomes faster and easier.

## Overview
- Enhances workflow efficiency by automating certain selection criteria.
- Improves [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of [[concepts/photo-organization-techniques|image organization]] through AI analysis.
- Supports decision-making processes in complex photography projects.

### Features
- **Assisted Culling**: Helps users identify photos that should be kept or deleted based on visual data analysis. AI-powered features
- **Auto Stacking**: Automatically groups similar images together, simplifying the [[concepts/organization|organization]] and management of large photo collections.
