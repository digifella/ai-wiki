# Lightroom Masking

Lightroom masking enables photographers to apply selective [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific areas of an image without altering the entire photo. Masks create defined regions where [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, clarity, and other [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] can be adjusted independently. This non-destructive approach allows for precise control over how edits affect different parts of a photograph, from skies and foregrounds to subject-specific areas.

## Automated AI Masks

Modern versions of Lightroom and Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] incorporate AI-powered mask generation that automatically detects common photographic subjects and regions. These tools can isolate landscapes, people, skies, and other elements with a single click, significantly reducing manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] time. The automated presets adapt to varied compositions and lighting conditions, making them useful for both batch processing and individual image refinement.

## Manual Masking and Edge Refinement

Beyond automated options, Lightroom provides tools for creating and [[concepts/mask-refinement|refining masks]] manually. Users can adjust mask edges, feather boundaries, and paint masks directly onto [[concepts/images|images]] for precise control. Edge adjustment methods allow for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] where masks meet, preventing visible halos or unnatural transitions between masked and unmasked areas—particularly important in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] where sky-to-land transitions require careful handling.

## Integration with Camera Raw

Adobe Camera Raw offers comparable masking functionality integrated into its workflow, allowing photographers to apply similar selective adjustments during the raw processing stage. The non-destructive [[entities/nature|nature]] of both Lightroom and Camera Raw masks means adjustments can be modified or removed at any point without degrading image quality, making these tools essential for iterative [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]].
