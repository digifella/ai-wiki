# Color range manipulation

The process of isolating and modifying specific color segments within an image's spectrum to achieve targeted aesthetic or corrective results without affecting the entire [[concepts/color-range-tool|color range]].

### Core Dimensions
- [[concepts/hue]]: Altering the fundamental color identity (e.g., shifting blue toward cyan).
- Saturation: Controlling the intensity, vividness, or purity of a specific color.
- Luminance: Adjusting the brightness or perceived [[concepts/light|light]] level of a specific color channel.

### Implementation & Tools
- Adobe [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] [[concepts/color-mixer]] (formerly HSL/Color panel): A dedicated interface used to perform precise, targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific color channels by manipulating the three dimensions above.
