# Mu

Mu denotes a series of product updates released by Anthropic during the 2025–2026 period, informally referred to as "Christmas Claude." These updates marked a significant shift in Claude's operational model, transitioning the system from primarily reactive interactions toward more proactive problem-solving approaches. The core objective of the Mu updates was to integrate agentic capabilities, enabling the AI to operate with greater autonomy when executing complex tasks.

## Agentic Capabilities

The Mu updates integrated agentic strategies that allow Claude to decompose tasks, plan execution sequences, and manage workflows with reduced human intervention. This architectural change enables the model to initiate and sustain multi-step processes independently, rather than waiting for explicit prompts for each step. By adopting this proactive stance, Claude can handle intricate workflows that require sustained attention and dynamic adjustment of strategies based on intermediate results.
