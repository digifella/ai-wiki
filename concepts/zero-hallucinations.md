# Zero Hallucinations

**[[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|Zero]]** hallucinations refers to the architectural or operational guarantee that an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] produces no fabricated, ungrounded, or factually incorrect outputs. This concept is critical for high-stakes [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] environments where [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and [[concepts/honesty|truthfulness]] are paramount.

## Key Developments

### Jev by TypeSafe.ai
Significant progress in this domain is represented by **Jev**, a decision-focused AI model developed by [[concepts/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]].

- **Core Promise:** Explicitly designed for "[[concepts/zero|zero]] hallucinations" in decision contexts, contrasting with traditional [[concepts/large-language-model]] that prioritize generative fluency over factual grounding.
- **[[concepts/creator|Creator]]:** Developed by [[entities/diogo-al
- **Architectural Nature:** Jev is not a new category of model but a refinement and extension of existing classification and decision models, specifically enhancing the ability to follow dynamic instructions.
- **Performance Profile:** Designed as a frontier model for rapid, structured decision-making in software applications, prioritizing speed and structure over generative fluency.
