# Fact Based Queries

Fact based queries represent a core capability of the [[entities/ai-assistant|Nemotron]] AI assistant, enabling it to answer questions posed by users on a wide range of topics. This functionality allows Nemotron to retrieve, process, and communicate factual information in response to direct inquiries, making it suitable for [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] where accurate [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] is required.

## Scope and Limitations

The effectiveness of fact-based query [[concepts/responses|responses]] depends on the [[concepts/training-data|training data]] and [[concepts/knowledge-cutoff|knowledge cutoff]] date of the underlying model. Nemotron can address questions within its [[concepts/training|training]] scope, though responses may be subject to the inherent limitations of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], including potential factual errors or outdated information for rapidly evolving topics.

## Related Capabilities

Fact-based querying operates alongside other [[concepts/character-development|Nemotron capabilities]] as part of its broader function as a text-based AI assistant. This foundational ability to answer questions integrates with the system's other functionalities to provide comprehensive assistance to users seeking information or [[concepts/explanations|explanations]].
