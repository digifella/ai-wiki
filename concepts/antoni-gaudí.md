# Antoni Gaudí

**[[entities/antoni-gaudí|Antoni Gaudí]] i Cornet** (1852–1926) was a Catalan architect and the foremost figure of Catalan [[concepts/catalan-modernism|Modernisme]]. Known for his highly individualistic, organic [[concepts/style|style]] that integrates Gothic and curvilinear Art Nouveau forms, his work is characterized by the innovative use of structural geometry and natural motifs.

## Key Works
- Casa Batlló
- Park Güell
- Casa Milà
- [[concepts/sagrada-família]]

## Architectural Philosophy
- **Organic Architecture:** Believed that nature is the ultimate source of inspiration, utilizing hyperboloids, paraboloids, and helicoids to create structurally efficient yet aesthetically complex forms.
- **[[concepts/payload-structure|Structural Integrity]]:** Developed the *catenary arch* model to ensure stability without excessive material use.
- **Integration of Arts:** Combined architecture with sculpture, ceramics, ironwork, and glasswork.

## The Sagrada Família
Gaudí dedicated the last 15 years of his life exclusively to this basilica, which remains unfinished. His [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] has been continued by subsequent architects using modern technology and his original models.

## Legacy
- Declared a UNESCO [[entities/earth|World]] [[concepts/cultural-heritage|Heritage]] Site.
- Influenced generations of architects through his rejection of straight lines and embrace of natural forms.
- His work remains a symbol of Catalan identity and [[concepts/architectural-innovation|architectural innovation]].
