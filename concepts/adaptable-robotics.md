# Adaptable Robotics

**Adaptable robotics** refers to the development of [[concepts/robotics|robotic systems]] capable of adjusting their behavior, manipulation strategies, and operational parameters in real-time to handle unstructured environments and novel tasks, rather than relying on rigid, pre-programmed scripts.

## Key Developments

### Google DeepMind & Gemini Integration
Recent advancements highlight the integration of [[concepts/multimodal-language-models|multimodal AI]] models, specifically [[entities/google-deepmind]]'s **[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]**, to enhance robotic general-purpose capabilities [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-Google-DeepMind-Robotics-Gemini-AI-for-Adaptable-General|Google DeepMind Robotics: Gemini AI for Adaptable, General-Purpose Robots]].

*   **Multimodal Perception:** Utilizing [[concepts/google-ai|Gemini]] to process diverse sensory inputs ([[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], language, tactile data) allows robots to interpret complex, unstructured environments more effectively.
*   **General-Purpose Utility:** Moving beyond rigid pre-programming, these systems aim for broader task [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]], enabling robots to learn and execute new [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] dynamically.
*   **Research Context:** Insights from the [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] robotics lab tour, guided by Director [[entities/kanishka-rao|Kanishka Rao]] and hosted by Professor [[entities/hannah-fry|Hannah Fry]], emphasize the shift toward AI-driven physical autonomy [[Google DeepMind Robotics: Gemini AI for Adaptable, General-Purpose Robots](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UALxgn1MnZo)].
