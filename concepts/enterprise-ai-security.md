# Enterprise AI Security

[[concepts/enterprise-ai|Enterprise AI]] [[concepts/security|security]] encompasses the frameworks, tools, and practices required to [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] agents safely within business environments. As organizations increasingly integrate [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] into critical operations, security considerations have become central to deployment strategies. The field addresses risks including [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]], data [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], model manipulation, and unintended system behaviors that could compromise business operations or violate regulatory requirements.

## Secure Agent Deployment

Deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] in enterprise settings requires multiple layers of [[concepts/secure|protection]]. Organizations must establish access controls, audit trails, and [[concepts/monitoring-systems|monitoring systems]] to track agent behavior and interactions. This includes securing the underlying models, managing API endpoints, and ensuring that agents operate only within defined parameters. [[concepts/containerization|Containerization]], encryption, and network [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] are common technical approaches used to prevent lateral [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and limit exposure in case of compromise.

## Platform Considerations

Different platforms offer varying levels of security features for [[concepts/ai-agent-implementation|AI agent deployment]]. Organizations evaluating solutions must consider factors such as data residency requirements, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] certifications, model [[concepts/governance|governance]] capabilities, and [[concepts/incident-response|incident response]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]]. Platform selection often depends on specific industry requirements, regulatory frameworks like [[concepts/gdpr|GDPR]] or [[concepts/hipaa|HIPAA]], and the sensitivity of data the AI systems [[entities/will|will]] access or process.

Enterprise [[concepts/ai-security|AI security]] remains an evolving field as threat landscapes shift and new AI capabilities emerge. Organizations typically combine vendor-provided security features with internal [[concepts/policies|policies]], employee training, and third-party assessments to create comprehensive security postures around their AI deployments.
