# Custom Models

Custom [[concepts/models|models]] refer to specialized [[concepts/large-language-models]] that are tailored to specific [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]] or domains. These models can be fine-tuned or trained from scratch to better suit particular needs, such as industry-specific [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], unique datasets, or specialized tasks.

## Key Characteristics
- **Domain [[concepts/specialization|Specialization]]**: Custom models are optimized for specific domains or tasks.
- **[[concepts/fine-tuning|Fine-Tuning]]**: Often involve fine-tuning [[concepts/pre-trained-models|pre-trained models]] on specialized datasets.
- **Flexibility**: Can be adapted to various [[concepts/software|applications]], from chatbots to data analysis.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/large-language-models]]
- [[concepts/fine-tuning]]
- [[concepts/machine-learning]]

## Use Cases
- **Industry-Specific Applications**: Custom models can be used in [[concepts/health|healthcare]], finance, or legal sectors to handle domain-specific language and tasks.
- **Personal Assistants**: Tailored models can provide more accurate and relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]] based on user preferences.
- **Research**: Custom models can be used to explore new areas of [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]].

## Tools and Frameworks
- **[[entities/ollama|Ollama]]**: A tool that simplifies [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/large-language-models]] locally, allowing for the creation and interaction with custom [[concepts/models|models]].
- **[[entities/hugging-face|Hugging Face]] [[concepts/transformers|Transformers]]**: A popular library for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] and deploying custom models.
