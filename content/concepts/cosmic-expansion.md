# Cosmic Expansion

**Core Definition:** Metric expansion of space characterized by increasing proper distance between gravitationally unbound regions over time. Fundamental component of the [[concepts/big-bang]] [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]] and Lambda-CDM Model.

## Dynamics & Drivers
- **Mechanism:** Expansion dynamics described by Friedmann Equations; current accelerated [[concepts/phase|phase]] attributed to Dark Energy ($\Omega_\Lambda$).
- **[[concepts/parameters|Parameters]]:** Expansion rate quantified by the Hubble Constant ($H_0$); spatial geometry constrained by Curvature of the Universe measurements.
- **Observables:** Inferred via Redshift surveys, Type [[concepts/information-architecture|Ia]] [[concepts/supernovae|Supernovae]] standard candles, Baryon Acoustic Oscillations (BAO), and [[concepts/cosmic-microwave-background]] (CMB) anisotropies.
