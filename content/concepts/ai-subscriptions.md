# AI Subscriptions

## Overview
AI subscriptions represent a model where users pay for access to advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] services, such as [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/claude|Claude]]. These plans often come with specific [[concepts/usage-limits|usage limits]] and financial implications.

## Recent Developments
- As of April 4th, 2026, [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] has implemented new [[concepts/policies|policies]] regarding the use of third-party tools like [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]] in conjunction with their AI subscriptions. 2026 04 10 Anthropics [[concepts/claude-ai-subscription-policy|Claude AI Subscription Changes]] [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] Ban [[concepts/rate-limits|Usage Limits]] an

### Summary Points:
- **Ban on [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]]**: Users are no longer allowed to use the [[entities/claude|Claude]] API through tools like [[entities/openclaw|OpenClaw]] with their subscriptions.
- **Additional Costs**: Those wishing to continue using third-party tools will need to enable "extra usage," which incurs additional fees.
- **Subscription Cancellation**: If users do not want to pay extra for third-party integration, they must cancel their existing AI subscription plans.
