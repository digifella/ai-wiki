# Audio To Video Conversion

[[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]] to video conversion is a content repurposing technique that transforms audio-based materials into video format by combining audio tracks with visual elements. This method is particularly useful for adapting audio-first content, such as podcast episodes, [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] discussions, audiobooks, or recorded lectures, into videos suitable for distribution on platforms like [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] and other video-sharing services. The approach extends the reach of existing audio content to audiences who prefer visual media or consume content on video-centric platforms.

In the context of NotebookLM, this process often involves generating personalized avatars that mimic the [[entities/speaker|speaker]]'s [[concepts/presence|appearance]] while utilizing the generated audio. A key technical component in this workflow is the use of SpeakerSplit, a tool designed for [[concepts/speaker-separation|speaker separation]]. This allows for the [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] of individual voices within a multi-speaker recording, ensuring that the visual avatar and audio track remain synchronized and accurately attributed to the correct speaker.

The resulting videos serve as an extension of the original audio material, maintaining the informational [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of the source while adding a visual layer. This technique is increasingly adopted by creators and educators looking to maximize the utility of their recorded [[concepts/notes|notes]] and discussions without producing original video footage from scratch.
