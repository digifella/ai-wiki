# Alibaba Ai

Alibaba Ai denotes the artificial intelligence division within Alibaba Group, responsible for the research, development, and maintenance of large language models and associated AI tools. The department’s strategic focus centers on creating practical applications for software development and enterprise environments, aiming to provide accessible AI solutions across various industries and deployment scenarios.

The division is best known for the Qwen series of language models, which encompasses multiple versions designed to address diverse computational requirements and specialized tasks. Among its notable offerings is Qwen Code, a command-line tool designed to support local AI-assisted coding workflows. This tool allows developers to integrate AI capabilities directly into their local development environments, facilitating efficient code generation and management.
