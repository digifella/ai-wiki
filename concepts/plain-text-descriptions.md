# Plain Text Descriptions

Plain text descriptions are textual representations of visual content, designed to convey information that would otherwise require images, [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]], or illustrations to communicate. They serve as an [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] tool, enabling people with visual impairments to understand visual material, while also creating machine-readable alternatives to images. By translating visual information into words, plain text descriptions preserve content in formats that both humans and computational systems can process without visual interpretation.

## Academic and Technical Use

In [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] and technical fields, plain text descriptions are particularly valuable for conveying complex information. They allow researchers to document experimental setups, data visualizations, mathematical diagrams, and conceptual illustrations in a format suitable for archiving, searching, and reusing.
