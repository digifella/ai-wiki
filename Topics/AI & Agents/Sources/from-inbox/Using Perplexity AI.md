### Video Summary

The video is a comprehensive [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] on how to use the [[concepts/ai-search|AI search]] engine **Perplexity**. The creator calls it her single most-used AI tool for [[concepts/learning|learning]] faster, boosting productivity, and improving her business.
The tutorial covers the following key features and [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]]:
**[[concepts/desktop-interface|Desktop Interface]] Overview:**

* **Main Features:** A walkthrough of the basic interface, explaining the difference between a quick "Search" and an in-depth "Research" (Pro) search.
* **[[concepts/search-customization|Search Customization]]:** Users can choose which AI model to use (like [[concepts/gpt-41|GPT-4.1]], [[concepts/claude-37-sonnet|Claude 3.7 Sonnet]], or [[entities/gemini-2-5-pro|Gemini 2.5 Pro]]) and filter search sources to the entire web, academic papers, or social media (like Reddit).
* **Discover Tab:** A personalized news feed that shows top headlines and stories tailored to the user's interests and past search history.
* **Spaces:** A feature for organizing research into separate folders or projects. Users can give [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] for each space to get tailored results for specific tasks, such as competitor analysis.
* **Library:** A central place to find and manage all past search threads and conversations.

**Mobile App Features:**

* The mobile app offers a similar experience to the desktop version.
* **Voice Assistant (iOS):** A powerful new feature that allows users to perform tasks with their voice, such as summarizing news and drafting it into an email.
* **Calendar [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** The voice assistant can set reminders and add events directly to the user's calendar.
* **Visual Search:** Users can take a picture of an object (like a shampoo bottle), and Perplexity [[entities/will|will]] identify it and provide detailed information, such as ingredients and benefits.

**Practical Use Cases Demonstrated:**

* **Fact-Checking:** Asking about the [[concepts/health|health]] benefits of hibiscus tea, with the results citing credible sources.
* **Learning & Simplification:** Asking Perplexity to explain a complex topic (what an API is) "like I'm a 5-year-old."
* **SEO & Marketing:** Finding high-traffic, low-competition keywords for a blog post.
* **Travel Planning:** Asking for "must-see" places and the best restaurants for a trip to St. Lucia.
* **Productivity:** Creating a master grocery list from multiple recipe links and organizing it by a grocery store's layout.
* **Image Generation:** Using a simple prompt to generate an image.
