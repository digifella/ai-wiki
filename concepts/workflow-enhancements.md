# Workflow Enhancements

Workflow enhancements are optimizations to processes, tools, or systems designed to reduce latency, minimize manual intervention, and accelerate output in creative and technical pipelines. These improvements address [[concepts/friction|friction]] points where creators spend time on repetitive tasks or wait for processing to complete, thereby increasing overall [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and enabling faster [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycles.

## Common Implementation Patterns

Workflow enhancements typically take several forms: automating repetitive operations, parallelizing previously sequential tasks, reducing file processing time, and integrating disparate tools into unified interfaces. In creative software, this might involve streamlining export procedures, adding [[concepts/batch-processing|batch processing]] capabilities, or providing direct access to advanced lighting controls without destructive edits.

### Adobe Photoshop Specific Enhancements

Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] have introduced significant workflow optimizations:

*   **[[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] & Automation**: [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] 27.6 introduced generative AI features that automate complex [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] and expansion tasks, reducing [[concepts/manual-masking|manual masking]] and painting time.
*   **[[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] Integration**: [[entities/camera-raw-184|Camera Raw 18.4]] improved [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] and [[concepts/gradient-tools|gradient tools]], allowing for more precise non-destructive [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] within the raw processing pipeline.
*   **[[concepts/light-adjustment-layer|Light Adjustment Layer]] (Beta 27.9+)**: The introduction of the new "[[concepts/light|Light]]" adjustment layer allows for non-destructive, global lighting adjustments. This feature enables users to modify scene illumination, [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], and highlights independently of [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]], streamlining the lighting [[concepts/phase|phase]] of the [[concepts/creative-pipeline|creative pipeline]].
