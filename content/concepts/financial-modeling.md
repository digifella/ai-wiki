# Financial Modeling

The practice of constructing mathematical representations of a company's financial performance, typically executed within [[entities/microsoft-excel|Microsoft Excel]], to facilitate Forecasting, Valuation, and Decision-making.

### AI & Automation
- [[entities/claude-ai|Claude AI]] Excel Add-in has emerged as a highly effective tool for finance professionals, demonstrating greater utility in specialized modeling tasks than native tools like [[entities/microsoft-copilot]].
  - Provides comprehensive support for financial modeling [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]
  - Highlights significant improvements over similar native AI offerings
- [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/claude|Claude]] directly into [[entities/microsoft-excel|Microsoft Excel]] represents a "[[concepts/state-of-matter-phase-transition|phase change]] in [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]]," enabling the construction of complex, multi-tab financial models in minutes rather than weeks.
