# Slide Deck Transformation

Slide Deck Transformation is a workflow that converts presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video format using Google Vids. NotebookLM, an AI-powered notebook application, automatically structures uploaded source documents into slide decks. These slides serve as the foundational content for subsequent video production, allowing users to repurpose text-based research and documentation into multimedia presentations.

The process begins within NotebookLM, where users upload source materials to generate a structured slide deck. Once the slides are finalized, the content is exported or linked to Google Vids. This integration enables the automatic synchronization of slide content with voiceovers and visual elements, streamlining the creation of unwatermarked video assets from static presentations.
