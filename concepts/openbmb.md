# OpenBMB

**[[entities/openbmb|OpenBMB]]** (Open [[concepts/pre-trained-model|Base Model]] Base) is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] organization dedicated to the development of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and related [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]]. The group is best known for the **MiniCPM** series, a family of lightweight, efficient models designed for [[concepts/edge-deployment|edge deployment]] and resource-constrained environments.

## Key Projects & Models

- **MiniCPM Series**: A [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|lineage]] of small-scale LLMs optimized for mobile and [[concepts/edge-devices|edge devices]], balancing parameter efficiency with performance.
