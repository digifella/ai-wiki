# Point-and-shoot cameras

Compact, automated [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] designed for ease of use and portability.

## Market Evolution
- smartphones have largely surpassed point-and-shoot cameras due to convenience and larger viewports.
- While traditional cameras (DSLR, mirrorless) continue to [[entities/excel|excel]] in video, [[concepts/low-light-performance|low light performance]], and [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]], the utility of dedicated compact cameras is declining.
- The rise of advanced [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]] [[concepts/software|software]], such as [[entities/adobe-project-indigo|Adobe Project Indigo]] (introduced by [[entities/colin-smith|Colin Smith]] of [[entities/photoshopcafe|PhotoshopCAFE]]), further shifts the focus from [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]-centric capture to [[concepts/software|software]]-driven mobile imaging.
