# Premiere Pro

Premiere Pro is a professional non-linear video editing application developed by Adobe, first released in 2003. It allows editors to arrange, trim, and manipulate video clips on a customizable timeline without requiring sequential processing. This non-destructive workflow has become an industry standard, enabling flexible editing of source material across various production scales.

The software is widely adopted in documentary production, broadcast media, and creative industries. It serves as a central tool for assembling raw footage, integrating audio, and applying visual effects. Its integration with other Adobe Creative Cloud applications facilitates a seamless pipeline for color grading, motion graphics, and final rendering.

In modern AI-assisted video workflows, Premiere Pro provides specific features to streamline documentary production. These include automated transcription for subtitles, text-based editing for rapid rough cuts, and AI-driven audio cleanup tools. These capabilities allow editors to manage large volumes of interview footage and sync media efficiently, reducing manual labor in the post-production phase.
