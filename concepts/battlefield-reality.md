# Battlefield Reality

**Definition:** The [[concepts/purpose|objective]] material state of [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] operations, defined by verifiable force dispositions, attrition metrics, logistical capacity, and terrain control. Contrasts with [[concepts/storytelling|Narrative]] Warfare, propaganda, and speculative forecasting. Ground truth requires correlation of multiple data sources to filter informational noise.

## Core Indicators
- **Material Attrition:** Equipment loss rates vs. production/mobilization output.
- **Operational Tempo:** Changes in [[concepts/exercise|movement]] [[concepts/density|density]] and engagement frequency independent of rhetoric.
- **Public Displays:** Correlation between ceremonial force presentation and actual combat readiness.
- **Frontline Stability:** Terrain [[concepts/storing|retention]] vs. claimed breakthroughs.
