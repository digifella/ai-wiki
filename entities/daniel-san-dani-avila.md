# Daniel San Dani Avila

Daniel San, known online as @Dani_Avila, is a content creator who produces instructional videos demonstrating the use of Anthropic's Claude Code platform on mobile devices. His work focuses on making AI-assisted coding tools accessible to developers who work primarily on smartphones and tablets rather than traditional desktop environments.

## Content Focus

San's videos showcase practical applications of Claude Code, Anthropic's coding assistance tool, with an emphasis on mobile workflows. By documenting how these tools function on phones and tablets, he addresses a gap in the market for developers who do not have immediate access to a computer. His content provides step-by-step guidance on setting up and utilizing the platform within mobile operating systems, highlighting the capabilities and limitations of coding on smaller screens.
