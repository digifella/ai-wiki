# Autonomous Driving Technology

Autonomous driving technology encompasses a range of advanced driver assistance systems (ADAS) and full autonomy solutions designed to reduce the need for human intervention in vehicle operation. Key players include Tesla with its [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving]] (FSD) system, Waymo's autonomous ride-hailing service, and various automotive manufacturers implementing SAE Levels 2-5 technologies.

### Notable Developments
- Tesla FSD has been approved for supervised driving in the Netherlands, setting a precedent for potential rollout across other EU countries. [[concepts/tesla-fsd-supervised|Tesla FSD Supervised]] European Approval: Netherlands Sets Precedent for EU Rollout
- Continued improvements in [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and AI algorithms enhance decision-making capabilities.
- Regulatory frameworks evolve to address safety concerns and liability issues.
- Sensor technologies such as LiDAR, radar, and cameras are refined for better environmental perception.
