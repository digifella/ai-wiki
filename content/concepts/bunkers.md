# Bunkers

Hardened, fortified structures engineered to protect personnel, equipment, and command-and-[[concepts/power|control]] (C2) infrastructure from kinetic threats, including [[concepts/ballistic-missiles]] and AD ([[concepts/a2ad|Anti-Access/Area Denial]]) operations.

### Tactical Observations
* In the context of AD, and Global Implications, bunkers represent a critical component in the operational triad of Blockades, Bunkers, and [[concepts/ballistic-missiles]].
