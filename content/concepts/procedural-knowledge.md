# Procedural Knowledge

Procedural knowledge refers to the type of knowledge that is gained through practice and experience. It involves knowing how to do something rather than just knowing about it. In [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] to declarative knowledge which consists of factual information, procedural knowledge includes [[concepts/skills|skills]] and abilities such as playing an instrument or solving [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]].
