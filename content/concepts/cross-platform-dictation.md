# Cross Platform Dictation

Cross-platform dictation refers to [[concepts/ai-powered-dictation|voice-to-text]] functionality that operates seamlessly across multiple [[concepts/software|applications]] and devices. Rather than being confined to a single app or operating system, cross-platform dictation systems aim to provide consistent voice input capabilities whether a user is working in email clients, document editors, messaging apps, or other software environments.

## Wispr Flow

Wispr Flow is an AI-powered dictation application that exemplifies this approach. The service raised $30 million in funding from [[entities/menlo-ventures|Menlo Ventures]], as reported by TechCrunch in June 2025. The app emphasizes effortless voice dictation that integrates with multiple applications users already rely on, positioning itself as a universal dictation [[concepts/solution|solution]] rather than a single-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] tool.
