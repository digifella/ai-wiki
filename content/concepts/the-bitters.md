# The Bitters

The bitters are a category of concentrated flavoring compounds traditionally used in cocktail [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. Originating from medicinal tinctures, bitters contain botanical ingredients steeped in alcohol and are typically added to drinks in small quantities to enhance flavor complexity and balance sweetness.

## Historical Connection to Pharmacy

The modern application of bitters in mixology has roots in pharmaceutical practice. In Heraklion, a former pharmacist transitioned from compounding medicinal preparations to creating bitters for cocktail use, leveraging existing knowledge of botanical extraction and alcohol-based formulations. This background illustrates the historical overlap between pharmacy and early bartending practices, when many cocktail ingredients were originally developed for medicinal purposes.

## Contemporary Use

Bitters remain a staple ingredient in classic and contemporary cocktails. Their concentrated [[entities/nature|nature]] means only a few dashes are required per drink, making them an efficient way to layer aromatic and herbal [[concepts/notes|notes]] into mixed beverages. The ingredient category continues to expand with craft bartenders and producers developing new flavor profiles beyond traditional varieties.
