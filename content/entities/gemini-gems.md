# Gemini Gems

Customizable AI personas/assistants designed for specialized task execution and expertise [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

## AI Consultant Workflow
In conjunction with [[entities/notebooklm]], [[concepts/automated-report-generator|Gemini Gems]] enables the "productization" of individual expertise:
- **Expertise Transformation**: Converts messy, unstructured personal [[concepts/notes|notes]] and proprietary knowledge into professional, monetizable AI consultants.
- **[[concepts/logic-manuals|Logic Manuals]]**: Synthesizes personal expertise into structured "[[concepts/logic-manuals|Logic Manuals]]" that serve as the foundational instruction set for [[concepts/custom-assistants|custom assistants]].
- **[[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] Stages**:
	- [[concepts/knowledge-capture|Knowledge Capture]] & [[concepts/organization|Organization]].
	- Synthesis of proprietary data into structured logic.
	- Deployment of the [[concepts/specialized-ai-assistant|specialized AI assistant]].
