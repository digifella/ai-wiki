# P0/P1 Clean Sweep

**P0/P1 Clean Sweep** refers to the systematic [[concepts/solution|resolution]] of critical (P0) and high-priority (P1) issues within an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]]'s operational lifecycle, ensuring baseline [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] before [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] complexity. This process is integral to maintaining agent stability and trustworthiness.

## Core Principles

- **Priority Triage**: Immediate [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to P0 (system-breaking) and P1 (significant degradation) errors.
- **[[concepts/verification|Verification]] [[concepts/loops|Loops]]**: Implementation of self-check [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] to validate outputs before execution.
- **[[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] [[concepts/honesty|Integrity]]**: Ensuring [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]] in [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] paths.
