# Large Hadron Collider

The **[[entities/large-hadron-collider|Large Hadron Collider]] (LHC)** is the world's largest and most powerful particle accelerator, located at [[entities/cern|CERN]]. It is designed to test predictions of high-energy physics, particularly the Standard Model, by colliding protons or heavy ions at near-light speeds.

## Key Scientific Achievements

*   **[[entities/higgs-boson|Higgs Boson]] Discovery**: Confirmed the existence of the Higgs boson, validating the mechanism by which elementary particles acquire mass.
*   **Mass from Energy**: Demonstrates the inverse process of nuclear fission/fusion, where [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]] is converted into new mass ($E=mc^2$), creating heavy particles from high-energy collisions.
*   **Quark-Gluon Plasma**: Recreated conditions similar to those just after the Big Bang, allowing study of the state of matter that existed microseconds after the universe's birth.
