**Generative [[concepts/engine|Engine]] [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]] (GEO)** is the strategic evolution of [[concepts/seo-optimization|Search Engine Optimization]] (SEO), focused on optimizing web content for visibility, citation, and inclusion within AI-generated search responses.

### Key Concepts
- **The Shift from SEO to GEO:** As AI-powered search engines replace traditional link-based [[concepts/data-indexing|indexing]], [[concepts/seo|traditional SEO]] is becoming increasingly obsolete.
- **Target Platforms:** Optimization must specifically address generative interfaces including [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/ai-overviews|AI Overviews]], [[entities/chatgpt]], [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], and [[entities/gemini]].
- **Adaptation Strategy:** Requires adapting website architecture and content to be more effectively parsed, synthesized, and cited by [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]].
- **Implementation Tools:** Emerging tools like [[entities/claude-code]] are being utilized to automate the restructuring of digital assets for AI-readability.
