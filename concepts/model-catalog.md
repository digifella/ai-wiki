# Model Catalog

A Model Catalog functions as a centralized registry for documenting, organizing, and managing artificial intelligence models, agents, and their associated configurations within an organization or platform. It serves as a discoverable inventory system that tracks deployed models, version histories, dependencies, and relevant metadata. By maintaining a single source of truth for model information, the catalog enables technical teams to identify existing AI assets, monitor their current operational state, and streamline governance processes.

In the context of Microsoft Foundry, the catalog acts as a unified interface for the AI application and agent factory. It integrates with the broader Foundry ecosystem to provide visibility into the lifecycle of AI components, from development and testing to deployment and monitoring. This integration ensures that models and agents are consistently managed across the infrastructure, reducing redundancy and improving operational efficiency.

The platform supports standardized metadata schemas and tagging systems to facilitate search and filtering capabilities. Users can query the catalog to locate specific models based on criteria such as performance metrics, training data sources, or compliance status. This structured approach aids in auditability and helps organizations maintain control over their AI portfolio as it scales.

Governance features within the catalog allow administrators to enforce policies regarding model usage and access. It supports the tracking of model lineage, providing a clear record of how models have evolved over time. This historical context is critical for debugging, compliance reporting, and understanding the impact of updates on downstream applications and agents.
