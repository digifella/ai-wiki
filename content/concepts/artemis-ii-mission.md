# Artemis II Mission Concept Overview

## Summary and Key Concepts

- **[[entities/artemis-ii|Artemis II Mission]]**: [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s ambitious program to return humans to the Moon after a long hiatus.
- **[[concepts/crew-operations|Crew Operations]]**: Includes [[concepts/training|training]], simulations, and real-time missions involving crew members interacting with [[concepts/orion-systems|Orion spacecraft]] systems.
- **[[concepts/orion-systems|Orion Systems]]**: Advanced life support, navigation, communication technologies critical for [[concepts/lunar-exploration|lunar exploration]].
- **[[concepts/starship|Starship Updates]]**: [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]]’s [[concepts/starship|Starship]] developments, complementing [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s efforts in deep [[concepts/human-spaceflight|space travel]].
