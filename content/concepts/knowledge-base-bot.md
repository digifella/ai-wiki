# Knowledge Base Bot

A Knowledge Base Bot is a [[concepts/custom-ai-agent|custom AI agent]] built using [[concepts/document-insights|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] that leverages organizational information and documents to provide intelligent [[concepts/responses|responses]]. Rather than relying solely on a [[concepts/copilot-chat|Copilot]]'s base [[concepts/training-data|training data]], a Knowledge Base Bot is configured to reference and reason from a specific collection of internal documents, [[concepts/policies|policies]], or domain expertise. This allows organizations to extend Copilot's capabilities with proprietary or specialized information.

## Building and Configuration

Creating a Knowledge Base Bot involves connecting Microsoft 365 Copilot to a curated set of documents or data sources that form the bot's [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]. The bot is then trained to retrieve relevant information from these sources when answering user queries, ensuring responses are grounded in organizational context. This approach is particularly useful for [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] with company policies, procedures, or specialized domain knowledge are critical.

## Use Cases

Knowledge Base Bots are commonly deployed for internal support functions, such as helping employees understand company policies, navigate procedures, or access institutional knowledge. They can also be adapted for customer-facing [[concepts/software|applications]] where consistent, accurate information delivery is important. By combining Copilot's natural language capabilities with a controlled knowledge base, these bots can reduce the burden on human support staff while maintaining quality and relevance of information provided.
