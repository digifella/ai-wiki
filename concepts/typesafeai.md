# TypeSafe.ai

**[[entities/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]]** is an [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] company focused on creating reliable, decision-oriented [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] models. The organization is led by **[[entities/diogo-almeida|Diogo Almeida]]**, a co-inventor of [[entities/chatgpt]].

## Core Technology: Jev

The primary product of TypeSafe.ai is **Jev**, a [[concepts/ai-specialization|specialized AI]] model designed to address the limitations of traditional [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs).

### Key Characteristics
*   **Decision-Focused:** Explicitly engineered for reliable [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] rather than general [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]].
*   **[[concepts/zero-hallucinations|Zero Hallucinations]]:** Designed to eliminate the factual inaccuracies common in standard LLMs.
*   **High [[concepts/speed|Speed]]:** Optimized for fast [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] and response times.
*   **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]:** Prioritizes accuracy and trustworthiness in outputs.
