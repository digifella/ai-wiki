# Mental Health Support

[[concepts/mental-health|Mental health]] support encompasses a range of resources, services, and interventions designed to promote psychological [[concepts/health|wellbeing]] and address mental health conditions across diverse patient populations. These supports operate across [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]], early intervention, and treatment contexts, recognizing that mental [[concepts/patient-needs|health needs]] vary significantly by age, life circumstances, and clinical presentation. Effective mental health support integrates clinical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] with accessible community resources and peer support [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].

## Service Models and Settings

Mental health support is delivered through multiple settings and service models. Primary care providers often serve as first-[[entities/contact|contact]] points for mental health concerns, while specialist mental [[concepts/health-care|health services]] provide assessment and treatment for complex conditions. Community mental health teams, outpatient clinics, inpatient psychiatric units, and [[concepts/crisis|crisis]] services form part of the broader system. Digital and [[concepts/telehealth|telehealth]] platforms have expanded access to support, particularly in underserved areas.

## Key Components

Effective mental health support typically includes assessment and diagnosis, psychotherapy or [[concepts/offering-counseling-services|counseling]], medication management, psychosocial interventions, and peer support. Many services emphasize a biopsychosocial approach that considers biological, psychological, and [[concepts/social-psychological-factors|social factors]] contributing to mental health. Crisis support and emergency services address acute mental health emergencies and suicide risk, while longer-term support focuses on recovery, resilience-building, and community integration.

## Access and Outcomes

Barriers to mental health support include stigma, cost, workforce shortages, and geographic limitations. Equitable access remains a significant challenge, with disparities often affecting marginalized populations. Outcomes of mental health support vary by condition, intervention type, and individual circumstances, with evidence supporting the effectiveness of various psychological therapies, medications, and combined approaches in reducing symptoms and improving [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].
