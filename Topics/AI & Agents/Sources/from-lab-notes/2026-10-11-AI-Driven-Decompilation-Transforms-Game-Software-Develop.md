## AI-Driven Decompilation Transforms Game Software Development and Modification
**Clip title:** Everything has been solved
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX2kD2yQf88

### Summary
The video discusses the disruptive impact of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) on the field of [[concepts/coding|software development]], particularly in game creation and modification. The main thesis is that AI's [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] at [[concepts/writing|writing]] and understanding code is fundamentally changing how software is developed and interacted with, enabling unprecedented levels of modification and recreation. The presenter demonstrates this by showcasing various examples of beloved game characters and mechanics being seamlessly integrated into different game worlds, such as [[entities/mario|Mario]] appearing in *[[entities/elden-ring|Elden Ring]]* or *Minecraft* elements in *Grand Theft Auto V*, and even a free, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] clone of [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] being created with AI.

At the core of this transformation is "decompilation," the process of converting compiled machine code back into human-readable source code. Traditionally, this was an arduous, painstaking process, requiring years of collaborative effort from human teams for even a single game, involving extensive "guess and check" iterations to match functionality. However, AI has revolutionized this by automating and accelerating the process. The presenter highlights that AI is ideally suited for decompilation due to its ability to handle code-heavy tasks and engage in verifiable, iterative [[concepts/loops|loops]]. AI can tirelessly propose code, test it against the desired output (the original game's behavior), and refine it, dramatically reducing the time frame from years to mere days, especially when tasks are distributed among parallel [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].

The video further elaborates on how AI facilitates various forms of game "mashups." These include simple **asset swaps**, where the [[concepts/presence|appearance]] of a character is changed while its underlying mechanics remain the same; **pass-through [[concepts/mods|mods]]**, which involve running two different game engines simultaneously and using an AI-powered bridge to translate actions and synchronize elements between them (e.g., *Minecraft* explosions causing damage in *GTA V*); and **mechanic transfers**, where specific behaviors like parkour systems or [[concepts/physics|physics]] are recreated in a new game [[concepts/engine|engine]]. The most intensive method, but increasingly feasible with AI, is **rebuilding the entire game** from scratch by observing its functionality and reconstructing its rules, which the presenter is attempting with *Super Mario [[entities/earth|World]]* for a handheld retro [[concepts/cli|console]].

The overarching conclusion extends beyond [[concepts/gaming|gaming]]: anything with a *verifiable outcome* is essentially "solved" by AI. This means AI can observe virtually any software, from Photoshop to [[entities/excel|Excel]], and recreate its functionality without needing direct access to the original source code. This capability extends to complex fields like [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], where AI has already generated novel proofs and discoveries, surpassing [[concepts/human-performance|human capabilities]] in certain areas. The presenter argues that the "days of human solving [[concepts/mathematics|math]] alone are gone," and this principle [[entities/will|will]] apply to [[concepts/science|science]], [[concepts/material-discovery|material discovery]], and other domains. In this rapidly evolving landscape, the unique value of [[concepts/human-ingenuity|human creativity]], taste, and the ability to connect emotionally with an audience becomes paramount. While AI can generate endless content, the human element of crafting compelling narratives, designing truly fun experiences, or creating art that resonates remains irreplaceable, emphasizing that human perspective is more important than ever in a [[entities/earth|world]] full of AI-generated noise.
