# Underground Living - Coober Pedy

Underground living is a form of habitation where dwellings are built or excavated within the earth's surface. This technique provides natural insulation from extreme temperatures and can be an environmentally sustainable option in challenging climates.

## Key Concepts
- **Thermal [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]]:** The earth maintains stable temperatures, reducing heating and cooling needs.
- **Sustainability:** Reduced energy consumption and protection against harsh weather conditions.
- **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]:** Innovative designs to accommodate modern living while utilizing natural resources efficiently.

## Examples of Underground Living
- [[entities/coober-pedy]]: A town in Australia where residents live underground to escape extreme desert temperatures.
- Other examples include:
  - Bamyan Cave City
  - Sudanese Underwater Village
