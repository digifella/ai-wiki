# System Intelligence

**System Intelligence** refers to the capacity of computational systems to adapt, optimize, and execute tasks through evolving architectural paradigms. Current trends indicate a strategic pivot from scaling model parameters toward enhancing Efficiency and Specialization via [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE) architectures.

## Key Developments

*   **Architectural Shift**: The industry is moving away from monolithic, uniformly scaled models toward specialized, efficient structures that prioritize performance-per-watt and task-specific accuracy [[lab-notes/2026-09-28-AI-Model-Evolution-Efficiency-Specialization-and-NASA-IB|AI Model Evolution: Efficiency, Specialization, and NASA-IBM Lunar AI]].
*   **Mixture of Experts (MoE)**: As highlighted in recent [[entities/ibm-technology|IBM Technology]] discussions, MoE allows models to activate only relevant sub-networks for specific inputs, significantly reducing computational overhead while maintaining high intelligence outputs.
*   **Domain-Specific Intelligence**: There is a growing emphasis on specialized AI applications, such as the collaboration between [[entities/nasa]] and IBM for lunar operations, demonstrating how system intelligence is being tailored for extreme, high-stakes environments.
*   **Efficiency Metrics**: Modern evaluation frameworks increasingly weigh [[concepts/inference-speed|inference speed]] and energy consumption alongside traditional accuracy metrics, reflecting the need for sustainable AI deployment.
