# Dr Eric Verdin

Dr. Eric Verdin is a researcher specializing in aging and longevity science. His work examines the biological and social factors that influence human lifespan and healthy aging, with particular emphasis on how social attitudes toward aging affect measurable health outcomes.

## Ageism and Health Outcomes

Verdin has contributed to scientific understanding of the relationship between ageism and individual wellbeing. His research explores how social perceptions and prejudice related to age can influence health trajectories, suggesting that defying ageist attitudes and social norms may support longer, healthier lives. This work bridges gerontology with social psychology, demonstrating connections between psychological factors and physiological aging processes.

His research has reached public audiences through media outlets including BBC Future, where his findings on ageism and longevity have been featured in discussions about extending healthspan and challenging age-related stereotypes.
