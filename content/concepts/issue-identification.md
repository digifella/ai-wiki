# Issue Identification

Issue identification is the process of recognizing, detecting, and categorizing problems, concerns, or gaps within a document, system, or [[concepts/workflow|workflow]]. It serves as a foundational step in [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] and [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]], enabling teams to pinpoint what needs [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]], analysis, or remediation.

## Application in Legal Document Review

Issue identification has practical [[concepts/software|applications]] in specialized workflows such as [[concepts/legal-document-review|legal document review]]. [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] integrated into common productivity software—such as [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s [[concepts/integration|integration]] with [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]]—can assist in identifying legal issues, inconsistencies, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] concerns, and textual problems within documents. By operating within familiar applications rather than requiring users to switch between tools, these systems [[entities/make|make]] issue identification more accessible as part of a document's editing process.

## Significance

The effectiveness of any problem-solving initiative depends largely on the quality of issue identification. Accurate and comprehensive recognition of issues allows organizations and individuals to prioritize resources appropriately, address root causes rather than symptoms, and implement targeted solutions. In document-focused work, systematic issue identification reduces the risk of overlooking critical problems before finalization or submission.
