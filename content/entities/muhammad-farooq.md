# Muhammad Farooq

Muhammad Farooq is known for his work in the field of [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) systems. He has been featured in [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]] and discussions about the construction and [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] of [[concepts/contextualized-language-understanding|RAG systems]], which are used to enhance [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] by integrating [[concepts/external-data|external data]] retrieval with generative [[concepts/models|models]].

His work has been highlighted in collaborative content, including resources shared by [[entities/dr-paul-cooper|Dr. Paul Cooper]], Director of [[concepts/getting-ready|Longboardfella Consulting]], who has promoted materials on building smarter RAG systems featuring Farooq's contributions.
