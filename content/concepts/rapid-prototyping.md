# Rapid Prototyping in AI Image Generation

Rapid prototyping is a crucial aspect of developing and iterating on [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], particularly for [[concepts/software|applications]] like image generation. This approach allows developers to quickly test hypotheses, refine algorithms, and enhance [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] through iterative cycles of [[concepts/design|design]], development, [[concepts/testing|testing]], and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

- Utilizes agile methodologies to speed up the product development cycle.
- Emphasizes quick iterations and frequent [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] from users or stakeholders.
- Enables teams to experiment with different features and functionalities rapidly.

### New Developments in Rapid Prototyping for AI Image Generation

- **Google [[concepts/nano-banana-2|Nano Banana 2]]** introduces a significant leap in rapid prototyping capabilities, combining speed with professional-grade quality.
- The model offers advanced features such as web utilization, making it easier to generate [[concepts/images|images]] based on internet data.
- Users can leverage the new features to create and test prototypes more efficiently.
