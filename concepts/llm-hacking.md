# LLM Hacking

**LLM Hacking** refers to the exploitation of vulnerabilities in [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) and AI-driven systems to facilitate malicious activities, ranging from data exfiltration to automated attack orchestration. This concept encompasses both direct manipulation of model outputs and the use of AI as a force multiplier for traditional cyber threats.

## Key Threat Vectors

*   **Prompt Injection & [[concepts/jailbreaking|Jailbreaking]]:** Manipulating input prompts to bypass safety filters or extract sensitive [[concepts/custom-dataset|training data]].
*   **AI-Powered Campaigns:** Leveraging AI to create more sophisticated, scalable, and adaptive cyberattacks [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-AI-Powered-Cyberattacks-Dark-Sourcery-LLM-Hacking-and-Ag|AI-Powered Cyberattacks: Dark Sourcery, LLM Hacking, and Agent Swarms]].
*   **[[concepts/agent-collaboration|Agent Swarms]]:** Utilizing coordinated [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] to execute complex, multi-stage attacks that mimic human behavior.
*   **Trust Exploitation:** Exploiting the inherent trust users place in chatbots and AI assistants to deliver social engineering payloads.
