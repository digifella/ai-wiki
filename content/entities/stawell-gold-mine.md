# Stawell Gold Mine

Stawell Gold Mine is a gold mining operation located in Stawell, Victoria, Australia. The mine is situated in the [[concepts/western-victoria|Western Victoria]] goldfields region, an area with a significant history of gold extraction dating back to the 19th century.

## Operations

The mine has operated as both an underground and open-pit operation [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] various stages of its history. It has been a major employer and economic driver for the Stawell region, contributing to the local economy through employment and mineral production.

## Historical Significance

The Stawell area became prominent during the Victorian gold rush, and gold mining has remained an important industry in the region. The Stawell Gold Mine represents the continuation of this mining [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]] into the modern era.
