## LongCat 2.0: China's Nvidia-Free 1.6T AI Model Achieves Top Performance
**Clip title:** LongCat-2.0: China Breaks Free From Nvidia to Train a 1.6T Model
**Author / channel:** Fahd Mirza
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=paJN1Og1dT4

### Summary
Meituan, a [[entities/xiaomi|Chinese tech giant]] primarily known for its food delivery service (often dubbed China's DoorDash), has quietly released LongCat 2.0, a formidable [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]]. This new [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] stands out not just for its impressive scale but notably for being trained entirely on domestic AI ASIC superpods, rather than [[concepts/nvidia-server-chips|NVIDIA GPUs]]. This [[concepts/success|achievement]] signals a significant shift in the landscape of [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]] training, demonstrating that high-scale [[concepts/knowledge-acquisition|model development]] is no longer exclusively reliant on NVIDIA's hardware.

LongCat 2.0 leverages a [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE) architecture, boasting a massive 1.6 trillion [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]], though only approximately 48 billion are actively engaged for any given token, a design choice enabled by its "LongCat [[concepts/sparse-attention-architecture|Sparse Attention]]" mechanism. This [[concepts/architectural-innovation|architectural innovation]], which evolves from the [[concepts/deepseek-ai|DeepSeek]] Sparse Attention approach, efficiently addresses common bottlenecks in [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]]. It optimizes [[concepts/memory|memory]] access, streamlines [[concepts/data-indexing|indexing]] processes by allowing neighboring layers to share a single indexing pass, and uses a coarse-to-fine scoring method to narrow down candidates before expensive fine-grained scoring. The pre-training involved over 35 trillion [[concepts/tokens|tokens]], reportedly without any catastrophic loss spikes or rollbacks, underscoring the [[concepts/robustness|robustness]] of their domestic infrastructure.

In terms of performance, LongCat 2.0 proves to be genuinely competitive. Benchmarks show it consistently outperforming [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini-models|Gemini 1.5]] Pro and trading blows with [[entities/opus-45|Claude Opus 4.5]] across various [[concepts/coding|coding]] and agentic tasks. It even pulls ahead on some challenging benchmarks like [[concepts/swe-bench-verified|SWE-Bench]] Pro. While it may not surpass the absolute cutting-edge of *closed-source* [[concepts/frontier-models|frontier models]] like [[entities/claude-opus-48|Opus 4.8]], it effectively closes the gap with last generation's best offerings. The video showcased several compelling demonstrations, including generating a complex, interactive "Global Railway Convergence Hub" as a single HTML file with features like day/night cycles and various train types. It also successfully translated a sentence into over 80 languages, including ancient Elder Futhark runes, and accurately solved a detailed staff scheduling problem with multiple constraints, presenting a perfectly aligned schedule.

Overall, Meituan's LongCat 2.0 represents a significant leap for [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. Its impressive scale, combined with its innovative architecture and, crucially, its successful training on non-NVIDIA hardware, marks it as a key development in the global AI race. The model demonstrates advanced capabilities in [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], extensive multilingual translation, and [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]], solidifying Meituan's position as a serious contender in the [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] domain.
