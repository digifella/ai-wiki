# FFmpeg

FFmpeg is a comprehensive [[concepts/command-line-interface|command-line]] multimedia framework used for processing video and audio files. It provides a suite of tools for recording, converting, and streaming media content across various formats and codecs. The [[concepts/software|software]] is widely used in professional and amateur video production, content delivery, and [[concepts/transcoding|media conversion]] workflows.

## Core Functionality

The FFmpeg toolkit enables users to perform a wide range of operations including format conversion, video encoding and decoding, [[concepts/audio-processing|audio processing]], and stream manipulation. Users interact with FFmpeg primarily through command-line interfaces, specifying input files, output [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], and various filters or transformations. The tool supports hundreds of video and audio codecs, making it highly versatile for different media requirements.

## Common Applications

Developers and media professionals use FFmpeg for tasks such as transcoding videos between formats, adjusting resolution and bitrate, extracting audio from video files, and applying visual or audio filters. It is frequently integrated into larger applications and [[concepts/automation|automation]] scripts for batch processing and [[concepts/workflow-optimization|workflow optimization]]. The cross-platform [[entities/nature|nature]] of FFmpeg makes it suitable for use on Linux, macOS, and [[entities/windows|Windows]] systems.
