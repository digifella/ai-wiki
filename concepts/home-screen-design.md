# Home Screen Design

## Overview
Home screen design refers to the layout, organization, and visual elements of the primary interface users interact with on their devices. It plays a crucial role in [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] (UX) and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

## Key Concepts
- **Widgets**: Interactive elements that display information or provide quick actions.
- **Grid System**: The underlying structure that organizes app icons and widgets.
- **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Options for users to personalize their home screen layout, themes, and wallpapers.
- **Dynamic Elements**: Features that adapt based on user behavior or context.
