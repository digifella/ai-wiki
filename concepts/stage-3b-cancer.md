# Stage 3B cancer

**Stage 3B cancer** refers to a specific classification of advanced malignancy where the tumor has grown significantly and/or spread to nearby lymph nodes or tissues, but has not yet metastasized to distant organs. It is generally considered locally advanced.

## Clinical Context
- Typically involves breast cancer where the tumor is large or has spread to the chest wall or skin (inflammatory breast cancer).
- Often requires multimodal treatment including chemotherapy, radiation, and surgery.
- Prognosis varies based on receptor status and response to neoadjuvant therapy.
