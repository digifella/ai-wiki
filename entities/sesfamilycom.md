# Sesfamilycom

Sesfamilycom is a digital health application developed at the University of Copenhagen. The platform provides interactive modules focused on conflict resolution and interpersonal communication, aiming to assist users in managing disputes and relationship challenges within personal and family contexts. The application serves as an educational tool designed to improve communication strategies and conflict resolution abilities.

It delivers evidence-based training through guided, interactive lessons that break down complex interpersonal dynamics into practical skills. These modules allow users to engage with scenarios that simulate real-life conflicts, offering structured guidance on de-escalation and constructive dialogue. The content is grounded in psychological research to ensure the techniques provided are effective for improving relational outcomes.

The app is intended for individuals seeking to enhance their ability to navigate difficult conversations and resolve disagreements. By providing accessible, self-paced learning resources, it aims to empower users with the tools necessary to foster healthier interactions in their personal lives. The development process involved collaboration between academic researchers and practitioners to ensure the material is both scientifically valid and practically applicable.
