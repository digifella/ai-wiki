## High-Performing Teams and Project Aristotle

Understanding [[concepts/group-dynamics|team dynamics]] is critical for effective conflict resolution. [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Project-Aristotle-Googles-Data-Driven-Insights-on-High-P|Project Aristotle: Google’s Data-Driven Insights on High-Performing Teams.]] highlights that [[concepts/psychological-safety|psychological safety]] is the foundational pillar of high-performing teams, outweighing individual talent or cognitive ability. Key insights include:

*   **[[concepts/vulnerability|Psychological Safety]]**: The belief that one [[entities/will|will]] not be punished or humiliated for speaking up with [[concepts/ideas|ideas]], questions, concerns, or mistakes.
*   **Dependability**: Team members count on each other to do [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] work by deadlines.
*   **Structure & [[concepts/clarity-slider|Clarity]]**: Clear roles, plans, and goals enable [[concepts/accountability|accountability]].
*   **Meaning & Impact**: Personal significance of work and [[concepts/meaning-in-life|sense of purpose]] contribute to [[concepts/team-performance|team performance]].
*   **Outcome**: Teams with higher psychological safety exhibit better [[concepts/learning|learning]] behaviors, [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]], and conflict resolution outcomes.
