# Great Train Journey

Great Train Journey refers to notable [[concepts/rail-travel|rail journeys]] and train-based holiday experiences available in Australia. These services combine transportation with leisure and sightseeing, allowing passengers to travel through diverse Australian landscapes by rail. Train holidays function as an established tourism category in Australia, ranging from short scenic routes to multi-day expeditions that traverse remote and regional areas.

## Major Australian Train Services

Australia operates several recognised train services marketed primarily as tourist experiences. The Indian Pacific connects [[entities/sydney|Sydney]] and Perth, traversing the continent across three time zones and passing through the Nullarbor Plain. Other prominent long-distance services include the Ghan, which runs from Adelaide to [[entities/charles-darwin|Darwin]] through the [[concepts/red-centre-of-australia|Red Centre]], and the West Coast Voyager, which links Perth to Kalbarri along the western coast. These journeys are characterized by their emphasis on panoramic viewing, onboard accommodation, and access to areas not easily reachable by other modes of transport.

## Regional and Scenic Routes

Beyond the major intercity expeditions, numerous regional operators provide shorter scenic trips. Services such as the Spirit of Queensland and the Spirit of the [[concepts/australian-outback|Outback]] offer connections between major cities and regional hubs, while specialized tourist trains like the Puffing Billy Railway in Victoria and the Kuranda Scenic Railway in Queensland focus on [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]] and natural beauty. These shorter excursions often highlight specific geological features, rainforests, or historical rail [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], catering to day-trippers and weekend travelers seeking localized sightseeing opportunities.
