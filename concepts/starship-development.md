## Artemis II Integration

The [[concepts/artemis-ii-mission|Artemis II mission]] represents a key testing ground for [[concepts/starship|starship]] systems in [[concepts/human-spaceflight|crewed spaceflight]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. Simulated mission operations evaluate how starship components perform alongside the [[entities/orion|Orion spacecraft]], which serves as the crew module for lunar transit. These simulations assess [[concepts/crew-operations|crew operations]] protocols, system redundancy, and the practical integration of hardware interfaces.
