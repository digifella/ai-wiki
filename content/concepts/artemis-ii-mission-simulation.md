# Artemis II Mission Simulation

The Artemis II Mission Simulation is an operational [[concepts/exercise|exercise]] that documents the planned crewed flight of NASA's [[entities/artemis-ii|Artemis II mission]]. Based on analysis from mission reports and supporting documentation, the [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] examines the performance of critical [[concepts/spacecraft-systems|spacecraft systems]], crew procedures, and vehicle operations across the complete mission profile, extending from launch [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] through Earth return. The exercise functions as a planning and evaluation tool to assess mission readiness and identify potential operational challenges before crewed flight.

## Mission Profile and Systems Analysis

The simulation provides detailed documentation of crew activities, decision points, and system [[concepts/responses|responses]] throughout key mission phases. It tracks [[entities/orion|Orion spacecraft]] performance, including life support systems, thermal management, and guidance and navigation functions. The exercise also incorporates updates regarding [[concepts/starship-development|Starship development]] and integration, which inform broader [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]]. By simulating nominal and contingency [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], the mission planners can validate procedures, timeline allocations, and crew workload across the lunar trajectory and return sequence.

## Operational Planning Applications

Results from the simulation support flight readiness reviews and inform refinements to crew [[concepts/training|training]] protocols and operational procedures. The exercise identifies technical constraints, timing dependencies, and interface requirements between vehicle systems that must be addressed before human flight. Through iterative analysis of the simulated mission profile, NASA engineers and flight controllers can optimize resource utilization and improve confidence in the mission's execution.
