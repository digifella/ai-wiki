# MedGemma 27B

[[concepts/dermatology-images|MedGemma 27B]] is a 27 billion parameter [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]], specifically designed to handle medical domain tasks involving both [[concepts/text|text]] and [[concepts/images|images]]. As part of [[entities/google|Google]]'s broader effort to apply [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] to [[concepts/health|healthcare]] [[concepts/software|applications]], the model represents an adaptation of foundational [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] optimized for medical comprehension and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].

## Capabilities and Design

The model is trained to process and understand medical literature, clinical documents, and medical imagery. Its dual capability in text and image comprehension makes it suitable for tasks that require analysis of medical records, diagnostic imaging, and clinical text data. The 27 billion parameter scale positions it as a mid-to-large sized model, balancing [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] with performance capacity for specialized medical applications.

## Context and Development

MedGemma was introduced as part of Google's initiative to develop specialized medical [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]]. The model builds on existing [[entities/gemma|Gemma]] architecture, with domain-specific [[concepts/training|training]] to improve its effectiveness in healthcare contexts where [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and domain knowledge are critical.
