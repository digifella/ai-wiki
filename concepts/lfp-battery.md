# LFP battery

**Lithium Iron Phosphate** (LFP) is a type of Lithium-ion battery chemistry known for its safety, long cycle life, and lower cost compared to NMC or NCA chemistries, though it typically suffers from lower energy density.

## Key Characteristics

*   **Chemistry**: LiFePO₄
*   **Advantages**:
    *   High thermal stability (reduced fire risk)
    *   Long cycle life (2000+ cycles)
    *   Cobalt-free (ethical sourcing)
*   **Disadvantages**:
    *   Lower energy density than nickel-based chemistries
    *   Performance degradation in extreme cold
