# Design

Design is a broad field encompassing visual, industrial, [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] (UX), graphic, and interactive design disciplines. It involves creating layouts, interfaces, branding elements, and other aesthetic features that enhance usability and appeal.

- Visual [[concepts/hierarchy|Hierarchy]]
- [[concepts/user-interface|User Interface]] (UI)
- Brand Identity

- Interaction Design
