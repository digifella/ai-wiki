# Pacing the Frontier

**Pacing the frontier** is a strategic [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] development advocating for measured, deliberate progress rather than uncontrolled acceleration. It emphasizes safety, alignment, and sustainable capability growth over raw speed-to-market.

## Core Principles
- **Deliberate Progress:** Prioritizing robust safety evaluations and alignment research alongside capability improvements.
- **[[concepts/risk-mitigation|Risk Mitigation]]:** Avoiding the deployment of models that outpace the community's ability to understand or control them.
- **Sustainable [[concepts/innovation|Innovation]]:** Focusing on long-term [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and efficiency rather than short-term competitive dominance.

- **[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] Ecosystem:** The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[concepts/ai-models|Claude Opus 5.5]] has intensified scrutiny on pacing strategies, particularly regarding its application in complex, high-stakes tasks.
