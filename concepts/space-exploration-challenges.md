# Space Exploration Challenges

## Overview
Persistent technical and environmental hurdles in deep space missions, planetary science, and atmospheric study.

### Planetary Atmospheres & Phenomena

- **[[concepts/pluto-atmosphere|Pluto's Atmosphere]]**: Observations indicate Pluto's atmosphere is fading, posing long-term challenges for surface temperature modeling and potential mission planning.

### Ocean Worlds & Access
- **Europa Subsurface Ocean**: Significant issues remain regarding water access from Europa's subsurface ocean. Current technology lacks the capability to penetrate the thick ice shell to sample or retrieve water directly, limiting our understanding of habitability potential.
