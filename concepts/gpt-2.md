# GPT-2

**[[entities/gpt-2|GPT-2]]** ([[concepts/generative-pre-trained-transformers|Generative Pre-trained Transformer]] 2) is a large-scale [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[entities/openai]]. It represents a significant step forward in [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]], demonstrating the power of [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] up [[concepts/code-size|model size]] and [[concepts/training-data|training data]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Architecture**: Based on the [[concepts/transformer-models|Transformer architecture]], specifically the decoder-only variant.
- **Scale**: Trained on 40GB of text data from the Common Crawl corpus, significantly larger than its predecessor GPT.
- **Capabilities**: Demonstrated strong few-shot [[concepts/learning|learning]] abilities, generating coherent and contextually relevant text across various domains.
- **Impact**: Highlighted the potential for emergent behaviors in [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]], paving the way for subsequent models like [[entities/gpt-3]].

## Evolution and Context
The development of GPT-2 laid the groundwork for understanding how [[concepts/parameter-scaling|scaling parameters]] and data impacts [[concepts/model-performance|model performance]]. It served as a critical benchmark before the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of more massive models.
