# Types Of Travel Documents

Travel documents are official credentials issued by governments that authorize citizens to cross international borders and establish their identity and citizenship status during travel. These documents serve as fundamental components of security infrastructure, enabling both individual mobility and state control over border security. The primary function of travel documents is to verify that a traveler is who they claim to be and has permission to enter or exit a country. The most widely recognized travel document is the passport, which serves as the standard international identifier for nationals.

## Electronic Travel Authorizations

In the context of modern digital infrastructure, electronic travel authorizations (eVisas) have become a critical tool for streamlining border management. Unlike traditional visas that require physical stamps or stickers in a passport, eVisas are digital records linked to the traveler's passport number. This system reduces administrative overhead for both issuing authorities and travelers, allowing for faster processing times and enhanced data accuracy.

## The Turkey eVisa System

The Turkey eVisa is a specific example of this digital infrastructure, designed for eligible foreign nationals visiting Turkey for tourism or business. Issued through an online platform, it eliminates the need for embassy visits or paper applications. The system validates the applicant's identity and eligibility against security databases before granting permission to travel. Upon arrival, border control officers verify the eVisa status electronically, ensuring that the traveler meets all entry requirements before being granted access to the country.
