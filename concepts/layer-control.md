# Layer Control

Layer Control is a functionality within Adobe Firefly's Prompt-to-Edit feature that enables selective modifications to specific elements or regions of digital images through text prompts. Rather than applying generative edits across an entire image, this capability allows creators to target particular layers or defined areas while preserving the rest of the composition unchanged. This selective approach provides greater precision when making adjustments to complex images with multiple elements.

## How It Works

The feature allows users to isolate specific parts of an image—whether through layer selection or spatial definition—and apply text-based editing instructions only to those areas. This means a creator can modify one object, person, or section of a composition without affecting surrounding elements. The generative AI processes the prompt in context with the selected region, producing edits that integrate naturally with the unchanged portions of the image.

## Practical Applications

Layer Control is particularly useful in workflows where composition integrity matters. Rather than regenerating entire images to fix or adjust specific elements, creators can make targeted modifications while maintaining their original work's structure and other details. This reduces iteration time and gives users finer control over the final result.
