# Apple TV+

Streaming subscription service operated by [[entities/apple]], launched 2019. Strategy centers exclusively on high-budget original content across film, series, and documentaries; platform does not [[concepts/license|license]] third-party titles. Bundled within Apple One and Apple Services tiers.
