# PDF classification

**PDF classification** refers to the process of categorizing PDF documents based on their content, structure, or [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] to facilitate downstream processing, [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], or analysis. This concept is critical for [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and [[concepts/document-management|document management]] systems that need to route or parse documents efficiently.

## Key Tools & Technologies

### Firecrawl pdf-inspector
A specialized tool for rapid PDF processing, emphasizing [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]].

- **Core Function:** Classifies [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]] and extracts content with [[entities/high-performance|high performance]].
- **Technical Stack:** Built with [[concepts/rust-programming-language|Rust]] for speed and efficiency.
- **Deployment:** Designed for [[concepts/local-processing|local processing]], enhancing [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and reducing latency.
- **Use Case:** Optimized for AI agents requiring fast, 100x faster [[concepts/pdf-parsing|PDF parsing]] compared to traditional methods.
