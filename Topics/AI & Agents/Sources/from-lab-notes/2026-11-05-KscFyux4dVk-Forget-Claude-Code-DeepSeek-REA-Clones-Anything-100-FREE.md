### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

The video presented by Maitis introduces a free workflow using DeepSeek Harness, REA (Reverse Engineer Anything), and OpenRouter to build functional app clones without writing code or paying for expensive AI subscriptions. The speaker demonstrates this setup by recreating working versions of Slack and ChatGPT entirely from plain-English prompts.

The workflow is broken down into three key levels:
1. **Setup:** Installing and configuring DeepSeek Harness as the AI developer.
2. **Connection:** Linking OpenRouter to access free AI models and REA to analyze existing app structures.
3. **Building:** Using detailed prompts to direct the AI tools to generate fully functional app interfaces and features.

The video concludes that users can leverage this combination of free AI tools and reverse-engineering capabilities to prototype and build functional applications without requiring technical coding skills or paid software licenses.
