# OECD

## Overview
The **[[concepts/oecd|OECD]]** (Organization for Economic Co-operation and Development) is an international organization consisting of 38 member countries, mostly in the developed [[entities/earth|world]], that promotes [[concepts/policies|policies]] to improve the economic and [[concepts/social-well-being|social well-being]] of people around the world. It provides a forum to [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] experiences, seek solutions to common problems, and coordinate domestic and international policies.

## Key Functions & Frameworks
- **Economic Analysis**: Provides data, statistics, and analysis on economies, trade, and development.
- **Policy [[concepts/recommendations|Recommendations]]**: Issues guidelines on corporate [[concepts/governance|governance]], anti-corruption, and tax avoidance (e.g., BEPS).
- **Education**: Publishes the Programme for International Student Assessment (PISA).
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] & Data [[concepts/secure|Protection]]**:
  - Established the OECD [[concepts/privacy-principles|Privacy Principles]], a foundational framework for international [[concepts/internet-security|data protection]] standards.
  - Influences global regulations such as the [[concepts/gdpr]], [[concepts/hipaa]], and [[concepts/ccpa]].
