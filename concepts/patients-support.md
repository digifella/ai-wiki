# Patients' Support

**Patients' Support** encompasses systemic and clinical interventions designed to empower patients in their care journey, shifting from passive recipients to active partners. This includes [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]], emotional backing, and structural [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for Shared [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]] and Coproduction of care.

## Key Mechanisms

### Point-of-Care Dashboards
Digital tools displayed during clinical visits that visualize patient-specific data (e.g., disease activity scores, medication history) to facilitate transparent [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] between clinicians and patients.

- **Promotes Coproduction**: Visualizing data in real-time encourages patients to [[concepts/tone|voice]] concerns and collaborate on treatment [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], rather than passively accepting [[concepts/recommendations|directives]].
- **Evidence in IBD**: Research on Inflammatory Bowel Disease demonstrates that these dashboards improve patient engagement and satisfaction by making complex [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]] accessible and actionable during consultations.
