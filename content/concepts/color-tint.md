# Color Tint

Color Tint is a feature within [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]'s new [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]] that allows users to adjust the color cast and tonal qualities of their [[concepts/images|images]]. As part of Adobe's updated [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] workflow, it functions as a refinement tool for correcting or creatively shifting the overall [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and hue balance of a photograph or design.

## Integration with Color and Vibrance

The Color Tint controls work in conjunction with the broader Color and Vibrance tool introduced in Photoshop 2025. This tool consolidated several color adjustment capabilities into a single panel, providing a more streamlined approach to [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] compared to previous versions. Users can access tint [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] alongside vibrance, saturation, and other color modification options without switching between multiple dialogs.

## Practical Application

The Color Tint feature is particularly useful for correcting white balance issues, adapting images to specific color grading styles, and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] in portrait work. It gives photographers and designers a non-destructive way to shift color temperature across warm-to-cool spectrums and make targeted adjustments to how colors appear in their compositions.
