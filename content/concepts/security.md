# Security in AI Agents

Security is a critical aspect of deploying [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] (AI) [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] within enterprises and across various industries. As the reliance on AI increases, so do concerns about [[concepts/ai-security|data privacy]], [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and protection against malicious activities.

## Key Concepts
- **Data [[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]**: Protecting sensitive information from unauthorized access.
- **[[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]**: Ensuring that data is accurate and has not been tampered with.
- **Malware Protection**: Guarding against [[concepts/software|software]] designed to harm or exploit systems.
- **[[concepts/secure|Secure]] [[concepts/authentication|Authentication]]**: Verifying the identity of users and ensuring they have proper access levels.
- **Cloudflare [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Enhancing security through Cloudflare configurations for AI [[concepts/agents|agents]].
