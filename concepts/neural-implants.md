# Neural Implants

**Neural implants** are devices surgically placed in the brain or nervous system to interface with neural tissue, enabling [[concepts/brain-computer-interface]] capabilities, sensory [[concepts/preservation|restoration]], or therapeutic modulation. They represent a critical convergence of [[concepts/neuroscience]], Materials [[concepts/science|Science]], and [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]].

## Core Concepts & Mechanisms
- **Signal Transduction**: Conversion of neural electrical activity into [[concepts/digital-information|digital data]] and vice versa.
- **Biocompatibility**: Materials must minimize Glia and [[concepts/immune-system-function|immune response]] to maintain long-term signal fidelity.
- **Power & Data Transmission**: Wireless power delivery and high-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] data offloading are primary [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] constraints.

## Key Developments & Infrastructure (2026)
Recent advancements have shifted focus from proof-of-concept to scalable clinical infrastructure and minimally invasive surgical techniques.

- **[[concepts/transdural-surgery|Transdural Surgery]]**: New protocols emphasize transdural approaches to reduce trauma and recovery time compared to traditional craniotomy methods. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-09-Neuralink-2026-Infrastructure-Transdural-Surgery-and-Pat|Neuralink 2026: Infrastructure, Transdural Surgery, and Patient Impact]] for detailed surgical workflows.
- **Infrastructure [[concepts/computational-scaling|Scaling]]**: Deployment of dedicated surgical suites and automated implantation robots to standardize procedure safety and efficiency.
- **Patient Impact**: Early clinical data suggests improved motor control restoration and potential applications in treating Paralysis and Neurodegenerative Diseases.

## Major Entities & Projects
- **[[entities/neura-pod-neuralink|Neuralink]]**: Leading private BCI [[concepts/developer|developer]] focusing on high-channel-count thread-based implants.
- **Synchron**: Competitor utilizing stentrode technology for vascular-based implantation.
- **Blackrock Neurotech**: Established player in research-grade BCI systems.

## Challenges & Ethical Considerations
- **Long-term Stability**: Degradation of electrode-tissue interface over time.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Data Privacy]]**: [[concepts/security|Security]] of neural data streams and cognitive privacy rights.
- **Access Equity**: High costs may limit availability to wealthy demographics initially.
