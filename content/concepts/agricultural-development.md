# Agricultural Development

Agricultural development refers to efforts aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] improving farming techniques, increasing crop yields, and enhancing the overall productivity of agricultural systems. These initiatives often involve technological advancements, infrastructure improvements, and [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]] designed to support sustainable growth in rural areas.

## Key Concepts
- **Sustainable [[concepts/agriculture|Agriculture]]**: Practices that aim to meet current food needs without compromising future generations' ability to produce food.
- **[[concepts/large-scale-irrigation|Irrigation Systems]]**: Technology used to deliver water to crops in regions where rainfall is insufficient for optimal crop growth.
- **Subsistence Farming vs. Commercial Farming**: Differences between farming aimed at meeting the farmer's family needs and farming oriented towards market production.
