## Why I Use DxO PureRAW 6 on Every Photo I Edit
**Clip title:** Why I Use DxO PureRAW 6 on Every Photo I Edit
**Author / channel:** Mark Dumbleton
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qFCPbvBBWDI
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Mark Dumbleton: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video demonstrates the use of DxO PureRAW 6 on three wildlife photographs taken at different ISO levels: ISO 100, ISO 6400, and ISO 25,600. The speaker, an affiliate of DxO, explains why he uses PureRAW as the first step in his editing workflow, beyond just noise reduction.

At ISO 100, the speaker shows that even low ISO images can benefit from PureRAW, particularly through lens sharpness optimization, which enhances fine details like feathers even when a photo is already sharp. For the ISO 6400 image of lion cubs, the software cleans the background while preserving fine fur texture, comparing favorably to Lightroom's noise reduction. At ISO 25,600, used for a caracal in very low light with a narrow aperture, PureRAW significantly reduces noise while maintaining usable detail and color.

The speaker explains how DeepPRIME technology combines noise reduction with demosaicing to process raw sensor data into clean, detailed color images. He also highlights a new high-fidelity DNG compression feature in PureRAW 6, which creates files about four times smaller than uncompressed DNGs without a noticeable loss in image quality. 

While acknowledging that PureRAW adds an extra processing step, increases file size (though improved in version 6), and has a cost, the speaker concludes that the improved starting image quality makes it an indispensable part of his editing workflow.
