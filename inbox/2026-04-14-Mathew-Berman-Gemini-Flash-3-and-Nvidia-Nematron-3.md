# AI News Summary

### **[[entities/gemini-3-flash|Gemini 3 Flash]] Released**

Google has launched **Gemini 3 Flash**, a model focused on speed, efficiency, and low cost.

* **Performance:** Nearly as good as [[entities/gemini-3-pro|Gemini 3 Pro]] and beats it on some benchmarks. It scored **78% on [[concepts/swe-bench-verified|SWE-bench Verified]]** (coding), beating Gemini 3 Pro and [[entities/claude-sonnet|Claude Sonnet]] 4.5.
* **Cost:** Priced at **$0.50 per million input tokens**, making it significantly cheaper than competitors (1/4th the cost of Gemini 3 Pro, 1/6th of Claude Sonnet 4.5).
* **[[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]:** Multimodal (video, audio, text) and currently the #3 model globally on the [[concepts/artificial-analysis-intelligence-index|Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index]].
* **Availability:** Available now in the [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini app]], Workspace products, and [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]].

### **NVIDIA Launches Nemotron 3**

NVIDIA released the **Nemotron 3** family of [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]], ideal for those wanting to own their data and fine-tune.

* **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]:** [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE) models that are 4x faster than the previous generation.
* **Sizes:**
	* **Nano:** 30B [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] (3B active).
	* **Super:** 100B parameters (10B active).
	* **Ultra:** 500B parameters (50B active).
* **Ecosystem:** Includes new tooling and datasets (3 trillion tokens) for [[concepts/training|training]] and is supported by platforms like [[entities/hugging-face|Hugging Face]] and [[entities/lm-studio|LM Studio]].

### **New ChatGPT Image Model**

OpenAI released a new version of **[[entities/chatgpt-images|ChatGPT Images]]**.

* **Improvements:** 4x faster generation, better text rendering, and more reliable [[concepts/instruction-following|instruction following]].
* **Editing:** Features precise editing capabilities.
* **Examples:** demonstrated high [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in complex prompts (e.g., specific grid layouts) and produced a humorous firefighter calendar image of [[entities/sam-altman|Sam Altman]].

### **Zoom’s "Federated AI" Breakthrough**

Zoom has unexpectedly entered the [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]] conversation with **Zoom Federated AI**.

* **Benchmark:** Scored **48.1% on Humanity's Last Exam**, beating Gemini 3 Pro (45.8%) and GPT-5 Pro (42%).
* **Method:** Instead of a single model, it uses a proprietary "Z-scorer system" to route prompts to the best available model (combining open-source, closed-source, and Zoom's own small models) for optimal results.

### **Bernie Sanders & Data Centers**

Senator Bernie Sanders has called for a moratorium on building new data centers.

* **The Argument:** Sanders cites concerns over unregulated growth and energy consumption benefitting only the 1%.
* **The Counter-Argument:** The host argues this would concede the AI race to China and suggests the US should focus on building more energy infrastructure (creating jobs for electricians, architects, etc.) rather than halting progress.

### **Integrations & Investments**

* **Apple Music & Adobe:** Apple Music is coming to ChatGPT, allowing users to create playlists via prompts. Adobe apps ([[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], Acrobat) are also integrating, signaling ChatGPT's shift toward becoming a "default entry point for the internet."
* **Amazon & OpenAI:** Amazon is in talks to invest **$10 billion** into OpenAI at a valuation exceeding **$500 billion**. The deal would require OpenAI to use Amazon's custom **Trainium [[concepts/ai-chips|AI chips]]**.

### **Meta Releases SAM Audio**

Meta introduced **SAM Audio** (Segment Anything Model Audio), the latest in the SAM 3 family.

* **Capabilities:** An [[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]] that allows users to isolate, extract, or remove specific sounds from complex audio mixtures (e.g., isolating a specific person speaking in a noisy restaurant or a bird call from ambient noise).
