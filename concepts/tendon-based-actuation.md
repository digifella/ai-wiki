# Tendon-Based Actuation

[[concepts/ambition|Drive]] mechanism transmitting torque via flexible elements (cables, tendons, strings) from remote actuators to joints. Enables distal [[concepts/weight-loss|weight reduction]], inherent [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and biomimetic kinematics. Prevalent in Humanoid [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]], prosthetics, and dexterous manipulators.

## Key Properties
- **Remote Actuation:** Decouples motor mass from moving links, minimizing [[concepts/inertia|inertia]] and improving dynamic responsiveness.
- **Compliance:** Tendon elasticity provides passive [[concepts/energy-dissipation|damping]], energy [[entities/storage|storage]], and collision tolerance.
- **Biomimetic Material Inspiration:** Research into [[concepts/biomimicry|biomimicry]] highlights [[concepts/spider-silk|spider silk]] as a benchmark for [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] synthetic tendons due to its exceptional [[concepts/strength|strength]] and [[concepts/toughness|toughness]] relative to weight.

## Biomimicry: Spider Silk Integration
The [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of optimal tendon materials drives interest in natural analogs. Spider silk exhibits a superior strength-to-weight ratio and toughness compared to many synthetic fibers, making it a critical reference for [[concepts/materials-science|materials science]] in robotics.

- **Strength & Toughness:** Spider silk demonstrates remarkable [[concepts/tensile-strength|tensile strength]] and energy absorption capabilities, often compared favorably to steel on a weight basis.
- **[[concepts/synthetic-production|Synthetic Production]] Challenges:** Replicating these properties synthetically remains difficult due to the complex protein folding and spinning [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] involved in natural production.
