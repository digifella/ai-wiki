# Deep Research Automation

Deep Research Automation is a workflow process that converts outputs from NotebookLM's Deep Research feature into formatted, professional websites without requiring coding knowledge. NotebookLM's Deep Research tool generates comprehensive research documents and structured information based on source materials provided by users. Deep Research Automation streamlines the subsequent step by taking these generated outputs and transforming them into web-ready formats suitable for publication or sharing.

## Process and Tools

The automation workflow typically involves exporting research documents from NotebookLM and processing them through no-code website builders or templated platforms. This allows researchers and professionals to present their findings in a polished, accessible format without manual HTML coding or web development expertise. The process bridges the gap between AI-generated research content and public-facing digital presentation.

## Use Cases

This approach is relevant for professionals who need to publish research findings, create knowledge bases, or share comprehensive analysis with non-technical audiences. It reduces the time and technical barriers between completing research and making it publicly available, particularly useful for academic researchers, business analysts, and content creators who rely on structured information gathering but lack web development skills.
