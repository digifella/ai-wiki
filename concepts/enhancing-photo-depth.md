# Enhancing Photo Depth

**Enhancing Photo Depth** is the post-processing technique of manipulating local [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], brightness, and color to create a three-dimensional illusion in two-dimensional images. The goal is to separate the subject from the background and guide the viewer's eye through the [[concepts/writing|composition]].

## Core Techniques

- **[[concepts/dodging|Dodging]] and [[concepts/burning|Burning]]**: The foundational method of selectively lightening (**Dodging**) and darkening (**Burning**) specific areas to simulate [[concepts/light|light]] falloff and volume.
	- Creates artificial [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] and highlights to define form.
- **Selective Contrast**: Increasing [[concepts/image-contrast|local contrast]] on focal points while reducing it in peripheral areas to draw [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]].
- **Color Temperature Shifts**: Warm tones advance visually, while cool tones recede; adjusting these locally can deepen spatial separation.
