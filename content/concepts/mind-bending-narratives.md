# Mind-Bending Narratives

**Mind-bending narratives** are [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]] structures designed to destabilize the audience's perception of reality, often employing Unreliable Narrator, non-linear timelines, or metaphysical twists. These narratives prioritize cognitive [[concepts/dissonance|dissonance]] and psychological engagement over linear plot progression, frequently blurring the lines between Dream vs. Reality and subjective experience.

## Core Mechanisms
*   **Reality Distortion**: Utilization of plot devices that question the [[entities/nature|nature]] of the depicted world (e.g., [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] Hypothesis, multiple Timelines).
*   **Psychological [[concepts/ambiguity|Ambiguity]]**: Internal conflicts manifested as external narrative crises, challenging the protagonist's (and viewer's) grasp on sanity.
*   **The Twist**: Structural subversion of expectations, often recontextualizing prior events.
