# Janus Electric

**[[concepts/charging-infrastructure|Janus Electric]]** is an Australian company specializing in the electrification of heavy-duty commercial vehicles through battery swapping technology. The company focuses on retrofitting existing diesel trucks rather than manufacturing new electric vehicles, aiming to accelerate fleet decarbonization by leveraging existing chassis infrastructure.

- **[[concepts/retrofit|Retrofit]] Strategy:** Janus Electric converts conventional diesel trucks to electric powertrains, allowing fleet operators to electrify assets without replacing the entire vehicle chassis Janus Electric: Battery Swapping Retrofit for Heavy-Duty Truck Electrification
