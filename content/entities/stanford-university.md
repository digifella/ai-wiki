# Stanford University

[[entities/stanford|Stanford]] University is a private research university located in Stanford, California. It is notable as the educational institution where [[entities/harper|Harper]] completed two degrees in Computer [[concepts/science|Science]], with a specialized focus on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].

## Connection to Harper

Harper earned both of her Computer Science degrees from Stanford, establishing the university as a foundational part of her academic background in AI. Her studies [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Stanford preceded her professional work in the field, including her subsequent position at [[entities/meta-ai|Meta]], where she worked on AI fundamentals and [[concepts/privacy-protection|privacy protection]].
