# OpenBMB

**[[concepts/openbmb|OpenBMB]]** (Open Big Model) is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] initiative and development team focused on creating efficient, [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and multimodal architectures. The group is known for optimizing [[concepts/models|models]] for [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]], [[concepts/on-device-inference|on-device inference]], and cost-effective [[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/integration|integration]].

## Key Projects & Models

- **MiniCPM Series**: Lightweight [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] optimized for edge devices and mobile platforms.
  - [[concepts/minicpm-v-46|MiniCPM-V 4.6]]: [[concepts/efficient-on-device-vision|Efficient On-Device Vision]] for [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI Agents]]: Released May 2026, this multimodal variant introduces efficient on-device [[concepts/vision-capabilities|vision capabilities]] specifically tailored for AI agents. It addresses latency and [[concepts/cost|cost]] issues associated with hosted [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] APIs by enabling local processing. See details in [[lab-notes/2026-05-20-MiniCPM-V-4.6-Efficient-On-Device-Vision-for-AI-Agents|MiniCPM-V 4.6: Efficient On-Device Vision for AI Agents]].

## Focus Areas

- **[[concepts/mobile-ai|On-Device AI]]**: Prioritizing models that run efficiently on consumer [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] without cloud dependency.
- **Multimodal Integration**: Combining [[concepts/text|text]] and vision [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] in compact architectures.
- **[[concepts/agent-efficiency|Agent Efficiency]]**: Reducing [[concepts/inference|inference]] costs and latency for [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]].
