# Amy C. Edmondson

**Amy C. Edmondson** is a prominent [[entities/amy-edmondson|Harvard Business School professor]] and [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] specializing in organizational behavior, [[concepts/leadership]], and [[concepts/group-dynamics|team dynamics]]. She is best known as the originator of the concept of [[concepts/psychological-safety]].

## Key Concepts & Research
- **[[concepts/vulnerability|Psychological Safety]]**: Defined as a shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking. Essential for [[concepts/learning|learning]] in organizations, [[concepts/innovation]], and [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] teams.
- **Fearless Organization**: Advocates for balancing high standards with safety, enabling teams to speak up, admit mistakes, and innovate.
- **[[concepts/tone|Voice]] Behavior**: Research focuses on why [[entities/employees|employees]] stay silent and how leaders can encourage constructive dissent and [[concepts/feedback|feedback]].
