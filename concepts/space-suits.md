# Space Suits

**Space suits** are complex life-support systems designed to provide a habitable environment for astronauts in the vacuum of Outer Space and other hostile environments. They function as miniature spacecraft, protecting against extreme temperatures, radiation, micrometeoroids, and the lack of atmospheric pressure.

## Core Functions
- **Pressure [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]]:** Maintains internal pressure to prevent ebullism and decompression sickness.
- **[[concepts/temperature-management|Thermal Control]]:** Manages heat exchange in environments ranging from extreme solar heating to [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep-space]] cold.
- **Life Support:** Supplies oxygen, removes carbon dioxide, and manages humidity.
- **Mobility:** Allows for necessary [[concepts/exercise|movement]] while maintaining structural [[concepts/honesty|integrity]].

## Materials and Technology

- **Multi-Layer Construction:** Suits utilize multiple layers, each serving a specific protective function, from the innermost bladder to the outer thermal micrometeoroid garment.
- **Extreme Environment Protection:** Advanced materials are engineered to withstand the [[concepts/vacuum-of-space|vacuum of space]], high radiation levels, and temperature fluctuations.
- **[[concepts/nasa|NASA]] Suit Architecture:** Current designs, such as those used by [[entities/nasa|NASA]], integrate these technologies to ensure [[concepts/man-maneuvering-units|astronaut safety]] during EVA ([[concepts/space-jetpacks|Extravehicular Activity]]).
