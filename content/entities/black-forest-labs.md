# Black Forest Labs

AI company specializing in diffusion models for image generation, best known for the **[[entities/flux1|FLUX.1]]** model series. Focuses on high-quality, efficient image synthesis with emphasis on [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and [[concepts/personalization|customization]].

## Key Models & Technologies
- **[[entities/flux1|FLUX.1]]**: Core [[concepts/image-generation-model|image generation model]] supporting:
  - [[concepts/lora-adapter|LoRA adapter]] [[concepts/training|training]] for task-specific [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]]
  - [[concepts/low-vram-requirements|Low VRAM requirements]] (e.g., <10GB for face [[concepts/training|training]])
  - High-fidelity output for diverse [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]
- **[[concepts/face-generation|Face Generation]] Capability**: Demonstrated via [[entities/adam-lucek|Adam Lucek]] - Flux model for Open AI generated image gen using LoRA adapters to reliably recreate specific person's [[concepts/images|images]] across varying contexts.
