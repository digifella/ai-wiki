# Sesfamilycom

Sesfamilycom is a digital health application developed at the University of Copenhagen. The platform provides interactive modules focused on conflict resolution and interpersonal communication, aiming to assist users in managing disputes and relationship challenges within personal and family contexts.

The application serves as an educational tool designed to improve communication strategies and conflict resolution abilities. It delivers evidence-based training through guided, interactive lessons that break down complex interpersonal dynamics into practical skills. These modules are structured to help users navigate difficult conversations and develop healthier interaction patterns.

By leveraging academic research, the platform offers a structured approach to learning conflict management techniques. The content is tailored for individuals seeking to enhance their ability to handle disagreements constructively, providing a resource for both personal development and relationship maintenance.
