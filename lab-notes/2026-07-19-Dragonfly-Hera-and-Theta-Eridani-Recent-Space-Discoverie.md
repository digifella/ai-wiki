Another significant finding concerns intermediate-mass black holes (IMBHs), a long-sought "missing link" between stellar-mass and supermassive black holes. Additionally, the video delves into historical astronomy, discussing how precise records from ancient Greek and Arab astronomers shed light on the variability of Theta Eridani. Modern observations revealed this star is part of a triple-star system whose highly elliptical orbits cause periodic dramatic brightening, a phenomenon that ancient astronomers carefully recorded.
