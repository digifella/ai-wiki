# AI guardrails

Safety mechanisms and operational constraints implemented within [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) to mitigate Adversarial Attacks, Prompt Injection, and the generation of harmful or prohibited content. The calibration of these [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]] is a central tension in AI Alignment.

### Key Modalities
- **Standard Guardrails**: Strict enforcement of safety protocols to prevent Malicious Use and unauthorized content generation.
- **Permissive Guardrails**: Intentionally loosened constraints designed for specialized, high-utility domains.
    - **[[concepts/gpt-5|GPT 5]].4 Cyber**: A "cyber-permissive" variant of [[entities/gpt-5|GPT 5]].4 optimized for [[concepts/cybersecurity]] [[concepts/software|applications]], allowing reduced restrictions to facilitate defensive research and [[concepts/secure|security]] modeling.
