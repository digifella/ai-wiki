# Intelligence Explosion

An **[[concepts/guiding-role|intelligence explosion]]** refers to a hypothetical future event where an intelligent [[entities/agent|agent]] (such as an artificial superintelligence) recursively improves itself, leading to a rapid and significant increase in its intelligence. This concept suggests that once an AI reaches a certain threshold of capability, it [[entities/will|will]] be able to [[entities/make|make]] exponential progress in its intellectual abilities.

- **Key Characteristics:**
  - Self-reinforcing [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]]
  - [[concepts/exponential-growth|Exponential growth]] rates
