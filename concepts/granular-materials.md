# Granular Materials

Granular materials are discrete, solid particles that interact through [[concepts/friction|friction]] and collision. They can [[concepts/flow|flow]] like liquids or behave like solids, depending on external forces applied to them.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/angle-of-repose|Angle of Repose]]:** The maximum angle above the horizontal at which a granular material [[entities/will|will]] remain in place without sliding down.
- **[[concepts/self-sorting|Self-Sorting]]:** Phenomenon where particles segregate based on size or [[concepts/density|density]] when poured into [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]].
- **[[concepts/vertical-stacking|Vertical Stacking]]:** Unusual behavior where granules stack vertically instead of creating a slope.
