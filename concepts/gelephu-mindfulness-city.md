# Gelephu Mindfulness City

**[[entities/the-b1m|Gelephu Mindfulness City]]** is a proposed special administrative region in southern [[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]], designed to integrate modern economic development with the nation's core [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] of [[concepts/gross-national-happiness|Gross National Happiness]] (GNH). Often described as the "Anti-Dubai," the project aims to address existential and economic challenges through mindful, sustainable [[concepts/city-planning|urban planning]].

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/mindful-development|Mindful Development]]**: Prioritizes [[concepts/psychological-well-being|mental well-being]], spiritual [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]], and environmental sustainability alongside economic growth.
- **Existential [[concepts/crisis|Crisis Response]]**: Aims to counteract global materialism and spiritual [[concepts/disconnection|disconnection]] by offering a model of development rooted in Buddhist principles.
- **Gross National [[concepts/happiness|Happiness]] (GNH)**: [[entities/san-francisco|The city]] serves as a practical application of Bhutan's national metric for [[concepts/success|success]], moving beyond GDP.
- **Special Administrative Region**: Designed to attract foreign investment and talent while maintaining strict cultural and ecological safeguards.

## Key Details
- **Location**: Gelephu, Sarpang District, southern Bhutan.
- **[[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]**: To create a global hub for [[concepts/mindfulness|mindfulness]], wellness, and sustainable [[concepts/innovation|innovation]].
- **Philosophy**: Balances modern [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] with traditional Bhutanese values, ensuring development does not erode cultural identity or environmental [[concepts/honesty|integrity]].
