## AionUI: Free Desktop Platform for Multi-Agent AI Management and Automation
**Clip title:** [[entities/hermes-agent|Hermes]] + Aion UI is Insane (FREE)!
**Author / channel:** [[entities/julian-goldie|Julian Goldie]] SEO
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vWxE6VO9TKo

### Summary
The video introduces [[entities/aionui|AionUI]] as a free, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], desktop-based platform designed to run and manage various [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]], including [[entities/hermes|Hermes]], [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]], OpenCode, and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] highlights that while these agents can be run via terminal, AionUI offers a significantly more organized and powerful [[concepts/solution|solution]]. It provides a clean, user-friendly graphical interface (GUI) that centralizes [[entities/agent|agent]] management, allowing users to switch between different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] instantly, schedule tasks, and even control everything from their phone.

AionUI distinguishes itself by enabling multi-agent execution, where multiple [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]] can work collaboratively and in parallel on tasks. The platform supports a wide array of AI models, from built-in agents to those requiring [[concepts/api-keys|API keys]] (like Google [[entities/gemini-pro|Gemini]], [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]], [[entities/amazon-web-services|AWS]] Bedrock, and various local models). It offers a "Skills Marketplace" to extend [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]], allowing users to find and install new skills or create [[concepts/custom-assistants|custom assistants]] for specific tasks, such as generating documents, presentations, or spreadsheets.

Key functionalities demonstrated include the ability to chat with different agents in separate sessions, track recent conversations, and view a workspace to manage [[concepts/files|files]] related to ongoing projects, similar to how agents like [[entities/claude-co-work|Claude Co-work]] operate with [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]]. AionUI also features a robust [[concepts/temporary-cron-jobs|scheduled tasks]] section, where users can set up automated jobs with customizable frequencies and prompts, ensuring tasks run 24/7 by preventing the computer from sleeping.

In conclusion, AionUI positions itself as a comprehensive and user-friendly alternative to complex terminal-based [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] setups. Its cross-platform compatibility ([[entities/mac|Mac]], [[entities/windows|Windows]], [[entities/linux|Linux]]), multi-agent management, extensive integrations, and automation features make it a powerful tool for individuals and businesses looking to streamline and enhance their [[concepts/ai-driven-workflows|AI-driven workflows]]. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] strongly recommends AionUI for anyone seeking a more efficient and intuitive way to harness the power of multiple [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] simultaneously.
