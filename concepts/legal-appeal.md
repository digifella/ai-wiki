# Legal Appeal

A formal request to a higher court or tribunal to review the decision of a lower court or tribunal. The process ensures legal correctness, procedural fairness, and appropriate sentencing or remedy.

## Core Components
- **Grounds for Appeal**: Errors of law, procedural irregularities, or manifest injustice in the original judgment.
- **Appellate Jurisdiction**: The authority of a higher court to hear appeals from lower courts.
- **Standard of Review**: The degree of deference the appellate court gives to the lower court's findings of fact vs. law.
- **Remedies**: Reversal, modification, remittal (sending back for retrial), or affirmation of the original decision.

## Australian Context
- Appeals in Australia typically move from State Supreme Courts to the High Court of Australia.
- Requires special leave to appeal in most final matters.
- Focuses on questions of law rather than re-litigating facts, unless the factual finding is unreasonable.
