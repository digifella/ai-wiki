# AI Methodology

AI Methodology encompasses systematic approaches for integrating large language models (LLMs) and autonomous agents into software development, website building, and creative workflows. Rather than replacing traditional practices, these methodologies augment existing processes by automating routine tasks, generating code scaffolding, and accelerating decision-making. The core principle involves establishing structure and clear prompting frameworks that allow AI systems to operate effectively within defined constraints and business objectives.

## Core Applications

In software development, AI methodologies enable developers to delegate boilerplate code generation, documentation writing, and test case creation, freeing cognitive resources for architecture and problem-solving. Website building benefits from template generation, content scaffolding, and design suggestion systems powered by LLMs. Creative workflows leverage AI for ideation support, iterative refinement, and rapid prototyping across copywriting, design, and content production.

## Key Practices

Effective AI methodology relies on prompt engineering, systematic evaluation of outputs, and human oversight at critical decision points. Organizations typically establish guidelines for when to apply AI assistance, how to validate generated artifacts, and how to integrate AI-augmented workflows with existing quality assurance and review processes. The methodology emphasizes treating AI as a productivity multiplier rather than an autonomous decision-maker, maintaining human expertise and judgment in outcomes that affect product quality and business impact.
