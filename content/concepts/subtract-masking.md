# Subtract masking

A [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] technique in [[entities/adobe-lightroom]] used to exclude specific areas from an existing mask, enabling highly targeted Local [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

### Core Functionality
- Used to refine selections by removing unwanted pixels from a previously defined mask.
- Works as part of a suite of [[concepts/masking-tools]] (including [[concepts/add-masking]] and [[concepts/intersect-masking]]) to achieve precision.
- Often used in conjunction with [[concepts/intersect-masking]] (introduced late 2023) to isolate specific overlapping regions for complex edits.
