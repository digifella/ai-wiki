# Automated Research Workflows

[[concepts/ai-driven-research|Automated Research Workflows]] is a multi-modal researcher tool that leverages [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini-25-models|Gemini 2.5]] models to coordinate complex research tasks across different data types. Built on the [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph]] and [[entities/langchain|LangChain]] frameworks, the system enables researchers to process and analyze diverse inputs—including text, [[concepts/images|images]], and other formats—within a unified workflow environment.

## Technical Architecture

The tool uses LangGraph to provide graph-based orchestration of research processes, managing task flow and sequencing across computational operations. LangChain integration handles the integration with Gemini 2.5 models, facilitating model interactions and prompt management. This architecture allows the system to decompose research problems into interconnected steps that can process multiple modalities simultaneously or sequentially as needed.

## Use Cases

Automated Research Workflows is designed to handle research scenarios that require analysis across mixed data types, such as combining textual analysis with visual content interpretation or integrating quantitative and qualitative information sources. The multi-modal approach reduces the need to manually transfer data between separate [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]], consolidating the research process into a single coordinated environment.
