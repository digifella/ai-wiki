# Photoshop 2025

[[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]] introduced an updated [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]], representing a significant enhancement to Adobe's [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] capabilities. This tool was highlighted in a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] by [[entities/blake-rudis|Blake Rudis]] of [[entities/f64-academy|f64 Academy]], distributed through Adobe's official YouTube channel in April 2026.

## The Color and Vibrance Tool

The new Color and Vibrance tool in Photoshop 2025 provides enhanced functionality for color correction and adjustment workflows. The tool allows users to manipulate color properties and vibrance with improved precision compared to previous versions, making it relevant for photographers and designers working with color grading tasks.
