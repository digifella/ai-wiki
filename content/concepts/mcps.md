# Mcps

Mcps refers to the strategic [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] of [[concepts/ai-coding-workflows|AI coding tools]] and [[concepts/agents|agents]] to achieve significant productivity improvements in small and [[entities/medium|medium]]-sized enterprises (SMEs). The concept gained prominence through discussions by technology leaders including [[entities/patrick-ellis|Patrick Ellis]], a CTO and startup co-founder, who presented on the topic [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] regarding how businesses can leverage these [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to reach 10x productivity gains.

## Core Application

The approach centers on using modern [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] and [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] to streamline [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]] within resource-constrained organizations. Rather than focusing purely on the technical capabilities of individual tools, Mcps emphasizes practical [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] strategies that recognize both the current maturity of AI systems and the real constraints of SME operations. The framework suggests that achieving meaningful productivity multipliers requires thoughtful [[concepts/integration|integration]] of these technologies into existing business processes rather than treating them as standalone solutions.

## Business Context

For SMEs particularly, Mcps represents an opportunity to compete with larger organizations by multiplying the [[concepts/output|output]] of smaller engineering teams. The concept acknowledges that while [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|AI coding]] tools have reached a threshold of capability, successful implementation depends on how well businesses [[concepts/structure|structure]] their use of agents and [[concepts/automation|automation]] within their specific operational contexts and workflows.
