# Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite

Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite is a specialized, lightweight variant of Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash language model. It is specifically engineered for deployment in resource-constrained environments where standard large language models may be impractical. By significantly reducing the memory footprint and computational requirements compared to the standard Flash variant, the model maintains core capabilities in reasoning and language processing while optimizing for efficiency.

The architecture is designed to support deployment on edge devices and mobile applications. This optimization allows for lower latency and reduced power consumption, making it suitable for on-device inference tasks. The model retains the foundational strengths of the Gemini 2.5 series while prioritizing accessibility and performance in hardware-limited scenarios.
