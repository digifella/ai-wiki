# Decimal Places

Decimal [[entities/places|places]] refer to the positions of digits after the decimal point in a number. They are crucial for representing fractional values accurately and are essential in fields such as [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/physics|physics]], and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]].

### Key Points:
- Each position to the right of the decimal point represents a tenth, hundredth, thousandth, etc., of a whole unit.
- The [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] of measurements increases with more decimal places.
- In practical applications, only a few decimal places are often sufficient for accuracy; however, in theoretical and computational contexts, many more may be necessary.
