# Adaptive Technology

## Definition
[[concepts/assistive-devices|Adaptive Technology]] refers to equipment, systems, or processes designed to help individuals with disability perform tasks more independently, efficiently, or effectively. It spans low-tech aids to high-tech digital interfaces, bridging the gap between user capability and environmental demands.

## Historical Evolution & Key Devices

### Tactile Writing Systems
*   **[[concepts/braille|Braille]] Origins**: Developed by Louis Braille in the 19th century as a system of raised dots representing letters and numbers, enabling tactile reading and [[concepts/writing|writing]] for blind individuals.
*   **The [[concepts/perkins-brailler|Perkins Brailler]]**: A pivotal mechanical device invented at Perkins School for the Blind that simplified braille transcription by using six keys corresponding to the six dots of a braille cell.
    *   *Significance*: Demystified braille creation, moving it from slow manual stylus use to rapid, typewriter-like input.
