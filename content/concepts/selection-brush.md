# Selection Brush

The Selection Brush is a tool introduced in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s July 2025 update, available in both the general release and [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]] versions. Designed to streamline the selection process, it functions as part of Adobe's broader effort to enhance user workflow and productivity in professional image editing tasks.

## Functionality

The Selection Brush enables users to make selections by painting directly onto an image, offering an alternative approach to traditional selection methods. This brush-based selection method integrates into Photoshop's existing selection toolkit, allowing for more intuitive and flexible [[concepts/image-masking|image masking]] and editing workflows.

## Release and Availability

The tool was highlighted as one of five significant features in the July 2025 Photoshop update cycle. It represents Adobe's continued investment in refining core editing capabilities through both stable releases and experimental features tested in the Photoshop Beta program.
