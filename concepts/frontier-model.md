# Frontier Model

## Definition
A [[concepts/state-of-the-art-offering|Frontier Model]] refers to the most advanced, large-scale [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] models that represent the state-of-the-art in [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]]. These models are characterized by extreme computational scale, high-level [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capabilities, and the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of complex, unprecedented abilities.

## Core Characteristics
- **[[concepts/advanced-reasoning|Advanced Reasoning]]:** Capable of complex [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], multi-step [[concepts/problem-solving|problem solving]], and high-order cognitive tasks.
- **Scale & Complexity:** Driven by massive parameter counts and unprecedented training [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
- **Emergent Capabilities:** Development of new [[concepts/skills|skills]] that arise as the model scales, often exhibiting behaviors not explicitly programmed.
- **Open-Weight & Sovereignty:** Increasing trend toward open-weight architectures to support [[concepts/european-ai-sovereignty|European AI Sovereignty]] and reduce dependency on closed ecosystems.

## Notable Implementations: Mistral Large 4
Recent developments highlight the rise of European-led frontier models, specifically **Mistral Large 4** (colloquially "Le Chonk"), which exemplifies the shift toward open-weight, sovereign [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]].

- **Architecture:** Utilizes a **[[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE)** architecture with 1 trillion total parameters and 49 billion [[concepts/activated-parameters|active parameters]], optimizing [[concepts/inference-efficiency|inference efficiency]].
- **Development:** Developed entirely in Europe from scratch, emphasizing [[concepts/data-sovereignty|data sovereignty]] and independent technological capability.
- **Capabilities:** Features unified [[concepts/multimodal-input|multimodal input]] capabilities and [[concepts/enhanced-reasoning|advanced reasoning]], positioning it as a direct competitor to other leading frontier models.
- **Licensing:** Released as an open-weight and [[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]], facilitating broader community integration and customization.
- **Analysis:** For detailed [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]] and capability breakdowns, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-07-European-AI-Sovereignty-Mistral-Large-4-Capabilities-Per|European AI Sovereignty: Mistral Large 4 Capabilities, Performance, Challenges]].
