# Agent-based research

A research paradigm utilizing autonomous [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to execute multi-step [[concepts/workflow|workflows]]—including [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]], synthesis, and [[concepts/verification|verification]]—to produce structured knowledge outputs.

## Core Implementations
- **[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s [[entities/storm|STORM]] AI**: An AI-powered system designed for verifiable research and automated knowledge curation.
    - **Mechanism**: Utilizes **STORM** (Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and [[concepts/multi-perspective-question-asking|Multi-perspective Question Asking]]) to [[concepts/structure|structure]] research.
    - **[[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**: Enables "[[concepts/deep-research-function|deep research]]" to be performed in minutes through [[concepts/automated-retrieval|automated retrieval]] and multi-perspective inquiry.
    - **Reference**: 2026 04 22 Stanfords [[concepts/agent-based-knowledge-curation|STORM AI]] Verifiable [[entities/agent|Agent]] Based Research and Knowledge Curation
