This includes CPU/GPU power, [[concepts/memory|memory]] (RAM), [[entities/storage|storage]] capacity, network [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]], and other related technologies.

### New Development

#### ThinkingCap: Local AI Efficiency via Reduced Reasoning Tokens

Recent advancements in optimizing [[concepts/computing-infrastructure|computational resources]] for [[concepts/hardware-heavy-models|local LLMs]] include the introduction of **[[concepts/llm-optimization|ThinkingCap]]**, a fine-tuned version of the [[concepts/qwen-36-27b-mtp|Qwen 3.6-27B]] model developed by [[entities/bottlecap-ai|BottleCap AI]]. This initiative focuses on reducing the number of [[concepts/thinking-tokens|reasoning tokens]] required for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], thereby improving efficiency on local hardware.

- **Core Premise:** Achieving significant efficiency gains in [[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]] (LLM) performance by minimizing the computational overhead associated with reasoning tokens.
- **Model Base:** Fine-tuned from the popular [[concepts/dense-model-architecture|Qwen 3.6-27B architecture]].
- **Impact:** Enhances the viability of running complex AI tasks on local machines by reducing resource consumption.
- **Source:** [ThinkingCap: Local AI Efficiency via Reduced Reasoning Tokens](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=m1gQu9ApmRQ)
