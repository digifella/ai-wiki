# Complex Reasoning

Complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] in [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] refers to the capability to perform multi-step logical [[concepts/inference|inference]], decompose complicated problems into manageable components, and synthesize solutions that require understanding context and dependencies. This represents a significant advancement beyond simple [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]], enabling [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to handle nuanced [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] tasks that demand higher-order [[concepts/cognitive-abilities|cognitive abilities]].

## Multi-Step Problem Solving

Complex reasoning involves breaking down intricate problems into smaller, interconnected subtasks. Rather than responding to isolated inputs, [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] with complex [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] can maintain [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]] of dependencies between steps, track intermediate results, and adjust their approach based on emerging information. This enables handling of [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where the [[concepts/solution|solution]] path is not linear or pre-defined.
