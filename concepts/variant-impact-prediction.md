# Variant Impact Prediction

**Variant impact prediction** refers to the computational and experimental methods used to determine the functional consequences of genetic variants on the [[concepts/human-genome|human genome]]. This field is critical for interpreting the "language of life" and understanding how specific mutations affect biological systems.

## Core Concepts

- **Functional Consequence:** Assessing whether a variant is benign, pathogenic, or of uncertain significance.
- **Genomic Context:** Analyzing variants within the context of regulatory elements, coding regions, and non-coding DNA.
- **AI-Driven Prediction:** Leveraging [[concepts/neural-networks|deep learning models]] to predict variant effects at scale, moving beyond traditional statistical methods.
