# Color And Vibrance Tool

The Color and Vibrance tool is a non-destructive adjustment layer introduced in Photoshop 2025 that provides refined control over color saturation and tonal intensity. As an adjustment layer, modifications made through this tool do not permanently alter the underlying image data, preserving the original pixel information and enabling flexible editing workflows. This approach allows users to revisit and modify color adjustments at any point during the editing process without degrading image quality.

## Functionality

The tool operates by separating color and vibrance controls into distinct parameters. Saturation adjustments affect the intensity of all colors uniformly, while vibrance adjustments intelligently boost color intensity while protecting skin tones and preventing oversaturation in already-saturated areas. This dual-control approach gives editors precise management over how colors are rendered in their images, from subtle refinements to more dramatic transformations.

## Workflow Integration

By functioning as an adjustment layer rather than a direct filter, the Color and Vibrance tool integrates seamlessly into non-destructive editing workflows. Users can stack multiple adjustment layers, apply layer masks for selective adjustments, and maintain full editability throughout their project. This maintains flexibility in post-production and allows adjustments to be refined or removed entirely without returning to the original source file.
