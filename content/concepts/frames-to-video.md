# Frames to Video

Compiling sequential image frames into a video file. Core process in animation, [[concepts/ai-video-automation|AI video generation]], and digital media pipelines.

## Key Applications
- AI [[concepts/video-generation|Video Generation]]: Many tools (e.g., FaceSwap, [[concepts/text-to-video|Text-to-Video]]) generate frame sequences that require conversion to video.
- [[concepts/integrity|Character]] [[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]]: Maintaining uniform facial features across frames in [[concepts/ai-videos|AI-generated videos]].
- [[concepts/tone|Voice]] Narration: Synchronizing generated frames with custom [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] tracks.

## Example: AI Video with Face and Text
- [Video Guide](https://youtu.be/_5LuBKXSV5k): Step-by-step [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] for creating realistic talking-head videos using your face reference and [[concepts/text|text]] input.
- Requires reference face (photo/clip) + text → generates frame sequence → compiles to video.
- Emphasizes [[concepts/character-consistency|character consistency]] and [[concepts/custom-voice-narration|custom voice narration]] across scenes.
