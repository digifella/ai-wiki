## Google Vids AI Video Creation and Editing Tool Summary Report
**Clip title:** [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s NEW [[concepts/ai-video-editor|AI Video Editor]] Is INSANE 🤯 ([[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] Full [[concepts/tutorial|Tutorial]] - Workspace)

### Summary
[[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]], a new [[concepts/ai-assisted-video-creation|AI-powered video creation]] and editing tool integrated into [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]], has recently undergone a significant transformation, moving beyond a basic editor to offer "mind-blowing" AI capabilities. The presenter emphasizes that it functions similarly to [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]] but for video, making it ideal for generating quick [[concepts/promotional-content|promotional content]], tutorials, and [[concepts/internal-walkthroughs|internal walkthroughs]]. A key advantage is its [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], as it's available for free to [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] subscribers. The video demonstrates how users can leverage these [[concepts/new-features|new features]] to streamline video production and enhance content quality.

One of the most impressive features showcased is the ability to generate high-definition product commercials from scratch. Users can upload a few product images, provide a detailed textual prompt describing the desired visuals and transitions, and the AI [[entities/will|will]] create a dynamic 3D video. This includes advanced effects like dynamic angles and smooth transitions, even accurately [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]] product logos. Complementing this, Google Vids can also generate custom instrumental music using Lyria 3.5, allowing users to specify genre, mood, and even include whispered brand names at the track's end, perfectly syncing with the visual content.

Beyond creation, Google Vids excels in video editing, allowing users to transform existing footage with text-based prompts. The presenter demonstrates how an uploaded selfie video can be dramatically altered by changing the background to a professional white studio, then switching her attire to a spacesuit and launching her into space, all controlled by precise verbal cues within the prompt. Furthermore, the platform offers a diverse range of [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]], including realistic, 3D [[concepts/cartoon|cartoon]], and 2D cartoon styles. Users can customize these avatars by defining attributes like gender, age, and clothing, and even upload their own company logos to be incorporated onto the avatar's attire. The system also supports generating personal [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]] from a short selfie video, which can then be used across various video projects with AI-generated voiceovers and instant captions.

In conclusion, Google Vids significantly lowers the barrier to entry for video production, offering powerful [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] that can generate and edit professional-grade content with remarkable ease. While the presenter noted minor limitations such as occasional choppier transitions in complex edits or pixelation in certain avatar resolutions, the overall capabilities are a game-changer for businesses and individuals creating [[concepts/video-resource|video content]]. Its integration with Google Workspace provides a seamless [[concepts/experience|experience]], making advanced video creation accessible and efficient.
