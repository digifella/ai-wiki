# Semantic Interoperability

**Semantic Interoperability** refers to the ability of disparate systems to exchange information with complete and unambiguous transfer of meaning. Unlike syntactic interoperability, which ensures data can be read, semantic interoperability ensures that the *context* and *intent* of the data are preserved across different platforms, ontologies, and agents.

## Core Principles

- **Shared Ontologies**: Systems must agree on the definitions of [[concepts/nodes|entities]] and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] (e.g., [[concepts/knowledge-graph]], Ontology [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]).
- **Context [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Data exchange must include [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] that defines the scope, provenance, and constraints of the information.
- **Machine-Readable Meaning**: Formats must allow automated agents (such as [[concepts/large-language-model]]s) to interpret data without human mediation.

## Ontologies as Guardrails for Agentic Systems

Recent analysis highlights the critical role of ontologies in ensuring [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] within complex AI architectures.

- **Essential [[concepts/ai-safety|Guardrails]]**: Ontologies provide necessary structural constraints for trustworthy [[concepts/large-language-model]] [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]], preventing [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] and ensuring [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]].
- **Neurosymbolic Integration**: They bridge the gap between [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] flexibility and symbolic [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] rigor, enabling [[concepts/neurosymbolic-ai]] approaches that combine [[concepts/learning|learning]] with [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].
- **Trustworthiness**: By enforcing shared definitions and relationships, ontologies mitigate risks associated with [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] exchange in multi-[[concepts/interactive-environments|agent environments]].

For a detailed breakdown of these guardrails, see [[lab-notes/2026-07-30-Ontologies-Essential-Guardrails-for-Trustworthy-Agentic|Ontologies: Essential Guardrails for Trustworthy Agentic Systems and Neurosymbolic AI]].
