# X-Ray Discovery

High-energy astrophysical detections characterizing anomalous emission sources in the [[concepts/primordial-universe|early universe]], driven by [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] observations.

## JWST LRD Findings
- [[entities/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] resolved "[[concepts/little-red-dots|little red dots]]" ([[concepts/lrds|LRDs]]) as ultra-compact, intensely luminous structures within the [[concepts/early-universe]], building on initial identifications since 2022.
- Spectroscopic evidence links [[concepts/lrd-little-red-dot|LRDs]] to Black Hole formation preceding Star genesis, supporting [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where Pre-Stellar [[concepts/black-holes|Black Holes]] form via direct gas collapse before [[concepts/solar-nucleosynthesis|stellar nucleosynthesis]] occurs.
- X-ray emissions dominate LRD spectra, indicating energetic accretion onto [[concepts/primordial-black-hole|primordial black holes]] rather than stellar populations, confirming black holes as the primary [[concepts/ionization|ionization]] sources in these regions.
- Detailed characterization establishes LRDs as tracers for rapid black hole growth, revising models of hierarchical structure formation [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-JWST-X-Ray-Discovery-LRD-Links-to-Pre-Stellar-Black-Hole|JWST X-Ray Discovery: LRD Links to Pre-Stellar Black Hole Formation]].
