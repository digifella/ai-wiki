# Claude Ai Subscription Policy

Claude Ai Subscription Policy refers to [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s guidelines governing access to and usage of [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] services through paid subscription tiers. As of April 2026, Anthropic implemented significant changes to its subscription framework affecting how users interact with the platform and the terms under which they can access [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s capabilities.

## Usage Limits and Access Changes

The [[concepts/policy-updates|policy updates]] introduced modifications to usage limits for Claude AI subscribers. These changes adjusted the rate limits and request volumes permitted under different subscription tiers, affecting both individual users and organizations relying on Claude for regular tasks.

## OpenClaw Ban

A notable component of the subscription policy update was the prohibition on OpenClaw, an automated information pipeline tool that had previously been used to automate interactions with Claude AI. This ban reflects Anthropic's effort to regulate how the service is consumed and to prevent certain categories of automated usage patterns.

## Financial and Pricing Implications

The [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]] carried financial implications for existing and prospective users, requiring [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to subscription costs and fee structures. These modifications were designed to align Anthropic's business model with its operational capacity and service [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].
