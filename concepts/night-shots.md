# Night Shots

Night Shots is a photography feature within the Adobe Lightroom beta camera app, designed for iPhone 13 Pro and later models. It enhances image capture in low-light environments by utilizing computational photography techniques to process multiple frames. This method aims to produce photographs with greater clarity and detail than standard smartphone night photography modes.

The feature leverages the advanced camera hardware and processing capabilities inherent in recent iPhone devices. By combining data from multiple exposures, Night Shots optimizes the final image output. The app also includes additional capabilities such as zoom functionality and tools for removing reflections from surfaces.
