# El Capitan Supercomputer

**El Capitan** is a leading exascale supercomputer located at the [[concepts/lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory]]. It serves as a critical [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] for National Nuclear [[concepts/security|Security]] Administration missions, including nuclear stockpile stewardship, climate modeling, and [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]] simulations.

## Key Characteristics
*   **Exascale Performance:** Recognized as one of the [[entities/earth|world]]'s most powerful supercomputers, capable of performing at least one exaFLOP ($10^{18}$ floating-point operations per second).
*   **[[entities/national-academies|Engineering]] Scale:** Features massive computational [[concepts/density|density]] and specialized [[concepts/cooling-systems|cooling systems]] to manage the thermal output of its GPU and CPU hybrid architecture.
*   **Mission Focus:** Primarily designed to support the US Department of Energy's scientific and [[concepts/export-control|national security]] objectives without relying on physical nuclear testing.
