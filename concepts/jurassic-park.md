# Jurassic Park

## Overview
A concept popularized by Jurassic Park (film) and Jurassic Park (novel) involving the resurrection of [[concepts/dinosaurs|dinosaurs]] through genetic [[entities/national-academies|engineering]]. Central to the fictional premise is the extraction of dinosaur DNA from mosquitoes preserved in [[concepts/amber]].

## Scientific Context & Evolution
While the film depicts amber as a perfect preservative for vertebrate DNA, real-[[entities/earth|world]] paleobotany reveals more about its formation and biological origins.

- **[[concepts/plant-resin|Plant Resin]] Defense:** Recent findings indicate that plant resin evolution is deeply tied to defense [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]]. The discovery of the oldest amber redefines our understanding of how ancient plants protected themselves against pests and pathogens.
- **Amber Formation:** Amber is fossilized tree resin, not sap. It preserves organic material through rapid burial and polymerization over millions of years.
- **DNA [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]:** In reality, DNA degrades too quickly to survive the millions of years required for amber formation, making the Jurassic Park premise scientifically impossible.
