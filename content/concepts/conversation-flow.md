# Conversation Flow

Conversation [[concepts/flow|Flow]] refers to a structured prompt template designed to optimize interactions between users and AI systems, particularly in business advisory contexts. It functions as a metacognitive framework that encourages the AI to actively refine user inputs before processing them, thereby improving the quality and relevance of [[concepts/responses|responses]]. This approach recognizes that prompt clarity directly impacts [[concepts/output|output]] utility.

## Core Function

The template operates through two primary mechanisms. First, it instructs the AI to revise incoming prompts into more effective formulations before generating responses, effectively translating user intent into optimized queries. Second, it requires the AI to explicitly identify and articulate potential limitations or constraints in its answer, creating transparency about the boundaries of the response.

## Application Context

Conversation Flow emerged in the context of [[concepts/business-consulting|business consulting]] and [[concepts/creative-assistance|creative assistance]], where nuanced [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] requires both comprehensive analysis and honest acknowledgment of knowledge gaps. By embedding constraint identification into the response protocol, the framework addresses a key limitation of AI systems: the tendency to provide confident answers without flagging areas of uncertainty or domain-specific restrictions. This makes it particularly relevant for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where understanding what an AI cannot reliably address is as important as understanding what it can.
