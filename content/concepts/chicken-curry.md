# Chicken Curry

Chicken curry is a foundational dish in North Indian cuisine, characterized by chicken cooked in a spiced sauce base. [[entities/neel-kajale|Chef Neel Kajale]], a prominent Indian chef based in New York City, has documented his approach to preparing this classic dish using a skillet method. His recipe demonstrates traditional North Indian techniques adapted for modern home cooking.

## Preparation Method

Kajale's skillet-based approach emphasizes building layers of flavor through the tempering of spices and gradual cooking of aromatics. The method typically involves blooming whole and ground spices in oil or ghee, developing a fragrant base before introducing onions, tomatoes, and other components. This technique allows for better [[concepts/flavor-integration|flavor integration]] than more hurried [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]], resulting in a balanced curry sauce that coats the chicken evenly.

## Key Characteristics

North Indian chicken curry, as prepared by Kajale, generally features a tomato-based or cream-[[concepts/cream-sauce|enriched sauce]] with warm spices such as cumin, coriander, garam masala, and turmeric. The cooking vessel—in this case a skillet—provides [[concepts/even-heat-distribution|even heat distribution]] and allows for controlled reduction of the sauce to the desired [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]]. The finished dish is typically served with rice or bread to complement the sauce.
