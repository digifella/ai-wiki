# Claude Mythos 5

**[[concepts/ai-benchmarks|Claude Mythos]] 5** is a high-capability [[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]] developed by [[entities/anthropic]], launched concurrently with [[concepts/claude-fable-5]]. It represents an advancement in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and specialized [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]] compared to previous iterations.

## Overview & Launch Context
- **Launch Date**: 2026-06-11
- **Source Event**: Official launch alongside [[entities/fable-5|Claude Fable 5]], documented in [[entities/sam-witteveen|Sam Witteveen]]'s coverage [[lab-notes/2026-06-11-Anthropics-Claude-Fable-5-and-Mythos-5-LLMs-Launch-Acces|Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 LLMs: Launch, Access, Pricing, Safety]].
- **Positioning**: Higher capability tier compared to the general-purpose [[concepts/claude-fable-5]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Capability Tier**: High-capability model designed for [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]], specialized domain tasks, and advanced [[concepts/multi-agent-workflows|agent workflows]].
- **Access Status (2026-06-14)**: Offline. Following its launch, the [[entities/us-government|US Government]] issued an unprecedented order requiring [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] to take both [[entities/mythos-5|Mythos 5]] and [[concepts/claude-fable-5]] offline, citing significant [[concepts/security-concersns|security concerns]] regarding unrestricted access to high-capability [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] [[lab-notes/2026-06-14-US-Government-Orders-Anthropic-Fable-5-Mythos-5-Offline|US Government Orders Anthropic Fable 5, Mythos 5 Offline: Unprecedented Access Restrictions]].
- **Regulatory Impact**: This event marks a shift in [[concepts/ai-governance|AI governance]], demonstrating immediate [[concepts/regulatory-intervention|regulatory intervention]] on [[concepts/ai-model-deployment|model deployment]] based on national [[concepts/security|security]] protocols.
