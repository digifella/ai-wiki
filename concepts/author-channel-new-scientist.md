# Author Channel New Scientist

[[entities/new-scientist|New Scientist]] is a weekly [[concepts/science|science]] and technology magazine founded in 1956. It functions as a major publishing channel for [[concepts/research-communications|science communication]], translating complex scientific research and technological developments into accessible content. The publication serves both specialist researchers and general audiences, establishing itself as a significant [[concepts/tone|voice]] in science journalism since its inception.

## Global Reach and Media Presence

The channel reaches millions of readers globally through its print editions, digital platforms, and associated media outlets. It covers a broad spectrum of topics including physics, biology, medicine, and engineering, often featuring original reporting alongside summaries of peer-reviewed studies. Its multimedia presence extends to podcasts, newsletters, and live events, facilitating widespread dissemination of scientific knowledge to diverse demographics.

## Editorial Focus and Impact

New Scientist maintains a distinct editorial stance that balances rigorous scientific accuracy with engaging narrative styles. It frequently addresses the societal implications of scientific advancements, fostering public discourse on issues such as climate change, artificial intelligence, and public health. By bridging the gap between academic communities and the general public, the channel plays a crucial role in shaping contemporary understanding of scientific progress and its applications.
