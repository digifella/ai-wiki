# 2026 04 29 Google Deepmind

Google DeepMind is the artificial intelligence research division of Google, formed through the merger of Google's Brain team and the independent DeepMind Technologies (acquired by Google in 2014). The organization focuses on developing advanced AI systems and conducting fundamental research in machine learning, reinforcement learning, and neural networks.

## Research and Development

DeepMind is known for creating AI systems that have demonstrated significant capabilities in game-playing, protein structure prediction, and optimization problems. Notable systems include AlphaGo, which defeated world champions in the game of Go, and AlphaFold, which solved the protein folding problem. The organization publishes research findings and contributes to the broader AI research community through academic papers and open-source tools.

## Licensing and Open Source

DeepMind releases many of its research artifacts and software tools under open-source licenses, including the MIT License, which permits broad use and modification of the code by researchers and developers. This approach facilitates adoption of DeepMind technologies across academic institutions and industry, supporting the advancement of AI research globally.
