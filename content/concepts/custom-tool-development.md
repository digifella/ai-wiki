# Custom tool development

The practice of building specialized extensions, scripts, or integrations to augment [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and automate repetitive workflows to increase productivity.

## Key Workflows and Strategies
* **[[concepts/test-driven-development|Test-Driven Development]] ([[concepts/testing|TDD]]) for [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI Agents]]**: Implementing TDD principles to ensure the [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], and validation of agentic outputs and [[concepts/tool-use-capabilities|tool-use capabilities]].
* **Iterative Tool Evolution**: Developing and refining [[concepts/custom-tools|custom tools]] over extended periods (e.g., 3+ month cycles) to optimize [[concepts/ai-coding|AI coding]] workflows.
* **Agentic Orchestration**: Utilizing [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] as a primary [[concepts/coding|coding]] [[entities/agent|agent]] to execute complex, tool-enhanced development tasks.
