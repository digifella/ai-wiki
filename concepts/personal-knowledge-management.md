# Personal Knowledge Management

[[concepts/pkm|Personal Knowledge Management (PKM)]] refers to the individual practices and systems through which people collect, organize, store, and retrieve information relevant to their work, [[concepts/learning|learning]], or personal interests. Rather than depending entirely on institutional knowledge systems or external sources, PKM emphasizes personal agency in building and maintaining a [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] tailored to specific contexts and goals. This approach acknowledges that individuals have different information needs and that personally curated systems often better serve long-term [[concepts/storing|retention]] and application than passive consumption of information.

## Core Components

Effective PKM typically involves several interconnected activities: capturing information from various sources, categorizing and organizing it according to personal schema, maintaining the system over time, and [[concepts/retrieving|retrieving]] [[concepts/relevant-knowledge|relevant knowledge]] when needed.

Recent developments highlight a shift toward standardizing these systems, particularly in the context of [[concepts/llm|Large Language Model]]-assisted workflows:

*   **Standardization of LLM Wikis:** There is a move away from individual, unstructured "LLM Wikis" toward standardized approaches to ensure interoperability.
*   **[[concepts/llm-wiki|Open Knowledge Format]]:** Initiatives such as the [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Open-Knowledge-Format-Standardizing-Karpathy-LLM-Wikis-f|Open Knowledge Format: Standardizing Karpathy LLM Wikis for Interoperability]] propose [[concepts/open-source|open standards]] to manage personal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] more effectively with LLMs, facilitating better integration and data portability across different [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]].
