# Electronic Health Record

**[[concepts/wallet|Electronic Health Record]] (EHR)** refers to the digital version of a patient's paper chart, designed to be a centralized repository for longitudinal patient health information. It encompasses [[concepts/health-information-technology]] (HIT) implementations aimed at improving the quality, safety, and efficiency of [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]].

## Overview & Evolution
*   **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]**: HIT has been integrated into [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] since the 1960s, initially utilized for financial transactions via mainframes Health Information Technology History.
*   **Core Function**: EHRs facilitate the sharing of health information among providers, reducing redundant testing and enabling coordinated care.

## Impact on Medical Outcomes
*   **Positive Effects**: Recent systematic reviews indicate that HIT continues to demonstrate positive effects on treatment outcomes and overall medical efficacy.
*   **Key [[concepts/flow-management|Research Findings]]**:
