# Internal Bot

An Internal Bot is a custom AI agent built on the Microsoft 365 Copilot platform designed to automate workflows and tasks specific to an organization's needs. Unlike general-purpose chatbots intended for broad public use, internal bots operate within an organization's existing infrastructure, integrating directly with deployed systems, data sources, and established business processes. They function as specialized tools tailored to particular departments or workflows rather than serving generic use cases.

## Key Characteristics

Internal bots leverage an organization's existing Microsoft 365 environment, including applications like Teams, Outlook, SharePoint, and custom business systems. This integration allows them to access organizational data, perform authenticated actions, and maintain security protocols consistent with enterprise standards. They are configured to handle domain-specific tasks such as data retrieval, process automation, employee assistance, or departmental workflows that would benefit from AI assistance.

## Development and Deployment

Building an internal bot requires defining the specific workflows and tasks the agent will handle, connecting it to relevant data sources and applications, and configuring appropriate permissions and access controls. The development process involves customizing the bot's capabilities to align with organizational requirements, training it on relevant internal processes, and ensuring compliance with security and data governance policies. Internal bots are typically deployed within a controlled environment where their usage can be monitored and refined based on organizational feedback.
