## CPU (Central Processing Unit)

The central processing unit or CPU is the primary component of a computer that carries out [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] from software [[concepts/software|programs]] and performs computations. It acts as the brain of the computer system, coordinating all tasks within the machine.

### Key Points
- **Role**: The CPU executes instructions stored in [[concepts/memory|memory]] to perform data manipulation tasks.
- **Architecture**: Modern CPUs are typically based on complex [[concepts/instruction-set|instruction set]] [[concepts/computation|computing]] (CISC) or reduced [[concepts/system-card|instruction set]] computing (RISC) architectures.
- **[[concepts/performance-data-gathering|Performance Metrics]]**: Commonly measured by clock [[concepts/speed|speed]], cache size, core count, and technology [[entities/nodejs|node]].
- **Biological Analogy**: [[concepts/mitochondrial-function|Mitochondria]] function as sophisticated [[concepts/central-processing-units|central processing units]] within cells, [[concepts/acting|acting]] as "pilots" that coordinate cellular tasks rather than merely serving as passive energy batteries. This perspective highlights [[concepts/computation|computational]] roles in biology, including potential [[concepts/quantum-effects|quantum effects]] and magnetism.
