Youtube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by Google in 2006. It functions as one of the internet’s largest repositories of video content, allowing users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos. The service hosts a diverse range of material, including user-generated content, professional productions, educational resources, and entertainment media.

Core Features

Users create accounts to upload and manage videos through a creator dashboard. The platform provides tools for video editing, monetization, and audience analytics. Viewers can search for content, subscribe to channels, and interact with videos through likes and comments. The algorithmic recommendation system plays a significant role in content discovery, tailoring suggestions based on viewing history and engagement patterns.

Business Model

YouTube operates primarily on a freemium model, offering free access to content supported by advertisements. Through the YouTube Partner Program, eligible creators can earn revenue from ads displayed on their videos. The platform also offers YouTube Premium, a subscription service that removes ads and provides access to exclusive content and background playback. Additionally, YouTube Music and YouTube TV offer specialized audio and live television services, respectively.
