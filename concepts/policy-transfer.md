# Policy Transfer

Policy transfer is a technique in reinforcement [[concepts/learning|learning]] (RL) and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] where learned [[concepts/policies|policies]] or strategies are transferred from one environment to another. This method aims to enhance the efficiency of learning by leveraging pre-existing knowledge gained in a different, but related task or setting.

## Challenges and Applications
- **[[concepts/robotics|Robotics]]:** In robotics, policy transfer can bridge the gap between [[concepts/environment-simulation|simulated environments]] and real-[[entities/earth|world]] applications, helping robots learn [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] more efficiently.
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Autonomous Systems]]:** Policy transfer is crucial for [[concepts/voice-assistants|autonomous systems]] to adapt quickly to new situations without extensive retraining.
