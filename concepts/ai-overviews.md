# AI Overviews

AI Overviews are synthesized summaries generated by artificial intelligence systems in response to search queries, displayed prominently at the top of search results pages. Google introduced this feature as part of its Search Generative Experience (SGE) to consolidate information from multiple sources into direct answers, reducing the need for users to visit individual websites. Rather than ranking individual web pages, the system generates new text that attempts to answer queries directly based on content it has indexed.

## Impact on Website Content

The emergence of AI Overviews has shifted content strategy considerations for website owners and developers. Since these summaries draw from and reference multiple sources, websites may receive traffic from being cited in an overview without the user necessarily clicking through to the full page. This represents a fundamental change from traditional search engine optimization, where ranking position directly correlates with visibility and click-through rates.

## Generative Engine Optimization

Adapting websites for AI Overviews involves a practice known as Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), which focuses on making content more likely to be selected and synthesized by AI systems. Strategies include structuring information clearly, providing direct answers to common questions, and using natural language that AI systems can easily parse and incorporate. Tools like Claude Code can assist in analyzing content performance and identifying optimization opportunities based on how AI systems process and select source material.
