# Promotional Video

A video asset designed to promote a product, service, or brand. In the context of [[concepts/generative-ai]], promotional videos are increasingly created using synthetic media and [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]] to reduce production costs and accelerate [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycles.

## AI-Generated Promotional Content

The landscape of promotional video production is shifting towards [[concepts/ai-driven-workflows|AI-driven workflows]], particularly for personal [[concepts/branding|branding]] and [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]].

- **Avatar [[concepts/cloning|Cloning]]**: Using AI to create digital twins of presenters for consistent, scalable [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]].
- **Key [[concepts/scientific-experiment|Experiment]]**: [[lab-notes/2026-07-24-Gemini-Omni-AI-How-I-AIs-Video-Avatar-Cloning-Experiment|Gemini Omni AI: How I AI's Video Avatar Cloning Experiment Report]]
    - **Tooling**: Utilized [[entities/google-flow]] and the [[entities/gemini-25-flash]] API.
    - **Outcome**: Host [[entities/claire-ho|Claire Ho]] successfully cloned her video avatar in approximately 15 minutes.
    - **[[concepts/ingredient-selection|Quality Assessment]]**: The resulting synthetic media was described as "terrifyingly good," indicating high fidelity in lip-syncing and facial expression replication.
    - **Implication**: Demonstrates the viability of [[concepts/digital-avatars|AI avatars]] for [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] promotional content without traditional filming setups.
