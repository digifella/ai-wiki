# Yann LeCun

## Profile
Chief AI Scientist [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] [[entities/meta]] and Professor at New York University. A foundational figure in Deep [[concepts/learning|Learning]] and Convolutional [[concepts/neural-networks|Neural Networks]].

## Research Philosophy
- **[[concepts/world-models]]**: Advocates for the development of architectures that can predict and model the physical world.
- **Anti-LLM Paradigm**: Maintains that "Language is not Intelligence"; argues that [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) lack the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] required for [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|AGI]].
- **Self-Supervised [[concepts/learning|Learning]]**: Focuses on enabling [[concepts/models|models]] to learn directly from sensory input without massive human-labeled [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].

## Key Research & Architectures
- **[[concepts/jepa|JEPA]] (Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]])**: A non-generative approach to [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]].
  - Proposed as a strategic path beyond [[concepts/large-language-models]], aiming to surpass token-based prediction limits by operating in latent embedding spaces rather than reconstructing pixels or [[concepts/tokens|tokens]].
