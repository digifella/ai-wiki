# Proposal System

The proposal system functions as the central intake and evaluation mechanism for managing and reviewing proposals across the organization. It operates as a structured workflow process that establishes standardized criteria and procedures for proposal handling, enabling consistent review practices among various departments. Through this system, proposals are submitted, assessed, and approved in a manner that ensures organizational standards are met during the evaluation phase.

Current implementation faces significant operational difficulties due to limitations within SharePoint and broader infrastructure constraints. These technical barriers hinder the efficiency of the workflow, creating bottlenecks in the submission and assessment stages. The rigid nature of the existing platform restricts flexibility and scalability, making it difficult to adapt to varying proposal volumes or complex evaluation requirements.

To address these challenges, there is potential for artificial intelligence to accelerate the review process. AI integration could streamline initial assessments, automate routine checks against standardized criteria, and reduce the manual burden on evaluators. This technological enhancement aims to mitigate the current infrastructure limitations and improve the overall speed and consistency of proposal handling.
