# AI Based Selection

AI-based selection refers to [[concepts/automated-masking|automated masking]] and selection techniques that use [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to detect and isolate subjects or specific areas within digital [[concepts/images|images]]. These tools analyze image content to identify edges, boundaries, and objects with greater precision than traditional manual selection methods. By leveraging machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] algorithms, AI-based selection reduces the time and effort required for photographers and editors to create accurate masks and refinements.

## Implementation in Photo Editing Software

Major photo editing applications including Lightroom and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] have integrated AI-based selection capabilities into their [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] workflows. These features allow editors to automatically detect subject boundaries and create masks without manual tracing. The technology is particularly effective at refining edge details, distinguishing between foreground and background elements, and improving the quality of selection boundaries for subsequent [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Practical Benefits

AI-based selection improves workflow efficiency by automating what traditionally required tedious manual work with selection tools like the lasso or pen tool. This is especially valuable when working with complex edges such as hair, foliage, or intricate textures where precision is difficult to achieve by hand. Editors can apply localized adjustments to masked areas with greater confidence that the selection boundaries accurately reflect the intended subject or region.
