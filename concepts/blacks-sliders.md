# Blacks Sliders

The Blacks slider is a fundamental adjustment tool in Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw that specifically targets the darkest tones in an image. Unlike the broader Exposure slider, which affects the entire tonal range, the Blacks slider adjusts only the shadow regions while leaving midtones and highlights relatively unchanged. This selective control allows photographers to deepen shadow areas and increase overall contrast without overexposing brighter regions of the photograph.

## How It Works

The Blacks slider remaps the darkest input values in an image, effectively setting where the black point begins. Moving the slider to the right darkens the shadows by pushing more tones toward pure black, while moving it left lightens shadows and compresses contrast in those regions. The adjustment creates a localized effect that is particularly useful in landscape photography, where controlling shadow depth can enhance perceived depth and drama without affecting exposed highlights like skies or bright terrain features.

## Practical Application

In landscape processing, the Blacks slider is commonly paired with masking features to apply adjustments selectively to specific areas. Photographers might use the slider to darken foreground shadows while protecting sky regions, or to increase shadow definition in specific parts of the composition. The mask-and-adjust workflow allows for nuanced tonal control across different zones of an image without the artifacts that can result from adjusting the entire shadow range uniformly.
