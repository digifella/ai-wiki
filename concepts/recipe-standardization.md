# Recipe Standardization

Recipe standardization is the process of refining and documenting recipes to ensure consistent results, scalability, and reproducibility. It involves precise measurement, defined techniques, and clear [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to eliminate [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]].

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]]:** Use weight-based measurements (grams) over volume for accuracy.
- **Reproducibility:** Instructions must yield identical results across different cooks and equipment.
- **Scalability:** Formulas should allow for easy adjustment of batch sizes without compromising quality.
- **[[concepts/clarity-slider|Clarity]]:** Step-by-step [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] that anticipates common failure points.
