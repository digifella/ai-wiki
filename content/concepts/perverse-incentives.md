# Perverse Incentives

A **perverse [[concepts/motivation|incentive]]** is a reward mechanism that encourages counterproductive behavior, generating outcomes that undermine the objectives of the system, organization, or policymakers. These arise when individual rationality exploits structural flaws, metric misalignments, or unintended consequences of rule [[concepts/design|design]].

## Core Dynamics
- **Misalignment:** [[entities/agent|Agent]] goals diverge from principal objectives.
- **Metric [[concepts/gaming|Gaming]]:** Optimization of measured proxies over actual value (Goodhart's Law).
- **Unintended Consequences:** Solutions exacerbate the problem (e.g., Cobra Effect).
- **Rent-Seeking:** Resources directed toward capturing value rather than creating it.

## Mechanisms and Examples
- **Fee Structures:** Volume-based payments incentivize overconsumption or unnecessary interventions (e.g., fee-for-service [[concepts/health|healthcare]], contingency fee law).
- **Performance Targets:** Short-term bonuses [[concepts/ambition|drive]] risk-taking or data manipulation to meet quotas (2008 Financial Crisis).
- **Regulatory Loopholes:** [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]] costs create barriers that incumbents exploit or that encourage [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in avoidance rather than improvement.
- **Social Signaling:** Status rewards encourage conspicuous waste or competitive escalation unrelated to utility.

## Geopolitics and Conflict
- **[[entities/war-profiteering|War Profiteering]]:** Financial gains tied to [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] duration or intensity create incentives to prolong hostilities, manufacture crises, or sabotage diplomatic resolutions.
- **[[concepts/insider-trading|Insider Trading]] on War:** Exploitation of non-public intelligence regarding military operations allows actors to bet on conflict outcomes, potentially distorting strategic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and eroding trust in institutions.
- **Prediction Market Manipulation:** Distortion of probability estimates can [[concepts/power|influence]] resource allocation and public perception, creating [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] that alter the underlying event.
- **[[concepts/military-corruption|Military Corruption]]:** Procurement incentives misaligned with combat effectiveness degrade operational readiness and enable insider exploitation.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-13-War-Profiteering-Insider-Trading-Prediction-Markets-and|War Profiteering: Insider Trading, Prediction Markets, and Military Corruption]]

## Mitigation Strategies
- **Alignment:** Restructure rewards to correlate strictly with desired outcomes.
- **Redundancy:** Use multiple, non-collinear metrics to prevent gaming.
- **Transparency:** Expose incentive structures to external scrutiny.
- **Adaptive Rules:** Regularly audit and update systems to close emerging arbitrage opportunities.
