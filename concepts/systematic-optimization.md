# Systematic Optimization

**Systematic Optimization** refers to structured methodologies for improving system performance, often applied in [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and [[concepts/machine-learning]] to enhance efficiency, [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]], and self-correction [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
