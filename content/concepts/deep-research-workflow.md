# Deep Research Workflow

[[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] [[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] describes a process that leverages [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s Deep Research feature to create professional websites without requiring [[concepts/coding|coding]] knowledge. The workflow combines [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]'s research [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] with web generation tools to produce functional, visually polished websites from research materials and content sources.

## Core Process

The workflow typically involves uploading source materials into NotebookLM, using the Deep Research feature to synthesize and organize information, and then converting the structured research [[concepts/output|output]] into website [[concepts/code|code]] or layouts. This approach bridges the gap between content research and [[concepts/website-building|web development]], allowing users without technical backgrounds to transform their research into presentable web properties.

## Applications

This workflow is particularly suited for creating informational websites, project portfolios, and documentation sites where the emphasis is on content [[concepts/organization|organization]] and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] rather than custom interactive functionality. It reduces barriers to web publishing by automating [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] and [[concepts/design|design]] decisions, though the resulting websites typically follow standard [[concepts/templates|templates]] and structures rather than highly customized designs.
