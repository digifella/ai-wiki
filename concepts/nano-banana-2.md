## Nano Banana 2

**Summary:** [[concepts/camera-angle-control|Nano Banana 2]] is a model designed to enable precise control over AI-generated images through the use of JSON code within [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] app. It aims to mitigate issues such as image distortion or "[[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]]" that often arise when attempting minor edits with traditional [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]].
