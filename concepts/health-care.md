# Health Care

**[[concepts/health|Health]] Care** encompasses the maintenance and improvement of health via the [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]], diagnosis, treatment, amelioration, or cure of disease, illness, injury, and other physical and mental impairments in people. It involves a broad spectrum of services, from primary care to specialized medical interventions.
