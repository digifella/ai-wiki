# Classic Car Modification

**Classic Car Modification** refers to the alteration of vintage or classic automobiles to improve performance, aesthetics, safety, or functionality while often balancing historical authenticity with modern technology. Key areas include [[concepts/engine|Engine]] Swaps, Suspension Upgrades, Interior [[concepts/preservation|Restoration]], and Modern Electronics Integration.

## Modern Electronics Integration

Integrating contemporary electrical systems into pre-1980s vehicles requires careful planning to preserve original wiring harnesses and aesthetics. Common modifications include:

- **LED Retrofits**: Replacing incandescent bulbs with LED Lighting for improved efficiency and brightness.
- **Hidden Safety Features**: Installing modern safety aids that are visually discreet to maintain period-correct [[concepts/presence|appearance]].
- **Digital Dash Clusters**: Replacing analog gauges with digital displays while retaining original housings.

## General Principles

- **Reversibility**: Modifications should ideally be reversible to preserve collector value.
- **Wiring Standards**: Use of Relay Circuits and Fuse Boxes to protect original wiring from modern electrical loads.
- **Aesthetic [[concepts/continuity|Continuity]]**: Modern components should be hidden or styled to match the era of the vehicle.
