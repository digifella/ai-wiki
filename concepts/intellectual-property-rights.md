# Intellectual Property Rights

Exclusive legal rights granted to creators/owners over intangible creations, balancing incentives for [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] with public domain access. Core mechanism for monetizing knowledge assets and structuring competitive moats.

## Categories
- **Patents**: [[concepts/secure|Protection]] for inventions, processes, designs; requires novelty, non-obviousness, utility; limited term; disclosure trade-off.
- **Copyright**: Protection for original expressions (code, text, art, media); automatic upon fixation; covers reproduction/distribution/derivative works; [[entities/meta-fair-lab|fair]] use/fair dealing limitations.
- **Trademarks**: Source identifiers (brands, logos); indefinite term contingent on use; prevents consumer confusion.
- **Trade Secrets**: Confidential business information ([[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], formulas, data); protection lasts indefinitely while secret; no registration; vulnerable to reverse engineering/independent discovery.

## Economic & Valuation Implications
- IP constitutes primary asset base for knowledge-intensive sectors; valuation relies on projected cash flows, [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] potential, and defensive barriers.
- Risk of IP Valuation#Volatility in private markets due to illiquidity, lack of standardized [[concepts/pricing|pricing]], and enforcement uncertainty.
- Integration with Capital Markets via securitization, IP-backed lending, and equity pricing models heavily weighted toward proprietary technology stacks.

## Enforcement & Market Integrity
- Remedies include injunctions, damages, seizure; cross-border enforcement varies significantly by jurisdiction.
- Unauthorized secondary markets for IP-heavy equity face heightened scrutiny; legal voiding of transfers can occur when transactions violate transfer restrictions, securities laws, or contractual covenants protecting IP asset [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
