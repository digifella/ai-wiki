# Lens blur

An optical effect where objects outside the focal plane are rendered with reduced [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]], simulating [[concepts/camera-settings]]. The aesthetic quality of this blur is termed bokeh.

## Key Concepts
- **Depth of Field (DoF)**: The [[concepts/range|range]] of distance within an image that appears acceptably sharp.
- **Focal Plane**: The specific plane where [[concepts/light|light]] is focused.
- **Physical Drivers**: Blur intensity is primarily controlled via aperture, [[concepts/focal-length|focal length]], and subject distance.

## Digital Simulation & Manipulation
- **Automated Tools**: [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] and [[concepts/lightroom]] feature automated Lens Blur tools that estimate depth.
- **Advanced Precision**:
    - [[concepts/photoshop]] [[concepts/depth-map]] technology enables precise 3D-[[concepts/style|style]] photo [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
    - Utilizes depth masks to achieve more realistic and granular results than standard automated tools.
