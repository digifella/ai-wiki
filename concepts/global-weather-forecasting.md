# Global Weather Forecasting

The practice of predicting atmospheric conditions over large geographic areas. Recent advancements in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] have shifted the paradigm from traditional numerical [[concepts/weather-forecasting|weather prediction]] (NWP) to data-driven models.

## Key Developments

*   **[[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[concepts/weathernext-3|WeatherNext 3]]**: A significant leap in AI-driven forecasting capabilities, offering higher [[concepts/solution|resolution]] and accuracy.
    *   Focuses on overcoming inherent limitations of previous models.
    *   Provides more accurate, timely, and local weather forecasts.
