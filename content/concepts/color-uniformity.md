# Color uniformity

The [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] of color characteristics—such as [[concepts/hue|hue]], saturation, and luminance—across a specific area or throughout an entire image.

### Tools & Techniques
- **[[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] [[concepts/variance-filter|Variance Filter]]** (Early Access)
    - Located within the **[[concepts/point-color]]** section of the **[[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]]**.
    - Enables control over the [[concepts/range|range]] of colors affected by a **[[concepts/point-color]]** adjustment after sampling a specific color.
