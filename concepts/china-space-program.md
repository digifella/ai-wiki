# China Space Program

The **China space program** is a state-directed effort by the [[entities/china|People's Republic of China]] to develop launch vehicles, launch facilities, and spacecraft for [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|space exploration]], resource exploitation, and military applications. Managed primarily by the China National Space Administration (CNSA) and the China Aerospace [[concepts/science|Science]] and Technology Corporation (CASC), it represents one of the most active national space agencies globally.

## Overview & Strategy
- **Primary Objectives**: Establish permanent presence on Moon, develop deep-space capabilities, maintain independent Global Positioning System (BDS), and advance [[concepts/human-spaceflight|human spaceflight]] via Tiangong [[concepts/space-station|Space Station]].
- **Key [[concepts/nodes|Entities]]**: CNSA (civilian), CASC (development/launch), CAS (science/research).
- **Strategic Context**: Part of broader national strategy for technological self-sufficiency and geopolitical influence, often contrasted with [[entities/nasa]] and ESA efforts, while competing commercially with [[entities/spacex]].

## Recent Developments (2026)
- **Crew Rotation & Operations**: Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] highlight changes in crew assignments and operational status of the Tiangong Space Station. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-27-Starship-IFT-3-China-Crew-Goonhilly-Sale-SpaceX-Financia|Starship IFT-3, China Crew, Goonhilly Sale, SpaceX Financials: Deep Space Updates]] for detailed context on the new crew lineup and associated mission profiles.
- **Lunar Ambitions**: Continued progress toward crewed [[concepts/lunar-landing|lunar landing]] preparations, including development of next-generation launch vehicles (e.g., Long March 9) and lunar rovers.

## Key Infrastructure
- **Launch Sites**: Jiuquan [[concepts/satellite-launch|Satellite Launch]] Center, Taiyuan Satellite Launch Center, Xichang Satellite Launch Center, Wenchang Spacecraft Launch Site.
- **Human Spaceflight**: Shenzhou Program (manned missions), Tianzhou Program (cargo resupply).

## Geopolitical & Commercial Landscape
- **International Cooperation**: Selective partnerships (e.g., with ESA for [[concepts/lunar-return|lunar missions]], though US-China cooperation is restricted by Wolf Amendment).
- **Commercial Sector**: Emerging private aerospace companies supported by state capital, focusing on small-sat launches and reusable rocket technologies to compete with global market leaders.
