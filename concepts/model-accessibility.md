# Model Accessibility

**Model [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]** refers to the degree to which [[concepts/ai-models|artificial intelligence models]] are available, usable, and modifiable by the broader public or specific communities, rather than being restricted to proprietary or closed ecosystems. It encompasses technical availability ([[concepts/parameters|weights]], code), economic barriers (cost of inference/training), and legal frameworks (licenses).

## Core Dimensions

*   **[[concepts/open-source-weights|Open Weights]] vs. Open Source:** Distinction between releasing [[concepts/model-weights|model weights]] for [[concepts/inference|inference]] and providing full training data/code for modification.
*   **[[concepts/economic-impact|Economic Impact]]:** Reduction of dependency on expensive proprietary APIs; democratization of [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] resources.
*   **Geopolitical Dynamics:** Shift in AI power structures as non-Western [[concepts/nodes|entities]] [[concepts/deployment|release]] competitive [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier models]].

## Recent Developments: Kimi K3

The release of **[[concepts/kimi-k3|Kimi K3]]** represents a significant milestone in model accessibility, particularly regarding the diversification of [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]] capabilities outside traditional Western hubs.

*   **Frontier Capability:** Kimi K3 is a newly released [[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]] from [[entities/china|China]], demonstrating capabilities comparable to leading closed-source models [[lab-notes/2026-07-22-Kimi-K3-Geopolitical-and-Economic-Impact-of-Open-Source|Kimi K3: Geopolitical and Economic Impact of Open-Source Chinese AI]].
*   **Geopolitical Significance:** Its release challenges the monopoly of closed-source ecosystems, potentially accelerating global [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|open standards]].
*   **[[concepts/economic-implications|Economic Implications]]:** By providing [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/open-weight-models|open weights]], it lowers the barrier to entry for developers and enterprises seeking to build upon frontier architectures without [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] fees.
