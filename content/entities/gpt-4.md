# GPT-4

Advanced [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], succeeding [[entities/gpt-35|GPT-3.5]]. Excels in complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/ai-coding|code generation]], and multi-modal tasks.

- **Hallucinations**: [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]] can generate [[concepts/ai-hallucinations]] (plausible but factually incorrect outputs), a widespread issue not limited to minor bugs. The [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] [[concepts/summary|Summary]] Report (20cal 03-06, [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Flash) details this pervasive problem in [[concepts/large-language-models|LLMs]].
- **[[concepts/training|Training]] [[concepts/cost|Cost]] & [[concepts/quantisation|Quantisation]]**: GPT-4's 2023 [[concepts/training|training]] cost was estimated [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] $78 million ([[entities/sam-altman|Sam Altman]] claimed higher), highlighting [[concepts/llm-training|LLM training]]'s economic challenges. [[concepts/quantisation|4-bit quantisation]] reduces computational costs by enabling 4-bit [[concepts/precision-training|precision training]], as explored in [[entities/julia-turc|Julia Turc]]'s [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] video.
