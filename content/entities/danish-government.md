# Danish Government

The Danish Government is the executive branch of Denmark's system of government, operating under a constitutional [[concepts/monarchy|monarchy]] framework. It is headed by the Prime Minister and comprises various ministries responsible for implementing policy and administering the state across areas including finance, defense, [[concepts/health|health]], education, and environment.

## Archaeological Policy

The Danish government has authorized private metal detectorists to conduct systematic searches of farm fields for archaeological artifacts. This policy reflects an approach to [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]] management that leverages public participation in archaeological discovery, subject to appropriate regulations and oversight to ensure artifacts are properly documented and preserved.
