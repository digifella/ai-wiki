# Deep Processing

**Deep Processing** refers to cognitive [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] that involve elaborate, meaningful, or structural engagement with information, as opposed to superficial or shallow [[concepts/encoding|encoding]]. In the context of [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]], it is associated with better [[concepts/storing|retention]] and understanding. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]], it relates to the internal representational states and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes that occur within model architectures.

## Key Characteristics
- **Elaborative Encoding**: Connecting new information to existing knowledge structures.
- **Structural Analysis**: Examining the form and organization of data rather than just surface features.
- **Semantic Engagement**: Focusing on meaning and implications.

## AI and Internal States
Recent investigations into [[concepts/llm-internal-states|LLM internal states]] reveal that models develop [[concepts/emergent-internal-models|emergent internal models]] to process information beyond simple token [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]]. As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-07-16-AI-Emergent-Internal-Models-Line-Length-Counters-and-Spa|AI Emergent Internal Models: Line-Length Counters and Spatial Understanding]], these mechanisms include:

- **[[concepts/line-length-counters|Line-Length Counters]]**: Emergent capabilities where models implicitly track structural metrics, such as line lengths, indicating a form of syntactic or structural [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
- **[[concepts/spatial-understanding|Spatial Understanding]]**: The development of internal representations that allow for the processing of spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and geometry, suggesting that deep processing in AI involves constructing coherent internal [[concepts/interactive-environments|world models]] rather than just statistical [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]].
