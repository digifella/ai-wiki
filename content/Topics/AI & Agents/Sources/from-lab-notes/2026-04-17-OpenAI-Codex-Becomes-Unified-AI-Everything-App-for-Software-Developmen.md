## OpenAI Codex Becomes Unified AI Everything App for [[concepts/app-creation|Software Development]]
**Clip title:** Codex Just Became the Everything App
**Author / channel:** Prompt Engineering
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QW_07aHH_L4

### [[concepts/summary|Summary]]
The video details a significant update to OpenAI's Codex, positioning it as an "everything app" designed to function as a powerful partner across the full software development lifecycle. Instead of the anticipated [[entities/gpt-5|GPT-5]], this release focuses on expanding Codex's capabilities to interact more directly and comprehensively with a user's computer and various applications, consolidating disparate tools into a unified AI-driven [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].

A cornerstone of this update is Codex's enhanced "computer use" capability, particularly on macOS. The AI can now operate applications by seeing, clicking, and typing with its own [[entities/cursor|cursor]], mimicking human interaction. A demonstration showcases Codex identifying and fixing a bug in a Tic-Tac-Toe game running in Xcode, illustrating its ability to execute, test, and debug [[concepts/code|code]] within the native development environment. Complementing this is the introduction of a native in-app browser, allowing developers to render web pages directly within Codex, provide visual feedback, and iterate on designs efficiently. This feature is expected to be highly beneficial for front-end and game development, with aspirations to extend its control beyond web applications to all aspects of computing.

Furthermore, the updated Codex integrates advanced image generation capabilities, similar to other [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], enabling users to create and embed visual assets directly into their projects, such as generating hero images for webpages. The platform also boasts a significant expansion of its plugin ecosystem, with over 90 new integrations for popular productivity applications like SharePoint, [[entities/google-calendar|Google Calendar]], Jira, [[entities/slack|Slack]], Notion, [[entities/github|GitHub]], and more. These [[concepts/plugins|plugins]] allow Codex to gather context from various sources, summarize complex documents, plan tasks, and take actions across a wide array of tools, thereby empowering both developers and non-developers in their daily work.

The video concludes by highlighting this strategic refocusing by OpenAI on core [[concepts/coding|coding]] and "super app" functionalities, which is viewed positively compared to previous 'side quests' that may have diluted efforts. The increased competition in the AI space, notably with rivals like [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] and Google, is driving rapid [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and delivering robust features to consumers. While these updates are initially available for macOS, they promise a more streamlined and powerful AI-assisted development and productivity experience, indicating a future where AI [[concepts/agents|agents]] can fluidly navigate and act within a user's entire digital environment.
