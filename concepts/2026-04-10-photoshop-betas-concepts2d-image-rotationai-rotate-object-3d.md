[[concepts/2d-image-rotation|AI Rotate Object]] is a beta feature in [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] that enables users to rotate objects within 2D images using AI-assisted [[concepts/3d-manipulation|3D transformation]] capabilities. Unlike traditional 2D rotation tools that simply pivot content around a fixed axis, this feature analyzes the spatial and dimensional properties of objects in photographs and applies [[concepts/3d-rotation|three-dimensional rotation]] principles to generate results that appear spatially coherent.

The functionality relies on [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to interpret the geometry and perspective of selected subjects. By estimating depth and surface orientation, the system calculates how the object would appear if rotated in a three-dimensional space, allowing for realistic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] that maintain lighting and shadow [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] relative to the new orientation.

This capability allows for more natural manipulation of subjects in composite images or perspective corrections. Users can adjust the angle of an object to match a specific viewpoint or create dynamic compositions without the distortion typically associated with standard 2D rotation tools. The feature remains in beta, indicating ongoing development and potential adjustments to its accuracy and performance parameters.
