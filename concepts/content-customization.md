# Content Customization

**Content [[concepts/customization|customization]]** refers to the deliberate modification of digital media feeds to align with user preferences, reduce noise, and enhance relevance. In the context of algorithmic platforms, this involves actively filtering or suppressing specific content types that degrade the [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]].

## Key Strategies

*   **Algorithmic Intervention**: Users can influence recommendation engines by actively disliking or hiding specific content categories, signaling a preference for traditional long-form content over short-form or synthetic media.
*   **Suppression of Shorts**: Actively reducing the visibility of [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] Shorts helps reclaim [[concepts/context-window|attention span]] and reduces the dominance of vertical, rapid-fire video formats in the main feed.
*   **Mitigation of [[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-Generated Content]]**: Identifying and filtering out [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|AI-generated videos]] is a growing practice to maintain content authenticity and quality standards within personal feeds.
