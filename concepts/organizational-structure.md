# Organizational Structure

Organizational structure refers to the formal system of task and reporting [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within an organization that defines how activities are divided, grouped, and coordinated. A well-defined organizational structure enables effective communication, [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], [[concepts/coordination|coordination]], and control in [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of organizational goals.

### Key Points
- Defines [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]] and roles
- Facilitates communication and information [[concepts/flow|flow]]
- Supports goal alignment and resource allocation
