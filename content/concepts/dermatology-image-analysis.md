# Dermatology image analysis

Backlink: [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] 04 14 [[concepts/dermatology-images|MedGemma 27B]] [[entities/fahd-merza|Fahd Merza]]

The application of [[concepts/computer-vision|Computer Vision]] and Deep [[concepts/learning|Learning]] to interpret clinical and dermoscopic [[concepts/images|images]] for the identification, classification, and monitoring of Skin Lesions and dermatological pathologies.

### Emerging Multimodal Models
- MedGemma 27B (Google):
    - Built on [[concepts/gemma-3-architecture|Gemma 3 architecture]].
    - Specialized in Multimodal Learning, enabling simultaneous comprehension of medical text and image data.
    - Available in multiple variants, including 4B parameter models (pre-trained and instruction-tuned) and 27B parameter versions.
    - Facilitates advanced Medical Image Analysis by correlating visual features with clinical documentation.
