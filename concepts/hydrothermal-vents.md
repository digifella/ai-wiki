# Hydrothermal Vents

**Hydrothermal vents** are fissures on the [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s surface that [[concepts/deployment|release]] geothermally heated water. They are critical sites for studying [[concepts/abiogenesis]] and extreme [[concepts/extremophiles|extremophiles]].

## Origin of Life Hypotheses

### Traditional View
- **[[concepts/primordial-soup|Primordial Soup]]**: The classical [[concepts/theory|theory]] suggesting life arose in shallow pools or oceans via chemical reactions driven by lightning or UV radiation.
- **RNA World**: Focuses on self-replicating RNA molecules as the precursor to life.

### Emerging Evidence: Dual Origins
Recent research challenges the singular origin [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]], proposing that life may have emerged through two distinct pathways within hydrothermal systems [[lab-notes/2026-08-28-Rethinking-LUCA-Evidence-for-Dual-Origins-of-Life-in-Hyd|Rethinking LUCA: Evidence for Dual Origins of Life in Hydrothermal Vents]].

- **Challenge to Primordial Soup**: New studies indicate that the "primordial soup" model is insufficient to explain the complexity of early life [[Rethinking LUCA: Evidence for Dual Origins of Life in Hydrothermal Vents](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zFk-8_0dXYo)].
- **Dual Origin Theory**: Evidence suggests two separate [[concepts/start-of-life|origins of life]] occurred in hydrothermal vent environments, potentially leading to the Last Universal Common Ancestor ([[concepts/luca|LUCA]]).
- **LUCA Re-evaluation**: The concept of LUCA is being rethought in [[concepts/light|light]] of these dual origins, implying a more complex web of early genetic exchange rather than a single linear descent.
