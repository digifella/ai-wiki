# Deep Research Workflow

Deep Research Workflow describes a process that leverages [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s Deep Research feature to create professional websites without requiring [[concepts/coding|coding]] knowledge. The workflow combines NotebookLM's research capabilities with web generation tools to produce functional, visually polished websites from research materials and content sources.

## Core Process

The workflow typically involves uploading source materials into NotebookLM, using the Deep Research feature to synthesize and organize information, and then converting the structured research output into website code or layouts. This approach bridges the gap between content research and [[concepts/website-building|web development]], allowing users without technical backgrounds to transform their research into presentable web properties.

## Applications

This workflow is particularly suited for creating informational websites, project portfolios, and documentation sites where the emphasis is on content [[concepts/organization|organization]] and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] rather than custom interactive functionality. It reduces barriers to web publishing by automating code generation and design decisions, though the resulting websites typically follow standard [[concepts/templates|templates]] and structures rather than highly customized designs.
