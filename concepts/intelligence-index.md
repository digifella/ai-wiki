# Intelligence Index

The **Intelligence Index** is a standardized metric used to quantify and [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] the cognitive capabilities of [[concepts/ai-models|artificial intelligence models]]. It serves as a primary benchmark for evaluating [[concepts/model-performance|model performance]] across various tasks, including [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and natural language understanding.
