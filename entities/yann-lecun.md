# Yann LeCun

**Yann LeCun** is a French-American computer scientist known for his pioneering work in Deep Learning, particularly in the development of Convolutional [[concepts/neural-networks|Neural Networks]] (CNNs). He is currently the Chief AI Scientist at [[entities/meta]] and a Professor at New York University.

## Key Contributions

*   **Convolutional Networks**: Developed the foundational architecture for modern computer vision systems.
*   **LeCun Loss**: Proposed a loss function for learning with structured output.
*   **Graph Neural Networks**: Contributed to the theoretical underpinnings of GNNs.
