# Digital Health Framework

A structured model for leveraging [[concepts/digital-health]] technologies to transform [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]], improve [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]], and optimize system efficiency. It serves as a roadmap for integrating technology into clinical workflows and organizational strategies.

## Core Components
- **[[concepts/person-enabled-health|Person-Enabled Health]]**: Shifts focus from provider-centric to patient-centric care, empowering individuals through accessible digital tools and data.
- **[[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|Predictive Analytics]]**: Utilizes data-driven models to anticipate [[concepts/health|health]] outcomes, enabling proactive rather than reactive care.
- **Digital Health Indicator**: Metrics used to assess the maturity and impact of digital health implementations within health systems.
- **Disease Control**: Integration of digital tools to monitor and manage [[concepts/population-health|population health]] and epidemic outbreaks.

## Implementation & Transformation
- Acts as a catalyst for [[concepts/medical-revolution|Healthcare Transformation]] by aligning technology investments with strategic organizational goals.
- Facilitates the transition from fragmented care systems to integrated, interoperable health networks.
- Supports the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/healthcare-informatization|Health Information Systems]] that enhance data exchange and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
