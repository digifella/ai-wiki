# Aihw

Aihw is a [[concepts/survey|survey]] component designed to measure respondent familiarity with the term "[[concepts/digital-health|digital health]]." It functions as a baseline assessment tool within broader digital health surveys, establishing the knowledge level of participants before they encounter more detailed questions about digital health practices, technologies, or services.

## Measurement Approach

The component employs a four-point Likert scale to capture self-assessed familiarity with the term. This standardized measurement approach enables consistent data collection across diverse populations, allowing researchers to gauge the general public's or specific demographic groups' understanding of digital health concepts. By quantifying this baseline familiarity, the tool helps contextualize subsequent survey responses and identifies potential gaps in public [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]] regarding digital health initiatives.
