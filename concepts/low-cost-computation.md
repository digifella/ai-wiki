# Low-Cost Computation

**Low-cost [[concepts/computation|computation]]** refers to computational strategies and architectures designed to minimize resource expenditure (energy, latency, and financial cost) while maintaining functional efficacy. This concept is critical for scalable AI deployment, [[concepts/edge-computing|edge computing]], and sustainable technology.

## Key Drivers
- **[[concepts/inference-efficiency|Inference Optimization]]**: Reducing the cost of running models post-training.
- **Hardware Efficiency**: Leveraging specialized chips ([[entities/tpus|TPUs]], NPUs) for specific workloads.
- **[[concepts/algorithm-optimization|Algorithmic Efficiency]]**: Using sparse models, [[concepts/precision-reduction|quantization]], and distillation.

## Emerging Architectures
Traditional [[concepts/large-language-model]] often suffer from high [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] costs due to sequential token generation. New approaches focus on:

- **Non-Sequential [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]**: Moving away from [[concepts/token-by-token-text-generation|autoregressive text generation]] toward direct action selection.
- **[[concepts/high-speed-inference|High-Speed Inference]]**: Prioritizing low-latency responses for real-time applications.
- **Cost-Effective [[concepts/computational-scaling|Scaling]]**: Achieving [[concepts/performance-gains|performance gains]] without proportional increases in [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power.

## Case Study: TypeSafe AI's Jev
Recent developments highlight a shift toward specialized decision-making engines that bypass traditional [[concepts/ai-limitations|LLM bottlenecks]].

- **Core [[concepts/innovation|Innovation]]**: [[lab-notes/2026-09-24-TypeSafe-AIs-Jev-High-Speed-Low-Cost-Decision-Making-AI|TypeSafe AI's Jev: High-Speed, Low-Cost Decision-Making AI]] represents a departure from [[concepts/sequential-text-generation|sequential text generation]].
- **Mechanism**: Jev makes rapid, decisive choices from a predefined set of actions, rather than generating text token-by-token.
- **Performance**: Designed for high-[[concepts/speed|speed]], low-cost decision-making, distinguishing it from [[concepts/general-purpose-models|general-purpose models]] like [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]].
- **Significance**: Demonstrates the viability of non-[[concepts/llm-models|LLM architectures]] for specific, high-frequency tasks where cost and speed are paramount.
