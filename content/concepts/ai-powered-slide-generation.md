# AI-powered slide generation

AI-powered slide generation uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automate slide creation from text prompts, outlines, or content, significantly reducing [[concepts/design|design]] time while maintaining professional quality.

- **[[entities/chat4o|Chat4o]] + Piktochart workflow**:
  * Generate slide content via [[entities/chat4o|Chat4o]] ([[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]]) and design with Piktochart's AI [[concepts/presentation-tool|presentation tool]]
