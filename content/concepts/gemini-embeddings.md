# Gemini Embeddings

[[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]] Embeddings are high-dimensional [[concepts/vector-representations|vector representations]] produced by the [[entities/gemini]] model family, used to encode semantic meaning for downstream [[concepts/natural-language-processing|NLP]] tasks.

## Research & Development
- Integral to [[entities/google-deepmind]]'s ongoing research into [[concepts/frontier-ai|Frontier AI]].
- Current research trajectories focus on the intersection of Intelligence and Sustainability in large-scale model architectures.
- Advancements in these embeddings are part of a broader strategy to evolve [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] as presented by [[entities/raia-hadsell|Raia Hadsell]] (VP of Research, [[entities/google-deepmind]]).
- Research emphasis includes the nexus of intelligence and sustainability within [[entities/gemini-pro|Gemini]]'s frontier [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].
