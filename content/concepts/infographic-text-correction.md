# Infographic Text Correction

[[concepts/infographic|Infographic]] [[concepts/text|Text]] Correction addresses the practical challenge of extracting and refining text from [[concepts/ai-generated-infographics|AI-generated infographics]], where [[concepts/aesthetic-design|visual design]] tools often produce imperfect or misaligned text elements. When AI systems generate infographics, the resulting text may contain OCR errors, formatting inconsistencies, or misaligned content that requires correction before the final product is usable. This [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] is particularly relevant for creators who use generative tools to produce visual content but need reliable [[concepts/text-accuracy|text accuracy]] for publication or [[concepts/professional-use|professional use]].

## Adobe Acrobat vs. Canva 'Grab Text'

[[entities/adobe-acrobat|Adobe Acrobat]] and [[entities/canva|Canva]]'s 'Grab Text' feature represent two different approaches to text extraction from infographics. Adobe Acrobat provides robust [[concepts/optical-character-recognition-ocr|optical character recognition (OCR)]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] designed for [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]], offering high accuracy on structured text and the ability to edit extracted text directly within [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]]. Canva's 'Grab Text' tool, by [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], is optimized for Canva's native design environment and prioritizes [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/integration|integration]] with existing [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]], making it more accessible for casual creators but potentially less precise with complex layouts.

The choice between these tools depends on workflow requirements. Adobe Acrobat suits projects requiring detailed text [[concepts/verification|verification]], batch processing, or final document [[concepts/preparation|preparation]], while Canva's 'Grab Text' works better for rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] within the design platform itself. Neither tool eliminates the need for manual review, particularly with AI-generated content where unusual layouts or stylized fonts may confuse [[concepts/automations|automated systems]]. Most professional infographic workflows benefit from using both tools in sequence: initial extraction via Canva for speed, followed by verification in Adobe Acrobat for accuracy.
