# Demo Access

Demo access refers to the provisional grant of access to experimental or [[concepts/beta-software|beta software]] tools and services, typically communicated to users via automated [[entities/email|email]] notification. In the context of the [[concepts/gemini-diffusion-model|Gemini Diffusion model]], this mechanism allows selected individuals to interact with [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s latest [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] capabilities before they are released to the general public. This approach enables the organization to manage server load and ensure that only qualified users engage with the system during its early development phases.

The primary purpose of such access [[concepts/software|programs]] is to gather targeted [[concepts/user-feedback|user feedback]] and identify technical issues that may not be apparent in controlled testing environments. By allowing a limited group of users to test [[concepts/new-features|new features]], organizations can refine product stability and functionality based on real-[[entities/earth|world]] usage patterns. This process is critical for improving the quality of the final [[concepts/deployment|release]] and ensuring that the tool meets the needs of its intended audience.

Implementation of demo access often involves a rigorous selection process to ensure that participants have the necessary technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] to provide meaningful insights. Users granted this access are expected to report bugs, evaluate [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]], and assess the usability of the interface. This collaborative effort helps developers address potential problems early in the lifecycle, reducing the risk of widespread issues upon public launch.
