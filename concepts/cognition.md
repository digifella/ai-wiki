Cognition is a broad term encompassing mental processes related to [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]], knowing, remembering, judging, and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. It involves activities such as [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]], [[concepts/memory|memory]], producing and understanding language, solving problems, and making decisions.

### Recent Discoveries
- [[concepts/bumblebees|Bumblebees]] exhibit advanced cognition, challenging previous assumptions about their intelligence. Despite having tiny brains the size of poppy seeds, these insects display remarkable abilities that were not expected.
  - **[[concepts/cumulative-culture|Cumulative Culture]]**: [[concepts/bumblebees|Bumblebees]] can learn from each other and build upon existing knowledge, showing a form of [[concepts/cumulative-culture|cumulative culture]] similar to what is seen in humans and some primates.
  - **Flood [[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]**: Studies have also shown bumblebee [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] in adapting to environmental challenges such as flooding.

- Recent research has revealed that bumblebees are far more complex and intelligent than previously understood, challenging long-held biological assumptions. Despite their small brains, these insects exhibit astonishing abilities that fundamentally alter our understanding of [[concepts/bumblebee-cognition|insect cognition]].
