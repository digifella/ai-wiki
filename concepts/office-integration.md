# Office Integration

**Office Integration** refers to the architectural and functional convergence of [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]]'s [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] suite with its [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] capabilities, specifically through the [[entities/microsoft-copilot]] ecosystem. This concept encompasses the transition from isolated tools to a unified, context-aware enterprise workspace.

## Core Components

The integration strategy relies on three primary pillars:

*   **[[concepts/unified-enterprise-workspace|Unified Enterprise Workspace]]:** A consolidated interface that aggregates data and actions across the [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]] stack, reducing context switching.
*   **Home & Autopilot:** The introduction of a centralized "Home" hub and "Autopilot" features that automate routine workflows and provide proactive assistance.
*   **Code Integration:** Deep embedding of [[concepts/ai-driven-app-development|AI-driven coding]] assistance directly into [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]] and [[concepts/business-applications|enterprise applications]].
