# Adobe Camera Raw

**[[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR)** is a raw [[concepts/image-processing|image processing]] plugin and standalone application developed by [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]. It serves as the engine behind the raw development capabilities in [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]]. ACR allows photographers to adjust exposure, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], color balance, and detail in raw files before importing them into other Adobe applications.

## Key Features
- **Raw Processing:** [[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-destructive editing]] of raw image data.
- **Gradient Filters:** Includes linear, radial, and Gradient Mask tools for localized adjustments.
- **[[concepts/bidirectional-gradient|Bidirectional Gradient]]:** A newer feature allowing gradients to expand from both ends of a line simultaneously, offering more precise control over tonal transitions.
- **AI-Powered Tools:** Includes Select Subject, Select Sky, and Masking features powered by Adobe Sensei.
- **VectorScope:** Introduced in ACR 18.4, this tool enables precise [[concepts/skin-tone-correction|skin tone correction]] by providing objective color data, addressing the unreliability of human perception.
