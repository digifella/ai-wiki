# Feynman Lectures Overview

Richard P. [[concepts/feynman|Feynman]]'s lectures on [[concepts/physics|physics]] are renowned for their [[concepts/lightroom|clarity]] and insight into complex scientific concepts. The lectures cover topics ranging from mechanics to quantum electrodynamics (QED), emphasizing not just the "how" but also the "why" of physical laws.

### Key Points
- Emphasis on [[concepts/conceptual-understanding|conceptual understanding]] over mere [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|procedural knowledge]].
- Use of simple examples to illustrate deep principles.
- Importance of questioning established notions and seeking deeper insights.
