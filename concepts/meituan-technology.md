# Meituan Technology

Meituan Technology is a Chinese technology company engaged in artificial intelligence model development. The company has focused on exploring alternative approaches to large-scale AI model training, particularly investigating hardware architectures and training methodologies that diverge from the industry-standard reliance on Nvidia processors.

## LongCat 2.0

Meituan Technology developed LongCat 2.0, a large language model with 1.6 trillion parameters. The model was trained without Nvidia hardware, representing an exploration of alternative computational approaches for scaling AI systems. This development is notable within the context of ongoing efforts across various technology companies to reduce dependency on specific hardware vendors for AI model training and deployment.
