# Golden Circle

The **Golden Circle** is a conceptual model developed by [[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]] that explains how great leaders and organizations inspire others to act through the power of their "why." It consists of three concentric circles:
- **Why**: The purpose or cause.
- **How**: The unique strengths or values used to fulfill [[concepts/core-purpose|the why]].
- **What**: The products, services, and tangible actions.

### Key Concepts
- **Inspiring [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]]**: Leaders who communicate from the inside out (starting with "why") inspire others more effectively than those who focus solely on what they do.
- **[[concepts/inspiration-through-communication|Purpose-Driven Communication]]**: Communicating one's purpose first creates a deeper emotional [[concepts/connection|connection]].
- **Golden Circle Model**: Helps individuals and organizations clarify their mission, differentiate themselves in crowded markets, and foster loyalty.

### Summary
[[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]’s TEDx talk, "[[concepts/start-with-why|Start With Why]]: How Great Leaders Inspire Action," explores the fundamental question of why some individuals and organizations achieve enduring [[concepts/success|success]] and inspire action, while others with seemingly equal resources and talent falter. Sinek begins by highlighting iconic examples such as [[entities/apple|Apple]] to illustrate how these successful [[concepts/nodes|entities]] articulate their purpose first.
