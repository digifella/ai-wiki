# Allied Health Services

[[concepts/allied-health|Allied health]] services comprise a diverse group of health professions and services distinct from nursing [[entities/national-academies|and medicine]], yet integral to [[concepts/health|healthcare]] delivery. These services focus on diagnosis, treatment, [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]], and rehabilitation.

## Scope and Integration
*   Includes disciplines such as Physiotherapy, Occupational Therapy, Dietetics, and Speech Pathology.
*   Increasingly integrated with [[concepts/telehealth]] models to enhance access and [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of care.
*   Critical for multidisciplinary team approaches in [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|chronic disease management]] and acute care settings.

## Recent Guidance & Developments
*   **Telehealth [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Specific guidance exists to support health professionals in delivering allied [[concepts/health-care|health services]] via [[concepts/corporate-platforms|digital platforms]], ensuring equitable patient access.
*   **Source Integration**: See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-GUIDANCE-Allied-health-and-telehealth-Accessible|GUIDANCE Allied health and telehealth Accessible]] for detailed [[concepts/recommendations|directives]] on remote [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]] standards.
*   **Key Principles** (per SCV/DHHS guidance):
    *   Ensuring appropriate clinical oversight in remote settings.
    *   Maintaining patient [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and data [[concepts/security|security]] during telehealth consultations.
    *   Adapting therapeutic interventions for digital delivery channels.
