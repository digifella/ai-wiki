# Intersect masking

A specialized [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] operation in [[entities/adobe-lightroom]] (introduced late 2023) that isolates only the area where two or more overlapping masks coincide.

### Key Capabilities
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] [[concepts/masking-tools|Local Adjustments]]:** Enables highly targeted edits by refining overlapping selection areas to create complex shapes.
- **Workflow Synergy:** Functions alongside [[concepts/add-masking|Add Masking]] and [[concepts/subtract-masking|Subtract Masking]] to allow for advanced, multi-layered selection refinement.
