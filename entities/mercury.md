# Mercury

The innermost planet of the Solar System, Mercury is characterized by its proximity to the Sun, extreme temperature variations, and lack of a substantial [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]].

## Exploration

### BepiColombo Mission
The [[concepts/project-mercury|BepiColombo mission]], a joint venture between the [[entities/esa|European Space Agency]] (ESA) and the [[entities/jaxa|Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency]] (JAXA), launched in 2018 aims to study Mercury's [[concepts/writing|composition]], geophysics, and magnetic field.

*   **Mission Complexity:** Reaching Mercury is more challenging than traveling to outer gas giants due to the intense [[concepts/solar-gravity]] and extreme thermal environment near the Sun.
*   **Trajectory:** The spacecraft utilizes a complex series of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] assists to shed [[concepts/velocity|velocity]] and enter orbit around the innermost planet.
*   **Resources:** For detailed analysis of the mission's challenges and navigation, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-BepiColombo-Mission-to-Mercury-Navigating-Solar-Gravity|BepiColombo Mission to Mercury: Navigating Solar Gravity and Extreme Heat]].
