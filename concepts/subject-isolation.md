# Subject Isolation

Subject isolation is a post-processing technique in digital photography that separates a subject from its background to create visual emphasis or apply targeted adjustments. The technique relies on creating precise masks around the subject and refining their edges to achieve a natural, seamless separation. This approach is particularly useful when the subject and background have similar tones or when selective editing is desired without affecting the entire image.

## Mask Edge Refinement

The core challenge in subject isolation is creating clean, accurate edges around the subject. Modern editing software like Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw includes dedicated tools for mask edge refinement, allowing photographers to manually paint or automatically detect subject boundaries. These tools typically offer options to adjust edge softness, feathering, and transparency, which helps blend the isolated subject naturally with its surroundings rather than producing harsh, artificial-looking cuts.

## Practical Applications

Subject isolation enables a range of editing workflows. Photographers can brighten or saturate only the subject while keeping the background unchanged, adjust sharpness selectively, or apply color grading to specific areas. The technique is also useful for background blur simulation or removal, selective noise reduction, and correcting exposure problems that affect only part of an image.
