# Adrian Twarog

Adrian Twarog is a content creator and educator specializing in agentic AI and autonomous AI agents. He produces instructional videos and written educational material designed to explain how agent-based AI systems function, including their architecture, configuration, and deployment. His work targets audiences seeking to understand these technologies without requiring extensive prior knowledge in AI or programming.

## Educational Focus

Twarog's content emphasizes the practical mechanics of agentic AI systems rather than theoretical foundations. His instructional approach covers how autonomous agents are structured, the configuration parameters that govern their behavior, and the methods used to implement them in practice. By breaking down complex technical concepts into accessible explanations, he helps learners understand the operational aspects of agent-based AI systems and how different configurations affect agent performance and capabilities.
