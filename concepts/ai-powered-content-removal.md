# Ai Powered Content Removal

[[concepts/ai-driven-distraction-removal|AI-powered content removal]] is a [[concepts/non-linear-editing|non-destructive editing]] feature introduced in [[concepts/camera-system-integration|Lightroom Classic v15]] that utilizes [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to detect and eliminate unwanted elements from photographs. The tool identifies objects, people, or artifacts within an image and removes them while preserving the surrounding context and overall [[concepts/writing|composition]]. This process relies on advanced [[concepts/image-analysis|image analysis]] to ensure that the removal appears natural and seamless.

The feature operates within a [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive workflow]], meaning the original image data remains intact and can be restored at any time. By leveraging machine learning, the system analyzes the visual content to intelligently fill the removed areas with plausible background details. This allows users to correct distractions or remove subjects without compromising the [[concepts/payload-structure|structural integrity]] or aesthetic balance of the final image.
