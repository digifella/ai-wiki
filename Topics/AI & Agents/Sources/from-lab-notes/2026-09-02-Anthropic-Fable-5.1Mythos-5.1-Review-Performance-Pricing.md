## Anthropic Fable 5.1/Mythos 5.1 Review: Performance, Pricing Claims, and Real-World Costs
**Clip title:** Anthropic went CRAZY (Mythos/Fable 5.1)
**Author / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=epogfA_0R4E

### Summary
The video provides a detailed review of Anthropic's newly released frontier models, [[entities/claude|Claude]] [[entities/fable-5|Fable 5]].1 and [[concepts/muse-spark-12|Mythos 5.1]]. The main topic revolves around the performance improvements, pricing changes, and new features introduced with these models, alongside a critical analysis of Anthropic's claims and comparison with competitors. Anthropic positions [[entities/fable-5|Fable 5]].1 as a superior model for coding, knowledge work, and complex [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]], claiming it's significantly cheaper—25% less for typical workloads and up to 45% for [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]], largely due to a 75% reduction in "cache read" costs for already processed inputs. The models also feature an Enterprise Frontier Safeguards (EFS) system allowing customers to control their data (stored on their [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], not Anthropic's) while still permitting Anthropic to read it for misuse detection, and comply with the EU AI Act by incorporating [[concepts/watermarks|watermarks]] into AI-generated text.

Initial benchmarks presented in the video largely support Anthropic's performance claims, showing Fable 5.1 significantly outperforming its predecessor, Fable 5, in areas like scientific research and [[concepts/agentic-coding|agentic coding]] (e.g., Terminal-Bench Science, CursorBench). Mythos 5.1, a less-guarded version of Fable 5.1, often scores even higher with similar or lower costs, illustrating the "guardrail dilemma" where restrictions can impede overall AI intelligence. However, independent analysis by Artificial Analysis, discussed later in the video, contradicts Anthropic's cost claims. Despite the reduced cache read price, Fable 5.1 is found to be *more expensive* per task than Fable 5 because it requires approximately 1.7 times more output tokens to achieve its intelligence level, making the overall cost for task completion higher than advertised.

The video also raises several key concerns and observations. The EFS zero-data retention policy is described as a "half-measure," as Anthropic still retains access to user data for misuse detection, which might not fully alleviate enterprise data [[concepts/privacy-concerns|privacy concerns]]. Anthropic's introduction of "anti-distillation mechanisms" to prevent other companies from "stealing" model intelligence is framed by the presenter as a commercial move rather than a pure safety measure, with implications for [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-source AI]]. Furthermore, tests of Fable 5.1's creative capabilities, such as generating websites and 3D biomes, yield mixed results; while some outputs are impressive, others contain flaws (e.g., incorrect 3D renderings) or raise eyebrows (like providing a specific physical address for a fictional "Apple website").

In conclusion, [[concepts/claude-fable-51|Claude Fable 5.1]] and Mythos 5.1 represent significant advancements in AI, solidifying Anthropic's strong position in the frontier AI landscape, with Anthropic models occupying many top spots in the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. However, the true cost-effectiveness of Fable 5.1 is contentious, with independent data suggesting it may be more expensive per completed task despite token price cuts. The ethical and operational implications of its data retention policies, anti-distillation safeguards, and the surprising similarity of some creative outputs to competitors like GLM 5.3 highlight ongoing challenges and complexities in the rapidly evolving AI space. For users prioritizing [[concepts/cost-efficiency|cost-efficiency]] alongside performance, models like GPT-5.6 Sol are noted as offering a better "bargain" for intelligence delivered.
