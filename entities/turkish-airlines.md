# Turkish Airlines

Turkish Airlines (Türk Hava Yolları, THY) is the national flag carrier and largest airline of Turkey. Founded in 1933, the airline operates scheduled domestic and international flights connecting destinations across Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas. Istanbul Airport serves as the airline's primary hub, making it a significant connection point for transcontinental travel between Europe and Asia.

## Operations and Network

The airline maintains an extensive route network spanning multiple continents. Turkish Airlines operates flights to major cities worldwide, serving both leisure and business travelers. The airline's hub position in Istanbul provides convenient transit options for passengers traveling between European and Asian destinations, leveraging Turkey's geographic location as a bridge between continents.

## International Presence

As Turkey's flagship carrier, Turkish Airlines represents the country in international aviation and maintains codeshare agreements with numerous global carriers. The airline is a member of the Star Alliance, one of the world's largest airline alliances, which enhances its connectivity and service offerings to international passengers.
