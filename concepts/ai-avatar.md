# AI Avatar

A digital representation of a person, created using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], designed to interact in a human-like manner. Common applications include [[concepts/voice-assistants|virtual assistants]], customer service, and personal [[concepts/branding|branding]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Realism**: Requires avoiding the [[concepts/uncanny-valley]] through proper filming and software workflow.
- **Ethical Use**: Must consider philosophical implications of digital duplication.
- **Efficiency**: Saves time compared to traditional [[concepts/video-creation|video production]].
