## Overview
**Type:** Entity

**Updated:** 2026-04-21

[[concepts/ai-driven-cybersecurity|Project Glasswing]] is a [[concepts/collaborative-initiative|collaborative initiative]] aimed at mitigating the potential risks associated with [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/gemini-30-pro|advanced AI model]], [[entities/claude|Claude]] [[entities/mythos|Mythos]]. The project involves major tech companies including [[entities/amazon-web-services|Amazon Web Services]], [[entities/anthropic-labs|Anthropic]], Apple, Broadcom, and [[entities/cisco|Cisco]] to address the unique challenges posed by this next-generation technology.
