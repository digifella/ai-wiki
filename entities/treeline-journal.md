# Treeline Journal

Treeline Journal is a content platform dedicated to ecological restoration, botany, and rewilding initiatives. The journal documents scientific and practical approaches to landscape regeneration, with particular emphasis on unconventional or counter-intuitive restoration methods. It serves as a resource for understanding alternative strategies in environmental management and native species recovery.

## Banks Peninsula Project

The journal is notably recognized for its coverage of ecological restoration work conducted by botanist Hugh Wilson in the Banks Peninsula region of New Zealand. This coverage highlights Wilson's specific focus on gorse-based ecosystem restoration, illustrating how invasive species management can contribute to broader landscape regeneration efforts. The documentation provides detailed narratives on the practical application of these methods within the local context.
