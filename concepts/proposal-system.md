# Proposal System

The proposal system serves as the central intake and evaluation mechanism for managing and reviewing proposals across the organization. It functions as a workflow process that establishes standardized criteria and procedures for proposal handling, enabling consistent review practices among various departments. Through this system, proposals are submitted, assessed, and approved in a structured manner, ensuring that organizational standards are met during the evaluation phase.

Current implementation faces significant operational friction due to dependencies on SharePoint infrastructure. The platform's limitations in handling complex workflow requirements create bottlenecks that hinder efficiency. These technical constraints contribute to delays and inconsistencies in the review process, necessitating a reevaluation of the underlying architecture to support the system's growing demands.

To address these challenges, there is potential for artificial intelligence to accelerate the review process. By integrating AI capabilities, the organization can streamline assessment tasks that are currently slowed by manual handling and infrastructure limitations. This approach aims to reduce operational friction and improve the speed and accuracy of proposal evaluations without altering the fundamental purpose of the system.
