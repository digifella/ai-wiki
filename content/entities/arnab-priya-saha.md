# Arnab Priya Saha

Arnab Priya Saha is a physicist and mathematician known for his contributions to [[concepts/theoretical-physics|theoretical physics]], particularly in the realm of [[concepts/string-theory|string theory]] and quantum field [[concepts/theory|theory]] (QFT). His work often bridges [[concepts/history-of-science-and-conceptsmathematicsmathematics|mathematical concepts]] with physical theories, leading to significant advancements in understanding fundamental aspects of the universe.
