# Tesla Semi

The Tesla Semi is an electric semi-truck developed by Tesla for commercial freight transportation. Announced in 2017, the vehicle represents Tesla's entry into the heavy-duty trucking market, which has traditionally relied on diesel-powered engines. The Semi is designed for long-haul and regional hauling operations, with an all-electric powertrain intended to reduce tailpipe emissions and lower operational costs through decreased energy expenses and maintenance requirements compared to conventional internal combustion engine trucks.

## Technical Specifications and Performance

The Tesla Semi features a large battery pack positioned under the trailer, distributing weight efficiently across the vehicle. The truck is capable of carrying maximum legal payload weights while maintaining performance adequate for commercial use. Tesla has emphasized the vehicle's energy efficiency, measured in kilowatt-hours per mile, as a key advantage over diesel competitors. The semi-truck design incorporates regenerative braking to recover energy during deceleration.

## Production and Market Status

Tesla began limited production of the Semi in 2023 after multiple delays from the original timeline. Early deliveries went to select customers for real-world testing and validation. The company has focused on production readiness improvements and engineering enhancements to optimize manufacturing processes and vehicle reliability. The broader adoption of the Tesla Semi depends on continued battery supply, charging infrastructure development, and customer confidence in electric heavy-duty vehicles for commercial operations.
