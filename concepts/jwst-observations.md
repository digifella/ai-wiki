# JWST Observations

**Summary:**
The [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] ([[entities/jwst|JWST]]) has been instrumental in making significant astronomical observations that are helping us understand the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]].

### Key Findings:
- jwst-discovery-[[concepts/population-iii-stars|population-iii-stars]]
  - Possible Discovery of First Ever [[concepts/stellar-objects|Stars]] in the Universe
  - [[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel: [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]]
  - URL: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VGekUw84lxQ
- [[concepts/solution|Resolution]] of "Capotauro" [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]]
  - Initially identified as a high-redshift [[concepts/primordial-universe|early-universe]] galaxy
  - Subsequent analysis revealed the object to be foreground stars within the [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]]
  - Author / channel: [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]]

### Detailed Points:
- Compelling evidence for [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|Population III stars]] has been detected, marking a significant milestone in the study of [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]].
- [[entities/gn-z11|GN-z11]] is one of the most [[concepts/distant-galaxies|distant galaxies]] observed by JWST, providing valuable insights into the cosmos' earliest stages.
- Clarification of misidentified high-redshift candidates ensures accurate reconstruction of [[concepts/galaxy-formation|galaxy formation]] timelines, distinguishing true primordial objects from local stellar phenomena.
