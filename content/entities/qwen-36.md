# Qwen 3.6

**[[concepts/qwen3-model|Qwen 3.6]]** refers to the third-generation [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of the Qwen series of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], specifically highlighted in [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] benchmarks for 2026. Key variants include the 27B and 35B [[concepts/parameter-models|parameter models]], optimized for [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] as [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]].

## Benchmarks & Performance
- **[[concepts/local-agent|Local Agent]] Capability**: Evaluated for [[concepts/coding|coding]] assistance and [[concepts/automated-task-execution|automated task execution]] in local environments.
- **Translation Tasks**: Specifically tested against Anki [[concepts/integration|integration]] workflows for translation [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and speed.
- **Comparative Analysis**:
  - Head-to-head [[concepts/testing|testing]] between 27B and 35B variants reveals trade-offs between [[concepts/speed|inference speed]] and translation fidelity.
