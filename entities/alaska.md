# Alaska

**Alaska** is the northernmost and westernmost state of the [[entities/us|United States]], bordering the Pacific Ocean to the west, the Bering Sea to the southwest, the Arctic Ocean to the north, and the Gulf of Alaska to the south. It is the largest US state by area.

## Geology & Mining
- Rich in natural resources including oil, natural gas, coal, [[concepts/major-gold-deposit|gold]], and copper.
- Major mining districts include Klondike Gold Rush sites and modern operations in the Kupoluk Mine area.

## Paleontology & Extinction Events
- **[[concepts/st-pauls-island|St. Paul's Island]] Mammoths**: The last known population of woolly mammoths persisted on St. [[entities/paul|Paul]]'s Island off the Alaskan coast, long after mainland populations vanished (~10,000 years ago).
  - Detailed chronology and causal analysis of this extinction event: [[lab-notes/2026-06-01-St.-Pauls-Island-Woolly-Mammoth-Extinction-Chronology-an|St. Paul's Island Woolly Mammoth Extinction: Chronology and Causes]] (2026-06-01).
  - Key source: [[entities/pbs-eons|PBS Eons]] ("The Second-to-Last Mammoths Ever").
