# Strategic Questions

Strategic Questions is a framework designed to structure business presentations and narratives by positioning artificial intelligence tools as research and synthesis partners rather than autonomous content generators. This methodology prioritizes human control over the organization of slides and the validation of content, ensuring that the final output aligns with specific strategic goals. By leveraging AI's capacity to process information and explore ideas, presenters can enhance their workflow without ceding editorial authority to the technology.

The approach relies on formulating precise, high-level inquiries that guide the AI’s output toward relevant insights rather than generic text. Instead of prompting for full slide drafts, users employ the framework to gather data, identify patterns, and challenge assumptions. This process allows the presenter to maintain the narrative arc and logical flow, using AI to fill in gaps or provide supporting evidence for pre-determined arguments.

Implementing this method requires a disciplined separation between the creative structuring phase and the content generation phase. Presenters first define the core message and slide hierarchy manually, then use AI to refine specific sections or generate alternative perspectives on key points. This ensures that the final presentation remains a coherent strategic tool rather than a collection of disjointed, algorithmically generated statements.
