# Automatic Dust Removal
Automatic dust removal is a feature found in various imaging software designed to remove unwanted specks and artifacts from digital photographs without requiring manual intervention. This process can significantly enhance the quality of images, especially those captured under less-than-optimal conditions such as shooting against bright backgrounds or using old equipment with worn-out lenses.

- **In [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] v15**, [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] has enhanced its automatic dust removal capabilities to leverage advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] that improve detection accuracy and efficiency.
- **[[concepts/ai-powered-content-removal|AI-Powered Content Removal]]**: This feature allows for more precise and intelligent handling of unwanted elements, including dust spots. The new [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]] can automatically identify and remove small details like fingerprints or lens scratches with greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] than previous versions.
- **Refined [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]**: Enhanced tools for refining selection masks now allow photographers to fine-tune the removal process, ensuring cleaner results without affecting surrounding image elements.
