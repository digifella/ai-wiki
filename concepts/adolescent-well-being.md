# Adolescent Well-being

Adolescent [[concepts/well-being|well-being]] encompasses a range of factors crucial to the development and [[concepts/health|health]] of teenagers including emotional, social, physical, and cognitive aspects. This page highlights key components influencing adolescent [[concepts/wellness|well-being]] with an emphasis on evidence-based practices.

### Key Components
- **Emotional Stability**: Understanding and managing emotions effectively.
- **Social Connections**: Building supportive [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within families and peer groups.
- **[[concepts/physical-health|Physical Health]]**: Maintaining a [[concepts/healthy-lifestyle|healthy lifestyle]] through [[concepts/diet|diet]], [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], and [[concepts/sleep|sleep]].
- **[[concepts/cognitive-evolution|Cognitive Development]]**: Enhancing [[concepts/learning|learning]] and [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical thinking]] [[concepts/skills|skills]].
