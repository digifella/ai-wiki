# Space mission

A planned or ongoing endeavor to reach outer space. [[concepts/space-exploration|Space missions]] involve complex [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] challenges, including propulsion, navigation, and environmental [[concepts/resilience|resilience]].

## Key Concepts

- **Orbital Mechanics**: Utilizing gravity-assist maneuvers to alter trajectory and [[concepts/speed|speed]] without excessive fuel consumption.
- **Thermal [[concepts/secure|Protection]]**: Critical for missions near the Sun, requiring advanced shielding against extreme heat.
- **Interagency Collaboration**: Increasingly common, involving partnerships between national space agencies.

## Notable Missions

### BepiColombo
The joint [[entities/esa|European Space Agency]] and [[entities/japan|Japan]] Aerospace Exploration Agency mission to Mercury, launched in 2018. It represents one of the most complex interplanetary missions due to the difficulty of reaching the innermost planet.

- **Challenges**: Navigating intense [[concepts/solar-gravity|solar gravity]] and extreme thermal environments.
- **Strategy**: Employs multiple gravity-assist flybys of [[entities/earth|Earth]], Venus, and Mercury to gradually reduce orbital energy.
