A Prompt Engineer designs and optimizes input prompts for [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to achieve desired outputs, requiring expertise in [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]], [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]], and creative [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. This role bridges human intent and [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to maximize performance across domains like [[concepts/coding|coding]], [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].
