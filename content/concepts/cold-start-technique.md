# Cold Start Technique
The cold start technique is a method used in various culinary [[concepts/software|applications]] to achieve consistent results, particularly when dealing with foods like bacon that require precise [[concepts/thermal-regulation|temperature control]]. This approach involves starting the cooking process [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] room temperature or close to it and gradually increasing heat to ensure even cooking throughout.

### Summary of Key Points
- **Definition:** Starting food [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] lower temperatures before ramping up to optimal cooking conditions.
- **[[concepts/software|Applications]]:** Commonly used in bacon frying, where cold-start ensures [[concepts/uniform-crispness|uniform crispness]] without overcooking edges.
