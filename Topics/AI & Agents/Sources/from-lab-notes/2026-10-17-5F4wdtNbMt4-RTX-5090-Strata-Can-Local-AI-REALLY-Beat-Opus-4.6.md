## RTX 5090 + Strata – Can Local AI REALLY Beat Opus 4.6?
**Clip title:** RTX 5090 + Strata – Can Local AI REALLY Beat Opus 4.6?
**Author / channel:** Bijan Bowen
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5F4wdtNbMt4

### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

### Main Topic
The video explores the capabilities of running local AI models on a high-end PC, specifically testing the performance of the Qwen 3.8 Flash Next model using Strata. The speaker also showcases an ASUS-sponsored PC build featuring an RTX 5090 GPU and tests the AI model's ability to generate an F1-style racing game using Blender and Godot.

### Key Points
- **PC Build & Setup:** The speaker unboxes and assembles a high-performance PC sent by ASUS, featuring an ROG Astral RTX 5090 GPU, ROG Thor 1000W Platinum III PSU, ROG Strix Z890-E motherboard, and 4x Kingston Fury Beast DDR5 memory.
- **Local AI Testing with Strata:** The speaker tests Strata, a tool designed to run advanced AI models locally. They run the Qwen 3.8 Flash Next model (a 125-billion parameter model) at different quantizations (Q4KM and IQ3_S) to evaluate token generation speeds and hardware utilization.
- **Game Development Prompt:** The AI is tasked with creating a simple F1 racing game using Blender (for 3D assets and track generation) and Godot (for game logic). The video shows the step-by-step process, debugging challenges, and final gameplay results.
- **Comparison with Cloud AI:** The video compares the local Qwen model's performance and output quality against older cloud-based frontier models like Claude Opus 4.6, demonstrating how far local AI capabilities have advanced.
- **ASUS Zenni Claw:** The video briefly introduces ASUS Zenni Claw, a local AI agent app that integrates local models with system workflows and productivity tasks.

### Conclusions & Takeaways
The speaker concludes that local AI models are rapidly closing the performance gap compared to older cloud-based frontier models. Running large models locally on powerful hardware like an RTX 5090 offers impressive token generation speeds and enables complex tasks, such as procedural game generation, entirely offline. Additionally, the video highlights the growing ecosystem of local AI tools, making advanced AI more accessible and capable without relying on external servers.
