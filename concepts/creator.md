# Creator

A [[entities/creator|creator]], in the context of [[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-generated content]], is an individual or entity that uses AI video and [[concepts/ai-generated-voices|voice cloning technology]] to produce digital media. Rather than creating content through traditional performance or production methods, these creators employ AI-generated [[concepts/digital-avatars|digital avatars]]—synthetic representations capable of speaking, performing, and presenting material—to generate videos, streams, and other content. This approach enables content production at scale with reduced demand for live performance, studio time, or traditional [[concepts/video-editing|post-production]] work.

## Technology and Methods

Creators using this approach typically rely on [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] that can synthesize realistic video and [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] from text inputs or minimal source material. [[concepts/ai-clone|Voice cloning]] technology allows a creator's vocal characteristics to be captured and reproduced by the [[concepts/digital-avatar|digital avatar]], while [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] systems create corresponding visual performances. Some implementations use existing footage or photographs as a basis for avatar creation, while others generate entirely synthetic performances.

## Applications and Revenue Models

AI-generated creator content has been applied across multiple domains, including educational videos, entertainment, marketing, and social media platforms. Creators may monetize this content through [[concepts/ad-revenue|advertising revenue]], sponsorships, subscription services, or direct audience support. The efficiency gains from using [[concepts/digital-clones|digital avatars]] can lower production costs compared to traditional [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]], potentially improving profit margins.

## Considerations

The widespread [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of creator technology raises questions around authenticity, disclosure, and labor displacement in creative industries. Regulatory frameworks around synthetic media identification and consent for [[concepts/tone|voice]] and likeness use remain developing in many jurisdictions.
