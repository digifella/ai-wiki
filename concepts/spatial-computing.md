# Spatial Computing

**Spatial [[concepts/computation|computing]]** refers to the discipline of computing that integrates the physical world with digital information, allowing users to interact with data in three-dimensional space. It encompasses technologies such as [[concepts/augmented-reality|augmented-reality]], [[concepts/virtual-reality|virtual-reality]], and mixed-reality, focusing on how computers understand and manipulate spatial relationships.

## Core Concepts

*   **3D Spatial Intelligence:** The ability of systems to perceive, reason about, and act within three-dimensional environments.
*   **Simulators:** Tools used to model spatial interactions before deployment in physical hardware or real-world scenarios.
*   **Open Source Ecosystem:** Growing community-driven development of spatial computing frameworks and simulators.
