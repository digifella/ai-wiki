# Google Drive Integration

Google Drive Integration refers to the incorporation of Google Gemini's artificial intelligence capabilities directly into Google's productivity applications and workflow. This integration enables users to access AI-powered features within their existing Google ecosystem, including Google Workspace and related tools. The system is designed to reduce friction by allowing users to leverage AI assistance without switching between applications or contexts.

The scope of this integration extends beyond static document creation to include real-time support within communication platforms. Recent updates have expanded these capabilities to include integration within Google Meet, aiming to enhance productivity during live interactions. This allows for immediate assistance and context-aware support during meetings, further embedding AI into the daily operational flow of users.
