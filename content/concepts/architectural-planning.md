# Architectural Planning

Architectural Planning is a workflow process that transforms research outputs from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s Deep Research feature into structured website content without requiring [[concepts/coding|coding]] knowledge. This approach bridges the gap between AI-generated research and web publication, enabling users to convert detailed research materials into professional web presentations through no-code tools and platforms.

## Process Overview

The workflow typically begins with NotebookLM's Deep Research functionality, which generates comprehensive research documents and analyses. These outputs are then fed into no-code website builders or content management systems that handle the structural and design aspects of web presentation. This eliminates the need for manual HTML, CSS, or programming expertise traditionally required for website development.

## Practical Applications

This method is particularly useful for researchers, analysts, and professionals who need to publish findings quickly and professionally. By automating the conversion from research document to web format, the process reduces manual formatting work and allows focus to remain on content quality rather than technical implementation. The resulting websites maintain the research [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] while presenting information in an accessible, navigable format for web audiences.
