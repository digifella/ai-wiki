# Chronic Diseases

**Chronic diseases** are long-lasting conditions requiring ongoing medical [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] or limiting activities of daily living. They account for the majority of morbidity and mortality in developed economies, necessitating shifts from acute care models to continuous [[concepts/pmi-frameworks|management frameworks]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Duration**: Persistent (>1 year)
- **Management**: Requires ongoing treatment/monitoring
- **Etiology**: Often multifactorial (genetic, environmental, lifestyle)
- **Examples**: Cardiovascular Disease, Diabetes, Cancer, Respiratory Diseases

## Evolution of Management Paradigms
Traditional management relies on episodic clinical visits. Emerging paradigms leverage [[concepts/digital-health]] technologies to enable [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]], remote patient engagement, and [[concepts/evidence-based-decision-making|data-driven decision-making]].

### Digital Health Integration
Recent [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] analysis highlights significant shifts in how chronic conditions are studied and treated via digital tools:
- **Therapeutic Focus Trends**: Data from 2006–2020 indicates specific therapeutic areas are receiving disproportionate [[concepts/attention|attention]] in digital [[concepts/health|health]] studies, reflecting market and clinical priorities [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Jiang---Key-Trends-in-Digital-Health-and-the-Future-of-C|Jiang - Key Trends in Digital Health and the Future of Clinical Trials in the US]].
- **Clinical Trial Evolution**: Digital health applications are increasingly embedded within clinical trial structures, allowing for real-[[entities/earth|world]] evidence generation and broader patient recruitment.
- **Study Volume (2016–2020)**: A surge in digital health studies during this period suggests a maturing infrastructure for digital therapeutics and [[concepts/remote-monitoring|remote monitoring]] solutions.

## Implications for Care
The integration of digital tools addresses gaps in traditional [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|chronic disease management]] by:
1. Enhancing adherence through automated reminders and [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].
2. Reducing barriers to clinical trial participation via decentralized trials.
3. Providing granular longitudinal data for [[concepts/personalized-medicine|personalized medicine]] approaches.
