# Basic Prompting

Basic [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] refers to the practice of providing simple, unrefined [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] (LLMs) without significant detail, context, or structural [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]]. This approach treats [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] as straightforward tools capable of generating adequate responses from minimal input. A basic prompt might simply ask "Write an essay about [[concepts/climate-change|climate change]]" without specifying desired length, target audience, [[concepts/tone|tone]], subject scope, or other contextual parameters.

As noted by [[entities/kevin-patrick-robbins|Kevin Patrick Robbins]], using overly simplistic prompts often results in superficial outputs that lack depth and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]]. Without explicit constraints or background information, the model must rely on its internal [[concepts/custom-dataset|training data]] to infer intent, which can lead to generic or misaligned responses. This limitation highlights the gap between the model's potential capabilities and the quality of the output when interaction is left to [[concepts/probability|chance]] rather than deliberate design.

The effectiveness of basic prompting diminishes as the complexity of the required task increases. While it may suffice for simple factual queries or [[concepts/creative-brainstorming|creative brainstorming]], it fails to leverage the full utility of advanced [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]]. Effective interaction typically requires [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative refinement]], where users provide specific roles, formats, and criteria to guide the model toward more accurate and useful results.
