# RAG 2.0

RAG 2.0 represents the evolution of [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] systems, integrating **[[concepts/agentic-rag|Agentic RAG]]** with **[[concepts/knowledge-graphs|Knowledge Graphs]]** to enable [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to autonomously search, reason, and utilize custom [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] with enhanced precision and context awareness.

## Core Innovations
- **[[concepts/agentic-rag-systems|Agentic RAG]]**: [[concepts/agents|Agents]] dynamically plan and execute multi-step retrieval workflows instead of static queries
- **[[concepts/vector-store|Knowledge Graph]] Fusion**: Semantic [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] in [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] guide [[concepts/context-aware-retrieval|context-aware retrieval]] and reduce hallucinations
- **Domain-Specific Optimization**: Tailored for efficient navigation of structured [[concepts/custom-knowledge-bases|custom knowledge bases]] (e.g., enterprise data, [[concepts/technical-documentation|technical documentation]])

## Implementation Focus
- Solves limitations of [[concepts/traditional-rag|traditional RAG]] in complex query [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]
- Enables agents to traverse knowledge graphs for inferential [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]
- Prioritizes relevance over simple keyword matching through graph-based [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]]
