# Skill Gaps

Skill gaps refer to the discrepancies between the competencies currently possessed by individuals or organizations and those required to meet present or future objectives. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], skill gaps often emerge from the limitations of [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|procedural knowledge]] that LLMs can reliably execute. These gaps become particularly relevant when deploying autonomous systems that must perform specialized tasks requiring domain expertise or precise technical execution.

## Analysis and Evaluation

Skill gaps can be systematically identified and analyzed through data-driven approaches. Tools like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] enable the evaluation of professional skill inventories against job requirements, such as those found in LinkedIn data. By comparing available [[concepts/skills|skills]] with market demands or organizational needs, gaps can be quantified and prioritized, providing a basis for targeted [[concepts/training|training]], hiring, or capability development initiatives.

## Application to AI Systems

For AI agents, addressing skill gaps involves both enhancing model training and implementing [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]] or verification mechanisms. When an agent lacks the procedural knowledge to complete a task reliably, strategies such as code execution environments, API integrations, or human-in-the-loop verification can bridge the gap. Recognizing these limitations is essential for building reliable systems that know when to defer to [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] or human expertise rather than attempting to operate beyond their reliable capabilities.
