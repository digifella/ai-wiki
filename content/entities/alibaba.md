[[entities/alibaba]] is a global technology leader and a primary [[concepts/developer|developer]] of advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] architectures, specifically through the [[entities/qwen|Qwen]] ecosystem of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLM).

- **Qwen Series**: Continuous development of [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/models|models]] focusing on [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and autonomy.
- **[[entities/qwen-36-plus]]** (Released 2026-04-07):
    - Represents a major evolutionary step towards the [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/agentic-ai|Real-World Agents]].
