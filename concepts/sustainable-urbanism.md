# Sustainable Urbanism

**Sustainable Urbanism** is an urban planning paradigm focused on creating environmentally, economically, and socially sustainable cities. It emphasizes compact, walkable, mixed-use development, efficient public transportation, and green [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] to reduce ecological footprints while enhancing quality of life.

## Core Principles
- **Density & Mixed-Use:** Maximizing land efficiency through high-density, mixed-use developments to reduce sprawl.
- **Walkability & Transit:** Prioritizing pedestrian infrastructure and public transit over private vehicle dependency.
- **Ecological Integration:** Incorporating green spaces, biodiversity corridors, and sustainable resource management.
- **Social Equity:** Ensuring accessible housing, public services, and community engagement.

## Emerging Paradigms: Mindful Development
Recent initiatives explore integrating psychological well-being and spiritual values into urban design, moving beyond purely metric-based sustainability.

- **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]] (GMC):** [[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]] is developing a new city in the southern lowlands that explicitly addresses the modern existential-crisis through "[[concepts/mindful-development|mindful development]]."
  - Conceptualized as an "anti-Dubai," focusing on Gross National Happiness (GNH) rather than pure GDP.
  - Integrates mindfulness practices into urban planning, architecture, and governance.
  - Aims to balance economic growth with environmental preservation and cultural heritage.
