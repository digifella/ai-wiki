# Unconscious mind

The Unconscious mind comprises the mental processes, structures, and information that operate outside of immediate awareness.

### Core Concepts
- The Mind can be conceptualized as a private, "inner universe" or [[concepts/inner-space|Inner Space]] unique to each individual.
- This internal space serves as the seat for Consciousness, [[concepts/memory]], and internal processing.
- The depth and complexity of this internal space are central to defining Human-Animal Differences.
