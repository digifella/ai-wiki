# Tesla FSD Supervised

[[entities/tesla|Tesla]]'s [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd-supervised|Full Self-Driving Supervised]] system received [[concepts/regulatory-approval|regulatory approval]] in the Netherlands in April 2026, marking the first European [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] for the advanced driver assistance technology. The approval followed Tesla's application to Dutch authorities and represents a significant regulatory milestone for the company's autonomous vehicle development efforts in [[entities/europe|Europe]].

## Regulatory Framework

The Dutch approval operates within the [[entities/eu|European Union]]'s [[concepts/regulatory-framework|regulatory framework]] for vehicle [[concepts/automation|automation]]. By securing clearance in one member state, Tesla established a precedent that could facilitate wider [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] across the [[entities/european-union|EU]]. The system's classification as "supervised" indicates it requires active driver engagement and oversight rather than operating as a fully autonomous vehicle, aligning with current European safety and liability [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].

## Broader Implications

The Netherlands authorization is expected to influence how other EU member states evaluate similar autonomous driving systems. The approval provides a reference point for regulatory discussions across Europe and may accelerate the timeline for comparable systems seeking market access in the region. However, each country retains [[concepts/power|authority]] to set its own requirements, meaning EU-wide rollout would require individual national approvals or harmonized standards.
