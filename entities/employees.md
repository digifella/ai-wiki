# Employees

## Overview
Employees are individuals engaged in work for an employer, typically in exchange for compensation. In organizational contexts, they are key stakeholders whose performance, [[concepts/storing|retention]], and satisfaction are influenced by [[concepts/leadership]], [[concepts/organizational-culture]], and Workplace [[concepts/policies|Policies]].

## Key Factors Influencing Employee State
- **Leadership [[concepts/style|Style]]**: Specifically, [[concepts/inclusive-leadership]] has been identified as a critical determinant of employee outcomes.
- **[[concepts/psychological-safety|Psychological Safety]]**: The degree to which individuals feel safe to take interpersonal risks at work.
- **[[concepts/crisis|Crisis]] Management**: How organizations and leaders respond during Trauma and crisis events significantly impacts [[concepts/employee-well-being|employee well-being]].

## Research & Evidence
- **Impact of Inclusive Leadership on [[concepts/well-being|Well-Being]]**:
	- A [[concepts/longitudinal-study|longitudinal study]] indicates that inclusive leadership plays a pivotal role in fostering the [[concepts/psychological-well-being]] of employees during periods of trauma and crisis.
