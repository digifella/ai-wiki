# AI Overviews

AI Overviews are synthesized summaries generated by AI systems in response to search queries, displayed prominently at the top of search results pages. [[concepts/google-search|Google]] introduced this feature to consolidate information from multiple sources into direct answers, fundamentally changing how search engines present information. Rather than ranking individual web pages, AI Overviews generate new text that attempts to answer user questions comprehensively. This shift alters the traditional relationship between search results and source attribution, as users may receive answers without immediately identifying the original sources.

## Impact on Web Content Strategy

The emergence of AI Overviews has created new considerations for website optimization. Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) refers to practices aimed at making content more likely to appear in or be cited by AI-generated summaries. This includes structuring information clearly, providing authoritative answers to common questions, and ensuring content is accessible to AI systems that aggregate results. Website owners increasingly need to consider how their content will be synthesized and presented in AI summaries, not just how it ranks in traditional search listings.

## Implementation with AI Tools

Developers and content creators can use [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] to help adapt their websites for improved visibility in AI Overviews. These tools can assist with analyzing content [[concepts/structure|structure]], identifying opportunities for better information presentation, and implementing changes that make content more suitable for AI aggregation. This represents an emerging dimension of [[concepts/website-building|web development]] that bridges search visibility with AI system compatibility.
