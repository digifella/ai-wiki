# Ubuntu

**[[entities/ubuntu|Ubuntu]]** is a Linux-based operating system distributed as free and [[concepts/open-source-system|open-source software]]. It is based on the Debian distribution and is known for its user-friendly interface, strong community support, and regular release cycle.

## Core Concepts
- **Philosophy**: Derived from the Nguni Bantu term meaning "humanity" or "I am because we are," emphasizing community and shared value.
- **Architecture**: Built on the Linux Kernel, utilizing the GNOME desktop environment by default.
- **Package Management**: Uses APT (Advanced Package Tool) and Snap packages for software distribution.
- **Use Cases**: Widely used in desktop [[concepts/computation|computing]], cloud [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], and Edge Computing due to its stability and security.
