# Cisco

Cisco is a networking and [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] company that provides infrastructure solutions for enterprise and service provider networks. The company specializes in [[concepts/hardware|hardware]], [[concepts/software|software]], and services across networking, [[concepts/security|security]], collaboration, and applications domains.

## Relationship to AI Security Research

Cisco's relevance to contemporary [[concepts/ai-security|AI security]] discussions includes monitoring developments in third-party AI systems and their security implications. The company has tracked research initiatives such as [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/ai-driven-cybersecurity|Project Glasswing]], which addresses [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] mitigation in AI systems, and competitive developments in [[concepts/enterprise-ai|enterprise AI agents]] from other vendors including [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]'s [[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]] platform.
