# Credibility Tiering

Credibility Tiering is a framework for categorizing information sources based on [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], authority, and verifiability. It assigns sources to specific tiers to streamline evaluation and reduce [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] during research.

## Core Principles

- **Source Provenance:** Identifying the origin and publisher of content.
- **[[concepts/verification|Verification]] Status:** Determining if claims are corroborated by independent evidence.
- **Platform Reliability:** Assessing the structural [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] and moderation standards of the hosting platform.
