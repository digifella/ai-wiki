# Significant Objects

[[entities/rob-walker|Rob Walker]]'s "Significant Objects" project involves buying ordinary, inexpensive objects for an average of $1 and selling them by including a short story about each item. This project illustrates the power of [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]] to significantly increase perceived value through narrative context.

- Ordinary items purchased cheaply were sold [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] much higher prices when accompanied by stories.
- Stories provide [[concepts/emotional-engagement|emotional engagement]] that can deeply [[concepts/power|influence]] perception and valuation of objects.
- The project emphasizes the importance of narrative in human [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] and [[concepts/consumer-behavior|consumer behavior]].
