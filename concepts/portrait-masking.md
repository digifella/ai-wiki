# Portrait Masking

Portrait Masking is an automated masking feature within Adobe Lightroom Classic that utilizes artificial intelligence to detect and isolate subjects within photographs. By analyzing facial features and subject boundaries, the tool generates precise selection masks with a single click. This functionality allows photographers to apply localized edits to specific areas of an image without the need for manual selection work, significantly reducing the time spent on repetitive tasks.

The technology extends beyond simple portrait isolation to include landscape detection, enabling the automatic identification of skies, foregrounds, and other distinct environmental elements. This capability allows for targeted adjustments to lighting and color balance in complex scenes where traditional manual masking would be labor-intensive. The AI model distinguishes between the subject and the background, creating accurate edges that accommodate fine details such as hair or foliage.

Users can refine the automatically generated masks using the mask overlay view and adjustment sliders to control the feathering and density of the selection. While the initial detection is automated, the interface provides tools for manual correction, ensuring that the final mask aligns precisely with the photographer's creative intent. This hybrid approach combines the speed of AI-driven segmentation with the precision required for professional post-processing workflows.
