### Summary
The video provides a comprehensive overview of the significant [[concepts/new-features|new features]] and updates introduced in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] After Effects 2026, along with some powerful functionalities currently available in its [[concepts/beta-version|beta version]]. The presenter highlights how these updates streamline workflows, enhance creative control, and bring previously external or complex capabilities directly into the [[concepts/software|software]], making it an "insane" upgrade for motion [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] artists and video editors.

Among the new useful utilities, After Effects 2026 introduces a highly anticipated Quick Apply menu (accessed via Ctrl/Cmd + Enter). This feature allows users to rapidly search for and apply effects, presets, or even navigate [[concepts/commands|commands]], eliminating the need for the older FX Console plugin or extensive menu browsing. Another notable addition is the enhanced ability to import Scalable Vector [[concepts/webgpu|Graphics]] (SVG) and [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] Illustrator (AI) [[concepts/files|files]], preserving all vector paths and colors for direct, editable manipulation within After Effects. The new Unmult effect is also a game-changer for compositing, providing a more effective way to remove black or white backgrounds from footage compared to traditional [[concepts/layer-styles|blend modes]], and notably, it works seamlessly with 3D layers.

The video also showcases advanced rotoscoping tools and improved [[concepts/text|text]] animation. The traditional Roto Brush has been replaced by an AI-powered suite including the Object Matte tool (for automatic object selection and tracking), Quick Selection tool, [[concepts/selection-brush|Selection Brush]] tool, and Refine Edge tool. These tools collectively offer more precise and efficient [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] and tracking for complex subjects and motion. Furthermore, After Effects 2026 introduces the capability to manipulate and animate Variable Fonts. Users can now keyframe properties like font width and weight, and combine this with the [[concepts/range|range]] selector to create dynamic and unique [[concepts/text|text]] animations that were previously time-consuming or impossible within the [[concepts/software|software]].

Perhaps the most impactful updates are in the 3D [[concepts/workflow|workflow]]. Users can now natively create basic 3D shapes (cubes, spheres, cones, cylinders, and planes) directly within After Effects, with various [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] for [[concepts/customization|customization]]. These shapes can be enhanced with downloadable materials from the Substance Community Assets, and a new Displacement option allows these materials to physically alter the geometry of the shapes, adding realistic [[concepts/texture|texture]] and depth. Crucially, the update removes previous limitations on shadow casting; Spot and Parallel lights can now cast [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], enabling more realistic [[concepts/integration|integration]] of 3D objects into scenes. Finally, the ability to add native Depth of Field (DoF) to 3D scenes using the camera controls allows for realistic focus and blur effects, including a "Link Focus Distance to Layer" option to automatically track focus on a chosen object.

In conclusion, After Effects 2026 represents a significant leap forward in functionality, offering powerful new tools that enhance efficiency and creative possibilities across various aspects of video production, from basic effects application to advanced 3D compositing and text animation. The video also briefly highlights Artlist's new AI Toolkit, featuring [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]] capable of [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]], generating video avatars, and translating content into different languages, further demonstrating the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] into modern video workflows.
