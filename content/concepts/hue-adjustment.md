# Hue Adjustment

[[concepts/hue|Hue]] adjustment is a color correction technique in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that allows photographers to modify the hue, saturation, and lightness of specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] within an image. Rather than adjusting all colors uniformly, this approach targets particular hues—such as reds, oranges, yellows, greens, cyans, blues, magentas, and neutrals—enabling [[concepts/targeted-color-adjustments|selective color grading]] that preserves other tones while enhancing or correcting particular color ranges.

## Color Mixer Tool

The primary tool for hue adjustment in Lightroom Classic is the [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]], accessible within the HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) adjustment panel. This tool provides three separate sliders for each color [[concepts/range|range]]: the Hue slider shifts the selected color toward adjacent hues on the color wheel, the Saturation slider controls [[concepts/color-saturation|color intensity]], and the Lightness slider adjusts brightness. This [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] makes it possible to warm [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], shift foliage colors, or correct [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] in specific areas of an image without affecting the overall color balance.

## Practical Applications

Hue adjustment is commonly used in [[concepts/portrait-retouching|portrait retouching]] to refine skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] appearance, in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] to enhance vegetation colors, and in general [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] workflows to achieve stylistic or corrective results. The technique works alongside other [[concepts/adjustments|Lightroom adjustments]] and is particularly effective when combined with targeted masks or local adjustment brushes for even more precise [[concepts/power|control]] over which areas receive color modifications.
