# Glass Reflections

Concept dealing with the management and removal of unwanted reflections from glass surfaces in photography.

## Challenges
*   Reflections often obscure subject details and reduce image [[concepts/contrast|contrast]].
*   Standard [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] tools have significant limitations when handling complex glass reflections.

## Workflow & Tools

### Lightroom Limitations
*   Built-in "Distraction Removal > [[concepts/reflection-removal|Reflection Removal]]" features are often ineffective for complex or high-contrast reflections.
*   Manual cloning/healing is time-consuming and may leave artifacts.

### Photoshop Generative Fill Workaround
*   Utilize [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]] for superior results when Lightroom fails.
*   Select the reflection area and use AI to reconstruct the background/subject behind the glass.
