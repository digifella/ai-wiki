# Victor Hess

Victor Franz Hess was an Austrian physicist who is most famous for his work on the discovery of [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]]. In 1912, he conducted a series of balloon flights to measure the intensity of ionizing radiation [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] different altitudes and found that the radiation increased with altitude, thus proving its extraterrestrial origin. His pioneering research led to significant advances in understanding [[concepts/high-energy-particles|high-energy particles]] and their sources.

## LHAASO's Confirmation: The Universe's Most Powerful Cosmic Ray Sources

### Summary
The video introduces the often-overlooked reality of [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]], [[concepts/high-energy-particles|high-energy particles]] that constantly bombard Earth from outer space. Despite the seemingly serene night sky, these tiny particles, predominantly [[concepts/protons|protons]], pose a significant threat. Upon entering Earth's atmosphere, they trigger a [[concepts/domino-effect|chain reaction]] of [[concepts/ionization|ionization]], which can damage satellites and disrupt communications.

### Key Points
- Cosmic [[concepts/rays|rays]] are a major source of high-energy particles.
- They originate from various sources in the universe, including [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]] and [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|active galactic nuclei]].
- The Large High Altitude Air Shower Observatory ([[concepts/lhaaso|LHAASO]]) has confirmed some of the most powerful cosmic ray sources known to date.
