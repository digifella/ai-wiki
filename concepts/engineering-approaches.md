# Engineering Approaches

Systematic methodologies for designing, optimizing, and maintaining complex technical systems, ranging from traditional software architecture to [[concepts/execution-orchestration|AI-agent orchestration]].

## Evolution of AI System Design

The trajectory of building reliable [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] has shifted from static prompt crafting to dynamic structural orchestration.

*   **[[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|Prompt Engineering]]**: Early focus on input optimization for zero/few-shot [[concepts/inference|inference]]. Limited by [[concepts/context-window|context window]] and static [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].
*   **[[concepts/context-engineering|Context Engineering]]**: Expansion into managing [[concepts/external-knowledge|external knowledge]] [[concepts/number-systems|bases]], [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval-augmented generation]] (RAG), and precise information injection.
*   **[[concepts/agent-harness-engineering|Agent Harness Engineering]]**: The current [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] moving beyond input/output tuning to structural control.
    *   **Definition**: Designing the "[[concepts/harness|harness]]" or framework that governs an agent's behavior, tool usage, [[concepts/memory-management|memory management]], and decision [[concepts/loops|loops]], rather than just its textual prompts.
    *   **Key Distinction**: Decouples the core model [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] from the operational constraints and execution [[concepts/flow|flow]], allowing for more robust and scalable autonomous behaviors.
    *   **See**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-25-Agent-Harness-Engineering-Evolution-from-Prompt-and-Cont|Agent Harness Engineering: Evolution from Prompt and Context.]]

## Core Principles

*   **Modularity**: Separating concerns ([[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] vs. tool execution vs. [[concepts/memory|memory]]).
*   **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]**: Implementing [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]] and [[concepts/verification|verification]] steps within the harness to mitigate [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] and drift.
*   **Observability**: Tracking agent states and decision paths for [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] and optimization.
