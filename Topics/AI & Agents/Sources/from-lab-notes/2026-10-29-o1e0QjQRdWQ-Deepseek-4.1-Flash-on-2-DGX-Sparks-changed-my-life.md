## Deepseek 4.1 Flash on 2 DGX Sparks changed my life
**Clip title:** Deepseek 4.1 Flash on 2 DGX Sparks changed my life
**Author / channel:** Alex Finn
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=o1e0QjQRdWQ

### Summary
Here's a 4-paragraph summary of the video content:

### Main Topic
The video details the unboxing, setup, and testing of a second NVIDIA DGX Spark AI computer purchased from Micro Center. The speaker, a technology enthusiast, aims to demonstrate how linking two DGX Sparks together can create a powerful, localized AI lab at home, potentially replacing cloud subscriptions for running advanced AI models.

### Key Points Discussed
* **Hardware Unboxing and Linking:** The speaker unboxes the NVIDIA DGX Spark, highlighting its compact design, 128GB of unified memory, and impressive AI processing power. He connects the new Spark to an existing unit using a designated link cable, effectively combining their memory to 256GB for running larger and more complex AI models.
* **Network Setup with Tailscale:** The setup process is simplified using Tailscale, a free tool that creates a private network connecting all of the speaker's devices (including Mac Studios, a Mac Mini, an AMD computer, and the DGX Sparks) so they can communicate seamlessly from anywhere in the world.
* **AI Model Testing and Performance:** Using GroqBot as an AI agent, the speaker sets up the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model on the linked DGX Sparks. The process is fully automated and completed in under a minute without needing a monitor attached to the DGX Sparks. The speaker then tests the local model's capabilities, demonstrating its ability to write and run a simple snake game and a more complex 3D rogue-like game entirely offline.

### Conclusions and Takeaways
The speaker concludes that using local AI models on a setup like the DGX Sparks offers significant advantages over cloud-based subscriptions, including unlimited, free usage (aside from electricity), constant availability, and enhanced privacy. While acknowledging these local models may not replace high-end commercial models for extreme coding, the speaker believes they provide exceptional value and versatility for hobbyists and developers interested in ambient intelligence.
