# Skin Invasion

## Overview
**Skin invasion** refers to the pathological process where malignant cells penetrate the dermal-epidermal junction and invade the surrounding dermal tissue. In the context of advanced oncology, particularly [[concepts/recurrent-breast-cancer|recurrent breast cancer]], this phenomenon is often associated with aggressive tumor behavior and poor prognosis.

## Key Concepts
*   **Dermal Invasion**: The physical breach of the basement membrane into the dermis.
*   **Metastatic Potential**: Skin invasion often precedes or accompanies lymphatic and hematogenous spread.
*   **Oncolytic Virotherapy**: A form of cancer treatment that uses viruses to selectively infect and kill tumor cells, potentially offering a mechanism to address skin invasion at the cellular level.
