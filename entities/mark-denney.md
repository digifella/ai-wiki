# Mark Denney

Mark Denney is a landscape photographer and educator known for producing instructional content focused on practical technical challenges within the field. His work aims to help photographers achieve sharp, detailed imagery across entire compositions by addressing specific methodological issues in landscape photography.

Denney is particularly recognized for his detailed explanations of focus stacking, a technique used to enhance overall sharpness. This method involves capturing multiple images at different focal distances and combining them during post-processing to extend the depth of field beyond the limits of a single exposure.
