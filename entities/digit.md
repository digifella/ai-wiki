# Digit

Digit is a humanoid robot engineered for mobile manipulation tasks in industrial and logistics environments. The robot combines bipedal locomotion with articulated upper-body systems to perform complex handling operations in spaces designed for human workers. Its design enables navigation through varied warehouse environments while performing precise manipulation tasks, making it suitable for applications including package handling, sorting, and other logistics operations.

## Design and Capabilities

The robot integrates mobility with dexterous manipulation capabilities, featuring a bipedal stance that allows it to traverse standard doorways and navigate stairs. It is equipped with a highly articulated arm and hand system capable of grasping and moving objects with precision. This dual focus on mobility and dexterity allows Digit to operate in existing infrastructure without requiring significant modifications to the workspace.

## Applications

Digit is primarily deployed in logistics and warehousing sectors to automate repetitive physical tasks. It is designed to work alongside human employees, assisting with the movement of goods, palletizing, and inventory management. By performing these duties, the robot aims to reduce physical strain on human workers and increase efficiency in supply chain operations.
