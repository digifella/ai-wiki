# Brainstorming

Brainstorming is a collaborative ideation technique designed to generate a large volume of [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] or solutions for a given problem or topic. In traditional settings, participants contribute thoughts freely with minimal initial judgment, creating an environment where creative [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] is encouraged. The method operates on the principle that quantity of ideas can increase the likelihood of discovering novel or effective solutions, as the variety of perspectives raises the chances of identifying viable approaches.

## AI-Assisted Brainstorming

[[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can facilitate brainstorming by [[concepts/acting|acting]] as a neutral participant that generates diverse suggestions without social bias or fatigue. These systems can rapidly produce numerous variations on a theme, helping users overcome creative blocks or explore angles they might not have considered. By processing [[entities/big-data|large datasets]], the assistant can also provide contextually relevant examples or analogies to stimulate further thought.

The [[concepts/nemotron-family|Nemotron]] text-based [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] supports this process by offering structured ideation capabilities. It can help organize raw ideas into categories, identify common themes, or suggest logical next steps in the creative process. This assistance allows users to focus on evaluating and refining concepts rather than solely on their initial generation, thereby increasing the overall efficiency of the brainstorming [[concepts/session|session]].
