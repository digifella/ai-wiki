# Respiratory Health

Respiratory health refers to the proper functioning of the lungs, airways, and the systems responsible for gas exchange in the body. The respiratory system delivers oxygen to the bloodstream and removes carbon dioxide as a waste product. When functioning optimally, this system maintains adequate oxygen levels throughout the body and supports overall physical performance and [[concepts/health|wellbeing]].

## Common Factors Affecting Respiratory Health

Multiple factors influence respiratory function, including air quality, smoking [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], physical fitness, and underlying medical conditions. Environmental pollutants, allergens, and infections can impair airway function. Conversely, regular [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]] and avoiding respiratory irritants generally support lung capacity and efficiency. Chronic conditions such as asthma, chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and bronchitis can significantly impact respiratory health over time.

## Maintenance and Support

Respiratory health can be supported through lifestyle practices including regular exercise, avoiding smoking and secondhand smoke, maintaining good air quality in living spaces, and managing allergies and infections promptly. Hydration also plays a role in respiratory function, as adequate fluid intake helps maintain the health of mucous membranes in the airways. Healthcare monitoring and appropriate medical treatment are important for individuals with respiratory conditions to prevent complications and maintain [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].
