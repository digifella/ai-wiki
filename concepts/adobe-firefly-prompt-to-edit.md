# Adobe Firefly Prompt To Edit

[[entities/adobe-firefly|Adobe Firefly]] [[concepts/generative-fill|Prompt-to-Edit]] is a [[concepts/generative-ai|generative artificial intelligence]] feature integrated into Adobe’s creative ecosystem that allows users to modify images through [[concepts/natural-language-descriptions|natural language descriptions]]. Instead of relying on traditional manual editing tools such as selection brushes, masks, or parameter sliders, users input text [[concepts/commands|commands]] to specify the desired changes. The system interprets these linguistic [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and applies the modifications directly to the source image, effectively bridging the gap between creative intent and technical execution.

The technology leverages Adobe’s [[concepts/advanced-ai-models|generative AI models]] to understand context and spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within an image. When a user provides a prompt, the AI identifies relevant regions and generates new pixels or alters existing ones to match the description while maintaining [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] with the original lighting, [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], and [[concepts/writing|composition]]. This approach streamlines complex editing workflows, enabling rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] and reducing the technical barrier for users who may not be proficient in [[concepts/master-of-editing|advanced photo editing]] software.

Prompt-to-Edit is primarily designed to enhance efficiency in digital [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] by allowing non-experts to achieve professional-grade results. It supports a variety of editing tasks, including [[concepts/object-removal|object removal]], addition, and stylistic transformations, all driven by textual input. By automating the technical aspects of image manipulation, the feature allows creators to focus more on conceptual development and less on the mechanical details of software interfaces.
