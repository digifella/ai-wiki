# Molecular structure

The three-dimensional spatial arrangement of atoms within a molecule, which dictates its chemical properties, molecular geometry, and [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]].

## Key Components
- Atomic bonding: The [[entities/nature|nature]] of covalent, ionic, or intermolecular forces.
- Polymorphism: The phenomenon where a substance exists in multiple crystal [[concepts/structure|structure]] forms.
- Molecular geometry: The 3D shape and spatial orientation of atoms.

## Critical Implications
Changes in structural arrangement can lead to catastrophic failures in drug manufacturing and pharmacology.
