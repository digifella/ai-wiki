# Alka Kolthari

Alka Kolthari is a researcher whose work examines paternal involvement during childbirth and the broader experiences of fathers within maternity care settings. Her research explores how men experience attending births and the psychological dimensions of their presence during the delivery process.

## Research Areas

Kolthari's documented research has given particular attention to cases involving traumatic birth outcomes and their impact on fathers. Her work addresses gaps in understanding the emotional experiences of men during childbirth and how maternity services can be enhanced to better support fathers alongside mothers and infants. This focus reflects broader efforts within healthcare research to recognize fathers as active participants in birth experiences rather than passive observers.
