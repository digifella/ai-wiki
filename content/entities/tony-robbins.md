# Tony Robbins

## Overview

Tony Robbins is a global leader in [[concepts/personal-development|personal development]], coaching and [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] individuals to achieve their goals through financial freedom and emotional [[concepts/health|health]].

### Five Elements Personality Theory

* The video "How To Influence ANYONE In Your Life (No Matter How Difficult...)" introduces the Chinese [[concepts/five-elements-theory|Five Elements theory]] applied to personality types.
  * Wood: Assertive and proactive
  * Fire: Charismatic and enthusiastic
  * Earth: Nurturing and supportive
  * Gold (Metal): Structured and organized
  * Water: Flexible and adaptable
