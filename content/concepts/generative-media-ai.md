# Generative Media AI

Generative media AI refers to systems trained to create or modify visual, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and textual content based on user prompts or [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. These tools have become integrated into both [[concepts/desktop-apps|standalone applications]] and existing creative [[concepts/software|software]] platforms. Common applications include image generation, video synthesis, [[concepts/text|text]] composition, and audio creation. The capability landscape varies significantly across [[concepts/models|models]], with different systems optimized for different tasks—some [[entities/excel|excel]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] photorealistic imagery while others prioritize artistic [[concepts/style|style]] or rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].

## Capabilities and Applications

Leading [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] differ in their core strengths and architectural approaches. Image generation models can create artwork from text descriptions, while language models assist with copywriting, ideation, and content planning. [[concepts/integration|Integration]] into existing professional software—such as [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]], and [[entities/canva|Canva]]—has made these tools accessible to photographers, designers, and content creators without requiring specialized technical knowledge. Tools like [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] demonstrate how [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] can transform research workflows by synthesizing information from multiple sources.

## Pricing and Business Models

Generative AI services operate under various [[concepts/pricing|pricing structures]], including free tiers with limitations, [[concepts/subscription-models|subscription models]] for regular users, and pay-per-use systems for enterprise applications. Pricing often [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] with usage frequency, [[concepts/output|output]] quality, or advanced features. Understanding the cost-benefit relationship between different platforms is essential for creators managing production budgets, particularly when selecting tools for commercial versus personal projects.
