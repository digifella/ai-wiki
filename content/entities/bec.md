# Bec

Bec conducted a comprehensive review of the AI masterplan, focusing on organizational readiness and [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] challenges. The review identified several critical barriers to effective AI [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], including [[concepts/infrastructure-limitations|infrastructure limitations]] that constrain system [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and [[concepts/tool-issues|tool issues]] that impede [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]].

## Key Findings

The review highlighted concerns regarding [[concepts/co-pilot-use|ungoverned AI solutions]] and the risks associated with [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]—informal, unmanaged AI implementations operating outside official channels. Bec recommended feeding these findings into Board discussions, emphasizing the need for faster [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] around implementation proposals.

## Challenges Identified

Specific pain points emerged during the assessment, including difficulties with the [[concepts/proposal-system|proposal system]] and operational constraints posed by SharePoint systems. Bec emphasized the importance of clarifying [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and exploring opportunities to accelerate analysis [[concepts/speed|speed]] and reduce manual review workload through AI-assisted processes.
