# Jungian Grief Model

The Jungian Grief Model is a [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] intervention framework developed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the University of Copenhagen that integrates Jungian psychological principles with grief [[concepts/offering-counseling-services|counseling]] practices. The model is structured around modular components designed to address emotional [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] and facilitate resolution through guided therapeutic processes.

## Core Components

The model's primary focus is on conflict resolution within the grief process, utilizing interactive digital modules that present psychological concepts in accessible formats. The framework emphasizes the relationship between individual [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] and the grief experience, drawing on Jungian analytical traditions to explore unconscious patterns that emerge during bereavement and loss.

## Implementation

The Jungian Grief Model has been operationalized through [[entities/ses-one-intervention|SES One Intervention]], a digital [[concepts/health|health]] platform delivered via [[concepts/ses-family|SESFamily.com]]. The platform features an interactive application with a simplified [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] designed to enhance engagement and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. This digital implementation reflects ongoing efforts to bridge the gap between evidence-based mental health interventions and [[concepts/adoption|technology adoption]], addressing what some researchers describe as the [[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]—the simultaneous desire for [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] solutions and resistance to their use.
