# Jeff Crume

Jeff Crume is a Distinguished Engineer at IBM and a recognized authority on the intersection of AI and [[concepts/security|security]]. His work includes significant contributions to understanding and mitigating risks associated with [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|AI agentic applications]].
