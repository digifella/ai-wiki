# Creative Brainstorming

Creative brainstorming is a cognitive process used by [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and human teams to generate novel ideas and solutions through collaborative exploration. In the context of AI systems, [[concepts/brainstorming|brainstorming]] involves iterative generation of multiple candidate approaches, often leveraging diverse perspectives or [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] paths to expand the [[concepts/solution|solution]] space before evaluation and refinement.

The process typically combines divergent [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]]—where many possibilities are generated without immediate judgment—with convergent thinking, where ideas are evaluated and synthesized. AI agents employing brainstorming strategies can explore parallel hypotheses, consider unconventional combinations of concepts, and test assumptions that might otherwise be overlooked in linear [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] approaches.

Brainstorming becomes particularly valuable in open-ended tasks where the problem space is ambiguous or multiple valid solutions exist. By explicitly generating and considering alternatives before committing to a direction, both human and artificial [[concepts/agents|agents]] can avoid premature optimization and discover more creative or effective outcomes than single-path reasoning would produce.
