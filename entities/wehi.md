# Wehi

Wehi is an initiative designed to facilitate dialogue between the fields of art and science. It achieves this through curated exhibitions and commissioned works that bring together practitioners from both disciplines. The project focuses on exploring how artistic practice can engage with scientific concepts, processes, and discoveries, aiming to bridge the traditional gap between these two domains.

The initiative operates on the premise that art and science share common ground in their methods of inquiry and interpretation. By presenting commissioned works in gallery settings, Wehi creates dedicated spaces for audiences to encounter scientific ideas through creative interpretation. These exhibitions promote a conversation about art in science, allowing viewers to engage with complex scientific topics via artistic lenses.
