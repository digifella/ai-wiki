# Camera Raw Filter

The [[entities/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]] is a powerful [[concepts/photoshop-tool|Photoshop tool]] that provides photographers and editors with [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over image processing. Originally designed to handle raw image [[concepts/files|files]] from [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]], the filter now works with any image format in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], functioning as a non-destructive adjustment layer. It offers precise manipulation of [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], white balance, [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], and detail enhancement, making it applicable across various photography genres.

## Portrait Enhancement

In [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], the Camera Raw Filter is particularly valued for its [[concepts/skin-tone-adjustment|skin tone adjustment]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and localized editing features. Photographers use it to refine complexion clarity, adjust warm and cool tones in facial features, and control [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]] selectively across different areas of a portrait. The tool's ability to [[entities/make|make]] targeted adjustments without permanently altering the original image makes it a standard step in professional portrait workflows.

## Technical Features

The interface presents organized panels for different adjustment categories: basic exposure and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] controls, tone curve refinement, color grading options, and clarity and [[concepts/texture|texture]] sliders. Advanced users leverage the graduated filter and adjustment brush tools to apply selective modifications to specific regions of an image. The non-destructive [[entities/nature|nature]] of the filter allows editors to return and modify settings [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] any point in their [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].
