# Algorithm Optimization

[[concepts/algorithm|Algorithm]] optimization encompasses techniques and methodologies for improving [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]], reducing resource consumption, and enhancing overall performance in algorithmic systems. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and systems operating under hardware constraints, optimization becomes particularly critical as it directly impacts feasibility and practical deployment. Optimization efforts typically focus on reducing time complexity, minimizing [[concepts/memory|memory]] usage, and decreasing computational overhead while maintaining functional [[concepts/accuracy|correctness]] and output quality.

## Performance Optimization

[[concepts/software-performance|Performance optimization]] targets the [[concepts/speed|speed]] and responsiveness of [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] through techniques such as algorithmic redesign, [[concepts/caching|caching]] strategies, [[concepts/parallel-processing|parallel processing]], and [[concepts/computational-complexity|algorithmic complexity]] reduction. For [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], this includes optimizing [[concepts/inference|inference]] pipelines, reducing model inference latency, and streamlining [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes. The goal is to achieve faster execution cycles, which enables agents to respond more quickly to environmental changes and make time-sensitive decisions effectively.

## Resource Efficiency

[[concepts/model-efficiency|Resource efficiency]] addresses constraints imposed by limited hardware availability, such as reduced memory capacity, lower computational power, or energy limitations. [[concepts/llm-optimization-techniques|Optimization techniques]] in this domain include [[concepts/model-quantization|model compression]], [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]], pruning [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]], and algorithmic approximations that trade minor accuracy for significant resource savings. These approaches are essential for deploying [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]] on [[concepts/edge-devices|edge devices]], embedded systems, or environments where [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] are restricted.

## Iterative Optimization

Modern [[concepts/algorithmic-optimization|algorithm optimization]] increasingly employs iterative and data-driven approaches, where [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]] are continuously measured and used to guide optimization decisions. AI-driven optimization methods can automatically identify bottlenecks, test alternative implementations, and refine algorithms based on empirical performance data, creating [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] that progressively improve system efficiency across multiple dimensions.
