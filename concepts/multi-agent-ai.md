# Multi-Agent AI

**Multi-Agent AI** refers to systems composed of multiple autonomous agents that interact to solve complex problems, coordinate tasks, or simulate social dynamics. Key aspects include Agent Architecture, Agent Communication, and Task Decomposition.

## Core Concepts

- **Agent Autonomy:** The capacity of individual agents to make decisions without direct human intervention.
- **Coordination Mechanisms:** Methods for agents to synchronize actions, such as Blackboard Architecture or Publish-Subscribe Systems.
- **Conflict Resolution:** Strategies for handling contradictory goals or resource contention among agents.
