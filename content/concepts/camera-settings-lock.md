# Camera Settings Lock

[[concepts/camera-settings|Camera Settings]] Lock is a feature found on [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] that allows photographers to lock specific camera controls to prevent accidental adjustment during shooting. This functionality is particularly useful in fast-paced shooting situations where camera settings might be inadvertently changed through [[entities/contact|contact]] with [[concepts/buttons|buttons]], dials, or menu controls. By locking desired settings, photographers can maintain consistent [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], focus, and other critical [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] throughout a shooting [[concepts/session|session]].

## Purpose and Benefits

The primary [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] of Camera Settings Lock is to protect established shooting configurations from unintended modification. This is especially valuable during handheld shooting, when using the camera in challenging environments, or when working with preset configurations that should remain stable across multiple shots. The feature reduces the risk of missing shots due to unexpected changes in aperture, shutter [[concepts/speed|speed]], ISO, or autofocus settings.

## Implementation

On Fujifilm cameras, the lock function typically allows photographers to selectively lock individual controls such as the command dials, function buttons, or menu access, rather than locking all settings simultaneously. This selective approach provides flexibility, enabling photographers to lock only the controls they wish to protect while keeping others accessible for adjustment as needed during shooting.
