# Australian government policy

The strategic frameworks, regulatory mechanisms, and administrative actions implemented by the Australian government to govern the nation. This encompasses public administration, the management of social [[concepts/secure|security]], and the [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of automated [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] systems.

### Key Case Studies & Failures
* **[[concepts/robodebt-scheme]]**:
    * An automated system introduced in 2016 designed to identify and recover alleged overpayments of [[concepts/welfare-benefits]].
    * Characterized by catastrophic failure and significant human [[concepts/cost|cost]], including reported deaths.
    * Functioned as an unlawful algorithm within the administrative framework.
    * Relevant to discussions on algorithmic [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] and administrative law.
