# Fda

The U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) is a federal regulatory agency operating under the Department of Health and Human Services. Its primary mandate is to protect public health by ensuring the safety, efficacy, and security of human and veterinary drugs, biological products, medical devices, the food supply, cosmetics, and radiological products. The agency achieves this through rigorous product reviews, inspections, and monitoring to enforce compliance with applicable laws and regulations.

In a significant shift toward operational modernization, the FDA has begun integrating agentic AI into its in-house operations. This initiative represents a strategic move to enhance internal efficiency and decision-making processes within the agency. By leveraging autonomous agents, the FDA aims to streamline complex workflows and improve the speed and accuracy of its regulatory functions.

This adoption of advanced technology aligns with broader efforts across the federal government to modernize legacy systems. The integration of agentic AI allows the agency to handle large volumes of data and regulatory tasks with greater precision. This technological upgrade supports the FDA's ongoing mission to adapt to evolving public health challenges while maintaining rigorous standards for product safety and efficacy.
