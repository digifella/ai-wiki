# Fritz Zwicky

Swiss-American astronomer and pioneer of High-Energy [[concepts/astrophysics|Astrophysics]].

## Scientific Legacy
- Provided the first observational evidence for [[concepts/dark-matter]] by identifying gravitational anomalies in the [[concepts/coma-cluster]].
- Established the fundamental framework for investigating non-[[concepts/baryonic-matter|luminous matter]] in the universe.
- His foundational work underpins current research into [[concepts/wimp-particles|WIMPs]] ([[concepts/wimp|Weakly Interacting Massive Particles]]), including recent potential detections of [[concepts/dark-matter]] [[concepts/annihilation-signals|Annihilation signals]] via [[entities/gamma|Gamma]] [[concepts/rays|Rays]] within the [[concepts/milky-way]].
