# Robots in Therapy

**Robots in therapy** refers to the deployment of [[concepts/robotics|robotic systems]] and multi-agent architectures to assist, support, or collaborate with [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] professionals in therapeutic settings. This domain intersects with Human-Robot Interaction, [[concepts/telehealth|Telemedicine]], and Clinical Decision Support Systems.

## Core Applications & Research

### Clinical Support & Collaboration
Recent [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] literature highlights the evolution of robots from simple [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]] to collaborative agents supporting physicians.

*   **[[concepts/expertise-based-ai-assistants|Multi-Agent Systems]] for Physician Support**: Research by Lanza et al. (2020) explores agents and robots designed to collaborate with and support physicians in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] environments. This includes monitoring patients, particularly in emergency care [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] such as during the COVID-19 pandemic [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Lanza---Agents-and-robots-for-collaborating-and-supporti|Lanza - Agents and robots for collaborating and supporting physicians in healthcare]].
*   **Patient Monitoring**: Utilization of robotics for continuous patient monitoring, reducing physical [[entities/contact|contact]] requirements and enhancing data collection accuracy in high-risk environments.

### Emergency Care & Pandemic Response
*   **Infection Control**: Robots deployed in emergency care settings to minimize [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] of medical [[entities/employees|staff]] to contagious diseases (e.g., [[concepts/covid-19]] response protocols).
*   **Telepresence**: Integration of robotic telepresence to enable remote diagnostic and therapeutic interactions.

## Key Concepts
*   **[[concepts/multi-agent-systems|Multi-Agent Systems]] (MAS)**: Distributed systems where multiple [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] interact to solve problems beyond the capability of individual agents, often used to coordinate robotic fleets in hospitals.
*   **Human-Robot Interaction (HRI)**: The study and design of robots that interact with humans, focusing on usability, safety, and social acceptability in therapeutic contexts.
