# Los Angeles

Los Angeles is a major metropolitan area in Southern California frequently affected by seismic activity due to its location near major fault lines, including the San Andreas Fault system. The city's [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to [[concepts/earthquakes|earthquakes]] has made it a crucial [[concepts/testing|testing]] ground for [[concepts/earthquake-engineering|earthquake engineering]] innovations and [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] strategies. Given the concentration of critical infrastructure, population density, and economic importance, earthquake preparedness and structural engineering solutions are central concerns for the region.

## Earthquake Engineering and Infrastructure Resilience

Modern earthquake engineering in Los Angeles incorporates advanced systems designed to protect buildings and infrastructure from seismic damage. [[concepts/earthquake-bearings|Base isolation systems]] and [[concepts/earthquake-base-isolation|earthquake bearings]] are among the technologies deployed to reduce the impact of ground motion on structures. These systems work by decoupling buildings from the ground during earthquakes, allowing the foundation to move while the [[concepts/structure|structure]] above remains relatively stable. Critical infrastructure facilities, including hospitals, [[concepts/water-management-systems|water systems]], and [[concepts/power|power]] plants, require specialized resilience measures to maintain functionality during and after seismic events.

The city's ongoing development and retrofitting of older structures reflect evolving [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] in seismic safety. Building codes and engineering practices have become increasingly sophisticated in response to historical earthquakes and advances in understanding seismic hazards. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of modern resilience systems in both new construction and infrastructure upgrades remains an active area of focus for Los Angeles, as the city balances growth with the need to protect its residents and essential services from earthquake impacts.
