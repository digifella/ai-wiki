# Fine-Tuning

Fine-tuning is the process of adapting a pre-trained [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] model to perform well on a specific task or dataset by updating its [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] through additional [[concepts/training|training]] with new data. This technique leverages existing knowledge in the base model, reducing the need for large amounts of labeled data and enabling more efficient development cycles.
