# GTC 2026

GTC 2026 is a technology conference hosted by NVIDIA. The event serves as a venue for NVIDIA to announce and demonstrate new products and tools for enterprise and [[concepts/developer|developer]] audiences.

## NemoClaw Announcement

At GTC 2026, NVIDIA unveiled NemoClaw, an agent toolkit designed for [[concepts/secure-enterprise-ai|secure enterprise AI]] [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]. The toolkit was presented as a [[concepts/solution|solution]] for organizations seeking to implement [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] while maintaining security and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements in production environments.
