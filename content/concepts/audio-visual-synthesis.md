# Audio Visual Synthesis

[[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]] Visual Synthesis is a creative technique that combines artificial intelligence-assisted content analysis with personalized video production. The method uses [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s audio generation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to create structured narration from source documents, then pairs this audio with custom video footage of a [[concepts/creator|creator]]'s face to produce a finished video. By automating the narration layer while preserving human presence in the visual presentation, the approach allows creators to produce video content more efficiently than traditional production methods.

## Process and Components

The technique relies on NotebookLM's ability to process written content—including [[concepts/notes|notes]], articles, and research documents—and generate coherent audio narration. Creators film themselves speaking or provide custom video footage, which is then synchronized with the AI-generated audio. Automated [[concepts/speaker-separation|speaker separation]] technology distinguishes between different voices or audio sources within the final video, allowing for flexible editing and refinement of the [[concepts/output|output]].

## Applications

Audio Visual Synthesis is particularly suited for educational [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], where clear explanation of complex material is central to the final product. The method can be applied to course lessons, explanatory videos, documentary-[[concepts/style|style]] content, and knowledge-sharing formats where the creator's presence adds credibility or personal [[concepts/connection|connection]] while the structured narration ensures comprehensive coverage of source material.
