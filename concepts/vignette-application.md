# Vignette Application

Vignette application is a post-processing technique that darkens or lightens the edges of a photograph while maintaining a brighter center, thereby directing viewer attention toward the main subject. This effect is commonly used in portrait, landscape, and product photography to create compositional emphasis and add subtle depth. The technique can be applied either during shooting through lens characteristics or, more commonly, during post-processing where it offers greater control and flexibility.

## Implementation in Lightroom Classic

In Lightroom Classic, vignette effects are applied through the Lens Corrections or Effects panels. The primary controls include Amount (intensity of darkening or lightening), Midpoint (how far the effect extends from the edges), and Roundness (shape of the vignette transition). Users can also adjust Feather to control the softness of the edge transition and Highlights to preserve detail in brightened vignettes. This approach allows photographers to apply consistent vignetting after capture without committing to the effect at the moment of shooting.

## Practical Considerations

Vignetting should be applied subtly to avoid appearing artificial or distracting. The technique is particularly effective in images where the subject is positioned away from the frame edges, as extreme vignetting can obscure important compositional elements. When combined with other post-processing adjustments like cropping or perspective correction, vignette settings may require readjustment to maintain visual balance. The grain effect, often applied alongside vignetting, adds texture that can complement the dimensional quality created by edge darkening.
