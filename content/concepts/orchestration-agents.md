# Orchestration Agents

Orchestration [[concepts/agents|Agents]] are [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]] designed to act as central controllers, coordinating multiple specialized [[concepts/autonomous-ai-agents]], tools, and [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] to achieve complex, multi-step objectives.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Custom [[concepts/skills|Skills]]**: The use of discrete, specialized capabilities or [[concepts/tool-calling|tool-use]] functions (e.g., [[concepts/claude-code]]) that agents can invoke to perform specific, granular tasks.
- **[[concepts/design|Design]] Systems**: The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of standardized frameworks to ensure structural and visual [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across all outputs generated by various agents in a workflow.
- **[[concepts/task-decomposition|Task Decomposition]]**: The ability to break down high-level, ambiguous prompts into a sequence of executable sub-tasks.
- **Tool Integration**: Connecting orchestrators to execution environments and [[concepts/software|software]] interfaces, such as [[entities/claude-code]].

## Use Cases
- **Automated Marketing**: Implementing end-to-end workflows that leverage custom [[concepts/skills|skills]] and design systems to automate [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], design adherence, and campaign [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
