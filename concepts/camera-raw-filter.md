# Camera Raw Filter

The [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]] is a non-destructive adjustment tool in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] that provides [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over [[concepts/image-input-processing|image processing]] parameters. Originally developed to process raw files from [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]], it now functions with all major image formats including JPEG, TIFF, and PNG. When applied as a smart filter, [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] remain fully editable, allowing photographers and editors to refine their work without permanently altering the original image data.

## Core Functionality

The filter offers a comprehensive range of adjustment sliders and controls organized into multiple panels. Users can modify [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], highlights, [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], whites, [[concepts/blacks|blacks]], [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]], along with color-specific adjustments through the HSL (Hue, Saturation, Luminance) panel. Additional tools include [[concepts/color-cast-correction|white balance correction]], vibrance and saturation controls, and localized adjustment brushes that permit selective edits to specific image areas.

## Portrait Enhancement Applications

For [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], the [[concepts/whites|Camera Raw Filter]] proves particularly valuable for skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] refinement and overall image quality improvement. The clarity and [[concepts/texture|texture]] sliders help define facial features, while the shadows and highlights controls allow photographers to sculpt lighting and reduce unwanted blemishes or imperfections. The localized adjustment brush enables targeted corrections to specific areas such as eyes or cheeks without affecting the broader image.

The tool integrates seamlessly into standard [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] workflows and serves as an alternative or complement to the traditional Adjustment Panels, offering a centralized workspace for complex [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] tasks.
