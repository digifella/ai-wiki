# Gpt 4

[[entities/gpt-4|GPT-4]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]. It represents a significant advancement over previous versions in terms of [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], including improved [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], longer [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]], and better performance across a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of tasks.

## Open-Source Developments

OpenAI has made steps toward [[concepts/open-source-language-models|open-source language models]] in recent years. The release of models like [[entities/gpt-2|GPT-2]] marked early efforts in this direction, and subsequent open-source releases have enabled researchers and developers to run capable language models locally without relying on cloud-based APIs.

## Local Deployment

[[concepts/running|Running]] language models locally has become increasingly feasible with tools and frameworks designed for this [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]. Platforms like [[entities/ollama|Ollama]] and [[concepts/automation-tools|automation tools]] such as [[entities/n8n|N8N]] allow users to deploy and integrate [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] in their own environments, providing [[concepts/cost|cost]] savings and greater [[concepts/power|control]] over data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] compared to [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud-based solutions]].
