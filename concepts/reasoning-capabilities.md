# Reasoning Capabilities

[[concepts/thinking-and-reasoning-capabilities|Reasoning capabilities]] in [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] refer to the ability of language models to break down complex problems, apply [[concepts/logical-steps|logical steps]], and arrive at justified conclusions. These capabilities enable models to move beyond [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] to perform more structured [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] tasks. Both [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/small-language-models-slms|small language models (SLMs)]] demonstrate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] abilities, though they differ significantly in their scale, computational requirements, and performance on reasoning-focused benchmarks.

## Large Language Models

[[concepts/large-language-models|Large language models]] such as [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]] and [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]] have become central to enterprise [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] through platforms like [[entities/microsoft-copilot|Microsoft Copilot]].

## Microsoft Copilot: Unified Enterprise Workspace

In September 2026, [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] unveiled a significant redesign of [[entities/microsoft-copilot|Microsoft Copilot]], transforming it from a simple AI [[concepts/chat-application|chat interface]] into a comprehensive, integrated workspace aimed at enhancing productivity across the enterprise. This evolution reflects the shift from isolated [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to unified operational environments.

Key features of this redesign include:

- **[[concepts/home-tab|Home Tab]]**: A centralized hub for managing interactions and context across different [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI services]].
- **[[concepts/autopilot-agent|Autopilot Agent]]**: An advanced [[concepts/ai-agents|autonomous agent]] capable of executing complex, multi-step workflows without continuous user intervention.
- **[[concepts/code-environment|Code Environment]]**: A dedicated space for app building and development, integrating [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] capabilities directly into the workflow.

For detailed technical specifications and feature breakdowns, see [[lab-notes/2026-09-30-Microsoft-Copilot-Redesign-Unified-Enterprise-Workspace|Microsoft Copilot Redesign: Unified Enterprise Workspace with Home, Autopilot, Code]].
