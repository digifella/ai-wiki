## Color Correction Challenges in Concert Photography

Concert venues employ intense colored lighting that changes frequently during performances, presenting significant obstacles for consistent image processing. Stage lighting typically combines multiple colored sources—reds, blues, greens, and moving spotlights—that create unpredictable color shifts from frame to frame. These extreme color casts are difficult to correct uniformly across an image set, as different areas of a single photograph may be illuminated by different colored lights simultaneously. Standard white balance adjustments often prove insufficient for managing these complex lighting conditions.

## Lightroom Classic Techniques

David's tutorial covers practical workflows within Adobe Lightroom Classic for addressing concert lighting challenges. The approach involves selective color correction tools, targeted adjustment of specific color channels, and strategies for batch processing multiple images while accommodating the varying light conditions within a series. The tutorial emphasizes techniques that allow photographers to recover natural skin tones and detail in highlights and shadows despite the challenging lighting environment.
