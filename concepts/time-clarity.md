# Time Clarity

The Time Clarity Interview System is a structured framework designed to help business owners identify and resolve clarity issues that manifest as time management challenges. Rather than prescribing scheduling techniques or productivity shortcuts, the system operates from the premise that most business owners struggle not with insufficient hours, but with insufficient clarity about their priorities, workflows, and decision-making processes.

## Core Function

The framework uses guided interviews as its primary mechanism. By asking strategic questions, it helps business owners surface hidden assumptions, competing priorities, and unclear processes that consume disproportionate time and mental energy. The system treats time problems as symptoms of deeper clarity deficits—ambiguous goals, undefined roles, or muddled workflows—and addresses the root causes rather than the surface symptoms.

## Practical Application

Business owners who work through the Time Clarity Interview System typically gain explicit understanding of their core activities, decision criteria, and operational bottlenecks. This clarity allows them to make more intentional choices about where their time goes, eliminate redundant or misaligned activities, and delegate or automate processes that previously consumed attention without clear purpose.
