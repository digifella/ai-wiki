# Ancient Rainforests

**Ancient rainforests** refer to dense forests with a high level of [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]] and precipitation. On Earth, ancient rainforests are significant ecosystems that have existed for millions of years, playing crucial roles in the global carbon cycle and harboring unique flora and fauna.

### Recent Developments

The discovery and study of potential ancient rainforest-like environments on Mars is a groundbreaking area of research within [[concepts/planetary-science|planetary science]].

**Key points:**

- Scientists are investigating geological formations on Mars that resemble features found in Earth's ancient tropical forests.
- Analysis of Martian rock samples suggests past conditions suitable for the existence of water-rich habitats, similar to those found in terrestrial rainforests.
- Current missions and planned expeditions aim to uncover more evidence regarding the planet’s early climate and possible [[concepts/habitability|habitability]].
