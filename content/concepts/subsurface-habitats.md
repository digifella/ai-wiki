## Subsurface Habitats

Subsurface habitats refer to environments beneath a planetary body's surface where life may thrive despite inhospitable conditions on the surface.

### Key Points
- **Deadly Soil:** [[concepts/martian-soil|Martian soil]] is extremely harsh and toxic, containing [[concepts/perchlorates|perchlorates]] that are harmful to most known forms of [[concepts/terrestrial-life|terrestrial life]].
  - Even [[concepts/tardigrades|tardigrades]], Earth’s [[concepts/extremophiles|extremophiles]] capable of surviving extreme conditions but not [[concepts/martian-soil-toxicity|Martian soil toxicity]], cannot survive in these conditions.
- **Hidden Niches:** Despite these challenges, subsurface habitats offer a refuge from radiation, extreme temperatures, and chemical hazards.
- **Water Activity:** Liquid water, even in small amounts, can exist beneath the surface due to pressure and temperature gradients, crucial for supporting microbial life.
