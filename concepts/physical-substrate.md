# Physical Substrate

The physical [[entities/medium|medium]] or material basis required for a system to exist and function. In the context of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], this concept is central to debates regarding whether consciousness or intelligence can emerge from non-biological substrates (e.g., silicon-based chips) or if it strictly requires a biological substrate.

## Key Considerations

*   **Biological Constraints**: Current consensus suggests that [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) lack the necessary biological constraints to achieve true consciousness.
*   **[[concepts/emergent-behavior|Emergence]] vs. [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]]**: The debate often hinges on whether complex [[concepts/computation|computation]] on a non-biological substrate can simulate consciousness or if it merely mimics it without [[concepts/conscious-thought|subjective experience]].
*   **Philosophical Frameworks**: Discussions frequently reference two prevalent viewpoints regarding the nature of [[concepts/the-mind|mind]] and matter, often cited in recent analyses of AI capabilities.
