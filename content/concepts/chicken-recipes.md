# Chicken Recipes

[[concepts/chicken-dishes|Chicken recipes]] span a wide variety of cuisines and [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking techniques]], from simple grilled preparations to complex marinated dishes. They are among the most versatile proteins in home cooking, adaptable to different dietary preferences and skill levels. Chicken can be prepared through [[concepts/bbq|grilling]], roasting, braising, stir-frying, and numerous other methods.

## Korean Dak Bulgogi

Dak bulgogi is a Korean-style marinated and grilled chicken dish that features [[concepts/quick-cooking|quick cooking]] and bold flavors. The recipe typically involves [[concepts/chicken-marination|marinating chicken]] in a mixture of soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, and garlic before cooking over high heat. [[concepts/eric-kim|Eric Kim]]'s version, featured on NYT Cooking, emphasizes the balance of sweet and savory elements characteristic of Korean barbecue preparations. The dish is known for its caramelized exterior and tender interior, achieved through both the marinade composition and rapid cooking method.
