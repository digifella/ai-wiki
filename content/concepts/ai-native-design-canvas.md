# AI-native design canvas

A [[concepts/design-thinking|design methodology]] and tooling paradigm where [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] is deeply integrated into the core workflow, enabling users to create, iterate, and refine designs through natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] rather than manual tooling. Unlike AI-assisted tools, AI-native canvases treat AI as the primary design engine.

## Key characteristics
- **Prompt-driven creation**: Design elements generated directly from text descriptions
- **Real-time [[concepts/ai-collaboration|AI collaboration]]**: Multiple users co-create with AI guidance
- **Context-aware [[concepts/iteration|iteration]]**: AI understands design system constraints and brand guidelines
- **Seamless output**: Direct export to development-ready assets (e.g., code snippets)

## Example: Google Stitch
Google Stitch (from [[entities/google-labs]]) recently evolved from a screenshot tool into a full [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|AI-native design canvas]], positioning it as a free alternative to [[entities/figma]].

**Recent developments (2026-04-14)**:
- Transformed from basic screenshot-to-design tool into comprehensive AI-native [[entities/canvas|canvas]]
- Integrates generative AI directly into design workflow (no separate tool switching)
- Offers free tier competing with paid design platforms
