# Internal Walkthroughs

**Internal walkthroughs** refer to structured, step-by-step demonstrations or audits of internal processes, tools, or workflows. They serve to document operational [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], verify [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and facilitate [[concepts/transfer-learning|knowledge transfer]] within the organization.

## Key Characteristics
- **Procedural [[concepts/clarity-slider|Clarity]]**: Breaks down [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] into actionable steps.
- **[[concepts/planning-errors|Tool Integration]]**: Often involves demonstrating specific software or [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] used in the workflow.
- **Auditability**: Provides a record of how decisions were made or how outputs were generated.
