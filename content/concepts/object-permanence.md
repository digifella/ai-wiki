# Object Permanence

The cognitive understanding that objects continue to exist even when they cannot be observed, heard, or otherwise sensed.

## Cognitive Foundations
- A fundamental milestone in Jean Piaget's stages of sensorimotor development.
- Requires the ability to maintain internal representations of entities across temporal and spatial gaps, forming the basis of [[concepts/world-models]].

## Implications for Artificial Intelligence
- A critical benchmark for achieving [[concepts/agi|AGI]], moving beyond pattern recognition toward true physical understanding.
- **Emerging Non-[[concepts/llm-models|LLM Architectures]]:**
    - [[concepts/vl-jepa]] ([[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] FAIR): A shift toward vision-language joint-embedding predictive architectures, moving away from the limitations of [[concepts/generative-ai]] and [[concepts/llm]] text-only [[concepts/models|models]].
    - Core Thesis: "Language is not intelligence"; true intelligence requires modeling the physical dynamics of the world.
    - Research Focus: Utilizing vision-based approaches to predict latent states, spearheaded by [[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]].
    - Goal: Developing architectures that can predict the [[concepts/data-persistence|persistence]] of objects in a visual field without relying on generative token prediction.
