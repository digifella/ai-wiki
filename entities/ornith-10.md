# Ornith-1.0

**[[concepts/qwen-llms|Ornith-1.0]]** is a family of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model]]s (LLMs) developed by DeepReinforce, specifically engineered for [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and [[concepts/autonomous-coding|autonomous coding]] tasks. The architecture emphasizes self-scaffolding capabilities, allowing the models to construct and refine their own [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes during [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex problem-solving]].

## Key Characteristics

- **Open-Source Architecture**: Released as part of DeepReinforce's initiative to democratize access to [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] agentic models, contrasting with proprietary alternatives like [[entities/gpt-56|GPT-5.6]].
- **[[concepts/real-world-coding|Agentic Coding]] Focus**: Optimized for software [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]], enabling [[concepts/autonomous-ai-coding-agent|autonomous code generation]], [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and [[concepts/integration|system integration]].
- **Self-Scaffolding Mechanism**: Utilizes internal [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]] to iteratively improve output quality without external human intervention during the [[concepts/inference|inference]] [[concepts/phase|phase]].
- **[[concepts/ai-agent-context|Contextual Awareness]]**: Designed to handle [[concepts/long-context-llms|long-context windows]] typical of large codebases, maintaining coherence across multiple files and dependencies.
- **35B MoE [[concepts/excellence|Superiority]]**: Recent evaluations highlight the 35B [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) variant as superior in agentic [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks, demonstrating enhanced efficiency and performance compared to dense counterparts. See [[lab-notes/2026-06-29-Ornith-1.0-Agentic-Coding-LLM-Evaluation-35B-MoE-Superio|Ornith-1.0 Agentic Coding LLM Evaluation: 35B MoE Superiority]] for detailed benchmarks.
