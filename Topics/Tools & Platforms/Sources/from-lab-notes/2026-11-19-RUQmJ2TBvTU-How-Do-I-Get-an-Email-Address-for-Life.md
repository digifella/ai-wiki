### Summary
In the video, Leo Notenboom discusses the importance of securing a reliable email address that you can keep for the rest of your life. He cautions against relying on email addresses provided by your Internet Service Provider (ISP) because they are subject to change if you move, switch ISPs, or if the ISP goes out of business.

Notenboom recommends using large company email services like Google (Gmail) or Microsoft (Outlook) as they are likely to be around for a long time. However, the best solution, according to Notenboom, is to own your own internet domain. This gives you complete control over your email address, allowing you to change email providers without changing your email address. Domain registrars and hosts often offer email services or email forwarding, and major email services support POP3 pickup, enabling you to access your domain email through your preferred email provider.
