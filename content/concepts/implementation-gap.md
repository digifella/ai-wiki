# Implementation Gap

**Definition:** The difference between the [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] and their practical application in real-world settings. This often [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] challenges related to [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], integration, [[concepts/training|training]], and [[concepts/governance|governance]].

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/adoption|adoption]]
- [[concepts/integration|integration]]
- [[concepts/training|training]]
- [[concepts/governance|governance]]

### Current Challenges

- **Lack of Partnerships:** Companies partnering with AI leaders like [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] may struggle due to a gap in understanding or capability.
- **Governance Struggles:** Differing philosophies on how to deploy and manage AI technologies are creating rifts between tech giants and their partners.

## Examples
- **[[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]]'s [[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] [[concepts/ai-safety|Guardrails]] vs. [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/consulting|Consulting]] Strategy**

- Nvidia has recently released [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]] for managing AI, addressing the need for practical solutions in deploying safe and [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|ethical AI]] systems.
  - In [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], OpenAI offers a [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] service where they guide companies on how to integrate their advanced AI technologies but charge significantly for this expertise.

- **[[concepts/summary|Summary]]:**
    - After working with various companies over a year, OpenAI discovered that many partners lacked the necessary frameworks or guardrails to safely deploy [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]].
    - This gap has led some to question their strategies and [[concepts/models|models]] of engagement.
    - Video analysis by [[entities/ai-news-strategy-daily|AI News & Strategy Daily]] highlights Nvidia's open-source approach as an alternative to paying for OpenAI’s consulting services. The video discusses how differing philosophies on deploying AI are shaping the industry.
