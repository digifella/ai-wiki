# Tiered Pricing Models

[[concepts/pricing-tiers|Tiered pricing models]] segment customers into multiple service levels, each offering different features, capacities, or support options at distinct price points. This approach allows businesses to serve diverse customer segments simultaneously—from price-sensitive users seeking basic functionality to premium customers willing to pay for enhanced capabilities. By creating multiple entry points, tiered models expand the addressable market and enable customers to select offerings aligned with their actual needs and budgets.

## Structure and Implementation

[[concepts/tiered-pricing|Tiered pricing]] typically consists of three to five distinct levels, commonly labeled as Basic, Professional, and Enterprise, though naming varies by industry. Each tier bundles different combinations of features, [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]], support responsiveness, and ancillary services. The progression between tiers is designed so that customers naturally migrate upward as their requirements grow, creating opportunities for expansion revenue without requiring large individual price increases.

## Business Advantages

This model reduces [[concepts/friction|friction]] in customer acquisition by lowering entry-level costs, which increases conversion rates among cost-conscious prospects. Simultaneously, it captures additional revenue from customers with higher demands or willingness to pay. The clear differentiation between tiers simplifies purchasing decisions and reduces the need for custom negotiations. Tiered models also provide valuable data about customer segments and demand elasticity across price points, informing [[concepts/product-design|product development]] and marketing strategies.

## Common Applications

Tiered pricing appears widely in [[concepts/saas|software-as-a-service]] (SaaS) platforms, cloud [[entities/storage|storage]] services, streaming media subscriptions, and telecommunications. The model works particularly well for businesses with scalable digital products where the cost of serving additional customers at a given tier is minimal, making it possible to maintain healthy margins across all price points.
