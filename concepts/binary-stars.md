# Mass Transfer

- **Astrophysical Context: Binary Stars**
  - **Definition:** Gravitational systems comprising two [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] bound to a common Barycenter, governing ~50-60% of solar-type stellar populations and >80% of massive stars.
  - **Classification & Detection:**
    - **Visual/Resolved:** [[concepts/direct-imaging|Direct imaging]] via adaptive optics/interferometry; yields Orbital Elements, Eccentricity, and inclination.
    - **Spectroscopic:** Doppler Effect modulation of spectral lines; provides mass function and minimum [[concepts/companion|companion]] mass.
    - **Eclipsing:** Photometric dips due to orbital inclination; allows precise determination of radii and temperatures.
    - **Mass Transfer:** Occurs when a star fills its Roche lobe, leading to accretion disks, novae, or [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]].

# Galactic Dynamics

- **[[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]] Rotation Curve**
  - **[[concepts/empirical-evidence|Observational Data]]:** Recent analysis of the Milky Way's rotation curve challenges standard cosmological models.
  - **[[concepts/dark-matter|Dark Matter]] [[concepts/tensile-stress|Tension]]:** Traditional models infer a dark matter halo containing ~10x more mass than [[concepts/baryonic-matter|baryonic matter]]. New data suggests discrepancies with this distribution.
  - **[[concepts/modified-gravity|Modified Gravity]]:** The data also poses challenges to Modified Newtonian Dynamics ([[concepts/modified-newtonian-dynamics-mond|MOND]]) theories, which attempt to explain rotation [[concepts/curves|curves]] without dark matter.
  - **Current Status:** The tension between observed rotation velocities and theoretical predictions remains a key problem in [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]].
