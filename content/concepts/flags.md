# Flags

Flags in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] are a marking system used to organize and manage photos within a [[concepts/catalog|catalog]] during the [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]]. They represent one of the foundational organizational tools available to photographers working with large image collections. Flags allow editors to quickly categorize [[concepts/images|images]] with simple status [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] as they review and sort through their work.

## Flag Types

Lightroom offers three flag states for each image: flagged (marked as a keeper), unflagged (neutral status), and rejected (marked for removal). These three-state system enables photographers to [[entities/make|make]] quick decisions about which images to keep, which to set aside for later review, and which to discard. The flagging system works independently of other organizational methods like ratings and [[concepts/color-labels|color labels]], providing a parallel sorting mechanism.

## Workflow Application

Flags are typically used during the initial culling [[concepts/phase|phase]] of [[concepts/photo-management|photo management]], where a photographer rapidly reviews a shoot and makes preliminary decisions about image quality and relevance. Because flagging is faster than assigning detailed ratings or [[concepts/keywords|keywords]], it serves as an efficient first-pass organizational tool. Photographers can then use flags to isolate keeper images for further editing work, while rejected images can be removed from the catalog or stored separately.
