# Cost Savings

Cost Savings refers to the reduction of expenditures through efficient resource management, leveraging existing assets, and adopting cost-effective technologies. It spans physical domains, such as minimizing waste in [[concepts/leftover-utilization|kitchen management]], and digital realms, particularly through the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] alternatives to proprietary services.

## Physical Resource Optimization
In physical environments, cost savings are achieved by maximizing the utility of purchased items before disposal. Key principles include:
*   **Waste Reduction:** Minimizing food sent to landfills via repurposing.
*   **Ingredient Utilization:** Using existing stock rather than purchasing new ingredients.
*   **Circular Practices:** Promoting a circular [[concepts/economic-system|economy]] approach to household consumption.
*   **Meal Planning:** Adjusting menus based on available surplus to prevent unnecessary purchases.

## Digital Infrastructure & AI Efficiency
Recent developments highlight significant cost savings in [[concepts/coding|software development]] and AI deployment through open-source projects. As noted in [[lab-notes/2026-06-13-Summary-Report-Open-Source-AI-Projects-for-Retrieval-Loc|Summary Report: Open-Source AI Projects for Retrieval, Local LLMs, and Cost Savings]], leveraging free tools can reduce reliance on expensive [[entities/api-calls|API calls]].

Key strategies identified include:
*   **[[concepts/local-ai|Local LLM Deployment]]:** Running [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] locally to eliminate per-token API costs associated with commercial providers.
*   **Open-Source [[concepts/document-retrieval|Retrieval]] Systems:** Utilizing free [[entities/github|GitHub]] projects for data retrieval and processing, reducing infrastructure overhead.
*   **[[concepts/local-ai-hosting|Self-Hosted AI]] Tools:** Adopting innovative open-source frameworks that enhance AI interaction without subscription fees.

## Cross-Domain Principles
*   **Efficiency:** Maximizing output per unit of input (ingredients or [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power).
*   **Sustainability:** Reducing environmental impact through reduced consumption and energy-efficient local processing.
*   **Creativity/Innovation:** Finding novel uses for surplus materials or alternative architectures for standard software tasks.
