# 333 Collins Street
A prominent skyscraper in the [[entities/melbourne-cbd|Melbourne CBD]] known for its unique integration of modern and historic [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].

### Architectural Features
- **[[concepts/structure|Structure]]:** Features a modern exterior (built 1990) that conceals a preserved Historic [[concepts/banking-chamber|Banking Chamber]] dating to the late 19th century.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]:** The building's architectural composition is the result of [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] efforts following two significant [[concepts/financial-crises|financial crises]].
