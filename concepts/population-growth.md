# Population Growth

Population growth is a critical factor influencing global resources and infrastructure needs, particularly in regions with limited natural resources like the [[concepts/gulf-cooperation-council|Gulf Cooperation Council]] (GCC) countries. As populations increase, so do demands for essential services such as water supply.

### Key Points
- **[[concepts/desalination|Desalination]] Role**: In [[concepts/gcc-countries|GCC countries]] facing severe [[concepts/water-scarcity|water scarcity]], large-scale [[concepts/desalination-plants|desalination plants]] are vital to meet growing water demands.
- **Technological Advancements**: [[concepts/multi-stage-flash-msf-technology|Multi-stage flash distillation]] (MSF) technology is widely used due to its efficiency and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] in producing potable water from seawater.
- **[[concepts/energy-consumption|Energy Consumption]]**: [[concepts/desalination|Desalination]] processes are energy-intensive, posing challenges for [[concepts/sustainable-development|sustainable development]].
