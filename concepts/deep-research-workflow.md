# Deep Research Workflow

The [[concepts/deep-research-agent-flow|Deep Research Workflow]] is a systematic process that leverages [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s [[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|Deep Research feature]] to synthesize information from multiple sources into organized, publication-ready content. Instead of manually conducting research and organizing findings, this workflow automates the synthesis stage by ingesting uploaded documents, web links, and reference materials. The system generates [[concepts/structured-output|structured output]] that can serve as a foundation for various content types, including websites, reports, and documentation.

The process begins by uploading [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]] such as [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]], or URLs to the NotebookLM interface. The tool then analyzes these inputs to identify key themes, [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]], and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], creating a comprehensive [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]. Users can subsequently prompt the system to generate specific outputs, such as detailed outlines, [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] text, or code snippets, based on the synthesized data. This approach reduces the time required for information gathering and allows for rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] on content structure.

In the context of [[concepts/web-application-development|web development]], this workflow enables the creation of professional websites without direct [[concepts/coding|coding]]. By generating structured HTML, CSS, or [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] content from the research [[concepts/phase|phase]], users can assemble site components efficiently. The resulting output provides a coherent narrative and logical layout, which can be adapted into [[concepts/static-website|static site]] generators or content management systems. This method is particularly useful for creating documentation, landing pages, or informational sites where accuracy and source integration are prioritized over custom design elements.
