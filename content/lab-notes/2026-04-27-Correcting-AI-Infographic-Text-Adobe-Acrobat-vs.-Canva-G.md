### Summary
The video demonstrates two practical methods for directly editing [[concepts/text|text]] within [[concepts/ai-generated-infographics|AI-generated infographics]], addressing the common issue of spelling mistakes or inaccuracies that often arise when using [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] like [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] to create visual content. The presenter highlights that simply re-[[concepts/prompting|prompting]] the AI is usually ineffective, necessitating manual intervention for precise corrections. The core objective is to show how to achieve a perfectly clean and error-free [[concepts/infographic|infographic]].

The first method discussed involves using **[[entities/adobe-acrobat|Adobe Acrobat]]**. After opening the AI-generated image (which Adobe converts into an editable PDF), users can select the "Edit a PDF" function. Acrobat then scans the image to identify distinct text blocks, allowing for direct editing, deletion, or modification of the text. While this method successfully enables [[concepts/text-manipulation|text manipulation]], it presents a significant flaw: when text is deleted, Adobe Acrobat often leaves a "blurry trace" or a "scrubbed-out" effect in the background, making the edits noticeable and less than ideal for a professional finish.

The second and highly recommended method utilizes **[[entities/canva|Canva]]**, a popular online graphic [[concepts/design|design]] platform. Users upload their AI-generated image to Canva and create a new [[concepts/design|design]] with the image's original dimensions. The key feature here is Canva's "Grab Text" tool, found under "[Magic Studio](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magic_Studio)." This tool intelligently identifies and isolates all text within the image, highlighting it in purple and making it fully editable. Users can then click on any text block to correct errors seamlessly. Furthermore, in the rare instance that deleting text leaves a slight blur in the background, Canva offers a "Pixel Eraser" tool to meticulously clean up these areas, ensuring a pristine result without any unwanted artifacts.

In conclusion, the video strongly advocates for using Canva over Adobe Acrobat for editing text in AI-generated [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]]. Canva's "Grab Text" feature provides a cleaner and more efficient way to modify content, overcoming the "blurry trace" issue encountered with Adobe Acrobat. Ultimately, while these methods offer solutions for post-generation fixes, the video briefly notes that achieving high-quality AI-generated content from the outset still depends on providing specific and well-structured prompts to the AI.
