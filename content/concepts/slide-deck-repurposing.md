# Slide Deck Repurposing

Slide Deck Repurposing is a workflow technique for converting presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]] while removing platform watermarks. The method leverages the output from NotebookLM's slide generation feature as a foundation, then processes it through Google Vids to produce a final video file without branding elements.

## Process Overview

The technique involves exporting slides created in NotebookLM, importing them into Google Vids, and using the platform's [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] and rendering capabilities to generate a clean output. This approach is useful for creators who want to transform AI-generated presentation content into video format for [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] across multiple platforms without watermark restrictions that might otherwise apply to native platform exports.

## Practical Applications

This repurposing method is particularly relevant for content creators working with NotebookLM's [[concepts/automated-slide-generation|automated slide generation]] features who need video deliverables. Rather than manually recreating slides in video editing software, the workflow provides a direct path from presentation to video, reducing production time while maintaining control over the final output's appearance and distribution rights.
