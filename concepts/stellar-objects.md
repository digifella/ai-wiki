# Stellar Objects

Astronomical [[concepts/nodes|entities]] formed via [[concepts/stellar-evolution]] pathways, spanning pre-main-sequence condensations to post-nuclear-[[concepts/burning|burning]] remnants. Governed by hydrostatic equilibrium, [[concepts/gravitational-collapse|gravitational collapse]] thresholds, and mass-dependent lifecycles. Classification integrates luminosity class, spectral type, mass function, and environmental context.

## Classification & Morphology
- **Protostars**: Pre-main-sequence cores within Molecular Clouds undergoing Jeans Instability collapse, accretion, and bipolar outflow [[concepts/regulation|regulation]].
- **Main Sequence**: Hydrogen-burning equilibrium [[concepts/phase|phase]]; mass-luminosity relation dictates longevity. Includes Red Dwarfs, G-Type Stars, O-Type Stars.
- **Post-Main Sequence**: Shell-burning giants ([Red Giant], [Asymptotic Giant Branch]), [[concepts/supernova|Supernova]] progenitors, and chemically enriched wind shedders.
- **[[concepts/compact-stellar-objects|Stellar Remnants]]**: Degenerate cores supported by quantum pressure (White Dwarfs, [[concepts/neutron-stars|Neutron Stars]]) or event horizons (Stellar [[concepts/black-holes|Black Holes]]) exceeding the Tolman–Oppenheimer–Volkoff Limit.
- **Substellar & Exotic**: Brown Dwarfs (deuterium/lithium fusion thresholds), Pulsars, Magnetars, and T Tauri Stars.

## Formation & Environmental Dynamics
- Triggered by Supernova shock compression, Galactic Spiral Arm [[concepts/density|density]] waves, or Cloud-Cloud Collisions.
- Initial mass distribution follows the Salpeter Initial Mass Function; low-mass objects dominate census, high-mass objects dominate ionizing flux.
- Binary Star System interactions [[concepts/ambition|drive]] [[concepts/binary-stars|mass transfer]], common envelope ejection, and merger transients ([Type Ia Supernova], [Gravitational Wave] sources).
- High-radiation/extreme-[[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] environments ([[concepts/galactic-center]], Globular Cluster cores) accelerate stellar stripping, tidal heating, and relativistic orbital decay.

## Recent Findings: Galactic Center G-Objects
- **G-Objects**: Transient, hybrid stellar-gas features on highly eccentric orbits around [[concepts/sagittarius-a]], exhibiting compact stellar cores with extended, photoionized dust/gas tails.
- **Origin [[concepts/solution|Resolution]]**: High-resolution interferometry and MHD simulations confirm G-objects originate from tidal disruption of binary companions within the [[entities/irs-16sw]] cluster.
- **Formation Mechanism**: Pericenter passages near [[entities/sgra|SgrA*]] induce Roche-lobe overflow and tidal stripping, leaving bound stellar remnants enveloped in expanding circumstellar material.
- **Implications**: Validates extreme-gravity binary evolution models; refines Stellar Dynamics near supermassive black holes and informs accretion-[[concepts/feedback|feedback]] coupling.
