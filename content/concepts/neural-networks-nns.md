# Neural Networks (NNs)

[[concepts/neural-networks|Neural networks]] are computational systems inspired by biological neural networks found in animal brains. They consist of interconnected [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] (artificial neurons) organized in layers that process information through weighted connections. Neural networks form the foundation of modern [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]], enabling systems to learn patterns from data and [[entities/make|make]] predictions or decisions without explicit programming for each task.

## Tesla's Multiplication to Addition Patent

[[entities/tesla|Tesla]] holds a patent for a significant optimization in [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] computation involving the conversion of multiplication operations to addition operations. This breakthrough reduces [[concepts/complexity-classes|computational complexity]] and energy requirements in neural network processing, which has important implications for [[concepts/ai-efficiency|AI efficiency]] in [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] implementations. The patent represents an engineering advancement in making neural networks more practical for [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] in resource-constrained environments, such as autonomous vehicles and [[concepts/edge-computing|edge computing]] devices.

This optimization approach addresses a fundamental bottleneck in neural network execution, where multiplication is typically more computationally expensive than addition. By reformulating certain neural network operations to rely primarily on addition rather than multiplication, the patent suggests a path toward faster and more [[concepts/cost-efficient-ai|efficient AI]] [[concepts/inference|inference]] systems. Such improvements in [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] are particularly valuable for real-time [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] where both [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/power|power]] consumption are critical constraints.
