# 24/7 Employee

**Definition**: A workforce model utilizing [[concepts/voice-assistants|autonomous systems]], software bots, or [[entities/ai]] agents to perform labor continuously without human rest cycles. Unlike traditional remote work, this concept eliminates downtime, enabling perpetual operational capacity for tasks such as customer support, data processing, and monitoring.

## Core Characteristics
*   **Perpetual Availability**: Operates across all time zones without shift handovers.
*   **[[concepts/recurring-actions|Task Automation]]**: Handles repetitive, rule-based, or increasingly complex cognitive tasks.
*   **Scalability**: Can be replicated infinitely without proportional cost increases in welfare or benefits.
*   **[[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]]**: Eliminates human variability in performance and error rates associated with fatigue.

## Technological Enablers
*   **[[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|Workflow Automation]] Tools**: Integration of [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] and middleware to connect disparate software systems.
*   **[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]**: [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/multi-modal-input|multi-modal AI]] capable of [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and content generation.
*   **No-Code/Low-Code Platforms**: Allow rapid deployment of automated agents by non-technical users.

## Implications

### Economic & Operational
*   **Cost Reduction**: Shifts labor costs from variable human wages to fixed infrastructure/compute costs.
*   **[[concepts/productivity|Productivity]] Gains**: Maximizes asset utilization by keeping "[[entities/employees|employees]]" active during off-hours.

### Ethical & Social
*   **Job Displacement**: Risks replacing human roles in entry-level and middle-management positions.
*   **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]]**: Challenges in attributing liability for errors made by [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]].
*   **[[concepts/augmentation-of-human-capabilities|Human-AI Collaboration]]**: Shifts human roles towards supervision, strategy, and exception handling rather than execution.
