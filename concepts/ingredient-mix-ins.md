# Ingredient Mix Ins

Ingredient mix-ins are components added to fried rice during the cooking process to enhance flavor, texture, and nutritional content. These additions are incorporated according to specific timing and techniques that ensure even distribution and proper texture in the finished dish. Mix-ins form a fundamental element of the fried rice formula, working alongside properly treated rice and cooking fat to create a balanced, cohesive dish.

## Categories by Cooking Time

Mix-ins are organized by when they are added during the cooking sequence to optimize their physical properties. Hard vegetables, proteins, and aromatics are typically introduced early in the process to allow for sufficient cooking and flavor development. This initial stage often involves sautéing these ingredients in oil before the rice is added, ensuring they are fully cooked and integrated into the base of the dish.

Delicate ingredients such as leafy greens, scallions, and soft vegetables are added toward the end of the cooking process. This timing preserves their color, crispness, and nutritional value while allowing them to warm through without becoming mushy. The final stage may also include the addition of sauces and seasonings, which are tossed with the rice to ensure uniform coating and flavor absorption without overcooking the components.
