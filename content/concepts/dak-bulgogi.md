# Dak Bulgogi

Dak Bulgogi is a popular Korean dish that translates to "chicken in the style of bulgogi." It's a type of [[concepts/korean-bbq|Korean BBQ]] chicken known for its tender meat and savory sauce. The dish combines marinated chicken with ingredients like garlic, soy sauce, sesame oil, and sugar.
