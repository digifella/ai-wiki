# Matthew Be

Matthew Be is a [[concepts/developer|developer]] and content creator associated with [[concepts/local-ai-assistants|Clawdbot]], an [[concepts/self-hosted-ai-assistant|open-source personal AI assistant]] platform. He has created educational content demonstrating the capabilities and setup of the system, including video tutorials that explain how Clawdbot functions as a self-hosted alternative to commercial AI assistants.

## Clawdbot

Clawdbot is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], self-hosted personal [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] that Be has promoted as a practical alternative to proprietary solutions. The platform is designed to run on users' own infrastructure, giving them control over their data and the underlying AI system. Be's content focuses on making the technical aspects of deploying and using such systems accessible to a broader audience.
