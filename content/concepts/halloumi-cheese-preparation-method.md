# Halloumi Cheese Preparation Method

Halloumi is a semi-hard, unripened cheese originating from Cyprus. It is famous for its ability to be grilled or fried, developing a crispy exterior while remaining soft and elastic inside.

## Preparation Overview

The optimal [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] method focuses on heat [[concepts/power|control]] and the correct cooking technique to achieve the desired [[concepts/texture|texture]] and flavor.

### Key Preparation Steps
*   **Cheese Selection:** Use high-quality Halloumi, typically fresh and firm.
*   **Grilling/Frying:** Direct, medium-high heat is essential to achieve proper caramelization and texture.
*   **Result:** The cheese should be golden brown and squeaky when properly cooked.
