# XAI (Explainable Artificial Intelligence)

## Definition
[[entities/xai|XAI]] refers to the effort in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research and development to create [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/models|models]] whose decisions and processes can be understood by humans. This includes methods for improving transparency, [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]], and trustworthiness of AI systems.

## Key Concepts
- **Transparency:** Making the workings of an AI system understandable.
- **Interpretability:** The ability to explain or present the [[concepts/output|output]] of a model in a comprehensible way.
- **Trust:** Building user confidence that an AI system [[entities/will|will]] behave as intended.

### Related Technologies:
- machine-[[concepts/learning|learning]]
- [[concepts/neural-networks|neural-networks]]

## Applications
- [[concepts/health|Healthcare]]: Enhancing patient trust by explaining medical diagnoses and treatment plans.
- Finance: Providing clear [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] for loan approvals or denials to applicants.
- Autonomous Vehicles: Allowing drivers to understand how an autonomous system makes decisions.

## Challenges
- Complexity of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] can hinder transparency and interpretability.
- Balancing between model [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and explainability.

### Ethical Considerations:
- Ensuring fairness in [[concepts/decision-making|algorithmic decision-making]].
- Addressing [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] concerns in data usage for explanations.
