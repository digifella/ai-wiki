# Healthcare Policy

**[[concepts/health|Healthcare]] policy** comprises the decisions, plans, and actions that are undertaken to achieve specific [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] goals within a society. It encompasses the broad scope of government and organizational strategies that influence the delivery, funding, and access of medical services.

## Core Components
- **Regulatory Frameworks**: Laws and regulations governing practitioner licensure, facility standards, and drug approval.
- **Funding [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]]**: Structures for resource allocation, including tax-based systems, social insurance, and private pay models.
- **Access and Equity**: [[concepts/policies|Policies]] designed to reduce disparities in health outcomes across demographic groups.

Recent analyses highlight critical considerations for improving health outcomes through better [[concepts/communication-strategies|communication strategies]] in these regions.
