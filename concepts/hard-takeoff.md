# Hard takeoff

A theoretical scenario in [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] where an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] system undergoes a rapid, discontinuous increase in capability, potentially leading to an [[concepts/guiding-role|intelligence explosion]]. Contrasted with "soft takeoff" (gradual progress), [[entities/hard-takeoff|hard takeoff]] implies a sudden, uncontrollable acceleration in AI intelligence.

- [[concepts/self-improvement]] is the mechanism enabling hard takeoff: [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] now significantly contribute to their own evolution.
- In "Hard Takeoff has started" ([https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhoFqhLXc3g](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mhoFqhLXc3g)), [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]] asserts we have officially entered the [[concepts/ai-recursive-self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]] [[concepts/phase|phase]], where [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] autonomously enhance their architectures and [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], potentially triggering an [[concepts/intelligence-explosion|intelligence explosion]].
