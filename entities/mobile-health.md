# Mobile Health

**[[concepts/mobile-health|Mobile Health]]** ([[concepts/mhealth|mHealth]]) refers to the practice of medicine and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] supported by [[concepts/portable-devices|mobile devices]], including mobile phones, patient monitoring devices, personal digital assistants (PDAs), and other wireless devices.

## Key Integrations & Ecosystem

*   **[[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|Remote Patient Monitoring]] (RPM)**: [[concepts/digital-health|mHealth]] serves as a foundational layer for Remote Patient [[concepts/monitoring-systems|Monitoring systems]], facilitating real-time data transmission from patients to providers.
*   **Wearable Device Integration**: Critical for continuous data collection; integrates seamlessly with mHealth platforms to track vitals and activity metrics.
*   **[[concepts/electronic-health-records|Electronic Medical Records]] (EMRs)**: mHealth data streams are increasingly integrated into EMR systems to create comprehensive patient profiles, though interoperability remains a technical challenge.
*   **[[concepts/telehealth|Virtual Care]]**: Complements virtual consultations by providing longitudinal [[concepts/health|health]] data between visits.

## Technical Infrastructure & Data Flow

*   **Data Collection & Transmission**: Relies on robust Data Transmission protocols to ensure low-latency [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] from mobile/wearable sources to central servers.
*   **[[concepts/security|Security]] Considerations**: High priority due to sensitive health data; requires [[concepts/end-to-end-privacy|end-to-end encryption]] and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] standards.
*   **[[concepts/data-integrity|Data Quality]]**: Current [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] [[concepts/faces|faces]] challenges regarding the accuracy and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] of data collected via consumer-grade devices.
*   **Core Technologies**: Includes IoT sensors, [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]], and AI-driven analytics for interpreting mobile health data.
