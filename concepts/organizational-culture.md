# Organizational Culture

**Organizational culture** refers to the shared values, beliefs, norms, and practices that shape the behavior and decision-making processes within an organization. It influences how teams prioritize tasks, manage risk, and interact with external stakeholders.

## Key Dimensions
- **Speed vs. Caution:** The tension between rapid deployment ("shipping") and rigorous safety/ethical review.
- **Decision-Making:** Centralized vs. decentralized authority structures.
- **Risk Tolerance:** Willingness to experiment with unproven methodologies.
