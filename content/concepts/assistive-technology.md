# Assistive Technology

Assistive technology (AT) refers to any item, piece of equipment, or product system that is used to increase, maintain, or improve the functional [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of people with disabilities Disability Studies. In the context of aging and [[concepts/health|healthcare]], AT includes both low-tech aids (e.g., canes, hearing aids) and high-tech solutions (e.g., robotic companions, smart home interfaces).

## Key Applications

- **Mobility Aids**: Devices supporting physical [[concepts/exercise|movement]] for individuals with limited mobility.
- **Communication Tools**: [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|Speech-to-text]] [[concepts/software|software]], eye-tracking devices, and braille displays.
- **Cognitive Support**: Reminder systems and [[concepts/memory|memory]] aids for neurodivergent or cognitively declining users.
- **Social [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]]**: Emerging use of robots for [[concepts/companionship|companionship]], particularly in geriatric care.
