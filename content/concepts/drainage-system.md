# Drainage System

A **drainage system** is a network of channels and structures designed to collect, transport, and distribute surface water or groundwater for the [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] of preventing flooding, improving soil conditions, and facilitating agricultural productivity. Modern drainage systems incorporate various types of pumps and mechanical devices such as [[concepts/wirtz-pump]], which are essential in overcoming challenges related to airlocks and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]].

### Key Concepts
- **Airlock:** A condition where air trapped in the system prevents fluid from flowing freely.
- **Hydrostatic Pressure:** The pressure exerted by a fluid [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] equilibrium, which is caused by the force of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]].
