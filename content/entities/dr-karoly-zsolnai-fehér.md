# Dr Karoly Zsolnai Fehér

Dr Karoly Zsolnai Fehér is a researcher associated with [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]]'s work on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems for [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]]. He has been involved with [[concepts/automated-scientific-research|Aletheia]], a self-correcting AI system developed by DeepMind that is designed to improve the [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] of AI-generated scientific research.

## Aletheia Project

Aletheia represents DeepMind's approach to addressing systematic errors in AI-assisted scientific work. The system incorporates mechanisms for self-correction, allowing it to identify and address errors in its own outputs—a significant consideration for [[concepts/software|applications]] in scientific research where accuracy is paramount.
