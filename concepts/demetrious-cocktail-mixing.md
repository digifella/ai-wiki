# Demetrious Cocktail Mixing

Demetrious Cocktail Mixing is a bartending operation based in Heraklion, Crete, specializing in cocktail [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] and craft beverage development. The business provides mixology services and custom drink creation within the local food and beverage sector, serving clients who require professional cocktail preparation for events and establishments.

## Founder Background

The venture was founded by [[concepts/trianna|Helen]], a former pharmacist who transitioned into cocktail mixing and mixology. Her background in pharmacy informed her approach to mixology, bringing [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and systematic methods to the craft. This professional history influences the operational standards and formulation processes used in the development of their beverages.
