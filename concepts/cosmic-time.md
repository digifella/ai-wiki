# Cosmic Time

The [[concepts/infinite-monkey-theorem|infinite monkey theorem]] is a thought [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] illustrating the relationship between [[concepts/probability|probability]] and infinity. It proposes that a monkey randomly typing on a typewriter for an infinite amount of time would eventually produce any given text, including the complete works of Shakespeare. While often presented as a paradox, the theorem is mathematically sound: given infinite attempts and infinite time, even events with vanishingly small probability become certain to occur.

## Probability Calculations

The likelihood of producing a specific text of n characters decreases exponentially with text length. Typing the 5-word phrase "METHINKS IT IS LIKE A WEASEL" (ignoring spaces) requires correctly selecting 23 characters sequentially from 26 possible letters, a probability of roughly 1 in 620 trillion. For the 884,421 words of Shakespeare's complete works, the probability becomes so small that it defies practical intuition. Yet mathematically, across sufficiently long timescales, the cumulative probability approaches certainty.

## Cosmic Timescales

Cosmic time—the age and projected lifespan of the [[concepts/observable-universe|observable universe]]—provides a meaningful context for evaluating such probabilities. The universe is approximately 13.8 billion years old, and stellar processes continue for trillions of years into the future. Even within these staggeringly large timescales, however, randomly producing Shakespeare remains astronomically unlikely. The [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] demonstrates that cosmic time, while vast, falls far short of the infinite duration required for the theorem's conclusion to achieve practical significance.

The [[concepts/crested-macaques|infinite monkey theorem]] thus serves as a useful illustration of how probability and infinity differ fundamentally from finite timescales—even one as immense as cosmic time. It underscores that "given enough time, anything happens" requires a conception of infinity that transcends [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]].
