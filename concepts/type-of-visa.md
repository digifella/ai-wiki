# Type Of Visa

A visa is an official [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] issued by a country's government that permits a foreign national to enter, stay, or transit through that country for a specified purpose and duration. Visas serve as a mechanism for countries to regulate border access, verify traveler information, and ensure [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with immigration laws. The requirements, validity periods, and processing procedures vary significantly between nations, reflecting each country's immigration [[concepts/policies|policies]] and international agreements.

## Common Visa Categories

Visas are typically classified by their intended purpose. Tourist or visitor visas permit short-term stays for leisure and sightseeing. Business visas allow entry for commercial activities, conferences, or professional meetings. Work visas authorize employment within the host country and often require sponsorship from an employer. Student visas enable enrollment at educational institutions. Transit visas permit passage through a country en route to another destination. Residence or long-term visas facilitate extended stays, often for relocation or family reunification purposes.

## Digital and Expedited Options

Many countries now offer electronic visa systems, such as e-visas or Electronic Travel Authorizations (ETAs), which streamline the application process. Turkey's e-visa system exemplifies this approach, allowing eligible travelers to apply online and receive approval without visiting an embassy or consulate. These digital options reduce processing times and administrative burden while maintaining border security standards. Processing durations range from same-day approvals for certain e-visa programs to several weeks for standard visa applications, depending on the country and visa type.
