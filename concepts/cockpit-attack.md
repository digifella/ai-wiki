# Cockpit Attack

A **cockpit attack** refers to an incident where an individual attempts to breach or take control of the flight deck of an aircraft, often with malicious intent. These events have significantly influenced aviation [[concepts/security|security]] protocols, including reinforced cockpit doors and crew resource management training.

## Notable Incidents

### FlyDubai Flight 1073
On September 30, 2026, FlyDubai Flight 1073 experienced a critical [[concepts/cybersecurity-incident|security breach]] involving its First Officer.

*   **Date:** September 30, 2026
*   **Aircraft:** [[concepts/boeing-737-max-8|Boeing 737 Max 8]]
*   **Route:** Dubai to Tel Aviv
*   **[[entities/employees|Personnel]]:** 174 passengers, 6 crew members (including two off-duty pilots)
*   **Perpetrator:** First Officer [[entities/hamam-al-hammami|Hamam Al-Hammami]] (Omani national)
*   **Context:** Approximately two hours into the flight, Al-Hammami initiated a [[concepts/crisis|crisis]] in the cockpit.
*   **Outcome:** The situation resulted in a desert [[concepts/emergency-landing|emergency landing]].
