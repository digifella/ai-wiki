# ASIC Login Portal

The ASIC Login Portal is a web-based platform operated by the [[entities/asic-company|Australian Securities and Investments Commission]] that provides authenticated access to company information and regulatory documentation. As Australia's primary corporate regulator, ASIC maintains comprehensive records of registered companies, and the portal serves as the centralized point of access for retrieving official business records. The portal is designed to support [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements, due diligence processes, and general corporate information searches.

## Access and Functionality

Users access the portal by providing identifying information such as an Australian Company Number (ACN) or business name to locate relevant company data. The portal displays registered details including company status, directorship information, financial statements, and other lodged regulatory documents. [[concepts/authentication|Authentication]] requirements vary depending on the type of information being accessed, with some basic searches available without registration while others require account creation and verification.

## Business Applications

The portal serves multiple stakeholder groups including investors, creditors, business partners, and legal practitioners who need to verify company legitimacy and review regulatory filings. Businesses use the portal during due diligence investigations, credit assessments, and partnership evaluations. Directors and company officers may access the portal to manage their own company's lodgements and update registered information with ASIC.
