# Multimodal Support

Multimodal support refers to the capability of AI systems to process and understand multiple types of input data simultaneously, including [[concepts/text|text]], [[concepts/images|images]], [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and video. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]], multimodal functionality enables more sophisticated interactions and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] by allowing [[concepts/agents|agents]] to analyze diverse information sources without requiring separate [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] for each [[concepts/modality|modality]].

[[concepts/anthropic-ai|Anthropic Claude]] [[entities/claude-opus-4|Opus 4]].7 introduced enhanced [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]] as part of broader improvements to its [[concepts/autonomous-ai-coding-agent|agentic coding]] and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] abilities. These advancements allow the model to better integrate visual information with textual analysis, making it more effective for tasks that require understanding content across different formats. This is particularly valuable for agents that need to interpret documents, screenshots, [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]], or other visual materials as part of their [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].

The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of multimodal support with agentic features means that [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can now perform more complex autonomous tasks. An [[entities/agent|agent]] might, for example, analyze an image, extract relevant information, and then take appropriate action based on that [[concepts/visual-understanding|visual understanding]] combined with contextual knowledge. This capability supports broader [[concepts/software|applications]] in areas like [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]], data analysis, and automated reasoning tasks where multiple information types are naturally present.
