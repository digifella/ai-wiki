# Llama 3.1

[[entities/llama-31|Llama 3.1]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by Meta, released in July 2024. It represents an update to the Llama 3 model family and is available in multiple parameter sizes, including 8B, 70B, and 405B variants. The 405B model marked Meta's entry into the frontier class of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], positioning it alongside other state-of-the-art systems.

## Technical Characteristics

The model was trained using a [[concepts/context-window|context window]] of 128,000 [[concepts/tokens|tokens]], enabling it to process longer documents and maintain coherence across extended inputs. Llama 3.1 was trained on a diverse mixture of publicly available data and underwent [[concepts/instruction-tuning|instruction tuning]] to improve its ability to follow user directives and engage in [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]].

## Availability and Distribution

Meta released Llama 3.1 under a free [[concepts/license|license]] for both research and commercial use, continuing the company's approach of open distribution for its language models. The model [[concepts/weights|weights]] became available through Meta's official channels and other [[concepts/model-repositories|model repositories]], allowing researchers and developers to run [[concepts/inference|inference]] and fine-tune versions locally.
