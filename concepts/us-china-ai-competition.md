# US-China AI Competition

## Overview
The strategic rivalry between the [[entities/united-states]] and [[entities/china]] for supremacy in [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) development, encompassing hardware, software, talent, and ecosystem influence.

## Key Pillars of Competition
- **[[concepts/compute|Compute]] & Hardware:** The struggle for control over the [[concepts/semiconductor-supply-chain|Semiconductor supply chain]], specifically high-end GPUs and [[concepts/neural-processing-units|AI Accelerators]] (e.g., [[entities/nvidia]]), and the impact of US-led Export Controls.
- **Algorithmic [[concepts/innovation|Innovation]]:** The tension between the US-led [[concepts/proprietary-ai]] models and the rise of [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/open-source]] architectures developed in [[entities/china]].
- **[[concepts/data-sovereignty|Data Sovereignty]]:** Competition over large-scale [[concepts/training-data|datasets]], [[concepts/ai-security]] regulations, and the ability to leverage massive-scale human-generated data for [[concepts/llm]] training.
- **Infrastructure & Energy:** The deployment of [[concepts/techno-economics|Data Centers]] and the critical requirement for scalable energy [[concepts/grids|grids]] to support massive [[concepts/compute]] demands.
