# Corporate AI Governance

Corporate AI Governance refers to the frameworks and policies that organizations establish to manage artificial intelligence systems and their associated risks within enterprise environments. These governance structures address both officially sanctioned AI deployments and the informal or unauthorized uses of AI tools by employees—a phenomenon known as Shadow AI. Effective governance requires organizations to balance innovation with risk management, ensuring that AI systems operate transparently, securely, and in compliance with regulatory requirements.

## Shadow AI and Risk Management

Shadow AI represents a significant challenge in corporate environments, where employees adopt AI tools without formal approval or oversight. This uncontrolled adoption can introduce data security risks, compliance violations, and inconsistent quality standards across the organization. Corporate governance frameworks must therefore establish clear policies around AI tool adoption while remaining flexible enough to accommodate the benefits that emerging AI technologies can provide to business operations.

## Implementation and Organizational Structure

Organizations typically implement AI governance through dedicated oversight structures, such as AI ethics committees or governance offices. These bodies establish standards for responsible AI use, evaluate new tools before deployment, monitor ongoing performance, and ensure alignment with organizational values and legal obligations. Effective governance also requires clear communication channels between IT departments, business units, and employees to create visibility into both authorized and unauthorized AI usage.
