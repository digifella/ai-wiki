# Tint Slider

The Tint Slider is a control within [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]'s new [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]], designed to adjust the [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and hue balance of an image. This feature allows users to shift colors toward warmer or cooler tones with precision, making it a practical component of the updated [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] workflow in the application.

## Usage in Color Grading

The Tint Slider functions as part of a broader set of [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] controls introduced in Photoshop 2025. It works alongside vibrance and saturation [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to give photographers and digital artists more [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over their image's overall color cast. The tool is particularly useful for correcting color temperature issues or applying intentional color grading effects to achieve a specific aesthetic.

## Integration with Photoshop 2025

As documented in Adobe's [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] content, the Tint Slider represents an evolution in how [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] handles non-destructive color adjustments. The feature fits within the modernized Color and Vibrance tool interface, reflecting Adobe's approach to streamlining color correction workflows for both professional and amateur users.
