# Praveen Kulkarni

Praveen Kulkarni is a thought leader and [[concepts/creator|creator]] who focuses on various scientific concepts, particularly those elucidated by [[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]]. His work often delves into the intersection of [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]], modern [[concepts/science|science]], and [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], highlighting their roles in understanding complex systems.

### Summary
[[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] discusses the critical distinction between [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|procedural knowledge]] (knowing how) and [[concepts/conceptual-insight|conceptual insight]] (understanding why). Using historical examples, such as Mayan [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], he illustrates that while complex mathematical procedures can be performed without deep understanding, true comprehension comes from grasping underlying concepts.

- [[concepts/feynman|Feynman]] emphasizes that [[concepts/physics|physics]] frequently involves dealing with "[[concepts/enormous-numbers|enormous numbers]]," leading to the development of sophisticated mathematical techniques.
- He argues that these techniques are often used as tools rather than being understood conceptually, a point illustrated through his analysis of Mayan [[concepts/number-systems|number systems]].
- The clip explores why true understanding is crucial for scientific progress and philosophical inquiry.
