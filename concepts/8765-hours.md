## The "8765 Hours" Concept

The concept of **8765 hours** refers to the total number of hours in a year (365 days x 24 hours). In [[concepts/health|healthcare]] contexts, it underscores the limited time patients spend within formal health systems compared to their daily lives. This idea emphasizes that patients' lived experiences and self-management outside clinical settings are crucial for understanding their overall [[concepts/well-being|well-being]].

- **Patient-Generated Knowledge:** Encourages patients to be active producers of knowledge rather than passive recipients of care.
- Challenging embedded assumptions in [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] systems about patient roles and capabilities.
