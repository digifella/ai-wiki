# Antoni Gaudí

**[[concepts/antoni-gaudí|Antoni Gaudí]] i Cornet** (1852–1926) was a Catalan architect and the foremost figure of [[concepts/catalan-modernism|Catalan Modernism]]. Known for his organic, nature-inspired designs and innovative structural techniques, his work is characterized by the use of hyperbolic paraboloids, helicoids, and catenary arches.

## Key Works
- [[concepts/sagrada-família]]: His magnum opus and unfinished masterpiece.
- Park Güell: Public park system composed of gardens and architectural elements.
- Casa Batlló: Residential building known for its skeletal, organic facade.
- Casa Milà: Also known as "La Pedrera," notable for its wrought-iron balconies and undulating stone facade.

## Architectural Philosophy
- **Organic Architecture**: Believed that nature is the ultimate source of inspiration, avoiding straight lines and right angles.
- **Structural Innovation**: Utilized gravity-based structural models (hanging chain models) to determine optimal load paths.
- **Integration of Crafts**: Collaborated extensively with ceramicists, glassblowers, and ironworkers to create a total work of art (*Gesamtkunstwerk*).
