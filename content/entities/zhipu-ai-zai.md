# Zhipu Ai Zai

[[entities/zhipu-ai|Zhipu AI]] (also known as Z.Ai) is a Chinese [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] company that develops [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and AI systems. The company is known for creating the [[entities/glm|GLM]] series of [[concepts/models|models]], which are designed to serve various practical [[concepts/software|applications]] in business and [[concepts/coding|software development]].

## GLM-4.7 Model

[[concepts/advanced-coding|GLM-4.7]] is Zhipu AI's [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]], developed with a focus on three primary [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]: coding, business [[concepts/automation|automation]], and [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]]. The model represents the company's effort to provide accessible [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] that can handle [[concepts/real-world-tasks|real-world tasks]] requiring advanced technical understanding and logical [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. As an open-source release, GLM-4.7 allows developers and organizations to deploy and modify the model for their specific needs.
