# Accelerating Universe

The **Accelerating Universe** is the observation that the expansion of the Universe is speeding up over time, rather than slowing down due to [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]]. This phenomenon is the primary evidence for the existence of [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|Dark Energy]], a hypothetical form of energy that permeates all of space and exerts a negative pressure.

## Key Evidence: Type Ia Supernovae

The discovery of cosmic acceleration relies heavily on observations of [[concepts/type-ia-supernovae]], which are used as [[concepts/standard-candles]] to measure cosmic distances.

- **Standard Candle Assumption**: Type Ia [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]] are assumed to have a consistent peak luminosity, allowing astronomers to calculate distance based on observed brightness.
- **Age-Bias Challenge**: Recent debates question whether systematic [[concepts/biases|biases]], such as the age of the progenitor [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], affect the luminosity calibration of these supernovae, potentially undermining the evidence for acceleration.
- **Scientific Consensus**: Despite challenges to the standard candle assumption, leading cosmologists, including Nobel Laureates, continue to defend the Dark Energy model as the most robust explanation for [[concepts/empirical-evidence|observational data]]. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Nobel-Laureates-Defend-Dark-Energy-Amidst-Type-Ia-Supern|Nobel Laureates Defend Dark Energy Amidst Type Ia Supernova Age-Bias Challenge]] for details on this ongoing debate.

## Theoretical Framework

- **Lambda-CDM Model**: The [[concepts/standard-model-of-particle-physics|standard model]] of [[concepts/big-bang|Big Bang]] [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]] incorporates a cosmological constant ($\Lambda$) representing Dark Energy, which accounts for approximately 68% of the total energy [[concepts/density|density]] of the universe.
- **Equation of State**: Dark Energy is characterized by an equation of state parameter $w \approx -1$, indicating constant energy density as the universe expands.
