# Digital Teammate

A [[entities/digital-teammate|Digital Teammate]] is an AI-powered assistant designed to work alongside humans in executing workflows and automating tasks. Rather than operating independently or requiring detailed [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] for each action, a Digital Teammate combines [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] with task execution to handle substantial portions of work with minimal supervision. This collaborative model differs from traditional [[concepts/automation-tools|automation tools]] by emphasizing partnership—the AI contributes expertise and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] while humans retain oversight and [[concepts/power|control]].

## Key Characteristics

Digital Teammates typically operate within specific domains or [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] contexts, leveraging language [[concepts/models|models]] and [[concepts/code-execution|code execution]] capabilities to understand objectives, break down [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], and implement solutions. They can adapt to variations in requirements and learn from [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] within a [[concepts/session|session]] or interaction. The effectiveness of a Digital Teammate depends on clear communication of goals and adequate context about the work environment, similar to onboarding a human colleague.

## Practical Implementation

Existing implementations demonstrate how Digital Teammates function across different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. These systems often integrate planning capabilities—where the AI reasons through an approach—with execution tools that allow direct interaction with workflows, [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], or external systems. The human remains in a position to review decisions, correct course, and handle exceptions or novel situations outside the AI's reliable capability [[concepts/range|range]].
