# Conceptual Design

**Conceptual design** is the [[concepts/phase|phase]] of the [[concepts/design-thinking|design process]] where the core idea, function, and aesthetic of a product are defined before detailed [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] or production begins. It involves translating abstract requirements into tangible visualizations and prototypes to explore possibilities.

## Key Principles
- **[[concepts/abstraction-layer|Abstraction]] to Concrete:** Moving from high-level user needs to specific interface or form factors.
- **Exploration:** Generating multiple variants to test feasibility and desirability.
- **Communication:** Using sketches, wireframes, or mockups to align stakeholders.

## Applications in Tech & Media
In software and hardware development, conceptual design often precedes actual [[concepts/coding|coding]] or manufacturing. It is frequently used in speculative fiction or tech commentary to visualize future iterations of existing products.

### Case Study: iOS 27 Conceptualization
Recent speculative designs have focused on how AI might refine future operating systems. A notable example is the conceptual design for **[[concepts/ios-27|iOS 27]]**, which imagines a future where [[concepts/system-refinements|system refinements]] are driven by [[concepts/frontier-ai-capability|advanced AI capabilities]].

- **Core Theme:** "Refinement" rather than radical overhaul, implying a mature ecosystem.
- **Key Features:**
  - **[[concepts/ai-photo-enhancements|AI Photo Enhancements]]:** Automated, intelligent editing and [[concepts/preservation|restoration]] of images.
  - **System Refinements:** Subtle UI/UX improvements for efficiency and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].
  - **Satirical Insight:** Highlights the gap between current tech trajectories and user expectations.
