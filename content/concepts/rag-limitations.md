# RAG Limitations

## Overview
The concept of Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) has emerged as a pivotal approach to integrating [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] with external information sources. However, [[concepts/traditional-rag|traditional RAG]] methods are facing several limitations, particularly in the handling of context and the integration of [[concepts/structured-output|structured data]].

## Key Limitations Identified

- **[[concepts/unstructured-data|Unstructured Data]] Overload**: The tendency to dump large volumes of unstructured data into AI systems can overwhelm these models and reduce their efficiency.
- **Context Inadequacy**: Current RAG approaches often struggle with providing sufficient [[concepts/contextual-information|contextual information]] necessary for nuanced understanding and generation tasks.
- **Scalability Issues**: As the volume and variety of [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources grow, traditional RAG methods face challenges in [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] efficiently.
