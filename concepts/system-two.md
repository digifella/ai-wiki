# System Two

**System Two** refers to the slow, effortful, and logical mode of [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] described in Thinking, Fast and Slow. It is traditionally contrasted with **[[concepts/system-one-architecture|System One]]**, which is fast, automatic, and intuitive.

## Evolution in AI Context

Recent developments in [[concepts/ai-system|AI architecture]] challenge the traditional dichotomy by introducing models optimized for rapid, probabilistic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] rather than generative text output.

### JEV: A System One Model

The introduction of **JEV** represents a shift toward high-[[concepts/speed|speed]], confident decision support systems.

- **Core Function**: JEV is a novel AI model developed by TypeSafe that focuses on rapid, probabilistic decision-making rather than [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] JEV: [[concepts/probabilistic-ai-models|Probabilistic AI]] for Fast, Confident Decision Support.
- **Classification**: Drawing from Kahneman's framework, JEV is categorized as a "[[concepts/system-one-intelligence|System One]]" model, characterized by speed and intuition-like processing.
- **Differentiation**: Unlike traditional [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) that prioritize generative capabilities, JEV prioritizes confidence and speed in decision support tasks.
- **Source**: JEV: Probabilistic AI for Fast, Confident Decision Support ([[entities/ibm-technology|IBM Technology]], 2026-10-05)
