# Various Outputs

Various Outputs is a multi-modal researcher tool constructed using the LangGraph framework and Google's Gemini 2.5 models. The system is designed to perform comprehensive investigations by processing diverse input data types, including text and images. By leveraging the multi-modal capabilities of the Gemini 2.5 architecture, the tool analyzes complex datasets to conduct thorough research tasks that extend beyond single-modality constraints.

## Architecture and Workflow

The architecture utilizes LangGraph to manage the state and flow of the research process, allowing for dynamic decision-making during execution. This graph-based structure enables the agent to navigate complex reasoning paths, adapt to intermediate findings, and coordinate multiple steps of data retrieval and analysis. The framework ensures that the investigation remains coherent and context-aware throughout the lifecycle of the query.

## Capabilities and Outputs

The tool generates various outputs based on the depth and nature of the investigation. It synthesizes information from both textual and visual inputs to produce detailed reports, summaries, or structured data. The multi-modal approach allows for cross-referencing visual evidence with textual context, providing a more holistic view of the subject matter compared to traditional text-only research agents.
