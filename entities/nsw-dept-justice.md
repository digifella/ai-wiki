# Nsw Dept Justice

The NSW Department of Justice is a government agency responsible for coordinating justice and social policy matters across New South Wales. It operates as a central repository for research, information, and evidence-based policy development, serving government agencies, legal practitioners, and stakeholders throughout the state's criminal justice and social welfare systems.

The department functions to develop and coordinate policy across multiple areas of the justice sector, drawing on research and evidence to inform government decision-making. It serves as a clearing house and an up-to-date research space focusing on critical areas such as children and family violence. By consolidating data and insights, the agency supports the broader ecosystem of legal and social services within the state.
