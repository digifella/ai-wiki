# Gaming Performance

**[[concepts/gaming|Gaming]] Performance** refers to the efficiency and capability of hardware and software systems to render, process, and deliver interactive visual experiences. In the context of modern [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]], it increasingly encompasses the role of [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) in procedural generation, [[concepts/digital-asset-creation|asset creation]], and real-time optimization.
