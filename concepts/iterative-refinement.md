# Iterative Refinement

Process of progressively improving a [[concepts/solution|solution]] through repeated cycles of evaluation and adjustment.

- **[[concepts/incremental-progress|Incremental Progress]]**: Small, manageable improvements over time
- **[[concepts/feedback|Feedback]] [[concepts/loops|Loops]]**: Continuous evaluation and incorporation of [[concepts/feedback|feedback]]
- **Adaptive**: Ability to adjust based on changing requirements or new information
