## Nutritious Chickpea Salad: Satiety and Weight Loss Recipe
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vfmjk6L4tfw

### Summary
This video presents a recipe for a nutritious [[concepts/chickpea-salad|chickpea salad]] designed to promote [[concepts/satiety|satiety]] and aid in [[concepts/weight-loss|weight loss]] without resorting to strict dietary restrictions or experiencing constant hunger. The main premise is that incorporating chickpeas into one's [[concepts/diet|diet]] can help manage appetite effectively.

The recipe begins with the [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] of chickpeas, which can be either dry (requiring a 6-hour soak and 60-90 minutes of boiling with salt) or conveniently canned. The video emphasizes the nutritional benefits of chickpeas, noting their role in stabilizing blood [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] levels, thus reducing cravings for sweets. Furthermore, chickpeas are highlighted as a good source of [dietary fiber](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dietary_fiber), which aids slow digestion and helps prevent constipation. Once boiled, the chickpeas are pan-fried in coconut oil with smoked paprika and dried garlic until golden brown, then set aside to cool slightly.

Complementing the spiced chickpeas, the salad incorporates a variety of fresh vegetables. Cherry tomatoes are halved, small cucumbers are diced, and an [[entities/avocado|avocado]] is cubed. These are joined by finely chopped red onion and sliced black olives. A key component of the salad is its homemade dressing, prepared by blending olive oil, lemon zest, lemon juice, Dijon mustard, honey, oregano, turmeric, salt, pepper mix, and fresh parsley. The video suggests that this unique dressing is instrumental in transforming the salad's flavor profile and its appetite-controlling properties.

Finally, all the prepared ingredients are combined in a large bowl, and the vibrant green dressing is poured over them, ensuring everything is thoroughly mixed. The video concludes by reiterating that this chickpea salad offers a genuinely filling meal that provides lasting satiety without the heavy feeling often associated with other dishes. It promotes a mindful approach to eating, where satisfaction is achieved through wholesome food choices rather than willpower, making it a sustainable option for managing hunger and weight, especially when topped with crumbled feta cheese for added richness.
