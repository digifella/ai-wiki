# Boeing 737-400

The **Boeing 737-400** is a member of the Boeing 737 Classic series (-300/-400/-500), developed as a stretched variant of the 737-300 to accommodate higher passenger capacities. It remains one of the most widely operated variants in the 737 family, known for its [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and efficiency in medium-haul routes.

## Technical Specifications
- **Manufacturer:** Boeing
- **Series:** 737 Classic
- **First Flight:** 1988
- **[[concepts/engine|Engine]] Options:** CFM56-3 or Pratt & Whitney JT8D (later models)
- **Capacity:** Typically 140–168 passengers (depending on configuration)
- **Range:** ~2,940 nmi (5,450 km)
- **Status:** Production ended in 2000; many still in active service or converted to freighters.

## Operational History & Significance
- Designed to bridge the gap between the 737-300 and larger wide-body aircraft.
- Dominated regional and domestic markets in North [[entities/america|America]], [[entities/europe|Europe]], and Asia during the 1990s and 2000s.
- Frequently retrofitted with CFM56 engines for improved fuel efficiency and noise reduction.

## Notable Incidents
- **[[lab-notes/2026-05-31-USAir-Flight-5050-LaGuardia-Incident-Exposes-Critical-Cr|USAir Flight 5050: LaGuardia Incident Exposes Critical Crew and CRM Failures]]**: On [[entities/usair-flight-5050|September 20]], 1989, a 737-400 operated by USAir encountered critical [[concepts/crew-resource-management-crm|crew resource management (CRM)]] failures during departure from [[entities/laguardia-airport|LaGuardia Airport]], highlighting systemic issues in pilot [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] and situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]]. This incident is frequently cited in aviation safety training regarding CRM breakdowns and runway incursions.

## Legacy
- The -400 variant set standards for single-aisle jet economics, influencing subsequent Boeing 737 NG and Boeing 737 MAX designs.
- Many airframes have been converted to freighters or extended service life via advanced avionics upgrades.
