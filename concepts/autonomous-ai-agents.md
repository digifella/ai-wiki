## AI Agent Hallucination
A critical challenge in [[concepts/autonomous-operation|autonomous systems]] is the tendency to generate incorrect or nonsensical outputs.
*   **[[concepts/causes|Causes]]**: Often stems from ambiguous prompts, insufficient context, or over-reliance on probabilistic generation without grounding.
*   **[[concepts/mitigation-strategies|Mitigation Strategies]]**:
    *   Implementing rigorous validation layers.
    *   Using [[concepts/prompt-engineering|Prompt Engineering]] to constrain output spaces.
    *   Integrating [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]] for continuous correction.
