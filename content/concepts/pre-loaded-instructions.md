# Pre-loaded Instructions

[[concepts/instructions|Instructions]] embedded within an AI system or tool, enabling immediate execution without additional configuration. This concept eliminates repetitive [[concepts/setup|setup]] steps for [[concepts/recurring-tasks|recurring tasks]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Context-aware**: Tailored to specific domains (e.g., meetings, reporting)
- **Reduced cognitive load**: Eliminates user input for standard workflows
- **Scalable**: Multiple [[concepts/instruction-sets|instruction sets]] coexist for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]

## Integration with AI Tools
- **[[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]] Gems** ([[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] Gems): [[concepts/custom-ai-assistants|Custom AI assistants]] built via pre-loaded instructions:
  - *[[concepts/meeting-prep-strategist|Meeting Prep Strategist]]*: Analyzes agendas and generates discussion points
  - *Automated Weekly Business Report*: Generates business reports automatically
