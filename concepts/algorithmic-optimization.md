# Algorithmic Optimization

[[concepts/software-algorithms|Algorithmic optimization]] is a critical area of computer [[concepts/science|science]] and [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]] that focuses on enhancing the efficiency and effectiveness of [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]]. This process can involve reducing time complexity, minimizing space usage, improving accuracy, or adapting to dynamic conditions.

## Key Concepts
- **Time Complexity**: Refers to the computational time taken by an [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]] as the input size grows.
- **Space Complexity**: Measures the amount of [[concepts/memory|memory]] used by an algorithm during its execution.
- **Accuracy and [[concepts/accuracy|Precision]]**: Important for algorithms dealing with data analysis, [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models, etc.
- **Adaptive Algorithms**: Capable of adjusting their performance parameters based on run-time information.

## Methods
- **Traditional [[concepts/algorithm-optimization|Optimization Techniques]]**
  - Manual tuning
  - Profiling and [[concepts/benchmark-testing|benchmarking]]
  - Algorithm selection and replacement

- **Modern Approaches**
  - Genetic algorithms for [[concepts/active-parameters|parameter optimization]]
  - [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|Machine learning models]] to predict optimal configurations

### New Methodologies Introduced
- **AI-driven [[concepts/iterative-learning|Iterative Learning]] (Auto-research)**
  - Automated adjustment of algorithm parameters based on real-time [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]].
  - Uses machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] and [[entities/big-data|data analytics]] to iteratively improve outcomes.
