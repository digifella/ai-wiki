# Dynamic Video Content

Dynamic Video Content is a video production methodology that leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] image generation models, particularly [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]], to create visual sequences through a structured creative process. Rather than relying solely on traditional cinematography or static pre-rendered assets, this approach generates video frames iteratively, allowing producers to refine and adjust visual output throughout production.

## Core Process

The method centers on iterative [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], a technique where detailed textual descriptions are progressively refined to guide [[concepts/ai-image-generation|AI image generation]] toward desired aesthetic and narrative outcomes. Producers begin with an initial prompt describing a scene, evaluate the generated imagery, then adjust [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], composition details, or stylistic elements in subsequent iterations. This cyclical refinement allows for greater creative control over the final visual product compared to single-pass generation.

## Creative Applications

Dynamic Video Content enables rapid prototyping of visual ideas and can reduce production timelines for projects where photorealism is not essential. The approach proves particularly suited to stylized content, conceptual visualization, and experimental film work where the [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative process]] itself becomes part of the creative exploration. By automating frame generation while maintaining human directorial control through prompt engineering, the method bridges automated production and intentional artistic decision-making.
