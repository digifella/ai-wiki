# Chronic Patients

**Chronic Patients** are individuals managing long-term [[concepts/health|health]] conditions requiring sustained self-management and continuous [[concepts/coordination|care coordination]]. Effective management relies heavily on [[concepts/emotional-engagement|Patient Activation]], a metric for assessing a patient's knowledge, [[concepts/skill|skill]], and confidence in managing their own health.

  - **Focus**: Development of the **Patient Activation Measure (PAM)**.
