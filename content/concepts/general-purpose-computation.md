# General-Purpose Computation (GPGPU)

General-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] computation on graphics processing units (GPGPU) is a recent development in computer [[concepts/science|science]] that leverages the massive parallelism of GPUs for tasks traditionally handled by CPUs.

### Key Concepts
- **GPU [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]**: Specialized [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] designed primarily to accelerate the creation and transformation of [[concepts/images|images]].
- **[[concepts/cuda|CUDA]]**: [[concepts/compute-unified-device-architecture|Compute Unified Device Architecture]], developed by [[entities/nvidia|Nvidia]], which enables [[concepts/software|software]] developers to use a CUDA-enabled GPU for general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] processing.

### Related Technologies
- [[concepts/general-purpose-computing|Parallel Computing]]
- AI and [[concepts/machine-learning|Machine Learning]]

### Notable Developments
- CUDA was launched in 2007, revolutionizing the way GPUs can be used beyond their original purpose of handling graphics.
- [[entities/ian-buck|Ian Buck]] and [[entities/john-nicholls|John Nicholls]] were instrumental in the development of CUDA.
- The platform has been pivotal in advancing AI by allowing the use of GPUs for complex computations.
