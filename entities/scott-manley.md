# Scott Manley

Scott Manley is a prominent aerospace engineer and science communicator known for his detailed analysis of rocketry, orbital mechanics, commercial spaceflight developments, and supercomputing [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].

## Key Contributions & Analysis

- **[[entities/boom-supersonic|Boom Supersonic]] Overture**: Provided in-depth technical breakdown of the [[concepts/overture-airliner|Overture airliner]]'s propulsion systems, specifically focusing on the development of the "Symphony" engine.
- **[[concepts/superpower-jet-engine|SuperPower Jet Engine]]**: Analyzed the viability and engineering challenges of Boom Supersonic's new jet engine technology in the context of returning [[concepts/commercial-supersonic-flight|commercial supersonic flight]].
- **Commercial Supersonic Ambitions**: Evaluated the broader industry efforts to revive supersonic passenger travel, highlighting the Overture as a primary candidate.
- **[[lab-notes/2026-09-27-El-Capitan-Supercomputer-Scale-Engineering-and-Mission-a|El Capitan Supercomputer: Scale, Engineering, and Mission at LLNL]]**: Hosted an in-depth tour of El Capitan at [[concepts/lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory]] (LLNL), highlighting the scale, engineering, and mission of one of the world's most powerful supercomputers.
