# Meeting Prep Strategist

A Meeting Prep Strategist is a specialized AI assistant created through Google Gemini's Gems feature. Rather than utilizing Gemini's general-purpose interface, this custom assistant is configured with specific instructions, knowledge bases, and behavioral parameters designed to support professional meeting preparation. This specialization allows the assistant to provide focused guidance tailored to specific meeting contexts, distinguishing it from generic conversational models.

The primary functionality involves analyzing meeting agendas to identify key discussion points and potential strategic angles. By leveraging its customized configuration, the assistant helps users anticipate questions, structure arguments, and organize relevant information prior to the engagement. This process aims to enhance user readiness and efficiency by providing context-aware recommendations rather than broad, untargeted advice.

Implementation relies on the user defining the assistant's scope and expertise through the Gems interface. This allows for the integration of domain-specific knowledge or personal notes, ensuring that the generated insights are relevant to the user's specific professional needs. The resulting tool serves as a dedicated preparatory aid, streamlining the workflow for individuals seeking structured support before professional interactions.
