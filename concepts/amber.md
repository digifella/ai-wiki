# Amber

**Amber** is fossilized tree resin, distinct from sap or [[entities/pitch]]. It serves as a critical [[entities/medium|medium]] for preserving [[concepts/fossils|fossils]], particularly insects and other small organisms, providing insights into ancient ecosystems.

## Recent Discoveries & Evolutionary Context

*   **Oldest Amber Discovery Redefines [[concepts/plant-resin|Plant Resin]] Defense Evolution**
    *   Recent findings challenge previous timelines for the evolution of plant resin as a defense mechanism.
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-07-24-Oldest-Amber-Discovery-Redefines-Plant-Resin-Defense-Evo|Oldest Amber Discovery Redefines Plant Resin Defense Evolution]] for detailed analysis of the discovery.
    *   The discovery suggests plant resin defense [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] evolved significantly earlier than previously thought, reshaping our understanding of plant-evolution and co-evolution with arthropods.

## Key Properties

*   **[[concepts/writing|Composition]]**: Primarily composed of terpenes and other organic compounds that polymerize over millions of years.
*   **Color**: Typically golden-yellow, but can range from red to black depending on impurities and oxidation.
*   **Hardness**: Relatively soft compared to gemstones, requiring careful handling.
*   **Flammability**: Burns with a resinous smell; historically used as a fuel source.

## Cultural & Scientific Significance

*   **[[concepts/paleontology|Paleontology]]**: Provides a window into Cenozoic and Mesozoic eras, preserving soft tissues rarely found in rock records.
*   **Jewelry**: Valued for its warmth and color; often used in jewelry and [[concepts/art]].
*   **Medicine**: Historically used in traditional medicine; modern research explores its antimicrobial properties.
*   **Pop Culture**: Famously depicted in *[[concepts/jurassic-park|Jurassic Park]]* as the medium for extracting dinosaur DNA.
