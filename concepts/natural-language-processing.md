# Natural Language Processing

**[[concepts/language-processing|Natural Language Processing]] (NLP)** is a subfield of [[concepts/linguistics|linguistics]], computer [[concepts/science|science]], and [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] concerned with the interactions between computers and human language.

## Key Developments & Models

*   **[[concepts/gpt-3|GPT-3]]**: Represents a significant leap in model scale, demonstrating emergent-abilities not present in smaller predecessors like [[concepts/gpt-2]].
    *   Focuses on unprecedented [[concepts/scaling-laws|scaling laws]] and their impact on [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] capabilities.
    *   Detailed analysis available in [[lab-notes/2026-09-25-GPT-3-Unprecedented-Scaling-and-Emergent-Language-Model|GPT-3: Unprecedented Scaling and Emergent Language Model Capabilities]].
    *   Discussed in the context of [[concepts/code-size|model size]] and performance by Computerphile in their video analysis [[concepts/neural-networks|GPT-3]]: Unprecedented Scaling and Emergent Language Model Capabilities(https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_8yVOC4ciXc).

## Core Concepts

*   **[[concepts/transformer-models|Transformer Architecture]]**: The underlying [[concepts/neural-network-architecture|neural network architecture]] powering modern LLMs.
*   **[[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs)**: Models trained on vast datasets to predict the next token in a sequence.
*   **Zero-shot/Few-shot [[concepts/learning|Learning]]**: Capabilities enabled by scaling, allowing models to perform tasks without explicit [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]].
