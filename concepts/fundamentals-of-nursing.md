# Fundamentals Of Nursing

Fundamentals of Nursing encompasses the core knowledge and practice frameworks that underpin professional nursing care across all settings. Within [[concepts/community-health-nursing|community health nursing]], this foundation extends beyond individual [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]] to address population-level [[concepts/health|health]] outcomes and the broader factors that influence [[concepts/wellness|wellness]] and [[concepts/health-promotion|disease prevention]].

## Prevention Levels in Community Health

[[concepts/community-health|Community health]] nursing operates across three distinct [[concepts/levels-of-prevention|prevention levels]]. [[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary prevention]] focuses on health promotion and [[concepts/primary-prevention-interventions-designed-to-prevent-disease-before-it-occurs|disease prevention]] in populations that are currently healthy, including [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]] [[concepts/software|programs]], health education, and environmental safety measures. [[concepts/secondary-prevention|Secondary prevention]] targets [[concepts/secondary-prevention|early detection and treatment]] of disease through screening programs and health assessments, aiming to identify conditions before symptoms become severe. [[concepts/tertiary-prevention|Tertiary prevention]] addresses management and rehabilitation of established diseases, working to minimize complications and improve [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] for those already affected.

## Determinants of Health

Health outcomes are shaped by multiple interconnected determinants that extend well beyond individual behaviors or medical intervention. These include socioeconomic factors such as income and education, environmental conditions including access to clean water and safe housing, [[concepts/social-determinants-of-health|social determinants]] like community support and social [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]], and [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]] services. Community health nurses recognize these determinants when planning interventions and understand that sustainable improvements in [[concepts/population-health|population health]] require [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to the structural and systemic factors that influence individual and [[concepts/social-well-being|community wellbeing]].

Community health nursing practice integrates understanding of prevention levels and health determinants to develop evidence-based, culturally appropriate interventions that address both immediate [[concepts/patient-needs|health needs]] and underlying [[concepts/causes|causes]] of health disparities within populations.
