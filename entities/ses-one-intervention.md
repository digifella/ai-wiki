# Ses One Intervention

The [[concepts/jungian-grief-model|SES One Intervention]] is a digital [[concepts/health|health]] program consisting of educational modules focused on [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] recognition and [[concepts/solution|resolution]] strategies. It was developed at the University of Copenhagen by [[concepts/ses-family|SES Family]], an organization with approximately 20 [[entities/employees|employees]]. The intervention addresses the practical challenge of [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], acknowledging what researchers term the "[[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]"—the gap between people's desire for digital health solutions and their actual willingness to engage with them.

## Design and Implementation

The intervention features an interactive application with a deliberately simple layout, designed to facilitate user engagement and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. The modular structure allows for flexible implementation and adaptation to different contexts and user needs related to conflict management and resolution.
