# User Experience Research

## Core Concepts
- **Definition**: Systematic investigation of target users and their requirements to add useful and usable features to product-design.
- **Goal**: Understand user behaviors, needs, and motivations through observation techniques, task analysis, and other feedback methodologies.

## AI in UX Research Context
Recent insights into AI mechanics are critical for modern UX researchers to understand model limitations and capabilities.

- **[[concepts/generative-prediction|Generative Prediction]]**: AI operates as a generative prediction system rather than a retrieval database [[lab-notes/2026-08-11-AI-as-a-Generative-Prediction-System-Mechanisms-and-Limi|AI as a Generative Prediction System: Mechanisms and Limitations Explained]].
- **Anthropic Perspective**: [[entities/jane-leibrock|Jane Leibrock]], Head of User Experience Research at Anthropic, demystifies the shift toward prompt-driven interactions.
- **[[concepts/prompt-driven-editing|Prompt-Driven Editing]]**: The rise of tools like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[entities/google|Google]] [[entities/google-gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/canva|Canva]], and [[entities/google-flow|Google Flow]] allows complex photo and [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] tasks to be simplified via natural language prompts, removing the need for [[concepts/software-10|traditional software]] skills like [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] [[lab-notes/2026-08-29-AI-Revolutionizes-Photo-Editing-with-Prompt-Driven-Gener|AI Revolutionizes Photo Editing with Prompt-Driven Generative Capabilities]].
