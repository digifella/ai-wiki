# Chemical Engineering

Application of physical sciences, [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], and economics to transform raw materials into valuable products via [[concepts/food-chemistry]] and physical unit operations. Focuses on design, analysis, optimization, and [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] of processes.

## Fundamental Principles
- Chemical thermodynamics: Energy balances, [[concepts/phase|phase]] equilibria, and process feasibility.
- Transport phenomena: [[concepts/preservation|Conservation]] of mass, momentum, and heat transfer.
- Reaction kinetics: Rate laws, [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], and reactor design.
- Process control: Automation, [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]], and system stability.
- Catalysis: Acceleration of reaction rates and selectivity using catalysts.

## Key Applications & Systems
- Petrochemicals: Refining, cracking, polymerization, and fuel synthesis.
- Pharmaceuticals: Synthesis, purification, and formulation of active ingredients.
- Food and [[concepts/beverage-manufacturing|Beverage Processing]]:
	- Application of separation processes and extraction techniques to modify chemical [[concepts/writing|composition]] while preserving sensory properties.
	- Specific case study on solvent-based and [[concepts/supercritical-fluid-extraction|supercritical fluid extraction]] for [[concepts/caffeine-removal|caffeine removal]] and alcohol reduction: [[lab-notes/2026-06-24-Decaffeination-and-Non-Alcoholic-Beverages-Methods-Histo|Decaffeination and Non-Alcoholic Beverages: Methods, History, and Chemistry]].
