# Light Control

**[[concepts/light|Light]] Control** refers to the manipulation of luminance, color temperature, and intensity within visual media. In digital workflows, this encompasses global [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] ([[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]]) and local corrections ([[concepts/dodging|dodging]], [[concepts/burning|burning]], [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]]).

## Core Principles
- **Luminance Management**: Adjusting brightness without altering chrominance.
- **Color Temperature**: Shifting hues toward warm (orange/red) or cool (blue) spectrums.
- **[[concepts/dynamic-range|Dynamic Range]]**: Preserving detail in highlights and [[concepts/shadows|shadows]].

## Digital Implementation
Modern [[concepts/image-processing-software|image editing software]] utilizes non-destructive workflows via [[concepts/adjustment-layers]] and masks. Key tools include:
- **Curves & Levels**: For precise tonal mapping.
- **Gradient Maps**: For [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] based on luminance.
- **Selective Color**: For targeted hue adjustments.
