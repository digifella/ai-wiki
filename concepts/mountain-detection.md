# Mountain Detection

Mountain Detection is a selective editing technique in landscape photography that utilizes landscape masks within Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw. This method allows photographers to isolate specific geographic features, such as mountain ranges, from the rest of the image. By targeting these distinct areas, users can apply localized adjustments to contrast, saturation, or exposure without affecting other elements of the composition.

The process relies on the software’s ability to recognize and segment complex terrain automatically. This enables a more nuanced post-processing workflow where different portions of an image receive tailored enhancements based on their content. The technique is particularly useful for balancing dynamic range and emphasizing structural details in high-contrast scenes.

This approach was demonstrated in a tutorial by Colin Smith, who highlighted the efficiency of using built-in masking tools for precise control. The method supports a non-destructive editing style, allowing for iterative refinements to mountain regions independently from the sky or foreground.
