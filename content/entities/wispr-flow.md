# Wispr Flow

[[concepts/auto-editing|Wispr Flow]] is an AI-powered dictation application that enables users to input text through voice [[concepts/commands|commands]] across various [[concepts/software|applications]]. The platform leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to process and transcribe spoken language, positioning itself as a tool for hands-free productivity and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

In June 2025, the company secured $30 million in Series funding led by venture capital firm [[entities/menlo-ventures|Menlo Ventures]]. This investment supports Wispr Flow's development and market expansion for its voice dictation technology.
