# Astronomical Survey

**Astronomical [[concepts/survey|Survey]]** refers to the systematic observation of the sky to detect, [[concepts/catalog|catalog]], and characterize celestial objects. These surveys aim to cover large areas of the sky repeatedly or continuously to identify changes, transient events, or undiscovered bodies.

## Key Characteristics
- **Systematic Coverage:** Unlike targeted observations, surveys prioritize breadth and repeatability to establish baselines for variability and motion.
- **Data Intensity:** Modern surveys generate terabytes to petabytes of data, requiring automated pipelines for detection, classification, and archival.
- **Time-Domain [[concepts/astronomy|Astronomy]]:** Critical for studying variable [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]], [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|active galactic nuclei]], and solar system dynamics.

## Major Instruments & Initiatives
- **Vera C. Rubin Observatory (LSST):** The Legacy Survey of Space and Time represents a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in survey scale, providing deep, rapid, and wide-field imaging of the southern sky.
- **Sloan Digital Sky Survey (SDSS):** Pioneered optical spectroscopic and photometric surveys of galaxies and quasars.
- **Pan-STARRS:** Precursor to LSST, focused on wide-field imaging and asteroid detection.

## Recent Developments
- **[[entities/vera-rubin-modules|Vera Rubin]] Initial Data [[concepts/deployment|Release]] (2026):**
	- In April 2026, the Rubin Observatory team submitted an unprecedented dataset to the International Astronomical Union [[concepts/minor-planet-center|Minor Planet Center]].
	- Initial analysis revealed the detection of **11,000 new minor planets**, significantly expanding the known catalog of solar system small bodies.
	- Details on the unexpected features in raw images and the implications for asteroid population models are documented in [[lab-notes/2026-05-31-Vera-Rubin-Observatory-Initial-Data-Reveals-11000-New-Mi|Vera Rubin Observatory: Initial Data Reveals 11,000 New Minor Planets]].

## Significance
- **Solar System Dynamics:** Enhanced mapping of near-[[entities/earth|Earth]] objects and Kuiper Belt objects improves impact [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]] and understanding of planetary formation.
- **[[concepts/cosmology|Cosmology]]:** [[concepts/cosmic-mapping|Large-scale structure mapping]] contributes to [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark energy]] and [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]] studies via weak lensing and [[concepts/supernova|supernova]] [[concepts/standard-candles|standard candles]].
