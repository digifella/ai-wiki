# Openclaw Strategy

[[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] is an [[concepts/ai-agent-platform|AI agent platform]] that enables autonomous systems to execute tasks and interact with enterprise environments. However, the platform has been identified as having significant [[concepts/security|security]] vulnerabilities that pose risks for production deployments, particularly in enterprise contexts where data protection and system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] are critical concerns.

## Enterprise Adoption Challenges

The security limitations of [[concepts/conversational-chatbots|OpenClaw]] prompted [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]] to develop [[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]], a hardened variant designed specifically for enterprise [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. [[concepts/enterprise-ai|NemoClaw]] addresses known vulnerabilities in the base [[concepts/2026-04-23-httpswwwyoutubecomwatchvvnt5c-rlwie-here-is-a-summary-of-the-openclaw|OpenClaw architecture]] while maintaining compatibility with existing [[concepts/multi-agent-workflows|agent workflows]]. This differentiation reflects broader industry efforts to [[entities/make|make]] [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] suitable for regulated and security-sensitive business environments.

## Industry Response

Multiple technology companies, including [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] and [[entities/salesforce|Salesforce]], have adjusted their platforms and [[concepts/policies|policies]] in response to OpenClaw's security profile. These [[concepts/responses|responses]] include [[concepts/usage-limits|usage restrictions]], subscription modifications, and strategic pivots toward more [[concepts/secure|secure]] alternatives like NemoClaw for customers requiring enterprise-grade protection.
