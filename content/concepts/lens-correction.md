# Lens Correction

Lens Correction is a feature introduced in Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] version 18.3. It provides photographers with tools to address optical distortions and aberrations that occur naturally when light passes through camera lenses. These corrections help restore [[concepts/images|images]] to a more neutral optical state.

## Functionality

The lens correction capabilities in Camera Raw 18.3 allow users to adjust for common lens imperfections including distortion, vignetting, and chromatic aberration. By applying these corrections during the raw processing stage, photographers can improve image quality before further editing or export.

## Context

The feature was highlighted alongside other notable additions in the 18.3 update, including [[concepts/depth-range-masking|Depth Range Masking]] and [[concepts/film-presets|Film-Inspired Presets]], as part of Adobe's continued development of Camera Raw's processing capabilities.
