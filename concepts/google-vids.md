# Google Vids

[[entities/google|Google]] Vids is an AI-powered tool developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] for creating and editing videos efficiently. It integrates with other Google services to offer seamless workflow for content creators and businesses.

## Features
- **AI-Powered [[concepts/video-creation|Video Creation]]:** Automates the process of converting written content or [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] into engaging [[concepts/video-creation|video formats]].
- **Integration with Other Tools:** Works closely with tools like [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], allowing users to streamline their [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] processes.
- **Unwatermarked Videos:** Offers options for creating [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] videos without visible watermarks.
- **[[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] Ecosystem:** As part of [[entities/google|Google]]'s 2026 [[concepts/cartoon|AI product updates]] (see 2026 04 14 [[concepts/6-second-clips|AI round up]] by [[entities/your-own-workflow|Your Own Workflow]] channel), now tightly integrated with [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[concepts/google-search|Google Search]], and [[concepts/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]].

## Usage Scenarios
- Creating educational tutorials and courses from existing [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]].
- Repurposing [[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|intellectual property]] into dynamic video formats for social media.
- **Creating 30-second ads:** Turning [[concepts/business-philosophy|business philosophy]] stored in [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] into cinematic 30-second ads using [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] (see 2026 04 14 Building a video from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] and [[concepts/veo|Google Veo]] Charles Terence [[entities/harper|Harper]] channel).
