# Match Exposure

**Match [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]** is a feature within [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] designed to streamline [[concepts/batch-processing|batch processing]] by automatically aligning the exposure levels of multiple images. This functionality enhances [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]] for photographers handling sequences with inconsistent lighting or shutter speeds.

## Key Developments

*   **ACR 18.7 Integration**: The feature was significantly improved in the [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] 18.7 Update: Match Exposure, Grid View, Undockable Panels (see [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-Camera-Raw-18.7-Update-Match-Exposure-Grid-View-Undockab|Camera Raw 18.7 Update: Match Exposure, Grid View, Undockable Panels]]).
*   **Workflow Efficiency**: The update focuses on offering greater flexibility, allowing users to quickly standardize exposure across a selection of photos without manual per-image adjustment.
*   **Related UI Changes**: The 18.7 update also introduced a new **Grid View** for browsing and **Undockable Panels** for customizable workspace layouts.
