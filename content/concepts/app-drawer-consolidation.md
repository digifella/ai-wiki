# App Drawer Consolidation

App Drawer Consolidation is a design feature introduced in [[entities/android|Android]] 16 that reorganizes how [[concepts/software|applications]] appear in the device's [[concepts/app-drawer|app drawer]]. The feature groups related applications into logical categories, reducing visual clutter and simplifying navigation for users with large numbers of installed applications. This organizational approach presents apps in a more structured layout, making it easier to locate specific applications without scrolling through an extensive unorganized list.

## Functionality and User Impact

The [[concepts/consolidation|consolidation]] mechanism automatically sorts applications by category or usage pattern, though the exact grouping criteria depend on the implementation in Android 16. Rather than displaying all apps in a single alphabetical or chronological list, related applications are bundled together, allowing users to find tools more efficiently. This is particularly beneficial for devices with many installed applications, where a flat app drawer [[concepts/structure|structure]] becomes unwieldy.

## Integration with Android 16

App Drawer Consolidation was introduced as part of Android 16's stable release, reflecting a broader focus on interface refinement and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] improvements. The feature integrates with the existing Android launcher ecosystem, though its availability and specific behavior may vary depending on the device manufacturer and launcher implementation used.
