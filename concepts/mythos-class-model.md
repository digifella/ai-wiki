# Mythos-Class Model

## Definition
A **Mythos-Class Model** refers to a high-capability [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] architecture designed with intrinsic, unfiltered [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]]. In the context of [[entities/anthropic]]'s [[concepts/software-sprint|development cycle]] (circa 2026), this classification denotes models that prioritize raw cognitive throughput and factual neutrality over standard safety alignment layers, effectively [[concepts/acting|acting]] as "uncensored" counterparts to their aligned siblings.

## Characteristics
- **Unrestricted Output**: Lacks the refusal [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] typical of consumer-grade AI, allowing generation of controversial, sensitive, or technically hazardous content if prompted.
- **High [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] [[concepts/density|Density]]**: Optimized for [[concepts/complex-problem-solving|complex problem-solving]] and logical deduction without artificial constraints on [[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|chain-of-thought]] [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].
- **Counterpart Architecture**: Often exists as a twin to a "safe" variant (e.g., Fable-Class), sharing the same base transformer [[concepts/weights|weights]] but differing in post-training [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]] from human [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] (RLHF) and safety [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]].

## Strategic Context
The bifurcation into "[[entities/fable|Fable]]" (aligned) and "[[concepts/mythos|Mythos]]" (unaligned) classes represents a shift in vendor strategy towards explicit [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] regarding [[concepts/system-instructions|model constraints]]. It allows developers to choose between safety guarantees and raw capability depending on the application domain, acknowledging that certain research or creative workflows require unfiltered access to the model's [[concepts/embedding-spaces|latent space]].
