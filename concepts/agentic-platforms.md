# Agentic Platforms

**Agentic platforms** refer to software ecosystems and [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] designed to facilitate the creation, deployment, and management of [[concepts/autonomous-ai-systems|autonomous AI]] agents. These platforms provide the necessary tools for agents to perceive environments, reason, plan, and execute actions with varying degrees of autonomy.
