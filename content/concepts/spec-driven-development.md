# Spec Driven Development

Spec Driven Development is an approach to [[concepts/coding|software development]] using [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]], where development tasks are guided by clear specifications and requirements rather than starting from scratch. This methodology emphasizes defining what the [[concepts/code|code]] should do before implementation, allowing [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to generate solutions that align with explicit goals and constraints. The approach leverages AI's ability to quickly interpret specifications and produce functional code while maintaining [[concepts/developer|developer]] [[concepts/power|control]] through [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]].

## Features and Capabilities

[[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] as a spec-driven development tool supports direct interpretation of requirements, enabling developers to provide detailed specifications that the [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] translates into working implementations. The assistant can handle complex [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks across multiple [[concepts/files|files]] and frameworks, adapting to project-specific patterns and conventions. This reduces the cognitive load on developers, who can focus on specification quality and validation rather than low-level [[concepts/implementation-details|implementation details]].

## Customization and Workflows

The [[concepts/customization|customization]] options within spec-driven [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]] allow teams to tailor how the [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] operates within their specific context—including code [[concepts/style|style]] preferences, architectural patterns, and domain-specific conventions. Advanced workflows may involve iterative specification refinement, where initial specs are tested and enhanced based on generated [[concepts/output|output]], creating a [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loop|loop]] between human direction and [[concepts/adoption|AI implementation]]. This enables more sophisticated development patterns than single-pass [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], accommodating complex projects where requirements evolve through interaction with working code.
