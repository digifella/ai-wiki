# Sans Institute

The SANS Institute is a cybersecurity training and education organization founded in 1989. It specializes in developing technical documentation and training materials focused on information security and defensive computing practices. The organization has established itself as a prominent provider of hands-on security training courses and certifications for information technology professionals and security practitioners.

A significant aspect of its operational scope involves the documentation regarding the hardening of air force bases. This work contributes to its broad portfolio of resources designed to enhance the security posture of critical infrastructure and military installations through standardized defensive measures.
