National Academies

National Academies of Sciences, Engineering, and Medicine (NASEM) is an American nonprofit, non-governmental organization that serves as an independent advisor to the U.S. government on scientific and technical matters.

Overview

  • Function: Provides objective, independent analysis and advice on complex scientific, engineering, and medical issues.
  • Structure: Comprises the National Academy of Sciences, the National Academy of Engineering, and the National Academy of Medicine.
  • Output: Publishes consensus reports, studies, and workshops covering health, technology, security, and environmental policy.

Key Publications & Reviews

Aerospace Medicine & Human Health

  • Conducted reviews of NASA’s evidence reports regarding human health risks in spaceflight.
  • 2018 Letter Report: Reviewed “Risk of Adverse Health Outcomes and Decrements in Performance Due to In-Flight Medical Conditions.”
    • Focuses on the probability of adverse health outcomes during space missions.
    • Assesses potential decrements in mission performance due to in-flight medical events.
    • See detailed notes: Review of NASA’s Evidence Reports on Human Health Risks: 2017 Letter Report (2018)
