# Brain-Computer Interfaces

**Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs)**, also known as brain-machine interfaces (BMIs), are direct communication pathways between the wired circuitry of an outside device and the living tissue of the brain. They enable bidirectional data transfer, allowing for control of external devices via neural signals and potential modulation of brain activity.

## Core Components & Mechanisms
- **Signal Acquisition**: Recording neural activity via electrodes (intracortical, epidural, or non-invasive EEG/fNIRS).
- **[[concepts/signal-processing|Signal Processing]]**: Decoding [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] translate neural spikes or field potentials into digital [[concepts/commands|commands]].
- **Actuation/Feedback**: Output devices execute commands (e.g., robotic limbs, cursors) or provide sensory [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] to the user.

## Key Developments & Entities
- **[[entities/neura-pod-neuralink|Neuralink]]**: A prominent private company developing high-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] intracortical implants. Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] highlight significant shifts in surgical methodology and infrastructure [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]]. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-09-Neuralink-2026-Infrastructure-Transdural-Surgery-and-Pat|Neuralink 2026: Infrastructure, Transdural Surgery, and Patient Impact]] for detailed analysis of their 2026 progress.
- **Surgical Evolution**: Transition from traditional craniotomy to less invasive techniques such as [[concepts/transdural-surgery|Transdural Surgery]], aiming to reduce infection risk and recovery time while maintaining electrode stability.
- **Infrastructure [[concepts/scaling|Scaling]]**: Development of specialized manufacturing and surgical [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] to support broader clinical deployment and patient impact assessment.

## Applications
- **Restorative**: Restoring motor function, communication ([[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]]), and sensory perception for individuals with paralysis or neurodegenerative diseases.
- **Augmentative**: Enhancing cognitive capabilities or enabling direct control of digital environments.
- **Diagnostic**: Monitoring brain states for epilepsy [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] or coma assessment.

## Challenges & Ethics
- **Biocompatibility**: Long-term stability of implants and [[concepts/immune-system-function|immune response]] management.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Data Privacy]]**: [[concepts/security|Security]] of neural data and mental privacy concerns.
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Ensuring equitable access to high-cost medical technologies.
