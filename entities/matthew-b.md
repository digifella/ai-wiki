**Clawdbot** (also referred to as **OpenClaw**) is a customizable AI assistant framework designed for individual users who require greater control over their assistant's behavior and configuration. Unlike commercial AI services that operate with fixed parameters and standardized responses, Clawdbot prioritizes user customization, enabling individuals to define how the assistant processes information, generates responses, and adapts to specific workflows or preferences.

## Configuration and Setup

The system is built around a modular architecture that allows users to configure multiple aspects of the assistant's operation. Users can establish custom instruction sets, define behavioral parameters, and integrate the assistant with their existing information systems. This approach makes Clawdbot suitable for users who need specialized functionality or wish to maintain greater autonomy over how their AI assistant operates.

## Use Cases

Clawdbot serves users who require personalized AI assistance beyond what standard pre-configured systems offer.
