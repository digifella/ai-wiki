## Gemini Carbon, NVIDIA CEO AFFAIR, Fable 5.2, Meta Muse Ember, GPT Image 2.6, Minimax H3: AI NEWS

### Summary
The video covers recent news surrounding Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia, and his public admission of an extramarital affair. According to the speaker, this situation has raised national security concerns, leading Nvidia's board to hire an independent investigator. Despite the controversy, Huang intends to remain at the helm of Nvidia.

Moving on to AI model developments, the video highlights the release of Claude Fable 5.2, which the speaker notes outperforms competitors like Gemini 4 Carbon, albeit at a higher cost. Demonstrations of Claude Fable 5.2 show its impressive capabilities, including creating an Excel clone and a Fall Guys game clone.

The video also discusses several new open-source models:
- **LongCat-2.5**: A 1.6-trillion-parameter model with 48 billion active parameters, strong visual understanding, and creative design capabilities.
- **Ripple-65B Preview**: A 65-billion-parameter dense LLM by Recursive Intelligence, excelling in coding tasks and supporting up to a 1-million-token context length.
- **Inklings 2 Small**: A model by Thinking Machines that scores well on intelligence indices and supports fine-tuning and inference.
- **SWE-2 Flash**: Cognition's fast and efficient coding model, ideal for delegation in coding tasks.
- **MiniMax H3 Image**: A 68-gigabyte open-weights image generation model based on MiniMax, with a quantized 18GB version available.

Additionally, the video touches upon OpenAI's GPT-Image 2.6, which features faster image generation and GIF creation, and Google's Gemini Omni 1.2 Flash, a video generation model with lower costs and enhanced features. ByteDance Seedream 5.5 is also mentioned as a top-tier image generation model with high realism and multi-turn editing capabilities.
