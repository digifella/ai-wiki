# Self-Evolving AI Agent Skills Optimization

**[[concepts/automated-diagnostic-analysis|Self-Evolving AI]] [[concepts/agent-harnesses|Agent Skills]] Optimization** refers to methodologies enabling [[concepts/ai-agent]]s to autonomously refine, expand, and optimize their [[concepts/skill|skill]] sets through systematic iterative processes, rather than relying solely on static post-training or manual [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]]. This paradigm shifts from fixed capability models to dynamic, adaptive systems that improve performance based on executive strategy and [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].

## Core Mechanisms

*   **Autonomous Skill Acquisition:** Agents identify performance gaps and independently generate or refine sub-routines/tasks.
*   **Executive Strategy:** High-level planning modules direct resource allocation toward specific skill domains based on utility and complexity.
*   **[[concepts/systematic-optimization|Systematic Optimization]]:** Continuous evaluation of agent [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]] to guide iterative improvements.
*   **Text-Based [[concepts/agent-evolution|Skill Evolution]] ([[entities/skillopt|SkillOpt]]):**
    *   Utilizes a "[[concepts/skill-document|skill document]]" (human-readable [[concepts/markdown|Markdown]]) as the primary interface for skill definition and evolution.
    *   Enables [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] and training without requiring heavy cloud-based retraining infrastructure.
    *   Facilitates transparent, interpretable [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]] via text-based modifications.
