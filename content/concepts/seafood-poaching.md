# Seafood Poaching

A gentle cooking technique where delicate proteins, such as Salmon, are submerged in a liquid substrate (e.g., Cider, Wine, Stock, or Water) maintained [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] a temperature just below boiling. This method is designed to preserve moisture and structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], avoiding the [[concepts/texture|texture]] changes associated with high-heat methods like [[concepts/skillet-frying|Pan Frying]].

## Notable Techniques & Recipes
- Normandy-[[concepts/style|Style]] [[concepts/salmon-poaching|Poached Salmon in Cider]] with [[concepts/cream-sauce|Cream Sauce]]
    - Demonstrates the application of classic Normandy ingredients to Seafood.
    - Utilizes Cider as the poaching [[entities/medium|medium]].
    - Often paired with a [[concepts/cream-sauce]] for flavor density.
