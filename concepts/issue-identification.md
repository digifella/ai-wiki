# Issue Identification

Issue identification is the foundational process of recognizing, detecting, and categorizing problems, concerns, or gaps within documents, systems, or workflows. It serves as a critical precursor to problem-solving and quality assurance, enabling both human operators and automated systems to prioritize concerns systematically. The process comprises two complementary activities: detection, which identifies the existence of a problem, and classification, which organizes these findings into actionable categories for further resolution.

In the context of artificial intelligence, this process is increasingly automated through specialized agents designed to analyze complex data structures. For instance, AI co-pilot functionalities in legal document review tools utilize natural language processing to scan text for inconsistencies, missing clauses, or regulatory non-compliance. These systems detect potential issues in real-time and categorize them by severity or legal domain, allowing legal professionals to address high-priority risks efficiently.

This capability extends beyond legal contexts to broader enterprise workflows where precision and speed are paramount. By automating the initial phase of problem recognition, AI agents reduce cognitive load on human experts and minimize the risk of oversight. The structured output of issue identification provides a clear roadmap for subsequent remediation steps, ensuring that resources are allocated to the most significant gaps in documentation or system performance.
