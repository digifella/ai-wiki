# Supply Chain Resilience

**Supply chain resilience** refers to the capacity of a supply network to anticipate, prepare for, respond to, and recover from disruptions. It involves diversifying sources, increasing visibility, and leveraging technology to mitigate risks associated with geopolitical instability, resource scarcity, and logistical failures.

## Key Strategies

*   **Diversification:** Reducing dependency on single-source suppliers or geographic regions.
*   **Substitution:** Identifying alternative materials or components that can replace critical inputs.
*   **Digitalization:** Using AI and automation for real-time monitoring and predictive analytics.
*   **Inventory Buffering:** Strategic stockpiling of critical components to absorb short-term shocks.
