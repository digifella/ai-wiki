# Thinking Processes

Thinking processes encompass the cognitive and computational activities underlying [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. In [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]], these processes involve the integration of sensory information, [[concepts/memory|memory]] [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and logical [[concepts/inference|inference]] to navigate complex situations. In [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems, thinking processes are implemented through [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] and neural architectures designed to simulate or approximate human-like reasoning. The study of thinking processes bridges cognitive [[concepts/psychology|psychology]], [[concepts/neuroscience|neuroscience]], and computer [[concepts/science|science]], providing insights into how both biological and artificial minds operate.

## Reasoning and Problem-Solving

Reasoning involves the application of logical rules and inference [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] to move from known [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]] toward conclusions or solutions. Problem-solving extends these capabilities by requiring systems—human or artificial—to identify relevant information, formulate intermediate steps, and evaluate outcomes. In AI contexts, approaches ranging from symbolic [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] to deep [[concepts/learning|learning]] have been employed to model these processes, with varying strengths in handling abstract reasoning versus [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] tasks.

## World Models and Internal Representation

A significant development in AI-based thinking processes is the use of [[concepts/world-models|world models]]—internal representations of environments that enable systems to simulate possible futures and evaluate actions without direct interaction. These models support reasoning by allowing agents to mentally "test" [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] before committing to decisions. [[entities/earth|World]] models have applications in [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], [[concepts/voice-assistants|autonomous systems]], and planning tasks where understanding [[concepts/environmental-dynamics|environmental dynamics]] is critical.

## Applications and Governance

Thinking processes are increasingly central to applications in [[concepts/systemic-inflammation|social robotics]], [[concepts/aged-care|elderly care]] systems, and [[concepts/health|healthcare]] decision support. As these systems make consequential decisions affecting human welfare, frameworks for [[concepts/explainable-ai|explainable AI]] and [[concepts/data-politics|data governance]] have become essential to ensure [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] and trustworthiness. The integration of [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] with [[concepts/governance|governance]] standards reflects growing recognition that effective thinking processes in AI require both technical sophistication and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] mechanisms.
