# Preserve Details 20

Preserve Details 2.0 is an AI-powered upscaling tool in Adobe Photoshop designed to enlarge images while maintaining fine details and texture quality. Part of Photoshop's Super Resolution feature set, it uses generative AI to improve upon traditional interpolation methods, which often produce blurred results or artifacts when scaling images to larger sizes. The tool analyzes image content and intelligently reconstructs detail rather than simply duplicating or blending pixels.

The algorithm uses machine learning trained on large image datasets to predict and generate plausible high-frequency information that was not present in the original low-resolution source. This approach allows for significant enlargement without the typical loss of sharpness associated with standard resizing techniques. By reconstructing edges and textures, the tool aims to preserve the visual integrity of the subject matter, making it particularly useful for preparing images for high-resolution output or print.

The feature is often discussed in the context of tutorials by creators such as Colin Smith from PhotoshopCAFE, who highlight its role within the broader suite of creative upscaling tools available in Photoshop. While distinct from the dedicated "Super Resolution" feature found in Adobe Camera Raw and Lightroom, Preserve Details 2.0 offers similar generative capabilities directly within the Photoshop interface, providing users with flexible options for enhancing image resolution during the editing workflow.
