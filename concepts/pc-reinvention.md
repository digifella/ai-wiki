# PC Reinvention

The [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in [[concepts/personal-computer|personal computing]] driven by the integration of [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and specialized hardware architectures, moving beyond traditional CPU/GPU reliance toward sovereign, locally-capable AI endpoints.

## Key Developments

*   **[[entities/nvidia]] [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] & Strategy**:
    *   [[entities/jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] declared the arrival of "[[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]]" at Computex 2026, defining it as [[concepts/ai-assistant|intelligent software]] agents capable of autonomous, complex, productive tasks.
    *   Introduction of [[concepts/vera-rubin-infrastructure|Vera Rubin infrastructure]], designed to support the computational demands of [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]] on local endpoints.
    *   See detailed analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-06-02-NVIDIA-Computex-2026-Keynote-Agentic-AI-Vera-Rubin-Infra|NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote: Agentic AI, Vera Rubin Infrastructure, PC Reinvention]]

## Technical Implications

*   **Hardware Architecture**: Transition to hybrid architectures optimized for AI [[concepts/inference|inference]] and [[concepts/agent-collaboration|agent orchestration]], reducing latency for [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]] compared to [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud-based solutions]].
*   **Software Ecosystem**: [[concepts/emergent-behavior|Emergence]] of [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] and frameworks enabling PCs to host multiple concurrent [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] for [[concepts/productivity|productivity]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and creative workflows.
*   **Local vs. Cloud**: Increased emphasis on [[concepts/edge-deployment|edge computing]] capabilities, allowing "reinvented" PCs to function as standalone AI workstations with reduced dependency on external servers for basic agentic operations.
