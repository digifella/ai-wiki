# AHPs (Allied Health Professionals)

[[concepts/allied-health-professionals|Allied Health Professionals]] (AHPs) refer to a diverse group of health practitioners, including physiotherapists, occupational therapists, dietitians, speech pathologists, and radiographers, who provide essential diagnostic, therapeutic, and rehabilitative services. In the Australian context, AHPs are increasingly integrated into primary and secondary care systems, requiring robust [[concepts/digital-infrastructure|digital infrastructure]] to manage clinical workflows effectively.

## Key Themes & Strategic Context

- **Workforce Classification**: AHPs represent the largest professional group in the Australian [[concepts/health-system|health system]] after medical doctors and nurses, yet often face distinct administrative burdens compared to clinical counterparts.
- **Digital Integration**: There is a critical need for interoperable systems that support complex scheduling, documentation, and billing requirements specific to non-medical disciplines.
- **Automation Potential**: [[concepts/software-services-transformation|Clinical workflow automation]] offers significant opportunities to reduce administrative overhead, allowing AHPs to focus on patient-facing activities rather than data entry.
