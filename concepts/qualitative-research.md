# Qualitative Research

Qualitative research is a method of inquiry employed in many disciplines that focuses on understanding human behavior and [[concepts/experience|experience]] through detailed examination. This approach seeks to uncover deeper meanings and contexts rather than just quantifiable data.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/vulnerability|Vulnerability]]:** The willingness to show up authentically, even when there’s risk involved.
- **Shame:** A deep-seated feeling of being flawed and therefore unworthy of acceptance and belonging.
- **[[concepts/human-connection|Human Connection]]:** The fundamental need for [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] with others and the sense of [[concepts/love|love]] and belonging that comes from these connections.

### Research Highlights

[[entities/brené-brown|Brené Brown]], a self-described "researcher-storyteller," has made significant contributions to qualitative research through her work on vulnerability, shame, and wholehearted human [[concepts/connection|connection]]. Her decade-long investigations have explored the complexities of human relationships and emotional [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]].

**Key Findings:**
- People often speak about love and belonging in the context of heartbreak and exclusion.
- Vulnerability is not weakness but a form of [[concepts/courage|courage]] that fosters deeper connections.
