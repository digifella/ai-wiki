# Yitang Zhang

**Yitang [[entities/peng-zhang|Zhang]]** is a Chinese-American mathematician known for his breakthrough results in [[concepts/number-theory]], specifically regarding bounded gaps between [[concepts/prime-numbers|prime numbers]].

## Key Contributions

- **[[concepts/bounded-prime-gaps|Bounded Prime Gaps]] (2013)**: Proved that there are infinitely many pairs of primes that differ by less than 70 million. This was the first finite bound on [[concepts/prime-gaps|prime gaps]], making significant progress toward the [[concepts/twin-prime-conjecture]].
    - Prior to this result, it was unknown whether the gaps between consecutive primes were bounded by any constant.
    - The bound was subsequently reduced by other mathematicians (e.g., the Polymath Project) to much smaller values.
- **[[entities/tomasz-janowski|Academic]] Background**: Held a professorship at University of California, San Diego before experiencing professional setbacks that led him to work various jobs while continuing his research in [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]].
