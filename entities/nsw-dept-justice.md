# NSW Department of Justice

The NSW Department of Justice is a government agency responsible for coordinating justice and social policy matters across New South Wales. The department operates as a central repository for research, information, and evidence-based policy development, serving government agencies, legal practitioners, and stakeholders throughout the state's criminal justice and social welfare systems.

## Key Functions

The department functions to develop and coordinate policy across multiple areas of the justice sector, drawing on research and evidence to inform government decision-making. It provides information and research capacity to support the implementation and evaluation of justice-related programs and legislation.

## Research Areas

The department maintains research capacity focused on contemporary justice and social policy issues, including children's services, family violence, and broader criminal justice matters. This research supports the development of evidence-based approaches to policy and practice within the justice system.
