# Product Safety

Product Safety is a documentary series created by Destin Sandlin that investigates the manufacturing processes and safety protocols governing consumer goods in the United States. Produced over a four-year period, the project aims to demystify how everyday items are designed, tested, and regulated to ensure they meet established safety standards. The series serves as an educational resource for understanding the complex intersection of engineering, regulatory compliance, and industrial practice.

The production combines direct observation of manufacturing facilities with interviews from engineers, safety officials, and industry experts. This methodology allows the series to document both the formal safety protocols required by law and the practical realities encountered during product development. By examining specific case studies across various American industries, the series highlights the rigorous testing and quality control measures necessary to prevent product failures and protect consumers.

The scope of the investigation covers the entire lifecycle of a product, from initial design concepts to final regulatory approval. It explores how different sectors approach risk mitigation and the challenges involved in maintaining safety standards amidst evolving manufacturing technologies. The series provides a detailed look at the collaborative efforts between private industry and government agencies to establish and enforce safety guidelines.
