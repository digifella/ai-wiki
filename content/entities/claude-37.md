# Claude 37

[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] 37 is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] that incorporates 4-bit [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]] as a core [[concepts/training|training]] methodology. This approach enables the model to be trained [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] reduced computational and financial costs compared to [[concepts/full-precision|full-precision]] alternatives, making it relevant to discussions about efficient [[concepts/knowledge-acquisition|model development]] in the mid-2020s.

## 4-Bit Quantization in Training

4-bit [[concepts/precision-reduction|quantization]] is a technique that reduces the precision of numerical values used during model training from standard 32-bit or higher formats down to 4-bit representations. This compression significantly decreases [[concepts/memory|memory]] requirements and computational overhead. For context, earlier large [[concepts/models|models]] like [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]] (2023) required estimated training costs in the tens of millions of dollars; advances in [[concepts/quantization-techniques|quantization techniques]] have made such efficiency gains increasingly practical for subsequent model iterations.

## Development Context

Claude 37's development reflects broader industry progress in 2025-2026 toward making advanced model training more resource-efficient. By adopting 4-bit quantization, the model demonstrates the feasibility of [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] training while managing computational budgets more effectively than previous generation approaches.
