#### Description
Every week we buy spinach at the market and say we're going to eat it every day… and by the end of the week, it’s still sitting there! If you have a bag of spinach about to go bad, make what I call "Popeye’s Pasta".

This Spinach Pesto is my favorite alternative to traditional basil pesto. Basil pesto requires so much basil and it can get expensive! Spinach goes a much longer way and gives you that same vibrant, fresh flavor.

It’s not just for pasta! I use this as a creamy dip, a spread for bread, or even a sandwich spread to level up lunches. It stays perfect in the fridge for a few days, and it’s freezer-friendly, so you can always have a healthy sauce ready to go.

Ingredients:
1 lb Spaghetti 
10 oz Fresh Spinach
Handful of Fresh Basil
1 cup Parmigiano Reggiano, grated
1 Garlic clove
2 tbsp Pine nuts
Squeeze of Lemon
1/4 cup Olive Oil (add more if needed)
2-3 Ice cubes
Salt and Pepper

[[concepts/instructions|Instructions]]:
1. Bring a pot of salted water to a boil and cook spaghetti until al dente.
2. In a pitcher or blender, add spinach, basil, parmigiano, garlic, pine nuts, lemon juice, olive oil, ice, salt, and pepper.
3. Blend until smooth (use an immersion blender or regular blender).
4. Pour pesto into a large bowl.
5. Add hot pasta directly on top with a splash of pasta water.
6. Toss until creamy and fully coated.
