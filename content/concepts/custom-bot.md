# Custom Bot

A Custom Bot is a specialized [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] built within the [[concepts/document-insights|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] ecosystem that extends the default [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of the platform to perform specific tasks tailored to organizational or individual needs. These [[concepts/agents|agents]] leverage the underlying [[concepts/power|power]] of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] while being configured with [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]], data sources, and workflows to address particular [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Building Custom Bots in Microsoft 365 Copilot

[[entities/jamie|Jamie]] from [[entities/teachers-tech|Teachers Tech]] provides a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] demonstrating the practical steps for constructing a [[concepts/custom-ai-agent|custom AI agent]] within [[concepts/microsoft-applications|Microsoft 365 Copilot]]. The process typically involves defining the [[entities/agent|agent]]'s [[concepts/motivation|purpose]], configuring its knowledge sources, and establishing the specific behaviors or outputs expected from user interactions. This approach allows users without advanced programming experience to create functional [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] integrated into their Microsoft 365 environment.

Custom bots built on this platform can be designed to automate repetitive tasks, provide specialized assistance within particular domains, or serve as intelligent assistants that draw from organizational data and resources. By utilizing Microsoft 365's existing infrastructure, these bots gain access to Office [[concepts/software|applications]], cloud [[entities/storage|storage]], and enterprise data while maintaining [[concepts/security|security]] and [[concepts/governance|governance]] [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].
