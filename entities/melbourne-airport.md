# Melbourne Airport

**[[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] Airport** (MEL) is the primary international airport serving Melbourne, Victoria, Australia. As of 2026, it remains an outlier among major Australian capital cities for lacking a direct rail link, a gap that has persisted for 56 years since its opening.

## Infrastructure & Connectivity

### Rail Link Status
*   **Current State:** No direct passenger rail service connects the [[concepts/cli|terminal]] precinct to [[entities/san-francisco|the city]] or broader network.
