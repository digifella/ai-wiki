## Hermes AI Updates: Bot Mode, Local Models, and Enhanced Productivity
**Clip title:** The newest [[concepts/hermes-agent|Hermes agent]] update is unbelievable
**Author / channel:** Alex Finn
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=K6G5fp8LB6w

### Summary
The video delves into significant recent updates to the [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-source AI]] agent, [[entities/hermes-ai-agent|Hermes]], positioning it as a powerful tool for enhancing personal and professional productivity. The main topic of the video is to showcase these new features and explain how to effectively integrate them into a daily workflow, particularly emphasizing Hermes' unique capabilities compared to other AI agents like [[entities/grokbot|Grokbot]] or [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]. The presenter aims to transform users into "productivity machines" by mastering these often-hidden functionalities.

A major enhancement introduced is "Bot Mode," which enables users to create and manage multiple Hermes bots, each capable of running a different underlying AI model. The presenter highlights a common misconception of assigning human-like roles to these bots, instead advocating for dedicating each bot to a specific LLM (e.g., GPT-6, [[entities/opus-5|Opus-5]], or a local model) to leverage its particular strengths. For instance, a GPT-6 powered bot might handle complex building tasks, while a local model could manage simpler, free-of-cost tasks. This strategy allows users to switch between models seamlessly, optimizing for speed, cost, or specialized capabilities directly within the Hermes desktop application.

Further expanding its versatility, Hermes now offers "Advanced Local Model Support." This feature automatically identifies and sets up the best [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] model for a user's computer hardware, regardless of its specifications. By navigating to the settings, users can easily activate a local model, which then operates for free, providing [[concepts/democratization-of-ai|accessible AI]] intelligence for various tasks. Additionally, the integration of "Hermes HUD" (Head-Up Display) and "ChatGPT Voice Mode" revolutionizes interaction. The HUD offers an always-on, screen-aware search bar that floats over any application, allowing for real-time, in-context queries. Coupled with voice commands, users can delegate tasks, orchestrate actions across different agents, or retrieve information without interrupting their current work, making AI interaction more intuitive and efficient.

The video also covers improved "Sub-agent Functionality" and "Cron Jobs" with enhanced [[concepts/memory|memory]]. Sub-agents can now be deployed for complex, multi-threaded tasks, offering better user interfaces to track each sub-agent's progress and even change its underlying model for diverse outputs. The updated Cron Jobs retain [[concepts/memory|memory]] of past executions, preventing redundant research and ensuring continuity in scheduled tasks, significantly improving the quality and relevance of repeated information gathering. The presenter concludes by outlining his personal AI stack: ChatGPT for deep technical work, Grokbot for general knowledge and mobile access, and Hermes for network administration and personal tasks, underscoring Hermes' strength in customizability, open-source nature, and ability to manage various [[concepts/computation|computing]] environments.
