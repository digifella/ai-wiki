# Advanced Plan

The [[entities/advanced-plan|Advanced Plan]] is a [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] tier in [[entities/evernote|Evernote]]'s [[concepts/subscription-access|subscription model]], positioned between the platform's [[concepts/freemium-model|free and premium]] offerings. It provides users with enhanced functionality including advanced search capabilities, increased [[entities/storage|storage]] allocation, and collaborative features designed for users whose needs exceed basic note-taking.

## Pricing and Market Position

The Advanced Plan was introduced as part of Evernote's broader restructuring of its pricing strategy. The tier targets individual users and small teams seeking functionality beyond the free tier while potentially offering a more cost-effective alternative to the highest-tier premium subscription.

## User Reception

The introduction of the Advanced Plan prompted considerable discussion within the Evernote community, with users evaluating whether the feature set justified the pricing. Reactions varied, with some users reconsidering their subscription levels in [[concepts/light|light]] of the new tier's positioning and cost.

## Migration Considerations

Following changes to Evernote's [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]], some users explored alternatives or investigated methods to export their data from the platform. This prompted increased interest in migration tools and [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]] for transferring [[concepts/notes|notes]] and attachments to other note-taking applications.
