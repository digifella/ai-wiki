### Video: From Clasp Locker to YKK - The History and Engineering of Zippers
- **Clip title:** Why are these 3 letters on almost all of my zippers?
- **Author / channel:** Veritasium
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9szhjhO9epA

### Summary Points
- The video showcases the history and engineering behind zippers, a device often overlooked despite its ubiquity.
- It highlights how zippers efficiently join or separate fabric with ease compared to older fastening methods like laces and buttons.
- The channel explores historical advancements in zipper technology from early designs by [[entities/whitcomb-judson|Whitcomb Judson]] and Gideon Sundback to modern [[concepts/ykk|YKK]] (Yoshida Kogyo Kabushikigaisha) manufacturing techniques.
