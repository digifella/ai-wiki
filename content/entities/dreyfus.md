# Dreyfus

[[entities/dreyfus-and-dreyfus|Dreyfus and Dreyfus]] proposed an influential model concerning the [[concepts/training|training]] and development of models and [[concepts/expert-systems|expert systems]]. Their work addressed how expertise is acquired and applied within computational frameworks, contributing to foundational discussions in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and knowledge engineering.

## Reception and Critique

The Dreyfus proposal has been subject to scholarly criticism, particularly from researchers including Gobel and [[entities/chassy|Chassy]]. These critiques have highlighted perceived limitations in their framework, especially regarding the practical constraints of expert systems and their capacity to replicate human expertise through training approaches alone.

The Dreyfus framework remains a significant reference point in discussions about the theoretical boundaries of artificial intelligence and the challenges involved in translating human knowledge into machine-readable and learnable formats.
