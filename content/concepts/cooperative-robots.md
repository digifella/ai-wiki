# Cooperative Robots

**Cooperative robots** (cobots) are [[concepts/robotics|robotic systems]] engineered to operate within a shared workspace alongside humans, prioritizing intrinsic safety, intuitive interaction, and collaborative task execution. Unlike traditional industrial robots isolated by safety cages, cobots utilize force limiting, collision detection, and adaptive [[concepts/power|control]] to enable direct Human-Robot Interaction.

## Core Characteristics
- **Intrinsic Safety:** [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]] via torque/force sensors, rounded geometries, and power/force limiting per [[ISO/TS 15066]].
- **Collaboration Modes:**
  - **Safety-rated monitored stop:** Robot halts upon human entry.
  - **[[concepts/speed|Speed]] and separation monitoring:** Dynamic speed reduction based on proximity.
  - **Guided operation:** Human-led teaching via physical guidance.
  - **Power and force limiting:** Interaction forces capped [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] safe thresholds to prevent injury.

## Social & Domestic Applications
- **Elderly [[concepts/companionship|Companionship]]:** Emerging research explores the viability of robots as companions for older adults, addressing [[concepts/loneliness|loneliness]] and providing cognitive support [[lab-notes/2026-05-21-Just-a-moment|URL Ingest Summary]].
- **[[concepts/ethical-considerations|Ethical Considerations]]:** [[concepts/deployment|Deployment]] in care settings requires balancing technological [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] with ethical implications of human-robot emotional bonds.
