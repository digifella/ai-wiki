# Google Gemma

Google Gemma is a family of open-source large language models developed and released by Google in 2024. The models are distributed under the Apache 2.0 license, which permits both research and commercial use without additional licensing restrictions. Gemma represents Google's strategic contribution to the expanding ecosystem of publicly available foundation models, aiming to provide accessible tools for developers and researchers.

## Model Variants and Design

The Gemma family comprises multiple model sizes tailored to different use cases and computational constraints. Smaller variants are specifically optimized for efficient inference on resource-limited hardware, enabling deployment on edge devices and consumer-grade systems. Larger versions offer enhanced capabilities for complex tasks, providing a scalable range of options that balance performance with accessibility for various technical requirements.
