# Ukraine

## Strategic Context
Ukraine's military strategy has expanded beyond traditional kinetic engagements to include targeted economic disruption of Russian supply chains. A significant component of this approach involves the degradation of Russian [[concepts/e-commerce-logistics|e-commerce logistics]] networks, specifically targeting the [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] of Wildberries.

## The Warehouse War
Ukraine has implemented a [[concepts/long-range-strike-campaign|long-range strike campaign]] focusing on civilian e-commerce logistics hubs. This strategy aims to disrupt the economic stability and logistical capacity of [[entities/russia|Russia]] by targeting key distribution nodes.

- **Target Profile:** Major logistics centers and warehouses associated with Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries.
- **Strategic Objective:** To degrade the operational capacity of Russian domestic supply chains and impact the economic morale of the Russian populace.
- **Operational Details:** Recent strikes have focused on high-volume distribution points, creating significant bottlenecks in the delivery network.
- **Russian Response:** Russia has attempted to mitigate these losses through logistical rerouting and increased security measures, though the effectiveness remains contested.
