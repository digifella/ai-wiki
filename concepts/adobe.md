# Adobe

[[entities/adobe|Adobe]] is a software company best known for its [[entities/creative-cloud|Creative Cloud]] subscription platform, which provides professional-grade tools for design, video, photography, and [[concepts/web-development|web development]]. Rather than purchasing individual applications as standalone products, users pay a recurring monthly or annual subscription fee to access Adobe's suite of creative applications and [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]]. This [[concepts/subscription-access|subscription model]] represents a shift from Adobe's earlier practice of selling perpetual licenses.

## Core Applications

Creative Cloud includes desktop applications such as [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] ([[concepts/image-editing|image editing]]), Illustrator (vector [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]]), [[entities/premiere-pro|Premiere Pro]] ([[concepts/video-editing|video editing]]), InDesign (layout and publishing), and [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] ([[concepts/photo-management|photo management]] and editing). The platform also provides web-based tools for [[concepts/collaborative-design|collaborative design]] and [[concepts/asset-management|asset management]].
