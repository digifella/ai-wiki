# Zubin Ghahramani

**Zubin Ghahramani** is a prominent researcher in machine learning and probabilistic modeling, currently associated with [[entities/google|Google]] [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]. His work focuses on the mathematical foundations of uncertainty, which is critical for developing robust and intelligent AI systems.
