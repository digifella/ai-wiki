## Clef 27B: Multimodal AI Decision Model for Structured Input Analysis
**Clip title:** Clef 27B Locally: Multimodal Decision-Maker From Text, Images and Video
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Fahd Mirza
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LJIm1EL4X6Y

### Summary
The video introduces Cloudflare's new multimodal [[concepts/decision-model|decision model]], Clef, a 27 billion-parameter AI designed for rapid, structured [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. Unlike traditional [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]], Clef doesn't generate text; instead, it takes various inputs—text, images, videos, or JSON data—and returns [[concepts/calibrated-probabilities|calibrated probabilities]] for specific questions within a single [[concepts/inference|forward pass]], eliminating generation overhead. The presenter emphasizes Clef's unique capability to understand and process diverse data types, positioning it as a significant advancement beyond existing decision models like CLM, [[entities/laya|Laya]], [[concepts/openjev|OpenJev]], and Julia, many of which the [[entities/speaker|speaker]] has reviewed in prior videos.

The core of the video demonstrates Clef's multimodal prowess through several practical tests. First, in a text-based "social [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] trap" scenario, Clef accurately identified a high-risk request for database access and recommended escalation to a [[concepts/security|security]] team, performing on par with leading models like CLM and Jev. Next, a multimodal image and text test involving a complex workplace relationship drama showcased Clef's ability to interpret emotional dynamics and discern motivations from visual and textual cues. Furthermore, a video test of a man holding a candle in a snowy forest yielded a remarkably poetic and accurate interpretation of emotion and context, suggesting a ritualistic act and a memorial candle with a low perceived danger, and predicting the man would survive alone.

Clef's versatility is further highlighted by its performance in specialized tasks. It successfully analyzed an Uzbek news website screenshot, accurately identifying the language, the main political topic, and the neutral [[concepts/tone|tone]], demonstrating strong multilingual and web content analysis capabilities. Finally, a chemistry titration curve diagram was presented, which Clef interpreted with near-perfect accuracy, identifying the diagram type, acid type, equivalence points, and final pH, showcasing its advanced understanding of [[concepts/scientific-evidence|scientific data]].

Overall, the video positions Clef as a highly capable and versatile multimodal decision model that excels in interpreting complex, diverse inputs to deliver fast, structured decisions with high accuracy. While benchmarks show Clef dominating most categories compared to other models, the [[entities/speaker|speaker]] [[concepts/notes|notes]] that Jev still holds an edge in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] requiring nuanced human judgment, such as knowing when to escalate a situation to a human agent. The key takeaway is that Clef represents a significant leap in AI [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], capable of handling a broad spectrum of real-[[entities/earth|world]] challenges through its unique multimodal and [[concepts/structured-output|structured output]] approach.
