# Dynamic range

The ratio between the largest and smallest [[concepts/light|light]] intensities a camera or imaging system can capture without distortion. Higher dynamic range preserves detail in both bright highlights and deep [[concepts/shadows|shadows]].

- Traditional camera systems (DSLRs, mirrorless) typically outperform [[concepts/smartphone-camera-apps|smartphone camera apps]] in dynamic range, along with video capabilities and [[concepts/low-light-performance|low-light performance]]
- Smartphone camera [[concepts/apps|apps]] have surpassed [[concepts/point-and-shoot-cameras|point-and-shoot cameras]] in convenience, larger viewfinders, and on-device editing/sharing capabilities
- [[entities/colin-smith]] (founder of [[entities/photoshopcafe]]) introduced [[entities/adobe-project-indigo|Adobe Project Indigo]]—a new [[concepts/camera-app|camera app]] for advanced mobile photography—noting that while traditional cameras [[entities/excel|excel]] in dynamic range, smartphones lead in [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] and workflow
- Critical for professional photography, especially in high-[[concepts/contrast|contrast]] scenes like landscapes or portraits with mixed lighting
