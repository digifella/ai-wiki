# Portrait Retouching

Portrait retouching refers to the post-processing techniques used to enhance and improve portrait photographs in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]. These techniques focus on refining skin appearance, adjusting facial features, correcting lighting and color issues, and overall image quality while maintaining a natural appearance. Retouching is a standard practice in professional [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], fashion photography, and headshot production.

## Common Techniques

Effective portrait retouching typically involves several core methods applied selectively depending on the specific image. Skin smoothing and blemish removal are fundamental techniques that address spots, wrinkles, and uneven [[concepts/texture|texture]]. Dodge and burn [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] help refine facial contours and direct viewer [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to key features. Color correction and [[concepts/tone|tone]] adjustment ensure consistent [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] and proper [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] across the portrait. Many retouching workflows also incorporate frequency separation, a technique that separates texture from color to allow independent adjustments of skin imperfections and color variations.

## Workflow Considerations

Professional retouchers approach portraits with the principle of maintaining naturalness while improving clarity and presentation. The extent of retouching varies based on the intended use—commercial work typically requires more extensive refinement than editorial portraits. Modern Photoshop workflows often integrate supplementary tools like [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] for initial adjustments and [[entities/snapseed|Snapseed]] for mobile-based edits, allowing photographers to maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across editing platforms. Effective retouching requires careful [[concepts/attention|attention]] to detail and an understanding of skin anatomy to avoid producing obvious or artificial-looking results.
