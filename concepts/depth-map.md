# Depth map

A grayscale representation of spatial distance within an image, where pixel intensity/luminance correlates to the proximity of objects relative to the camera lens.

### Key Applications
- **Advanced Photo [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]**: Utilized in [[concepts/photoshop]] to execute precise, realistic 3D-[[concepts/style|style]] manipulations.
- **[[concepts/depth-range-masking|Depth Masking]]**: Acts as a "hidden [[concepts/depth-maps|depth mask]]" to simulate 3D effects and object [[concepts/disconnection|separation]] in 2D imagery.
- **Lens [[concepts/simulation-technology|Simulation]]**: Serves as the essential data source for driving the [[concepts/lens-blur|Lens Blur]] tool in [[concepts/camera-raw]] and [[concepts/lightroom]].
