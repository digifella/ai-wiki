# Quark Substructure

The hypothesis that quarks are not fundamental, [[concepts/point-like-particles|point-like particles]] but instead possess an internal [[concepts/writing|composition]] of even smaller constituents.

## Theoretical Framework
- Under the [[concepts/standard-model]], quarks are classified as [[concepts/elementary-particles|elementary particles]] with no known internal structure.
- Any evidence of internal complexity would necessitate the development of [[concepts/physics|physics]] beyond the [[concepts/standard-model]].

## Experimental Investigations
- High-energy particle collisions are used to probe for deviations from point-like behavior:
    - LHC [[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] Tests for Quark Substructure: Analyzes data from the [[entities/lhc-cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] at the LHC to investigate whether quarks possess any internal structure or if there are even smaller particles beneath them.
