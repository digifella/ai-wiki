# Outfit Editing

**Outfit Editing** refers to the targeted modification of clothing or accessories within [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]], typically leveraging [[concepts/generative-ai]] and [[concepts/computer-vision]] techniques to replace, alter, or enhance garments while preserving the subject's identity and background context.

## Core Techniques

*   **Semantic Segmentation**: Isolating clothing regions from the rest of the image using models like SAM ([[concepts/segment-anything-model|Segment Anything Model]]) or ControlNet.
*   **[[concepts/image-inpainting|Inpainting]]**: Filling masked regions with new content generated by [[concepts/image-and-video-diffusion-models|diffusion models]], conditioned on text prompts or reference images.
*   **Pose [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Ensuring the edited outfit conforms to the subject's body shape and pose, often using OpenPose or [[concepts/depth-maps|depth maps]].

## Workflows and Tools

*   **[[concepts/comfyui|ComfyUI]]**: A [[concepts/node-based-interface|node-based interface]] for Stable Diffusion that allows for complex, modular workflows.
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-06-26-ComfyUI-Inpainting-Workflow-SAM-Powered-Automatic-Maskin|ComfyUI Inpainting Workflow: SAM-Powered Automatic Masking and Targeted Image Editing]] for a detailed breakdown of SAM-powered [[concepts/automatic-masking|automatic masking]].
*   **Automatic [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]]**: Using AI to detect and mask specific objects (e.g., shirts, pants) without manual drawing, reducing user effort and increasing [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].
*   **Targeted Editing**: Applying changes only to specific masked areas to avoid artifacts in unaffected regions.

## Key Considerations

*   **Mask Precision**: [[concepts/excellence|High-quality]] masks are critical to prevent bleeding of new textures into the background or skin.
*   **Contextual [[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]]**: The new outfit must match the lighting, [[concepts/style|style]], and perspective of the original image.
*   **Latency**: [[concepts/complex-workflows|Complex workflows]] involving multiple models (segmentation + inpainting) can be computationally expensive.
