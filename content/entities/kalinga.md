# Kalinga

Kalinga was an ancient region of India, located in what is now Odisha and parts of Andhra Pradesh. It held significant importance in the political and cultural history of the Indian subcontinent, particularly during the [[concepts/mauryan-empire|Mauryan Empire]] period.

## Historical and Mathematical Significance

Kalinga is referenced in historical discussions of mathematical and numerical development in [[concepts/ancient-india|ancient India]]. The region contributed to the broader context of intellectual advancement in the subcontinent during antiquity, including the development of [[concepts/history-of-science-and-conceptsmathematicsmathematics|mathematical concepts]] that would later [[concepts/power|influence]] global scientific thought.

The kingdom of Kalinga was notable for its interactions with major Indian empires and its role in the dissemination of knowledge and cultural practices across the Indian subcontinent during the classical period.
