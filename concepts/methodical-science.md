# Methodical Science

Methodical Science is a tutorial authored by David that details the post-processing workflow for concert photography using Adobe Lightroom Classic. The guide addresses the specific technical challenges inherent in live music environments, where stage lighting creates extreme and rapidly shifting color casts across images. These conditions present significant obstacles for maintaining consistent color balance and accurate white balance in the final output.

The tutorial focuses on techniques for managing intense colored lighting that changes frequently during performances. It provides a structured approach to correcting these dynamic lighting conditions, aiming to restore natural skin tones and accurate environmental colors despite the artificial stage illumination. The content is designed to help photographers overcome the difficulties of white balance consistency in high-contrast, multi-colored light scenarios.
