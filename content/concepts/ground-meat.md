# Ground Meat

Ground meat is a versatile ingredient in many cuisines, used for dishes like burgers, meatballs, and sauces. It's made by grinding various cuts of meat into smaller pieces.

## Enhancing Ground Meat with Baking Soda

- **Improves [[concepts/texture|Texture]]:** Adds tenderness without overprocessing.
- **Enhances Juiciness:** Helps retain moisture during cooking.
- **Aids Browning:** Improves color and flavor development.
