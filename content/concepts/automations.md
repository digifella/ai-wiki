# Automations

2026 04 14 Antigravity AiStudio integration world of AI

Automations refer to the use of technology to perform tasks with minimal human intervention. This can [[concepts/range|range]] from simple scripting to complex [[concepts/agentic-development]] environments.

## Key Aspects

- **Efficiency:** Automations streamline repetitive tasks, freeing up human time for more complex work.
- **Scalability:** Automated systems can handle larger workloads without proportional increases in resources.
- **[[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]]:** Automations reduce human error by performing tasks in a standardized manner.

## Related Concepts

- [[concepts/agentic-development]]: A paradigm shift in [[concepts/software|software]] development where intelligent [[concepts/agents|agents]] handle [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] autonomously.
- [[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]]: A platform for building and deploying [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], recently upgraded with Antigravity.
