# Cosmic Event

A **cosmic event** refers to a significant occurrence within the Universe, often involving [[concepts/gravitational-effects|celestial mechanics]], high-energy phenomena, or structural changes in Space-time. These events range from local interactions within a Solar System to galactic-scale transformations such as [[concepts/supernovae|Supernovae]] or Gamma-ray Bursts.

## Key Characteristics
- **Scale**: Can span from planetary impact events to intergalactic collisions.
- **Duration**: Ranges from transient flashes (milliseconds) to epochal shifts (millennia).
- **Impact**: Often drives [[concepts/stellar-evolution]], Planetary Formation, or biological Extinction Events.

## Recent Observations & Data
- **Interstellar [[entities/medium|Medium]] Interaction**:
  - Evidence suggests the Solar System is currently traversing the Local Interstellar Cloud.
  - Specific isotopic signatures found in Antarctic Ice cores provide geochemical confirmation of this transit.
