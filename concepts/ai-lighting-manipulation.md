# AI lighting manipulation

**AI [[concepts/lighting-manipulation|lighting manipulation]]** refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to generate, adjust, or simulate lighting conditions within [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]] or 3D scenes. This technique allows for [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]], enabling users to alter the direction, intensity, color, and quality of [[concepts/light|light]] after the initial capture or creation of an asset.

## Core Concepts

- **[[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-destructive editing]]**: Modern [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] allow lighting changes without permanently altering the original pixel data, preserving flexibility for future [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
- **[[concepts/light-source-generation|Light source generation]]**: AI can synthesize new light sources that interact realistically with existing geometry and materials, simulating complex global illumination effects.
- **Material response**: The technology calculates how different materials (e.g., skin, [[concepts/metal|metal]], fabric) reflect or absorb the newly generated light, ensuring physical plausibility.

## Recent Developments

### Photoshop Beta: Relight Layer

[[concepts/adobe|Adobe]] has introduced the **[[concepts/relight-layer|Relight Layer]]** feature in [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]], designed to replace the legacy "Lighting Effects" tool. This feature leverages AI to provide precise control over lighting on individual layers.

- **Functionality**: Allows users to generate new light sources and manipulate their position, intensity, and color non-destructively.
- **Target Audience**: Replaces older, removed tools with a more intuitive, AI-driven workflow for [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] and [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]].
- **Availability**: Currently available in the [[entities/photoshop-beta]] channel.
- **Documentation**: For detailed feature overview and demonstration, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-Photoshop-Beta-Relight-Layer-AI-Lighting-Manipulation-Fe|Photoshop Beta: Relight Layer AI Lighting Manipulation Feature Overview]].
