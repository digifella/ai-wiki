# VPS deployment

The process of installing, configuring, and managing software services on a Virtual Private Server to maintain control over data, [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]].

### Key Use Cases
- **[[concepts/self-hosted-ai|Private AI Infrastructure]]**:
    - [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]] ([[concepts/remote-chat|Clawdbot]]): A personal [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] deployed on private, self-managed [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].
    - **Capabilities**: Enables real-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]] through integrations with [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], [[entities/asana|Asana]], [[entities/slack|Slack]], and [[entities/telegram|Telegram]].
    - **Reference**: 2026 04 14 [[concepts/telegram-integration|Clawbot]] Matthew Berman channel (via [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]]).

### AI Data Security Challenges
As organizations adopt [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]], traditional [[concepts/risk-mitigation|security measures]] often lag behind, leading to [[concepts/data-disclosure|sensitive data exposure]]. Key risks include:
- **Escalating Exposure**: Rapid AI [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] outpaces security protocols, increasing the risk of [[concepts/data-privacy|data privacy]] breaches.
- **Invisible Risks**: Organizations may face [[concepts/ai-data-security|AI Data Security Challenges and Exposure Risks for Organizations]] that are difficult to detect without specialized monitoring.
- **Mitigation**: Maintaining control via [[concepts/vps-deployment|VPS deployment]] allows for stricter [[concepts/data-management|data governance]] and reduced reliance on third-party cloud [[concepts/ai-platforms|AI services]].

For detailed analysis of these exposure risks, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-AI-Data-Security-Challenges-and-Exposure-Risks-for-Organ|AI Data Security Challenges and Exposure Risks for Organizations]].
