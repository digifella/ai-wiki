# Resilience

Resilience is the capacity to recover quickly from difficulties; toughness. It involves adaptability, emotional [[concepts/regulation|regulation]], and the ability to bounce back from adversity.

## Key Points:
- [[concepts/mental-toughness|Mental toughness]] can be enhanced through cognitive and behavioral strategies.
- Brain-derived neurotrophic factor ([[concepts/bdnf|BDNF]]) plays a critical role in resilience, particularly in the context of [[concepts/chronic-pain|chronic pain]] management.
- Strategies for building [[concepts/mental-toughness|mental toughness]] include [[concepts/mindfulness|mindfulness]], positive reframing, and goal-setting.

### New Information from Joe Risser's TEDx Talk
- Demonstrated the internal struggle of performing a 60-second plank [[concepts/exercise|exercise]].
- Introduced [[entities/george-hood|George Hood]]’s story, highlighting remarkable resilience in overcoming physical challenges.
- Emphasized the [[concepts/connection|connection]] between mental toughness and BDNF for [[concepts/chronic-pain|chronic pain]] management.

### Summary from TEDx Talk
Dr. [[entities/joe-risser|Joe Risser]]'s TEDx talk, "The Secrets and Science of [[concepts/mental-toughness|Mental Toughness]]," begins with a compelling demonstration: a 60-second plank, highlighting the internal struggle and desire to quit that accompanies such a physically demanding exercise. He then immediately introduces the remarkable story of [[entities/george-hood|George Hood]], who held a plank for an extended period despite physical challenges.
