# Paul Cooper

Dr. Paul Cooper is the Director of Longboardfella Consulting Pty Ltd, an Australian-based firm specializing in consulting and health information services. He holds a Ph.D. and has established a professional career spanning the consulting and health information sectors.

Cooper maintains several professional credentials and memberships that reflect his expertise and standing in the industry. These include Fellowship of the Australian Institute of Directors and Health (FAIDH) and Chartered Health Information and Analytics Professional (CHIA) certification.
