# Medium

Medium is an online publishing platform established to democratize digital publishing by allowing writers to share long-form content directly with readers. It serves as a hub for articles and essays covering diverse subjects such as technology, culture, business, and science. The platform caters to both independent authors and established publications, aiming to remove traditional barriers to distribution and reduce reliance on media gatekeeping.

The site primarily focuses on hosting long-form articles and essays rather than short-form updates or social media interactions. By providing a clean reading and writing interface, Medium enables authors to reach audiences without requiring intermediaries. This structure supports a model where content creators can engage directly with their readership, fostering a community centered around substantive written work.
