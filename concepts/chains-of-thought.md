# Chains of Thought

**Chains of Thought** (CoT) refers to the technique of breaking down [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks into intermediate steps to improve AI [[concepts/model-performance|model performance]]. While traditionally associated with "System 2" deliberate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], recent developments highlight a shift toward efficient, direct classification for specific domains.

## Evolution in Software Automation

The paradigm of [[concepts/usamo-2025|AI reasoning]] is expanding beyond pure "reasoning" capabilities to address practical [[concepts/software-automation|software automation]] needs.

- **[[concepts/system-1-classification|System 1 Classification]]**: [[lab-notes/2026-09-19-Jev-TypeSafe-AIs-System-1-Classification-for-Software-Au|Jev: TypeSafe AI's System 1 Classification for Software Automation]] introduces a model optimized for direct classification rather than extended reasoning traces.
- **[[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] with Frontier [[entities/labs|Labs]]**: While labs like [[entities/openai]], [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]], and others focus on enhancing "reasoning" capabilities, [[entities/typesafe-ai|TypeSafe AI]] emphasizes practical automation through [[entities/jev]].
- **Efficiency**: This approach prioritizes [[concepts/speed|speed]] and accuracy in software contexts over the computational cost of [[concepts/deep-reasoning|deep reasoning]] chains.
