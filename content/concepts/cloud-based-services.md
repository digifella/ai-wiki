## Cloud-Based Services
Cloud-based services refer to on-demand delivery of computing resources over the internet for [[concepts/software|applications]] and services. These include but are not limited to [[entities/storage|storage]], databases, software, analytics, and networking.

### Key Concepts:
- **Scalability:** Easily scale up or down based on demand.
- **[[concepts/cost-optimization|Cost Efficiency]]:** Pay as you go; no upfront costs.
- **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]:** High availability with built-in redundancy.
- **[[concepts/security|Security]]:** Managed security services provided by cloud providers.
- **[[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** Easy integration with various tools and platforms.
