# Sakana Ai

Sakana AI is an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research company focused on developing [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]] that simulate the survival and coexistence of multiple AI species. The company's research explores how different [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] interact, compete, and cooperate within constrained [[concepts/virtual-environments|virtual environments]].

## Research Focus

The company's core work involves creating simulations where AI entities operate under resource constraints and environmental pressures similar to biological ecosystems. These digital environments are designed to study emergent behaviors, evolutionary dynamics, and the conditions under which different AI systems can coexist sustainably. The research aims to understand fundamental principles about [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] interaction that could inform the [[concepts/design|design]] of more robust and cooperative AI systems.
