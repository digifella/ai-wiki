# Interview Preparation Concepts

## Overview
Interview [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] encompasses strategies and techniques used by candidates to enhance their performance during job interviews. With the rise of [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] (GenAI), traditional interview methods face new challenges and opportunities.

## Key Points
- Effective preparation includes understanding the role, company culture, and common interview questions.
- Use of GenAI tools for resume building and answer crafting is becoming increasingly prevalent among candidates.

### Integrating GenAI in Interview Preparation

- Candidates may use AI to generate tailored resumes and practice interview [[concepts/responses|responses]].
- Hiring managers must adapt their evaluation methods to assess genuine candidate [[concepts/skills|skills]] over polished AI-generated answers.
