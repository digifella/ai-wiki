# Quality Assurance

Quality assurance (QA) in the context of software and documentation ensures that products meet specific standards for quality. In [[concepts/legal-document-review|legal document review]] and editing, QA involves meticulous checks to ensure [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with regulatory requirements, accuracy, and completeness.

### Key Concepts
- **Automated [[concepts/redlining|Redlining]]:** The process of using AI to highlight changes made in a document compared to previous versions.
- **Critical [[concepts/issue-identification|Issue Identification]]:** AI-driven analysis that [[concepts/flags|flags]] potential issues such as missing clauses or inconsistencies in legal documents.
- **Quality Assurance Tasks:** Systematic checks performed to ensure that the final product meets specified requirements.
