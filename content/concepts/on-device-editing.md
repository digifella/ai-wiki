# On Device Editing

On Device Editing refers to the capability to perform [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] and [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] directly within a camera application on a mobile device, rather than requiring transfer to a desktop or separate editing [[concepts/software|software]]. This approach streamlines the [[concepts/photography-workflow|photography workflow]] by enabling photographers to refine [[concepts/images|images]] immediately after capture, using the same device where the photo was taken.

## Adobe Project Indigo

[[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]], founder of [[entities/photoshopcafe|PhotoshopCAFE]], introduced [[concepts/camera-app-design|Adobe Project Indigo]] as a camera application designed to advance [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. The app integrates on-device editing features that allow photographers to apply sophisticated adjustments and enhancements within the camera interface itself, bridging the gap between capture and post-processing workflows on smartphones.

The significance of on-device editing lies in its efficiency and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. By consolidating camera functions with editing tools, mobile photography becomes more immediate and intuitive, reducing [[concepts/friction|friction]] in the creative process and making advanced editing techniques available to users who may not have desktop editing experience or infrastructure.
