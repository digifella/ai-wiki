# Muscle Invasion

**Muscle invasion** refers to the pathological process where malignant cells penetrate the basement membrane and infiltrate surrounding muscular tissue, often serving as a critical prognostic indicator in cancer staging and progression.

## Key Concepts
- **Pathological Mechanism**: Involves the degradation of the extracellular matrix and subsequent infiltration of muscle fibers by tumor cells.
- **Clinical Significance**: Often associated with advanced disease stages and higher risk of metastasis.
- **Diagnostic Methods**: Typically identified via biopsy, MRI, or histological examination.
