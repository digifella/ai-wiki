# Spacecraft Systems

Spacecraft systems encompass the integrated technical and operational frameworks that enable crewed and uncrewed [[concepts/space-exploration|space missions]]. These systems include structural components, propulsion, life support, avionics, and mission management protocols. Understanding spacecraft systems requires examination of real-[[entities/earth|world]] operational data, mission simulations, and hardware development across multiple [[concepts/software|programs]].

## Artemis Program Operations

The [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]] has provided substantial operational data through simulated missions and crewed flight tests. [[entities/artemis-ii|Artemis II]] involved simulated return procedures and [[concepts/crew-operations|crew operations]] within the [[entities/orion|Orion spacecraft]], generating documentation on systems performance during critical mission phases. Subsequent [[concepts/lunar-exploration|Artemis missions]], including the planned [[concepts/spaceflight-operations|lunar flyby operations]], continue to build operational knowledge of [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep space]] crewed flight. The program has identified specific in-flight challenges and system requirements that inform ongoing development of both Orion and supporting ground infrastructure.

## Orion Spacecraft

The [[concepts/orion-systems|Orion spacecraft]] serves as the primary crew vehicle for Artemis missions. Its systems include life support, thermal management, power generation, and crew interface components. Operational data from Artemis missions has documented the performance of these systems during launch, deep space operations, and return phases. Ongoing development addresses identified challenges and prepares Orion for extended [[concepts/lunar-return|lunar missions]] planned under Artemis 3, which [[entities/will|will]] require additional lunar surface support systems.

## Related Development Programs

Concurrent development of supporting systems, including the Human Lunar Lander and associated infrastructure, continues alongside crewed spacecraft advancement.
