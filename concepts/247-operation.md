# 24/7 Operation

System or service availability without interruption, operating continuously 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

**Characteristics:**
- [[concepts/zero|Zero]] downtime through redundant infrastructure
- [[concepts/autonomous-operation|Autonomous operation]] without human intervention
- Critical for mission-critical services and persistent [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]

**Examples:**
- [[entities/openclaw]]: [[concepts/local-ai-assistant|Local AI assistant]] (formerly ClawdBot/MoltBot) running 24/7 on [[entities/raspberry-pi|Raspberry Pi]], described as a "blank [[entities/canvas|canvas]]" with autonomous capabilities, similar to fictional [[entities/jarvis|Jarvis]] (Iron Man)
- Raspberry Pi-based automation systems requiring persistent [[concepts/background-processes|background processes]]
- [[concepts/cloud-based-services|Cloud infrastructure]] with 99.999% [[concepts/uptime|uptime]] guarantees
