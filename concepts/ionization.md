# Ionization

Ionization is the process by which an atom or a molecule acquires a positive or negative charge through gaining or losing one or more electrons. In the context of [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]], [[concepts/high-energy-particles|high-energy particles]] such as [[concepts/protons|protons]] and other atomic nuclei enter [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]] and collide with air molecules, leading to a cascade of ionizing events.

### Summary
- Ionization is triggered when energetic particles from space interact with the [[entities/earth|Earth]]’s [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]].
- The process leads to the formation of charged particles that can damage electronics and satellites in orbit around the [[entities/earth|Earth]].

The video introduces the often-overlooked reality of cosmic [[concepts/rays|rays]], high-energy particles that constantly bombard Earth from outer space. Upon entering Earth’s atmosphere, they trigger a [[concepts/domino-effect|chain reaction]] of ionization, which can damage satellites, disrupt communications, and even affect terrestrial systems.
