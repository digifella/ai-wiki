# Conversation Flow

Conversation Flow is a structured prompt template designed to optimize interactions between users and AI systems within business advisory contexts. It functions as a metacognitive framework that guides AI assistants to clarify and refine user inputs before generating substantive responses. By introducing a mandatory clarification step, the template addresses the common limitation of ambiguous or incomplete requests, which often result in outputs misaligned with user intentions or actual information needs.

The process operates by directing the AI to analyze the initial query for missing context, implicit assumptions, or conflicting constraints. Instead of immediately attempting to answer, the system identifies potential gaps in the provided data and formulates targeted questions to resolve them. This iterative refinement ensures that the subsequent advice is grounded in accurate and complete information, thereby reducing the risk of hallucination or irrelevant suggestions.

This approach is particularly valuable in complex domains such as earth-systems geology and climate, where precise terminology and specific boundary conditions are critical. By enforcing a preliminary validation phase, Conversation Flow helps align the AI’s output with the specific technical requirements of the user’s scenario. The result is a more reliable advisory interaction that prioritizes accuracy and contextual relevance over speed.
