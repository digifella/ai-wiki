# Local AI Cluster Concept Overview
A [[concepts/offline-ai|local AI]] cluster refers to a network of interconnected computing resources dedicated to processing and [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] locally within an [[concepts/organization|organization]] or personal environment. It contrasts with [[concepts/cloud-based-ai-services|cloud-based AI services]] by offering greater control over [[concepts/ai-security|data privacy]], reduced latency, and potential cost savings for intensive workloads.
