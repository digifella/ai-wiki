# Electric Reliability
Electric [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] refers to the ability of a [[concepts/electricity-grid|power grid]] to provide electricity consistently without interruptions. Key factors affecting this include maintenance schedules, reserve capacity, and emergency response plans.

## Notable Events Impacting Grid Reliability
- **2021 [[entities/texas|Texas]] [[concepts/power-grid-outage|Power Grid Outage]]**: A major [[concepts/winter-storm|winter storm]] led to an unprecedented power outage across Texas in February 2021.
    - 2021 Texas Power Grid Outage Technical Analysis and [[entities/ercot|ERCOT]] Actions
    - Caused by record low temperatures, leading to widespread [[concepts/power-outages|blackouts]] and significant loss of life.
