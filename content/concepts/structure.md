# Structure

Structure refers to the foundational [[concepts/organization|organization]] and [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] of [[concepts/design|design]] systems, platforms, and tools. In the context of modern [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] and development, structure encompasses both the technical frameworks that enable these tools to function and the organizational principles that guide how designers and developers interact with them.

## AI Design Tools and Platforms

Contemporary [[concepts/ai-design-agents|AI design agents]] like SuperDesign provide structured approaches to automating [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]], bridging the gap between [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]] and design interfaces. Similarly, platforms such as [[concepts/automated-front-end-component-generation|Memories.ai]] establish structured frameworks for processing and understanding video content [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] scale, allowing users to extract meaning and organize visual information systematically.

## Integration and Open Standards

The effectiveness of these tools depends on clear structural foundations—whether through [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] design principles, standardized plugin architectures, or consistent APIs that allow [[concepts/integration|integration]] across different development environments. This structural approach ensures that [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] can be embedded seamlessly into existing workflows rather than requiring entirely separate [[concepts/software|applications]].
