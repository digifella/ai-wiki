# Speaker Detection

Speaker Detection is a capability that identifies and distinguishes between different speakers in audio or video recordings. This feature is particularly useful for transcription tasks where understanding who is speaking at any given moment enhances the utility and clarity of the [[concepts/text-transcript|transcript]]. By automatically labeling speech segments with [[entities/speaker|speaker]] identifiers, speaker detection enables more organized and readable transcripts, especially for multi-speaker content such as interviews, meetings, and conversations.

## WhisperX Implementation

WhisperX is a tool built on top of [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[entities/whisper-ai|Whisper]] that extends its transcription capabilities with speaker detection functionality. While Whisper provides accurate [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|speech-to-text]] conversion, WhisperX adds the ability to attribute transcribed segments to specific speakers, creating more [[concepts/structured-output|structured output]]. This enhancement makes WhisperX particularly valuable for users who need to process audio and video files with multiple participants and require speaker attribution in their transcripts.
