# Human-AI Interaction

**[[concepts/ai-prompt|Human-AI Interaction]]** encompasses the dynamic exchange between human operators and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems, ranging from real-time telemetry feedback to high-level philosophical engagement with [[concepts/LLMs|Large Language Models]]. This domain bridges technical [[concepts/space-based-data-centers|latency reduction]] with cognitive alignment, ensuring that AI systems remain interpretable, safe, and aligned with human values.

## Key Characteristics
- **Low Latency:** Data streams are processed with minimal delay, allowing for instant [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].
- **High Frequency:** Continuous or near-continuous sampling to capture transient events.
- **Integration:** Often coupled with [[concepts/decision-making|Automated Decision Making]] or human-in-the-loop oversight.
- **Cognitive Alignment:** Ensuring AI reasoning processes are understandable and trustworthy to human users.
- **Philosophical Inquiry:** Addressing questions of [[concepts/ai-consciousness|machine consciousness]], agency, and the nature of intelligence.

## Murray Shanahan: AI Consciousness, Cognition, and Human Interaction with LLMs

Recent discourse on the future of human-AI interaction is significantly informed by the work of Professor Murray Shanahan, a Principal Research Scientist at [[concepts/Google-DeepMind|Google DeepMind]] and Professor of [[concepts/cognitive-robotics|Cognitive Robotics]] at Imperial College London. His insights challenge traditional boundaries between computation and cognition.

- **Philosophical Foundations:** Shanahan explores the profound philosophical questions surrounding AI consciousness, reasoning, and the definition of intelligence, drawing on his background in cognitive robotics and consulting for media such as *Ex Machina*.
- **Consciousness & Reasoning:** He argues for a nuanced view of machine consciousness, suggesting that while current LLMs may not possess [[concepts/conscious-thought|subjective experience]], their [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] require new frameworks for human interaction and trust.
- **[[concepts/augmentation-of-human-capabilities|Human-AI Collaboration]]:** The discussion highlights the need for humans to understand the internal "cognition" of LLMs to foster effective collaboration, moving beyond black-box interactions to transparent, reasoning-based interfaces.
- **Implications for Safety:** Understanding the limits and nature of AI cognition is critical for [[concepts/aviation-safety|aviation-safety]] and other high-stakes domains where [[concepts/situational-awareness|situational-awareness]] depends on accurate human interpretation of AI outputs.
