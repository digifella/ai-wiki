# OWASP

[[concepts/owasp|OWASP]] (Open Worldwide Application [[concepts/security|Security]] Project) is a nonprofit [[concepts/organization|organization]] dedicated to improving [[concepts/software-cybersecurity|software security]] through education, tools, and [[concepts/community-collaboration|community collaboration]]. The organization develops and maintains security guidance, [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], and [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]] for [[concepts/software|software]] developers, security professionals, and organizations.

## AI Security Guidance

In response to the growing [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]], OWASP has published the Top 10 Security Risks for [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|AI Agentic Applications]]. This framework identifies and documents the most critical security vulnerabilities and risks specific to AI-powered [[concepts/agents|agents]] that operate with varying degrees of autonomy. The guidance provides organizations with a structured approach to understanding and mitigating security threats in [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] deployments.

OWASP's work on [[concepts/ai-security|AI security]] reflects the organization's broader mission to address emerging threats in modern [[concepts/coding|software development]]. By extending its established frameworks to cover [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], OWASP helps developers and security teams anticipate and prevent security issues before they can be exploited in production environments.
