# AlphaGenome Atlas

**AlphaGenome [[concepts/atlas-computer|Atlas]]** is a groundbreaking platform developed by [[entities/google-deepmind]] designed to demystify the [[concepts/human-genome|human genome]], often referred to as "the language of life." It leverages advanced AI to predict the impact of human genome variants, addressing the central challenge of interpreting [[concepts/genomic-data|genomic data]].

- **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]:** To make the complex "language of life" accessible and interpretable for researchers
- **Technology:** Utilizes [[entities/gemini-25-flash]] for processing and analysis
