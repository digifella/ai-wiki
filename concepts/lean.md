# Lean

Lean is a project management and business methodology focused on maximizing customer value while minimizing waste. The approach originated from lean manufacturing principles developed at Toyota in the automotive industry during the mid-20th century, where it became foundational to the company's production system. Since then, Lean has been adapted across multiple sectors including software development, healthcare, and general organizational management.

## Core Principles

The methodology is built upon five core principles: identifying value from the customer's perspective, mapping the value stream to identify all steps in the process, creating flow to ensure work moves smoothly without interruption, establishing pull systems where production is driven by demand, and pursuing perfection through continuous improvement. These principles guide organizations in eliminating non-value-added activities and optimizing resource utilization.

## Evolution and Application

Originally designed for manufacturing, Lean has evolved into a broader strategic framework applicable to various industries. In software development, it often intersects with Agile methodologies to enhance delivery speed and quality. Healthcare organizations utilize Lean to improve patient outcomes and reduce operational costs, while service industries apply its concepts to streamline processes and enhance customer satisfaction. The adaptability of Lean allows it to address inefficiencies in diverse operational contexts.
