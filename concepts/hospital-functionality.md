# Hospital Functionality

The functionality of a hospital encompasses all operations that ensure [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]], administrative tasks, and facility maintenance are carried out efficiently during normal operations as well as emergency [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] like [[concepts/earthquakes|earthquakes]].

### Key Components:

- **Patient Care:** Ensures the provision of medical treatment in various departments such as ER, ICU, surgery.
- **Administrative Tasks:** Management of hospital records, billing systems, [[entities/employees|staff]] scheduling.
- **Facility Maintenance:** Upkeep of equipment and building structures to maintain operational readiness.

### Emergency Preparedness:
Hospitals must be equipped with [[concepts/emergency-protocols|emergency protocols]] that include:

- **Disaster Response Plans:** Protocols for managing influxes of patients in case of [[concepts/severe-weather-events|natural disasters]] or other emergencies.
- **Communication Systems:** Reliable communication channels between different departments and external [[concepts/nodes|entities]] such as EMS, police, and fire services.

### Integration of Earthquake Base Isolation Systems

Earthquake [[concepts/base-isolation-systems|base isolation systems]] are critical to ensuring hospital functionality during [[concepts/earthquakes|seismic events]]. Key points from the video "How [[concepts/earthquake-base-isolation|Earthquake Bearings]] Work" by Practical [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]:

- **Reduction in Seismic Impact:** By decoupling a building's foundation from its superstructure, these systems significantly reduce the force transmitted during an earthquake.
- **[[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis|Cost-Benefit Analysis]]:** Despite high initial costs, the long-term benefits of reduced damage and operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] make base [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] economically viable for critical infrastructure like hospitals.
