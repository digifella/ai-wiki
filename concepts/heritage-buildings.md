# Heritage Buildings

**[[concepts/cultural-heritage|Heritage]] buildings** are structures recognized for their historical, architectural, or cultural significance, warranting [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] for future generations. This concept encompasses not only monumental landmarks but also "everyday structures" that contribute to the collective [[concepts/memory|memory]] and identity of a place.

## Key Characteristics
*   **[[concepts/historical-significance|Historical Significance]]:** Association with important events, figures, or periods.
*   **Architectural Value:** Distinctive design, craftsmanship, or construction techniques.
*   **Cultural Identity:** Role in shaping the social fabric and visual character of a community.
*   **[[concepts/adaptive-reuse|Adaptive Reuse]]:** Potential for continued use while retaining heritage fabric.
