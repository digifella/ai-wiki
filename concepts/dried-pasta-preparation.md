# Dried pasta preparation

The process of hydrating and cooking dehydrated dough to achieve specific culinary objectives regarding mouthfeel, coating, and taste.

## Core Objectives
- [[concepts/texture|Texture]]: Achieving desired firmness (e.g., *[[concepts/al-dente-texture|al dente]]*).
- [[concepts/sauce-adhesion|Sauce Adhesion]]: Ensuring the sauce effectively clings to the pasta surface.
- Flavor Integration: Maximizing the synergy between the pasta starch and the sauce.

## Optimization Strategies
- Tailor specific [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]] to the requirements of individual pasta shapes and sauce types.
- Challenge traditional cooking "rules" to achieve superior results in [[concepts/texture-slider|Texture]], [[concepts/sauce-adhesion|Sauce Adhesion]], and [[concepts/flavor-integration|Flavor Integration]].
