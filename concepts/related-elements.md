# Related elements

**Related elements** refers to the intelligent [[concepts/ai-agent-context|contextual awareness]] feature within [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s **Photoshop Remove Tool**. This capability allows the tool to identify and remove not just the primary selected object, but also its associated visual artifacts, such as [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], reflections, and other dependent components, ensuring a seamless background reconstruction.

## Key Features
- **Intelligent Contextual Removal**: Automatically detects and removes associated shadows and reflections alongside the target object.
- **[[concepts/remove-tool|Project Trace Erase]] Integration**: Functionality mirrors the "Project Trace Erase" technology previously showcased at Adobe Max, now integrated into the standard Remove Tool workflow.
- **Enhanced Background Reconstruction**: Utilizes AI to fill gaps left by removed elements and their related artifacts more naturally than traditional content-aware fill methods.
