# Observable Universe

The observable universe is a spherical volume centered on the observer, comprising all matter that can be observed from [[entities/earth|Earth]] at present time, due to the finite [[concepts/speed|speed]] of [[concepts/light|light]] and the [[concepts/universe-age|age of the universe]]. The current size of this sphere is approximately 93 billion light-years in diameter.

### Key Characteristics & Expansion Paradox
- **Size vs. Age Discrepancy**: Although the universe is approximately 13.7 billion years old, the observable universe is ~92–93 billion light-years wide. This is resolved by [[concepts/metric-expansion|metric expansion]]: space itself has expanded during the time light has been traveling, carrying distant objects further away than the distance light could travel in a static universe.
