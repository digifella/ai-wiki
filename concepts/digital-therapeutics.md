# Digital Therapeutics

**Digital Therapeutics (DTx)** refer to evidence-based therapeutic interventions driven by [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] software [[concepts/software|programs]] to prevent, manage, or treat a medical disorder or disease. They are distinct from general [[concepts/wellness|wellness]] [[concepts/apps|apps]] by requiring rigorous clinical validation and often falling under regulatory oversight as medical devices.

## Key Dimensions

*   **Clinical Application**: Focuses on the delivery of therapeutic interventions via software to achieve clinically significant outcomes [[concepts/digital-health]].
*   **Workforce Development**: Emphasizes the need for specialized training for [[concepts/health|healthcare]] professionals to integrate DTx into standard care pathways Digital Health Therapeutic [[concepts/innovation|Innovation]].
*   **Safety & Education**: Prioritizes patient safety protocols and continuous education for both providers and patients to ensure effective [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] [[concepts/ai-tool-adoption|Digital Health Therapeutic Innovation]].
