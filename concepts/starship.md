# Starship

A starship is a spacecraft designed for extended missions beyond [[entities/earth|Earth]] orbit, capable of supporting human crews or advanced payloads across interplanetary or [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep-space]] distances. Unlike conventional spacecraft optimized for short-duration missions or [[concepts/low-earth-orbit-leo|low Earth orbit]] operations, starships require integrated systems for long-term life support, radiation [[concepts/secure|protection]], efficient propulsion, and [[concepts/autonomous-operation|autonomous operation]] over months or years of transit. The [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] demands scale substantially with mission duration and distance, requiring robust power systems, thermal management, and redundant [[concepts/ai-safety|safety mechanisms]].

## Key Design Considerations

[[concepts/starship-development|Starship development]] involves several critical engineering challenges. Life support systems must recycle air and water reliably over extended periods, while [[concepts/subsurface-habitats|radiation shielding]] protects crews from [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] and solar radiation beyond [[concepts/earths-magnetic-field|Earth's magnetic field]]. Propulsion systems must balance fuel efficiency with the ability to achieve necessary velocities for interplanetary travel. Navigation and communication systems operate at vast distances where signal delays prevent real-time ground control.
