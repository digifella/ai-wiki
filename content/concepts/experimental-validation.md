# Experimental Validation

Experimental validation is the process of confirming or refuting a scientific hypothesis through observation and experimentation. This crucial step in the [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific method]] helps to determine whether theoretical predictions align with [[concepts/empirical-evidence|empirical evidence]].
