# Cassini Mission

The [[entities/cassini|Cassini]]–Huygens mission was a flagship-class space research mission to study the Saturn system. Launched in 1997, it arrived in 2004 and operated until 2017. The mission comprised the Cassini orbiter and the Huygens probe, which successfully landed on [[entities/titan]].

## Key Discoveries & Observations

*   **Saturn's [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Atmosphere]]:** Extensive study of [[concepts/atmospheric-dynamics|atmospheric dynamics]], including [[entities/storm|storm]] systems and jet streams.
*   **[[concepts/saturns-hexagonal-north-pole|Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole]]:** The mission provided critical data on the persistent hexagonal cloud pattern at Saturn's north pole.
    *   First observed by [[concepts/voyager-2]] in 1981.
    *   Cassini extensively studied the structure, stability, and meteorological [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] of this geometric vortex.
    *   Recent analysis suggests explanations for its perfect hexagonal shape, linking it to deep atmospheric flows.
    *   See also: [[lab-notes/2026-07-18-Saturns-Hexagonal-North-Pole-Mystery-and-Explanations|Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole: Mystery and Explanations]]
*   **Rings:** Detailed mapping of ring [[concepts/writing|composition]], structure, and "spokes."
*   **Moons:**
    *   **[[concepts/oceangate-titan|Titan]]:** Huygens probe data on surface [[concepts/geology|geology]] and methane cycle.
    *   **Enceladus:** Discovery of water-ice plumes and subsurface ocean.
    *   **Iapetus:** Two-[[concepts/tone|tone]] coloration and equatorial ridge.
