# Qwen Team

The [[entities/qwen|Qwen]] Team is responsible for developing the Qwen series of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and related [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]]. The team operates under [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]]'s broader AI initiatives and focuses on creating [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] and commercially available [[concepts/models|models]] for various [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Qwen3-TTS

In April 2026, the Qwen Team open-sourced the [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family of models, a text-to-speech system designed for natural [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]]. The Qwen3-TTS models support features including [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] and [[concepts/instruction-control|voice control]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], enabling users to customize speech [[concepts/output|output]] characteristics. The open-source release made these models accessible to developers and researchers for [[concepts/integration|integration]] into their own applications.
