# Slide Deck Conversion

Slide Deck Conversion is the process of transforming presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. NotebookLM automatically structures source documents into organized slide presentations, which serve as the foundation for video production. Google Vids then converts these slides into formatted videos, streamlining the transition from document analysis to multimedia output within Google's integrated toolset.

## Workflow and Process

The conversion workflow typically begins with document upload to NotebookLM, which generates thematic slides based on the provided sources. These slides are then exported or linked to Google Vids, where they are automatically arranged into a video timeline. The platform applies default transitions, voiceovers, and visual formatting to create a cohesive presentation without manual editing.

## Output Characteristics

The resulting videos are unwatermarked and designed for direct sharing or embedding. This process allows users to leverage NotebookLM’s summarization capabilities for content creation while utilizing Google Vids’ multimedia features for delivery. The integration reduces the time required to produce professional-looking video summaries from static documents.
