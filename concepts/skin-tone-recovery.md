# Skin Tone Recovery

Skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] recovery in concert photography refers to the process of correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] and lighting irregularities that distort how skin appears in images taken under stage lighting. Concert venues typically feature colored gels, spotlights, and inconsistent lighting that can render [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] with unwanted color tints—such as excessive blue, red, or green hues. Recovery techniques aim to restore natural, neutral skin tones while preserving the overall mood and character of the performance.

## Adobe Lightroom Techniques

[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] provides several tools for skin tone correction in concert photographs. The White Balance adjustment allows photographers to neutralize dominant color casts by selecting a reference point or manually adjusting color temperature and tint sliders. The HSL (Hue, Saturation, Luminance) panel enables targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]], allowing correction of particular color tints affecting skin without altering other elements in the image. The [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration panel]] offers [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] of color channels across [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], midtones, and highlights.

Local adjustment tools, such as the Adjustment Brush or Graduated Filter, allow photographers to selectively modify skin tone areas without affecting the stage lighting and background elements. These targeted corrections help preserve the concert's atmospheric lighting while restoring skin to a more natural [[concepts/presence|appearance]]. [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] by color or luminance range further refines adjustments to isolate skin tones specifically.

## Balancing Correction with Authenticity

Effective skin tone recovery requires balancing corrected skin appearance with the authentic conditions of the concert environment. Over-correction can result in artificially neutral tones that contradict the venue's intentional lighting design. Skilled photographers typically aim for skin tones that appear natural and healthy while acknowledging that some color cast influence may remain as part of the photograph's character and documentation of the live event.
