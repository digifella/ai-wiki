# Smartphone Lifecycle

The **smartphone lifecycle** encompasses the stages of a device's existence from market introduction to end-of-life, including hardware durability, software support longevity, and the economic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process regarding upgrades.

## Key Factors in Lifecycle Management

*   **Software Support Horizon**: The duration for which a manufacturer provides OS [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] and [[concepts/security|security]] patches is the primary determinant of functional lifespan.
*   **Hardware Degradation**: Battery [[concepts/health|health]] and component wear (e.g., screen burn-in, charging port failure) often precede software obsolescence.
*   **Upgrade Viability**: The [[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis|cost-benefit analysis]] between maintaining an older device versus adopting [[concepts/new-features|new features]] in newer models.
