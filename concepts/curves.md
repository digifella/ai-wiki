# Curves

## Definition
A fundamental tool in [[concepts/digital-photography]] and [[concepts/image-processing]] that maps input brightness values to output brightness values, allowing for precise control over [[concepts/tone|Tone]] Curve, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], and color balance.

## Key Applications
- **Contrast Adjustment:** S-curve to increase contrast; inverse S-curve to decrease.
- **[[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]]:** Adjusting individual RGB channels to shift color balance.
- **Mood Manipulation:** [[concepts/white-balance-adjustments|Strategic two-color grading]] techniques can significantly alter the emotional impact of a photograph [[lab-notes/2026-10-11-Changing-Photo-Mood-Strategic-Two-Color-Grading-Techniqu|Changing Photo Mood: Strategic Two-Color Grading Techniques]].
