# Tropical Farming Overview

[[concepts/tropical-agriculture|Tropical farming]] refers to agricultural practices in tropical climates characterized by high temperatures and abundant rainfall. These regions offer unique opportunities for crop cultivation due to their year-[[concepts/rounding|round]] growing season but also face challenges such as soil degradation, [[concepts/water-scarcity|water scarcity]], and pest infestations.
