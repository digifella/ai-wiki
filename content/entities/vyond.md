# Vyond

Vyond is a cloud-based [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] platform designed to enable users without extensive video production experience to generate animated videos. The platform provides a library of pre-built [[concepts/stock-characters|stock characters]] and [[concepts/templates|templates]] that users can customize for their specific needs. Users can input or create scripts, which the platform then uses to generate video content.

## Vyond Go

[[entities/vyond-go|Vyond Go]] is the platform's AI-assisted [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] feature. This tool streamlines the video creation process by allowing users to select stock characters and provide scripts before [[concepts/automatic-video-generation|automatic video generation]]. The feature represents the platform's approach to reducing the technical barriers to video production.

## Use Cases

The platform is primarily used for corporate [[concepts/training|training]], marketing communications, educational content, and explainer videos. Organizations use Vyond to create consistent, branded video content [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] scale without requiring in-house video production teams or specialized technical [[concepts/skills|skills]].
