# Classical bits

The fundamental unit of information in [[concepts/classical-computing|Classical Computing]], representing a binary state of either **0** or **1**.

### Comparison with quantum computing
Unlike the deterministic nature of classical bits, quantum systems leverage quantum-mechanical properties, though the field [[concepts/faces|faces]] specific challenges:
- Distinction between significant scientific promise and current industry hype (Ref: [[concepts/quantum-computing|Quantum Computing]]: Hype vs. Scientific Promise and Practical Challenges).
- Differences in operational mechanics regarding how information is processed compared to binary [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]].
