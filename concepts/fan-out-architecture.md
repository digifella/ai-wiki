# Fan-out architecture

**Fan-out architecture** is a design pattern where a single input or request is distributed to multiple parallel processing units, agents, or services. In the context of AI and [[concepts/software-engineering|software engineering]], this pattern enables complex tasks to be decomposed and executed simultaneously, improving throughput and handling [[concepts/complex-tasks|intricate workflows]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Parallelism**: Tasks are split and processed concurrently.
- **Decomposition**: Complex problems are broken into sub-tasks.
- **Aggregation**: Results from parallel units are combined into a final output.
- **Scalability**: Easily [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] by adding more processing units.
