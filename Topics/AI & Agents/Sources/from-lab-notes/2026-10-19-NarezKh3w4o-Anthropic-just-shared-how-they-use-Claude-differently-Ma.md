### Summary
Here is a summary of the video:

The video, presented by Jay, explores the concept of "ambition" when working with AI models, based on insights from Felix Rieseberg, lead engineer of the Claude app at Anthropic. Rieseberg's post, "How to be more ambitious: A tutorial for humans with models," discusses how users can elevate their interactions with AI to achieve better, more creative results. The main topic centers on bridging the gap between average AI use and the strategic, highly effective approaches used by the top 1% of AI users.

Jay breaks down Rieseberg's advice into three core dimensions of ambition: altitude, depth, and range. Altitude refers to focusing on the ultimate outcome rather than just the task, such as asking an AI to keep an app crash-free rather than fixing individual bugs. Depth involves insisting on expert-level tools, setting unreasonable goals to push boundaries, and avoiding self-judging by using outside validation. Range expands the perspective of a task by bringing in multiple roles or points of view, much like a heist movie crew combining diverse talents. 

The video concludes that while AI capabilities are advancing rapidly, the user's "self-efficacy"—the belief in one's ability to achieve goals—plays a crucial role in success. By applying these techniques (believing it's possible, watching experts, and permitting oneself to fail), users can become more ambitious and effective with AI. Jay reminds viewers to maintain a clear purpose and pace themselves, balancing speed with intention.
