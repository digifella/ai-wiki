# Astronomical observation

The systematic monitoring and recording of celestial objects and phenomena. This practice relies on detecting [[concepts/light|electromagnetic radiation]] (and other signals) to infer [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]], [[concepts/writing|composition]], and evolution of the universe.

## Key Methodologies

- **[[concepts/deep-field|Deep Field]] Imaging**: Prolonged [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] of seemingly "empty" regions of sky to detect faint, distant objects.
- **Multi-wavelength Analysis**: Combining data from radio, infrared, optical, and X-ray bands to build comprehensive models.
- **Long-exposure Integration**: Accumulating light over extended periods to overcome the faintness of high-redshift sources.

## Notable Observations & Case Studies

- **[[concepts/james-webb-space-telescope|James Webb Space Telescope]] ([[concepts/jwst|JWST]]) Deep Field**:
  - Recent analysis of a 100-hour observation in the [[concepts/fornax-constellation]] constellation revealed a dense population of [[concepts/distant-galaxies|distant galaxies]] previously hidden in "empty" space.
  - This observation complements earlier work by the [[concepts/hubble-space-telescope]], demonstrating the evolution of observational depth and [[concepts/solution|resolution]].
