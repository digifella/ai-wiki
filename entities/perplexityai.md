# Perplexityai

Perplexityai refers to a category of governance and cybersecurity risk that arises when artificial intelligence systems are deployed within organizations without formal oversight, compliance frameworks, or standardized controls. This risk landscape encompasses ungoverned AI solutions—such as co-pilot tools and generative AI applications—that users adopt independently, often without IT approval or formal risk assessment. These shadow AI implementations operate outside organizational visibility, creating exposure to data security, intellectual property, and regulatory compliance issues.

## Agentic Frameworks and Autonomous Systems

A significant component of perplexityai risk involves agentic frameworks—AI systems designed to operate with substantial autonomy and minimal human intervention. These systems can make decisions, access resources, and execute actions based on learned patterns or user instructions, often with limited oversight mechanisms. The reduced human-in-the-loop controls inherent in agentic AI increase the potential for unintended consequences, including unauthorized data access, policy violations, or system failures that may be difficult to detect or remediate.

## Governance and Compliance Implications

Organizations face material risks when AI adoption outpaces governance capability. Ungoverned AI solutions can bypass data protection protocols, introduce model bias into business processes, or create audit and compliance gaps. These risks are particularly acute in regulated industries where AI use must align with legal and ethical requirements. Establishing clear AI governance policies, inventory processes, and risk assessment procedures is essential to mitigate the threats posed by shadow AI deployment.
