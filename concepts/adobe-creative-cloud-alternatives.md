# Adobe Creative Cloud alternatives

## Overview
A collection of software ecosystems and individual applications designed to replace or compete with [[concepts/adobe|Adobe Creative Cloud]]. This includes proprietary competitors, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] suites, and emerging tools leveraging new technologies.

## Emerging Open-Source Alternatives

### ArtCraft's Rust Open-Source Suite
A significant development in the open-source creative tooling landscape, focusing on [[concepts/operating-system-compatibility|cross-platform compatibility]] and performance.

*   **Core Technology:** Built from scratch using [[entities/rust]], emphasizing [[concepts/memory|memory]] safety and [[entities/high-performance|high performance]].
*   **Scope:** Presents direct, from-scratch alternatives to major [[entities/adobe-creative-cloud|Adobe Creative Cloud]] applications.
*   **Reception:** Described as "breaking news" in the photography and creative community due to the ambitious scope of replacing established industry standards.
*   **Key Resource:** [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-ArtCrafts-Rust-Open-Source-Suite-Cross-Platform-Adobe-Cr|ArtCraft's Rust Open-Source Suite: Cross-Platform Adobe Creative Cloud Alternatives]]

## Other Notable Alternatives
*   **Affinity Suite:** Proprietary, one-time purchase model (Affinity Photo, Designer, Publisher).
*   **GIMP:** Open-source raster [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] editor.
*   **Inkscape:** Open-source vector graphics editor.
*   **Krita:** Open-source [[concepts/digital-painting|digital painting]] application.
*   **DaVinci Resolve:** Professional [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] and color correction software.
