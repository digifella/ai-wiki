# AI Specialization

**AI [[concepts/specialization|Specialization]]** refers to the architectural and [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] development from monolithic, [[concepts/general-purpose-models|general-purpose models]] toward specialized, efficient, and domain-specific systems. This evolution prioritizes performance-per-watt, latency, and task-specific accuracy over raw [[concepts/model-size|parameter scale]].

## Core Concepts

*   **[[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE):** A [[concepts/model-architecture|model architecture]] that routes inputs to specific sub-networks ("experts") based on the task, allowing for massive model capacity with lower computational cost during [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] [[concepts/mixture-of-experts]].
*   **Efficiency over Scale:** Moving away from the "bigger is better" paradigm toward optimizing [[concepts/ai-model-optimization|Model Efficiency]] for specific hardware constraints and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
*   **Domain-Specific Adaptation:** Tailoring models for niche applications (e.g., scientific research, edge [[concepts/computation|computing]]) rather than maintaining broad [[concepts/jacks-of-all-trades|generalist]] capabilities.

## Recent Developments

*   **Shift in Industry Focus:** Current trends highlight a critical pivot from simply increasing [[concepts/code-size|model size]] to focusing on AI Model Evolution and specialized architectures [[lab-notes/2026-09-28-AI-Model-Evolution-Efficiency-Specialization-and-NASA-IB|AI Model Evolution: Efficiency, Specialization, and NASA-IBM Lunar AI]].
*   **NASA-IBM Collaboration:** Recent initiatives, such as the collaboration between [[entities/nasa]] and IBM, demonstrate the application of specialized AI in extreme environments, such as [[concepts/lunar-return|lunar missions]], where efficiency and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] are paramount.
*   **Emerging Tools:** The landscape includes new frameworks and tools like TypeSafe's [[concepts/democratization-of-creativity|Jev AI]], which aim to streamline the development of specialized, type-safe [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]].

## Key Drivers

1.  **Computational Cost:** Reducing the energy and [[concepts/hardware-compatibility|hardware requirements]] for running large models.
2.  **Latency Requirements:** Need for real-time processing in [[concepts/edge-devices|edge devices]] and critical systems.
3.  **Accuracy:** [[concepts/custom-models|Specialized models]] often outperform generalists in specific domains due to focused [[concepts/training-data|training data]].
