# Sidebar

A [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] element typically located on the side of a window or application, providing quick access to navigation, tools, or information.

## Key Characteristics
- **Positioning**: Usually vertical, but can be horizontal in some designs.
- **Functionality**: Contains links, widgets, or controls for efficient access.
- **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]**: Often configurable to suit user preferences.

## Use Cases
- **Navigation**: Quick access to different sections of an application.
- **Tools**: Housing frequently used functions or utilities.
- **Information Display**: Showing relevant data or status updates.
