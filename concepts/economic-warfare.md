# Economic Warfare

Economic warfare refers to the use of economic resources and methods to weaken, disrupt, or destroy the economic capacity of an adversary. In modern conflicts, this extends beyond traditional sanctions to include kinetic strikes on critical [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], [[concepts/supply-chain-disruption|supply chain disruption]], and targeted attacks on commercial [[concepts/supply-lines|logistics networks]].

## Key Dimensions

- **Kinetic Logistics Disruption**: Direct physical attacks on supply nodes to degrade the adversary's ability to move goods and resources.
- **Commercial Targeting**: Strategic strikes against private sector [[concepts/nodes|entities]] that support the war [[concepts/economic-system|economy]], blurring the line between civilian and military targets.
- **Supply Chain Interdiction**: Disrupting the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of essential materials, technology, and consumer goods to create economic [[concepts/pressure|pressure]].

## Case Study: The Warehouse War

Recent operations have highlighted a shift toward targeting the logistics backbone of Russian e-commerce, specifically the Wildberries network. This strategy aims to erode domestic economic stability and morale by disrupting the distribution of goods.

- **Operational Focus**: [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]] has implemented a [[concepts/long-range-strike-campaign|long-range strike campaign]] targeting civilian [[concepts/e-commerce-logistics|e-commerce logistics]] hubs, with a specific emphasis on Wildberries warehouses.
- **Strategic [[concepts/purpose|Objective]]**: To degrade the logistical capacity of Russian commercial entities that facilitate the [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of goods, thereby impacting the broader Russian economy.
- **Impact**: These strikes represent a new dimension in the [[concepts/conflict|conflict]], directly attacking the intersection of civilian commerce and [[concepts/military-logistics|military logistics]].
- **Russian Response**: Analysis of the Russian reaction to these strikes provides insight into their [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] in protecting critical commercial infrastructure.
