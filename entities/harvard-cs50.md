# Harvard CS50

**Harvard CS50** is [[entities/harvard-university|Harvard University]]'s introductory course on the intellectual enterprises of computer [[concepts/science|science]] and the art of programming, taught at Harvard University and Harvard Extension School, and offered to the public for free online at edX.

## Core Curriculum & Extensions
