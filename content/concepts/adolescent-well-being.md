# Adolescent Well-being

Adolescent well-being encompasses a range of factors crucial to the development and [[concepts/health|health]] of teenagers including emotional, social, physical, and cognitive aspects. This page highlights key components influencing adolescent well-being with an emphasis on evidence-based practices.

### Key Components
- **Emotional Stability**: Understanding and managing emotions effectively.
- **Social Connections**: Building supportive [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within families and peer groups.
- **[[concepts/physical-health|Physical Health]]**: Maintaining a healthy lifestyle through [[concepts/diet|diet]], [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], and sleep.
- **Cognitive Development**: Enhancing [[concepts/learning|learning]] and [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical thinking]] [[concepts/skills|skills]].

  - Sleep: Adequate sleep is essential for cognitive development, emotional [[concepts/regulation|regulation]], and overall health. sleep-habits
