# Rogue Planets

**Rogue planets** (also known as free-floating planets or nomad planets) are planetary-mass objects that do not orbit a star. They drift through interstellar space, often detected via gravitational microlensing or direct infrared imaging.

## Key Concepts
- **Formation**: May form in protoplanetary disks and be ejected via gravitational interactions, or form directly from cloud collapse like stars.
- **Detection**: Primarily identified through microlensing events where the planet's gravity bends light from a background star.
- **Prevalence**: Estimates suggest there may be more rogue planets than stars in the Milky Way.
