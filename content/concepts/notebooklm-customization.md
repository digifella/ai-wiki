# NotebookLM Customization

NotebookLM customization refers to the ability to modify design elements and visual presentation within [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] platform, powered by [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] capabilities. This functionality allows users to tailor the appearance and formatting of generated content—including [[entities/google-slides|slides]], mindmaps, and [[concepts/interactive-visuals|interactive visuals]]—to suit professional or branded presentation requirements. The [[concepts/customization|customization]] tools leverage Gemini's generative capabilities to produce styled outputs that can be adapted for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Design and Visual Elements

Users can adjust design properties of generated materials, including mindmaps and presentation slides created from source documents. The platform enables modification of visual styling to match organizational [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] or personal preferences. This represents a shift in [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s functionality toward competing with traditional design [[concepts/software|software]], expanding its role beyond document analysis and into presentation creation.

## Output Formats and Export

NotebookLM customization extends to various output formats. Users can transform generated slides into video content while managing watermarking and attribution options. The integration with Gemini allows for more sophisticated generation and customization of interactive visuals and multimedia presentations derived from uploaded source materials.
