## Swift 1.5 Qwen3.8-27B GSQ-RCO IQ3_S 16GB LLM Performance Benchmark
**Clip title:** Swift 1.5 [[concepts/large-language-model|Qwen3.8-27B]] · GSQ-RCO tested - 16GB [[concepts/local-ai-configuration|Local LLM setup]]
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Luke's Dev Lab
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=aNOUkWk9piU

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive benchmark of the UkisAI Swift-1.5-Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-[[concepts/gguf|GGUF]] model, specifically focusing on its IQ3_S [[concepts/precision-reduction|quantization]] performance on a local [[concepts/ubuntu|Ubuntu]] server setup with an [[concepts/rtx-2000-ada|RTX 2000 Ada]] (16GB [[concepts/vram|VRAM]]) GPU. The presenter, Luke, details a wide array of tests designed to evaluate the model's capabilities in performance, [[concepts/memory|memory]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and practical [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks across various domains including [[concepts/web-application-development|web development]] ([[concepts/kanban|Kanban]]), simulations (Sand [[concepts/physics|Physics]]), game development (Dungeon Crawler, Blender, Godot), and [[concepts/python|Python]] challenges (HumanEval Remix). The overarching goal is to assess how well this highly quantized model performs on real-[[entities/earth|world]] and [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], particularly in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where it can fit entirely within the GPU's VRAM.

In terms of raw performance, the model demonstrated a significant boost when its context fit entirely within the 16GB VRAM. For a 128K context (requiring overflow to [[concepts/ram-capacity|system RAM]]), the decode throughput was approximately 9.9 tokens/second. However, when the context was reduced to 48K, allowing it to reside fully on the GPU, the decode [[concepts/speed|speed]] tripled to an impressive 29.9-30.0 tokens/second. Prefill throughput also saw substantial increases, ranging from ~167-234 tokens/second (cold) and ~1100-43000 tokens/second (cached) when overflowing, to ~342-390 tokens/second (cold) and ~2160-88700 tokens/second (cached) when GPU-resident. The memory test, "[[concepts/needle-in-a-haystack|Needle-in-a-Haystack]]" with a 256K context, yielded a perfect 100% [[concepts/pass-rate|pass rate]], consistently [[concepts/retrieving|retrieving]] information regardless of its depth within the context, although the output formatting was sometimes verbose.

The model also showed commendable abilities in [[concepts/reasoning-skills|logical reasoning]] and coding. In the reasoning benchmark (48 questions across varying difficulties), it achieved a 73% overall pass rate, scoring 100% on [[entities/medium|medium]] questions, 67% on hard, and 33% on expert. Interestingly, it had a minor unusual miss on one easy question (92% pass rate for easy). For the HumanEval Remix, a suite of 100 Python challenges, the model achieved an 86% pass rate and a 100% [[concepts/solution|answer]] rate, indicating it consistently provided code solutions without refusing prompts.

The agent tests, which involve generating code for practical applications, highlighted the model's [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]] capabilities. It successfully created a functional [[concepts/kanban|Kanban board]], a cellular automata (Sand Physics) [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] with correct liquid/acid [[concepts/flow|flow]] (after an initial fix with re-[[concepts/prompting|prompting]]), and a 2D dungeon map (also refined after re-prompting to be less empty). Most impressively, in the Blender test, it generated a detailed 3D "[[concepts/starship|Starfall]] Lantern" asset with minimal context usage (40.3% and [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] compactions), and in Godot, it produced a playable 3D platformer game, which, despite initial issues with player [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and respawn [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], was successfully debugged and fixed through further prompting. Overall, the Swift 1.5 GSQ-RCO model demonstrates very strong performance for its quantization level, particularly in complex [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]], making it a promising option for [[concepts/cross-platform-ai-development|local AI development]].
