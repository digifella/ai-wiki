# Historical Context of Pi Series Development in Mathematics and Physics

The exploration of Pi (π) has been a cornerstone in the [[concepts/history-of-science-and-conceptsmathematicsmathematics|history of mathematics]], with numerous formulas and series developed over centuries. The transition to modern [[concepts/physics|physics]] and its [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]] introduced novel methods and contexts for understanding this ancient constant.

## Key Contributions from Different Eras
- **Ancient Mathematicians:** Archimedes used polygons to approximate Pi.
- **17th Century:** Sir Isaac Newton contributed his well-known series for Pi.
- **20th Century:** Ramanujan's contributions introduced new, incredibly efficient methods for calculating Pi.

## Modern Physics Insights
In recent years, the intersection of [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and physics has led to fresh perspectives on Pi. [[concepts/string-theory|String theory]], a [[concepts/theory|theoretical framework]] in which the [[concepts/point-like-particles|point-like particles]] of [[concepts/particle-physics|particle physics]] are replaced by one-dimensional objects called strings, has brought about unexpected developments related to this mathematical constant.

- **String Theory's Role:** Theoretical physicists working within string theory have derived new series for calculating Pi.
