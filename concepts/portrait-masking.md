# Portrait Masking

Portrait Masking is an automated masking feature in Lightroom Classic that uses artificial intelligence to detect and isolate portrait subjects in photographs. The tool generates selection masks with a single click, allowing photographers to apply localized edits to specific areas of an image without manual selection work. This functionality reduces time spent on repetitive masking tasks and streamlines the editing workflow.

## How It Works

When activated, Portrait Masking analyzes an image to identify facial features and subject boundaries. The AI then creates a precise mask around the detected portrait subject, which photographers can refine or adjust as needed. The mask can be used as a foundation for subsequent edits, such as adjusting exposure, color, or applying effects to only the isolated subject area.

## Related Functionality

Portrait Masking operates within Lightroom Classic's broader masking system alongside other automated mask types. It represents part of Adobe's implementation of AI-assisted selection tools designed to reduce manual editing labor while maintaining user control over final results. The feature is particularly useful for photographers working with high volumes of portraits who need efficient batch-processing capabilities.
