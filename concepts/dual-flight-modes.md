# Dual flight modes

The ability of an aircraft to transition between two distinct aerodynamic configurations, typically involving the shift between vertical [[entities/lift|lift]] (rotorcraft) and horizontal lift (fixed-wing).

## Case Study: V-22 Osprey
The [[concepts/vertical-takeoff-and-landing|V-22 Osprey]] utilizes [[concepts/tiltrotor-technology|tiltrotor technology]] to execute complex transitions between flight regimes.

- **[[entities/national-academies|Engineering]] Complexity:** Requires highly sophisticated engineering to manage the transition between modes.
- **Operational Utility:** Serves as a critical tool for high-stakes maneuvers, such as night rescue missions in the Libyan desert.
