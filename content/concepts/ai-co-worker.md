# AI Co-Worker

An AI co-worker is a desktop application that integrates artificial intelligence capabilities into a user's workflow to assist with professional tasks. These applications function as software assistants designed to work alongside humans in knowledge work environments, providing real-time support for activities such as writing, analysis, research, and problem-solving.

## Core Capabilities

AI co-worker applications typically offer assistance with text-based tasks including drafting, editing, summarization, and content generation. They can help analyze information, answer questions about documents or projects, and provide suggestions to improve work quality and efficiency. The systems are designed to be accessible within the user's existing workflow rather than requiring context-switching to separate tools.

## Integration and Interaction

These applications operate as desktop software that can be accessed alongside other work tools and applications. Users interact with AI co-workers through natural language interfaces, allowing them to ask questions, request assistance, and receive responses within the context of their current work. The goal is to reduce friction in professional tasks by making AI assistance immediately available during active work sessions.
