# Rehabilitation Planning

Rehabilitation planning is the process of designing and implementing structured [[concepts/software|programs]] to help patients recover function and independence following injury, illness, or surgery. In [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] settings, rehabilitation planning involves assessment of the patient's current capabilities, identification of realistic goals, and selection of appropriate therapeutic interventions. Effective rehabilitation planning requires collaboration between multiple [[concepts/health|health]] professionals, including physiotherapists, occupational therapists, speech pathologists, and other specialists depending on the patient's needs.

## Role of Wearable Devices

Wearable devices with [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] have become increasingly valuable tools in rehabilitation planning and monitoring. These devices—worn on the body as watches, armbands, or patches—continuously collect data on [[concepts/exercise|movement]], activity levels, heart rate, and other physiological [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. This real-time information allows allied health professionals to track patient progress objectively, adjust rehabilitation protocols based on actual performance data, and provide patients with immediate [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] on their recovery. Wearable technology enables rehabilitation to extend beyond the clinical setting, supporting patient [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] and [[concepts/motivation|motivation]] during home-based recovery programs.
