# Wikipedia Style Article Generation

Wikipedia Style Article Generation refers to the automated creation of encyclopedic articles through [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]], exemplified by [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s STORM (Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and [[concepts/multi-perspective-question-asking|Multi-perspective question asking]]). Rather than generating articles from scratch, STORM employs a multi-[[concepts/agent-workflow|agent workflow]] that systematically researches topics, gathers information from multiple sources, and synthesizes findings into structured article formats resembling Wikipedia entries.

## Architecture and Process

STORM operates through coordinated [[concepts/agent-based-knowledge-curation|agent-based research]] where different [[concepts/agents|agents]] perform specialized tasks including [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]], perspective gathering, and content verification. The system constructs articles by first generating diverse research questions from multiple viewpoints, then retrieving relevant sources to answer those questions, and finally organizing the results into coherent sections with proper attribution. This approach prioritizes [[concepts/factual-accuracy|factual accuracy]] and source verification over generation speed.

## Applications and Significance

The technology addresses challenges in automated knowledge curation by combining web search capabilities with structured [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] about information credibility. Systems like STORM are designed for [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where article quality and verifiability matter—such as creating initial drafts for human editors, generating reference materials, or synthesizing research across multiple sources. The agent-based approach allows the system to model how human researchers approach unfamiliar topics: by asking questions systematically and cross-referencing information sources rather than relying on probabilistic [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] alone.
