## Governance and Trust Frameworks

As [[concepts/agentic-ai]] systems evolve toward greater autonomy, traditional oversight mechanisms require critical re-evaluation.

*   **Critique of "Human in the [[concepts/loop|Loop]]" (HITL):** The standard HITL model is increasingly viewed as insufficient for governing complex [[concepts/agentic-ai]] deployments.
    *   HITL often reduces to superficial review of AI output without deep [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]], failing to address [[concepts/causes|root causes]] of errors or alignment issues.
    *   This approach creates a false sense of [[concepts/security|security]] for stakeholders while lacking robust [[concepts/governance|governance]] capabilities.
    *   A shift toward a comprehensive **[[concepts/trust|Trust]] Framework** is required, moving beyond simple oversight to active governance structures.
    *   For detailed analysis, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Critiquing-Human-in-the-Loop-A-Trust-Framework-for-AI-Go|Critiquing \"Human in the Loop\": A Trust Framework for AI Governance]].
