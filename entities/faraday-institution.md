# Faraday Institution

The Faraday Institution is a national institute for [[concepts/battery-technology]] and [[concepts/materials-design]]. It serves as a central hub for collaborative research aimed at accelerating the development of next-generation energy storage solutions.

## Key Initiatives & Research Areas

*   **AI-Driven Materials Discovery**: Leveraging [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] to invent and optimize materials that do not yet exist, significantly reducing the timeline for battery innovation [[lab-notes/2026-08-19-AIs-Role-in-Revolutionizing-Battery-Technology-and-Mater|AI's Role in Revolutionizing Battery Technology and Materials Design]].
*   **Cross-Sector Collaboration**: Bridging the gap between academic research and industrial application to scale battery technologies.
*   **Sustainable Energy Storage**: Focusing on the lifecycle, recycling, and environmental impact of battery materials.
