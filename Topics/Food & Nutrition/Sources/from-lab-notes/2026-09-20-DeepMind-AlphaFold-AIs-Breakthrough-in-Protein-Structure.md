## DeepMind AlphaFold: AI's Breakthrough in Protein Structure Prediction
**Clip title:** AlphaFold: The making of a scientific breakthrough
**Author / channel:** [[entities/google|Google]] DeepMind
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gg7WjuFs8F4

### Summary
This video chronicles [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s journey and breakthrough with its AlphaFold AI system, focusing on its ability to accurately predict protein structures. The challenge of protein folding, where a linear sequence of [[concepts/amino-acids|amino acids]] folds into a complex, functional [[concepts/three-dimensional-shape|three-dimensional shape]], has long been considered one of biology's "holy grail" problems. Understanding these shapes is fundamental to comprehending how life works, as proteins are essentially the molecular machines driving biological processes. Traditional experimental methods for determining protein structures are often painstaking and time-consuming, prompting DeepMind to explore whether [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] could offer a faster, more efficient solution.

DeepMind's AlphaFold first entered the Critical Assessment of Structure Prediction (CASP) competition in 2018 (CASP 13), a biennial "Olympics of protein folding" that rigorously tests computational methods against experimentally determined structures. While biologists typically approach computational results with skepticism, AlphaFold's initial system demonstrated a significant leap in accuracy, scoring notably higher than any previous AI approach. The Global Distance Test (GDT) metric is used to evaluate predictions, with a score over 90 considered an accurate solution at atomic resolution. AlphaFold 1 achieved GDT scores in the high 50s, representing a substantial advance but still indicating considerable room for improvement.

The period following CASP 13 presented DeepMind with "brick walls" as they strived for further improvements. However, the onset of the COVID-19 pandemic in early 2020 underscored the critical importance of rapidly understanding viral proteins for drug and vaccine development. This urgency spurred the DeepMind team to intensify their efforts. In CASP 14, AlphaFold's updated system achieved a monumental breakthrough, consistently producing predictions with atomic accuracy, often exceeding 90 GDT for many targets. This level of accuracy was demonstrated on challenging new proteins, including the Orf8 protein from SARS-CoV-2, effectively "solving the problem" of protein folding for the first time.

The implications of AlphaFold's success are immense, heralding a new era for biological research and medicine. The ability to reliably predict protein structures using AI will drastically accelerate drug discovery, allowing scientists to design new therapeutics more effectively. It will also deepen our understanding of diseases, from cancer to neurodegenerative disorders, and enable the exploration of fundamental biological questions at an unprecedented pace. The scientists involved express both excitement for the future discoveries AlphaFold will facilitate and awe at the unpredictable yet profound nature of scientific breakthroughs, emphasizing that these advances unlock new levels of understanding in the natural world.
