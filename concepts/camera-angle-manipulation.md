# Camera Angle Manipulation

Camera angle manipulation refers to techniques for controlling and adjusting camera perspectives in AI-generated imagery. Rather than relying solely on natural language descriptions, modern AI image editing systems employ structured control mechanisms to achieve precise adjustments to framing, viewpoint, and compositional elements. This approach enables creators to specify exact parameters for how a scene should be viewed, moving beyond vague textual prompts to deterministic geometric controls.

Tools such as Nano Banana 2 utilize JSON control to enable precise AI image editing within Gemini. By encoding camera specifications into structured data formats, these systems allow for granular manipulation of the virtual camera's position, orientation, and lens properties. This method ensures that specific artistic or technical requirements regarding perspective are met with higher accuracy than traditional prompt-based methods.

The implementation of structured controls addresses the limitations of natural language ambiguity in defining spatial relationships. It allows for the systematic adjustment of elements such as focal length, depth of field, and viewing angle. Consequently, this facilitates more consistent and reproducible results in creative workflows where specific visual outcomes are required.
