# Fda

The U.S. [[entities/fda|Food and Drug Administration]] (FDA) is a federal regulatory agency operating under the Department of [[concepts/health|Health]] and Human Services. Its primary mandate is to protect [[concepts/population-health|public health]] by ensuring the safety, efficacy, and [[concepts/security|security]] of human and veterinary drugs, biological products, medical devices, the food supply, cosmetics, and radiological products. The agency achieves this through rigorous product reviews, inspections, and monitoring to enforce [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with applicable laws and regulations.

In a move to modernize its internal workflows, the FDA has begun integrating [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] into its in-house operations. This implementation focuses on leveraging [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] to handle complex, [[concepts/multi-step-tasks|multi-step tasks]], aiming to enhance [[concepts/efficient-operation|operational efficiency]] and reduce administrative burdens. By adopting these advanced computational tools, the agency seeks to streamline processes that traditionally require significant human oversight and manual [[concepts/coordination|coordination]].

This [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] represents an evolution in how the FDA manages its vast regulatory portfolio. The integration of agentic AI is intended to support staff by automating routine data processing and decision-support functions, allowing [[entities/employees|personnel]] to focus on higher-level regulatory challenges. While the technology is currently applied to internal operations, it reflects a broader trend in federal agencies to adopt [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] for improved [[concepts/governance|governance]] and resource management.
