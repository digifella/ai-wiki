# Model Behavior

Model behavior refers to the observable actions and responses of a model (e.g., [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]]) in interaction with inputs and contexts.

- [[entities/anthropic]] researchers discuss LLMs as "more than [[concepts/auto-complete|auto-complete]]," emphasizing their complex internal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes (Ritchie, 2026).
- The [[concepts/interpretability]] of LLMs is central to their work, with research focused on "tracing thoughts" to understand model decision pathways (see [Tracing Thoughts in Language Models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tracing-thoughts-language-model)).
- Key question: "What exactly are we talking to when we interact with an LLM?" (Ritchie, 2026).
- Recent investigations explore whether models like [[entities/claude|Claude]] possess an [[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]] akin to human [[concepts/conscious-thought|consciousness]], including both conscious and unconscious [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]], via "[[concepts/j-space|J-Space]]" analysis (see [[lab-notes/2026-07-08-Claudes-Internal-Mental-Life-Anthropics-J-Space-Investig|Claude's Internal Mental Life: Anthropic's J-Space Investigation]]).
