# Fluid Flow

**Fluid [[concepts/flow|flow]]** describes the motion of liquids and gases. It is governed by the Navier-[[entities/stokes|Stokes]] equations, which describe the [[concepts/preservation|conservation]] of mass, momentum, and energy for continuum fluids.

## Mathematical Foundation

The behavior of fluid flow is defined by the [[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes equations]]. A central challenge in this field is the **Millennium Prize Problem** regarding the "[[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|Existence and Smoothness]] of the Navier-Stokes Equation," which questions whether smooth solutions always exist or if [[concepts/blow-up|blow-up]] (singularities) can occur in finite time.
