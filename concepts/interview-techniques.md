# Interview Techniques

Interview techniques are structured methods for evaluating candidates during hiring processes. Organizations use these approaches to assess qualifications, competencies, and cultural fit before making employment decisions. The effectiveness of interview techniques depends on their ability to differentiate between candidates based on genuine capability rather than preparation or external assistance.

## Challenges in the Age of Generative AI

The widespread availability of generative AI systems has fundamentally altered the interviewing landscape. Candidates can now use AI tools to prepare responses, research companies, and even participate in interviews through various forms of assistance. This development has created a credibility gap for traditional interview formats, which often cannot distinguish between a candidate's authentic knowledge and responses generated or enhanced by AI systems. Organizations must therefore reconsider which interview techniques reliably assess genuine talent and capability.

## Effective Interview Approaches

Research from MIT Sloan Management Review identifies several interview techniques that maintain validity in this changing environment. These approaches generally involve methods that require real-time problem-solving, spontaneous communication, or demonstration of skills that are difficult to simulate or prepare for comprehensively. By focusing on dynamic interaction and immediate application of knowledge rather than prepared statements, interviewers can better evaluate whether candidates possess the capabilities they claim.

Organizations implementing these techniques recognize that the interview process itself must evolve to remain a reliable talent assessment tool. The shift reflects a broader recognition that hiring practices need continuous refinement as technology changes the relationship between preparation and authentic performance.
