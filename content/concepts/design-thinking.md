# Design Thinking

Design thinking is a [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] methodology that applies creative and analytical approaches to generating solutions. When applied to visual composition—particularly background creation—design thinking emphasizes understanding the functional and aesthetic requirements of a piece before execution. This approach contrasts with purely intuitive or technical methods by incorporating deliberate conceptual planning alongside practical implementation.

## Application to Background Design

In the context of background creation, design thinking involves identifying the [[concepts/core-purpose|core purpose]] a background serves within a larger composition, whether that is establishing mood, providing context, or supporting focal elements. Rather than treating backgrounds as secondary or decorative, this methodology treats them as integral design problems requiring systematic exploration of visual solutions that balance technical execution with conceptual clarity.

The process typically includes research into relevant visual references, [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] on compositional approaches, and evaluation against the intended function. This structured approach can lead to more coherent and purposeful backgrounds that support rather than distract from the overall work.
