# Tesla Fsd Beta

Tesla FSD Beta is a limited-release software update for Tesla vehicles, designed to test the company's Full Self-Driving (FSD) capabilities in real-world conditions. Distributed to a select group of subscribers, this beta program functions as an intermediate stage between controlled development environments and a full public release. Its primary objective is to gather extensive performance data across diverse driving scenarios and geographic locations, allowing Tesla to refine its autonomous driving algorithms before broader deployment.

The system relies on Tesla's vision-based hardware configuration, utilizing a suite of external cameras to perceive the surrounding environment rather than relying on LiDAR or radar sensors. Participants operate their vehicles under the premise of supervised autonomy, meaning the driver must remain attentive and ready to take control at any moment. This setup allows Tesla to collect vast amounts of edge-case data from everyday driving situations, which is critical for training the neural networks that power the vehicle's decision-making processes.

As a beta product, FSD Beta is subject to frequent updates and changes in functionality. The software is not considered fully autonomous, and Tesla explicitly states that drivers are responsible for the safe operation of their vehicles at all times. The program serves as a large-scale field test, enabling the company to validate its self-driving technology against the complex and unpredictable nature of public roads while adhering to regulatory requirements for advanced driver-assistance systems.
