# Iterative Prompt Engineering

A [[concepts/iterative-feedback|cyclical refinement]] methodology used to optimize generative model outputs through the repeated adjustment of linguistic inputs, structural constraints, and descriptive parameters. This process is essential for achieving high-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] results in complex generative tasks.

### Key Applications & Observations
- **Advanced [[concepts/professional-commercial-production|AI Video Production]]**: Utilized as a core workflow to manage temporal [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] and visual fidelity in high-end video synthesis.
- **Model-Specific Optimization**:
	- Critical for leveraging [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]], specifically for maximizing its superior capabilities in [[concepts/text-realism|Text Realism]] and UI [[concepts/visual-rendering|Rendering]].
	- Used to navigate performance deltas between emerging architectures, such as comparing [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]] against [[entities/nano-banana-pro]].
- **Precision Control**: Enables the [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] of complex visual elements that standard single-pass [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] fails to capture.
