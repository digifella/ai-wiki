# Power Semiconductors

Core [[concepts/power-electronics|power electronics]] components enabling high-voltage, high-current switching. Essential for modern grid [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], [[concepts/renewable-energy]] integration, and electric vehicles.

## Key Technologies & Applications

- **Solid-State Transformers (SST):** Next-generation power conversion devices replacing traditional magnetic transformers with high-frequency switching power semiconductors.
  - SSTs promise significant advantages in size, weight, and control over legacy transformers, though [[concepts/market-adoption|market adoption]] faces technical and economic obstacles [[lab-notes/2026-08-26-Solid-State-Transformer-Evolution-Advantages-and-Obstacl|Solid-State Transformer: Evolution, Advantages, and Obstacles to Market Adoption]].
  - Relies heavily on wide-bandgap semiconductors (e.g., SiC, GaN) for efficiency at high frequencies.
  - Source analysis: [Solid-State Transformer: Evolution, Advantages, and Obstacles to Market Adoption](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Oytqz3zuB7w)
