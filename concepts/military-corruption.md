# Military Corruption

Systemic exploitation of defense institutions, resources, and information asymmetries for illicit personal, corporate, or political gain, compromising operational [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] and strategic stability.

## Forms & Mechanisms
- Procurement Fraud: Kickbacks, cost inflation, and delivery of substandard materiel within defense [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]].
- Resource Diversion: Siphoning of defense budgets to offshore accounts or private holdings.
- [[entities/war-profiteering|War Profiteering]]: Economic extraction during [[concepts/conflict|conflict]], exacerbated by [[concepts/insider-trading]] on kinetic events.
- **Speculative Incentives:** [[concepts/prediction-markets]] and financial wagers on war outcomes introduce [[concepts/perverse-incentives|Perverse Incentives]] where actors benefit from prolonged instability or specific military failures, directly linking capital markets to violence escalation. [[lab-notes/2026-05-13-War-Profiteering-Insider-Trading-Prediction-Markets-and|War Profiteering: Insider Trading, Prediction Markets, and Military Corruption]] details how insider information flows from command structures to traders, destroying army [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] and fueling conflict for profit.
- [[concepts/document-snapshots|State Capture]]: Defense industrial complexes influencing policy to maximize contracts over [[concepts/security|security]] needs.

## Systemic Impacts
- Degradation of combat readiness and troop morale due to resource theft.
- Erosion of public [[concepts/trust|trust]] and civil-military relations.
- Extension of conflicts driven by vested financial interests rather than strategic objectives.
- Amplification of geopolitical risk through misaligned incentives between policymakers and profit-seekers.
