# Neural Filters

[[entities/neural-filter|Neural Filters]] are [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] integrated into [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] that enable automated [[concepts/photo-enhancement|image enhancement]] and manipulation. These filters leverage [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to perform tasks such as upscaling, detail [[concepts/preservation|preservation]], and [[concepts/style-transfer|style transfer]]. They represent Photoshop's [[concepts/integration|integration]] of generative [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into its standard [[concepts/workflow|workflow]], allowing users to process [[concepts/images|images]] with less manual intervention than traditional editing methods.

## Upscaling Tools

Photoshop offers multiple AI-based upscaling options with different strengths. [[concepts/ai-powered-upscaling|Generative Upscale]] uses [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to enlarge images while adding detail, making it suitable for images that benefit from creative interpretation. [[concepts/image-size|Preserve Details 2.0]], located in the Image Size dialog, focuses on maintaining [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]] and [[concepts/texture|texture]] during enlargement, prioritizing fidelity to the original image rather than generation of new content. The choice between these tools depends on whether the user prioritizes detail preservation or acceptable enhancement through generation.

## Style Transfer Applications

Beyond upscaling, Neural Filters extend to [[concepts/style|style]] transfer capabilities such as [[concepts/landscape-mixer|Landscape Mixer]], which applies aesthetic transformations to images. These tools allow photographers and designers to apply consistent visual styles across multiple images or to explore alternative artistic directions without manual painting or complex layer work.
