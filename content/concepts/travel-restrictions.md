# Travel Restrictions

Travel restrictions refer to limitations imposed by governments on the [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of people across borders or within territories. These restrictions may be temporary or permanent and can vary based on citizenship, visa status, [[concepts/health|health]] conditions, security concerns, or international agreements. Understanding applicable travel restrictions is essential for planning journeys and avoiding legal complications.

## Turkey Electronic Visas

Turkey offers an electronic visa (e-Visa) system for eligible travelers, allowing visa [[concepts/software|applications]] to be completed online rather than through traditional embassy or consulate procedures. The e-Visa process is typically faster and more convenient, with approval decisions often made within hours. Applicants must meet specific eligibility criteria based on their nationality and intended [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] of visit, whether for tourism, business, or other authorized activities. Documentation requirements and processing fees apply according to Turkish immigration regulations.
