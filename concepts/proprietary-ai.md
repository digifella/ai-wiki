# Proprietary AI

Proprietary AI refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] technologies and models developed by a specific company or organization for exclusive use within its own systems. This contrasts with [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] AI frameworks that are available for public development and modification.

### Key Points
- **Exclusive Control:** Proprietary AI gives the owning entity complete control over how the technology is used, including restrictions on who can access it.
- **Economic Incentives:** Companies developing proprietary AI often aim to leverage these models as competitive advantages in the market by keeping them from being replicated or improved upon by competitors.
- **[[concepts/ai-workflow|Customization]] and [[concepts/security|Security]]:** Proprietary models allow for tailored features that cater specifically to a company's needs, potentially offering better security against unauthorized use.
- **Market [[concepts/contrast|Contrast]]:** The dominance of proprietary models is being challenged by releases like [[lab-notes/2026-07-17-Inkling-Thinking-Machines-Labs-Open-Multimodal-AI-Breakt|Inkling: Thinking Machines Lab's Open Multimodal AI Breakthrough]], which demonstrates the viability of [[concepts/open-source-weights|open-weights]] [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]].
