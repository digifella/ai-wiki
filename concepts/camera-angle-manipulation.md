# Camera Angle Manipulation

Camera angle manipulation refers to techniques for controlling and adjusting camera perspectives in AI-generated imagery. Rather than relying solely on natural language descriptions, modern AI image editing systems employ structured control mechanisms to achieve precise adjustments to framing, viewpoint, and compositional elements. This approach enables creators to specify exact parameters for how a scene should be viewed—such as camera height, rotation, and field of view—with greater consistency and accuracy than text prompts alone.

## Structured Control Methods

Systems like Nano Banana 2 use JSON-formatted parameters to define camera properties within AI image generation workflows. By encoding camera data as structured code rather than prose, creators can specify precise numerical values for rotation angles, focal lengths, and positional coordinates. This method integrates with platforms such as Gemini, allowing users to combine parametric camera control with AI image editing capabilities in a programmatic workflow.

## Practical Applications

Camera angle manipulation proves useful across various creative contexts, including architectural visualization, product photography, scene composition for storytelling, and iterative design work. The ability to adjust viewpoint parameters systematically allows creators to explore multiple perspectives on a single subject without regenerating entire images, reducing computational overhead and maintaining consistency across variations.
