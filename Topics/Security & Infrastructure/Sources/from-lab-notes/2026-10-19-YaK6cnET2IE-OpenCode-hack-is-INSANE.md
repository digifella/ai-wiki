## OpenCode hack is INSANE.
**Clip title:** OpenCode hack is INSANE.
**Author / channel:** David Ondrej
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YaK6cnET2IE

### Summary
Here's a 4-paragraph summary of the video:

The video discusses the recent hacking of OpenCode, an open-source AI coding tool. The speaker explains that this was a large-scale "distillation attack," meaning that attackers accessed a legacy system to steal API keys from users, particularly those linked to OpenAI, Anthropic, and Google. This allowed them to use these tokens to train their own models. The speaker notes that this attack is significant because it targeted individual consumers rather than just major AI labs, signaling a shift in cybersecurity threats.

The video highlights that this type of attack is becoming more common, with state-backed actors often using similar tactics to acquire data. The speaker shares that even employees of OpenCode experienced personal account attacks, illustrating the growing risk of AI-powered hacking. They emphasize that traditional security methods are no longer sufficient to protect against these types of attacks, urging viewers to adapt and improve their cyber defenses.

To protect oneself, the speaker introduces the concept of "agent loops," which are autonomous processes running in the cloud to test and monitor codebases for vulnerabilities. They explain how they use these agent loops to continuously check for issues like missing access controls and outdated dependencies, allowing them to proactively secure their systems without manual intervention.

The video concludes by promoting "Cloudroom," an open-source platform designed to help teams run agent loops effectively in isolated cloud environments. The speaker encourages businesses and developers to adopt these tools to secure their codebases and stay ahead of emerging threats in the era of AI-powered cyber attacks.
