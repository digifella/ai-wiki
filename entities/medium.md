# Medium

Medium is an online publishing platform founded to democratize digital publishing and enable writers to share long-form content directly with readers. The platform hosts articles and essays across diverse topics including technology, culture, business, and science, serving both independent writers and established publications. By removing traditional barriers to distribution, Medium allows authors to reach audiences without requiring intermediaries or media gatekeeping.

## Platform Features and Content

The platform primarily focuses on hosting long-form articles and essays rather than short-form content. Writers can publish on Medium's own domain or contribute to topical publications curated by editors and communities. The platform includes reader engagement features such as highlighting, responses, and recommendations, which help surface quality content and facilitate discussion between writers and audiences.

## Business Model

Medium operates through a subscription model where readers can access unlimited articles from participating writers and publications. Writers earn compensation based on reader engagement with their content, incentivizing both quality writing and audience development. The platform also supports independent publications that maintain their own editorial standards while leveraging Medium's distribution infrastructure.
