# Cruise Missiles

**Cruise missiles** are guided weapons designed for subsonic or supersonic flight at low altitudes over long ranges, utilizing aerodynamic [[entities/lift|lift]] and jet propulsion. Unlike [[concepts/ballistic-missiles|ballistic missiles]], they follow a terrain-hugging trajectory, making them difficult to detect and intercept until the final approach. Key components include a **warhead**, [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]] systems (GPS, INS, terrain contour matching), and a turbojet [[concepts/engine|engine]].

## Operational Characteristics
- **[[concepts/stealth|Stealth]] & Low Altitude**: Fly below radar horizon using terrain following/avoidance [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]].
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]]**: High accuracy allows for surgical strikes on specific infrastructure or command centers.
- **Versatility**: Launchable from land, sea, and air platforms; used for [[concepts/strategic-depth|strategic depth]] attacks or tactical battlefield support.

## Integration in Modern Conflict: 2026 Context
Recent developments highlight the evolution of cruise missile usage in hybrid warfare [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], particularly regarding [[concepts/logistics-strikes|supply chain disruption]].

*   **[[concepts/mid-range-strike|Mid-Range Strike]] Evolution**: As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Ukraines-Mid-Range-Strike-Campaign-Disrupting-Russian-Su|Ukraine's Mid-Range Strike Campaign: Disrupting Russian Supply Lines and Air Defence]], the 2026 operational landscape features a distinct "mid-range" layer. This involves targeting **Russian [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]]** and **[[concepts/air-defence|Air Defence]]** systems deep behind frontlines.
*   **Logistical Targeting**: Strategic use of cruise missiles to degrade enemy logistics rather than just frontline combat power, forcing dispersion of resources.
*   **System Disruption**: Coordinated strikes aimed at blinding air defense networks, enabling subsequent air operations or deeper penetration by Stealth Technology assets.

## Notable Systems
- Tomahawk: US land-attack cruise missile.
- SCALP: Franco-British long-range stand-off weapon.
- Kh 555: Russian air-launched cruise missiles.
- PrSM (Precision Strike Missile): Emerging US mid-range capability replacing [[concepts/vintage-computing|legacy systems]].

## Strategic Impact
- **Force Multiplication**: Allows smaller militaries to project power disproportionately to their size by leveraging precision over mass.
- **Civilian [[concepts/infrastructure-risk|Infrastructure Risk]]**: Modern [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] often blurs lines between military and civilian targets, leading to significant collateral damage concerns in densely populated areas.
- **Escalation Dynamics**: Use of long-range cruise missiles can trigger broader geopolitical responses, as seen in the [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]] War.
