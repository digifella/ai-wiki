# Pigeon Navigation

**Pigeon Navigation** refers to the complex [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] allowing Homing Pigeon (*Columba livia*) to return to their loft over long distances. This process involves a multisensory integration of celestial cues, olfactory maps, visual landmarks, and Magnetoreception.

## Mechanisms

Current research identifies several primary systems:
- **Celestial Cues**: Utilization of sun position and polarized [[concepts/light|light]] patterns.
- **Olfactory Map**: Association of geographic location with wind-borne scent gradients.
- **Visual Landmarks**: Recognition of terrain features for local orientation.
- **Magnetoreception**: Sensing of the [[concepts/earths-magnetic-field]] via two distinct pathways:
	1. **Cryptochrome-based**: Light-dependent radical pair mechanisms in the retina.
	2. **Magnetite-based**: Mechanical transduction via magnetite particles.
