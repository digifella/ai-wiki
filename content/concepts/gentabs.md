# Gentabs

[[concepts/disco|Gentabs]], also referred to as [[entities/disco|Disco]], is a feature introduced in [[concepts/cc|Google Gemini updates]]. The feature is designed to organize and manage multiple conversational threads or contexts within a single interface, allowing users to maintain parallel discussions or research streams simultaneously.

## Functionality

The tool enables users to work across multiple tabs or conversation branches without losing context or having to start new sessions. This approach streamlines workflows where users need to reference, [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]], or develop [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] across different topics or data sets within the same [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] instance.

## Development Context

[[entities/gentabs|Gentabs]] was featured in [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] update announcements, with documentation and demonstrations of the feature available through official [[concepts/google-search|Google]] product channels. The feature represents part of [[entities/google|Google]]'s broader efforts to enhance the [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] interface with improved organizational and multitasking [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
