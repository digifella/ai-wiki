# Andre Karpathy

Andre Karpathy is a computer scientist and AI researcher recognized for his contributions to deep learning, computer vision, and neural networks. He has held prominent research and engineering roles at major technology firms, including OpenAI and Tesla, where he focused on visual perception systems and the development of large-scale AI models.

His professional work has significantly advanced practical applications of machine learning, particularly in the realm of visual recognition. During his tenure at Tesla, he led the Autopilot team, overseeing the development of neural networks for real-time image processing and autonomous driving capabilities. At OpenAI, he contributed to the early development of large language models and generative AI technologies.

In recent years, Karpathy has shifted his focus toward the infrastructure of artificial intelligence. He has proposed architectures for AI-maintained knowledge bases and AI context layers, aiming to improve how models store, retrieve, and utilize information. His current research emphasizes the structural foundations required for more robust and scalable AI systems.
