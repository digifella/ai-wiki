# Performance Benchmarks
Performance benchmarks are standardized measures used to evaluate and [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] the capabilities of different systems or models, particularly in computational contexts. In [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]], benchmarks provide critical insights into a model's performance across various tasks, from [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]] to image recognition.
