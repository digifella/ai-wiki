# Stokes

## Overview
**Stokes** refers to the [[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes equations]], a set of nonlinear partial differential equations describing the motion of viscous Newtonian fluids. These equations are central to [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid dynamics]] and aerodynamics.

## Millennium Prize Problem
The Clay Mathematics Institute designated the "[[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|Existence and Smoothness]] of the Navier-Stokes Equation" as one of the seven [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems]]. The problem asks whether smooth, physically reasonable solutions always exist in three dimensions, or if [[concepts/singularity]]s (blow-ups) can form in finite time.
