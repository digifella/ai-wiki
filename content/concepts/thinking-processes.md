# Thinking Processes

## Overview
Thinking processes refer to the cognitive activities involved in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. These processes can be studied in both [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems.

## Key Aspects
- **Human [[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]]**: Involves conscious and unconscious mental activities, including [[concepts/memory|memory]], [[concepts/attention|attention]], and logical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].
- **[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]**: Models like [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] simulate aspects of human thinking through pattern recognition and statistical analysis.
