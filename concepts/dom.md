# Dom

"[[concepts/integrated-agent|Christmas Claude]]" refers to a series of product [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] released by [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] during the 2025 holiday period as part of the company's broader agentic strategy. These updates were designed to enhance Claude's capabilities as an [[concepts/ai-operator|autonomous agent]], defined as an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] capable of executing extended sequences of actions to accomplish user-defined objectives with minimal human intervention.

## Agentic Capabilities

The updates focused on expanding Claude's ability to operate independently within defined parameters. Key improvements included enhanced [[concepts/acting|tool use]], better planning and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] across multi-step workflows, and increased [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] in [[concepts/complex-task-execution|complex task execution]]. This shift aimed to transition Claude from a [[concepts/conversational-interface|conversational interface]] to a functional tool capable of managing end-to-end processes.
