# Mindmap Generation

[[concepts/node-hierarchy|Mindmap Generation]] is a [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] technique used with [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] and similar [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to automatically create Mermaid.js mindmap visualizations from source documents or transcripts. The technique leverages the [[entities/agent|agent]]'s ability to parse information and [[concepts/structure|structure]] it hierarchically, producing machine-readable diagram [[concepts/code|code]] that renders as interactive visual maps. This approach is particularly useful for quickly synthesizing complex documents, video transcripts, or research materials into navigable visual formats.

## Methodology

The technique involves providing source material to an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] along with [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to identify key concepts, establish hierarchical [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between [[concepts/ideas|ideas]], and [[concepts/output|output]] the result in Mermaid.js mindmap syntax. [[concepts/information-architecture|Information architecture]] principles guide the structuring process, ensuring that parent-child relationships between concepts reflect genuine logical or causal dependencies rather than arbitrary groupings. The resulting mindmap code can be rendered directly in compatible documentation tools or [[concepts/markdown|markdown]]-based note systems.

## Applications

Mindmap Generation serves several practical purposes in [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] and [[concepts/learning|learning]] workflows. It enables rapid document [[concepts/summarization|summarization]] where complex multi-part sources are distilled into their conceptual structure, aids in lecture or video note-taking by automatically organizing [[concepts/text-transcript|transcript]] content, and supports research synthesis by creating visual overviews of interconnected ideas across multiple materials. The output remains editable, allowing users to refine the generated structure or modify relationships as needed.
