# Licensing

Licensing is a legal agreement that governs the use and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of [[concepts/software|software]] or [[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|intellectual property]]. It specifies how users can access, modify, distribute, and utilize products under specific terms and conditions.

## Key Concepts

- **Open Source Licenses:** These licenses grant broad permissions for software to be used, modified, and shared freely among developers.
  - **[[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache License 2.0]]:** A permissive [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/license|license]] that allows significant freedom while providing legal protection for contributors. [[concepts/apache-license|apache-license]]
  - **GPL (GNU General Public License):** Ensures the software remains free and open-source by requiring modifications to be released under the same terms.
