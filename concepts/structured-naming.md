# Structured Naming

**Structured Naming** refers to the systematic application of consistent, logical, and machine-readable patterns to files, directories, variables, and data [[concepts/nodes|entities]]. It ensures interoperability, ease of [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and [[concepts/app-updates|version control]] [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] across systems.

## Core Principles

- **[[concepts/logical-consistency|Consistency]]**: Apply uniform syntax (e.g., `snake_case`, `kebab-case`) across all related entities.
- **Descriptiveness**: Names must convey content, context, or purpose.
- **[[concepts/operating-system-compatibility|Cross-Platform Compatibility]]**: Patterns must function reliably across different operating systems and file systems to support [[concepts/cross-platform-compatibility|cross-platform compatibility]].

## ArtCraft Suite & Cross-Platform Alternatives

The [[concepts/version-numbers|ArtCraft]] suite represents a significant shift in [[concepts/creative-software|creative software]] and [[concepts/developer-tooling|developer tooling]], offering [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], cross-platform alternatives to proprietary ecosystems.

### Microsoft Office Re-implementations

Recent developments include clean-room re-implementations of [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] Office applications, developed using [[concepts/rust-programming-language|Rust]] and characterized by "[[concepts/vibe-coding|vibe-coding]]" methodologies. These projects aim to provide robust, [[concepts/cross-platform-compatibility|cross-platform compatible]] office suites.

- **WordCraft**: A re-implementation of [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]].
- **GridCraft**: A re-implementation of [[concepts/2026-04-23-excel|Microsoft Excel]].
- **DeckCraft**: A re-implementation of Microsoft PowerPoint.

These applications build upon the team's previous [[concepts/success|success]] with [[concepts/adobe|Adobe]] software clones, emphasizing [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]] and [[concepts/version-control|version control]] through structured naming and logical organization.
