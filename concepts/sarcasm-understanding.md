# Sarcasm Understanding

**Sarcasm understanding** refers to the computational capability of [[concepts/large-language-model]] and [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to detect, interpret, and generate sarcasm, irony, or pragmatic contradictions where literal meaning diverges from intended meaning. It is a subset of [[concepts/natural-language-processing]] involving deep Pragmatics, Contextual Analysis, and often multimodal cues ([[concepts/tone|tone]], facial expression).

## Core Challenges

*   **Literal vs. Intended Meaning**: Models must reconcile semantic content with pragmatic intent.
*   **Context Dependency**: Reliance on prior [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] turns, cultural knowledge, or situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
*   **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Integration]]**: Text-only models often fail without [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] (prosody) or visual cues.
