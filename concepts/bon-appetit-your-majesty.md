# Bon Appétit, Your Majesty

*[[entities/bon-appétit|Bon Appétit]], [[entities/bon-appétit-your-majesty|Your Majesty]]* (2026) is a 12-episode [[concepts/netflix|Netflix]] K-drama that combines elements of historical drama and [[concepts/culinary-arts|culinary arts]]. The series follows Yeon Ji-young, a modern South Korean chef trained in French cuisine who finds herself transported back to the [[concepts/joseon-era|Joseon era]] after an accident with an antique book.
