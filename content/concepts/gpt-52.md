# GPT-5.2

GPT-5.2 is [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] that has been compared against [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/claude-opus-45|Claude Opus 4.5]] in recent [[concepts/benchmark-testing|benchmarking]] efforts. A notable evaluation published in April 2026 used the One-Shot Build benchmark, a complex assessment designed to measure model performance on challenging, real-world tasks rather than traditional standardized metrics.

## The One-Shot Build Benchmark

The One-Shot Build benchmark presents models with comprehensive Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) and evaluates their ability to interpret and execute complex specifications with minimal guidance. This benchmark differs from conventional model comparisons by focusing on practical capability to build and implement based on detailed written specifications in a single interaction.

The comparative analysis between GPT-5.2 and Claude Opus 4.5 provided insights into how these models handle sophisticated, multi-faceted tasks requiring deep comprehension and sustained [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. Such benchmarks reflect the growing emphasis on evaluating [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] for their ability to perform complex work rather than performance on isolated test cases.
