# Red Hat

Red Hat is a [[concepts/software|software]] company known for its enterprise Linux distributions and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] software offerings. The company develops and maintains various tools and platforms used in enterprise computing environments, including middleware, cloud infrastructure, and [[concepts/containerization|containerization]] solutions.

Red Hat is associated with [[concepts/docling|Docling]], an open-source toolkit developed by [[entities/ibm-research|IBM Research]] for [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] in AI workflows. Docling is designed to handle various document formats and extract structured information to support [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] applications. This [[concepts/connection|connection]] reflects Red Hat's broader involvement in supporting open-source projects relevant to enterprise and AI [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
