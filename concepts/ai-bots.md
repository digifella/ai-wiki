# AI Bots

**AI bots** are software applications that run automated tasks via [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]]. They range from simple scripted responders to complex [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] capable of [[concepts/deep-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]] and [[concepts/acting|tool use]].

## Key Characteristics
* **Automation**: Perform repetitive or data-intensive tasks without human intervention.
* **Interaction**: Engage with users through [[concepts/chat-interfaces|chat interfaces]], [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], or direct [[concepts/integration|system integration]].
* **[[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]**: Modern bots utilize [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) to understand context and generate dynamic responses.

The dominance of AI on the internet has shifted significantly toward bot-driven interactions. Key developments include:
