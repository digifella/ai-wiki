# Team Effectiveness

**Team effectiveness** is the degree to which a team achieves its goals while maintaining the [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] and viability necessary for sustained performance. It is multidimensional, encompassing task performance, member satisfaction, and the team's capacity to function as a coherent unit over time.

## Key Dimensions
*   **Task Performance**: [[concepts/success|Achievement]] of specific objectives and quality of output.
*   **Member Satisfaction**: Level of commitment, morale, and individual [[concepts/happiness|fulfillment]] within the group.
*   **Continued Viability**: The team's ability to maintain collaboration and resolve conflicts for future tasks.

## Leadership and Dynamics
The interplay between [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] styles and team outcomes is critical. Recent peer-reviewed analysis suggests that **[[concepts/servant-leadership|servant leadership]]** significantly enhances team effectiveness by fostering [[concepts/psychological-safety|psychological safety]] and ethical climate [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Team-Effectiveness-Servant-Leadership-Regent-University| Regent University]].
