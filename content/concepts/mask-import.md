# Mask Import

Mask Import is a feature in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that enables photographers to apply AI-powered mask presets with a single click. These presets automatically detect and select specific elements within photographs, such as landscapes or people, without requiring manual selection tools. This functionality streamlines the [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]] by reducing the time spent creating precise selections for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## How It Works

The feature leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to identify common photographic subjects and generate appropriate masks accordingly. When activated, Lightroom Classic analyzes the image content and creates selections based on the preset chosen—whether for landscape elements like sky and foliage, or people-related masks for [[concepts/faces|faces]] and bodies. Users can then apply adjustments selectively to the masked areas, such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, or clarity modifications.

## Practical Application

Photographers benefit from Mask Import when working on batches of similar [[concepts/images|images]] or when quick adjustments are needed without detailed manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]]. Rather than spending time with selection tools, editors can apply a preset mask and immediately begin adjusting specific tonal ranges or regions. The feature is particularly useful for [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], where skies and foregrounds often require different treatment, and portrait work, where facial or body adjustments need isolation from background elements.
