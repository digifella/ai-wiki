# Chest X-ray analysis

The clinical process of evaluating thoracic radiographs to identify anatomical abnormalities, pathologies (e.g., pneumonia, pneumothorax, pleural effusion), and structural changes.

## AI-Assisted Interpretation
Advancements in Computer-Aided Diagnosis (CAD) are increasingly leveraging [[concepts/unified-multimodal-models|Multimodal Models]] to integrate visual radiological data with clinical [[concepts/text|text]].

- **[[concepts/dermatology-images|MedGemma 27B]]** (developed by [[entities/google]]):
    - Built on the [[concepts/gemma-3-architecture|Gemma 3 architecture]].
    - Specialized for high-level medical text and Medical [[concepts/image-analysis|Image Analysis]] (image comprehension).
    - Available in multiple [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]], including a 27B parameter version and a 4B multimodal model (available in both pre-trained and instruction-tuned variants).
    - Reference: [Video Overview](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QBuBvMA0oSw)
