Fossil evidence is crucial for understanding prehistoric life and [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|evolutionary history]]. It includes preserved remains or traces of organisms from past geological periods. This evidence helps scientists piece together the timeline of [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]].

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/paleontology|Paleontology]]**: The study of ancient life through [[concepts/fossils|fossils]].
- **Stratigraphy**: The branch of [[concepts/geology|geology]] concerned with rock layers and their chronological [[concepts/ecological-succession|succession]].
- **[[concepts/evolutionary-biology|Evolutionary Biology]]**: Studies the processes that have brought about the diversity of life on Earth as inferred through paleontological records.
