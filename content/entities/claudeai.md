# Claudeai

[[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]].ai is an AI service operated by [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] that provides conversational and generative [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to users. The platform is accessible through a web interface and offers both free and subscription-based tiers of service.

## Agent Skills and Autonomous Execution

A distinguishing feature of [[concepts/claude|Claude]].ai is its [[concepts/agent-harnesses|agent skills]] functionality, which enables the system to execute tasks autonomously on behalf of users. This capability extends beyond conversation to include actions such as [[concepts/remote-desktop|remote desktop]] [[concepts/integration|integration]] and interaction with [[concepts/third-party-applications|external applications]].

## Integration and Applications

Claude.ai has been integrated into various [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]] and workflows. Notable integrations include an [[entities/excel|Excel]] add-in designed for [[concepts/financial-modeling|financial modeling]] and spreadsheet work. The service also supports [[concepts/mobile-to-desktop-ai-control|remote desktop AI integration]], allowing for broader [[concepts/automation|automation]] of user tasks.

## Pricing and Access Restrictions

Claude.ai operates under a subscription model with [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] and [[concepts/pricing-tiers|pricing tiers]]. As of 2026, [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] has implemented restrictions on certain features and capabilities, including limitations on [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] usage, as part of its service [[concepts/structure|structure]] and financial management.
