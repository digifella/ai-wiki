# Ai Generated Shorts

AI Generated Shorts is an automated workflow that combines Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, with Blotato, a content distribution platform, to streamline the creation and publishing of short-form video content. The system integrates video generation, editing, and multi-platform distribution into a coordinated process, reducing manual effort across these separate stages.

The workflow utilizes Claude Code to handle the technical aspects of content production, including script generation, asset assembly, and video rendering. By leveraging spec-driven development principles, the system ensures that the output meets predefined quality and format specifications before moving to the distribution phase. This automation minimizes the need for manual intervention in the technical pipeline, allowing for consistent output at scale.

Once the video assets are generated, the workflow automatically transfers them to Blotato for cross-platform distribution. Blotato manages the uploading, metadata optimization, and scheduling of content across various social media channels. This integration ensures that the final product is published efficiently and consistently, completing the end-to-end cycle from concept to public release without manual handoffs.
