# Wall Street

Wall Street refers to the financial district in lower Manhattan that has become synonymous with the American financial services industry, including stock exchanges, investment banks, and brokerage firms. The term is commonly used to describe the broader financial markets and the professionals who work within them.

## Role in Market Analysis

Wall Street analysts play a significant role in evaluating emerging investment opportunities and assessing their financial viability. In recent discussions, analysts have turned their [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to potential initial public offerings (IPOs) of major technology companies, including the possible public listing of [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]]'s Starlink [[concepts/satellite-internet|satellite internet]] division. These evaluations typically focus on revenue projections, competitive positioning, and long-term profitability potential.

## Starlink Valuation Considerations

The valuation of a potential [[concepts/light-web|Starlink IPO]] presents distinct challenges for Wall Street analysts, as the business model involves both established satellite internet service and broader technological implications. Analysts have debated how to properly value the company given its infrastructure costs, [[concepts/market-size|market size]], and the competitive landscape of broadband internet provision.
