## Fossilization
Fossilization is the process by which remains of organisms (such as animals or plants) become [[concepts/fossils|fossils]], preserved in rock layers over millions of years. This often involves mineral replacement, [[concepts/model-efficiency|compression]], and other processes that alter organic material into durable geological evidence.
