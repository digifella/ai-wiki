# Risk Controls

Risk controls are [[concepts/governance|governance]] mechanisms and [[concepts/security|security]] measures designed to mitigate organizational vulnerabilities and protect institutional [[concepts/integrity|integrity]]. Within security infrastructure, risk controls address both procedural governance and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized activities and ensure appropriate use of organizational resources.

## Governance and Communication Security

A primary concern in risk [[concepts/power|control]] frameworks involves the unauthorized use of communication channels, particularly [[entities/email|email]] systems. When organizational email addresses are used to distribute communications without proper [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] or oversight, this represents a breach of governance protocols that can undermine institutional credibility and expose the [[concepts/organization|organization]] to reputational and [[concepts/legal-risks|legal risks]]. Effective risk controls in this area establish clear authorization procedures for mass communications and audit trails to verify legitimate use of organizational [[entities/contact|contact]] lists.

## Implementation and Oversight

Risk controls require active monitoring and periodic review to remain effective. This includes establishing [[concepts/policies|policies]] that define acceptable uses of institutional resources, [[concepts/training|training]] stakeholders on [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements, and investigating potential violations. Organizations must balance [[concepts/cost|operational efficiency]] with the need to maintain control over sensitive resources such as employee contact information and official communication channels.
