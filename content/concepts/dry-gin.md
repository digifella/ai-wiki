# Dry Gin

A [[concepts/style|style]] of gin characterized by minimal sugar content and a prominent profile of botanicals, most notably juniper.

    - Examination of the theories regarding [[entities/james-bond|James Bond]]'s iconic preference for "shaken, not stirred."
