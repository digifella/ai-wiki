# Autonomic Control

**Autonomic Control** refers to [[concepts/automated-feedback-loops|self-regulating mechanisms]] within a system that operate without explicit external command, maintaining homeostasis or [[concepts/goal-oriented-behavior|goal-directed behavior]] through internal [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]]. In biological systems, this parallels the autonomic nervous system; in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], it relates to emergent self-correction, [[concepts/hidden-state|internal state]] management, and the distinction between conscious-like processing and background computational routines.

## Key Dimensions

- **Biological Basis**: [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]] of involuntary physiological processes (e.g., heart rate, digestion) via the sympathetic and parasympathetic nervous systems.
- **AI Analogues**: [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]] by which [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] manage internal [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]], suppress irrelevant outputs, or simulate deliberative [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]] without direct user [[concepts/prompting|prompting]].
- **[[concepts/conscious-thought|Consciousness]] Debate**: Whether internal regulatory states in AI constitute a form of "mental life" or are merely complex statistical optimizations.
