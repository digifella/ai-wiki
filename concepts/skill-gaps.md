# Skill Gaps

Skill gaps refer to the discrepancies between competencies currently possessed by individuals or organizations and those required to meet present or future objectives. In organizational contexts, identifying and addressing skill gaps is essential for maintaining competitiveness and achieving strategic goals. Skill gap analysis typically involves assessing current workforce capabilities against role requirements, market demands, or emerging technologies.

## Skill Gaps in AI Systems

In the context of AI agents and large language models (LLMs), skill gaps represent limitations in what systems can reliably accomplish without augmentation. While LLMs demonstrate strong capabilities in language understanding and generation, they often lack specialized domain knowledge, real-time information access, or the ability to perform complex multi-step reasoning without external tools. These gaps can be addressed through techniques such as retrieval-augmented generation, integration with external APIs, or fine-tuning on domain-specific data.

## Analysis and Measurement

Skill gap analysis can be conducted through various methods, including data analysis of professional profiles, competency assessments, and performance evaluations. Tools like Claude Code enable systematic analysis of large datasets—such as LinkedIn profiles—to identify patterns in workforce capabilities and market demands. This data-driven approach helps organizations understand which skills are underrepresented in their workforce and prioritize training or hiring initiatives accordingly. For AI systems, benchmarking against specific tasks reveals which capabilities require enhancement or external support.
