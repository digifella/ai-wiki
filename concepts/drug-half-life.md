# Drug half-life

The time required for the concentration of a drug in the plasma to reduce by 50% through [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|metabolism]] and excretion. A fundamental metric in pharmacokinetics.

## Key Parameters
- Governs dosing interval and the time required to reach steady state.
- Dependent on clearance and volume of distribution.
