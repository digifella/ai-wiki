# Ai Avatars

[[concepts/digital-avatars|AI avatars]] are digital characters powered by [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] that serve as visual interfaces within applications and software platforms. Rather than interacting solely through text or traditional UI elements, users engage with animated or rendered representations that respond to input, deliver content, and perform specific tasks. These avatars function as intermediaries between users and underlying [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], combining visual presentation with computational capabilities to create more direct and intuitive interactions.

## Integration in Google Products

[[concepts/google-search|Google]] has incorporated avatar technology into its ecosystem to enhance user engagement and content delivery. A primary example is the integration within [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]], where AI-generated avatars act as presenters for [[concepts/video-resource|video content]]. These avatars are synchronized with [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] and visual elements to create dynamic presentations without the need for human actors or complex [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] workflows.

In [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], avatars are utilized to provide an auditory and visual layer to the AI's analysis of source documents. This feature allows users to listen to and watch AI-generated summaries and discussions, transforming static text-based insights into a more accessible multimedia [[concepts/experience|experience]]. The technology aims to make information consumption more engaging by adding a human-like presence to the AI's output.
