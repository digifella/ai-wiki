# Windows 10 End Of Life

Windows 10 reached its end of support on October 14, 2025, marking the conclusion of nearly eleven years of mainstream support since its initial release in July 2015. Following this date, Microsoft ceased providing security updates, bug fixes, and technical support for the operating system. This cessation left systems running Windows 10 increasingly vulnerable to security exploits and created compatibility issues with newly released software and hardware.

The end of life prompted widespread migration across both enterprise and consumer sectors. Organizations and individuals were required to transition to supported operating systems to maintain security standards and software compatibility. This shift necessitated significant planning and resource allocation to ensure continuity of operations and data protection in the absence of official vendor support.
