# Jensen Huang
## Overview
Jensen Huang is the CEO of NVIDIA, a leading company in [[concepts/graphics-processing-units-gpus|graphics processing units (GPUs)]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].

## Career Highlights
- Founded NVIDIA in 1993.
- Spearheaded numerous technological advancements, focusing on GPUs for [[concepts/gaming|gaming]], professional markets, data centers, and autonomous machines.
- Keynote speeches at major tech events such as GTC have been instrumental in introducing groundbreaking technologies like [[concepts/nemoclaw|NemoClaw]].

### Recent Developments (2026)
- **NVIDIA [[entities/nemoclaw|NemoClaw]] Platform**: Introduced as a [[concepts/enterprise-ai-agent-platform|secure enterprise AI agent platform]] to address the vulnerabilities present in [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]], an [[concepts/open-source|open-source AI]] agent.
- Emphasized the importance of adopting an "[[entities/openclaw|OpenClaw]] Strategy" for all companies to integrate advanced AI technologies securely and efficiently.
