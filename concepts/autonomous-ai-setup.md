# Autonomous AI Setup

Autonomous [[concepts/ai-setup|AI setup]] refers to the process of configuring, securing, and deploying [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems with minimal human intervention. This encompasses the automated selection of models, infrastructure provisioning, [[concepts/security|security]] configuration, and ongoing monitoring. The [[concepts/autonomous-workflow-automation|Hermes Agent]] facilitates this process by generating comprehensive guides that address the technical and operational requirements for deploying [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] independently.

## Security Considerations

Security is a critical component of [[concepts/ai-system|autonomous AI]] deployment. This includes implementing [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] and [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], encrypting data in transit and at rest, managing [[concepts/api-keys|API keys]] and credentials, and establishing audit trails for system actions. Autonomous setups must incorporate security validation at multiple stages—during model selection, infrastructure configuration, and runtime operation—to prevent [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]] and data [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].

## Deployment and Operations

Deployment of [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]] requires selecting appropriate infrastructure, configuring [[concepts/containerization|containerization]] or serverless environments, and establishing [[concepts/monitoring-systems|monitoring systems]]. The process typically involves [[concepts/app-updates|version control]] for models and code, [[concepts/automated-software-testing|automated testing]] pipelines, and rollback mechanisms. Documentation and [[concepts/configuration-management|configuration management]] ensure that deployments remain reproducible and maintainable across different environments and time periods.
