# Sean Darragh

Sean Darragh is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] who produces educational videos about [[concepts/running|running]] footwear and foot [[concepts/health|health]]. He has gained recognition for his technical analysis of [[concepts/foot-strength|shoe lacing techniques]] and their impact on runner performance and injury [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]].

## Bar Lacing vs. Cross-Lacing

Darragh advocates for [[concepts/bar-lacing|bar lacing]] as a superior alternative to the traditional cross-lacing method commonly used in athletic shoes. According to his analysis, bar lacing—where [[concepts/laces|laces]] run horizontally across the shoe rather than diagonally—offers biomechanical advantages for runners. He argues that this lacing pattern reduces unnecessary pressure points and distributes tension more evenly across the foot, potentially contributing to better foot health and comfort during running.

His work emphasizes practical modifications runners can [[entities/make|make]] to their footwear [[concepts/setup|setup]] to support foot strength and reduce injury risk. The video "STOP Cross-lacing Your Shoes (Use Bar Lacing Instead)" presents the case for reconsidering conventional lacing practices in favor of techniques that may better accommodate the foot's natural mechanics.
