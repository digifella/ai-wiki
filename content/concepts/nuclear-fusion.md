# Nuclear Fusion

The process of combining [[concepts/light|light]] atomic nuclei to release massive amounts of energy, fundamentally mimicking the nucleosynthesis occurring within a Star.

## Core Concepts
- Aims to replicate the colossal energy output of the Sun to create a sustainable power source for Earth.
- Relies on the fusion of elements such as Hydrogen and Helium.
- Focuses on the scientific quest to harness the power of a Star via terrestrial replication.
