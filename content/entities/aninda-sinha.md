# Aninda Sinha
## Overview
Aninda Sinha is a notable physicist known for his contributions to [[concepts/string-theory|string theory]] and [[concepts/quantum-field-theory|quantum field theory (QFT)]]. His work has led to significant advancements in understanding the mathematical underpinnings of [[concepts/theoretical-physics|theoretical physics]], particularly in unexpected derivations that bridge pure [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and physical theories.

### Notable Contributions
- **New [[concepts/pi-series|Pi Series]] Discovery**: In collaboration with [[entities/arnab-priya-saha|Arnab Priya Saha]], Sinha contributed to a groundbreaking [[concepts/series-expansion|series expansion]] for the mathematical constant Pi (π), derived from their research in quantum field [[concepts/theory|theory]]. This discovery was recognized for its novelty and implications for both mathematics and [[concepts/physics|physics]].
