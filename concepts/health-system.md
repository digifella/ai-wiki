# Health System

The [[concepts/health|health]] system encompasses the organization, financing, and delivery of [[concepts/health-care|health care]] services to a population. It includes all organizations, people, and actions whose primary intent is to promote, restore, or maintain health.

## Core Components

- **[[concepts/service-delivery|Service Delivery]]**: Hospitals, clinics, and primary care providers.
- **Financing**: Insurance schemes, tax funding, and out-of-pocket payments.
- **Workforce**: Doctors, nurses, administrative [[entities/employees|staff]], and emerging digital health professionals.
- **[[concepts/governance|Governance]]**: Regulatory bodies and policy frameworks.
