# Climate Outlook

A **[[concepts/long-range-forecast|climate outlook]]** refers to a probabilistic forecast of long-term [[concepts/weather-patterns|weather patterns]], typically covering seasonal [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] (e.g., 3–6 months). It synthesizes data from climate models, ocean-[[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]] interactions (such as ENSO), and historical baselines to inform [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] in [[concepts/agriculture|agriculture]], [[concepts/moisture-control|water management]], and disaster preparedness.

## Key Components
- **[[concepts/temperature-trends|Temperature Trends]]**: Projections of mean, minimum, and maximum temperatures relative to long-term averages.
- **Rainfall [[concepts/probability|Probability]]**: Likelihood of above- or below-average precipitation across specific regions.
- **Seasonal [[concepts/causes|Drivers]]**: Identification of dominant climate phenomena influencing the outlook period.
