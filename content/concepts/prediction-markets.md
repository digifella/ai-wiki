# Prediction Markets

[[entities/wall-street|Financial markets]] where participants trade securities based on future event outcomes; prices function as probabilistic forecasts via Information Aggregation.

## Core Mechanics
- **Price as Probability:** Market price approximates the aggregate belief of event likelihood; efficient [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] requires Liquidity and diverse participation.
- **[[concepts/motivation|Incentive]] Alignment:** Financial stakes reward accurate Forecasting, reducing bias compared to unstructured polls or expert consensus.
- **Resolution:** Contracts settle based on verified outcomes, enforcing Truthfulness through economic penalties for misprediction.

## Risks & Market Failures
- **[[concepts/insider-trading]]:** Asymmetric information advantages distort prices, invalidating forecasts and raising ethical concerns regarding fairness and manipulation.
- **[[concepts/perverse-incentives|Perverse Incentives]]:** Profit motives may encourage actors to [[concepts/power|influence]] or sabotage outcomes, creating Moral Hazard and compromising market [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
- **[[lab-notes/2026-05-13-War-Profiteering-Insider-Trading-Prediction-Markets-and|War Profiteering: Insider Trading, Prediction Markets, and Military Corruption]]**: [[concepts/highlights|Highlights]] critical failure modes in [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] zones where insider knowledge and [[concepts/military-corruption|military corruption]] exploit prediction mechanisms, generating perverse incentives that degrade institutional trust and amplify human tragedy.
- **Censorship & Manipulation:** Sensitive markets face regulatory suppression; Sybil Attacks or coordinated capital flows can skew prices independent of true probabilities.

## Applications & Value
- **Decision Support:** Provides quantifiable risk assessments for [[concepts/geopolitics]], Economics, and strategic planning, often outperforming traditional expert analysis.
- **Early Warning:** Rapid price shifts can signal emerging threats or Black Swan events before official channels detect them.
- **Information Discovery:** Uncovers hidden consensus in opaque domains, aiding [[concepts/game-theory]] analysis and resource allocation.
