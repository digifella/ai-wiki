# Scoping Review

A methodological approach to map the existing literature on a topic, identifying key concepts, types of evidence, and research gaps. Unlike systematic reviews, it explores the breadth of a field to inform future research [[concepts/design|design]], policy, or practice without critically appraising individual studies.

## Key Applications

* [[entities/bmj|BMJ]] Review: A 2026 study developing a practice-oriented [[concepts/ai-governance|AI governance]] framework for [[concepts/health|healthcare]], addressing implementation gaps between [[concepts/ethics|ethics]] principles and operational oversight (including [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]] and embedding AI governance into existing processes).
