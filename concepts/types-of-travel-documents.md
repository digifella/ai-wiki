# Types Of Travel Documents

Travel documents are official credentials issued by governments that authorize citizens to cross international borders and establish their identity and citizenship status during travel. These documents serve as fundamental components of security infrastructure, enabling both individual mobility and state control over border security. The primary function of travel documents is to verify that a traveler is who they claim to be and has permission to enter or exit a country.

The most widely recognized travel document is the passport, which is issued by national authorities to certify citizenship and facilitate international travel. Passports typically contain biographical data, a photograph, and security features to prevent forgery. In addition to traditional passports, various other documents exist depending on the traveler's status and destination, including visas, which are endorsements placed in or linked to a passport granting permission to enter a specific country.

In the context of digital infrastructure and modern border control, electronic travel authorizations have become increasingly prevalent. A prominent example is the Turkey eVisa, an official digital document that allows eligible foreign nationals to obtain travel authorization online prior to arrival. This system streamlines the verification process, reducing physical paperwork while maintaining rigorous security protocols for cross-border movement.
