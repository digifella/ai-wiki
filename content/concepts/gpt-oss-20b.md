# Gpt Oss 20b

[[entities/gpt-oss-20b|GPT-OSS-20B]] is an [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] released by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] with approximately 20 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. As an [[concepts/open-weight-model|open-weight model]], its [[concepts/weights|weights]] are publicly available, allowing researchers and developers to download, modify, and deploy it without restrictions on commercial or research use. This [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] distinguishes it from OpenAI's closed [[concepts/models|models]] and positions it within the broader ecosystem of community-driven [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]].

## Fine-tuning and Customization

The model can be fine-tuned using custom datasets to adapt its behavior and outputs toward specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] or personas. This process involves [[concepts/training|training]] the model on domain-specific or task-specific data, allowing it to learn particular communication styles, knowledge domains, or behavioral patterns. [[concepts/fine-tuning|Fine-tuning]] GPT-OSS-20B on curated datasets enables the creation of specialized versions suited to particular [[concepts/software|applications]] without requiring training from scratch.

## Practical Applications

Common applications include training the model to embody specific personas or expert roles by fine-tuning on relevant datasets. For example, the model can be adapted to respond as a particular character, professional, or entity by training it on corresponding [[concepts/text|text]] examples. This flexibility makes GPT-OSS-20B useful for applications ranging from [[concepts/conversational-ai|conversational agents]] to domain-specific assistants, provided adequate [[concepts/training-data|training data]] is available.
