# Meituan Technology

Meituan Technology is a Chinese technology enterprise that has significantly expanded its research and development efforts into the field of artificial intelligence. The company has prioritized the advancement of large-scale model development, focusing on innovative methodologies for training complex AI systems. This strategic shift aims to enhance computational efficiency and reduce reliance on traditional hardware dependencies.

A key achievement in this domain is the development of LongCat 2.0, a massive AI model comprising 1.6 trillion parameters. Notably, this model was developed without the use of Nvidia hardware, highlighting Meituan's focus on alternative computational architectures and training strategies to overcome hardware constraints in large language model development.
