# Star Ratings

Star ratings in Lightroom are a fundamental organizational tool for managing and prioritizing photos within your [[concepts/catalog|catalog]]. They provide a quick, visual method to mark [[concepts/images|images]] with quality assessments, ranging from one to five stars, allowing photographers to efficiently sort and filter their work during the culling and editing process.

## Implementation in Workflow

Star ratings are typically applied during the initial review phase after importing images into your Lightroom catalog. By assigning ratings consistently—for example, using five stars for keeper images, three stars for images requiring further consideration, and one star for obvious rejects—you create a structured system for managing large volumes of photos. This approach becomes particularly valuable when working with extensive shoots where manual review of every image would be time-consuming.

## Integration with Catalog Management

Within the broader context of mastering your Lightroom catalog, star ratings work alongside other organizational features such as flags and [[concepts/color-labels|color labels]] to create a comprehensive filtering system. When combined with [[concepts/smart-collections|smart collections]] and search functionality, star ratings enable you to quickly locate and access specific subsets of your photos for batch editing, export, or deletion, streamlining your post-production workflow.
