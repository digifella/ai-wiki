# AI Lab

## Overview
An AI Lab is a research and [[concepts/coding-workspace|development environment]] focused on advancing [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities, including [[concepts/training-process|model training]], [[concepts/agent-collaboration|agent orchestration]], and [[concepts/performance-benchmarking|performance benchmarking]].
