# Peer-to-Peer Network

A decentralized architecture where participants (peers) act as both clients and servers, sharing resources directly without centralized [[concepts/coordination|coordination]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Decentralization**: No single point of failure or control.
- **Resource Sharing**: Direct exchange of [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]], [[entities/storage|storage]], or [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]**: Network remains functional despite individual [[entities/nodejs|node]] failures.

## Applications & Implementations
- **Distributed [[concepts/computation|Computing]]**: Aggregating idle hardware for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]].
- **[[concepts/decentralized-ai-supercomputer|Decentralized AI]]**: Running [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] models across consumer devices.
- **[[concepts/file-sharing|File Sharing]]**: Protocols like BitTorrent.

- **Mechanism**: Utilizes peer-to-peer networking to distribute computational load across individual users' devices.
