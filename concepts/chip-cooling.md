# Chip Cooling

## Overview
Thermal management of integrated circuits, relying on [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]] to dissipate heat generated by [[concepts/electronic-components|electronic components]]. Effective cooling is critical for maintaining performance stability and preventing [[concepts/hardware-unreliability|hardware failure]].

## Theoretical Foundations
- **[[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes Equations]]**: The fundamental partial differential equations governing [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid motion]], essential for modeling coolant [[concepts/flow|flow]] and heat transfer in heat sinks and liquid [[concepts/cooling-systems|cooling systems]].
- **Millennium Prize Problem**: The [[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|existence and smoothness]] of solutions to the 3D [[entities/stokes|Navier-Stokes equations]] remains one of the seven Clay [[concepts/mathematics|Mathematics]] Institute.
