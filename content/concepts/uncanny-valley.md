# Uncanny Valley

The **uncanny valley** is a hypothesis in [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] and computer graphics describing the emotional response of humans to non-human entities that appear almost, but not quite, human. When a [[concepts/integrity|character]] or robot closely resembles a human but contains subtle imperfections (e.g., unnatural [[concepts/exercise|movement]], expression, or [[concepts/tone|voice]]), it evokes feelings of unease, revulsion, or eeriness in observers.
