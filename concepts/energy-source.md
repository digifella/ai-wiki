# Energy Source

Energy Sources are natural or processed fuels that produce energy in the form of [[concepts/light|light]], heat, or motion. They are categorized by origin and sustainability.

## Classification

### Renewable
- **Solar**: Photovoltaic and thermal conversion of sunlight.
- **Wind**: Kinetic [[concepts/energy-capture|energy capture]] via turbines.
- **Hydro**: Flowing water energy.
- **Geothermal**: [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s internal heat.

### Non-Renewable
- **Fossil Fuels**: Coal, oil, natural gas.
- **Nuclear Fission**: Splitting heavy atoms (e.g., Uranium).

### Emerging/Advanced
- **[[concepts/hydrogen-fusion|Nuclear Fusion]]**: Combining light atoms to [[concepts/deployment|release]] energy. Considered a potential limitless, [[concepts/clean-power|clean energy]] source if commercialization hurdles are overcome.
	- Recent advancements include the Stellarator design utilized by [[entities/germany|Germany]]'s [[concepts/wendelstein-7-x|Wendelstein 7-X]].
	- See: [[lab-notes/2026-05-21-Germanys-Fusion-Breakthroughs-Wendelstein-7-X-Record-and|Germany's Fusion Breakthroughs: Wendelstein 7-X Record and 2045 Commercial Power Plant]] for details on 2026 records and the projected 2045 commercial plant timeline.

## Key Metrics
- **Energy [[concepts/density|Density]]**: Amount of energy stored per unit volume or mass.
- **Efficiency**: Ratio of useful output to input.
- **Carbon Intensity**: Greenhouse gas emissions per unit of energy produced.
