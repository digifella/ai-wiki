# Stanford AI Index Report

The **[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]] AI Index Report** is an annual publication by the [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] Institute for Human-Centered [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (HAI) that tracks, compiles, distills, and visualizes data related to [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]]. It serves as a comprehensive resource for understanding trends in [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]], development, investment, and public perception.

## Key Findings & Trends (2026)

Recent analysis highlights critical shifts in the [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]], particularly regarding model capabilities and deployment strategies:

*   **[[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]] Evolution**: The 2026 report emphasizes significant advancements in [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs), noting increased efficiency and capability in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and multimodal tasks.
*   **[[concepts/open-source-ai-agents|Open-Source AI Agents]]**: A notable trend is the proliferation of [[concepts/open-source|open-source AI]] agents for business integration. Specifically, GitHub-hosted solutions have seen massive [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], with reports indicating over 160,000 clones of specific "AI Employee" frameworks (e.g., [[concepts/agent-skills|Claude Skills]]). This democratization of agent-based workflows is reshaping enterprise automation.
    *   See also: [[lab-notes/2026-07-07-Utilizing-GitHubs-Open-Source-AI-Agents-for-Business-Int|Utilizing GitHub's Open-Source AI Agents for Business Integration]]

## Methodology

The report aggregates data from [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] publications, industry reports, government statistics, and surveys to provide a holistic view of the [[concepts/ai-ecosystem|AI ecosystem]]. It aims to inform policymakers, researchers, and the public about the state of AI technology.
