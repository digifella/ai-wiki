# Number System

A number system is a mathematical notation for representing quantities using a consistent set of symbols and rules. [[concepts/number-systems|Number systems]] form the foundation of [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], computation, and [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]], enabling humans to perform calculations, record data, and communicate numerical information. Different cultures and time periods have developed distinct number systems, each reflecting their practical needs and philosophical frameworks.

## Historical Development

Early human societies used [[concepts/tallying|tallying]] systems to track quantities, employing physical marks or objects to represent counts. These systems functioned effectively for basic enumeration but lacked the [[concepts/abstraction|abstraction]] necessary for advanced mathematics. A critical milestone in mathematical history was the development of the [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] symbol, which allowed for positional notation and dramatically expanded computational capability. Eastern mathematical traditions adopted zero earlier than Western mathematics, partly due to philosophical differences in how "nothingness" was conceptualized. [[concepts/western-resistance|Western resistance]] to zero persisted for approximately 1500 years after its invention in [[concepts/mathematics-in-eastern-thought|Eastern mathematics]], reflecting cultural and philosophical [[concepts/biases|biases]] against representing absence or void.

## Modern Number Systems

Today, the decimal (base-10) system dominates global mathematics and commerce, inherited largely from Hindu-Arabic numeral traditions. However, other positional systems remain essential in specialized fields: binary (base-2) systems underpin all digital computing and cryptography, while hexadecimal (base-16) and octal (base-8) systems serve practical roles in programming and data representation. These systems demonstrate how the same quantity can be expressed differently depending on the base and symbols chosen.
