# Topic Outline Synthesis

Topic outline synthesis refers to the process of automatically generating structured outlines of topics using [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]. In the context of [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s [[entities/storm|STORM]] system, this represents an initial stage where an agent-based framework organizes information into a hierarchical outline before conducting deeper research or knowledge curation tasks.

## Role in STORM

Within STORM (Synthesis of Topic Outline through Multimodal Research), topic outline synthesis serves as a foundational step for verifiable, [[concepts/agent-based-knowledge-curation|agent-based research]]. The system uses this synthesis [[concepts/phase|phase]] to establish the conceptual [[concepts/structure|structure]] of a topic, which then guides subsequent research activities by AI agents. By organizing subjects into logical hierarchies and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], the outline helps ensure that research efforts remain focused and comprehensive.

## Implementation Approach

The synthesis process typically involves analyzing existing information about a topic and identifying key subtopics, relationships, and areas requiring investigation. Rather than relying on a single monolithic [[entities/agent|agent]], STORM employs multiple [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] that work with the generated outlines to conduct targeted research, verify information, and synthesize findings into curated knowledge outputs. This multi-agent approach allows for more systematic exploration of topic spaces compared to conventional search or retrieval methods.
