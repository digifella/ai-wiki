[[entities/google-gemma|Gemma]] 3 is a model [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] developed by [[entities/google]].

## Specialized Variants
- [[concepts/dermatology-images|MedGemma 27B]]: A medical AI model built on the [[concepts/gemma-3-architecture|Gemma 3 architecture]], specifically trained for medical text and image comprehension.
