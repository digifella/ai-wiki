# Compute Capacity

The total quantifiable processing power (CPU, GPU, TPU) available within a distributed system, cloud environment, or local device to execute computational workloads. It serves as the primary physical bottleneck for [[entities/ai]] [[concepts/training-process|model training]] and large-scale [[concepts/inference|inference]].

## Core Components
- **Hardware Architectures**: Includes general-purpose GPUs (e.g., [[entities/nvidia]]) and application-specific integrated circuits like [[entities/tpus|TPUs]].
- **[[concepts/infrastructure|Infrastructure]] [[concepts/scaling|Scaling]]**: The ability of [[concepts/cloud-computing]] environments to provision [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] clusters on demand.
- **Local & Edge Hardware**: Capability to run models on diverse hardware ranging from [[concepts/microcontrollers|microcontrollers]] to high-end local [[concepts/gpu-clusters|GPU clusters]], constrained by [[concepts/memory|memory]] and processing limits.

## Strategic Trends & Developments
- **Specialized [[concepts/silicon|Silicon]]**: Increasing strategic focus on [[concepts/tpu-development|TPU development]] to optimize [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] efficiency and performance-per-watt.
- **Democratization of [[concepts/ai-inference|Inference]]**: Shift towards running [[concepts/local-ai|Local AI Models]] on [[concepts/consumer-grade-hardware|consumer-grade hardware]], reducing reliance on centralized cloud [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] for [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] tasks.
- **Hardware Diversity**: Utilization of varied hardware tiers (microcontrollers to GPU clusters) based on specific memory and processing requirements, as detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-09-30-Local-AI-Models-Hardware-Capabilities-and-Project-Ideas|Local AI Models: Hardware Capabilities and Project Ideas Summary]].
