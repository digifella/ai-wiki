# Automated itinerary creation

The process of leveraging [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] and [[concepts/automation-tools|automation tools]] to generate, organize, and visualize travel schedules, significantly reducing the manual labor involved in research and logistical planning.

### Key Methodologies
- **AI-Map [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Utilizing [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] in conjunction with Google My Maps to streamline the transition from text-based travel suggestions to spatial, visual route planning.
- **[[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] Streamlining**: Transforming traditional, labor-intensive trip planning into an efficient, automated process.
