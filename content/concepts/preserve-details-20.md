# Preserve Details 20

[[concepts/image-size|Preserve Details 2.0]] is an upscaling tool in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] that uses [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to enlarge [[concepts/images|images]] while attempting to maintain fine details and [[concepts/texture|texture]]. It is one of several [[concepts/ai-powered-upscaling|AI-powered upscaling]] options available within Photoshop's Super Resolution feature set.

## Related Upscaling Tools

Photoshop offers multiple approaches to image enlargement through generative AI. These tools operate on different principles: some prioritize detail [[concepts/preservation|preservation]], others focus on smoothness or content-aware expansion, and some are optimized for specific image types such as [[concepts/faces|faces]] or [[concepts/text|text]]. The choice between them depends on the source material and desired [[concepts/output|output]] characteristics.
