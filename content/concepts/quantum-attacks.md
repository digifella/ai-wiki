# Quantum attacks

Quantum attacks leverage the computational advantages of [[entities/quantum-computing]] to compromise classical [[concepts/cryptography]] [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and undermine Digital [[concepts/security|Security]].

## Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC)
- Research and development of [[concepts/post-quantum-cryptography]] (PQC) is critical to addressing the inherent threats posed by future quantum [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
- PQC focuses on creating algorithms resistant to quantum-based decryption, even as current quantum [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] remains limited.
