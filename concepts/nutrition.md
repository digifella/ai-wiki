# Nutrition

Nutrition is the study of nutrients in food, how the body uses them to maintain life and growth, and the relationship between [[concepts/diet|diet]], [[concepts/health|health]], and disease. Nutrients are the components of food that contribute to an individual's [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] by supporting normal physiological functions.

## Key Concepts
- **Macronutrients**: Carbohydrates, proteins, fats; required in large amounts for energy, muscle building, and essential bodily functions.
- **Micronutrients**: Vitamins and minerals; needed in smaller quantities but crucial for bone health, [[concepts/immune-system-function|immunity]], and [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|metabolism]] [[concepts/regulation|regulation]].
- **Dietary Fiber**: Indigestible plant material that aids digestion and helps control blood [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] levels.
- **Antioxidants**: Protect cells from damage caused by free radicals.

- **[[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary Prevention]]**: Focuses on preventing health problems before they start through education, [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]] [[concepts/software|programs]], and [[concepts/healthy-lifestyle|healthy lifestyle]] choices.
- **[[concepts/secondary-prevention|Secondary Prevention]]**: Early detection of diseases to prevent progression. Includes screenings and regular check-ups.
- **[[concepts/tertiary-prevention|Tertiary Prevention]]**: Managing chronic conditions to improve [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] and reduce complications.

### Determinants of Health
- **Social and Economic Factors**: Income inequality, education levels.
- **Physical Environment**: Access to clean water, air quality.
- **Biological Factors**: Genetics, age.
- **[[concepts/health-care|Health Services]]**: [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]] and affordability of healthcare services.
