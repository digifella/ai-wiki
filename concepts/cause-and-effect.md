# Cause And Effect

VL-JEPA ([[concepts/vl-jepa|Vision-Language Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture]]) is a machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] framework developed by Meta that represents a distinct architectural approach to [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] in [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. Rather than organizing [[concepts/computation|computation]] primarily around language tokens, VL-JEPA positions [[concepts/visual-understanding|visual understanding]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|predictive modeling]] as foundational components. This design reflects a broader exploration of how [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems might develop [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] through [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] other than [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language model (LLM)]] token [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]].

The architecture operates by learning to predict future visual states from current observations without relying on intermediate textual representations. By jointly embedding visual and language inputs, VL-JEPA aims to establish a direct mapping between sensory data and semantic concepts. This approach contrasts with traditional [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal models]] that often translate visual information into text before processing, thereby potentially losing spatial and structural nuances inherent in raw visual data.

In the context of AGI research, VL-JEPA serves as a case study for non-[[concepts/llm-analytical-capabilities|LLM reasoning]] architectures. It demonstrates how predictive [[concepts/coding|coding]] in the visual domain can foster causal understanding and [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]]. By focusing on the prediction of visual outcomes, the system attempts to mimic the way biological agents learn about the physical world through interaction and observation, offering an alternative [[concepts/pathway-platform|pathway]] to intelligent behavior that does not depend solely on linguistic [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]].
