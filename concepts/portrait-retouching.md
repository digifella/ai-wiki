# Portrait Retouching

Portrait retouching encompasses the post-processing techniques applied to enhance and refine portrait photographs within Adobe Photoshop. This discipline focuses on improving skin texture, adjusting facial features, and correcting lighting or color inconsistencies while preserving a natural appearance. It is a standard practice in professional portrait photography, fashion imagery, and headshot production, aiming to elevate image quality without compromising authenticity.

Core methodologies typically involve skin smoothing and blemish removal, often achieved using tools such as the Clone Stamp and Healing Brush. Advanced workflows frequently employ frequency separation to decouple texture from color, allowing for independent adjustment of surface details and tonal values. Additional steps may include dodging and burning to sculpt facial structure, as well as precise color grading to ensure consistent lighting and mood across the final output.
