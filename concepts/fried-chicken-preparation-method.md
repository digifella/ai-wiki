# Fried Chicken Preparation Method

General techniques for preparing fried chicken, varying by region and batter [[concepts/writing|composition]]. Key methods include:

*   **Southern [[concepts/style|Style]]:** Typically involves buttermilk brining and a heavy flour-based dredge, often fried in lard or peanut oil.
*   **Katsu:** Uses panko breadcrumbs for a lighter, crispier crust compared to traditional Western frying.
*   **[[concepts/japanese-fried-chicken|Karaage]]:** A Japanese technique focusing on tender, juicy meat with a thin, [[concepts/crispy-coating|crispy coating]] achieved through specific marinades and starches.
