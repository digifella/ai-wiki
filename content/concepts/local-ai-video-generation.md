# Local AI video generation

The practice of executing [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|generative video]] [[concepts/models|models]] on local [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] to bypass cloud-based constraints.

## Key Benefits
- **[[concepts/privacy]]**: Local processing ensures data [[concepts/secure|security]] and data residency.
- **Cost-Efficiency**: Eliminates the need for recurring [[concepts/subscription-models|Subscription models]].
- **No Constraints**: Removes [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]], rate-limiting, and cloud-side censorship.

## Tooling & Models
- [[entities/pinokio|Pinokio]]: An automated installer and browser used to simplify the [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/open-source|Open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] on personal computers.
- Supported Models: [[entities/ltx-2|LTX-2]], [[entities/wan|Wan]].

## Hardware Requirements
- **GPU**: A dedicated, [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] graphics processing unit is essential due to the resource-intensive [[entities/nature|nature]] of video synthesis.
