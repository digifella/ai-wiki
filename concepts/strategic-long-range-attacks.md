# Strategic Long-Range Attacks

**Strategic long-range attacks** refer to military operations conducted at distances beyond the immediate tactical battlefield, targeting high-value assets such as command centers, logistics hubs, [[concepts/air-defence|air defense]] infrastructure, and industrial capacity. These strikes aim to degrade an adversary's war-making potential, disrupt [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], and shape the operational environment for ground forces.

## Core Objectives
- **Logistical Disruption**: Severing fuel, ammunition, and equipment [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] to frontline units.
- **Air Defense Suppression (SEAD/DEAD)**: Neutralizing surface-to-air missile (SAM) systems to enable air [[concepts/excellence|superiority]].
- **Force Degradation**: Targeting mobilization centers, training facilities, and strategic reserves.

## Operational Evolution & Recent Context
The [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] in Eastern [[entities/europe|Europe]] has highlighted the critical [[concepts/value|importance]] of mid-to-long-range strike capabilities as a distinct operational layer. Recent developments indicate a shift toward sustained campaigns targeting deep rear areas rather than isolated incidents.

- **[[concepts/mid-range-strike|Mid-Range Strike]] Campaigns**: [[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Ukraines-Mid-Range-Strike-Campaign-Disrupting-Russian-Su|Ukraine's Mid-Range Strike Campaign: Disrupting Russian Supply Lines and Air Defence]] illustrates how sustained mid-range strikes have become essential for disrupting Russian logistics and air defense networks.
- **Target Prioritization**: Current doctrine emphasizes hitting fuel depots, rail junctions, and integrated air defense system (IADS) [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] to paralyze enemy mobility and [[concepts/secure|protection]].
- **Asymmetric Engagement**: Utilizing precision-guided munitions and unmanned aerial vehicles (UAVs) to achieve strategic effects with [[concepts/limited-resources|limited resources]].

## Key Components
1. **Platforms**: Long-range UAVs, fighter-bombers, [[concepts/cruise-missiles|cruise missiles]], and [[concepts/ballistic-missiles|ballistic missiles]].
2. **Intelligence Integration**: Real-time ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, Reconnaissance) data fusion for target acquisition.
3. **Logistics Targeting**: Focus on the "tyranny of distance" in adversary supply chains.
