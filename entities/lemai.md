# Lemai

Lemai is an organization dedicated to artificial intelligence research and development, with a specific focus on healthcare applications. The entity works to create practical frameworks and methodologies that facilitate the adoption and implementation of AI technologies within health services.

A primary initiative involves a collaboration with Sandeep to develop an AI maturity model tailored for the health sector. This project utilizes agentic AI approaches to assist healthcare organizations in assessing their current capabilities and advancing their overall readiness for AI integration.
