# Brainstorming

Brainstorming is a collaborative ideation technique designed to generate a large volume of ideas or solutions for a given problem or topic. In traditional settings, participants contribute thoughts freely with minimal initial judgment, creating an environment where creative thinking is encouraged. The method operates on the principle that quantity of ideas can increase the likelihood of discovering novel or effective solutions, as the variety of perspectives raises the chances of identifying viable approaches.

In the context of AI agents, brainstorming capabilities allow text-based models to simulate this divergent thinking process. The Nemotron assistant utilizes these capabilities to explore multiple angles of a query, generating diverse hypotheses or potential answers before converging on the most probable or useful response. This approach helps mitigate the risk of early convergence on suboptimal solutions by explicitly considering a broader solution space.

The implementation of brainstorming in AI systems involves iterative generation and evaluation cycles. The agent may produce a set of initial ideas, critique them for feasibility or relevance, and then refine or expand upon the most promising candidates. This structured yet flexible process enables the AI to handle complex, open-ended problems that require creative problem-solving rather than simple factual retrieval.
