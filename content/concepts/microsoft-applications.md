# Microsoft Applications

Microsoft Applications encompass the suite of productivity and business tools developed by Microsoft, including Microsoft 365 and its integrated [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]]. These [[concepts/software|applications]] serve as platforms for organizations to manage communication, document creation, data analysis, and collaborative work across distributed teams.

## Microsoft 365 Copilot and AI Integration

Microsoft 365 Copilot represents the integration of AI assistance into Microsoft's core applications. [[entities/prof-ryan-ahmed|Dr. Ryan Ahmed]], a professor and online instructor, documented a six-month period of experimentation and [[concepts/testing|testing]] with Microsoft 365 Copilot and [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]], examining how these tools function within practical business and educational contexts. His investigation focused on understanding the capabilities and limitations of AI-assisted features across Microsoft's application ecosystem.

## Practical Testing and Implementation

The testing of Microsoft 365 Copilot and related AI agents involved real-world usage patterns to evaluate their effectiveness in business-strategy contexts. This type of hands-on evaluation helps organizations understand how AI agents can be deployed within existing Microsoft application workflows and what value they may provide to end users.
