# Camera App Zoom

Adobe Lightroom released a beta camera application for [[entities/iphone-13-pro|iPhone 13 Pro]] and later models that integrates advanced computational photography features directly into the camera interface. The app leverages the processing capabilities of newer iPhones to enhance image capture at the point of shooting rather than relying solely on post-processing [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Key Features

The [[concepts/camera-app|camera app]] supports zoom functionality with improved optical and computational methods suited to the iPhone 13 Pro's [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] capabilities. Night shot modes enable improved low-light photography through algorithmic image processing. A [[concepts/reflection-removal|reflection removal]] feature can reduce unwanted reflections from glass surfaces such as [[entities/windows|windows]] and eyeglasses, a traditionally difficult problem in [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]].

## Technical Considerations

Early versions of the beta app experienced thermal issues on some devices due to the computational intensity of the processing algorithms, though this was addressed in subsequent updates. The app's advanced features are optimized for iPhone 13 Pro and later models, which have the necessary processing power and thermal management systems required for reliable operation.
