# Speed Record

A **[[concepts/speed|speed]] record** is an unofficial or official statistic of the highest velocity achieved by a vehicle, athlete, or natural phenomenon within a specific category or timeframe. These records serve as benchmarks for technological advancement and [[concepts/human-performance|human performance]].

## Historical Context & Space Exploration

Speed records in vehicular history often intersect with [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] breakthroughs. Notable examples include lunar surface mobility milestones:

- **[[concepts/lunar-roving-vehicle|Lunar Roving Vehicle]] (LRV):** Developed by [[concepts/nasa|NASA]] for the [[concepts/apollo-mission|Apollo program]], specifically Apollo 15, 16, and 17, to extend the range of Extravehicular Activities (EVA).
	- **Record Holder:** [[entities/eugene-cernan|Eugene Cernan]] set a lunar speed record of **17.9 km/h (11.2 mph)** during the Apollo 17 mission.
	- **Engineering Significance:** The LRV represented a critical engineering challenge involving lightweight materials, mobility in low-[[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] environments, and communication systems. For detailed analysis of its construction and role, see [[lab-notes/2026-06-17-Building-NASAs-Lunar-Roving-Vehicle-Engineering-for-Apol|Building NASA's Lunar Roving Vehicle: Engineering for Apollo Moon Missions]].

## Key Metrics
- **Land Speed Record:** Currently held by the ThrustSSC (1,228 km/h).
- **Airliner Speed:** Mach numbers vary by aircraft model; commercial flights typically cruise around Mach 0.78–0.85.
- **Human Speed:** Usain [[entities/bolt|Bolt]]’s top speed during his 9.58s 100m record was approximately 44.72 km/h (27.8 mph).
