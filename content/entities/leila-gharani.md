# Leila Gharani

Leila Gharani is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] and educator specializing in [[entities/excel|Microsoft Excel]] tutorials and advanced spreadsheet techniques. She operates a [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel focused on teaching practical solutions to common and complex Excel tasks, with an emphasis on efficiency and [[concepts/automation|automation]].

## Content and Expertise

Gharani's [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] [[concepts/catalog|catalog]] covers a [[concepts/range|range]] of Excel functions and features designed to streamline [[concepts/data-management|data management]] and analysis. Her content includes tutorials on dynamic calculations using OFFSET, [[concepts/information-extraction|data extraction]] with REGEX, and [[concepts/csv-file-management|CSV file management]] through IMPORTCSV. She also produces guidance on specialized techniques such as [[concepts/trimrange|TRIMRANGE]] for managing [[concepts/dynamic-data-ranges|dynamic data ranges]] and multiple approaches to [[concepts/data-cleaning|data cleaning]] tasks.

## Notable Topics

Among her most viewed content are techniques for removing blank rows from spreadsheets, which she demonstrates using three distinct methods: [[concepts/go-to-special|Go To Special]], Filter functionality, and [[concepts/power-query|Power Query]]. She has also created tutorials on linking and managing multiple CSV [[concepts/files|files]] within Excel, addressing workflows that require [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources.
