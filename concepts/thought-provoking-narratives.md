# Thought-Provoking Narratives

Story frameworks designed to stimulate critical inquiry, challenge assumptions, and explore complex ethical, philosophical, or societal themes. Prioritizes intellectual engagement over passive consumption, often employing speculative [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], moral [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]], and subversive tropes to force audience reflection.

## Core Characteristics
- **[[concepts/cognitive-dissonance|Cognitive Dissonance]]:** Introduces scenarios that disrupt viewer/worldview comfort zones.
- **Thematic Depth:** Focuses on implications of technology, human nature, or power structures rather than plot mechanics alone.
- **Ambiguity:** Resists didactic [[concepts/solution|resolution]], encouraging interpretation and discussion.

## Common Genres & Vehicles
- [[concepts/science|Science]] Fiction: Utilizes speculative futures and alternate realities to examine contemporary issues.
- Psychological Thriller: Deconstructs perception, identity, and sanity.
- Dystopian Fiction: Warns against societal trajectories through exaggerated extremes.
