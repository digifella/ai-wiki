# Andre Karpathy

Andre Karpathy is a computer scientist and AI researcher known for his contributions to deep learning, computer vision, and neural networks. He has held prominent research and engineering roles at major technology firms, including OpenAI and Tesla, where he focused on visual perception systems and the development of large-scale AI models. His professional work has significantly advanced practical applications of machine learning, particularly in the realm of visual recognition.

During his tenure at Tesla, Karpathy led the Autopilot team, overseeing the development of neural network architectures for autonomous driving. He played a key role in transitioning Tesla’s vision-based approach to self-driving technology, helping to establish the company's reliance on camera inputs and deep learning for real-time object detection and path planning.

Prior to Tesla, Karpathy was a research scientist at OpenAI, where he contributed to the development of early large-scale neural network models. He is also recognized for his educational initiatives in artificial intelligence, having created widely used online courses and open-source tools that have helped democratize access to deep learning concepts.

In recent years, Karpathy has proposed new architectural frameworks for artificial intelligence, including AI-maintained knowledge bases and AI context layer architectures. These proposals aim to address limitations in current large language models by introducing more structured and persistent memory systems, reflecting his ongoing interest in the scalability and reliability of AI systems.
