# Adobe Photoshop

[[entities/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] is a raster graphics editor developed and published by Adobe [[entities/inc|Inc.]] It was originally created in 1988 by Thomas and [[entities/chef-john|John]] Knoll and has since become the industry standard for image editing and manipulation.
