# Color Range Tool

The [[concepts/color-range-control|Color Range]] Tool is a [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] feature in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] that creates selections based on specific colors within an image. Unlike traditional masking methods that depend on shape, luminosity, or manual brush strokes, this tool isolates particular [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] throughout a photograph regardless of their spatial location. Photographers sample a target color and expand the selection to include similar tones, allowing [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to be applied selectively to those colors alone.

## Application in Sky Masking

The Color Range Tool is particularly effective for isolating and adjusting skies in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]]. By sampling the blue or cyan hues of the sky, users can create a precise mask that separates the sky from the foreground elements. This method often yields cleaner edges than gradient masks, especially in complex scenes with trees or mountains that intersect the horizon.

Adjustments made through this mask typically involve modifying [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], saturation, and temperature to enhance the sky's [[concepts/presence|appearance]] without affecting the rest of the image. The tool's tolerance slider allows for [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] the selection's breadth, ensuring that subtle color variations in the sky are included while excluding unwanted elements like clouds or atmospheric haze that do not match the sampled range.
