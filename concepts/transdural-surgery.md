# Transdural Surgery

**Transdural surgery** refers to surgical procedures that involve penetrating the **dura mater**, the outermost and toughest of the three meningeal layers surrounding the brain and spinal cord. This approach is critical for accessing intracranial structures, particularly in the context of implanting devices such as **[[concepts/brain-computer-interfaces|Brain-Computer Interfaces]] (BCIs)**.

## Clinical Context & BCI Integration

In the realm of neurotechnology, transdural access is a pivotal step for high-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] [[concepts/neural-implants|neural interfaces]]. Unlike non-invasive methods, transdural procedures allow for direct electrode placement on or within cortical tissue, enabling higher signal fidelity but requiring precise surgical navigation to minimize trauma and infection risk.
