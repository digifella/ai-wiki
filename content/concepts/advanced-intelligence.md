# advanced_intelligence

## Definition
Advanced intelligence refers to a level of cognitive capability that surpasses human norms in complexity, creativity, [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and adaptability. It often implies an understanding or application of highly sophisticated concepts across multiple domains.
