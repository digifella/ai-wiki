### Summary
The video explores the document scanning capabilities of iPhones, demonstrating how users can utilize built-in apps like Notes and Files to scan multi-page documents, extract text, and organize files without needing third-party applications.

The speaker highlights several methods for scanning documents on an iPhone. Through the Files app, users can initiate a scan and save the resulting PDF directly to their device. Similarly, the Notes app allows for both document scanning and text extraction, where users can capture text directly from a physical document and insert it into a note. The speaker also explains how to adjust scan settings to automatically save scanned documents to the iPhone's photo library. To streamline this process further, the video demonstrates how to create a custom shortcut using the Shortcuts app, allowing users to place a dedicated scan button on their home screen or control center for quick access.

The video concludes by emphasizing the convenience and efficiency of using built-in iPhone features for document management. By setting up shortcuts and utilizing native scanning tools, users can eliminate the need for traditional photography or third-party apps, making document archiving quick and seamless.
