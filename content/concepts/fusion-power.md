# Fusion Power

**[[concepts/hydrogen-fusion|Nuclear fusion]]** is the process by which two [[concepts/light|light]] atomic nuclei combine to form a heavier nucleus, releasing vast amounts of energy. It is the [[concepts/power|power]] source of [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] and a leading candidate for a future, carbon-free, high-density [[concepts/energy-source|energy supply]] for Earth. Unlike Nuclear Fission, fusion produces minimal long-lived radioactive waste and carries no risk of meltdowns, but requires extreme temperatures and pressures to overcome electrostatic repulsion between nuclei.

## Key Challenges
- **Ignition:** Achieving a self-sustaining reaction where energy [[concepts/output|output]] exceeds input (Q > 1).
- **Confinement:** Maintaining plasma stability [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] temperatures exceeding 100 million degrees Celsius.
- **Materials:** Developing materials capable withstanding high-energy neutron bombardment.

## Confinement Methods
1. **Magnetic Confinement:** Uses strong magnetic fields to contain plasma.
   - Tokamak: Toroidal device; dominant approach (e.g., ITER).
   - Stellarator: Twisted magnetic coils; inherently stable but complex to build.
2. **Inertial Confinement:** Uses lasers or ion beams to compress fuel pellets (e.g., National Ignition Facility).

## Recent Developments & Milestones
- **2022:** National Ignition Facility achieved net energy gain (ignition) via inertial confinement.
- **2026:** Significant progress in magnetic confinement stability and operational duration.
  - See [[lab-notes/2026-05-21-Germanys-Fusion-Breakthroughs-Wendelstein-7-X-Record-and|Germany's Fusion Breakthroughs: Wendelstein 7-X Record and 2045 Commercial Power Plant]] for details on [[concepts/wendelstein-7-x|Wendelstein 7-X]] records and the timeline for a 2045 commercial plant.

## Economic & Environmental Impact
- **Abundance:** Fuel sources (Deuterium, Tritium, Helium-3) are widely available.
- **Decarbonization:** Critical component for achieving net-[[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] emissions in heavy industry and baseload power.
- **Grid Stability:** Provides constant baseload power, complementing intermittent renewables like Solar Power and Wind Power.
