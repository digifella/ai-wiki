# Kiwi

Kiwi is a beta camera application developed by Adobe, designed specifically for iPhone 13 Pro and later models. The application integrates directly with Adobe Lightroom, enabling users to capture photos within the Lightroom environment rather than relying on the native iOS Camera app and subsequently transferring images. This integration allows for immediate access to Lightroom’s editing tools and cloud storage workflows at the moment of capture.

By leveraging the computational photography hardware available in supported iPhone devices, Kiwi performs advanced image processing at the point of capture. The app includes features such as enhanced zoom capabilities and improved low-light performance for night photography. Additionally, it offers tools for reflection removal, providing users with greater control over image quality and composition before the editing phase begins.
