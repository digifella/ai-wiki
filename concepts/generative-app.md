# Generative App

A generative app is a software application that leverages [[concepts/advanced-ai-models|generative AI models]] to create, modify, or enhance content based on user input. These applications integrate technologies such as [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs), [[concepts/image-generation-systems|image generation systems]], and [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] to automate or support tasks across creative and [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] domains. By processing [[concepts/natural-language-search|natural language queries]] or other input formats, [[concepts/generative-apps|generative apps]] can produce text, images, code, and other media outputs.

## Common Applications

Generative apps serve diverse [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] including [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], data analysis, customer support automation, and creative design assistance. They function across multiple platforms—from web interfaces to [[concepts/apps|mobile applications]] and integrated [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]]—making AI-assisted capabilities accessible to broader user [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] beyond specialized technical settings.

## Platform Examples

Major technology companies have developed generative app features integrated into their existing platforms. [[concepts/google-search|Google]], for instance, has introduced [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to its [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] that include capabilities like [[entities/disco|Disco]] (for dynamic content organization) and [[concepts/gentabs|GenTabs]] (for automated tab generation and management), which extend generative functionality into productivity and [[concepts/data-management|information management]] workflows.

## Technical Foundation

These applications depend on underlying [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] trained on [[entities/big-data|large datasets]], which enable them to recognize patterns and generate contextually relevant outputs. The quality and accuracy of generative apps vary based on [[concepts/training-process|model training]], [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] approaches, and how effectively they handle domain-specific or specialized tasks. Users typically interact with these systems through [[concepts/conversational-interfaces|conversational interfaces]] or [[concepts/chat-prompt|structured input]] prompts.
