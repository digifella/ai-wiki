# Viewer attention direction

The strategic manipulation of a viewer's gaze within a visual field to establish a hierarchical order of [[concepts/value|importance]] and emphasize a specific Focal Point.

## Mechanisms of Visual Guidance
- **[[concepts/contrast|Contrast]]:** Utilizing differences in luminance, [[concepts/saturation-adjustment|saturation]], or [[concepts/texture|texture]] to create visual weight.
- **Leading Lines:** Using geometric or natural paths to facilitate eye [[concepts/exercise|movement]] through the frame.
- **Color Dynamics:** Leveraging color temperature and saturation to influence visual priority.
- **Lighting:** Implementing highlights and [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] to define depth and importance.
