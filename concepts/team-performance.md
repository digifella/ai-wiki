# Team Performance

**[[concepts/team-effectiveness|Team Performance]]** refers to the collective output, efficiency, and goal attainment of a group working toward a shared [[concepts/purpose|objective]]. It is a function of individual [[concepts/skills|skills]], [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], and the social climate within the group.

## Key Drivers
- **Coordination**: Minimization of process losses through clear roles and communication protocols.
- **[[concepts/motivation|Motivation]]**: Collective efficacy and commitment to shared goals.
- **Psychological Climate**: The extent to which team members feel safe to take interpersonal risks.

## Psychological Safety
A critical substrate for [[entities/high-performance|high performance]], defined as a shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking.
- **Definition**: The ability to be vulnerable in the workplace without fear of negative consequences such as punishment, humiliation, or rejection.
- **Impact**: High levels correlate with increased [[concepts/learning|learning]] behavior, [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], and error reporting, directly enhancing Team Performance.
- **Authority**: Established by [[entities/amy-c-edmondson|Amy C. Edmondson]] as a prerequisite for leveraging diverse [[concepts/expertise|expertise]].
- **Reference**: See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Psychological-Safety-Amy-C.-Edmondson|Psychological Safety – Amy C. Edmondson]] for detailed commentary and source [[concepts/verification|verification]].

## Interdependencies
- **[[concepts/trust|Trust]]**: [[concepts/psychological-safety|Psychological safety]] is often mistaken for trust, but is distinct; it is about the system's response to failure rather than interpersonal bonds.
- **[[concepts/conflict|Conflict]]**: Facilitates constructive [[concepts/cognitive-dissonance|cognitive conflict]] while mitigating affective conflict.
