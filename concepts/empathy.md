# Empathy

**Definition:** Empathy is the ability to understand and share the feelings of another person, serving as a foundational element for [[concepts/psychological-safety|psychological safety]] in high-performing environments.

### Applications in Influence, Communication, and Team Performance
- **Personal Growth:** Enhancing empathetic abilities improves [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and professional [[concepts/success|success]].
- **[[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] Effectiveness:** Empathetic leaders better motivate teams and navigate dynamics by understanding individual needs.
- **Building [[concepts/vulnerability|Psychological Safety]]:** Empathy is critical for creating an environment where team members feel safe to take risks and be vulnerable.
