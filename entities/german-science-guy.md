# German Science Guy

**German Science Guy** is a content creator and science communicator known for producing educational videos on complex scientific topics, particularly those involving environmental chemistry and public health.

### Toxic PFAS in Water
In September 2026, the creator released content addressing the pervasive issue of Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances, widely known as "forever chemicals." The work highlights their man-made origins dating back to the late 1940s, their virtual indestructibility, and strategies for removal from water systems.
