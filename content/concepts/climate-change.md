# Climate Change

Climate change refers to significant changes in global temperatures and [[concepts/weather-patterns|weather patterns]] over time that alter the Earth's climate system. These shifts can result from natural factors such as volcanic activity or variations in solar radiation, but currently, anthropogenic activities (primarily greenhouse gas emissions) are driving rapid and unprecedented changes.

## Key Concepts
- **Greenhouse Effect**: The process by which certain gases trap heat within the atmosphere.
- **Global Warming**: A gradual increase in Earth's temperature over time due to higher levels of CO2 and other [[concepts/greenhouse-gases|greenhouse gases]].
- **Weather Extremes**: Increased frequency and intensity of hurricanes, droughts, floods, and [[concepts/wildfires|wildfires]].

## Health Risks
- Heat-related illnesses: [[concepts/heat-stroke|heat-stroke]], [[concepts/heat-stress|heat exhaustion]], [[concepts/dehydration|dehydration]].
- Respiratory issues: Asthma exacerbations, allergies from pollen changes.
- Vector-borne diseases: Increased transmission rates for malaria, Lyme disease, Zika virus.
- [[concepts/mental-health|Mental health]] impacts: [[concepts/anxiety|Anxiety]], [[concepts/depression|depression]] related to displacement and loss.

## Social Impacts
- **Migration**: People moving due to unlivable conditions or economic opportunities linked to climate change.
- **Economic Disparities**: [[concepts/vulnerability|Vulnerability]] of low-income communities who lack the resources to adapt.
- **Infrastructure Strain**: Damage to infrastructure from [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|extreme weather events]], leading to higher costs for rebuilding.

## Policies and Initiatives
- Green New Deal: Proposes sweeping changes to [[entities/america|America]]’s energy, transportation, building, [[concepts/agriculture|agriculture]], and waste sectors.
- Paris Agreement: Global pact with targets to limit global warming this century well below 2°C above pre-industrial levels.
