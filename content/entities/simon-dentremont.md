# Simon d'Entremont
## Professional Wildlife and Nature Photographer

**Summary**:
Simon D'Entremont is a professional photographer known for his expertise in wildlife and nature photography. He challenges traditional photo processing methods by advocating the use of "dark and moody" techniques to enhance [[concepts/photo-editing|dramatic effect]] and transform uninteresting or problematic photos.
