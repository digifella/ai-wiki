# Edward Taub

Edward Taub is a neuroscientist known for his research on neuroplasticity and rehabilitation following brain injury. His work has contributed significantly to understanding how the brain can reorganize and adapt following damage, with practical [[concepts/software|applications]] in stroke recovery and physical rehabilitation.

## Research Focus

Taub's research has explored constraint-induced [[concepts/exercise|movement]] therapy and related approaches to motor recovery. His investigations into the brain's capacity for functional reorganization have informed therapeutic strategies designed to encourage the use of affected limbs and promote neural adaptation in patients with neurological conditions.
