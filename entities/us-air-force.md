# Us Air Force

The [[entities/us|United States]] Air Force (USAF) is the aerial and space warfare service branch of the [[entities/united-states|United States]] Armed Forces. Established in 1947 as an independent military department, the USAF operates strategic bombers, fighter aircraft, transport planes, and space systems. It serves as one of the primary components of American military deterrence and defense strategy.

## Base Hardening and Infrastructure

A significant operational focus for the USAF involves the hardening of airbase infrastructure against potential threats. This includes physical defensive measures, dispersal of assets, improved shelter construction, and redundancy in critical systems. Base hardening efforts aim to protect [[entities/employees|personnel]], equipment, and operational capabilities from conventional and emerging threats, ensuring sustained combat readiness and [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of operations during [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].

The USAF continues to evolve its infrastructure [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] strategies in response to changing [[concepts/security|security]] environments and technological developments in military capabilities.
