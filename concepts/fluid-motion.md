# Fluid Motion

**Fluid motion** refers to the [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of liquids and gases, governed by complex [[concepts/fundamental-laws-of-physics|physical laws]] and mathematical models.

## Core Concepts
- **[[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes Equations]]**: The fundamental partial differential equations describing the motion of viscous Newtonian fluids.
- **Turbulence**: Chaotic [[concepts/fluid-flow|fluid flow]] characterized by changes in [[concepts/pressure|pressure]] and [[concepts/flow|flow]] [[concepts/velocity|velocity]].
- **Continuum Mechanics**: Framework for analyzing fluid motion as a continuous mass rather than discrete particles.
