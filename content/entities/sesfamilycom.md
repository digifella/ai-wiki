# Sesfamilycom

[[concepts/ses-family|SES Family]] is an interactive digital [[concepts/health|health]] application developed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the University of Copenhagen. The company focuses on providing evidence-based modules designed to help users navigate [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] resolution and interpersonal challenges. The platform emphasizes [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] through a simple, user-friendly interface that prioritizes engagement without unnecessary complexity.

## Core Offering

The application centers on conflict and resolution modules, drawing on psychological frameworks to support users in managing relationship difficulties and communication challenges. SES Family operates with a team of approximately 20 employees and positions itself at the intersection of digital [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and practical [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience design]].

## Context

The development of SES Family reflects broader considerations in [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], including what has been termed the "[[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]"—the tension between recognizing the potential benefits of digital health tools and actual user uptake and sustained engagement. The company's work acknowledges this challenge by prioritizing interface simplicity and practical utility in its app [[concepts/design|design]].
