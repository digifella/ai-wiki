# Solar nucleosynthesis

The fundamental process occurring within [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], such as the Sun, where energy is generated through nuclear reactions in the [[concepts/solar-core|stellar core]].

## Mechanism
- Driven by [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]] of [[concepts/light|light]] atomic nuclei.
- Primary process involves the conversion of Hydrogen into Helium.
- The immense gravitational pressure and temperature of stars facilitate the continuous energy [[concepts/output|output]] characteristic of stellar bodies.

## Human Energy Applications
- Research is actively exploring the replication of stellar-scale [[concepts/power|power]] production on Earth to create a sustainable energy future.
