# Meal Prep

Meal prep is a food [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] technique that applies restaurant-style [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]] to home cooking, specifically for preparing vegetables efficiently in advance. The method combines blanching and steaming to preserve the [[concepts/texture|texture]], color, and nutritional quality of vegetables while enabling quick assembly of meals throughout the week.

## Blanching and Steaming Method

The technique involves two primary steps: blanching vegetables briefly in boiling water to partially cook them and deactivate enzymes that cause deterioration, followed by steaming to finish cooking while maintaining firmness. This two-stage approach produces vegetables with better texture and appearance than single-method cooking, making them more appealing when reheated during the week.

## Practical Application

By preparing vegetables using these restaurant methods ahead of time, home cooks can significantly reduce daily meal preparation time. Pre-cooked vegetables can be portioned, stored, and quickly incorporated into various meals, making nutritious eating more convenient and consistent throughout the week.
