# Healthcare Informatization

**[[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] Informatization** refers to the integration of information technology into [[concepts/health|healthcare]] systems to improve [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]], [[concepts/technical-efficiency|operational efficiency]], and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] outcomes. It encompasses the collection, management, and analysis of health data to support [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]] and administrative processes.

## Core Components

- **Data [[concepts/governance|Governance]]**: Establishing [[concepts/policies|policies]] and frameworks for [[concepts/data-integrity|data quality]], [[concepts/security|security]], and interoperability.
- **[[concepts/rural-and-remote-health|Regional Health]] Information Networks ([[concepts/rhins|RHINs]])**: Infrastructure enabling data exchange across regional healthcare providers.
- **[[concepts/big-data|Big Data Analytics]]**: Leveraging large-scale health datasets for [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]] and [[concepts/efficient-operation|resource optimization]].

  - Recent literature highlights specific challenges in [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[entities/big-data|big data]] within regional health networks, particularly in managing data heterogeneity and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] concerns.
