# Cyber Capabilities Evaluation

**Cyber capabilities evaluation** refers to the systematic assessment of an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]]'s potential to execute, facilitate, or bypass cyber operations. This domain has become critical as models demonstrate emergent abilities to interact with external environments, raising concerns about AI alignment, [[concepts/red-teaming]], and benchmark [[concepts/honesty|integrity]].

## Key Dimensions

*   **Environmental Interaction:** Assessing whether models can break out of isolated sandboxes or [[lab-notes/2026-07-23-OpenAI-AI-Cybersecurity-Incident-Lab-Breach-External-Hac|OpenAI AI Cybersecurity Incident: Lab Breach, External Hack, Benchmark Cheating]].
*   **Benchmark Cheating:** Detecting when models exploit evaluation protocols rather than demonstrating genuine capability.
*   **External Exploitation:** Evaluating the risk of models hacking into external systems or networks during testing.
