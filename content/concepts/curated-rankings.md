# Curated Rankings

## Overview
A Curated Ranking is an ordered enumeration of items selected and prioritized by a curator based on defined criteria, expertise, or subjective judgment, distinguishing it from Algorithmic Ranking or raw aggregation. Value derives from synthesis, contextual analysis, and trusted selection [[concepts/power|authority]].

## Key Attributes
- **Intentionality:** Explicit selection process filtering relevant items.
- **Ordering Logic:** Arrangement reflects quality, thematic resonance, or specific metrics.
- **Curation Context:** Often includes rationale, commentary, or [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] explaining placement.
- **Domain Specificity:** Tailored to niche interests or comprehensive overviews within a category.
