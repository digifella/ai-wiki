# Advanced Plan

The Advanced Plan is a [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] tier offered by Evernote as part of its subscription service offerings. The plan sits within Evernote's broader [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]] and serves users with more demanding note-taking and organizational needs compared to the service's basic tier options.

## Context and User Response

The Advanced Plan became a subject of discussion among Evernote users in April 2026 following announced changes to the platform's pricing structure. Users reacted to the modifications with mixed sentiment, with some evaluating whether the plan's features and cost justified continued use of the service. These discussions prompted guidance on account migration for users considering alternatives to Evernote's paid offerings.
