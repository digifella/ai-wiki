# Dr Robert Ballard

Dr. Robert Ballard is an oceanographer and underwater explorer best known for locating the wreck of the RMS Titanic in 1985. His discovery of the ship, which sank in 1912, resolved one of maritime history's most enduring mysteries and became a landmark achievement in deep-sea exploration.

## The Titanic Discovery

Ballard discovered the Titanic while leading a joint French-American expedition in the [[concepts/north-atlantic|North Atlantic]]. The discovery occurred during a [[entities/us-navy|US Navy]] mission tasked with locating two lost nuclear submarines, the USS Thresher and USS Scorpion. After successfully completing the Navy objectives, Ballard used the remaining time and resources to search for the Titanic, locating the wreck [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] a depth of approximately 3,800 meters (12,500 feet).

## Career and Legacy

Beyond the Titanic, Ballard has led numerous deep-sea expeditions and made significant contributions to oceanographic research and underwater archaeology. He has discovered additional notable wrecks and has been instrumental in advancing deep-sea exploration technology. His work has helped establish modern [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for underwater archaeological investigation and deep-ocean research methodologies.
