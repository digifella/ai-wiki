# Google Vids Automation

Google Vids Automation refers to a workflow that converts [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video files without watermarks using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]]. This process bridges two [[concepts/google-search|Google]] products to create finished video content from [[concepts/llm-driven-slide-generation|AI-generated presentations]], enabling users to produce polished multimedia outputs from text-based source material.

## Workflow Overview

The [[concepts/automation|automation]] begins with NotebookLM, which generates structured slide presentations from documents or research materials. These [[entities/google-slides|slides]] are then imported or recreated in Google Vids, Google's [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool. By working within Google's ecosystem, users can leverage both platforms' capabilities while maintaining the content [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] from the initial NotebookLM output.

## Watermark Removal

A key aspect of this automation is the ability to produce videos without watermarks. This distinguishes the output from watermarked versions that some [[concepts/video-creation-tools|video creation tools]] apply by default. The final Google Vids exports can be downloaded or published as clean, unbranded content suitable for professional or creative use.
