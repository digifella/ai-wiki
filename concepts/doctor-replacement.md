# Doctor Replacement

The ongoing debate and technical implementation of replacing or augmenting human physicians with [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] systems. This concept encompasses clinical decision support, administrative automation, and the ethical/legal implications of AI autonomy in [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]].

## Key Themes

### The Replacement Debate
- **Autonomy vs. Assistance**: Distinguishing between AI as a diagnostic aid versus a primary care provider.
- **Public & Expert [[concepts/opinions|Opinion]]**: Growing discourse on the acceptability of AI-led care models.
- **Economic [[concepts/causes|Drivers]]**: [[concepts/expenditure-reduction|Cost reduction]] and scalability as primary motivators for [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].

### Technical Challenges: Scribe Errors
- **[[concepts/data-hallucination|Hallucination]] Risks**: AI scribes may invent medical histories or drug interactions not present in the patient record.
- **[[concepts/data-integrity|Data Integrity]]**: The need for rigorous validation of AI-generated clinical [[concepts/notes|notes]].
- **Human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|Loop]]**: Critical requirement for physician oversight to correct AI-generated errors before charting.

### Oversight & Governance
- **Liability**: Determining [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] for AI-induced medical errors.
- **Regulatory Frameworks**: Evolving standards for AI certification in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] settings.
- **Patient Consent**: [[concepts/opacity|Transparency]] requirements regarding AI involvement in care.
