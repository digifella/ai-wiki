# Hslcolor Mixer

The HSL [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]] is a targeted [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] tool in [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]] that allows photographers to modify specific colors in an image based on their hue, saturation, and lightness values. Located in the [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]]'s color adjustment panels, it provides [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over individual [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] without affecting the entire image uniformly. This selective approach makes it useful for enhancing or correcting particular colors while leaving others unchanged.

## How It Works

The tool operates by isolating colors based on their position in the HSL (Hue, Saturation, Lightness) color space. Users can select a specific color channel, such as red, blue, or green, and adjust its properties using sliders. Changing the hue shifts the color toward adjacent tones on the color wheel, while saturation controls the intensity or vividness of that specific color. Lightness [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] alter the brightness of the selected [[concepts/color-range-control|color range]], allowing for precise corrections like darkening a blue sky or brightening a subject's skin [[concepts/tone|tone]].

## Practical Applications

This functionality is particularly valuable for correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] or enhancing natural landscapes. For instance, a photographer might increase the saturation of greens to make foliage appear more vibrant or adjust the lightness of blues to deepen a sky without impacting other elements. Because the adjustments are confined to specific color ranges, the mixer enables significant [[concepts/creative-control|creative control]] while maintaining overall image balance and avoiding the unnatural look often associated with global color filters.
