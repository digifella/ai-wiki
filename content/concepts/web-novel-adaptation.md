# web novel adaptation

The structural transformation of a web novel into alternative media formats, such as K-Drama, manhwa, or webtoon, involving the reconfiguration of narrative pacing, visual realization, and [[concepts/character-development|character development]] to suit the new [[entities/medium|medium]].

## Core Characteristics
- **Transmedia [[concepts/storytelling|Storytelling]]**: The translation of prose-based descriptions into visual or auditory frameworks.
- **Narrative Reconfiguration**: The expansion or condensation of plotlines to fit episodic (e.g., [[entities/netflix]] series) or panel-based constraints.
- **Genre Tropes**: Often utilizes themes of [[concepts/time-travel]], historical fiction, and romance.
