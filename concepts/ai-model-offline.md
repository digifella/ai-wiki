# AI Model Offline

**Definition**: The state in which an [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] model is disconnected from public or private access networks, [[concepts/visual-rendering|rendering]] it unavailable for [[concepts/inference|inference]] or interaction. This can occur voluntarily (maintenance, safety [[concepts/software-updates|updates]]) or involuntarily (government order, [[concepts/security|security]] breach).

## Recent Events & Case Studies

- **[[concepts/claude-fable-5|Anthropic Fable 5]] & [[concepts/claude-mythos-5|Mythos 5]] Takedown**: On June 14, 2026, the U.S. government issued an unprecedented order requiring [[entities/anthropic]] to take its advanced models, specifically [[entities/fable-5]] and [[entities/mythos-5]], offline. This event marks a significant escalation in state-level intervention in AI deployment.
	- See details: [[lab-notes/2026-06-14-US-Government-Orders-Anthropic-Fable-5-Mythos-5-Offline|US Government Orders Anthropic Fable 5, Mythos 5 Offline: Unprecedented Access Restrictions]]

## Context & Implications

- **Regulatory Precedent**: The forced offline status of proprietary models like [[concepts/fable-5-model|Fable 5]] highlights the tension between commercial [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and national [[concepts/security-concersns|security concerns]].
- **Access Restrictions**: Moves beyond traditional Content Moderation to complete infrastructure denial, affecting both enterprise and consumer access points.
- **Industry Response**: Likely to trigger increased internal [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] measures and pre-emptive offline protocols among major [[entities/ai-labs|AI labs]] (e.g., [[entities/openai]], [[entities/google-deepmind]]) regarding high-capability models.
