# ai agent

An [[entities/agent|ai-agent]] is an autonomous [[concepts/software|software]] entity that performs tasks, makes decisions, or provides services based on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Autonomy**: Operates independently to achieve goals.
- **Adaptability**: Learns and adjusts to new situations.
- **Interactivity**: Communicates with users or other systems.

## Applications
- **Customer Service**: Chatbots and [[concepts/voice-assistants|virtual assistants]].
- **[[concepts/automation|Automation]]**: [[concepts/workflow-automation|Workflow automation]] and [[concepts/workflow-enhancements|process optimization]].
- **Research**: Data analysis and knowledge retrieval.
