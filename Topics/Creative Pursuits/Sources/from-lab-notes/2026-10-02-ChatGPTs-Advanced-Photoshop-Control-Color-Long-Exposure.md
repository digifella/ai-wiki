## ChatGPT's Advanced Photoshop Control: Color, Long Exposure, and Complex Cutouts
**Clip title:** ChatGPT Can Now ACTUALLY Use Photoshop, and It's Impressive
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Glyn Dewis
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZGsgdObKOZA

### Summary
The video demonstrates [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]'s impressive capability to control and execute complex editing tasks within [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]], building on a previous video that showcased its integration with [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]. The main topic revolves around answering the common user question of whether ChatGPT can manipulate Photoshop as effectively as it handles [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]. Through three distinct, challenging [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], the presenter, [[entities/glyn-dewis|Glyn Dewis]], illustrates the AI's surprising [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] and intelligence.

The first key point explored is color correction. Glyn presents an image of a biker with a noticeable color cast and prompts ChatGPT to neutralize it non-destructively, ensuring editability. ChatGPT successfully identifies the issue and applies a "Neutral colour – Curves" adjustment layer, utilizing a Color blend mode to effectively correct the cast while preserving the image's brightness and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]]. The second task involves creating a long [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] effect with moving clouds on a car photograph. ChatGPT intelligently selects the sky, [[entities/places|places]] it on a smart object layer, and applies a "Radial Blur" filter. Crucially, the AI recognizes the need to adjust the blur's origin to the bottom of the image, perfectly mimicking natural cloud [[concepts/exercise|movement]] – a technique the presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] he would also employ.

For the third and most challenging task, Glyn instructs ChatGPT to perform a precise cutout of a Viking [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]], focusing on the intricate details of hair and beard against a gray background. ChatGPT utilizes Photoshop's "Select and Mask" feature, demonstrating its ability to refine edges and create a detailed, editable layer mask. While acknowledging that the result isn't absolutely perfect (with some minor residue around loose hairs), the presenter is highly impressed with the AI's overall performance on such a complex subject. The AI even goes a step further by renaming the output layer, showcasing an unexpected level of sophistication.

In conclusion, the video highlights that ChatGPT's current ability to control Photoshop is remarkably effective, exhibiting a deep understanding of various tools and non-destructive workflows. Glyn emphasizes that this level of AI capability is only the beginning, stating that "this is the worst it [[entities/will|will]] ever be," implying rapid future advancements. He also touches upon the potential for AI to auto-generate tutorials with embedded screenshots, suggesting a revolutionary shift in how creative professionals learn and work.
