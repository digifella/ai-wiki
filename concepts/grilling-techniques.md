# Grilling techniques

Fundamental methodologies for managing [[concepts/cooking-time|heat application]], moisture [[concepts/storing|retention]], and flavor infusion in protein [[concepts/preparation|preparation]].

## Core Pillars of Grilling
- **Marinade**: The use of liquid solutions to enhance flavor and assist in moisture retention.
- **Meat Cut**: The selection and physical [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] of protein to manage heat penetration and structural [[concepts/data-integrity|integrity]].
- **Cook Techniques**: Controlled application of thermal energy to achieve desired internal temperatures while avoiding Dryness.

## Application: Chicken Juiciness
Strategies for preventing common pitfalls that lead to dry meat (Source: [[entities/keiran-mustafa|Keiran Mustafa]]):
- Implementation of precise Marinade, Meat Cut, and Cook Techniques to maximize moisture and flavor.
- Avoidance of common errors in [[concepts/temperature-management|heat management]] and preparation that compromise meat [[concepts/texture|texture]].
