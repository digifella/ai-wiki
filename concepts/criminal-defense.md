# Criminal Defense

**Criminal defense** refers to the practice of defending individuals or [[concepts/nodes|entities]] accused of criminal acts. It encompasses the legal strategies, procedural rights, and judicial processes designed to protect the accused from wrongful conviction or excessive punishment.

## Core Components
- **Procedural Rights:** Ensuring due process, [[entities/meta-fair-lab|fair]] trial, and [[concepts/secure|protection]] against self-incrimination.
- **Legal Representation:** The role of defense counsel in navigating legal-system complexities.
- **Appeals Process:** Post-conviction remedies when errors in trial procedure or evidence handling are alleged.
