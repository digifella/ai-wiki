# Shadows Adjustment

Shadows Adjustment is a post-processing technique in Lightroom Classic that targets the tonal values in the darker areas of an image during editing. Rather than applying adjustments globally across an entire photograph, this approach uses Lightroom's masking features to selectively modify only the shadow regions. This selective adjustment allows photographers to enhance detail and tonality in darker areas while preserving the tonal characteristics of midtones and highlights.

## Application in Landscape Photography

In landscape photography, shadows adjustment is particularly useful for recovering detail in deep shadows caused by high-contrast lighting conditions, such as midday sun or dense forest canopies. By utilizing the Shadow Masking tool, editors can isolate dark areas and increase exposure, clarity, or texture without affecting the brighter parts of the scene. This prevents the common issue of "blown out" highlights that often occurs when using global exposure sliders to brighten shadows.

The technique relies on the precision of Lightroom’s AI-driven masking algorithms, which distinguish between shadow tones and other elements in the frame. Users can refine the mask edges to ensure that the adjustments do not bleed into midtones or highlights, maintaining natural contrast and depth. This method is essential for creating balanced exposures in high dynamic range scenes where a single exposure cannot capture the full range of light.
