# Positron Discovery

## Overview
The discovery of the [[concepts/positron|positron]] in 1932 by [[entities/carl-anderson|Carl Anderson]] is a pivotal moment in the history of [[concepts/physics|physics]]. This groundbreaking finding confirmed the existence of [[concepts/antimatter|antimatter]] and laid the foundation for further research into [[concepts/granular-physics|particle physics]].

### Historical Context
[[entities/carl-anderson|Carl Anderson]]'s discovery came from studying [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]], leading to the identification of particles that were positively charged but behaved similarly to electrons. This observation led to the term "positron" being coined and marked a significant step in understanding antimatter.
