# Thu Vu

Thu Vu is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] known for technical tutorials and demonstrations on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] topics. Their work focuses on practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of modern [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools and frameworks]], with particular emphasis on making complex concepts accessible through hands-on examples.

## Knowledge Graph Construction

Thu Vu has created instructional content demonstrating how to build [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] from [[concepts/unstructured-text|unstructured text]] using a technical stack that includes [[entities/python|Python]], [[entities/langchain|LangChain]], and [[entities/neo4j|Neo4j]]. This [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] approach shows how to process raw [[concepts/text|text]] data and convert it into structured, queryable [[concepts/graph-databases|graph databases]]—a technique useful for organizing and retrieving information from large document collections.

## AI Research and Agents

Beyond [[concepts/structured-representation|knowledge graph construction]], Thu Vu explores emerging developments in [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]], including [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] similar to those discussed in frameworks like [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s [[concepts/automated-code-modification|AutoResearch]].
