# Pan Frying Bacon

Pan frying bacon is a stovetop cooking method in which bacon strips are placed in a skillet or frying pan and cooked over direct heat. As the bacon heats, its fat renders, creating both the cooking medium and much of the finished product's characteristic flavor. This method remains popular in home kitchens because it requires minimal equipment and technique, though it does demand attention to prevent burning.

The process typically begins by placing bacon strips in a cold or preheated pan, depending on the desired level of crispiness. Cooking over medium heat allows the fat to render gradually, ensuring even cooking without scorching the meat. Starting with a cold pan is often recommended for thicker cuts, as it helps render the fat more completely before the protein firms up, resulting in a more uniform texture.

Once the bacon reaches the preferred level of doneness, it is removed from the pan and drained on paper towels to absorb excess grease. The remaining rendered fat, often referred to as bacon grease, can be saved for other culinary uses due to its distinct savory flavor. Proper monitoring throughout the cooking process is essential, as bacon can transition from perfectly cooked to burnt in a matter of seconds.
