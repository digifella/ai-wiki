# Abbott Laboratories

A global [[concepts/health|healthcare]] company involved in the development and manufacture of medical devices, diagnostics, and pharmaceuticals.

## Key Product Incidents
* **Ritonavir [[concepts/quality-control|Quality Control]] Crisis**:
    * Involved the unexpected failure of the [[concepts/hiv-medication|HIV medication]] Ritonavir during quality [[concepts/power|control]] [[concepts/testing|testing]] approximately two years after its 1996 introduction.
    * The failure was characterized by unexpected polymorphism within the drug's [[concepts/molecular-structure|molecular structure]].
    * Further investigation and details are documented in Ritonavir [[concepts/crystalline-structure|Polymorph Crisis]]: Unraveling the Mystery of a Failing HIV Drug.
