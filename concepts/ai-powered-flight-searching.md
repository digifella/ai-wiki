# Ai Powered Flight Searching

Ai Powered Flight Searching utilizes large language models, such as ChatGPT, to assist users in identifying affordable airfare options. This method shifts the workflow from manual comparison across multiple booking platforms to a conversational interface where users describe their travel requirements using natural language. The system interprets these inputs to filter and synthesize data from various sources, aiming to present cost-effective solutions based on specific constraints like dates, destinations, and flexibility.

## Prompt Engineering for Flight Search

Effective use of this technology relies on precise prompt engineering. Users must provide clear parameters regarding origin, destination, travel dates, and budget limits. Including details about flexibility, such as willingness to adjust dates by a few days or consider nearby airports, significantly improves the accuracy of the results. The model processes these variables to generate tailored search queries or direct links to relevant booking engines.

## Limitations and Verification

While these tools streamline the discovery process, they do not have real-time access to live inventory or dynamic pricing algorithms used by major airlines. Consequently, the prices and availability indicated by the AI are estimates and may change rapidly. Users are advised to verify all final details, including baggage fees and cancellation policies, directly on the airline’s official website or a trusted booking platform before completing a purchase.
