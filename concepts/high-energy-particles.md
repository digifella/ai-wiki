# High Energy Particles

High energy particles are [[concepts/subatomic-particles|subatomic particles]] that possess extremely high [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]], typically measured in gigaelectronvolts (GeV) or higher. These particles are studied through cosmic ray observations and particle accelerator experiments. They originate from violent cosmic phenomena including [[concepts/supernovae|supernovae]], [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|active galactic nuclei]], pulsars, and [[concepts/black-holes|black hole]] accretion events. The detection and analysis of high energy particles provides insights into fundamental [[concepts/physics|physics]], particularly regarding the [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] of particle acceleration in extreme astrophysical environments.

Recent observations by the [[concepts/lhaaso|Large High Altitude Air Shower Observatory]] (LHAASO) have confirmed the existence of extremely powerful cosmic ray sources within the [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]]. These findings identify specific regions capable of accelerating particles to energies exceeding those previously recorded, challenging existing models of cosmic ray propagation and acceleration limits. The data collected by LHAASO helps distinguish between hadronic and leptonic origins of high-energy [[concepts/gamma-rays|gamma rays]], offering critical evidence for the role of [[concepts/supernova|supernova]] remnants and other [[concepts/compact-stellar-objects|stellar remnants]] in the cosmic ray spectrum.

Research into high energy particles bridges the gap between [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]] and [[concepts/granular-physics|particle physics]]. By analyzing the [[concepts/writing|composition]] and energy distribution of these particles, scientists can infer the properties of their sources and the interstellar [[entities/medium|medium]] through which they travel. This field continues to evolve with advancements in detector technology, allowing for more precise measurements of particle fluxes and helping to resolve long-standing questions about the origin of ultra-high-energy [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]].
