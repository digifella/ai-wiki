# Retrofit

**Retrofit** refers to the process of adding new technology or features to older systems, equipment, or vehicles. In the context of electrification, it involves converting [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine|internal combustion engine]] assets to electric powertrains or enhancing them with modern efficiency components, often as a cost-effective alternative to full replacement.

## Key Applications

### Heavy-Duty Transport
*   **[[concepts/battery-swapping|Battery Swapping]] Retrofit**: A specialized approach for heavy-duty trucks where the existing chassis is retained, but the powertrain is replaced with an electric system utilizing a battery swapping mechanism.
    *   **[[entities/janus-electric|Janus Electric]]**: An Australian company pioneering this specific retrofit methodology. Their [[concepts/solution|solution]] focuses on rapid battery exchange to minimize downtime for commercial fleets [[lab-notes/2026-07-30-Janus-Electric-Battery-Swapping-Retrofit-for-Heavy-Duty|Janus Electric: Battery Swapping Retrofit for Heavy-Duty Truck Electrification]].
    *   **Advantages**: Reduces [[concepts/charging-infrastructure|charging infrastructure]] burden and allows for [[concepts/247-operation|continuous operation]] in high-utilization [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
    *   **Analysis**: Detailed technical breakdown and viability assessment available in the [[lab-notes/2026-07-30-Janus-Electric-Battery-Swapping-Retrofit-for-Heavy-Duty|Janus Electric: Battery Swapping Retrofit for Heavy-Duty Truck Electrification]].

### General Industrial Retrofit
*   **Motor Upgrades**: Replacing inefficient induction motors with high-efficiency permanent magnet synchronous motors.
*   **Control Systems**: Installing variable frequency drives (VFDs) to optimize [[concepts/energy-consumption|energy consumption]] in pumps and fans.
*   **Insulation & Sealing**: Upgrading thermal and acoustic insulation in existing buildings or vessels to improve energy [[concepts/storing|retention]].

## Strategic Considerations
*   **[[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis|Cost-Benefit Analysis]]**: Retrofitting is often justified when the capital expenditure (CapEx) of new equipment exceeds the lifecycle savings of the retrofitted system.
*   **Compatibility**: Assessing mechanical and electrical interface compatibility is critical to ensure the new technology integrates seamlessly with legacy infrastructure.
*   **Regulatory [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]]**: Ensuring retrofitted systems meet current safety and emissions standards.
