# Citation Based Answers

Citation Based Answers refers to the integration workflow between [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], two [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] that work together to generate [[concepts/responses|responses]] grounded in specific source documents. This approach enables users to receive answers that are directly tied to citations from their uploaded materials, reducing [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] and improving [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in AI-generated content.

## Workflow Overview

The integration allows users to input notes, documents, or other reference materials into NotebookLM, which then leverages Google Gemini's language capabilities to process and understand the content. When queries are submitted, the system generates answers with explicit citations pointing back to the original source material, creating a traceable chain between the response and its supporting evidence.

## Practical Applications

This citation-based approach is particularly valuable for research, [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and [[concepts/automated-synthesis|knowledge synthesis]] tasks where verifiable sources are essential. By combining NotebookLM's [[concepts/document-management|document management]] with [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]'s generative capabilities, users can efficiently extract insights from their materials while maintaining transparency about where information originates. The workflow essentially transforms passive document collections into interactive, cite-able knowledge bases.
