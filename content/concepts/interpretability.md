# Interpretability

The study of making [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] models' internal processes understandable to humans. Critical for [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], safety, and trust in complex systems like LLMs.

- [[entities/anthropic]]'s [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] research on LLM interpretability: Video discussion (see [Tracing Thoughts in Language Models](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tracing-thoughts-language-model)) featuring researchers challenging LLMs as "merely glorified [[concepts/auto-complete|auto-complete]]" and exploring internal [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]].
- [[entities/anthropic]]'s work focuses on tracing thought patterns within LLMs to reveal decision pathways beyond surface-level [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]].
- [[entities/anthropic]] Discussion about how LLM think (2026-04-14): Video featuring four researchers discussing the [[entities/nature|nature]] of LLMs and interpretability, challenging the "auto-complete" view and exploring internal cognitive processes.
- [[entities/stuart-ritchie|Stuart Ritchie]] from [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/research-communications|Research Communications]] team opens the discussion on the [[entities/nature|nature]] of LLMs.
