The video [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] five new upgrades for [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] that enhance its power and [[concepts/integration|integration]] with other [[entities/google|Google]] tools.

### **1\. [[concepts/data-table-generation|Data Table Generation]]**

NotebookLM now allows users to turn any source—including [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] videos, websites, and uploaded [[concepts/files|files]]—into [[concepts/structured-data|structured data]] tables. You can customize these tables by defining specific columns and types of information to extract, such as movie rankings, directors, or major awards from a film review transcript.

### **2\. Enhanced Exporting and Source Integration**

[[concepts/notes|Notes]] created within a [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] can now be:

* **Exported directly to Google Docs or Google Sheets:** Useful for further formatting or data analysis.
* **Converted into sources:** Individual notes or groups of notes can be added back into the notebook as new source material, allowing the AI to reference and build upon your previously generated summaries.

### **3\. Notebook Integration with [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]]**

You can now import entire NotebookLM notebooks as sources into Google Gemini. This enables:

* **Combining multiple notebooks:** Synthesize information from different projects.
* **Asset generation:** Use the knowledge from your notebooks to have Gemini generate [[concepts/code|code]] for landing pages, web apps, or even images through its built-in tools like Canvas.
* **[[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|Deep research]]:** Augment your notebook data with real-time online research via Gemini.

### **4\. Powered by [[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]]**

NotebookLM’s search and chat functionalities have been upgraded to run on Gemini 3. This leads to significantly faster performance, more thorough and accurate analysis, and more natural, "human-like" interactions when chatting with your sources.

### **5\. Advanced [[concepts/conversation-history|Chat History]] and Sharing Controls**

Several management features have been added to streamline the user experience:

* **Chat History Management:** The ability to delete chat history within a notebook.
* **Finer Sharing Permissions:** Choose between sharing full notebook access or limiting collaborators to "chat only."
* **Configurable Chat Behavior:** You can now set specific goals for the chat (e.g., "Guided [[concepts/learning|Learning]]" or a custom persona like "PhD Student") and define your preferred response length.
