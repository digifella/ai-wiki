# Structureux

Structureux is a systematic methodology designed for the rigorous critique of responses, outputs, or creative works. It operates on the principle that critique is not merely a final judgment but an essential refinement step intended to enhance quality and reliability. By moving beyond surface-level evaluation, the process encourages a deliberate examination of content against established criteria to identify gaps, errors, inconsistencies, and areas requiring strengthening.

## Core Principles

The framework emphasizes multi-dimensional assessment, requiring the evaluator to analyze structural integrity, logical coherence, and factual accuracy simultaneously. This approach ensures that feedback addresses both the foundational arguments and the nuanced details of the work. The methodology prioritizes objectivity, guiding critics to distinguish between subjective preference and objective deficiencies in execution or reasoning.

## Application Process

Implementation involves a phased review where initial observations are categorized by severity and impact. Evaluators are instructed to document specific instances of weakness rather than offering generalized comments, allowing for targeted revisions. This structured feedback loop supports iterative improvement, ensuring that the final output meets the predefined standards of rigor and clarity established at the outset of the critique.
