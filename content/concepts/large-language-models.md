# Large Language Models

[[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are neural network-based systems designed to process and generate human-like [[concepts/text|text]]. Built on [[concepts/transformers|transformer architecture]], they contain billions of [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] trained on vast corpora of textual data from books, websites, and other written sources. This [[concepts/training|training]] enables them to learn statistical patterns in language, allowing them to predict and generate coherent text sequences across diverse topics and [[concepts/writing|writing]] styles.
