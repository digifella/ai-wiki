# Disease Mechanisms

Disease [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] refer to the biological processes and pathways that lead to the onset, progression, and [[concepts/presence|manifestation]] of pathological states. Understanding these mechanisms requires dissecting the complex interactions between genetic factors, environmental triggers, and cellular responses.

## Key Drivers and Complexity

*   **Cellular Complexity:** The human cell operates with extreme complexity, making traditional reductionist approaches insufficient for fully understanding disease etiology.
*   **Technological Integration:** Modern research relies on the convergence of three primary technologies to decode biological language:
    *   [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] for [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|predictive modeling]].
    *   [[concepts/crispr]] for precise genetic editing and functional validation.
    *   Single-Cell Sequencing for high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] mapping of cellular heterogeneity.
