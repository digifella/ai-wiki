# LA approach

A framework for critically evaluating information by explicitly identifying **Limitations** and **Assumptions**. Used to enhance the utility of AI-generated content, particularly when combined with the [[concepts/qec-framework]].

- **Core function**: Prevents uncritical acceptance of AI outputs by questioning underlying [[concepts/biases|biases]], data constraints, and unstated premises.
- **Application**:
  - After generating content via [[concepts/qec-framework]], list limitations (e.g., [[concepts/training-data|training data]] gaps, temporal constraints) and assumptions (e.g., implicit context, unstated user intent).
  - Refine conclusions by addressing these gaps (e.g., "This conclusion assumes no cultural bias in source data").
- **[[concepts/purpose|Purpose]]**: Transforms [[concepts/ai-generated-notes|AI-generated notes]] from passive summaries into actionable, critical knowledge.

Related concepts: [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical thinking]], [[concepts/qec-framework]], AI-generated content
