# Tool Issues

The organization faces significant operational friction stemming from limitations within its current tooling infrastructure. The existing proposal system has evolved into a primary source of workflow bottlenecks, directly impeding decision-making processes and slowing overall project progression. These inefficiencies result in extended review cycles and hinder the timely advancement of initiatives, creating a measurable drag on organizational velocity.

SharePoint, serving as the primary content management platform, further constrains operational efficiency through its rigid structure. This lack of flexibility complicates data retrieval and collaboration, exacerbating the delays caused by the proposal system. The combination of these two platforms creates a compounded effect where administrative overhead outweighs productive output.

To address these challenges, the organization is exploring the integration of artificial intelligence to accelerate analysis and automate manual tasks. By leveraging AI capabilities, the goal is to streamline the proposal review process and reduce the dependency on rigid content management workflows. This shift aims to restore agility to the decision-making pipeline and improve overall operational throughput.
