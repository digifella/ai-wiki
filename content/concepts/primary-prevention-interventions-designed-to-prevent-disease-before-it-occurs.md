# Primary Prevention Interventions Designed To Prevent Disease Before It Occurs

[[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary prevention]] refers to [[concepts/health|health]] interventions applied before disease occurs, targeting populations or individuals who are currently healthy. These interventions aim to reduce the incidence of disease by modifying risk factors or strengthening protective factors in the general population. Primary prevention differs from [[concepts/secondary-prevention|secondary prevention]], which detects early-stage disease, and [[concepts/tertiary-prevention|tertiary prevention]], which manages established disease to prevent complications.

## Types and Examples

Primary prevention interventions span multiple domains. [[concepts/population-health|Public health]] measures include [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]] [[concepts/software|programs]], water fluoridation, and sanitation infrastructure. Individual-level interventions include [[concepts/health-promotion|health education]] on [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]] and [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], smoking cessation support, and injury [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] through safety equipment or legislation. Environmental interventions modify conditions to support health, such as [[concepts/urban-planning|urban planning]] that encourages active transport or [[concepts/policies|policies]] that restrict harmful substances.

## Effectiveness and Implementation

The effectiveness of primary prevention depends on reaching sufficient population coverage and sustained behavior change or environmental modification. Some interventions, like vaccination, achieve protection through biological mechanisms independent of individual behavior. Others, such as dietary [[concepts/offering-counseling-services|counseling]], require ongoing individual engagement. [[concepts/adoption|Implementation]] typically involves collaboration across healthcare, [[concepts/public-health|public health]], education, and policy sectors, as well as community participation in identifying relevant risk factors and culturally appropriate solutions.
