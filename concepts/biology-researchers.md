# Biology Researchers

Biology researchers are scientists dedicated to the study of living organisms and life processes. Their work spans molecular biology, genetics, ecology, and [[concepts/evolutionary-biology|evolutionary biology]], often relying on interdisciplinary tools including bioinformatics and advanced computational modeling.

## Methodology & Tools
- **Computational Integration**: Increasing reliance on [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) for literature review, hypothesis generation, and code assistance in data analysis pipelines.
- **Recent AI Developments**:
  - [[lab-notes/2026-06-11-Anthropics-Claude-Fable-5-and-Mythos-5-LLMs-Launch-Acces|Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 LLMs: Launch, Access, Pricing, Safety]] highlights the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]].
  - **[[concepts/claude-fable-5|Claude Fable 5]]**: Generally available; potentially useful for general scientific [[concepts/summarization|summarization]] and broad biological queries.
  - **[[concepts/claude-mythos-5|Claude Mythos 5]]**: Higher capability tier; may offer enhanced [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] for complex genomic data interpretation or intricate [[concepts/experimental-design|experimental design]] [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], subject to access restrictions.

## Key Domains
- Genomics: Analysis of DNA sequences and gene functions.
- Proteomics: Study of protein structures and interactions.
- Ecology: Examination of organism-environment [[concepts/relationships|relationships]].
