# Glass Containers

## Overview
Glass [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]] are widely used for food [[entities/storage|storage]], [[concepts/preservation|preservation]], and recycling due to their non-porous nature and chemical inertness. Proper maintenance, including the removal of labels and [[concepts/adhesive-residue|adhesive residue]], is critical for reuse, aesthetic upcycling, or efficient recycling processing.

## Maintenance & Cleaning
Effective removal of adhesive and labels ensures containers are ready for reuse or recycling. Key methods include:

- **[[concepts/cooking-time|Heat Application]]**: Using a hairdryer to soften adhesive before peeling.
- **Solvent Use**: Applying oils (e.g., olive oil, coconut oil) or alcohol-based cleaners to dissolve residue.
- **Mechanical Removal**: [[concepts/scraping|Scraping]] with plastic cards or abrasive pads for stubborn labels.
- **Soaking**: Immersing containers in warm, soapy water to loosen adhesives.
