# Chicxulub Impact

The **Chicxulub Impact** was a catastrophic asteroid strike occurring approximately 66 million years ago at the Cretaceous–Paleogene boundary. The event is widely accepted as the primary cause of the [[concepts/cretaceous-paleogene-extinction-event|K-Pg extinction]] event, responsible for the demise of non-avian [[concepts/dinosaurs|Dinosaurs]] and ~75% of all plant and animal species on [[entities/earth|Earth]].

## Event Characteristics
- **Location**: Chicxulub crater, present-day Yucatán Peninsula, Mexico.
- **Impactor**: Estimated diameter of 10–15 km.
- **Energy [[concepts/deployment|Release]]**: Equivalent to ~100 million megatons of TNT.
- **[[concepts/immediate-impact|Immediate Effects]]**: Massive seismic activity, tsunamis, and global [[concepts/wildfires|wildfires]] ignited by ejected molten rock.

## Aftermath & Recovery
- **Impact Winter**: Ejection of dust and sulfur aerosols blocked sunlight, causing photosynthesis collapse and [[concepts/global-cooling|global cooling]] for months to years.
- **Food Chain Collapse**: Herbivores starved due to lack of vegetation; carnivores followed.
- **Recovery**: Transitioned Earth into the Cenozoic Era, often termed the "Age of [[entities/mammals|Mammals]]," as surviving mammalian and avian lineages diversified into vacant ecological niches.

## Related Concepts
