Health Care

Health Care encompasses the maintenance and improvement of health via the prevention, diagnosis, treatment, amelioration, or cure of disease, illness, injury, and other physical and mental impairments in people. It involves a broad spectrum of services, from primary care to specialized medical interventions.

Technological Integration & IoT

The integration of the Internet of Things (IoT) is transforming health care systems by enabling continuous monitoring, data-driven decision-making, and remote patient management.

Emerging Bio-Engineering Frontiers

Beyond digital integration, 2026 has seen breakthroughs in fundamental biological engineering aimed at altering cellular metabolism for therapeutic purposes.

  • Photosynthetic Mammalian Cells: Research led by Anton Petrov explores introducing photosynthesis into animal and human cells, bridging the traditional divide between plant and animal kingdoms. This approach aims to leverage light energy for cellular function, potentially offering novel pathways for disease treatment and metabolic support.
