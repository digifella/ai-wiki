# Hslcolor Mixer

The HSL [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]] is a targeted [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] tool in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that allows photographers to modify specific colors in an image based on their [[concepts/hue|hue]], saturation, and lightness values. Located in the [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]]'s color adjustment panels, it provides [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over individual [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] without affecting the entire image uniformly. This selective approach makes it particularly useful for refining [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]], or enhancing specific color elements in a composition.

## Core Function

The Color Mixer operates by isolating adjustments to specific hue ranges—typically red, orange, yellow, green, cyan, blue, magenta, and sometimes purple. For each color [[concepts/range|range]], users can independently adjust hue (shifting the color itself), saturation (increasing or decreasing [[concepts/color-saturation|color intensity]]), and luminance (brightening or darkening that color). This three-dimensional control allows photographers to [[entities/make|make]] precise corrections that would be difficult or impossible with global color adjustment tools.

## Practical Applications

The tool is commonly used for enhancing skin tones by selectively warming or cooling reds and oranges, correcting unwanted color shifts in specific areas of an image, and creatively grading photographs by emphasizing certain colors while subduing others. It works effectively in combination with other [[concepts/adjustments|Lightroom adjustments]] like the [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration panel]], which provides [[concepts/foundational-color|foundational color]] grading, allowing for a layered approach to color correction and creative color work.
