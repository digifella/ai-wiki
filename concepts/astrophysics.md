# Astrophysics

Astrophysics is the branch of [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]] that applies the principles of [[concepts/physics|physics]] to understand celestial objects and cosmic phenomena. It combines [[concepts/empirical-evidence|observational data]] from telescopes and satellites with theoretical models grounded in mechanics, thermodynamics, [[concepts/superheavy-nuclei-stability|nuclear physics]], and [[concepts/electromagnetic-interaction|electromagnetism]]. By analyzing the [[concepts/light|light]], radiation, and particles emitted by [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], galaxies, and other cosmic [[concepts/nodes|entities]], astrophysicists determine their [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]], chemical [[concepts/writing|composition]], and [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|evolutionary history]].

## Stellar Astrophysics

This subfield focuses on the life cycles of stars, from their formation in nebulae to their eventual death as white dwarfs, [[concepts/neutron-stars|neutron stars]], or [[concepts/black-holes|black holes]].

## Galactic Dynamics and Dark Matter

Recent observational data regarding the [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]]'s rotation curve presents significant challenges to both standard [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]] models and [[concepts/modified-newtonian-dynamics|modified gravity (MOND)]].

*   **Rotation Curve Anomalies:** New data indicates that the Milky Way's rotation profile defies current theoretical explanations, contradicting the traditional belief that the galaxy contains approximately ten times more dark matter than normal matter.
*   **Distribution [[concepts/ai-inference|Inference]]:** The distribution of dark matter, previously inferred from flat rotation [[concepts/curves|curves]], is now under scrutiny as new measurements suggest discrepancies in mass distribution models.
*   **Theoretical Implications:** These findings necessitate a re-evaluation of how [[concepts/galactic-structure|galactic structure]] is modeled, potentially impacting our understanding of [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]] and the nature of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] on galactic [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]].

For detailed analysis of this specific dataset and its implications, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Milky-Way-Rotation-Data-Challenges-Dark-Matter-and-Modif|Milky Way Rotation Data Challenges Dark Matter and Modified Gravity]].
