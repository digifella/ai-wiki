# Document To Slide Workflow

The Document To Slide Workflow is a process for converting written notes and documents into presentation slides using AI-powered tools, primarily Google NotebookLM and Gemini. This workflow leverages automated analysis and generation capabilities to transform source material into formatted presentations, reducing the manual effort required for slide creation and enabling faster iteration on content organization and design.

## Process Overview

The workflow typically begins by uploading source documents or notes into NotebookLM, which analyzes the content and identifies key concepts, arguments, and structural elements. Users can then prompt Gemini or similar language models to generate slide outlines, speaker notes, and organized talking points based on this analysis. The AI-generated structure provides a foundation that users can refine, reorganize, and customize according to their specific presentation needs.

## Benefits and Application

This approach is particularly useful for converting lengthy research documents, lecture notes, or meeting transcripts into coherent presentation formats without requiring manual extraction and reorganization of content. By automating the initial structuring phase, the workflow allows creators to focus on design, narrative flow, and delivery rather than the time-consuming work of identifying and formatting key points. The iterative nature of the process means presentations can be quickly adjusted or regenerated with different emphases or structures.
