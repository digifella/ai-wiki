# Self-Administered Treatment

**Self-administered treatment** refers to medical interventions where patients manage the delivery or administration of therapeutic agents without direct, continuous clinical supervision. This approach is increasingly relevant in chronic-disease-management, palliative-care, and experimental oncology contexts.

## Key Concepts
- **Patient Autonomy**: Empowers individuals to take control of their therapeutic regimen.
- **Risk Profile**: Requires rigorous patient education to mitigate risks of improper dosing, contamination, or adverse reactions.
- **Technological Enablers**: Relies on simplified delivery mechanisms (e.g., oral formulations, auto-injectors) and digital health monitoring.
