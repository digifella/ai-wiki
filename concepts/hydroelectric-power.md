# Hydroelectric Power

**Hydroelectric power** is energy derived from the force of falling or flowing water. It is a renewable [[concepts/energy-source|energy source]] that converts [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]] into electrical energy via turbines and generators.

## Key Concepts
- **Renewable Energy**: Relies on the water cycle, driven by [[concepts/solar-energy|solar energy]].
- **[[concepts/infrastructure|Infrastructure]]**: Requires dams, reservoirs, penstocks, and power stations.
- **Environmental Impact**: Alters local ecosystems, fish migration, and sediment [[concepts/flow|flow]].
