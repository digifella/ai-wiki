# Macbook Pro

The MacBook Pro is a line of laptop computers manufactured by Apple that runs macOS. Introduced in 2006, it represents Apple's premium portable computing offering, positioned above the standard MacBook in terms of performance, build quality, and price. The product line targets professional users and power consumers who require sustained computational performance in a portable form factor.

MacBook Pro models have evolved significantly since their introduction, particularly following Apple's transition to in-house silicon chips beginning in 2020. This shift to Apple Silicon, including the M1, M2, and M3 series, has enhanced energy efficiency and processing power, enabling the devices to handle demanding tasks such as running local language models via software like LM Studio for portable AI access.

The hardware configuration typically includes high-resolution Retina displays, advanced thermal management systems, and a variety of connectivity ports such as Thunderbolt and HDMI. Over the years, the design has maintained a consistent aluminum unibody construction while adapting to changes in input devices, such as the introduction of the Touch Bar and its subsequent removal in favor of physical function keys.
