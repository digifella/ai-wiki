# Kiwi

Kiwi is a beta camera application developed by [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] for [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]], exclusive to [[entities/iphone-13-pro|iPhone 13 Pro]] and later [[concepts/models|models]]. The app integrates directly with Lightroom's ecosystem, enabling users to capture and edit photos within a unified [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].

## Features

The application includes advanced computational photography [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], such as enhanced zoom functionality, improved night mode performance, and [[concepts/reflection-removal|reflection removal]] technology designed to eliminate unwanted reflections from [[entities/windows|windows]] and glasses in photographs. These features leverage machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] and computational processing to enhance image quality beyond standard [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] camera [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].

## Technical Background

During initial [[concepts/testing|testing]] phases, the app was reported to cause overheating on some devices, though Adobe addressed this issue in subsequent updates. The app remains in beta status, indicating ongoing development and refinement before a general release.
