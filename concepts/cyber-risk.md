# Cyber Risk

Cyber risk in organizational contexts encompasses threats to information [[concepts/security|security]], system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] arising from digital [[concepts/cybersecurity-threats|attack vectors]] and vulnerabilities. In contemporary business environments, these risks are increasingly compounded by the rapid deployment of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] solutions that operate outside formal [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks.

## Ungoverned AI and Shadow AI

Organizations frequently implement [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] and systems without comprehensive security protocols, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] oversight, or integration with existing risk management structures. These [[concepts/co-pilot-use|ungoverned AI solutions]]—sometimes termed [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]—create vulnerabilities through unvetted data handling, unauthorized API integrations, and systems operating outside organizational visibility. The decentralized nature of these deployments often bypasses traditional perimeter security, allowing sensitive data to exfiltrate or be processed by external models without consent.

## IBM's Five AI Risks and Human Consequence

Recent analysis highlights specific vectors where AI misuse leads to severe organizational and individual liability. As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-05-25-Summary-Report-IBMs-Five-AI-Risks-Career-Ending-Data-Lea|Summary Report: IBM's Five AI Risks & Career-Ending Data Leakage]], the convergence of AI capabilities and human error creates high-stakes failure modes:

- **Career-Ending [[concepts/data-leakage|Data Leakage]]**: Accidental inclusion of Personally Identifiable Information (PII), proprietary code, or confidential strategy in public [[concepts/ai-templates|AI prompts]] results in irreversible [[concepts/exposure|exposure]]. This violates [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] standards and data [[concepts/secure|protection]] regulations, leading to immediate termination and legal recourse.
- **[[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|Intellectual Property]] Compromise**: Using company IP to train or query external [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] may constitute unauthorized [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] or trade secret theft, exposing the organization to legal liability and competitors.
- **[[concepts/data-hallucination|Hallucination]] and Decision Error**: Reliance on AI-generated insights without [[concepts/verification|verification]] can lead to catastrophic business decisions based on fabricated data, undermining [[concepts/trust|trust]] and operational [[concepts/honesty|integrity]].
- **Compliance Violations**: Automated processes may inadvertently violate industry-specific regulations (e.g., [[concepts/gdpr|GDPR]], [[concepts/hipaa|HIPAA]], SEC rules) due to lack of [[concepts/contextual-awareness|contextual awareness]], triggering fines and reputational damage.
- **Security Bypass via Social [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]**: [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] can be used to craft highly sophisticated phishing or social engineering attacks, or conversely, [[entities/employees|employees]] may fall victim to AI-generated scams, bypassing traditional security [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]] training.

These risks emphasize that [[concepts/ai-risk-management|AI risk management]] must extend beyond technical controls to include strict [[concepts/data-politics|data governance]] [[concepts/policies|policies]] and clear individual [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] frameworks.
