# AI likeness

**AI likeness** refers to the fidelity and accuracy with which [[concepts/ai-models|artificial intelligence models]] replicate the visual, auditory, and behavioral characteristics of a specific individual. It encompasses [[concepts/ai-avatar-creation|digital-twin]], deepfake, and synthetic-media technologies that generate personalized representations for [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], communication, or [[concepts/simulation|simulation]].

## Key Dimensions
- **Visual Fidelity**: Geometric accuracy of facial features, skin [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], and lighting [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
- **Temporal Dynamics**: Realism of micro-expressions, blink rates, and head movements.
- **Audio-Visual Sync**: [[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] in lip-syncing and emotional [[concepts/tone|tone]] matching.
- **Behavioral Mimicry**: Replication of speech patterns, gestures, and personality traits.

## Recent Developments (2026)
- **[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] Omni Integration**: [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[entities/gemini-omni|Gemini-Omni]] model enables rapid [[concepts/character-generation|avatar generation]] with high temporal coherence.
- **[[concepts/speed|Speed]] vs. Quality Trade-off**: New workflows allow for [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] in under 15 minutes, raising concerns about [[concepts/verification|verification]] and consent.
- **Platform Shift**: Increased [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[entities/google-flow]] for streamlined avatar pipeline management.

## Case Study: Claire Ho Experiment
- **Source**: [[lab-notes/2026-07-24-Gemini-Omni-AI-How-I-AIs-Video-Avatar-Cloning-Experiment|Gemini Omni AI: How I AI's Video Avatar Cloning Experiment Report]]
- **Method**: Host [[entities/claire-ho|Claire Ho]] used [[entities/google-flow]] and Gemini-Omni to clone her video avatar.
- **Outcome**: Generated a "terrifyingly good" replica in 15 minutes, highlighting the diminishing barrier to entry for high-fidelity AI-likeness creation.
- **Implications**: Demonstrates the potential for widespread misuse and the urgent need for watermarking and provenance standards in synthetic media.

## Ethical & Technical Considerations
- **Consent**: Legal frameworks for biometric data usage in AI training.
- **Verification**: Development of deepfake-detection tools and content-[[concepts/authentication|authentication]] protocols.
- **Identity Theft**: Risks associated with unauthorized replication of digital-identity.
