# Google NotebookLM

## Overview
[[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] is an advanced tool designed for creating interactive and dynamic content within a [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] environment. It integrates seamlessly with other [[entities/google|Google]] services to enhance productivity and creativity.

## Features
- **Dynamic [[concepts/infographic-design|Infographics]]:** Create rich, interactive [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]] that can be easily shared or embedded.
- **[[concepts/integration|Integration]] [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]:** Seamlessly integrate with [[concepts/gemini|Gemini AI]], [[entities/google-sites|Google Sites]], and more for comprehensive workflows.
- **[[concepts/personalization|Customization]] Options:** Tailor your notebooks with custom CSS and JavaScript to fit specific needs.
- **Professional Presentation [[concepts/design|Design]]:** Apply polished [[concepts/templates|templates]] and styles using [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] AI to transform notebooks into presentation-ready content (see 2026 04 14 [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] Customizing Design for Professional Presentations via [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]).
