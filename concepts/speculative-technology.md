# Speculative Technology

**Speculative technology** refers to the conceptualization and design of technological capabilities that do not yet exist but are projected to emerge based on current trajectories in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], hardware miniaturization, and software evolution. It serves as a bridge between current [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] constraints and future [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] paradigms.

## Key Characteristics
- **Extrapolation:** Extends current trends (e.g., [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]], [[concepts/neural-implants|neural interfaces]]) to logical or radical conclusions.
- **[[concepts/storytelling|Narrative]] Framing:** Often presented through media, prototypes, or conceptual videos to explore [[concepts/societal-impact|societal impact]].
- **Refinement Focus:** Shifts from feature addition to systemic optimization and [[concepts/hidden-engineering|seamless integration]].
