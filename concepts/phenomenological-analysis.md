# Phenomenological Analysis

**Phenomenological analysis** is a qualitative research methodology rooted in phenomenology, focusing on the systematic investigation of lived [[concepts/experience|experience]]. It seeks to describe the essence of phenomena as they appear to [[concepts/conscious-thought|consciousness]], suspending preconceived notions through the process of epoché (bracketing).

## Core Principles

- **[[concepts/personal-experience|Lived Experience]] (*Erlebnis*):** Prioritizes subjective consciousness and individual perception over [[concepts/purpose|objective]] measurement.
- **Epoché:** The methodological suspension of judgment regarding the existence of the external [[entities/earth|world]] or pre-existing theories.
- **Horizonal Listening:** Engaging with data to uncover meanings that are implicit rather than explicit.
- **Intentionality:** The focus on consciousness being directed toward an object or phenomenon.

## Key Approaches

- **Transcendental Phenomenology:** Associated with Edmund Husserl, focusing on the structures of consciousness and the reduction of phenomena to their essences.
- **Hermeneutic Phenomenology:** Associated with Hans-Georg Gadamer and [[entities/martin-keen|Martin]] Heidegger, emphasizing interpretation, [[concepts/historical-context|historical context]], and the fusion of horizons.
- **Existential Phenomenology:** Explores human existence, freedom, and being-in-the-world.

## Methodological Process

1. **Data Collection:** In-depth interviews, reflective journals, or ethnographic observations.
2. **Horizontalization:** Treating all statements as equal value initially to avoid bias.
3. **[[concepts/coding|Coding]]:** Identifying meaning units related to the phenomenon.
4. **Cluster/Theme Development:** Grouping codes into themes representing the structure of the experience.
5. **Textural & Structural Description:** Describing *what* was experienced and *how* it was experienced.
6. **Synthesis:** Integrating textural and structural descriptions into a composite [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] of the essence.
