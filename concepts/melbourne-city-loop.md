# Melbourne City Loop

The [[entities/melbourne-city-loop|Melbourne City Loop]] is an underground rail line encircling the [[concepts/central-business-district|Melbourne CBD]]. Built in stages during the early 1980s, its primary purpose was to distribute passengers more evenly across the city center.

## Key Details
- **Construction:** Early 1980s
- **Function:** [[concepts/passenger-distribution|Passenger distribution]] within the CBD
- **Structure:** Underground loop
