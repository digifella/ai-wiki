# Medium

Medium is an online publishing platform founded to democratize digital publishing and enable writers to share long-form content directly with readers. The platform hosts articles and essays across diverse topics including technology, culture, business, and science, serving both independent writers and established publications. By removing traditional barriers to distribution, Medium allows authors to reach audiences without requiring intermediaries or media gatekeeping.

## Platform Features and Content

The platform primarily focuses on hosting long-form articles and essays rather than short-form social updates. It provides tools for writers to format text, embed media, and manage their audience through built-in analytics and membership options. The interface emphasizes readability and minimalism, allowing content to stand out without visual clutter.

## Community and Monetization

Medium operates on a hybrid model that supports both free access and paid subscriptions. Writers can earn revenue through the Medium Partner Program, which distributes a portion of subscription fees based on member engagement. The platform also facilitates community interaction through claps, highlights, and comments, fostering a networked environment for intellectual discourse.
