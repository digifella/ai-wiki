# Remove Background

The process of isolating a subject from its surrounding context, typically by creating a Mask or Selection. Modern workflows increasingly rely on AI to handle complex boundaries that traditional tools struggle with.

## AI-Driven Edge Refinement

Recent updates to [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] have introduced specialized [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] to address the "halo effect" and fine-detail preservation, particularly for hair and fur.

*   **[[concepts/enhance-edge|Enhance Edge]] Feature**: Introduced in [[concepts/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] 27.11.0, this tool uses AI to refine selection boundaries automatically.
    *   **[[concepts/hair-masking|Hair Masking]]**: Specifically optimized to detect individual strands and fine textures, reducing the need for manual brush refinement.
    *   **Halo Reduction**: Addresses the common issue of colored fringes or semi-transparent artifacts around the subject's edge.
    *   **[[concepts/generative-credits|Generative Credits]]**: Utilizes generative AI capabilities to fill in missing details or smooth transitions where data is ambiguous.
*   **Workflow Impact**: Significantly reduces time spent on manual Channel Masking or Pen Tool adjustments for complex subjects.
