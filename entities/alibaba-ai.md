# Alibaba Ai

Alibaba's AI department develops and maintains large language models and AI tools focused on practical applications in software development and enterprise use cases. The department is best known for the Qwen series of language models, which encompasses various versions designed to meet different computational requirements and specialized tasks. These models represent Alibaba's approach to providing accessible AI solutions across multiple industries and deployment scenarios.

## Qwen Code

Qwen Code is a command-line tool developed by Alibaba's AI department to support local, AI-assisted coding workflows. The tool enables developers to integrate Qwen language models into their development environments for code generation, completion, and related programming tasks without requiring cloud-based infrastructure.

## Development Focus

The department prioritizes making its AI models and tools practical for real-world deployment, with particular emphasis on supporting software development teams and enterprise applications. This focus reflects Alibaba's broader strategy of positioning AI as a productivity tool across its business ecosystem.
