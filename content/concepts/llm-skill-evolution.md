# LLM Skill Evolution

LLM [[concepts/skill|Skill]] Evolution is a framework for standardizing and advancing the [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] within [[concepts/agentic-systems|agent systems]]. It addresses the technical challenge of enabling [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to develop, share, and execute [[concepts/skills|skills]] consistently across different platforms and implementations. The framework emerged from collaborative efforts by major AI companies to establish common [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for skill representation and execution.

## Industry Standardization

In April 2026, [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]], [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], and [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] reached agreement on unified file formats for [[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI skills]], marking a significant step toward interoperability in [[entities/agent|agent]] systems. This standardization effort aimed to reduce fragmentation and enable skills developed for one platform to be more readily adapted or deployed across others. The unified format represents a shift toward [[concepts/agent-first-organizational-infrastructure|agent-first infrastructure]], where [[concepts/organization|organizational systems]] are built around [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] capabilities rather than traditional [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].

## Practical Implementation

The standardized approach facilitates skill evolution by establishing common specifications for how capabilities are defined, packaged, and executed. This allows development teams to focus on advancing skill functionality without managing multiple incompatible formats. The framework supports both the creation of new skills and the refinement of existing ones, enabling continuous improvement of agent performance across different domains and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
