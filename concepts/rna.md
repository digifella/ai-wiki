# Rna

RNA (ribonucleic acid) is a nucleic acid molecule present in all living cells that plays critical roles in gene expression and protein synthesis. Like DNA, RNA is composed of nucleotides with a sugar-phosphate backbone and nitrogenous bases. However, RNA typically exists as a single-stranded molecule rather than the double helix structure of DNA, and contains the sugar ribose instead of deoxyribose. These structural differences reflect RNA's distinct biological functions compared to DNA.

## Structure and Function

RNA molecules are built from nucleotides consisting of a ribose sugar, a phosphate group, and one of four nitrogenous bases: adenine, cytosine, guanine, and uracil. The presence of uracil instead of thymine is a key chemical distinction from DNA. The single-stranded nature of RNA allows it to fold into complex three-dimensional shapes, enabling it to perform diverse catalytic and regulatory roles beyond its function as a messenger.

## Role in Gene Expression

In the central dogma of molecular biology, RNA serves as the intermediary between genetic information stored in DNA and the synthesis of proteins. Messenger RNA (mRNA) carries genetic instructions from the nucleus to the ribosomes, where transfer RNA (tRNA) and ribosomal RNA (rRNA) facilitate the translation of these codes into amino acid sequences. Additionally, various non-coding RNAs regulate gene expression at transcriptional and post-transcriptional levels, influencing cellular processes and development.

## Evolution of CRISPR and Gene Editing

Recent advancements in biotechnology have leveraged RNA's capabilities for precise genome editing. Dr. Feng Zhang at the Broad Institute has been instrumental in the evolution of CRISPR technology, utilizing guide RNA to direct the Cas9 enzyme to specific DNA sequences. This application demonstrates how RNA's ability to base-pair with complementary sequences can be harnessed for targeted genetic modification, marking a significant shift in the field of genetic engineering and therapeutic development.
