# Electronic Visa System E Visa

The Electronic Visa System (E Visa) is [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]]'s official digital platform for processing visa applications. Launched to streamline Turkey's visa procedures, the system enables eligible foreign nationals to apply for Turkish visas entirely online without visiting a physical embassy or consulate. Applicants can complete visa application forms, upload required documentation, and receive visa decisions through a web-based interface accessible from any location with internet [[concepts/connection|connection]].

## Application Process

The E Visa application process requires applicants to create an account, fill out a standardized online form with personal and travel information, and upload supporting documents such as passport scans and photographs. After submission, the application enters a processing queue where Turkish immigration authorities review the materials. Applicants receive email notifications regarding their application status and decision. Once approved, the electronic visa is issued and linked to the applicant's passport number, eliminating the need for physical visa stamps or documents.

## Eligibility and Coverage

Not all nationalities are eligible for the E Visa system. Turkey maintains a list of countries whose citizens can apply through this digital channel, while other nationals must use traditional visa application methods through Turkish diplomatic missions. The E Visa typically covers short-term visits for tourism, business, and other temporary purposes, with processing times generally ranging from a few hours to several days depending on the application volume and complexity.
