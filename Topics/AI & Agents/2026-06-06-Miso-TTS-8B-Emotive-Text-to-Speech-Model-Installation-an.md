## Miso TTS 8B Emotive Text-to-Speech Model: Installation and Performance Review
**Clip title:** MisoTTS - Most Emotive [[concepts/tone|Voice]] Model in the World - Really?
**Author / channel:** Fahd Mirza
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=A7UPTQS5Dhc

### Summary
The video provides a detailed [[concepts/installation|installation]] guide and performance review of [[concepts/miso-tts-8b|Miso TTS 8B]], a "State-of-the-[[concepts/art|Art]] [[concepts/text-to-speech-model|Text-to-Speech Model]]" developed by Miso [[entities/labs|Labs]]. The presenter, [[entities/fahd-mirza|Fahd Mirza]], introduces the model by showcasing several [[concepts/tone|voice]] samples, some generated by Miso TTS 8B, and highlights the [[concepts/developer|developer]]'s claim that it is the "best ever model" for producing emotive, conversational speech with high fidelity and consistent voice continuation. The primary goal of the video is to install this model locally and test its capabilities, particularly its ability to handle emotional and multi-[[entities/speaker|speaker]] dialogues.

Technically, Miso TTS 8B is described as utilizing a dual transformer [[concepts/design|design]] with a large [[entities/llama|LLaMA]] 8B backbone for processing [[concepts/text|text]] and [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] frame embeddings. This is coupled with a smaller 300 million parameter auto-regressive decoder that predicts higher-order [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] codecs within each frame. The audio tokenization is handled by MIMI across 32 codebooks. The [[concepts/installation|installation]] process is demonstrated on an [[entities/ubuntu|Ubuntu]] system with a powerful [[concepts/nvidia-rtx|NVIDIA RTX]] A6000 GPU, emphasizing the necessity of substantial [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. The model itself is quite large, [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] 32.8 GB, and requires nearly all of the 48GB [[concepts/vram|VRAM]] available on the GPU during [[concepts/inference|inference]], indicating high resource consumption.

During the practical [[concepts/testing|testing]] [[concepts/phase|phase]], the presenter uses three different conversational prompts to evaluate the model's performance: a casual [[concepts/communication|dialogue]], an emotional breakup scene, and a movie-like [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] filled with fear and determination. In the first casual conversation, the generated audio was noted to be "bit fast" with noticeable "prosody issues" and a lack of naturalness. The emotional breakup scene, intended to showcase the model's emotive capabilities, also revealed "a lot of mistakes," with the presenter remarking that the emotions felt unnatural.

The final test, a dramatic movie-like dialogue, was deemed "pretty disappointing." The model struggled significantly with [[entities/speaker|speaker]] [[concepts/logical-conceptslogical-consistencyconsistency|consistency]], producing male and female voices that were "all over the place," and failed to convincingly convey the intended emotions. The presenter concludes that Miso TTS 8B does not live up to its claim of being the world's best emotive Text-to-Speech model, suggesting that prior models like "Sesame" offered superior performance in terms of naturalness and emotional delivery. The high [[concepts/hardware-requirements|hardware requirements]] coupled with the subpar emotional and [[concepts/conversational-fluency|conversational fluency]] lead to a critical assessment of the model's current state.
