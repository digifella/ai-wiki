# 3D Character

A 3D character is a digital model of a human, animal, or creature constructed using computer graphics software. Unlike 2D illustrations, 3D characters exist as three-dimensional data structures composed of vertices, edges, and polygons that form a mesh. This geometric foundation allows the character to be viewed and manipulated from any angle within digital space, making it suitable for animation, interactive media, and real-time visualization.

## Creation and Development

Creating a 3D character typically involves several sequential stages. Modeling establishes the basic geometry and proportions of the character's form using specialized software such as Blender, Maya, or ZBrush. Texturing adds surface detail and color information to the mesh, while rigging creates an internal skeleton that enables the character to move and pose. Finally, animation brings the rigged character to life through keyframe animation or motion capture data.

## Applications

3D characters are widely used across entertainment, gaming, film, and interactive media. They serve as playable avatars in video games, main characters in animated films, non-player characters in virtual environments, and digital actors in visual effects. Modern AI-assisted tools have streamlined aspects of character creation, from generating initial models to automating texture application, though skilled artists remain essential for refining detail and achieving intended aesthetics.
