# Dehaze effect

A digital adjustment tool used to mitigate or enhance the [[concepts/presence|appearance]] of atmospheric haze, fog, or mist within an image to alter [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]].

### Related Tools & Mechanics
- Operates as one of the three key "Presence" sliders in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]].
- Works in conjunction with [[concepts/texture|Texture]] and [[concepts/lightroom]] sliders to provide subtle, distinct enhancements to [[concepts/image-detail|image detail]] and mid-[[concepts/tone|tone]] [[concepts/contrast|contrast]].
