# Edge Portal

Edge Portal is a web-based service provided by the [[entities/asic-company|Australian Securities and Investments Commission]] (ASIC) that allows users to access company information and related services. The portal serves as a centralized platform for retrieving details about registered Australian companies and managing interactions with ASIC's corporate registry functions.

## Accessing Company Information

Users can access the Edge Portal by navigating to the designated ASIC portal URL and entering relevant company details, such as the Australian Company Number (ACN). Once accessed, the portal displays official company information maintained in ASIC's corporate registry, including company registration status and associated documentation.

## Purpose and Use

The portal is designed to provide convenient online access to company records that would otherwise require formal requests or in-person inquiries. It forms part of ASIC's broader digital service offerings for businesses, investors, and members of the public seeking to verify or review company details.
