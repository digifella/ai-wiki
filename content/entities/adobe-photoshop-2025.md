# Adobe Photoshop 2025

[[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] 2025 introduces an updated [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]], representing a refinement to the [[concepts/software|software]]'s [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. This tool is designed to enhance the way users can manipulate [[concepts/color-saturation|color saturation]] and vibrancy in their [[concepts/images|images]] with improved controls and functionality compared to previous versions.

The updated [[concepts/color-and-vibrance-tool|Color and Vibrance tool]] allows photographers and designers to [[entities/make|make]] targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to [[concepts/color-vibrancy|color intensity]] and saturation within images. The tool maintains the [[concepts/core-purpose|core purpose]] of its predecessor while incorporating interface improvements and potentially expanded functionality to meet contemporary editing workflows.
