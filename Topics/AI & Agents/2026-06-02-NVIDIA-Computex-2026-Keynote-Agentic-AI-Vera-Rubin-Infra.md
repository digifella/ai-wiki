## NVIDIA Computex 2026 Keynote: Agentic AI, Vera Rubin Infrastructure, PC Reinvention
**Clip title:** Nvidia’s Computex 2026 Keynote in Less Than 12 Minutes
**Author / channel:** CNET
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ugNnw4lAMWA

### Summary
[[entities/jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]], CEO of [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]], opened his [[entities/gtc-conference|GTC]] Taiwan keynote by declaring the arrival of "[[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]]" – intelligent [[concepts/software|software]] agents capable of performing complex, productive tasks. He detailed this new computing pattern where users explain their intent to an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]], which then generates [[concepts/code|code]] or utilizes tools to achieve the desired outcome, replacing traditional click-and-type interactions. This represents a fundamental shift towards a disaggregated and distributed computing model, where [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], powered by [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) and managed by a "[[concepts/harness|harness]]," [[entities/will|will]] orchestrate work across various domains, revolutionizing how we interact with technology and solve problems.

NVIDIA is positioning itself as a "full-stack [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] company," moving beyond just GPUs to provide comprehensive systems. Huang introduced "Vera Rubin," describing it not as a single chip but an entire end-to-end AI infrastructure. This includes Vera Rubin GPUs, [[concepts/nvlink|NVLink]] 72, Vera CPUs, Spectrum-X switches, Grace [[concepts/cpu|CPU]] trays, and BlueField-4 [[entities/storage|storage]] trays, all designed to build "AI factories." Notably, the "Vera" CPU is purpose-built for AI agents, contrasting with past CPUs designed for humans. It features incredible bandwidth (3.6 terabytes per second) and is integrated with Vera [[concepts/compute|Compute]] for orchestration and Vera BlueField Storage for high-[[concepts/speed|speed]] data access, showcasing NVIDIA's [[concepts/ambition|ambition]] to provide complete, liquid-cooled AI supercomputers.

A significant announcement was NVIDIA's collaboration with Microsoft to "reinvent the PC." This new era of personal computing will be powered by "[[concepts/nvidia-rtx|NVIDIA RTX]] Spark," a chip integrating a powerful Blackwell RTX GPU (with 6,144 [[concepts/compute-unified-device-architecture|CUDA]] Cores and 1 PetaFLOP of AI performance) and a custom 20-core Grace CPU developed with MediaTek, all fused with 128GB of unified [[concepts/memory|memory]] via NVLink. This [[concepts/integration|integration]] will enable [[entities/windows|Windows]] PCs to run both traditional [[concepts/software|applications]] and sophisticated AI agents, offering unprecedented performance and memory (with desktop models boasting up to 768GB [[concepts/ram|RAM]] and 20 PetaFLOPS of AI compute). Huang envisioned PCs evolving into intelligent companions, akin to R2D2 or C3PO, capable of running trillion-[[concepts/parameter-models|parameter models]] right on your desk.

Finally, NVIDIA is extending its AI agent [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] to [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] and autonomous systems, ushering in the "age of physical AI." The company unveiled "Cosmos 3," a [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation model]] for physical AI that enables robots to observe the physical world, understand events, and generate physics-accurate synthetic videos for training. For autonomous vehicles, "Alpamayo 2" was introduced as an open model for self-driving cars, with NVIDIA's [[concepts/motivation|DRIVE]] Hyperion platform already adopted by over 80% of global automakers. Furthermore, NVIDIA announced the "Isaac GROOT Humanoid Robot Platform," an open development platform for [[concepts/humanoid-robots|humanoid robots]], featuring Isaac Simulators, GROOT Open Models, and tools for [[concepts/language-data|training data]] generation, all running on the Isaac OS, providing a complete ecosystem for developing sophisticated robots.
