# Application Customization

Application customization refers to the process of modifying and adapting [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and tools to meet specific use case requirements. Within the context of [[concepts/open-source-machine-learning|Hugging Face]], an [[concepts/open-source-ai-platform|open-source AI platform]], [[concepts/customization|customization]] enables developers and organizations to tailor pre-trained models to their particular domains, datasets, and performance objectives without building models from scratch. This approach reduces development time and computational costs while allowing teams to leverage existing model architectures optimized by the research community.

## Common Customization Approaches

[[concepts/fine-tuning|Fine-tuning]] is the most prevalent customization method, where developers train a [[concepts/pre-trained-model|pre-trained model]] on [[concepts/domain-specific-data|domain-specific data]] to improve performance on particular tasks. Hugging Face provides libraries and tools that simplify this process, enabling practitioners to adapt models from [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], and other domains with relatively minimal code. Beyond fine-tuning, customization can involve adjusting model hyperparameters, modifying input [[concepts/data-preprocessing|preprocessing]] pipelines, or combining multiple models through ensemble methods to achieve desired performance characteristics.

## Practical Applications

Organizations use customization to address challenges such as [[concepts/sentiment-analysis|sentiment analysis]] in specialized industries, [[concepts/named-entity-recognition|named entity recognition]] for technical documents, or image classification for medical imaging applications. By starting with pre-trained models from Hugging Face rather than [[concepts/training|training]] from scratch, teams can deploy custom solutions more rapidly while reducing the [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] required. This democratizes access to advanced [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] for organizations with limited machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] expertise or infrastructure.
