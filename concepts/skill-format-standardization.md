- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Agent-First Approach]]**: The new standard emphasizes an agent-centric perspective where skills are designed for modular and scalable use across multiple [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] rather than being specific to individual users or devices.
- **Interoperability & Portability**: Standardizing skill formats enhances interoperability, allowing skills developed in one environment to work effectively in another without significant modification.
