# Pmi Frameworks

The Project Management Institute (PMI) frameworks constitute a collection of standardized methodologies designed to guide the planning, execution, and delivery of projects across diverse industries. These frameworks provide structured approaches to managing core project dimensions, including scope, schedule, cost, quality, resources, communications, risk, and stakeholder engagement. Grounded in documented best practices and refined through extensive organizational experience, these standards are widely adopted in enterprise settings to ensure consistency and predictability in project outcomes.

## Core Methodologies

The most prominent framework is the Project Management Body of Knowledge (PMBOK), which outlines processes and standards for traditional project management. It emphasizes a predictive approach where requirements are defined early and changes are managed through formal control systems. This methodology remains foundational for organizations requiring rigorous documentation and compliance, particularly in construction, engineering, and government sectors.

## Adaptive and Hybrid Approaches

In response to the need for flexibility, PMI has developed frameworks such as the Standard for Agile Project Management and the Standard for Program Management. These guides support iterative development and value-driven delivery, allowing teams to adapt to changing requirements more rapidly than traditional models. Hybrid approaches combine predictive planning with agile execution, enabling organizations to balance stability with responsiveness in complex environments.

## Governance and Standards

Beyond specific methodologies, PMI provides standards for portfolio and program management that align project outputs with organizational strategy. These frameworks focus on benefits realization, resource optimization, and strategic alignment. By adhering to these guidelines, enterprises can standardize their project management language and processes, facilitating better communication and performance measurement across global operations.
