# Quality Gates

Quality gates are structured checkpoints designed to systematically evaluate information or outputs before they advance within a process or reach stakeholders. They function as verification barriers that prevent flawed, ambiguous, or incomplete work from progressing without scrutiny. Rather than accepting outputs by default, quality gates require explicit assessment against predefined criteria covering accuracy, clarity, completeness, and relevance to the intended context or use case.

## Purpose and Function

The primary purpose of quality gates is to enforce rigorous critique mechanisms that identify incorrect, unclear, or incomplete information within a response. By establishing these barriers, organizations ensure that only verified and high-quality data moves forward, reducing the risk of errors propagating through downstream stages. This approach shifts the evaluation process from passive acceptance to active verification, ensuring that all outputs meet specific standards of quality before they are considered final.

## Implementation and Criteria

Effective quality gates rely on clear, measurable criteria tailored to the specific domain or output type. These criteria typically include checks for factual accuracy, logical consistency, and adherence to established guidelines. The implementation often involves a combination of automated tools and human review, where automated systems handle initial filtering for obvious errors, and human experts conduct deeper analysis for nuance and context. This layered approach ensures comprehensive coverage while maintaining efficiency in the evaluation process.
