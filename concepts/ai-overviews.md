# Ai Overviews

AI Overviews are synthesized summaries generated by artificial intelligence systems in response to search queries, displayed prominently at the top of search results pages. Google introduced this feature as part of its Search Generative Experience (SGE) to consolidate information from multiple sources into direct answers. This approach aims to reduce the need for users to visit individual websites by providing immediate, aggregated responses to user intent. The underlying mechanism relies on large language models to analyze and synthesize content from across the web.

The emergence of this technology has shifted the focus of digital visibility from traditional search engine optimization to Generative Engine Optimization (GEO). Websites must now adapt their content structures to be more easily understood and cited by AI agents. This involves providing clear, authoritative, and well-structured data that large language models can accurately parse and reference.

Adapting to this new landscape often requires technical tools to streamline the optimization process. Developers and SEO professionals utilize platforms like Claude Code to assist in refining content strategies and ensuring that website data is formatted in a way that maximizes the likelihood of being selected for AI-generated summaries. This adaptation is critical for maintaining organic traffic as search behavior evolves toward direct answer consumption.
