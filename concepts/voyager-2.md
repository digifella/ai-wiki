# Voyager 2

**[[entities/voyager-2|Voyager 2]]** is a space probe launched by [[entities/nasa]] in 1977 to study the outer planets. It remains the only spacecraft to have visited both Uranus and Neptune, and the only one to have visited all four giant planets.

## Key Discoveries

### Saturn
During its flyby in 1981, Voyager 2 provided the first close-up images of Saturn's rings and moons. A significant discovery was the [[concepts/polar-jet-stream|Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole: Mystery and Explanations]], a persistent, massive hexagonal cloud pattern at the planet's north pole.

- First observed by Voyager 2 in 1981
- Described as a stable, geometric cloud pattern
- Later extensively studied by the Cassini-Huygens mission
