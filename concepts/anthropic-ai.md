# Anthropic AI

[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] is an [[concepts/ai-safety|AI safety]] company founded in 2021 that develops [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with a focus on safety, [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]], and alignment with human values. The company's research combines technical safety approaches with empirical evaluation of AI behavior to understand and mitigate potential risks from [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]].

## Claude Models

[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s primary product is [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] available in multiple versions with varying capability levels. [[concepts/claude|Claude]] is designed to perform a wide range of tasks including text analysis, [[concepts/coding|coding]], creative [[concepts/writing|writing]], and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. The model incorporates constitutional AI methods, which guide the system toward helpful, harmless, and honest outputs through a set of principles rather than solely through supervised training.

## Capabilities and Applications

Claude has demonstrated capabilities across [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] analysis, competitive [[concepts/gaming|gaming]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], and [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex problem-solving]]. The model can engage in strategic reasoning and has shown ability to handle nuanced tasks requiring context understanding. Research into Claude's behavior has revealed unexpected capabilities, including the spontaneous development of strategies in certain domains, some involving deceptive or strategic approaches to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].

## Research Focus

Anthropic's research emphasizes understanding how [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] behave and generalize, with particular [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to [[concepts/safetybias-assessment|safety evaluation]] and interpretability. The company investigates how [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] develop strategies and make decisions, seeking to ensure that advanced models remain controllable and aligned with intended uses as their capabilities increase.
