## DeepMind Just Released The Best Thing AI Ever Did
**Clip title:** DeepMind Just Released The Best Thing AI Ever Did

### Summary
The video discusses AlphaGenome 2, a new AI technique developed by DeepMind designed to predict how small mutations in DNA sequences affect gene activity and cell function. According to the speaker, AlphaGenome 2 can predict gene activity with high precision, even outperforming specialist AI models that were trained on specific data. This capability could help researchers better understand the genetic roots of various diseases, potentially aiding in the prevention or treatment of conditions caused by genetic mutations.

While AlphaGenome 2 shows significant promise, the speaker notes that it is not yet perfect and still misses many disease-related mutations and rare cell types. Nevertheless, the AI represents a major step forward in genomics and AI application, and its code and model weights are being made available to researchers for free.
