# Depth Range Masking

Depth Range Masking is a feature introduced in Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] 18.3 that allows photographers to selectively apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] based on the depth information in an image. This [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] technique enables users to target specific planes of focus—such as foreground, subject, or background elements—without requiring manual selection tools. By leveraging depth data, editors can isolate adjustments to particular distance ranges within a photograph.

The feature was released alongside other enhancements to Camera Raw 18.3, including improved [[concepts/lens-correction|lens correction]] tools and a collection of [[concepts/film-presets|film-inspired presets]]. This update reflected Adobe's focus on streamlining the post-processing workflow by providing more sophisticated, [[concepts/automated-masking|automated masking]] capabilities. Depth Range Masking is particularly useful for [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], landscape editing, and other [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where selective adjustment of different spatial planes enhances the final image.

The tool integrates with Camera Raw's existing adjustment framework, allowing users to combine depth-based masks with standard editing [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], and sharpening. This capability reduces reliance on layer masks or external depth-mapping [[concepts/software|software]] for certain editing tasks.
