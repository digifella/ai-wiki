# AI Engineering

AI [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]] is an engineering discipline focused on the practical design, development, and deployment of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems at scale. It bridges the gap between theoretical [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] and [[concepts/production-grade-infrastructure|production systems]], addressing real-[[entities/earth|world]] implementation challenges including system [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], [[concepts/software-performance|performance optimization]], and maintainability. Rather than focusing solely on algorithmic [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], AI engineering emphasizes the complete lifecycle of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] from conception through operation.

## Core Components

The discipline encompasses the design and implementation of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]], and [[concepts/agentic-systems|agentic systems]]. Key engineering concerns include:

*   **[[concepts/ai-model-deployment|Model Deployment]] & Optimization:** Managing [[concepts/inference|inference]] latency, throughput, and [[concepts/ram-limitations|memory constraints]]. Recent benchmarks highlight the viability of [[concepts/local-control|local deployment]] for specialized tasks, such as the evaluation of [[concepts/quantization|quantized]] models like [[entities/qwen-36-27b|Qwen 3.6 27B]] for [[concepts/coding|coding]] assistance, as detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-07-14-Qwen-3.6-27B-Local-LLMs-TitleForge-Performance-Replacing|Qwen 3.6 27B Local LLM's TitleForge Performance: Replacing Claude Code]].
*   **[[concepts/enterprise-integration|System Integration]]:** Embedding AI capabilities into existing software architectures, ensuring seamless interaction between [[concepts/llm-systems|LLM pipelines]] and traditional application logic.
*   **Reliability & Safety:** Implementing [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]], monitoring, and evaluation frameworks to ensure consistent performance and mitigate hallucinations or unsafe outputs in production environments.
