## Definition
XAI refers to the effort in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research and development to create [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models whose decisions and processes can be understood by humans. This includes methods for improving [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]], and trustworthiness of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]].

## Key Concepts
- **Transparency:** Making the workings of an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] understandable.
- **Interpretability:** The ability to explain or present the output of a model in a comprehensible way.
- **[[concepts/trust|Trust]]:** Building user confidence that an AI system [[entities/will|will]] behave as intended.
