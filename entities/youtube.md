# Youtube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] in 2006. It functions as one of the internet's largest repositories of [[concepts/video-resource|video content]], allowing users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos. The service hosts a diverse range of material, including user-generated content, professional productions, [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]], and entertainment media.

## Core Features

Users create accounts to upload and manage videos through a [[concepts/creator|creator]] dashboard. The platform provides tools for video editing, analytics, and monetization, enabling creators to generate revenue through advertising and channel memberships. Viewers can interact with content via likes, comments, and subscriptions, while the recommendation algorithm curates personalized feeds based on viewing history and engagement patterns.

## Impact and Usage

As a primary source of online video consumption, YouTube has significantly influenced digital culture, media distribution, and online education. It supports multiple video formats and resolutions, catering to both casual viewers and professional broadcasters. The platform also facilitates live streaming, allowing real-time interaction between creators and audiences, and has become a critical infrastructure for independent media production and global communication.
