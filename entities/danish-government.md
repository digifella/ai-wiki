# Danish Government

The Danish Government is the executive branch of Denmark's system of government, operating under a constitutional monarchy framework. It is headed by the Prime Minister and comprises various ministries responsible for implementing policy and administering the state across areas including finance, defense, health, education, and environment.

## Archaeological Policy

The Danish government has authorized private metal detectorists to conduct systematic searches of farm fields for archaeological artifacts. This policy allows qualified individuals to search for and report discoveries of historical objects, contributing to the country's archaeological record while engaging the public in heritage preservation. Metal detectorists operating under this authorization are required to report significant finds to appropriate authorities to ensure proper documentation and study of discovered artifacts.
