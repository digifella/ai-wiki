# iPad Peripheral Integration

[[entities/ipad|iPad]] peripheral [[concepts/integration|integration]] refers to the technical and functional [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that allow external devices to connect to iPad [[concepts/models|models]] equipped with [[concepts/usb-30|USB-C]] ports. This [[concepts/connection|connection]] standard enables iPads to interface with a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of accessories and professional equipment, extending their functionality beyond built-in [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. The [[entities/usb-c-port|USB-C port]] serves as the primary interface for both data transfer and [[concepts/power|power]] delivery, supporting multiple protocols and device categories simultaneously.

## Common Peripheral Categories

iPads can connect to various peripheral types including [[entities/storage|storage]] devices, input accessories, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] interfaces, displays, and specialized professional equipment. [[concepts/external-storage|External storage]] solutions provide additional capacity for media and project [[concepts/files|files]]. Input peripherals such as keyboards, mice, and styluses enhance productivity workflows. Audio peripherals including microphones, headphones, and professional audio interfaces support creative and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] work. Display connectivity enables [[concepts/output|output]] to external monitors for expanded screen real estate.

## Technical Considerations

The integration of peripherals depends on both hardware compatibility and [[concepts/software|software]] support. iPadOS manages driver requirements and device recognition, with many standard USB devices requiring no additional installation. Power delivery specifications determine which peripherals can be both connected and charged simultaneously. Data transfer speeds vary based on the USB generation supported by the specific iPad model and the peripheral's capabilities. Some professional equipment may require specific iPadOS versions or [[concepts/companion|companion]] applications to function optimally.
