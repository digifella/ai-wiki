## 10 Circuit Simulation Websites Every Electronics Maker Should Know

### Summary
The video reviews 10 online simulation websites for building, testing, and analyzing electronic circuits without needing physical hardware. 

The speaker highlights several distinct simulation platforms suited for different skill levels and project types. **Falstad** and **EveryCircuit** are presented as user-friendly options for visualizing real-time voltage and current changes, with EveryCircuit offering an extensive community library. For more detailed analysis, **CircuitSim** utilizes a full SPICE simulation engine, supporting DC, AC, and transient analysis. **Tinkercad** and **Wokwi** are recommended for beginners and microcontroller projects, such as Arduino and ESP32 boards, allowing users to test code alongside breadboard setups. **de:volt** provides a virtual electronics lab with measurement tools like oscilloscopes and multimeters, while **CircuitVerse** focuses on digital logic design using logic gates and flip-flops. Finally, **designElectronics**, **Antika Circuit** (notable for its AI assistant features), and **Zen Circuit Lab** offer advanced tools for multi-view circuit design and complex systems like solar power setups.

In conclusion, the video emphasizes that while online simulators cannot entirely replace physical hardware testing, they provide accessible, cost-effective ways to learn electronics, experiment with circuit designs, and troubleshoot projects before physical assembly.
