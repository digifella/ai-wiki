# Codebase comprehension

Understanding the [[concepts/structure|structure]], functionality, and interdependencies of a [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/code|codebase]]. Critical for maintenance, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and [[concepts/feature-development|feature development]].

**Key aspects**:
- [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]] and [[concepts/design|design]] pattern recognition
- File/module navigation and dependency mapping
- Data flow and control path tracing
- Bottleneck identification and refactoring planning

## AI coding assistants comparison

The [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] landscape includes tools that aid codebase comprehension. A 2026 comparison highlights:

- **[[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]]** ([[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]):
  - Environment: Terminal-based (CLI) [[concepts/command-line-interface|Command-line interface]]
  - Strengths: [[concepts/full-stack-developer|Full-stack developer]] capabilities [[concepts/full-stack-development|Full-stack development]]

- **[[entities/github-copilot-agent|GitHub Copilot Agent]]** (GitHub/Microsoft):
