# Unconscious mind

The Unconscious mind comprises the [[concepts/cognition|mental processes]], structures, and information that operate outside of immediate awareness.

### Core Concepts
- [[concepts/the-mind|The Mind]] can be conceptualized as a private, "inner universe" or [[concepts/inner-space|Inner Space]] unique to each individual.
- This internal space serves as the seat for [[concepts/conscious-thought|Consciousness]], [[concepts/memory]], and internal processing.
- The depth and complexity of this internal space are central to defining Human-Animal Differences.
- Recent investigations extend these concepts to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], specifically examining whether models like [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] possess an [[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]] akin to human consciousness, including both conscious and unconscious [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]].
