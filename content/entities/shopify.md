## Shopify
### Overview
Shopify is a Canadian e-commerce company that provides a cloud-based platform for merchants to set up and manage their online stores. The company offers a suite of products and services designed to help businesses sell online, in-person, via social media, and [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] pop-up shops.

### Key Features
- **[[concepts/saas|SaaS]] Platform:** Shopify's core offering is its [[concepts/software|Software]] as a Service (SaaS) platform for building e-commerce websites.
- **POS Solutions:** Point-of-sale systems for brick-and-mortar stores.
- **Marketing Tools:** [[entities/email|Email]] marketing, abandoned cart recovery, and analytics tools to help merchants understand their customers.
