# Qwen Team

The Qwen Team is responsible for developing the Qwen series of large language models and related AI technologies under Alibaba's broader AI initiatives. The team focuses on creating both open-source and commercially available models designed for various applications, ranging from foundational language models to specialized systems across different modalities.

## Qwen3-TTS

The Qwen Team recently open-sourced the Qwen3-TTS family of models, expanding the Qwen ecosystem beyond text-based applications into text-to-speech synthesis. This release represents the team's effort to provide accessible tools for developers working on voice generation and audio-related AI applications.
