# Post Corporate World

Post Corporate World refers to a conceptual framework for how individuals might manage their digital lives and personal data independently, without relying on centralized corporate platforms. Rather than outsourcing data management, communication, and digital services to companies like Google, Meta, or Microsoft, the framework envisions individuals maintaining control over their own digital infrastructure. This shift represents a fundamental change in how people interact with technology and their own information.

## Personal AI Infrastructure (Kai)

Daniel Miessler developed the concept of Personal AI Infrastructure, referred to as Kai, as a practical approach to implementing Post Corporate World principles. Kai represents a system where individuals deploy AI agents and tools on their own infrastructure to handle tasks traditionally managed by corporate services—from email and document management to data analysis and personal organization. This framework was discussed in detail during a presentation hosted by Clint Gibler.

## Core Principles

The Post Corporate World framework is built on the idea of personal data sovereignty and operational independence. By maintaining their own digital infrastructure, individuals retain control over how their data is used, processed, and stored. This approach prioritizes privacy, autonomy, and the ability to customize tools and workflows without constraints imposed by corporate terms of service or business models dependent on user data monetization.
