## Winter Storm Concept

### Definition
A severe weather condition characterized by heavy snowfall and extremely low temperatures, often resulting in significant disruptions to daily life, infrastructure damage, and [[concepts/public-health|public health]] concerns.

- [[concepts/winter-weather|winter_weather]]

### Recent Incidents
- **2021 [[entities/texas|Texas]] Winter [[entities/storm|Storm]]** - A major winter storm in February 2021 led to widespread [[concepts/power-outages|power outages]] across the state, affecting millions of residents and causing significant infrastructure damage.

### Analysis and Insights
- The event was triggered by record low temperatures that overwhelmed the state's energy grid.
- [[concepts/power|Power]] plants failed due to ice storms and freezing weather conditions.
- The storm caused severe water shortages as pipes froze and burst, leading to major disruptions in supply.

  - Technical reasons for power plant failures and water supply disruptions.
  - Actions taken by [[entities/ercot|ERCOT]] ([[concepts/electric-reliability|Electric Reliability]] Council of Texas) in response to the crisis.
