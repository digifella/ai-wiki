# Chroma Context 1

[[concepts/self-editing-search-agents|Chroma Context-1]] is a [[concepts/efficient-rag|self-editing search agent]] designed to enhance the efficiency of retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems. As an agent-based approach, it automates the process of refining and improving [[concepts/document-retrieval|document retrieval]] in RAG pipelines, addressing performance limitations in traditional retrieval methods.

The system operates by iteratively evaluating and editing its search queries and retrieval strategies to better match user intent and retrieve more relevant information. This self-improvement capability reduces the need for manual query refinement and improves the quality of retrieved context used by language models in generating [[concepts/responses|responses]].

Chroma Context-1 represents an advancement in RAG technology by combining automated [[concepts/search-optimization|search optimization]] with agent-based decision-making, making retrieval-augmented generation systems more practical and efficient for real-world [[concepts/software|applications]].
