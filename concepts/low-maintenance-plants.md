# Low Maintenance Plants

Low maintenance plants are indoor plants grown in water rather than soil, designed to require minimal care and [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]]. These plants absorb nutrients directly from the water, eliminating the need for traditional potting soil, complex watering schedules, and frequent repotting. The hydroponic or semi-hydroponic growing method reduces the common problems associated with soil-based [[concepts/agriculture|cultivation]], such as overwatering, soil-borne pests, and nutrient imbalances.

## Common Species

Several plant species naturally tolerate water-based growing conditions. [[entities/pothos|Pothos]] and philodendrons are among the most popular choices, as they develop roots readily in water and tolerate low [[concepts/light|light]] conditions. Lucky bamboo, spider plants, and peace lilies are also commonly cultivated hydroponically. These species generally require infrequent water changes, occasional nutrient supplementation, and minimal pruning.

## Growing Conditions and Care

Water-grown plants thrive in indirect light and room temperature environments typical of indoor spaces. The primary maintenance involves changing or topping up the water regularly—usually every two to four weeks depending on water [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] and evaporation rates. Some gardeners use hydroponic nutrient solutions to provide essential minerals, though many plants grow adequately in filtered tap water. [[concepts/containerization-technology|Containers]] should allow roots to access oxygen, making clear glass or containers with adequate drainage important for plant [[concepts/health|health]].

## Applications

Low maintenance plants suit various indoor settings, including offices, apartments, and spaces with limited room for traditional gardening. They are also used in educational contexts to demonstrate basic [[concepts/botany|botany]] and hydroponic principles, and they appeal to gardeners seeking a simplified approach to indoor [[concepts/plant-domestication|plant cultivation]] without sacrificing visual appeal or air-purifying benefits.
