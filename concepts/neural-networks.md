# Neural Networks

## Overview
**[[concepts/ai-models|Neural networks]]** are computational models inspired by biological neurons, designed to recognize patterns through layered architectures. They form the backbone of modern [[concepts/vanishing-gradient-problem|deep learning]] and [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]].

## Paradigm Shift: Software 1.0 to 2.0
The development and application of neural networks represent a fundamental shift in how we approach [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]] in computer [[concepts/science|science]].

*   **[[concepts/software-10|Software 1.0]] (Traditional Programming):** Relies on explicit rules and [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] written by humans. The programmer defines the [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]], and the data is processed to produce output.
*   **[[concepts/software-20|Software 2.0]] (Neural Networks):** The "program" is the [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] itself, defined by its architecture and [[concepts/parameters|weights]]. Instead of [[concepts/writing|writing]] explicit rules, we provide data and let the network learn the mapping.

## Scaling and Emergent Capabilities
As neural networks scale in [[concepts/parameter-count|parameter count]] and [[concepts/training-data|training data]], they exhibit behaviors not explicitly programmed, known as emergent capabilities.

*   **[[concepts/gpt-3|GPT-3]] Breakthrough:** [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[entities/gpt-3|GPT-3]] demonstrated that massive [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] of language models leads to unprecedented performance and new capabilities.
*   **Emergent Properties:** GPT-3 showed abilities such as few-shot learning and [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex reasoning]] that were not present in smaller predecessors like [[concepts/gpt-2|GPT-2]].
*   **Implications:** This highlights the [[concepts/value|importance]] of scale in deep learning and suggests that increasing [[concepts/code-size|model size]] can unlock qualitative shifts in performance.
