## SpaceX Starship Flight 14: Orbital Insertion, Starlink Deployment, Controlled Splashdowns
**Clip title:** What Exactly Happened On SpaceX's 14th Starship Flight Test?
**Author / channel:** TheSpaceBucket
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=EKB1l82zV-E

### Summary
The [[concepts/starship-flight-14|SpaceX Starship Flight 14]] marked a highly significant milestone, achieving the vehicle's first [[concepts/orbital-insertion|orbital insertion]], successful deployment of 26 [[concepts/starlink-v3|Starlink V3]] satellites, and controlled splashdowns for both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage. The mission incorporated several upgrades, including reused [[concepts/heat-shield-tiles|heat shield tiles]] and [[concepts/engine-filtering|engine filtering]], reflecting continuous improvements. Despite some engine anomalies during both ascent and descent phases, the flight demonstrated Starship's robust design and mission adaptability.

The launch proceeded smoothly, with all 33 Raptor engines on the Super Heavy booster igniting and the vehicle clearing the tower without any holds. Although one booster engine shut down early during ascent, the Super Heavy's engine-out capability allowed the flight to continue nominally through Max Q and stage separation. Following separation, the booster executed a boost-back burn, though two engines failed to relight (one being the one that shut down early during ascent). Nonetheless, the booster performed a controlled soft splashdown in the ocean, a crucial first for the V3 variant, demonstrating precision maneuvers akin to a ground landing before its planned flight termination system activation.

Meanwhile, the Starship upper stage continued its journey towards orbit. During its orbital insertion burn, one of the three Raptor vacuum engines shut down prematurely. Despite this, mission control, after deliberation, deemed the vehicle healthy enough to proceed with a modified, shorter burn. This burn successfully propelled Starship into orbit, marking a historic achievement for the program. Following orbital insertion, Starship proceeded to deploy 26 Starlink V3 satellites from its payload bay, representing the first operational payload delivered by Starship.

After deploying its payload and completing fewer orbits than initially planned due to the early deorbit decision, Starship initiated its re-entry sequence. It performed a nominal deorbit burn, experienced peak heating upon re-entering the atmosphere, and executed a controlled belly-flop maneuver before reorienting vertically for its terminal descent. The Starship then performed a controlled, accurate splashdown in the North Pacific Ocean, showcasing its heat shield integrity. Overall, Flight 14 successfully achieved all major mission objectives, providing invaluable data and significantly advancing [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]]'s efforts towards fully reusable Starship and Super Heavy flights, with future missions expected to attempt catch landings.

#### Description
For the first time, early this morning, SpaceX launched and placed Starship in orbit. This led to the deployment of satellites in an operational orbit, followed by reentry and an eventual splashdown north of Hawaii in the Pacific Ocean. As for the booster, we saw the first soft splashdown on a V3 variant.

On this flight, there were a number of upgrades and changes in order to address various issues and make improvements where needed. This included reused heat shield tiles, along with engine filtering and general hardware alterations.
