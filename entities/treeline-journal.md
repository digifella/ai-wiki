# Treeline Journal

Treeline Journal is a content platform dedicated to ecological restoration, botany, and rewilding initiatives. The journal documents scientific and practical approaches to landscape regeneration, with particular emphasis on unconventional or counter-intuitive restoration methods. It serves as a resource for understanding alternative strategies in environmental management and native species recovery.

## Banks Peninsula Project

The journal is notably recognized for its coverage of ecological restoration work conducted by botanist Hugh Wilson in the Banks Peninsula region of New Zealand. Wilson's approach challenges conventional restoration practices by demonstrating how gorse, typically considered an invasive weed, can be utilized as a nurse plant to facilitate the recovery of native ecosystems. This work highlights the complex interactions between introduced species and native flora in post-disturbance landscapes.
