# Cirrus

## Overview
A prominent manufacturer of general aviation aircraft, known for integrating advanced avionics and safety features such as the Cirrus Airframe Parachute System (CAPS).

## Navigation & Avionics Context
The evolution of aircraft navigation has shifted from early auditory-based systems to modern glass cockpits. This progression is critical for understanding the operational context of modern Cirrus aircraft, which rely on sophisticated instrumentation for Instrument Flight Rules (IFR).
