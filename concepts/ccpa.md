# CCPA

**California Consumer [[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] Act** (CCPA) is a state statute intended to enhance privacy rights and consumer [[concepts/secure|protection]] for residents of California. It grants consumers rights to know what personal data is being collected, to opt out of the sale of their data, and to delete personal information held by businesses.

## Key Provisions
- Right to Know
- Right to Delete
- Right to Opt-Out of Sale
- Non-Discrimination
