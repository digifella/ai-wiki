# Slide Deck Conversion

Slide Deck Conversion refers to the process of transforming presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], with a focus on removing or avoiding watermarks that may appear in the output.

## Process Overview

The conversion workflow leverages NotebookLM's slide generation capabilities as a starting point, then imports or recreates those slides within Google Vids for video production. This approach allows users to repurpose text-based or image-heavy presentation content into video format while maintaining control over the final output's appearance and branding.

## Watermark Considerations

A key aspect of this conversion process is producing videos without watermarks. Google Vids may apply branding elements to generated content by default, so the conversion technique involves methods to eliminate or circumvent these additions, resulting in cleaner final videos suitable for [[concepts/professional-use|professional use]] or [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] without platform attribution.
