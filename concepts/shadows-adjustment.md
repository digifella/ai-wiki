# Shadows Adjustment

Shadows Adjustment is a post-processing technique in Lightroom Classic that targets the tonal values in the darker areas of an image during editing. Rather than applying adjustments globally across an entire photograph, this approach uses Lightroom's masking features to selectively modify only the shadow regions. This selective adjustment allows photographers to enhance detail and tonality in darker areas while preserving the tonal characteristics of midtones and highlights.

## Application in Landscape Photography

In landscape photography, shadows adjustment is particularly useful for recovering detail in underexposed areas such as shaded terrain, forest floors, or areas beneath overhanging rock formations. By isolating these regions through masking, photographers can increase exposure, clarity, or vibrance without affecting the overall exposure balance of the image. This preserves natural contrast and prevents the overprocessed appearance that can result from global shadow adjustments.

## Technique

The technique typically involves creating a mask that targets shadow tones using Lightroom's range masking or brush tools, then adjusting parameters such as exposure, contrast, or texture within that masked area. The precision of modern masking features allows photographers to refine the mask's boundaries and feathering to create seamless transitions between adjusted and unadjusted regions. This localized approach maintains the structural integrity of the photograph while improving visibility and tonal separation in shadow areas.
