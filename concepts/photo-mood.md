# Photo Mood

**Photo mood** refers to the emotional [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]] or psychological impact conveyed by an image through visual elements such as color, lighting, and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]]. It is a deliberate outcome of post-processing decisions rather than a random artifact.

## Strategic Color Grading

Effective mood manipulation often relies on specific technical approaches rather than generic presets.

- **Two-[[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]] Technique**: A strategic method involving the selective application of two dominant colors to shift the emotional [[concepts/tone|tone]] of a photograph.
- **Intentionality**: Impactful editing requires consciously deciding how a photo should "feel" before applying [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], rather than relying on automated tools.
- **Depth and Emotion**: Proper [[concepts/color-grading-panel|color grading]] adds visual depth and evokes specific emotional responses from the viewer.
