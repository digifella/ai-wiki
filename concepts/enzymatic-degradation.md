# Enzymatic Degradation

**Enzymatic degradation** refers to the breakdown of complex materials, particularly synthetic polymers like plastics, through the catalytic action of enzymes produced by living organisms. This process offers a sustainable alternative to traditional mechanical or chemical recycling methods.

## Mechanisms and Applications

*   **Polymer Breakdown:** Specific enzymes (e.g., hydrolase, lipase) cleave the chemical bonds in plastic polymers, converting them into monomers or oligomers that can be reprocessed or metabolized.
*   **Biological Agents:** Research focuses on identifying and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] microorganisms capable of efficient plastic consumption.
*   **Environmental Impact:** Addresses the accumulation of plastic waste, which has reached 8.3 billion tons since the 1950s.
