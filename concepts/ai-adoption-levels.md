# AI Adoption Levels

A framework describing the progressive stages of integrating [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] into workflows, ranging from basic gated access to autonomous "God Mode" [[concepts/productivity|productivity]].

## Core Framework: Claude Code Adoption Levels

Developed by [[entities/boris-cherny|Boris Cherny]], [[concepts/creator|creator]] of [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]], this model outlines the "Steps of AI [[concepts/adoption|Adoption]]" for users and organizations. It details how to ascend through [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] levels to maximize utility.

- **Gated Access**: Initial entry points where usage is restricted or requires specific permissions.
- **Progressive Unlocking**: Moving through tiers of capability as [[concepts/trust|trust]] and proficiency increase.
- **[[concepts/god-mode-productivity|God Mode Productivity]]**: The apex state of autonomous, high-[[concepts/velocity|velocity]] [[concepts/development-speed|AI-assisted development]].

## Key Concepts

- **Creator Insight**: The framework is explicitly designed to help users understand the mechanics of leveling up their interaction with [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].
- **Organizational [[concepts/computational-scaling|Scaling]]**: The levels apply not just to individual [[concepts/skill|skill]] but to how teams integrate these tools into broader workflows.
- **Productivity Arc**: The goal is to move from manual oversight to automated, high-leverage output.
