# Notebooklm Slide Transformation

NotebookLM Slide Transformation is a workflow that converts source documents into video content using two Google products in sequence. The process leverages Google's NotebookLM to automatically generate presentation slides from uploaded documents, then imports those slides into Google Vids for further refinement and video production. This two-step approach enables users to transform text-based information into visual media without manual slide design or video editing software.

The first stage involves uploading documents to NotebookLM, which analyzes the content and generates a structured slide deck. These slides serve as the foundational visual assets for the subsequent video creation phase. By utilizing NotebookLM's source-grounded capabilities, the workflow ensures that the visual output remains closely aligned with the original textual data.

In the second stage, the generated slides are imported into Google Vids. This tool facilitates the assembly of the final video by adding transitions, audio, and other production elements. The integration allows for the creation of unwatermarked video content directly from the slide deck, streamlining the path from document analysis to finished visual media.
