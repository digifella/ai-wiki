# Residence Visa For Business

A residence visa for business in Turkey is a long-term permit category designed for foreign nationals who intend to establish or manage commercial operations within the country. This visa allows holders to reside in Turkey while conducting legitimate business activities, serving as a distinct alternative to standard tourist or short-term visit visas. It is specifically tailored for entrepreneurs, investors, and executives who require legal residency to oversee their enterprises effectively.

Eligibility for this visa typically requires applicants to demonstrate a clear business plan and sufficient financial means to support their stay and operations. Applicants must often provide documentation proving the establishment of a company or a significant investment in an existing Turkish entity. The process involves submitting various legal and financial records to the local Directorate of Migration Management, ensuring that the proposed business activities comply with national economic regulations.

Holders of this permit are granted the right to live in Turkey for a specified period, usually renewable annually, provided the business remains active and compliant. This status facilitates easier travel within the region and provides a legal framework for conducting banking, leasing, and employment activities. It serves as a foundational step for foreign business owners seeking long-term stability and integration into the Turkish market without the constraints of short-term visitor regulations.
