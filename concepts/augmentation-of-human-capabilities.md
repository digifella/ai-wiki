# Augmentation Of Human Capabilities

Augmentation of [[concepts/human-performance|human capabilities]] refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to extend and enhance human cognitive and creative abilities rather than replace them. This approach positions AI as a complementary tool within workflows, where AI handles specific tasks—such as [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]], [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], or analysis—while humans retain oversight, judgment, and [[concepts/enterprise-strategy|strategic direction]]. The goal is to create complementary [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between human and [[concepts/machine-intelligence|machine intelligence]], where each handles tasks suited to their respective strengths.

## Implementation Through Personal Infrastructure

Effective augmentation relies on building [[concepts/aimm-ai-maturity-model|personal AI infrastructure]] that integrates [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] into individual workflows. This involves configuring AI systems to work with [[concepts/domain-specific-knowledge|domain-specific knowledge]], maintaining context across conversations, and establishing [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] where human judgment shapes system behavior. Rather than treating AI as a generic service, augmentation approaches treat AI as a customizable extension of personal capability—adapted to specific work domains, communication preferences, and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] standards.

## Scaffolding and Cognitive Support

AI augmentation functions as cognitive scaffolding, providing structure and support for complex [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] tasks. This can include breaking down problems into manageable components, generating multiple perspectives for consideration, maintaining organized information, or handling routine cognitive work that would otherwise consume human [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]]. The human retains [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] for synthesis, evaluation, and final judgment, while the AI provides structured support that increases the depth and breadth of what humans can accomplish.

Human-AI augmentation differs from automation in that it does not aim to remove humans from processes, but rather to shift human effort toward higher-judgment activities by automating lower-level tasks and supporting human [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] at scale.
