# Ai Driven Iteration

AI Driven [[concepts/iteration|Iteration]] is a design and development methodology that embeds [[concepts/ai-powered-tools|AI-powered tools]] directly into creative and productive workflows. Rather than treating conception, review, and refinement as separate sequential phases, this approach leverages AI assistance continuously throughout the working process. This integration allows practitioners to generate multiple variations, evaluate alternatives, and implement refinements at each stage, effectively compressing traditional [[concepts/systems|feedback loops]].

The methodology relies on platforms such as [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|Google Stitch]] to facilitate [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]] and real-time adjustment. By enabling immediate generation of design assets or code snippets, these tools reduce the latency between idea and execution. This continuous cycle of generation and evaluation supports a more fluid workflow where changes are tested and integrated incrementally, rather than waiting for a final review [[concepts/phase|phase]].

This approach is particularly relevant in domains requiring high [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]], such as [[concepts/coding|software development]] and digital design. It shifts the role of the practitioner from manual execution to strategic oversight and curation. The focus moves toward defining parameters and evaluating AI outputs, allowing for faster exploration of [[concepts/solution|solution]] spaces and more responsive adaptation to changing requirements.
