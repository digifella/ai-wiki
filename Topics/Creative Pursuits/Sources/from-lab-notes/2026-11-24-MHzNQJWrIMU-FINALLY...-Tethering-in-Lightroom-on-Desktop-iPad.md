## FINALLY... Tethering in Lightroom on Desktop & iPad
**Clip title:** FINALLY... Tethering in Lightroom on Desktop & iPad
**Author / channel:** Glyn Dewis
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MHzNQJWrIMU
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Glyn Dewis: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video demonstrates how to use the new tethered capture feature in Adobe Lightroom, available in Lightroom Desktop version 10.0 and Lightroom on iPad version 12.0. The speaker, Glyn Dewis, explains that while Adobe's documentation and iPad interface might suggest tethering requires the cloud, users can perform tethered shoots without involving the cloud.

The speaker walks through setting up tethered capture on both Lightroom Desktop and Lightroom on iPad. For Lightroom Desktop, he demonstrates connecting a camera via a USB-C cable, configuring session names, file naming, destination folders, presets, metadata, and auto-advance settings. He shows how camera settings are mirrored in Lightroom and how to use live view and crop overlays.

For Lightroom on iPad, the speaker explains the hardware requirements (iPadOS 18-compatible iPad with direct USB-C camera connection) and compatibility with camera brands like Canon, Nikon, Sony, and Fujifilm. He emphasizes a key workaround: by putting the iPad in airplane mode before starting a tether session, users can prevent RAW files from automatically syncing to Creative Cloud. He then shows how to export captured images directly to an external SSD and safely delete them from the iPad.

The video concludes that the new tethering features in Lightroom Desktop and iPad are a welcome addition that works quickly and efficiently, though he suggests Adobe should update the UI to prevent potential confusion regarding cloud syncing on mobile devices.

#### Description
Tethering has finally arrived in Lightroom Desktop and Lightroom on the iPad, but is it cloud only? Looking at Adobe's documentation and the iPad interface you'd think so, but it isn't. In this video I set up a tethered session in Lightroom Desktop shooting straight to a folder on my hard drive, then show the iPad workflow I use to tether with no cloud involved at all, and how to get the raw files onto an external SSD afterwards without anything syncing to Creative Cloud.
