# Cognitive evolution

The evolutionary trajectory and development of complex [[concepts/cognition|mental processes]], information processing, and the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of subjective [[concepts/experience|experience]] within biological organisms.

## Key Dimensions
- **[[concepts/inner-space|Inner Space]]**: The concept of [[concepts/the-mind|the mind]] as a private, "inner universe" unique to each individual.
- **Foundations of Subjectivity**: The mind functions as the primary seat for [[concepts/conscious-thought|Consciousness]], [[concepts/memory|memory]], and introspection.
- **Biological Divergence**: The investigation of the cognitive gap and fundamental qualitative differences between humans and animals.
- **[[concepts/storytelling|Narrative]] as Evolutionary [[concepts/algorithm|Algorithm]]**: Recent analysis suggests that [[concepts/human-stories|human stories]] function as a specific [[concepts/evolutionary-learning-algorithm|evolutionary learning algorithm]], a mechanism potentially missing in current [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial-intelligence]] architectures. This perspective posits that narrative structures are not merely cultural artifacts but fundamental cognitive tools for processing complex social and environmental data.
