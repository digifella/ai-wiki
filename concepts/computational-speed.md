# Computational Speed

**Computational [[concepts/speed|Speed]]** refers to the rate at which a system or model processes inputs to generate outputs. In the context of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs), this encompasses both raw [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] latency and the efficiency of [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks.

## Key Drivers of Speed
- **[[concepts/model-architecture|Model Architecture]]:** Optimizations in [[concepts/transformer-layers|transformer layers]] and [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] reduce token generation time.
- **[[concepts/hardware-acceleration|Hardware Acceleration]]:** Utilization of specialized GPUs/TPUs and [[concepts/quantization-techniques|quantization techniques]].
- **Agentic Capabilities:** The ability to execute multi-step workflows autonomously reduces human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] latency.
