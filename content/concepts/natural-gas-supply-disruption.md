# Natural Gas Supply Disruption

Natural gas supply disruption refers to any event that significantly reduces the availability of natural gas for use in heating, electricity generation, industrial processes, and other [[concepts/software|applications]]. Such disruptions can lead to widespread economic impacts and social issues.

### Key Points
- **Causes:** Major [[concepts/winter-storms|winter storms]], pipeline failures, geopolitical conflicts, maintenance shutdowns.
- **Effects:** Loss of heat and [[concepts/power|power]], disruption in manufacturing, transportation bottlenecks.
- **[[concepts/mitigation-strategies|Mitigation Strategies]]:** Diversification of supply sources, emergency reserves, alternative energy generation methods (e.g., coal, wind).

### Summary
- **Event Trigger:** Major [[concepts/winter-storm|winter storm]] in February 2021.
- **Geographical Impact:** Widespread [[concepts/power|power]] and water outages across [[entities/texas|Texas]].
- **Impact:** Loss of life, significant economic damage.

### Key Insights
- **Frozen Natural Gas Pipelines:** Contributed to the shortage of natural gas supply.
- **Generator Failures:** Due to freezing temperatures, many generators failed to operate.
- **[[concepts/ercot-electric-reliability-council-of-texas|ERCOT]] Actions:** Emergency measures implemented by ERCOT included rolling [[concepts/power-outages|blackouts]] and [[concepts/energy-conservation|energy conservation]] requests.
