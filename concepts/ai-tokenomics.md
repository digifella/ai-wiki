# AI Tokenomics

**AI Tokenomics** refers to the strategic management of [[concepts/llm]] usage to balance computational cost against output quality. It moves beyond basic [[entities/prompt-engineering]] to optimize the economic efficiency of AI interactions.

## Core Principles

*   **Cost-Quality Trade-off:** Understanding the marginal utility of [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] to maximize value per dollar spent.
*   **[[concepts/integrated-ai-systems|Multi-Model Workflows]]:** Routing tasks to specific models based on complexity and cost constraints rather than using a single default model.
*   **[[concepts/token-optimization|Token Efficiency]]:** Minimizing input/output token counts through precise [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] and [[concepts/context-management|context management]].
