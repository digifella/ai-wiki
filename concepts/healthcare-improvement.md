# Healthcare Improvement

**[[concepts/health|Healthcare]] improvement** refers to the systematic application of methods to enhance the quality, safety, efficiency, and equity of [[concepts/health-care|health services]]. In the digital era, this increasingly relies on data-driven insights, interoperable systems, and the ethical [[concepts/secondary-use|secondary use]] of [[concepts/patient-information|health records]] to inform policy and research.

## Key Drivers
- **Data Utilization**: Leveraging [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]] for [[concepts/population-health|population health]] management and clinical decision support.
- **Interoperability**: Ensuring seamless data exchange between providers via standards like HL7 and FHIR.
- **Policy & [[concepts/ethics|Ethics]]**: Balancing [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] with [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] protections and ethical oversight.

- **Secondary Use of [[entities/my-health-record|My Health Record]]**: Analysis by the Australian [[concepts/digital-health|Digital Health]] Agency highlights how de-identified data from My Health Record can [[concepts/ambition|drive]] public health outcomes through research and monitoring.   - Improved resource allocation based on population needs.

  - [[concepts/opacity|Transparency]] in data handling to maintain public [[concepts/trust|trust]].
