# Excel filtering

Core functionality in [[entities/excel]] used for data [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], analysis, and [[concepts/data-cleaning]].

## Techniques for Row Management
- **Filter**: Used to isolate specific criteria or identify blank rows for deletion.
- **[[concepts/go-to-special]]**: A rapid method to select all blank cells within a [[concepts/range|range]] to facilitate bulk deletion.
- **[[concepts/power-query|Power Query]]**: An automated approach for scalable [[concepts/data-transformation|data transformation]] and removing null/empty rows.
