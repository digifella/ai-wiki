# OWASP Overview
OWASP (Open Worldwide Application Security Project) is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] community focused on enabling organizations to develop, purchase, and maintain [[concepts/secure|secure]] [[concepts/software|software]]. It offers a wealth of resources including documentation, tools, methodologies, and application security [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].

## Key Projects
- **Top Ten Vulnerabilities:** Identifies the most critical [[concepts/web-application|web application]] vulnerabilities.
- **OWASP [[concepts/genai-security|GenAI Security]] Project:** Focuses on the unique security challenges introduced by [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] technologies and their applications in various sectors.
