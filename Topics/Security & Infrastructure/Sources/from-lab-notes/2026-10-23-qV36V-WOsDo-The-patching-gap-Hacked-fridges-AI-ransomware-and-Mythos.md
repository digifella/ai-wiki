## The patching gap: Hacked fridges, AI ransomware and Mythos finds
**Clip title:** The patching gap: Hacked fridges, AI ransomware and Mythos finds
**Author / channel:** IBM Technology
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qV36V-WOsDo

### Summary
This video is an episode of IBM’s weekly cybersecurity podcast, Security Intelligence, hosted by Patrick Lucas Austin. The episode features two expert guests: Claire Nunez, Creative Director at IBM’s X-Force Cyber Range, and MJ Vaidya, Global Senior Partner for Cyber Strategy and Risk at IBM.

The discussion centers on the growing role of AI in cybersecurity and ransomware attacks. Austin shares a statistic from Semperis's 2026 ransomware risk report, stating that 84% of ransomware attacks are now generated with the help of AI. Nunez and Vaidya discuss the implications of this trend, noting that AI is not only being used by hackers to launch more sophisticated attacks, but also by organizations to manage and mitigate risks. 

The speakers emphasize the importance of continuous patch management, security governance, and the challenges of managing third-party risks and AI agents in the workplace. Vaidya highlights the need for continuous controls monitoring and a resilient cybersecurity strategy, noting that patching and updates should be an ongoing process rather than a one-time event. Nunez points out that as AI agents become more prevalent, organizations must adapt their security playbooks and policies to address both internal and external threats effectively.

First, attackers hijack refrigeration controllers from Danfoss, Copeland and Dixell at French supermarkets, pushing cases to 50°C. Team82 flagged the flaws and Danfoss shipped a fix, yet thousands of controllers remain exposed online. Is planned downtime the price of staying patched?

Finally, Bank of America brings on 1,000 contractors to patch vulnerabilities found by Anthropic’s Mythos and other AI security models. The panel debates whether AI can fix what it finds, and why patching can’t be a one-time push.
