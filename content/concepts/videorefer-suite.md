[[entities/alibaba]]'s **[[entities/videorefer-suite|VideoRefer Suite]]** is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] video model ([[concepts/apache-2-license|Apache 2 license]]) designed to enhance [[concepts/large-language-models]] with fine-grained [[concepts/spatial-temporal-object-understanding|spatial-temporal object understanding]]. It enables LLMs to track and reason about specific objects within videos across time and space.

- **Core [[concepts/purpose|purpose]]**: Improves [[concepts/video-llms|video LLMs]] by enabling fine-grained [[concepts/spatial-temporal-object-understanding|spatial-temporal object understanding]] and tracking
- **[[concepts/license|License]]**: Apache 2 (fully [[concepts/open-source|open-source]])
- **Key capability**: Allows LLMs to reason about specific objects within video content [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] a detailed level
