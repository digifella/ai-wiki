# Full Self Driving (FSD) Supervised

[[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self Driving (FSD)]] Supervised is an advanced driver assistance system developed by [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] that operates [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] SAE Level 3 autonomy. The system is designed to handle driving tasks under specified conditions while requiring the driver to remain attentive and ready to intervene when necessary. FSD Supervised represents an intermediate stage between conventional advanced driver assistance systems and fully autonomous vehicles, combining automated [[concepts/power|control]] with mandatory human oversight.

## European Regulatory Approval

In April 2026, the Netherlands became the first [[entities/eu|European Union]] member state to formally approve Tesla's FSD Supervised for use on public roads. This approval marked a significant regulatory milestone, as it satisfied the stringent safety and liability requirements of European automotive [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]. The Dutch approval established a precedent for how other EU nations might evaluate and potentially authorize similar Level 3 autonomous driving systems, demonstrating that such technologies could meet existing European regulatory frameworks.

## Implications for EU Rollout

The Netherlands' approval of FSD Supervised has implications for the broader European automotive landscape. The decision suggests that other EU member states may follow with their own approvals, potentially accelerating the introduction of Tesla's [[concepts/autonomous-driving-technology|autonomous driving technology]] across Europe. However, [[concepts/regulatory-approval|regulatory approval]] in individual countries does not guarantee uniform adoption, as variations in national traffic laws and insurance frameworks continue to affect [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] timelines across different jurisdictions.
