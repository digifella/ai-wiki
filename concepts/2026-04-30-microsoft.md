# 2026 04 30 Microsoft

[[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] is a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington, founded in 1975. As of April 2026, the company operates across multiple business segments including [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud computing]] through [[entities/azure|Azure]], [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] software via [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]], [[concepts/gaming|gaming]] through Xbox, professional networking via LinkedIn, and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] services.

## Strategic Priorities

Microsoft's business strategy as of 2026 centers on three interconnected domains: [[concepts/cloud-based-computer|cloud infrastructure]], artificial intelligence integration, and [[concepts/quantum-computing|quantum computing]] research. The company continues to expand its Azure platform to support enterprise [[concepts/digital-innovations|digital transformation]], while embedding AI capabilities deeply into its productivity suite to enhance automation and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]]. Concurrently, Microsoft maintains significant investment in quantum computing research, aiming to achieve practical applications in [[concepts/complex-problem-solving|complex problem-solving]] and [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]].

## Corporate Structure and Operations

The corporation's [[concepts/revenue-streams|revenue streams]] are diversified across its core segments. Azure remains a primary driver of growth, competing directly with other major cloud providers. Microsoft 365 serves as the foundation for its productivity offerings, leveraging AI to improve [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]]. The gaming division, anchored by Xbox hardware and Game Pass subscriptions, alongside the professional networking platform LinkedIn, provides stable recurring revenue. This multi-segment approach allows Microsoft to mitigate risks associated with fluctuations in any single technology sector while maintaining its position as a leader in enterprise software and cloud services.
