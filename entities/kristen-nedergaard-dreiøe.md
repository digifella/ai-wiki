Kristen Nedergaard Dreiøe is a Danish amateur archaeologist and [[concepts/metal|metal]] detectorist active in Denmark's government-deputized metal detectorist program, which has significantly advanced archaeological knowledge of the country through public participation.

Key Contributions:
- Participated in the Denmark Metal Detectorist Program, a state initiative allowing trained amateurs to excavate artifacts in agricultural fields.
- Collaborated with [[entities/marie-aagaard-larsen|Marie Aagaard Larsen]] on fieldwork in southern Denmark's grain-growing regions, using [[concepts/metal-detectors|metal detectors]] to uncover artifacts.
- Contributed to revealing Denmark's historical landscape in unprecedented detail through systematic [[concepts/artifact-recovery|artifact recovery]].
- Featured in the [[entities/scientific-american|Scientific American]] article Denmark Let Amateurs Dig for Treasure—And It Paid Off, documenting the program's [[concepts/success|success]].
