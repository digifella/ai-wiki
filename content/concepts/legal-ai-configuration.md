# Legal AI Configuration

Legal AI configuration involves customizing [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] to perform [[concepts/legal-work|legal work]] while maintaining professional [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and protecting sensitive information. These tools can assist with legal research, document drafting, contract analysis, and case [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] when properly configured with appropriate [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and safeguards. Configuration requires establishing clear [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] around [[concepts/output|output]] format, confidentiality, [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] standards, and ethical constraints specific to legal practice.

## Custom Instructions and Prompting

Effective legal AI configuration relies on setting detailed [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] that define the model's behavior for legal tasks. These instructions should specify requirements for citation accuracy, the distinction between legal analysis and legal advice, use of appropriate legal [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], and the necessity of human review before any [[concepts/professional-output|professional output]]. Custom instructions allow practitioners to establish consistent response formats, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements, and quality standards across multiple interactions without repeating [[concepts/setup|setup]] information in each prompt.

## Privacy and Security Considerations

Organizations using [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] for legal work must address [[concepts/data-privacy-risks|data privacy risks]], particularly when handling confidential client information or proprietary documents. Configuration should include [[concepts/policies|policies]] on what types of information can be shared with public AI services, consideration of [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] options for sensitive work, and compliance with relevant regulations such as attorney-client privilege requirements. The choice between cloud-based and locally-hosted AI solutions depends on the specific sensitivity of materials being processed and institutional data [[concepts/governance|governance]] requirements.
