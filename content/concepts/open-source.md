# Open-Source

Open-source refers to [[concepts/software|software]] that is freely available for use, modification, and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] by anyone. It contrasts with proprietary or closed-source [[concepts/software|software]], which restricts access to its source [[concepts/code|code]].
