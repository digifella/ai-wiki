# Independent Assessment

An **independent assessment** is an [[concepts/purpose|objective]] evaluation of a system, model, or artifact conducted by a third party distinct from the original developers. This process ensures impartiality, verifies claimed capabilities, identifies hidden flaws (e.g., [[concepts/coding-flaws|coding errors]], bias), and validates [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] without vendor influence.

## Key Characteristics
- **Third-party neutrality**: Evaluators have no stake in the product's commercial [[concepts/success|success]].
- **Reproducibility**: Methods allow other researchers to replicate results.
- **Focus on real-[[entities/earth|world]] performance**: Moves beyond benchmark scores to practical utility and safety.
