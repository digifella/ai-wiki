# Custom Knowledge Bases

Custom Knowledge [[concepts/number-systems|Bases]] are specialized data repositories designed to enhance [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] through advanced retrieval and [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]]. Rather than relying on static, pre-trained knowledge alone, these systems allow [[concepts/agents|agents]] to access dynamically organized, domain-specific information tailored to particular [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. This approach bridges the gap between an [[entities/agent|agent]]'s inherent [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and the specific knowledge domains it needs to operate effectively.

## Agentic RAG and Knowledge Graphs

The most advanced implementations of Custom Knowledge Bases combine Agentic [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) with [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|Knowledge Graphs]]. In this [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], [[concepts/agentic-rag-systems|Agentic RAG]] enables [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to autonomously decide when and how to retrieve relevant information, rather than passively receiving query results. Knowledge Graphs provide structured representations of [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between entities and concepts, allowing agents to navigate complex information spaces and understand contextual connections. Together, these components create systems where agents can reason over connected data while dynamically accessing relevant knowledge as needed.

## Practical Benefits

This combined approach addresses fundamental limitations of [[concepts/traditional-rag|traditional RAG]] systems. Agents gain the ability to perform more sophisticated [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] tasks, including multi-hop [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] across interconnected data, validation of retrieved information against structured relationships, and [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of queries based on intermediate findings. Custom Knowledge Bases thus enable AI agents to handle more complex problems requiring specialized domain knowledge and nuanced understanding of relationships within that domain.
