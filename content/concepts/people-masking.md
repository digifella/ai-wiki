# People Masking

People [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] is an AI-powered feature in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that automatically generates masks for people in photographs. The feature uses machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] to detect and isolate human subjects, eliminating the need for manual [[concepts/mask-creation|mask creation]] through traditional selection tools. This allows photographers to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] selectively to people within an image while leaving the background unaffected.

## Single-Click Presets

The feature operates through single-click preset functionality, making it accessible to users of varying technical [[concepts/skill|skill]] levels. Rather than requiring manual drawing or complex selection processes, photographers can apply a People Masking preset to instantly create masks around detected subjects. This [[concepts/automation|automation]] significantly reduces editing time for common workflows involving people-focused adjustments.

## Practical Applications

People Masking enables photographers to quickly adjust [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], apply localized [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] corrections, or add effects to subjects while preserving the background. The [[concepts/ai-detection|AI detection]] adapts to different compositions, though effectiveness may vary depending on [[concepts/sharpness|image clarity]], subject positioning, and lighting conditions. The masked areas can then be refined further using Lightroom's standard adjustment tools if needed.
