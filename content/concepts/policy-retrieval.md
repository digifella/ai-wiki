# Policy Retrieval

A mechanism for dynamically accessing and delivering policy information in response to user queries, typically through [[concepts/automations|automated systems]].

- **Agent-based [[concepts/adoption|implementation]]**: [[entities/copilot|CoPilot]] [[concepts/agents|Agents]] (currently in preview) perform [[concepts/policy-lookup|policy lookup]] and [[concepts/response-generation|response generation]] as core functions.
- **[[entities/agent|Agent]] [[concepts/design|design]] requirements**: Must maintain friendly, helpful [[concepts/tone|tone]] with clear [[concepts/responses|responses]]; examples include [[entities/georgia-engel|Georgia Engel]]'s [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]] [[concepts/training|training]] agent for FAQs and task guidance.
- **Current state**: [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] remain in preview mode with limited [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
