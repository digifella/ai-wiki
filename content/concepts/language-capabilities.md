# Language Capabilities

Functional spectrum of AI systems to process, understand, generate, and interact via human language and related modalities.

## Key Domains
- **Textual [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]:** Semantic comprehension, [[concepts/code|code]] synthesis, logical [[concepts/inference|inference]], [[concepts/style|style]] adaptation.
- **Speech Processing:** [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|Automatic Speech Recognition]] (`ASR`), Text-to-Speech (`TTS`), acoustic modeling.
- **Embeddings & Retrieval:** Dense vector representation, [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]], clustering.
- **Multimodal Fusion:** Cross-modal alignment (text-vision-[[concepts/audio-modality|audio]]), unified representation [[concepts/learning|learning]].

- **[[entities/ibm|IBM]] [[entities/granite|Granite]] 4.1 Suite:** Release of [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] spanning language, [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], speech, and embedding domains [[lab-notes/2026-05-08-IBM-Granite-Speech-4.1-ASR-Models-Features-Accuracy-and|IBM Granite Speech 4.1 ASR Models: Features, Accuracy, and Enterprise Applications]].
- **Granite ASR Performance:** Specialized [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] [[concepts/models|models]] evaluated for high-[[concepts/speed|speed]] inference; analysis "Is This The Fastest ASR?" [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] competitive latency and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] metrics against enterprise benchmarks [[lab-notes/2026-05-08-IBM-Granite-Speech-4.1-ASR-Models-Features-Accuracy-and|IBM Granite Speech 4.1 ASR Models: Features, Accuracy, and Enterprise Applications]].
- **Enterprise Modularity:** Granite [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] emphasizes [[concepts/developer-readiness|production readiness]], customizable [[concepts/weights|weights]], and [[concepts/vertical-integration|vertical integration]] for [[concepts/secure-deployment|secure deployment]].
