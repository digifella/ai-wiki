# Wirtz Pumps
Wirtz pumps are spiral-shaped devices designed to lift water efficiently using a combination of mechanical and [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]] principles.

### Overview
- **[[concepts/design|Design]]**: Unique spiral design allows for the efficient lifting of liquids.
- **Functionality**: Operates through rotation, utilizing airlocks and [[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] challenges to overcome limitations in conventional pump designs.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/airlock|Airlock]] Challenges**: The inability of early Wirtz pumps to lift water significantly due to airlocks.
- **[[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|Hydrostatic Pressure]]**: The pressure exerted by a column of fluid at equilibrium, with respect to [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]]. Crucial in understanding the limitations and design improvements of Wirtz pumps.
