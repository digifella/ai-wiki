# Subterranean Living
Subterranean living is a form of habitat construction where homes and communities are built underground rather than above ground. This unique approach to housing has been adopted in various parts of the world for reasons ranging from environmental adaptation, [[concepts/inference-optimization|resource efficiency]], and cultural practices.

## Key Concepts
- **Geological Suitability:** The selection of areas with suitable [[concepts/geology|geology]] for subterranean living is crucial. Typically, regions with soft stone or soil formations are preferred.
- **[[concepts/climate-adaptation|Climate Adaptation]]:** Subterranean structures provide natural insulation against extreme temperatures. In hot climates like Australia’s [[concepts/australian-outback|outback]], they offer cooler refuge during summer and warmer shelter in winter.
