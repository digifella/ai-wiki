# Manus

Manus is an AI-powered tool designed to automate presentation creation. It operates within a category of [[concepts/generative-apps|generative AI applications]] focused on reducing the manual effort required to produce slideshows and visual presentations. Like other tools in this space, Manus leverages language models and design [[concepts/automation|automation]] to generate content and layouts from user input.

## Comparative Context

Manus has been evaluated alongside other [[concepts/consumer-demand-drivers|AI presentation tools]] including [[concepts/skywork|Skywork]] and general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] AI assistants like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]].
