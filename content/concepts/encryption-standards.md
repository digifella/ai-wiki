# Encryption standards

Standardized protocols and algorithms utilized in [[concepts/cryptography]] to ensure data confidentiality, [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and authenticity.

## Threats and Vulnerabilities
- **Quantum Acceleration:** Rapid advancements in [[entities/quantum-computing]] are significantly accelerating the timeline for breaking current [[concepts/cryptographic-algorithms|cryptographic primitives]].
- **[[concepts/q-day|Q-Day]] Projections:** The emergence of "Q-Day"—the point [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] which existing encryption becomes obsolete—is anticipated as early as 2029, according to [[concepts/quantum-computing|Quantum Computing]] Accelerates [[concepts/cryptography|Cryptography]] Threat: Q-Day Anticipated by 2029.
- **Mitigation Requirement:** The accelerating threat necessitates an urgent transition toward [[concepts/post-quantum-cryptography]] (PQC) to defend against quantum-enabled cryptanalysis.
