# Deep Learning Model

A deep learning model is a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] composed of multiple layers of interconnected [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] that learn hierarchical representations of data. These [[concepts/models|models]] [[entities/excel|excel]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] identifying complex patterns and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within large datasets by progressively refining abstract features through each successive layer. Deep [[concepts/learning|learning]] has become foundational to modern [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]], from [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] to [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]].

## Application in AI Agents

[[concepts/deep-learning-models|Deep learning models]] serve as the computational backbone for advanced [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]. When integrated into [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]], these models enable [[concepts/agents|agents]] to process information more intelligently, reason about complex problems, and generate contextually appropriate [[concepts/responses|responses]]. The combination of deep learning with multi-[[entities/agent|agent]] frameworks allows for the construction of sophisticated research and analysis systems that can decompose tasks, collaborate across [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]], and iteratively refine their outputs.

## Building Deep Research Agent Flows

Practical implementations of deep learning in [[concepts/agentic-systems|agent systems]] can be developed using [[concepts/no-code|no-code]] platforms like [[concepts/flowise|Flowise]] combined with [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] from providers such as [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]. These platforms enable builders to construct [[concepts/deep-research-agent|deep research agent]] flows—systems that perform comprehensive information gathering and analysis by orchestrating multiple specialized agents. This approach leverages deep learning [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] without requiring extensive custom code, making advanced [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] construction more accessible to practitioners.
