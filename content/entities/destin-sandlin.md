# Destin Sandlin

American [[concepts/science|science]] communicator and host of [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]], known for engineering experiments and educational content.

## Manufacturing in America Project
- **Project**: [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] (safer, more durable grill brush alternative to wire bristle brushes)
- **Duration**: [[concepts/four-year-experiment|Four-year experiment]] (2022–[[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]])
- **Goal**: Determine if entirely American-made products can be competitive in the marketplace
- **[[concepts/motivation|Motivation]]**: Personal [[concepts/connection|connection]] to [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]] (parents were [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]])
- **Documentation**: Full process detailed in [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] video series: [Smarter Scrubber: Manufacturing in America](https://youtu.be/3ZTGwcHQfLY)
- **Key Insight**: Explored challenges of domestic [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], labor, and [[concepts/cost|cost]] competitiveness
