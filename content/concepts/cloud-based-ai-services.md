# Cloud-Based AI Services

[[concepts/cloud-ai|Cloud-based AI]] services provide a scalable and flexible platform for deploying [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] without significant upfront infrastructure investments. These services [[concepts/range|range]] from [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing (NLP)]] to [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], making them accessible to developers with varying levels of expertise.
