# Developer

In this specific context, a developer is a professional who leverages Claude AI and structured documentation to streamline product development workflows. The core practice involves creating Markdown-based Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) that translate abstract product specifications into concrete, actionable tasks. This approach systematizes the conversion of high-level requirements into executable work items, thereby reducing friction and enhancing clarity in the development process.

## Workflow and Tools

The developer's workflow centers on generating task managers and subagents to automate and organize the execution of product builds. By utilizing AI capabilities, the developer converts the structured data within the PRDs into dynamic operational components. These components function as specialized agents that handle specific aspects of the development lifecycle, ensuring that the output aligns precisely with the documented requirements.

## Impact on Development

This methodology shifts the developer's role from manual coding to architectural oversight and prompt engineering. The use of Markdown-based PRDs serves as the single source of truth, allowing for rapid iteration and consistent generation of code structures. This reduces the cognitive load associated with context switching and ensures that the resulting product builds are directly traceable to the initial product specifications.
