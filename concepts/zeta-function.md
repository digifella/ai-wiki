# Complex Analysis

Complex analysis is the branch of [[concepts/mathematics]] concerning functions of complex-numbers. It explores properties such as differentiability (holomorphy), integration, and series expansions. A central feature is that complex differentiable functions are necessarily analytic and preserve angles locally, a property known as conformality.

## Key Concepts and Applications

*   **Analytic Continuation**: Many functions, like the [[concepts/riemann-zeta-function|Riemann zeta function]], are initially defined by series on limited domains but can be extended to larger regions of the complex plane while preserving their analytic nature.
*   **Conformal Mapping**: Holomorphic functions with non-[[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] derivatives act as conformal maps, preserving local angles and shapes. This property allows for the transformation of geometries in ways that are useful in [[concepts/physics|physics]] ([[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]]) and art.
