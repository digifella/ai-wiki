# Surgical Infrastructure

**Surgical Infrastructure** encompasses the physical facilities, technological systems, and procedural frameworks required to perform complex medical interventions. In the context of modern neurotechnology, this includes specialized operating theaters, robotic assistance systems, and sterile environments designed for high-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] implantation procedures.

## Key Components

- **Facility Requirements**: Clean-room standards, electromagnetic shielding, and real-time imaging integration.
- **[[concepts/robotics|Robotic Systems]]**: Automated needle delivery systems for precise electrode placement.
- **Sterilization Protocols**: Advanced protocols for handling [[concepts/neural-implants|neural implants]] and surgical tools.

## Recent Developments (2026)

- **[[entities/neura-pod-neuralink|Neuralink]] Advancements**:
	- Integration of [[concepts/brain-computer-interface]] technology into standard surgical workflows.
	- Shift towards [[concepts/transdural-surgery]] techniques to minimize invasive craniotomy requirements.
	- Enhanced patient impact metrics regarding recovery times and implant stability.
