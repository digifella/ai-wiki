# 2026 04 10 Evernote New Pricing Structure User Reactions And Migration Guidance

In April 2026, Evernote announced a significant restructuring of its subscription pricing model, consolidating its previous multi-tiered offering into a simplified two-tier system. The new structure consisted of a free basic plan and a single premium subscription tier. Evernote's leadership indicated that the redesign was driven by operational sustainability requirements and the need to allocate resources toward platform development and infrastructure improvements.

## Changes to Service Offerings

The revised free tier implemented several functional constraints to differentiate it from the premium offering. These limitations included reduced monthly note storage capacity, restrictions on cross-device synchronization frequency, and reduced access to certain collaborative features. The premium tier preserved most functionality previously available to higher-paying users, consolidating what had been multiple subscription levels into a single paid option with a unified feature set and pricing point.

## User Response and Migration Patterns

The announcement generated mixed reactions from the user community. Some users expressed concern about the reduced functionality in the free tier, particularly those who relied on unrestricted device synchronization and storage capacity. Others viewed the simplified structure as more transparent and easier to understand. The transition prompted a segment of users to evaluate alternative note-taking platforms, while some premium subscribers remained with the service based on their established workflows and data investment in the platform.
