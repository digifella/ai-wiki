# Author Channel MIT Sloan Management Review

MIT Sloan Management Review is a peer-reviewed business publication produced by the MIT Sloan School of Management at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology. The journal was established to bridge academic research and practical business application, publishing articles that combine scholarly rigor with actionable insights for managers and organizational leaders.

## Scope and Content

The publication reaches a global audience of executives, managers, and business professionals seeking evidence-based approaches to management challenges. Articles cover topics including organizational strategy, leadership, innovation, business economics, and operational performance. The journal draws on research from MIT's faculty and visiting scholars while also featuring contributions from business practitioners and consultants.

## Editorial Model

MIT Sloan Management Review operates as an open-access and subscription-based publication, making content available through both digital and print formats. The editorial process emphasizes translating complex research findings into language accessible to business practitioners, distinguishing it from purely academic journals while maintaining standards for accuracy and scholarly substance.
