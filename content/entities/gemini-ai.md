# Gemini AI
**Definition:**
[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] is a powerful [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] system developed by Google for enhancing the creation and transformation of digital content.

## Overview
- Utilizes advanced [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] techniques to streamline the process of converting static visual data into [[concepts/interactive-html-web-applications|interactive web applications]].
- Integrates seamlessly with other [[concepts/google-search|Google]] services such as [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] and [[entities/google-sites|Google Sites]], enabling users to create dynamic online experiences from existing [[concepts/infographic|infographics]] or [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]].

### Workflow Features
- **[[concepts/automation|Automation]]:** [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] can automatically detect elements within an [[concepts/infographic|infographic]] and convert them into interactive features.
- **[[concepts/ai-workflow|Customization]]:** Users have extensive control over the [[concepts/design|design]] and functionality of the generated [[concepts/web-applications|web applications]].
- **[[concepts/deployment|Deployment]]:** The tool facilitates easy deployment through Google Sites, making it accessible to both technical and non-technical users.
