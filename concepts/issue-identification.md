# Issue Identification

[[concepts/debugging|Issue identification]] is the process of recognizing, detecting, and categorizing problems, concerns, or gaps within documents, systems, or workflows. It serves as a foundational step in [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]] and [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]] workflows, enabling systems and operators to prioritize and address concerns systematically. This process consists of two complementary activities: detection, which discovers that something is problematic, and classification, which organizes [[concepts/challenges-discussed|identified issues]] by type, severity, domain, or other relevant attributes.

In the context of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]], this capability is demonstrated through tools like [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s AI co-pilot functionality for [[concepts/document-changes-analysis|legal document review]] in [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]]. The agent scans the document to detect potential [[concepts/legal-risks|legal risks]], inconsistencies, or missing clauses. Upon detection, it classifies these findings into specific categories such as liability [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] gaps, or drafting errors, allowing [[concepts/legal-professionals|legal professionals]] to quickly assess the nature and urgency of each issue.

By automating the initial stages of problem recognition, AI-driven issue identification reduces [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] and minimizes human error. It ensures that critical concerns are not overlooked and provides a [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]] for subsequent remediation steps. This systematic approach enhances the efficiency and accuracy of review processes across various domains where document [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] and compliance are paramount.
