# Google Vids

[[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] is an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]-powered [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool integrated into the [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] suite of productivity [[concepts/software|applications]]. The product leverages [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to streamline video production, enabling users to create videos more efficiently than traditional manual methods.

## Features and Integration

As part of [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]], [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] is designed to work alongside other [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]], allowing for seamless [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] [[concepts/integration|integration]]. The tool utilizes [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] technology to assist with various aspects of [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]], reducing the technical barriers typically associated with video production.

## Context within Google AI Products

Google Vids represents one of several AI product initiatives across Google's ecosystem. It is positioned alongside other AI-enhanced offerings including [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/google-search|Google Search]] enhancements, and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] as part of Google's broader strategy to integrate artificial intelligence into user-facing products and services.
