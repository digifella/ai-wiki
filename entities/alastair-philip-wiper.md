# Alastair Philip Wiper

Alastair Philip Wiper is a journalist and writer specializing in archaeology, cultural history, and scientific innovation. He has contributed to international publications, focusing on how traditional academic and professional disciplines adapt to incorporate unconventional methods and new participants. His work often examines the intersection of established fields with emerging practices.

Wiper has documented Denmark’s archaeology initiative, a program that formalizes the role of private metal detectorists in unearthing historical artifacts. This model represents an unusual approach to archaeological practice, integrating amateur enthusiasts into the formal discovery and reporting process. His reporting highlights how this system allows for the identification of significant finds while engaging a broader segment of the public in heritage preservation.
