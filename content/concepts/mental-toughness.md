# Mental Toughness

Mental toughness refers to an individual's capacity to manage stressors, maintain focus under pressure, and persist towards goals despite adversity. It is a key element in achieving success across various fields including sports, business, and personal life.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]**: The ability to recover from or adjust easily to misfortune or change.
- **Neuroplasticity**: The brain's capacity to form and reorganize synaptic connections, especially in response to [[concepts/learning|learning]] or experience.
- **BDNF (Brain-Derived Neurotrophic Factor)**: A protein that promotes the survival of neurons and encourages neurogenesis.
