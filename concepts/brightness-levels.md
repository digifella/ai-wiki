# Brightness Levels

Brightness in [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] refers to the overall lightness or darkness of an image. Adjusting brightness can alter the mood and [[concepts/tone|tone]] of a photograph, making it appear more vibrant or subdued. This concept is crucial for achieving visual balance and enhancing aesthetic appeal.

### Applications

Adjusting brightness levels impacts the overall [[concepts/presence|appearance]] of images in various [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]:

- **Color correction**: Enhancing colors by adjusting brightness.
- **Mood setting**: Altering the emotional tone through lightness or darkness [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

### Tools and Techniques
- **[[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s Brightness/[[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] Tool**:
  - Allows direct adjustment of brightness without affecting other aspects of an image, contributing to precise [[concepts/lightroom|color grading]] and editing workflows.
