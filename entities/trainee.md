# Trainee

A trainee is a person undergoing structured training or education within an institutional setting, typically working toward professional qualification or skill development. Trainees occupy an intermediate position between students and fully qualified practitioners, combining theoretical learning with practical experience under supervision. In academic and professional contexts, trainees work alongside more experienced practitioners while completing formal requirements for their chosen field.

Trainees are typically required to contribute meaningfully to the operational and educational goals of their host institution. Their role involves balancing active participation in professional duties with the acquisition of specific competencies under guidance. This dual focus ensures that practical application reinforces theoretical knowledge, facilitating a transition toward independent practice.

In the context of the College, the term "trainee" specifically denotes individuals who are subject to particular governance communications regarding allegations. A recent communication concerning governance matters was directed to both Fellows and trainees, highlighting the distinct administrative and ethical obligations associated with this status within the organization.
