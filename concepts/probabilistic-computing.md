# Probabilistic computing

**Probabilistic computing** refers to computational paradigms that utilize stochastic processes or probabilistic bits ([[concepts/p-bits|p-bits]]) to perform calculations, offering significant advantages in energy efficiency and hardware resilience compared to traditional deterministic logic. This approach is increasingly relevant for AI hardware optimization, particularly in addressing the escalating energy consumption of large-scale models.
