## NEW 🎉 Obsidian can finally manage content outside your vault.
**Clip title:** NEW 🎉 Obsidian can finally manage content outside your vault.
**Author / channel:** Vladimir Campos [EN]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9xFWNEW7BZs
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Vladimir Campos [EN]: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video discusses a new feature in Obsidian that allows users to open individual Markdown files outside of their vaults. The speaker, Vladimir Campos, demonstrates this by creating a Markdown document in a separate folder and opening it in Obsidian. He shows how Obsidian acts as a markdown reader/editor and how users can add code, images, and links to external files. 

Campos emphasizes that users no longer need to keep all their files inside their vaults. They can keep large files, like videos and images, outside the vault and still use them in Obsidian. He demonstrates how to link an image from outside the vault and even view it in Obsidian. 

In conclusion, Campos suggests that this new feature opens up a world of possibilities for users, allowing them to manage and reference large files outside of their vaults more efficiently. He encourages viewers to try it out and share their thoughts.
