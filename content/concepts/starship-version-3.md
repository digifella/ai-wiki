# Starship Version 3

## Overview
Starship Version 3 (V3) is the third major [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of the [[concepts/starship]] launch system, engineered to enhance rapid reusability, structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and thermal protection. V3 constitutes the primary upper stage configuration for Starship Flight 12, marking a critical step toward operational missions.

## Flight 12 Integration
- **Configuration:** [[concepts/version-3|V3 upper stage]] mated with Super Heavy 19 booster.
- **Status:** Active preparations and [[concepts/integration|integration]] [[concepts/testing|testing]] ongoing as of 2026-05-17.
- **Scope:** Flight 12 campaign includes validation of V3 upgrades and broader Space Industry developments.
- **Details:** Comprehensive analysis of V3 specifications, SH19 readiness, and flight objectives documented in [[lab-notes/2026-05-17-SpaceX-Starship-Flight-12-Preparations-V3-Super-Heavy-19|SpaceX Starship Flight 12 Preparations: V3, Super Heavy 19, and Space Industry Updates]].
