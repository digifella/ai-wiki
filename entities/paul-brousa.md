# Paul Brousa

**[[entities/paul|Paul]] Brousa** is an academic researcher specializing in [[concepts/data-management]], [[entities/big-data]], and [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]. His work focuses on establishing trustworthy frameworks for algorithmic [[concepts/governance|governance]] and information sharing in the context of open and linked data.
