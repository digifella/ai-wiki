# Emily Pulse It

Emily Pulse It is an entity associated with WEHI (Walter and Eliza Hall Institute) and its engagement with masterpieces in the context of [[concepts/art|art and science]]. The work centers on facilitating conversations about the intersection of artistic and scientific practice, bridging two traditionally distinct disciplines.

## Role and Focus

Through WEHI's initiatives, Emily Pulse It promotes [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] around how art and science can inform and enhance one another. This involves examining masterworks and creative output as they relate to [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific inquiry]] and understanding, fostering interdisciplinary exchange among researchers, artists, and scholars interested in exploring connections between these fields.
