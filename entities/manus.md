# Manus

Manus is an artificial intelligence-powered software application designed to automate the creation of presentations and structured visual content. It operates within the knowledge systems domain by assisting users in generating slides and organizing content, thereby reducing the manual effort traditionally required for slide design and structural planning.

The tool has been subject to comparative evaluation against other AI-driven presentation platforms, specifically Skywork and ChatGPT.
