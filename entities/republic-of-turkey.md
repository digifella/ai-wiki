# Republic Of Turkey

The Republic of Turkey is a transcontinental country located at the crossroads of Europe and Asia, with its capital in Ankara. Covering approximately 783,562 square kilometers, Turkey straddles two continents, with roughly 3% of its territory in Europe and 97% in Asia Minor (Anatolia). The country is bordered by eight nations: Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq, and Syria. Turkey's strategic location has made it historically significant as a cultural and political bridge between Eastern and Western civilizations.

## Visa Requirements

This page contains documents and information related to Turkey visas. Visitors to Turkey are typically required to obtain a visa unless they are citizens of one of the countries exempt from visa requirements. Turkey offers several visa categories for different purposes of travel, including tourism, business, and residence. Many nationalities can obtain an e-visa online, while others must apply through Turkish diplomatic missions. Requirements and procedures vary depending on nationality and the intended length and purpose of stay.
