# Multimodal AI

[[concepts/multimodal-ai]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/models|models]] capable of ingesting and/or generating data across various [[concepts/data-management|Data-Modalities]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/modality|Modality]]**: A specific data type or format used as input or [[concepts/output|output]].
- **Common Modalities**: Includes [[concepts/text|Text]], [[concepts/images|Images]], [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]], [[concepts/lidar|Lidar]], and [[concepts/thermal-imaging|Thermal-Imaging]].
- **Processing [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**: Models are distinguished by their ability to integrate and reason across these different data streams simultaneously.
