# Voice Design

[[concepts/tone|Voice]] [[concepts/design|design]] refers to the creation and [[concepts/customization|customization]] of synthetic voices for text-to-speech [[concepts/software|applications]]. The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family of [[concepts/models|models]], released as [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] software by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]], provides tools and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for voice design alongside related functionalities. These models enable developers and creators to generate natural-sounding speech from [[concepts/text|text]] while maintaining [[concepts/power|control]] over vocal characteristics.

## Key Capabilities

The [[concepts/instruction-control|Qwen3-TTS]] models support three primary features: voice design, [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]], and text-to-speech generation. [[concepts/ai-cloning|Voice cloning]] allows users to replicate specific voice characteristics from source [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], while the design functionality enables customization of vocal properties for newly generated speech. These capabilities work within the broader text-to-speech generation framework that converts written text into spoken audio [[concepts/output|output]].

## Applications

By making these tools open-source, the [[entities/qwen|Qwen]] team has made voice design technology more accessible to researchers, developers, and creators. This approach allows for experimentation and [[concepts/integration|integration]] of advanced [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]] capabilities into various applications, from [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] tools to creative projects requiring custom [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|voice generation]].
