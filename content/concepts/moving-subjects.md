# Moving Subjects

Photographing moving subjects requires precise autofocus and timing. Key techniques:

- **[[concepts/autofocus-mode|Autofocus mode]]**: Prioritize **[[concepts/continuous-autofocus|Continuous Autofocus]] (AF-C)** for tracking motion ([[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]]: AF-C mode).
- **Shutter [[concepts/speed|speed]]**: Minimum 1/500s to freeze motion (adjust based on subject speed).
- **Focus tracking**: Enable subject tracking (e.g., [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]]'s "AF-C" with subject recognition).

### Fujifilm Autofocus Modes
- **AF-S (Single)**: Locks focus on [[concepts/stationary-subjects|stationary subjects]] (not suitable for moving subjects).
- **AF-C (Continuous)**: Continuously adjusts focus for moving subjects (essential for this concept).
- **Manual (M)**: Disables autofocus; requires [[concepts/manual-focus|manual focus]] adjustment.

The Fujifilm Autofocus [[concepts/setup|Setup]] Guide: Modes, Features, and Optimization details these modes, though the guide emphasizes AF-S optimization (for still subjects). For moving subjects, AF-C settings are critical.
