# AI Platforms

AI platforms are software systems and services that provide artificial intelligence capabilities for use across various industries and applications. These platforms typically offer pre-built tools, APIs, and frameworks that enable organizations to integrate machine learning and AI functionality into their own applications and workflows without developing these systems from scratch. Common capabilities include natural language processing, image recognition, data analysis, and predictive modeling.

## Legal Applications

AI platforms have increasingly been adapted for use in legal work, where they support tasks such as document review, contract analysis, legal research, and due diligence processes. These applications leverage natural language processing to identify relevant information within large volumes of text, classify documents, and extract key terms and conditions. Legal professionals use such platforms to improve efficiency in routine analytical tasks, though human review and judgment remain essential for complex legal matters.

## General Characteristics

Most AI platforms operate as cloud-based services or software-as-a-service (SaaS) offerings, allowing users to access computational resources without significant infrastructure investment. They vary widely in scope, from general-purpose platforms offering multiple AI capabilities to specialized tools designed for specific industries or use cases. Organizations typically integrate these platforms through APIs or user interfaces, with varying levels of customization and training required depending on the platform and intended application.
