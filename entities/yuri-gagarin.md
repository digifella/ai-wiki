# Yuri Gagarin

**Yuri Alekseyevich Gagarin** (1934–1968) was a Soviet pilot and cosmonaut. He was the **Humanity** to journey into outer space, marking a pivotal moment in [[entities/the-space-race|the Space Race]].

## Key Achievements
- **Vostok 1 Mission (1961):** On April 12, 1961, Gagarin orbited the [[entities/earth|Earth]], completing one [[concepts/revolution|revolution]] in 108 minutes.
- **Historical Significance:** His flight proved that humans could survive and function in space, providing critical data for subsequent [[concepts/software|programs]].
- **Legacy:** His success pressured the [[entities/united-states|United States]] to accelerate its own space efforts, including [[concepts/project-gemini|NASA Project Gemini: Bridging Mercury and Apollo, Mastering Spaceflight Challenges]] and the eventual [[concepts/apollo-mission|Apollo program]].
