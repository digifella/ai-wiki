# Daniel Jindoo

## Overview
Daniel Jindoo is a prominent figure in discussions about AI and its impact on [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and [[concepts/security|security]]. His work often delves into the complexities of local versus [[concepts/cloud-based-ai|cloud-based AI]] systems.

### Notable Works and Insights
- **[[concepts/running|Running]] [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI Agents]] Locally = Safe...? Think Again**
  - **Clip title:** Running AI on Your Machine Does Not Make It Private
  - **Author / channel:** Daniel Jindoo
  - **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GWUnDzzkE

### Summary of Video Insights
- Debunks the myth that hosting AI locally guarantees [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
- Uses a compelling analogy to explain differences between cloud and [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]]: renting an apartment (cloud), where the landlord holds a copy of your keys, vs. owning your own house (local).
- Highlights potential risks in [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] setups, emphasizing the need for robust [[concepts/security|security]] measures.
