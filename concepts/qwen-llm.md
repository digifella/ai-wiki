# Qwen LLM

**[[entities/alibaba-qwen|Qwen]]** is a series of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] developed by [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]] Group's Tongyi Lab. The family includes variants optimized for text, code, [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], and [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], known for competitive [[concepts/benchmark-performance|benchmark performance]] and [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] availability.

## Key Developments & Innovations

- **SWiReasoning Integration (Rio de Janeiro Government)**: In June 2026, the Municipal Company of Rio de Janeiro City Government demonstrated a post-training [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] using [[entities/qwen|Qwen]].
	- Implemented **SWiReasoning** techniques to enhance logical deduction capabilities.
	- Highlighted as a surprising government-led AI [[concepts/llm-optimization|model optimization]] case study.
	- See full details: [[lab-notes/2026-06-14-Rio-de-Janeiro-Governments-AI-Innovation-SWiReasoning-in|Rio de Janeiro Government's AI Innovation: SWiReasoning in Qwen LLM]]

## Model Architecture & Capabilities

- **Multimodal**: Supports text, image, and video analysis.
- **Long Context**: Capable of processing extensive document contexts (up to 256k [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] in newer iterations).
- **Agentic [[concepts/function-calling|Function Calling]]**: Designed for complex [[concepts/deep-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]] and tool usage.
