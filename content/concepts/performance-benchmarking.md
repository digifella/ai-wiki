# Performance Benchmarking in AI and LLMs

Performance [[concepts/benchmark-testing|benchmarking]] is the process of evaluating and comparing the effectiveness of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] models, particularly [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]], based on predefined metrics. This evaluation can include aspects such as [[concepts/algorithm-efficiency|computational efficiency]], response [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], context understanding, [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] features, and cost-effectiveness.
