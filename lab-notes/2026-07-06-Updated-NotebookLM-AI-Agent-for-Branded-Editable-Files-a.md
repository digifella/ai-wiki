## Updated NotebookLM: AI Agent for Branded, Editable Files and Data Analysis
**Clip title:** UPDATED: [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] Just Became a Completely Different Tool

### Summary
[[concepts/notebooklm|Google NotebookLM]] has received a significant update, transforming it from a tool primarily used for summarizing information into a powerful [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] capable of generating [[concepts/editable-files|editable files]], deeply analyzing data, and conducting comprehensive research. This evolution centers around making generated outputs genuinely impressive and actionable, moving beyond generic summaries to help users "finish the work" efficiently. The video emphasizes key strategies for leveraging these [[concepts/new-features|new features]], particularly focusing on how to guide the AI's behavior and customize its output for professional and branded results.

A major shift in NotebookLM's interface is the elevated role of the "Chat" function, which has become the central hub for most advanced operations. Users can now configure the chat by assigning it a custom persona (e.g., an operations analyst) and specifying response lengths, [[concepts/acting|acting]] like a [[concepts/system-card|system prompt]] to guide the AI's [[concepts/tone|tone]] and focus. Beyond simple text responses, the chat can be prompted to create full, editable documents such as PowerPoint presentations, [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], and [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] spreadsheets. A crucial tip for achieving impressive, on-brand outputs is to include styling documents (like a PDF with preferred colors and fonts) as source material, allowing NotebookLM to automatically incorporate brand guidelines into its generated files. Images can also be integrated directly into these documents, enhancing their visual appeal.

The updated NotebookLM, powered by Google's [[concepts/gemini-30|Gemini 3]].5 model and its [[concepts/advanced-coding|advanced coding]] environment, demonstrates remarkable capabilities in data analysis and research. It can ingest messy data from files like CSVs, clean and organize them into functional Excel spreadsheets with active formulas, and even generate multi-tab workbooks (e.g., financial summaries, operational deep dives) without explicit instruction. The AI now displays its "[[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]" process, allowing users to understand the steps it takes to arrive at a conclusion. For research, it can audit existing sources, identify gaps in information, and actively find new external sources from the web, compiling them into comprehensive [[concepts/deep-research-function|deep research]] reports. Additional Studio features like customizable video overviews (including a new cinematic [[concepts/style|style]]), enhanced [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]] (editable in [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]] like Canva), [[concepts/audio-overviews|audio overviews]], [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]], flashcards, and quizzes are also available, with ongoing improvements to the mobile app [[concepts/experience|experience]].

In conclusion, NotebookLM's update positions it as a highly accurate and reliable tool, particularly for projects requiring grounded answers directly from specified sources. Unlike general-purpose AI assistants such as Gemini or [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], NotebookLM excels in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where faithfulness to specific documents and custom source selection are paramount, making it ideal for detailed analysis, report generation, and presentation creation. While some of the most advanced chat-based file building and analysis features are currently rolling out to [[concepts/ai-ultra-subscription|Google AI Ultra]] subscribers and certain Workspace business accounts, with broader availability expected, the core enhancements streamline complex, multi-step projects into a single, cohesive environment, significantly boosting [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and the quality of professional outputs.
