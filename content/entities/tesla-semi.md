# Tesla Semi

The [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] Semi is an [[concepts/electric-semi-truck|electric semi-truck]] under development by Tesla, designed to provide zero-emission hauling [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for commercial transportation. As of April 2026, the vehicle is undergoing active [[concepts/developer-readiness|production readiness]] improvements and engineering enhancements to prepare for increased manufacturing and delivery.

## Development Status

Recent updates indicate Tesla has made upgrades to the Semi's [[concepts/design|design]] and engineering specifications. The company is focusing on refining production processes and addressing technical requirements necessary for [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] manufacturing operations. These enhancements reflect Tesla's commitment to bringing the vehicle to market-ready status while maintaining performance and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for commercial use.

The Tesla Semi represents a significant step toward electrifying the trucking industry, which traditionally relies on diesel-powered vehicles. By offering an electric alternative with long-[[concepts/range|range]] capabilities, the vehicle targets fleet operators seeking to reduce operating costs and environmental impact.
