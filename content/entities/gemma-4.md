# Gemma 4

An advanced series of [[concepts/open-weight|open-weights]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] developed by [[entities/google]].

## Key Characteristics
- Recognized as [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s most intelligent [[concepts/open-source]] [[concepts/models|models]] to date.
- Specifically optimized for efficient [[concepts/edge-deployment]].
- Supports local, [[concepts/private-execution|private execution]] on [[concepts/hardware|computers]] and mobile phones without [[concepts/subscription-models|subscription]] fees.
- Released under the [[concepts/open-source|Apache 2.0 license]], allowing for unrestricted commercial use, modification, and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].
- Comprises four distinct models, including 2.3B parameter multimodal edge versions ([[concepts/e2b|E2B]] and E4B) designed to exhibit [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] comparable to 70B-class models.
