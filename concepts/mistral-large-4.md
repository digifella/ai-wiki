# Mistral Large 4

**[[entities/mistral-large|Mistral Large]] 4** is a frontier [[concepts/large-language-model]] developed by [[entities/mistral-ai]]. It represents a significant milestone in European [[concepts/ai-sovereignty|AI sovereignty]], being developed entirely in [[entities/europe|Europe]] from scratch.

## Key Specifications
- **Architecture**: [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE)
- **Parameters**:
  - Total: 1 trillion
  - Active: 49 billion
- **[[concepts/license|License]]**: [[concepts/open-weight|Open-weight]] and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]]
- **Capabilities**: Multimodal input support

## Context & Analysis
- The model has been humorously referred to as "Le Chonk" in recent community discussions.
- It aims to unify [[concepts/instruction-following|instruction following]] and [[concepts/multi-modal-input|multimodal processing]] within a single framework.
