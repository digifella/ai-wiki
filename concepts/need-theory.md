# Need Theory
Need theory is a foundational concept in [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] and sociology that explores the relationship between human needs and [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]]. It seeks to understand how unmet or satisfied needs influence behavior, [[concepts/motivation|motivation]], and overall [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].
