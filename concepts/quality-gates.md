# Quality Gates

Quality gates are structured checkpoints designed to systematically evaluate information or outputs before they advance within a process or reach stakeholders. They function as verification barriers that prevent flawed, ambiguous, or incomplete work from progressing without scrutiny. Rather than accepting outputs by default, quality gates require explicit assessment against predefined criteria covering accuracy, clarity, completeness, and relevance to the intended context or use case.

## Purpose and Function

Quality gates serve to identify and filter out incorrect, unclear, or incomplete information early in a workflow, reducing the cost and impact of errors downstream. They establish accountability by requiring someone to certify that work meets minimum standards before it moves forward. This systematic approach to critique differs from informal or ad-hoc review by making evaluation criteria explicit and consistent across iterations or projects.

## Implementation Considerations

Effective quality gates establish clear criteria specific to the domain and stakeholder needs. They require sufficient time and expertise to conduct meaningful evaluation, and they must be calibrated to prevent either rubber-stamping approvals or unnecessary bottlenecks. Quality gates work most effectively when combined with feedback mechanisms that help originators understand why their work did not meet standards, enabling continuous improvement rather than simple rejection.
