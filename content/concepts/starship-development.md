# Starship Development

Starship development encompasses the engineering, [[concepts/testing|testing]], and operational [[concepts/integration|integration]] of next-generation [[concepts/spacecraft-systems|spacecraft systems]] designed to support [[concepts/nasa|NASA]]'s [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]]. These efforts focus on creating vehicles capable of crewed lunar missions and beyond, with particular emphasis on compatibility with existing and planned spacecraft infrastructure.

## Artemis II Integration

The [[concepts/artemis-ii-mission|Artemis II mission]] represents a key testing ground for [[concepts/starship|starship]] systems in [[concepts/human-spaceflight|crewed spaceflight]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. Simulated mission operations evaluate how starship components perform alongside the [[entities/orion|Orion spacecraft]], which serves as the crew module for lunar transit. These simulations assess [[concepts/crew-operations|crew operations]] protocols, system redundancy, and the practical integration of newer propulsion and life support technologies with established spacecraft platforms.

## Orion Systems Compatibility

Development work involves ensuring that starship systems can operate effectively within the broader Artemis [[concepts/architecture|architecture]]. This includes testing communications interfaces, docking mechanisms, and [[concepts/power|power]] transfer systems between the Orion spacecraft and supporting starship infrastructure. Such integration testing is essential for validating mission profiles and ensuring crew safety during complex maneuvers and emergency scenarios.
