# Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index

The [[entities/artificial-analysis|Artificial Analysis]] [[concepts/intelligence-index|Intelligence Index]] is a [[concepts/benchmark-testing|benchmarking]] framework developed by the research firm Artificial Analysis. It is designed to evaluate and compare [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] and broader [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] using standardized [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]]. The framework aims to provide independent assessments across multiple operational dimensions, allowing developers and organizations to assess models based on measurable criteria rather than marketing claims.

The index focuses on quantifying key aspects of [[concepts/ai-model-performance|AI model performance]], including [[concepts/inference-speed|inference speed]], [[concepts/algorithm-efficiency|computational efficiency]], and cost-effectiveness. By establishing consistent evaluation standards, it facilitates objective comparisons between different model architectures and providers. This data-driven approach helps stakeholders identify models that best fit specific technical requirements and budget constraints.

Recent updates to the [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]], such as the release of Google's [[concepts/flash-models|Gemini 3 Flash]], highlight the index's relevance in tracking advancements in speed and efficiency. The framework continues to serve as a reference point for understanding the trade-offs between performance capabilities and [[concepts/operational-costs|operational costs]] in the rapidly evolving field of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].
