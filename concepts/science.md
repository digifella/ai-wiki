# Science

Science is a systematic method of inquiry dedicated to understanding the natural world through observation, experimentation, and [[concepts/reasoning-skills|logical reasoning]]. Rather than a single discipline, science encompasses multiple fields—including [[concepts/physics|physics]], chemistry, biology, and [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]]—that share common methodological principles. These principles include the formulation of testable hypotheses, empirical investigation, [[concepts/document-review|peer review]], and [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative refinement]] of theories based on evidence.

## Core Methodology

The [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific method]] forms the foundation of scientific practice. Researchers observe phenomena, develop explanatory hypotheses, design experiments to test those hypotheses, and analyze results to draw conclusions. This cyclical process allows knowledge to accumulate and theories to be refined or rejected based on [[concepts/empirical-evidence|empirical evidence]] rather than assumption or authority. Reproducibility is a central concern: findings must be capable of [[concepts/verification|verification]] by independent researchers working under similar conditions.

## Knowledge Production and Validation

Science operates as a collective enterprise in which findings are subjected to peer review before publication and acceptance into the body of established knowledge. This process helps identify errors, inconsistencies, and methodological flaws. Scientific knowledge is provisional rather than absolute—theories are accepted as the best current explanation of available evidence but remain open to revision or replacement when new evidence emerges or when alternative explanations prove superior.

## Scope and Integration

The boundaries between scientific disciplines are increasingly porous, with physics providing foundational principles for chemistry, chemistry informing biology, and multiple disciplines contributing to fields like [[entities/earth|Earth]] science and materials science. This integration reflects the underlying unity of natural phenomena and the interconnectedness of different [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] of inquiry, from subatomic particles to cosmic structures.
