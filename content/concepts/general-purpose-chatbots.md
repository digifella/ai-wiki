# General Purpose Chatbots

General purpose chatbots are [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] designed to assist with a wide range of productivity tasks across different domains and workflows. Rather than being specialized for single functions, these tools are built to handle multiple types of requests—from [[concepts/writing|writing]] and analysis to [[concepts/coding|coding]] and research—making them foundational [[concepts/software|applications]] for many professionals and knowledge workers.

## Common Use Cases

These chatbots typically serve as starting points for tasks like drafting content, [[concepts/brainstorming|brainstorming]] ideas, answering questions, summarizing information, and [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] code. Their versatility makes them useful across industries, though users often supplement them with [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] for domain-specific work that requires deeper functionality.

## Selection and Implementation

According to productivity expert [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]], most users can accomplish approximately 90% of their AI-assisted work using just a small selection of general purpose chatbots rather than maintaining subscriptions to numerous specialized tools. The key to effective use lies in selecting tools that align with individual workflows and [[concepts/learning|learning]] how to prompt them effectively, rather than adopting every available [[concepts/ai-application|AI application]].
