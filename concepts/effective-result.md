# Effective Result

Effective Result is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] technique for [[concepts/10x-developer-productivity|AI-assisted coding]] named after Ralph Wiggum, a character from the animated series *The Simpsons*. The term was popularized by technology educator [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]], who utilized the character's distinctively blunt and literal communication style to illustrate [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]] for interacting with [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]]. The name serves as a mnemonic device, encouraging developers to adopt a direct and unambiguous approach when prompting AI tools.

The core principle of the technique involves providing [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] with clear, explicit instructions regarding desired outcomes, rather than relying on implicit understanding or vague context. By framing requests in a straightforward manner—mirroring Ralph Wiggum’s characteristic lack of subtlety—developers can reduce the likelihood of misinterpretation by the model. This method emphasizes precision in defining the end goal, ensuring that the AI generates code that aligns closely with the user's specific intent without requiring extensive iterative clarification.

In practice, Effective Result advocates for structuring prompts to highlight the final state of the code or the specific problem to be solved, rather than focusing solely on the process. This approach helps mitigate common issues such as hallucinated functionality or irrelevant [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]]. By prioritizing the clarity of the result over the complexity of the instruction, developers can improve the efficiency and accuracy of AI-assisted workflows, making it a notable concept in the discourse on [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] and [[concepts/augmentation-of-human-capabilities|human-AI collaboration]].
