# Iran

[[entities/iran|Iran]] is a country located in Western Asia, situated [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the crossroads of the Middle East, South Asia, and Central Asia. With a population of approximately 88 million people, it is the second-most populous country in the Middle East. The capital and largest city is Tehran. Iran's strategic location has made it historically significant for trade, culture, and [[concepts/geopolitics|geopolitics]], controlling key passages including the [[entities/strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], through which a substantial portion of global oil trade flows.

## Geography and Climate

Iran covers an area of approximately 1.65 million square kilometers and is characterized by diverse geography, including the Alborz and Zagros mountain ranges, high plateaus, and coastal regions along the Persian Gulf, Gulf of Oman, and Caspian Sea. The country experiences varied climates across regions, from Mediterranean conditions in the northwest to arid and semi-arid zones in the interior. [[concepts/winter-rainfall|Winter rainfall]] in certain regions, particularly the western highlands, plays a critical role in the country's water supply and agricultural productivity.

## Political Structure and International Role

Iran is an Islamic republic with a complex political system combining republican and theocratic elements. Since the 1979 Iranian [[concepts/revolution|Revolution]], it has maintained significant [[concepts/power|influence]] in Middle Eastern affairs and regional [[concepts/security|security]] dynamics. The country's geopolitical importance stems from its oil and natural gas reserves, its control of maritime chokepoints, and its regional [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], making it a central actor in Middle Eastern political and security considerations.
