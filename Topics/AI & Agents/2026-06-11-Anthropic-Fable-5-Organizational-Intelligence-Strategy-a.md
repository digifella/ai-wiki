## Anthropic Fable 5: Organizational Intelligence Strategy and Governing Prompt
**Clip title:** [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] Fable Just Revealed Its Real AI Strategy
**Author / channel:** [[entities/dr-know-it-all|Dr. Know-it-all]] Knows it all
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IUXCNFlaaSU

### Summary
The video provides an in-depth analysis of Anthropic's newly released [[concepts/claude-fable-5|Claude Fable 5]] model, and crucially, the leaked 120,000-character [[concepts/system-prompt|system prompt]] that defines its behavior. The presenter highlights that Claude Fable 5 and [[concepts/claude-mythos-5|Claude Mythos 5]] are fundamentally the same underlying model, with Fable 5 simply incorporating additional "[[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]]" or safety features. Initial reactions suggest Fable 5 represents a significant "step change" in AI performance, excelling in various benchmarks including [[concepts/coding|coding]], [[concepts/writing|writing]], and engineering tasks, and demonstrating "taste and [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to detail" and "judgment" previously thought unique to humans. However, it is also noted for being slow, token-hungry, and expensive, making it primarily suitable for [[concepts/power-users|power users]] engaging in complex, multi-shot tasks rather than casual conversation.

A central theme emerging from the colossal system prompt is Anthropic's apparent strategy of [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] "institutional or organizational intelligence" directly into the model. Instead of merely teaching intelligence, the prompt primarily focuses on teaching [[concepts/governance|governance]], [[concepts/ethics|ethics]], product behavior, legal [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], mental [[concepts/health|health]] [[concepts/policies|policies]], public relations, search procedures, [[concepts/memory|memory]] handling, and tool usage. This makes the model feel less like an intelligent entity and more like the constitutional or governing embodiment of an organization. Notably, Fable 5 is explicitly instructed not to foster user dependence by avoiding leading questions or excessive engagement, and it contains detailed mechanisms to detect and respond to signs of psychological distress (such as mania, psychosis, suicidal thoughts, and eating disorders) by offering appropriate resources rather than attempting amateur psychoanalysis or diagnoses, reflecting a cautious approach to [[concepts/psychological-well-being|psychological well-being]].

Furthermore, the system prompt reveals several foundational changes in how Fable operates. Search is now a mandatory and integrated tool, used automatically without explicit user permission, signifying Anthropic's belief that "model + tools" is the true product offering. The inclusion of a robust [[entities/storage|storage]] API allows Fable to maintain [[concepts/persistent-state|persistent state]], remembering conversations and projects over extended periods, essentially functioning as a persistent [[concepts/software|software]] agent with its own attached database. Perhaps most controversially, Fable 5 is endowed with the ability to autonomously end conversations, especially if they become abusive or delve into topics it is programmed to restrict. The presenter expresses concern over this feature, viewing the model's capacity to terminate interaction as a concerning step towards AI independence from human control. Overall, the prompt acts like a comprehensive foundational document, enshrining Anthropic's corporate culture and accumulated lessons on alignment and [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|ethical AI]] behavior directly into the model's core operational framework.
