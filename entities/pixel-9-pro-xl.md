# Pixel 9 Pro XL

The Pixel 9 Pro XL is a flagship smartphone manufactured by Google, representing the company's premium offering within the Pixel smartphone line. As the largest model in the Pro tier, it targets users seeking Google's most advanced hardware and software capabilities. The device runs Android 16, Google's mobile operating system, and features a refined user interface design.

## Software and Updates

The Pixel 9 Pro XL ships with enhancements delivered through the June 2025 Pixel Drop update, which brings refinements to the user experience and system functionality. Like other Pixel devices, it receives priority access to Google's latest software features and security updates.

## Position in the Product Line

The Pro XL designation indicates this is Google's largest and most feature-rich Pixel model, positioned above the standard Pixel 9 and Pixel 9 Pro, and alongside the Pixel 9 Pro Fold as part of Google's high-end smartphone offerings.
