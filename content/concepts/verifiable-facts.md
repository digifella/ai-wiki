# Verifiable Facts

Verifiable Facts refers to information or claims that can be objectively confirmed through evidence, [[concepts/testing|testing]], or logical demonstration. In the context of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and advisory systems, verifiable facts form the foundation of trustworthy interactions by distinguishing between established information and speculation or opinion. An AI system operating with a commitment to verifiable facts will prioritize [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] over completeness, acknowledge uncertainty where it exists, and cite or explain the basis for claims when requested.

## Role in Expert Advisory Systems

Within expert advisory frameworks, verifiable facts serve as the baseline for credible guidance. Rather than generating confident-sounding but unsubstantiated [[concepts/responses|responses]], an AI advisor operating under this principle will seek clarification on ambiguous queries, reframe requests to ensure scope and precision, and deliver [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] that can be followed and evaluated by the user. This approach builds [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] into the interaction by making claims transparent and auditable.

## Application to Step-by-Step Reasoning

When breaking down complex problems using step-by-step reasoning, verifiable facts ensure that each intermediate conclusion rests on established ground rather than assumption. This method allows users to identify where reasoning diverges from known information and where [[concepts/inference|inference]] or judgment enters the process, creating clarity about the confidence level appropriate for different parts of an analysis or recommendation.
