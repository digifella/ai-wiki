# Google Vids Automation

[[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] Automation is a two-stage workflow that converts text-based source material into finished video files by combining [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s slide generation with Google Vids' video production capabilities. The process begins with NotebookLM, an AI-integrated [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] tool that analyzes raw text input and automatically generates [[concepts/slide-decks|presentation slides]] in [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]] format. These generated slides then serve as the foundation for [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] in Google Vids.

## Workflow and Process

The [[concepts/automation-workflow|automation workflow]] leverages the output structure of NotebookLM to streamline video production. Users first upload documents or paste text into NotebookLM, which synthesizes the information into a structured slide deck. This deck is then exported or linked to Google Vids, where the platform utilizes the slide content to automatically generate a narrated video. The system maps the slide text to voiceovers and selects relevant stock media or images to accompany the [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]], reducing the manual effort typically required for [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]].

## Output and Limitations

The resulting video files are produced without [[concepts/watermarks|watermarks]], allowing for direct use in presentations or social media. However, the automation is limited by the quality and structure of the initial source material; complex or poorly organized text may result in disjointed slide transitions or irrelevant media suggestions. Users often need to manually review and adjust the generated video to ensure narrative [[concepts/coherence|coherence]] and visual accuracy, particularly when dealing with nuanced topics that require specific contextual imagery not available in the standard stock library.
