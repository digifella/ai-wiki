# Gemini Ultra

- **[[concepts/training|Training]] Cost**: The 2023 [[concepts/training|training]] cost for [[entities/gemini-ai|Google's Gemini]] Ultra was estimated at $191 million (per a [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] report), significantly higher than [[entities/gpt-4|GPT-4]]'s $78 million (though [[entities/sam-altman|Sam Altman]] claimed it was even more).
- **[[concepts/4bit-quantisation|4bit Quantisation]]**: The video How does 4bit [[concepts/quantisation|quantisation]] work (by [[entities/julia-turc|Julia Turc]]) discusses the evolution of training LLMs with [[concepts/reduced-precision|reduced precision]], particularly the shift towards [[concepts/floating-point-numbers|4-bit floating-point]] (FP4) training as a technique to reduce computational and [[concepts/memory|memory]] costs in large [[concepts/training-process|model training]].
