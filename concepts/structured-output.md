The practice of constraining LLM responses to a predefined format, schema, or template to ensure [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and integration into downstream [[concepts/automation]] workflows.

## Core Techniques & Workflows
- **Prompt Transformation**: Converting "messy" or vague natural language inputs into clean, standardized, and consistent outputs.
- **Automated [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]]**: Utilizing a [[entities/notebooklm]] and [[entities/gemini]] workflow to automate the structural refinement of prompts, potentially reducing the need for manual [[entities/prompt-engineering]].
  - New Workflow: [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] + [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] can optimize [[concepts/ai-templates|AI prompts]] into clean, structured outputs.
- **Standardization & Interoperability**:
  - Evolution from personal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] (e.g., [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s "[[concepts/llm-wiki|LLM Wiki]]") to standardized formats for [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] interoperability.
  - **[[concepts/data-management|Open Knowledge Format]] (OKF)**: [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s proposed standard to unify personal knowledge [[concepts/number-systems|bases]], enabling seamless data exchange between [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and structured repositories.
  - See: [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Googles-OKF-Standardizing-Karpathys-LLM-Wiki-for-AI-Inte|Google's OKF: Standardizing Karpathy's LLM Wiki for AI Interoperability]]
