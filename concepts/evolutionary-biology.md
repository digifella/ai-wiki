# Evolutionary Biology

## Core Concepts
- **Natural Selection**: The differential survival and reproduction of individuals due to differences in phenotype.
- **Genetic Drift**: Random changes in allele frequencies over time, especially in small populations.
- **Speciation**: The formation of new and distinct species in the course of evolution.
- **Adaptation**: Heritable characteristics that increase an organism's ability to survive and reproduce in its environment.
- **Brain [[concepts/hemispheric-specialization|Hemispheric Specialization]]**: The evolutionary divergence of functional roles between the left and right cerebral hemispheres, influencing cognitive processing and behavioral adaptation. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Evolutionary-Origins-and-Impact-of-Brain-Hemispheric-Spe|Evolutionary Origins and Impact of Brain Hemispheric Specialization]].
