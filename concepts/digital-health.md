# Digital Health

Digital health encompasses the use of digital technologies, tools, and platforms to improve health outcomes and [[concepts/health|healthcare]] delivery. This includes [[concepts/electronic-health-records]], [[concepts/telehealth|telemedicine]], [[concepts/health-surveillance|health monitoring]] applications, [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]], and [[entities/big-data|data analytics]] systems that support [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]] and patient engagement. The field represents a convergence of [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] practice, information technology, and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] initiatives aimed at making healthcare more accessible, efficient, and data-driven.

Recent developments include the FDA utilizing [[concepts/agentic-ai]] in regulatory processes and the expansion of [[concepts/digital-therapeutics|digital therapeutics]] into specialized clinical domains. *   Integration of digital health strategies into nursing curricula and mental health workflows to address workforce development needs.
