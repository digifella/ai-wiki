# Google Gemini Gems

Customized iterations of [[entities/gemini]] designed to automate specific tasks and optimize [[concepts/workflow|Workflow]].

### Core Concepts
- **The [[concepts/code-size|LLM Efficiency]] Gap**: Many users of [[concepts/llm]]s (including [[entities/chatgpt]] and [[entities/claude]]) use them inefficiently, trapped in a "back-and-forth" chat loop attempting to refine answers.
- **Problem**: This iterative [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] results in time waste and dissatisfaction, which is particularly costly for Solopreneurs and small teams.
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: [[concepts/google-gemini-gems]] allow for pre-configured [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to streamline interactions and reduce the need for repetitive refinement.
