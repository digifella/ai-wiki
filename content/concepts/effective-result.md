# Effective Result

Effective Result is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] technique for [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|AI-assisted coding]] that takes its name from [[entities/ralph-wiggum|Ralph Wiggum]], a character from the animated television series *The Simpsons*. The technique was popularized through educational content created by [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]], a technology educator and YouTuber known for explaining programming concepts and software development practices.

## Core Approach

The technique leverages [[concepts/ai-coding-workflows|AI coding tools]] by adopting a pragmatic approach to [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] and [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]]. Rather than seeking perfect or [[entities/theoretically-media|theoretically]] optimal solutions, the Effective Result method prioritizes getting working code that solves the problem [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] hand. This approach acknowledges that [[concepts/ai-generated-code|AI-generated code]] may not always be elegant or follow every best practice, but if it produces the desired functional outcome, it serves its [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] in the development [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].

## Application and Context

Effective Result reflects a practical [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] particularly suited to [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]] and iterative development. By accepting "good enough" solutions from [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], developers can maintain momentum and move forward with [[concepts/feature-development|feature development]], refactoring, and optimization in subsequent phases if needed. The technique is particularly relevant in educational contexts and for developers working with [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] as part of their daily workflow.
