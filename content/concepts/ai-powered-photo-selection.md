# AI Powered Photo Selection

AI-powered [[concepts/photo-culling|photo selection]] refers to automated culling and organization [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] integrated into [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], designed to streamline the [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] of photographers managing large batches of [[concepts/images|images]]. These features leverage [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] algorithms to assist in identifying and grouping similar or duplicate images, reducing the manual effort required during the initial review [[concepts/phase|phase]] of [[concepts/photo-management|photo management]].

## Assisted Culling

The [[concepts/assisted-culling|assisted culling]] feature helps photographers filter through image collections by using AI to recognize factors such as image quality, [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]], and composition. Rather than manually reviewing each photograph individually, users can leverage these recommendations to quickly identify keepers and rejects, accelerating the selection process significantly.

## Auto Stacking

Auto stacking functionality automatically groups similar images together, such as burst sequences or variations of the same shot taken in rapid succession. This organization method allows photographers to collapse redundant images into stacks, keeping the library view cleaner while preserving access to all variants when needed for detailed comparison.

These capabilities represent part of a broader trend toward integrating [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] and machine learning into creative [[concepts/software|software]] workflows, aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] reducing repetitive tasks and allowing photographers to focus on more creative aspects of their work.
