### Recent Developments
- First Historic Transportation of [[concepts/antimatter|Antimatter]] by Truck Using CERN's [[concepts/penning-trap|Penning Trap]] (2026-04-12)
  - The video discusses a significant milestone in [[concepts/granular-physics|particle physics]]: the first successful transportation of antimatter from one location to another, specifically at CERN in Switzerland.
  - References historical discovery of [[concepts/positron|positron]] in 1932 by [[entities/carl-anderson|Carl Anderson]] confirming existence of antimatter.

- Fermilab's [[concepts/muon|Muon]] g-2 Experiment (2026-04-14)
  - High-precision measurement of muon magnetic moment at Fermilab shows significant deviation from [[concepts/standard-model|Standard Model]] predictions (2025 results), indicating potential new [[concepts/physics|physics]] beyond current [[concepts/models|models]].
  - Uses a large-scale facility to test fundamental properties of [[concepts/muons|muons]], a type of lepton.
