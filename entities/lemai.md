# Lemai

Lemai is an organization focused on artificial intelligence research and development, with particular emphasis on healthcare applications. The organization works to develop practical frameworks and methodologies that support the adoption and implementation of AI technologies within health services.

## AI Maturity Model Development

Lemai is collaborating with Sandeep on the development of an AI maturity model designed specifically for health services. This project leverages agentic AI approaches to help healthcare organizations assess and advance their AI capabilities and readiness.
