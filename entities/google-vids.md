# Google Vids

[[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] is an artificial intelligence-powered [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool developed by Google as part of its [[entities/google-workspace]] [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] suite. The application is designed to streamline video production by automating key aspects of the creation process, enabling users to produce polished videos without requiring extensive technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] or manual editing work.

The tool leverages [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to assist with multiple stages of video creation. Users can input text, scripts, or prompts, which the system then transforms into [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] by generating visuals, selecting appropriate stock media, and adding voiceovers. This automation allows for the rapid assembly of presentations, training materials, and marketing content directly within the [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] environment.

Integration with existing Google services allows users to pull in content from [[entities/google-docs]], [[entities/google-slides]], and [[entities/google-drive]], facilitating a seamless workflow for collaborative teams. By reducing the barrier to entry for video production, Google Vids aims to make [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] [[concepts/visual-communication|visual communication]] accessible to a broader range of professionals who may not have specialized [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] [[concepts/skills|skills]].
