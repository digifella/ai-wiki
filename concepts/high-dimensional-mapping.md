# High-Dimensional Mapping

## Definition
A computational technique that projects data into a space with more dimensions than the original dataset, enabling the separation of complex patterns that are non-linearly separable in lower-dimensional spaces.

## Key Applications
- **Genomics & Pathogen [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|Prediction]]**:
  - Addresses limitations of traditional Phylogenetic Tree approaches, which fail to account for mutations shared across different evolutionary branches.
  - [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] map [[concepts/genomic-data|genomic data]] into high-dimensional spaces to create accurate mutational "fingerprints," improving the prediction of future pathogen strains for timely [[concepts/vaccine-production|vaccine production]].
  - Key contributor: [[entities/yatish-jain|Yatish Jain]] (C
- **[[concepts/data-tables|Tabular Data]] & [[concepts/foundation-model|Foundation Models]]**:
  - Emerging paradigm of applying foundation models to tabular data, traditionally dominated by classical [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]].
  - Enables "one model for everything" approach for databases and spreadsheets, similar to LLMs for text.
  - See [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Google-TabFM-Zero-Shot-Foundation-Model-for-Tabular-Data|Google TabFM: Zero-Shot Foundation Model for Tabular Data]] for details on zero-shot capabilities.
