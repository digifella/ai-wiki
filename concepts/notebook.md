# Notebook

Notebook refers to a category of digital tools and platforms designed for creating animated whiteboard videos through AI-powered applications. These tools convert written content, scripts, or verbal input into visual video formats commonly used for educational explanations, tutorials, and marketing materials. The workflow typically integrates multiple stages of content processing—from initial generation or input through to final video output—within a single platform or coordinated set of applications.

## Common Platforms and Tools

NotebookLM, Nano Banana Pro, and Pictory represent prominent examples in this category. NotebookLM functions as an AI research assistant that can generate scripts and outlines from source materials, which can then be converted into video format. Pictory specializes in transforming text-based content directly into animated videos with whiteboard-style visuals. Nano Banana Pro offers similar automation capabilities for video creation from written input. Each platform varies in its approach to automation, customization options, and output quality.

## Typical Workflow

Users generally begin by providing source material—such as articles, research papers, scripts, or transcripts—which the platform processes through AI language models to generate or refine content. The platform then converts this text into a visual narrative, selecting or generating appropriate illustrations and animations. Voice narration is typically added either through text-to-speech synthesis or by uploading audio files. The final output is an animated video suitable for online sharing, presentations, or educational content distribution.
