# NotebookLM Based Knowledge Management

NotebookLM Based Knowledge Management refers to a workflow approach that leverages [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] tool alongside complementary [[concepts/google-search|Google]] services to create and refine knowledge artifacts. The practice emerged as users sought to extend the capabilities of [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s automatically generated study materials—particularly [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]]—into alternative formats while removing platform watermarks. This methodology sits at the intersection of note-taking [[concepts/automation|automation]] and content repurposing.

## Video Export and Watermark Removal

The core technique involves converting NotebookLM-generated [[entities/google-slides|slides]] into video format using Google's available tools, with particular focus on producing unwatermarked outputs. Rather than accepting the platform's native presentation format, practitioners use supplementary Google [[concepts/software|applications]] to render slides into video content suitable for broader [[concepts/distribution|distribution]], archival, or integration into other knowledge systems. This addresses limitations users perceive in NotebookLM's default output options.

## Integration with Gemini

NotebookLM's mind map generation feature has been noted as producing suboptimal results in certain contexts. Users have found that feeding NotebookLM outputs into Google's [[entities/gemini-models|Gemini AI]] model can substantially improve the quality and utility of generated concept maps. This two-stage process—generating initial artifacts in NotebookLM, then refining them through Gemini interaction—represents a practical workflow for [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] that compensates for individual tool limitations through sequential application.

The approach reflects broader patterns in [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]] where single tools are combined into custom pipelines to achieve outcomes neither could produce independently.
