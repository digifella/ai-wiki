# Fortress Belt

The **[[concepts/defensive-fortifications|Fortress Belt]]** refers to the defensive infrastructure and [[concepts/strategic-depth|strategic depth]] established by [[entities/ukraine]] to counter [[concepts/russian-offensive|Russian offensive]] operations, particularly along the eastern and southern fronts. This concept emphasizes layered defense, logistical [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and the integration of static fortifications with mobile reserve forces.

## Strategic Context (2026)

As of mid-2026, the operational dynamic has shifted from rapid maneuver warfare to a grinding attritional [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] characterized by:

- **Slowed Russian Offensives:** Russian advances have decelerated due to increased Ukrainian defensive [[concepts/density|density]] and logistical disruptions.
- **Logistics-Centric Warfare:** The focus has moved to targeting [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] rather than just frontline troop concentrations.
- **Defensive Depth:** The Fortress Belt serves as a multi-layered barrier designed to absorb and bleed attacking forces before they can reach strategic objectives.
