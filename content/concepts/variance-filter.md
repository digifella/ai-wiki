# Variance Filter

The Variance Filter is an early-access feature available in Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] that extends the [[concepts/software|software]]'s selective editing capabilities. It allows photographers to isolate and adjust specific tonal ranges or color variations within an image with increased precision compared to standard filtering tools.

## Functionality

The filter works by analyzing pixel-level variance within selected areas, enabling users to target subtle differences in tone, [[concepts/color-saturation|color saturation]], or luminosity. This approach differs from traditional mask-based selection methods, offering an alternative workflow for photographers who need to make nuanced [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to complex [[concepts/images|images]] without manual [[concepts/mask-creation|mask creation]].

## Availability and Status

As an early-access feature, the Variance Filter is available to [[entities/creative-cloud-app|Adobe Creative Cloud]] subscribers who have opted into [[concepts/testing|testing]] new experimental tools. Its inclusion in Camera Raw represents Adobe's ongoing development of machine [[concepts/learning|learning]]-assisted editing capabilities for the creative-pursuits domain.
