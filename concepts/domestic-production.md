# Domestic production

Manufacturing goods within a nation's own borders, emphasizing local [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]], labor, and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]].

## Key Aspects
- **Supply chain [[concepts/resilience|resilience]]**: Reduces reliance on international logistics and geopolitical risks.
- **Labor impact**: Supports domestic employment and can align with [[concepts/union-labor]] practices.
- **Cost dynamics**: Higher labor costs may be offset by lower tariffs, transportation, and quality control expenses.
- **Market differentiation**: Enables Made in USA [[concepts/branding|branding]] for consumer [[concepts/trust|trust]] and premium positioning.

## Case Study
- **[[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]]'s [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] project**: A [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] documented in [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] to manufacture a competitive [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] ([[concepts/space-jetpacks|safer]] grill brush) entirely within the U.S., motivated by his parents' [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]] background. Demonstrated feasibility of domestic manufacturing while addressing market gaps.
