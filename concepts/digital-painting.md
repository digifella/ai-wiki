# Digital Painting

Digital painting is a [[concepts/visual-art|visual art]] technique that uses digital tools and software to create images on computers or tablets. The primary method involves brush tools found in [[concepts/software|programs]] such as [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]], which simulate the [[concepts/experience|experience]] of traditional painting while offering capabilities unique to digital media. This approach has become a standard practice across illustration, concept art, and fine art disciplines.

## Tools and Software

Digital painting relies on specialized applications that provide brush engines, color palettes, and layering systems. [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] remains one of the most widely used platforms for digital painting, though alternatives such as Corel Painter, Clip Studio Paint, and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] software like Krita are also popular. Artists typically work with [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] tablets or pen displays that allow for more natural input methods compared to traditional mouse or trackpad control.

## Workflow and Techniques

Digital painting workflows differ from traditional painting in several practical ways. Artists can easily undo actions, adjust colors without mixing new pigments, and work non-destructively using layers. These capabilities allow for rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] and experimentation. The [[entities/medium|medium]] supports both raster-based painting (working with pixels) and vector-based approaches, enabling artists to switch between techniques or combine them within a single work.

## Applications and Practice

Digital painting is used across professional and creative contexts, including commercial illustration, game and film concept art, character design, and fine art. Many professional illustrators and concept artists have adopted digital painting as their primary medium, while others use it alongside traditional techniques. The discipline continues to evolve with advancing graphics technology and software capabilities.
