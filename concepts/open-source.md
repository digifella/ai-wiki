Open source is a development model where software source code is freely available for inspection, modification, and distribution by anyone, increasingly applied to [[concepts/ai-agents]], [[concepts/local-llm]], and infrastructure. Open standards, such as the [[concepts/model-context-protocol]], extend this philosophy to ensure interoperability between disparate systems.

### Core Principles
*   **Transparency & Collaboration**: Source code is accessible for inspection and modification, fostering community-driven improvement.
*   **Interoperability**: Adherence to open standards prevents vendor lock-in and enables [[concepts/hidden-engineering|seamless integration]] across systems.
*   **Privacy & Control**: A growing subset of open-source AI focuses on local-first execution to minimize data exposure to cloud providers.

### Recent Developments in Open-Source AI
*   **[[concepts/autonomous-skill-creation|Self-Improving Agents]]**: Development of agents like [[entities/hermes]] that exhibit self-improving capabilities.
*   **[[concepts/custom-models|Specialized Models]]**: Emergence of coding-focused models like Ornith and efficient architectures such as [[entities/mixture-of-experts]].
*   **Local-First Frameworks**:
    *   **OpenJarvis**: A local-first, open-source [[concepts/personal-ai-framework|personal AI framework]] developed by [[entities/stanford-university]]'s [[entities/hazy-research]] and Scaling Intelligence Labs. It prioritizes running powerful AI models directly on personal devices, emphasizing privacy and control over cloud reliance.
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-06-27-OpenJarvis-Stanfords-Local-First-Personal-AI-Framework-w|OpenJarvis: Stanford's Local-First Personal AI Framework with Ollama]] for detailed analysis.
    *   Integrates with [[entities/ollama]] to facilitate [[concepts/edge-deployment|local inference]].
*   **Infrastructure & Tools**:
    *   Use of PocketBase and Firebase for backend infrastructure.
    *   Adoption of [[entities/langchain]] and [[entities/comfyui]] for [[concepts/agent-collaboration|agent orchestration]] and [[concepts/media-generation|media generation]].
    *   Focus on [[concepts/cost-optimization]] and [[concepts/cost-efficiency]] through [[concepts/model-compression]] and [[concepts/inference-optimization]].
