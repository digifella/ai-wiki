# Style Transfer

[[concepts/style|Style]] Transfer is a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]]-based technique that applies the visual characteristics of one image to another. In [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], this capability is implemented through [[concepts/neural-filters|Neural Filters]], allowing users to analyze the aesthetic properties—such as color palette, brushwork, lighting, and [[concepts/texture|texture]]—of a source photograph and apply those qualities to a different image. The feature automates what would traditionally require manual adjustment across multiple editing parameters.

## Technical Implementation

The process relies on [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] trained to isolate and separate style elements from image content. When a user selects a reference image, the [[entities/neural-filter|neural filter]] analyzes its artistic qualities and transfers those attributes to the target image while attempting to preserve the underlying subject matter and [[concepts/writing|composition]]. This differs from simple filters or effects, as the transformation adapts to the specific structural and semantic content of the target image rather than applying a uniform overlay.
