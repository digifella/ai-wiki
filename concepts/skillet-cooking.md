# Skillet Cooking

Skillet cooking is a fundamental culinary technique that utilizes a flat-bottomed pan with sloped or curved sides to prepare food over direct heat. The design of a skillet, typically featuring a wide cooking surface and angled edges, facilitates efficient heat distribution and allows cooks to manage temperature control and ingredient placement during preparation. This method is valued for its adaptability across various kitchen environments and culinary traditions.

The versatility of skillets allows them to be employed on numerous heat sources, including stovetop burners, ovens, and open flames. This adaptability supports a wide range of cooking techniques, from searing and sautéing to braising and baking. The ability to transition seamlessly between different heat sources makes the skillet a central tool in both professional and home kitchens.

In specific culinary contexts, such as North Indian cuisine, skillet cooking is utilized to prepare complex dishes like chicken curry. Chef Neel Kajale’s approach to skillet chicken curry demonstrates how the method can be applied to create rich, flavorful meals by leveraging the pan’s heat retention and distribution properties. This application highlights the skillet's role in achieving desired textures and flavor profiles in diverse global cuisines.
