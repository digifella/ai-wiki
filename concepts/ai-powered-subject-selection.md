# Ai Powered Subject Selection

AI Powered [[concepts/select-subject-tool|Subject Selection]] is a feature within [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] that utilizes [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to automatically identify and isolate specific elements within a photograph. The technology analyzes image data to detect distinct objects, such as people, animals, and landscape components, generating precise selection masks around them. This automation significantly reduces the manual effort required for complex selections compared to traditional tools.

The process involves the software scanning the image content to distinguish foreground subjects from the background. Once identified, the system creates a mask that can be refined using standard adjustment brushes or [[concepts/gradient-tools|gradient filters]]. This allows users to apply localized edits, such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] or color corrections, without affecting the rest of the image.

By automating the initial selection [[concepts/phase|phase]], this feature streamlines the post-processing workflow. It enables more accurate edge detection for intricate details like hair or foliage, which are often difficult to isolate manually. The resulting masks serve as the foundation for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], enhancing the efficiency and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] of [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] tasks.
