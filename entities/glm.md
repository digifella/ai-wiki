# Glm

GLM (General Language Model) is an open-source large language model developed by researchers at Tsinghua University. It was designed to provide an accessible alternative to proprietary language models, offering advanced natural language processing capabilities for research, commercial, and community applications. By releasing its technology under an open license, the project aims to democratize access to state-of-the-art language technology and foster broader participation in the development and application of large language models.

## Architecture and Training

GLM distinguishes itself through its use of an autoregressive blank-filling training objective. This approach combines the strengths of both masked language modeling and causal language modeling, allowing the model to learn from both context and future tokens during pre-training. This hybrid methodology enables GLM to achieve strong performance across a variety of natural language understanding and generation tasks while maintaining the flexibility required for diverse downstream applications.
