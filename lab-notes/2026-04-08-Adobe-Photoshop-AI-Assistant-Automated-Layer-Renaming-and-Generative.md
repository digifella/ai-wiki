## Adobe Photoshop AI Assistant: Automated Layer Renaming and Generative
Markup Tools
**Clip title:** [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] Renames All Your Layers Automatically (photoshop
web)
**Author / channel:** [[entities/colin-smith-channel|photoshopCAFE]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eT_muXSPkeo

### Summary
This video by Colin Smith introduces Adobe's newly launched [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI Assistant]]
and [[concepts/ai-markup|AI Markup]] tools, primarily available in Photoshop for the web, but with
direct implications and [[concepts/integration|integration]] for desktop Photoshop users. The main
topic revolves around how these generative AI capabilities can streamline
[[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] workflows, enhance creativity, and offer unprecedented levels
of automated assistance. Smith emphasizes that Photoshop for the web is
free to use, and premium AI features are included with a standard Photoshop
subscription, making these powerful tools accessible to a broad audience.

Smith demonstrates several key features of the AI Assistant. First, he
showcases the intelligent layer renaming capability, where the AI can
analyze an uploaded Photoshop document (PSD) and automatically assign
descriptive names to unnamed layers, such as "Camera Body Top View" or
"White Background." This feature dramatically improves organization,
especially in complex multi-layered [[concepts/files|files]]. Second, he illustrates the AI
Markup tool's ability to perform targeted edits using natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]]. By adding an annotation to a specific part of an image, users can
instruct the AI to change colors, for example, turning "CAFE" [[concepts/text|text]] orange,
with the AI even suggesting relevant color palettes.

Further demonstrating its generative power, Smith uses the AI Markup tool
to generate new content from a simple [[concepts/sketch|sketch]]. Drawing a rough outline on
the [[concepts/canvas|canvas]], he prompts the AI to "[[entities/make|make]] a CF card," resulting in
realistic-looking SD cards placed precisely where sketched. A significant
takeaway is the seamless integration: all changes and AI-generated content,
including the intelligently renamed layers, are saved as [cloud documents](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cloud_Documents)
and are fully accessible and editable when the file is opened in desktop
Photoshop. This allows users to leverage the AI's efficiency on the web and
then refine or continue their work with the full power of the [[concepts/desktop-application|desktop application]]. Smith also highlights that for simple tasks like color
changes, it's advisable to use basic [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] (like "[[entities/nano|Nano]] Banana," which
is currently unmetered for a limited time) to conserve credits for more
complex generative tasks.

In conclusion, the video positions Adobe's AI Assistant and Markup tools as
transformative additions to the Photoshop ecosystem, offering intuitive,
AI-powered solutions for both mundane and creative tasks. The ability to
automatically rename layers, make precise localized edits with text
prompts, and generate elements from sketches significantly boosts
productivity. The smooth [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] between Photoshop for the web and desktop
Photoshop means these innovations can immediately impact how designers and
photographers approach their work, even if they aren't directly using the
web version for all their edits.
