# Local Realism

Local realism is a principle in [[concepts/physics|physics]] which asserts that [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]] of objects exist independently of observation and are determined by local factors. This concept has been central to the development of classical mechanics and was also assumed implicitly in early formulations of [[concepts/quantum-mechanics|quantum mechanics]].

## Key Points
- **Independence of Observables:** The value of an observable property of one particle does not depend on any measurement performed simultaneously on a distant particle.
- **Reality:** [[concepts/material-properties|Physical properties]] exist prior to and independent of measurement, reflecting the nature of reality at a fundamental level.
- **Locality:** Information cannot travel faster than [[concepts/light|light]]; hence, physical processes are constrained by causal influences limited to light [[concepts/speed|speed]].

## Critiques and Challenges
Local realism is famously challenged by [[concepts/quantum-entanglement|quantum entanglement]] phenomena, such as Bell's theorem experiments which reveal correlations between particles that can't be explained by any local hidden variable [[concepts/theory|theory]]. These results suggest the universe may not conform to classical notions of locality and reality as intuitively understood.

## Related Concepts
- **Bell's Theorem:** A fundamental result in [[concepts/quantum-mechanics|quantum mechanics]] that shows the impossibility of explaining correlations between particles using local hidden variables.
- [[concepts/quantum-entanglement|Quantum Entanglement]]: Describes an intimate [[concepts/connection|connection]] between pairs or groups of particles such that the state of one particle cannot be described independently of the state of the others, even when the particles are separated by large distances.
- **Non-Locality:** The ability of two objects to instantaneously affect each other's states regardless of distance, which challenges classical [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] of causality and locality.
