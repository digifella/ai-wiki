# Tool Definition Bloat & Digital Safety

Tool Definition Bloat refers to the accumulation and proliferation of [[concepts/tool-definitions|tool definitions]] within systems that integrate [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]—particularly in [[concepts/mcps|Model Context Protocol]] (MCP) implementations and [[entities/docker-desktop|Docker]]-based orchestration platforms. As systems scale, the number of available tool definitions grows, creating challenges in management, maintainability, and [[concepts/model-efficiency|resource efficiency]]. The issue arises when tools are duplicated across contexts, poorly organized, or defined with excessive specificity that could be consolidated.

Beyond software [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], "safety" extends to clinical domains, specifically regarding the integration of [[concepts/digital-therapeutics|digital therapeutics]] in nursing and [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] practice. Key considerations include:

*   Clinical application of [[concepts/digital-interventions|digital health technologies]], emphasizing safety protocols, education, and workforce development as outlined in *[[entities/clinical-practice|Clinical Practice]] for Nurses and Mental Health Professions* (Wilson, 2026).
*   The intersection of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and patient safety, requiring rigorous oversight similar to traditional EMR/EHR systems.
*   [[concepts/secure-control|Secure control]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for [[concepts/production-ai|production AI]] agents via platforms like Archest.AI to mitigate risks associated with automated clinical decision support.

For detailed bibliographic context on [[concepts/digital-health|digital health therapeutic innovation]], see [[inbox/2026-09-29-Digital-Health-Therapeutic-Innovation|Digital Health Therapeutic Innovation]].
