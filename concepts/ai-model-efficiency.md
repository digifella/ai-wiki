# AI Model Efficiency

**AI [[concepts/ai-model-optimization|Model Efficiency]]** refers to the optimization of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]], [[concepts/energy-consumption|energy consumption]], and latency in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] systems without compromising performance. The field is currently undergoing a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] from [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[concepts/active-parameters|model parameters]] to architectural innovations that prioritize [[concepts/mixture-of-experts]] and domain-specific [[concepts/specialization|specialization]].

## Key Trends

*   **Shift from Scale to Efficiency**: Modern development prioritizes specialized architectures over brute-force [[concepts/ai-scaling-laws|parameter scaling]] to reduce [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] costs and environmental impact.
*   **Specialization**: Models are increasingly designed for specific tasks or domains, improving performance per watt compared to [[concepts/jacks-of-all-trades|generalist]] [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]].
*   **NASA-IBM Lunar AI**: Recent collaborations, such as the partnership between [[entities/nasa]] and IBM, focus on developing robust [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] for extreme environments, specifically for [[concepts/lunar-return|lunar missions]]. This work highlights the necessity of high efficiency and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] in off-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/computation|computing]].
*   **[[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE)**: [[concepts/adoption|Adoption]] of MoE architectures allows models to activate only relevant subsets of parameters for a given input, significantly enhancing [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]].
