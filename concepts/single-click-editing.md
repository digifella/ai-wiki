# Single Click Editing

Single Click Editing is a workflow feature in Lightroom Classic that allows photographers to apply multiple automated AI masks to an image with a single click. Rather than manually creating and adjusting individual masks for different parts of a photograph, users can save and apply preset combinations that automatically isolate specific elements like skies, subjects, or backgrounds. This functionality streamlines repetitive editing tasks, particularly for photographers working with consistent subjects or conditions.

The feature operates by leveraging Adobe's machine learning algorithms to detect and segment common photographic components. When a user selects a preset, the software generates a composite mask that applies distinct adjustments to the identified regions simultaneously. For landscape photography, this typically involves separating the sky from the foreground, while for people photography, it focuses on isolating the subject from the background.

Presets for Single Click Editing can be customized and saved by the user, allowing for the creation of specific workflows tailored to particular genres or lighting conditions. These saved configurations can be applied to individual images or batch-processed across multiple files, significantly reducing the time required for complex masking operations. The tool is integrated into the Masking panel, providing a direct interface for accessing and managing these automated selections.
