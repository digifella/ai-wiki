# Generative Ai Models

[[concepts/advanced-ai-models|Generative AI models]] are [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]] trained to produce new content—text, images, code, or other data—based on patterns learned from training data. These models operate by predicting the next token (word, character, or data unit) in a sequence, repeating this process iteratively to generate coherent outputs. Modern [[concepts/generative-ai|generative models]] power applications across [[concepts/coding|software development]], creative industries, research, and business analytics by enabling [[concepts/ai-driven-content-generation|automated content creation]] and [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|problem-solving]] at scale.

## Context Windows and Model Capability

A critical factor in the evolution of generative AI is the [[concepts/context-length|context window]], which defines the amount of prior information a model can process during inference. Early models were limited to short sequences, restricting their ability to maintain coherence over long documents or complex multi-turn interactions. As [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]] expand, models can ingest larger datasets, reference extensive codebases, and retain more detailed instructions, significantly enhancing their utility in [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]].

## Agentic RAG Systems

The maturation of context windows has facilitated the development of [[concepts/agentic-rag|Agentic Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) systems. Unlike [[concepts/traditional-rag|traditional RAG]], which passively retrieves static documents, [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]] actively query [[concepts/external-knowledge|external knowledge]] bases, reason over the retrieved data, and execute actions based on the results. This dynamic interaction allows models to overcome inherent knowledge cutoffs and reduce hallucinations by grounding responses in real-time, verified information.

## Future Trajectory

As context windows continue to grow and efficiency improves, the boundary between [[concepts/short-term-memory|short-term memory]] and long-term [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge retrieval]] blurs. This shift enables more [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] that can manage complex, [[concepts/multi-step-tasks|multi-step tasks]] without constant human intervention. The ongoing refinement of these architectures focuses on balancing computational cost with the ability to process vast amounts of contextual data, driving the next phase of generative AI maturity.
