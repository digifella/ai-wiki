# Secondary Market Transfers

Post-issuance exchange of securities or assets between holders, distinct from primary market issuance. Drives liquidity, price discovery, and capital reallocation without altering issuer equity [[concepts/structure|structure]].

## Operational Characteristics
*   **Settlement:** Mediated by transfer [[entities/agent|agent]], clearinghouse, or smart contracts; requires adherence to KYC/AML protocols.
*   **Restrictions:** Private placements and restricted stock impose transfer limitations, accreditation checks, and rights of first refusal.
*   **Valuation Dynamics:** Prices diverge from issuance terms based on supply/demand, often exhibiting higher volatility in illiquid private markets.

## Enforcement & Anomalies
*   **Unauthorized Transfer Voiding:** Entities may legally invalidate trades violating transfer agreements or accreditation [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], retroactively nullifying ownership claims.
