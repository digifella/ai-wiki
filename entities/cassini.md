# Cassini

**Cassini** refers primarily to the Cassini–Huygens mission, a joint NASA/ESA/ASI spacecraft mission that studied the Saturn system from 2004 to 2017. It provided critical data on Saturn's [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]], rings, and moons, complementing earlier findings from [[entities/voyager-2]].

## Key Discoveries & Observations

*   **[[concepts/saturns-hexagonal-north-pole|Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole]]:** Cassini extensively studied the massive, stable, geometric cloud pattern at Saturn's North Pole, first discovered by [[entities/voyager-2]] in 1981.
*   **[[concepts/atmospheric-dynamics|Atmospheric Dynamics]]:** The mission helped refine explanations for the hexagon's [[concepts/data-persistence|persistence]] and structure, linking it to deep atmospheric jet streams.
