# Peking University

Peking University is a leading research institution in China that has contributed to developments in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and academic technology. The university has collaborated with [[concepts/google-search|Google]] and other partners on the [[concepts/automated-academic-illustration|PaperBanana]] framework, a tool designed to automate the generation of illustrations for academic papers.

## PaperBanana Framework

PaperBanana represents a joint effort to address the time-consuming process of creating figures and [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]] for academic publications. The framework applies machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] techniques to automatically generate visual illustrations based on academic paper content, potentially streamlining the research publication workflow for scientists and researchers.
