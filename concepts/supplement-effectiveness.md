# Supplement Effectiveness

**Definition:** The degree to which a dietary supplement produces the intended physiological effect, validated by clinical trials, meta-analyses, and systematic reviews. Effectiveness is often distinct from safety; many supplements are safe but lack robust evidence for efficacy.

## General Principles of Evaluation
- **Hype vs. Evidence:** Marketing claims rarely correlate with [[concepts/statistical-significance|statistical significance]] in independent studies.
- **Individual Variability:** Genetic factors, baseline nutrient status, and microbiome [[concepts/writing|composition]] significantly alter response to supplementation.
- **Placebo Effect:** Subjective measures (energy, pain reduction) are highly susceptible to placebo effects without double-blind control.
