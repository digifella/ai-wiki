# Hypothetical Magic Pill

## Concept Overview
The hypothetical magic pill is a thought experiment in which an idealized pharmaceutical [[concepts/solution|solution]] addresses multiple [[concepts/health|health]] and wellness issues simultaneously. This idea is often used as a teaching tool to highlight the importance of real-world practices like [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], [[concepts/sleep|sleep]], and other [[concepts/healthy-habits|healthy habits]] that cannot be replicated by any single drug.
