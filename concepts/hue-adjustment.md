# Hue Adjustment

Hue adjustment is a color correction technique in Lightroom Classic that allows photographers to modify the hue, saturation, and lightness of specific color ranges within an image. Unlike global color corrections that affect an entire photograph uniformly, hue adjustment targets particular colors—such as reds, oranges, yellows, greens, cyans, blues, and magicias—enabling selective modifications that preserve untouched tones while enhancing or correcting specific color areas.

## The Color Mixer Tool

Lightroom Classic accomplishes hue adjustment primarily through the Color Mixer panel, located in the Develop module. This tool provides independent sliders for each major color range, allowing photographers to adjust hue (shifting a color toward neighboring hues), saturation (increasing or decreasing color intensity), and lightness (brightening or darkening) for individual colors without affecting others. For example, a photographer might shift the hue of sky blues toward cyan while simultaneously reducing the saturation of skin tones in the same image.

## Practical Applications

Hue adjustment serves numerous practical purposes in photographic workflows. Common uses include correcting color casts in specific tonal ranges, enhancing or muting particular colors for aesthetic effect, and addressing technical color issues that cannot be resolved through white balance alone. The technique is particularly valuable when targeted adjustments offer better results than attempting corrections through curves or other global tools, or when preserving the natural appearance of unaffected colors is essential to the final image.
