# Crimea

**Crimea** is a peninsula in Eastern [[entities/europe|Europe]], disputed between [[entities/ukraine]] and [[entities/russia]]. Since 2014, it has been under de facto Russian control, serving as a critical strategic hub for [[concepts/russian-offensive|Russian military operations]] in the Black Sea and the southern front of the Russian invasion of Ukraine.

## Strategic Significance
- **Naval Base:** Hosts the Russian Black Sea Fleet, providing power projection capabilities in the Mediterranean and Black Sea.
- **Land Bridge:** Connects Russian territory to Occupied Territories of Ukraine, facilitating [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] to Donbas and Zaporizhzhia.
- **Fortifications:** Heavily fortified with [[concepts/air-defence|air defense]] systems and coastal batteries.
