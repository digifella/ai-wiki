# 1X Technologies

**1X Technologies** is a [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] company specializing in advanced prosthetic limbs, most notably the **NEO** robotic hand. The company focuses on biomimetic design to replicate natural human hand [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and sensation.

## Key Products & Technology

### NEO Robotic Hand
The flagship product, **NEO**, utilizes a unique mechanical design that differs from traditional multi-motor prosthetics.

*   **Biomimetic Design**: NEO employs a single motor and a complex tendon-driven system to mimic the natural coupling of human finger movements, rather than controlling each [[entities/digit|digit]] independently.
*   **Technical Analysis**: Recent analysis highlights the efficiency of this approach, noting that it reduces weight and complexity while maintaining dexterity. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-13-NEOs-Hands-Biomimicry-and-Technical-Design-Analysis-Repo|NEO's Hands: Biomimicry and Technical Design Analysis Report]] for detailed technical breakdowns.
*   **Expert Insight**: Insights from [[concepts/dr-john-knowlton|Dr. John Knowlton]] and [[concepts/dr-scott-walter|Dr. Scott Walter]] emphasize that traditional multi-motor approaches often fail to replicate the nuanced [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] of biological hands, a gap NEO aims to fill.
