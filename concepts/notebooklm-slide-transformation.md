# NotebookLM Slide Transformation

NotebookLM Slide Transformation is a workflow that converts source documents into video content using two Google products in sequence. The process leverages Google's NotebookLM to automatically generate presentation slides from uploaded documents, then imports those slides into Google Vids for further refinement and video production. This two-step approach enables users to transform text-based source material into structured visual presentations and ultimately into video format.

## Workflow Components

The transformation begins in NotebookLM, which analyzes uploaded documents and generates slide decks with structured content and layouts. Users can then export these slides and import them into Google Vids, a video creation tool that allows editing, narration addition, and media enhancement. Google Vids processes the slides into video format, with the key advantage that videos generated through this pathway do not include watermarks, distinguishing them from videos created through some alternative methods.

## Practical Application

This workflow is particularly useful for content creators, educators, and professionals who need to convert document-based information into multimedia presentations. Rather than manually creating slides and then building videos from scratch, users can leverage NotebookLM's automatic slide generation as a foundation, then use Google Vids to produce polished video output suitable for presentations, tutorials, or documentation purposes.
