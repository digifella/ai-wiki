# Mcps

Mcps refers to the strategic implementation of [[concepts/ai-coding-workflows|AI coding tools]] and agents to enhance [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] in small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs). The concept addresses how smaller organizations can leverage [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to streamline [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]], reduce manual [[concepts/coding|coding]] effort, and accelerate product delivery. Unlike large enterprises with dedicated [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] teams, SMEs face distinct constraints around budget, [[concepts/expertise|expertise]], and infrastructure, making targeted [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/ai-developer-tools|AI coding tools]] a practical approach to [[concepts/unique-value-proposition|competitive advantage]].

## Technical Implementation

The technical dimension of Mcps involves selecting and integrating appropriate [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] and [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] into existing [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]]. These tools range from code completion systems to more sophisticated agents capable of handling entire coding tasks with minimal human intervention. Successful implementation requires careful evaluation of tool compatibility with current tech stacks, [[concepts/security|security]] considerations, and the specific workflows of individual development teams.

## Organizational Adoption

Beyond tooling, Mcps encompasses the organizational practices necessary for effective integration. This includes [[concepts/developer|developer]] training, process redesign to accommodate AI-assisted workflows, and establishing clear guidelines for when and how to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] agents versus traditional development approaches. The human element remains central—teams must develop judgment about which tasks benefit most from AI assistance and maintain code quality standards.

[[entities/patrick-ellis|Patrick Ellis]], a CTO and startup co-founder, has been instrumental in advancing discussion around Mcps, emphasizing practical strategies for SMEs seeking to adopt these technologies without overcommitting resources or abandoning established development practices.
