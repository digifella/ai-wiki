# WIMP annihilation

The process by which [[concepts/wimp]] ([[concepts/wimp-particles|Weakly Interacting Massive Particles]]) annihilate upon collision, converting their mass into secondary [[concepts/standard-model-of-particle-physics|Standard Model]] particles. This mechanism is a primary method for the indirect detection of [[concepts/dark-matter]].

### Detection Signatures
- **Primary Products:** Production of high-energy [[entities/gamma|Gamma]] [[concepts/rays|rays]], neutrinos, and [[concepts/antimatter|antimatter]].
- **Observation Targets:** Regions of high [[concepts/dark-matter]] [[concepts/density|density]], such as the [[concepts/galactic-center|galactic center]] of the [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]].

### Recent Developments
- **Potential Signal Detection:** Recent reports indicate a possible [[concepts/particle-annihilation|WIMP annihilation]] signal detected via [[concepts/gamma-rays|Gamma rays]] within the [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]] (Ref: 2026 04 24 Dark Matter [[concepts/wimps|WIMP Annihilation]] Signal Detected in Milky Way Gamma Rays).
- **Scientific Significance:** This may represent the first direct observation of [[concepts/dark-matter]], an invisible substance estimated to be six times more abundant than regular [[concepts/baryonic-matter|baryonic matter]].
