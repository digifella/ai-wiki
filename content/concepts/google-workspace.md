# Google Workspace Overview

Google Workspace is a suite of cloud-based apps and services designed to help teams collaborate more effectively, manage email and video meetings, create documents and spreadsheets online, and much more. It includes [[concepts/software|applications]] like Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, [[entities/google-slides|Slides]], Meet, Chat, and Sites.

## Key Features
- **Collaboration:** Enables real-time editing of documents, spreadsheets, and presentations.
- **[[entities/storage|Storage]] & Sync:** Provides cloud storage with [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Drive for file management and sharing.
- **Communication:** Supports email communication through Gmail and video conferencing via Google Meet.
- **[[concepts/security|Security]] & Management:** Offers advanced security features such as data loss [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] and device management.
