# AI Driven Iteration

AI Driven Iteration is a design and development methodology that embeds AI-powered tools directly into creative and productive workflows. Rather than treating conception, review, and refinement as separate sequential phases, this approach leverages AI assistance continuously throughout the working process. Tools like Google Stitch and similar platforms enable practitioners to generate multiple variations, evaluate alternatives, and implement refinements at each stage, compressing traditionally extended timelines into accelerated cycles.

## Workflow Integration

The methodology shifts AI from a peripheral tool to a core component of iterative practice. Practitioners use AI systems to rapidly prototype ideas, produce alternative versions, and receive real-time feedback on their work. This integration reduces friction between ideation and execution, allowing creators to explore broader solution spaces and make more informed refinements within compressed timeframes. The approach is applicable across design, writing, software development, and other knowledge work domains.

## Practical Implications

By automating routine generation and variation tasks, AI Driven Iteration frees practitioners to focus on higher-level creative decisions and strategic direction. Workers can test concepts earlier and more frequently, reducing the risk of investing heavily in directions that may require fundamental revision. The methodology requires practitioners to develop new skills in prompt engineering and AI tool management, alongside domain expertise.
