# Manual Controls

Manual controls in mobile photography refer to the user's ability to directly adjust camera settings such as exposure, focus, white balance, and ISO on a smartphone device, rather than relying solely on automatic modes. These controls enable photographers to have greater creative control over their images and adapt to challenging lighting conditions that automatic systems may struggle with.

The implementation of these features has evolved significantly with the introduction of advanced applications like Adobe Project Indigo. Such tools provide professional-grade interfaces on mobile platforms, allowing for precise adjustments that were previously limited to dedicated digital cameras. This shift represents a move toward democratizing professional photography techniques by making complex settings accessible through intuitive touch-based interfaces.

By offering granular control over image parameters, these applications bridge the gap between casual snapshotting and professional workflow requirements. Users can now fine-tune technical aspects of their photography to achieve specific artistic visions or correct for environmental factors that automated algorithms might misinterpret.
