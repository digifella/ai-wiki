# Bruce McCandless

**Bruce Edward McCandless II** (1937–2017) was an American [[entities/nasa]] astronaut and naval aviator, best known for performing the first untethered spacewalk in history.

## Key Achievements
- **First Untethered Spacewalk**: On February 7, 1984, during the STS-41-B mission, McCandless used the Manned Maneuvering Unit (MMU) to drift up to 320 feet (98 meters) away from the Space Shuttle *Challenger*, marking the first time a human traveled in space without a physical [[concepts/connection|connection]] to a spacecraft.
- **Space Shuttle Missions**: Flew on two Space Shuttle missions: STS-41-B (1984) and STS-51-C (1985).

## Equipment and Technology
- **[[concepts/space-jetpacks|Extravehicular Activity]] (EVA) Suit**: McCandless's historic maneuver highlighted the critical role of advanced [[concepts/radiative-cooling|Space Suit Materials]] and Technology for [[concepts/extreme-space-protection|Extreme Space Protection]] in ensuring astronaut survival in the [[concepts/vacuum-of-space|vacuum of space]]. The suit provided life support, [[concepts/thermal-regulation|thermal regulation]], and pressure containment necessary for extended [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to the extreme environment of outer space.
- **Manned Maneuvering Unit (MMU)**: A jetpack-like device worn on the back of the space suit, allowing astronauts to maneuver freely in space.
