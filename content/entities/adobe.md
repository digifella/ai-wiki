# Adobe
Adobe is a multinational corporation headquartered in San Jose, California, known for its [[concepts/software|software]] and digital marketing solutions. It specializes in multimedia and creativity [[concepts/software|software]] products.

### Products
- **[[entities/creative-cloud|Creative Cloud]] (CC)**
    - Includes applications such as Photoshop, Illustrator, InDesign, [[entities/premiere-pro|Premiere Pro]], and [[concepts/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]].
- **Document Cloud**
    - Provides cloud-based document collaboration and sharing services.
- **Experience Cloud**
    - Offers a suite of marketing tools to manage customer experiences across various platforms.

### AI Integration
Adobe has been integrating [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] into its products to enhance [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] and efficiency. The latest developments include:

- **[[concepts/ai-assistant|AI Assistant]] and [[concepts/generative-markup-tools|AI Markup tools]] in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] for the Web**
    - Automatically renames layers based on content (demonstrated in colin smith chan

### Recent Developments
- **[[entities/adobe-project-indigo|Adobe Project Indigo]]**
    - A new [[concepts/mobile-photography|mobile photography]] app introduced by [[entities/colin-smith|Colin Smith]] ([[entities/photoshopcafe|PhotoshopCAFE]]).
