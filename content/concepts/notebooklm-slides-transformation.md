# Notebooklm Slides Transformation

NotebookLM Slides Transformation refers to a workflow for converting [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] generated by [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]], with the ability to remove watermarks from the output. This process bridges two [[concepts/google-search|Google]] AI products to enable content creators to repurpose text-based research materials into video presentations without platform branding.

## Process Overview

The transformation workflow leverages [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s slide generation capabilities as a starting point, then imports or recreates those [[entities/google-slides|slides]] within Google Vids for video production. Google Vids provides [[concepts/ai-driven-video-production|automated video creation]] features that can incorporate the slide content, apply transitions, and generate accompanying audio or visual elements. The method allows users to produce final video outputs that lack the default watermarking that typically appears on Google Vids-generated content.

## Practical Applications

This technique is particularly useful for content creators, educators, and researchers who want to convert research notes or documents into polished video content without visible platform attribution. By combining NotebookLM's organizational and synthesis capabilities with Google Vids' multimedia production tools, users can create branded or neutral video presentations suitable for various [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] channels.
