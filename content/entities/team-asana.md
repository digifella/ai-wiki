# Team Asana

Team Asana is the [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] and editorial team at Asana, a work management platform company. The team produces educational and instructional content focused on project management practices, methodologies, and frameworks. Their published materials serve to inform professionals about various approaches to organizing and executing projects.

## Content Focus

The team has authored comprehensive resources examining popular [[concepts/pmi-frameworks|project management methodologies]] and frameworks. Their work includes comparative analyses of different approaches to project planning and execution, designed to help practitioners understand the strengths and [[concepts/software|applications]] of various methodologies. This content is published through Asana's resource library and educational channels.
