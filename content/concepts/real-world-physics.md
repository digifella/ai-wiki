# Real-World Physics

**Real-World Physics** refers to the physical laws, principles, and phenomena that govern the natural universe, including mechanics, thermodynamics, [[concepts/electromagnetic-interaction|electromagnetism]], and quantum mechanics. In computational contexts, it often involves the simulation of these laws to create realistic environments for analysis, engineering, or [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] training.

## Core Principles
- **Deterministic Systems**: Classical mechanics where future states are determined by initial conditions and laws of motion.
- **Stochastic Systems**: Systems involving [[concepts/probability|probability]] and randomness, crucial in quantum mechanics and statistical thermodynamics.
- **Conservation Laws**: Conservation of energy, momentum, and charge remain fundamental constraints in any accurate physical model.

## Computational Integration
- **Simulation Fidelity**: [[concepts/enhanced-simulations-that-better-capture-the-complexities-of-real-world|High-fidelity simulations]] require solving partial differential equations (PDEs) to model [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]], structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and heat transfer.
- **Physics-Informed [[concepts/machine-learning|Machine Learning]]**: Integrating physical constraints into [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] to ensure outputs adhere to known physical laws, improving generalization and data efficiency.
- **World Models in AI**: 
	- AI systems increasingly utilize [[concepts/world-models]] to internalize an understanding of [[concepts/environmental-dynamics|environmental dynamics]], allowing for predictive [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and planning.
	- See detailed analysis: [[lab-notes/2026-05-24-World-Models-in-AI-Concept-Implementations-and-Applicati|World Models in AI: Concept, Implementations, and Applications]]
	- Key implementations involve latent variable models that compress sensory input into structured representations of state transitions.

## Applications
- **Robotics**: Learning [[concepts/policies|policies]] that respect physical constraints for safe and efficient [[concepts/exercise|movement]].
- **[[concepts/scientific-discovery|Scientific Discovery]]**: Using AI to accelerate material science and drug discovery by simulating molecular interactions.
- **[[concepts/virtual-environments|Virtual Environments]]**: Creating photorealistic and physically accurate simulations for training [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]].
