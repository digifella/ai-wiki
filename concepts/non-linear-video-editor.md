# Non-linear Video Editor

A non-linear video editor (NLE) enables [[concepts/non-linear-editing|random-access editing]] of video clips stored digitally, decoupling editing operations from the physical sequence of source media. NLEs utilize a timeline-based interface with independent tracks for video, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and effects, allowing precise manipulation, transitions, and [[concepts/video-editing|post-production]] without degradation of original assets.

## Core Features
- Random access to digital media files.
- Multitrack [[concepts/writing|composition]] and layering.
- Real-time preview and non-destructive effect application.
- Support for diverse codecs and [[concepts/solution|resolution]] workflows.
