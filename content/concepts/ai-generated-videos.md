# AI Generated Videos

[[concepts/ai-videos|AI-generated videos]] are digital video content created using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools that convert text-based descriptions into realistic video outputs. These tools accept natural [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|language prompts]] from users describing desired video content and generate corresponding footage without requiring traditional filming equipment or extensive [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] expertise. The technology has made video production more accessible to creators with minimal technical [[concepts/skills|skills]], enabling the rapid production of content that would traditionally require professional crews and post-production work.

## How It Works

The underlying technology typically combines [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] with diffusion [[concepts/models|models]] or other generative techniques. Users provide [[concepts/text|text]] descriptions of the video they want to create, specifying elements like scenes, actions, camera movements, and [[concepts/style|style]]. The AI system interprets these descriptions and generates video frames sequentially, maintaining [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across the [[concepts/output|output]]. The process generally takes minutes to hours depending on video length and complexity, producing results that [[concepts/range|range]] from simple animations to photorealistic footage.

## Current Capabilities and Limitations

Existing [[concepts/ai-driven-video-production|AI video generation]] tools can create short videos, typically ranging from a few seconds to a minute in length. They perform well with descriptive prompts that specify clear visual concepts and straightforward actions. However, current systems struggle with maintaining perfect logical consistency across longer sequences, precisely matching complex [[concepts/physics|physics]] simulations, or generating highly specific real-world details. Generation quality varies significantly between different tools, and outputs often require some degree of post-processing or refinement for [[concepts/professional-use|professional use]].

## Applications and Adoption

[[concepts/ai-generated-video|AI video generation]] has [[concepts/software|applications]] across [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], marketing, education, and entertainment. Organizations use the technology to produce social media content, product demonstrations, and explanatory videos more efficiently. The tools have also enabled independent creators to experiment with video production [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] lower [[concepts/cost|cost]] and faster [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] speeds than traditional methods allow.
