# Starter Plan

The Starter Plan is an entry-level subscription tier offered by Evernote, designed for individual users who require basic note-taking and organizational capabilities without access to advanced features. Positioned as the most accessible paid option, it serves as a bridge for users transitioning from free accounts, providing core Evernote functionality at a lower cost than premium subscription levels.

This tier includes essential tools such as text formatting and basic organization through notebooks. It ensures synchronization across devices, allowing users to maintain their notes seamlessly. By focusing on fundamental utility, the plan caters to those who need reliable storage and retrieval without the complexity or expense of higher-tier offerings.

The introduction of this plan is discussed in the context of Evernote's broader pricing structure updates and user migration guidance. It aims to retain users who require more capacity than the free tier allows but do not yet need the extensive features found in professional or business plans. This structure allows Evernote to segment its user base effectively while offering a clear upgrade path for growing needs.
