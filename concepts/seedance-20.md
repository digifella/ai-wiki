# Seedance 20

Seedance 20 is a structured workflow designed for producing professional commercial videos by integrating Claude AI prompting with the Seedance 2.0 image-to-video model. This approach utilizes Claude’s language processing capabilities to translate creative briefs and concept descriptions into precise, contextually appropriate technical prompts. These optimized prompts serve as the primary input for the Seedance 2.0 engine, guiding the generation of high-fidelity video content.

The process aims to reduce manual production time and technical overhead by automating the translation of abstract creative ideas into specific visual instructions. By leveraging the synergy between large language model reasoning and advanced image-to-video synthesis, creators can achieve broadcast-quality results with greater efficiency. The workflow emphasizes the importance of prompt precision, ensuring that the generated video aligns closely with the intended commercial narrative and aesthetic requirements.
