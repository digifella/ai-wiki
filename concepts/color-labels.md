# Color Labels

Color labels in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] are a fundamental organizational tool used to categorize and manage photographs within a catalog. They provide a quick visual system for flagging images during the [[concepts/editing-workflow|editing workflow]], allowing photographers to group similar images or mark them according to custom criteria. This system works alongside other Lightroom features like [[concepts/star-ratings|star ratings]] and [[concepts/flags|flags]] to create a comprehensive [[concepts/organizational-structure|organizational structure]].

## Application in Editing

Color labels are assigned to individual photographs using Lightroom's labeling interface, with six default colors available: red, yellow, green, blue, purple, and orange. Photographers can develop their own labeling conventions to suit their workflow—for example, using colors to indicate editing status, subject matter, client approval, or intended use. Because color labels are visible at a glance in both grid and loupe view, they enable rapid sorting and filtering during the editing process, reducing the time spent navigating large catalogs.

## Integration with Workflow

The flexibility of color labels makes them particularly valuable when combined with other organizational methods. A photographer might use flags to mark initial selections, star ratings to indicate quality, and color labels to denote the next action or processing stage. Smart collections can be created based on color label criteria, allowing photographers to automatically group images that share the same label for batch processing or review.
