# Visa Expiration

Visa expiration denotes the final date on which a visa remains valid for travel and entry purposes. Once this date passes, the document can no longer be used to enter the destination country, and travelers must obtain a new visa if they wish to travel. The expiration date is a critical piece of information printed on all visa documents, whether in physical or digital form, and travelers are responsible for checking this date before attempting to cross a border.

Electronic visas, or e-visas, operate under the same expiration principles as traditional paper visas. For the specific case of Turkey e-visas, the expiration date indicates the last day the holder may enter the country. It is distinct from the duration of stay, which determines how long the traveler can remain within the borders after entry. Failure to enter before the expiration date renders the e-visa invalid, requiring a new application.

Travelers must ensure their intended date of entry falls within the validity period specified on their visa document. Border authorities typically deny entry to individuals presenting expired visas, regardless of the remaining validity of their passport. Consequently, verifying the expiration date is a mandatory step in pre-travel planning to avoid denied entry or legal complications at the border.
