## Google DeepMind's Gemma 4: High-Performance, Accessible Open-Source AI Models

### Summary
The video introduces [[concepts/23b-parameter-models|Gemma 4]], [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s latest family of [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]], emphasizing its significant advancements in performance, efficiency, and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. Cassidy, a researcher at Google [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]], highlights that [[concepts/e4b-model|Gemma 4]] aims to set a new precedent for small, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. A key strategic decision underscored in the launch is the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of the [[concepts/apache-2.0-license|Apache 2.0 license]], which makes the models more accessible for developers to integrate into their development lifecycles, from initial [[concepts/testing|testing]] to [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] and building new applications.

The Gemma 4 family includes four models tailored for diverse applications. The smaller "effective" models, E2B and E4B, are optimized for on-device applications, designed to run locally on phones, tablets, and laptops. These models deliver strong performance at a compact scale. For more demanding tasks, the family offers two larger models: a 26B [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE) model and a 31B dense model. The 31B dense model, lauded for its "insane performance," ranks highly on the global Arena AI leaderboard, reportedly outperforming models 20 times its size. The 26B MoE model achieves impressive efficiency by activating only 3.8 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] during [[concepts/inference|inference]], despite having 128 total experts, a first for the Gemma family.

Gemma 4's superior performance is attributed to several technical innovations within its architecture. Enhanced [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]] feature a 5:1 ratio of local to global layers (4:1 for the [[concepts/e2b-model|E2B model]]), utilizing a sliding [[concepts/context-window|context window]] for local layers and global [[concepts/attention|attention]] for the final layer, ensuring comprehensive context understanding. This is complemented by Grouped Query [[concepts/attention|Attention]] (GQA), which boosts [[concepts/memory-efficiency|memory efficiency]] by allowing multiple queries to share key and value heads. For the smaller "effective" models, Per-Layer Embeddings (PLE) are crucial; they enable storing embedding tables in flash [[concepts/memory|memory]] rather than expensive [[concepts/vram|VRAM]], significantly reducing the operational [[concepts/parameter-count|parameter count]] and allowing these models to run effectively on edge devices without requiring cloud [[entities/api-calls|API calls]].

Beyond [[concepts/architectural-improvements|architectural improvements]], Gemma 4 introduces enhanced native [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]]. Building on [[entities/gemma-3|Gemma 3]]'s introduction of [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], Gemma 4 now supports variable aspect ratios and resolutions for image processing, giving developers flexibility in allocating soft token budgets based on image quality needs. [[concepts/images|Images]] are broken into patches, projected, and processed by a transformer encoder. Additionally, the E2B and E4B models now natively support audio input. This is facilitated by a 305M conformer [[concepts/audio|audio]] encoder that processes embeddings generated by an [[concepts/audio|audio]] tokenizer, paving the way for advanced [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] and translation applications directly on mobile devices.

In summary, Gemma 4 represents a significant leap forward for open-source AI, offering a versatile suite of models from compact, on-device solutions to powerful, [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] engines. The combination of an permissive [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0 license]], innovative architectural designs like MoE and PLE, and native [[concepts/multimodal-support|multimodal support]] ([[concepts/text|text]], vision, and audio) democratizes access to cutting-[[concepts/edge-ai|edge AI]]. Developers can easily get started by downloading and self-hosting the models through platforms such as [[concepts/open-source-machine-learning|Hugging Face]], Kaggle, and [[entities/ollama|Ollama]], or utilize cloud-hosted options like [[entities/ai-studio|AI Studio]] and Vertex AI for seamless [[concepts/integration|integration]] and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
