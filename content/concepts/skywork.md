# Skywork

[[entities/skywork|Skywork]] is an AI-powered presentation creation tool designed to automate the process of generating [[entities/google-slides|slides]] and presentation content. It functions within the broader category of [[concepts/generative-apps|generative AI applications]] that assist users in creating business and visual materials.
