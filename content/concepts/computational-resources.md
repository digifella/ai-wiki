## Computational Resources

**[[concepts/llms|Definition]]:** The term "computational resources" refers to the [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] and [[concepts/software|software]] tools used for performing computations in computer science. This includes CPU/GPU power, [[concepts/memory|memory]] ([[concepts/ram|RAM]]), [[entities/storage|storage]] capacity, network bandwidth, and other related technologies.
