# Gen Z

Gen Z refers to the generation born approximately between 1997 and 2012. As digital natives who grew up with widespread internet access and mobile technology, Gen Z represents a significant demographic cohort with distinct characteristics that influence [[concepts/consumer-behavior|consumer behavior]], workplace dynamics, and business strategy.

## Characteristics and Market Relevance

Gen Z consumers prioritize authenticity, social responsibility, and digital-first experiences. They are accustomed to rapid technological change and expect seamless integration of digital tools in both personal and professional contexts. This generation demonstrates strong preferences for brands that demonstrate genuine social and environmental commitment, and they tend to engage more readily with peer recommendations and user-generated content than traditional advertising.

## Business Strategy Implications

For businesses, Gen Z represents both a growing consumer market and an increasingly important workforce segment. Companies developing products and services targeting this demographic must emphasize digital [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], sustainability, and transparency. In workplace settings, Gen Z employees often expect flexibility, continuous [[concepts/learning|learning]] opportunities, and clear alignment between corporate values and business practices.
