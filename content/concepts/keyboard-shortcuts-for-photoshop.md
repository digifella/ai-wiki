# Keyboard Shortcuts For Photoshop

Keyboard shortcuts in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] are customizable key combinations that trigger [[concepts/commands|commands]], activate tools, or execute functions without requiring menu navigation. They form a central part of efficient [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] in the application, allowing users to reduce repetitive mouse movements and menu searching. Photoshop ships with an extensive default set of shortcuts covering [[concepts/tool-selection|tool selection]], layer operations, view controls, and filter application. Users can view, modify, and create custom shortcuts through the Keyboard Shortcuts dialog found under Edit > Keyboard Shortcuts.

## Common Shortcuts

Frequently used shortcuts include Ctrl+Z (Cmd+Z on [[entities/mac|Mac]]) for undo, Ctrl+S for save, and the bracket keys [ and ] for adjusting [[concepts/brush-size|brush size]]. Tool shortcuts typically use single letters—B for brush, M for move, C for crop—allowing rapid tool switching without leaving the keyboard. View shortcuts like the spacebar (for panning) and number keys (for zoom levels) facilitate navigation through complex compositions.

## Customization and Efficiency

The ability to customize shortcuts means experienced users can optimize their workflow to match personal preferences or conform to shortcuts from other [[concepts/software|software]]. Many professionals develop muscle [[concepts/memory|memory]] around their chosen shortcuts, which can significantly reduce production time on repetitive tasks. Memorizing the most frequently used shortcuts for one's particular workflow—whether focused on [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]], [[concepts/digital-painting|digital painting]], or graphic [[concepts/design|design]]—is often cited as a practical approach to improving productivity without attempting to memorize the full command set.
