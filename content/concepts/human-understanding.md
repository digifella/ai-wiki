# Human Understanding

The cognitive capacity to perceive, interpret, and internally simulate [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]], enabling predictive [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], causal [[concepts/inference|inference]], and adaptive interaction. Unlike purely statistical processing, human understanding emerges from multisensory grounding, embodied [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]], and iterative [[concepts/hypothesis-driven-experimentation|hypothesis testing]].

## Core Mechanisms
- **Perceptual Grounding**: Direct sensorimotor coupling establishes stable representations of [[concepts/object-permanence|object permanence]], spatial topology, and physical invariants
- **Predictive [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]]**: Internal forward [[concepts/models|models]] forecast state transitions before action, reducing real-time computational load
- **Causal [[concepts/abstraction|Abstraction]]**: Distills invariant laws from noisy observations, enabling zero-shot transfer across novel physical domains
- **[[concepts/theory|Theory]] of Mind**: Attributes intentional states and reasoning processes to other [[concepts/agents|agents]], enabling collaborative prediction and social coordination

## AI Alignment & World Models
- Contrasts human embodied [[concepts/learning|learning]] with [[concepts/large-language-model]] token-prediction, exposing deficits in intrinsic physical grounding and causal reasoning
- Introduces [[concepts/world-models]] as differentiable simulators that approximate human-like internal dynamics through environmental interaction
- [[concepts/highlights|Highlights]] that humans develop an internal simulation engine through continuous sensorimotor calibration, whereas current AI relies on distributional [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] without mechanistic grounding
- Proposes hybrid architectures merging generative world simulation with symbolic [[concepts/logical-consistency|constraint satisfaction]] to narrow the comprehension gap
