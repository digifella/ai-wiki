# Kai

Kai is a presentation on [[concepts/aimm-ai-maturity-model|personal AI infrastructure]] delivered by [[entities/daniel-miessler|Daniel Miessler]] and hosted by [[entities/clint-gibler|Clint Gibler]] on the [[entities/tldr-sec|Unsupervised Learning channel]]. The presentation addresses the [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] and implementation of individual-scale AI systems, examining how personal AI infrastructure can be designed and deployed.

The concept emerged from discussions about building localized AI scaffolds—systems that integrate [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into personal computing environments rather than relying solely on centralized cloud-based services. Miessler's framework considers practical approaches to deploying and managing [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] at the individual level, including infrastructure patterns and architectural decisions relevant to personal [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

Kai represents part of a broader conversation within AI communities about decentralization, personal agency in AI systems, and alternatives to purely commercial AI platforms. The presentation serves as a reference point for understanding how individuals might architect their own [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] rather than remaining dependent on third-party services.
