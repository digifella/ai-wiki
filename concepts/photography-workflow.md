Photography workflow refers to the systematic process photographers follow from capture through final delivery of images. A typical workflow encompasses several stages: image ingestion and organization, culling and selection, editing and enhancement, and export or publication. The specific steps and tools used vary depending on the photographer's genre, output requirements, and personal preferences.

## Digital Organization and Culling

After importing images, photographers sort and organize their digital files for efficient management. This stage includes reviewing images, removing duplicates and poor shots, and rating or flagging keepers. Modern tools increasingly incorporate AI-[[concepts/assisted-culling|assisted culling]] features that help identify technically sound images or automatically stack similar shots, reducing manual review time for large batches.

## Post-Processing and Editing

The editing [[concepts/phase|phase]] involves adjusting [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], and other attributes to enhance the image. This phase often utilizes software like [[concepts/lightroom]] or [[concepts/photoshop]].

### Software Stability and Troubleshooting

Maintaining a stable editing environment is critical to [[concepts/session-resumption|workflow continuity]]. Persistent [[concepts/software-crashes|software crashes]] can disrupt the editing [[concepts/phase|phase]] and lead to data loss. Recent investigations into [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]] stability issues highlight the potential of AI-driven diagnostics for resolving persistent crashes.

*   **AI-Assisted Diagnostics:** [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] can analyze [[concepts/catalog|catalog]] data and system logs to identify [[concepts/causes|root causes]] of unexpected freezes or crashes during routine tasks (e.g., copying develop settings, rating photos).
*   **Case Study:** A documented case involving persistent Lightroom Classic crashes was resolved by granting AI access to the catalog, demonstrating the utility of automated troubleshooting in complex editing environments. See [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-AI-Driven-Troubleshooting-of-Persistent-Adobe-Lightroom|AI-Driven Troubleshooting of Persistent Adobe Lightroom Classic Crashes]] for detailed findings.

## Export and Delivery

The final stage involves exporting images in formats suitable for their intended [[entities/medium|medium]] (web, print, social media) and delivering them to clients or audiences.
