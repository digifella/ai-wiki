# Pitch

## Overview
Pitch is an AI [[concepts/presentation-tool|presentation tool]] used for automated [[concepts/slide-deck-creation|slide deck creation]].

## Capabilities & Limitations
- **Use Case**: Highly effective for low-stakes presentations.
- **Critical Limitation**: Lacks the ability to perform deep [[concepts/insight-generation]]; specifically fails at uncovering the critical business insights necessary for high-stakes [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
- **The Insight Gap**: A significant gap exists between the identification of meaningful insights and the automated construction of [[entities/google-slides|slides]].
