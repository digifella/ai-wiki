# File Removal

File removal refers to the process of deleting or exporting files stored within [[entities/evernote|Evernote]]. This became a widespread concern in April 2026 following Evernote's announcement of significant changes to its [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]]. As the platform modified its [[concepts/pricing-model|pricing model]], many users reassessed their [[entities/storage|storage]] usage and account configurations to determine whether to maintain their subscriptions or reduce their data footprint.

## Deletion Methods

Users can remove files from Evernote through several approaches. Direct deletion involves selecting individual [[concepts/notes|notes]] or attachments and permanently removing them from the trash. Alternatively, users may export data to local formats before cancellation to preserve information outside the platform. These methods allow for [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over data [[concepts/storing|retention]] during account downgrades or [[concepts/fastening-devices|closures]].

## Strategic Considerations

The shift in pricing [[concepts/policies|policies]] prompted a broader evaluation of digital storage [[concepts/habits|habits]]. Users often utilized file removal as a strategy to lower monthly costs or prepare for migration to alternative services. Understanding the distinction between soft deletion and permanent erasure became critical for ensuring data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with new storage limits imposed by the updated tier system.
