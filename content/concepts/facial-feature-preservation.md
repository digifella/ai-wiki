# Facial Feature Preservation

Techniques ensuring distinct facial characteristics (eyes, nose, mouth) remain intact during [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], preventing distortion or loss of identity in processed visuals.

## Related Tools

- [[entities/hidream-e11|HiDream-E1.1]]: [[concepts/open-source|Open-source]] image editing model supporting [[concepts/dynamic-resolution|dynamic resolution]] and improved [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]]. Capable of [[concepts/detail-removal|detail removal]] (e.g., raindrops from street scenes while maintaining background [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]]), directly applicable to preserving facial features during edits.
