# Mythos 5

**[[concepts/mythos|Mythos]] 5** is an advanced class of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] developed by **[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]**, characterized by superior [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] and complex [[concepts/app-creation|application development]] [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]]. It represents the upper tier of [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s model [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]], designed for high-fidelity tasks requiring deep [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]] and precise [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]].

## Model Architecture & Positioning
- **Class Definition**: High-[[concepts/compute|compute]], specialized model tier focusing on [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]] and [[concepts/advanced-coding|advanced coding]] architectures.
- **Relation to [[entities/fable-5|Fable 5]]**: [[concepts/claude-mythos-5|Mythos 5]] serves as the foundational architecture for the consumer-facing [[concepts/claude-fable-5|Fable 5]] variant, which optimizes [[concepts/speed|inference speed]] and [[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|cost-efficiency]] while retaining core [[concepts/thinking-and-reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].

## Access Restrictions & Regulatory Status
As of June 2026, access to [[entities/claude-mythos|Mythos]] 5 (and its derivative [[concepts/fable-5-model|Fable 5]]) has been severely restricted due to [[entities/us-government|US government]] enforcement of [[concepts/us-export-controls|export controls]] on [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]] systems.
- **Immediate Shutdown**: Anthropic abruptly disabled access for foreign nationals, including those residing in the US, effectively halting usage for non-compliant [[concepts/nodes|entities]].
- **Sovereignty Implications**: The shutdown marks a pivotal shift in [[concepts/ai-sovereignty|AI sovereignty]], redefining frontier AI access as a regulated national [[concepts/security|security]] asset rather than an open commercial tool.
- **Impact on Development**: The restriction has disrupted downstream applications relying on high-fidelity [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], forcing developers to pivot to lower-tier models or alternative providers not subject to the same [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] frameworks.
