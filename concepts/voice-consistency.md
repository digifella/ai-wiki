# Voice Consistency

Uniformity of vocal characteristics ([[entities/pitch|pitch]], tone, pacing) across AI-generated [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] or video outputs, critical for maintaining authenticity in synthetic media. Ensures seamless [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] and brand recognition.

## Key Principles
- Requires synchronized [[concepts/text-to-speech-synthesis|text-to-speech]] (TTS) modeling and [[concepts/audio|audio]] parameter alignment
- Prevents [[concepts/cognitive-dissonance|cognitive dissonance]] in [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] [[concepts/continuity|continuity]]
- Essential for professional applications (e.g., marketing, eLearning)
