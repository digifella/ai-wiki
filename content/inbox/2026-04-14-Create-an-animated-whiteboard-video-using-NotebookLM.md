# Create an animated [[concepts/whiteboard|whiteboard]] video using [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]

---
---

Charles Terence [[entities/harper|Harper]]

Okay, here's a breakdown of how to create an animated video using [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], [[entities/nano-banana-pro|Nano Banana Pro]], and [[entities/pictory|Pictory]], as illustrated in the video:

1. **Starting Point**:

* Go to NotebookLM.
* Log into your account.
* Click the “Create New [[concepts/notebook|Notebook]]” button.

2. **Upload the script**.

* Click the “Add Source” button.
* Click “Upload [[concepts/files|Files]]” to upload the script from a file.
* Upload the script for your video to NotebookLM.

3. **Generate a Slide Deck**:

* Click on the "Slide Deck" option in the Studio.
* Select your format (detailed deck).
* Choose your language (English).
* Set the length.
* Add a high-level outline, or guide the audience, [[concepts/style|style]], and focus: "Create 1 (One) Slide covering the Main Points, Hand Drawn Style for Professionals.”
* Click “Generate.”

4. **Generate an image to represent main points from the script**.

* Go to [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]].
* Upload the same script to [[entities/google|Google]] [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]].
* Activate “Create Image”.
* Describe what you want to be shown.
* Click enter.
* While the NotebookLM slide deck is generating, also generate an image using [[entities/nano-banana|Nano Banana]] Pro.

5. **Image Animation**

* Go to the image animator (e.g. speedpaint.co).
* Click and upload the hand-drawn style image representing the main points of the content you have already created.
* Select and change the the sketching duration, hand style, sketching options, and custom sequence settings, as desired.
* Click animate.
* When finished, download.

6. **Using [[concepts/pictory|Pictory]]**:

* Go to Picstory ([[concepts/text-to-video|text to video]]).
* Paste the script into the “Text to video” input.
* Click “Generate Video.”
* Choose a theme.
* Choose your video and match with the audio from the generated image and audio in 11Labs and apply it to the entire video.
* Click Apply.
* Preview the video.
* If it looks good, export or download.

Hopefully, this [[entities/will|will]] help you. If you need any more clarifications just ask.
