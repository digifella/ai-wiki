# O1

O1 is an AI model developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], introduced as part of their [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]-focused model series. The model is designed to tackle complex problems across [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/science|science]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and logic by using extended reasoning processes before generating [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Capabilities

O1 excels [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] tasks requiring [[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]] and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. It is particularly noted for performance on technical benchmarks, including standardized tests and competitive programming challenges. The model uses a "chain of thought" approach internally, allowing it to work through problems methodically rather than generating immediate responses.

## Release and Availability

OpenAI released O1 as a limited preview, making it available to select users before broader [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]. The model represents OpenAI's direction toward reasoning-oriented AI systems, positioning it as distinct from faster, more general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] [[concepts/models|models]] in their product lineup.
