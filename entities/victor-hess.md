# Victor Hess

[[concepts/victor-hess|Victor Franz Hess]] was an Austrian physicist who discovered that [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] originate from outside of [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]] in 1912. His work earned him the Nobel Prize in [[concepts/physics|Physics]] in 1936.

## Key Contributions
- Discovery of cosmic radiation, also known as cosmic [[concepts/rays|rays]].
- Proved that these high-energy particles emanate from space and not atmospheric interactions.
