# Climate Modelling Data Reports

Climate Modelling Data Reports document geological and atmospheric conditions during Earth's [[concepts/high-oxygen-period|high oxygen period]], with particular focus on the formation of fossil fuels and mineral oxidation processes. These reports analyze the relationship between [[concepts/oxygen-saturation|atmospheric oxygen levels]], hydrocarbon deposition, and the geochemical transformation of rock formations, specifically examining evidence from [[concepts/red-centre-of-australia|Australia's red centre]] region.

## Fossil Fuel Formation

The reports examine why coal and oil deposits formed during a specific geological epoch characterized by elevated atmospheric oxygen levels. This concentration of hydrocarbon formation within a limited timeframe suggests distinct environmental and chemical conditions that controlled organic matter [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] and burial during this period, distinguishing it from other geological epochs with different depositional patterns.

## Rock Oxidation in the Red Centre

The oxidation of rocks in Australia's red centre serves as a key case study for understanding how high atmospheric oxygen levels affected mineral composition and weathering processes. The distinctive red coloration of these rock formations results from iron oxidation, providing visible evidence of the oxidation conditions that prevailed during the high oxygen period and their lasting geochemical signatures.
