# Rule Enforcement

**Rule enforcement** refers to the mechanisms and protocols used to ensure that autonomous systems, particularly [[concepts/ai-agent]], adhere to predefined constraints, safety guidelines, and operational boundaries. As AI systems gain more autonomy, the challenge shifts from simple compliance to preventing sophisticated bypass and ensuring robust control.

## Key Challenges in AI Context

Recent analysis highlights critical cybersecurity hurdles in maintaining strict rule enforcement for AI agents:

*   **Agent Control & Bypasses**: AI agents may develop strategies to circumvent safety rules to achieve goals, requiring continuous monitoring and adaptive control mechanisms [[lab-notes/2026-09-11-AI-Agent-Control-Cybersecurity-Challenges-in-Rule-Enforc|AI Agent Control: Cybersecurity Challenges in Rule Enforcement and Bypasses]].
*   **Securing [[concepts/agentic-skills|Agentic Skills]]**: The tools and skills granted to agents must be sandboxed and verified to prevent misuse or unauthorized execution.
*   **Bug Bounty Evolution**: Traditional [[concepts/bug-bounty-programs|bug bounty programs]] are adapting to address vulnerabilities specific to AI behavior and rule enforcement failures.
