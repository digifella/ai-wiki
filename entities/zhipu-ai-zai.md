# Zhipu Ai Zai

[[entities/zhipu-ai|Zhipu AI]], also known as Z.Ai, is a Chinese [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] company specializing in the development of [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] and [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. The organization focuses on creating practical AI solutions tailored for business automation, [[concepts/coding|software development]], and [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks.

The company's primary product is the [[concepts/advanced-coding|GLM-4.7]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]]. This model is specifically designed to address [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] in coding, business automation, and complex reasoning. It represents Zhipu AI's effort to provide [[concepts/democratization-of-ai|accessible AI]] tools for developers and organizations seeking efficient [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]].
