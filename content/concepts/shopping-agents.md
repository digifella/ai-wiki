# Shopping Agents

Shopping agents are [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] designed to automate or assist with shopping-related tasks, including product search, price comparison, purchase decisions, and transaction completion. These [[concepts/agents|agents]] operate across e-commerce platforms and retail environments, leveraging language [[concepts/models|models]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to interpret user preferences and navigate shopping workflows.

## Technical Architecture

Shopping agents are frequently discussed in the context of [[concepts/mixture-of-experts|mixture of experts]] (MoE) models and [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] approaches within the broader [[concepts/cloud-agents|AI agent development]] landscape. The modular [[entities/nature|nature]] of MoE architectures—where [[concepts/specialized-expert|specialized expert]] networks handle different task types—aligns well with the diverse subtasks involved in shopping operations, such as product categorization, price analysis, and purchase [[concepts/verification|verification]]. This architectural approach enables more efficient [[concepts/scaling|scaling]] of [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]] without proportionally increasing computational costs.

## Applications and Development

The development of shopping agents has become increasingly prominent in industry discussions around [[concepts/agent-deployment|agent deployment]], particularly within major technology and e-commerce companies. These agents represent a practical domain for [[concepts/testing|testing]] [[concepts/reasoning-steps|agent reasoning]], tool use, and user interaction patterns in real-world commercial contexts, contributing to broader research on [[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and performance [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] scale.
