# Google Drive Integration

[[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]] [[concepts/integration|Integration]] refers to the incorporation of [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] directly within [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s productivity ecosystem, particularly through Google Meet and other [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] [[concepts/software|applications]]. This integration enables users to access AI-powered assistance while working within familiar Google tools, streamlining workflows without requiring users to switch between separate applications.

## Gemini in Google Meet

One of the primary integration points is the embedding of [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] directly into Google Meet video calls. This allows participants to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] assistance during meetings, potentially supporting real-time collaboration and productivity tasks without interrupting the meeting [[concepts/flow|flow]].

## Broader Productivity Applications

The integration extends beyond Meet as part of Google's strategy to enhance productivity across its suite of tools. By embedding Gemini into existing Google applications, the company aims to [[entities/make|make]] AI features more accessible and contextually relevant to users' existing workflows, reducing [[concepts/friction|friction]] in [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and practical use.
