# OKF

**Open Knowledge Foundation** (OKF) is an international non-profit organization dedicated to promoting open knowledge, open data, and [[concepts/open-source|open source software]]. It supports initiatives that foster [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], collaboration, and the free [[concepts/flow|flow]] of information.

## Key Initiatives & Projects
- **OpenStreetMap**: Supports the development of the free editable map of the [[entities/earth|world]].
- **Open [[concepts/knowledge-base|Knowledge Base]]**: Infrastructure for open data and knowledge sharing.
- **Local [[concepts/ai-ecosystem|AI Ecosystem]]**: Collaborates on frameworks for decentralized and local [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems, focusing on [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and user sovereignty.

## Recent Developments
- **Local [[concepts/ai-agent-recall|AI Memory Systems]]**: In collaboration with [[entities/llm-wiki]], OKF is involved in exploring solutions for [[concepts/persistent-memory|persistent memory]] in [[concepts/local-ai-agents|local AI agents]].
	- A notable development involves a "Librarian System" designed to provide evolving, persistent [[concepts/memory|memory]] for [[concepts/hardware-heavy-models|local LLMs]], addressing the limitation of stateless interactions.
