# Uiux Design

UI/UX design encompasses the creation and optimization of user interfaces and user experiences in digital products. UI ([[concepts/user-interface|user interface]]) focuses on the visual and interactive elements—[[concepts/buttons|buttons]], menus, layouts, and visual [[concepts/feedback|feedback]]—while UX ([[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]]) addresses the broader journey a user takes when interacting with a product, including how intuitive, efficient, and satisfying that interaction feels.

## Core Design Principles

Several foundational concepts underpin effective UI/UX design. **[[concepts/affordances|Affordances]]** are visual or structural cues that suggest how an element should be used—for example, a button's appearance suggesting it can be clicked. **[[concepts/hierarchy|Hierarchy]]** organizes information by importance, guiding users' [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] through [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], size, color, and positioning. **Grids** provide underlying structural frameworks that create alignment, [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], and rhythm across layouts. **[[concepts/typography|Typography]]** involves selecting and arranging typefaces to enhance readability, establish visual hierarchy, and reinforce a design's character and brand.

These principles work together to create interfaces that are not only visually coherent but also intuitive and accessible. Effective UI/UX design reduces cognitive load on users, minimizes [[concepts/friction|friction]] in workflows, and creates experiences that feel natural and purposeful rather than arbitrary or confusing.
