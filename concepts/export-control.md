# National Security

**National security** in the digital domain refers to government-imposed restrictions and regulatory frameworks designed to protect critical infrastructure, prevent unauthorized access to advanced capabilities, and mitigate risks posed by adversarial actors. This includes [[concepts/export-control|export controls]] on technologies and proactive measures against emerging cryptographic threats.

## Export Control & Dual-Use Technologies
**Export control** involves restrictions on the transfer, export, and re-export of goods, technologies, software, and data. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], these regulations aim to prevent [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]] to advanced capabilities that could pose national [[concepts/security|security]] risks or be weaponized.

### Key Principles & Scope
- **Dual-Use Technology:** Regulations target "dual-use" items—technologies with both commercial and military applications. [[concepts/high-performance-ai|High-performance AI]] models and underlying infrastructure fall increasingly into this category.
- **National Security Interests:** Governments restrict exports to protect critical infrastructure and prevent the proliferation of capabilities that could destabilize global security balances.

## Post-Quantum Cryptography (PQC) Transition
Beyond export restrictions, national security strategies now address the threat of [[concepts/quantum-computing|quantum computing]] to current [[concepts/encryption-standards|encryption standards]]. The transition to [[concepts/post-quantum-cryptography|Post-Quantum Cryptography]] is critical to prevent "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks.

- **[[concepts/q-day|Q-Day]] Threat:** Refers to the hypothetical future date when [[entities/quantum-computing|quantum computers]] become powerful enough to break widely used public-key [[concepts/cryptographic-algorithms|cryptographic algorithms]], compromising long-term data confidentiality.
- **US [[concepts/executive-order|Executive Order]]:** Recent [[concepts/recommendations|directives]] mandate the transition to PQC standards to safeguard government and critical infrastructure data against future quantum decryption capabilities.
- **Strategic Integration:** PQC [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] is now a core component of national [[concepts/compliance|security compliance]], parallel to export control adherence for AI and dual-use technologies.
