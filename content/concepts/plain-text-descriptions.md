# Plain Text Descriptions

Plain [[concepts/text|text]] descriptions are textual representations of visual content, designed to convey information that would otherwise be communicated through [[concepts/images|images]], [[concepts/diagrams|diagrams]], or illustrations. These descriptions serve as an [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] tool and a means of preserving information in formats that can be processed by both humans and machines without requiring visual interpretation.

## Purpose and Application

Plain text descriptions are particularly valuable in academic and technical contexts where complex visual information needs to be documented, shared, and understood across different platforms and by diverse audiences. They enable researchers to communicate methodologies, results, and [[concepts/conceptual-frameworks|conceptual frameworks]] in a format that remains portable and machine-readable, supporting both accessibility [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and information [[concepts/preservation|preservation]].

## Use in Academic Contexts

In fields such as [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and computer [[concepts/science|science]], plain text descriptions have become increasingly important for documenting research processes and results. Frameworks like [[concepts/automated-academic-illustration|PaperBanana]] have emerged to automate the generation of these descriptions for academic illustrations, allowing researchers to efficiently create detailed textual representations of their visual work without manual transcription.
