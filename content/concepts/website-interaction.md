# Website Interaction

Interaction with websites involves automated or semi-automated processes that enable [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/agents|agents]] to perform tasks such as [[concepts/data-extraction|data extraction]], form submission, and navigation.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/automation|Automation]]**: Use of scripts or [[concepts/agents|agents]] to perform repetitive tasks on websites.
- **[[concepts/web-scraping|Web Scraping]]**: Extracting data from websites for analysis or [[entities/storage|storage]].
- **API [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Direct interaction with a website's backend services.
