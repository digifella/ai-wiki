# Spider Silk

**Spider silk** is a natural protein fiber known for its exceptional mechanical properties, including high [[concepts/tensile-strength|tensile strength]] and toughness. It serves as a primary example of biomimicry in materials [[concepts/science|science]], inspiring research into synthetic alternatives for applications ranging from medical sutures to ballistic armor.

## Key Properties
*   **Strength-to-Weight Ratio:** Often compared to steel, spider silk exhibits a tensile strength comparable to high-grade steel fibers but with significantly lower [[concepts/density|density]] material-science.
*   **Toughness:** Superior to Kevlar in terms of energy absorption before breaking, due to its combination of strength and elasticity toughness.
*   **Biocompatibility:** Naturally degradable and non-toxic, making it ideal for biomedical applications biomedical-[[entities/national-academies|engineering]].

## Synthetic Production Challenges
Despite its superior properties, replicating spider silk synthetically remains difficult due to:
*   **Protein Complexity:** The precise amino acid sequence and secondary structure (beta-sheets) are difficult to replicate outside the spider's spinneret.
*   **Production Scale:** Spiders are cannibalistic and cannot be farmed densely, limiting natural harvest biotechnology.
*   **Spinning Process:** The natural spinning process involves complex pH and ion gradients that are hard to mimic industrially [[concepts/chemical-engineering]].
