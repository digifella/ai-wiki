# Artifact recovery

The systematic process of locating, excavating, and preserving historical objects from archaeological contexts.

Key developments:
- Denmark's government deputized private [[concepts/metal-detecting|metal detecting]] enthusiasts to search for artifacts in agricultural fields, revealing the nation's history with unprecedented detail (Brown & Wong, 2026).
- This program uncovered Viking Age artifacts without disrupting farming, demonstrating successful integration of [[concepts/amateur-archaeology|amateur archaeology]] with land use.
- The initiative generated significant historical knowledge while fostering public engagement with archaeology and [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]] [[concepts/preservation|preservation]].
