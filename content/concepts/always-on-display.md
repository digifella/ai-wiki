# Always-On Display

An display technology feature that keeps a portion of the screen active even when the device is locked or in standby mode.

## Key Features
- Shows time, date, notifications, and other relevant information without fully waking the device.
- Conserves battery life by using optimized display modes.
- Customizable content and [[concepts/design|design]] options.
