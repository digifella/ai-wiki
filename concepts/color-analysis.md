# Color Analysis

## Core Concepts
- **Vectorscope**: A waveform display used to analyze color information, specifically hue and saturation, independent of brightness. Essential for verifying skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] accuracy.
- **Skin Tone Line**: A standard reference line on the vectorscope representing natural human [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]]. Deviations indicate [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]].
- **[[concepts/ai-based-selection|AI Masking]]**: Utilization of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] to isolate specific regions (e.g., skin) for targeted color correction without affecting the rest of the image.

## Techniques & Workflow
- **Vectorscope Analysis**:
  - Monitor skin tones against the skin tone line on the Vectorscope to ensure natural hue and saturation.
  - Use [[concepts/camera-raw-filter]] in [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] to access advanced vectorscope tools.
- **AI-Assisted Correction**:
  - Combine [[concepts/ai-masking]] with vectorscope [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] for precise [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
  - Isolate skin regions to apply color corrections that maintain natural [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] and lighting.
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] Editing**:
  - Avoid global color shifts that can distort skin tones unnaturally.
  - Use [[lab-notes/2026-08-09-Vectorscope-AI-Masking-for-Precise-Photoshop-Skin-Tone-C|Vectorscope & AI Masking for Precise Photoshop Skin Tone Correction]] for detailed tutorials on integrating these tools.
