# Regulatory Framework

The adoption of agentic AI by regulatory bodies marks a significant evolution in institutional governance. In December 2025, the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) initiated the deployment of agentic AI technologies for its internal operations. This move signifies a strategic shift toward automating core regulatory functions using autonomous systems capable of structured decision-making.

This development reflects growing institutional confidence in the reliability of AI agents within established regulatory workflows. By integrating these technologies in-house, the FDA aims to enhance operational efficiency and consistency in its administrative processes. The implementation focuses on leveraging autonomous agents to handle complex, data-intensive tasks that traditionally require significant human oversight.

The integration of agentic AI into the FDA's internal infrastructure serves as a precedent for other regulatory agencies. It demonstrates a practical application of advanced AI systems in high-stakes environments where accuracy and compliance are paramount. This internal adoption allows the agency to refine its understanding of AI capabilities and limitations before considering broader external applications.
