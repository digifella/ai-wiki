# Medical Imaging Technology

**Medical Imaging Technology** encompasses the techniques and processes used to create visual representations of the interior of a body for clinical analysis and medical intervention. It plays a critical role in diagnostics, treatment planning, and [[concepts/health|health]] monitoring.

## Core Modalities
- **Ultrasound**: Uses high-frequency sound waves to produce real-time images. Non-invasive and lacks ionizing radiation.
- **X-ray/CT**: Utilizes ionizing radiation for high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] structural imaging.
- **MRI**: Uses magnetic fields and radio waves for detailed soft-tissue [[concepts/contrast|contrast]].
- **Nuclear Medicine**: Involves radioactive tracers for functional imaging.

## Emerging Trends & AI Integration
- **AI-Enhanced Diagnostics**: [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|Machine learning models]] assist in detecting anomalies with higher accuracy than traditional methods.
- **Longitudinal [[concepts/health-surveillance|Health Monitoring]]**: Shift from episodic care to continuous tracking of physiological changes over time.
- **Democratization of Imaging**: Development of portable, [[concepts/resource-constrained-devices|low-cost devices]] for remote or resource-limited settings.
