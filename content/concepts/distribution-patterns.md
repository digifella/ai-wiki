# Distribution Patterns

[[concepts/distribution|Distribution]] patterns refer to the structured or seemingly random ways in which elements are arranged within a given system. In [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and statistics, this concept is crucial for understanding underlying structures and predicting future occurrences. This page specifically focuses on distribution patterns as they relate to [[concepts/prime-numbers|prime numbers]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/prime-numbers|Prime Numbers]]**: Integers greater than 1 that cannot be formed by multiplying two smaller natural numbers.
- **[[concepts/riemann-hypothesis|Riemann Hypothesis]]**: A conjecture about the distribution of the zeros of the [[concepts/riemann-zeta-function|Riemann zeta function]], which has implications for the distribution of prime numbers.
