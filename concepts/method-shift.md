# Method Shift

**Definition:** A paradigm shift in AI interaction models where systems transition from passive instruction-following to autonomous goal-directed behavior, reducing or eliminating the need for explicit human prompting.

## Core Concepts

- **Autonomous Superagents:** AI entities capable of executing complex, [[concepts/multi-step-tasks|multi-step tasks]] without continuous human oversight.
- **AGI Arrival:** The theoretical or practical emergence of [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|Artificial General Intelligence]], characterized by broad adaptability and self-direction.
- **Instruction Obsolescence:** The diminishing relevance of detailed prompts as models gain the ability to infer intent and execute strategies independently.

## Key Developments

- **[[concepts/gpt-6-astra|GPT-6 Astra]] Capabilities:**
  - Demonstrates autonomous decision-making and task execution [[lab-notes/2026-09-07-GPT-6-Astra-Autonomous-Superagent-Abilities-and-AGI-Meth|GPT-6 Astra: Autonomous Superagent Abilities and AGI Method Shift]].
  - Operates as a "superagent" that does not require explicit instructions for standard operational flows.
  - Represents a tangible example of the Method Shift from reactive to proactive AI systems.
