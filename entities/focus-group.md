# Focus Group

A **Focus Group** is a [[concepts/qualitative-research|qualitative research]] method where a small group of people are interviewed about a particular subject, such as a new product or service concept. It is widely used in Market Research, Social Sciences, and [[concepts/product-design|Product Development]] to gain insights into consumer attitudes, perceptions, and motivations.

## Methodological Characteristics
- **[[concepts/group-dynamics|Group Dynamics]]**: Leverages [[concepts/social-interaction|social interaction]] to stimulate discussion and reveal nuances that individual interviews might miss.
- **Moderation**: Requires a trained Moderator to guide the conversation without leading participants.
- **Data Type**: Produces rich, unstructured qualitative data requiring [[concepts/thematic-analysis]] or Content Analysis.
