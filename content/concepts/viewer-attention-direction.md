# Viewer attention direction

The strategic manipulation of a viewer's gaze within a visual field to establish a hierarchical order of importance and emphasize a specific Focal Point.

## Mechanisms of Visual Guidance
- **[[concepts/contrast|Contrast]]:** Utilizing differences in luminance, [[concepts/saturation-adjustment|saturation]], or [[concepts/texture|texture]] to create visual weight.
- **Leading Lines:** Using geometric or natural paths to facilitate eye [[concepts/exercise|movement]] through the frame.
- **Color Dynamics:** Leveraging [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and saturation to [[concepts/power|influence]] visual priority.
- **Lighting:** Implementing [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] and [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] to define depth and importance.

## Practical Implementation in Post-Processing
Techniques for controlling directionality via digital manipulation (Ref: 2026 04 22 [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] [[concepts/photo-enhancement|Photo Enhancement]] Top 5 Instant Improvement Techniques):
- Correcting [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] to balance or redirect visual equilibrium.
- Utilizing targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to direct visual interest.
