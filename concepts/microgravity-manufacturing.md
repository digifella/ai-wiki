# Microgravity Manufacturing

**Microgravity manufacturing** refers to the production of materials, devices, and biological products in the low-[[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] environment of space. The absence of sedimentation, buoyancy-driven convection, and [[concepts/deep-sea-pressure|hydrostatic pressure]] allows for the creation of structures and substances with properties unattainable on [[entities/earth|Earth]].

## Key Advantages
- **Material Purity**: Elimination of sedimentation allows for uniform mixing of materials with different densities, crucial for semiconductor crystals and alloys.
- **[[concepts/payload-structure|Structural Integrity]]**: Reduced gravitational [[concepts/stress|stress]] enables the growth of larger, defect-free protein crystals for pharmaceutical development.
- **Novel Geometries**: Ability to fabricate complex, hollow structures (e.g., fiber [[concepts/optics|optics]], ceramics) without sagging or deformation.

## Current Developments & Context
Recent advancements in launch infrastructure and orbital logistics are accelerating the viability of space-based industrial operations.

- **Launch Infrastructure**: The successful landing of reusable rockets by [[entities/china|China]] highlights the global trend toward reducing launch costs, a critical factor for the [[concepts/commercial-viability|economic feasibility]] of Microgravity Manufacturing. - **Orbital Platforms**: Emerging commercial space stations and dedicated manufacturing modules are being designed to support continuous production cycles.
- **[[concepts/deep-space-exploration|Deep Space]] Logistics**: [[concepts/software-updates|Updates]] on asteroid flybys and deep space activities indicate a broader shift in spaceflight capabilities, potentially extending manufacturing beyond [[concepts/low-earth-orbit-leo|Low Earth Orbit (LEO)]] in the future.

## Applications
- **Pharmaceuticals**: Protein crystal growth for drug design.
- **Optics**: Production of [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] fiber optics and lenses.
- **Metallurgy**: Creation of novel alloys and [[concepts/metal|metal]] foams.
- **Biotechnology**: Tissue [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] and organoid development.
