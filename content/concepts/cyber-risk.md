# Cyber Risk

Cyber risk in organizational contexts encompasses threats to information security, system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] arising from digital attack vectors and vulnerabilities. In contemporary business environments, these risks are increasingly compounded by the rapid [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] solutions that operate outside formal [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks.

## Ungoverned AI and Shadow AI

Organizations frequently implement [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] and systems without comprehensive security protocols, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] oversight, or integration with existing risk management structures. These [[concepts/co-pilot-use|ungoverned AI solutions]]—sometimes termed Shadow AI—create vulnerabilities through unvetted data handling, unauthorized API integrations, and systems operating outside organizational visibility. The decentralized [[entities/nature|nature]] of such deployments makes threat detection and [[concepts/incident-response|incident response]] substantially more difficult.

## Agentic Frameworks

[[concepts/action-oriented-ai|Agentic AI]] frameworks, which operate with increased autonomy and decision-making authority, introduce additional cyber risk dimensions. These systems may execute actions across organizational networks with minimal human oversight, potentially exposing sensitive systems to compromise if the framework itself is targeted or manipulated. The complexity of securing autonomous [[concepts/agents|agents]] across distributed infrastructure presents novel attack surfaces not adequately addressed by traditional [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] approaches.

## Governance Integration

Effective cyber risk management requires bringing AI solutions within formal governance structures, including security assessments, access controls, and monitoring frameworks. Organizations lacking clear [[concepts/policies|policies]] for AI deployment face compounded [[concepts/exposure|exposure]]: direct cybersecurity threats alongside governance and compliance violations. Addressing cyber risk in AI contexts requires alignment between security infrastructure teams, governance functions, and technical stakeholders.
