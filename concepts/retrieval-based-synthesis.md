# Retrieval-based synthesis

A computational process where an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] retrieves information from [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources and synthesizes it into structured, coherent, and human-readable outputs (e.g., summaries, reports, or essays). Unlike simple [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], synthesis requires the integration of disparate data points into a unified [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] or logical framework.

## Key Implementations & Advancements

- **[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s [[entities/storm|STORM]] AI**: An advanced [[concepts/event-driven-system|agent-based system]] designed for verifiable research and knowledge curation.
	- **Mechanism**: Utilizes "Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and [[concepts/multi-perspective-question-asking|Multi-perspective Question Asking]]."
	- **Process**: Employs [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to perform [[concepts/deep-research-function|deep research]] by generating multi-perspective questions that [[concepts/motivation|drive]] the retrieval process.
	- **Capability**: Automates complex, multi-step research tasks, capable of producing [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]], curated knowledge in minutes.
