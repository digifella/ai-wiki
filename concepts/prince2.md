# Prince2

Prince2 (Projects in Controlled Environments) is a structured project management methodology developed by the UK government's Office of Government Commerce in 1989. It provides a comprehensive framework for organizing and controlling projects from initiation through closure, and is widely adopted across the United Kingdom and Europe. The methodology is based on established best practices in project delivery and has become a standard approach in both public and private sector organizations.

## Core Framework

Prince2 operates on a stage-based approach to project delivery, dividing work into manageable phases with clear decision points between stages. The methodology emphasizes defined roles and responsibilities, with distinct positions such as project manager, project board, and project assurance. It incorporates continuous monitoring and control mechanisms to track progress against planned outcomes and manage deviations through formal change control processes.

## Application and Adoption

The methodology is applicable to projects of varying scale and complexity across different industries, though it is particularly prevalent in government, finance, and large-scale organizational initiatives. Prince2 certification has become a recognized qualification for project professionals, with training and accreditation managed by AXELOS (a joint venture between the UK government and Capita). Organizations adopting Prince2 typically report improved project governance, risk management, and stakeholder communication, though implementation requires investment in training and process adjustment.
