## Google NotebookLM: Customizing Design for Professional Presentations via
[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]
**Clip title:** Google's [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Just DESTROYED [[entities/canva|Canva]] With 1 Update
(It's FREE)
**Author / channel:** [[entities/vaibhav-sisinty|Vaibhav Sisinty]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hqquu7H7X0w

### Summary
This video provides an in-depth guide on transforming Google's [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]
from a basic content generator into a powerful, customized presentation and
research tool. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], who has extensive experience in AI education
and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], acknowledges [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]'s strength in generating
accurate, [[concepts/source-grounded-content|source-grounded content]] (from uploaded [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], videos, documents,
or deep web research) but highlights its major drawback: generic and
unappealing default designs. The core of the video demonstrates several
workflows to overcome these [[concepts/design|design]] limitations and leverage new features
for enhanced productivity and professionalism.

The first key [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] addresses [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s generic design output. The
speaker illustrates how default [[concepts/infographics|infographics]] or [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]], while
accurate, lack customizability in terms of color, layout, and [[concepts/style|style]], making
them appear "factory-made." The innovative [[concepts/solution|solution]] involves a "[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]
Reverse Engineering Trick." Users find a desired aesthetic (e.g., a
specific [[concepts/infographic|infographic]] style) on Google [[concepts/images|Images]], upload it to [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]]
(the free version works), and prompt [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] to describe its visual features
in detail. This detailed description, which includes elements like
illustration style, color palette, [[concepts/typography|typography]], and composition, is then
copied and pasted into NotebookLM's custom instruction field before
generating the desired asset. This technique allows users to produce
visually intentional and branded [[concepts/infographics|infographics]] and presentations.

Beyond aesthetic [[concepts/customization|customization]], the video explores practical enhancements
and workflow optimizations within NotebookLM. For presentations, the
speaker demonstrates how the Gemini-generated design prompt can be further
refined by specifying the target audience, which influences not just the
visual style but also the content's [[concepts/tone|tone]] and depth (e.g., making
explanations beginner-friendly). A crucial workaround for the persistent
NotebookLM watermark on generated slides is also revealed: converting the
PDF output to an editable PowerPoint file using a free online converter,
then opening it in [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]] to easily delete the watermark,
effectively providing a clean, professional deck without subscriptions.

Finally, the video showcases newly introduced features that significantly
boost NotebookLM's utility as a comprehensive AI workspace. The "[[entities/deep-research|Deep Research]]" function automates the exhaustive process of gathering relevant
sources (websites, papers, forums) for any given topic, compiling them into
an organized [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] in minutes—a task that would typically take hours.
The new "[Slide Revision](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slide_Revision)" feature allows users to modify individual slides
within a generated deck by providing specific prompts, eliminating the need
to regenerate the entire presentation. Lastly, "[[concepts/data-tables|Data Tables]]" enable users
to extract [[concepts/structured-data|structured data]] from various sources into organized, sortable
spreadsheets, which can be directly exported to Google Sheets. These
features collectively empower users to conduct thorough research and create
polished, accurate, and customized outputs, making NotebookLM a powerful
tool for diverse professional and educational needs.
