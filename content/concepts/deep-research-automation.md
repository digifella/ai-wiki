# Deep Research Automation

Deep Research Automation refers to the process of converting outputs from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]'s Deep Research feature into formatted, professional websites without requiring [[concepts/coding|coding]] knowledge. NotebookLM's Deep Research tool generates comprehensive research documents and structured information based on source materials. Deep Research Automation streamlines the next step by taking these outputs and transforming them into web-ready formats suitable for publication or presentation.

## Workflow and Approach

The [[concepts/automation|automation]] process typically involves exporting Deep [[concepts/flow-management|Research findings]] and feeding them into no-code website builders or conversion tools. These tools parse the structured research output—including sections, citations, and hierarchical information—and organize them into web layouts. The result is a functional website that maintains the research content's [[concepts/organization|organization]] and readability without manual HTML or CSS work.

## Use Cases

Common [[concepts/software|applications]] include creating research portfolios, publishing analysis reports, building knowledge bases, and sharing findings with wider audiences. The approach is particularly useful for researchers, analysts, and content creators who need to move from research compilation to public presentation quickly, without acquiring [[concepts/website-building|web development]] [[concepts/skills|skills]] or hiring technical resources.
