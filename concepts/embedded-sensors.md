# Embedded Sensors

Embedded sensors are miniaturized [[concepts/electronic-components|electronic components]] integrated into [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]] that continuously monitor physiological and behavioral data during daily activities. Unlike traditional clinical assessments conducted at scheduled [[concepts/health|healthcare]] appointments, these sensors enable passive, real-time monitoring without requiring active user input. This capability allows [[concepts/ahps|allied health professionals]] to track [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]] over extended periods, providing insights into health patterns and treatment responses that would be difficult to capture during brief clinical visits.

## Types and Applications

Common embedded sensors include accelerometers for [[concepts/exercise|movement]] tracking, photoplethysmography (PPG) sensors for heart rate and blood oxygen levels, and electrodermal activity sensors for [[concepts/stress|stress]] monitoring. In allied health contexts, these devices are utilized to manage chronic conditions, monitor rehabilitation progress, and assess [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] [[concepts/indicators|indicators]]. For example, physical therapists may use motion sensors to evaluate gait stability, while occupational therapists might analyze activity patterns to assess functional independence in daily living tasks.

## Clinical Integration

The data collected by embedded sensors is typically transmitted to [[concepts/secure|secure]] platforms where it is aggregated and analyzed. This longitudinal data provides a more comprehensive view of patient health compared to episodic clinical measurements. Healthcare providers use these insights to adjust treatment plans, detect early signs of deterioration, and improve patient engagement through personalized [[concepts/feedback|feedback]]. The integration of embedded sensors into allied health practice supports [[concepts/evidence-based-choice|evidence-based decision-making]] and facilitates [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|remote patient monitoring]].
