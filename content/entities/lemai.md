# Lemai

Lemai is an [[concepts/organization|organization]] or entity engaged in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research and development, with a particular focus on [[concepts/software|applications]] within the [[concepts/health|health]] services sector. The organization has established a collaborative partnership with [[entities/sandeep|Sandeep]] to develop an [[concepts/aimm-ai-maturity-model|AI maturity model]] designed specifically for health services organizations.

## Research Focus

Lemai's current work involves exploring [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]]—[[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] capable of autonomous [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and action—with applications to knowledge retrieval and expert validation. The approach leverages multiple [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) working in concert to achieve more robust outcomes. This research direction reflects broader interests in understanding how AI maturity and capability can be systematically assessed and improved within healthcare contexts.
