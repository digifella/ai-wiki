# Dynamic Agent-Environment

A dynamic system where agents and environments co-evolve through continuous interaction, adaptation, and [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]]. This co-evolution can give rise to [[concepts/swarm-intelligence|collective intelligence]] and learning dynamics at the system's edge.

## Key Characteristics

- **Co-evolution:** Agents and environments influence each other's development.
- **[[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]:** Both agents and environments adjust to changing conditions.
- **[[concepts/feedback|Feedback]] Loops:** Information flows bidirectionally, enabling [[concepts/learning|learning]] and optimization.
- **Collective Emergence:** Groups of agents can exhibit collective intelligence that exceeds individual capabilities, as highlighted in [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Anita-Woolley-on-Collective-Intelligence-and-Learning-on|Anita Woolley on Collective Intelligence and Learning on the Edge]].
- **[[concepts/edge-learning|Edge Learning]]:** Adaptive systems often demonstrate highest [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and adaptation rates at the boundaries or "edges" of stability.
