# Koen

Koen is an automation system designed to streamline the creation and distribution of AI-generated video content across multiple social media platforms. The system combines Claude Code, an AI-assisted coding tool, with Blotato, a multi-platform distribution service, to reduce manual intervention in content production and posting workflows.

## How It Works

The process leverages Claude Code to automate the generation and formatting of video content according to platform-specific requirements. Once content is generated, Blotato handles the distribution phase by simultaneously posting to multiple social media channels. This two-stage approach eliminates repetitive manual tasks involved in creating variations of content for different platforms and managing individual upload processes.

## Purpose

Koen is intended to increase efficiency in video content workflows by automating both creation logic and distribution management. Rather than manually producing separate versions of content for each platform or uploading to channels individually, the system handles these steps programmatically, allowing creators to focus on higher-level decisions about content strategy and messaging.
