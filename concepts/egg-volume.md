# Egg Volume

Egg volume refers to the physical space occupied by an egg, whether raw or cooked. Understanding egg volume is crucial in culinary applications as it affects [[concepts/cooking-time|cooking time]] and outcomes such as [[concepts/texture|texture]] and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].

## Key Points

- **Raw vs Cooked**: The volume of eggs increases when they are cooked due to [[concepts/denaturation|protein denaturation]].
- **Egg Size Variability**: Large, [[entities/medium|medium]], and small eggs have different volumes, affecting recipes' ingredient ratios.
- **Impact on Recipes**: Egg volume directly influences the [[concepts/success|success]] of baked goods and custards.
