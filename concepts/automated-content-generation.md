# Automated content generation

The use of [[concepts/generative-ai]] to autonomously or semi-autonomously produce various media formats by synthesizing information from diverse inputs.

## Core Principles
- [[concepts/notebooklm|Source-grounded AI]]: Constraining models to specific, user-provided [[concepts/training-data|datasets]] to minimize hallucinations.
- [[concepts/content-creation]]: The automated transformation of multi-modal inputs into structured, actionable outputs.

## Specialized Tooling: notebooklm
A productivity-focused tool that functions as a comprehensive content system.
- **[[concepts/pointing-mechanisms|Input Modalities]]**: Processes PDF, [[entities/google-docs]], [[concepts/google-slides]], [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]], and URL.
- **Generation Capabilities**:
    - [[entities/google-slides|Slides]]
    - [[concepts/infographic]]
    - Videos
    - [[concepts/data-tables]]
