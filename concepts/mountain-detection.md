# Mountain Detection

Mountain Detection is a selective editing technique used in landscape photography that employs landscape masks within Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw to isolate and adjust mountain regions with precision. By utilizing masking tools to target specific geographic features, photographers can apply localized adjustments—such as contrast, saturation, or exposure—to mountains independently from other landscape elements. This approach allows for more nuanced post-processing workflows where different portions of an image receive tailored enhancements based on their content.

## Technique and Application

The technique leverages the masking capabilities built into Adobe's post-processing software to automatically or manually select mountainous terrain. Rather than applying adjustments across an entire image, Mountain Detection enables photographers to refine specific features like peak definition, cloud contrast, or color grading in elevated regions. This is particularly useful in landscape photography where mountains often require different tonal or color treatment than valleys, skies, or foreground elements to achieve balanced, visually compelling results.

## Learning Resources

The technique gained broader recognition through tutorials by photographer Colin Smith, who demonstrated practical workflows for implementing landscape masks effectively. These resources typically show step-by-step processes for creating, refining, and applying masks to mountain regions, making the approach accessible to photographers working at various skill levels.
