## Hermes Agent: Autonomous AI Employee Setup, Features, and Use Cases
**Clip title:** [[concepts/autonomous-workflow-automation|Hermes Agent]] is the greatest AI tool ever made. Here's how to set it up
**Author / channel:** Alex Finn
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RoBD7Lc-0MI

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive guide to Hermes Agent, positioning it as a powerful, self-improving AI tool that can act as a 24/7 employee. The presenter highlights Hermes Agent's ability to learn everything about a user and their work, operate autonomously, and improve its [[concepts/skills|skills]] over time. It is accessible from anywhere and can integrate with [[concepts/communication|messaging]] platforms like [[entities/telegram|Telegram]], which is recommended for its AI-agent-friendly features. A key distinction made is its superiority over [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] due to being lighter-weight, more performant, offering more reliable updates, and having better community engagement.

The video details the [[concepts/installation|installation]] process, which involves a simple curl command and a setup wizard for quick configuration. Users can choose from various [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], including Anthropic/Claude (more expensive), OpenAI/ChatGPT (moderate), or cheaper built-in Nous portal models. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] advises that even the moderate ChatGPT option now offers decent functionality. After installation, the crucial first step is to introduce yourself, your business, goals, and ambitions to Hermes Agent so it can learn and proactively assist you.

Further practical features include the Hermes Agent dashboard, which allows for easy model swapping, managing scheduled tasks (cron jobs), and browsing/enabling skills and [[concepts/plugins|plugins]]. A standout feature is the [[concepts/kanban|Kanban board]], which automatically splits user-defined tasks into sub-tasks and assigns them to different AI [[concepts/sub-agents|sub-agents]] for efficient multitasking. The video showcases several [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], such as turning [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] videos into daily [[concepts/learning|learning]] sessions with quizzes, acting as a computer administrator for cross-device file management using Tailscale, and providing [[concepts/session|session]] [[concepts/recall|recall]] to retrieve past conversations or ideas.

Regarding its unique [[concepts/memory|memory]] and skills, Hermes Agent stores all information in [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] files on your local computer, allowing for transparency and auditability. The core strength lies in its self-improving nature; every task it performs helps it learn the best way to execute and updates its skills, making it more customized and effective over time. Finally, the presenter addresses [[concepts/security-concersns|security concerns]], arguing they are largely overrated. He emphasizes that the agent only performs actions it is explicitly told to do, advising users to [[concepts/exercise|exercise]] [[concepts/decision-making|good judgment]] and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] when issuing [[concepts/commands|commands]], rather than over-complicating setup with [[concepts/isolated-environments|isolated environments]] or Virtual Private Servers.
