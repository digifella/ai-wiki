# Grok

[[entities/grok-bot|Grok]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by xAI, an [[concepts/ai-research|artificial intelligence research]] company founded by [[entities/elon-musk|Elon Musk]]. The model was released in late 2024, entering a competitive landscape already dominated by established leaders such as [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]’s [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]], [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]’s [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/google-search|Google]]’s [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]. Its initial distribution strategy leveraged Musk’s social media platform, X, by making the service available exclusively to Premium subscribers.

As of mid-2025, Grok occupies a secondary position in the large language model market. While the model has demonstrated competitive capabilities in certain benchmarks, it trails behind the primary industry leaders in overall market share and widespread [[concepts/adoption|adoption]]. The integration with X provides a unique distribution channel, but the model continues to compete for prominence against more mature ecosystems developed by OpenAI and Anthropic.
