# Gretchen Rubin

Gretchen Rubin is an author and [[entities/speaker|speaker]] known for her work on [[concepts/personal-development|personal development]] and the [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of [[concepts/happiness|happiness]] through small, manageable changes. She is best known for her book "The [[concepts/happiness|Happiness Project]]," where she outlines steps to improve one's life by integrating positive [[concepts/habits|habits]] into [[concepts/daily-routines|daily routines]].

### Summary
Gretchen Rubin shares her insights on the [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of [[concepts/happiness|happiness]], emphasizing that it's not about making big changes but cultivating [[concepts/habits|daily habits]]. Her journey started with a moment of reflection where she realized she wasn't actively working towards being happier despite having the potential for contentment.

- Gretchen [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] how focusing on small, manageable habits can lead to significant improvements in one’s life.
- She discusses the importance of identifying personal values and aligning habits with them to create lasting happiness.
