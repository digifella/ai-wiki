# NSW Department of Justice

The NSW Department of Justice operates as a clearing house for research and information in the justice and social policy sectors. The department maintains an active research space dedicated to contemporary issues affecting NSW communities, with a particular focus on [[concepts/vulnerable-populations|vulnerable populations]] and systemic challenges.

## Research Focus Areas

The department's research activities emphasize [[concepts/children-and-family-violence|children and family violence]] as key priority areas. This work involves synthesizing current [[concepts/flow-management|research findings]] and making evidence-based information accessible to policymakers, service providers, and stakeholders working in these domains. The department engages with various methodological approaches to gather insights and inform policy development, including collaborative research methods such as [[concepts/group-model-building|group model building]] for focus group facilitation.
