# AI Cognition

**AI [[concepts/cognition|Cognition]]** refers to the [[concepts/internal-working-mechanisms|computational processes]] by which [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems perceive, interpret, [[concepts/purpose|reason]], and act within complex environments. Unlike static [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], cognitive [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] exhibit [[concepts/acting|adaptive behavior]], requiring robust frameworks for [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration]] and ethical [[concepts/accountability|accountability]].

## Core Dimensions

- **Perception & Interpretation**: How AI models process sensory data (visual, auditory, textual) to construct situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
- **[[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] & [[concepts/decision-making|Decision-Making]]**: The logical structures used to derive actions from perceived states, including probabilistic [[concepts/inference|inference]] and causal modeling.
- **Agency & Responsibility**: The distribution of accountability between human operators and [[concepts/system-autonomy|autonomous systems]], particularly in high-stakes domains like Aviation Safety.

## Case Studies & Applications

### Aviation Safety and Human Responsibility
Recent incidents highlight the critical intersection of AI cognition and human oversight. The [[lab-notes/2026-07-01-Air-India-Crash-Human-Responsibility-and-Evolving-AI-Cog|Air India Crash: Human Responsibility and Evolving AI Cognition in Aviation]] serves as a pivotal case study for understanding failure modes in hybrid human-AI systems.

Key insights from this event include:
- **The "[[concepts/human-responsibility|Responsibility Gap]]"**: [[concepts/ambiguity|Ambiguity]] in decision chains when AI systems provide [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]] that humans override or fail to interpret correctly.
- **[[concepts/cognitive-load|Cognitive Load]]**: The strain on human operators when AI interfaces present complex data without clear semantic grounding.
- **Systemic Evolution**: The necessity for AI cognition to evolve from mere automation to collaborative partnership, ensuring that machine outputs align with human ethical and [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]].
