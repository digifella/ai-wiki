# Velocity

**Velocity** is a vector quantity that denotes the rate of change of an object's position with respect to a frame of reference, and is the rate of change of its position with respect to time.

## Core Concepts
- **Definition**: $\vec{v} = \frac{d\vec{x}}{dt}$
- **Distinction**: Unlike *speed* (scalar), velocity includes direction.
- **Context**: Fundamental to kinematics, dynamics, and fluid-mechanics.

## Navier-Stokes Equations
The Navier-[[entities/stokes|Stokes]] equations describe the motion of viscous Newtonian-fluid. They are central to understanding how velocity fields evolve in space and time.

- **Millennium Prize Problem**: The Clay Mathematics Institute designated the "[[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|Existence and Smoothness]] of the Navier-Stokes Equation" as one of the seven [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems]].
- **Key Challenge**: Determining whether smooth solutions always exist for all initial conditions, or if [[concepts/fluid-flow]] can experience "[[concepts/blow-up|blow-up]]" (singularities) where velocity becomes infinite.
