# Multidisciplinary Teams

Multidisciplinary teams (MDTs) are collaborative groups of [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] professionals from different specialties working together to provide comprehensive care for patients. This model integrates [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] from various fields to optimize [[concepts/diagnostic-accuracy|diagnostic accuracy]], treatment planning, and [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]].

## Key Characteristics

* **[[concepts/interdisciplinary-collaboration|Interdisciplinary Collaboration]]**: Integration of distinct professional perspectives to reduce silos in care.
* **Shared [[concepts/decision-making|Decision-Making]]**: Collective [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] for patient plans rather than unilateral authority.
* **Holistic Care**: Addressing medical, psychological, and social needs simultaneously.

## Technological Enablers

Effective [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] within MDTs relies heavily on shared [[concepts/knowledge-management|information systems]]. The implementation of [[concepts/electronic-health-records]] and [[concepts/emr|Electronic Medical Records]] is critical for maintaining [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of care across disciplines.

* **Information [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Digital records ensure that all team members have simultaneous access to updated [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]], reducing errors and redundancy.
* **Coordination Efficiency**: As highlighted in [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-The-Biggest-Benefit-of-EMRs-EHRs-for-Allied-Health|The Biggest Benefit of EMRs EHRs for Allied Health]], the primary advantage for [[concepts/ahps|allied health professionals]] is enhanced safety and coordination through immediate access to comprehensive [[concepts/patient-information|patient information]].
* **Workflow Integration**: EMRs facilitate seamless handoffs between specialists, nurses, and [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] [[entities/employees|staff]], ensuring that interventions are synchronized.

## Benefits

* Improved patient safety through reduced medication errors and diagnostic delays.
* Enhanced efficiency in resource allocation and treatment timelines.
* Higher patient satisfaction due to cohesive and consistent care plans.
