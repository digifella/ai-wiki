# Process Mapping

Process mapping is a foundational technique for identifying, documenting, and visualizing the workflows and operational procedures within an organization. It involves creating detailed representations of how tasks, decisions, and data flow through business processes, making implicit procedures explicit and measurable. This documentation serves as the baseline for understanding current operations before implementing changes or improvements.

## Purpose and Application

The primary purpose of process mapping is to create a clear, shared understanding of how work actually gets done. By rendering workflows visible and structured, organizations can identify inefficiencies, redundancies, and bottlenecks that may not be apparent through casual observation. Process maps also serve as communication tools, enabling stakeholders across departments to align on procedures and expectations.

## Role in AI Integration

Process mapping has become recognized as an essential first step when integrating artificial intelligence into business operations. A well-documented process provides the clarity needed to identify where AI can add value—whether through automation, decision support, or optimization. Without understanding current workflows in detail, organizations struggle to determine appropriate AI applications or to measure the impact of AI interventions against baseline performance.

## Common Approaches

Process maps can take various forms depending on organizational needs and complexity, ranging from simple flowcharts to detailed process documentation that includes decision trees, data requirements, and stakeholder roles. The level of detail captured should align with the intended use case, whether the goal is process improvement, compliance documentation, or preparation for automation and AI deployment.
