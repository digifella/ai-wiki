# Issuance Of Visas

The Republic of Turkey operates an electronic visa (e-visa) system designed to allow eligible foreign nationals to obtain travel authorization for tourism, business, or short-term visits. This digital document functions as a valid entry permit, effectively replacing the traditional requirement for applicants to visit a Turkish embassy or consulate in person. The implementation of this system aims to streamline the application process and significantly reduce processing times for qualifying individuals.

Eligibility for the e-visa is primarily determined by the applicant's nationality, with specific countries granted access to the online portal while others must apply through diplomatic missions. Applicants must possess a passport valid for at least six months beyond their entry date and a valid email address to receive the approved visa. The system also requires payment via credit or debit card, and the resulting e-visa is electronically linked to the passport number provided during the application.

Once issued, the e-visa is typically valid for multiple entries within a specified period, allowing stays of up to 90 days within any 180-day period for most eligible nationalities. Travelers are required to carry a printed copy or digital version of the e-visa alongside their passport when entering Turkey. Immigration officials verify the document against the central database upon arrival, and discrepancies or invalid statuses can result in denied entry.
