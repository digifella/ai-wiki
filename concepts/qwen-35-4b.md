# Qwen 3.5-4B

**[[entities/qwen-35-4b|Qwen 3.5-4B]]** is a compact 4-billion-parameter [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] base, notable for its efficiency on single-GPU setups. It serves as the foundational architecture for [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] like [[concepts/web-tools|Microsoft FrogNano]] 4B: Budget AI Debugs Nusantara Ferry [[concepts/occupancy-bug|Occupancy Bug]].
