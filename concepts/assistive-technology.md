# Healthcare Technology

[[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] Technology, often overlapping with [[concepts/assistive-technology]], refers to any item, piece of equipment, or product system used to increase, maintain, or improve the functional capabilities of people with disabilities and optimize health outcomes. In the context of aging and [[concepts/health|healthcare]], it includes both low-tech aids (e.g., canes, hearing aids) and high-tech solutions (e.g., robotic companions, smart home interfaces).

## Key Applications

- **Mobility Aids**: Devices supporting physical [[concepts/exercise|movement]] for individuals with limited mobility.
- **Communication Tools**: [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|Speech-to-text]] software, eye-tracking devices, and [[concepts/braille|braille]] displays.
- **Cognitive Support**: Reminder systems and [[concepts/memory|memory]] aids for neurodivergent or cognitively declining users.
- **Social [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]]**: Emerging use of robots for [[concepts/companionship|companionship]] and care assistance.

## Digital Health Integration

Recent frameworks emphasize the evolution of [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] tools in care delivery:

- **Progress in [[concepts/promise-of-digital-health|Digital Medicine]]**: Analysis of the trajectory from early digital tools to current AI-integrated health systems highlights significant advancements in [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]] efficiency and data utilization [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future|The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future]].
- **National Health Progress**: Key figures from institutions like the National Academy of Medicine and [[entities/epic-systems|Epic Systems]] have documented the role of digital tools in shaping national health strategies and improving access to care.
- **[[concepts/future-predictions|Future Trends]]**: Continued integration of high-tech [[concepts/assistive-devices|assistive devices]] with broader digital health ecosystems aims to bridge gaps in [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and [[concepts/personalized-medicine|personalized medicine]].
