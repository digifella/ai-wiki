# Process Mapping

[[concepts/problem-identification|Process mapping]] is a foundational technique for identifying, documenting, and visualizing the workflows and operational procedures within an [[concepts/organization|organization]]. It involves creating detailed representations of how tasks, decisions, and data flow through business processes, making implicit procedures explicit and measurable. This documentation serves as the baseline for understanding current operations before any changes or improvements are implemented.

## Role in AI Integration

Process mapping has become recognized as an essential preliminary step when organizations plan to integrate [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] into their operations. By establishing clear visibility into existing workflows, businesses can identify which processes are suitable for [[concepts/automation|automation]], where [[concepts/data-conceptsintegrityintegrity|data quality]] issues exist, and how AI systems should be positioned within larger operational contexts. This mapping prevents the common pitfall of deploying [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] without understanding the processes they are meant to support or improve.

## Practical Application

Effective process mapping typically involves stakeholder interviews, observation of actual workflows, and documentation of both standard procedures and exception handling. The resulting maps serve multiple purposes: they reveal inefficiencies in current operations, provide reference points for measuring improvement after [[concepts/adoption|AI implementation]], and help teams understand how AI tools like autonomous [[concepts/agents|agents]] or code generation systems will integrate with their existing work. This clarity is particularly important when tools access or manipulate business-critical data and workflows.
