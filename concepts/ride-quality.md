# Ride quality

The subjective perception of smoothness, stability, and comfort experienced by passengers during vehicle transit.

## Key Determinants
- **NVH (Noise, Vibration, Harshness)**: The mitigation of acoustic and tactile disturbances within the cabin.
- **Suspension Tuning**: The [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] of [[concepts/energy-dissipation|damping]] and spring rates to manage road surface irregularities.
- **Chassis Rigidity**: Structural stiffness used to minimize uncontrolled torsional flex.
- **Unsprung Mass**: The influence of Tire and wheel assembly weight on reactive forces and road-holding.
