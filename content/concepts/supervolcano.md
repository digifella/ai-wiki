# Supervolcano

A supervolcano is a volcano capable of producing an eruption of magnitude eight on the [[concepts/volcanic-explosivity-index|Volcanic Explosivity Index]] (VEI), which indicates an enormous eruptive event. These eruptions release more than 1,000 km³ of magma and are characterized by massive pyroclastic flows that can cover large areas. The term "supervolcano" does not have a precise scientific definition but is used to describe volcanoes with the potential for extraordinarily devastating effects.

- **[[concepts/cerberean-supervolcano|Cerberean Supervolcano]]**:
  - Located just northeast of [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]], Australia.
  - One of Australia's largest known eruptions from the [[concepts/devonian-period|Devonian era]].
  - Characterized by a massive collapsed caldera spanning approximately 27 kilometers.
