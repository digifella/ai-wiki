# Structured Decision Models

**[[concepts/structured-decision|Structured Decision]] Models** are specialized architectures designed to enhance the efficiency, [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and determinism of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] within iterative loops. Unlike traditional agent architectures that may suffer from unstructured [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] or high latency, these models provide a formalized framework for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].

## Core Concepts

*   **Efficiency & Reliability:** Optimizes agent performance by reducing computational overhead and improving decision accuracy in complex environments.
*   **Agent Loops:** Specifically integrated into the iterative "agent loops" where agents perceive, reason, and act.
*   **[[concepts/custom-models|Specialized Models]]:** Examples include [[entities/jev]] and [[entities/openjev]], which serve as dedicated decision engines rather than [[concepts/general-purpose-llms|general-purpose LLMs]].
