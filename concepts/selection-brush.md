# Selection Brush

The Selection Brush is a feature introduced in the July 2025 update of Adobe Photoshop and Photoshop Beta. It allows users to create selections by painting directly onto the image canvas, providing an alternative to traditional geometric selection tools like the Marquee and Lasso tools. This functionality is designed to accommodate varying user preferences and working styles within the image editing workflow.

The tool operates by enabling manual painting of selection areas, which can be more intuitive for complex or organic shapes compared to defining boundaries through precise geometric constraints. By offering a painting-based approach, it aims to enhance workflow efficiency and productivity for users who prefer direct manipulation over precise path definition.
