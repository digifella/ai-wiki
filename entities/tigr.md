# Tigr

Tigr is a gene-editing technology developed as a successor to CRISPR-Cas9. The system builds upon foundational CRISPR technology to provide improved precision and expanded capabilities for genetic modification. Like CRISPR, Tigr represents an advancement in molecular biology and genetic engineering techniques.

## Development

Dr. Feng Zhang at the Broad Institute has been instrumental in developing Tigr technology. The work was featured in a SciShow episode that explored the system's mechanisms and potential applications in the broader context of gene-editing advances.
