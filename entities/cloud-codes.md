### Context Language Models (CLMs)
Explores novel architectures where LLMs self-manage [[concepts/conversational-context|conversational context]] and efficiency, moving away from external harnesses.

- **Core Concept:** LLMs treat the entire conversation as an editable file, gaining native control over context [[concepts/storing|retention]] and [[concepts/summarization|summarization]].
- **Benefit:** Addresses efficiency flaws in traditional LLMs by allowing the model to manage its own [[concepts/context-length|context window]] dynamically.
