[[entities/minimax|MiniMax]] M2.7 is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] under a modified [[entities/mit|MIT]] [[concepts/license|license]], boasting 229 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and utilizing a Mixture-of-Experts (MoE) [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] for efficient parameter sharing across tasks.

### Technical Overview
- **[[concepts/parameters|Parameters]]**: 229 billion
- **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]**: [[concepts/mixture-of-experts-architecture|MoE Architecture]] Mixture-of-Experts
- **License**: Modified [[concepts/mit-license|MIT License]]

### Unique Features
- Self-evolutionary development process, allowing the model to improve continuously through iterative updates.
- Scalable [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] options due to its efficient architecture and [[concepts/licensing|licensing]].
