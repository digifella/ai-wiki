# Mark Zuckerberg

**Mark Zuckerberg** is the co-founder, chairman, and CEO of [[entities/meta|Meta]] Platforms. He has positioned himself as a central figure in the democratization of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]], advocating for open-access models and widespread compute availability.

## AI Philosophy & Strategy

Zuckerberg's recent public discourse emphasizes a shift from exclusive AI development to broad empowerment, contrasting with competitors who maintain tighter control over their models.

- **Core Vision:** Outlined in his essay "The Future is for Everyone," the strategy focuses on making advanced AI accessible to everyone, not just a few large corporations.
- **Compute Access:** Advocates for lowering the economic barriers to entry for AI development, challenging the "economic reality" of compute scarcity that currently favors incumbents.
- **Public Critique:** Recently called out [[entities/dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]] and Anthropic for their approach to [[concepts/model-safety|AI safety]] and access, arguing that their stance hinders the broader empowerment goal.
- **Open vs. Closed:** Pushes for a more open ecosystem to prevent a monopoly on AI capabilities by a small group of entities.
