# General Purpose AI Agent

A General Purpose AI Agent is a feature within ChatGPT designed to automate complex, multi-step tasks by combining browser control capabilities with deep research functions. Unlike traditional chatbots that respond to individual queries, these agents execute sequences of actions—such as navigating websites, gathering information, and analyzing data—to complete comprehensive tasks with minimal human intervention.

## Capabilities

General Purpose AI Agents operate by breaking down larger objectives into actionable steps. They can navigate web interfaces, extract information from multiple sources, perform comparisons, and synthesize findings into structured outputs. The deep research capability enables the agent to independently investigate topics and gather relevant data, while browser control allows interaction with web-based tools and applications.

## Use Cases

These agents are designed for tasks that would typically require sustained human attention across multiple steps or platforms. Common applications include competitive analysis, market research, data collection, content generation informed by current information, and administrative workflows that span multiple websites or applications.
