# CISA

**Full Name:** [[concepts/cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]] and Infrastructure [[concepts/security|Security]] Agency
**Jurisdiction:** [[entities/united-states|United States]] Department of Homeland Security
**Focus:** Protecting critical infrastructure, [[concepts/internet-security|cybersecurity]] [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and [[concepts/incident-response|incident response]].

## Overview
CISA serves as the nation’s lead agency for cybersecurity and infrastructure [[concepts/secure|protection]]. It coordinates federal efforts to safeguard networks, systems, and critical infrastructure from cyber threats, including state-sponsored attacks and emerging risks in [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] and [[concepts/large-language-models]].

## Strategic Initiatives & Partnerships
CISA collaborates with private sector [[concepts/nodes|entities]], such as [[entities/ibm]], to advance defensive capabilities and [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] discovery methodologies. Recent engagements focus on integrating AI-driven security tools and establishing standardized frameworks for detecting vulnerabilities in [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]].
