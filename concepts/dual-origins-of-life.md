# Dual Origins of Life

The hypothesis that life on [[entities/earth|Earth]] did not emerge from a single common ancestor or a singular event, but rather from two distinct lineages or [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]]. This challenges the traditional [[concepts/last-universal-common-ancestor]] ([[concepts/luca|LUCA]]) model which often implies a monophyletic origin.

## Core Concepts

*   **Rejection of [[concepts/primordial-soup|Primordial Soup]]**: Recent evidence suggests the classic "primordial soup" [[concepts/theory|theory]] is insufficient to explain the complexity of early life [[concepts/abiogenesis]].
*   **Hydrothermal Vent Hypothesis**: New studies point to [[concepts/hydrothermal-vents]] as the primary site for life's [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]], offering specific chemical gradients and mineral catalysts.
*   **Dual [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|Lineage]] Theory**: Evidence indicates two separate origins or [[concepts/independent-evolution|distinct evolutionary paths]] that later converged or coexisted before the establishment of the dominant cellular lineage.
