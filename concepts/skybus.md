# SkyBus

## Overview
**SkyBus** refers to the airport link service connecting [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] Airport to [[entities/san-francisco|the city]] center. While SkyBus provides road-based connectivity, Melbourne remains an outlier among major Australian capital cities for lacking a direct rail link to its airport, 56 years after the airport's opening.

## Current Infrastructure Context
- **Road Link:** SkyBus operates as the primary dedicated transport option between the airport and [[entities/melbourne-cbd]].
- **Rail Gap:** Unlike other capitals, Melbourne has historically lacked a direct rail [[concepts/connection|connection]], creating a significant infrastructure discrepancy.

## Recent Developments (as of 2026-08-05)
- **Sunshine Superhub:** Progress on the [[concepts/sunshine-superhub]] is a key component of the broader [[concepts/melbourne-airport-rail-link]] project.
- **Airport Connection Challenges:** Significant hurdles remain regarding the final integration of the rail link with the airport [[concepts/cli|terminal]].
