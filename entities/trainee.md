# Trainee

A trainee is a person undergoing structured training or education within an institutional setting, typically working toward professional qualification or skill development. Trainees occupy an intermediate position between students and fully qualified practitioners, combining theoretical learning with practical experience under supervision. In academic and professional contexts, trainees work alongside more experienced practitioners while completing formal requirements for their chosen field.

## Roles and responsibilities

Trainees are typically required to contribute meaningfully to their organization or institution while simultaneously fulfilling educational objectives. They may participate in governance-related communications and institutional matters, alongside their supervisory practitioners. This dual responsibility reflects their status as developing professionals with increasing involvement in organizational life.

## Institutional context

Within colleges and universities, trainees form part of the broader academic community and may be included in communications regarding institutional governance, policy changes, and matters affecting the college community. Their inclusion in such communications reflects recognition of their role within the institution's functioning and their stake in its governance structures.
