# Multiple Entry Visa

A multiple entry visa is a travel document that permits the holder to enter and exit a country on more than one occasion during the visa's validity period. Unlike single entry visas, which become invalid after one border crossing, multiple entry visas allow repeated journeys into and out of the country without requiring a new visa application for each trip. This type of visa is commonly issued to frequent travelers, business professionals, and individuals who plan several visits within a designated timeframe.

## Duration and Conditions

The validity period of a multiple entry visa varies significantly depending on the issuing country and the specific visa category. For Turkey, these visas are typically issued for durations ranging from 30 days to one year, with some long-term options extending up to five years for eligible applicants. The total length of stay permitted per visit is also subject to specific regulations; for instance, short-term multiple entry visas for Turkey often limit stays to 90 days within any 180-day period.

Holders must adhere to the expiration date printed on the visa, after which it can no longer be used for entry. While the visa allows for multiple entries, each individual stay must comply with the maximum duration limits imposed by immigration authorities. Travelers are responsible for ensuring their passport remains valid for the required period beyond their intended stay and for monitoring their cumulative days of presence to avoid overstaying, which can result in fines or future entry bans.
