# Angle of Repose

The [[concepts/granular-dynamics|angle of repose]] is a measure of the steepest slope at which bulk [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] can naturally rest without sliding or collapsing. It varies depending on the material's properties such as [[concepts/friction|friction]] and [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]].

- The concept applies to various materials like sand, flour, seeds, and more.
- Critical in understanding sediment transport and stability.
- Influences [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] projects such as building dams and retaining walls.
