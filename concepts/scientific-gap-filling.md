# Scientific Gap Filling

**Scientific Gap Filling** refers to the epistemological and methodological practice of addressing discontinuities in empirical data, [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]], or experimental domains. Unlike Hypothesis Generation, which proposes new [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], gap filling focuses on reconciling existing disparate findings or extending known principles into untested regions to restore coherence in a body of knowledge.

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/continuity|Continuity]] [[concepts/preservation|Restoration]]**: Identifying logical or empirical breaks between established phenomena and proposing minimal necessary extensions to bridge them.
- **Risk of Interpolation Error**: Danger of assuming linearity or uniformity where non-linear dynamics or unknown variables exist.
- **Interdisciplinary Synthesis**: Often occurs at the intersection of fields where [[concepts/terminology|terminology]] or methodology differs (e.g., [[concepts/astrophysics]] and Biology).

## Applications in Space Life Sciences
Recent discourse highlights critical gaps in understanding biological responses to long-duration spaceflight, particularly regarding microgravity-induced physiological changes.

- [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Bridging-the-Gaps-in-Space-Life-Sciences-and-Health-Rese|Bridging the Gaps in Space Life Sciences and Health Research]]
- **Context**: Commentary by Nickerson, Ott, and Burer (Spring 2024) emphasizes the disconnect between short-term ISS data and long-term [[concepts/mars|Mars]] mission requirements.
- **Key Gap Areas**:
    - Neurovestibular adaptation limits.
    - Radiation biology uncertainties beyond current shielding models.
    - Psychological [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] metrics in isolated, confined environments.
- **Methodological Shift**: Advocacy for "gap-bridging" experiments that specifically target unknowns rather than merely replicating Earth-based protocols in space.

## Relation to Null Hypothesis and Falsifiability
Gap filling hypotheses must remain falsifiable; they should not serve as *ad hoc* [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to save a failing [[concepts/theory|theory]] but as structured inquiries into missing data points. See Confirmation Bias for risks in selective gap identification.
