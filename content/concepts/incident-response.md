# Incident Response

Incident response refers to the systematic process of managing and mitigating [[concepts/security|security]] breaches and adverse events within IT infrastructure and [[concepts/organization|organizational systems]]. It encompasses the procedures, tools, and personnel involved in detecting, analyzing, containing, and resolving security incidents to minimize damage and restore normal operations.
