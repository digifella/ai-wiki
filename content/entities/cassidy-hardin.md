# Cassidy Hardin

Cassidy Hardin is an [[entities/ai-engineer|AI engineer]] associated with [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s work on the [[concepts/23b-parameter-models|Gemma 4]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] AI model. Hardin has contributed to both the development and documentation of Gemma 4, which represents Google DeepMind's efforts in creating accessible, [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/open-source-language-models|open-source language models]].

## Professional Focus

Hardin's documented involvement centers on the technical [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] aspects of the Gemma 4 project, working within Google DeepMind's broader initiative to provide open-source [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to the research and [[concepts/developer|developer]] communities.
