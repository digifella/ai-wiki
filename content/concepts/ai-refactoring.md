# AI refactoring

AI refactoring applies [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automatically improve code [[concepts/structure|structure]], readability, and maintainability without altering external behavior. It represents a specialized subset of [[concepts/ai-coding]] focused on [[concepts/code-refactoring|Code refactoring]].
