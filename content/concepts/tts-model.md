# Tts Model

The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family represents an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/text-to-speech-synthesis|text-to-speech synthesis]] initiative released by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]]. These models are designed to convert written text into spoken audio output, forming part of the broader ecosystem of multilingual [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] developed by Qwen.

## Key Characteristics

Qwen3-TTS models support [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] and [[concepts/customization|customization]] capabilities, allowing users to generate speech with varied characteristics. The open-source release makes the technology accessible for integration into various [[concepts/software|applications]] and research projects without proprietary restrictions.

## Context

The development of Qwen3-TTS reflects the growing focus on [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]] within the [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] and [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] space. As part of the [[concepts/qwen-model|Qwen model]] family, these TTS systems are positioned alongside other language and [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]] to support more comprehensive [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]].
