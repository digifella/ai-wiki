# The Royal Institution

The Royal Institution is a charity in London that aims to inspire people to see science as an exciting and accessible process of exploration, and to engage them with scientific discourse.

- Founded in 1799
- Well known for Christmas Lectures which began in 1825
- Hosts lectures, workshops, and discussions on a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of topics including the sciences, technology, engineering, and [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]].
