# Siri

Siri is Apple's virtual assistant, available on iOS, macOS, watchOS, and tvOS devices. It uses voice recognition and [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]] to respond to user queries, control device functions, and perform tasks like setting reminders, sending messages, and searching the web. Siri was first introduced in 2011 as part of iOS 5.

## Functionality and Features

Siri operates through voice [[concepts/commands|commands]] and can be activated by saying "Hey Siri" on most Apple devices. It integrates with Apple's ecosystem to control smart home devices, play media, retrieve information, and manage calendar and [[entities/contact|contact]] data. The assistant has improved significantly over the years, with enhanced [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in understanding context and regional accents.

## Comparison to Alternative Solutions

While Siri remains the primary assistant for Apple users, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] alternatives like [[concepts/local-ai-assistants|Clawdbot]] offer self-hosted options for users prioritizing [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and control over their [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] data. These alternatives operate independently of corporate infrastructure and allow [[concepts/customization|customization]] by individual users or developers.
