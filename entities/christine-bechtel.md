# Christine Bechtel

**Christine Bechtel** is a [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] and [[entities/tasia-custode|author]] contributing to the field of [[concepts/healthcare-policy|healthcare policy]] and patient engagement. She is notably a co-author of foundational frameworks regarding family and patient involvement in care systems.
