# AI Driven Video Production

AI-driven video production is a workflow that combines automated slide generation with video synthesis to streamline content creation. The process typically begins with [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], which generates structured [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source materials such as documents, research papers, or transcripts. These slides serve as the foundation for video content, providing both visual structure and narrative flow. The generated slides are then processed through [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]], a video synthesis tool that converts slides into finished video outputs.

## Workflow and Process

The typical workflow involves uploading source material to NotebookLM, which analyzes the content and produces a presentation-ready slide deck with speaker notes or accompanying text. This deck is then imported into Google Vids, which automatically generates video content by combining the slides with synthesized narration, transitions, and visual effects. A key advantage of this approach is the ability to produce unwatermarked output, making the videos suitable for professional distribution across educational platforms, marketing channels, or documentary projects.

## Applications and Benefits

This method reduces the manual work required in traditional [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] while maintaining output quality. It is particularly effective for converting written content, research, or presentations into engaging video format without requiring specialized video production skills or software expertise. The approach scales well for producing multiple videos from diverse source materials, making it applicable to educational content creation, corporate communications, and knowledge dissemination workflows.
