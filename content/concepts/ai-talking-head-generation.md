# AI Talking Head Generation

AI talking head generation is a technique that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to create realistic video content of a person's face speaking text that was not originally recorded by that person. The technology combines facial animation, [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]], and video rendering to produce videos where a person appears to deliver any provided script. This differs from traditional [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]], which manipulates existing footage, as talking head generation creates new video content from synthetic components.

## Technical Components

The generation process typically involves three core components. Speech synthesis converts input text into audio with natural prosody and timing. Facial animation models predict how facial features and expressions should move to match the synthesized speech. Video rendering then produces the final visual output, blending the animated face onto a video background or generating a complete scene. Modern approaches often use [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] trained on video data to capture realistic lip-sync, eye [[concepts/exercise|movement]], and subtle facial expressions.

## Applications and Considerations

Talking head generation has applications in [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], education, [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and customer service, where it can reduce production costs and time compared to traditional video recording. The technology also raises questions around consent, authenticity, and potential misuse, as generated videos can be difficult for viewers to distinguish from authentic recordings. Jurisdictions are developing [[concepts/policies|policies]] around disclosure requirements and restrictions on creating talking head videos of real people without consent.
