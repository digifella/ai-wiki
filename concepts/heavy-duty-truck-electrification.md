# Heavy-Duty Truck Electrification

Strategies and technologies for transitioning commercial freight vehicles from internal combustion engines to electric powertrains. Key approaches include [[concepts/battery-swapping]], [[concepts/charging-infrastructure]], and Retrofitting.

## Battery Swapping
Rapid replacement of depleted battery packs with fully charged ones to minimize downtime. Critical for high-utilization heavy-duty applications where charging times are prohibitive.

- **[[entities/janus-electric|Janus Electric]]**: Australian company specializing in [[concepts/battery-swapping]] systems for heavy-duty trucks.
- **[[concepts/retrofit|Retrofit]] Focus**: Janus Electric offers retrofit solutions, allowing existing diesel truck fleets to be electrified without replacing the entire chassis.
- **Efficiency**: Enables near-instant refueling, addressing range [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]] and operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] in heavy logistics.
