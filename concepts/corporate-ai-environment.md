# Corporate AI environment

The ecosystem of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], tools, and practices deployed within an organization, including sanctioned and unsanctioned implementations. A critical challenge is **[[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]**—unauthorized [[concepts/ai-projects|AI projects]] operating without IT or [[concepts/security|security]] oversight.

- **[[concepts/shadow-it|Shadow AI]] definition**: Unauthorized or [[concepts/unauthorized-ai-deployments|undiscovered AI instances]] within corporate networks, as defined by IBM's [[entities/jeff-crume|Jeff Crume]] (2026), posing significant security and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] risks [[concepts/shadow-ai]]
- **Key risk**: [[entities/employees|Employees]] using [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] for tasks without [[concepts/governance|governance]], leading to [[concepts/data-leakage|data leakage]], regulatory non-compliance, and uncontrolled [[concepts/ai-model-deployment|model deployment]] [[concepts/ai-governance|AI Governance]]
- **IBM's management strategy**: Proactive visibility into all AI instances required to mitigate risks, emphasizing [[concepts/collective-awareness|organizational awareness]] beyond formal IT channels [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|AI Governance]]
- **Source**: [IBM Shadow AI](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=YBE6hq-OTFI) ([[entities/jeff-crume|Jeff Crume]], [[entities/ibm-technology|IBM Technology]], 2026)
