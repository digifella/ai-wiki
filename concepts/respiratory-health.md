# Respiratory Health

Respiratory health encompasses the function and wellbeing of the lungs, airways, and oxygen exchange systems in the body. The respiratory system continuously supplies oxygen to the bloodstream while removing carbon dioxide waste, a process essential for cellular function and physical performance. When functioning properly, these systems adapt efficiently to varying activity levels and environmental conditions, maintaining adequate oxygen saturation during rest and exertion alike.

## Key Components

The respiratory system includes the airways (nose, trachea, and bronchi), the lungs with their millions of alveoli where gas exchange occurs, and the muscles that control breathing. Healthy respiratory function depends on the structural integrity of these components and their ability to work together seamlessly. The diaphragm, the primary breathing muscle, contracts to draw air into the lungs and relaxes during exhalation.

## Factors Affecting Respiratory Health

Multiple factors influence respiratory wellbeing, including air quality, smoking exposure, physical fitness level, and genetic predisposition. Respiratory infections, chronic diseases such as asthma or chronic obstructive pulmonary disease (COPD), and environmental pollutants can compromise lung function. Regular physical activity strengthens respiratory muscles and improves oxygen utilization, while avoiding irritants and maintaining good hygiene reduces infection risk. Age-related changes and certain occupational exposures also affect respiratory capacity over time.
