## AI-Powered Tools in Lightroom Classic
[[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] has been at the forefront of integrating AI-powered tools to enhance photographers' [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]]. These features leverage [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automate tasks that were previously time-consuming, such as culling through large photo collections and organizing them into stacks.

### Key Features

- **[[concepts/assisted-culling|Assisted Culling]] (Early Access)**
  - Streamlines the process of selecting photos by analyzing image content and suggesting [[concepts/high-quality-images|high-quality images]].
  - Integrates seamlessly with [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Lightroom]] Classic's existing culling tools to provide a more efficient workflow.

- **Auto Stacking (Early Access)**
  - Automatically groups similar or related photos into stacks, making it easier to find specific images within large collections.
  - Enhances organization and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of photo archives.
