# Andrew Reiss

Andrew Reiss is a researcher known for his participation in the Biosphere 2 project, a large-scale ecological experiment conducted near Oracle, Arizona. Biosphere 2 was designed as a sealed environmental system to study how self-sustaining ecosystems function in isolation. The project aimed to advance the understanding of Earth's biosphere and complex ecological processes by creating a controlled, closed-loop environment.

Reiss contributed to the scientific objectives of the project through his work within the sealed structure. His involvement provided empirical data on the behavior of interconnected ecological systems, offering insights into the dynamics of closed ecosystems. This research helped inform broader scientific discussions regarding sustainability, resource management, and the resilience of biological systems under isolated conditions.
