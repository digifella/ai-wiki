### Summary
The video features an interview with AI researcher Jeremy Berman, who explains how Large Language Models (LLMs) are constructed, from initial training to post-training reinforcement learning. 

According to Berman, the foundation of neural networks begins with basic mathematical functions and "knobs" or parameters that map inputs to outputs. He uses a simplified real estate price prediction example to illustrate how models use backward propagation to iteratively adjust parameters and minimize errors. 

The discussion then transitions to Large Language Models (LLMs), contrasting how numeric models use specific numerical inputs with how language models operate on text and token sequences. Berman demonstrates how base models like Qwen are initially trained to autocomplete text based on massive datasets, and how post-training techniques like reinforcement learning and instruct tuning help models adopt specific personas, follow system prompts, and perform step-by-step reasoning.
