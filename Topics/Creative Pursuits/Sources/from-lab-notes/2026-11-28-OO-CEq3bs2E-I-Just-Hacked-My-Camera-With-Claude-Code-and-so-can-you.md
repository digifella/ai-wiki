## I Just Hacked My Camera With Claude Code (and so can you)
**Clip title:** I Just Hacked My Camera With Claude Code (and so can you)
**Author / channel:** Pat Simmons
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=OO-CEq3bs2E

### Summary
The video demonstrates how to control a Fujifilm X-T5 camera using the AI assistant Claude. The speaker connects the camera to a computer via USB and interacts with Claude to read and modify camera settings, apply film simulations, and take photos remotely.

Claude is used to research and create film recipes, such as a Portra 800-inspired look, and save them directly to the camera's custom settings. The speaker also explores the potential of applying film recipes to existing RAW files on the computer, bypassing Fujifilm's software. Furthermore, the video touches upon more advanced tasks, such as examining the camera's firmware to understand how photo processing works.

The video concludes that while some tasks, like firmware modification, present significant risks and limitations, Claude proves capable of handling many camera functions and photography tasks. The speaker highlights the potential of using AI assistants for camera control and creative experimentation, while advising caution for risky procedures like firmware hacking.
