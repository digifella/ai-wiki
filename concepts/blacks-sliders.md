# Blacks Sliders

The [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] slider is a fundamental adjustment tool in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] that specifically targets the darkest tones in an image. Unlike the broader [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]] slider, which affects the entire tonal range, the Blacks slider adjusts only the shadow regions while leaving midtones and highlights relatively unchanged. This selective control allows photographers to deepen shadow areas and increase overall [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] without overexposing brighter regions of the photograph.

## How It Works

The Blacks slider remaps the darkest input values in an image, effectively setting where the pure [[concepts/black-point-adjustment|black point]] begins. Moving the slider to the left compresses the shadow tones, pushing them toward absolute black and increasing [[concepts/perceived-contrast|perceived contrast]]. Conversely, moving the slider to the right lifts the darkest pixels, which can reveal detail in underexposed areas but may result in a "muddy" or gray [[concepts/presence|appearance]] if overused.

## Application in Landscape Processing

In [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], the Blacks slider is often used to restore depth and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] to shadowed areas such as tree lines, rock formations, or foreground elements. By carefully adjusting this slider, photographers can prevent [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] from appearing flat or washed out, thereby enhancing the three-dimensional quality of the scene. It is typically adjusted after primary exposure and contrast corrections to fine-tune the [[concepts/exposure-adjustment|tonal balance]] without introducing noise or artifacts in the darker regions.
