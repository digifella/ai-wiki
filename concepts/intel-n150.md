# Intel N150

The Intel N150 is a low-power processor from Intel's Alder Lake lineup, designed for embedded and edge computing applications. Part of Intel's efficiency-focused processor family, the N150 prioritizes minimal power consumption over raw computational performance, making it suitable for devices where thermal and energy constraints are significant factors.

## Applications and Use Cases

The processor is commonly found in network-attached storage systems, small form factor computers, and virtualization appliances. Specific implementations include storage management devices such as the TerraMaster F4-425 Plus, where the N150 handles virtualization workloads and NAS management tasks. The low power envelope makes it suitable for always-on systems where operational costs and heat dissipation are important considerations.

## Technical Characteristics

As part of the Alder Lake family, the N150 represents Intel's effort to provide capable processing for non-demanding workloads without the power requirements of higher-performance processors. The architecture balances sufficient computational capability for storage, networking, and light virtualization duties against energy efficiency requirements typical of edge and embedded deployment scenarios.
