# Urban Planning & Ancient Civilizations

[[concepts/city-planning|Urban planning]] is the process of designing and managing the development of human settlements. It involves organizing land, resources, and infrastructure to create functional, sustainable, and aesthetically pleasing environments. In the context of [[concepts/ancient-civilizations|Ancient civilizations]], planning strategies often intersected with survival challenges, including [[concepts/disease-outbreaks|disease outbreaks]] that decimated populations.

## Core Principles of Urban Planning

*   **Sustainability:** Ensuring that [[concepts/urban-development|urban development]] meets present needs without compromising future generations.
*   **Functionality:** Designing spaces for efficient [[concepts/exercise|movement]], access, and utility (e.g., transportation networks, zoning).
*   **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]:** Creating environments that withstand environmental challenges, economic shifts, demographic changes, and biological threats.
    *   Historical resilience was often tested by pandemics; see [[lab-notes/2026-06-15-Report-on-Civilizations-Decimated-by-Historical-Pandemic|Report on Civilizations Decimated by Historical Pandemics]] for analysis of civilizations impacted by disease.
*   **Equity:** Ensuring benefits and burdens of development are distributed fairly.
