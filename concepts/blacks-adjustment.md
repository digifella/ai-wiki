# Blacks Adjustment

The Blacks adjustment is a tonal control slider in Lightroom Classic that modifies the darkest values in an image during post-processing. Located in the Develop module's Tone Curve panel, this slider specifically targets the black point—the deepest shadow areas of a photograph. Unlike the Shadows slider, which affects a broader range of mid-dark tones, the Blacks adjustment operates on a narrower tonal range at the extreme end of the histogram, making it a precise tool for controlling only the deepest shadows.

## Adjustment Direction and Effects

Moving the Blacks slider to the right brightens the shadow areas, recovering detail that may be obscured in very dark regions. This technique is useful for lifting underexposed areas or adding visibility to shadow detail in landscape photography. Conversely, moving the slider to the left deepens the blacks, increasing contrast and making shadow areas more prominent. This can enhance the visual punch of an image, particularly in high-contrast scenes.

## Practical Application

The Blacks adjustment is particularly valuable in landscape processing where selective control over shadow tones is needed without affecting the overall midtone or highlight structure. It allows photographers to preserve detail in dark areas or intentionally deepen blacks for creative effect, offering more targeted control than broad global adjustments like Exposure. The narrower tonal range it targets distinguishes it from other shadow recovery tools in the adjustment workflow.
