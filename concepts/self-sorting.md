# Self-Sorting

Self-sorting refers to the phenomenon where [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] naturally segregate themselves based on properties such as size or [[concepts/density|density]]. This occurs due to various [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] including percolation, avalanching, and fluidization within bulk piles of particles.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/angle-of-repose|Angle of Repose]]**: The maximum angle at which a granular material can rest against a vertical surface without sliding down under the influence of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]].
- **Granular Materials**: Composed of discrete solid particles that interact primarily through direct [[entities/contact|contact]]; examples include sand, gravel, and cereal.
- **Self-Sorting Mechanisms**: Processes like convection currents within granular piles leading to separation of differently sized or shaped grains.
