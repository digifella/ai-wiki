# Structure

Structure refers to the foundational organization and architecture of design systems, platforms, and tools. In the context of modern [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] and development, structure encompasses both the technical frameworks that enable these tools to function and the organizational principles that guide how designers and developers interact with them.

## AI Design Agents and Platforms

Contemporary [[concepts/ai-design-agents|AI design agents]] provide structured approaches to automating and augmenting [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]]. These tools establish clear frameworks for how design tasks are decomposed, processed, and executed. The structural design of such platforms determines how users can input requirements, receive outputs, and iterate on results. Platforms like SuperDesign exemplify how structure in [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] defines the relationship between human intent and machine capability.

## Memory and Context Management

Modern design platforms increasingly incorporate [[concepts/memory|memory]] systems that maintain context across interactions. The [[concepts/automated-front-end-component-generation|Memories.ai]] platform demonstrates how structural decisions around data [[concepts/storing|retention]], context [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and state management affect the [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] and coherence of design work. These [[concepts/memory-structures|memory structures]] allow both designers and [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to reference prior decisions, iterations, and project specifications without requiring complete re-specification with each interaction.

## Integration and Workflow

The structure of design platforms also determines how they integrate with existing workflows and tools. Well-structured systems provide clear interfaces and protocols for connecting design agents to [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] pipelines, [[concepts/app-updates|version control]] systems, and collaborative environments. This structural approach enables seamless incorporation of AI capabilities into established design and production processes.
