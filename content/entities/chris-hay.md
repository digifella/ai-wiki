# Chris Hay

Chris Hay is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] and researcher who produces educational material on [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and their technical foundations. He is known for presenting novel perspectives on how LLMs function and can be queried.

## LLMs as Databases

Hay has explored the conceptual framework of treating [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] as database systems. In his work on [[concepts/gema-3-model|Larql]]—a method for querying and modifying LLM internal database structures—he presents the thesis that LLMs can be approached as queryable data structures rather than purely generative systems. This perspective offers potential approaches for accessing and manipulating the information encoded within [[concepts/active-parameters|model parameters]].

His video "LLMs Are Databases - So Query Them" outlines these methods for interacting with LLM internals, contributing to the growing discussion around [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] and practical interfaces for [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] systems.
