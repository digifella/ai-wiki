# AI Tool Adoption

[[concepts/ai-adoption-trends|AI tool adoption]] refers to the process by which organizations implement and integrate [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems into their operations and workflows. This encompasses both the technical deployment of AI solutions and the broader organizational challenge of enabling [[entities/employees|employees]] across various roles to effectively use these tools. Successful [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] requires alignment between technology [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], workforce capabilities, and business objectives.

## The Skills Gap Challenge

A significant barrier to AI adoption is the [[concepts/skills-gap|skills gap]] that exists within many organizations. Many companies invest in AI technologies without having sufficient internal [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] or user familiarity to effectively leverage them. This gap extends beyond technical roles—it affects how employees across departments can practically engage with new systems.
