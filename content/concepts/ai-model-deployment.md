# AI model deployment

The operational process of transitioning AI model architectures from [[concepts/training|training]] stages to production environments, focusing on [[concepts/inference]] optimization, [[concepts/infrastructure-scalability|infrastructure scalability]], and resource management.

## Core Elements
- **[[concepts/compute]] Provisioning:** Managing the allocation of [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] resources (GPUs/TPUs) to meet workload requirements.
- **Scalability & Demand:** Engineering systems to handle fluctuating user traffic and prevent service degradation.
- **[[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]]:** Ensuring high availability to mitigate the risk of "[[concepts/compute|compute]] crunches" and service outages.

## Recent Observations
- **[[entities/anthropic-labs|Anthropic]] Compute Miscalculation (April [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]]):**
    - A miscalculation in [[concepts/compute]] resource planning for [[entities/claude]] led to a significant "[[concepts/compute-crunch|compute crunch]]."
    - The resulting service instability created a public relations challenge for [[entities/anthropic]].
    - Competitors, specifically [[entities/openai]], are actively exploiting these deployment-related vulnerabilities to gain market share.
