# Hozier

Hozier is an Irish singer-songwriter born Andrew Hozier-Byrne in Wicklow. He achieved international prominence with his 2014 debut single "Take Me to Church," which became a major commercial and critical success. His musical style combines folk traditions with contemporary indie rock production, creating a distinctive sound that has remained consistent throughout his career.
