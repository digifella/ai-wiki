# Cooking Time

Duration and thermal trajectory applied to food substrates to achieve target physicochemical states, including [[concepts/denaturation|protein denaturation]], starch gelatinization, Maillard browning, and leavening expansion.

## Governing Variables
- **Thermal Input:** Intensity, mode (conduction/convection/radiation), and uniformity.
- **Substrate Properties:** [[concepts/density|Density]], [[concepts/moisture-content|moisture content]], surface-area-to-volume ratio, thermal conductivity.
- **Reaction Kinetics:** Activation energy thresholds, leavening gas generation rates, enzymatic degradation.
- **[[concepts/phase|Phase]] Changes:** Ice melting, water evaporation, fat [[concepts/visual-rendering|rendering]], coagulation points.
