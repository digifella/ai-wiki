# Smoothness Breakdown

**Smoothness breakdown** refers to the theoretical scenario in [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid dynamics]] where the solutions to the [[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes equations]] cease to be smooth (differentiable) after a finite time, implying the formation of singularities or infinite energy dissipation rates. This concept is central to the [[concepts/navier-stokes-millennium-prize-problem|Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem]], one of the seven [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems]].
