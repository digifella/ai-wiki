# Comprehensive Investigations

Comprehensive Investigations is a [[concepts/multi-modal-research|multi-modal research]] tool designed to automate business intelligence and research workflows. It leverages [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini-25-models|Gemini 2.5]] language models in conjunction with [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph]], a framework within [[entities/langchain|LangChain]], to process and analyze information across multiple data sources simultaneously. The system is engineered to handle complex research questions that require both natural language understanding and [[concepts/reasoning-capability|logical inference]], enabling structured [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].

The architecture integrates Gemini 2.5's advanced [[concepts/language-processing|language processing]] with LangGraph's state management and [[concepts/llm-orchestration|workflow orchestration]]. This combination allows the tool to execute [[concepts/deep-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]] processes, maintaining context across diverse inputs such as text, documents, and potentially other data formats. By utilizing LangGraph, the system can define complex execution paths, ensuring that research tasks are decomposed into manageable steps and executed with [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].

The primary application of Comprehensive Investigations lies in automating the collection, synthesis, and analysis of business data. It supports workflows that demand high levels of accuracy and [[concepts/coherence|coherence]], making it suitable for tasks such as competitive analysis, market research, and strategic planning. The tool's ability to [[concepts/purpose|reason]] across multiple modalities allows it to draw connections between disparate pieces of information, providing users with comprehensive insights derived from automated, structured investigation processes.
