# Health System Strengthening

**[[concepts/health-system|Health system]] strengthening** refers to the process of improving the capacity of [[concepts/health|health]] systems to deliver essential [[concepts/health-care|health services]] efficiently, equitably, and sustainably. It encompasses multiple building blocks defined by the WHO including [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]], health workforce, information, medical products, financing, and [[concepts/leadership|leadership]].

## Core Dimensions
- **Service Delivery**: Enhancing quality and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of care.
- **Health Workforce**: Strengthening recruitment, [[concepts/storing|retention]], and training of health professionals.
- **[[concepts/healthcare-informatization|Health Information Systems]]**: Improving data collection and utilization for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
- **Medical Products**: Ensuring availability of essential medicines and vaccines.
- **Financing**: Implementing [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for equitable access and financial [[concepts/secure|protection]].
- **Leadership/Governance**: Establishing effective oversight and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]].

## Key Developments
- Recent focus on integrating digital tools to enhance [[concepts/robustness|system resilience]] and efficiency.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-WHO-guideline|WHO guideline]] provides specific [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]] on [[concepts/digital-interventions|digital interventions]] for health system strengthening, published in 2019 by the [[entities/earth|World]] Health Organization.
