# AI Generated Shorts

AI Generated Shorts is an automated workflow that combines Claude Code, an AI coding assistant, with Blotato, a content distribution platform, to streamline the creation and publishing of short-form video content. The system integrates video generation, editing, and multi-platform distribution into a coordinated process, reducing manual effort across these separate stages.

## Workflow Components

Claude Code handles the automation of scripting, video generation parameters, and editing instructions. It processes source content or user specifications and generates the technical instructions needed to produce video assets. Blotato manages the distribution phase, handling the publishing of completed videos across multiple social media platforms and other distribution channels simultaneously.

## Practical Application

The workflow reduces friction in short-form content production by eliminating manual transitions between content creation and platform-specific publishing. Rather than creating videos and then manually uploading to individual platforms, the system automates both generation parameters and distribution logistics. This is particularly useful for content creators, marketing teams, or organizations that produce regular short-form video content for multiple platforms.
