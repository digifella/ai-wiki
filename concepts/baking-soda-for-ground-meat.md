# Baking Soda for Ground Meat

## Introduction
[[concepts/baking-soda|Baking soda]] is a common household ingredient with surprising culinary applications. When used in the right quantities and methods, it can significantly enhance the [[concepts/texture|texture]], juiciness, and browning of [[concepts/ground-meat|ground meat]].

## Key Benefits
- **[[concepts/texture-slider|Texture]]:** Improves the chewability and reduces toughness.
- **Juiciness:** Increases moisture [[concepts/storing|retention]] during cooking.
- **Browning:** Enhances surface color for an appetizing [[concepts/presence|appearance]].

## Methods
1. Mix a small amount (1/4 teaspoon per pound) of baking soda with ground meat before forming into patties or adding to recipes.
2. Allow the mixture to sit for at least 15 minutes but not more than an hour before cooking.
3. Rinse thoroughly under cold water and pat dry before cooking.
