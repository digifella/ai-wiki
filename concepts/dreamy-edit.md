# Dreamy Edit

A photographic editing aesthetic characterized by soft [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], muted tones, and atmospheric diffusion to evoke a surreal or nostalgic mood.

## Core Techniques

- **Atmospheric Diffusion**: Use of Orton Effect or softening filters to reduce micro-contrast and create a glowing halo around highlights.
- **[[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]]**: Shift towards cool blues/purples in [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] and warm golds/peaches in highlights to enhance the "dreamlike" quality.
- **Selective [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]]**: Precise [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] of elements to apply [[concepts/masking-tools|localized adjustments]] without affecting the entire image.
