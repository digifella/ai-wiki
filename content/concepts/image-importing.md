# Image importing

The process of bringing external visual assets into a digital workspace, such as [[entities/adobe-photoshop]], to facilitate editing or Compositing.

## Workflow Integrations
- **[[concepts/lightroom]] [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Utilize the "Edit" command by right-clicking selected [[concepts/images|images]] in [[concepts/lightroom]] to automate the transfer of assets into the active [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] environment.
- **High-Fidelity [[concepts/setup|Setup]]**: For advanced Compositing workflows, assets should be imported into documents configured for maximum data retention:
    - **Bit Depth**: 16-bit
    - **Color Space**: [[concepts/adobe-rgb]]
    - **Resolution**: 300 [[concepts/pixels-per-inch|ppi]]
