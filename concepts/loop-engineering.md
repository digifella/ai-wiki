# Loop Engineering

**[[concepts/loop|Loop]] [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]** is a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in [[concepts/ai-agent]] development that moves beyond static [[entities/prompt-engineering]] to focus on the iterative loops and systemic behaviors of [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]]. Unlike traditional [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], which optimizes single-turn [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], [[concepts/prompt-engineering|loop engineering]] designs the [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], state management, and execution cycles that allow agents to operate autonomously over time.

## Core Concepts

- **Beyond Single Instructions**: Traditional approaches rely on crafting perfect one-off prompts.
- **Iterative Optimization**: Focuses on structured, iterative workflows that allow AI agents to autonomously perform and improve [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] over time.
- **State Management**: Critical for maintaining context and progress across multiple turns of interaction.
- **Feedback Mechanisms**: Systems that enable the agent to self-correct and refine outputs based on previous results.

## Karpathy Loop Method

The "[[concepts/karpathy-loop|Karpathy Loop]]" represents a specific application of [[concepts/autonomous-ai-agent-design|loop engineering]] principles, emphasizing high-leverage iterative optimization for AI agents. This method has been highlighted for its ability to significantly enhance the performance of [[concepts/ai-coding-agents|coding agents]] (e.g., Claude Code) by structuring the [[concepts/autonomous-development|autonomous development]] process.

- **[[concepts/complex-task-execution|Complex Task Execution]]**: Moves beyond simple instructions to handle multi-step, [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]] with greater reliability.
