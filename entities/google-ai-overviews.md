# Google AI Overviews

Google AI Overviews is a feature within Google Search that uses generative artificial intelligence to synthesize and display direct answers to user queries at the top of search results. Rather than presenting traditional ranked links, the system generates concise summaries by aggregating information from multiple sources. This represents a significant shift in search result formatting, as AI-generated overviews appear prominently before traditional organic search listings.

## How It Works

When a user enters a query, Google's AI system analyzes relevant web pages and generates a summary that directly addresses the search intent. The system pulls information from multiple sources to create a comprehensive answer, citation sources are displayed alongside the overview. This process aims to provide users with immediate, synthesized information without requiring them to click through individual links.

## Generative Engine Optimization

The rise of AI Overviews has prompted the development of Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), a practice focused on optimizing website content specifically for AI-generated summaries. Website owners and content creators use tools and techniques to increase the likelihood that their content will be selected and featured in AI Overviews. This includes structuring content clearly, providing authoritative information, and ensuring pages directly answer common search queries.
