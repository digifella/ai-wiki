# Heygen

[[entities/heygen|Heygen]] is an artificial intelligence-powered platform designed to streamline video production by automating technical workflows. The service enables users to generate and animate [[concepts/digital-avatars|digital avatars]], edit [[concepts/video-resource|video content]], and manage post-production tasks that traditionally require significant manual effort. By handling these elements automatically, the platform allows content creators and businesses to produce videos more efficiently than with conventional methods.

The platform’s core functionality centers on [[concepts/character-generation|avatar generation]], which creates customizable digital characters capable of delivering spoken content. These avatars are integrated into a broader suite of tools that facilitate rapid [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]], reducing the dependency on physical filming equipment and human editors. This automation extends to various stages of production, aiming to lower the barrier to entry for [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] video output.

Recent developments in the platform include the introduction of a [[concepts/video-agent-tool|Video Agent Tool]], which has been highlighted in instructional content on [[entities/youtube|YouTube]]. This feature represents an evolution in how the platform assists users in managing complex video projects through automated agents. The tool is designed to further reduce the time and [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] required to produce professional-grade video content.
