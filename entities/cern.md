# CERN

**CERN** (Conseil Européen pour la Recherche Nucléaire) is the European Organization for Nuclear Research, renowned for operating the world's largest and most powerful particle accelerator complex.

## Key Facilities & Discoveries

### Large Hadron Collider (LHC)
The LHC is the primary instrument for high-energy physics experiments, facilitating the study of fundamental particles and forces.

*   **Mass from Energy:** The LHC demonstrates the inverse process of nuclear fission/fusion by converting [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]] into new mass, creating particles that did not previously exist [[lab-notes/2026-09-13-CERNs-LHC-Creating-Mass-from-Energy-and-Discovering-the|CERN's LHC: Creating Mass from Energy and Discovering the Higgs Boson]].
*   **[[entities/higgs-boson|Higgs Boson]] Discovery:** The facility was instrumental in confirming the existence of the Higgs Boson, validating the mechanism by which elementary particles acquire mass.
*   **Operational Context:** Recent analyses highlight the LHC's role in exploring the boundaries of the Standard Model and testing energy-to-mass conversion principles [[CERN's LHC: Creating Mass from Energy and Discovering the Higgs Boson](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2zPRAej9h3o)].
