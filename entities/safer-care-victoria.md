# Safer Care Victoria

**[[concepts/space-jetpacks|Safer]] Care Victoria (SCV)** is a statutory body within the Victorian [[concepts/health-system|health system]] responsible for improving [[concepts/health-care|health care]] safety and quality. It provides [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]], education, and support to [[entities/health-service|health service]] organisations and professionals to reduce harm and enhance patient outcomes.
