# AI Security

The discipline of protecting [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] systems, models, and [[concepts/agentic-ai]] from adversarial manipulation, unauthorized access, and unintended autonomous actions.

### Emerging Threat Landscape
- **Evolution of the [[concepts/attack-surface|Attack Surface]]**: The transition from [[concepts/conversational-chatbots|conversational Chatbots]] (such as [[concepts/siri|Siri]]) to [[concepts/agentic-ai]] has shifted the [[concepts/security-protocol|security]] focus from simple [[concepts/text-manipulation|text manipulation]] to the [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] of unauthorized system-level actions.
- **[[concepts/local-execution|Local Execution]] Vulnerabilities**:
    - [[concepts/agentic-ai|Agents]] capable of interacting with a user's local environment pose significant risks to machine [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
    - [[entities/openclaw]] serves as a primary case study for local environment risks.
    - **Google Gemma 4 and Localized Processing**: The emergence of [[concepts/open-weight-models|open-weight models]] like google gemma 4 enables [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] on mobile and desktop devices; while this enhances [[concepts/privacy]] by removing cloud-dependency, it shifts the security perimeter to the local [[concepts/attack-surface|attack surface]].
