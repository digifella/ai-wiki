# Università degli Studi di Palermo

**Università degli Studi di Palermo** (UniPA) is a public university in Palermo, Sicily, [[entities/italy|Italy]]. Founded in 1806, it is one of the oldest universities in Italy and serves a significant population in Southern Italy.

## Structure and Departments
- **Dipartimento di Ingegneria** (Department of Engineering): A key faculty focusing on mechanical, electronic, and computer engineering.
- Medical School: Collaborates on bioengineering and [[concepts/digital-health|health-tech]] initiatives.

## Research Highlights
Recent research outputs demonstrate strong interdisciplinary work between engineering [[entities/national-academies|and medicine]]:

- [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Lanza---Agents-and-robots-for-collaborating-and-supporti|Lanza - Agents and robots for collaborating and supporting physicians in healthcare]]: Published by Elsevier (2020), this study by [[entities/francesco-lanzaa|Francesco Lanza]], Valeria Seidita, and Antonio Chella investigates [[concepts/expertise-based-ai-assistants|multi-agent systems]] for [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] in therapy and emergency care, specifically for [[concepts/covid-19|COVID-19]] patient monitoring and human-robot interaction.
- Focus areas include [[concepts/multi-agent-systems]], [[concepts/telehealth|telemedicine]], and assistive robotics.
