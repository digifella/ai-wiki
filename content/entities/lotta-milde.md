# Lotta Milde

Lotta Milde is a content creator known for producing instructional video tutorials on film photography techniques. She has created educational content for [[entities/bh-photo|B&H Photo]], a major retailer and media company specializing in photography and video equipment.

## Film Scanning Tutorial

Milde is recognized for her video tutorial on [[concepts/high-quality-images|scanning film at home]], published on the B&H Photo YouTube channel. The tutorial provides practical guidance for photographers interested in digitizing film negatives and [[entities/google-slides|slides]] using accessible home scanning methods, addressing a common need among film photography practitioners who wish to create digital copies of their work.
