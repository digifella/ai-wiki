# Photo compositing

The process of merging visual elements from multiple images into a single, cohesive [[concepts/writing|composition]].

### Core Techniques & Applications
- **[[concepts/scenarios|Use Cases]]**:
    - Bringing specific elements from one photo into another.
    - Correcting flaws in group photos, such as fixing closed eyes.
- **Workflow**:
    - Loading multiple images into a stack.
    - Utilizing auto-alignment (effective even when images were not shot with a tripod).
    - Applying [[concepts/layer-masks|layer masks]] to selectively reveal parts of different layers.
