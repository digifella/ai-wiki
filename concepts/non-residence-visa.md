# Non Residence Visa

A Non Residence Visa is an electronic visa (e-visa) category administered by Turkey that permits foreign nationals to enter and remain in the country for specified purposes without establishing formal residency. Processed entirely through Turkey's online e-visa system, this visa type accommodates short-to-medium term visits and is intended for individuals who do not plan to become permanent residents.

## Purpose and Duration

The Non Residence Visa is designed for temporary visits to Turkey. It allows foreign nationals to enter the country for tourism, business meetings, conferences, or similar short-term activities. The visa is typically valid for a limited period, with the exact duration and conditions determined by Turkish immigration authorities at the time of application. Holders are not required to register as residents and do not acquire residency rights through this visa category.

## Application Process

Applications for the Non Residence Visa are submitted through Turkey's official e-visa portal. The process is conducted entirely online, eliminating the need to visit a Turkish embassy or consulate in person. Applicants must provide standard documentation including a valid passport, travel details, and purpose of visit. Processing times are generally brief, with most applications approved within hours or days of submission.
