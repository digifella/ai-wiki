## I Spent $2,000 on Lightroom Presets. Now Claude Can Make Them For Free
**Clip title:** I Spent $2,000 on Lightroom Presets. Now Claude Can Make Them For Free
**Author / channel:** Shoot To Change
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9HmT_A7OqLM

### Summary
Sure, here's a summary of the video content:

**Main Topic:** The video explores the capabilities of the Claude 4.8 LLM in generating Lightroom presets. The speaker tests Claude's ability to create presets with varying levels of prompt detail and compares the results to manually created presets and popular styles.

**Key Points:**
- **Initial Test:** A simple prompt asked Claude to generate 15 film-style presets. The results were okay, but the speaker preferred their own handmade presets.
- **Style Emulation:** The speaker tested Claude's ability to replicate the style of photographer Peter McKinnon. While the presets worked reasonably well, the speaker emphasized that results vary based on the starting image.
- **iPhone Photos:** The speaker tested presets generated for iPhone photos, noting that while they were decent, iPhone photos are generally less malleable for preset editing.
- **Importance of Context:** The speaker highlights that an LLM's output is only as good as its input. Providing a working Lightroom file and specific prompts for different types of photos (e.g., landscapes, portraits) yields better results.

**Conclusions/Takeaways:**
- **Presets as a Base:** The speaker concludes that Lightroom presets should be used as a base, not an end-all solution. Manual adjustments and masking are often necessary for optimal results.
- **AI vs. Manual Editing:** While AI can save time in creating a base style, knowing how to edit photos from scratch remains a crucial skill.
- **Product Offering:** The speaker offers their own Lightroom preset pack, which now includes the Claude-generated presets from the video.
