## Photoshop's Enhanced Depth Range Mask: Creating Depth Maps for Any Photo
**Clip title:** Adobe SNUCK a NEW Feature in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]
**Author / channel:** Anthony Morganti
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2Es3HmHpjYU

### Summary
This video introduces a significant enhancement to the Depth [[concepts/range|Range]] Mask feature within [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], now available in the current shipping version of [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]. Previously, this tool was limited to [[concepts/images|images]] containing embedded depth information, typically generated by smartphone cameras using [[concepts/portrait-mode|portrait mode]] with multiple lenses. The presenter, [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]], highlights that Adobe quietly integrated this updated functionality, making it vastly more versatile and useful for a wider [[concepts/range|range]] of photographs, including those captured with traditional mirrorless cameras and even JPEGs.

The core functionality of the improved [[concepts/depth-range-masking|Depth Range Mask]] allows users to make selective [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] based on the varying depths within an image. To activate it, users navigate to the [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] panel in [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], select 'Range,' and then 'Depth Range.' An [[concepts/eyedropper-tool|eyedropper tool]] appears, which the user clicks on a desired area of the photo to establish a depth point. A "Show [[concepts/depth-map|Depth Map]]" option visually represents the depth, with white areas indicating features closest to the camera, gradually transitioning through shades of grey for mid-ground elements, and ending with black for the furthest parts of the scene. A red overlay highlights the specific areas that [[entities/will|will]] be affected by [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

Crucially, the tool now generates its own depth map for any image, regardless of whether it originally contained depth data. This enables photographers to apply [[concepts/local-adjustments|local adjustments]] to specific foreground, mid-ground, or background elements with precision. Sliders are provided to fine-tune the selected depth range, allowing users to control the feathering (falloff) of the mask and narrow or broaden the area receiving 100% of the adjustments. The presenter demonstrates how these depth masks can also be combined or refined further using other [[concepts/masking-tools|masking tools]] like brushes, offering even greater creative control over image edits.

The main takeaway is that this quietly introduced Depth Range Mask is a powerful new addition, empowering photographers to perform sophisticated, depth-aware selective edits on virtually any image. Whether working with RAW [[concepts/files|files]] from high-end cameras or standard JPEGs, the feature streamlines the process of isolating and enhancing specific areas of a photo based on their distance from the viewer, ultimately helping users achieve more refined and impactful results.
