# Peer-Reviewed Report

A Peer-Reviewed Report is a formal document subjected to evaluation by experts in the relevant field, ensuring [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] rigor and credibility. In this [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]], it serves as a primary source type for high-[[concepts/integrity|integrity]] information, typically assigned a credibility_tier_key status with a `credibility_tier_value` of 5.

## Characteristics
- **Validation**: Undergoes critical review by independent peers prior to publication.
- **Source [[concepts/honesty|Integrity]]**: Maintained via [[concepts/flags|flags]] (e.g., `source_integrity_flag: ok`) and regular availability checks.
- **Publisher Types**: Often issued by academic presses, government bodies, or international organizations like the [[entities/earth|World]] [[concepts/health|Health]] Organization or [[entities/oecd]].
