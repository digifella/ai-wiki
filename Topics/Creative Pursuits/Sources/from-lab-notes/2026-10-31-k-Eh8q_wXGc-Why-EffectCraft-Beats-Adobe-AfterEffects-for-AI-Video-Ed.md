## Why EffectCraft Beats Adobe AfterEffects for AI Video Editing (With MCP)
**Clip title:** Why EffectCraft Beats Adobe AfterEffects for AI Video Editing (With MCP)
**Author / channel:** Applorable
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k-Eh8q_wXGc

### Summary
Here's a clear, well-structured summary of the video content:

### **Main Topic**
The video explores the capabilities of **EffectCraft**, an application designed for motion graphics, animation, and visual effects, by testing its integration with **Codex** using the **Model Context Protocol (MCP)**. The speaker demonstrates how Codex can directly communicate with and control EffectCraft software to create animated promotional videos from a written prompt, without manual intervention.

### **Key Points Discussed**
* **EffectCraft & MCP Integration:** EffectCraft supports MCP, allowing AI tools like Codex to directly issue commands, create compositions, manage layers, apply effects, and set keyframes.
* **Experiment 1 (2D Motion Graphics):** Codex was tasked with creating a 15–20 second 2D promotional sequence for a fictional technology company named "Nova." The AI handled everything from installation and setup to creating the layout, colors, typography, and logo reveal. 
* **Experiment 2 (3D Mechanical Animation):** A second, more complex experiment involved creating a 3D futuristic mechanical structure with a spherical core, rotating rings, depth of field, and multiple lighting effects. The result featured over 160 layers and 1,200+ keyframes.
* **Technical vs. Creative Quality:** While the technical execution by Codex was praised—successfully producing fully editable, complex projects in under 18 minutes—the speaker noted that the creative output was somewhat basic and could be improved with more detailed prompts and creative direction from a human designer.

### **Conclusions & Takeaways**
The video concludes that while AI tools like Codex cannot yet completely replace an experienced motion graphics designer, they are exceptionally useful for generating initial compositions and handling heavy technical animation work. The speaker views EffectCraft’s MCP integration as a powerful technical achievement and invites viewers to share their thoughts on whether AI-driven motion graphics have a bright future.
