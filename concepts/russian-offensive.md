# Russian Offensive

The **Russian Offensive** refers to the sustained military operations conducted by the [[entities/russia|Russian Federation]] against [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]], characterized by shifts in tactical intensity, logistical dependencies, and defensive counter-measures.

## 2026 Campaign Dynamics

The [[concepts/2026-campaign-season|2026 campaign season]] marks a distinct shift in operational tempo compared to previous years. Key developments include:

*   **Slowed Offensive Momentum:** Russian advances have decelerated, transitioning from rapid [[concepts/territorial-gains|territorial gains]] to attritional grinding, particularly in the eastern theater.
*   **Logistics as Primary Target:** Ukrainian strategy has increasingly focused on disrupting Russian [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] stretching from [[entities/crimea|Crimea]] to the frontlines near Kostiantynivka.
*   **[[concepts/fortress-belt|Fortress Belt]] Construction:** Ukraine has established a dense defensive network, referred to as the "[[concepts/defensive-fortifications|Fortress Belt]]," designed to absorb and blunt Russian armored thrusts through layered fortifications.

## Strategic Analysis

Recent assessments highlight the critical role of logistics in determining the pace of the offensive. The integration of long-range strike capabilities has allowed Ukrainian forces to target rear-echelon support, thereby reducing the sustainability of Russian forward positions.
