# 1970 Lincoln Continental Mark III

The **1970 Lincoln Continental Mark III** is a luxury personal luxury car produced by Ford Motor Company under the Lincoln brand. It represents the final year of the Mark III series before the introduction of the Mark IV, characterized by its distinctive "suicide" rear doors, heavy chrome trim, and V8 powertrain.
