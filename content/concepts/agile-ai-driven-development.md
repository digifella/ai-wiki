# Agile AI Driven Development

Agile AI-Driven Development is a software development approach that integrates AI agents and assistants into established agile methodologies and team workflows. Rather than treating AI tools as replacements for traditional development practices, this framework emphasizes maintaining structured processes—such as sprint planning, code review, and testing—while leveraging AI capabilities to accelerate specific tasks like code generation, documentation, and problem-solving. This integration requires explicit consideration of how AI outputs fit into quality gates, team responsibilities, and overall project governance.

## Process and Team Integration

The approach recognizes that AI tools have lowered barriers to entry for application development, allowing developers of varying skill levels to contribute more effectively. However, this accessibility does not eliminate the need for disciplined development practices. Teams adopting this framework must establish clear protocols for AI tool usage, including how generated code is validated, who retains decision-making authority, and how AI assistance is balanced with human expertise and oversight. Documentation, testing, and architectural decisions remain human-driven responsibilities, even when AI contributes to implementation details.

## Key Considerations

Successful adoption requires teams to define boundaries around where AI assistance adds value without introducing risk. This includes understanding the limitations of AI-generated outputs, maintaining traceability of decisions made with AI support, and ensuring that knowledge remains distributed across the team rather than concentrated in tool usage. Organizations implementing this approach typically find that structured agile practices provide the governance framework necessary to deploy AI tools responsibly while capturing their productivity benefits.
