# Italy

The Italy detector is a particle physics instrument located deep underground in the [[entities/stawell-gold-mine|Stawell gold mine]] in Victoria, Australia. Positioned at significant depth to shield its sensitive equipment from [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic radiation]], the detector is designed to search for [[concepts/wimp-particles|weakly interacting massive particles]] ([[concepts/wimp|WIMPs]]), theoretical candidates for [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]].

## Purpose and Design

The detector operates as part of a collaborative effort to detect WIMPs, which are hypothesized to interact only weakly with ordinary matter and pass through the Earth largely unimpeded. By situating the detector underground, the overlying rock provides natural shielding that reduces interference from cosmic rays, allowing for more sensitive measurements of potential WIMP interactions with detector materials.

## Collaboration

Italy operates in conjunction with the [[concepts/saber-detector|Saber detector]] in the same facility. This collaborative approach allows researchers to cross-verify potential detections and improve the reliability of experimental results in the search for dark matter candidates.
