# AI-driven cyberattacks

The [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] to automate, optimize, and scale the execution and sophistication of cyberattacks.

### Key Capabilities & Risks
- **Automated [[concepts/vulnerability|Vulnerability]] Discovery**: Utilization of advanced models, such as [[concepts/mythos|Claude Mythos]] AI, to accelerate the identification of [[concepts/cybersecurity]].
- **Paradigm Shift Debate**: Ongoing industry assessment of whether AI-driven exploits represent "marketing hype" or a fundamental shift toward the "end of [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]]."
- **Enhanced Social Engineering**: Leveraging LLMs to scale personalized phishing and social engineering campaigns.

### Industry Intelligence
- **Expert Analysis**: Research and discussion from [[entities/ibm-technology]] specialists, including [[entities/sridhar-muppidi|Sridhar Muppidi]], [[entities/michelle-alvarez|Michelle Alvarez]], and [[entities/dustin-heywood|Dustin Heywood]] (aka [[entities/evilmog|EvilMog]]), regarding the evolution of [[concepts/secure|security]] intelligence.
