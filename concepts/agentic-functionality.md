# Agentic Functionality

Agentic functionality refers to the capacity of AI systems to autonomously perceive, reason, and execute actions to achieve complex goals. This concept is increasingly shaped by the availability of [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]], sovereign models that enable robust local or regional deployment.

## European AI Sovereignty & Mistral Large 4

Recent developments in [[concepts/european-ai-sovereignty]] highlight the emergence of **[[concepts/mistral-large-4|Mistral Large 4]]**, a critical component for independent agentic [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].

*   **Model Identity:** Dubbed "Le Chonk," this is Mistral AI's new [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]], developed entirely in Europe from scratch [[lab-notes/2026-10-07-European-AI-Sovereignty-Mistral-Large-4-Capabilities-Per|European AI Sovereignty: Mistral Large 4 Capabilities, Performance, Challenges]].
*   **Architecture:** Utilizes a **[[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] (MoE)** architecture with 1 trillion [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]] but only **49 billion [[concepts/activated-parameters|active parameters]]**, optimizing [[concepts/inference-efficiency|inference efficiency]] for [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]].
*   **Licensing:** Released as **open-weight** and **open-source**, facilitating transparent integration into sovereign agentic stacks.
*   **Capabilities:** Features unified [[concepts/multimodal-input|multimodal input]] capabilities, enhancing the model's ability to process diverse data streams within [[concepts/autonomous-workflows|autonomous workflows]].
