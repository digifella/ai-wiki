## Mitochondria: Beyond Powerhouses, Running the Body, Quantum Effects, and Magnetism
**Clip title:** [[concepts/mitochondrial-function|Mitochondria]] Are Not Powerhouses, They're Running the Entire Body
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Anton Petrov
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BUyvFMIGynw

### Summary
The video explores the fascinating and increasingly complex role of [[concepts/cpu|mitochondria]], moving beyond the traditional textbook definition of them as mere "powerhouses of the cell." Far from being passive biological batteries, recent discoveries reveal mitochondria as sophisticated [[concepts/central-processing-units|central processing units]], akin to "pilots" that actively direct various cellular functions. These functions include [[concepts/inter-cellular-communication|inter-cellular communication]], setting the internal clock for aging and cellular change, altering [[concepts/gene-expression|gene expression]], and surprisingly, responding to [[concepts/earths-magnetic-field|Earth's magnetic field]].

Two groundbreaking studies are highlighted, significantly expanding our understanding. The first, conducted by Chinese researchers, investigated the vibrational properties of living human and mouse cells. They uncovered an anomalous signal at 71 Terahertz (THz), equivalent to 71 trillion vibrations per second. This signal was only present when mitochondrial structures were intact, vanishing when cells were destroyed. Further modeling suggested that mitochondria act as microscopic optical cavities, where mid-infrared [[concepts/light|light]] at 87 THz couples with natural vibrations of carbon-hydrogen bonds in lipid bilayers to form a hybrid quantum state called a "mito-polariton." [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]] to this 71 THz frequency was shown to increase cellular ATP production by 10%, indicating that quantum mechanical effects significantly enhance mitochondrial [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] in living cells.

The second study, from UK researchers, demonstrated the profound impact of [[concepts/earths-magnetic-field|Earth's magnetic field]] on mitochondrial [[concepts/yeast-fermentation|metabolism]] and lifespan, using fruit flies. Healthy flies shielded from the natural geomagnetic field experienced a slightly reduced lifespan but a temporary boost in physical strength. More remarkably, mutant flies modeling Parkinson's disease showed a significant 20% increase in lifespan when shielded from the magnetic field. This suggests that the Earth's magnetic field actively influences mitochondrial respirometry and electron transport chain activity. For healthy cells, it might contribute to oxidative [[concepts/stress|stress]], but for compromised cells, its removal could allow alternative metabolic pathways to function more effectively, essentially bypassing defects.

These revelations collectively paint a picture of mitochondria as deeply integrated and dynamic components of life, constantly interacting with their environment on a quantum level and influencing fundamental biological processes like aging and disease. Other points mentioned include the maternal inheritance of mitochondria (sperm mitochondria are often damaged and rejected), the transfer of mitochondrial DNA fragments (NUMTs) into nuclear DNA which contributes to aging, and the social nature of mitochondria communicating across tissues via chemicals in vesicles. This expanded understanding underscores that our bodies are essentially intricate support systems designed to maintain optimal mitochondrial [[concepts/health|health]], with factors like [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], [[concepts/sleep|sleep]], and even the planet's magnetic field playing crucial roles in their efficiency and, consequently, our overall [[concepts/well-being|well-being]] and lifespan.
