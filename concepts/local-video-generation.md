# Local video generation

The process of synthesizing [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] using [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] models hosted on private hardware rather than [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]]. This approach prioritizes [[concepts/privacy]], offline [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and the utilization of [[concepts/open-weights]] models.

### Key Developments
- **[[entities/ltx-2|LTX-2]]**: A significant advancement in [[concepts/open-source]] video synthesis.
	- Enables local generation of video with [[concepts/synchronized-audio|synchronized audio]].
	- Optimized for [[concepts/consumer-grade-gpus|consumer-grade GPUs]] with [[concepts/low-vram-requirements|low VRAM requirements]].
