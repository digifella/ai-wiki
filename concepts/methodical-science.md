# Methodical Science

This article documents a tutorial by David on color correction techniques for concert photography using Adobe Lightroom Classic. The tutorial addresses the specific post-processing challenges that arise when photographing live music events, where stage lighting creates extreme and rapidly shifting color casts across images.

Concert venues employ intense colored lighting that changes frequently during performances, presenting significant obstacles for consistent image processing. Stage lighting typically combines multiple light sources with varying intensities and hues, resulting in complex white balance issues that standard automatic corrections often fail to resolve accurately.

The methodology focuses on establishing a systematic approach to neutralizing these color casts while preserving the atmospheric intent of the original lighting design. By utilizing specific tools within Adobe Lightroom Classic, the process aims to achieve consistent skin tones and accurate background colors across a series of images taken under fluctuating stage conditions.
