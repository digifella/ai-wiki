# Roles In Community Settings

[[concepts/health|Healthcare]] professionals working in community settings operate across diverse environments including primary care clinics, [[concepts/population-health|public health]] departments, schools, workplaces, and patients' homes. These settings differ fundamentally from hospital-based care in their focus on [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]], health maintenance, and management of chronic conditions within populations rather than acute interventions. The roles of [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] professionals in these contexts require broader [[concepts/skill|skill]] sets that integrate clinical knowledge with community engagement and [[concepts/health-promotion|health promotion]]. Recent [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]] emphasizes the integration of digital tools to enhance [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]].

## Core Professional Functions

Community-based healthcare professionals serve multiple functions simultaneously. They provide direct clinical care while also [[concepts/acting|acting]] as educators, advocates, and coordinators between patients and wider health systems. [[concepts/community-health|Community health]] nurses, for example, conduct health assessments, deliver patient care, and manage care transitions.

### Emerging Modalities and Guidance

*   **Telehealth Integration**: Modern community care increasingly incorporates [[concepts/telehealth|telehealth]] to bridge geographical gaps and improve access for underserved populations.
*   **Service Delivery Frameworks**: Guidance from bodies like [[entities/safer-care-victoria|Safer Care Victoria]] (SCV) and the Department of Health and Human Services (DHHS) provides critical structure for health professionals regarding [[concepts/clinical-care|clinical]] service delivery via remote means [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-GUIDANCE-Allied-health-and-telehealth-Accessible|GUIDANCE Allied health and telehealth Accessible]].
*   **[[concepts/allied-health|Allied Health]] Roles**: There is a distinct focus on defining accessible roles for [[concepts/allied-health-professionals|allied health professionals]] within telehealth frameworks to ensure equitable health services distribution.
