# Medgemma 27b

MedGemma 27B is a 27 billion parameter large language model developed by Google specifically for medical applications. It is designed to process and comprehend both text and images within clinical and healthcare contexts, enabling it to handle multimodal tasks that involve medical documentation, diagnostic imagery, and health data analysis.

The model combines text and image understanding to support medical professionals in clinical workflows. It is trained to interpret medical images, process healthcare-related information, and analyze medical documentation, aiming to assist in tasks that require integrating visual and textual data for accurate clinical insights.
