# Cheap Flight Discovery

Cheap Flight Discovery is the practice of using [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools, particularly [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], to identify and locate inexpensive airline tickets. Rather than manually browsing multiple travel websites, users input specific travel parameters into ChatGPT prompts to receive targeted search strategies and [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]]. This approach leverages [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] to process travel preferences, budget constraints, and scheduling flexibility to generate customized flight-hunting guidance.

## How It Works

Users provide ChatGPT with travel details such as departure and destination cities, travel dates, budget limits, and scheduling flexibility. The [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-tool|AI tool]] then generates strategies for finding cheaper flights, which may include suggestions to fly on off-peak days, use flight comparison tools, set price alerts, or consider alternative airports. ChatGPT can also provide information about airline sales patterns, optimal booking [[entities/windows|windows]], and cost-saving techniques based on its [[concepts/language-data|training data]].

## Practical Applications

This approach is particularly useful for flexible travelers who can adjust their dates or routes to save money. Users might ask ChatGPT to explain which day of the week typically has cheaper fares, how to use hidden city ticketing responsibly, or how to combine different airlines for lower total costs. The tool can process multiple constraints simultaneously and provide a prioritized strategy rather than requiring users to research flight principles manually.

## Limitations

While ChatGPT can provide general guidance about flight [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] principles and search strategies, it cannot directly access real-time flight databases or booking systems. Users must ultimately perform searches on actual travel websites to [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] prices and complete bookings. Additionally, the AI's recommendations are based on general patterns rather than current market conditions.
