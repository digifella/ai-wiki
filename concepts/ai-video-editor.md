# AI Video Editor

Software utilizing [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] to automate, enhance, or redefine Video [[concepts/video-editing|Post-Production]] workflows. Enables semantic editing, generative augmentation, and non-linear manipulation driven by multimodal [[concepts/inference|inference]] rather than manual frame manipulation.

## Core Capabilities
* **[[concepts/generative-editing|Generative Editing]]:** Content-aware fill, [[concepts/object-removal|object removal]], [[concepts/style-transfer|style transfer]], and video-to-video transformation via diffusion or [[concepts/transformer-architectures|transformer models]].
* **Automated Assembly:** Script-to-video, auto-cutting based on speech/activity detection, and intelligent synchronization.
* **[[concepts/local-execution|Local Execution]]:** Growing class of tools supporting [[concepts/local-ai]] for data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], unlimited inference, and [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] marginal cost per edit.
* **Non-Linear Integration:** AI features embedded within traditional NLE timelines for hybrid workflows.

## Prominent Tools
* **[[concepts/ltx-desktop|LTX Desktop]]:**
    * First native, free, [[concepts/offline-ai|local AI]] Video Editor offering fully offline, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/non-linear-editing|non-linear editing]].
    * Utilizes [[entities/ltx-23|LTX 2.3]] multimodal [[concepts/engine|engine]] for integrated generative and editing capabilities.
    * Eliminates cloud dependency while providing professional-grade AI features.
