Search-friendly [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]] are visual content designed to include [[concepts/keywords|keywords]] and context that search engines can index, improving SEO visibility for the content they accompany.

* Piktochart's latest version enables creating search-friendly infographics, allowing [[concepts/google-search|Google]] to rank content for keywords embedded within the [[concepts/infographic|infographic]] (no design [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] required).
* For presentation integration, [[entities/chat4o|Chat4o]] and Piktochart can be combined to generate AI-powered [[entities/google-slides|slides]] with SEO-optimized visuals (see How to use [[entities/chat4o|Chat4o]] and piktochart to make great presentations).
* Key advantage: Keywords in infographics become discoverable via search, driving organic traffic without traditional text-heavy content.
