# Sky Masking

Sky masking is a selective editing technique in Adobe Lightroom that isolates the sky portion of an image to apply targeted adjustments without affecting other elements. By creating a mask around the sky, photographers can modify exposure, contrast, saturation, and color independently from the foreground and middle ground. This approach is particularly valuable in landscape photography, where skies often require different treatment than the land below to achieve balanced, visually compelling results.

## Challenges with Foreground Elements

A common difficulty in sky masking occurs when tree branches or other foreground objects intersect with the sky. Standard automatic selection tools may struggle to distinguish between the complex edges of leaves and the sky, often resulting in halo artifacts or incomplete masking. This issue is exacerbated in high-contrast scenes where the lighting difference between the sky and the foliage is minimal or inconsistent.

## Refinement Techniques

To address these challenges, users can employ the "Select Sky" feature followed by manual refinement using the brush tool. Adjusting the mask's feathering and density settings helps soften the transition between the masked sky and the foreground elements. Additionally, using the "Range Mask" feature to limit the selection based on luminance or color can improve accuracy, ensuring that only the intended sky areas are affected while preserving the integrity of the tree branches.
