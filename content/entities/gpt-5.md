# GPT-5

GPT-5 is the next-generation [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/openai]], succeeding [[entities/gpt-4]]. It demonstrates advanced capabilities in [[concepts/ai-coding]], [[concepts/interactive-simulation]], [[concepts/multimodal-understanding]], and complex [[concepts/reasoning]].

Key demonstrations (via [[entities/mathew-berman]]'s video, 2026-04-14: [GPT-5 Demonstration](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BUDmHYI6e3g) and [[entities/creator-magic]]'s video, 2026-04-14: [GPT5 vibe coding](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gAox5Srjkmc)):
- **Rubik's Cube [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] (0:26)**: Generated a fully interactive Rubik's Cube simulator using HTML, JavaScript, and [[concepts/threejs]], supporting [[concepts/dynamic-sizing|dynamic sizing]] (up to 20×20×20), [[concepts/color-coded-faces|color-coded faces]], camera controls, [[concepts/layer-rotation|layer rotation]], and a "Solve" button.
- **Real-time Hype Tracker (0:00-0:44)**: Live-coded a [[concepts/web-application|web application]] to track GPT-5 hype in real-time using the [[concepts/xai-api|XAI API]], demonstrating [[concepts/ai-coding]] capabilities and the emerging [[concepts/vibeops|VibeOps]] paradigm.
