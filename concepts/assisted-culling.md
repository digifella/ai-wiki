# Assisted Culling

Assisted Culling refers to a feature in software designed to help photographers more efficiently manage their photo collections. By utilizing advanced [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], the process of selecting [[concepts/high-quality-images|high-quality images]] from [[entities/big-data|large datasets]] becomes faster and easier.

## Overview
- Enhances [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]] by automating certain selection criteria.
- Improves accuracy of [[concepts/photo-organization-techniques|image organization]] through AI analysis.
- Supports [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes in complex photography projects.

### Features
- **Assisted Culling**: Helps users identify photos that should be kept or deleted based on [[concepts/image-input-processing|visual data analysis]]. AI-powered features analyze visual data to prioritize [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] shots.
- **Auto Stacking**: Automatically groups similar images together to reduce redundancy.
