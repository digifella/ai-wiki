# Verily

**Verily Life Sciences** (formerly [[concepts/google-search|Google]] Life Sciences) is a life sciences company founded by **Alphabet [[entities/inc|Inc.]]** focused on leveraging technology to improve [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] outcomes. It integrates digital tools, data [[concepts/science|science]], and clinical research to address systemic challenges in health.

## Key Initiatives & Publications
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future|The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future]] (2022): A Deloitte-published [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] analysis featuring insights from **Amy Abernethy**, MD, PhD (Verily) and **Laura Adams**, MS (National Academy of Medicine). The work examines the trajectory of **[[concepts/digital-health]]**, emphasizing progress in care tools and national health systems.
- Collaborative authors include **Meredith Barrett**, **[[entities/christine-bechtel|Christine Bechtel]]**, **X Health**, **Patricia Brennan**, **Atul Butte** ([[entities/san-francisco|San Francisco]]), **Judith Faulkner** (**[[entities/epic-systems|Epic Systems]]**), **Elaine Fontaine**, and the **National Academy**.
- Focus areas: Integration of **health** data, advancement of **medicine** through **digital** tools, and evaluation of care progress.

## Strategic Focus
- Development of AI-driven diagnostic tools and [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|predictive analytics]].
- Partnerships with [[concepts/health|healthcare]] providers like **Epic Systems** and academic institutions such as the **National Academy of Medicine**.
- Emphasis on [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] **digital-health** solutions to improve [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and efficacy in modern care.
