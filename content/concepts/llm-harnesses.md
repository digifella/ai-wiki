# LLM Harnesses

LLM Harnesses are systematic frameworks designed to optimize the performance and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of language [[concepts/models|models]] through autonomous architectural modifications. Rather than relying solely on manual tuning or static configurations, these harnesses enable models to iteratively improve their own operational [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and structural elements. This approach treats [[concepts/llm-conceptsalgorithmic-optimizationoptimization-techniques|LLM optimization]] as a dynamic process where the system can propose, test, and implement changes to enhance efficiency across various tasks.

## Architectural Modification and Self-Evolution

The core mechanism of LLM Harnesses involves enabling models to examine their own performance constraints and suggest improvements to their operational [[concepts/structure|structure]]. This includes modifications to how models process information, handle specific task types, and manage [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]]. By automating this [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loop|loop]], harnesses reduce the manual overhead traditionally required for [[concepts/llm-optimization|model optimization]] and allow for continuous performance refinement without human intervention for each adjustment.

## Practical Applications

LLM Harnesses have particular relevance for agent-based systems where autonomous [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and task execution are required. They enable [[concepts/agents|agents]] to adapt their internal processes—such as tool usage patterns, [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] strategies, and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] methods—in response to [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]]. Organizations implementing these frameworks have explored [[concepts/software|applications]] ranging from data [[concepts/scraping|scraping]] efficiency to subscription model optimization, with evidence suggesting that code-based approaches offer advantages over [[concepts/markdown|markdown]]-based alternatives for certain operational tasks.
