# Jalapeño

**[[concepts/hardware-architecture|Jalapeño]]** is the codename for [[entities/openai]]'s first custom-designed [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|Artificial Intelligence]] chip. This initiative represents a [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] toward proprietary hardware to optimize AI training and [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] workloads.

## Key Developments

- **First Benchmarks and Design**: Initial technical specifications and [[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|performance metrics]] have been released, highlighting the chip's architecture and efficiency gains over previous generations.
- **Strategic Implications**: The development of [[concepts/purpose-built-processor|Jalapeño]] underscores [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s move to reduce dependency on third-party GPU suppliers and tailor [[concepts/silicon|silicon]] specifically for [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] training.
- **[[concepts/technical-overview|Technical Overview]]**: Early reviews focus on the chip's [[concepts/minimalist-design|design philosophy]], [[concepts/energy-efficiency|power efficiency]], and computational throughput relative to industry standards.
