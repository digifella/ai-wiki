# Virologist

A scientist who studies viruses, their structure, classification, and effects on host cells. In clinical contexts, virologists may engage in virotherapy or immunotherapy research.

## Notable Case Studies

### Dr. Beata Halassy
*   Croatian virologist diagnosed with recurrent, aggressive breast cancer.
*   Pursued self-administered [[concepts/oncolytic-virotherapy]] after standard treatments (surgery, four rounds of chemotherapy) failed.
*   Documented in the case study: [[lab-notes/2026-09-16-Self-Administered-Oncolytic-Virotherapy-in-Recurrent-Bre|Self-Administered Oncolytic Virotherapy in Recurrent Breast Cancer: Dr. Halassy's Case]].
*   Case highlights the potential for virologists to apply their expertise to personal medical interventions, though such methods remain experimental and high-risk.
