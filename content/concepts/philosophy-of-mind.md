# Philosophy Of Mind

Philosophy of mind is the branch of [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] concerned with the [[entities/nature|nature]] of consciousness, mental phenomena, and their relationship to the physical world. Central to the discipline is the **mind-body problem**: the question of how mental states—such as thoughts, sensations, and emotions—relate to physical brain states and processes. This inquiry addresses fundamental questions about the nature of subjective experience, intentionality, and whether mental phenomena can be fully explained through physical processes.

## Major Positions

The field encompasses several competing [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]]. **Physicalism** holds that all mental phenomena ultimately reduce to or supervene upon physical brain processes. **Dualism** maintains that mind and body are fundamentally distinct substances or properties. **Functionalism** proposes that mental states are defined by their causal roles—their [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] to inputs, outputs, and other mental states—rather than by their physical substrate. Other approaches include **phenomenology**, which emphasizes the first-person [[concepts/structure|structure]] of conscious experience, and **emergentism**, which argues that mental properties arise from but are not reducible to [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]].

## Key Questions

Philosophers of mind investigate the **hard problem of consciousness**: why physical processes give rise to subjective experience [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] all. They also examine the nature of qualia (the intrinsic felt qualities of experiences), the basis of personal identity and [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of consciousness, and whether concepts like belief, desire, and intention can be rigorously defined within our understanding of [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]]. These inquiries have significant implications for [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], [[concepts/neuroscience|neuroscience]], and theories of human behavior.
