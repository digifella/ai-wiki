# Business Plan

A [[entities/business-plan|business plan]] is a strategic document that outlines an organization's objectives, strategies, and [[concepts/financial-projections|financial projections]]. It serves as a formal roadmap for establishing organizational direction, securing funding from investors or lenders, and measuring progress against defined goals. Business plans are used across organizations of all sizes, from startups seeking initial capital to established companies planning new initiatives or market expansions.

## Core Components

A comprehensive business plan typically includes several essential sections. These generally encompass an executive summary, company description, market analysis, organization and management structure, product or service line details, marketing and sales strategies, and financial projections. The executive summary provides a high-level overview of the entire document, while the market analysis validates the demand for the proposed [[concepts/solution|solution]]. Financial projections often include income statements, cash [[concepts/flow|flow]] statements, and balance sheets to demonstrate viability.

## Strategic Utility

Beyond securing financing, the business plan functions as an internal management tool. It helps founders clarify their [[concepts/business-model|value proposition]] and operational model, forcing a rigorous examination of assumptions and potential risks. By establishing clear milestones and key [[concepts/model-performance-metrics|performance indicators]], the document allows [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] to track execution against the original strategy. This alignment ensures that resources are allocated efficiently and that the organization remains focused on its long-term [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]].
