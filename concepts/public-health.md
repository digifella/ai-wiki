# Public Health

[[concepts/population-health|Public health]] is a field dedicated to improving the overall [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] of populations through various strategies such as education, [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]], research, and community development. It focuses on preventing disease and injury by addressing environmental hazards, reducing unhealthful behaviors, and teaching about hygiene practices.

## Key Points
- **Definition:** Public Health involves promoting and protecting health and preventing illness, disability, and death at the population level.
- **Role:** Improves [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] through [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] and control of infectious diseases; reduction of [[concepts/environmental-risks|environmental risks]]; education on healthy lifestyles; ensuring a safe food supply; monitoring [[concepts/disease-outbreaks|disease outbreaks]]; and advocating for [[concepts/policies|policies]] to improve public welfare.
- **Data Utilization:** Leverages [[concepts/secondary-use|secondary use]] of [[concepts/electronic-health-records|electronic health records]], such as the [[entities/my-health-record|My Health Record]], for research and [[concepts/healthcare-improvement|healthcare improvement]] to optimize outcomes, subject to data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] standards and [[concepts/ethics|ethics approval]] frameworks. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-ADHA---How-secondary-use-of-My-Health-Record-data-can-im|ADHA - How secondary use of My Health Record data can improve health outcomes]].
