# Global connectivity

The seamless interconnection of people, systems, and information across geographical boundaries through technological infrastructure. Enables real-time communication, economic [[concepts/integration|integration]], and [[concepts/cultural-exchange|cultural exchange]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] planetary scale.

## Key Drivers
- **[[concepts/satellite-internet|Satellite internet]] networks** like Starlink democratizing access in remote regions, reducing digital divide
- Internet infrastructure evolution toward resilient, low-latency global networks
- Societal transformation through unprecedented information [[concepts/flow|flow]] and participation

## Starlink Impact (2026)
- Validating Musk's claims: Starlink becoming planetary default [[concepts/internet-layer|internet layer]] through rapid [[concepts/deployment|deployment]]
- Enabling [[concepts/information-access|information access]] in [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] zones and rural areas previously offline
- Accelerating digital inclusion through affordable, high-bandwidth connectivity
