# Just Booked It

A method for leveraging [[entities/notebooklm]], [[entities/gemini]], and [[tools/google-my-maps]] to transform post-flight booking [[concepts/travel-planning|travel planning]] from overwhelming research into interactive, visual, and shareable experiences.

- **Core Problem**:
  - Booking flights is exciting, but detailed planning (exploring towns, finding restaurants, checking schedules) becomes overwhelming.
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]] Workflow**:
  - Use [[entities/notebooklm]] to curate trusted travel research (guidebooks, articles) into a structured [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]].
  - Query [[entities/notebooklm]] for specific needs (e.g., "top sushi restaurants in Kyoto").
  - Use [[entities/gemini]] to enhance answers, generate personalized itineraries, and create shareable summaries.
  - Integrate [[tools/google-my-maps]] to automate the creation of visual, interactive maps from AI-generated itineraries.
- **Key Benefit**:
  - Turns fragmented research into actionable, interactive, and spatially-mapped trip plans without manual organization.
