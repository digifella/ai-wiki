# General Purpose LLMs

General [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] are AI systems designed to handle a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of tasks across multiple domains, from natural language understanding to [[concepts/coding|coding]] and technical [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. Unlike [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] built for single [[concepts/software|applications]], these systems aim for versatility by [[concepts/training|training]] on diverse datasets that enable them to perform competently across different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. This broad capability makes them suitable for both conversational applications and technical tasks.

## Coding and Development Applications

A growing category of general [[concepts/purpose|purpose]] LLMs includes [[concepts/models|models]] with enhanced coding [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], such as [[concepts/qwen-code|Qwen Coder]], which demonstrate competitive performance on programming tasks traditionally served by commercial AI services. These models can generate [[concepts/code|code]], debug applications, and assist with software [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]]. The availability of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] coding-capable models has created alternative options to proprietary commercial solutions, allowing organizations and developers to run [[concepts/inference|inference]] locally and avoid subscription costs.

## Deployment and Accessibility

General purpose LLMs vary significantly in size and resource requirements, ranging from smaller language models ([[concepts/slms|SLMs]]) operating efficiently on 4GB systems to larger models requiring substantial computational infrastructure. The choice between [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] and [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]] depends on specific use case requirements, latency constraints, and [[concepts/cost|cost]] considerations. Open-source implementations and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] frameworks have reduced barriers to [[concepts/customization|customization]], enabling users to adapt [[concepts/general-purpose-models|general purpose models]] to specific domain needs using custom datasets.
