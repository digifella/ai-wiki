# Docling

Docling is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] toolkit developed by [[entities/ibm-research|IBM Research]] for processing documents within [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] workflows. The [[concepts/software|software]] is designed to facilitate the extraction and transformation of document content into formats suitable for downstream [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]], particularly in [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval augmented generation]] (RAG) systems where document understanding is critical for generating accurate [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Core Functionality

The toolkit addresses the challenge of converting various document formats into structured, machine-readable representations. This process is essential for AI systems that need to ingest, index, and retrieve information from documents as part of their operation. By providing standardized [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] capabilities, Docling aims to reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]] in integrating document understanding into AI pipelines.

## Use Cases

Docling is particularly relevant for organizations building [[concepts/contextualized-language-understanding|RAG systems]], where pre-processing of source documents directly impacts the quality of AI-generated outputs. The open-source [[entities/nature|nature]] of the project makes it accessible to researchers and developers working on document understanding and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] tasks.
