# Privacy Principles

Core ethical and legal frameworks governing the collection, processing, and [[entities/storage|storage]] of personal data. These principles balance individual rights with organizational needs, often codified in regulations like [[concepts/gdpr]], [[concepts/hipaa]], and [[concepts/ccpa]].

## Key Frameworks & Concepts

- **Data Minimization**: Collect only data strictly necessary for the specified purpose.
- **Purpose Limitation**: Data used only for the purpose explicitly stated at collection.
- **Individual Rights**: [[concepts/opacity|Transparency]], access, correction, and deletion (Right to be Forgotten).
- **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]]**: Organizations must demonstrate [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] laws.
