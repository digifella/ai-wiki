## Pi - Digits and Measurement

Pi (π) is an [[concepts/irrational_number|irrational number]], essential in [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] and [[concepts/physics|physics]] for its role in calculations involving circles and spheres. The concept of [[entities/digit|digit]] [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] becomes particularly interesting when applied to π.

### Key Points
- Only [[concepts/only-39-digits-of-pi-are-necessary-to-measure-the-circumference-of-the|39 digits of Pi]] are necessary to measure the circumference of the [[concepts/observable-universe|observable universe]] with accuracy down to the width of a [[concepts/hydrogen-atom|hydrogen atom]].
- Calculating trillions of [[concepts/decimal-places|decimal places]] serves modern [[concepts/computational-testing|computational testing]], [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]], and understanding the nature of numbers.
