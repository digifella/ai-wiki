# ChatGPT Superpower

ChatGPT Superpower refers to a framework presented by [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]] in a 2026 video that identifies the specific [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] sufficient for accomplishing the majority of professional work. Rather than promoting [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of numerous specialized AI platforms, Su demonstrates that a focused toolkit—centered around a core set of [[concepts/complementary-tools|complementary tools]]—can address most practical needs in productivity, [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]].

## Core Approach

Su's methodology emphasizes depth over breadth, arguing that mastering a small number of well-integrated AI tools proves more effective than superficially exploring dozens of alternatives. This approach reduces context-switching, creates stable workflows, and allows for deeper optimization of individual tools. The framework categorizes these tools by function, with [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] serving as a central component alongside [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]-specific [[concepts/software|applications]] for tasks like content generation, analysis, and [[concepts/automation|automation]].

## Practical Application

The toolset Su recommends is presented in accessible, actionable terms designed for professionals seeking to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] without investment in expensive or redundant software. By focusing on tools that integrate well together and cover the essential workflows of knowledge work, this framework provides a replicable model for other users to [[concepts/structure|structure]] their own AI-assisted processes effectively.
