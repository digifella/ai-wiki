# Text To Speech Generation

Text-to-speech (TTS) generation is an artificial intelligence capability that converts written text into spoken audio output. Modern TTS systems utilize neural networks to synthesize natural-sounding speech, marking a significant advancement over earlier rule-based and concatenative synthesis methods. These systems typically process input text through linguistic analysis, acoustic modeling, and vocoding stages to produce intelligible and contextually appropriate audio.

The Qwen3-TTS family of models expands this domain by integrating advanced features such as voice design and voice cloning. Voice design allows for the creation of specific vocal characteristics, while voice cloning enables the replication of existing speech patterns. These capabilities are built upon the foundational text-to-speech generation technology, allowing for greater customization and realism in the synthesized output.

By combining standard TTS generation with these specialized features, the Qwen3-TTS family addresses the need for both high-fidelity speech synthesis and personalized voice profiles. This integration supports a wider range of applications where specific vocal identities or custom voice attributes are required, moving beyond generic speech synthesis to more tailored audio generation solutions.
