# Opal Labs

Opal Labs is an experimental no-code artificial intelligence tool developed by Google Labs. It is designed to enable users to create and share AI mini-applications without requiring programming knowledge. The platform aims to lower the barrier to entry for AI development by allowing individuals to build functional tools through natural language descriptions of their requirements.

The core functionality of Opal Labs relies on translating user intent into working applications. Instead of writing traditional code, users construct AI-powered applications by describing their needs in plain language. This approach democratizes AI tool creation, making it accessible to a broader audience who may not have technical coding skills.
