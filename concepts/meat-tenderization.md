# Meat Tenderization

Meat tenderization encompasses techniques designed to improve the texture, moisture retention, and cooking properties of meat. These methods function by breaking down tough muscle fibers and connective tissue, resulting in meat that is easier to chew and more palatable. The process can be achieved through mechanical means, enzymatic processes, or chemical treatments.

## Baking Soda Method

Baking soda serves as an effective and accessible tenderizing agent, particularly for ground meat. When applied to ground meat prior to cooking, baking soda raises the pH of the meat's surface. This alkaline environment allows muscle fibers to relax, which enhances the meat's ability to retain moisture and improves its browning capacity during the cooking process.
