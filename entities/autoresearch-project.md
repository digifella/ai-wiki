# Autoresearch Project

The Autoresearch Project is a research initiative focused on developing and studying self-evolving AI systems capable of autonomously improving their own performance. The core concept explores AI agents that modify their own computational processes—referred to as "harnesses"—without requiring external human intervention. This approach investigates a form of recursive self-improvement where systems can identify performance bottlenecks and iteratively refine their own operational parameters.

## Methodology and Focus

The project centers on automated experimental evaluation, utilizing iterative harness modification to optimize system efficiency. By allowing AI agents to autonomously adjust their underlying structures, the initiative aims to create feedback loops that drive continuous performance gains. This methodology seeks to reduce reliance on manual engineering for routine optimization tasks, enabling systems to adapt to new challenges through self-directed learning and structural adjustment.
