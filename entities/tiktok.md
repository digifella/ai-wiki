# TikTok

TikTok is a short-form video social media platform where users create, share, and discover video content typically lasting between 15 seconds and 10 minutes. Launched internationally in 2018, the platform evolved from Douyin, a Chinese video application developed by ByteDance. TikTok rapidly expanded to become one of the world's most widely used social media applications, with hundreds of millions of monthly active users globally.

## Content and Algorithm

The platform hosts primarily user-generated content spanning entertainment, education, music, comedy, and lifestyle categories. TikTok's recommendation system uses machine learning algorithms to display content on users' "For You" feeds based on viewing behavior, engagement patterns, and video metadata rather than follower networks. This algorithmic approach differs from chronological feeds and has contributed significantly to the platform's engagement rates and content discovery model.

## Business Model and Regulation

TikTok generates revenue primarily through advertising and in-app features such as virtual gifts and premium subscriptions. The platform has faced regulatory scrutiny in multiple countries regarding data privacy, content moderation, and national security concerns, particularly regarding its Chinese parent company's data handling practices. Various governments have proposed or implemented restrictions on the platform's operations within their jurisdictions.
