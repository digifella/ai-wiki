The convergence of digital technologies and [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]], aiming to enhance outcomes, efficiency, and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

## Core Concepts
- **[[concepts/digital-innovations|Digital Transformation]] in Medicine**: The integration of tools like [[concepts/electronic-health-records]], AI in Diagnostics, and [[concepts/telehealth|Telemedicine]] into standard care pathways.
- **Data-Driven Insights**: Leveraging [[concepts/big-data|big data]] for [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|predictive analytics]] and [[concepts/personalized-medicine|personalized medicine]].
- **Patient Empowerment**: Shifting from paternalistic models to [[concepts/personalised-care|patient-centered care]] through accessible digital interfaces.

## Key Developments & Literature
- **2022 Review**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future|The-Promise-of-Digital-Health-Then-Now-and-the-Future]] outlines the trajectory from early EHR [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] to current AI-integrated systems.
	- **Authors**: Amy Abernethy ([[entities/verily|Verily]]), Laura Adams (NAM), Atul Butte ([[entities/epic-systems|Epic Systems]]), and others.
	- **Publisher**: Deloitte / National Academy of Medicine.
	- **Key Findings**:
		- Progress in digital tools has been uneven, with significant gaps in interoperability.
		- "Then, Now, and Future" framework highlights the shift from digitization (scanning records) to digitalization ([[concepts/workflow-enhancements|process optimization]]) to digital transformation (ecosystem change).
		- Critical need for addressing equity in digital access to prevent widening [[concepts/health|health]] disparities.

## Challenges
- **Interoperability**: Fragmented systems hinder seamless data exchange between providers.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] & [[concepts/security|Security]]**: Balancing data utility with patient [[concepts/secure|protection]] regulations (e.g., [[concepts/hipaa]]).
- **[[concepts/digital-divide|Digital Divide]]**: Socioeconomic factors limiting access to [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] resources.
