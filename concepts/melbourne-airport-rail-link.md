# Melbourne Airport Rail Link

The **[[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] Airport Rail Link** is a proposed rail infrastructure project aimed at providing a direct passenger rail [[concepts/connection|connection]] between Melbourne's central business district and [[entities/melbourne-airport|Melbourne Airport]]. As of 2026, Melbourne remains an outlier among major Australian capital cities for lacking such a direct link, despite the airport opening 56 years prior.
