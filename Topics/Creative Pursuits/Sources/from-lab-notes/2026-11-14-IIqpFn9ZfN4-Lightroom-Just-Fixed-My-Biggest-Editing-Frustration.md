### Summary
The video covers the new features in Lightroom Classic 16.0, focusing on how they solve common editing frustrations, particularly for wildlife photography. Key points discussed include color grading inside masks, a new depth mask tool for selecting in-focus areas, a glow effect for softening light sources, custom masking panel order, and the focus point visual tool. The speaker emphasizes that these tools offer more precise control and efficiency in editing. The conclusion is that while these updates provide significant improvements, mastering the tools is only part of the process, and knowing the right editing workflow is crucial for the best results.
