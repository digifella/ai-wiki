# Gemini 2.5 Pro

[[concepts/gemini-25-pro|Gemini 2.5 Pro]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] as part of the [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] family of AI systems. The model is positioned as a mid-tier offering within the Gemini product line, designed to balance capability and efficiency across a [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]] tasks.

## Deep Think Capabilities

A defining feature of Gemini 2.5 Pro is its [[concepts/deep-think-mode|Deep Think]] functionality, which enables extended [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] over complex problems. This capability allows the model to allocate additional [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] steps, potentially improving [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and coherence on tasks that benefit from deliberate, multi-step analysis.
