# Combining Photos

Combining photos is a creative technique where multiple [[concepts/images|images]] are merged or layered together to create a single composite image. This can involve placing photographs side by side, overlaying them, or blending them seamlessly. The practice spans both traditional darkroom methods and modern digital approaches, allowing creators to tell more complex visual stories or achieve effects that would be difficult or impossible to capture in a single shot.

## Digital Methods

Digital photo combining is typically accomplished through image editing [[concepts/software|software]] such as [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], GIMP, or Lightroom. These tools offer various blending modes, [[concepts/layer-masks|layer masks]], and alignment features that facilitate combining images with precision. Common applications include creating panoramic images by stitching multiple overlapping photos together, compositing elements from different photos into a unified scene, or layering images to produce artistic effects like multiple exposures.

## Creative Applications

Photo combining serves diverse purposes across photography and [[concepts/visual-arts|visual arts]]. Travel photographers often stitch images to capture expansive landscapes, while portrait photographers may combine multiple shots to achieve perfect expressions across subjects. In conceptual and fine art photography, combining images enables artists to visualize composite narratives or surreal [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. The technique also has practical applications in documentation, product photography, and creating visual guides.
