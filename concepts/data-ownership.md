# Data Ownership

Control and authority over personal or organizational data, including rights to access, modify, share, and monetize it. Historically dominated by [[concepts/corporate-platforms|digital platforms]] through [[concepts/informational-asymmetry]], where platforms held all data while presenting users with filtered views.

## Key Developments
- **[[concepts/informational-asymmetry|Informational asymmetry]]**: Platforms have historically hoarded user data while showing only a curated view designed to serve platform interests (e.g., targeted advertising).
- **Power shift**: Late 2025/2026 marks a pivotal transition toward user-centric [[concepts/data-sovereignty|data control]], enabled by [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] that allow individuals to reclaim and analyze their own data.
- **AI-driven reclamation**: AI empowers users to decode platform data patterns, breaking informational asymmetry and enabling transparent data utilization.
- **Ecosystem impact**: This shift challenges traditional platform business models and fosters equitable [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]] where users regain agency over their digital footprint.
