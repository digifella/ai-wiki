# Particle Detection

Particle detection refers to the scientific methods and instruments used to identify and measure [[concepts/elementary-particles|elementary particles]] and their properties. These detectors are essential tools in experimental [[concepts/physics|physics]], allowing researchers to observe particles that cannot be seen directly by confirming their presence through the interactions they produce with detector materials.

## WIMP Detection

One significant application of particle detection is the search for [[concepts/wimp-particles|Weakly Interacting Massive Particles]] (WIMPs), hypothetical particles that may constitute [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]]. The [[entities/saber-detector|SABER detector]], located in the [[entities/stawell-gold-mine|Stawell gold mine]] in Victoria, Australia, is employed for this [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]. The detector operates in collaboration with the [[entities/perimeter-institute|Perimeter Institute]] and Italian research partners, leveraging the mine's depth to shield experiments from cosmic ray interference. This international cooperation aims to identify WIMPs through their rare interactions with atomic nuclei.
