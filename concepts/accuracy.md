# accuracy

---
type: concept
---

Accuracy measures the degree to which a system's output matches the true or desired value. In AI and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], it is a critical metric for evaluating [[concepts/vllm|model performance]] against ground truth data.

- **[[concepts/speech-recognition|Speech recognition]] applications**: [[concepts/whisper-ai]] achieves high transcription accuracy, outperforming built-in tools like [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] captions. As demonstrated in 2026 04 14 [[entities/elle-wang|Elle wang]] [[concepts/audio-to-text-transcription|audio to text transcription]], it offers:
  - Superior accuracy compared to alternative captioning systems
  - Free usage with no downloads required
  - Implementation via [[entities/google-colab|Google Colab]] for accessible deployment
  - Reliable results for audio-to-text conversion
