# Gemini 3 Pro

[[entities/google|Google]]'s premium model in the [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] series, designed for complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]] requiring [[entities/high-performance|high performance]]. Positioned as the flagship for enterprise and [[concepts/developer|developer]] workloads.

## Key Features
- **Performance**: Outperforms [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Pro in [[concepts/coding|coding]] and agentic benchmarks while maintaining efficient [[concepts/inference|inference]]
- **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]**: Optimized for multi-step [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] with enhanced context handling
- **[[concepts/use-cases|Use Cases]]**: Enterprise [[concepts/software|applications]], complex [[concepts/ai-coding|code generation]], and advanced [[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/workflow|workflows]]
