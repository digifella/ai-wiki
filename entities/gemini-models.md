# Gemini Models

[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] is a family of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]], designed to perform a wide range of [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]] tasks. The models are available in multiple sizes and capability tiers, allowing developers and organizations to select versions appropriate for their specific [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] and application requirements.

## Model Architecture and Capabilities

[[concepts/gemini-models|Gemini models]] support multiple modalities including text, image, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and video understanding. This multimodal capability enables the models to process and analyze information across different formats, making them suitable for diverse applications. The family includes several variants, such as [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini Pro]] and [[entities/gemini-25-pro|Gemini Ultra]], which differ in complexity and performance characteristics.

## Availability and Access

Google has integrated Gemini models into its products and made them available to developers through [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] and the [[concepts/gemini-api|Gemini API]]. The models are accessible via Google's [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud services]], allowing developers to build applications that leverage these language models. Additionally, Google has released related [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tools and frameworks for [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]], enabling broader [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and [[concepts/customization|customization]].

## Use Cases

Gemini models are applied across various domains including content generation, [[concepts/fact-based-queries|question answering]], [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], and [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]]. Organizations use these models to automate text-based tasks, enhance search capabilities, and build [[concepts/ai-application|intelligent applications]]. The tiered approach to model sizes allows for both resource-constrained deployments and [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] applications requiring advanced capabilities.
