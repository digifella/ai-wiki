# Yann LeCun

## Profile
Chief AI Scientist at [[entities/meta]] and Professor at New York University. A foundational figure in Deep [[concepts/learning|Learning]] and Convolutional [[concepts/neural-networks|Neural Networks]].

## Research Philosophy
- **[[concepts/world-models]]**: Advocates for the development of architectures that can predict and model the physical world.
- **Anti-LLM Paradigm**: Maintains that "Language is not Intelligence"; argues that [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) lack the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] capabilities required for [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|AGI]].
- **Self-Supervised [[concepts/learning|Learning]]**: Focuses on enabling models to learn directly from sensory input without massive human-labeled [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].

## Key Research & Architectures
- **JEPA (Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]])**: A non-generative approach to [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]].
- **[[concepts/vl-jepa]]**: A recent advancement in [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]]-language modeling representing a [[concepts/strategic-shift|strategic shift]] away from [[concepts/generative-ai]] architectures toward a vision-centric approach to AGI.
- **[[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] [[entities/meta-fair-lab|FAIR Lab]]**: Primary laboratory for research into non-generative, predictive [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] architectures.
