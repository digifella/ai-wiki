# Custom Instructions

Custom [[concepts/instructions|Instructions]] are persistent configuration settings in [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] that allow users to specify how the AI should behave across all conversations. Rather than repeating context, preferences, or role information with each new chat, users define these details once in their account settings, and ChatGPT applies them automatically to subsequent interactions. This reduces redundancy and helps maintain consistent behavior and response [[concepts/style|style]] throughout multiple conversations.

## Purpose and Use Cases

Custom Instructions serve to establish context about the user's needs, communication preferences, and intended [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. Users might specify their profession, the type of [[concepts/responses|responses]] they prefer (formal or conversational), specific goals they're working toward, or constraints to follow. For example, a [[concepts/software|software]] engineer might instruct ChatGPT to assume technical knowledge and provide [[concepts/code|code]] examples, while a marketing professional might request responses focused on campaign strategy and metrics.

## Relationship to Other Features

Custom Instructions work alongside other ChatGPT features like Projects, which provide dedicated workspaces for organizing related conversations around specific ongoing tasks such as client work or creative projects. While Custom Instructions establish baseline behavior across the entire account, Projects allow users to group conversations and maintain additional context for particular initiatives. Together, these features create a more personalized and organized [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] experience.
