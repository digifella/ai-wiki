# Art

Art encompasses creative human expression across visual, auditory, literary, and performative forms. It serves both aesthetic and communicative purposes, reflecting cultural values, individual perspectives, and historical contexts. The definition and boundaries of what constitutes art remain subject to ongoing philosophical and cultural debate, with different cultures and historical periods establishing distinct criteria for artistic legitimacy and value.

## Art and Science

The relationship between art and science has historically been treated as separate intellectual domains, though this distinction has increasingly blurred in contemporary practice. Recent dialogues, such as those involving Emily Pulse-It and WEHI, highlight the intersection of these fields, exploring how artistic methodologies can inform scientific inquiry and vice versa. This convergence emphasizes the role of creativity in scientific communication and the application of scientific principles to artistic creation, fostering a multidisciplinary approach to understanding complex phenomena.
