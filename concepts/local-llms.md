# Local LLMs

**Local LLMs** refer to [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] that are run locally on personal hardware rather than via cloud APIs. This approach emphasizes data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], offline capability, and the democratization of powerful AI tools.

## Key Developments & Projects

Recent trends highlight a shift toward open-source ecosystems that enhance [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]] capabilities, including management tools, [[concepts/ai-native-communication|AI-native communication]], and [[concepts/3d-modeling|3D modeling]] integration.

- **Open-Source Ecosystem**: Focus on democratizing access to powerful AI tools through community-driven development [[concepts/open-source-ai]].
- **Local Management**: Tools designed to streamline the deployment and management of Local LLMs on [[concepts/consumer-hardware|consumer hardware]].
- **AI Agents**: Integration of autonomous agents that can operate within local environments.
- **Multimodal Capabilities**: Expansion beyond text to include AI-native communication and [[concepts/3d-reconstruction|3D modeling]] workflows.
