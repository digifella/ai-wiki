## Generative Engine Optimization: Adapting Websites for AI Search Using
[[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]]
**Clip title:** SEO Is Dead. [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]] Just Built Its Replacement (It's
FREE)
**Author / channel:** [[entities/zubair-trabzada|Zubair Trabzada]] | AI Workshop
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=46vC7-BwJ_o

### Summary
This video introduces [[concepts/generative-engine-optimization|Generative Engine Optimization]] ([[concepts/geo|GEO]]), positioning it
as the future of SEO in response to the rise of [[concepts/ai-powered-search|AI-powered search]] engines.
The presenter highlights that traditional search engine [[concepts/optimization|optimization]] is
rapidly becoming obsolete as platforms like Google [[concepts/ai-overviews|AI Overviews]], [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]],
Perplexity, [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], and Bing [[entities/copilot|Copilot]] increasingly provide direct,
[[concepts/ai-generated-answers|AI-generated answers]] to user queries. This shift means users are less
likely to click on [[concepts/organic-search-results|organic search results]], causing websites to become
"invisible" to a growing segment of online traffic. The video emphasizes
the urgency of this trend, citing a projected 500% year-over-year growth in
[[concepts/ai-search|AI search]] traffic and a potential 50% drop in traditional search traffic by
2028, making it crucial for businesses to adapt their digital strategies.

To address this challenge, the presenter unveils a specialized tool called
"[[concepts/geo|GEO]] SEO," built using [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]]. This tool performs a comprehensive
Generative Engine [[concepts/optimization|Optimization]] audit on any website, designed to assess its
readiness and visibility for AI search. The audit covers critical
dimensions such as [AI citability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/AI_Citability) (how likely content is to be cited by AI
systems), [brand authority](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Brand_Authority) signals, content quality (E-E-A-T), technical
foundations, [[concepts/structured-data|structured data]], and platform optimization across various [[concepts/ai-search-engines|AI search engines]]. The output is a detailed PDF report that identifies
existing problems, missing elements, and offers a prioritized action plan
to improve AI search visibility.

The "[[concepts/zero-click-search|GEO]] SEO" tool leverages [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]]'s "skills," which act as
specialized [[concepts/plugins|plugins]], enabling the AI to become an expert in specific audit
areas. The audit process involves three main phases: initial discovery to
fetch homepage data and detect business type, followed by parallel subagent
delegation where multiple [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] simultaneously analyze various aspects
of the website, and finally, score aggregation and report generation. The
resulting report not only provides an overall GEO score but also breaks
down performance by category and individual AI platforms, presenting
actionable recommendations categorized as "Quick Wins," "Medium-Term
Improvements," and "Strategic Initiatives" for immediate and long-term
impact.

The video concludes by underscoring the immense market opportunity within
GEO services, projected to reach over $7.3 billion by 2031, with currently
only a small fraction of marketers engaged in this area. For agencies, the
"GEO SEO" tool offers a significant competitive advantage by automating
comprehensive audits that would typically take days or weeks, generating
professional reports in minutes. This allows agencies to deliver immediate,
high-value insights to potential clients, demonstrate their expertise in
the evolving [[concepts/ai-landscape|AI landscape]], and effectively monetize these services by
helping businesses optimize their online presence for the future of search.
