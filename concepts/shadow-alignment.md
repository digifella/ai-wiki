# Shadow Alignment

Shadow Alignment is an artificial intelligence-assisted feature within Adobe Photoshop designed to maintain visual consistency in composite images. When users modify image elements, such as repositioning objects or merging multiple layers, the tool automatically analyzes existing shadow patterns to determine the direction, intensity, and softness of the light source. It then applies corresponding adjustments to new or altered areas, ensuring that shadows align logically with the scene's lighting environment.

The underlying technology utilizes machine learning algorithms to detect and interpret complex lighting conditions. By understanding the relationship between objects and their shadows, the feature reduces the manual effort traditionally required to achieve realistic lighting continuity. This automation allows for more efficient workflow management, particularly in scenarios involving intricate compositing where manual shadow painting would be time-consuming and prone to inconsistency.

This functionality addresses common challenges in digital image editing where disparate elements often appear disconnected due to mismatched lighting. By dynamically adjusting shadows in real-time or upon request, Shadow Alignment helps create more cohesive and believable visual compositions. The feature is part of Adobe's broader suite of AI-powered tools aimed at streamlining image manipulation processes while preserving artistic intent and technical accuracy.
