# Cloud AI

[[concepts/cloud-based-ai-services|Cloud AI]] refers to the deployment and execution of large-scale [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models and computational workloads on centralized, remote server clusters.

## Key Dynamics
- **Economic Constraints:** The rising complexity of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] is driving significant-pressure on [[concepts/cloud-economics|Cloud Economics]], incentivizing a shift away from purely centralized processing.
- **Transition to Localized Intelligence:** There is an emerging strategic [[concepts/exercise|movement]] from cloud-dependency toward [[concepts/on-device-ai]] and [[entities/bitnet|Edge AI]] to mitigate latency and [[concepts/operational-costs|operational costs]].
- **[[concepts/hardware-centric-strategy|Hardware-Centric Strategy]]:** Recent [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] shifts at [[entities/apple]]—specifically the appointment of [[entities/john-ternus|John Ternus]] as CEO and [[entities/johny-srouji|Johny Srouji]] as Chief Hardware Officer—signal a focus on optimizing [[concepts/silicon|silicon]] for [[concepts/local-ai-processing|local AI execution]] to reduce reliance on [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud infrastructure]].
