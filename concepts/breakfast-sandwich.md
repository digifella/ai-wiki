# Breakfast Sandwich

A breakfast sandwich is a prepared food item consisting of fillings placed between slices of bread, typically served as a morning meal. The sandwich format provides convenient assembly and portability, making it a practical choice for breakfast across many cultures. Common fillings include proteins such as eggs, bacon, sausage, or ham, frequently combined with cheese and vegetables like tomatoes, lettuce, or onions. The bread base may range from standard sliced bread to English muffins, bagels, croissants, or flatbreads depending on regional preferences and availability.

## Preparation and Serving

Breakfast sandwiches are prepared in both domestic kitchens and commercial food establishments. The simplicity of preparation and ease of consumption have made them a staple option for quick morning meals. Many variations exist, with ingredients typically cooked separately before assembly. Fast food chains and casual restaurants have standardized numerous breakfast sandwich formats, contributing to their widespread availability and consistency across different locations.

## Cultural Variations

Different regions have developed distinctive breakfast sandwich traditions. Korean street toast represents a notable regional variation, featuring customized fillings and preparation methods adapted to local tastes. Similar sandwich-based breakfast foods exist across various cuisines, each reflecting local ingredients and culinary preferences while maintaining the core concept of portable, assembled breakfast food.
