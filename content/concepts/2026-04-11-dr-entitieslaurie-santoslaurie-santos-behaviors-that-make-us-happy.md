# 2026 04 11 Dr Laurie Santos: Behaviors That Make Us Happy

## Overview

This presentation by Dr. Laurie Santos examines behavioral habits and healthy practices that contribute to psychological well-being in adolescents. Drawing on research from behavioral science and psychology, Santos identifies specific behaviors that individuals can adopt to improve their mental health and life satisfaction during the teenage years.

## Key Focus Areas

The research addresses the gap between what scientific evidence shows promotes happiness and what many young people actually practice. Santos emphasizes evidence-based habits rather than intuitive assumptions, examining how behavioral changes—from sleep patterns to social connection to physical activity—correlate with measurable improvements in adolescent well-being.

## Relevance to Youth Development

Understanding which behaviors reliably contribute to happiness is particularly important for adolescents, a developmental period marked by significant physical, social, and emotional change. The work aims to translate findings from behavioral science into actionable guidance that teenagers and those supporting them can implement in everyday life.
