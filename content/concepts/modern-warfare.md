# Modern warfare

[[concepts/slms|Definition]] of contemporary conflict characterized by the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of kinetic and non-kinetic domains, high-technology systems, and the exploitation of global interdependencies.

## Key Strategic Elements & Lessons
- **AD ([[concepts/a2ad|Anti-Access/Area Denial]]):** Implementation of strategies to restrict adversary [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and access within contested theaters.
- **Economic Warfare:** The use of [[concepts/trade-disruption]] and maritime Blockades to exert global pressure.
- **Hardened Assets & Delivery Systems:** The critical interplay between [[concepts/ballistic-missiles]] and the use of reinforced [[concepts/bunkers]] for survivability.
- **Theater Volatility:** Observations from recent hostilities involving [[concepts/iran]], [[entities/israel|Israel]], and the [[entities/united-states]].
