# AI Adoption Levels

A framework describing the progressive stages of integrating AI tools into workflows, ranging from basic gated access to autonomous "God Mode" productivity.

## Core Framework: Claude Code Adoption Levels

Developed by [[entities/boris-cherny|Boris Cherny]], creator of [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]], this model outlines the "Steps of AI Adoption" for users and organizations. It details how to ascend through proficiency levels to maximize utility.

- **Gated Access**: Initial entry points where usage is restricted or requires specific permissions.
- **Progressive Unlocking**: Moving through tiers of capability as trust and proficiency increase.
- **[[concepts/god-mode-productivity|God Mode Productivity]]**: The apex state of autonomous, high-[[concepts/velocity|velocity]] AI-assisted development.

## Key Concepts

- **Creator Insight**: The framework is explicitly designed to help users understand the mechanics of leveling up their interaction with AI agents.
- **Organizational Scaling**: The levels apply not just to individual skill but to how teams integrate these tools into broader workflows.
- **Productivity Arc**: The goal is to move from manual oversight to automated, high-leverage output.
