# Structured Decision

**Structured Decision** refers to decision-making processes that rely on explicit frameworks, algorithms, or systematic steps to reduce ambiguity and cognitive bias. In the context of modern AI, this concept is increasingly associated with "[[concepts/system-one-intelligence|System One]]" models designed for speed and efficiency.

## Core Characteristics
- **Speed:** Prioritizes rapid [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] and low-latency responses.
- **Efficiency:** Optimized for cost-effective operation, often using smaller or specialized models.
- **Determinism:** Relies on clear inputs and predictable outputs to ensure reliability.
- **Automation:** Suitable for high-volume, repetitive decision tasks where human oversight is minimal.
