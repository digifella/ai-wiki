# Model Catalog

A Model Catalog serves as a centralized registry for documenting, organizing, and managing artificial intelligence models, agents, and their associated configurations within an organization or platform. It functions as a discoverable inventory system that tracks deployed models, version histories, dependencies, and relevant metadata. By maintaining a single source of truth for model information, the catalog enables technical teams to identify existing AI assets, monitor their current operational state, and understand their integration across various applications and systems.

The platform provides several core operational functions, primarily focusing on discoverability and governance. It allows users to search for specific models or agents based on criteria such as capability, performance metrics, or licensing terms. This structured approach reduces redundancy by preventing the re-creation of existing assets and ensures that teams can leverage proven solutions rather than building from scratch. Additionally, it supports lifecycle management by tracking model updates and deprecations, ensuring that downstream applications utilize supported and secure versions.

In the context of Microsoft Foundry, the Model Catalog acts as a unified AI application and agent factory. It integrates with the broader infrastructure to streamline the development process by providing a standardized interface for accessing pre-built models and custom agents. This integration facilitates rapid prototyping and deployment, allowing developers to compose complex AI workflows using verified components. The catalog thereby enhances efficiency and consistency across the organization’s AI initiatives by centralizing access to critical resources.
