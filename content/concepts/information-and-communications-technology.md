# Information And Communications Technology

Information and Communications Technology (ICT) encompasses the tools, systems, and infrastructure used to process, transmit, and manage information electronically. In [[concepts/health|health]] and wellbeing contexts, ICT includes digital platforms, [[concepts/software|software]] applications, telecommunications networks, and devices that support healthcare delivery, patient communication, and health information management. The [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and effective use of ICT in health settings can influence service [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], care quality, and health outcomes.

## Digital Health Familiarity Assessment

Measuring public and professional familiarity with digital health [[concepts/terminology|terminology]] is important for understanding awareness levels and adoption readiness across different populations. A Likert scale [[concepts/survey|survey]] measuring familiarity with the term "digital health" provides quantifiable data on how well individuals understand and recognize this concept. This type of assessment typically uses a four-point scale ranging from "not familiar" to "very familiar," allowing respondents to indicate their level of knowledge without forced binary choices.

Survey instruments that measure ICT and digital health familiarity help identify gaps in understanding and inform education and implementation strategies. Baseline familiarity data can reveal whether awareness campaigns have been effective and whether specific population groups require targeted information or [[concepts/training|training]]. Results from such surveys can guide policy decisions, training programs, and [[concepts/communication-strategies|communication strategies]] within health organizations and across health systems.
