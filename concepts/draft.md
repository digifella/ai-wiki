# Draft

A **draft** is a preliminary version of a document, plan, or work of art, subject to revision and refinement before finalization. In technical and [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] contexts, drafts serve as iterative stages of development, allowing for [[concepts/document-review|peer review]], stakeholder [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], and structural [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Characteristics
- **Iterative Nature**: Drafts are mutable and intended to evolve through multiple revisions.
- **Preliminary Status**: They lack final approval, formatting, or publication readiness.
- **Feedback Mechanism**: Used to solicit input via Peer Review, editorial comments, or collaborative editing.
