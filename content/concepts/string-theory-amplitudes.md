# String Theory Amplitudes

String theory amplitudes refer to the calculations of probabilities for particle interactions in [[concepts/string-theory|string theory]]. These amplitudes are crucial for understanding and predicting phenomena within the framework of string theory, which posits that [[concepts/elementary-particles|fundamental particles]] are one-dimensional "strings" rather than point-like objects.

## Historical Development
- **Early Developments**: Initial work by Green, Schwarz, and others showed that string theory could potentially unify [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] with other fundamental forces.
- **Modern Approaches**: Advances in computational techniques and theoretical understanding have led to the development of more sophisticated methods for calculating amplitudes, such as BCFW recursion relations and the double copy method.
