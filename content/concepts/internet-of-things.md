# Internet Of Things

The Internet of Things (IoT) refers to a network of physical devices embedded with sensors, [[concepts/software|software]], and connectivity that enable them to collect, exchange, and act on data. In [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] contexts, IoT applications focus particularly on [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]] worn directly on the body that continuously monitor physiological metrics and behavioral patterns.

## Wearable Devices in Health

Wearable [[entities/iot-devices|IoT devices]] collect real-time data on [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] such as heart rate, [[concepts/sleep|sleep]] patterns, [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], and body temperature. These devices transmit information to connected platforms where data can be analyzed and accessed by users and healthcare providers. The continuous [[entities/nature|nature]] of this monitoring provides insights that traditional periodic health assessments cannot capture, supporting both individual health awareness and [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]].

## Applications in Allied Health

IoT wearables have particular relevance in [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] settings, where remote monitoring and patient engagement are increasingly important. Devices can support rehabilitation tracking, [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|chronic disease management]], and [[concepts/primary-prevention-interventions-designed-to-prevent-disease-before-it-occurs|preventive health interventions]]. The data collected helps practitioners understand client progress outside clinical environments and adjust treatment approaches accordingly.
