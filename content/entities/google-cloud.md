A division of [[entities/google]] providing enterprise-grade computing services, specializing in distributed systems, data analytics, and [[entities/ai|Artificial Intelligence]] infrastructure.

## AI Infrastructure & Hardware
- **Strategic Focus**: Deep investment in [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI Infrastructure]] to support large-scale [[concepts/training-process|model training]] and [[concepts/inference|inference]].
