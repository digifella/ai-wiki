# Secure

A state of protection against [[concepts/security-exposure|unauthorized access]], exploitation, or harm to [[concepts/knowledge-management|information systems]], data, or infrastructure.

## IBM Shadow AI
- [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]]: Unauthorized or undiscovered [[concepts/ai-projects|AI projects]] running within corporate environments (e.g., [[entities/employees|employees]] using [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] tools without IT oversight) [[concepts/shadow-it|Shadow IT]].
- Risks: Unsancitoned AI deployments pose significant threats including [[concepts/data-leakage|Data Breach]], [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]] Violation, and hidden vulnerabilities in [[concepts/ai-governance|AI Governance]].
- Management: Requires proactive identification and policy frameworks to mitigate risks (per [[entities/ibm]] Technology's [[entities/jeff-crume|Jeff Crume]]).
