# Verifiable AI

Verifiable AI refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems whose outputs, decisions, and behaviors can be objectively validated against specified constraints, safety requirements, or ethical guidelines through formal methods, audits, or transparency mechanisms.
