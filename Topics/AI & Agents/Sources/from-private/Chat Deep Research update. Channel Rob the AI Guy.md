# ChatGPT Deep Research Update: Features & Walkthrough

This update provides users with [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over research sources and integrates directly with third-party applications, making it significantly more powerful than the legacy Deep Research model.

## 🚀 How to Access

* Open ChatGPT.
* Locate **Deep Research** on the left-hand sidebar (grouped with Pulse, Images, and Apps).
* Alternatively, click the **"+" (plus)** icon in the chat bar to toggle Deep Research on.

## 🛠 Key New Features

### 1\. App [[concepts/integration|Integration]]

You can now grant ChatGPT Deep Research access to external apps to query their specific databases rather than just "searching the web."

* **Supported Apps:** Expedia, [[entities/slack|Slack]], Zillow, [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]], [[entities/github|GitHub]], HubSpot, Stripe, and over 60 others.
* **Categories:** The apps are sorted into Featured, Lifestyle, and Productivity.
* **Functionality:** This allows the AI to generate reports based on _your_ private data (e.g., Amplitude, Conductor) or specific platform data.

### 2\. Granular Site Control

You have total control over where the AI looks for information.

* **Specific Sites:** You can instruct it to search _only_ specific URLs.
* **Manage Sites:** You can exclude specific sites or domains.
* **List Management:** You can copy, save, or clear lists of sites for future use.

### 3\. "Human-in-the-Loop" [[concepts/workflow|Workflow]]

Unlike the legacy version, this process is interactive.

* **Transparency:** You can see the AI's steps (Collecting, Extracting, Identifying, Comparing, Synthesizing).
* **Intervention:** You can **Pause** or click **Update** during the research process to:
	* Ask follow-up questions.
	* Make adjustments.
	* Add new constraints or advice mid-research.

### 4\. Comprehensive Reporting

The output is a long-form, academic-style report (can take 20–45 minutes to generate).

* **Content:** Includes executive summaries, citations, [[concepts/data-tables|data tables]], and "Mermaid" causal flowcharts.
* **Export Options:** Reports can be exported to PDF or Word.
* **Depth:** In the examples shown, the AI performed over 400 searches and used 20+ citations for a single report.

## 💡 Use Case Examples

### Use Case 1: Travel Trends (Expedia Integration)

**Goal:** Analyze flight and hotel price fluctuations from NYC (JFK) to Florida (PBI) for a 5-star trip throughout the year.

* **Process:** The AI queried the Expedia database directly.
* **Result:** It produced a report showing:
	* Seasonal price dynamics.
	* Lowest and highest combined monthly costs.
	* Peak-to-trough price swings.
	* Data tables for every month of the year.

### Use Case 2: Real Estate Relocation (Zillow Integration)

**Goal:** Determine the best towns in Montana to move to with specific criteria ($500k–$1M budget, >3,000 sq ft, >5 acres of land).

* **Process:** The AI accessed Zillow to find live inventory data rather than generic articles.
* **Result:** It generated an executive summary including:
	* Specific towns with the highest inventory (e.g., Kalispell, Helena).
	* Median list prices and square footage per town.
	* Specific example listings with links.
	* Assumption and limitation [[concepts/notes|notes]] regarding the data.

* * *

## ⚠️ Important Note

This new version of Deep Research is **slower** than the legacy version (taking 20+ minutes per report) because it mimics a human research workflow. However, the depth of data and the ability to customize sources make the output significantly more usable.
