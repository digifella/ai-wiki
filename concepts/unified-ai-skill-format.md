# Unified AI Skill Format

The [[concepts/agent-first-organizational-infrastructure|Unified AI Skill Format]] is a standardized specification for packaging and deploying discrete AI capabilities across different organizational platforms and [[concepts/agentic-systems|agent systems]]. Developed through collaboration between [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]], [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]], and [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]], the format establishes common protocols for defining [[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI skills]]—reusable units of AI functionality—that enable interoperability between different [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and systems.

## Purpose and Scope

The format addresses fragmentation in the [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] ecosystem by creating a common language for [[concepts/skill|skill]] definition, allowing organizations to build, share, and integrate AI capabilities without [[concepts/vendor-lock-in|vendor lock-in]]. [[concepts/skills|Skills]] packaged in this format can be deployed across multiple platforms and agent frameworks, reducing duplication of effort and accelerating the development of complex [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]]. The specification covers skill [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], input/output schemas, execution requirements, and integration guidelines.

## Implementation

Organizations adopting the [[concepts/ai-agent-ecosystem|Unified AI Skill Format]] leverage standardized interfaces to integrate diverse [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. Recent demonstrations highlight the practical application of these standards in high-complexity orchestration [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]:

*   **Orchestration Capabilities**: [[concepts/fugu-ultra|Sakana Fugu Ultra]] demonstrates advanced orchestration by [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] [[concepts/saas|web applications]] from screenshots and solving intricate problems, showcasing the potential of standardized skill execution in dynamic environments [[lab-notes/2026-07-08-Fugu-Ultra-AI-Orchestrates-App-Cloning-and-Complex-Probl|Fugu Ultra AI Orchestrates App Cloning and Complex Problem Solving]].
*   **Interoperability**: The format ensures that skills defined for one [[entities/llamaindex|agent framework]] can be utilized by others, facilitating the creation of composite agents that combine specialized capabilities.
