# Adobe Lightroom Develop Module

The [[concepts/develop-module|Develop Module]] is the primary editing workspace in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], designed for photographers to adjust and refine their [[concepts/images|images]]. Unlike destructive editing in traditional image editors, the Develop Module performs all edits non-destructively. This means the original image file remains unaltered and all [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are recorded as [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] that can be modified or discarded [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] any time without affecting the source file.

## History Panel

The [[concepts/history-panel|History Panel]] displays a chronological record of every adjustment made to an image during the current editing [[concepts/session|session]]. Each step appears as a separate entry, allowing photographers to review their editing progression and return to any previous state by clicking on an earlier history entry. This feature is useful for comparing different editing approaches or reverting unwanted changes without needing to undo sequentially.

## Before and After View

The Before/After View allows photographers to [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] their edited image against the original or against previous versions side-by-side. Multiple view options are available, including side-by-side comparisons, top-and-bottom layouts, and split-screen views. This tool helps photographers assess whether their adjustments have improved the image and [[entities/make|make]] decisions about further refinements.

## Adjustment Panels

The Develop Module includes numerous adjustment panels organized into sections such as Basic ([[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], [[concepts/whites|whites]], [[concepts/blacks|blacks]]), [[concepts/tone|Tone]] Curve, HSL ([[concepts/hue|hue]], saturation, luminance), [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]], Detail (clarity, [[concepts/texture|texture]], dehaze), and Effects. These panels provide comprehensive [[concepts/power|control]] over image appearance while maintaining the [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive workflow]] that defines Lightroom's editing approach.
