# Liquid Glass

## Overview
A [[concepts/conceptual-design|conceptual design]] language and interface paradigm associated with [[entities/apple|Apple]]'s ecosystem updates, focusing on transparency, depth, and material realism.

## watchOS 27 Integration
With the release of [[concepts/watchos-27]], the "Liquid Glass" aesthetic is prominently featured in the new UI improvements.

- **Compatibility**: Supports [[entities/apple-watch-se-3|Apple Watch SE 3]], Series 9-12, and Ultra 2-4. Older models are deprecated.
- **Installation**: Available via the iPhone's Watch app or directly on the device.
- **Key Focus Areas**:
    - Major UI improvements aligning with the Liquid Glass visual style.
    - Significant battery optimizations to maintain performance on newer hardware.
    - Enhanced overall user experience through refined interactions.
