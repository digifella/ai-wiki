# Noel Tichy

## Overview
Noel Tichy is a scholar and practitioner known for contributions to [[concepts/organizational-structure]], [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] Development, and Transformational Leadership. His work often intersects with the mechanics of executive [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and the structural elements required for sustainable organizational improvement.

## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/organizational-structure]]: Focus on aligning individual, team, and organizational levels.
- [[concepts/leadership-development|Leadership Development]]: Emphasis on coaching and experiential [[concepts/learning|learning]].
- Transformational Leadership: Moving beyond transactional management to inspire systemic shift.
