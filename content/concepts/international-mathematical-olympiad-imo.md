# International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO)

The [[concepts/international-mathematical-olympiad|International Mathematical Olympiad]] is the world's premier [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] competition for high school students, held annually since 1959. It brings together the most talented young mathematicians from over 100 countries to solve challenging proof-based problems across algebra, combinatorics, geometry, and [[concepts/number-theory|number theory]]. Participation in the IMO is considered a significant achievement, with gold medals awarded to approximately the top 1% of competitors.

In 2026, [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[entities/gemini-2-5-pro|Gemini 2.5 Pro]] model, equipped with [[concepts/deep-think-mode|Deep Think mode]], achieved [[concepts/gold-medal-standard|gold-medal standard]] performance on the International Mathematical Olympiad. The achievement highlighted the potential of extended [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] techniques in AI systems for tackling complex, formal [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]].
