# Layer Control

Layer Control is a functionality within Adobe Firefly's Prompt-to-Edit feature that enables selective modifications to specific elements or regions of digital images through text prompts. Rather than applying generative edits across an entire image, this capability allows creators to target particular layers or defined areas while preserving the rest of the composition unchanged. This selective approach provides greater precision when making adjustments to complex images with multiple elements.

## How It Works

The feature allows users to isolate specific parts of an image, whether by selecting distinct objects or defining custom regions. By focusing the generative AI on these isolated areas, users can apply changes such as color adjustments, texture replacements, or structural modifications without affecting surrounding content. This mechanism ensures that the integrity of the original image remains intact outside the targeted zone, offering a more controlled editing workflow compared to global image generation.
