# Water Flow Concept Overview

Water [[concepts/flow|flow]] is a fundamental aspect of hydraulic systems and natural phenomena. It involves the [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of water through pipes, channels, or open spaces under various conditions such as [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]], pressure differences, and pumping mechanisms.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/hydrostatic-pressure|Hydrostatic Pressure]]:** The pressure exerted by a fluid [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] equilibrium due to the force of gravity.
- **Pumping Mechanisms:** Devices used to move fluids from one place to another against natural forces like gravity or [[concepts/friction|friction]].
  - [[concepts/wirtz-pump|Wirtz Pump Mechanics Overcoming Airlock and Hydrostatic Pressure Challenges]]
- **Airlocks:** Phenomena where air pockets form in pipes, disrupting fluid flow.
