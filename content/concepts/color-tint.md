# Color Tint

Color Tint is a feature within [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]'s new [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]] that allows users to adjust the color cast and tonal qualities of their [[concepts/images|images]]. As part of [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s updated [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]], it functions as a refinement tool for correcting or creatively shifting the overall [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and [[concepts/hue|hue]] balance of a photograph or [[concepts/design|design]].

## Integration with Color and Vibrance

The Color Tint controls work in conjunction with the broader [[concepts/color-and-vibrance-tool|Color and Vibrance tool]] introduced in [[entities/photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]. This tool consolidated several color adjustment [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] into a single panel, providing a more streamlined approach to [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] compared to previous versions. Users can access tint [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] alongside vibrance, saturation, and other color modification options without switching between multiple dialogs.

## Practical Application

The Color Tint feature is particularly useful for correcting white balance issues, adapting images to specific color grading styles, and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] in portrait work. It gives photographers and designers a non-destructive way to shift color temperature across warm-to-cool spectrums and [[entities/make|make]] targeted adjustments to how colors appear in their compositions.
