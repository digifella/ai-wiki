# Loneliness

Loneliness is a complex subjective feeling of distress about one's social [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and the perceived discrepancy between desired and actual levels of [[concepts/human-connection|social connection]]. It is distinct from solitude, which can be pleasurable or neutral in its emotional impact.
