# Malaria vaccination

Strategies and technologies designed to induce [[concepts/long-term-immunity|long-term immunity]] against Malaria.

## Delivery Innovations
- [[concepts/mosquito-mediated-vaccine-delivery|Malaria vaccination via mosquitos]]
	- Utilizes mosquitoes as vectors for drug-based [[concepts/providing-vaccinations|vaccine delivery]].
	- Experimental observations indicate that animals achieve lifelong protection from reinfection, despite the drug used having a very short half-life.
