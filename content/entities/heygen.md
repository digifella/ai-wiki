# Heygen

AI platform for creating realistic [[concepts/digital-avatars|digital avatars]] (digital twins) through video synthesis, typically integrated with [[entities/eleven-labs|ElevenLabs]] for voice [[concepts/cloning|cloning]]. Focuses on avoiding the [[concepts/uncanny-valley]] through optimized [[concepts/workflow|workflows]].

## Key Points
- **Primary Use Case**: Creating realistic [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]] for [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], virtual representation, and scalable media production (e.g., personalized videos, [[concepts/training|training]] modules).
- **Core Workflow**:
  - Film subject with proper lighting (1-2 min clip, minimal [[concepts/exercise|movement]]).
  - Process via Heygen to generate 3D avatar from video input.
  - Clone voice using ElevenLabs from audio samples.
  - Sync avatar and voice for natural video output.
- **Critical Success Factors**:
  - High-quality input video (avoiding low-light or rapid motion).
  - Careful calibration to prevent [[concepts/uncanny-valley]].
  - Seamless [[concepts/integration|integration]] between Heygen (visual) and ElevenLabs (audio).
- **Reference Resource**: [How to Create the Perfect AI Avatar: Step-by-Step Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Qy08KtUDXVk)
