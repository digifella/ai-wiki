# AI Powered Flight Searching

AI-powered flight searching leverages large language models like ChatGPT to help users identify affordable airfare options. Instead of manually comparing prices across multiple travel booking websites, users describe their travel requirements through natural language prompts. These prompts typically include destination preferences, travel dates, budget constraints, schedule flexibility, and airline or routing preferences. The AI processes this information to provide recommendations, insights about fare trends, and strategies for finding cheaper flights.

## How It Works

Users interact with ChatGPT by describing their travel needs in conversational language. The model can suggest optimal travel dates based on historical pricing patterns, identify less popular routes that may be cheaper, recommend flexible booking strategies, and help identify which airports or nearby alternatives might offer better fares. The AI can also analyze trade-offs between convenience and cost—for example, suggesting connecting flights or alternative travel dates that significantly reduce ticket prices.

## Practical Limitations

While AI assistants can provide valuable guidance on flight-searching strategies and general fare patterns, they cannot directly access real-time flight databases or current pricing information. Users must still visit actual booking platforms to complete purchases and verify current prices. The AI's value lies primarily in helping users understand search strategies, identify patterns, and make more informed decisions rather than replacing traditional flight search engines entirely.
