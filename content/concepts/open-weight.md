# Open Weight

Open-weight refers to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|artificial intelligence models]] whose [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] and [[concepts/weights|weights]] are publicly released, allowing researchers, developers, and organizations to download, modify, and deploy them locally. Unlike closed [[concepts/models|models]] that are only accessible through APIs or proprietary interfaces, [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] provide transparency and enable [[concepts/customization|customization]] for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Key Characteristics

[[concepts/open-weight-language-models|Open-weight models]] democratize access to advanced [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] by removing dependency on proprietary platforms. Users can run these models on their own infrastructure, ensuring data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and reducing latency compared to cloud-based alternatives. This approach has been adopted by organizations including [[concepts/google-search|Google]] ([[entities/gemma|Gemma]] series) and others, reflecting a broader industry trend toward [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] in [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]].

## Technical and Strategic Implications

The release of open-weight models enables researchers to audit [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]], improve safety practices, and conduct independent evaluations. However, it also raises considerations around resource requirements, as [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] demands significant computational capacity. The tension between openness and [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|responsible AI]] [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] remains an ongoing discussion within the field.
