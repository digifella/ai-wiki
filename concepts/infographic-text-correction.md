# Infographic Text Correction

[[concepts/infographic|Infographic]] Text Correction is a post-processing workflow step in the production of [[concepts/ai-generated-infographics|AI-generated infographics]] that focuses on extracting, validating, and [[concepts/text-editing-in-images|correcting embedded text]]. When [[concepts/image-generation-systems|image generation systems]] create infographics, the text elements frequently contain errors stemming from [[concepts/optical-character-recognition|optical character recognition]] (OCR) limitations, formatting inconsistencies, or misaligned text [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]]. This correction process has become a necessary stage in [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]] for designers working with [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] tools.

The primary challenge in this domain lies in the disparity between traditional document editors and modern design platforms regarding text layer [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. [[entities/adobe-acrobat|Adobe Acrobat]] traditionally excels at extracting text from static [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]] and images using advanced OCR engines, allowing for precise editing of existing content. In [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], [[entities/canva|Canva]]’s "[[concepts/canva-grab-text-feature|Grab Text]]" feature utilizes AI to identify text within flat graphic designs and convert it into editable layers, a capability specifically tailored for the non-linear layout structures common in AI-generated visual assets.

Comparing these tools reveals distinct operational strengths. Adobe Acrobat provides robust accuracy for dense, document-[[concepts/style|style]] text but may struggle with the stylized, fragmented [[concepts/typography|typography]] often found in infographics. Canva’s approach offers greater convenience for designers by preserving the visual context and allowing immediate stylistic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], though it may require manual [[concepts/verification|verification]] for complex or heavily stylized fonts. The choice between these methods often depends on whether the priority is raw [[concepts/text-accuracy|text accuracy]] or rapid visual integration.
