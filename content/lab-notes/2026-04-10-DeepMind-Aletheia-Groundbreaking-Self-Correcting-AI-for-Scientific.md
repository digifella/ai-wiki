## DeepMind Aletheia: Groundbreaking Self-Correcting AI for Scientific Discovery
**Clip title:** DeepMind’s New AI Just Changed Science Forever
**Author / channel:** [[entities/two-minute-papers|Two Minute Papers]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Io_GqmbNBbY

### Summary
This video, presented by Dr. Karoly Zsolnai-Fehér of [[entities/two-minute-papers|Two Minute Papers]],
discusses [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s latest [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]], [[entities/aletheia|Aletheia]], and its
groundbreaking ability to contribute to novel scientific research. Dr.
Zsolnai-Fehér begins by addressing initial skepticism about AIs performing
truly "insane" research, noting that many attempts result in poor or
fabricated papers. However, he then reveals his recent visit to the
DeepMind research group, where he learned about "[[concepts/deep-think|Deep Think]]," an AI capable
of achieving gold medal-worthy performance on the [[concepts/international-mathematical-olympiad|International Mathematical Olympiad]], and its even more advanced [[concepts/iteration|iteration]], [[concepts/automated-scientific-research|Aletheia]],
which surpasses this.

Aletheia's innovative architecture addresses several core challenges
traditionally faced by AI in generating novel research. Firstly, it employs
a sophisticated "generator-verifier-reviser" [[concepts/loop|loop]]. The generator proposes
solutions, which are then rigorously checked by a verifier. Critically
flawed solutions are sent back to the generator, while those needing minor
[[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] go to a reviser. This [[concepts/self-correction|self-correction]] mechanism is crucial for
preventing the AI from "hallucinating" or blindly agreeing with its own
potentially incorrect outputs, ensuring the final results are robust and
accurate.

Beyond its internal validation, Aletheia demonstrates remarkable
advancements in computational efficiency and knowledge [[concepts/integration|integration]]. Through
significant optimizations, the model achieves state-of-the-[[concepts/art|art]] intellectual
capabilities with 100 times less [[concepts/computational-power|computational power]] than previous
versions. Crucially, Aletheia is equipped with the ability to search, read,
and combine techniques from dozens of cutting-edge research papers without
succumbing to logical inconsistencies. This capability allowed it to
autonomously solve four previously open mathematical "Erdős problems,"
which, while considered somewhat accessible, had been largely overlooked by
human experts for years.

The video concludes by highlighting Aletheia's most significant
achievement: its collaboration with human scientists to produce the core
content for four new, [peer-reviewed research](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peer-reviewed_Research) papers in advanced fields like
[arithmetic geometry](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Arithmetic_Geometry) and interacting [[concepts/particle-physics|particle physics]]. These contributions
were independently verified for correctness and novelty, marking a crucial
step where AI isn't just solving existing problems but actively generating
new knowledge that pushes the boundaries of human understanding. Dr.
Zsolnai-Fehér categorizes this as "Level 2: Publishable Research" within a
human-[[concepts/ai-collaboration|AI collaboration]] framework, expressing excitement about the future
potential for AI to accelerate [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]] and improve human lives.
