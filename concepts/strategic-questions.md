# Strategic Questions

Strategic Questions is a framework for structuring business presentations and narratives using [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] as research and synthesis partners rather than autonomous generators. The approach emphasizes maintaining human control over presentation organization and content validation while leveraging AI's ability to process information and explore [[concepts/ideas|ideas]]. By asking targeted, deliberate questions before and during slide creation, presenters can clarify their core message, understand audience needs, and establish logical [[concepts/flow|flow]] more effectively than relying on AI to produce complete presentations automatically.

## The Core Method

The process begins by identifying a central thesis or key [[concepts/purpose|objective]], then proceeds through structured questioning that validates assumptions and tests arguments. Rather than asking AI to "create a presentation," this method uses specific prompts that push the tool to help research topics, synthesize information, and organize [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]]. Questions might address what the audience needs to know, what evidence supports the core claim, what objections should be addressed, and how concepts logically connect. This maintains the presenter as the decision-maker while using AI to accelerate research and expose gaps in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].

## Practical Application

The framework works both before slide creation—to establish clear structure and [[concepts/communication|messaging]]—and during the [[concepts/editing-workflow-optimization|editing process]], where AI can help refine language, identify weak arguments, or suggest reorganization. By treating AI as a thinking partner that responds to questions rather than a tool that generates finished work, presenters retain [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] for accuracy, appropriateness, and alignment with their actual message. This human-centered approach reduces the risk of misleading content while making better use of AI's computational and organizational capabilities.
