# USB-C Port Utility

The USB-C port on modern iPads serves as a unified interface that consolidates multiple functions into a single connection point. This standardized connector enables data transfer, power delivery, and video output through one physical port, replacing the proprietary Lightning connectors found on earlier iPad models. The USB-C standard allows iPads to connect with a broader ecosystem of peripherals and accessories that use the same universal connector.

## Productivity and Creative Applications

The USB-C port supports external displays, allowing users to extend or mirror their workspace for enhanced productivity tasks. It also enables connection to professional peripherals such as external storage drives, cameras, and creative input devices like stylus holders and external keyboards. These connections facilitate workflows that previously required desktop computers, making iPads more viable for professional content creation, video editing, and design work.

## Power and Charging Capabilities

USB-C power delivery on iPads enables faster charging compared to older connector standards and allows the device to accept power from a wider variety of chargers and power banks. The bidirectional power capability means the iPad can also charge other devices when needed. This flexibility simplifies the peripheral ecosystem a user needs to maintain.
