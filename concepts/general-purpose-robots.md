# General-Purpose Robots

**General-purpose robots** are [[concepts/autonomous-operation|autonomous systems]] designed to perform a wide variety of tasks across different domains, rather than being specialized for a single, repetitive function. This concept relies heavily on advanced [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], particularly [[concepts/multimodal-ai]], to enable [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]] and real-time [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] in unstructured environments.

## Key Developments

### Google DeepMind & Gemini Integration
Recent advancements highlight the integration of [[entities/gemini-ai]] into physical [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] to enhance adaptability. Key insights from recent lab tours and research include:

*   **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Integration]]:** [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] is actively integrating [[concepts/multimodal-language-models|multimodal AI]], specifically the [[entities/gemini]] model, into physical robots to improve environmental perception and interaction [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-Google-DeepMind-Robotics-Gemini-AI-for-Adaptable-General|Google DeepMind Robotics: Gemini AI for Adaptable, General-Purpose Robots]].
*   **Beyond Rigid Programming:** The focus is shifting from rigidly pre-programmed behaviors to adaptable systems that can learn and adjust to new [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] dynamically.
*   **[[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] & Vision:** [[entities/kanishka-rao|Kanishka Rao]], Director of Robotics at [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]], outlines the lab's direction in collaboration with experts like Professor [[entities/hannah-fry|Hannah Fry]], emphasizing the move toward truly general-purpose capabilities.
