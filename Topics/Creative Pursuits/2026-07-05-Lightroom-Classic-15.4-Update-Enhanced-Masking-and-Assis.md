## Lightroom Classic 15.4 Update: Enhanced Masking and Assisted Culling Features
**Clip title:** What's NEW in Lightroom Classic ver 15.4
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Anthony Morganti
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=RZ5LxL4ONqo

### Summary
The video provides an overview of the [[concepts/new-features|new features]] and improvements introduced in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom Classic]] version 15.4. The presenter, [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]], apologizes for the delay in creating the content, attributing it to personal matters and ongoing projects, including courses on [[concepts/luminar-neo|Luminar Neo]] and [[concepts/macro-photography|macro photography]]. He clarifies that this video focuses specifically on [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], with subsequent videos planned for Lightroom (cloud version) and [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] [[concepts/software-updates|updates]]. While noting that none of the [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] are "earth-shattering," he highlights several useful enhancements.

A significant improvement detailed in the video is the enhanced subject and background [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] capabilities. Morganti demonstrates this with a [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]], showcasing Lightroom Classic's ability to precisely select and adjust the background, even around intricate details like flyaway hair – an area where previous versions often struggled. He further illustrates this by selecting the background behind a bicycle, noting the accurate [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] around the spokes. This advancement allows for more refined and automated [[concepts/local-adjustments|local adjustments]], saving photographers considerable time in post-processing.

Another key update is the improved [[concepts/assisted-culling|Assisted Culling]] feature, particularly beneficial for event photographers. The video demonstrates how Lightroom Classic can now more effectively identify subjects with closed eyes within group photos, which was previously a challenge. This improved [[concepts/ai-detection|AI detection]] helps streamline the culling process, allowing photographers to quickly identify and reject suboptimal shots. Additionally, new AI filters have been integrated into the [[concepts/metadata|Metadata]] panel, enabling users to sort their images based on whether they've had AI edits applied, such as [[concepts/ai-based-selection|AI masking]] or [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] removals.

Finally, Morganti introduces the highly anticipated Duplicate Finder. This feature allows users to scan their entire [[concepts/library-module|Lightroom library]] (or specific folders/collections) to identify identical images. Crucially, the [[concepts/duplicate-detection|duplicate detection]] is based on [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]] rather than just filenames, making it more accurate in finding true duplicates, even if they have different names or minor [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] variations. The detected duplicates are presented in stacks, offering an organized way to review and remove them, thereby helping photographers manage [[entities/storage|storage]] and [[concepts/decluttering|declutter]] their extensive libraries.

In conclusion, while the updates in Lightroom Classic version 15.4 might not be revolutionary, they represent practical and valuable enhancements to core functionalities. The improved [[concepts/masking-tools|masking tools]] offer greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]], Assisted Culling streamlines the selection of group photos, the new AI metadata filters provide better organization for AI-enhanced images, and the Duplicate Finder is a powerful tool for efficient library management. These features collectively contribute to a more efficient and precise workflow for photographers.
