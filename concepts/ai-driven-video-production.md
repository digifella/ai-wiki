# AI Driven Video Production

AI-driven video production is a workflow that automates the conversion of text-based source materials into finished video content through sequential application of AI tools. The process combines slide generation and video synthesis to reduce manual effort in content creation, enabling faster production of video resources from written materials such as research documents, long-form articles, or structured notes.

## Core Workflow

The typical workflow begins with NotebookLM, which generates presentation slides from source documents or transcripts. These slides serve as the structural foundation for video content. The generated slides are then imported into Google Vids, an AI video creation tool that automatically synthesizes the slides into video format with generated narration, transitions, and visual elements. This two-stage process eliminates the need for manual slide creation or video editing while maintaining control over source material and final output.

## Output Characteristics

Videos produced through this workflow are generated without watermarks, making them suitable for direct publication or distribution without modification. The resulting videos contain AI-generated audio narration synchronized with the visual slide content, allowing organizations to produce video resources at scale from existing documentation or research materials.
