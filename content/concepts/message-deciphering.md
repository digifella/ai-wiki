# Message Deciphering

Message deciphering is a critical process in [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]] and the broader field of information [[concepts/security|security]]. It involves translating encrypted or encoded messages into their original, readable format. This process often requires understanding [[concepts/cryptographic-algorithms|encryption algorithms]] and keys used by the sender to ensure [[concepts/secure|secure]] communication.
