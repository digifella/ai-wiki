# Universal Technique

The Universal Technique for fried rice is a methodical approach to preparing fried rice that emphasizes proper rice treatment and strategic ingredient selection. Rather than treating fried rice as a simple leftover dish, this technique applies consistent principles that work with any combination of ingredients, making it a reproducible formula for consistently good results.

## Rice Treatment

The foundation of the Universal Technique begins with rice [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. The technique prioritizes using rice that has been properly dried, either through refrigeration of cooked rice or through specific [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]] that reduce [[concepts/moisture-content|moisture content]]. This prevents the final dish from becoming soggy or clumped. The rice grains should be separated and individual before cooking to ensure even heat [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] and proper [[concepts/texture|texture]].

## Ingredient Mix-ins

The technique provides a flexible framework for combining ingredients rather than prescribing a fixed recipe. By understanding how different components—proteins, vegetables, aromatics, and seasonings—interact during the cooking process, the Universal Technique allows cooks to adapt fried rice to available ingredients without compromising quality. The method emphasizes technique over specific ingredient lists, making it applicable across different cuisines and ingredient availability.
