# Light Source Generation

**[[concepts/light|Light]] source generation** refers to the computational process of creating, simulating, or manipulating illumination within a digital workspace. In modern [[entities/adobe-photoshop]] workflows, this has shifted from manual filter-based techniques to AI-driven non-destructive manipulation.

## Core Concepts

*   **Non-Destructive Lighting:** Modern tools allow light source attributes (position, intensity, color) to be adjusted without altering the underlying [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]] of the subject.
*   **AI-Assisted Relighting:** Utilizes [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to infer depth, [[concepts/material-properties|material properties]], and existing lighting conditions to generate realistic new light sources.
*   **Replacement of Legacy Tools:** AI relighting features are designed to supersede older, raster-based effects like the Lighting Effects filter, offering greater flexibility and realism.

## Implementation: Photoshop Beta

The **[[concepts/relight-layer|Relight Layer]]** feature in [[entities/photoshop-beta]] exemplifies current advancements in light source generation.

*   **Functionality:** Allows users to generate new light sources over a selected layer, manipulating direction, intensity, and color via an intuitive interface.
*   **Workflow Integration:** Replaces the removed "Lighting Effects" tool, providing a more streamlined, AI-powered approach to [[concepts/lighting-manipulation|lighting manipulation]].
*   **Key Demonstrations:** Detailed walkthroughs of the feature's capabilities are available in external tutorials, such as those by [[entities/photoshopcafe]].
