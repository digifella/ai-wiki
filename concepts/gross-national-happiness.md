# Gross National Happiness

**Gross National [[concepts/happiness|Happiness]]** (GNH) is a development [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] and metric originating in [[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]], prioritizing collective [[concepts/well-being|well-being]], cultural [[concepts/preservation|preservation]], and environmental conservation over purely economic [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] like Gross Domestic Product.

## Core Principles
*   **Holistic Development:** Balances material and spiritual needs.
*   **Sustainable Development:** Emphasizes environmental [[concepts/secure|protection]] and [[concepts/resilience|resilience]].
*   **Cultural Preservation:** Protects traditional values and social [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]].
*   **Good [[concepts/governance|Governance]]:** Promotes [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], [[concepts/accountability|accountability]], and participation.

## Modern Applications & Case Studies
*   **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]]:** A new initiative in Bhutan designed to address modern existential crises through mindful [[concepts/city-planning|urban planning]], serving as a practical application of GNH principles in a contemporary context. See [[lab-notes/2026-08-07-Bhutans-Gelephu-Mindfulness-City-Addressing-Existential|Bhutan's Gelephu Mindfulness City: Addressing Existential Crisis with Mindful Development]] for detailed analysis.
*   **Policy Integration:** GNH indices are used to evaluate legislative proposals and national budgets.
