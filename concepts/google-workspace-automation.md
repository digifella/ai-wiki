# Google Workspace automation

Strategies and frameworks for automating workflows and deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai]] within the [[entities/google-workspace]] ecosystem.

## No-Code AI Agent Implementation
- **Alternative Frameworks**: Utilizing [[entities/notebooklm]] and [[entities/gemini]] as a safe, [[concepts/no-code|no-code]] alternative to [[entities/openclaw]] and complex [[concepts/coding|coding]]-heavy frameworks.
- **[[concepts/automation-tools|Workflow Automation]]**: Creating agents that ingest specific business rules to automate tasks, such as drafting [[entities/email|email]] responses.
- **Source**: Charles Terence [[entities/harper|Harper]] channel ([Video Link](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CRznndHNHNA))

## Proactive Automation with Gemini Spark
- **Reactive vs. Proactive**: Unlike standard [[entities/gemini]] Chat which is reactive (answering only when prompted), [[entities/gemini-spark|Gemini Spark]] acts as a [[concepts/ai-workflow|proactive AI]] agent capable of initiating actions within the workspace.
- **Implementation**: Functions as a beginner-friendly agent for automating routine tasks without manual [[concepts/prompting|prompting]], enhancing the [[concepts/no-code|no-code]] automation stack.
- **Source**: [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]] ([Google Gemini Spark: Proactive AI for Google Workspace Automation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=7GkIWPPC9i0))
