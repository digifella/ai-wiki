# Atomic Level

The fundamental scale of matter where individual atoms and molecules dictate physical and chemical properties. In computational biology and drug design, operating at the "atomic level" implies modeling interactions with high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] to predict binding affinities, structural conformations, and reaction [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].

## Key Concepts

- **[[concepts/molecular-dynamics|Molecular Dynamics]]**: [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] of physical movements of atoms and molecules, crucial for understanding stability and interaction at the atomic level.
- **Structure-Based Drug Design**: Utilizing 3D structures of biological targets to design new drugs, relying on accurate atomic-level predictions.
- **AlphaFold**: A breakthrough in predicting protein structures from amino acid sequences, enabling precise atomic-level modeling of biological targets.

Recent advancements in [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] have transformed the ability to model and manipulate matter at the atomic level, particularly in pharmaceutical research.
