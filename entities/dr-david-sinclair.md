# Dr David Sinclair

Dr. David Sinclair is a researcher specializing in gerontology, with a focus on the psychological and social dimensions of aging. His work examines how societal attitudes toward aging influence health outcomes and longevity in older populations. Rather than approaching aging exclusively through a biological framework, Sinclair investigates the intersection of psychology, social factors, and aging to understand how individuals experience and adapt to growing older.

## Ageism and Longevity

A central area of Sinclair's research concerns ageism and its measurable effects on health. He argues that defying ageism can help individuals live longer by mitigating the negative psychological and physiological impacts associated with negative societal stereotypes. His studies highlight the correlation between positive self-perception of aging and improved physical health metrics, suggesting that social and psychological interventions are as critical as biological ones in extending lifespan.
