# Vibrance Adjustment Layer

The Vibrance Adjustment Layer is a color grading tool in Adobe Photoshop 2025 that provides selective control over color saturation in digital images. Unlike the standard Saturation adjustment, which affects all colors uniformly, Vibrance applies saturation intelligently by protecting skin tones from oversaturation while boosting muted or desaturated colors. This selective approach makes it particularly useful for portrait photography and images where preserving natural skin tones is important while enhancing overall color impact.

## How It Works

The Vibrance tool uses an algorithm that detects and protects already-saturated colors—especially those in the red and orange ranges typical of skin—while targeting undersaturated colors for enhancement. This means photographers can increase color intensity without the risk of making faces appear unnatural or overly processed. The adjustment includes a dedicated Saturation slider for more traditional, uniform color adjustments when needed.

## Practical Use

In Photoshop 2025, the Vibrance Adjustment Layer can be applied non-destructively, allowing photographers to adjust or remove the effect without permanently altering the original image. This layer-based approach also enables selective application through masking, making it possible to enhance vibrance in specific areas while leaving others unchanged. The tool is commonly used in workflows for landscape, portrait, and commercial photography.
