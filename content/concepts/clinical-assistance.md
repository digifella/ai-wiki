# Clinical Assistance

Systems and tools supporting [[concepts/health|healthcare]] professionals in diagnosis, treatment planning, [[concepts/workflow-optimization|workflow optimization]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. Scope expanding from administrative [[concepts/automation|automation]] and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] to advanced cognitive augmentation.

## Current Landscape

- **Evolution to [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]:** AI transitioning from Clinical Assistance to active [[concepts/clinical-reasoning]], engaging in diagnostic [[concepts/inference|inference]] and complex decision logic rather than passive support [[lab-notes/2026-05-14-AI-in-Clinical-Reasoning-Medical-Paradox-and-OpenEvidenc|AI in Clinical Reasoning, Medical Paradox, and OpenEvidence Europe Exit]].
- **Medical Paradox:** Emerging tensions defined as the [[concepts/medical-paradox]], where [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] outpace validation frameworks, regulatory adaptation, or practical clinical [[concepts/integration|integration]] trust [[lab-notes/2026-05-14-AI-in-Clinical-Reasoning-Medical-Paradox-and-OpenEvidenc|AI in Clinical Reasoning, Medical Paradox, and OpenEvidence Europe Exit]].
