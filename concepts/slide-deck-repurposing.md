# Slide Deck Repurposing

Slide Deck Repurposing is a workflow technique that converts presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process begins with NotebookLM's ability to automatically create structured slide decks from source documents, including articles, PDFs, and other text materials. These slides are then imported into Google Vids, where they serve as the foundation for video production, extending the utility of the original content across multiple formats.

## Workflow and Process

The technique involves uploading NotebookLM-generated slides to Google Vids, where they act as the structural backbone for a new video asset. Within Google Vids, users can apply templates, add voiceovers, and insert media to transform the static presentation into a dynamic video. This method allows creators to bypass manual slide design and animation, leveraging the AI-generated structure to accelerate the production of video summaries or educational content.

## Strategic Value

This approach addresses the need for multi-format content distribution by reusing existing intellectual property. By converting text-based insights into visual media, organizations can reach audiences who prefer video consumption without creating content from scratch. The workflow emphasizes efficiency, utilizing the automated slide generation capabilities of NotebookLM to ensure consistency between the source material and the final video output.
