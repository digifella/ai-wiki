# Asana

Asana is a web and mobile-based project management platform designed to help teams organize, track, and manage their work. The company provides comprehensive resources, guides, and educational content focused on various [[concepts/pmi-frameworks|project management methodologies]] and frameworks, making it a reference source for [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]] in the field.

## Core Offerings

The platform offers project management tools that enable teams to [[concepts/structure|structure]] tasks, set timelines, assign responsibilities, and monitor progress across different types of work. Asana's [[concepts/educational-resources|educational resources]] cover multiple established methodologies, including frameworks relevant to contemporary project management practices.

## Educational Resources

Beyond its [[concepts/software|software]] platform, Asana publishes guides and articles examining different project management approaches and their applications. These resources serve both users of the platform and the broader project management community seeking to understand and implement various methodologies in their organizations.
