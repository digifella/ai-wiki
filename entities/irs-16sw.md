# IRS 16SW

Massive stellar system within the S-cluster at the [[concepts/galactic-center]], located in immediate proximity to the supermassive [[concepts/black-holes|black hole]] [[concepts/sagittarius-a]]. Part of the IRS 16 complex; IRS 16SW denotes the southwest component.

## Characteristics
- Modeled as a high-mass binary or triple system comprising evolved massive [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], potentially Wolf-Rayet types or O-type stars.
- Subject to extreme tidal forces due to proximity to [[concepts/sagittarius-a]].

## Recent Findings
- G-objects near [[concepts/sagittarius-a]] resolved as stellar debris/clouds originating from interactions with IRS 16SW [[concepts/binary-stars|binary stars]].
- Analysis confirms G-objects are products of IRS 16SW binary evolution or disruption events.
