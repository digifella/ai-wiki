# Night Mode

**Night Mode** refers to computational photography techniques designed to enhance image quality in low-[[concepts/light|light]] conditions by extending [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] times and applying noise reduction [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]].

## Evolution & Hardware Integration

*   **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]:** Early implementations, such as those on the [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] 11 Pro, marked a significant shift in mobile [[concepts/astrophotography|astrophotography]] capabilities, described as a "pretty exciting" introduction to the feature [[lab-notes/2026-10-10-iPhone-18-Pro-Max-Astrophotography-Evaluating-Variable-A|iPhone 18 Pro Max Astrophotography: Evaluating Variable Aperture Performance]].
*   **Recent Developments:** Subsequent iterations on the iPhone 12, 13, and 14 series progressively refined sensor sensitivity and [[concepts/computational-speed|processing speed]].
*   **2026 Benchmark:** The [[concepts/iphone-18-pro|iPhone 18 Pro]] Max introduces [[concepts/variable-aperture|variable aperture]] evaluation for astrophotography, allowing for deeper analysis of light gathering capabilities in extreme low-light [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] [[lab-notes/2026-10-10-iPhone-18-Pro-Max-Astrophotography-Evaluating-Variable-A|iPhone 18 Pro Max Astrophotography: Evaluating Variable Aperture Performance]].

## Key Concepts

*   **Computational Long Exposure:** Combining multiple frames to simulate longer shutter speeds without motion blur.
*   **Noise Reduction:** AI-driven algorithms to minimize grain in high-ISO images.
*   **Variable [[concepts/aperture|Aperture]]:** Hardware-level control over light intake, critical for balancing [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]] in night scenes.
