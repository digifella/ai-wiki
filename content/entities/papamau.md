# Papamau

Papamau is a YouTube content creator known for instructional cooking videos that focus on practical kitchen techniques and methods. The channel demonstrates various culinary [[concepts/skills|skills]] with an emphasis on clarity and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] for home cooks.

## Vinegar Pre-Soak Method for Poached Eggs

Papamau's notable contribution to cooking instruction is a video titled "The Soaking Trick for Perfect Poached Eggs," which presents a vinegar pre-soak technique for preparing poached eggs. The method aims to simplify the poaching process, which is often considered challenging for home cooks due to the difficulty of maintaining egg shape in simmering water. The vinegar pre-soak approach is intended to make the technique more reliable and reproducible for viewers.
