# Degradation Rate

Quantitative measure of performance decline over time, cycles, or operational [[concepts/stress|stress]]. Defines system lifespan, [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] bounds, and maintenance intervals.

## Key Drivers
- **Environmental:** Temperature extremes, humidity, radiation.
- **Operational:** Load cycles, voltage/current stress, mechanical fatigue.
- **Chemical/Material:** [[concepts/phase|Phase]] changes, corrosion, SEI growth, particle cracking.

## Battery Systems
- **Lithium-Ion Mechanisms:** Capacity fade results from [[concepts/solid-electrolyte-interphase|solid electrolyte interphase]] (SEI) thickening, electrolyte decomposition, and active material loss in electrodes.
- **EV vs. Consumer Electronics:**
- Electric vehicles exhibit significantly lower degradation rates than smartphones despite shared [[concepts/lithium-ion-battery]].
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-11-EV-Battery-Longevity-Actual-Degradation-Data-for-Buyers|EV Battery Longevity: Actual Degradation Data for Buyers]] presents empirical longevity data for buyers, highlighting that rapid decline fears are often misplaced due to superior Thermal Management, optimized duty cycles, and robust [[concepts/electric-vehicle-ev-battery-management|Battery Management System]] [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] in automotive [[concepts/software|applications]].
- **Factors Influencing Rate:** High [[concepts/state-of-charge|State of Charge]] (SOC), elevated operating temperatures, fast-charging frequency, and depth of discharge accelerate degradation.
