# AI Powered Photo Selection

AI-powered [[concepts/photo-culling|photo selection]] refers to automated culling and organization capabilities integrated into [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], designed to streamline the workflow of photographers managing large batches of images. These features leverage [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to assist in identifying and grouping similar or duplicate images, reducing the manual effort required during the initial review [[concepts/phase|phase]] of [[concepts/photo-management|photo management]].

## Assisted Culling

The [[concepts/assisted-culling|assisted culling]] feature helps photographers filter through image collections by using AI to recognize factors such as image quality, [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]], and [[concepts/writing|composition]]. Rather than manually reviewing each photograph individually, users can leverage these [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]] to quickly identify keepers and rejects, accelerating the selection process significantly.

## Auto Stacking

Auto stacking functionality automatically groups similar images together, such as burst sequences or variations of the same shot taken in rapid [[concepts/ecological-succession|succession]]. This organization method allows photographers to collapse redundant images into stacks, keeping the library view cleaner while preserving access to all variants when needed for detailed comparison.

These capabilities represent part of a broader trend toward integrating [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] into creative software workflows, aimed at reducing repetitive tasks and allowing photographers to focus on more creative aspects of their work.
