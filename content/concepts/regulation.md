# Regulation

Regulation refers to rules or directives established by a governmental body or [[concepts/organization|organization]] designed to govern behavior and practices within specific domains such as technology, finance, [[concepts/health|healthcare]], etc. These regulations aim to ensure safety, fairness, and the overall well-being of society.
