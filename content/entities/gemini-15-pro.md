# Gemini 15 Pro

Gemini 15 Pro is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]]. It represents an [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] in the [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] family of models, building on the capabilities of earlier Gemini versions with improvements across various [[concepts/performance-benchmarks|performance benchmarks]] and capabilities.

## Capabilities

The model demonstrates enhanced performance in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and [[concepts/multimodal-understanding|multimodal understanding]] tasks. Gemini 15 Pro is designed to handle complex prompts and longer [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]], enabling it to process and generate [[concepts/responses|responses]] based on extended input documents and conversations.

## Deployment

Gemini 15 Pro is available through Google's [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]] and integrated into various Google products and [[concepts/third-party-applications|third-party applications]]. The model supports both text and multimodal inputs, allowing users to interact with it across different types of content.
