# World Economic Forum

The [[entities/earth|World]] Economic Forum (WEF) is an international organization based in Geneva, Switzerland, that engages leaders from business, government, and academia to shape global, regional, and industry agendas. It hosts the Annual Meeting each January in Davos-Klosters, Switzerland.

## Key Initiatives
- Promoting public-private cooperation
- Addressing global challenges such as [[concepts/climate-change|climate change]], inequality, and social mobility
- Fostering [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] among stakeholders on critical issues affecting society
