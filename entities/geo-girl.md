### Basalt Column Formation
Analysis of the cooling and contraction [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] of [[concepts/basalt-column|Basalt Column]] Formation: [[concepts/lava-cooling|Lava Cooling]], Contraction, and [[concepts/hexagonal-cracking|Hexagonal Cracking]].

- **Core Mechanism**: Formation of regular, often polygonal rock structures (e.g., in Iceland and [[entities/united-kingdom|Northern Ireland]]) results from the cooling and subsequent contraction of hot volcanic rock, specifically basaltic lava.
- **Process**: As [[concepts/lava-flows|lava flows]] cool, thermal contraction leads to hexagonal cracking patterns.
