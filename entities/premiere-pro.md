# Premiere Pro

Premiere Pro is professional video editing software developed by Adobe, initially released in 2003. It operates on a non-linear editing model, allowing editors to arrange, trim, and manipulate video clips on a customizable timeline without requiring sequential processing. This approach has become standard in modern video production, enabling editors to work flexibly and non-destructively with source material.

## Documentary and Production Use

The software has achieved widespread adoption in documentary production, broadcast media, and creative industries as a central tool in contemporary video workflows. Its integration with other Adobe Creative Cloud applications—particularly After Effects for motion graphics and Audition for audio editing—makes it a common choice for comprehensive post-production pipelines. Premiere Pro supports a broad range of video formats and resolutions, from standard definition through to 8K, accommodating both independent and large-scale productions.

## Core Capabilities

Premiere Pro provides comprehensive tools for video assembly and modification, including multi-track editing, color correction, audio mixing, and effects processing. The software supports collaborative workflows through frame.io integration and shared project features, allowing multiple editors to work on the same project remotely. Performance is managed through proxy workflows and GPU acceleration, enabling editors to work with high-resolution media on varying hardware configurations.
