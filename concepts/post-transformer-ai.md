# Post-Transformer AI

**Post-Transformer AI** refers to emerging [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] architectures that move beyond the limitations of the Transformer model, specifically addressing issues with static knowledge, computational inefficiency, and lack of true continuous learning.

## Core Concepts
*   **Continuous Learning:** Unlike traditional LLMs that require full retraining or fine-tuning for new data, post-[[concepts/transformer-architectures|Transformer models]] aim to ingest and update knowledge in real-time without catastrophic forgetting.
*   **Latent Thinking:** Incorporating internal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] states or latent variables to improve decision-making processes beyond simple [[concepts/generative-prediction|next-token prediction]].
*   **Dynamic Architecture:** Systems that adapt their structure or processing paths based on input complexity, reducing computational overhead and improving reliability.

## Emerging Implementations
*   **Jev ([[entities/typesafe-ai|TypeSafe AI]]):** A "[[concepts/system-one-model|System One model]]" designed for [[concepts/reliable-automation|reliable automation]], positioning itself as a significant departure from traditional LLMs. It addresses the reliability gap in LLM limitations by focusing on deterministic outputs and structural integrity rather than pure generative probability.
    *   See [[lab-notes/2026-09-18-Jev-TypeSafe-AIs-New-Frontier-Model-for-Reliable-Automat|Jev: TypeSafe AI's New Frontier Model for Reliable Automation]] for detailed analysis.
    *   Key differentiator: Prioritizes "TypeSafe" execution paths to mitigate hallucination and drift common in standard transformer-based agents.
