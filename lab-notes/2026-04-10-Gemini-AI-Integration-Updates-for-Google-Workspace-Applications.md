## Gemini AI Integration Updates for Google Workspace Applications
**Clip title:** NEW [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] in [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] Updates for Docs, Sheets,
Slides, [[concepts/motivation|Drive]]
**Author / channel:** [[entities/tasia-custode|Tasia Custode]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=bhIkY4g5_Sc

### Summary
The video provides a comprehensive overview of the new [[concepts/gemini-ai|Gemini AI]]
integrations across [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] applications, including Docs, Sheets,
Slides, and Drive. The central theme is how [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] reimagines productivity
and collaboration by acting as an [[concepts/intelligent-co-pilot|intelligent co-pilot]], automating [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] and streamlining workflows. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], [[entities/tasia-custode|Tasia Custode]], highlights
key updates that enable users to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] directly within their daily
work environment, drawing information from their entire [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]]
ecosystem.

In Google Docs, [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] introduces several powerful features. Users can now
utilize "Help Me Create" to generate fully formatted first drafts of
documents by synthesizing information from emails, chats, Drive [[concepts/files|files]], and
even the web, all directly within the document editor. Additionally, Gemini
enhances co-editing by allowing users to refine additions and ensure
consistent writing styles with a single click. Another significant update
is the ability to "Match Doc Format," which enables the creation of new
documents that automatically adhere to the [[concepts/style|style]] and [[concepts/structure|structure]] of an
existing reference document, simplifying [[concepts/brand-consistency|brand consistency]] and template
usage.

For data organization and presentation, Gemini brings transformative
changes to Google Sheets and Slides. In Google Sheets, users can now build
and edit entire spreadsheets using [[concepts/natural-language-prompts|natural language prompts]], pulling
relevant data from various sources to create formatted sheets and
dashboards in seconds. The "Gemini Fill" feature further automates data
entry by intelligently filling in blanks based on context, eliminating
manual formatting. In [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]], Gemini facilitates slide generation
from scratch, creating visuals and content aligned with an overall theme
and drawing from diverse sources. It also allows for effortless editing of
existing slides, enabling users to modify elements like color palettes or
styles with simple conversational [[concepts/commands|commands]].

Finally, [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]] benefits from Gemini's [[concepts/integration|integration]] with "[[concepts/ai-overviews|AI Overviews]]" and "Ask Gemini." AI Overviews provide instant, summarized
insights from search results, complete with clickable links to all source
documents, making [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] exceptionally fast. "Ask Gemini"
allows users to ask complex questions and engage in conversational queries
with Drive, which then pulls information from documents, emails, calendars,
and the web to provide coherent answers. This capability functions within
individual files or entire folders, offering a new level of intelligent
search and content analysis. These [[concepts/cc|Gemini features]] are rolling out to
[[entities/google-ai|Google AI]] Pro and Ultra subscribers, and Gemini Alpha business customers,
with global availability for Docs, Sheets, and Slides, and initial US
availability for Drive.
