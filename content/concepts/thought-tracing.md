# Thought Tracing

Thought tracing is a technique in AI [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] that reconstructs and analyzes the intermediate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] steps (or "thoughts") generated by a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) during task execution, revealing its internal decision-making process rather than treating it as a black box.

## Key Insights
- [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] researchers challenge the view of LLMs as mere "glorified [[concepts/auto-complete|auto-complete]]" systems, emphasizing their complex internal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes through interpretability work [[concepts/interpretability]]
- [[entities/stuart-ritchie|Stuart Ritchie]] ([[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] [[concepts/research-communications|Research Communications]]) led discussions questioning: *"What exactly are we talking to when we interact with an LLM?"* and the [[entities/nature|nature]] of their [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]]
- This work directly enables **thought tracing** as a method to map [[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM reasoning]] paths for transparency and [[concepts/debugging|debugging]]
