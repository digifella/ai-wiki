# Open standard protocols

Non-proprietary, publicly available [[concepts/technical-specs|specifications]] that enable seamless communication and data exchange between disparate systems and [[concepts/software|software]] components.

## AI & LLM Integration
- **[[concepts/application-programming-interfaces-apis|Application Programming Interfaces]]**: The traditional mechanism used by [[concepts/large-language-models]] to interact with [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources, services, and tools.
- **[[concepts/model-context-protocol]]**: A newer protocol designed to facilitate external interaction and [[concepts/context-management|context management]] for [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and [[concepts/large-language-models]].

- Interoperability
