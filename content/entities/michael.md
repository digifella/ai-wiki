# Michael

Michael is exploring a strategic focus on mitigating cyber and [[concepts/governance|governance]] risks created by [[concepts/co-pilot-use|ungoverned AI solutions]] within organizations. This emerging area of concern centers on the challenges posed by uncontrolled AI implementations, particularly [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic frameworks]] and [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]] systems that operate outside formal organizational oversight.

## Focus Area

The identified problem space addresses the organizational risks introduced when powerful but ungovernedAI tools proliferate without adequate security or [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] frameworks. This includes both deliberate deployments of agentic systems and ad-hoc Shadow AI usage by employees. Michael views this as an anticipated significant challenge for organizations as AI [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] accelerates.
