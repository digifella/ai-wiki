# Multi Horse Race

"Multi Horse Race" refers to an evaluation of the [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] landscape as of mid-2025, presented by Dave Plummer, a retired Microsoft [[concepts/software|software]] engineer. The assessment examines the competitive state of leading LLM systems during this period and their relative capabilities and characteristics based on hands-on usage.

## Methodology and Scope

Plummer's evaluation is based on direct experience with the top four LLM offerings available at that time. Rather than theoretical analysis, his assessment comes from actually subscribing to and extensively using these models, allowing for practical observations about their performance, [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]], and suitability for different tasks. This ground-level perspective grounds the evaluation in real-world usage patterns rather than marketing claims or benchmark results alone.

## Context

The video was published on Plummer's Dave's Garage YouTube channel, where he regularly discusses technology topics from a practical engineering perspective. The mid-2025 timeframe places this evaluation at a moment when the LLM market had matured beyond its initial novelty phase but was still characterized by rapid development and competition between multiple capable systems.
