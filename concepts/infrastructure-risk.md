# Infrastructure Risk

## Definition
**Infrastructure risk** encompasses the [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] of critical physical and digital networks to failure, disruption, or degradation due to natural hazards, human error, cyber threats, or systemic decay. It represents the intersection of asset vulnerability, hazard [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], and lack of [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] in essential services.

## Key Dimensions
- **Physical Vulnerability:** Susceptibility of energy [[concepts/grids|grids]], transport networks, and [[concepts/water-management-systems|water systems]] to [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|extreme weather events]] and geological instability.
- **Systemic Interdependence:** Cascading failures where disruption in one sector (e.g., power) propagates to others (e.g., communications, [[concepts/health|healthcare]]).
- **[[concepts/climate-change|Climate Change]] Amplification:** Increasing frequency and intensity of [[concepts/severe-weather-events|natural disasters]] such as floods, [[concepts/wildfires|wildfires]], and storms, elevating baseline risk levels.

## Recent Developments
- **2026 Climate Impact:** Growing evidence indicates that networks are under increasing pressure from climate-induced events.
- **Regional Focus (Victoria):** Specific analysis highlights rising risks to Victorian infrastructure due to changing climate patterns. See detailed findings in [[lab-notes/2026-05-19-The-networks-we-rely-on-face-growing-risk-from-natural-d|URL Ingest Summary]].
- **Digital Resilience:** Concurrent threats to [[concepts/digital-infrastructure|digital infrastructure]] via [[concepts/cybersecurity]] incidents require integrated risk models that account for both physical and virtual failures.

## Mitigation Strategies
- **Redundancy Design:** Implementing backup systems and alternative routes for critical services.
- **Adaptive Planning:** Updating building codes and [[concepts/urban-planning|urban planning]] to reflect future climate projections rather than historical data.
- **Real-time Monitoring:** Utilizing IoT sensors and AI-driven analytics to predict and respond to emerging threats before catastrophic failure.
