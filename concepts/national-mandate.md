# National Mandate

**National Mandate** refers to the compulsory [[concepts/recommendations|directives]] or authoritative [[concepts/commands|commands]] issued by a sovereign state or its executive agencies, often applied to regulate [[concepts/population-health|public health]] infrastructure, standardize clinical protocols, or enforce [[concepts/digital-innovations|digital transformation]] within the [[concepts/health|healthcare]] sector. In the context of [[concepts/allied-health-professionals]] ([[concepts/ahps|AHPs]]), mandates frequently dictate workflow standards, data interoperability requirements, and automation [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] rates.
