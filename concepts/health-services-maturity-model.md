# Health Services Maturity Model

The [[concepts/health-care|Health Services]] Maturity Model is a framework currently under development by [[entities/sandeep|Sandeep]] and [[entities/lemai|Lemai]] to assess and guide the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] within [[entities/health-service|health service]] organizations. The model applies established maturity model methodologies—standardized approaches for measuring organizational capability across defined stages—to the specific context of AI implementation in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] settings.

The primary purpose of the model is to help health service organizations understand their current state of AI readiness and identify pathways for progress. It focuses on leveraging [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] for [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge retrieval]], aiming to provide a structured approach for evaluating how effectively [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] can be integrated into existing health service workflows.

By utilizing this framework, organizations can systematically evaluate their capabilities and plan for incremental improvements. The model serves as a diagnostic and planning tool, allowing stakeholders to benchmark their current position against defined maturity stages and determine necessary steps for advancing their [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] and operational effectiveness.
