# Breakthrough Method For Agile AI Driven Development

The [[concepts/bmad-method|BMAD method]] is a universal [[concepts/ai-agent-framework|AI agent framework]] designed to bring structured development practices to AI-driven application creation. It addresses a gap in current development practices where [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] have made it easier for individuals to build [[concepts/software|applications]], but often without the rigor of established [[concepts/development-methodologies|development methodologies]]. By combining agile principles with [[concepts/ai-agent-workflows|AI agent workflows]], BMAD provides a systematic approach to managing the iterative [[entities/nature|nature]] of AI-powered projects.

## Core Approach

BMAD integrates [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/agents|agents]] into the agile development lifecycle, enabling teams to leverage [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] while maintaining consistent quality [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] and project [[concepts/power|control]]. The framework establishes structured processes for how [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] interact with development tasks, [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], [[concepts/testing|testing]], and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] cycles. This approach acknowledges that [[concepts/application-development-automation|AI-driven development]] differs from traditional [[concepts/coding|software development]] in pace and [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] patterns, requiring adapted methodologies rather than wholesale [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of older practices.

## Key Application

The method is intended to be applicable across different types of AI-driven projects and team structures. Rather than being prescriptive about specific tools or technologies, BMAD focuses on establishing principles and workflows that can accommodate various AI implementations while ensuring that projects maintain visibility, traceability, and [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]] throughout their development lifecycle.
