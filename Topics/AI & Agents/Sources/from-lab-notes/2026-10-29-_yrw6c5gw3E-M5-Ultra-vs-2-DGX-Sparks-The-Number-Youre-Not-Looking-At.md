## M5 Ultra vs 2 DGX Sparks… The Number You're Not Looking At
**Clip title:** M5 Ultra vs 2 DGX Sparks… The Number You're Not Looking At
**Author / channel:** Alex Ziskind
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_yrw6c5gw3E

### Summary
Certainly! Here is a summary of the video content:

The video discusses the performance comparison between the M5 Ultra Mac Studio and a cluster of two NVIDIA DGX Spark devices. The speaker tests both setups using various AI models, such as DeepSeek V4 Flash and Qwen3.8-Flash-Next, to evaluate their capabilities in tasks like token generation and prompt processing.

Key points discussed in the video include:

- **Token Generation Speed**: The M5 Ultra Mac Studio and the dual DGX Spark cluster showed similar token generation speeds when using DeepSeek V4 Flash, but the Mac outperformed the Sparks with Qwen3.8-Flash-Next.
- **Prompt Processing Time**: The dual DGX Spark cluster demonstrated faster prefill times for longer prompts compared to the M5 Ultra Mac Studio.
- **Memory and Bandwidth**: The M5 Ultra Mac Studio has 256GB of unified memory with high bandwidth, while the dual DGX Spark cluster combined offers 256GB of memory connected via a high-speed RoCE cable.
- **Concurrency**: The dual DGX Spark cluster scaled better with multiple concurrent users compared to the Mac Studio.

In conclusion, the video highlights that while the M5 Ultra Mac Studio is powerful and efficient for single-user workloads and specific models, the NVIDIA DGX Spark cluster excels in handling larger prompts and multiple concurrent users. The choice between the two depends on the specific use case, such as whether you are prioritizing single-user performance or multi-user scalability.
