### Summary
The video showcases the capabilities of [[concepts/fugu-ultra|Sakana Fugu Ultra]], an [[concepts/ai-orchestration|AI orchestration]] model, by challenging it with two distinct and [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]]: [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] a [[concepts/web-application|web application]] from a screenshot and solving an intricate, [[concepts/deep-reasoning|multi-step reasoning]] problem. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]], [[entities/fahd-mirza|Fahd Mirza]], highlights [[entities/sakana-ai|Sakana]]'s [[concepts/ambition|ambition]] and the Fugu model's design, which orchestrates various "[[concepts/frontier-models|frontier models]]" to tackle diverse problems effectively.

In the first demonstration, [[concepts/fugu-ultra|Fugu Ultra]] was tasked with creating a full-stack, runnable clone of a FIFA [[entities/earth|World]] Cup Fantasy League website based solely on a screenshot. The detailed prompt included requirements for a FastAPI and [[entities/sqlite-databases|SQLite]] backend, a plain HTML/CSS/JS frontend that visually matched the original, specific UI elements (like top navigation, a 15-player squad [[entities/pitch|pitch]], captain/vice badges, budget/transfers panels, and a player pool table with add/remove actions), and seeding the database with realistic player data. The AI successfully executed this task, producing a fully functional "Fantasy Premier League" application. Impressively, it not only replicated the visual structure but also semantically reinterpreted [[concepts/branding|branding]] and implemented dynamic backend functionalities like player transfers and budget management, proving its ability to go beyond simple visual [[concepts/cloning|cloning]].

The second, more abstract challenge involved a "deliberately absurd escape" plan. Fugu Ultra was given a complex [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] about a woman fleeing captors across five extreme waypoints, facing language barriers, mandatory pickups, and constant [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]]. The AI was prompted to produce a single, coherent, step-by-step escape plan, detailing how each leg would be survived, how language barriers would be handled, and how to reach the next waypoint, including workarounds for seemingly impossible legs and identifying the weakest link.

Fugu Ultra responded with a remarkably coherent and logical multi-step plan. Its reasoning demonstrated a deep understanding of the intricate constraints and dependencies within the prompt. It provided plausible workarounds for difficult situations, such as navigating foreign territories without papers by leveraging informal networks and bartering, and overcoming language barriers through pre-arranged countersigns. The model was also candid about the least plausible segments of the journey, highlighting areas of high risk. Overall, the demonstrations underscored Fugu Ultra's prowess in both practical [[concepts/app-creation|application development]] from high-level [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and complex [[concepts/reasoning-skills|logical reasoning]], validating Sakana's claims about its advanced orchestration capabilities.
