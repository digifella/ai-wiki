# Instruction Control

Instruction [[concepts/power|Control]] refers to the mechanisms by which users direct and constrain the behavior of AI systems through explicit [[concepts/commands|commands]] or guidelines. In the context of text-to-speech and [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]] systems, instruction control allows users to specify desired outputs such as [[concepts/tone|voice]] characteristics, speaking [[concepts/style|style]], and acoustic properties through textual input or parameter settings.

## Application in Voice Synthesis

The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family of [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] incorporates instruction control [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that enable fine-grained [[concepts/customization|customization]] of speech [[concepts/output|output]]. Users can provide [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to guide [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] choices, clone specific voice characteristics, and control various aspects of the text-to-speech generation process. This approach allows both technical and non-technical users to generate speech that meets specific requirements without requiring direct model retraining.

## Significance for Open-Source AI

By implementing instruction control mechanisms, the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]]'s [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] releases democratize access to advanced [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]] capabilities. Rather than offering only fixed, pre-trained outputs, instruction control empowers users to adapt these [[concepts/models|models]] to diverse [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], from personalized [[concepts/voice-assistants|voice assistants]] to [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] [[concepts/software|applications]] and creative content generation.
