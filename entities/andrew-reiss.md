# Andrew Reiss

Andrew Reiss is a researcher recognized for his participation in the Biosphere 2 project, a large-scale ecological experiment conducted near Oracle, Arizona. Biosphere 2 was designed as a sealed environmental system to study how self-sustaining ecosystems function in isolation. The project aimed to advance the understanding of Earth's biosphere and complex ecological processes by creating a controlled, closed-loop environment.

Reiss contributed to the scientific objectives of the project through his work within the sealed structure. His involvement provided empirical data on the interactions between biological and physical components within a confined ecosystem. This research helped inform broader discussions regarding environmental sustainability, resource management, and the potential for closed-loop life support systems in future space exploration efforts.
