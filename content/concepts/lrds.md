# LRDs

**[[concepts/little-red-dots|Little Red Dots]]** (LRDs) are hyper-luminous, compact astrophysical sources observed in the [[concepts/early-universe]] via [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]]. Characterized by extreme brightness and small angular size, LRDs challenge conventional [[concepts/structure|structure]] formation [[concepts/models|models]].

- **Detection & Properties:** Identified since 2022; defined by intense flux and compact morphology distinct from standard galaxy populations.
- **X-Ray Correlation:** [[entities/jwst|JWST]] data links LRD emissions to Pre-Stellar [[concepts/black-holes|Black Hole]] Formation, suggesting these objects are powered by or associated with supermassive black hole progenitors active before star formation.
- **Chronological [[concepts/anomaly|Anomaly]]:** Observations support hypotheses that Black Hole formation preceded the ignition of the [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|first Stars]], revising timelines of cosmic dawn.
