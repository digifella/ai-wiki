# Helium fusion

The [[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]] process wherein Helium nuclei undergo fusion to [[concepts/deployment|release]] energy, typically occurring in the later stages of [[concepts/stellar-evolution]] once Hydrogen is depleted in the [[concepts/solar-core|stellar core]].

### Stellar Dynamics
- A critical component of the energy output in the Sun.
- The Sun functions as a massive reservoir of Hydrogen and Helium, driving its colossal energy production.
- A specific mechanism within the broader field of [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]].

### Earth-based Energy Research
- Human scientific efforts are focused on replicating the energy-generating power of [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] through controlled [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]] on [[entities/earth|Earth]].
