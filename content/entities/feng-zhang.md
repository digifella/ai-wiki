# Feng Zhang

Dr. [[entities/dr-feng-zhang|Feng Zhang]] is a researcher [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the [[entities/broad-institute|Broad Institute of MIT and Harvard]], where he leads work in the field of [[concepts/gene-editing|gene editing]] technologies. His research has focused on the development and application of [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR]]-based tools, contributing to the broader understanding of how these molecular systems function and can be adapted for therapeutic purposes.

## CRISPR Research

Zhang's laboratory has been involved in exploring the natural evolution and diversity of CRISPR systems found across different bacterial species. This work has included the discovery and characterization of novel CRISPR variants that may offer advantages over previously known versions, potentially expanding the toolkit available for gene editing [[concepts/software|applications]]. His research aims to understand the mechanisms underlying these systems and their capacity for precise genetic modification.

## Impact and Recognition

Zhang's contributions to gene editing have established him as a notable figure in the field. His research at the Broad Institute continues to investigate both the fundamental biology of CRISPR systems and their practical applications in addressing genetic diseases, reflecting the institute's focus on translating basic biological discoveries into clinical tools.
