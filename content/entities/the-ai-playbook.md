# The Ai Playbook

The Ai Playbook is a video resource created by [[entities/rick-mulready|Rick Mulready]] that focuses on practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of [[concepts/instruction-reuse|Claude Skills]], also known as [[concepts/agent-harnesses|Agent Skills]]. The series provides [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] and demonstrations of how these [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] can be implemented in real-world [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].

## Content Focus

The playbook explores various [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]] for [[concepts/progressive-disclosure|Claude Skills]], examining how [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] can leverage different tools and capabilities to accomplish tasks. The series also covers related technologies and workflows that complement [[concepts/agent-development|agent development]], including [[concepts/web-crawling|web data extraction]] tools and AI-powered [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]].

## Related Tools and Technologies

The playbook references several [[concepts/complementary-tools|complementary tools]] in the [[concepts/ai-agent-ecosystem|AI agent ecosystem]], including [[concepts/firecrawl-ai|Firecrawl AI]] for web [[concepts/data-extraction|data extraction]] and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] for converting [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]] into [[concepts/interactive-html-web-applications|interactive web applications]]. These resources are presented as part of a broader [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] for building and deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]].
