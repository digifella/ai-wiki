# Adaptive Strategy

Adaptive strategy is a planning and decision-making framework that prioritizes flexibility and responsiveness to dynamic conditions. Unlike traditional approaches that rely on fixed, predetermined plans, this methodology emphasizes continuous monitoring of the environment and real-time adjustment of tactics based on new information. It is designed to handle high uncertainty and unpredictability by treating plans as iterative hypotheses rather than static recommendations.

The core mechanism of adaptive strategy involves a continuous feedback loop where agents or systems gather data, evaluate the current state against objectives, and modify their actions accordingly. This approach allows for rapid pivoting when external variables change, ensuring that goals remain achievable despite shifting circumstances. It is particularly effective in complex environments where initial assumptions may quickly become obsolete.

In the context of artificial intelligence, adaptive strategy enables agents to navigate non-stationary environments more effectively. By integrating real-time learning with execution, these systems can optimize their performance over time without requiring complete reprogramming. This capability distinguishes adaptive strategies from rigid protocols, offering a robust solution for tasks characterized by volatility and incomplete information.
