## Hermes-Agent: AI Operating System Use Cases for Entrepreneurial Automation
**Clip title:** 7 Mind-Blowing [[concepts/scenarios|Use Cases]] for [[concepts/agentic-ai|Hermes Agent]]
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** [[entities/rob-the-ai-guy|Rick Mulready]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JZWJzSSHYqU

### Summary
This video introduces Hermes-Agent, a free [[concepts/ai-augmented-workflows|AI operating system]] designed for entrepreneurs to automate and intelligently manage various business operations. The core problem it addresses is the ongoing effort required to organize, update, and provide context to traditional [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] like ChatGPT, which often necessitates repeating [[concepts/instructions|instructions]]. Hermes-Agent differentiates itself by [[concepts/learning|learning]] as you work, keeping information organized autonomously, and leveraging [[concepts/persistent-memory|persistent memory]] to understand your business goals, offers, audience, and priorities, thereby becoming smarter and more integrated over time. It operates through various platforms, including Slack, Telegram, and even a desktop app, making it accessible for delegating tasks.

The presenter highlights seven "mind-blowing" use cases for Hermes-Agent. Firstly, it transforms a single idea into a comprehensive [[concepts/automated-publishing|content machine]] by breaking down tasks like research, angle development, outlining, and [[concepts/writing|writing]] into roles for its [[concepts/sub-agents|sub-agents]], producing full marketing packages from YouTube titles to [[concepts/social-media-carousels|social media posts]]. Secondly, it assists in building and [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] new offers by researching competitor pricing and offers, comparing them to your own, and providing strategic [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]] on positioning and structure. Thirdly, Hermes can self-manage projects on autopilot using a Kanban-[[concepts/style|style]] dashboard, moving tasks through triage, to-do, scheduled, and in-progress stages, thereby automating project management.

Further use cases demonstrate its strategic and operational depth. Fourthly, it acts as a strategic advisor, utilizing its comprehensive understanding of your business to offer high-leverage recommendations for weekly focus, akin to a human consultant. Fifthly, Hermes helps build a centralized "business command center" within tools like [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]], consolidating all projects, tasks, and critical business information into one highly visible dashboard for complete operational oversight. Sixthly, it excels at [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] with memory, where a dedicated "[[entities/tomasz-janowski|Researcher]]" agent continuously monitors for useful signals, separates evidence from mere claims, verifies information, and stores it in your [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] for future reference by other agents.

Finally, for the seventh use case, Hermes allows for seamless delegation of work from your phone. You can verbally assign tasks while away from your desk, and Hermes [[entities/will|will]] process them using its [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]] of your business, [[concepts/tone|voice]], and preferred formats, delivering finished work ready for review. The overarching takeaway is that Hermes-Agent transcends the limitations of typical [[concepts/ai-chatbots|AI chatbots]] by actively learning and applying context, thereby transforming from a mere tool into an evolving operating system that efficiently runs and optimizes various parts of an entrepreneur's business, fostering real [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and strategic advantage without constant human oversight.
