# UI Realism

UI Realism is a technique in [[concepts/ai-film-direction|AI video production]] that leverages advanced image generation [[concepts/models|models]], particularly [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]], to create realistic [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] elements and digital environments. The approach centers on using [[concepts/iterative-prompt-engineering|iterative prompt engineering]]—the process of refining and adjusting [[concepts/text|text]] prompts through multiple iterations—to achieve high-fidelity visual outputs that accurately represent interface designs, [[concepts/software|software]] environments, and digital interactions.

## Application in Video Production

In video production workflows, UI Realism allows creators to generate or enhance sequences showing software interfaces, app interactions, and digital displays without requiring traditional screen recording or [[concepts/design|design]] mockups. By systematically refining prompts across iterations, producers can direct the AI toward increasingly precise representations of specific UI elements, layouts, and visual styles that align with their project requirements.

## Iterative Prompt Refinement

The technique's effectiveness depends heavily on the iterative [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loop|loop]] between [[concepts/creator|creator]] and model. Rather than attempting to capture desired results in a single prompt, creators test outputs, analyze results, and adjust their prompts accordingly. This methodical approach helps bridge the gap between conceptual intent and generated [[concepts/output|output]], making the technique practical for professional video production contexts where visual [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] matters.
