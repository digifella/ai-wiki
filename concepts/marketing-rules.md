# Marketing Rules

Marketing Rules is a systematic methodology designed to convert detailed research and knowledge content into compact 30-second video commercials. The approach addresses the practical challenge of transforming long-form written analysis into short-form video content by providing a structured workflow that leverages Google's AI-powered tools. This process enables users to repurpose existing intellectual assets efficiently without requiring extensive manual editing or production resources.

## Workflow and Tools

The core of the methodology relies on the integration of NotebookLM, Google's AI-powered notebook, which serves as the primary source for generating video scripts and storyboards. Users upload their existing documents, notes, or research materials into NotebookLM to create a grounded knowledge base. The system then utilizes Gemini, Google's large language model, to analyze this content and extract key narratives suitable for short-form video formats.

The generated output is subsequently processed through Google Vids, an AI-driven video creation tool. This platform automates the assembly of the video by selecting relevant stock footage, images, and audio tracks that align with the script generated from the NotebookLM data. The result is a cohesive 30-second commercial that maintains the factual integrity of the original source material while adapting it for rapid consumption.

This workflow allows businesses and content creators to scale video production significantly. By automating the translation of text-based insights into visual media, the method reduces the time and technical expertise traditionally required for video marketing. It ensures that complex information is distilled into clear, engaging messages without the need for manual scripting or extensive post-production editing.
