# Decentralized AI Inference

**Decentralized [[concepts/ai-inference|AI inference]]** refers to the distribution of [[concepts/machine-learning-model|machine learning model]] execution across a network of independent [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] rather than centralized [[concepts/techno-economics|data centers]]. This approach aims to reduce latency, enhance [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and lower [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] costs by leveraging idle [[concepts/computation|computing]] resources from end-users.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/distributed-computing|Distributed Computing]]**: Utilizing peer-to-peer networks to aggregate fragmented [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] power.
- **[[concepts/local-processing|Edge Inference]]**: Processing data closer to the source to minimize [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] usage and latency.
- **[[concepts/efficient-operation|Resource Optimization]]**: Monetizing idle [[concepts/compute-capacity|hardware capacity]] for participants.

## Recent Developments: Darkbloom
A notable implementation of this concept is **Darkbloom**, which specifically targets the [[entities/apple|Apple]] [[concepts/silicon|Silicon]] ecosystem.

- **Core Mechanism**: Creates a distributed, [[concepts/peer-to-peer-network|peer-to-peer network]] utilizing the [[concepts/idle-computing-power|idle computing power]] of individual users' Apple Silicon Macs.
- **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]**: Revolutionize AI [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] by aggregating fragmented local compute resources.
- **Economic Model**: Allows users to earn revenue by contributing their hardware's idle cycles to the network.
