# Image adjustment tracking

The process of monitoring, auditing, and managing modifications applied to a digital asset during the [[concepts/image-editing]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] to maintain [[concepts/non-destructive-editing]] [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] and facilitate visual comparison.

### Implementation: lightroom
* **[[concepts/history-panel]]**: Provides a chronological record of all [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] made within the [[concepts/develop-module]], allowing users to revert to specific previous states.
* **After View**: A diagnostic tool used to visually compare the original source image against the current state of [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
