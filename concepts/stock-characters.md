# Stock Characters

Stock characters are pre-designed, reusable character [[concepts/templates|templates]] available in [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] platforms like [[entities/vyond-go|Vyond Go]]. They enable users to populate animated videos without creating characters from scratch. These templates are sourced from platform libraries and come in various appearances, styles, and demographic representations, allowing creators to select options that align with their project requirements.

## Use in Video Production

In the [[concepts/script-building|video creation workflow]], stock characters function as a practical resource for rapid production. Users can choose from available character templates and incorporate them into their scripts before the platform generates the final video. This approach streamlines the creative process by eliminating the need for custom character design while maintaining visual variety across projects.

## Benefits and Limitations

Stock characters reduce production time and technical barriers for creators with varying [[concepts/skill|skill]] levels. They provide consistent, professionally-designed options that integrate seamlessly with platform tools. However, their pre-made nature means projects using identical character libraries may share visual similarities, and creators with specific character requirements may find template options limiting.
