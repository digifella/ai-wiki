# Preventive Healthcare

Preventive [[concepts/health|healthcare]] encompasses measures taken to prevent diseases rather than treating them after onset. It involves screening, [[concepts/vaccination|immunization]], [[concepts/health-promotion|health education]], and lifestyle modifications to reduce the incidence of illness and improve overall [[concepts/population-health|population health]] outcomes.

## Core Strategies

- **Screening and [[concepts/secondary-prevention|Early Detection]]**: Regular assessments to identify risk factors or early signs of disease before symptoms appear.
- **Immunization**: Vaccination [[concepts/software|programs]] to prevent infectious diseases.
- **Lifestyle Interventions**: Promotion of healthy [[concepts/diet|diet]], [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], and avoidance of harmful substances (e.g., tobacco, excessive alcohol).
- **[[concepts/risk-assessment|Risk Assessment]]**: Evaluating individual genetic, environmental, and behavioral risks to tailor [[concepts/primary-prevention|preventive measures]].

## Technological Integration

Advancements in technology are reshaping [[concepts/preventive-care|preventive care]] by enabling more frequent, accessible, and precise monitoring.

- **AI-Driven Diagnostics**: [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|Machine learning models]] assist in interpreting [[concepts/medical-imaging-technology|medical imaging]] and predicting health risks.
- **Longitudinal Monitoring**: Continuous or frequent tracking of [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]] to detect subtle changes over time.
- **[[entities/midjourney-medical|Midjourney Medical]] Initiative**: A notable development in this space is the announcement of "Midjourney Medical," a division planning to [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] a global network of full-body [[entities/ai]] ultrasound scanners. This initiative aims to facilitate widespread [[concepts/longitudinal-health-monitoring|longitudinal health monitoring]], potentially democratizing access to early detection tools.
