# Massed Compute

Massed [[concepts/compute|Compute]] is a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] demonstrating how to fine-tune [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/gpt-oss-20b|GPT-OSS-20B]] [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] using a [[concepts/custom-dataset|custom dataset]]. The tutorial focuses on adapting the model to embody and represent a specific persona through targeted [[concepts/training|training]] [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. By leveraging [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] rather than closed proprietary systems, this approach allows practitioners to customize [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] scale while maintaining [[concepts/power|control]] over the [[concepts/training-process|training process]].

## Fine-tuning Process

The tutorial provides [[concepts/step-by-step-guidance|step-by-step guidance]] on preparing custom datasets and adjusting the model [[concepts/weights|weights]] of [[entities/gpt-oss-20b|GPT-OSS-20B]] to align with desired persona characteristics. This involves selecting relevant [[concepts/training-data|training data]], configuring [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], and iteratively refining the model's outputs to match the target behavior or communication [[concepts/style|style]]. The open-weight [[entities/nature|nature]] of GPT-OSS-20B makes this process accessible to users who want to deploy customized versions without reliance on proprietary APIs.
