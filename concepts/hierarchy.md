[[concepts/visual-hierarchy|Visual hierarchy]] is a design principle that organizes and prioritizes information on a [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] by making certain elements more prominent than others. Through variations in size, color, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], position, and [[concepts/typography|typography]], designers guide users' [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to the most important content first, then secondary elements, and finally tertiary information. This ordering helps users quickly understand [[entities/html|page structure]] and navigate interfaces more efficiently.

## Purpose and Function

Effective visual hierarchy reduces [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] by establishing a clear reading order. Rather than presenting all information with equal emphasis, hierarchy signals what matters most through deliberate design choices. This is particularly important in interfaces where users scan content quickly rather than reading thoroughly, such as websites, [[concepts/apps|mobile applications]], and dashboards.

## Design Techniques

Designers create hierarchy using several interconnected methods. Size differences make important elements stand out, while color can draw [[concepts/attention|attention]] to key actions or information. [[concepts/whitespace|Whitespace]] and positioning establish [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between elements, and typography weight and [[concepts/style|style]] create distinction between heading levels and body content. Contrast—both in color and [[concepts/tone|tone]]—further emphasizes focal points within a [[concepts/writing|composition]].

## Practical Application

Visual hierarchy is fundamental to [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience design]] across [[concepts/corporate-platforms|digital platforms]]. In e-commerce interfaces, hierarchy directs attention to products and purchase options. In [[concepts/information-visualization|data visualization]], it helps users identify key metrics and trends. Well-executed hierarchy aligns with user goals and mental models, enabling efficient task completion and reducing navigation [[concepts/friction|friction]].
