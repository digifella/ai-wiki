# Reasoning

**Reasoning** refers to the cognitive process of drawing conclusions, making inferences, or forming judgments based on evidence, [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], and prior knowledge. In the context of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], it encompasses the ability of models to process complex inputs, such as visual data, and derive coherent, logical outputs.

## Multimodal Reasoning

Modern [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] extend beyond text to include [[concepts/multimodal-large-language-models|Vision-Language Models]] (VLMs) that process images alongside textual prompts. This integration allows for more nuanced understanding of context, spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and visual semantics.
