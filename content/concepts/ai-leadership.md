# AI Leadership

Strategic competition among Nation-States and Organizations to achieve dominance in [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. Encompasses model performance, [[concepts/compute|compute]] infrastructure, talent acquisition, regulatory alignment, and geopolitical [[concepts/power|influence]]. [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]] determines economic advantage, [[concepts/security|security]] posture, and normative [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] for [[concepts/ai-safety]] and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

## Anthropic 2028 Projections
- [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] published "2028: Two [[concepts/scenarios|Scenarios]] for Global AI Leadership" framed as a geopolitical warning regarding trajectory and outcomes [[entities/anthropic]].
- Analysis centers on US-[[entities/china|China]] Geopolitical Competition as the primary driver, identifying 2028 as a decisive horizon for leadership [[concepts/consolidation|consolidation]] or shift.
- Scenarios delineate potential bifurcations in global AI access and control, highlighting risks of escalation, fragmentation, or cooperative stabilization.
