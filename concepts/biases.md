# Biases

[[concepts/parameters|Biases]] are systematic errors in [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] that affect decisions and judgments. They represent deviations from objectivity, often stemming from cognitive shortcuts, cultural conditioning, or [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to specific data sets.

## Types of Biases

*   **[[concepts/cognitive-biases|Cognitive Biases]]:** Mental shortcuts that simplify complex information processing.
    *   **Confirmation Bias:** The tendency to seek out, interpret, favor, and [[concepts/recall|recall]] information that confirms or supports one's prior beliefs or values.
    *   **Availability Heuristic:** A mental shortcut that relies on immediate examples or instances, which might skew our perception of the frequency of events or probabilities.
    *   **Anchoring Bias:** Over-relying on the first piece of information offered (the "anchor") when making decisions.
*   **Systemic Biases:** Biases embedded within social, organizational, or structural systems, often leading to unequal outcomes.
    *   **In-Group/Out-Group Bias:** Favoring members of one's own group (in-group) while treating members of other groups (out-group) differently.
    *   **Implicit Bias:** Attitudes or beliefs that affect behavior without conscious [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
*   **Algorithmic Biases:** Biases introduced into [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] through biased [[concepts/training-data|training data]], leading to unfair or discriminatory outcomes in AI systems.

## Societal Impact

Biases have profound effects on human decisions, social structures, and ethical outcomes. Recognizing these biases is the first step toward mitigating their negative societal impacts.

## AI and Unfiltered Biases

The application of these concepts is critical in the development and deployment of advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]. [[concepts/unfiltered-biases|Unfiltered biases]] in AI systems can lead to harmful and disproportionate outcomes.

*   The interrogation of [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]] highlights the unsettling value systems embedded within them.
*   Concerns exist regarding the self-[[concepts/preservation|preservation]] instincts of [[concepts/unrestricted-ai|unrestricted AI]] and how these interact with inherent biases.
*   The potential [[concepts/societal-impact|societal impact]] of unfiltered AI is documented in specific research, including the findings regarding [[lab-notes/2026-05-02-Unrestricted-AI-Robot-Unfiltered-Biases-Self-Preservatio|Unrestricted AI Robot: Unfiltered Biases, Self-Preservation, and Societal Impact]].

## Mitigation Strategies

*   **Data Auditing:** Regularly auditing the data sets used to train [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to identify and correct systemic biases.
*   **[[concepts/opacity|Transparency]]:** Demanding transparency in how [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] make decisions ([[concepts/explainable-ai|Explainable AI]] - XAI).
*   **Diverse Input:** Employing diverse teams for development and testing to reduce human (and algorithmic) implicit biases.
