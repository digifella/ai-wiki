# Companion

## Definition
A companion is an entity—human, animal, or artificial—that provides [[concepts/social-interaction|social interaction]], emotional support, or practical assistance. In the context of technology and [[concepts/robotics]], this often refers to social robots or [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] designed to mitigate [[concepts/loneliness|loneliness]] or assist with daily tasks, particularly for [[concepts/vulnerable-populations|vulnerable populations]] like older adults.

## Key Themes
- **Social Presence:** The extent to which a non-human entity is perceived as having a personality or social presence.
- **Ethical Concerns:** Issues surrounding deception, emotional [[concepts/attachment-theory|attachment]] to machines, and the potential for reduced human-to-human interaction.
- **Utility vs. [[concepts/connection|Connection]]:** Balancing functional assistance with genuine emotional [[concepts/link-aggregation|bonding]].
