# Gpt Oss 20b

[[entities/gpt-oss-20b|GPT-OSS-20B]] is an [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]], featuring approximately 20 billion parameters. As an [[concepts/open-weight-model|open-weight model]], its [[concepts/parameters|weights]] are publicly available for download and modification, allowing researchers and developers to utilize the architecture for both commercial and research applications without the [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] restrictions associated with OpenAI's proprietary models like [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-4]]. This [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] distinguishes it from OpenAI's [[concepts/closed-ecosystem|closed ecosystem]], providing greater [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] and control over the underlying technology.

A primary use case for GPT-OSS-20B is [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] on custom datasets to adapt the model for specific applications. This capability enables users to imbue the model with a specific persona or [[concepts/experience|domain expertise]], making it suitable for specialized [[concepts/cloud-agents|AI agent development]]. By leveraging its open nature, developers can integrate the model into various workflows, tailoring its behavior and output to meet precise operational requirements.

The model serves as a bridge between [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] accessibility and the performance standards set by larger proprietary systems. Its design facilitates experimentation and [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in the field of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], particularly for those seeking to build customized solutions without relying on external API dependencies. This approach supports a more decentralized [[concepts/coding-workspace|development environment]] where the community can contribute to and benefit from advancements in [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] technology.
