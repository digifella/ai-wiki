# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is an Indian-American business executive who has served as the Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft since February 2014. He joined the company in 1992, spending over two decades in various technical and leadership roles before ascending to the top position. His appointment marked a pivotal moment for the technology giant, initiating a period of significant strategic transformation.

## Leadership and Strategic Shift

Under Nadella's leadership, Microsoft shifted its focus toward cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and subscription-based services. This repositioning emphasized collaboration and open-source compatibility, moving away from the company's previous proprietary-centric approach. The strategy successfully revitalized Microsoft's enterprise business, particularly through the growth of Azure, and fostered a cultural change within the organization centered on empathy and a "growth mindset."
