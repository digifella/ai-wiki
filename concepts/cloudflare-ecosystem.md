# Cloudflare Ecosystem

Cloudflare is a global content delivery network (CDN) and [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud infrastructure]] platform that provides services for website performance, [[concepts/security|security]], and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]]. The company operates a distributed network of [[concepts/techno-economics|data centers]] worldwide to deliver these services at scale. Its ecosystem comprises interconnected tools addressing web [[concepts/software-performance|performance optimization]], DDoS [[concepts/secure|protection]], DNS management, and application security.

## Core Services

Cloudflare's primary offerings include content delivery network services that cache and serve content from servers geographically closer to end users. The platform provides DNS hosting, DDoS mitigation, [[concepts/web-application|Web Application]] Firewall (WAF) functionality, and SSL/TLS certificate management. These services are designed to work together, allowing organizations to manage multiple aspects of their [[concepts/web-infrastructure|web infrastructure]] through a [[concepts/unified-interface|unified interface]].

## Email Service Beta

Cloudflare's [[concepts/email-service-beta|Email Service Beta]] extends the platform's capabilities into [[entities/email|email]] infrastructure, offering integrated email sending, routing, and [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]]. The service includes AI-driven features for email processing and management, positioning it as an addition to Cloudflare's broader application and infrastructure toolkit rather than a standalone email provider. This service allows customers to manage email operations alongside their existing Cloudflare configurations.

## Market Position

Cloudflare serves a range of customers from small websites to large enterprises, competing in the infrastructure-as-a-service space alongside providers like AWS, [[entities/google-cloud|Google Cloud]], and Akamai. The company's business model combines free-tier services with tiered premium offerings, enabling broad [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] while generating revenue from organizations requiring advanced features and higher performance tiers.
