## DeepMind AlphaGenome Atlas: AI Predicts Human Genome Variant Impact
**Clip title:** AlphaGenome [[concepts/atlas-computer|Atlas]]: Understanding the human genome
**Author / channel:** [[entities/google|Google]] DeepMind
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=U0aToL5C-bQ

### Summary
This video introduces [[entities/google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]]'s [[concepts/alphagenome-atlas|AlphaGenome Atlas]], a groundbreaking platform designed to demystify the [[concepts/human-genome|human genome]], which researchers refer to as "the language of life." The central challenge highlighted is the immense complexity and sheer scale of the human genome, comprising three billion base pairs. Scientists currently understand very little about how the intricate instructions within this genetic code interact or how specific mutations translate into observable human traits, known as phenotypes, or contribute to diseases. Manually deciphering this vast amount of information would take decades, making rapid scientific progress incredibly difficult.

To address this, AlphaGenome Atlas employs advanced AI modeling to predict the functional consequences of genetic mutations. The platform's core capability lies in its ability to analyze segments of the genome sequence and forecast the impact of individual changes. This analysis culminates in the Allelic Variant Impact (AVI) score, a single numerical value that quantifies how deleterious or impactful a specific genetic variant is. A higher AVI score suggests a greater likelihood of a variant affecting a phenotype or causing disease. The project has already pre-computed predictions for an astonishing nine billion possible single-letter changes across the human genome, offering an unprecedented scope of genetic insight.

The practical applications of AlphaGenome Atlas are poised to significantly accelerate genomic research. By distilling complex genomic data into a straightforward AVI score, the platform drastically reduces the "haystack" of genetic information, making it far easier for scientists to pinpoint critical mutations relevant to their studies. Described as the most comprehensive and accurate variant effect prediction platform, it leverages a massive petabyte of data. Importantly, Google DeepMind has also developed an accessible web-based "genome browser," enabling biologists, even those not proficient in coding, to visually explore and interpret complex genomic data, thereby democratizing access to cutting-edge genomic insights.

Ultimately, AlphaGenome Atlas represents a pivotal step towards building a comprehensive "thesaurus for the language of life." By integrating various specialized AI models, such as AlphaFold and AlphaGenome, into a unified framework, Google DeepMind aims to empower scientists worldwide. This initiative promises to fundamentally transform how genomic research is conducted, fostering much faster scientific progress and opening new avenues for understanding, diagnosing, and treating some of humanity's most challenging health conditions.

#### Description
We’ve used AlphaGenome to predict the molecular impact of every possible single-letter change in the human genome. All nine billion of them.

AlphaGenome Atlas is a 1PB dataset containing every single possible nucleotide variant alongside an AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score. This score helps researchers instantly understand the potential impact of any given variation.

AlphaGenome Atlas is available to researchers around the world, with coding and non-coding variations available.
