# Space Life Sciences

**Space Life Sciences** is an interdisciplinary field encompassing the study of biological processes in the [[concepts/vacuum-of-space|space environment]], including the effects of microgravity, radiation, and [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] on human physiology, plants, and microorganisms. It integrates [[concepts/astrobiology]], Human Factors, and Systems Biology to ensure crew [[concepts/health|health]] and mission [[concepts/success|success]].

## Core Domains

- **Human Health & Countermeasures**: Focuses on skeletal muscle atrophy, bone [[concepts/density|density]] loss, cardiovascular deconditioning, and immune system dysregulation.
- **Radiation Biology**: Analysis of cosmic ray impacts on DNA repair [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and cancer risk.
- **Plant Growth & Regenerative Life Support**: Optimization of Controlled Environment [[concepts/agriculture|Agriculture]] for food [[concepts/security|security]] and oxygen production.
- **Microbial Ecology**: Monitoring pathogen virulence changes and bioregenerative systems stability.

## Research Integration & Gaps

Recent commentary highlights significant disconnects between basic biological research and applied health outcomes in [[concepts/space-exploration|space missions]]. Key points from [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Bridging-the-Gaps-in-Space-Life-Sciences-and-Health-Rese|Bridging the Gaps in Space Life Sciences and Health Research]] (Nickerson, Ott, & Burer, Spring 2024) include:

- **Fragmented Data Silos**: Lack of standardized data sharing between [[concepts/nasa|NASA]], ESA, and [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] institutions hinders meta-analysis.
- **Translation Lag**: Basic findings in Radiation Biology are slow to translate into operational countermeasures for long-duration missions.
- **Interdisciplinary Barriers**: Insufficient collaboration between engineers (life support systems) and [[concepts/biology-researchers|life scientists]] (physiological monitoring).
- **Funding Misalignment**: Short-term grant cycles do not support the longitudinal studies required for multi-year mission [[concepts/conducting-health-screenings|health assessment]].
- **Recommendation**: Establish unified frameworks for [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]] and cross-agency collaboration to accelerate [[concepts/health-research|health research]] applicability.
