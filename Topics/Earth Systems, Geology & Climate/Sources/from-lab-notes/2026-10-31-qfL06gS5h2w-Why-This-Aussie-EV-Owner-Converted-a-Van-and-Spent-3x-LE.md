### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

The video features an interview with Kay, who converted a Citroën E-Dispatch van into Australia's first electric campervan. Kay shares the details of her conversion process, the van's features, and her experiences traveling with it.

Kay explains that she converted the van last year with a focus on lightness, sustainability, and adaptability. The van has a range of 320 kilometers and features AC and DC charging. It is equipped with a pull-out bed, storage shelves, a portable table, a fridge, a freezer, and induction cooktops. Kay also carries an electric bike and a portable power station to supplement the van's battery. She discusses her recent 3.5-week trip to Lake Eyre, highlighting the van's reliability and the ease of charging along the way.

Kay's conversion demonstrates the potential for electric campervans and challenges the misconception that electric vehicles are not suitable for long-distance travel. Her successful trip to Lake Eyre and the positive experience with charging stations showcase the viability of sustainable travel options.

#### Description
Can you actually road trip across the Australian Outback in an electric van? Meet Kay, who converted a secondhand Citroën ë-Dispatch into one of Australia's very first electric campervans! 

In this episode of "Ask An EV Owner", we do a full walkaround tour of Kay’s custom, lightweight, and sustainable DIY EV camper build. From dual-use handyman versatility and pull-out sofa beds to an induction cooktop kitchen setup and off-grid portable power station, she shows us how it all works.

Plus, Kay busts the myth that EVs can't tour regional Australia—sharing her 3.5-week, round-trip adventure to Lake Eyre / Kati Thanda and Marree, how she charged along the route (and at caravan parks for next to nothing), and how her fuel costs were less than a third of a diesel 4WD towing a caravan!
