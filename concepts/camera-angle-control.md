# Camera Angle Control

Camera Angle Control refers to the techniques and methods used to manage camera positioning and orientation within [[concepts/ai-image-editing|AI image editing]] workflows. In the context of [[concepts/gemini|Gemini AI]] image editing, this functionality is implemented through JSON-based control structures that enable users to specify precise camera angles and perspectives programmatically. This approach allows for [[concepts/granular-manipulation|granular manipulation]] of the virtual camera's position, [[concepts/focal-length|focal length]], and orientation relative to the subject.

[[concepts/camera-angle-manipulation|Nano Banana 2]] leverages JSON formatting to define these parameters, offering a structured and reproducible alternative to manual adjustment interfaces or slider controls. By [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] camera data into a machine-readable format, users can achieve consistent results across multiple generations and easily integrate camera specifications into automated editing pipelines. This method prioritizes [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and repeatability, ensuring that specific viewpoints can be replicated exactly without the variability inherent in manual input.
