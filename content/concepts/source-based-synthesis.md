# Source Based Synthesis

Source Based Synthesis is a method of aggregating and reorganizing information from source materials into new formats or presentations. Rather than creating content from general knowledge, this approach explicitly draws from and structures existing sources to produce coherent outputs tailored to specific purposes or audiences.

## Application in AI-Assisted Tools

[[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] demonstrates this method through its ability to process source documents and automatically generate customized presentations. By analyzing provided source materials, the tool synthesizes key information into design-formatted outputs suitable for professional contexts. This application shows how source-based synthesis can bridge research and presentation, converting raw source material into structured, audience-appropriate formats without requiring manual extraction and reformatting by the user.

The approach differs from general content generation by maintaining explicit grounding in source materials rather than relying on broad [[concepts/training-data|training data]]. This makes source-based synthesis particularly useful for contexts where traceability, [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], and relevance to specific materials are priorities, such as professional presentations, research summaries, or documentation that must clearly derive from identified sources.
