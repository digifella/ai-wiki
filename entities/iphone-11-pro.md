# iPhone 11 Pro

The **[[entities/iphone|iPhone]] 11 Pro** is a smartphone designed and marketed by [[entities/apple|Apple Inc.]] It was announced on September 10, 2019, and released on [[entities/usair-flight-5050|September 20]], 2019. It is part of the 13th generation of iPhones and succeeded the iPhone XS.

## Key Features
- **Triple-camera system:** First iPhone to feature three rear cameras (Wide, [[concepts/ultra-wide-lens|Ultra Wide]], and [[concepts/telephoto-lens|Telephoto]]).
- **[[concepts/night-mode|Night Mode]]:** Introduced in iOS 13, allowing for low-[[concepts/light|light]] photography.
- **A13 Bionic chip:** Enhanced performance and efficiency.
- **ProRAW support:** Later added via [[concepts/app-updates|software updates]] to allow for raw image capture.

## Astrophotography Context
While the iPhone 11 Pro was the first iPhone to feature Night Mode, its [[concepts/astrophotography|astrophotography]] capabilities were limited compared to later models. It lacked the sensor-shift stabilization and larger apertures found in subsequent Pro models.

### Evolution of iPhone Astrophotography
- **iPhone 11 Pro:** Initial introduction of computational Night Mode for low-light scenes.
- **iPhone 12 Pro:** Improved Night Mode with faster shutter speeds.
- **[[entities/iphone-13-pro|iPhone 13 Pro]]:** Enhanced [[concepts/sensor-size|sensor size]] and improved Night Mode performance.
- **[[entities/iphone-18-pro-max|iPhone 18 Pro Max]]:** Hypothetical future model featuring [[concepts/variable-aperture|variable aperture]] for advanced astrophotography.
