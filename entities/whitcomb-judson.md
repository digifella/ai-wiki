# Whitcomb Judson

Whitcomb Judson was an American inventor best known for inventing a type of [[concepts/zipper-history|fastening device]] that would eventually become the modern zipper. The early version of this invention was called "[[entities/clasp-locker|Clasp Locker]]," which was presented at the 1893 [[entities/earth|World]]'s Columbian Exposition in Chicago. Despite not being the commercial [[concepts/success|success]] it could have been, Judson's innovative design laid the groundwork for future developments.

## Key Points
- **Invention:** Clasp Locker (later known as zipper)
- **Date of Invention:** Late 19th century
- **First Public Presentation:** 1893 World's Columbian Exposition
