# Advanced Blending

Advanced blending techniques in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] are used to achieve [[concepts/pixel-transparency|pixel-perfect transparency]] and blend layers with precision. These methods allow for detailed [[concepts/power|control]] over how layers interact based on their properties like brightness or color.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/blend-if|Blend If]] Sliders**: Found within the Layer [[concepts/style|Style]]'s Advanced Blending options, these sliders provide precise layer [[concepts/opacity|opacity]] [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
  - Video [[concepts/tutorial|Tutorial]]: [Photoshop's "Blend If" Explained](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Wkti_IX3Qzk) by [[entities/jesús-ramirez|Jesús Ramirez]] on the Photoshop [[concepts/training|Training]] Channel. It covers:
    - Comprehensive breakdown of Blend If sliders
    - Control over layer transparency based on [[concepts/brightness-levels|brightness levels]] or individual colors
    - Technique to achieve pixel-perfect transparency quickly
