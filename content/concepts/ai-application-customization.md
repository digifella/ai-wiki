# AI application customization

The process of adapting [[concepts/machine-learning]] models and [[entities/ai]]-driven architectures to meet specific functional requirements, [[concepts/training-data|datasets]], or user-centric [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]].

## Key Platforms
- [[entities/hugging-face]]: A pivotal [[concepts/open-source]] ecosystem used to develop and tailor [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI-powered applications]].
    - Provides core functionalities that allow users to leverage pre-trained models for [[concepts/app-creation|application development]].
    - Enables the creation and [[concepts/personalization|customization]] of specialized AI-driven tools through accessible platform resources.
