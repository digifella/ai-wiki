# Preserve Details 20

Preserve Details 2.0 is an AI-powered upscaling feature within Adobe Photoshop designed to enlarge images while maintaining fine details and texture quality. As part of the software's Super Resolution capabilities, it utilizes generative artificial intelligence to improve upon traditional interpolation methods, which often result in blurred outputs or visual artifacts when scaling images to larger dimensions. The tool analyzes the content of an image to intelligently reconstruct detail rather than simply duplicating or blending existing pixels.

The underlying algorithm emulates the behavior of generative models to infer missing information, allowing for significant increases in resolution without the typical loss of sharpness associated with standard scaling techniques. This approach enables users to recover texture and edge definition that might otherwise be lost during the enlargement process, making it particularly useful for preparing low-resolution assets for high-output requirements.

The feature was highlighted in a tutorial by Colin Smith of PhotoshopCAFE, which demonstrated its application as one of three key upscaling tools within the Creative Cloud ecosystem. By leveraging these advanced computational methods, the tool provides a more robust alternative to conventional resizing filters, offering greater control over the final visual fidelity of upscaled media.
