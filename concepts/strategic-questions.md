# Strategic Questions

Strategic Questions is a framework designed to structure business presentations and narratives by treating AI tools as research and synthesis partners rather than autonomous content generators. This approach prioritizes human control over the organization of slides and the validation of content, ensuring that the final output aligns with specific strategic goals. By leveraging AI's capacity to process information and explore ideas, presenters can enhance their workflow without ceding editorial authority to the technology.

The methodology relies on asking targeted, deliberate questions before and during the creation process to clarify the core message and understand audience needs. These inquiries help establish a logical flow and ensure that each slide serves a distinct purpose within the broader narrative. This structured interaction prevents the common pitfall of generic AI outputs, allowing for a more coherent and persuasive presentation structure.

Implementing this framework requires a shift from passive prompting to active interrogation of AI capabilities. Presenters must define their objectives clearly and use AI to refine arguments, identify gaps in logic, or suggest alternative perspectives. This collaborative dynamic ensures that the technology supports the strategic intent of the presentation while maintaining the integrity and accuracy of the information presented.
