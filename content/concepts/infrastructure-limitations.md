# Infrastructure Limitations

The current organizational infrastructure presents several challenges that impact [[concepts/cost|operational efficiency]] and decision-making processes. The [[concepts/proposal-system|proposal system]] has proven particularly difficult to work with, creating [[concepts/friction|friction]] in how ideas and initiatives are evaluated and advanced. SharePoint serves as another significant pain point, limiting the [[concepts/organization|organization]]'s ability to manage and share information effectively across teams.

## Potential for AI-Assisted Analysis

One identified opportunity lies in leveraging [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to accelerate the analysis and review process. AI review systems could potentially handle routine analytical work, reducing manual effort and allowing teams to focus on higher-level strategic decisions. This approach addresses the need to improve speed in processing proposals and recommendations without expanding the existing staffing requirements.

The infrastructure limitations, while constraining current workflows, have prompted consideration of how emerging tools and technologies might help the organization work more efficiently within its existing systems.
