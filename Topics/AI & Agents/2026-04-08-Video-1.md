## NotebookLM Mind Maps Are Bad! But Gemini Fixes Them
**Clip title:** [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Mind Maps Are Bad! But [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] Fixes Them
**Author / channel:** Educraft
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0cCWbYLGysI

### Summary
This video demonstrates three methods to transform "boring" [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]]
generated by [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] into visually rich, engaging, and detailed versions
using Google's [[concepts/gemini-ai|Gemini AI]]. The main topic revolves around leveraging [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] to enhance [[concepts/information-visualization|information visualization]], making complex data more
digestible and shareable.

The first method involves generating a standard mind map within [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]
(which sources its information from uploaded documents or web research) and
then exporting it as an image. This image is subsequently uploaded to
[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], with a prompt instructing [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] to create a detailed mind map
using the image as a reference. This process, when Gemini's "create [[concepts/images|images]]"
and "thinking" modes are enabled, results in a significantly more appealing
mind map featuring descriptive icons and brief explanations for each [[entities/nodejs|node]]
and its connections. Users can regenerate the map if the initial output
isn't satisfactory.

The second method utilizes Gemini exclusively. Here, the user first prompts
Gemini to research a chosen topic (e.g., "the climate crisis for 7th
graders"), asking it to organize the information into clusters with bullet
points. In the same conversation, a follow-up prompt then asks Gemini to
generate a detailed mind map based on its previous response, complete with
visual elements and explanations. While this method is quicker as it
doesn't require prior [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] in [[entities/notebook-lm|NotebookLM]], the [[entities/speaker|speaker]] highlights a
slight risk of "AI hallucinations," meaning the generated information might
not be entirely accurate since Gemini is creating content from scratch
rather than from specific, pre-fed sources.

The third method, deemed the speaker's favorite, combines the strengths of
both tools within the Gemini interface. After enabling image generation and
[[concepts/thinking-mode|thinking mode]] in Gemini, instead of uploading an image, the user directly
integrates a [[concepts/notebooklm-notebook|NotebookLM notebook]] as a source. This allows for a prompt that
specifies the *exact* topic to visualize from the [[concepts/notebook|notebook]]'s content (e.g.,
"Alexander's Greatest Battles"). This approach ensures 100% [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]], as
Gemini is drawing information from the user-controlled sources within
NotebookLM while simultaneously enhancing its visual presentation. The
conclusion emphasizes that this [[concepts/integration|integration]] of Gemini's image generation
and visualization capabilities with NotebookLM's precise [source management](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Source_management)
offers the most effective way to produce highly accurate, customized, and
visually compelling mind maps. The video also briefly touches on methods to
correct minor errors in AI-generated images.
