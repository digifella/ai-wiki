# watchOS 27

**[[concepts/watchos-27|watchOS 27]]** is a major software update for the [[entities/apple|Apple]] Watch ecosystem, released in September 2026. It introduces significant UI improvements, battery optimizations, and new features, marking a shift in hardware support boundaries.

## Compatibility
Support has been restricted to newer hardware models. The following devices are compatible:
*   [[entities/apple-watch-se-3|Apple Watch SE 3]]
*   Apple Watch Series 9
*   Apple Watch Series 10
*   Apple Watch Series 11
*   Apple Watch Series 12
*   Apple Watch Ultra 2
*   [[entities/apple-watch-ultra-3|Apple Watch Ultra 3]]
*   Apple Watch Ultra 4

*Note: Older models are no longer supported.*

## Key Features & Improvements
*   **UI Enhancements:** Significant visual updates to the interface for improved readability and interaction.
*   **Battery Optimizations:** Improved power management algorithms to extend device longevity.
*   **Installation Methods:**
    *   Via the Watch app on the paired iPhone.
    *   Directly on the Apple Watch device.
