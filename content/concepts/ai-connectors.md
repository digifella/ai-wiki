# AI Connectors

AI connectors are integrations and APIs that link [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and language models—such as [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and similar systems—to existing [[concepts/software|applications]], workflows, and data sources. These connectors enable AI systems to interact across multiple platforms and services, allowing them to access information, execute [[concepts/commands|commands]], and trigger actions in [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]. By connecting [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] directly to enterprise software, databases, and third-party services, AI connectors reduce manual data transfer and context-switching between different applications.

## How They Work

AI connectors function as intermediary layers that translate requests from AI systems into [[entities/api-calls|API calls]] or commands that [[concepts/third-party-applications|external applications]] understand. When an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] receives a task, it can use these connectors to query databases, fetch real-time information, modify records, or initiate processes in connected tools. The connector handles [[concepts/authentication|authentication]], [[concepts/data-formatting|data formatting]], and error handling, allowing the AI system to operate seamlessly across disparate platforms without requiring direct access to underlying systems.

## Applications and Impact

Organizations use AI connectors to automate multi-step workflows that previously required manual intervention or custom integration work. Common applications include automating customer support [[concepts/responses|responses]] with access to ticketing systems, enabling AI agents to retrieve and analyze business data for reporting, or connecting AI [[concepts/writing|writing]] tools to content management systems. This [[concepts/automation|automation]] can significantly reduce repetitive tasks and allow creative and analytical work to be refined or generated more efficiently.
