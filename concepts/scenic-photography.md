# Scenic Photography

**Scenic photography** involves capturing landscapes, seascapes, and natural vistas with an emphasis on composition, lighting, and atmosphere. It often requires careful post-processing to enhance the mood and detail of the scene.

## Key Techniques

*   **Light Management:** Utilizing golden hour or blue hour for optimal lighting conditions.
*   **Composition:** Applying the rule of thirds, leading lines, and foreground interest.
*   **Post-Processing:** Enhancing dynamic range, color grading, and local adjustments.
