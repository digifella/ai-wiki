# Data Center Model

The traditional data center model relies on centralized, high-[[concepts/density|density]] server farms to provide [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] resources. Emerging paradigms challenge this by leveraging distributed, idle hardware across consumer devices.

## Decentralized Alternatives

Projects are increasingly utilizing [[concepts/consumer-grade-hardware|consumer-grade hardware]] to create decentralized [[concepts/computation|computing]] networks, reducing reliance on traditional [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].

- **[[concepts/decentralized-ai-inference|Darkbloom]]**: A novel project aiming to revolutionize [[concepts/ai-inference]] by creating a distributed, [[concepts/peer-to-peer-network|peer-to-peer network]] utilizing the [[concepts/idle-computing-power|idle computing power]] of individual users' [[entities/apple|Apple]] [[concepts/silicon|Silicon]] Macs.
- **Mechanism**: Harnesses Apple Silicon efficiency for cost-effective, scalable [[concepts/model-inference|inference]] tasks.
- **Economic Model**: Enables users to earn revenue by contributing idle compute resources.
