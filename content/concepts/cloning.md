# Cloning

Cloning is a process of creating a genetically identical copy of an organism through various techniques. The most famous example is Dolly the sheep, who was cloned in 1996 using somatic cell nuclear transfer (SCNT).

## Overview
- Biological replication without sexual reproduction.
- Utilizes SCNT for animals and plants.
- [[concepts/software|Applications]] [[concepts/range|range]] from medical research to agricultural advancements.

### Challenges
- Genetic abnormalities due to methylation issues.
- Limited [[concepts/success-rates|success rates]] in [[concepts/de-extinction|mammalian cloning]].
- Ethical concerns regarding human cloning.

### Recent Studies
- A 20-year Japanese study published in *[[entities/nature|Nature]] Communications* [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the limitations of [[concepts/serial-cloning|serial cloning]] in mammals.
    - Reveals significant genetic dead ends after multiple generations of cloning.
    - Challenges [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] like de-extinction and [[concepts/biological-immortality|biological immortality through cloning]].
