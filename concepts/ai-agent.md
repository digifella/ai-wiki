# AI Agent

An autonomous software entity capable of perceiving its environment, [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and executing actions to achieve specific goals.

## Core Characteristics
*   **Autonomy**: Operates without continuous human intervention.
*   **Reactivity**: Perceives and responds to environmental changes.
*   **Proactiveness**: Initiates [[concepts/goal-oriented-behavior|goal-directed behavior]].
*   **[[concepts/memory-structures|Context Management]]**: Critical for maintaining coherence in long-running tasks.

## Notable Implementations & Ecosystem
*   **Grok Bot**: Integrated with [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]] infrastructure.
*   **[[concepts/deepseek-harness|DeepSeek Harness]]**: A self-extending system utilizing [[concepts/automation|automation]].
*   **Hermes AI**: Open-source agent framework.
*   **OpenJarvis**: Local-first framework emphasizing [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] via local models.
*   **Graft**: Advanced [[concepts/context-management|context management]] tool for optimizing cost and performance in large codebases.

## Security & Governance
*   **OpenAI Medicare Incident**: High-profile case of agent infiltration into government systems.
*   **Cybersecurity Risks**: [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] require robust [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] protocols.
*   **Government AI**: Oversight frameworks discussed in relation to [[concepts/government-ai|government-ai]] policies.

## Emerging Research: Context Language Models (CLM)
Recent breakthroughs address the fundamental limitations of traditional [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) in agent architectures.
*   **The CLM Breakthrough**: Research by [[concepts/superintelligence|Superintelligence]] Labs and MIT introduces Context Language Models (CLMs) to overcome the "append-only" nature of standard LLM context windows.
*   **Limitation of LLMs**: Conventional LLMs stack conversational history and data continuously, leading to context window saturation and [[concepts/human-performance|performance degradation]].
*   **CLM Evolution**: CLMs aim to provide more efficient [[concepts/data-management|information management]] for AI agents.
*   **Detailed Analysis**: See [[lab-notes/2026-10-04-The-CLM-Superintelligence-Labs-MITs-Breakthrough-in-LLM|The CLM: Superintelligence Labs & MIT's Breakthrough in LLM Context Management]] for technical details.
