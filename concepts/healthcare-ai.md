# Healthcare AI

## Overview
The application of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] in [[concepts/clinical-diagnostics|medical diagnostics]], treatment planning, and administrative workflows. Key tensions exist between efficiency gains and the risk of automation bias, particularly regarding [[concepts/doctor-replacement|Doctor Replacement]] Debate and [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]].

## Key Themes

### Doctor Replacement Debate
- Growing discourse on whether AI can fully substitute clinical judgment, highlighted by recent commentary suggesting AI should replace doctors [[lab-notes/2026-09-04-Healthcare-AI-Doctor-Replacement-Debate-Scribe-Errors-an|Healthcare AI: Doctor Replacement Debate, Scribe Errors, and Oversight Needs]].
- Concerns center on the loss of human [[concepts/empathy|empathy]] and the potential for systemic errors if oversight is removed.

### AI Scribe Errors & Data Integrity
- **[[concepts/data-hallucination|Hallucination]] Risks:** AI scribes may invent drug histories or clinical details not present in the patient record.
- **[[concepts/verification|Verification]] Necessity:** Requires strict human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|loop]] oversight to validate generated [[concepts/notes|notes]] against source audio/visual data.
- **Impact:** Errors in documentation can lead to incorrect treatment plans or legal liabilities.

### Oversight Needs
- **Human-in-the-Loop:** Essential for validating AI-generated clinical data.
- **Audit Trails:** Need for transparent logging of AI contributions to medical records.
- **Regulatory [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]]:** Ensuring [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] meet [[concepts/medical-device|medical device]] standards and data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] laws.
