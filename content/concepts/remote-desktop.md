## Remote Desktop Overview
Remote desktop is a form of technology that allows users to access and [[concepts/power|control]] the graphical [[concepts/user-interface|user interface]] of one computer from another computer or mobile device. This [[concepts/connection|connection]] is usually established over a network (such as the internet) and enables remote management, troubleshooting, collaboration, and access to [[concepts/files|files]] and [[concepts/software|applications]].

### Features & Benefits
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]:** Access your desktop from anywhere.
- **Collaboration:** Share screens and workspaces in real-time with others.
- **Management:** [[concepts/power|Control]] multiple devices centrally from a single location.
- **Maintenance:** Remotely troubleshoot and resolve issues without physical presence.

### Security Considerations
- Encrypting connections to protect data transfer.
- Limiting access to [[concepts/secure|secure]] networks only.
- Regularly updating software to patch vulnerabilities.
