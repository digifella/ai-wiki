# Trade disruption

The interruption of the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of goods, energy, or services through maritime, aerial, or terrestrial corridors via kinetic, political, or economic interference.

### Primary Mechanisms
- AD ([[concepts/a2ad|Anti-Access/Area Denial]]) operations designed to restrict transit within contested theaters.
- Blockades targeting critical maritime choke points.
- Deployment of [[concepts/ballistic-missiles|Ballistic Missiles]] and Asymmetric Warfare to strike logistics and transit infrastructure.
- Utilization of [[concepts/bunkers|Bunkers]] and hardened military assets to sustain long-term denial of access.

### Global Impacts
- Destabilization of Global [[concepts/supply-chains|Supply Chains]].
- Fluctuations in Energy [[concepts/security|Security]] and commodity market volatility.
- Heightened Geopolitical Risk in international shipping lanes.

### Recent Military Lessons (2026)
- Observations from the Iran-US-[[entities/israel|Israel]] [[concepts/conflict|Conflict]]:
    - Tactical efficacy of Blockades in disrupting maritime commerce.
    - The role of missile-based technology in facilitating large-scale trade denial.
    - The impact of modern munitions on the viability of established transit corridors.
