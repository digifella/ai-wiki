# Archival Preservation

Archival preservation encompasses the practices and processes used to maintain historical documents, records, and materials in their original or stable condition for long-term access and research. This discipline combines conservation science, environmental management, and institutional planning to protect archives from physical deterioration, environmental damage, and loss. [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]] efforts ensure that primary sources remain available to scholars, researchers, and the public for future generations.

## Physical Conservation

Physical preservation involves controlling the conditions under which archival materials are stored and handled. Temperature, humidity, light [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], and air quality are critical factors managed through climate-controlled facilities and proper [[entities/storage|storage]] systems. Conservation professionals employ techniques to stabilize damaged materials, including cleaning, repair, and sometimes treatment to arrest degradation caused by age, handling, or previous poor storage conditions.

## Digitization and Access

[[concepts/digital-preservation|Digital preservation]] has become an increasingly important component of archival practice, allowing institutions to create backup copies while simultaneously improving public access. Digitization projects convert paper documents, photographs, and other analog materials into digital formats, reducing wear on originals while enabling remote research. However, digital preservation itself presents ongoing challenges, as formats and technologies continue to evolve and require ongoing migration strategies to prevent obsolescence.

## Institutional Standards

Archives maintain preservation through established institutional frameworks, including documented [[concepts/policies|policies]], trained staff, and dedicated resources. Professional standards set by organizations such as archival associations guide [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]] in material handling, storage design, and conservation procedures. Institutional commitment to preservation reflects the understanding that archives serve as essential historical records and [[concepts/cultural-heritage|cultural heritage]] resources.
