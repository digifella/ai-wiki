# Audio Editing

**[[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]] editing** is the process of manipulating and modifying digital audio signals. It involves tasks such as cutting, splicing, crossfading, and applying effects to improve quality or create artistic compositions.

## Key Tools & Software

### Audacity
Audacity is a widely used free, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] audio-editing software. The ecosystem continues to evolve with new releases focusing on usability and performance.

## Core Concepts

- **Waveform Editing:** Direct manipulation of the audio amplitude over time.
- **[[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-Destructive Editing]]:** Editing without permanently altering the original source file.
- **Effects Processing:** Applying [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] such as noise reduction, compression, or equalization.
