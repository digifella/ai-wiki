# Gemini 25 Models

[[entities/gemini|Gemini 2.5]] is a multimodal [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] model developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] designed to process and generate content across text, images, and other data formats. As a multimodal system, it accepts diverse input types and produces corresponding outputs, enabling simultaneous [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and analysis across different modalities. This architecture allows the model to integrate information from various sources, making it suitable for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] that require cross-domain understanding.

In the context of [[concepts/cloud-agents|AI agent development]], Gemini 2.5 models serve as the computational foundation for multimodal [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] tools built on [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph]]. LangGraph provides a framework for constructing stateful, multi-actor applications, allowing agents to manage [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes. By leveraging Gemini 2.5's capabilities within this structure, these tools can perform [[concepts/comprehensive-investigations|comprehensive investigations]] that involve analyzing mixed media inputs and synthesizing results dynamically.

The integration of Gemini 2.5 into LangGraph-based systems enhances the ability of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to handle intricate research queries. The model's capacity to reason across multiple data types supports the agent's ability to gather, evaluate, and correlate information from disparate sources. This combination facilitates the creation of robust research assistants capable of delivering detailed and accurate findings in environments requiring high levels of [[concepts/ai-agent-context|contextual awareness]] and [[concepts/multi-modal-input|multimodal processing]].
