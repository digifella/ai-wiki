# Power Grid Outage

The term "[[concepts/electricity-grid|power grid]] outage" refers to a situation where a significant portion of an electrical power [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] network experiences an interruption in service. This can occur due to various factors including [[concepts/severe-weather-events|extreme weather]] conditions, equipment failure, or human error.

## 2021 Texas Power Grid Outage: Technical Analysis and ERCOT Actions

- The event was triggered by a major [[concepts/winter-storm|winter storm]] that brought record low temperatures across the U.S. Central Plains.
- Widespread power and water outages occurred in [[entities/texas|Texas]], leading to loss of life and significant economic damage.
- Detailed technical and chronological explanation available [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Practical Engineering - What Really Happened During the Texas Power Grid Outage?.
- Key entities involved include [[concepts/ercot-electric-reliability-council-of-texas|ERCOT (Electric Reliability Council of Texas)]] and various power generation companies in Texas.
