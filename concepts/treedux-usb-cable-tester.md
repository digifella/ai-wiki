## Overview
The TreeDux USB Cable Tester is a $50 diagnostic device designed to resolve USB [[concepts/cable-compatibility|cable compatibility]] issues by revealing the true capabilities of various cables. It addresses the common frustration of identifying unknown or mislabeled cables.

## Key Features & Insights
- **Demystification:** Identifies the actual specifications of mystery cables, distinguishing between marketing claims and [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]].
- **Compatibility Analysis:** Helps users understand why certain devices fail to charge or transfer data at expected speeds.
- **Standard Identification:** Clarifies differences between USB-A, [[concepts/usb-30|USB-C]], and legacy standards.
- **Performance [[concepts/verification|Verification]]:** Tests for data transfer rates and power delivery capabilities.
