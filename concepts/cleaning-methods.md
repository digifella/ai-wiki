# Cleaning Methods

## Label and Residue Removal

### Effective Methods for Glass and Plastic
*Source: [[lab-notes/2026-10-04-Effective-Methods-for-Removing-Labels-and-Residue-from-G|Effective Methods for Removing Labels and Residue from Glass and Plastic]]*

*   **Target Surfaces:** Applicable to various [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]] including plastic tubs, bottles, and glass jars.
*   **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]:** Facilitates reuse or recycling by removing [[concepts/adhesive-residue|adhesive residue]].
*   **Key Techniques:**
    *   Utilization of heat and solvents to loosen adhesive bonds.
    *   Mechanical removal of bulk label material prior to residue treatment.
    *   Application of oil-based or alcohol-based cleaners for final residue cleanup.

### General Cleaning Principles
*   **[[concepts/preparation|Preparation]]:** Empty and rinse containers to remove food debris.
*   **Safety:** Ensure adequate ventilation when using chemical solvents.
*   **Sustainability:** Prioritize methods that allow for material recovery (recycling) or extended lifecycle (reuse).
