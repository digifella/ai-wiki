# Note Organization

Note Organization refers to the systematic methods and tools used to [[concepts/structure|structure]], categorize, and retrieve information captured in digital [[concepts/notes|notes]]. Effective note organization enables users to store information in a way that supports both immediate access and long-term [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] across personal, professional, and research contexts.

## Digital Note Systems

Modern note organization typically relies on digital platforms that support various organizational structures. These systems may employ tagging systems, folder hierarchies, linked references, or a combination of these approaches. The choice of [[concepts/organization|organization]] method depends on individual [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] preferences and the volume of information being managed. Platforms like [[entities/evernote|Evernote]], [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]], and similar tools provide different architectural approaches to organizing notes, from traditional folder-based systems to more flexible linking-based structures.

## Integration and Workflow

Contemporary note organization often benefits from [[concepts/integration|integration]] with other tools and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] assistance. Users increasingly combine multiple [[concepts/software|applications]]—such as [[concepts/note-taking-software|note-taking platforms]] with [[concepts/code|code]] editors or AI assistants—to enhance their ability to capture, process, and retrieve information. These integrations can streamline workflows by allowing notes to be created, organized, and enhanced through multiple interfaces and automated processes.
