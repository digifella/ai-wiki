# Quality Gates

Quality gates are structured checkpoints designed to systematically evaluate information or outputs before they advance within a process or reach stakeholders. They function as verification barriers that prevent flawed, ambiguous, or incomplete work from progressing without scrutiny. Rather than accepting outputs by default, quality gates require explicit assessment against predefined criteria covering accuracy, clarity, completeness, and relevance to the intended context or use case.

## Purpose and Function

The primary purpose of quality gates is to identify and filter out incorrect, unclear, or incomplete information before it impacts downstream decisions or final deliverables. By enforcing rigorous critiques at specific stages, organizations can catch errors early, reducing the cost of rework and ensuring that only validated content moves forward. This approach shifts the evaluation process from a passive review to an active verification step, ensuring that all outputs meet established standards of quality and utility.

## Implementation and Criteria

Effective quality gates rely on clear, measurable criteria tailored to the specific domain or project requirements. These criteria typically include checks for factual accuracy, logical consistency, adherence to style guides, and alignment with strategic objectives. The implementation often involves designated reviewers or automated tools that assess the output against these benchmarks. If an output fails to meet the threshold, it is returned for revision, creating a feedback loop that improves the overall quality of the final result.
