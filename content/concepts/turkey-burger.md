# Turkey Burger

[[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] burgers are a popular alternative to beef burgers for those looking to reduce their fat and cholesterol intake while still enjoying the taste of [[concepts/ground-meat|ground meat]]. Here's an enhanced way to prepare your next batch of turkey burgers:

## Ingredients
- Ground Turkey
- [[concepts/baking-soda|Baking Soda]] (optional, see below)
- Salt
- Pepper

## Preparation Tips
- Mix in a small amount of [[concepts/baking-soda|baking soda]] with the ground turkey before shaping into patties for improved [[concepts/texture|texture]] and juiciness. Refer to 2026 04 12 Enhancing [[concepts/ground-meat|Ground Meat]] Baking Sodas Effect on Texture Juiciness and Bro for detailed [[concepts/instructions|instructions]].

## Additional Information
- [[concepts/baking-soda|Baking soda]] can be added at a ratio of 1/4 teaspoon per pound of [[concepts/ground-meat|ground meat]]. It helps to tenderize the protein and retain moisture during cooking.
- Season with salt and pepper before [[concepts/bbq|grilling]] or [[concepts/skillet-frying|pan-frying]] your turkey burgers.
- Preheat your grill or skillet for best results.
