# Nursing Education

## Overview
Nursing education encompasses the formal training, professional development, and continuous [[concepts/learning|learning]] required to prepare individuals for practice in [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] settings. It integrates clinical [[concepts/skills|skills]], theoretical knowledge, and ethical standards to ensure competent, [[concepts/personalised-care|patient-centered care]].

## Key Components
- **Curriculum Standards**: Aligned with regulatory bodies such as the NMC (Nursing and Midwifery Council) and HEE ([[concepts/health-promotion|Health Education]] [[entities/united-kingdom|England]]).
- **Clinical Placements**: Practical [[concepts/experience|experience]] in diverse [[concepts/health|healthcare]] environments.
- **Professional Development**: Ongoing education to maintain registration and adapt to evolving healthcare technologies.
