# Limitations And Assumptions

The Limitations and Assumptions (LA) approach is a methodology designed to enhance the quality and reliability of AI-generated notes. When applied alongside the QEC (Question, Evidence, Conclusion) framework, LA assists users in critically evaluating the boundaries and foundational premises of AI-generated content. This dual-framework strategy addresses the common challenge of distinguishing between claims supported by solid evidence and those that rely on unstated assumptions or operate within specific constraints.

The core function of the LA approach involves prompting users to identify the implicit conditions under which an AI's output is valid. By explicitly mapping out these constraints, users can better understand the scope of the information provided and recognize where the AI's reasoning may break down or become speculative. This process encourages a more rigorous examination of the data, ensuring that conclusions are not accepted at face value but are instead scrutinized for their underlying logical structure.

Integrating LA with the QEC framework creates a more robust analytical process. While QEC structures the logical flow from inquiry to resolution, LA adds a layer of critical distance by highlighting what is known, what is assumed, and what is excluded. This combination helps mitigate the risk of hallucination or overgeneralization by forcing a conscious assessment of the AI's operational limits, thereby producing notes that are more accurate, transparent, and useful for decision-making.
