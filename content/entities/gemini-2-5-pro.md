# Gemini 2.5 Pro

Gemini 2.5 Pro is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] as part of the [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] family of AI systems. The model is positioned as a mid-tier offering within the Gemini product line, designed to balance capability and efficiency across a range of [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]] tasks.

## Deep Think Capabilities

A defining feature of Gemini 2.5 Pro is its Deep Think functionality, which enables extended [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] over complex problems. This capability allows the model to allocate additional [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] steps, potentially improving [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and coherence on tasks that benefit from deliberate, multi-step analysis.

## Use Cases

Gemini 2.5 Pro has been referenced in contexts involving autonomous [[concepts/agents|agents]], [[concepts/multimodal-reasoning|multimodal reasoning]], and code-assisted workflows. Its design appears to target scenarios where models integrate with [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]] and knowledge systems, including integration with [[concepts/developer-platforms|development environments]] and content generation pipelines.
