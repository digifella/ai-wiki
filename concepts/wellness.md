# Wellness

Wellness is a multidimensional [[concepts/world-health-organization-definition-of-health|health concept]] that extends beyond the absence of disease to encompass overall physical, mental, and [[concepts/mental-health|social well-being]]. Rather than focusing solely on illness [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]], wellness describes an active, ongoing process of making choices and developing practices that contribute to a healthier and more fulfilling life. The term gained prominence in [[concepts/health-promotion|health promotion]] and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] contexts during the late 20th century, offering a more holistic [[concepts/framework-for-understanding|framework for understanding]] health than the traditional medical model.

## Dimensions of Wellness

Wellness typically comprises several interconnected dimensions. Physical wellness includes [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]], [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], and [[concepts/sleep|sleep]]; mental wellness encompasses emotional [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] and cognitive function; social wellness involves meaningful [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and community [[concepts/connection|connection]]; and spiritual wellness relates to purpose and personal values. Many models also include occupational, financial, and environmental dimensions. These aspects are understood as interdependent rather than separate categories.

## Connection to Mental Health

Wellness is closely linked to mental health outcomes. Research has documented associations between [[concepts/healthy-lifestyle|wellness practices]] and reduced symptoms of [[concepts/depression|depression]], [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]], and other mental health conditions. Conversely, poor wellness outcomes—such as physical inactivity, social [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], or chronic [[concepts/stress|stress]]—are recognized risk factors for depression and other mental health difficulties. This bidirectional relationship underpins many mental health interventions that incorporate wellness-focused approaches.

## Application in Health Systems

Wellness has become integrated into [[entities/clinical-practice|clinical practice]], workplace health [[concepts/software|programs]], and public health initiatives. It emphasizes individual agency and personal [[concepts/accountability|responsibility]] while acknowledging that wellness is influenced by [[concepts/social-determinants-of-health|social determinants]] and structural factors beyond individual control. The concept continues to evolve, with ongoing debate about how to measure wellness outcomes and ensure equitable access to wellness resources across populations.
