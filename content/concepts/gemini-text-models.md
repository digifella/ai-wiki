# Gemini Text Models

[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] [[concepts/text|Text]] [[concepts/models|Models]] are [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s family of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] designed for text-based tasks within the [[entities/google|Google]] [[concepts/ai-ecosystem|AI ecosystem]]. These models serve as foundational components for various [[entities/google-ai|Google AI]] products and integrations, including [[concepts/generative-uiux|AI-native design]] tools and development platforms. The models are available across different capability tiers to support diverse [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and computational requirements.

## Integration with Google Products

Gemini text models function as the [[concepts/natural-language-processing-nlp|language processing]] backbone for Google's [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]], including [[concepts/design|design]] and creative tools like [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|Google Stitch]]. They enable natural language understanding and generation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that [[concepts/power|power]] features such as prompt-based design, [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] via natural language, and AI-assisted [[concepts/ai-driven-workflow-automation|workflow automation]]. The models integrate with Google's broader [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] to provide consistent performance across the company's product ecosystem.

## Model Variants and Availability

Google offers Gemini text models in multiple configurations, including the [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[entities/gemma|Gemma]] series under permissive licenses like [[concepts/apache-2.0|Apache 2.0]], as well as closed commercial variants. These models span different scale levels, from lightweight versions optimized for [[concepts/edge-deployment|edge deployment]] to larger variants designed for [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks. The availability of both open and proprietary options reflects Google's strategy of serving different [[concepts/developer|developer]] and enterprise needs.
