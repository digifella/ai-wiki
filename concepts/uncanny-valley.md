# Uncanny Valley

The **uncanny valley** is a hypothesis in [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] and computer [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] describing the emotional response of humans to non-human [[concepts/nodes|entities]] that appear almost, but not quite, human.
