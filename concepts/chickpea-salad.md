# Chickpea Salad

Chickpea salad is a cold dish combining cooked chickpeas with fresh vegetables and a light dressing, typically served as a side dish or light main course. The base ingredient, chickpeas (also called garbanzo beans), are legumes native to the Mediterranean and Middle East regions and are now cultivated worldwide. A standard preparation includes chickpeas mixed with vegetables such as tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions, dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, or vinegar, and seasoned with herbs and spices like parsley, cumin, or garlic.

## Nutritional Profile

Chickpeas are rich in plant-based protein and dietary fiber, making chickpea salad a relatively satiating dish. The combination of protein and fiber helps promote fullness and can support weight management by reducing overall calorie intake throughout the day. A typical serving also provides essential micronutrients including folate, iron, and manganese, along with heart-healthy monounsaturated fats from olive oil dressing.

## Preparation and Variations

The salad can be prepared with dried chickpeas that are soaked and cooked, or with canned chickpeas for convenience. Vegetable additions and dressing ingredients vary widely based on regional traditions and personal preference. Common variations include Mediterranean versions with feta cheese, Middle Eastern preparations with tahini-based dressings, and Indian-inspired versions incorporating yogurt and warm spices. The dish can be made in advance and stored in the refrigerator, making it practical for meal preparation.
