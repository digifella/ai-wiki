# Y Combinator

Y Combinator is a startup accelerator and venture capital firm founded in 2005 by [[entities/paul|Paul]] Graham, Jessica Livingston, Robert Morris, and Trevor Blackwell. Based in San Francisco, it provides seed funding, mentorship, and resources to early-stage technology companies, typically in exchange for a small equity stake. The [[concepts/organization|organization]] operates through intensive batch [[concepts/software|programs]] that last three months, during which founders receive guidance on product development, fundraising, and business strategy.

The accelerator has become one of the most influential startup funding organizations globally, having invested in thousands of companies since its inception. Notable Y Combinator-backed companies include Airbnb, Dropbox, Stripe, and Reddit. The organization's selection process is highly competitive, and acceptance into a Y Combinator batch is considered a significant validation for early-stage founders seeking to scale their ventures.

In recent years, Y Combinator has increasingly engaged with developments in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and autonomous systems, reflecting broader trends in the technology sector. The organization continues to fund and support companies working on AI-related projects and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tools that address contemporary challenges in [[concepts/coding|software development]] and [[concepts/automation|automation]].
