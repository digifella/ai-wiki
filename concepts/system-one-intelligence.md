# System One intelligence

**[[concepts/system-one-model|System One]] intelligence** refers to fast, intuitive, and reliable decision-making processes, often contrasted with slower, deliberative [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. In the context of AI, it emphasizes immediate, high-confidence outputs suitable for automation.

## Key Characteristics
- **Speed:** Prioritizes low-latency responses over exhaustive analysis.
- **Reliability:** Focuses on accuracy in specific domains (e.g., code) rather than broad generative creativity.
- **Automation:** Designed for direct integration into workflows without human-in-the-loop verification for routine tasks.
