[[entities/profit-studio|Profit Studio]] is a channel producing tutorials for building modern [[concepts/web-applications|web applications]]. Key [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]]: **Build Full-stack WebApp plus Firebase** (video: [https://youtu.be/juEfJERgBE8](https://youtu.be/juEfJERgBE8)).

- **Core Technologies:** Firebase backend, [[entities/google-ai-studio]] frontend
- **App Features:**
  - [[concepts/user-authentication|User authentication]] (sign up/login)
  - User-specific data [[entities/storage|storage]] and management
  - [[concepts/file-uploads|File uploads]] with data [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] (users only see their own files)
- **Goal:** Create an app where users manage their own data without cross-user access.
