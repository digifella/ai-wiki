## Open Source, Faster Jev is HERE
**Clip title:** Open Source, Faster Jev is HERE
**Author / channel:** CoderOne
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kWToHpdxScE

### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

### Main Topic

The video discusses the capabilities and use cases of new "System 1" AI models, specifically focusing on "Jev" by TypeSafe AI and the open-source "Laya" model. These models are designed for fast classification and structured decision-making rather than conversational text generation, enabling rapid, real-time automation.

### Key Points Discussed

*   **System 1 AI Models:** The speaker explains the concept of "System 1" thinking—fast, instinctive, and emotional—as applied to AI, contrasted with slower "System 2" reasoning models like traditional LLMs. Jev and Laya output structured JSON decisions (e.g., boolean, choice, or scores) in sub-millisecond timeframes.
*   **Performance and Speed:** Laya achieves around 30 decisions per second with inference times under 30 milliseconds on local hardware (like Apple Silicon). 
*   **Practical Applications:** Users have demonstrated Jev and Laya powering real-time tasks like playing video games (Snake and Super Mario Bros.), classifying high-intent sales leads for personalized outreach, and controlling web browsers for tasks like booking flights or reading specific articles.
*   **Open-Source Alternatives:** The open-source model **Laya** and related projects like **Kev** provide local, open-weight alternatives to Jev, allowing users to run similar decision engines locally without API costs.

### Conclusions and Takeaways

The speaker views the emergence of open-weight System 1 decision models as a game-changer for AI automation. Because these models can make structured decisions in milliseconds on local hardware, they open the door for ultra-fast, highly automated workflows ranging from gaming bots to browser agents. The speaker is optimistic that open-source alternatives like Laya will make these capabilities widely accessible and significantly shift perspectives on AI utility.
