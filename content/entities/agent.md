# Agent

An agent in knowledge systems refers to an autonomous or semi-autonomous computational entity designed to perceive its environment, make decisions, and take actions to achieve specific objectives. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and knowledge systems, [[concepts/agents|agents]] typically operate with some degree of independence, processing information and executing tasks with minimal human intervention.

## Characteristics

Agents within knowledge systems generally possess the ability to interact with their operating environment—whether that environment is digital, physical, or a combination of both. They combine information processing capabilities with decision-making logic to perform tasks ranging from simple [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] to complex [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. The sophistication of an agent depends on the complexity of its underlying logic and the tools available to it.

## Applications

Contemporary applications of agents in knowledge systems include autonomous research assistants, code-execution agents integrated with language models, and systems designed for data processing and integration tasks. Such agents can leverage multiple capabilities—including code execution, [[concepts/web-crawling|web scraping]], file management, and [[concepts/integration|API integration]]—to accomplish multifaceted objectives more efficiently than single-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] tools.

## Integration with AI Systems

Modern agents often function as extensions of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], enhancing their practical utility by enabling them to execute actions beyond [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]]. This integration allows agents to bridge the gap between [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and implementation, making them increasingly relevant for [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]], [[concepts/coding|software development]], and [[concepts/ai-driven-research|automated research workflows]].
