# Survival Threshold

A survival threshold is the minimum set of conditions or [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] required for an entity to persist and function within a given environment. In the context of [[concepts/multi-agent-simulation|Sakana AI's Digital Ecosystems]] research, the concept applies to simulated [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] competing in computational environments where resource availability, task performance, and behavioral adaptation determine which agents continue to operate and which are eliminated from the system.

## Application in Digital Ecosystems

Sakana AI's Digital Ecosystems project uses survival thresholds to study how [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems interact, compete, and coexist when subjected to environmental pressures analogous to natural selection. Agents must meet minimum performance [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] to access resources, reproduce, or maintain their presence in the simulation. These thresholds create selective pressure that shapes the evolution of AI behaviors and strategies over time.

## Implications for AI Research

By establishing and varying survival thresholds within digital ecosystems, researchers can observe emergent behaviors, cooperation patterns, and evolutionary dynamics among AI species. This approach provides insights into how AI systems might develop diverse strategies for [[concepts/data-persistence|persistence]], whether through [[concepts/specialization|specialization]], cooperation, or [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]. The framework bridges [[concepts/evolutionary-biology|evolutionary biology]] concepts with artificial intelligence, offering a novel lens for understanding multi-[[entities/agent|agent]] system dynamics and long-term AI behavior patterns.
