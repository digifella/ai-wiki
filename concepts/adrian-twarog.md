# Adrian Twarog

Adrian Twarog is a content creator and educator specializing in agentic AI and autonomous AI agents. He produces instructional videos and written educational material designed to explain how agent-based AI systems function, including their architecture, configuration, and deployment. His work targets audiences seeking to understand these technologies without requiring extensive prior knowledge in AI or programming.

## Educational Focus

Twarog’s content primarily focuses on the practical implementation and theoretical underpinnings of agentic systems. He breaks down complex concepts such as tool use, memory management, and planning algorithms into accessible formats. By demonstrating specific configurations and workflows, he aims to bridge the gap between abstract AI theory and actionable development practices for learners at various technical levels.
