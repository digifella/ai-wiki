## Agentic RAG Systems

[[concepts/retrieval-augmented-generation-rag|Agentic RAG systems]] represent an evolution of [[concepts/traditional-rag|traditional RAG]] approaches by incorporating [[concepts/ai-agent|autonomous agent]] [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] into the retrieval and generation pipeline. Rather than passively retrieving documents and generating [[concepts/responses|responses]], these systems enable [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to actively plan retrieval strategies, evaluate information relevance, and iteratively refine queries. This approach proves particularly valuable in complex security infrastructure [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where multiple data sources must be consulted and synthesized to provide comprehensive answers.
