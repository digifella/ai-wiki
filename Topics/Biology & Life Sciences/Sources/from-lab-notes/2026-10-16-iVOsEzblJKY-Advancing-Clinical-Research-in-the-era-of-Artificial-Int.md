Professor Karin Verspoor’s presentation, "Advancing Clinical Research in the Era of Artificial Intelligence," explores how AI can transform clinical research and healthcare decision-making. She discusses the limitations of traditional randomized controlled trials (RCTs), which often focus on an "average patient" and have strict inclusion criteria that do not reflect diverse populations. Verspoor advocates for "precision medicine" and leveraging big data, including electronic health records (EHRs) and unstructured text, to gain deeper insights into patient care.

Verspoor emphasizes the potential of natural language processing (NLP) to extract valuable information from clinical notes and unstructured data, aiding in symptom identification, visit categorization, and condition detection. She also highlights the challenges of integrating data across various sources and emphasizes the need for governance, consent policies, and robust cybersecurity frameworks. Ultimately, Verspoor envisions a future where AI and data science enable personalized healthcare, allowing clinicians to make decisions grounded in real-world evidence and improving patient outcomes.
