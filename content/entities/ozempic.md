# Ozempic

**Ozempic** is a brand-name GLP-1 agonist medication containing semaglutide, primarily approved for the treatment of type 2 diabetes but widely utilized off-label for [[concepts/weight-loss|weight management]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Mechanism**: Mimics the hormone glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) to stimulate insulin release, suppress glucagon secretion, and slow gastric emptying.
- **Administration**: Subcutaneous injection, typically once weekly.
- **Primary Indication**: Type 2 diabetes management; increasingly associated with significant [[concepts/weight-management|weight loss]] benefits.

## Context & Discussion
- **Clinical Impact**: Considered part of a potential [[concepts/medical-revolution|medical revolution]] in treating Obesity and metabolic disorders alongside related [[concepts/agents|agents]] like Mounjaro (tirzepatide).
- **Public Perception**: High visibility in media and public discourse regarding [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], side effects, and long-term efficacy.
