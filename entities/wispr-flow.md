# Wispr Flow

[[concepts/auto-editing|Wispr Flow]] is an [[concepts/ai-powered-dictation|AI-powered dictation]] application that enables users to input text through [[concepts/tone|voice]] [[concepts/commands|commands]] across various applications. The platform leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to process and transcribe spoken language, positioning itself as a tool for hands-free [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

In June 2025, the company secured $30 million in Series funding led by venture capital firm [[entities/menlo-ventures|Menlo Ventures]]. This investment supports [[concepts/effortless-voice-assistants|Wispr Flow]]'s development and market expansion for its voice dictation technology.
