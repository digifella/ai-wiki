# Electronic Components

Electronic components are discrete physical devices used in electrical circuits to control, direct, and modify the flow of electric current. These fundamental building blocks serve functions such as storing energy, restricting current flow, amplifying signals, or switching circuits on and off. Common examples include resistors, capacitors, inductors, diodes, and transistors. Components are typically categorized as either passive (requiring no external power source) or active (requiring power to operate).

## Open-Source Hardware in Electronics

The field of electronics has increasingly embraced open-source methodologies, making designs and specifications freely available for study, modification, and reproduction. This approach democratizes access to electronic design and enables collaborative development across distributed communities. Open-source electronics projects range from simple educational kits to complex computing platforms, fostering [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and reducing barriers to entry for hobbyists, students, and researchers.

The Nano-banana project represents an example of open-source hardware development within the broader electronics ecosystem. Such projects demonstrate how community-driven design can contribute to accessible electronic innovation.
