# Allied Health

Allied health encompasses a broad category of healthcare services provided by professionals distinct from physicians and nurses. This diverse workforce includes physiotherapists, occupational therapists, dietitians, speech-language pathologists, and radiographers, among other specialized practitioners. These professionals operate collaboratively with medical doctors and nursing staff to deliver comprehensive patient care across various settings, including clinical hospitals, rehabilitation facilities, and community-based programs.

## Scope of Practice

The scope of allied health is defined by specialized diagnostic, therapeutic, and preventive services that address specific patient needs. Practitioners in this field often work in multidisciplinary teams to manage chronic conditions, support rehabilitation, and promote public health. Their roles vary significantly by specialty, ranging from the assessment and treatment of physical impairments to the management of nutritional plans and the interpretation of medical imaging data.

## Technology Integration

Wearable devices and embedded sensors are increasingly used in allied health for continuous monitoring and data collection. These technologies enable practitioners to track patient progress in real-time, allowing for more precise adjustments to treatment plans and greater accountability in rehabilitation outcomes. The integration of such digital tools supports remote patient monitoring and enhances the efficiency of care delivery in both clinical and home environments.
