# Diet
Diet encompasses the variety of foods consumed by a person or group. It can significantly influence [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellness|wellness]] through its impact on [[concepts/weight-loss|weight management]], [[concepts/health-promotion|disease prevention]], and overall [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]]. A balanced diet includes adequate portions from all food groups to provide essential nutrients for the body.

## Prevention Levels & Determinants of Health
Understanding these factors is vital for addressing [[concepts/public-health|public health]] issues effectively:
- **[[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary Prevention]]:** Actions to prevent diseases before they occur. Examples include [[concepts/vaccination|vaccination]] and public health campaigns.
- **[[concepts/secondary-prevention|Secondary Prevention]]:** [[concepts/secondary-prevention|Early detection and treatment]] of disease through screenings and regular check-ups.
- **[[concepts/tertiary-prevention|Tertiary Prevention]]:** Managing chronic conditions and disabilities to improve [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].

### Determinants of Health
Factors influencing the health status of individuals and communities:
- Environmental factors such as air, water, soil, and noise pollution.
- Socioeconomic status including income, education level, and employment opportunities.
- Social support networks like family, friends, colleagues, and community organizations.
- Personal choices related to diet, [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], smoking, alcohol consumption, etc.
