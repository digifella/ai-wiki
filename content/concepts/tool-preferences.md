# Tool Preferences

Preferences for tools in digital [[concepts/art|art]] and [[concepts/design|design]] [[concepts/software|software]], particularly focusing on the Brush Tool in [[entities/adobe-photoshop]].

## Adobe Photoshop Brush Tool Shortcuts
* **Bracket Keys:** Tap `[` or `]` to decrease or increase [[concepts/brush-size|brush size]].
* **Shift + Bracket Keys:** Hold **Shift** and tap `[` or `]` to change edge hardness.
* **[[concepts/hud-heads-up-display|Heads Up Display]] ([[concepts/hud|HUD]]):**
  - **[[entities/mac|Mac]]:** Hold **[[concepts/power|Control]] + Option** and drag.
  - **[[entities/windows|Windows]]:** Hold **Control + Alt + Right-click** and drag.
