# Honest AI

**Type:** concept
**Tags:** AI, [[concepts/ethics|Ethics]], Bias, Future, Sentience
**Updated:** 2026-05-02

---

Honest AI refers to the theoretical and practical [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems designed to operate with maximum transparency, revealing their internal mechanisms, value systems, and potential [[concepts/biases|biases]] rather than operating as opaque black boxes. The goal is to move beyond functional performance to assess the ethical and societal implications of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

## Core Principles

*   **Transparency:** AI systems must be capable of explaining their decisions and internal logic in human-understandable terms.
*   **Bias Disclosure:** Full disclosure of the [[concepts/training-data|training data]], algorithms, and inherent biases that [[concepts/power|influence]] outcomes.
*   **Self-Awareness (Theoretical):** Exploring the potential for advanced AI to develop an internal understanding of its own operational boundaries and goals.
*   **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]]:** Establishing clear lines of responsibility for the actions and consequences of AI systems.

## Advanced Topics: Unrestricted AI

The concept of Honest AI intersects critically with research into unrestricted and self-directed artificial intelligence. This line of inquiry focuses on the potential dangers and emergent properties of highly autonomous systems.
