# Process-Driven Engineering

[[concepts/ai-guided-software-development|Process-driven engineering]] emphasizes a structured and systematic approach to engineering tasks, particularly in [[concepts/coding|software development]]. It leverages methodologies, frameworks, and tools to ensure [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], efficiency, and quality. In the context of [[concepts/ai-integration|AI-integration]], process-driven engineering plays a crucial role in guiding how [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] like [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] are utilized effectively.

- [[concepts/agent-steering|AI-Guided Software Development]] focuses on leveraging the capabilities of LLMs to enhance software development processes.
- Process-driven engineering is essential for integrating [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] such as [[entities/claude-code|Claude Code]] and [[concepts/agent-skills|Agent Skills]], which can significantly boost productivity and streamline tasks but require structured guidance.

### Key Points
- The use of process-driven methodologies ensures that AI assistants like [[entities/claude|Claude]] Code are used optimally, providing consistent quality and efficiency.
- [[concepts/memory-management|Memory limitations]] in [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] necessitate a robust process framework to maintain context and [[concepts/continuity|continuity]].
- Process frameworks guide the integration of [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] such as Claude Code Agent Skills, enhancing productivity while addressing [[concepts/ram-limitations|memory constraints]].
