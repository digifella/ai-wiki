# Detention Center

Facility for holding individuals in custody, distinct from Prison systems, often utilized for pre-trial confinement, administrative detention, immigration processing, or military custody of combatants.

## Classification
* **Civil Detention**: Pre-trial holding; administrative confinement without criminal conviction.
* **Military Detention**: Custody of combatants, POWs, or [[concepts/security|security]] threats under military law or emergency powers.
* **Immigration Detention**: Confinement of non-citizens pending deportation or status determination.
* **Juvenile Detention**: Facilities for minors awaiting adjudication or serving sentences.

## Operational Characteristics
* Security levels ranging from minimum to maximum.
* [[concepts/governance|Governance]] via Legal Framework including due process rights, International Law, or executive orders.
* Controversies often center on Human Rights, indefinite detention, Torture, and conditions of confinement.

## Notable Facilities & Case Studies
* [[concepts/guantanamo-bay]]: [[entities/cuba|Cuba]] territory leased by [[entities/united-states]]; history includes US acquisition, establishment as a [[concepts/military-base|Military Base]], and transformation into a controversial detention center; ongoing debates regarding its legal status, existence, and human rights implications [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-Guantanamo-Bay-US-Acquisition-Military-Base-and-Detentio|Guantanamo Bay: US Acquisition, Military Base, and Detention Center Controversies]].
* Bagram: Former US detention facility in Afghanistan.
* Black Sites: Unacknowledged CIA detention and interrogation centers.
* Internment Camp: Mass detention of specific populations (e.g., WWII Japanese American internment).
