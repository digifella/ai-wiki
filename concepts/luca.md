# LUCA

**[[concepts/last-universal-common-ancestor|Last Universal Common Ancestor]]** (LUCA) is the most recent population of organisms from which all organisms now living on Earth have a common Last Universal Common Ancestor.

## Recent Developments
*   **[[concepts/dual-origins-of-life|Dual Origins Hypothesis]]:** Emerging evidence challenges the singular "[[concepts/primordial-soup|primordial soup]]" model, suggesting life may have had two separate origins within [[concepts/hydrothermal-vents|Hydrothermal Vents]].
*   **New Evidence:** Recent studies indicate distinct lineages or origins that complicate the traditional tree of life, pointing toward a more complex genesis than previously assumed.
*   **Source Integration:** For detailed analysis of this dual-origin evidence, see [[lab-notes/2026-08-28-Rethinking-LUCA-Evidence-for-Dual-Origins-of-Life-in-Hyd|Rethinking LUCA: Evidence for Dual Origins of Life in Hydrothermal Vents]].
