# Tiiny AI Pocket Lab

**Definition:** A compact hardware device enabling the local, [[concepts/private-execution|private execution]] of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs), eliminating dependency on cloud [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]] and expensive GPU infrastructure.

## Core Capabilities
- **[[concepts/local-execution|Local Execution]]:** Runs models on-device, ensuring data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] and offline [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].
- **Model Scale:** Capable of handling large [[concepts/parameter-models|parameter models]], specifically demonstrated with **120B** parameter models.
- **Hardware Efficiency:** Challenges traditional constraints by operating without extensive, high-cost GPU arrays.
