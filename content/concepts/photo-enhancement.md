# Photo Enhancement

Photo enhancement is the process of improving photographs through digital editing, adjusting visual elements such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], color, and overall mood to increase their impact and appeal. This practice has become accessible to photographers of all skill levels through dedicated [[concepts/software|software]] applications and built-in tools across devices.

## Common Tools and Software

Popular photo enhancement software includes Lightroom, [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], and [[entities/snapseed|Snapseed]], each offering different capabilities suited to various editing needs. Lightroom provides a non-destructive editing environment ideal for batch processing and creating consistent looks across multiple [[concepts/images|images]]. Photoshop offers more advanced manipulation capabilities for detailed retouching and compositing work. Snapseed operates as a free mobile application with professional-grade features, making enhancement accessible on smartphones and tablets.

## Enhancement Techniques

Photo enhancement typically involves adjusting exposure to correct lighting issues, modifying [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] and saturation to establish mood, and refining contrast to add visual depth. More dramatic enhancements may include creating [[concepts/dark-and-moody-photo-processing|dark and moody]] aesthetics through selective darkening and [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], or applying [[concepts/film-presets|film-inspired presets]] that evoke specific photographic styles. Many modern tools incorporate [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] features to automate certain [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] or provide intelligent background processing.

Photo enhancement remains a fundamental skill in contemporary photography, bridging the gap between the image captured by a camera and the final result intended by the photographer.
