LLM [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] refers to the [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]] within [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] that enable logical step-by-step [[concepts/output-generation|output generation]], [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and [[concepts/inference|inference]]. It encompasses both the observable output patterns and the underlying [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] that produce them.
