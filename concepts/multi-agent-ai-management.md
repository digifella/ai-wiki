# Multi-Agent AI Management

The orchestration, deployment, and centralized oversight of multiple autonomous or semi-autonomous [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to execute complex, multi-step workflows.

## Core Objectives
- **Orchestration**: Coordinating interactions, [[concepts/leadership|task delegation]], and communication protocols between [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]].
- **Automation**: Implementing autonomous [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]] to reduce manual intervention in [[concepts/ai-automation]] pipelines.
- **Interoperability**: Enabling diverse model architectures to function within a unified management environment.
