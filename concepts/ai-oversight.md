# AI Oversight

Systematic monitoring and [[concepts/governance|governance]] of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and deployment to ensure safety, [[concepts/ethics|ethics]], and alignment with organizational values.

- **[[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] gap**: Rapid [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]] by healthcare professionals occurs without adequate governance structures, creating safety risks [[concepts/healthcare-policy|Healthcare governance]].
- **Framework limitation**: Existing approaches prioritized superficial oversight, often relying on the "human in the [[concepts/loop|loop]]" (HITL) paradigm which may only involve reviewing AI output without deep understanding or causal insight.
- **[[concepts/trust|Trust]] Framework Evolution**: Recent critiques argue that HITL is insufficient for [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]]. Effective oversight requires a robust trust framework that moves beyond mere review to active, informed governance and real-time [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] checks via specialized [[concepts/ai-safety|observer agents]].
- **Observer Agents**: Increasingly, governance utilizes specialized observer agents for real-time reliability checks, reducing reliance on passive human review.
