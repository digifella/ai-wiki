# AI Pacing

**AI Pacing** refers to the strategic slowing down of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] development and deployment to mitigate risks associated with rapid advancement. Key [[concepts/causes|drivers]] include concerns over [[concepts/existential-risk]], the concentration of power among a few tech giants, and the lack of robust safety protocols.

## Key Perspectives & Developments

*   **[[entities/dario-amodei|Dario Amodei]]'s Warning:** [[lab-notes/2026-09-16-Dario-Amodeis-AI-Warning-Pacing-Existential-Risks-and-Po|Dario Amodei's AI Warning: Pacing, Existential Risks and Power Concentration]] highlights a significant shift in the industry landscape.
    *   Amodei, CEO of [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]], has publicly called for an industry-wide slowdown.
    *   The warning emphasizes that current trajectories may lead to unmanageable [[concepts/existential-risk]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
    *   A critical component of the argument is the risk of [[concepts/power-concentration|power-concentration]], where control over [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]] becomes overly centralized.
