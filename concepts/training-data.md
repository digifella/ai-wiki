# Training Data

**Training data** refers to the specific set of examples used to train a machine learning model. It serves as the foundational input that allows generative AI systems to learn patterns, correlations, and structures necessary for making predictions or generating content.

## Core Characteristics
- **Volume & Scale**: Modern [[concepts/large-language-models]] (LLMs) require massive datasets, often comprising billions of tokens from diverse sources.
- **Quality & Curation**: The signal-to-noise ratio in training data directly impacts [[concepts/model-performance|model performance]], hallucination rates, and bias.
- **Diversity**: Represents the breadth of human knowledge and language to ensure generalization capabilities.
