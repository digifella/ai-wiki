# Stanford Hazy Research

**Stanford [[entities/hazy-research|Hazy Research]]** is a research group at [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford University]] focused on scalable [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]], [[concepts/ai-specialization|efficient AI]] systems, and the intersection of hardware and software optimization. It operates in close collaboration with Scaling Intelligence Labs.

## Key Initiatives & Projects

### OpenJarvis
A novel local-first [[concepts/personal-ai-framework|personal AI framework]] developed jointly by Stanford Hazy Research and Scaling Intelligence Labs.

*   **Core [[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]:** Empowers users through [[concepts/local-execution|on-device processing]], prioritizing privacy and [[concepts/algorithm-efficiency|computational efficiency]].
*   **Technical Focus:** Optimizes AI agents to track resource usage (e.g., wattage) and integrates with [[concepts/ollama|Ollama]] for [[concepts/local-ai-hosting|local model execution]].
*   **Key Characteristics:**
    *   [[concepts/open-source|Open-source]] architecture allowing full [[concepts/user-control|user control]].
    *   Eliminates reliance on [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud services]] for core processing.
    *   Designed for efficient tracking of [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]].

For detailed analysis of this framework's implementation and [[concepts/usage-statistics|resource tracking]] capabilities, see [[lab-notes/2026-06-27-OpenJarvis-Stanfords-Local-First-Personal-AI-Framework-w|OpenJarvis: Stanford's Local-First Personal AI Framework with Ollama]].
