## Key Concepts
- [[concepts/lightroom|Calibration Panel]]: Acts as the "fundamental ingredients" of a photo's color profile; essential for establishing [[concepts/foundational-color|foundational color]], [[concepts/skin-tones]], and Cinematic aesthetics.
- Basic [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]: Tools such as [[concepts/contrast|Contrast]] and Saturation function as "seasoning" applied to the underlying color foundation.
