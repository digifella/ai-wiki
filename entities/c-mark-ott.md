# C Mark Ott

C. Mark Ott is a researcher and author specializing in space life sciences and health research. His work focuses on identifying and addressing gaps between these interconnected disciplines, recognizing that advances in space research have significant implications for understanding human health on Earth and vice versa.

## Research Focus

Ott's research emphasizes the practical and theoretical connections between space life sciences—the study of biological and physiological responses to spaceflight—and terrestrial health research. By highlighting areas where these fields can better inform one another, his work contributes to a more integrated approach to understanding human health and adaptation in extreme environments.

## Publications

In 2024, Ott co-authored a commentary addressing the disconnect between space life sciences and health research communities, arguing for greater collaboration and knowledge exchange between the two fields.
