# Portal Security Check

A Portal Security Check is a verification process conducted on the ASIC Company Portal to authenticate user access and validate the legitimacy of information requests. This mechanism ensures that sensitive company data is only displayed to authorized requestors after confirming the validity of the Australian Company Number (ACN) provided. The check serves as a critical gatekeeping step within the tools-platforms-infrastructure domain, preventing unauthorized access to corporate records.

When a user enters an ACN into the portal, the system initiates an immediate security validation routine. This process cross-references the provided identifier against official registries to confirm its existence and current status. By verifying the ACN before granting access to detailed corporate filings, the system mitigates the risk of data breaches and ensures that only legitimate inquiries proceed to the information retrieval stage.

The procedure functions as a fundamental component of the platform's security architecture. It acts as the first line of defense by filtering out invalid or malicious inputs before they can interact with deeper database layers. This approach maintains the integrity of the ASIC Company Portal by ensuring that all subsequent data disclosures are predicated on a verified and authentic company identifier.
