## Inkling 2 Mini vs Qwen 3.8 27B coding battle - Local LLM setup

### Summary
The video features a head-to-head comparison between Inkling 2 Mini and Qwen 3.8 27B across seven programming and rendering tests: Pocket Planet, Neuro Racers, Pinball, Path Tracer, Blender Dominoes, Blender Windmill, and Godot Truck. The speaker tests the models' performance on coding tasks without further prompting after initial setup.

Both models pass all seven tests with very similar results, demonstrating comparable context usage and compactions. For example, in the Pocket Planet test, Inkling 2 Mini uses 60.5% context with two compactions, while Qwen 3.8 27B uses 31.7% context with one compaction. Across all tests, both models show strong performance, though Inkling 2 Mini runs slightly faster as an MoE (Mixture of Experts) model and often uses fewer tokens.

The conclusion is that Inkling 2 Mini and Qwen 3.8 27B perform almost interchangeably at the Q4K_XL level with Unsloth GGUF. The speaker notes that Inkling 2 Mini's MoE architecture makes it a compelling choice due to its speed and performance on par with Qwen 3.8 27B.
