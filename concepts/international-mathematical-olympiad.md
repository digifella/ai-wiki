# International Mathematical Olympiad

The International Mathematical Olympiad (IMO) is an annual [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] competition for high school students, established in 1959. It is the oldest and most prestigious international [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] competition for pre-university students, featuring six problems solved over two days.

**Key [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]]:**
- Hosted by a different country each year
- Problems require deep mathematical insight ([[concepts/number-theory|number theory]], algebra, combinatorics, geometry)
- Medals awarded to top 50% of participants ([[concepts/major-gold-deposit|gold]]: top 8%, silver: next 15%, bronze: next 27%)
- Participants must be under 20 and not enrolled in university
