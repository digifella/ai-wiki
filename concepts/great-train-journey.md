# Great Train Journey

Great Train Journey refers to notable rail journeys and train-based holiday experiences available in Australia. These services combine transportation with leisure and sightseeing, allowing passengers to travel through diverse Australian landscapes by rail. Train holidays function as an established tourism category in Australia, ranging from short scenic routes to multi-day expeditions that traverse remote and regional areas.

## Major Australian Train Services

Australia operates several recognised train services marketed primarily as tourist experiences rather than standard public transport. The Ghan connects Adelaide to Darwin, crossing the continent through the Red Centre. The Indian Pacific links Sydney and Perth, traversing the vast distances of the Australian Outback. The West Coast Wilderness Railway in Tasmania offers a historic narrow-gauge experience through rugged terrain.

Other significant services include the Spirit of Queensland, which runs between Brisbane and Cairns along the coast, and the Great Southern Railway’s Indian Pacific and The Ghan. These long-distance trains are known for their luxury accommodations and curated onboard dining, catering to international and domestic tourists seeking comprehensive travel packages.

Regional and short-distance scenic trains also contribute to this sector. The Puffing Billy Railway in Victoria operates a heritage steam train through the Dandenong Ranges. In Western Australia, the Swan Valley Wine Train provides a leisurely tour through a major wine-producing region. These services often focus on historical preservation and local tourism, offering shorter durations compared to the major cross-continental journeys.
