# Generative AI

Generative AI refers to [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] technologies that can produce new and original content autonomously, such as images, text, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], video, and more. This concept encompasses a wide range of applications from creative industries like design, marketing, and entertainment to technical fields including [[concepts/coding|software development]] and scientific research.

- **[[concepts/llms|Definition]]**: [[concepts/tts-model|Generative models]] are trained on large [[concepts/training-data|datasets
- **Spatial Intelligence & Robotics**: Recent developments highlight the role of generative AI in addressing data scarcity in robotics through spatial intelligence. Fei-Fei Li, CEO of World AI, discusses how this approach solves critical challenges in robotic perception and interaction, particularly following World AI's acquisition of Scenix [[lab-notes/2026-08-02-Fei-Fei-Li-Spatial-Intelligence-Solves-Robotics-Data-Sca|Fei-Fei Li: Spatial Intelligence Solves Robotics Data Scarcity]].
