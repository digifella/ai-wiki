# Capability Framework

A structured approach to defining, measuring, and developing the specific [[concepts/skills|skills]], knowledge, and behaviors required for effective performance within a specific domain or organization.

## Core Components
- **Competency Mapping**: Identification of essential skills aligned with strategic objectives.
- **Assessment Metrics**: Tools for evaluating current [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] levels against desired states.
- **Development Pathways**: Structured [[concepts/learning|learning]] interventions to close [[concepts/skill-gaps|capability gaps]].

## Sector-Specific Applications

### Healthcare & Nursing
- **Digital Literacy Integration**: Modern capability frameworks in [[concepts/health|health]] sectors increasingly prioritize digital proficiency alongside clinical skills.
- **Person-Centred Focus**: Capabilities are defined not just by technical knowledge but by the ability to apply digital tools in patient-centric workflows.

See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Improving-digital-literacy---NHS|Improving digital literacy - NHS]] for detailed analysis of digital literacy requirements within the NHS context, including contributions from [[concepts/health-promotion|Health Education]] [[entities/united-kingdom|England]] and the Royal College of Nursing.

## Key Considerations
- **Dynamic Evolution**: Frameworks must adapt to technological advancements and shifting industry standards.
- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Requires collaboration between [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], educators, and frontline practitioners.
- **Measurable Outcomes**: Must link individual capability growth to organizational [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]].
