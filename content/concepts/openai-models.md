# OpenAI Models

[[entities/openai|OpenAI]] has released a series of [[concepts/ai-licensing|open-weights models]] collectively referred to as [[concepts/gpt-4|GPT-OSS]] (Open Source Series). These [[concepts/models|models]] represent a shift in OpenAI's [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] strategy, making model [[concepts/weights|weights]] publicly available rather than restricting access through API endpoints. This change allows developers and researchers to download, modify, and deploy the models locally.

## Model Access and Deployment

The [[concepts/open-weights-models|open-weights models]] can be deployed on personal [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] or cloud infrastructure without requiring OpenAI's direct services. This approach differs from OpenAI's previous closed-model strategy and enables broader experimentation and [[concepts/customization|customization]] by the [[concepts/developer|developer]] community. Users gain the ability to fine-tune models for specific [[concepts/software|applications]] and maintain full [[concepts/power|control]] over [[concepts/inference|inference]] infrastructure.

## Implications for AI Development

The release of open-weights models has significant implications for the [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] landscape. It democratizes access to advanced language models and reduces dependency on proprietary APIs, while also distributing the computational and operational burden across independent deployments. This shift aligns with broader trends in the [[concepts/ai-industry|AI industry]] toward [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/model-releases|model releases]].
