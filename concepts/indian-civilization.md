# Indian Civilization

Indian civilization encompasses the complex societies and cultures that developed across the Indian subcontinent over more than five thousand years. The earliest known urban civilization in this region, the Indus Valley Civilization, flourished from approximately 3300 to 1300 BCE across what is now Pakistan and northwestern India. Contemporary with ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia, it featured planned cities with standardized drainage systems, consistent weights and measures for trade, and a writing system that remains only partially deciphered.

Following the decline of the Indus Valley cities, the Vedic period emerged, characterized by the composition of the Vedas and the development of early Hindu philosophical traditions. This era saw the rise of the Mahajanapadas, or great kingdoms, which laid the groundwork for subsequent political structures. The Maurya Empire, founded by Chandragupta Maurya in the 4th century BCE, marked the first major expansion of a unified political entity across much of the subcontinent, facilitating extensive internal trade and cultural exchange.

Subsequent centuries witnessed the Gupta Empire, often regarded as a "Golden Age" for Indian arts, sciences, and mathematics. During this period, significant advancements were made in astronomy, algebra, and the concept of zero. The subcontinent also became a central node in global trade networks, exporting textiles, spices, and precious stones to Rome, Southeast Asia, and East Africa, thereby influencing economic and cultural developments across the ancient world.

Later periods were defined by the rise of regional powers and the eventual establishment of the Mughal Empire, which further integrated the subcontinent into broader Eurasian trade systems. Throughout its history, Indian civilization has been characterized by its religious diversity, linguistic plurality, and continuous interaction with external cultures, shaping a distinct yet interconnected historical trajectory within the broader context of global history.
