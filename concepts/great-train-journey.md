# Great Train Journey

Great Train Journey refers to notable rail journeys and train-based holiday experiences available in Australia. These services combine transportation with leisure and sightseeing, allowing passengers to travel through diverse Australian landscapes by rail. Train holidays function as an established tourism category in Australia, ranging from short scenic routes to multi-day expeditions that traverse remote and regional areas.

## Major Australian Train Services

Australia operates several recognised train services marketed primarily as tourist experiences. The Indian Pacific connects Sydney and Perth across the continent, traversing approximately 4,352 kilometres over three days. The Ghan operates between Adelaide and Darwin, passing through central Australia's interior regions. The Great Southern Rail operates these services and other shorter regional routes. State-based operators also provide scenic train experiences, including services through mountainous terrain in Queensland, Victoria, and New South Wales.

## Experience and Appeal

These train journeys appeal to travellers seeking extended travel experiences that emphasise landscape viewing and onboard hospitality. Passengers typically have access to dining facilities, sleeping accommodation, and observation areas designed for sightseeing. The relatively slow pace of rail travel allows for viewing of areas often inaccessible by road. Train holidays attract both domestic and international tourists, with many services operating year-round or seasonally depending on regional climate conditions.
