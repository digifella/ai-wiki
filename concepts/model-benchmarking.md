# Model Benchmarking

**[[concepts/benchmark-performance|Model Benchmarking]]** is the systematic process of evaluating and comparing the performance, efficiency, and quality of different [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) or AI systems against standardized metrics and real-world use cases. It serves as a critical component of AI [[concepts/infrastructure|Infrastructure]] and Model Selection strategies.

## Core Dimensions
Effective benchmarking typically assesses models across three primary axes:
*   **Performance:** Accuracy, latency, throughput, and task-specific [[concepts/success-rates|success rates]].
*   **Cost:** [[concepts/token-pricing|Token pricing]], [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] costs, and total cost of ownership (TCO) for specific workloads.
*   **Quality:** Output coherence, [[concepts/factual-accuracy|factual accuracy]], safety alignment, and user satisfaction.
