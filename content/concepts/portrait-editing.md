# Portrait Editing

Portrait editing encompasses a [[concepts/range|range]] of post-processing techniques used to enhance photographs of people. These [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] can address technical issues such as lighting and focus, as well as aesthetic improvements to skin, features, and overall composition. Portrait editing is a standard practice in professional photography and is accessible to photographers of varying [[concepts/skill|skill]] levels through [[concepts/software|software]] like [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]].

## Photoshop Techniques

[[entities/piximperfect|Piximperfect]] has documented several foundational [[concepts/photoshop-techniques|Photoshop techniques]] for portrait improvement. These techniques address common challenges in [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], from basic [[concepts/setup|setup]] considerations to targeted adjustments. The approaches focus on practical, immediately applicable methods rather than complex or time-intensive workflows.

Portrait editing workflows typically begin with fundamental considerations—such as monitor calibration and color [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]]—before moving to more detailed retouching. Subsequent techniques generally progress through skin refinement, feature enhancement, and overall image optimization to achieve a polished final result.
