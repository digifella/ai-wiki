# Data Embedding

Data embedding is the process of converting [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]]—such as text, images, or documents—into numerical vectors that [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] can process. These vectors, typically arrays of [[concepts/floating-point-numbers|floating-point numbers]], capture the semantic meaning and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within the original data in a form that computers can efficiently analyze. [[concepts/dense-vectors|Embeddings]] are generated by [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] models trained to recognize patterns and similarities across [[entities/big-data|large datasets]].

## How Embeddings Work

The embedding process transforms high-dimensional, human-readable information into a lower-dimensional [[concepts/large-number-management|mathematical representation]]. A text embedding, for example, converts words or passages into vectors where [[concepts/semantic-similarity|semantic similarity]] is reflected in spatial proximity—synonyms or related concepts cluster together in the [[concepts/embedding-spaces|vector space]]. This allows [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]] to perform tasks like similarity matching, clustering, and [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] without requiring explicit programming of semantic rules.

## Applications

[[concepts/vector-representations|Embeddings]] are foundational to many modern [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]]. They enable [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval-augmented generation]] (RAG) systems to search and retrieve relevant documents, power recommendation engines by identifying similar items or users, and allow language models to process and generate text. Search engines, [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]], and semantic analysis tools all rely on embeddings to understand and relate different pieces of information.

## Common Embedding Models

Popular [[concepts/embedding-models|embedding models]] include those based on [[concepts/transformer-architectures|transformer architectures]], which have become the standard for generating [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] [[concepts/text-embeddings|text embeddings]]. These models can be [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] and run locally, or accessed through cloud [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]]. The choice of [[concepts/embedding-model|embedding model]] affects the quality and efficiency of downstream tasks, with different models optimized for various data types and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
