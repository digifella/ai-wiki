# Next Tech and AI
Next Tech and AI is a leading platform for exploring the latest developments in [[concepts/llms|small language models (SLMs)]] and their [[concepts/software|applications]]. The channel focuses on identifying champions among [[concepts/llms|SLMs]] with specific constraints such as [[concepts/memory-management|memory footprint]].

## Key Concepts
- **Small Language Models (SLMs)**: Specialized [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] that operate under strict resource limitations, often 4GB or less.
- **General Problem Solving**: Evaluating the versatility and effectiveness of SLMs in handling a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of tasks.
- **[[concepts/benchmark-testing|Benchmarking]]**: A systematic process to assess the performance of SLMs.
