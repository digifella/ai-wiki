# Artemis II Mission Simulation

The [[concepts/artemis-ii-mission|Artemis II Mission]] [[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] is an operational [[concepts/exercise|exercise]] developed by [[concepts/nasa|NASA]] to evaluate the planned crewed flight of the Artemis II mission. The simulation examines performance across critical [[concepts/spacecraft-systems|spacecraft systems]], crew procedures, and vehicle operations throughout the complete mission profile, from launch [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] through Earth return. By analyzing mission reports and supporting documentation, the exercise serves as a planning and validation tool for flight operations teams.

## Mission Overview

Artemis II represents NASA's second crewed test flight of the Space Launch System (SLS) and [[entities/orion|Orion spacecraft]]. The mission is designed to carry a crew of four astronauts on a [[concepts/lunar-flyby|lunar flyby]] trajectory, [[concepts/testing|testing]] life support systems, navigation, and other spacecraft [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] in the deep [[concepts/vacuum-of-space|space environment]] before a subsequent landing mission.

## Simulation Focus Areas

The simulation documents [[concepts/crew-operations|crew operations]] procedures, including nominal and contingency activities during various mission phases. It incorporates detailed analysis of Orion spacecraft systems performance, propulsion characteristics, and guidance and [[concepts/power|control]] functions. The exercise also evaluates updates to ground support equipment and mission operations infrastructure required to support the crewed flight. By modeling realistic mission [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], the simulation helps identify procedural refinements and operational requirements ahead of the actual flight.
