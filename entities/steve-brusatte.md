# Steve Brusatte

**Steve Brusatte** is a paleontologist and evolutionary biologist, affiliated with the University of Edinburgh. He is a leading expert on [[concepts/dinosaur-evolution|dinosaur evolution]], particularly theropods, and their relationship to modern Birds.

## Research & Publications
- Authored *The Story of Birds* (2026), detailing the evolutionary trajectory of avian [[concepts/dinosaurs|dinosaurs]].
- Research focuses on macroevolutionary patterns in Theropoda and the origins of flight.

## Key Themes & Media
- **Birds as Dinosaurs**: Advocates for the classification of Birds as the sole surviving [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|lineage]] of Dinosaurs, emphasizing [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] rather than extinction at the K-Pg boundary.
- **Public Engagement**: Frequent media contributor, including interviews with *[[entities/new-scientist|New Scientist]]* on the phylogenetic links between [[concepts/flightless-dinosaurs|non-avian dinosaurs]] and modern avifauna.
