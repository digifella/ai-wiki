# Lowest Temperature Ever Recorded

## Current Record
The lowest directly recorded air temperature on [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s surface is **-89.2°C (-128.6°F)**.
- **Location:** [[concepts/vostok-research-station|Vostok Station]], Antarctica.
- **Date:** July 21, 1983.
- **Context:** Recorded during a clear sky night in the polar winter.

## Subglacial Extremes & Lake Vostok
While air temperatures define the standard record, subglacial environments present distinct thermal and biological conditions. Recent analysis of [[entities/lake-vostok|Lake Vostok]] highlights its [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]] beneath ~4km of ice.
- See detailed [[concepts/notes|notes]]: [[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Lake-Vostok-Antarcticas-Isolated-Subglacial-World-Discov|Lake Vostok: Antarctica's Isolated Subglacial World, Discovered Beneath Four Kilometers of Ice]]

## Notable High-Altitude Measurements
Satellite data suggests even lower temperatures in high-elevation interior regions where direct sensor placement is difficult.
- **East Antarctic Plateau:** Readings as low as **-98°C (-140°F)** have been inferred by satellite radiometers over ridges higher than Vostok Station.
- **[[concepts/verification|Verification]]:** These figures require ground-truthing and are not considered "directly recorded" in the traditional meteorological sense pending specific probe deployment MCM Glacier.
