# Academic Report

An **[[entities/tomasz-janowski|Academic]] Report** is a formal document presenting [[concepts/flow-management|research findings]], analysis, or policy [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]], typically subject to [[concepts/document-review|peer review]] or institutional scrutiny. These reports serve as primary or secondary sources in scholarly and policy contexts.

## Key Characteristics
- Structured methodology and evidence-based arguments
- Formal citation and reference standards
- Often published by academic institutions, international organizations, or research bodies
