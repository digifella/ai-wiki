# Health Research

**[[concepts/health|Health]] Research** encompasses systematic investigation to establish [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]] and draw new conclusions concerning the health and [[concepts/well-being|well-being]] of humans and other organisms. In the context of [[concepts/space-life-sciences]], it addresses unique physiological challenges posed by microgravity, radiation, and [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]].
