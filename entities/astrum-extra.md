# Astrum Extra

Astrum Extra is a reference designation for NASA's Project Gemini, a human spaceflight program that operated between 1961 and 1966. The initiative served as a critical developmental bridge between the earlier Mercury program and the subsequent Apollo program. By addressing specific technical gaps, Gemini allowed NASA to test and refine essential techniques required for complex lunar missions.

## Program Objectives

Project Gemini focused on mastering fundamental spaceflight challenges necessary for the Apollo moon landings. Key objectives included developing procedures for crewed spacewalks and perfecting orbital rendezvous and docking capabilities. These efforts were vital for establishing the operational protocols needed to support future deep-space exploration.
