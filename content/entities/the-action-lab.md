### Breaking the Angle of Repose: Self-Sorting and Stacking of Granular Materials
- **Clip title:** I Broke The [[concepts/angle-of-repose|Angle of Repose]]
- **Author / channel:** The Action Lab
- **URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G6pTelud5-E

**Summary**
This video explores the fascinating [[concepts/physics|physics]] behind the "angle of repose," the maximum slope a pile of granular material can naturally maintain, and delves into how this fundamental property can be manipulated to create unexpected behaviors like [[concepts/self-sorting|self-sorting]] and even [[concepts/vertical-stacking|vertical stacking]]. The presenter begins by illustrating that grains, such as sand or wheat, typically align themselves at an angle determined by their size and shape.

### Related Concepts
- **[[concepts/angle-of-repose|Angle of repose]]:** A fundamental property in physics and engineering that defines the maximum slope a pile of [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] can maintain without collapsing under its own weight.
- **Granular materials:** Particulate matter such as sand, grain, or gravel that behaves collectively but does not form a single solid mass.
