## One-Shot Build Benchmark

The One-Shot Build benchmark is a comparative evaluation framework used to assess the performance of advanced language models on rapid task completion with minimal examples. In comparative analyses with OpenAI's GPT-5.2, Claude Opus 4.5 and competing models are evaluated on their ability to understand and execute complex instructions from single examples, measuring both accuracy and the quality of generated outputs across diverse task categories. This benchmark provides insight into each model's capacity for few-shot learning and generalization without extensive fine-tuning or prompt engineering.
