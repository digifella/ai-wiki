# Bismarck Sea

The Bismarck Sea is a marginal sea located in the southwestern Pacific Ocean, situated within the region of Oceania. It lies between the island of New Britain and the Bismarck Archipelago to the north, and the mainland of Papua New Guinea to the south. The body of water connects to the Solomon Sea in the east and is bounded by major landmasses including New Ireland and New Britain.

The sea derives its name from Otto von Bismarck, the German statesman instrumental in the unification of Germany. This nomenclature reflects the area's historical status as part of German New Guinea during the late 19th and early 20th centuries.

The region is geologically active and is notably associated with Ritter Island, a small volcanic island located within the sea. In 1888, Ritter Island experienced a major volcanic flank collapse that generated a mega-tsunami, causing significant destruction and loss of life in the surrounding areas.
