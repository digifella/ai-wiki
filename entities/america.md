# America

"America" is a documentary project initiated by Destin Sandlin of the YouTube channel Smarter Every Day. The series investigates the feasibility of manufacturing a consumer product entirely within the United States while maintaining cost competitiveness in the global marketplace. The project serves as an exploration of domestic industrial capabilities and economic constraints.

The documentary examines the practical and economic realities of domestic manufacturing at scale. It analyzes key factors influencing production viability, including labor costs, supply chain infrastructure, and regulatory environments. By attempting to build a product from raw materials to final assembly on American soil, the project highlights the complexities involved in reshoring industrial processes.

Through this experimental framework, the series documents the challenges faced when navigating the current economic landscape. It provides a factual account of the logistical hurdles and financial implications associated with local production. The resulting content offers an analysis of whether domestic manufacturing can remain viable without significant subsidies or price premiums.
