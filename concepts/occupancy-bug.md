# occupancy bug

## Definition
A software defect or logical error related to the calculation, tracking, or enforcement of occupancy metrics within a system. In the context of recent [[concepts/large-scale-infrastructure|infrastructure projects]], this specifically refers to errors in real-time passenger counting or capacity management [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]].

## Recent Developments
### Microsoft FrogNano 4B Intervention
A significant breakthrough in [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] this issue was achieved using [[concepts/web-tools|Microsoft FrogNano]] 4B, a compact 4-billion-parameter [[concepts/smart-coding-agent|coding agent]].

- **Context**: The bug was identified in the Nusantara Ferry occupancy tracking system.
- **Tool**: [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] FrogNano 4B was utilized for its efficiency on GPU-poor environments.
- **[[concepts/pre-trained-model|Base Model]]**: Built upon [[concepts/qwen-35-4b]].
- **Training Method**: Underwent unique [[concepts/reinforcement-learning]] (RL) training across ~1,500 [[concepts/synthetic-software-engineering-tasks|synthetic software engineering tasks]].
- **Outcome**: Successfully debugged the occupancy [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] without requiring massive [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]].
