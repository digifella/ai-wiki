# Dark Energy

**[[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|Dark Energy]]** is a hypothetical form of energy that permeates all of space and tends to accelerate the [[concepts/expansion-of-the-universe|expansion of the universe]]. It is the dominant component of the universe's energy [[concepts/density|density]] (~68%) and is distinct from [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]].

## Current Research & DES/DESI Findings
Recent analyses from the [[concepts/dark-energy-survey|Dark Energy Survey (DES)]], the [[concepts/desi|Dark Energy Spectroscopic Instrument (DESI)]], and related studies have sparked renewed interest in the nature of dark energy, challenging the standard [[concepts/lambda-cdm|ΛCDM]] model.

- **Reassessment of Acceleration**: New data suggests the equation of state of dark energy may not be constant, potentially indicating a dynamic field rather than a simple [[concepts/cosmological-constant|cosmological constant]].
- **Tension with [[concepts/standard-model-of-particle-physics|Standard Model]]**: Discrepancies in measurements of the Hubble Constant and the growth of structure hint at possible new physics beyond the standard model of [[concepts/cosmology|cosmology]].
- **Methodology**: These surveys utilize [[concepts/standard-candles|standard candles]] like [[concepts/type-ia-supernovae|Type Ia supernovae]] and [[concepts/baryon-acoustic-oscillations|baryon acoustic oscillations]] to map the universe's expansion history with unprecedented [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].

For detailed analysis of these recent reassessments, see [[lab-notes/2026-07-22-Dark-Energy-DESDESI-Reassess-Universes-Accelerating-Expa|Dark Energy: DES/DESI Reassess Universe's Accelerating Expansion]].

## Theoretical Framework
- **Cosmological Constant (Λ)**: The simplest explanation, representing the energy density of empty space.
- **Quintessence**: A dynamic, time-varying scalar field that could explain the observed acceleration.
- **Modified [[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]]**: Theories suggesting that General Relativity may need modification on cosmological [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] to explain acceleration without dark energy.
