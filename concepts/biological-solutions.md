# Biological Solutions

**Biological solutions** refer to the application of living organisms, enzymes, or biological processes to address environmental challenges, particularly waste management and pollution remediation.

## Plastic Waste Remediation

A primary focus of current biological solutions is the degradation of persistent synthetic materials. Research into [[concepts/plastic-pollution|Plastic Pollution]] has identified several promising biological agents:

*   **[[concepts/enzymatic-degradation|Enzymatic Degradation]]:** Specific enzymes are being engineered to break down polymer chains in plastics, offering a targeted approach to waste processing.
*   **Microbial Consumption:** Certain bacteria and fungi have demonstrated the ability to metabolize plastic materials, effectively consuming them as a carbon source.
*   **Scale of Impact:** With humanity having produced 8.3 billion tons of plastic since the 1950s, biological interventions are critical for mitigating long-term ecological damage, such as entanglement hazards for marine life Marine Ecosystems.
