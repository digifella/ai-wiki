# Determinants of Health

Determinants of health are various factors that [[concepts/power|influence]] the overall state of [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/wellbeing|well-being]] of individuals or populations. These include both individual characteristics (such as genetics) and environmental conditions (like socioeconomic status and [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]]).

### Key Concepts
- **Biological Factors:** Genetic predispositions, age-related changes.
- **Behavioral Factors:** [[concepts/diet|Diet]], [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], smoking [[concepts/habits|habits]].
- **Societal Factors:** Social support networks, cultural norms.
- **Environmental Factors:** Pollution levels, housing conditions.
- **Healthcare System Factors:** [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]] to healthcare services, quality of care provided.

### Prevention Levels
[[concepts/preventive-care|Prevention]] in [[concepts/public-health|public health]] can be categorized into different levels based on their timing and focus:
1. **[[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary Prevention]]**: Aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] preventing disease before it occurs through measures like vaccinations and education.
2. **[[concepts/secondary-prevention|Secondary Prevention]]**: Focuses on early detection and intervention to prevent the progression of diseases, such as screening [[concepts/software|programs]].
3. **[[concepts/tertiary-prevention|Tertiary Prevention]]**: Aims to improve [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] for individuals with existing conditions by minimizing disability or pain.
