# Selection Brush

The Selection Brush is a feature introduced in the July 2025 update of Adobe Photoshop and Photoshop Beta. It allows users to create selections by painting directly onto the image canvas, providing an alternative to traditional geometric selection tools like the Marquee and Lasso tools. This functionality is designed to accommodate varying user preferences and working styles within the image editing workflow.

## Functionality

The tool operates by enabling manual painting of selection areas, where the brush strokes define the boundaries of the selected region. This method offers a more intuitive approach for complex or organic shapes, allowing for precise control over selection edges through direct interaction. By treating selection creation as a painting process, it reduces the need for multiple adjustment points or complex path tracing often required by conventional tools.

## Integration and Workflow

As part of the broader July 2025 update, the Selection Brush aims to enhance productivity by streamlining the initial stages of image editing. It integrates with existing Photoshop features, allowing users to seamlessly transition from selection to masking or adjustment layers. The inclusion of this tool in both the standard and Beta versions indicates Adobe's focus on refining core selection mechanics to support diverse creative needs.
