# Codebase Architecture

**[[concepts/code|Codebase]] Architecture** refers to the structural organization of a software project, defining how components interact, how data flows, and how the system is partitioned. Effective architecture ensures scalability, maintainability, and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] for both human developers and [[concepts/automations|automated systems]].

## Key Principles
- **Modularity**: [[concepts/separation-of-concerns|Separation of concerns]] into distinct, manageable units.
- **Scalability**: Ability to handle increased load or complexity without [[concepts/payload-structure|structural failure]].
- **Maintainability**: Ease of updating, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and extending the codebase.
- **Documentation**: Critical for onboarding and long-term viability; increasingly automated via [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]].
