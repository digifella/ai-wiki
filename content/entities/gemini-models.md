# Gemini Models

[[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] is a family of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]], designed for a [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]] tasks. The [[concepts/models|models]] are available across different [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], enabling [[concepts/software|applications]] from simple [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] to [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks. [[entities/google|Google]] has made various versions of [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]] available through different access methods, including cloud APIs and integration with Google's broader AI services ecosystem.

## Technical Architecture

[[concepts/gemini-models|Gemini models]] employ transformer-based architectures optimized for both performance and efficiency. The model family includes variants designed for different computational constraints, allowing [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] across various platforms from mobile devices to large-scale cloud infrastructure. Like other contemporary language models, Gemini supports multimodal inputs and has been trained on diverse [[concepts/text|text]] sources to develop broad language understanding capabilities.

## Availability and Integration

Google provides access to Gemini models through multiple channels, including the [[entities/ai-studio|Google AI Studio]], Vertex AI platform, and direct [[concepts/integration|API integration]]. The models have been integrated into various Google products and services, reflecting their role as a core component of Google's AI strategy. Documentation and [[concepts/developer|developer]] tools support [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] across different programming languages and deployment [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
