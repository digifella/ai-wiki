# Manual camera controls

Manual camera controls enable photographers to adjust key [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] (aperture, shutter [[concepts/speed|speed]], ISO, focus) directly, bypassing automatic modes for creative precision and optimal [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] in challenging conditions.

- Adobe Project Indigo, introduced by [[entities/colin-smith]] (founder of [[entities/photoshopcafe]]), is a mobile [[concepts/camera-app|camera app]] designed to provide [[concepts/manual-controls|manual controls]] for advanced photography, addressing limitations in traditional [[concepts/smartphone-camera-apps|smartphone camera apps]].
- [[entities/photoshopcafe]]'s [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]] argues that while traditional cameras (DSLRs, mirrorless) [[entities/excel|excel]] in video, low [[concepts/light|light]], and [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]], smartphones have surpassed [[concepts/point-and-shoot-cameras|point-and-shoot cameras]] in convenience, [[concepts/viewfinder-size|viewfinder size]], and [[concepts/on-device-editing|on-device editing]], provided they utilize apps like [[entities/adobe-project-indigo|Adobe Project Indigo]] for professional-level manual control.
