# Science Integration

Science Integration is an interdisciplinary framework that combines artistic perspectives and methodologies with scientific research and practice. This approach addresses how creative practices can enhance traditional scientific work, particularly in the areas of communication, visualization, and discovery processes. By leveraging the complementary strengths of both fields, the concept seeks to bridge conventional boundaries between creative and scientific disciplines.

The integration manifests across multiple domains of scientific practice, aiming to improve the clarity and impact of scientific output. It involves the application of artistic techniques to data visualization and the use of scientific rigor to inform creative projects. This bidirectional exchange facilitates more effective dissemination of complex information and fosters innovative problem-solving strategies within research environments.

A notable example of this concept in action is the collaboration between WEHI and Emily Pulse-it. This partnership exemplifies the practical application of integrating art within science, demonstrating how structured interdisciplinary engagement can support institutional goals and enhance public understanding of scientific concepts.
