# Hubjam10

Hubjam10 is a method for converting content generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] into video format while incorporating a user's personalized face and audio. The technique bridges the gap between text-based AI-generated content and more engaging video media by allowing creators to integrate their own visual presence and voice into the output.

## Process and Tools

The method leverages NotebookLM's audio generation capabilities as a foundation and combines them with facial video synthesis to create personalized video content. A key tool in this workflow is SpeakerSplit, which automatically separates different speakers in audio tracks, enabling cleaner integration of multiple voices or [[entities/speaker|speaker]] segments within the final video product.

## Applications

This approach enables content creators to repurpose NotebookLM-generated materials into video format without requiring traditional video production expertise or re-recording of content. It represents a practical workflow for [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] educational, explanatory, or informational content into more shareable video formats while maintaining [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] and authenticity through the creator's own appearance and voice.
