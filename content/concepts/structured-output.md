# Structured Output

The practice of constraining LLM [[concepts/responses|responses]] to a predefined format, schema, or template to ensure [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and [[concepts/integration|integration]] into downstream [[concepts/automation]] workflows.

## Core Techniques & Workflows
- **Prompt Transformation**: Converting "messy" or vague natural language inputs into clean, standardized, and consistent outputs.
- **Automated [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]]**: Utilizing a [[entities/notebooklm]] and [[entities/gemini]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] to automate the structural refinement of prompts, potentially reducing the need for manual [[entities/prompt-engineering]].
  - New Workflow: [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] + [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] can optimize AI prompts into clean, structured outputs.
