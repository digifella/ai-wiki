# Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope

The **[[concepts/falcon-heavy|Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope]]** (formerly WFIRST) is a [[entities/nasa|NASA]] observatory designed to study [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark energy]], [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]], and exoplanets. It serves as a powerful complement to the [[concepts/james-webb-space-telescope|James Webb Space Telescope]] ([[concepts/jwst|JWST]]), offering a wide field of view for surveying the sky.

## Key Capabilities

*   **Dark Matter Mapping:** Utilizes gravitational lensing to map the distribution of dark matter across the universe.
*   **[[concepts/exoplanet-discovery|Exoplanet Discovery]]:** Employs coronagraph technology and transit [[concepts/photometry|photometry]] to detect and characterize planets outside our solar system.
*   **Wide-Field Infrared [[concepts/survey|Survey]]:** Designed to capture vast areas of the sky in infrared [[concepts/light|light]] with high [[concepts/solution|resolution]].
