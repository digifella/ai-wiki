# Data Governance & Management

Data management and [[concepts/governance|governance]] encompass a range of practices aimed at organizing and managing information to ensure its [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], [[concepts/security|security]], and usability. This includes operational activities such as data [[entities/storage|storage]], backup strategies, platform transitions, and full-stack [[concepts/web-application|web application]] development, as well as strategic frameworks for organizing data to support trustworthy [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[entities/storage|Storage]] Solutions:** Options for [[concepts/storing|storing]] and [[concepts/retrieving|retrieving]] your data efficiently.
  - cloud-storage
  - [[concepts/local-storage|local-storage]]
- **Backup Strategies:** Methods to ensure that your information is protected against loss or corruption.
- **Interoperability Standards:** Frameworks for ensuring data [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across different systems and [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]].
  - **[[concepts/llm-wiki|Open Knowledge Format]]:** A standardized approach for managing personal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] with [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs), evolving from unstructured "LLM Wikis" to interoperable systems. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-03-Open-Knowledge-Format-Standardizing-Karpathy-LLM-Wikis-f|Open Knowledge Format: Standardizing Karpathy LLM Wikis for Interoperability]].
