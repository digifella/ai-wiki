# Emily Pulse It

Emily Pulse It is an initiative of the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI) dedicated to exploring the intersection of art and science. The project creates platforms for dialogue and engagement between artistic and scientific communities, working to bridge traditionally separate disciplinary boundaries through collaborative conversation and shared inquiry.

## Focus and Approach

The initiative examines how artistic works and masterpieces can be engaged with in scientific contexts, and conversely, how scientific practices inform artistic thinking. By bringing together practitioners from both fields, Emily Pulse It facilitates conversations that reveal complementary approaches to understanding and representing the world. The project operates on the premise that artistic and scientific perspectives can mutually enrich one another when given structured opportunities for exchange.

## Context

As part of WEHI's broader engagement activities, Emily Pulse It reflects an institutional commitment to demonstrating that scientific research and artistic practice are not isolated endeavors but interconnected modes of human inquiry and expression.
