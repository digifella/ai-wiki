# Automated Masking

Automated Masking in Lightroom Classic refers to AI-powered mask presets that apply selections with a single click, designed specifically for common photography [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. These presets use [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automatically identify and isolate key subjects or elements in an image, eliminating the need for manual [[concepts/mask-creation|mask creation]] in many cases.

## Application in Landscape Photography

For landscape [[concepts/images|images]], [[concepts/portrait-masking|automated mask presets]] can quickly isolate sky, foreground, or other environmental elements. This allows photographers to apply targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]—such as [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] or [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]]—to specific areas without manual selection work. The presets recognize common landscape compositions and segment them accordingly.

## Application in People Photography

In people photography, automated masking presets can identify and select subjects such as faces, skin, or entire figures. This enables rapid application of retouching or exposure adjustments specific to the subject, streamlining workflows where consistent edits are needed across multiple portraits.

The feature significantly reduces editing time for photographers working with multiple images, as the single-click presets handle initial mask creation automatically rather than requiring manual drawing or refinement of selection boundaries.
