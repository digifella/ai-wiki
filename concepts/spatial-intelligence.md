# Spatial Intelligence

**Spatial Intelligence** refers to the capability of artificial systems to perceive, understand, and interact with the physical [[entities/earth|world]] in three dimensions. It bridges the gap between [[concepts/digital-information|digital data]] and [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]], enabling robots and [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to navigate, manipulate objects, and [[concepts/purpose|reason]] about spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] without relying solely on pre-programmed rules or massive datasets of labeled images.

## Core Principles
- **3D Perception**: Moving beyond 2D image recognition to understand depth, geometry, and volume.
- **Physical [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]**: Understanding how objects interact with [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]], [[concepts/friction|friction]], and other physical forces.
- **[[concepts/abstraction|Generalization]]**: Applying learned spatial concepts to novel environments without extensive retraining.

## Key Developments & Applications

### Robotics Data Scarcity Solution
Traditional robotics relies on massive datasets for training, which are expensive and difficult to collect for every specific environment. Spatial Intelligence offers a pathway to overcome this [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] by enabling models to learn generalizable [[concepts/fundamental-laws-of-physics|physical laws]] rather than memorizing specific visual patterns.

- **[[entities/fei-fei-li|Fei-Fei Li]]'s Vision**: As CEO of [[concepts/world-ai|World AI]], Fei-Fei Li advocates for spatial intelligence as the critical missing link in achieving robust robotic autonomy.
- **World AI & [[entities/scenix|Scenix]] Acquisition**: The acquisition of Scenix by World AI highlights a strategic move to integrate advanced [[concepts/spatial-understanding|spatial understanding]] capabilities into broader AI frameworks. This combination aims to solve the "hardest problem in robotics" by reducing dependency on scarce, [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] [[concepts/custom-dataset|training data]] through better generalization [[lab-notes/2026-08-02-Fei-Fei-Li-Spatial-Intelligence-Solves-Robotics-Data-Sca|Fei-Fei Li: Spatial Intelligence Solves Robotics Data Scarcity]].
- **Impact**: By focusing on how the world works physically rather than just how it looks, these systems can adapt to new tasks and environments more efficiently.
