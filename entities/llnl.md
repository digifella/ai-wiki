# LLNL

[[concepts/lawrence-livermore-national-laboratory|Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory]] (LLNL) is a federally funded research and development center managed by University of California for the U.S. Department of Energy. It focuses on national security, nuclear weapons, and high-performance [[concepts/computation|computing]].

## Key Infrastructure

### El Capitan Supercomputer
LLNL hosts **El Capitan**, recognized as one of the world's most powerful supercomputers.

- **Scale & Engineering**: Features extreme scale architecture designed for exascale computing.
- **Mission**: Supports critical national security missions, including nuclear stockpile stewardship and high-energy density physics.
