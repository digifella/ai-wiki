# Customization

Customization in [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] refers to the ability to modify, configure, and adapt an agent's behavior, capabilities, and outputs to meet specific user needs or organizational requirements. This encompasses [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] ranging from simple parameter tuning to more comprehensive modifications of the agent's underlying models, [[concepts/training-data|training data]], and integration points. The degree and scope of customization available varies significantly across different [[concepts/ai-agent-platforms|AI agent platforms]] and implementations.

## Configuration and Parameter Adjustment

Many [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] allow users to customize behavior through configuration settings without requiring access to underlying code or models. This includes adjusting response [[concepts/tone|tone]], output format, domain-specific [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], and task-specific parameters. Integration customization—connecting agents to specific tools, [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], and data sources—enables agents to operate within particular workflows and access relevant information for their intended [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Model and Training Customization

More [[concepts/granular-control|advanced customization]] involves [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] language models on custom datasets or modifying agents' training procedures to specialize in particular domains or tasks. This approach requires greater technical capability but can significantly improve agent performance for specialized applications. [[concepts/model-fine-tuning|Fine-tuning]] can be performed locally on specialized hardware or through [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]] that manage the [[concepts/technical-infrastructure|technical infrastructure]].

## Persistent and Contextual Customization

Some platforms support persistent customization through integration with external systems—such as note-taking applications, design software, or specialized [[concepts/advanced-tools|professional tools]]—creating customized operating environments. This enables [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]] to maintain context across sessions and adapt their functionality based on the specific workflows and tools users rely on in their regular work.
