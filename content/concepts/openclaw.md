## OpenClaw Overview
OpenClaw is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] project by NVIDIA designed to provide a [[concepts/secure|secure]] framework for deploying and managing [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] within enterprises. It aims to address the challenges of integrating AI into traditional business environments while ensuring robust [[concepts/security|security]] measures are in place.

### Key Features
- **Security:** Implements advanced [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] protocols tailored for [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI applications]].
- **Scalability:** Supports large-scale [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] across multiple enterprise systems.
- **Flexibility:** Offers [[concepts/ai-workflow|customization]] options to fit various organizational needs and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements.

## NemoClaw Introduction
NVIDIA announced the introduction of "[[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]]" at the [[entities/gtc-conference|GTC conference]] in 2026, positioning it as an extension or enhancement to their OpenClaw project. CEO [[entities/jensen-huang|Jensen Huang]] emphasized that every company should consider developing a comprehensive "[[concepts/openclaw-strategy|OpenClaw strategy]]" in light of increasing AI [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and [[concepts/ai-security|data security]] concerns.

### Comparison Points
- **Security Enhancements:** NemoClaw brings additional layers of protection specifically designed for enterprise use.
- **Integration with Existing Systems:** NemoClaw is designed to integrate seamlessly with OpenClaw, offering enhanced capabilities without requiring a complete overhaul of existing systems.
- **Support and Maintenance:** Users of both OpenClaw and NemoClaw will receive continued support from NVIDIA, ensuring that their AI frameworks remain up-to-date and secure.
