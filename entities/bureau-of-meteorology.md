# Bureau of Meteorology

The **Bureau of Meteorology** (BoM) is Australia's national weather, climate, and water agency. It provides forecasting, warning, and observational services to support safety, economic activity, and environmental management across the continent.

## Key Responsibilities
- **[[concepts/weather-forecasting|Weather Forecasting]]:** Issuing short-to-[[entities/medium|medium]] range forecasts and severe weather warnings.
- **Climate Monitoring:** Tracking long-term climate trends and variability, including ENSO, IOD, and SAM.
- **[[concepts/moisture-control|Water Management]]:** Providing long-range climate outlooks to inform water resource planning and agricultural decisions.
- **Observation Network:** Operating a vast network of radar, satellite, and ground-based stations.
