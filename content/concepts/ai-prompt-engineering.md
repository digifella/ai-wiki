# Ai Prompt Engineering

Ai Prompt Engineering refers to the practice of designing and refining text inputs—prompts—to guide AI language models toward producing desired outputs. This involves selecting specific language, [[concepts/structure|structure]], and context to optimize how AI assistants like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] interpret and respond to requests. The technique has become essential as organizations seek to extract consistent, high-quality results from general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] AI systems.

## Professional and Legal Applications

In professional contexts, particularly [[concepts/legal-work|legal work]], [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] enables practitioners to configure AI assistants for specialized tasks. [[concepts/custom-instructions|Custom instructions]] can be established to ensure outputs meet professional [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], maintain appropriate tone and formality, and adhere to domain-specific requirements. This [[concepts/customization|customization]] helps bridge the gap between general [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] and the specific needs of legal research, document drafting, and case analysis, where precision and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] are critical.

## Core Techniques

Effective prompt engineering combines several approaches: providing explicit context and constraints, specifying desired output formats, including relevant background information, and iteratively refining prompts based on results. Practitioners often establish system-level [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] that persist across multiple interactions, rather than repeating instructions with each query. [[concepts/testing|Testing]] and adjustment remain central to the process, as subtle wording changes can significantly affect output quality and relevance.
