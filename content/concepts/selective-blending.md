# Selective blending

A technique for merging elements from multiple [[concepts/images|images]] or layers into a single cohesive composition by selectively revealing or hiding specific parts of each source. Commonly used to enhance or correct photographs without visible seams.

**Core [[concepts/software|applications]]:**
- Fixing imperfections (e.g., closed eyes in group photos)
- Combining optimal elements from similar shots (e.g., best lighting, expressions)
- Creating seamless composites from non-tripod-stabilized images

**Key [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] method ([[concepts/photoshop]]):**
- Stack source images and Image alignment
- Use [[concepts/layer-masks|Layer masks]] to selectively reveal desired elements from each layer
- Blend transitions to maintain natural appearance

**AI-powered advancements ([[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]]):**
- [[concepts/contextual-task-bar|Harmonize Feature]]: Automatically blends color, lighting, and shadow across layers
