# Review Prioritization

## Core Concept
Review prioritization is the process of determining the order in which reviews should be conducted, based on factors such as urgency, importance, and resource availability.

## Key Factors
- **Urgency**: Time-sensitive reviews that need immediate [[concepts/attention|attention]].
- **Importance**: High-impact reviews that significantly affect [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
- **Resource Availability**: Reviews that can be completed with available resources.
