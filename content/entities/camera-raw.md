# Camera Raw

Adobe's image processing engine and plugin used for non-destructive RAW development within [[concepts/photoshop]] and [[concepts/lightroom]].

## Features & Capabilities
- Advanced RAW data manipulation, [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], and [[concepts/exposure-adjustment|exposure adjustment]].
- **[[concepts/lens-blur|Lens Blur]] Tool**: Part of recent updates in [[concepts/camera-raw]] and [[concepts/lightroom]] to simulate realistic depth of field.
- **Depth-Based [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]**: Leverages [[concepts/depth-maps|depth maps]] to enable advanced, realistic photo [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] and precise 3D-like effects.
- **Advanced [[concepts/masking|Masking]]**: Utilizes depth information for complex, realistic [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] and [[concepts/3d-photo-manipulation|3D photo manipulation]].
