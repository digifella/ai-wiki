# Specialized Expert

A **specialized expert** is a model fine-tuned from a general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] base model to become proficient in specific tasks or domains. This process involves [[concepts/training|training]] the model on domain-specific datasets to enhance its performance and [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] for targeted [[concepts/software|applications]].
