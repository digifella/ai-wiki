# Shadows Adjustment

Shadows Adjustment is a post-processing technique in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that focuses on refining the tonal values in the darker areas of an image. This approach is particularly useful in [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]], where shadow detail often determines the overall visual impact of the composition. By selectively targeting shadow regions rather than adjusting the entire image, photographers can preserve highlight detail while bringing out [[concepts/texture|texture]] and dimension in darker areas.

## Masking and Selective Application

The technique relies on Lightroom Classic's [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] features to isolate [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific shadow areas. Rather than applying blanket tonal changes across an image, masking allows photographers to target [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] in particular regions—such as foreground elements, tree canopies, or cliff [[concepts/faces|faces]]—with precision. This selective approach prevents unwanted side effects on other tonal ranges and maintains the natural appearance of the photograph.

## Practical Application

In landscape photography, shadows adjustment typically involves using the Shadows slider in the Basic panel in conjunction with targeted masks, or employing the dedicated shadow/highlight recovery tools in the [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]]. Common adjustments include lifting shadow detail to reveal hidden textures, adjusting shadow color cast, or darkening specific shadowed regions to increase [[concepts/contrast|contrast]]. This technique works best when combined with careful [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] metering during the initial capture.
