# Ai Work Team

An [[concepts/ai-work-team|AI Work Team]] is a system of [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] that collaborate to complete tasks, typically built using [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] and [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/claude-api|Claude API]]. Rather than relying on a single AI model to handle all aspects of a project, a work team distributes responsibilities across multiple [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] that can communicate and coordinate with one another.

## Building and Implementation

Tutorials on constructing AI Work Teams focus on practical techniques for creating multiple [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] instances that can be configured with different roles and capabilities. The agents within such a team can be designed to handle specific aspects of a project—such as [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], testing, documentation, or project management—with the ability to delegate tasks, request assistance, and share information across the [[concepts/team-composition|team structure]].

## Benefits and Use Cases

An autonomous work team approach enables more complex [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] by breaking tasks into specialized components. This architecture can improve efficiency by allowing agents to work in parallel on different aspects of a project, and can provide built-in validation and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]] through inter-agent review and collaboration.
