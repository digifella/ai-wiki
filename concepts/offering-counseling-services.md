# Offering Counseling Services

Offering counseling services refers to the provision of professional [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] and psychosocial support within [[concepts/health|healthcare]] settings, particularly as an integrated component of [[concepts/community-health-nursing|community health nursing]]. These services address emotional, behavioral, and social needs of individuals and populations, functioning as both preventive and therapeutic interventions. Counseling in this context is delivered by trained [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] professionals, including nurses, social [[entities/employees|workers]], and dedicated counselors, often in community-based or primary care settings rather than specialized mental health facilities. Effective delivery increasingly relies on coproduction models that engage patients as active partners.

## Scope and Types

Counseling services within [[concepts/fundamentals-of-nursing|community health nursing]] encompass various modalities tailored to community needs. These may include individual counseling for mental health concerns, family counseling to address relationship dynamics, health behavior counseling focused on lifestyle modifications, and [[concepts/crisis|crisis]] counseling for acute psychological distress.

## Enhancing Coproduction via Technology

Integrating digital tools facilitates the shift from passive receipt of care to active coproduction, particularly in [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|chronic disease management]].

- **Point-of-Care Dashboards**: Tools like those discussed in [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Citters---Point-of-care-dashboards-promote-coproduction|Citters - Point-of-care dashboards promote coproduction]] demonstrate how real-time [[concepts/information-visualization|data visualization]] supports shared [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
- **Inflammatory Bowel Disease (IBD) Management**: Research indicates that dashboards displaying clinical metrics during visits enhance patient-provider [[concepts/communication|dialogue]], fostering shared decision-making and aligning treatment plans with patient priorities.
- **Patient Engagement**: By making clinical data accessible and interpretable at the point of care, these systems reduce [[concepts/algorithmic-filtering|information asymmetry]], enabling patients to contribute meaningfully to their [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]] pathways.
- **Outcomes**: Such interventions correlate with improved patient satisfaction and adherence, bridging the gap between clinical data and lived [[concepts/experience|experience]].
