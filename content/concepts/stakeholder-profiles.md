# Stakeholder Profiles

Stakeholder Profiles are intelligence status cards that provide at-a-glance visibility into the information maturity of individual stakeholder records. Each profile card displays the current intelligence status, indicating either the number of matched intel items associated with that profile or a warning that no intelligence has been matched.

## Intelligence Status Indicators

Profile cards automatically show one of two status states: a green indicator displaying the count of matched intel items, or an amber warning indicating "no intel" when a profile has no associated intelligence. This status updates automatically whenever a profile is saved, created, or imported via CSV, ensuring the intelligence display remains current.

## Filtering by Intelligence Status

A dedicated filter tab labeled "No Intel (N)" appears within the profile type bar, allowing users to quickly identify and access profiles that lack matched intelligence. This filter displays the exact count of profiles with no intelligence, making it straightforward to prioritize intelligence-gathering efforts or identify gaps in stakeholder knowledge.
