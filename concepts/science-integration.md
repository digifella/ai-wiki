# Science Integration

Science Integration is an interdisciplinary framework that combines artistic perspectives and methodologies with scientific research and practice. This approach addresses how creative practices can enhance traditional scientific work, particularly in the areas of communication, visualization, and discovery processes. By leveraging the complementary strengths of both fields, the concept seeks to bridge conventional boundaries between creative and scientific disciplines.

The integration manifests across multiple domains of scientific practice, aiming to improve the accessibility and impact of scientific findings. It involves structured collaboration between researchers and artists to refine data representation and foster new modes of inquiry. This framework operates within the broader tools, platforms, and infrastructure domain, providing the structural support necessary for sustained cross-disciplinary engagement.

A key component of this initiative is the partnership between the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI) and Emily Pulse-it. This collaboration exemplifies the practical application of the framework, focusing on embedding artistic methodologies into the daily workflows of scientific institutions. The goal is to create sustainable models where artistic insight informs scientific communication and public engagement without compromising research integrity.
