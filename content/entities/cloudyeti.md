# CloudYeti

CloudYeti is a YouTube channel that focuses on practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of local [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems. The channel covers topics including local AI tooling, the return on investment from AI implementations, and [[concepts/developer|developer]] workflows for integrating AI into [[concepts/coding|software development]] processes.

## Content Focus

The channel produces technical content comparing [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] and [[concepts/proprietary-ai|proprietary AI]] solutions in real-world [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. Notable videos include performance comparisons between local models like [[concepts/ai-driven-code-editing|Qwen Code]] running on Ollama and commercial alternatives such as [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] for [[concepts/autonomous-ai-coding-agent|agentic coding]] tasks. This testing approach provides developers with empirical data for evaluating different AI tools in their own workflows.
