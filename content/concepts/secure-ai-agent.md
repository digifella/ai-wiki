# Secure AI Agent

[[concepts/secure|Secure]] [[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]] is [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]'s enterprise-focused framework for building and deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] with built-in [[concepts/security|security]] considerations. It is positioned as an alternative to [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], offering organizations a different approach to implementing [[concepts/files|autonomous AI systems]] in production environments.

## Positioning and Purpose

The framework is designed to address enterprise requirements where security, [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and [[concepts/governance|governance]] are critical priorities. Rather than replacing existing solutions outright, Secure AI Agent represents NVIDIA's strategic offering in the competitive landscape of [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] platforms, providing enterprises with options that align with their specific security and operational needs.

## Relationship to NemoClaw

Secure AI Agent is closely associated with NVIDIA's [[concepts/agent-toolkit|NemoClaw]] initiative, which explores how newer frameworks can complement or differentiate from established solutions like [[concepts/conversational-chatbots|OpenClaw]]. The comparison between these approaches [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] ongoing developments in how enterprises can architect [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]] with varying trade-offs between flexibility, security, and ease of [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
