# Cooking Techniques

Cooking techniques are the fundamental methods and procedures used to transform raw ingredients into prepared food through [[concepts/cooking-time|heat application]] and ingredient manipulation. These techniques form the foundation of [[concepts/culinary-arts|culinary practice]] across all cuisines and [[concepts/skill|skill]] levels, from basic home cooking to professional kitchens. Understanding proper technique improves food safety, [[concepts/texture|texture]], flavor development, and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] in results.

## Heat-Based Methods

[[concepts/cooking-methods|Cooking methods]] are broadly categorized by how heat is applied to food. Dry heat methods, such as roasting, baking, [[concepts/bbq|grilling]], and [[concepts/skillet-frying|sautéing]], cook food without added liquid or fat. These methods are effective for browning surfaces and developing complex flavors through the [[concepts/maillard-reaction|Maillard reaction]]. Moist heat methods, including boiling, steaming, [[concepts/seafood-poaching|poaching]], and braising, use water, stock, or other liquids to cook food. These techniques are gentler on delicate ingredients and help preserve certain nutrients, though they may result in less browning and flavor concentration than dry methods.

## Ingredient Manipulation

Beyond heat application, cooking techniques encompass how ingredients are prepared and combined before cooking. This includes knife [[concepts/skills|skills]] for cutting and sizing ingredients, mixing and emulsifying ingredients, and timing the addition of components to achieve desired outcomes. Proper [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] ensures even cooking, influences the final [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] of dishes, and affects how flavors develop and combine during cooking.

## Skill and Consistency

Mastering cooking techniques requires practice and understanding how variables such as temperature, timing, and ingredient quality affect results. Consistent technique allows cooks to reliably reproduce dishes and troubleshoot problems when results vary. This foundational knowledge supports both traditional recipes and creative culinary [[concepts/innovation|innovation]].
