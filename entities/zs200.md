# ZS200

The ZS200 is a superzoom compact camera from Panasonic's ZS series. As a point-and-shoot model, it is designed to deliver extended optical zoom capabilities in a portable package, making it accessible to photographers seeking zoom range without the bulk of interchangeable lens systems.

## Position in the ZS Series

The ZS200 occupies a middle position within Panasonic's ZS line, which has continued to evolve with successive releases. The model was eventually succeeded by the ZS300, a later iteration in the series. According to PetaPixel's analysis, the ZS300 offered only minimal superzoom upgrades over the ZS200 relative to its cost, suggesting that the ZS200 remained a competitive option for users considering the newer model.
