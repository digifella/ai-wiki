# Bootcamp Sessions

Vyond Bootcamp Sessions are guided [[concepts/learning|learning]] experiences within the Vyond platform designed to help users create animated videos. These sessions provide structured tutorials and hands-on practice for both new and experienced users working with Vyond's animation tools.

## AI Assistance

Bootcamp Sessions include AI assistance features that help users throughout the [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] process. This AI support provides guidance and suggestions to streamline the creation [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] and improve the quality of animated content.

## Stock Characters in Vyond Go

Within Bootcamp Sessions on [[entities/vyond-go|Vyond Go]], users have the ability to select and customize [[concepts/stock-characters|stock characters]] before generating their videos. This allows creators to choose character assets that align with their project needs and creative [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] early in the production process, rather than making character selections after [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
