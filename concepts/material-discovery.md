# Material Discovery

**Material discovery** refers to the systematic process of identifying, designing, and validating new materials with specific properties for industrial or scientific application. Traditionally reliant on trial-and-error experimentation, the field is increasingly shifting toward **AI-driven** approaches to accelerate timelines and reduce costs.

## Key Drivers & Challenges

*   **[[concepts/geopolitical-vulnerability|Geopolitical Vulnerability]]:** Dependence on critical raw materials, particularly **rare earth elements**, creates supply chain risks.
*   **AI Acceleration:** Machine learning models are being used to predict material properties and optimize synthesis pathways, significantly reducing the "time-to-market" for new materials.
*   **[[concepts/lab-automation|Lab Automation]]:** Integration of robotic systems with AI allows for high-throughput experimentation, enabling rapid iteration cycles.
