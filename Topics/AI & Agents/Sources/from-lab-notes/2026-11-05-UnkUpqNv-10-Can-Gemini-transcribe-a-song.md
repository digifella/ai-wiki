## Can Gemini transcribe a song?
**Clip title:** Can Gemini transcribe a song?
**Author / channel:** Google Cloud Tech
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UnkUpqNv-10
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Google Cloud Tech: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
In the video, Gabe Weiss, a Data Cloud Developer Relations Manager at Google Cloud, discusses his excitement for Gemini 4 and its potential to assist musicians. As a musician who plays the mandolin, Gabe highlights the common challenge of finding sheet music for obscure or newly discovered songs. He challenges Gemini 4 to generate sheet music for an old folk tune called "La Basura."

The video shows Mollie Pettit testing different versions of Gemini to generate the sheet music. First, they try Gemini 3.5 Flash Lite, which Gabe evaluates while playing the mandolin. He notes that the generated sheet music is in the wrong key and has incorrect clefs, resulting in a tune that is far from the original song. 

Next, they try Gemini 3.8 Flash, which shows some improvement with the correct key, but the notes and time signature are still off. Finally, they try Gemini 4 Argon, which gets much closer to the original tune, though still with some errors in time signature and notes. Gabe is impressed by how Gemini 4 Argon gets close enough for him to pick up the rest by ear, demonstrating the potential of AI in assisting with complex musical tasks like solving the polyphonic problem.
