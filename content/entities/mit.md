## Meta-Harness Project Summary
Meta-Harness is a groundbreaking system developed by teams at [[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]], MIT, and [[entities/krafton|KRAFTON]]. The project focuses on the end-to-end optimization of AI model harnesses, aiming to enhance the performance and autonomy of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]].

### Key Concepts Introduced in the Video
- **Harness:** Traditional code wrapped around an LLM that dictates its operation (e.g., storing memories, searching text, [[concepts/writing|writing]] code).
- **LLM:** [[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]]
