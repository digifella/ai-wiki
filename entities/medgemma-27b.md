# MedGemma 27B

MedGemma 27B is a 27 billion parameter large language model developed by Google for medical applications. The model is designed to process and comprehend both text and images within clinical and healthcare contexts, making it suitable for multimodal tasks involving medical documentation, diagnostic imagery, and health data analysis.

## Capabilities

The model combines text and image understanding to support medical professionals in clinical workflows. As a specialized medical model, MedGemma 27B aims to provide domain-specific performance for healthcare use cases that require understanding of both written medical content and visual diagnostic data.
