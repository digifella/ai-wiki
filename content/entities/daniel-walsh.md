# Daniel Walsh

[[entities/daniel-miessler|Daniel]] Walsh is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] known for educational videos about [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/machine-learning-systems|machine learning systems]]. He has produced technical explainer content focused on emerging AI architectures and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tools.

## GraphRAG and LightRAG

Walsh authored a video explaining [[concepts/graph-retrieval-augmented-generation|GraphRAG]] ([[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] enhanced with [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]]), which demonstrates how structured knowledge representations can improve AI system performance. In this work, he showcased LightRAG, an open-source system designed to enhance [[entities/agent|agent]] [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] through the use of knowledge graphs structured as [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]]. This video was published in April 2026.
