# Personalized Medicine

**Personalized medicine** (also known as **[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] medicine**) is a medical model that customizes treatment and [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] strategies to individual patient characteristics, primarily genetics, environment, and lifestyle. Unlike the traditional "one-size-fits-all" approach, it aims to optimize therapeutic efficacy while minimizing adverse effects.

## Core Principles

- **Genomic Profiling**: Utilizing [[concepts/dna-analysis|DNA sequencing]] and biomarker analysis to predict drug response.
- **Targeted Therapy**: Developing drugs that specifically target molecular [[concepts/causes|drivers]] of disease (e.g., oncology).
- **Pharmacogenomics**: Studying how genetic variation affects individual responses to drugs.
- **Data Integration**: Combining clinical data with large-scale datasets ([[entities/big-data]], [[concepts/machine-learning]]) for [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]].
- **Advanced Biological Decoding**: Leveraging [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|AI]] to interpret complex biological data, including [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR]] applications and [[concepts/single-cell-sequencing|single-cell sequencing]], to unravel cellular complexity and [[concepts/disease-mechanisms|disease mechanisms]] [[lab-notes/2026-07-15-Decoding-Biologys-Language-AI-CRISPR-and-Single-Cell-Seq|Decoding Biology's Language: AI, CRISPR, and Single-Cell Sequencing for Disease]].
