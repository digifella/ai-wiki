# Naive But Good Natured AI

Naive But Good Natured AI is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] technique for [[concepts/mcps|AI coding agents]] named after the character [[entities/ralph-wiggum|Ralph Wiggum]] from the animated series *The Simpsons*. The technique, popularized by the [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]], applies [[entities/ralph-ramos|Ralph]]'s characteristic approach to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]—straightforward, well-intentioned, but lacking sophistication—as a deliberate [[concepts/design|design]] pattern for AI systems.

## Core Approach

The technique involves building [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] that operate with simple, direct logic rather than complex optimization strategies. Like Ralph Wiggum's innocent and earnest attempts [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] tasks, Naive But Good Natured AI systems prioritize transparency and benevolence in their [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes over computational elegance. This approach can be valuable in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] and alignment with human intentions matter more than raw efficiency.

## Application

Developers using this technique design AI systems that follow straightforward behavioral [[concepts/loops|loops]] and rules, making their actions predictable and easy to understand. The method trades algorithmic sophistication for clarity of [[concepts/motivation|purpose]], which can reduce unexpected behaviors and [[entities/make|make]] systems easier to debug and maintain. This approach is particularly relevant for [[concepts/safe-ai-use|AI safety]] considerations, where well-understood and benign [[entities/agent|agent]] behavior is preferable to opaque optimization.
