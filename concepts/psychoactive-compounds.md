# Psychoactive Compounds

**Psychoactive compounds** are chemical substances that affect the central nervous system, altering perception, mood, [[concepts/conscious-thought|consciousness]], [[concepts/cognition|cognition]], or behavior. This category includes stimulants, depressants, hallucinogens, and nootropics.

## Key Categories

### Stimulants
- **Caffeine**: The most widely consumed psychoactive substance globally. Found in coffee, tea, and energy drinks. Acts as an adenosine receptor antagonist.
- **Nicotine**: Found in tobacco products; acts on nicotinic acetylcholine receptors.
- **Synthetic Stimulants**: Includes amphetamines and methylphenidate.

### Depressants
- **Alcohol (Ethanol)**: A central nervous system depressant. Widely consumed socially and recreationally.
- **Benzodiazepines**: Prescription sedatives [[concepts/acting|acting]] on GABA receptors.
- **Opioids**: Pain relievers with significant abuse potential.

### Hallucinogens
- **Psilocybin**: Found in certain mushrooms.
- **LSD**: A potent synthetic hallucinogen.
- **DMT**: A powerful psychedelic compound.

## Processing and Modification of Psychoactive Content

The concentration and presence of psychoactive compounds in consumables can be altered through industrial processing methods.

- **[[concepts/caffeine-removal|Decaffeination]]**: The process of removing Caffeine from coffee beans, tea leaves, or other sources. Methods include solvent-based extraction (using methylene chloride or ethyl acetate), water processing (Swiss [[concepts/water-process|Water Process]]), and supercritical carbon dioxide extraction. See [[lab-notes/2026-06-24-Decaffeination-and-Non-Alcoholic-Beverages-Methods-Histo|Decaffeination and Non-Alcoholic Beverages: Methods, History, and Chemistry]] for detailed chemical and [[concepts/historical-context|historical context]].
- **[[concepts/non-alcoholic-beverages|Non-Alcoholic Beverages]]**: Production of beers and wines with reduced or [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] Alcohol content. Techniques involve [[concepts/vacuum-distillation|vacuum distillation]], [[concepts/desalination-technology|reverse osmosis]], or arrested fermentation to remove ethanol while preserving flavor profiles.
