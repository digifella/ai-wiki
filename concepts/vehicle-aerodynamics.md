# Vehicle Aerodynamics

## Core Principles
- Study of air motion around vehicles to optimize drag reduction and downforce generation.
- Relies heavily on solving the navier stokes equations, which describe the motion of viscous fluid substances.
- Critical for fuel efficiency, stability, and thermal management in automotive and aerospace engineering.

## Computational Challenges
- The [[concepts/navier-stokes-millennium-prize-problem]] remains one of the most significant unsolved challenges in mathematics, concerning the [[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|existence and smoothness]] of solutions for [[concepts/fluid-flow|fluid flow]].
- Historically, vehicle aerodynamics has relied on CFD (Computational [[concepts/fluid-motion|Fluid Dynamics]]) approximations due to the intractability of exact analytical solutions.
