# Operational Order

The Operational Order is a systematic framework developed by America's Test Kitchen to standardize the preparation of fried rice. Presented by Lan Lam in the video "The Formula to Great Fried Rice with Any Ingredients," this guide separates core technical procedures from specific ingredient selections. By establishing reproducible techniques, the framework allows cooks to apply consistent principles across various ingredient combinations rather than relying on rigid, fixed recipes.

The methodology emphasizes the critical importance of rice texture and moisture control. It dictates that day-old, cold rice is essential because the refrigeration process dries out the grains, preventing them from becoming mushy or clumping together during the high-heat stir-frying process. This technical prerequisite ensures that individual grains remain distinct and achieve the desired chewy yet firm consistency.

Ingredient integration follows a specific sequence to optimize flavor and texture. Aromatics such as garlic and ginger are typically sautéed first to infuse the oil, followed by proteins that are cooked through before being set aside. Vegetables are added next to ensure proper cooking times, after which the rice is introduced and broken up thoroughly. The final steps involve seasoning the rice with sauces like soy sauce or fish sauce, returning the proteins and aromatics to the pan, and finishing with fresh herbs or eggs to maintain their structural integrity and brightness.
