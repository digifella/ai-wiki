# Global context awareness

The ability of an AI system to maintain a consistent understanding of an entire scene or document during operations, ensuring edits or modifications are contextually coherent across all elements rather than isolated components.

- **Adobe Firefly [[concepts/prompt-to-edit|Prompt-to-edit]]**: Demonstrates global context awareness by enabling natural language-driven [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] (e.g., adjusting materials, textures, mood) while preserving overall composition through [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]]. 2026 04 14 [[concepts/adobe-firefly-prompt-to-edit|Adobe Firefly Prompt to edit]] feature [[entities/julianne-kost|Julianne Kost]]
- **Key distinction from alternatives**: Unlike [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]] (which shares AI [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] foundations), Firefly's approach prioritizes holistic scene understanding, making it suitable for complex contextual edits requiring compositional consistency.
