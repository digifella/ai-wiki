# Mental Ocean Analogy

The **Mental Ocean Analogy** conceptualizes [[concepts/the-mind|the mind]] as a vast body of water, distinguishing between surface-level awareness and deep, submerged processes. It serves as a heuristic for understanding the relationship between [[concepts/conscious-thought|Consciousness]] and the [[concepts/unconscious-mind]], particularly in the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] internal states.

## Core Metaphor
- **Surface Waves**: Represent immediate, conscious [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] and active processing.
- **Deep Currents**: Symbolize latent knowledge, implicit [[concepts/biases|biases]], and unconscious computational pathways.
- **Tides**: Reflect rhythmic shifts in [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] or emotional state.

## Application to AI Cognition
Recent investigations into [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) utilize this analogy to probe whether models exhibit structured internal mental lives.
