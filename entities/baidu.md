# Baidu

## Overview
Chinese multinational technology company headquartered in Beijing. Core operations span internet search, [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud infrastructure]], autonomous driving, and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. Major domestic player in the Chinese Technology Sector and competitive actor in global [[concepts/large-language-models]] development.

## AI & Foundation Models
Primary R&D focus on the ERNIE (Enhanced Representation through [[concepts/knowledge-integration|Knowledge Integration]]) series. Models prioritize Chinese language optimization, enterprise [[concepts/integration|API integration]], and cost-efficient training pipelines. [[concepts/strategic-pivot|Strategic pivot]] from web-scale services to AI-native infrastructure reduces reliance on foreign model providers.
