# Watch Mechanism

A Watch Mechanism is the internal assembly of a timepiece responsible for regulating timekeeping. It typically consists of a power source, an escapement, and a time-regulating element.

## Tourbillon Mechanism

The Tourbillon Watch Mechanism: Purpose, [[concepts/gravity-compensation|Gravity Compensation]], and 3D-Printed Model is a specialized complication designed to improve accuracy.

### Purpose and Function
*   **Gravity Compensation:** The primary purpose is to counteract the effects of gravity on the Escapement and balance wheel, which can cause positional errors in pocket watches and wristwatches.
*   **Rotating Cage:** The mechanism rotates the entire escapement and balance wheel assembly (often once per minute) to average out positional errors over time.
*   **Accuracy:** Historically critical for pocket watches; in modern wristwatches, it serves more as a display of Horology craftsmanship, though it still offers marginal accuracy benefits in specific positions.
