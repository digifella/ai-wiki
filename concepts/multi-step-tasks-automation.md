# Multi Step Tasks Automation

Multi-step tasks automation refers to the use of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to execute [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]] that require sequential actions across multiple systems or platforms. [[concepts/general-purpose-ai-agent|ChatGPT Agent]] is a feature within [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] designed to handle this type of automation by breaking down larger objectives into smaller, manageable steps and executing them in sequence without continuous human intervention.

## Capabilities and Functionality

[[concepts/chatgpt-agent|ChatGPT Agent]] can interact with [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]] and systems, including web browsers and [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], to complete tasks that span multiple stages. This allows the automation of processes that traditionally required manual switching between applications or repeated human input. The agent can make decisions based on intermediate results and adjust its approach as needed throughout the execution of a task.

## Applications

Practical applications of multi-step [[concepts/recurring-actions|task automation]] include marketing workflows, data processing pipelines, research compilation, and administrative processes. Organizations have begun exploring how agents can reduce time spent on repetitive procedural work, allowing human teams to focus on higher-level strategic activities.
