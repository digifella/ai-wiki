# Automated article generation

The autonomous production of structured, long-form text using [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and [[entities/llms]]. This process extends beyond [[concepts/simple-prompting|simple prompting]] to include multi-step [[concepts/agentic-ai]] involving [[concepts/deep-research-function|deep research]], outline synthesis, and fact-checking.

## Key Implementations
- **[[entities/storm|STORM]] AI** ([[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s [[concepts/agent-based-knowledge-curation|STORM AI]]: Verifiable, [[concepts/agent-based-research|Agent-Based Research]] and Knowledge Curation): A specialized system for verifiable, agent-based research and knowledge curation.
	- Utilizes **STORM** (Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and [[concepts/multi-perspective-question-asking|Multi-perspective Question Asking]]) to build comprehensive content.
	- Employs multi-perspective interrogation to ensure depth and breadth in topic coverage.
	- Focuses on transforming raw retrieval into structured, research-backed knowledge.

## Core Methodologies
- [[concepts/retrieval-augmented-generation-rag]]: Integrating external, verifiable data sources into the generation [[concepts/loop|loop]].
- Multi-perspective Questioning: Using [[concepts/agents|agents]] to simulate different viewpoints to expand topic outlines.
- [[concepts/agentic-ai]]: Iterative [[concepts/loops|loops]] of drafting, critiquing, and refining content.
