# Military Logistics

**Military logistics** is the discipline of planning and executing the [[concepts/exercise|movement]] and maintenance of forces. It encompasses the procurement, acquisition, distribution, transportation, evacuation, and maintenance of material, as well as the movement, housing, and medical support of [[entities/employees|personnel]]. In modern asymmetric conflicts, logistics often dictates the tempo and sustainability of operations more than tactical [[concepts/excellence|superiority]].

## Core Components
- **Supply Chain Management**: The [[concepts/flow|flow]] of ammunition, fuel, food, and spare parts from production to the front line.
- **Transportation Networks**: Utilization of rail, road, and air assets for strategic and tactical mobility.
- **Maintenance and Repair**: Field-level sustainment of equipment to ensure operational readiness.
- **Medical Logistics**: Evacuation and treatment infrastructure for casualties.

## Strategic Vulnerabilities
Logistical [[concepts/nodes|nodes]] are high-value targets. Disruption of [[concepts/supply-lines|supply lines]] can halt offensives or force retreats. Key vulnerabilities include:
- **Chokepoints**: Bridges, tunnels, and narrow roads that concentrate traffic.
- **[[entities/storage|Storage]] Facilities**: Ammunition depots and fuel farms.
- **Command and Control**: Communication hubs that coordinate distribution.

## Case Study: Ukraine-Russia Conflict (2026)
The [[concepts/2026-campaign-season|2026 campaign season]] highlighted the critical intersection of logistics and defensive fortification.

- **[[concepts/logistics-strikes|Logistics Strikes]]**: [[concepts/russian-offensive|Russian offensive]] efforts slowed significantly due to targeted strikes on logistical infrastructure. These strikes disrupted the flow of materiel necessary to sustain high-intensity operations.
- **[[concepts/fortress-belt|Fortress Belt]]**: [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]] implemented a "[[concepts/defensive-fortifications|Fortress Belt]]" strategy, leveraging defensive depth to stretch and attrit enemy supply lines. This approach forced adversaries to extend their logistics further into contested territory, increasing [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] to interdiction.
- **Regional Focus**: Operations spanning from [[entities/crimea|Crimea]] to Kostiantynivka demonstrated how logistical degradation can neutralize numerical advantages. The integration of drone warfare and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] strikes against rear-area assets became a decisive factor in slowing the Russian advance.
