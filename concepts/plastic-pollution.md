# Plastic Pollution

**Plastic pollution** refers to the accumulation of synthetic plastic objects in the [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s environment that adversely affects wildlife, wildlife habitat, and humans. Since the 1950s, humanity has produced approximately 8.3 billion tons of plastic, with a significant portion persisting in ecosystems due to slow degradation rates.

## Biological Remediation Strategies

Recent research focuses on leveraging biological agents to mitigate plastic waste, moving beyond traditional mechanical recycling.

*   **[[concepts/enzymatic-degradation|Enzymatic Degradation]]**: Scientists are developing enzymes capable of breaking down polymer chains in plastics, offering a potential [[concepts/pathway-platform|pathway]] for biodegradable waste management.
*   **Organic Consumption**: Certain living organisms have been identified that can consume or break down plastic materials, presenting natural solutions to accumulation.
