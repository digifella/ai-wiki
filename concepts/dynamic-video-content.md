# Dynamic Video Content

[[concepts/dynamic-videos|Dynamic Video Content]] is a video production methodology that leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] image generation models to create video frames through systematic [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative refinement]]. Rather than relying exclusively on traditional cinematography or pre-rendered digital assets, this approach generates individual frames using [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] such as [[entities/gpt-image-2|GPT Image 2]]. The producer functions as a director of the generative process, crafting and refining [[concepts/text-prompts|text prompts]] to guide the model toward desired visual outputs across sequential frames.

## Production Process

The core workflow involves a cyclical process of [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] and frame generation. Producers begin by defining the visual parameters of each shot through detailed textual descriptions. These prompts are fed into the [[concepts/image-generation-model|image generation model]] to produce static frames. The producer then evaluates the output for [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], lighting, and [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] alignment, adjusting the prompts iteratively to correct deviations or enhance specific elements.

This iterative refinement continues until each frame meets the required aesthetic and [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] standards. Once the sequence of frames is finalized, they are compiled into a video file. The method allows for [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over visual details that may be difficult to achieve with standard [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] models, though it requires significant manual oversight to maintain temporal [[concepts/coherence|coherence]] between frames.
