# Joggai

Joggai is a software tool designed to automatically convert AI-generated audio podcasts into video format. The tool addresses a gap in podcast production by automating the transformation of audio files into finished video content, reducing the need for manual editing and post-production work.

## Functionality

The tool processes existing audio podcast files and converts them into video content with minimal user intervention. By automating this conversion process, Joggai streamlines content creation workflows, allowing creators to repurpose audio-only podcasts as video products without extensive manual labor. This automation is particularly relevant for creators working with AI-generated audio content who want to distribute their podcasts across multiple formats and platforms.
