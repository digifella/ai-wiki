## Overview
Research tools encompass a range of software and services designed to enhance [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] in [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]], professional, and personal research. These include AI-driven applications, reference management systems, data analysis platforms, and more.

## Key Concepts

### Google NotebookLM
- **[[concepts/llms|Definition]]:** An advanced tool developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] for enhanced research and multi-format [[concepts/content-creation|content synthesis]].
- **Features:**
  - Synthesis of information from diverse sources
  - Facilitates efficient note-taking and collaboration
  - Supports various formats including text, images, videos, and interactive widgets
  - Integrates [[concepts/ai-workflow|AI capabilities]] for smarter [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]
  - Grounded in specific user-provided sources to minimize hallucinations
  - Supports diverse inputs: [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]], [[entities/google-slides|Slides]], [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]], and URLs
  - Functions as a content system capable of generating [[concepts/google-slides|slides]], [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]], videos, and [[concepts/data-tables|data tables]]
