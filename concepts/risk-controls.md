# Risk Controls

Risk controls are governance mechanisms and security measures designed to mitigate organizational vulnerabilities and protect institutional integrity. They function as procedural frameworks and technical safeguards within organizational infrastructure, establishing boundaries on resource use and preventing unauthorized activities. Effective risk controls balance operational efficiency with protective oversight, ensuring that institutional assets remain secure while enabling legitimate business functions.

## Governance and Communication Security

A central concern in risk control is the governance of communication channels, specifically regarding the unauthorized use of email addresses. This involves implementing strict protocols to verify sender identity and recipient authorization, thereby preventing spoofing, phishing, and data leakage. By enforcing authentication standards and monitoring outbound traffic, organizations can maintain the confidentiality and authenticity of their external communications.

These controls extend to broader infrastructure management, where access rights are regularly audited and privileges are granted on a least-privilege basis. This approach minimizes the attack surface and reduces the potential impact of internal or external breaches. Continuous monitoring and automated alerting systems further support these measures by detecting anomalous behavior in real-time, allowing for rapid incident response and remediation.
