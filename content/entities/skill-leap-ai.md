# Skill Leap Ai

Skill Leap Ai is a content creator focused on practical guides for deploying and [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] on personal computers. The channel provides step-by-step tutorials designed to make advanced [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] accessible to individual users without requiring [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]] or external APIs.

## Content Focus

The channel's primary emphasis is on [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] of [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]], particularly Meta's Llama series. Notable content includes guides for running [[entities/llama-31|Llama 3.1]] with a stated setup time of approximately 10 minutes, aimed at users seeking private, on-device [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] [[concepts/inference|inference]]. The channel also covers related topics such as [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] and [[concepts/browser-control|browser control]] capabilities.
