# Roadmap Shifts

**Roadmap Shifts** refer to significant deviations from planned trajectories in technological development, project management, or scientific research. These shifts often occur due to unforeseen technical bottlenecks, changes in resource allocation, or the correction of initial over-optimistic projections (hype cycles).

## Key Characteristics
- **Expectation vs. Reality:** The gap between public/industry hype and actual deliverable milestones.
- **Technical Bottlenecks:** Unanticipated physical or [[concepts/scaling-bottlenecks|computational limits]] that force a re-evaluation of timelines.
- **Resource Reallocation:** Shifting focus from theoretical exploration to [[entities/grady|practical engineering]] challenges.

## Case Study: Quantum Computing
The field of [[entities/quantum-computing]] exemplifies roadmap shifts driven by the tension between hype and practicality. Recent analyses highlight a divergence between early optimistic projections and current [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] realities.

- **Hype vs. Practicality:** Significant scrutiny is being applied to the current state of quantum development, noting that progress is often slower than public narratives suggest.
- **Shifting Timelines:** Roadmaps are being adjusted to reflect the difficulty of [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[concepts/qubits|qubits]] and maintaining coherence, rather than adhering to initial [[concepts/exponential-growth|exponential growth]] assumptions.
