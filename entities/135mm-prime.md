# 135mm Prime

A 135mm prime lens is a fixed focal length optic categorized as a short telephoto lens. It provides moderate subject magnification and pronounced background compression, making it distinct from wider angle lenses. As a prime lens, it offers a single, non-variable focal length, which typically results in a wider maximum aperture and sharper optical performance compared to zoom lenses of equivalent range.

The lens is commonly used in portrait and telephoto photography due to its ability to isolate subjects from their backgrounds. The compression effect flattens perspective, which can be advantageous for creating intimate or detailed compositions. This characteristic allows photographers to achieve specific aesthetic qualities that are difficult to replicate with wider focal lengths.

In specialized applications, the 135mm focal length is utilized in panorama stitching to achieve wide-angle compositions. By leveraging long lens compression, photographers can capture high-resolution images that maintain detail across a broad field of view. This technique exploits the lens's optical properties to create expansive scenes with reduced distortion compared to ultra-wide alternatives.
