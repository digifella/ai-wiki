# Cinematic Techniques

**Cinematic techniques** encompass the visual and auditory methods used to create a film-like aesthetic, emotional resonance, and narrative [[concepts/structure|structure]]. This concept bridges Filmmaking, Photography, and Visual [[concepts/storytelling|Storytelling]], focusing on how technical choices [[concepts/power|influence]] psychological impact.

## Core Principles

*   **Visual Language**: Use of Composition, Lighting, and Camera [[concepts/exercise|Movement]] to convey subtext.
*   **[[concepts/color-conceptstheorytheory|Color Theory]]**: Strategic manipulation of [[concepts/hue|hue]], saturation, and luminance to define mood (e.g., Teal and Orange).
*   **Temporal Manipulation**: Control of pacing through Editing Rhythms and frame rate variations.

## Color Grading & Post-Production

[[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color grading]] is pivotal in establishing [[concepts/tone|tone]]. Unlike Color Correction, which fixes technical errors, grading stylizes the image.

*   **[[concepts/lightroom-workflow|Lightroom Workflow]]**: While primarily a photo editor, [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] offers robust grading tools for cinematic stills.
*   **Emotional Influence**: Color choices dramatically shift the viewer's emotional response and aesthetic perception [[lab-notes/2026-05-23-Lightroom-Color-Grading-Foundations-Tools-and-Cinematic|Lightroom Color Grading: Foundations, Tools, and Cinematic Techniques]].
*   **Key Resources**:
    *   *The Trick to Perfect Color Grades in Lightroom* by [[entities/sean-dalton|Sean Dalton]] outlines foundations and tools for achieving natural, emotive grades.
