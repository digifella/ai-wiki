# Vinegar Pre Soak Method

The vinegar pre-soak method is a technique for preparing poached eggs that involves soaking the eggs in vinegar before cooking. This approach is designed to help eggs maintain their shape and achieve a more compact, cohesive result when poached, addressing one of the common challenges home cooks face when attempting this cooking method.

## How It Works

The method typically involves placing raw eggs in a bowl or container with vinegar for a period of time before transferring them to the poaching water. The vinegar is believed to help firm up the egg white, making it less likely to disperse into the cooking water and resulting in a neater finished product. This [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] step adds minimal time to the overall cooking process.

## Application

When using this method, eggs are poached in the standard way after the pre-soak—gently lowered into simmering water, typically containing vinegar in the cooking liquid itself. The combination of the pre-soak and vinegar in the poaching water is intended to produce eggs with well-defined shapes and minimal wispy strands of white.
