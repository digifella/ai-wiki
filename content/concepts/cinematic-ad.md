# Cinematic Ad

A cinematic ad is a 30-second video advertisement created through a systematic process that leverages [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] notebooks as source material. The workflow converts written content—typically containing business [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] or marketing concepts—into polished video content using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]] and [[entities/gemini-models|Gemini AI]]. This approach bridges note-taking and video production, allowing creators to transform documentation into promotional material without traditional filming or editing expertise.

## Process

The creation process begins with organizing relevant information in a NotebookLM [[concepts/notebook|notebook]], which serves as the foundational content source. [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] then processes this material to [[concepts/structure|structure]] it for video adaptation, while Google Vids handles the actual [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] and assembly. The output is a concise, professionally formatted 30-second advertisement suitable for digital platforms and social media [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].

## Applications

This method is particularly useful for creators who have substantial written documentation about their business philosophy or products but lack video production [[concepts/skills|skills]] or resources. By automating the conversion from text to video, the cinematic ad approach reduces production time and technical barriers while maintaining quality output appropriate for commercial use.
