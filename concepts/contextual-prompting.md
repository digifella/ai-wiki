# Contextual Prompting

Contextual prompting is a technique for customizing AI language models to produce output tailored to specific professional domains and use cases. By providing detailed custom instructions to models like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini, users establish persistent context that shapes how the AI responds to subsequent queries. This method allows practitioners to define the AI's role, tone, and operational constraints without needing to repeat background information in every individual interaction.

In legal work, this approach is particularly valuable for standardizing document drafting and analysis. Custom instructions can specify jurisdictional preferences, citation styles, and the desired level of formality, ensuring that generated content aligns with professional standards from the outset. This reduces the cognitive load on attorneys by minimizing the need for extensive post-generation editing and reformatting.

The effectiveness of contextual prompting relies on the clarity and specificity of the initial instructions. Users typically define the AI's persona, such as a senior associate or a legal researcher, and outline specific workflows for tasks like contract review or memo writing. This persistent configuration ensures consistency across multiple sessions, allowing the model to maintain a coherent understanding of the user's professional requirements over time.
