# Deep Research Max

[[concepts/visualization-generation|Deep Research]] Max is an [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] enhancement tool developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] that leverages the [[entities/gemini-25-flash|Gemini 2.5 Flash]] API to improve research [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. The platform integrates proprietary data sources with visual content generation to support users conducting academic and professional research tasks.

## Functionality

The tool is designed to augment traditional research workflows by combining API-driven [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] capabilities with curated data resources and automated visualization features. This [[concepts/integration|integration]] allows researchers to access both [[concepts/numerical-analysis|computational analysis]] and visual representations of complex information within a single interface.

## Integration with Google Services

Deep Research Max operates within Google's broader ecosystem of AI [[concepts/research-tools|research tools]], building on foundations established by earlier products like [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]. The use of Gemini 2.5 Flash as its underlying API positions it as part of Google's latest generation of [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] for professional and academic [[concepts/software|applications]].
