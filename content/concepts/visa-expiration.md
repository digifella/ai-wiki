# Visa Expiration

Visa expiration refers to the date on which a visa document ceases to be valid for travel or entry purposes. For electronic visas (e-visas), including [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]]'s [[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-visa]] system, the expiration date is specified on the visa document itself and determines the final date on which the holder may use that visa to enter the destination country.

## Turkey E-Visas

Turkey offers an electronic visa system that allows eligible travelers to obtain travel [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] online. Turkish e-visas are issued with specific validity periods and expiration dates that must be observed for entry into Turkey. The validity period typically allows for either single or multiple entries within the specified timeframe, depending on the visa type granted.

Once a visa has expired, it cannot be used for travel and a new visa application must be submitted if continued access to the country is required. Travelers should verify their visa expiration date well in advance of planned travel to avoid complications [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] border checkpoints or during the [[concepts/travel-planning|travel planning]] process.
