# Crop and Expand

The process of framing an image and utilizing AI to generate plausible content beyond the original sensor boundaries.

## Core Concepts

- **Generative Expand**: AI-driven extension of image borders, allowing for aspect ratio changes without cropping out subject matter.
- **[[concepts/render-to-dng|Render to DNG]]**: Exporting AI-expanded images as raw DNG files to preserve [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] and allow further [[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|non-destructive editing]] in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]].
- **[[concepts/mask-refinement|Mask Refinement]]**: Improved [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] in selecting areas for expansion or editing, reducing artifacts at boundaries.

## Recent Updates

### Lightroom Desktop 9.5
Significant enhancements to the expand workflow were introduced in version 9.5. Key changes include:

- **Mask Refinement**: Better edge detection for expansion zones, resulting in more natural blending with existing content [[lab-notes/2026-08-08-Lightroom-Desktop-9.5-Updates-Mask-Refinement-Render-to|Lightroom Desktop 9.5 Updates: Mask Refinement, Render to DNG, Generative Expand]].
- **Render to DNG**: New capability to export generative expansions as DNG files, maintaining raw [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]] for post-processing.
- **Generative Expand**: Enhanced AI model for content generation, improving [[concepts/coherence|coherence]] in complex backgrounds.
