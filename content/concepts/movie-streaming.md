# Movie Streaming

Movie streaming refers to the delivery of films and television [[concepts/software|programs]] to viewers over the internet on demand. Rather than purchasing physical media or waiting for scheduled broadcast times, viewers can access content immediately through dedicated platforms. This technology relies on [[concepts/video-compression|video compression]] and content delivery networks to stream video [[concepts/files|files]] to consumer devices including computers, smartphones, tablets, and smart televisions.

## Streaming Platforms

Numerous [[concepts/subscription-based-services|subscription-based services]] dominate the streaming landscape, each offering different content libraries, [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] [[concepts/models|models]], and features. Some platforms focus primarily on films, while others provide a mix of movies and television series. Alongside paid subscription services, free ad-supported streaming options have emerged as an alternative method for accessing movies and television content, supported by [[concepts/ad-revenue|advertising revenue]] rather than subscription fees.

## Access and Availability

The availability of specific titles varies significantly across platforms and geographic regions due to [[concepts/licensing-agreements|licensing agreements]] and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] rights. A single film or show may be available on multiple platforms simultaneously, or exclusive to one service for a defined period. Internet [[concepts/connection|connection]] [[concepts/speed|speed]] and bandwidth requirements affect streaming quality, with most platforms offering adjustable video resolution to accommodate different network conditions.
