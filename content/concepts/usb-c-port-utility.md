# USB-C Port Utility

The [[entities/usb-c-port|USB-C port]] on modern iPads enables direct [[concepts/connection|connection]] to a [[concepts/range|range]] of external peripherals and accessories, expanding the device's [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] beyond its built-in [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. This connectivity option allows users to attach [[concepts/external-storage|external storage]] drives, displays, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] interfaces, and other compatible devices, creating a more flexible computing environment for both productivity and creative work.

## Productivity Applications

For productivity workflows, the USB-C port facilitates connections to external keyboards, mice, trackpads, and displays that enhance the [[entities/ipad|iPad]]'s functionality as a laptop alternative. Users can also connect external storage for managing [[concepts/files|files]], backing up work, and accessing large datasets that would otherwise consume internal storage space.

## Creative Workflows

Creative professionals can leverage [[concepts/usb-c-connectivity|USB-C connectivity]] to integrate specialized equipment into their iPad-based workflows. This includes audio interfaces for music production, external graphics tablets for digital [[concepts/art|art]], video capture devices, and professional cameras. The ability to connect these peripherals directly to the iPad enables more sophisticated creative processes that were previously limited to traditional computers.
