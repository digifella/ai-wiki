# Vision Model

A **vision model** refers to an [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] system capable of processing, interpreting, and generating visual data (images, video) often in conjunction with textual inputs.

## Key Developments & Ecosystem

*   **[[concepts/deepseek-v4-flash|DeepSeek V4-Flash]]**: Noted for [[concepts/advanced-reasoning|advanced reasoning]] capabilities in multimodal contexts.
*   **Meta [[concepts/computer-usage|Muse Spark 1.3]]**: Optimized for agentic work and multimodal processing.
*   **OpenAI GPT-6 Astra**: Focuses on advanced graphics, physics-simulation, and [[concepts/scientific-simulation|scientific simulation]].
*   **Graft**: Specializes in optimizing [[concepts/ai-agent-performance|AI agent performance]] and cost within [[concepts/large-codebases|large codebases]].
*   **[[entities/gev|God's Eye View (GEV)]]**: An open-source [[concepts/3d-spatial-intelligence|3D spatial intelligence]] simulator.
*   **Jev**: A "System One" intelligence focused on fast, reliable [[concepts/code-decision-automation|code decision automation]].

## Claude Sonnet 5.5 Integration

Recent evaluations highlight **[[concepts/cost-efficiency|Claude Sonnet 5.5]]** as a significant upgrade within the Claude 5.5 series, particularly for agentic-work and [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] tasks.

*   **Performance & Efficiency**: Hailed for being faster and more cost-efficient than previous iterations, with rigorous testing across diverse domains.
*   **[[concepts/agentic-coding|Agentic Coding]]**: Demonstrates strong capabilities in code generation and decision-making, relevant to tools like [[entities/graft]] and [[entities/jev]].
*   **Multilingual & Spatial Support**: Benchmarks include testing across 80 languages and complex 3D game physics simulations, indicating robust 3d-spatial reasoning.
