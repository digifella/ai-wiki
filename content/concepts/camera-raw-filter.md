# Camera Raw Filter

The Camera Raw Filter is a [[concepts/photoshop-tool|Photoshop tool]] designed to process and enhance digital [[concepts/images|images]] with precise control over [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, and detail. While it was originally developed for processing raw image files from [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]], the filter can be applied to any image in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], making it a versatile adjustment layer for portrait work and other photography.

## Portrait Enhancement Applications

In [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]], the Camera Raw Filter provides several advantages for improving image quality. The tool allows photographers and retouchers to adjust white balance, [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], highlights, and clarity in ways that are non-destructive and easily reversible. These [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] can enhance [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]], increase definition in facial features, and overall improve the visual impact of portrait images before moving to more specialized retouching techniques.

The filter's interface presents adjustment sliders organized by category, enabling users to make targeted modifications. Common portrait improvements include reducing unwanted [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]], brightening underexposed areas around the eyes, and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] to add dimension to the face. The Camera Raw Filter is typically one of the first steps in a [[concepts/portrait-retouching|portrait retouching]] workflow.
