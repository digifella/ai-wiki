# Mobile AI inference

The execution of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models, specifically [[concepts/large-language-models]], directly on mobile hardware (e.g., [[entities/iphone|iPhone]], [[entities/ipad]]) rather than via cloud-based [[concepts/application-programming-interfaces-apis|APIs]].
