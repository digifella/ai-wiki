# Astronaut Health

## Bone Density & Osteoporosis Prevention

Astronauts in microgravity [[concepts/experience|experience]] rapid [[concepts/bone-density|bone mineral density]] loss, mimicking accelerated [[concepts/osteoporosis|osteoporosis]]. [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s Solutions for Preventing Osteoporosis on [[entities/earth|Earth]] details specific [[concepts/countermeasures|countermeasures]] developed for spaceflight that have terrestrial applications.

### Key Countermeasures & Solutions
*   **High-Intensity Resistance Training:** Utilization of advanced resistive [[concepts/exercise|exercise]] devices (e.g., ARED) to simulate Earth-like loading conditions.
*   **Pharmacological Interventions:** Use of bisphosphonates and other bone-protective agents to inhibit osteoclast activity.
*   **Nutritional Optimization:** Strict [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] of calcium, vitamin D, and protein intake to support bone remodeling.
*   **Vibration Therapy:** Exploration of whole-body vibration as a supplementary stimulus for bone maintenance.
*   **Translational Research:** Insights from spaceflight studies inform new protocols for preventing age-related and post-menopausal osteoporosis on Earth.
