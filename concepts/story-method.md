## Story Method
The **story method** is a technique developed by [[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]] to help individuals uncover their core purpose or "why." It involves a structured approach that encourages self-reflection and [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]], enabling people to articulate their passions and values clearly. This method has been applied in various contexts, including professional development and personal growth.

- Jake Humphreys discusses his journey of finding his "why" despite having a diverse career spanning children's television, [[entities/formula-1|Formula 1]], and football presenting.
