## NotebookLM Transforms into Agentic AI Coder and Research Assistant
**Clip title:** [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|Agentic AI]] Update Is HUGE! Agentic Coder Now?
**Author / channel:** WorldofAI
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=57L3vmQLzwQ

### Summary
[[concepts/notebooklm|Google NotebookLM]] has received substantial upgrades, transforming it from a simple "chat with your documents" tool into a powerful, [[concepts/agentic-research|agentic research]] assistant. The core of these enhancements lies in its integration with Google's [[concepts/qwen-36-35b-a3b|Gemini 3.5 Flash]] model and Antigravity technology, which enables more [[concepts/thinking-and-reasoning-capabilities|advanced reasoning]], multi-step [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and improved output generation. This evolution aims to make complex research tasks significantly easier and more efficient for users.

A key improvement is the enhanced chat [[concepts/experience|experience]], which now provides "more thoughtful" AI responses and greater [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] into the model's "thinking process." Each notebook is now equipped with a secure cloud computer, granting access to over 100 curated software skills. This capability allows NotebookLM to write and run code for deeper research, complex data analysis, and [[concepts/source-discovery|source discovery]]. Benchmark evaluations against its prior system indicate a significant performance boost, with an average win rate exceeding 65% across core dimensions, and achieving an impressive 78.2% win rate for advanced web research and source discovery tasks.

The new update also introduces a game-changing feature: downloadable outputs. Instead of merely providing text-based answers, NotebookLM can now assemble information from various sources and generate diverse, shareable file formats. These include data visualizations, charts, reports (PDFs, Markdown documents), Excel spreadsheets, PowerPoint slides, CSVs, JSON files, and even images. This capability fundamentally alters the research workflow, allowing users to transform raw data and complex specifications directly into polished, actionable deliverables.

This upgraded NotebookLM is designed to benefit a wide range of users. Researchers can combine and clean messy data from disparate sources, conduct web research, perform analyses, and generate comprehensive reports. Technical professionals can decipher intricate customer integration specifications, transforming them into simplified guides, [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]], and step-by-step roadmaps. Small business owners can leverage it to analyze campaign data, sales figures, and ad spend to calculate financial impact and inform strategic decisions like market expansion. Overall, these updates signify Google's intent to position NotebookLM as a comprehensive, intelligent research agent that actively assists users from initial ideation to final deliverable creation, making the entire research process more effortless and productive.
