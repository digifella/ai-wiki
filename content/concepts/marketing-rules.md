# Marketing Rules

Marketing Rules is a systematic approach to converting research and knowledge stored in [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] notebooks into brief video advertisements. The process leverages [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s suite of AI-powered tools—specifically [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]—to streamline the transformation of written content into compelling 30-second video commercials suitable for digital [[concepts/marketing-campaigns|marketing campaigns]].

## Process Overview

The workflow begins with a populated NotebookLM [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] containing business [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]], product information, or marketing messaging. This source material is then processed through Gemini to generate script concepts and messaging frameworks optimized for video format. Google Vids subsequently handles the video production, combining generated scripts with visual elements to create the final [[concepts/cinematic-ad|30-second commercial]] output.

## Practical Application

This approach is particularly valuable for businesses seeking to rapidly generate multiple marketing variations from a single [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]]. Rather than developing video content from scratch, teams can repurpose existing written materials and institutional knowledge, reducing production time and ensuring message [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across video advertisements. The method works best when source notebooks contain clear business messaging and well-articulated value propositions that can be distilled into concise video narratives.
