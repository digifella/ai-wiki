# Skin Tone Editing

Skin tone editing in Lightroom is a technique focused on color-correcting and enhancing the appearance of human subjects in photography. Rather than applying isolated adjustments to individual images, photographers often utilize the Calibration Panel to establish foundational color corrections. This method influences how the camera's sensor data is interpreted, providing a non-destructive baseline that affects all subsequent edits and helps maintain consistency across a series of images.

The Calibration Panel allows for precise manipulation of the primary color channels—Red, Green, and Blue Primary. By adjusting the hue and saturation of these primaries, editors can shift the overall color balance of the image in a way that naturally complements human skin tones. This approach is particularly effective for achieving cinematic styles or correcting white balance issues at the root level, ensuring that skin appears natural and consistent throughout the post-processing workflow.
