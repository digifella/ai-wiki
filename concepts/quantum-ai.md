# Quantum AI

**Quantum AI** refers to the synergistic application of **[[concepts/quantum-computing|quantum computing]]** principles to enhance **[[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]** [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], specifically leveraging [[concepts/quantum-entanglement|quantum superposition and entanglement]] to process high-dimensional data structures more efficiently than classical systems. This field sits at the intersection of [[concepts/quantum-mechanics]] and [[concepts/machine-learning]], aiming to solve optimization problems, accelerate training times, and improve [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] in complex datasets.

## Core Concepts

- **Quantum Machine [[concepts/learning|Learning]] (QML):** Integration of quantum algorithms (e.g., Variational Quantum Eigensolver, Quantum Support Vector Machines) with ML pipelines.
- **[[concepts/quantum-advantage|Quantum Advantage]] in AI:** Theoretical speedups in linear algebra operations, particularly in matrix inversion and eigenvalue estimation.
- **Qubit [[concepts/encoding|Encoding]]:** Mapping classical data into quantum states via feature embedding, allowing high-dimensional data to be represented in exponentially large Hilbert spaces.
- **Entanglement Utilization:** Leveraging quantum entanglement to capture complex correlations within datasets that classical systems struggle to model efficiently.
