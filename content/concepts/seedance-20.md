# Seedance 20

Seedance 20 is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] for producing professional-grade commercial videos by combining [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] techniques with the Seedance 2.0 [[concepts/image-to-video|image-to-video]] model. The approach leverages [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s [[concepts/language-capabilities|language capabilities]] to generate detailed, structured prompts that guide the [[concepts/image-to-video-model|image-to-video generation]] process, enabling creators to produce polished commercials without extensive manual video production.

## Workflow Components

The workflow integrates two primary tools: [[concepts/claude|Claude AI]] handles the creative direction and [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], while Seedance 2.0 generates video sequences from still [[concepts/images|images]]. Users provide creative briefs or commercial concepts to Claude, which structures them into optimized prompts for the video model. This combination allows for [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of outputs, with Claude helping to troubleshoot, adjust, and enhance results through successive generations.

## Application

The method is particularly suited to commercial production, where [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], professional quality, and rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] are valued. Rather than requiring traditional video production resources, the workflow enables smaller teams or individual creators to generate advertisement-quality content by focusing on prompt [[concepts/design|design]] and creative direction. Results depend significantly on the quality of initial briefs and iterative refinement of prompts through Claude.
