## Melbourne Metro Tunnel Opening & Fare Policy: Train Ridership Impact Analysis
**Clip title:** The Huge Success of the Metro Tunnel
**Author / channel:** TransitTube
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t3E0_xocN5I

### Summary
This video analyzes the impact of Melbourne's [[concepts/metro-tunnel|Metro Tunnel]]'s full operational opening and subsequent fare policies on the city's train ridership. The main topic revolves around whether these substantial investments and policy changes have translated into increased [[concepts/public-transport-usage|public transport usage]], utilizing recently released monthly ridership data from the [[concepts/department-of-transport|Department of Transport]]. The video seeks to understand the immediate effects of the Metro Tunnel and the temporary free and discounted fare periods.

Upon its full operational opening on February 1st, which included over 1,000 new services, primarily benefiting the Sunbury Line, initial data indicated a positive trend. February 2025 saw a 7.8% year-on-year ridership jump, further strengthening in March with a 10.8% increase compared to the previous March. This March surge was attributed to higher fuel prices and the return of university students, suggesting commuters were already adapting to the Metro Tunnel's new services. This initial period, though early, showed promising signs of increased public transport adoption.

The introduction of free public transport across the state in April and May, in response to a fuel crisis, had a dramatic effect. April experienced a massive 44.4% increase in network usage year-on-year, indicating a significant mode shift from cars to trains. This strong turnaround suggests the policy, despite concerns about fiscal irresponsibility, was effective in driving ridership. May continued this trend with a 23% increase, and even with half-price fares in June, a 16.1% jump was observed, demonstrating sustained positive momentum.

Looking ahead, the video predicts that overall ridership growth might smooth off to a 10-20% year-on-year increase. A specific focus on the Sunbury Line, which received over 1,000 new services a week effectively doubling its frequency, predicts the biggest ridership jump this decade. By applying the concept of "service elasticity" (estimated at 0.8 for urban environments), an 80% increase in service levels on the Sunbury Line is projected to lead to a long-term 64% increase in ridership, with a short-term increase of 20-30%. Beyond individual lines, the analysis highlights the "network effect," where improving multiple interconnected lines (like through the Metro Tunnel) compounds benefits, making the entire transport system more useful, improving connections, and reducing road congestion for all road users.
