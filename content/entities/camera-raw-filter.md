# Camera Raw Filter

The Camera Raw Filter is a [[concepts/photoshop-tool|Photoshop tool]] designed for processing and enhancing raw image files and other supported formats. It provides non-destructive editing capabilities, allowing photographers to make [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] without permanently altering the original image data. The filter is commonly used in [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]] and general [[concepts/photo-enhancement|photo enhancement]] workflows.

## Functionality and Applications

The filter enables a range of image processing operations including [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]], tonal adjustments, and overall [[concepts/image-quality-enhancement|image quality enhancement]]. Users can adjust [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], [[concepts/whites|whites]], [[concepts/blacks|blacks]], and other fundamental image [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] within the filter's interface. Because it operates non-destructively, changes can be modified or reverted at any time during the editing process, making it a flexible option for photographers working with [[concepts/camera-raw|raw files]] captured by [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]].

## Use in Photography Workflows

Camera Raw Filter features prominently in portrait improvement and photo enhancement techniques. It is frequently referenced in photography tutorials covering topics such as achieving [[concepts/sharpness|sharp focus]], [[concepts/color-exaggeration|color enhancement]], and tonal correction. The tool integrates directly into [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]]'s editing environment, allowing photographers to process [[concepts/images|images]] before applying additional filters or adjustments.
