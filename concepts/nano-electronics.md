# Nano Electronics

[[entities/nano|Nano]] electronics refers to the design, manufacture, and application of electronic devices and systems at the nanometer scale, typically involving structures smaller than 100 nanometers. At this scale, classical [[concepts/physics|physics]] gives way to quantum mechanical effects, which fundamentally alter how electrons behave and how devices function. The field encompasses transistors, [[concepts/memory|memory]] devices, sensors, and integrated circuits built from individual atoms or molecules.

## Current State of Development

Modern semiconductor manufacturing has approached nanometer-scale dimensions through photolithography and other fabrication techniques. Commercial [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]] and memory chips now incorporate features measured in tens of nanometers. However, further miniaturization [[concepts/faces|faces]] both technical challenges—such as [[concepts/heat-dissipation|heat dissipation]], electron tunneling, and manufacturing [[concepts/accuracy|precision]]—and fundamental physical limits as devices approach atomic dimensions.

## Quantum Effects and Device Behavior

At the nanoscale, [[concepts/quantum-phenomena|quantum phenomena]] become dominant. Electrons exhibit wave-particle duality, tunneling through barriers, and quantized energy states. These effects can degrade conventional device performance but also enable novel applications such as single-electron transistors, quantum dots, and molecular electronics. Understanding and controlling quantum behavior is essential for continued advancement in the field.

## Applications and Future Directions

Nanoelectronic components are used in computing, telecommunications, medical diagnostics, and energy systems. Emerging research areas include carbon nanotubes, graphene-based electronics, and molecular switches. Scientists continue to explore whether fundamentally new computing paradigms—such as [[concepts/quantum-computing|quantum computing]] or DNA-based information processing—[[entities/will|will]] emerge from [[concepts/nanotechnology|nanotechnology]] research.
