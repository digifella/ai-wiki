# Cybersecurity Awareness

**[[concepts/cybersecurity|Cybersecurity]] [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]]** refers to the collective understanding and vigilance of individuals and organizations regarding digital threats, risks, and [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]] for [[concepts/secure|protection]]. It serves as the human layer of defense against sophisticated attacks.

Recent intelligence highlights specific impersonation and exploitation techniques that require heightened awareness:
