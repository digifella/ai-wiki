# Annihilation signals

Signals produced by the process of particle-antiparticle annihilation, serving as a primary method for the indirect detection of [[concepts/dark-matter]].

## Key Mechanisms
- **[[concepts/dark-matter]] Context**: [[concepts/dark-matter|Dark matter]] is an invisible substance estimated to be six times more abundant than regular matter and is fundamental to the universe's large-scale structure.
- **[[concepts/wimp]] Annihilation**: A hypothesized mechanism where [[concepts/wimp-particles|Weakly Interacting Massive Particles]] ([[concepts/wimp-particles|WIMPs]]) annihilate, releasing energy.
- **[[entities/gamma|Gamma]] [[concepts/rays|Rays]] as Tracers**: The conversion of mass into energy during annihilation frequently produces high-energy [[concepts/light|photons]], making gamma-ray [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]] a critical observational tool.

## Recent Observations
- **[[concepts/milky-way]] Detection**: Potential detection of a [[concepts/wimp]] annihilation signal via [[concepts/gamma-rays|Gamma Rays]] originating from within the [[concepts/milky-way]] (Ref: 2026 04 24 Dark Matter [[concepts/particle-annihilation|WIMP Annihilation]] Signal Detected in [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]] Gamma Rays).
