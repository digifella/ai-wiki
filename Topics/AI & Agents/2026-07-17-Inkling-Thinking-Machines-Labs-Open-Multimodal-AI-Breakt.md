### Summary
This video introduces Inkling, [[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]] Machines Lab's first [[concepts/open-source-weights|open-weights]] [[concepts/multimodal-language-models|multimodal AI]] model, highlighting its significant impact on the [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-landscape|AI landscape]]. The [[concepts/deployment|release]] is notable because, unlike many proprietary [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier models]] from Western labs (e.g., GPT, [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]), Inkling's weights are open-sourced under an [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0 license]], allowing users to download and own a copy rather than just rent access via an API. Furthermore, Inkling was trained from scratch with its own unique architecture, making it a truly novel offering rather than a remix of existing models. The video positions Inkling as the biggest open-weights model release from a Western lab (excluding [[concepts/unsloth-optimization|Nvidia]]), filling a critical void in the market, which has previously relied on Chinese labs for comparable open-source options.

Inkling boasts a substantial architecture, featuring 975 billion [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]] with 41 billion [[concepts/activated-parameters|active parameters]], built on a [[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) design. It leverages 256 experts, with six active per token, and operates with a [[concepts/1-million-token-context|1 million token context]] window. The model was trained on an expansive 45 trillion multimodal [[concepts/tokens|tokens]], encompassing text, images, audio, and video, making it natively multimodal from its inception. While benchmarks show Inkling is not strictly state-of-the-art across all individual metrics, it demonstrates remarkable performance as a well-rounded [[concepts/jacks-of-all-trades|generalist]], particularly strong in agentic tasks, factual [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and design capabilities. A smaller preview variant, Inkling-Small, is also available, with 276 billion parameters (12 billion active), designed for more accessible deployment on consumer hardware.

Thinking Machines Lab also offers the Tinker platform, an API for researchers and developers to fine-tune models. Inkling is readily available on Tinker, enabling users to customize the model with their own data without needing to manage GPU infrastructure. The video showcased Inkling's impressive ability to handle [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], including generating detailed HTML/CSS websites based on prompts and creating real-time interactive dashboards like an ISS orbital tracker. This demonstrates its [[concepts/advanced-coding|advanced coding]] capabilities and [[concepts/acting|tool-use]] integration, where it can perform interleaved web searches and display its [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]]. The model's [[concepts/system-card|system prompt]] reveals a [[concepts/knowledge-cutoff|knowledge cutoff]] of April 2026, indicating its up-to-date training.

In conclusion, Inkling represents a monumental step for the open-source AI community, particularly from a Western scientific perspective. Its open-weights, multimodal nature, and robust architecture provide an unparalleled opportunity for [[concepts/customization|customization]] and [[concepts/innovation|innovation]]. While the current [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] might have specific performance and [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] considerations on the Tinker platform, its open availability empowers a broader range of developers and researchers. As the first major open-weights release from Thinking Machines Lab, Inkling lays a strong foundation, and future iterations are anticipated to push the boundaries of multimodal AI even further.
