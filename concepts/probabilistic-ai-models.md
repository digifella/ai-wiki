# Probabilistic AI Models

## Overview
Probabilistic [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] utilize statistical methods to make predictions or decisions under uncertainty. Unlike deterministic systems, these models output [[concepts/probability|probability]] distributions over possible outcomes, allowing for nuanced [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]] and [[concepts/acting|adaptive behavior]].

## JEV: Fast, Confident Decision Support
JEV represents a specialized application of probabilistic AI focused on rapid decision-making rather than generative text output. It is categorized as a "System One" model, drawing from Daniel Kahneman's framework for fast, intuitive processing.

- **Core Function**: Differentiates from traditional [[concepts/llm|LLMs]] by prioritizing [[concepts/speed|speed]] and confidence in decision support over [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]].
