### Summary
This video addresses the challenge faced by users of [[concepts/claude-mythos-5|Claude Fable 5]] as its availability changes and costs become prohibitive. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] proposes a "third move" to extract and preserve Fable 5's unique planning and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] intelligence for future use with other, potentially cheaper or [[concepts/open-source-ai-models|open-source AI models]]. The core idea is to shift from simply asking Fable to "plan" a task to having it "wargame" it, treating it as a strategic [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] rather than a straightforward execution.

Wargaming, as explained, involves making the AI break down every course of action, move by move, considering all possible [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], including reactions, counteractions, and potential failures. This approach goes beyond linear planning, which often assumes a "blue sky" scenario, by forcing Fable to anticipate and plan for "unknown unknowns" and real-[[entities/earth|world]] complexities. By doing this, users can leverage Fable's superior intelligence to generate highly robust and battle-tested plans, rather than just basic outlines or simple plan files that other models might struggle to fully execute without constant human intervention.

To implement this, users are advised to create detailed "mission briefs" that guide Fable through the wargaming process for various projects. These prompts define the initial "action," specify expected "observations" for [[concepts/success|success]], detail "most likely failures" and their "[[concepts/causes|causes]]," and outline "counter-moves." Additionally, they include "assumptions" that Fable should make (or flag if unresolvable) and "abort conditions" to halt the simulation if unrecoverable errors occur. The speaker demonstrates this by structuring multiple prompts for diverse [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]] such as rebuilding a marketing website, [[concepts/writing|writing]] copy, setting up a [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]], tax optimization, and upgrading [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]].

The practical application involves organizing these mission briefs into a structured folder system (e.g., `fable-last-week/tasks`, `wargames`, `success.md`, `ledger.md`). Fable 5 can then be instructed to process all these prompts in parallel, generating comprehensive [[concepts/markdown-files|markdown files]] for each "wargamed" project. These detailed documents, containing Fable's simulated actions, reactions, and counteractions, effectively become blueprints. Once generated, these "wargamed" blueprints can be fed to other AI models (like [[concepts/opus|Opus 4.8]], [[concepts/code-debugging|GPT-5.5]], or open-source alternatives) to execute the actual tasks, enabling them to navigate potential pitfalls with significantly higher confidence and autonomy, thus preserving Fable's valuable insights beyond its direct [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].
