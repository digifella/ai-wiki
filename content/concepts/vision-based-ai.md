# Vision-based AI

A subset of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] focused on the development of systems capable of perceiving, interpreting, and understanding visual data ([[concepts/images|images]], video, and 3D environments) to build [[concepts/world-models]].

## Core Architectures & Approaches

- **Transformer-based [[concepts/models|Models]]**: Current standard for processing visual [[concepts/tokens|tokens]].
- **[[concepts/jepa|JEPA]] (Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]])**:
	- A non-generative approach focused on predicting missing parts of a signal in latent space.
	- **VL-JEPS**: A recent development from [[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] FAIR Lab representing a shift toward [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]]-centric intelligence.
- **[[concepts/generative-ai]] (Contrastive Context)**: While dominant in [[concepts/text|text]] (via [[concepts/llm]]), critics argue generative approaches lack the [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] depth found in predictive-[[concepts/coding|coding]] architectures.

## Key Research & Philosophical Shifts

- **[[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] & [[entities/meta|Meta]] Research**:
	- Advocacy for moving away from purely text-based [[concepts/llm]] architectures.
	- **Central Thesis**: "Language is not intelligence"; true [[concepts/agi|AGI]] requires visual-based [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and world-modeling.
	- Emphasis on architectures that prioritize understanding [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]] over next-token prediction.
- **Non-[[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM Reasoning]]**: Emerging focus on architectures that utilize [[concepts/spatial-understanding|visual perception]] as the primary driver for cognitive development rather than linguistic patterns.
