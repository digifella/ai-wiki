# Jewelry Heists

Jewelry heists represent a significant category of historical crime, characterized by the theft of valuable objects and precious materials. These crimes have frequently involved elaborate planning, specialized knowledge of [[concepts/security|security]] systems, and coordinated execution across multiple perpetrators. Notable cases often intersect with other serious criminal activities, including violence and murder, resulting in complex investigations that have engaged law enforcement agencies and generated substantial public interest.

## The South Seas Café Incident

A notable case involving a jewelry murder heist occurred at the [[entities/south-seas-cafe|South Seas]] café, distinguished by the destruction of a mosaic floor containing 1.2 million tiles. The perpetrators targeted the establishment's valuables, leading to a violent confrontation that resulted in homicide. The extensive damage to the mosaic floor became a key forensic element in the subsequent investigation, highlighting the chaotic nature of the execution and the high stakes involved in the robbery.
