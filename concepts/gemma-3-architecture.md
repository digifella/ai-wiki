The foundational architecture powering [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[entities/gemma]] model series, supporting [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]].

### Specialized Implementations
- [[concepts/dermatology-images|MedGemma 27B]]

    - Optimized for medical text and image comprehension tasks.
