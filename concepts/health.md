# Health

Health encompasses the state of physical, mental, and [[concepts/social-well-being|social well-being]], extending beyond the mere absence of disease or infirmity. This foundational concept represents a multidimensional understanding of human [[concepts/wellness|wellness]] that acknowledges the interconnectedness of bodily function, psychological state, and social circumstances. Health outcomes are shaped by numerous factors including genetics, environmental conditions, individual behaviors, and [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]] resources.

## Determinants and Factors

The promotion and maintenance of health depends on a complex interplay of influences. Lifestyle choices—including [[concepts/diet|diet]], [[concepts/exercise|physical activity]], [[concepts/sleep|sleep]], and [[concepts/stress-management|stress management]]—significantly affect health status. Environmental factors such as air and water quality, housing conditions, and [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to toxins also play important roles. [[concepts/social-determinants-of-health|Social determinants]] including education, employment, income, and community support networks have measurable impacts on health outcomes across populations.

## Knowledge and Practice

Individual health literacy—the ability to understand health information and make informed decisions—is essential for effective self-care and [[concepts/health-promotion|disease prevention]]. Health practices encompass both [[concepts/primary-prevention|preventive measures]], such as vaccinations and regular screenings, and management strategies for existing conditions. Dietary factors represent a particularly significant modifiable aspect of health, influencing the risk and progression of numerous [[concepts/chronic-diseases|chronic diseases]] including cardiovascular disease, diabetes, and obesity.

## Healthcare Access

Access to quality healthcare services remains central to achieving and maintaining health at individual and population levels. Healthcare systems provide diagnosis, treatment, rehabilitation, and palliative care services. Disparities in healthcare access and quality contribute to health inequities across different demographic groups and geographic regions.
