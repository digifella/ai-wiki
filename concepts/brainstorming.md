# Brainstorming

Brainstorming is a collaborative ideation technique designed to generate a large volume of ideas or solutions for a given problem or topic. In traditional settings, participants contribute thoughts freely with minimal initial judgment, creating an environment where creative thinking is encouraged. The method operates on the principle that quantity of ideas can increase the likelihood of discovering novel or effective solutions, as the variety of perspectives raises the chances of identifying viable approaches.

## AI-Assisted Brainstorming

AI agents can facilitate brainstorming by generating candidate ideas, organizing contributions, and identifying patterns across large sets of suggestions. Text-based AI assistants can quickly produce multiple perspectives on a problem, help refine vague initial concepts, and suggest connections between seemingly disparate ideas. This capability allows teams to augment human creativity with machine-generated alternatives, potentially accelerating the ideation phase of problem-solving processes.

## Application in Practice

When using AI agents for brainstorming, the technology works most effectively as a collaborative tool rather than a replacement for human judgment. Users can pose open-ended questions, iterate on suggestions, and ask the AI to approach a problem from different angles or domains. The AI's role is to expand the solution space and provide material for human evaluation, while human participants retain responsibility for assessing feasibility, relevance, and merit of generated ideas.
