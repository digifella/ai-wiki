# AMD Ryzen AI Halo Developer Platform

The **[[concepts/open-source-ai-projects|AMD Ryzen AI Halo]] [[concepts/developer|Developer]] Platform** is a compact workstation architecture designed to facilitate [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] [[concepts/cross-platform-ai-development|local AI development]]. It leverages a unified [[concepts/memory-structures|memory architecture]] to overcome traditional [[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] and capacity limitations associated with [[concepts/discrete-gpu|discrete GPU]] setups.

## Key Features

- **Unified Memory Architecture**: Enables seamless data sharing between CPU and GPU, reducing latency and improving efficiency for [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] (LLMs) and other AI workloads.
- **Compact Form Factor**: Designed as a small workstation, ideal for developers needing powerful AI capabilities in a limited physical space.
- **[[concepts/ai-model-processing|Local AI Execution]]**: Optimized for running large [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] entirely on-premise, ensuring [[concepts/privacy|data privacy]] and reducing dependency on cloud inference services.
- **Developer Focus**: Tailored specifically for [[entities/ai-coders|AI developers]] requiring robust hardware support for training, [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]], and inference tasks.
