# Western Resistance

The concept of "Western resistance" primarily refers to historical opposition in the Western world towards certain [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] or innovations from other cultures and philosophical systems. This includes resistance towards [[concepts/mathematical-concepts|mathematical concepts]] such as [[concepts/zero|zero]].

### Summary
- The number [[concepts/zero|zero]] was initially banned for over 1500 years due to cultural, religious, and philosophical resistances within the Western world.
- Early societies, including those in [[entities/europe|Europe]], did not have a concept of "nothing" or need for a symbol representing absence.
- Introduction of the concept of [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] from Eastern cultures faced significant resistance before being accepted.

- The video titled "Why the number 0 was banned for 1500 years," by [[entities/up-and-atom|Up and Atom]] on [[entities/youtube|YouTube]], explores how early societies in Europe did not have the mathematical concept of zero.
- It discusses why this symbol (which originated from Eastern cultures) faced significant resistance before being accepted into Western [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]].
