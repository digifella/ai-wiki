# Lunar Return

The lunar return refers to the upcoming missions that will see humanity returning to the Moon after a prolonged absence, marking significant advancements in [[concepts/space-exploration|space exploration]] and technology. This term encapsulates not only the physical journey but also the technological innovations and scientific objectives associated with renewed lunar activity.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis Program]]:** [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s initiative aimed at establishing sustainable human exploration of the Moon.
- **[[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] [[concepts/starship|Starship]]:** A spacecraft designed for interplanetary travel, featuring critical roles in both Earth-Moon transportation and beyond.
- **Orion Capsule:** The crewed capsule developed by Lockheed Martin for deep space missions.
