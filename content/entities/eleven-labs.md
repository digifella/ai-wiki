# Eleven Labs

Eleven Labs is a company specializing in AI-powered text-to-speech and [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]] technology. The company develops voice models and tools for generating natural-sounding speech from text, serving [[concepts/software|applications]] across [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and media production.

## Voice Cloning Capabilities

Eleven Labs has developed [[concepts/ai-generated-voices|voice cloning technology]] that enables users to synthesize speech in specific voices. As of April 2026, the company released updated voice cloning capabilities, with analysis and coverage of these developments provided by various AI technology commentators including [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]].

## Voice Models

The company has released new generations of voice models designed to improve speech quality and naturalness. These models represent ongoing development in the company's core text-to-speech platform, with updates documented and reviewed within the AI technology community.
