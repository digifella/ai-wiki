# Slack Acquisition

The acquisition of Slack by Salesforce in 2018 was a landmark event for both companies. This strategic move aimed to integrate real-time messaging and [[concepts/team-management|team collaboration]] tools into the broader suite of customer relationship management (CRM) services offered by Salesforce, thereby enhancing its position as a leader in enterprise [[concepts/software|software]] solutions.

## Key Points

- **Integration with CRM:** The acquisition allowed Slack to be more deeply integrated with Salesforce's CRM platform, enabling seamless communication and data sharing between teams.
- **Enhanced [[concepts/collaboration-tools|Collaboration Tools]]:** By combining Slack's intuitive messaging interface with Salesforce's extensive customer relationship management features, the companies sought to create a unified workspace for businesses of all sizes.
- **Competitive Advantage:** The acquisition gave Salesforce an edge in competing with other enterprise software providers like Microsoft Teams, providing users with a comprehensive [[concepts/solution|solution]] that bridges communication and productivity.
