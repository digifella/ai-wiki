# Self Improvement

Self-improvement in [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] refers to processes where an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] iteratively enhances its own capabilities, performance, or behavior without external intervention. This occurs through recursive self-optimization, where a system analyzes its outputs, identifies inefficiencies or errors, and modifies its own parameters, [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], or approaches to perform better on subsequent iterations. The concept represents a departure from traditional machine [[concepts/learning|learning]], where improvement typically requires human-directed retraining or external [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]].

## Applications in Scientific Discovery

Recent implementations of [[concepts/self-improving-ai|self-improving AI]] have shown particular promise in scientific research contexts. Systems like [[entities/deepmind|DeepMind]]'s [[concepts/automated-scientific-research|automated scientific research]] tools utilize recursive loops to hypothesize, test, and refine experiments autonomously.
