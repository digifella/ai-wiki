# Texas

Texas is the second-largest U.S. state by area and population. It is home to a substantial portion of the nation's [[concepts/energy-infrastructure|energy infrastructure]], including oil refineries, natural gas operations, and electrical generation facilities. The state's [[concepts/electricity-grid|power grid]] is managed by [[entities/ercot|ERCOT]] ([[concepts/ercot-electric-reliability-council-of-texas|Electric Reliability Council of Texas]]), an independent system operator responsible for approximately 90% of Texas's electricity load.

## 2021 Power Grid Outage

In February 2021, Texas experienced a major [[concepts/power|power]] grid failure during an unusual [[concepts/winter-weather|winter weather]] event. Unusually cold temperatures and [[concepts/winter-storms|winter storms]] caused widespread [[concepts/power-outages|blackouts]] affecting millions of residents. The outage lasted several days in some areas and resulted in significant disruptions to heating, water supply, and other essential services. Investigation into the event revealed vulnerabilities in grid [[concepts/preparation|preparation]], power plant winterization, and demand forecasting by ERCOT and participating utilities.
