# Baidu

## Overview
Chinese multinational technology company headquartered in Beijing. Core operations span internet search, cloud infrastructure, autonomous driving, and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. Major domestic player in the Chinese Technology Sector and competitive actor in global [[concepts/large-language-models]] development.

## AI & Foundation Models
Primary R&D focus on the ERNIE (Enhanced Representation through [[concepts/knowledge-integration|Knowledge Integration]]) series. [[concepts/models|Models]] prioritize Chinese language optimization, enterprise [[concepts/integration|API integration]], and cost-efficient [[concepts/training|training]] pipelines. [[concepts/strategic-pivot|Strategic pivot]] from web-scale services to AI-native infrastructure reduces reliance on foreign model providers.

### 2026 Developments
- Launched **ERNIE 5.1**, a next-generation multimodal [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation model]] engineered to compete with [[entities/anthropic-claude]] and [[entities/gemini]] across [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], generation, and cross-modal alignment benchmarks.
- Achieves industry-comparable performance while significantly reducing training costs through architectural optimization and [[concepts/mixture-of-experts-moe|sparse mixture-of-experts]] routing.
- Expanded [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]] enable tighter integration with Baidu Cloud [[concepts/saas|SaaS]] offerings and third-party enterprise workflows.
- Full technical breakdown and [[concepts/benchmark-testing|benchmarking]] covered in: [[lab-notes/2026-05-10-ERNIE-5.1-Baidus-AI-Model---High-Performance-Cost-Effici|ERNIE 5.1: Baidu's AI Model - High Performance, Cost-Efficient, Multimodal Capabilities]]

## Core Business Units
- **Search & Ecosystem**: Dominant Chinese search engine; integrated commerce, mapping, and [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]] services.
- **Baidu Cloud**: AI-native infrastructure platform competing with [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]] Cloud and Tencent Cloud; emphasizes model-as-a-service endpoints.
- **Autonomous Driving**: Apollo platform for L4 [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], high-definition mapping, and fleet management.
- **AI Services**: [[concepts/pre-trained-model|Pre-trained model]] APIs, [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] tools, and vertical industry solutions.

## Strategic Position
Acceleration of ERNIE [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycles responds to global model commodification and domestic regulatory shifts. Focus on [[concepts/vertical-integration|vertical integration]], lower [[concepts/inference|inference]] costs, and multimodal versatility positions Baidu to capture mid-to-large enterprise [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] while maintaining price competitiveness against Western alternatives.
