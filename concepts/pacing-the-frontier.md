# Pacing the Frontier

**Pacing the frontier** is a strategic philosophy in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] development advocating for measured, deliberate progress rather than uncontrolled acceleration. It emphasizes safety, alignment, and sustainable capability growth over raw speed-to-market.

## Core Principles
- **Deliberate Progress:** Prioritizing robust safety evaluations and alignment research alongside capability improvements.
- **Risk Mitigation:** Avoiding the deployment of models that outpace the community's ability to understand or control them.
- **Sustainable Innovation:** Focusing on long-term reliability and efficiency rather than short-term competitive dominance.

## Recent Developments
- **Anthropic's Shift:** Anthropic has historically advocated for this approach, but recent releases have sparked debate regarding its adherence to the principle.
- **[[concepts/single-gpu-performance|Claude Opus 5.5]] Release:** On 2026-09-23, Anthropic launched [[entities/bijan-bowen|Claude Opus 5.5]], marking its first significant model release since explicitly championing "pacing the frontier."
- **Performance Metrics:** The new model is described as offering unrivaled performance, efficiency, and cost savings, challenging previous assumptions about the trade-offs between speed and safety.
