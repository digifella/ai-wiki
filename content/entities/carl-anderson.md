# Carl Anderson

Carl [[entities/david|David]] Anderson was an American physicist known for his groundbreaking work in experimental atomic and nuclear [[concepts/physics|physics]], particularly the discovery of the [[concepts/positron|positron]] (the electron's antiparticle) in 1932. This discovery confirmed the existence of [[concepts/antimatter|antimatter]].

### Key Achievements
- **Discovery of Positron**: Carl Anderson is credited with the first observation of a particle which was later identified as the positron, an antiparticle counterpart to the electron.
- **Awarded Nobel Prize in [[concepts/physics|Physics]] (1936)**: He received this prestigious award for his contributions to atomic and nuclear physics.
