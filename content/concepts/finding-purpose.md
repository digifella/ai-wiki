# Finding Purpose

Finding [[concepts/purpose|purpose]] refers to the process of identifying personal meaning and direction in life. [[concepts/motivation|Purpose]] functions as a foundational element of [[concepts/health|wellbeing]], providing motivation, [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and a sense of alignment between values and actions. The concept extends beyond career or achievement to encompass broader life direction and what individuals consider worth dedicating their effort toward.

## Simon Sinek's 'Why' Framework

[[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]'s approach to finding purpose centers on articulating your "why"—the core reason or belief that drives your actions. Rather than focusing on what you do or how you do it, Sinek's method encourages individuals to examine deeper motivations and values. This story-based approach involves reflecting on personal experiences and identifying patterns that reveal underlying purpose. The method can be applied within a structured timeframe, making it accessible for practical [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] in everyday contexts.

## Purpose and Wellbeing

The [[concepts/connection|connection]] between purpose and [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] is well-documented in health and psychological research. Having a defined sense of purpose correlates with improved [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] outcomes, greater [[concepts/quality-of-life|life satisfaction]], and increased resilience during challenging periods. The process of uncovering purpose requires honest self-examination and often involves exploring personal history, values, and the impact one hopes to have.
