# Lemai

Lemai is an organization dedicated to artificial intelligence research and development, with a specific focus on healthcare applications. The entity works to create practical frameworks and methodologies that facilitate the adoption and implementation of AI technologies within health services. Its primary objective is to assist healthcare organizations in assessing their current capabilities and advancing their overall readiness for AI integration.

A key initiative involves a collaboration with Sandeep to develop an AI maturity model tailored for the health sector. This project utilizes agentic AI to establish standards and guidelines that help healthcare providers evaluate and improve their technological infrastructure. The resulting model aims to provide a structured approach for navigating the complexities of AI adoption in clinical and administrative settings.
