# Image Upscaling

[[concepts/image-resolution|Image upscaling]] refers to the process of enlarging digital [[concepts/images|images]] while maintaining or improving visual quality. Traditional upscaling methods rely on interpolation algorithms that estimate pixel values based on surrounding data, often resulting in blurry or artifacts-prone [[concepts/output|output]]. Modern generative upscaling techniques, by [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], use [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] trained on large image datasets to intelligently reconstruct detail and [[concepts/texture|texture]] when enlarging images.

## Photoshop Upscaling Tools

[[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] offers multiple generative upscaling options that [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] to enlarge images. These tools differ in their underlying [[concepts/models|models]], processing approaches, and the degree of [[concepts/power|control]] they offer users. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of these features into Photoshop's standard [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] reflects a broader shift toward [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] as a core creative tool within professional [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] [[concepts/software|software]].

The effectiveness of any upscaling method depends on several factors, including the original image quality, the degree of enlargement requested, and the specific subject matter. Users working with photographs, artwork, or other visual content should test multiple tools to determine which produces results suitable for their particular needs. As these technologies continue to evolve, the quality and [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of upscaling features are expected to improve further.
