# Research To Website Transformation

Research To Website Transformation is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] process that converts raw research [[concepts/notes|notes]] and data into published website content using [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] tools. The approach leverages these platforms' complementary [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to streamline the journey from initial research documentation to final web-ready materials, reducing manual formatting and rewriting work.

## Process Overview

The workflow typically begins with research notes stored in [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], which can generate structured visualizations such as [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]] from unorganized source material. These visualizations and summarized content are then processed through [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]], [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]], which refines, expands, or reformats the material into web-appropriate content. This two-step transformation enables researchers and content creators to move from raw notes to publication-ready [[concepts/text|text]] more efficiently than traditional manual transcription methods.

## Applications

This transformation approach is particularly useful for converting academic research, [[concepts/technical-documentation|technical documentation]], and project notes into blog posts, wiki articles, educational content, and [[concepts/interactive-web-pages|interactive web pages]]. The combination of [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]'s organizational capabilities and Gemini's [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] allows creators to maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across source materials while adapting them to different content formats and audiences without starting from scratch.
