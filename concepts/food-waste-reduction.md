# Food Waste Reduction Strategies

Type: concept
Tags: food waste, sustainability, [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] waste, cooking, food management
updated: 2026-05-04

## Overview
Food waste reduction is the practice of minimizing the amount of edible food discarded throughout the food supply chain, from production to consumption. Effective strategies focus on planning, proper [[entities/storage|storage]], creative cooking, and composting to conserve resources and reduce environmental impact.

## Key Strategies

### 1. Planning and Shopping
*   **Meal Planning:** Plan meals based on ingredients you already have to prevent over-purchasing and subsequent spoilage.
*   **[[concepts/inventory-management|Inventory Management]]:** Maintain an inventory of existing food to ensure items are used before they expire.
*   **Smart Shopping:** Buy only what is needed, focusing on fresh, seasonal produce, and understanding unit costs.

### 2. Storage Techniques
*   **Proper Refrigeration:** Understand optimal storage temperatures for different food types (e.g., vegetables, dairy).
*   **FIFO Method:** Implement the First-In, First-Out method to ensure older items are consumed before newer ones.
*   **Creative [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]:** Learn methods to extend the life of food, such as freezing, dehydrating, or fermenting.

### 3. Cooking and Usage
*   **"Use It Up" Meals:** Incorporate leftover ingredients into new recipes to utilize all components.
*   **Understanding Expiration Dates:** Distinguish between "Best By" and "Use By" dates to ensure food safety and quality.
*   **Creative Repurposing:** Use food scraps (like vegetable trimmings) for stocks or broths.

### 4. Composting and Donation
*   **Composting:** Divert unavoidable food scraps to composting systems to create nutrient-rich soil amendment.
*   **Food Donation:** Donate surplus edible food to local food banks or charities.
