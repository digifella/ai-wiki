# AI Based Selection

AI-based selection refers to automated masking and selection techniques that use artificial intelligence to detect and isolate subjects or specific areas within digital images. These tools analyze image content to identify edges, boundaries, and objects with greater precision than traditional manual selection methods. By leveraging machine learning algorithms, AI-based selection reduces the time and effort required for photographers and editors to create accurate masks and selections for targeted adjustments.

## Application in Photo Editing

In photo editing software like Adobe Lightroom and Camera Raw, AI-based selection tools are particularly useful for refining mask edges. Rather than manually drawing selections or relying on basic color-range tools, these intelligent systems can recognize the contours of subjects and create clean, precise boundaries. This capability allows editors to apply localized adjustments—such as exposure, color, or clarity changes—to specific areas without affecting surrounding content.

## Practical Benefits

The primary advantage of AI-based selection is efficiency combined with accuracy. Editors can quickly isolate complex subjects like hair, foliage, or intricate object edges that would be time-consuming to select manually. The technology learns from patterns in images to automatically detect where one element ends and another begins, making it particularly effective for separating subjects from backgrounds or enhancing specific regions within a photograph.
