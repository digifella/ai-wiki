# Oven Cooking Method

Oven cooking is a dry heat cooking method that uses an enclosed chamber heated to controlled temperatures to cook food. The consistent, surrounding heat environment allows for even cooking of various foods without direct [[entities/contact|contact]] with a heat source, making it suitable for a wide range of ingredients and dishes.

## Heat Transfer and Temperature Control

Heat in an oven is distributed through convection, radiation, or a combination of both, depending on the oven type. This allows cooks to set precise temperatures and maintain them throughout the cooking process. The enclosed environment prevents moisture loss compared to some open [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]], though the degree of moisture retention varies by food type and cooking duration.

## Common Applications

Oven cooking is used for baking breads and pastries, roasting vegetables and meats, and preparing casseroles and prepared dishes. The method can also be applied to foods traditionally associated with other techniques—for example, cooking bacon or potatoes in an oven can produce uniform results by exposing all surfaces to consistent heat. The versatility of oven cooking across different temperature ranges and cooking times makes it a fundamental technique in both home and professional kitchens.
