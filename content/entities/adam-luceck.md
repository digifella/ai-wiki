Adam Luceck specializes in optimizing [[concepts/rag]] [[concepts/text-chunking|text chunking]] strategies for [[concepts/vector-databases|vector databases]], with key contributions detailed in the ChromaDB technical report "Evaluating [[concepts/chunking-strategies|Chunking Strategies]] for Retrieval." His work demonstrates:

- Semantic chunking outperforms fixed-size methods in context retention for retrieval tasks
- Optimal chunk size balances precision (reducing noise) and [[concepts/recall|recall]] (preserving context)
- Hybrid approaches (combining semantic and structural cues) yield highest retrieval [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]]
- Empirical evaluation across document types informs strategy selection for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]
