## Transforming NotebookLM Slides to Unwatermarked Google Vids
**Clip title:** How to Turn [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] Slides Into Unwatermarked Videos
**Author / channel:** Charles Terrence [[entities/harper|Harper]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i2cFCqh1HIs

### Summary
This video [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] focuses on leveraging Google's [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]], primarily
[[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] and [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]], to efficiently repurpose existing coaching
intellectual property, particularly [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]], into various video
formats. The main topic revolves around transforming static content into
dynamic, engaging video assets for purposes like infographics and webinars,
with options for different styles and levels of [[concepts/customization|customization]].

The core process begins within [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], which acts as a centralized,
[[concepts/secure|secure]] repository for a user's expertise, organized into notebooks for
different client topics or workshop themes. To create a video, a slide deck
(either a PDF or PPTX) is first downloaded from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] and then
re-uploaded as a source within a new NotebookLM chat. The user then selects
only the presentation source and navigates to the "Studio" section to
choose the "Video Overview" option. Here, three video formats are
available: "Cinematic" (a rich, immersive experience), "Explainer" (a
structured overview), and "Brief" (a bite-sized summary). The user inputs
custom prompts, ideally generated by [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] for optimal specificity
regarding [[concepts/tone|tone]] and call to action, to guide the AI in producing the desired
video content. Generated videos are reviewed for their alignment with the
intended message and call to action.

A significant point discussed is the presence of watermarks on videos
directly generated by NotebookLM. To circumvent this, the [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]]
introduces two methods using Google's ecosystem. First, the NotebookLM
slide deck can be uploaded to `vids.google.com` (Google Vids), a tool that
converts presentations into videos with AI voiceover, script, and
background music, *without* the NotebookLM watermark. Alternatively, the
user can upload their watermarked slide deck or [[concepts/images|images]] from it to [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]
and prompt [[entities/gemini|Gemini]]'s "[[concepts/canvas|Canvas]]" tool to create a *new* [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]]
presentation. This newly generated Google Slides presentation will be
unwatermarked, allowing it to then be imported into `vids.google.com` for
creating a clean, professional video.

In conclusion, the video highlights how coaches and content creators can
significantly streamline their content repurposing efforts using AI. While
NotebookLM offers quick [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] directly from [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]],
understanding its watermarking limitation is crucial. By integrating [[entities/gemini|Gemini]]
for precise prompt generation and utilizing `vids.google.com` (either with
direct slide imports or via Gemini-created unwatermarked Google Slides),
users gain enhanced control over the final output, producing diverse,
high-quality video content that is free from proprietary watermarks and
tailored to specific marketing or educational objectives.
