# Encryption methods

Processes used to encode information to ensure data confidentiality and [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].

## Core Architectures
- Symmetric Encryption: Utilizes a single shared key for both encryption and decryption processes.
- Asymmetric Encryption: Employs a public-private key pair, where the public key encrypts and the private key decrypts.

## Quantum Threats and Mitigation
- [[entities/quantum-computing]] poses a significant threat to the [[concepts/mathematical-concepts|mathematical foundations]] of current Asymmetric [[concepts/encryption-standards|Encryption standards]] (e.g., RSA, ECC).
- [[concepts/post-quantum-cryptography]] (PQC) is a critical research priority aimed at developing [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] resistant to [[concepts/quantum-attacks|quantum-based attacks]].
