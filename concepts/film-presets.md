# Film Presets

Film Presets are a collection of camera raw processing presets introduced in Adobe Camera Raw 18.3 and Lightroom Classic 15.3 that emulate the visual characteristics of traditional film stocks. These presets allow photographers to apply film-inspired color grading and tonal adjustments to digital images by simulating the appearance of various analog film types. The presets operate within Adobe's raw processing engine, enabling adjustment and customization beyond their default application.

## Function and Application

The presets work by applying predetermined combinations of color, contrast, and tonal curve adjustments that replicate the distinctive rendering qualities of different film emulsions. Users can apply these presets as a starting point for image editing and then further refine them using standard adjustment tools. This approach combines the aesthetic appeal of film photography with the flexibility of digital post-processing.

## Context within Adobe Updates

The introduction of Film Presets occurred alongside other significant updates to Adobe's raw editing tools, including enhancements to depth range masking, lens correction algorithms, Firefly AI integration, and background processing capabilities. These features were part of Adobe's broader effort to expand the editing capabilities available to photographers working with raw files in both Camera Raw and Lightroom Classic.
