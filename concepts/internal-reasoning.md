# Internal Reasoning

**Internal [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]** refers to the latent [[concepts/internal-working-mechanisms|computational processes]] and representational states within an AI model that occur between input ingestion and [[concepts/output-generation|output generation]]. Unlike surface-level [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], internal reasoning involves the model's "thought" [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]], including [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] patterns, [[concepts/hidden-state|hidden state]] activations, and emergent cognitive structures.

## Key Concepts & Mechanisms

- **[[concepts/embedding-spaces|Latent Space]] Dynamics**: The high-dimensional vector space where semantic meaning is encoded and manipulated during [[concepts/inference|inference]].
- **[[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|Chain-of-Thought]] (CoT)**: A [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] technique that encourages models to generate intermediate [[concepts/reasoning-steps|reasoning steps]], often serving as a proxy for accessing deeper internal [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]].
- **Emergent [[concepts/conscious-processing|Global Workspace]]**: Recent research suggests that [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] may develop an internal "global workspace" analogous to human [[concepts/conscious-thought|consciousness]], allowing for the integration of disparate information streams.
	- See [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/j-space|J-space]]: Emergent Internal Global Workspace in [[concepts/ai-models|AI Models]] for details on [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s identification of this "[[concepts/surface-level-thoughts|J-space]]."

## Recent Developments

- **Anthropic's J-space (2026)**:
	- Anthropic published research identifying a specific internal processing region dubbed "J-space."
	- This space functions as an emergent global workspace, facilitating [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] and information integration.
