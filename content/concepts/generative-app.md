# Generative App

A generative app is a [[concepts/software|software]] application that leverages [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] to create, transform, or enhance user-generated content. These applications integrate [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], image generation systems, or [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] to assist users with creative and productive tasks. [[concepts/generative-apps|Generative apps]] span multiple domains including design, [[concepts/writing|writing]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], functioning as tools that augment human capability rather than replace manual processes.

## Key Features

Generative apps typically operate through natural language interfaces, allowing users to describe desired outcomes and receive generated outputs in real-time. They may include features for [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]], where users can adjust [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], provide [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], or regenerate results. Integration with existing workflows—such as embedding generative capabilities within design software or document editors—characterizes many modern implementations.

## Notable Examples

[[concepts/google-search|Google]] has released several generative app features as part of its [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] product line, including tools designed to streamline design workflows and content generation. These updates reflect a broader industry trend toward embedding generative capabilities into everyday [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]] rather than isolating them in [[concepts/desktop-apps|standalone applications]].
