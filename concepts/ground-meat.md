# Ground Meat

Ground meat is a versatile ingredient in many cuisines, used for dishes like burgers, meatballs, and sauces. It's made by grinding various cuts of meat into smaller pieces.

## Enhancing Ground Meat with Baking Soda

- **Improves [[concepts/texture|Texture]]:** Adds tenderness without overprocessing.
- **Enhances Juiciness:** Helps retain moisture during cooking.
- **Aids Browning:** Improves color and flavor development.

## Myosin Development

Mechanical manipulation of ground meat facilitates [[concepts/myosin|myosin]] extraction, which is critical for binding and [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] in specific preparations.

- **Binding & Structure:** Essential for creating the cohesive structure in [[concepts/sausage|sausage]] and [[concepts/meatballs|meatballs]].
- **[[concepts/uniform-crispness|Texture Control]]:** Determines the "snap" or firmness of the final product; excessive mixing can lead to a rubbery texture, while insufficient mixing results in crumbly burgers.
- **Application:** Key technique for mastering the distinct textures required for burgers versus sausages.
