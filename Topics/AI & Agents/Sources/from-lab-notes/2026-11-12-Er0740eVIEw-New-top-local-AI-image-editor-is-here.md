## New top local AI image editor is here!
**Clip title:** New top local AI image editor is here!
**Author / channel:** AI Search
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Er0740eVIEw
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from AI Search: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video introduces MiniMax H3-Image, a powerful new open-weight image generation model. It stands out for its speed, generating a 4K (4096 x 4096) image in under 10 seconds. The model also supports image editing and reference images (up to 16 reference images). It is noted for its flexibility, handling various art styles, text, and design well.

The video compares MiniMax H3-Image with Qwen Image 2.1 and Krea 2. While MiniMax is fast and performs well in text-to-image and image editing, other models may be better in certain aspects like specific pose understanding or text accuracy. The speaker demonstrates how to install and run MiniMax H3-Image offline using ComfyUI, providing workflows for text-to-image and image editing. They also discuss how to handle different model sizes and VRAM requirements, including downloading GGUF versions for lower VRAM.

Additionally, the video briefly highlights Higgsfield, the sponsor, and its video generation model Seedance 2.5, which can generate 4K resolution videos up to 30 seconds long and offers control over the final video.

In conclusion, MiniMax H3-Image is presented as a strong open-source option for generating and editing high-resolution images quickly, especially when used with ComfyUI. The video provides a comprehensive tutorial on setting it up and using it effectively.
