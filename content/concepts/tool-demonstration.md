# Tool Demonstration

A **tool demonstration** is a focused presentation or walkthrough that validates, teaches, or markets the [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of a Tool, [[concepts/software|Software]] Application, or [[concepts/hardware|Hardware]] Device. Effective demonstrations translate technical specifications into observable utility, often highlighting [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] [[concepts/integration|integration]] and efficiency gains.

## Key Attributes
*   **Functional Validation:** Confirms feature stability and performance against release criteria.
*   **Workflow Context:** Illustrates placement within larger Production Pipeline or Creative Process.
*   **Update Impact:** Quantifies improvements introduced in [[concepts/version-updates|version updates]] relative to prior iterations.
