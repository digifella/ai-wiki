# Disconnection

*Concept overview*: Disconnection refers to a state where individuals feel isolated or estranged from others and themselves. This can occur on multiple levels including emotional, social, and spiritual disconnection. It often results in feelings of [[concepts/loneliness|loneliness]], alienation, and a lack of belonging.
