# Regulatory Framework

The adoption of agentic AI by regulatory bodies represents a significant milestone in institutional governance. In December 2025, the FDA began deploying agentic AI technologies for in-house operations, marking a notable shift in how regulatory agencies approach the automation of their core functions. This development reflects growing institutional confidence in autonomous AI systems capable of handling structured decision-making processes within established regulatory workflows.

## Institutional Implementation

The FDA's deployment of agentic AI focuses on automating routine administrative and analytical tasks. By integrating these systems into internal operations, the agency aims to enhance efficiency and consistency in processing regulatory data. This move signals a broader trend among government entities to leverage advanced artificial intelligence for managing complex bureaucratic processes while maintaining oversight of automated decision-making.
