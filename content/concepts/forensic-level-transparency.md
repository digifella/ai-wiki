# Forensic-Level Transparency

Complete visibility into all processes, data flows, and dependencies across an entire system — not just isolated components.

**Key Insight from [[entities/paul|Paul]]'s [[concepts/knowledge-base|Knowledge Base]]:**
- **The [[concepts/farah-jama-principle|Farah Jama Principle]]**: The [[entities/farah-jama|Farah Jama]] forensic case demonstrated that scientific validity requires "[[concepts/whole-of-case-visibility|whole-of-case visibility]]" — understanding upstream (data sources) and downstream (impact) processes, not just isolated segments. This directly mirrors AI initiative failures: organizations treat AI like traditional IT projects with segmented oversight, causing untraceable errors and misaligned outcomes.

**Why It Matters:**
- Prevents "black box" failures in AI/data systems by enabling end-to-end auditability.
- Eliminates siloed [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] (e.g., data engineers can trace issues to model [[concepts/training|training]] stages).
- Required for regulatory [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] (e.g., GDPR, HIPAA) and [[concepts/responsible-ai-use|ethical AI]].

**Related Concepts:**
- Data Provenance (tracking data lineage)
- [[concepts/xai|Explainable AI]] ([[entities/xai|XAI]]) (interpreting model decisions)
- AI Project Management (integrating transparency into workflows)
- Whole-Of-Case Visibility (forensic [[concepts/science|science]] framework)
