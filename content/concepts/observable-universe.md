# Observable Universe

The observable universe is a spherical volume centered on the observer, comprising all matter that can be observed from Earth at present time, due to the finite [[concepts/speed|speed]] of [[concepts/light|light]] and the age of the universe. The current size of this sphere is approximately 93 billion light-years in diameter.

This fact underscores both the mathematical and practical limits of Pi's application in cosmological measurements.
