# Deep Learning Model

A deep learning model is a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] composed of multiple layers of interconnected nodes that learn hierarchical representations of data. These models excel at identifying complex patterns and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] within large datasets by progressively refining abstract features through each successive layer. Deep [[concepts/learning|learning]] has become foundational to modern [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]], from [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] to [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]].

## Application in AI Agents

[[concepts/deep-learning-models|Deep learning models]] serve as the computational backbone for advanced AI agents. When integrated into [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]], these models enable [[concepts/agents|agents]] to process information more intelligently, reason about complex problems, and generate contextually appropriate [[concepts/responses|responses]]. The combination of deep learning with multi-agent frameworks allows for the construction of sophisticated research and analysis systems that can decompose tasks, collaborate across [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]], and iteratively refine their outputs.

## Building Deep Research Agent Flows

Practical implementations of deep learning in [[concepts/agentic-systems|agent systems]] can be developed using no-code platforms like [[concepts/flowise|Flowise]] combined with [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] from providers such as [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]. These platforms enable builders to construct deep research agent flows—systems that perform comprehensive information gathering and analysis by orchestrating multiple specialized agents. This approach leverages deep learning capabilities without requiring extensive custom code, making advanced [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] construction more accessible to practitioners.
