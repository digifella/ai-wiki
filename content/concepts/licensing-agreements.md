# Licensing Agreements

Licensing agreements are formal contracts that establish the terms under which one party (the licensor) grants another party (the licensee) permission to use [[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|intellectual property]], technology, or other proprietary assets. In the context of [[concepts/software|software]] and [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|artificial intelligence models]], licensing agreements define usage rights, restrictions, [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] terms, and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements that govern how recipients can deploy and modify the licensed technology.

## Open Source vs. Proprietary Models

Open source licensing agreements, such as those used for [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], typically permit broader access and modification of the underlying [[concepts/code|code]] or model [[concepts/weights|weights]], often with conditions regarding attribution and derivative work distribution. Proprietary licensing agreements, by [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], restrict usage to specific applications or organizations and may prohibit modification or redistribution. The choice of [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] framework significantly impacts [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] rates, community contribution, and [[concepts/commercial-viability|commercial viability]] of technological products.

## Importance in AI Development

For AI [[concepts/model-releases|model releases]], licensing agreements clarify the legal status of trained [[concepts/models|models]], [[concepts/training-data|training data]] usage, and liability protections. They determine whether users can fine-tune models for their own purposes, incorporate them into commercial products, or share improvements with others. Clear licensing terms are essential for establishing trust and enabling organizations to assess compliance risk when integrating external models into their systems.
