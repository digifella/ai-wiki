# Non Residence Visa

A Non Residence Visa is an electronic visa (e-visa) category administered by Turkey that permits foreign nationals to enter and remain in the country for specified purposes without establishing formal residency. Processed entirely through Turkey's online e-visa system, this visa type accommodates short-to-medium term visits and is intended for individuals who do not plan to become permanent residents.

## Purpose and Duration

The Non Residence Visa is designed for temporary visits to Turkey. It allows foreign nationals to enter the country for tourism, business meetings, or other short-term activities. The visa is strictly for those who do not intend to establish permanent residency, serving as a convenient entry mechanism for travelers with temporary needs.

## Application Process

Applications are handled exclusively through Turkey's official online e-visa portal. Applicants must complete the digital form and pay the required fees electronically. The system is designed to streamline the entry process for eligible foreign nationals, ensuring that documentation is verified and issued without the need for physical embassy visits for most standard cases.
