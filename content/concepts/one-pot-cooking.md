# One Pot Cooking

One pot cooking is a culinary technique in which an entire dish is prepared in a single cooking vessel, typically a skillet or heavy-bottomed pan. This method streamlines [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] and cleanup while allowing flavors to develop through the gradual combination of ingredients in one space. One pot cooking is particularly well-suited to dishes like currys, braises, and stews, where layering flavors and extended cooking times benefit the final result.

## North Indian Skillet Chicken Curry

[[entities/neel-kajale|Chef Neel Kajale]], based in New York City, has documented a North Indian cuisine approach to skillet [[concepts/chicken-curry|chicken curry]] that exemplifies the one pot cooking method. This recipe combines chicken with aromatic spices and a tomato-based sauce, all prepared sequentially in a single skillet. The technique involves building the curry base with spices and aromatics before introducing the protein and liquid components, allowing each layer to contribute to the overall depth of flavor characteristic of North Indian cuisine.

One pot [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]] like this offer practical advantages for home cooks and professional kitchens alike, reducing both the number of dishes and active [[concepts/cooking-time|cooking time]] while maintaining consistent heat [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].
