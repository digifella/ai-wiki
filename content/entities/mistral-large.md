# Mistral Large

Mistral Large is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/mistral|Mistral]] AI. When quantized, the model can run on [[concepts/nvidia-server-chips|NVIDIA GPUs]] with 48GB of VRAM, making it suitable for [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] on mid-to-high-end consumer and professional [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. The model is designed to handle well-instructed tasks, where clear prompts and structured inputs guide its outputs.

## Positioning and Comparisons

Mistral Large operates in the same capability tier as competing models such as [[entities/llama-31|Llama 3.1 70B]] and [[entities/qwen-2|Qwen 2 72B]]. These models represent practical options for organizations and individuals seeking to run capable language models locally rather than through cloud APIs. The [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]] of such large models is necessary to fit them within the [[concepts/ram-limitations|memory constraints]] of standard GPU configurations.
