# Deakin University

Deakin University is an Australian university associated with research and development in digital [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] interventions. The institution has been involved in projects examining the intersection of digital technology and psychological wellbeing, including work on addressing the [[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]—the tension between the desire for digital health solutions and reluctance to use them.

## Research and Development

[[entities/prof-martin-held|Prof Martin Held]] at Deakin University has been involved in developing interactive digital health [[concepts/software|applications]]. This work has included collaboration on the [[entities/ses-one-intervention|SES Family intervention]], which originated from the University of Copenhagen. The research focuses on modules addressing [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] resolution and mental health support, with applications designed to feature simple, user-friendly layouts that encourage engagement.
