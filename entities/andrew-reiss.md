# Andrew Reiss

Andrew Reiss is a researcher who participated in the Biosphere 2 project, a large-scale ecological experiment conducted near Oracle, Arizona. Biosphere 2 was designed as a closed environmental system to study how self-sustaining ecosystems function in isolation and to advance understanding of Earth's biosphere and ecological processes.

## Research Contributions

Reiss contributed to the scientific objectives of Biosphere 2 through his work within the sealed structure. His participation in the experiment provided empirical data on the behavior of interconnected ecological systems when separated from external environmental influences. The research conducted during the project contributed to broader knowledge about ecosystem dynamics, nutrient cycling, and the complexity of maintaining balanced biological systems.

## Significance to Biosphere Research

The Biosphere 2 experiment, in which Reiss participated, served as an important case study in ecological science and systems biology. By observing how ecosystems responded under controlled conditions, researchers including Reiss helped identify critical factors necessary for ecosystem stability and identified challenges in creating self-sustaining closed systems—findings applicable to theoretical models of planetary ecology and future space exploration scenarios.
