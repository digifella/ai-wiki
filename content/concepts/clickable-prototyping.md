# Clickable Prototyping

Clickable prototyping is an approach to interactive design that enables creators to build functional, navigable prototypes without extensive [[concepts/coding|coding]]. These prototypes allow users to click through interfaces, test user flows, and validate design decisions before full development. Clickable prototypes bridge the gap between static mockups and fully functional [[concepts/software|applications]], making them valuable for [[concepts/design-iteration|design iteration]], user [[concepts/testing|testing]], and stakeholder communication.

## Google Stitch 2.0

[[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|Google Stitch]] is a prototyping tool that facilitates the creation of clickable prototypes through an intuitive interface. Version 2.0 represents an update to the platform's capabilities, allowing designers to convert design assets and wireframes into [[concepts/interactive-experiences|interactive experiences]]. The tool integrates with design workflows and supports the rapid assembly of prototype interactions, making it accessible to designers at various skill levels.

## Educational Resources

Teacher's Tech, a YouTube channel focused on design and [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]], has documented walkthroughs of Google Stitch 2.0 functionality. These videos serve as practical guides for users [[concepts/learning|learning]] to work with the tool, demonstrating features such as interaction mapping, asset [[concepts/organization|organization]], and prototype testing. Such educational content helps demystify the prototyping process for newcomers to interactive design.
