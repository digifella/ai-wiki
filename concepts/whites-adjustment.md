# Whites Adjustment

The Whites Adjustment is a tonal control slider in Adobe Lightroom Classic's Develop module that selectively brightens or darkens the lighter midtones and near-white areas of a photograph. Located in the Basic panel alongside related tools like Exposure and Highlights, it operates independently of shadow adjustments, making it useful for fine-tuning the brightness of highlights without affecting the overall contrast or darker tones of an image.

The slider works by targeting a specific tonal range, primarily the brighter midtones and near-white pixels, allowing photographers to recover detail in overexposed areas or enhance the luminance of bright elements such as clouds or snow. Unlike the Highlights slider, which affects the upper midtones and highlights more broadly, the Whites tool specifically targets the extreme bright end of the histogram, providing precise control over the image's brightest points.

This feature is particularly valuable in landscape photography, where maintaining detail in bright skies and reflective surfaces is crucial. By adjusting the Whites slider, users can ensure that white areas remain pure without clipping, preserving texture and color information in the brightest parts of the scene. This capability supports the workflow demonstrated in tutorials by experts like Anthony Morganti, who emphasize the importance of balanced tonal adjustments for high-quality landscape processing.
