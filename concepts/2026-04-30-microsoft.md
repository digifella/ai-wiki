# 2026 04 30 Microsoft

Microsoft is a multinational technology corporation headquartered in Redmond, Washington, founded in 1975. As of April 2026, the company operates across multiple business segments including cloud computing through Azure, productivity software via Microsoft 365, gaming through Xbox, professional networking via LinkedIn, and artificial intelligence services.

## Strategic Priorities

Microsoft's business strategy in 2026 centers on the integration of generative artificial intelligence across its core product lines. The company has prioritized embedding AI capabilities into the Microsoft 365 suite to enhance enterprise productivity and workflow automation. Concurrently, Azure continues to expand its infrastructure to support large-scale AI model training and inference, maintaining its position as a leading cloud provider for enterprise digital transformation.

## Research and Development

Beyond immediate commercial applications, Microsoft maintains significant investment in long-term technological frontiers. The corporation continues to fund research into quantum computing, aiming to solve complex problems in chemistry, logistics, and cryptography that are intractable for classical computers. This dual focus on immediate AI integration and foundational quantum research defines the company's technological roadmap for the mid-2020s.
