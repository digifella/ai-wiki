# Color blending

The process of merging chromatic, luminance, and shadow properties to create visual unity between disparate elements within a single composition.

## Digital Imaging & Automation
- **[[entities/adobe-photoshop]] (Beta)**:
    - **Harmonize Feature**: An AI-powered tool designed to automatically blend color, lighting, and shadow between layers to achieve seamless Compositing.

## Core Principles
- **Light and Shadow**: Essential for establishing consistent depth and volume across integrated layers.
- **Color [[concepts/theory|Theory]]**: Utilizing [[concepts/hue]], Saturation, and Luminance to align separate objects with a primary light source.
- **Digital Image Manipulation**: The broader application of blending techniques to ensure visual [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] in [[concepts/photo-editing]].
