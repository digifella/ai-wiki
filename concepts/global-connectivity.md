# Global connectivity

The seamless interconnection of people, systems, and information across geographical boundaries through technological infrastructure. Enables real-time communication, economic integration, and [[concepts/cultural-exchange|cultural exchange]] at planetary scale.

## Key Drivers
- **[[concepts/satellite-internet|Satellite internet]] networks** like Starlink democratizing access in remote regions, reducing [[concepts/digital-divide|digital divide]]
- Internet infrastructure evolution toward resilient, low-latency global networks
- Societal transformation through unprecedented information [[concepts/flow|flow]] and participation

## Starlink Impact (2026)
- Validating Musk's claims: Starlink becoming planetary default [[concepts/internet-layer|internet layer]] through rapid deployment
- Enabling [[concepts/information-access|information access]] in [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] zones and rural areas previously offline
- Accelerating digital inclusion through affordable, high-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] connectivity
