# Paleontology
Paleontology is the study of prehistoric life through fossil records and other evidence from deep time to reconstruct ancient ecosystems and [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|evolutionary history]]. Key areas include vertebrate paleontology (focusing on fish, amphibians, reptiles, birds, and [[entities/mammals|mammals]]) and invertebrate paleontology (studying organisms without backbones like mollusks, arthropods, and echinoderms).
