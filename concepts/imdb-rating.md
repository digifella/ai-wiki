# IMDB Rating

**[[entities/imdb|IMDb]] (Internet Movie Database) Rating** is a crowdsourced scoring system used to evaluate films, television series, and other media. It aggregates user submissions to produce a weighted average score, typically on a scale of 1 to 10.

## Overview
- **Mechanism**: Uses a weighted [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]] to mitigate vote stuffing and bias, prioritizing votes from verified users and those with consistent [[concepts/vote|voting]] history.
- **Significance**: Serves as a primary heuristic for audience reception and [[concepts/ingredient-selection|quality assessment]] in the entertainment industry.
- **[[concepts/data-structure|Data Structure]]**: Includes aggregate scores, user review counts, and demographic breakdowns.
