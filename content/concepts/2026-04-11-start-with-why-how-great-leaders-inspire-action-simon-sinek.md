# 2026 04 11 Start With Why How Great Leaders Inspire Action Simon Sinek

"Start with Why" is a popular framework presented by organizational consultant and author Simon Sinek in a TEDxPugetSound talk. The talk presents Sinek's theory that effective leaders and organizations distinguish themselves by communicating their fundamental purpose or reason for existing—their "why"—before explaining what they do or how they do it. This inverted approach to communication and business strategy contrasts with the conventional method of leading with products, services, or tactical details.

## Core Principle

Sinek argues that most organizations and leaders communicate in an outside-in manner, starting with concrete details (what) and occasionally mentioning how they do things, but rarely articulating the deeper purpose that drives their work. He proposes instead an inside-out approach, beginning with purpose. This framework draws on observations about how companies like Apple, the Wright brothers, and various influential leaders have succeeded by inspiring people around a shared sense of purpose rather than merely promoting functional benefits.

## Broader Impact

The talk resonated widely and contributed to Sinek's subsequent book of the same title, which expanded the concept into a broader business philosophy. The "Start with Why" framework has influenced discussions on leadership, organizational culture, and marketing strategy, becoming a reference point in conversations about authentic motivation and long-term organizational vision versus short-term commercial messaging.
