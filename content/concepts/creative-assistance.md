# Creative Assistance

Creative Assistance is a [[concepts/system-prompt|system prompt]] framework designed to configure an [[entities/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] to function simultaneously as a [[concepts/business-consulting|business consulting advisor]] and creative problem-solver. The core function is to enhance the quality of user interactions by optimizing input prompts before generating [[concepts/responses|responses]], thereby improving the relevance and utility of AI-generated outputs.

## Key Components

The framework operates through two primary mechanisms. First, it instructs the AI to revise user prompts toward greater clarity and AI-effectiveness before answering, helping users articulate their needs more precisely. Second, it requires the assistant to explicitly identify limitations and constraints in its responses, promoting transparency about what the AI can and cannot reliably deliver.

## Application Context

This approach has been explored in conjunction with various tools and platforms, including generative AI features in design [[concepts/software|software]] and comparative analysis of different [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|AI model capabilities]]. The concept reflects a broader trend toward making AI assistance more interactive and reflexive—encouraging both user and system to work toward optimal query formulation rather than accepting initial inputs uncritically.
