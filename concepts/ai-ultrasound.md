# AI Ultrasound

**AI Ultrasound** refers to the application of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to enhance, automate, or generate ultrasound imaging data. This field encompasses diagnostic assistance, [[concepts/image-inpainting|image reconstruction]], and emerging concepts of longitudinal [[concepts/health|health]] monitoring through accessible scanning technologies.

## Key Developments

### Midjourney Medical Initiative
In June 2026, Midjourney announced the formation of "[[entities/midjourney-medical|Midjourney Medical]]," a new division focused on deploying a global network of [[concepts/full-body-ultrasound|full-body ultrasound]] scanners. This initiative aims to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] for [[concepts/longitudinal-health-monitoring|longitudinal health monitoring]], potentially democratizing access to continuous physiological data.

- **Source Integration**: [[lab-notes/2026-06-21-Midjourney-Medical-AI-Ultrasound-Scanners-for-Longitudin|Midjourney Medical: AI Ultrasound Scanners for Longitudinal Health Monitoring]]
- **Core Proposal**: Development of a distributed network of scanners for routine, non-invasive health tracking.
- **Implication**: Shifts ultrasound from episodic diagnostic tool to [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|continuous monitoring]] infrastructure.

## Technical Context

- **[[concepts/photo-enhancement|Image Enhancement]]**: [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] improve [[concepts/solution|resolution]] and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] of low-quality ultrasound feeds.
- **Automated Analysis**: Algorithms detect anomalies (e.g., tumors, structural defects) with higher [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] than human operators.
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Portable AI-driven devices reduce dependency on specialized radiologists for initial screenings.
