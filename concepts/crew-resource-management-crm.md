# Crew Resource Management (CRM)

**Crew Resource Management (CRM)** is a set of procedures, systems, and techniques designed to optimize [[concepts/human-performance|human performance]] and safety by emphasizing interpersonal communication, [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/skills|skills]] in the workplace. Originally developed for aviation cockpit crews, CRM principles have been adapted for other high-[[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] organizations, including [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] and emergency services.

## Core Principles

CRM focuses on the effective use of all available resources, including hardware, software, and human resources. Key components include:

- **Communication:** Clear, concise, and closed-[[concepts/loop|loop]] communication among team members.
- **Situational [[concepts/conscious-thought|Awareness]]:** Maintaining an accurate perception of the current environment and anticipating future states.
- **Leadership and Followership:** Effective command structures balanced with [[concepts/active-participation|active participation]] and challenge from subordinates.
- **Decision-Making:** Structured approaches to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] under pressure.

## AI Cognition and Human Responsibility

Recent analyses of aviation incidents, such as the Air [[entities/india|India]] crash, highlight evolving challenges at the intersection of [[concepts/human-cognition|human cognition]] and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. Key insights include:

- **[[concepts/human-responsibility|Human Responsibility]]:** The critical role of human oversight in systems where [[concepts/ai-cognition|AI cognition]] may lack [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]] or ethical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]].
- **Evolving AI [[concepts/cognition|Cognition]]:** The need for CRM frameworks to adapt to AI-driven decision support systems, ensuring humans remain "in the loop" for critical safety judgments.
- **Systemic Integration:** Integrating AI outputs into existing CRM protocols without compromising situational awareness or team communication dynamics.
