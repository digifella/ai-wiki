# Knowledge work automation

The application of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] and [[concepts/large-language-models]] to automate cognitive, non-routine tasks and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes.

## Core Paradigms
- **[[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: A [[concepts/strategic-shift|strategic shift]] from developing "better models" to achieving deep integration within existing professional ecosystems.
- **[[concepts/proprietary-data-access|Proprietary Data Access]]**: Prioritizing the ability for AI to interact with and leverage internal, non-public [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].

## Recent Breakthroughs
- [[entities/claude-4|Claude]] In Excel (Ref: [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] in Excel   Channel [[entities/nate-b-jones|Nate B Jones]])
    - Direct integration of [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s Claude into [[entities/microsoft-excel|Microsoft Excel]].
    - Enables the construction of complex, multi-tab [[concepts/financial-modeling]] in minutes rather than weeks.
    - Represents a "phase change" in the automation of [[concepts/knowledge-work]] via tool-native AI.
