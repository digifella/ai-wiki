# Chicken Curry

Chicken curry is a foundational dish in North Indian cuisine, characterized by chicken cooked in a richly spiced sauce base. The dish represents a core cooking tradition across the region, with numerous regional and family variations reflecting local ingredient availability and taste preferences. The sauce typically combines aromatics such as onions and garlic, a blend of dry spices, and either tomato or yogurt as its base, creating a complex flavor profile that develops through careful cooking technique and timing.

## Chef Neel Kajale's Skillet Method

Chef Neel Kajale's approach to chicken curry uses a skillet-based technique that streamlines the traditional preparation process.
