# AI Assisted Video Creation

AI-assisted [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] refers to the use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools to automate and streamline the video production process. These platforms reduce technical and creative barriers by providing pre-built assets, [[concepts/templates|templates]], and automated generation capabilities. Rather than requiring specialized [[concepts/skills|skills]] in cinematography, animation, or editing, users can create videos by selecting from available components and customizing them for their needs. This approach significantly decreases production time and makes video creation accessible to organizations and individuals without dedicated media production teams.

## How It Works

Platforms like Vyond exemplify this approach by offering [[concepts/stock-characters|stock characters]], animations, and scenes that users combine to build complete videos. Users typically work within a template-based interface where they can select characters, define scenes, add narration or text, and adjust timing. The AI handles the technical aspects of rendering, synchronizing audio with visuals, and ensuring consistent quality across the final output. This [[concepts/abstraction|abstraction]] of technical complexity allows creators to focus on narrative and messaging rather than production mechanics.

## Applications and Impact

AI-assisted video creation is commonly used for corporate [[concepts/training|training]], marketing content, explainer videos, and internal communications. The technology has made video a more practical [[entities/medium|medium]] for organizations that previously relied on text or static presentations due to resource constraints. By reducing both the cost and time required to produce videos, these tools have expanded the range of [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where video becomes a viable communication option.
