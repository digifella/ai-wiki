# Chicken breast preparation

Focuses on maximizing moisture retention and flavor infusion during thermal processing to prevent protein [[concepts/dehydration|dehydration]].

## Key Techniques for Juiciness
- Marinade: Utilized for flavor infusion and moisture retention.
- Cut Techniques: Proper [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] of the meat to ensure [[concepts/even-heat-distribution|even heat distribution]] and reduced [[concepts/cooking-time|cooking time]].
- [[concepts/cooking-techniques]]: [[concepts/adoption|Implementation]] of specific methods, particularly [[concepts/bbq|Grilling]], to avoid common pitfalls that lead to dry meat.

## Core Problem
- Dry Chicken: Primarily caused by improper [[concepts/thermal-regulation|temperature control]] and failure to utilize preparatory techniques.
