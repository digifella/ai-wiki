# Mark Denney

Mark Denney is a landscape photographer and educator known for producing instructional content on photographic techniques. His work focuses on addressing practical technical challenges in landscape photography, particularly methods for achieving sharp, detailed imagery across entire compositions.

## Focus Stacking

Denney is recognized for his detailed explanations of focus stacking, a technique used to enhance overall sharpness in landscape photographs. Focus stacking involves capturing multiple images at different focal distances and combining them in post-processing to achieve depth of field that exceeds what is possible in a single exposure. Through his educational content, Denney has helped photographers understand when and how to apply this method to improve image quality.
