[[concepts/context-aware-processing|Context-aware knowledge retrieval]] dynamically accesses relevant information from a [[concepts/knowledge-base|knowledge base]] based on the current query context, reducing hallucinations and improving response [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] compared to static LLM parameter reliance.

- [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] [[concepts/engram|engram]]: Solves fundamental LLM inefficiency (e.g., in [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]) through context-aware knowledge retrieval, as demonstrated in [[entities/two-minute-papers|Two Minute Papers]]' video "DeepSeek Just Fixed One Of The Biggest Problems With AI" (YouTube: [https://youtu.be/DmtoVnTkQnM](https://youtu.be/DmtoVnTkQnM)).
