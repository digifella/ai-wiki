# Europe

Europe is a continent comprising numerous countries across Western, Central, Eastern, and Northern regions. The continent has historically developed distinct regulatory frameworks for emerging technologies, with individual nations often serving as testing grounds for EU-wide policy adoption. As of 2026, Europe has begun formal approval processes for advanced autonomous driving systems, with individual member states establishing precedents that inform broader European Union regulatory direction.

## Autonomous Vehicle Regulation

European regulatory bodies have undertaken systematic evaluation of autonomous driving technologies for potential market approval. The Netherlands has played a leading role in this process, establishing approval frameworks that are expected to influence EU-wide standards. Tesla's Full Self-Driving technology underwent formal assessment under these emerging regulatory mechanisms, with approval in the Netherlands signaling a shift toward broader European acceptance of advanced autonomous systems.

The approval process reflects Europe's approach to technology regulation, which typically emphasizes safety validation and standardized testing before widespread deployment. Member states coordinate through EU institutions to develop harmonized standards, though individual nations retain authority to approve technologies within their jurisdictions. The precedent established through Netherlands approval is anticipated to facilitate similar evaluations and approvals across other European countries in subsequent years.
