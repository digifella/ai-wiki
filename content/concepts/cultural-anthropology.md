# Cultural Anthropology

2026 04 14 [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]] Prion and [[concepts/start-of-life|start of life]]

## Core Concepts
- Study of human cultures, their origins, development, and interactions.
- Focuses on cultural diversity, social practices, and symbolic systems.

## Key Areas of Study
- **Ethnography:** Fieldwork-based research on specific cultures.
- **Ethnology:** Comparative study of cultures.
- **Linguistic Anthropology:** Study of language in cultural contexts.
- **Archaeological Anthropology:** Material culture and historical contexts.

## Notable Theories
- **Cultural Relativism:** Understanding cultures within their own contexts.
- **Structuralism:** Analysis of underlying structures in cultural phenomena.
- **Symbolic Anthropology:** Interpretation of cultural symbols and meanings.
