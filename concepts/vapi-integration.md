# Vapi Integration

**Vapi** is an AI-first platform enabling the rapid development of [[concepts/tone|voice]] agents. Integrating Vapi with [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] like [[entities/hermes]] allows for fully automated phone interactions, bridging the gap between [[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM reasoning]] and real-time voice interfaces.

## Core Capabilities
- **[[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|Voice Synthesis]] & Recognition**: Low-latency TTS/STT pipelines for natural [[concepts/conversation-flow|conversation flow]].
- **[[concepts/agent-collaboration|Agent Orchestration]]**: Connects LLMs to telephony services for autonomous [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] during calls.
- **Business Automation**: Handles scheduling, support queries, and lead qualification without human intervention.
