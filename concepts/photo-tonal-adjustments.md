# Photo Tonal Adjustments

Photo tonal adjustments involve modifying the brightness, contrast, and color balance of digital photographs to enhance visual impact or correct exposure issues. These modifications target specific segments of the tonal range, including shadows, midtones, and highlights, allowing for precise control over the image's luminance and color characteristics. This process is a fundamental component of post-processing workflows in both photography and digital image editing, enabling the establishment of a consistent aesthetic across a series of images.

The Photoshop Camera Raw Filter provides a specialized interface for executing these adjustments non-destructively. By applying the filter, editors can manipulate tonal data without permanently altering the original pixel information, preserving the integrity of the source file. This tool facilitates detailed control over exposure, contrast, and color balance, serving as a central mechanism for refining the dynamic range and overall mood of digital photographs within the Adobe ecosystem.
