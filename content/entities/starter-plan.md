# Starter Plan

The Starter Plan is an entry-level subscription tier offered by Evernote as part of its [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]]. It represents one of the service's core offerings for users seeking basic note-taking and organizational features without committing to higher-cost subscription levels.

## Pricing and Context

In 2026, Evernote introduced changes to its pricing structure that prompted discussion among existing users about plan options and migration between tiers. The Starter Plan was positioned within this revised pricing framework, with various user reactions documented regarding the adjustment and its implications for service value.

## User Migration and Adoption

Following the pricing changes, the Starter Plan became a relevant reference point for users evaluating whether to maintain their subscriptions or transition between different service tiers. Guidance materials emerged to help users understand their options in relation to the new [[concepts/pricing-model|pricing model]].
