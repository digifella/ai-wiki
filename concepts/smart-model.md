# Smart Model

Smart Model refers to the integration of advanced language models into Microsoft 365 Copilot and Copilot Studio, representing an evolution in the capabilities available to enterprise users. This integration leverages improved versions of large language models to enhance the performance of AI agents operating within Microsoft's productivity ecosystem. The approach enables these systems to process information more rapidly while maintaining higher levels of accuracy and contextual understanding.

The primary focus of this integration is the deployment of GPT-5, which brings significant improvements in reasoning speed and capability to the platform. By embedding these enhanced models directly into the Copilot suite, Microsoft aims to provide enterprise users with more robust tools for complex tasks. This update allows AI agents to handle nuanced queries and multi-step workflows with greater efficiency than previous iterations.

This development marks a shift toward deeper integration of generative AI within standard productivity applications. The enhanced reasoning capabilities allow for better contextual awareness, enabling the system to interpret user intent more accurately across various Microsoft 365 services. As these models are refined and deployed, they serve as the foundational engine for next-generation AI agents designed to assist with professional and organizational tasks.
