# Wehi

Wehi is an initiative that facilitates dialogue between art and science through curated exhibitions and commissioned works. The project brings together practitioners from both disciplines to explore how artistic practice can engage with scientific concepts, processes, and discoveries. By presenting these works in gallery settings, Wehi creates spaces for audiences to encounter scientific ideas through creative interpretation.

## Purpose and Approach

The initiative operates on the premise that art and science, while traditionally viewed as separate domains, share common ground in how they investigate and communicate ideas about the world. Wehi's exhibitions and masterpieces are designed to encourage viewers to think critically about both creative and scientific methods. The project positions artistic practice as a means of deepening public engagement with scientific subjects and promoting cross-disciplinary conversation.
