# CloudYeti

CloudYeti is a YouTube channel focused on practical applications of local artificial intelligence systems. The channel produces technical content aimed at developers and technology practitioners interested in running and deploying AI models on personal hardware rather than relying exclusively on cloud-based services.

The channel covers practical aspects of local AI implementation, including tooling, frameworks, and workflows for running AI models locally.
