# Film grain

The visible [[concepts/texture|texture]] or "noise" in analog photography produced by the random distribution of light-sensitive silver halide crystals within the film emulsion.

## Characteristics
- Influences perceived [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] and [[concepts/sharpness|image sharpness]].
- A fundamental element in film-emulation workflows.
- Distinct from [[concepts/digital-image-noise|Digital noise]], though often synthetically replicated in [[concepts/smartphone-camera-apps|digital photography]].

## Digital Application & Post-Processing
- Grain is frequently added during post-processing to provide organic [[concepts/integrity|character]] to digital files.
- Often paired with [[concepts/vignette-effects|Vignette effects]] to simulate the aesthetic of vintage [[concepts/optics|optics]].
- **Techniques in [[concepts/lightroom]]:**
    - Grain and Vignette can be applied to simulate filmic qualities.
    - There are three distinct methods available for creating Vignette effects within the software.
