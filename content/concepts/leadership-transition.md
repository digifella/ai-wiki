# Leadership Transition

[[entities/apple|Apple]]'s [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] division is undergoing a strategic reorientation under its current [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] toward on-device [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. This shift reflects a deliberate move away from cloud-dependent AI architectures toward processing and [[concepts/inference|inference]] performed directly on consumer devices, including iPhones, iPads, and [[entities/mac|Mac]] computers.

## Strategic Rationale

The transition is motivated by changing [[concepts/cloud-economics|cloud economics]] and the [[concepts/cost|cost]] structures associated with server-based AI services. By shifting computational workloads to edge devices, Apple can reduce ongoing infrastructure expenses while improving user [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] through [[concepts/local-data-processing|local data processing]]. This approach aligns with Apple's existing positioning around device-level [[concepts/security|security]] and user data protection.

## Implementation Context

The [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] involves hardware [[concepts/design|design]] decisions that prioritize [[concepts/on-device-processing|on-device processing]] capacity, including specialized neural engines and accelerators integrated into Apple's [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]]. This requires coordination between hardware [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], [[concepts/software|software]] optimization, and product roadmap decisions across the company's device categories.
