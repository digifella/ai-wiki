# Alzheimer's disease

**Alzheimer's disease** is a progressive neurodegenerative disorder and the most common cause of [[concepts/dementia]]. It is characterized by the accumulation of amyloid-beta plaques and tau tangles in the brain, leading to [[concepts/cognitive-impact|cognitive decline]], [[concepts/amnesia|memory loss]], and behavioral changes.

## Epidemiology and Trends

Recent data suggests a shift in the trajectory of dementia incidence. Contrary to historical projections of rising case numbers due to aging populations, evidence indicates a potential decline in new dementia cases in certain demographics.

*   **Incidence Decline:** Recent reports highlight that dementia rates are plummeting in specific regions, attributed to improved [[concepts/cardiovascular-health|cardiovascular health]], education levels, and environmental factors [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Dementia-Plummeting-Causes-Prevention-and-Alzheimers-Tre|Dementia Plummeting: Causes, Prevention, and Alzheimer's Treatment Progress]].
*   **[[concepts/preventive-care|Prevention]] Focus:** The decline is linked to better management of risk factors such as hypertension, diabetes, and smoking, alongside advancements in [[concepts/secondary-prevention|early detection]] and treatment protocols.

## Pathophysiology

The disease involves the death of brain cells and the breakdown of neural connections. Key pathological features include:

*   **Amyloid Plaques:** Clumps of beta-amyloid protein fragments that build up between nerve cells.
*   **Neurofibrillary Tangles:** Twisted fibers of tau protein that build up inside cells.
*   **Neuroinflammation:** [[concepts/systemic-inflammation|Chronic inflammation]] plays a significant role in the progression of neuronal damage.

## Treatment and Management

While there is currently no cure, management strategies focus on slowing progression and improving [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]].

*   **Pharmacological:** Cholinesterase inhibitors (e.g., donepezil) and NMDA receptor antagonists (e.g., memantine) are standard treatments for symptom management.
*   **Emerging Therapies:** Recent progress in Alzheimer's Treatment Progress includes monoclonal antibodies targeting amyloid-beta, which have shown modest but significant benefits in slowing cognitive decline in early-stage patients.
*   **Non-Pharmacological:** Cognitive stimulation, [[concepts/exercise|physical exercise]], and social engagement are critical components of care.

## Prevention and Risk Reduction

Lifestyle modifications are strongly associated with reduced risk:

*   **Cardiovascular [[concepts/health|Health]]:** Managing blood [[concepts/pressure|pressure]], cholesterol, and blood [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] levels.
*   **[[concepts/diet|Diet]]:** Mediterranean or [[concepts/the-mind|MIND]] diets rich in antioxidants and healthy fats.
*   **Cognitive Engagement:** Lifelong [[concepts/learning|learning]] and mental stimulation help build cognitive reserve.
