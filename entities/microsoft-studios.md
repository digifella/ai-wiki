# Microsoft Studios

Microsoft Studios is the video game development and publishing division of Microsoft, responsible for creating and distributing games across the company's platforms, including Xbox consoles, personal computers, and cloud gaming services. The division serves as Microsoft's primary vehicle for first-party game development and operates a portfolio of both internally developed studios and acquired development companies that produce content for the Xbox ecosystem and other Microsoft gaming platforms.

## Development Strategy

The division has expanded its gaming portfolio through strategic acquisitions and internal growth, establishing a diverse network of studios to support its publishing goals. This approach allows Microsoft to secure exclusive titles and maintain a steady stream of first-party content for its hardware and subscription services. By integrating these studios into its broader gaming infrastructure, the division aims to enhance the value proposition of the Xbox brand and compete effectively in the global video game market.
