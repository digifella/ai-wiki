# James Webb Space Telescope

## Overview
The [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] (JWST) is the premier successor to the [[entities/nasa|NASA]] Hubble Space Telescope, designed primarily for infrared [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]]. It serves as a cornerstone for modern [[concepts/astrophysics|astrophysics]], enabling the study of the [[concepts/primordial-universe|early universe]], [[concepts/galaxy-formation|galaxy formation]], and exoplanet atmospheres.

## Key Observations & Deep Fields
JWST's high sensitivity allows for unprecedented [[concepts/deep-field|deep-field]] observations, revealing [[concepts/distant-galaxies|distant galaxies]] in seemingly empty patches of sky.

- **Fornax Deep Field Analysis**: Recent 100-hour integration observations in the [[concepts/fornax-constellation|Fornax constellation]] have unveiled numerous distant galaxies previously hidden in "empty" space. This data highlights the telescope's capability to detect faint, [[concepts/early-universe|early-universe]] structures.
- **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]**: These observations continue the [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|lineage]] of deep-field surveys initiated by the [[concepts/hubble-space-telescope|Hubble Space Telescope]], but with significantly greater depth and infrared [[concepts/solution|resolution]].
- **Detailed Breakdown**: For a specific analysis of these 100-hour observations and the galaxies revealed, see [[lab-notes/2026-09-16-James-Webbs-100-Hour-Deep-Field-Unveiling-Distant-Galaxi|James Webb's 100-Hour Deep Field: Unveiling Distant Galaxies in Fornax]].

## Related Missions & Context
While JWST dominates current infrared observations, it is part of a broader lineage of [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s Great Observatories program. Upcoming missions aim to complement JWST's capabilities, particularly in wide-field surveys and [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]] mapping.

### Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope
The [[concepts/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope]] (formerly [[concepts/falcon-heavy|WFIRST]]) is scheduled to launch in the mid-2020s. It [[entities/will|will]] focus on [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark energy]], exoplanet surveys, and infrared sky surveys, providing a wide-field counterpart to JWST's deep-field capabilities.
