# Supernova

A **supernova** is a powerful and luminous stellar explosion that occurs during the final stages of a massive star's life or in a binary system involving a white dwarf. It briefly outshines an entire galaxy, radiating energy across the electromagnetic spectrum.

## Types

- **Core-Collapse Supernova (Type II, Ib, Ic)**: Occurs when massive [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] ($M > 8 M_\odot$) exhaust their nuclear fuel, causing the iron core to collapse under [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]]. This triggers a rebound shockwave that ejects the outer layers.
- **Thermonuclear Supernova (Type Ia)**: Results from the runaway fusion of carbon and oxygen in a White Dwarf within a Binary System, typically exceeding the Chandrasekhar Limit.

## Nucleosynthesis & Chemical Enrichment

[[concepts/supernovae|Supernovae]] are primary engines for **[[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]]**, dispersing heavy elements into the [[concepts/galactic-center|Interstellar Medium (ISM)]].

- **Heavy Elements**: Responsible for creating elements heavier than iron via rapid neutron capture (**r-process**) and slow neutron capture (**s-process**).
- **[[concepts/radioactive-atoms|Radioactive Isotopes]]**: Produce short-lived radionuclides that serve as chronometers for recent cosmic events.
	- **[[concepts/iron-60|Iron-60]] (Fe-60)**: A [[concepts/radioactive-isotope|radioactive isotope]] with a half-life of ~2.6 million years. Its presence in [[entities/earth|Earth]]'s geological record indicates nearby supernova activity.
	- See: [[lab-notes/2026-06-02-Antarctic-Ice-Fe-60-Solar-Systems-Entry-into-Local-Inter|Antarctic Ice Fe-60: Solar System's Entry into Local Interstellar Cloud]] for details on Fe-60 detection in Antarctic ice cores and its implications for the Solar System's entry into the Local Interstellar Cloud.

## Remnants

- **Neutron Star**: The collapsed core of a massive star (if mass $< \sim 3 M_\odot$). Includes Pulsars.
- **[[concepts/black-holes|Black Hole]]**: Forms if the remnant core exceeds the Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limit.
- **Supernova Remnant (SNR)**: The expanding shockwave and ejected material interacting with the ISM (e.g., Crab Nebula).

## Significance

- **Galactic Evolution**: Drives chemical enrichment, enabling the formation of terrestrial planets and life.
- **Cosmic Ray Acceleration**: SNRs are leading candidates for sources of Galactic [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] via diffusive shock acceleration.
- **[[concepts/standard-candles|Standard Candles]]**: [[concepts/type-ia-supernovae|Type Ia supernovae]] are used to measure cosmic distances and were instrumental in discovering [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|Dark Energy]].
