# Night Shots

Night Shots is a photography feature in the Adobe Lightroom beta camera app, available for iPhone 13 Pro and later models. The feature enhances image capture in low-light environments by processing multiple frames through computational photography techniques. This approach yields photographs with improved clarity and detail compared to standard smartphone night photography methods.

## Technical Implementation

The feature takes advantage of the advanced camera hardware and processing capabilities built into recent iPhone models. By combining data from multiple exposures, Night Shots reduces noise and preserves detail in shadows, enabling users to capture usable images in conditions where conventional mobile photography would produce degraded results.

## Related Capabilities

The Lightroom beta camera app includes additional features alongside Night Shots, such as digital zoom functionality and tools for removing reflections from surfaces like glass and water. These complementary features expand the app's utility for various shooting scenarios and post-capture editing needs.
