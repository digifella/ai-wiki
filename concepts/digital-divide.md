# Digital Divide

The **Digital Divide** refers to the gap between those who have ready [[concepts/information-access|access to Information]] Technology and those who do not, often exacerbated by socioeconomic status, age, location, and language. In the context of [[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]], this divide creates significant disparities in [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]] and access to care.

## Key Dimensions

### 1. Linguistic and Cultural Exclusion
A critical facet of the divide is the lack of [[concepts/multi-language-support|localization]] in [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] tools.
- **Language Barriers**: Many tools are exclusively available in English, systematically excluding non-English speaking populations [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Module-3-Fleshed-Out-Interactions-V3|Module 3 Fleshed Out Interactions V3]].
- **Cultural Relevance**: Tools often fail to account for culturally and linguistically diverse (CLD) needs, reducing efficacy and [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] among minority groups.

### 2. Source Analysis
Recent assessments from [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] bodies highlight the severity of these barriers:
- **Publisher**: Ey (Consulting Company)
- **Credibility**: Peer-Reviewed (Tier 5)
- **Focus**: [[concepts/digital-readiness|Digital Readiness]] and the impact of the divide on patient engagement in [[concepts/health|health]] contexts.

## Implications
- **Access Inequality**: Non-English speakers are disproportionately excluded from [[concepts/telehealth|telehealth]] and [[concepts/digital-surveillance|digital monitoring]] tools.
- **Data Gaps**: Lack of diverse data in tool design reinforces existing [[concepts/biases|biases]] in algorithmic health [[concepts/recommendations|recommendations]].
