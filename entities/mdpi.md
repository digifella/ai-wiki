# MDPI

**MDPI** (Multidisciplinary Digital Publishing Institute) is an academic publisher based in Basel, Switzerland, that specializes in peer-reviewed, open access journals. The organization operates over 300 journals spanning natural sciences, engineering, medicine, social sciences, and humanities. All MDPI journals operate under open access models, making published research freely available to readers online.

## Publishing Operations

MDPI's journal portfolio covers a wide range of academic disciplines. The publisher employs a peer review process for submissions and charges article processing fees to authors rather than subscription fees to readers, a model characteristic of open access publishing. The organization has grown substantially since its founding and is recognized as one of the larger open access publishers by number of journals operated.

## Scope and Accessibility

By making research openly accessible, MDPI contributes to the open access movement in academic publishing. The breadth of its journal coverage allows researchers across multiple disciplines to publish and access peer-reviewed work without subscription barriers.
