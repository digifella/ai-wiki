# Focus Lock

A camera feature allowing focus to be locked on a subject before recomposing the frame, ensuring [[concepts/sharpness|sharpness]] during composition. Typically activated via half-pressing the shutter or dedicated AF-L button.

## Fujifilm Implementation
- [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] cameras utilize [[concepts/single-autofocus|Single Autofocus]] (AF-S) for static subjects, with focus lock achieved through half-press shutter or AF-L button.
- AF-C mode requires AF-L button for focus lock without recomposition (less common for static subjects).
- The Fujifilm Autofocus [[concepts/setup|Setup]] Guide: Modes, Features, and Optimization details optimization of AF-S for focus lock and recomposition [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].
