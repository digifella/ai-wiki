# Tech With Tim

Tech With [[entities/tim|Tim]] is a [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel founded by programmer Tim that focuses on practical tutorials for [[concepts/coding|software development]], [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], and modern technology tools. The channel emphasizes accessible, step-by-step instruction aimed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] both aspiring developers and experienced hobbyists seeking to deepen their technical knowledge.

## Content Focus

The channel covers a broad [[concepts/range|range]] of programming-related topics, with particular emphasis on [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and emerging technologies. [[concepts/tutorial|Tutorial]] content typically breaks down complex concepts into manageable segments, making advanced topics approachable for viewers with varying levels of experience. The channel has produced guides on implementing local language [[concepts/models|models]], [[concepts/ai-agent-setup|AI agent setup]], and [[concepts/integration|integration]] of various development tools and platforms.
