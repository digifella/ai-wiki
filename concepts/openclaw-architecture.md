# Openclaw Architecture

Openclaw Architecture is a documented framework for designing and deploying personal-scale AI assistant systems. It provides standardized workflows and integration patterns intended to enable individual users and small teams to implement customized AI agents without requiring enterprise-level infrastructure or resources. The architecture prioritizes transparency by documenting its operational components and decision pathways, making it accessible to users with varying technical expertise.

## Relationship to Enterprise Variants

Openclaw is distinguished from NVIDIA's NemoClaw, an enterprise-oriented variant designed for large-scale organizational deployment. While NemoClaw targets high-volume, production environments with corresponding resource requirements, Openclaw focuses on modularity and cost-effectiveness for independent use cases. The two architectures share foundational design principles but diverge in their infrastructure assumptions and operational complexity.

## Key Characteristics

The architecture emphasizes reproducibility through detailed documentation of system components, data flows, and configuration options. This documentation-first approach allows users to understand how their AI assistants function and to modify or extend functionality as needed. Openclaw systems are designed to operate with standard computing resources while maintaining integration points for external services and knowledge sources.
