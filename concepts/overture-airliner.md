# Overture airliner

The **Overture airliner** is a proposed supersonic passenger jet developed by [[entities/boom-supersonic|Boom Supersonic]]. The project aims to restore commercial [[concepts/supersonic-travel|supersonic travel]] through advanced aerodynamic design and next-generation propulsion systems.

## Key Developments

*   **Propulsion Strategy**: Development of the "Symphony" engine is central to the Overture's viability, focusing on efficiency and noise reduction to meet regulatory standards.
*   **Recent Analysis**: Detailed examination of the [[concepts/superpower-jet-engine|SuperPower Jet Engine]] confirms the technical feasibility of Boom's propulsion ambitions [[lab-notes/2026-09-05-Boom-Supersonic-Overture-Symphony-Engine-Development-and|Boom Supersonic Overture: Symphony Engine Development and Commercial Supersonic Flight Ambitions]].
*   **Commercial Goals**: The ultimate objective is to launch a fleet of Overture aircraft, reducing transcontinental flight times by approximately 50% while maintaining economic sustainability.
