# Proprietary Data Access

Controlled access to sensitive, company-specific data not publicly available, protected by legal/technical measures. Enables competitive advantage through unique insights without external [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].

## Strategic Shifts
- [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[entities/claude]] into Microsoft [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] (via Channel [[entities/nate-b-jones|Nate B Jones]]) enables construction of complex, multi-tab financial models in minutes rather than weeks, signaling a [[concepts/strategic-shift|strategic shift]] from "better models" to dominating **[[concepts/workflow|workflow]] integration** and **proprietary data access**.
- Represents a **[[concepts/state-of-matter-phase-transition|phase change]] in [[concepts/knowledge-work|knowledge work]]** through direct integration of LLMs into core productivity [[concepts/software|software]].
