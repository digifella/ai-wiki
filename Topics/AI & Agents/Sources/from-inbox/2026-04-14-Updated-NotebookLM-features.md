# Updated [[entities/rob-the-ai-guy|NotebookLM features]]

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sSa8-tRIwak>
Here is a summary of the video detailing **7 Business [[concepts/use-cases|Use Cases]] for [[entities/google-notebooklm|Google NotebookLM]]'s New Features**, presented by [[entities/rick-mulready|Rick Mulready]].

### **Overview**

Google has updated NotebookLM (accessible at `notebooklm.google`) with powerful new features including detailed infographics, [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]], [[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|deep research]] capabilities, and customizable video overviews. These tools are designed to streamline operations, save time, and increase profitability for online businesses.

* * *

### **1\. [[concepts/client-team-education|Client & Team Education]] Infographics**

* **The Goal:** Simplify complex information for clients or team members.
* **The Process:**
	* Upload your [[concepts/notes|notes]] or documents (e.g., a guide on [[concepts/generative-engine-optimization|Generative Engine Optimization]]) into a notebook.
	* Select the **[[concepts/infographic|Infographic]]** feature.
	* **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]:** Use the "pencil" icon to customize the prompt. You can specify the layout, the number of key points, and crucially, input your specific **brand hex codes** to ensure the output matches your branding.
* **The Result:** A professional, spell-checked infographic suitable for lead magnets or training materials.

### **2\. Market Deep Research + Visuals**

* **The Goal:** Conduct comprehensive market research and visualize the data without leaving the platform.
* **The Process:**
	* Use the **Deep Research** feature directly within NotebookLM (no longer requires a separate [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] window).
	* Enter a prompt (e.g., "Market research on AI education for entrepreneurs").
	* Once the report is generated, import it and its sources into the notebook.
	* Generate an infographic based specifically on the new research findings.
* **The Result:** A visual summary of deep market trends and data points, created in minutes.

### **3\. [[concepts/fast-research|Fast Research]]: Compare Models**

* **The Goal:** Quickly compare business strategies or models to aid [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
* **The Process:**
	* Use the **Fast Research** (web search) feature to find information (e.g., "Best business models for AI educators").
	* Import the top search results as sources.
	* Prompt the Infographic tool to create a comparison chart (e.g., "Launch vs. Evergreen vs. Partnership").
* **The Result:** A clear, side-by-side visual comparison of different methodologies or pricing strategies.

### **4\. Enhance Posts with Infographics**

* **The Goal:** Repurpose text-based social media posts into high-value visual content.
* **The Process:**
	* Copy the text of a recent post or announcement (e.g., a [[concepts/software|software]] update summary).
	* Paste it into NotebookLM as a source.
	* Generate a summary infographic using your brand colors.
* **The Result:** Provides your audience with a visual "TL;DR" of your content, increasing engagement and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].

### **5\. Instant Slide Decks**

* **The Goal:** Create presentation slides based on your research sources.
* **The Process:**
	* Select the **Slide Deck** feature.
	* Customize settings (Detailed Deck vs. Presenter Slides, length, and style).
* **The Limitation:** NotebookLM currently exports slides as a **PDF**, not an editable presentation file.
* **The Workaround:** Upload the generated PDF into **Gemini 1.5 Pro** and prompt it to "Convert these PDF slides into a PowerPoint file."

### **6\. Interactive Strategy Board**

* **The Goal:** Create an interactive HTML tool based on your research.
* **The Process:**
	1. Perform research in NotebookLM.
	2. Generate a **Mind Map** of the research and download the image.
	3. Go to **[[entities/google-ai-studio|Google AI Studio]]** (using the Gemini 1.5 Pro model).
	4. Upload the Mind Map image.
	5. **The Prompt:** Ask Gemini to [[concepts/code|code]] an "Interactive Strategy Board" in HTML/CSS based on the image (e.g., a table where hovering over ROI numbers shows the calculation).
* **The Result:** A fully functional, interactive web tool that can be embedded for internal team use or client demonstrations.

### **7\. Custom Explainer Videos**

* **The Goal:** Turn text sources into engaging animated video content.
* **The Process:**
	* Select the **Video Overview** feature.
	* **Customization:** Choose a visual style (e.g., Anime, Watercolor, Claymation) and write a prompt to focus the script on a specific angle (e.g., "Focus on the importance for online entrepreneurs").
* **The Result:** An animated video with audio narration explaining the topic. _Note: This process takes longer to generate (approx. 20 minutes)._
