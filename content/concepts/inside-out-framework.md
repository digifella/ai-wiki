# Inside Out Framework

The Inside Out Framework is a strategic methodology for integrating [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] into business operations by beginning with comprehensive [[concepts/problem-identification|process mapping]]. Rather than implementing [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] broadly across an [[concepts/organization|organization]], this approach starts by documenting and analyzing existing workflows to identify where AI can add genuine value. The framework emphasizes understanding internal processes before selecting specific AI solutions, creating a foundation for more targeted and effective [[concepts/adoption|technology adoption]].

## Process Mapping as Foundation

The core principle of the Inside Out Framework involves mapping current business processes in detail before introducing AI interventions. This internal analysis phase reveals bottlenecks, redundancies, and inefficiencies that AI might address. By starting from inside the organization and working outward, decision-makers gain clarity on which processes are candidates for [[concepts/automation|automation]], augmentation, or redesign, rather than pursuing AI implementation for its own sake.

## Implementation Considerations

Practitioners of this framework recognize that AI tools vary significantly in cost, complexity, and suitability for different tasks. The approach acknowledges practical constraints, including budget limitations and the varying affordability of different AI solutions. By connecting specific business processes to appropriate technology options through systematic mapping, organizations can make more informed choices about which AI implementations will deliver measurable returns relative to their investment.
