# Fornax constellation

**Fornax** is a southern constellation located in the second celestial hemisphere. It is known for hosting the Fornax Cluster, a significant group of galaxies, and for its association with [[concepts/deep-field|deep-field]] observations that reveal distant cosmic structures.

## Key Observations & Research

*   **James [[entities/jwst|Webb]] Deep Field**: Recent analysis of a 100-hour observation of a seemingly "empty" patch of sky in Fornax has unveiled numerous [[concepts/distant-galaxies|distant galaxies]], highlighting the region's value for [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep-space]] study [[lab-notes/2026-09-16-James-Webbs-100-Hour-Deep-Field-Unveiling-Distant-Galaxi|James Webb's 100-Hour Deep Field: Unveiling Distant Galaxies in Fornax]].
*   **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]**: This deep-field work continues the legacy of the [[concepts/hubble-space-telescope]], which previously mapped similar "empty" regions to reveal hidden cosmic structures.
*   **Galactic [[concepts/density|Density]]**: Despite appearing empty to the naked eye, the Fornax field contains high densities of distant galaxies, making it a critical target for understanding [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] formation.
