# Marketing Campaigns

A marketing campaign is a coordinated series of steps designed to promote a product, service, or idea to a target audience. Campaigns can span multiple channels and often involve various tactics such as advertising, content marketing, [[entities/email|email]] marketing, and social media engagement.

## Key Components
- **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]**: Clear, measurable goals (e.g., brand [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]], lead generation, sales).
- **Target Audience**: Defined demographics, psychographics, and behaviors.
- **Channels**: Platforms used to reach the audience (e.g., digital, print, TV).
- **Content**: [[concepts/communication|Messaging]], creative assets, and media used to communicate the campaign.
- **Metrics**: KPIs to track [[concepts/success|success]] (e.g., engagement, conversion rates, ROI).

## Types of Campaigns
- **Digital Campaigns**: Online advertising, SEO, social media, email.
- **Traditional Campaigns**: Print, TV, radio, billboards.
- **Integrated Campaigns**: Combining multiple channels for a cohesive strategy.
