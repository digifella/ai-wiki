# Beskar

**Beskar** (also known as Mandalorian iron) is a fictional metal in the *[[entities/star-wars|Star Wars]]* universe, renowned for its extreme durability and resistance to Lightsaber blades. It is central to Mandalorian culture, used for armor, weapons, and ceremonial objects.

## Properties
- **Durability**: Virtually indestructible by conventional means; immune to Lightsaber cuts.
- **Malleability**: Can be forged into intricate designs when heated, though it requires specialized techniques.
- **Conductivity**: Poor conductor of electricity and heat compared to standard [[concepts/metal|metals]].

## Cultural Significance
- **Armor**: The primary component of Beskar Armor, providing [[concepts/secure|protection]] against blaster fire and melee attacks.
- **Religious/Spiritual**: Considered sacred by many Mandalorians; often used in rituals and as a symbol of identity.
- **[[concepts/limited-resources|Scarcity]]**: Rare and highly valued; mining rights are fiercely contested.
