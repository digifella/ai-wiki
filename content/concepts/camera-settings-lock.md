# Camera Settings Lock

Camera Settings Lock is a feature found on [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] that allows photographers to lock specific camera controls to prevent accidental adjustment during shooting. This functionality is particularly useful in fast-paced shooting situations where camera settings might be inadvertently changed through [[entities/contact|contact]] with [[concepts/buttons|buttons]], dials, or menu controls. By locking desired settings, photographers can maintain consistent [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], focus, and other [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] without interruption.

## Scope and Implementation

Fujifilm's implementation of this feature covers a comprehensive range of controls—reportedly up to 53 different camera functions across their camera lineup. This extensive locking capability means photographers can selectively protect critical settings while leaving other controls accessible. The feature typically integrates into the camera's menu system, allowing users to customize which controls they wish to lock based on their specific shooting needs and workflow.

## Practical Applications

The [[concepts/autofocus-mode|lock feature]] is most valuable during situations where accidental adjustment could compromise image [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], such as during continuous shooting sequences, video recording, or when the camera is being handled frequently. Rather than locking all controls globally, the selective locking approach enables photographers to balance protection of important settings with the flexibility to adjust other parameters in real-time.
