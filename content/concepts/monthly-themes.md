# Monthly Themes

Monthly Themes is a structured approach to [[concepts/personal-development|personal development]] introduced by [[concepts/happiness|happiness]] researcher [[entities/gretchen-rubin|Gretchen Rubin]] as part of her Happiness Project framework. The method involves selecting a specific area of life or [[concepts/health|wellbeing]] to focus on each month, then identifying and practicing concrete [[concepts/daily-routines|daily habits]] that support improvement in that domain. Rather than attempting broad life changes all [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] once, this incremental approach distributes behavioral goals across the year, making them more manageable and sustainable.

## Implementation

The process typically involves choosing a theme relevant to one's values or problem areas—such as [[concepts/sleep|sleep]], [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], or [[concepts/organization|organization]]—and then determining which daily habits would meaningfully contribute to that theme. Practitioners track their adherence to these [[concepts/habits|habits]] throughout the month, building [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] before moving to a new focus area. This cyclical [[concepts/structure|structure]] provides natural checkpoints for reflection and adjustment.

## Underlying Principles

The concept works on the assumption that sustained [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] improves through deliberate practice of specific behaviors rather than reliance on willpower alone. By concentrating [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] on one area monthly, individuals can develop deeper awareness of their patterns and [[entities/make|make]] meaningful progress without the overwhelm of simultaneous major changes. The method has been adopted by many people seeking to systematically address multiple aspects of their health and happiness over time.
