# Liquid Addition in Cooking: Techniques for Perfect Scrambled Eggs

This page explores the technique of liquid addition in cooking, focusing on scrambled eggs as a practical example. Understanding how to manage liquids is crucial for achieving desired textures and flavors.

### Key Concepts
- **Salting:** Proper salting enhances flavor without overcooking.
- **Liquid Addition:** Using milk or cream improves moisture content and creates tender results.
- **Equipment Selection:** Choosing the right cookware can make a significant difference in [[concepts/texture|texture]] quality.
