# Incremental Progress

**Incremental Progress** is the strategy of achieving significant goals through small, consistent, and manageable steps rather than abrupt, large-scale transformations. It emphasizes sustainability, [[concepts/psychological-safety|psychological safety]], and the accumulation of marginal gains over time.

## Core Principles

*   **Sustainability:** Small changes are less likely to trigger burnout or resistance than radical shifts.
*   **[[concepts/systems|Feedback Loops]]:** Iterative steps allow for continuous correction and refinement based on immediate results.
*   **Compound Effect:** Minor improvements accumulate into substantial outcomes over extended periods.
*   **[[concepts/vulnerability|Psychological Safety]]:** Reduces the fear of failure associated with high-stakes, all-or-nothing attempts.

## Applications and Examples

### Technology and AI Adoption
In high-stakes environments like [[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]], resistance to new technologies often stems from fear of obsolescence or loss of control. Incremental [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] mitigates this by:
*   Introducing tools alongside existing workflows rather than replacing them immediately.
*   Allowing practitioners to build [[concepts/trust|trust]] and competence gradually.
*   Addressing specific, low-risk pain points first to demonstrate value.
