# Dark Sourcery

**Dark Sourcery** refers to the adversarial application of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to create sophisticated, scalable, and [[concepts/internet-crime-trends|automated cyber threats]]. It encompasses the use of [[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) for [[concepts/llm-hacking]], the orchestration of [[concepts/agent-collaboration|Agent Swarms]] for coordinated attacks, and the exploitation of AI-driven vulnerabilities in [[concepts/security-intelligence]] frameworks.

## Key Threat Vectors

*   **AI-Enhanced Campaigns:** Attackers [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] to generate more sophisticated phishing, malware, and social [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] campaigns, moving beyond static scripts to dynamic, context-aware threats.
*   **LLM Exploitation:** Direct manipulation of [[concepts/llm-hacking]] vectors, including prompt injection and data poisoning, to bypass safety [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]] and extract sensitive information.
*   **Automated Orchestration:** Use of Agent Swarms to coordinate multi-stage attacks, allowing for rapid reconnaissance, exploitation, and lateral [[concepts/exercise|movement]] with minimal human intervention.
*   **[[concepts/trust|Trust]] Erosion:** The core concern is the degradation of trust in digital interactions, as [[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-generated content]] becomes indistinguishable from legitimate communications.
