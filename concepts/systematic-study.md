# Systematic Study

A rigorous, protocol-driven approach to identifying, evaluating, and synthesizing all relevant research evidence regarding a specific Research Question or phenomenon. Unlike [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] reviews, systematic studies minimize bias through explicit, reproducible methods for literature search, selection, [[concepts/data-extraction|data extraction]], and [[concepts/quality-assessment|quality assessment]].

## Core Principles

- **[[concepts/opacity|Transparency]]**: Every step of the process is documented to allow replication.
- **Comprehensiveness**: Exhaustive search strategies across multiple databases to minimize selection bias.
- **Critical Appraisal**: Standardized tools are used to assess the quality and risk of bias in included studies.
- **Synthesis**: Data is aggregated qualitatively or quantitatively (e.g., Meta-Analysis) to draw robust conclusions.

## Methodological Frameworks

- **PRISMA**: Preferred Reporting Items for Systematic Reviews and Meta-Analyses; a checklist and [[concepts/flow|flow]] diagram for reporting systematic reviews.
- **Cochrane Handbook**: [[concepts/major-gold-deposit|Gold]] standard guidelines for conducting systematic reviews in [[concepts/health|healthcare]].
