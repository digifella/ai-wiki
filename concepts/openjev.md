# OpenJev

**OpenJev** is an [[concepts/open-source-framework|open-source framework]] and ecosystem centered around **Jev**, a specialized [[concepts/decision-model|decision model]] designed to optimize the efficiency and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of AI agents within iterative "[[concepts/agent-loops|agent loops]]." It addresses the limitations of traditional agent architectures by introducing structured [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes.

## Core Concepts

*   **Jev**: A specialized decision model that enhances [[concepts/ai-agent-performance|AI agent performance]] by providing structured [[concepts/decision-making-frameworks|decision frameworks]].
*   **Agent Loops**: The iterative cycles in which AI agents operate; OpenJev aims to optimize the efficiency and reliability of these loops.
*   **[[concepts/structured-decision-models|Structured Decision Models]]**: The underlying methodology used by Jev to reduce ambiguity and improve agent outcomes compared to traditional unstructured approaches.
