# NFSA Film Australia

**National Film and Sound Archive (NFSA)** is the Australian government agency responsible for collecting, preserving, and sharing the nation's audiovisual heritage. It serves as a primary repository for Film Australia productions and other significant Australian media.

## Key Holdings & Productions

### Kiewa Hydroelectric Scheme Documentation
The NFSA holds significant footage related to the [[concepts/kiewa-hydroelectric-scheme]], particularly the 1959 film *[[concepts/below-the-high-plain|Below the High Plain]]*. This work highlights the engineering feats in the Victorian Alps and the relationship between [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and the landscape around [[concepts/mount-beauty]].
