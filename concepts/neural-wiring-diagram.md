# Neural Wiring Diagram

A **neural wiring diagram** (or [[concepts/connectome|connectome]]) is a comprehensive map of the neural connections in a brain or nervous system. It serves as the structural foundation for understanding neural circuitry, information processing, and behavior in biological systems.

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]]**: In September 2026, [[entities/google|Google]] and [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus open-sourced the complete connectome of a male fruit fly (*Drosophila melanogaster*), including its brain and nerve cord Google's Open-Sourced [[concepts/fruit-fly-brain|Fruit Fly Brain]] Connectome: Structure and Implications.
*   **Open Science Impact**: This release provides a full "wiring diagram" of the fly nervous system, enabling broader research into neural [[concepts/computation|computation]] and synaptic connectivity.
*   **Structural Implications**: The dataset allows for detailed analysis of how specific neural pathways contribute to sensory processing and motor control in insects.
