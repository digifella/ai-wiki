# Compact Stellar Objects

Remnant cores of massive [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] following nuclear fuel exhaustion, characterized by extreme density and support via degeneracy pressure or [[concepts/gravitational-collapse|gravitational collapse]]. Classification governed by progenitor mass and equation of state.

## Classification
- White Dwarf: Electron degeneracy pressure; Chandrasekhar limit ~1.4 M☉; endpoints of low-to-intermediate mass stars.
- Neutron Star: Neutron degeneracy pressure; Tolman-Oppenheimer-Volkoff limit; subtypes include Pulsar and Magnetar.
- Stellar [[concepts/black-holes|Black Hole]]: Gravitational collapse exceeding TOV limit; [[concepts/event-horizon|event horizon]]; no quantum pressure resistance.
- Exotic candidates: Quark stars, boson stars, gravastars (theoretical).

## Formation & Evolution
- Endpoints of [[concepts/stellar-evolution]] determined by initial mass, metallicity, and rotation.
- Supernova mechanisms: Core-collapse (Type II/Ib/Ic), Thermonuclear (Type [[concepts/information-architecture|Ia]] via White Dwarf accretion/merger).
- Binary dynamics: Accretion-induced collapse, common envelope evolution, compact binary mergers (NS-NS Merger, NS-BH Merger).

## Galactic Center & Sgr A* Phenomena
- [[concepts/sagittarius-a]] hosts dense stellar cusp with high fraction of compact remnants.
- **G-Objects**: Luminous, dusty entities with stellar orbits near Sgr A*; morphology suggests extended envelopes.
- Origin resolved via [[entities/irs-16sw|IRS 16SW]] cluster analysis:
- G-Objects originate from tidal interactions and mergers involving IRS 16SW [[concepts/binary-stars|binary stars]].
- Binary dynamics within IRS 16SW produce the observed G-Object population characteristics.
