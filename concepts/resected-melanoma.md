# Resected Melanoma

**Resected melanoma** refers to melanoma that has been surgically removed, often with the goal of preventing recurrence through [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]].

## Adjuvant Therapy Landscape

Standard of care for high-risk resected melanoma has evolved to include immune checkpoint inhibitors and targeted therapies. Recent developments include the integration of personalized mRNA vaccines.

### Moderna Merck mRNA Vaccine + Keytruda

Significant progress has been made in combining personalized mRNA vaccines with existing immunotherapies for adjuvant treatment [[lab-notes/2026-08-29-Moderna-Merck-mRNA-Cancer-Vaccine-and-Keytruda-for-Resec|Moderna Merck mRNA Cancer Vaccine and Keytruda for Resected Melanoma Adjuvant Therapy]].

*   **Trial Results:** [[entities/moderna|Moderna]] and [[entities/merck|Merck]] announced positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results for the personalized [[concepts/mrna-cancer-vaccine|mRNA cancer vaccine]] **[[entities/intismeran-autogene|Intismeran Autogene]]**.
*   **Combination Therapy:** The vaccine is evaluated in combination with **Keytruda** ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]) for patients with resected melanoma.
*   **Significance:** This represents a major development in personalized cancer treatment, aiming to reduce recurrence rates in high-risk populations.
