# Core Ai Workflows

Core AI workflows refer to standardized processes and patterns for integrating [[concepts/ai-coding-workflows|AI coding tools]] and [[concepts/agents|agents]] into development and business operations. These workflows represent practical methodologies for leveraging [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to automate routine tasks, augment [[concepts/developer-productivity|developer productivity]], and streamline decision-making processes within organizations.

## Productivity Applications

Patrick Ellis, a CTO and startup co-founder, has documented approaches to achieving significant productivity gains through systematic use of AI coding tools. The core concept involves identifying repetitive or cognitively intensive tasks that can be delegated to [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]], allowing human expertise to focus on higher-level [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] and strategic decisions. This approach applies across both [[concepts/coding|software development]] contexts and general business operations for small to [[entities/medium|medium]] enterprises.

## Tool Integration and Capability

Modern [[concepts/mcps|AI coding agents]] possess sufficient capability to handle code generation, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and architectural suggestions when properly guided. Effective workflows typically involve establishing clear boundaries for agent responsibilities, defining input specifications, and implementing review processes to maintain code quality and security. The workflow patterns adapt based on domain requirements, from straightforward coding tasks to complex multi-step business processes requiring [[concepts/multi-agent-orchestration|agent coordination]].
