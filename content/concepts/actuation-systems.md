# Actuation Systems

Mechanisms converting energy into controlled motion for robotic degrees of freedom. Critical [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]: torque density, power-to-weight ratio, bandwidth, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and efficiency.

## Technologies
- **Electric Motors:** Brushless DC, frameless motors; standard for precision and agility.
- **Hydraulics:** High force density; used in heavy lifting and mobile [[concepts/number-systems|bases]].
- **Pneumatics:** Soft [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], compliant interaction.
- **Quasi-Direct [[concepts/ambition|Drive]]:** High-ratio gearboxes minimizing stiffness for human-safe torque [[concepts/power|control]].
