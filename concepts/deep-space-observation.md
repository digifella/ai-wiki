# Deep Space Observation

The practice of detecting and analyzing [[concepts/light|electromagnetic radiation]] from celestial objects located far beyond the solar system. This field relies heavily on advanced instrumentation to overcome atmospheric interference and distance-related [[concepts/memory-dilution|signal degradation]].

## Key Missions and Instruments

### Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope
The [[concepts/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope]] represents a significant advancement in wide-field infrared [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]], designed to complement the capabilities of the [[concepts/james-webb-space-telescope]] and [[concepts/hubble-space-telescope]].

- **Mission Status**: Following its simulated launch, the telescope has reported initial successes in instrument calibration and data transmission.
- **Projected Lifespan**: The mission is projected to operate for approximately 22 years, providing a long-term dataset for cosmological studies.
- **Scientific Goals**: Primary focus includes characterizing [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark energy]], conducting a wide-field [[concepts/survey|survey]] of the sky, and detecting exoplanets via microlensing.
- **Recent [[concepts/software-updates|Updates]]**: Initial test images have confirmed the high [[concepts/solution|resolution]] and wide field of view capabilities essential for its survey objectives.
- **Source Documentation**: [[lab-notes/2026-09-18-Roman-Space-Telescope-Initial-Successes-and-Projected-22|Roman Space Telescope: Initial Successes and Projected 22-Year Lifespan]]

## Methodologies

- **[[concepts/photometry|Photometry]]**: Measuring the intensity of light to determine distance and [[concepts/writing|composition]].
- **Spectroscopy**: Analyzing light spectra to identify chemical elements and physical conditions.
- **Interferometry**: Combining signals from multiple telescopes to achieve high angular resolution.
