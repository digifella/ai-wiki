### Summary
The video discusses the emergence of open-source language models developed by unexpected companies, specifically KRAFTON, a South Korean gaming company known for *PUBG: BATTLEGROUNDS*. KRAFTON AI has released open models such as *Raon-1 nano* and *Raon-1 mini*, designed to support on-device AI applications, NPC behaviors, and player interactions within games like *inZOI* and *PUBG Ally*.

The speaker explores KRAFTON’s transition from a mixture-of-experts (MoE) architecture to dense models through specialized pruning and distillation techniques, which were accepted as an oral presentation at NeurIPS 2026. Key benefits of these small, local models include reduced reliance on cloud infrastructure, low latency, and efficient GPU usage, making them suitable for character dialogue and decision-making on standard hardware.

Ultimately, the video suggests that gaming companies are creating specialized models tailored for interactive agents rather than general knowledge tasks, aligning with the concept of a "cognitive core" optimized for specific environments.
