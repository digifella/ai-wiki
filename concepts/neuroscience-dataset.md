# Neuroscience Dataset

A structured collection of biological data related to the nervous system, used for computational modeling, analysis, and machine learning applications.

## Key Characteristics
- **Scale**: Ranges from single-cell transcriptomics to whole-brain connectomics.
- **Format**: Often includes graph structures (nodes/edges), imaging stacks, or tabular metadata.
- **Accessibility**: Increasing trend toward open-source datasets to facilitate reproducibility and collaborative research.

## Notable Examples

### MaleCNS v1.0
- **Description**: The complete [[concepts/connectome|connectome]] of a male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord, representing a full "wiring diagram."
- **Source**: Jointly released by [[entities/google]] and [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus.
- **Significance**: Provides a high-resolution structural map for studying neural circuitry and behavior in *Drosophila melanogaster*.
- **Related Note**: [[lab-notes/2026-09-13-Googles-Open-Sourced-Fruit-Fly-Brain-Connectome-Structur|Google's Open-Sourced Fruit Fly Brain Connectome: Structure and Implications]]

## Applications
- **Circuit Mapping**: Tracing [[concepts/synaptic-connections|synaptic connections]] to understand information flow.
- **Computational Modeling**: Simulating neural activity based on structural constraints.
- **Comparative Neuroscience**: Aligning datasets across species to identify conserved neural motifs.
