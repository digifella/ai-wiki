# Sonar

Sonar is a feature of [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity AI]], an AI-powered search and research platform. The tool is designed to enhance the platform's capabilities for [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] and synthesis, integrating real-time data with AI analysis to provide users with comprehensive answers to queries.

## Capabilities and Use Cases

According to power user [[entities/eliot-prince|Eliot Prince]], Sonar has been highlighted as capable of consolidating functionality that would otherwise require multiple paid subscription services. The feature appears to focus on synthesizing information from various sources and presenting it in a unified manner, though specific technical details about its underlying mechanisms remain limited in available documentation.

Sonar represents part of Perplexity AI's broader approach to offering integrated research and information discovery tools within a single platform, positioning itself as a potential alternative to maintaining numerous separate subscriptions for research, analysis, and reference services.
