# Company Nordvpn

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012 and headquartered in Panama. The company operates a global network of servers distributed across numerous countries, enabling users to encrypt their internet traffic and obscure their IP addresses. NordVPN provides services to both individual consumers and organizations seeking privacy and security for their online activities.

## Core Operations

The company's primary function is routing user internet connections through encrypted VPN tunnels, allowing customers to browse the web with their actual location hidden. This infrastructure supports secure access to restricted content and protects data from interception. The service is designed to maintain user anonymity while providing reliable connectivity across various devices and platforms.
