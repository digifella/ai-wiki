# KIC 8462852

**KIC 8462852**, commonly known as **[[concepts/tabbys-star|Tabby's Star]]**, is a main-sequence star located in the constellation Cygnus. It gained significant [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] due to its anomalous [[concepts/light|light]] curve, characterized by irregular and substantial dips in brightness that do not align with standard planetary transit models.

## Key Characteristics
- **Location:** Constellation Cygnus
- **[[concepts/anomaly|Anomaly]]:** Irregular, deep dimming events (up to 22% flux drop)
- **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]:** Subject of intense speculation regarding artificial [[concepts/megastructures|megastructures]] (Dyson spheres) due to the non-periodic and asymmetric nature of its dimming.
