# Cryptography

Cryptography is the practice and study of techniques for [[concepts/secure|secure]] communication in the presence of third parties called adversaries. It involves creating written or generated codes that allow information to be kept secret. Cryptography has ancient origins but has become a cornerstone of modern digital [[concepts/security|security]].

## Key Concepts
- **Encryption:** The process of [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] messages or information in such a way that only authorized parties can access it.
- **Decryption:** The reverse process of transforming encrypted data back into its original form.
- **Ciphers and [[concepts/algorithms|Algorithms]]:** Specific methods used for encryption and decryption, often relying on keys for security.

- [[concepts/cybersecurity|Internet Security]]: SSL/TLS protocols protect internet communications using [[concepts/encryption-methods|cryptographic techniques]].
- Blockchain Technology: Cryptographic hash functions ensure [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] and immutability.
