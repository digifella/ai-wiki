# Starter Plan

[[entities/evernote|Evernote]]'s [[entities/starter-plan|Starter Plan]] is the entry-level tier in the company's restructured [[concepts/pricing-model|pricing model]] introduced in 2026. As part of a shift toward tiered subscription offerings, the plan targets users with basic note-taking needs and limited feature requirements. The Starter Plan sits below mid-tier and premium options, allowing the company to serve a broader range of customers across different usage patterns and budgets.

## Pricing Changes and User Response

The introduction of the Starter Plan accompanied significant changes to Evernote's overall [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]], including increases to higher-tier plans. These [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] generated mixed reactions from the user base. Some users appreciated the availability of a lower-cost entry option, while others expressed concern about price increases affecting existing subscribers on mid-tier and premium plans. The restructuring reflected broader industry trends toward value-based pricing segmentation.

## Market Position

By offering a [[concepts/hierarchical-system|tiered structure]] anchored by an affordable Starter Plan, Evernote positioned itself to compete across multiple market segments, from individual casual users to [[concepts/power-users|power users]] requiring advanced collaboration and [[entities/storage|storage]] features. The strategy aimed to balance [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] with revenue growth, though [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] rates and customer [[concepts/storing|retention]] remained subjects of ongoing discussion within the user community.
