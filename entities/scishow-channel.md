# SciShow Channel

SciShow is an educational [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel dedicated to presenting [[concepts/science|science]] content to a general audience. The channel produces videos covering a wide range of scientific topics, combining clear explanations with engaging presentation styles to make complex subjects accessible to viewers of all backgrounds.

## Content and Coverage

The channel's content spans multiple areas of scientific research and discovery. Notable episodes have included coverage of gene editing technologies and features on prominent scientists such as Dr. [[entities/feng-zhang|Feng Zhang]] at the [[entities/broad-institute|Broad Institute]]. By presenting developments in contemporary science alongside foundational scientific concepts, SciShow aims to foster [[concepts/understanding-science|scientific literacy]] and curiosity among its viewers.
