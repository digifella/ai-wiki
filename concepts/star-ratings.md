# Star Ratings

Star ratings in Lightroom are a fundamental organizational tool for managing and prioritizing photos within your catalog. They provide a quick, visual method to mark images with quality assessments, ranging from one to five stars, allowing photographers to efficiently sort and filter their work during the culling and editing process. This system enables photographers to make consistent judgments about image quality without manually organizing files into folders or creating duplicate copies.

## Using Star Ratings Effectively

The star rating system works best when applied consistently throughout your workflow. Rather than assigning ratings arbitrarily, establish a personal rating standard—for example, five stars for keeper images ready for delivery, four stars for strong alternates, three stars for usable images with minor issues, and lower ratings for images to be discarded. Applying ratings as you review images helps you quickly identify your best work and creates a sortable, filterable subset of your catalog that can be edited together or exported as a collection.

## Integration with Catalog Management

Star ratings integrate seamlessly with Lightroom's filtering and searching capabilities, allowing you to view only images within specific rating ranges. This capability becomes particularly valuable when managing large shoots or maintaining a growing catalog, as you can instantly isolate your top-rated images for further refinement or export. Star ratings also sync across devices when using Lightroom's cloud features, ensuring your organizational structure remains consistent whether you're working on desktop or mobile platforms.
