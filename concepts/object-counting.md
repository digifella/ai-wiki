Object counting refers to the process of accurately identifying and quantifying objects within visual data, a critical task in [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]]. The challenge lies in developing robust [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] that can handle variations in object [[concepts/presence|appearance]], scale, occlusion, and context. Recent advancements focus on integrating [[concepts/vision-language-models|Vision Language Models]] ([[concepts/llms|VLMs]]) with image segmentation techniques to enhance [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].

- **Key Challenges:**
  - Overcoming limitations of standalone VLMs in precise counting tasks
  - Integrating segmentation models for better [[concepts/spatial-understanding|spatial understanding]]
