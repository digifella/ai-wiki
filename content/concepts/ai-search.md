# AI Search

A paradigm shift in [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]], transitioning from traditional keyword-based indexing to generative synthesis powered by [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]].

## Key Developments
- **[[concepts/generative-engine-optimization]] ([[concepts/geo|GEO]]):** The emerging framework for adapting web content to ensure visibility, citation, and [[concepts/power|authority]] within [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] outputs, serving as the successor to traditional Search Engine [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]] (SEO).
- **Obsolescence of [[concepts/seo|Traditional SEO]]:** The rise of generative-first platforms is rapidly making legacy SEO methodologies obsolete.
- **Primary Platforms:** The ecosystem is currently driven by engines such as [[entities/chatgpt]], [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], [[entities/gemini]], and [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/ai-overviews|AI Overviews]].
