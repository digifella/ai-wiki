# Drug Discovery

**Drug discovery** is the process by which new potential medications are identified, involving the identification of Biomarkers, drug targets, and the screening of chemical compounds or Biologics for their therapeutic effects. The pipeline typically progresses from target identification to lead optimization, preclinical testing, and clinical trials.

## Key Processes
- **Target Identification:** Using Genomics and Proteomics to find biological targets associated with disease.
- **Hit Identification:** High-throughput screening (HTS) or virtual screening to find initial active compounds.
- **Lead Optimization:** Modifying chemical structures to improve efficacy, safety, and pharmacokinetics.
- **Preclinical Development:** In vitro and in vivo testing for toxicity and efficacy.
