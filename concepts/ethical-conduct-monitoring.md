# Ethical Conduct Monitoring

Ethical Conduct Monitoring refers to the systematic oversight of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems to ensure they operate reliably and in accordance with established ethical standards. This practice involves deploying [[concepts/ai-specialization|specialized AI]] agents or monitoring frameworks that observe, evaluate, and report on the behavior of primary [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] during operation. The goal is to detect deviations from intended behavior, identify potential harms, and maintain [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] in AI deployments.

[[concepts/monitoring-systems|Monitoring systems]] typically track whether AI outputs align with predefined safety guidelines and [[concepts/internal-instructions|operational constraints]]. By utilizing tools such as [[concepts/ai-oversight|Anthropic Observer Agents]], these systems provide real-time analysis of model interactions, allowing for the identification of drift or unintended consequences before they escalate. This continuous evaluation helps organizations maintain [[concepts/trust|trust]] in their [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] by ensuring that automated decisions remain transparent and auditable.

The implementation of such monitoring frameworks is critical for mitigating risks associated with complex AI behaviors. It establishes a layer of [[concepts/verification|verification]] that complements initial training and alignment processes, addressing the dynamic nature of model outputs. Through structured reporting and [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]] detection, ethical conduct monitoring supports regulatory [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] and internal [[concepts/governance|governance]], ensuring that AI systems continue to function within their designed ethical boundaries throughout their lifecycle.
