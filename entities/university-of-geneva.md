# University of Geneva

The **University of Geneva** (*Université de Genève*) is a public research university in Geneva, Switzerland. It is a leading institution in [[concepts/health|health]] sciences, international relations, and humanities.

## Research & Publications

*   **[[concepts/health-information-exchange|Health Information Exchange]]**: [[concepts/collaborative-research|Collaborative research]] on the status of health information exchange across countries, involving institutions such as the University of Washington. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Payne---Status-of-health-information-exchange|Payne - Status of health information exchange]] for details on the 2019 publication by Thomas H. Payne et al., published in *JMIR* (DOI: 10.1136/jamia.2009.000570).
*   Key authors associated with this work include Thomas H. Payne, [[entities/charles-gutteridge|Charles Gutteridge]], [[entities/claudia-pagliari|Claudia Pagliari]], and [[entities/cui-yong|Cui Yong]].
*   Focus areas include [[concepts/clinical-care|clinical care]] transfer, international health data standards, and cross-border [[concepts/knowledge-management|information systems]].
