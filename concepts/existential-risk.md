# Existential Risk

**Existential Risk** refers to hypothetical future events that could cause human extinction or the permanent and drastic destruction of human potential.

## Key Drivers

*   **[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]**: Rapid advancements in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] capabilities pose significant concerns regarding alignment and control.
*   **[[concepts/ai-recursive-self-improvement|Recursive Self-Improvement]]**: The potential for [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to iteratively improve their own architecture, leading to [[concepts/ai-singularity|Intelligence Explosion]] or [[concepts/singularity]].
*   **Loss of Control**: [[concepts/scenarios|Scenarios]] where advanced systems pursue goals misaligned with human values, resulting in catastrophic outcomes.
*   **[[concepts/power-concentration|Power Concentration]]**: The risk that control over existential-capable technologies becomes concentrated in few hands or [[concepts/nodes|entities]], exacerbating misalignment risks.
