# Speculative Technology

**Speculative technology** refers to the conceptualization and design of technological capabilities that do not yet exist but are projected to emerge based on current trajectories in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]], hardware miniaturization, and software evolution. It serves as a bridge between current engineering constraints and future user experience paradigms.

## Key Characteristics
- **Extrapolation:** Extends current trends (e.g., generative AI, neural interfaces) to logical or radical conclusions.
- **Narrative Framing:** Often presented through media, prototypes, or conceptual videos to explore societal impact.
- **Refinement Focus:** Shifts from feature addition to systemic optimization and seamless integration.
