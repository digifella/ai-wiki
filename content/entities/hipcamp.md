# Hipcamp

Hipcamp is a platform connecting travelers with unique outdoor accommodations, including campsites, farm stays, and glamping locations on private land. It emphasizes direct host-guest [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and [[entities/nature|nature]]-focused experiences.

## Key Features
- **Diverse listings**: 10,000+ unique stays across the US (e.g., forest cabins, vineyard stays)
- **Dog-friendly options**: Explicit filtering for pet-friendly properties
- **Direct booking**: Hosts manage listings and reservations without intermediaries
