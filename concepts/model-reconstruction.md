# Model Reconstruction

**Model reconstruction** refers to the process of inferring, reverse-engineering, or rebuilding the internal architecture, weights, or operational logic of an AI model, often from external observations or leaked artifacts. This concept is critical in understanding how complex [[concepts/multi-agent-ai|multi-agent-systems]] are structured and how they communicate.

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/ai-agent|Grok Bot]] Architecture Leak**: Recent analysis of an accidental leak by the [[entities/cursor|Cursor]] team has provided unprecedented insight into the internal structure of [[entities/grok-bot]].
*   **Multi-Agent Blueprint**: The leaked materials detail the specific architecture, communication protocols, and [[concepts/task-flow|task flow]] mechanisms used by [[concepts/cloud-computer|Grok Bot]]'s multi-agent system.
*   **Implications for Reconstruction**: The exposure allows for a more accurate model reconstruction of how such systems coordinate tasks and manage [[concepts/model-interoperability|inter-agent communication]].
