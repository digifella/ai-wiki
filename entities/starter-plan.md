# Starter Plan

The Starter Plan is an entry-level subscription tier offered by Evernote as part of its pricing structure. It is designed for individual users who require basic note-taking and organizational capabilities without advanced features. The plan provides access to core Evernote functionality at a lower cost than premium subscription options, making it the most accessible paid tier for users transitioning from free accounts.

## Features and Functionality

The Starter Plan includes essential note-taking tools such as text formatting, basic organization through notebooks, and synchronization across devices. It typically offers limited offline access and a monthly upload allowance suited to casual and light-to-moderate users. The plan does not include advanced features such as collaboration tools, advanced search capabilities, or integration with third-party applications that are reserved for higher-tier subscriptions.

## Pricing and User Migration

As part of Evernote's broader pricing restructuring efforts, the Starter Plan has served as a key tier for guiding users from free accounts into paid subscription options. The plan's positioning aims to balance affordability with access to core premium functionality, allowing users to evaluate whether a paid subscription meets their needs before committing to more expensive plans.
