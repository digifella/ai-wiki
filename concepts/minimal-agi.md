# minimal AGI

**Minimal AGI** refers to the threshold of [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|Artificial General Intelligence]] where an artificial agent demonstrates the capability to perform cognitive tasks across a broad range of domains, comparable to human-level [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]]. This concept serves as a foundational baseline for measuring progress toward full [[concepts/agi]].

## Key Definitions & Trajectory

*   **Shane Legg's Perspective**: As Chief AGI Scientist and Co-founder of [[entities/google-deepmind]], Shane Legg defines minimal AGI specifically as an artificial agent capable of performing cognitive tasks with general competence.
*   **Core Characteristics**:
    *   Ability to generalize across diverse domains.
    *   [[concepts/cognitive-flexibility|Cognitive flexibility]] comparable to human baselines.
    *   Distinction from narrow AI through breadth of application rather than just depth.
*   **Societal Implications**: The arrival of minimal AGI marks a critical inflection point in AI trajectory, necessitating rigorous evaluation of safety, alignment, and [[concepts/societal-impact|societal impact]].
