## Earth-moon footage

**Definition:**
Earth-moon footage refers to any visual recordings capturing the dynamic relationship between Earth and its natural satellite. This includes various stages of space missions such as launch, orbit insertion, [[concepts/lunar-flyby|lunar flyby]], and return phases.
