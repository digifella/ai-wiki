# 3D reconstruction

**3D reconstruction** is the process of creating a three-dimensional model of an object or scene from two-dimensional images or sensor data. It serves as the foundational technology for spatial [[concepts/computation|computing]], [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], and [[concepts/augmented-reality|augmented reality]].

## Core Concepts
- **Geometry**: Reconstructing the shape and structure of objects.
- **Photogrammetry**: Deriving 3D measurements from photographs.
- **SLAM**: Simultaneous [[concepts/multi-language-support|Localization]] and Mapping for real-time [[concepts/spatial-perception|spatial awareness]].
- **Point Clouds**: Sets of data points in space used to represent external surfaces.
