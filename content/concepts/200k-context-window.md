# 200K context window

A 200K [[concepts/context-window|context window]] enables [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to process and generate text based on contexts of up to 200,000 [[concepts/tokens|tokens]], supporting extended conversations, full-document analysis, and complex [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] without truncation.

## Key Features
- **Extended Processing**: Handles entire books, legal contracts, or multi-hour meeting transcripts in a single interaction.
- **Enterprise [[concepts/software|Applications]]**: Critical for [[concepts/technical-documentation|technical documentation]], legal review, and long-form content generation.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] Availability**: Models like [[entities/glm-47|GLM-4.7]] provide [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] without cloud dependency.
