# Human Perception

Active process of selecting, organizing, and interpreting sensory inputs to construct functional internal representations of the environment. Perception relies on predictive [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and embodied [[concepts/experience|experience]] rather than passive data recording.

## Characteristics
- **[[entities/earth|World]] Modeling:** Humans maintain dynamic [[concepts/world-models]] [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] [[concepts/fundamental-laws-of-physics|physical laws]], causal [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and spatial geometry, enabling [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] and [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] of future states.
- **Sensory Grounding:** Perceptual priors are derived from multisensory interaction with the physical environment, anchoring abstract concepts to motor and sensory [[concepts/feedback|feedback]].
- **Active [[concepts/inference|Inference]]:** Perception minimizes prediction error by updating internal models based on sensory evidence; related to Predictive [[concepts/coding|Coding]] frameworks.

## Comparison with AI Systems
Analysis highlights structural divergences between human perception and [[concepts/large-language-models]] regarding [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]] understanding:
- LLMs primarily operate on statistical token correlations, often lacking grounded semantics or causal understanding of physical dynamics.
- Humans generalize effectively to novel physical [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] via embodied priors; LLMs exhibit hallucinations and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] failures in out-of-distribution physical contexts.
- **World Models** are investigated as a technical bridge to impart [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] with simulation capabilities and physical reasoning comparable to human internal modeling [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-World-Models-Bridging-Human-AI-Understanding-of-Physical|World Models: Bridging Human-AI Understanding of Physical Reality]].
