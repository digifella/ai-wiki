# Mdpi

MDPI (Multidisciplinary Digital Publishing Institute) is an academic publisher headquartered in Basel, Switzerland. The organization specializes in the publication of peer-reviewed, open access journals across a broad spectrum of academic disciplines. Its operational model ensures that all published research is freely available to readers online immediately upon publication, adhering to the principles of open access publishing.

The publisher operates a portfolio of over 300 journals covering natural sciences, engineering, medicine, social sciences, and humanities. Each journal within the portfolio follows a rigorous peer-review process to maintain academic standards while facilitating rapid dissemination of scientific findings. This structure allows researchers to share their work without the traditional subscription barriers, promoting wider accessibility to scholarly content globally.

## Operational Model

MDPI utilizes a continuous publication model, where articles are published online as soon as they are accepted, rather than waiting for a specific issue release date. This approach reduces the time between submission and publication, which is particularly valued in fast-moving scientific fields. The publisher generates revenue primarily through article processing charges paid by authors or their institutions, a common practice in the open access publishing sector.

## Scope and Impact

The journal portfolio is designed to cover both specialized and interdisciplinary topics, aiming to serve the global research community. By focusing on multidisciplinary coverage, MDPI facilitates cross-pollination of ideas between different scientific fields. The publisher's metrics and indexing in major academic databases contribute to its visibility and influence within the international academic landscape.
