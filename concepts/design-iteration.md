# Design Iteration

Design iteration is the cyclical process of creating, testing, and refining designs based on [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] and testing results. It involves making incremental improvements to a design through multiple rounds of creation and evaluation, allowing designers to move from initial concepts toward more refined solutions. This approach acknowledges that effective design rarely emerges fully formed and instead benefits from repeated cycles of prototyping, [[concepts/user-feedback|user feedback]], and refinement.

## Role in Creative Workflows

Design iteration is fundamental to most creative and [[concepts/product-design|product design]] processes. Rather than attempting to achieve a final design in a single attempt, iterative workflows allow designers to explore multiple directions, test assumptions, and validate [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] with users or stakeholders. Modern design tools facilitate this process by enabling [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]] and collaboration, reducing the time and cost associated with each [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycle.

## AI-Enhanced Iteration

Emerging [[concepts/generative-uiux|AI-native design]] tools are beginning to reshape how design iteration occurs. These tools can accelerate certain phases of the [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative process]] by automating routine tasks, generating [[concepts/design-variations|design variations]], and providing real-time feedback. This integration of AI into [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]] aims to reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]] between conception and refinement, potentially allowing designers to focus more deeply on strategic and creative decisions rather than manual execution.
