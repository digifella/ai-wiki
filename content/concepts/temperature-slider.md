# Temperature Slider

The Temperature Slider is a component of the [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]] introduced in [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Adobe Photoshop 2025]]. This feature allows users to adjust the [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] of [[concepts/images|images]], shifting them toward warmer (yellow/orange) or cooler (blue) tones. The tool is designed to streamline [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] workflows by providing intuitive [[concepts/power|control]] over thermal color properties.

## Integration with Color and Vibrance Tool

The Temperature Slider functions as part of [[entities/photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]]'s broader [[concepts/color-and-vibrance-tool|Color and Vibrance tool]], which consolidates multiple [[concepts/tint-adjustment|color adjustment]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] into a single interface. Rather than requiring users to navigate between separate adjustment panels, the slider provides direct access to temperature [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] alongside other color modification options, improving efficiency in post-processing workflows.

## Practical Application

Users can apply the Temperature Slider to correct [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] caused by different lighting conditions, such as tungsten or daylight sources, or to creatively adjust the mood of an image. The adjustment can be applied globally across an image or selectively through [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]], allowing for localized temperature corrections. This feature is particularly useful in color grading for photography and videography [[concepts/software|applications]] where precise thermal [[concepts/tone|tone]] control is essential.
