# Existence and Smoothness

The **[[concepts/navier-stokes-millennium-prize-problem|Navier-Stokes existence and smoothness problem]]** is one of the seven [[concepts/millennium-prize-problems]] posed by the Clay [[concepts/mathematics|Mathematics]] Institute. It concerns the mathematical properties of solutions to the [[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes equations]], which describe the motion of [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid dynamics]] such as air and water.

## Core Problem
The problem asks whether, given an initial [[concepts/velocity|velocity field]], a [[concepts/solution|solution]] to the [[entities/stokes|Navier-Stokes equations]] exists for all time and remains smooth (i.e., does not develop singularities or infinite energy in finite time).
