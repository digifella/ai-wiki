# Creator

A creator in the context of AI-generated content refers to an individual or entity that leverages AI video and [[concepts/ai-generated-voices|voice cloning technology]] to produce digital content. Rather than creating content through traditional means, creators using this technology employ AI-generated [[concepts/digital-avatars|digital avatars]]—synthetic representations that can speak, perform, and present material—to generate videos, streams, and other media. This approach allows creators to produce content at scale without being physically present for each piece of output.

## Revenue Generation

One significant application of AI creator technology is [[concepts/autonomous-content-generation|autonomous content generation]] that can generate revenue. Digital avatars can be deployed across social media platforms, [[concepts/streaming-services|streaming services]], and content [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] networks to create videos, commentary, and other media that earn income through advertisements, sponsorships, or platform monetization. This enables a creator to earn revenue from content produced by their digital avatar without direct ongoing participation in filming or recording.

## Technical Foundation

The technology relies on advances in [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]] and video synthesis, which allow AI systems to convincingly replicate a person's voice, speech patterns, and visual appearance. These systems can be trained on existing recordings and [[concepts/images|images]] to create avatars capable of generating new content in the creator's style. The quality and realism of these avatars have improved significantly, raising both practical and ethical considerations around authenticity, attribution, and consent in digital media.
