# iPhone 15 Pro Max

The [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] 15 Pro Max is a flagship smartphone manufactured by [[entities/apple|Apple]], featuring a titanium design, A17 Pro chip, and advanced camera system.

## Camera System

- **Main Sensor:** 48MP Main camera with 2nd-generation sensor-shift optical image stabilization.
- **[[concepts/telephoto-lens|Telephoto]]:** 12MP 5x Telephoto camera with tetraprism design.
- **Ultrawide:** 12MP Ultrawide camera with [[concepts/macro-photography|macro photography]] support.
