# Hardware Classification

## Overview
Classification of [[concepts/computation|computing]] devices based on their capacity to run [[concepts/local-llm|Local AI Models]], specifically focusing on [[concepts/storage-bandwidth|memory bandwidth]], [[concepts/vram|VRAM]], and processing throughput.

## Key Classifications
*   **[[concepts/microcontrollers|Microcontrollers]] / [[concepts/edge-devices|Edge Devices]]**: Tiny form factor, limited [[concepts/memory|memory]], suitable for basic [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] tasks.
*   **Consumer GPUs**: High-end [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]] cards offering the best price-to-performance ratio for [[concepts/local-ai|local AI]].
*   **[[concepts/gpu-clusters|GPU Clusters]]**: High-end setups for training and large-scale [[concepts/inference|inference]].
