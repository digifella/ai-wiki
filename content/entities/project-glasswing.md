# Project Glasswing

## Overview
**Type:** Entity
**Tags:** AI, [[concepts/security|Security]], Collaboration, [[concepts/mythos|Mythos]]
**Updated:** 2026-04-21

[[concepts/ai-driven-cybersecurity|Project Glasswing]] is a [[concepts/collaborative-initiative|collaborative initiative]] aimed at mitigating the potential risks associated with [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s advanced AI model, [[entities/claude|Claude]] Mythos. The project involves major tech companies including Amazon Web Services, [[entities/anthropic-labs|Anthropic]], Apple, Broadcom, and Cisco to address the unique challenges posed by this next-generation technology.

## Key Points
- **[[concepts/purpose|Purpose]]:** To mitigate the [[concepts/cybersecurity|zero-day vulnerability]] capabilities of [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/mythos|Claude Mythos]] AI.
- **Partnerships:** Includes leading tech firms like AWS, Anthropic, Apple, Broadcom, and Cisco.
- **[[entities/nature|Nature]]:** Considered revolutionary yet "frightening" due to the advanced [[entities/nature|nature]] of [[concepts/ai-benchmarks|Claude Mythos]].
- **Risk Analysis:** [[entities/ibm-technology|IBM Technology]] experts ([[entities/sridhar-muppidi|Sridhar Muppidi]], [[entities/michelle-alvarez|Michelle Alvarez]], and [[entities/dustin-heywood|Dustin Heywood]]) have identified significant risks regarding Claude Mythos's potential for [[concepts/cybersecurity-vulnerability-discovery|cybersecurity vulnerability discovery]].
