# Jim Al-Khalili

**Jim Al-Khalili** is a British theoretical physicist and [[entities/steve-mould|science communicator]], known for his work in quantum [[concepts/physics|physics]] and his extensive contributions to public understanding of [[concepts/science|science]] through television, radio, and [[concepts/writing|writing]].

## Overview
- Professor of [[concepts/theoretical-physics|Theoretical Physics]] at the University of Surrey.
- Host of the BBC Radio 4 series *Unbaffled*.
- [[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] of numerous popular science books, including *Quantum* and *Paradox*.
- Focuses on making complex scientific concepts accessible to general audiences.
