# Mindful Development

**Mindful Development** is an urban and economic planning paradigm that prioritizes psychological well-being, spiritual heritage, and ecological balance over purely metric-driven growth. It seeks to address modern existential-crisis by integrating [[concepts/gross-national-happiness]] principles into [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and policy.

## Core Principles
- Prioritizes mental health and spiritual fulfillment alongside economic stability.
- Integrates traditional cultural values with modern sustainable technology.
- Views urban spaces as environments for contemplation and community cohesion rather than mere consumption hubs.

## Case Study: Gelephu Mindfulness City
[[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]] is pioneering this concept through the development of the **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]]**, a project designed to counter the hyper-commercialized model of cities like Dubai.

- **Objective:** To create a "Anti-Dubai" that addresses the global existential-crisis through [[concepts/sustainable-urbanism|mindful urbanism]].
- **Philosophy:** Rooted in Bhutan's national philosophy of [[concepts/gross-national-happiness]] and its spiritual heritage as the "Land of the Thunder Dragon."
- **Integration:** Combines high-tech infrastructure with deep mindfulness practices to foster holistic well-being.
