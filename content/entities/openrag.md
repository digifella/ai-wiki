# Openrag

OpenRAG is a framework developed by IBM for constructing agentic retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) systems. The framework was publicly explained and promoted by IBM [[concepts/developer|Developer]] Relations Engineer [[entities/david-jones-gilardi|David Jones-Gilardi]]. It enables developers to build AI systems that combine language models with [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] capabilities, allowing [[concepts/agents|agents]] to access and reason over [[concepts/external-data|external data]] sources dynamically.

## Context and Positioning

As of 2026, OpenRAG exists within a broader ecosystem of RAG frameworks and tools. Notes from that period indicate comparative cost considerations, with [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] cited as a potentially expensive alternative. OpenRAG's place in the market reflects IBM's investment in making [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] more accessible to enterprise developers.
