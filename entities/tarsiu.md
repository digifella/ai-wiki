# Tarsiu

**Tarsiu** (Cantonese) or [[concepts/char-siu]] is a traditional Chinese cuisine dish consisting of Roast Pork. It is characterized by its sweet and savory flavor profile, typically achieved through a marinade involving Honey, Hoisin Sauce, Soy Sauce, and Five-Spice Powder.
