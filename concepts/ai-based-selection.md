# Ai Based Selection

AI-based selection refers to [[concepts/ai-masking|automated masking]] and selection techniques that use [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to detect and isolate subjects or specific areas within [[concepts/digital-images|digital images]]. These tools analyze image content to identify edges, boundaries, and objects with greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] than traditional manual [[concepts/pointing-mechanisms|selection methods]]. By leveraging [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], AI-based selection reduces the time and technical [[concepts/skill|skill]] required to create accurate masks and selections in post-processing workflows.

## Application in Adobe Software

In applications like [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], AI-based selection allows users to quickly isolate complex subjects such as people, skies, or objects. This capability is particularly useful for refining mask edges, ensuring that [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] apply cleanly without bleeding into adjacent areas. The technology automates the detection of fine details, such as hair or foliage, which are traditionally difficult to mask manually.

## Impact on Creative Workflows

Demonstrations by experts like [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]] highlight how these tools streamline the [[concepts/editing-workflow-optimization|editing process]] in creative pursuits. By automating the initial selection [[concepts/phase|phase]], photographers can focus more on creative adjustments rather than technical precision. This efficiency enables faster turnaround times and more consistent results across large batches of images, making advanced editing techniques accessible to a broader range of users.
