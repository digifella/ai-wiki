# Develop module

The [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]] is the primary workspace for non-destructive [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] within [[entities/adobe-lightroom]] (including [[concepts/lightroom]], [[entities/lightroom-cc|Lightroom CC]], and [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw]]).

## Key Functions & Workflow
- **Initial Image [[concepts/preparation|Preparation]]**:
	- [[concepts/straightening|Straightening]] and geometric [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
	- Basic panel [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]: [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], Highlights, [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], [[concepts/whites|Whites]], and [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]].
- **[[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] Capabilities**:
	- [[concepts/landscape-masking]]: A specialized feature for targeted environmental enhancement.
- **Review & Comparison**:
	- **[[concepts/history-panel|History Panel]]**: Tracking edit sequences and steps.
	- **Before/After View**: Evaluating [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive]] transformations.
