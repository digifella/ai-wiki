# AI Co Worker

An AI co-worker is a desktop application that integrates artificial intelligence capabilities directly into a user's professional computing environment. Unlike web-based chatbots accessed through browsers, AI co-worker applications operate as persistent software tools designed to remain available throughout the workday. They function as contextual assistants within knowledge work settings, supporting tasks such as writing, analysis, research, and document preparation.

## Core Functionality

AI co-worker applications provide assistance with common workplace activities including drafting emails, summarizing documents, brainstorming ideas, and answering questions relevant to ongoing work. The applications maintain availability as background or foreground tools that users can access alongside their primary work applications. This proximity to the user's workspace differentiates them from conversational AI accessed through separate browser tabs or websites.

## Design Approach

These applications prioritize accessibility and seamless integration into existing workflows rather than requiring users to switch between multiple tools. By operating as desktop software, they can potentially maintain context about a user's work environment and provide assistance that accounts for specific projects or documents in progress. The goal is to reduce friction in accessing AI assistance during professional tasks.
