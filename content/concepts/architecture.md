# Architecture

## Overview
Architecture encompasses the [[concepts/art|art]] and science of designing and constructing buildings and other physical structures. It involves the planning, functional [[concepts/design|design]], and technical details of building projects.

### Key Concepts
- **Urban Planning**: The process of land use allocation with the goal of guiding urban growth.
- **Sustainability**: Practices that enhance environmental quality and reduce resource depletion.
- **Historic [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]**: Efforts to protect significant buildings, districts, objects, or landscapes from deterioration or destruction.
- **[[concepts/light-rag|Light RAG]] System**: A retrieval-augmented generation system that involves [[concepts/chunking-documents|chunking documents]], extracting [[concepts/nodes-and-relationships|nodes and relationships]], and storing them in both a [[concepts/vector-store|vector store]] and a [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]].
- **[[concepts/graph-rag|Graph RAG]] System**: A variant of RAG that emphasizes the use of graph structures for knowledge representation.
