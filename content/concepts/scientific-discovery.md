# Scientific Discovery

The systematic process of expanding human knowledge through observation, experimentation, and the validation of new phenomena.

## Core Framework
- Hypothesis generation and [[concepts/testing|testing]]
- [[concepts/empirical-evidence|Empirical evidence]] acquisition
- Peer review and reproducibility
- Falsifiability

### Feynman's Three-Step Method
[[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] introduced a three-step process for [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific inquiry]]:
- **Guess:** Formulate a hypothesis or new law.
- **[[concepts/compute|Compute]]:** Derive the logical consequences of the guess.
- **Compare:** Validate these consequences with empirical observations.

## Advancements via Artificial Intelligence
- Transition from passive data analysis to autonomous [[concepts/agentic-ai]] capable of active research participation.
- **[[entities/deepmind|DeepMind Aletheia]]**
    - A groundbreaking [[concepts/self-improvement|self-correcting AI]] agent designed to contribute to novel scientific research. ([Backlink](#2026-04-10))
    - Engineered to overcome the limitations of previous models and address skepticism regarding AI's ability to perform [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]].
