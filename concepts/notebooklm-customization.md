# Notebooklm Customization

NotebookLM Customization refers to the ability to modify design elements and visual presentation within Google's NotebookLM platform. Powered by Gemini's generative capabilities, this feature enables users to adjust the appearance and formatting of content generated from their notebooks to align with specific professional requirements and organizational standards.

## Design and Formatting Control

Users can customize various design aspects of their notebook outputs, including visual styling, color schemes, and layout options. This allows content generated by NotebookLM to be tailored for specific contexts, such as professional presentations or internal documentation. The integration of Gemini facilitates these adjustments by interpreting user preferences and applying consistent formatting rules across the generated material.
