# Smarter Scrubber

A grill brush designed as a [[concepts/space-jetpacks|safer]], more durable alternative to traditional wire bristle brushes, manufactured entirely within the [[entities/united-states|United States]].

- Developed through [[entities/destin-sandlin]]'s [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] documented in [[entities/smarter-every-day]]'s video "Destin - manufacturing in [[entities/america|America]]" (2026-04-14)
- Addresses safety hazards of wire bristle brushes (which can break off and contaminate food)
- Motivated by Sandlin's parents' careers as [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]], reflecting his [[concepts/connection|connection]] to [[concepts/american-manufacturing|American manufacturing]]
- Designed to prove U.S.-made products can compete in the marketplace without outsourcing
