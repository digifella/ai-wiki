## Adobe Camera Raw Updates: Enhanced Masking, Local Color Grading, White Balance
**[[concepts/clip-title|Clip title]]:** [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] Just Got Better: [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] Refinements, Local [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]] & Extended White Balance
**Author / channel:** Julieanne Kost
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LUp5TLrFKb0

### Summary
This video, presented by [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]], provides a clear overview of several new updates and enhancements within [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]]. The main topic revolves around increasing precision and creative [[concepts/power|control]] for photographers through improved [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] edge refinements, the addition of [[concepts/local-color-grading|local color grading]], and an extended [[concepts/range|range]] for [[concepts/white-balance-adjustments|white balance adjustments]].

The first key update detailed is in **masking edge refinements**. When using AI-powered masks (like Select Sky, Subject, Background, Landscape, or People), two new sliders, "Feather" and "Edge," offer greater [[concepts/power|control]] over mask boundaries. The "Feather" slider softens the mask's transition, allowing for more natural blending of adjustments into the image. The "Edge" slider enables photographers to either "choke" the mask (decrease edge value) to contract it, preventing it from cutting into intricate details like hair or leaves, or "spread" the mask (increase edge value) to expand it further into the surrounding area, useful for eliminating halos or creating [[entities/glow|glow]] effects.

Secondly, **local color grading controls** have been integrated directly into the masking panel. Previously available only for global adjustments, users can now apply specific color tints to [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], midtones, and [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] within selected masked areas. The video demonstrates this by desaturating the background and adding a magenta tint to its [[concepts/shadows|shadows]], while simultaneously adding yellow to the [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] and green to the midtones of the subject (a frog), allowing for targeted creative [[concepts/color-contrast|color contrast]] and mood setting.

Finally, [[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] introduces an **extended temperature [[concepts/range|range]] for white balance**. For raw [[concepts/files|files]], the [[concepts/temperature-slider|temperature slider]]'s lower limit has been extended from 2000 Kelvin down to 1500 Kelvin. This enhancement is particularly beneficial for photographers capturing [[concepts/images|images]] in very warm, low-[[concepts/light|light]] conditions, such as candlelight or firelight, enabling them to achieve a more accurate and neutral white balance where previously the range was insufficient. For JPEG [[concepts/files|files]], the range also extends further down to -150. These updates collectively offer photographers significantly more precision and creative flexibility in their [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] workflows.
