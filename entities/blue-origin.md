# Blue Origin

**Blue Origin LLC** is an American privately held aerospace manufacturer and sub-orbital spaceflight services company headquartered in Kent, Washington. Founded by Jeff Bezos in 2000, it competes primarily with [[entities/spacex]] and traditional launch providers.

## Key Vehicles & Programs
- **New Shepard**: Suborbital reusable launch vehicle for crewed and uncrewed missions.
- **[[entities/new-glenn|New Glenn]]**: Heavy-[[entities/lift|lift]] orbital launch vehicle designed to compete with Falcon Heavy.
- **Blue Moon**: Lunar lander intended for the [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]] commercial lunar payload service (CLPS).
