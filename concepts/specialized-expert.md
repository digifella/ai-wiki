# Specialized Expert

A **specialized expert** is a model fine-tuned from a general-purpose [[concepts/pre-trained-model|base model]] to become proficient in specific tasks or domains. This process involves training the model on domain-specific datasets to enhance its performance and accuracy for targeted applications.
