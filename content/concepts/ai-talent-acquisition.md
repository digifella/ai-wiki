# AI talent acquisition

The strategic process of identifying and securing human capital specialized in [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] development, heavily influenced by [[concepts/compute|Compute]] Infrastructure availability and [[concepts/geopolitics|Geopolitics]].

### Environmental Influences
- **[[concepts/hardware|Hardware]] [[concepts/limited-resources|Scarcity]] & [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]]**:
    - [[concepts/us-export-controls]] and shortages of [[entities/nvidia]] [[entities/h20-chips|H20 chips]] (e.g., the delay of [[entities/deepseek]] R2) shift talent requirements from large-scale [[concepts/training|training]] specialists toward experts in [[concepts/algorithmic-optimization]] and hardware-efficient [[concepts/machine-learning]].
- **R&D Quality Drivers**:
    - Discrepancies between Model Performance and [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] expectations [[concepts/motivation|drive]] intensified competition for high-tier [[concepts/ai-research]] engineers capable of overcoming [[concepts/scaling|scaling]] bottlenecks.
