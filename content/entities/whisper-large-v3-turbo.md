# Whisper Large V3 Turbo

[[concepts/openai-whisper-model|Whisper Large V3 Turbo]] is an [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|automatic speech recognition]] (ASR) model developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]. It is part of the [[entities/whisper-ai|Whisper]] family of [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] [[concepts/models|models]] and is specifically optimized for [[concepts/real-time-asr|real-time transcription]] tasks. The "Turbo" designation indicates a focus on reduced latency and [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] compared to the standard Whisper Large V3 model.

The model maintains the multilingual [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] characteristic of the Whisper family, supporting speech recognition across numerous languages. Like other Whisper models, it is designed to handle diverse [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] conditions and accents, making it suitable for a [[concepts/range|range]] of practical [[concepts/software|applications]] from [[concepts/live-transcription|live transcription]] to embedded systems with limited [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]].

As a specialized variant within the Whisper ecosystem, Whisper Large V3 Turbo represents OpenAI's effort to balance model [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] with performance requirements for applications where [[concepts/speed|processing speed]] is a critical constraint. Its [[concepts/integration|integration]] into various platforms and tools demonstrates its utility in production environments requiring efficient [[concepts/speech-translation|speech-to-text]] conversion.
