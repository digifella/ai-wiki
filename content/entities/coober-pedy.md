# Coober Pedy
Coober Pedy is a unique town in the South [[concepts/australian-outback|Australian outback]] known for its subterranean residences and businesses. The town's distinctive architectural [[concepts/style|style]] emerged as an adaptation to extreme desert weather conditions, offering residents refuge from intense heat and cold.

## Overview
- **Location:** Outskirts of Coober Pedy, [[entities/south-australia|South Australia]], Australia.
- **Economy:** Primarily reliant on [[concepts/workflow-creation|opal]] mining, with tourism playing a significant role.
- **[[concepts/geology|Geology]]:** Characterized by soft sandstone formations conducive to [[concepts/subsurface-excavation|underground excavation]].

## Features
- **[[concepts/underground-dwellings|Subterranean Dwellings]]:** Underground homes and businesses are carved directly into the earth.
- **Underground Church:** A unique place of worship exists within the town's subterranean complex.
