# Camera App Zoom

[[concepts/camera-app|Camera App]] Zoom refers to a beta feature within the [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] mobile application that integrates computational photography tools directly into the capture interface. Available for [[entities/iphone-13-pro|iPhone 13 Pro]] and later models, this functionality allows users to apply [[concepts/image-input-processing|image processing]] at the moment of capture rather than relying solely on post-processing [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. By leveraging the advanced neural engines and [[concepts/hardware-capabilities|hardware capabilities]] of recent iPhone devices, the app enhances image quality during the shooting process.

The feature set includes three primary computational photography tools designed to improve image fidelity under various conditions. Zoom functionality enables users to capture images at different focal lengths with computational enhancement to maintain [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]]. Night mode capabilities allow for improved [[concepts/low-light-performance|low-light performance]] by optimizing [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] and noise reduction in real-time. Additionally, the app offers [[concepts/reflection-removal|reflection removal]] tools to mitigate unwanted glare from glass surfaces or other reflective materials during capture.
