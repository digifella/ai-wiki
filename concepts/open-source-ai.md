# Open-Source AI

**Open-source AI** refers to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] models, tools, and datasets whose source code and weights are publicly available for inspection, modification, and redistribution. This paradigm contrasts with proprietary closed-source models, enabling community-driven innovation, transparency, and local deployment capabilities.

## Key Developments

*   **[[entities/meta|Meta]]'s Strategic Re-entry:** Meta has re-entered the open models domain with significant releases aimed at democratizing access to advanced AI capabilities.
*   **[[entities/meta-muse-glimmer-30b|Meta Muse Glimmer 30B]]:** A key release in Meta's open-source strategy, providing high-performance models for community use.
*   **[[entities/nous-research|Nous Research]]'s [[concepts/hermes-agent|Hermes Agent]]:** Introduces automated recurring task capabilities via its `/loop` feature, enhancing agentic workflows.
*   **Market Consolidation Risks:** The potential acquisition of [[entities/hugging-face|Hugging Face]] by [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]] for an estimated $12.9 billion has sparked debate regarding the future of open-source [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and centralization.
