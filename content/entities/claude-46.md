# Claude 46

Claude 46 represents a conceptual shift in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] development practices observed around 2026. Rather than focusing primarily on selecting between different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] or refining prompts to elicit better outputs, the emerging paradigm emphasizes the infrastructure and systems that orchestrate AI execution. This reflects a maturation in how organizations deploy and utilize [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] in production environments.

## Harness Engineering

The core concept underlying Claude 46 is that effective AI system performance depends less on the underlying model itself and more on the "harness"—the [[concepts/execution-orchestration|execution framework]] that manages how the model operates. Harness engineering encompasses the [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], orchestration logic, and operational systems that guide [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] and integrate it into broader workflows. This approach suggests that engineering effort should shift from [[concepts/ai-prompt-engineering|prompt optimization]] toward building robust systems for coordinating AI tasks.

## Execution Orchestration

Closely related to harness engineering is execution orchestration, which refers to the management of complex, multi-step AI operations. Rather than treating an AI model as an isolated component, orchestration involves coordinating sequences of model calls, managing context, handling failures, and integrating outputs with other systems. This reflects a practical recognition that real-world [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] require sophisticated coordination layers beyond the model itself.
