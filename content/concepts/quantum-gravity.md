# Quantum Gravity

**Quantum [[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]]** is a field of [[concepts/theoretical-physics|theoretical physics]] aiming to unify General Relativity (GR) and [[concepts/quantum-mechanics]] (QM). It seeks to describe the **gravitational force** according to the principles of QM, resolving the mathematical incompatibilities between the smooth, continuous spacetime of GR and the discrete, probabilistic [[entities/nature|nature]] of QM [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] high energies and small [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] (the **[[entities/planck|Planck]] scale**).

## Core Challenges
- **Non-renormalizability**: Standard perturbative [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]] of GR leads to infinite results that cannot be removed, unlike other forces in the [[concepts/standard-model]].
- **Problem of Time**: In QM, time is a background parameter; in GR, time is a dynamic coordinate intertwined with space.
- **Singularities**: Both the [[concepts/big-bang|Big Bang]] and the centers of [[concepts/black-holes]] represent points of infinite density where GR breaks down, necessitating a quantum gravitational description.

## Major Approaches
- **[[concepts/string-theory|String Theory]]**: Replaces point particles with vibrating strings, naturally including a graviton. Requires extra dimensions.
- **[[concepts/loop|Loop]] Quantum Gravity (LQG)**: Quantizes spacetime itself into discrete [[concepts/loops|loops]] or spins. Suggests spacetime is granular at the Planck scale.
- **Causal Dynamical Triangulations (CDT)**: Uses path integrals over discrete spacetime geometries.
- **Asymptotic Safety**: Proposes that gravity remains well-behaved at high energies due to a non-trivial fixed point in the renormalization group [[concepts/flow|flow]].

## Phenomenological Implications
- **Black Hole Information Paradox**: Reconciling the unitarity of QM with the apparent information loss in black hole evaporation via Hawking Radiation.
- **Cosmological Constant Problem**: Explaining the discrepancy between the observed vacuum energy density and theoretical predictions.
- **Planck Scale [[concepts/physics|Physics]]**: Effects expected only at $\sim 10^{-35}$ meters, currently beyond experimental reach but potentially visible in high-energy [[concepts/cosmic-rays|cosmic rays]] or primordial [[concepts/gravitational-waves|gravitational waves]].
