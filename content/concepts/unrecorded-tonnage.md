# Unrecorded Tonnage

This page serves as a repository for geological data, mining anomalies, and undocumented resource assessments where standard recorded tonnage figures are lacking or disputed. It focuses on regions exhibiting anomalous geological formations that may hold significant, unquantified mineral wealth.

## Core Concepts

*   **Tonnage Definition:** The volume or mass of ore, rock, or material extracted from a specific geological area.
*   **Recorded vs. Unrecorded Tonnage:** The distinction between officially mapped and quantified reserves versus potential or anomalous deposits that have not been formally registered in public geological databases.
*   **Geological Anomalies:** Specific geological features (like the Wangerrip deposit) that deviate significantly from regional norms, often [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] the need for re-evaluation of existing tonnage estimates.

## Case Study: Wangerrip Gold Deposit

The Wangerrip [[concepts/gold-deposit|Gold Deposit]] exemplifies the challenges posed by unrecorded or anomalous geological features in resource estimation.

*   This deposit is highlighted as an anomalous geological mystery in Victoria's [[concepts/otway-ranges|Otway Ranges]].
*   It challenges the established patterns of known goldfields in the region.
*   Further details on this [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]] are documented in [[lab-notes/2026-05-02-Wangerrip-Gold-Deposit-An-Anomalous-Geological-Mystery-i|Wangerrip Gold Deposit: An Anomalous Geological Mystery in Victoria's Otway Ranges]].
*   Investigating such areas requires specialized geological review, often bridging the gap between published data and local field observations.

## Data Management Protocol

*   All data concerning unrecorded tonnage must be cross-referenced with regional geological surveys [[geology/regional-surveys]].
*   Discrepancies in tonnage often lead to uncertainty in mining projections [[economics/resource-modeling]].
*   A standardized protocol is needed for documenting geological anomalies before they are formally integrated into official records.
