# Mdpi

MDPI (Multidisciplinary Digital Publishing Institute) is an academic publisher headquartered in Basel, Switzerland. The organization specializes in the publication of peer-reviewed, open access journals across a broad spectrum of academic disciplines. Its operational model ensures that all published research is freely available to readers online immediately upon publication, adhering to the principles of open access publishing.

The publisher operates a portfolio of over 300 journals covering natural sciences, engineering, medicine, social sciences, and humanities. Each journal within the MDPI network employs a standard peer review process to evaluate submissions for scientific rigor and quality. By maintaining a diverse range of subject-specific publications, MDPI aims to facilitate the rapid dissemination of scholarly work to a global audience.

Financially, MDPI journals typically operate on an article processing charge (APC) model, where authors or their institutions pay a fee to cover the costs of publication and peer review. This structure supports the open access framework by shifting the cost burden from readers to publishers. The organization continues to expand its journal offerings while maintaining its focus on multidisciplinary academic communication.
