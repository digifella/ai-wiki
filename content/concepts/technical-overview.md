# Technical Overview: MiniMax M2.7 & Mistral 3 Large

## MiniMax M2.7 Open Source LLM

**[[entities/m27|MiniMax M2.7]]** is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] with significant advancements in both scale and development methodology. It operates under a modified [[concepts/mit-license|MIT license]], making it accessible for a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/software|applications]].

### Key Features
- **[[concepts/parameter-count|Parameter Count]]:** [[concepts/229-billion-parameters|229 billion parameters]]
- **[[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]:** Utilizes a [[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) [[concepts/architecture|architecture]]

### Self-Evolutionary Development Process
MiniMax M2.7 stands out due to its unique approach to continuous improvement and adaptation, emphasizing [[concepts/self-evolution|self-evolution]] through advanced techniques.
