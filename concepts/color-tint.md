# Color Tint

Color Tint is a feature within Adobe Photoshop 2025's Color and Vibrance adjustment tool that enables users to modify the color cast and overall hue balance of digital images. Operating as a non-destructive adjustment layer, it preserves the original image data while allowing changes to color temperature and tonal qualities without permanent alteration to the underlying pixels.

The feature supports both corrective and creative workflows. Photographers use Color Tint to neutralize unwanted color casts caused by mixed lighting conditions or to enhance the atmospheric mood of a scene. By adjusting the tint slider, users can shift the balance between green and magenta tones, providing precise control over the image's white point and overall color harmony.

In addition to color correction, the tool facilitates creative color grading. Users can apply specific tints to shadows, midtones, and highlights independently, allowing for complex stylization that separates the color cast from the image's luminance. This separation ensures that the structural details and contrast of the original photograph remain intact while the color palette is significantly altered.

The implementation integrates with Photoshop 2025's updated interface for the Color and Vibrance panel, offering a streamlined approach to global color adjustments. It serves as a foundational step in post-processing pipelines, often used before fine-tuning saturation and vibrance to ensure the base color balance is accurate before intensifying specific hues.
