# Product Utility

**Product Utility** refers to the practical [[concepts/value|usefulness]] and value a product provides to its users, often defined by its ability to solve specific problems or fulfill needs efficiently. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]], utility is increasingly measured not just by feature richness but by the economic efficiency of intelligence delivery.

## Key Dimensions
- **Functional Efficacy**: The degree to which a product performs its intended function without [[concepts/friction|friction]].
- **Economic Efficiency**: The cost-benefit ratio of achieving outcomes, particularly relevant in [[concepts/economics-of-intelligence|AI Economics]] where [[concepts/compute-costs|compute costs]] fluctuate.
- **User Perceived Value**: [[concepts/opinions|Subjective assessment]] of utility based on [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], [[concepts/speed|speed]], and relevance of outputs.

## AI Strategy and Economic Shifts (2026)
Recent industry analysis suggests a shift in how product utility is derived from AI capabilities:

*   **Rising Cost of Intelligence**: Contrary to early trends of plummeting costs, specific high-fidelity intelligence tasks are seeing increased marginal costs due to model complexity and [[concepts/verification|verification]] overhead [[lab-notes/2026-06-14-Google-IO-AI-Strategy-Product-Utility-and-the-Economics|Google I/O AI Strategy: Product Utility and the Economics of Intelligence]].
*   **[[concepts/google-search|Google]]’s [[concepts/strategic-pivot|Strategic Pivot]]**: Announcements at [[entities/google-io|Google I/O]] 2026 emphasize optimizing utility through [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]] (e.g., [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] variations) rather than raw [[concepts/jacks-of-all-trades|generalist]] [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]], aiming to balance performance with economic sustainability.
*   **Utility over Novelty**: The market is correcting towards products that demonstrate consistent, low-latency utility for core workflows, deprioritizing experimental features with high maintenance costs.

## Relationships
- **Product-Market Fit**: Utility is a primary driver of sustainable PMF in AI-native applications.
- **[[concepts/computational-resources|Compute]] Economics**: Directly influences the scalability of product features.
