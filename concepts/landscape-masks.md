# Landscape Masks

Landscape masks are a selective adjustment feature available in Adobe Lightroom and the Camera Raw Filter that automatically identify and isolate distinct regions within landscape photographs. Rather than requiring manual geometric selections or brush work, landscape masks use algorithms to detect common landscape elements such as the sky, foreground, and mid-tone areas. This automated detection enables photographers to apply independent adjustments to different parts of an image without affecting the entire photograph.

The feature analyzes the tonal and spatial characteristics of an image to generate precise masks. By distinguishing between the sky and the ground, the tool allows for targeted corrections that would otherwise be difficult to achieve with global adjustments. For instance, a photographer can darken the sky to enhance contrast while simultaneously brightening the foreground to reveal detail in shadows, all within a single workflow.

This functionality streamlines the post-processing workflow by reducing the time spent on manual masking. It is particularly useful for high-contrast scenes where the dynamic range exceeds the sensor's capability, allowing for balanced exposure across the frame. The masks can be further refined using the mask panel controls if the automatic detection requires minor adjustments to edge precision or coverage area.
