# Deep Think

Deep Think is the designation used by DeepMind for Aletheia, an artificial intelligence system developed to facilitate scientific discovery. The system is engineered to support researchers by generating hypotheses and analyzing data within complex scientific domains. Its primary function is to enhance the efficiency and scope of computational research through advanced machine learning techniques.

A defining characteristic of Deep Think is its self-correcting architecture. Unlike traditional models that present outputs as static conclusions, Aletheia is designed to identify and address errors in its own reasoning during the research process. This iterative self-correction mechanism aims to improve the reliability and accuracy of AI-generated results, ensuring that findings are robust before being applied to broader scientific contexts.
