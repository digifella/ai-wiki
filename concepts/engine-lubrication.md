# Engine lubrication

The process of applying a lubricant to reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]], heat, and wear between moving components within an [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine]].

### Key Functions and Criticality
- **[[concepts/friction|Friction]] Reduction**: Prevents direct metal-on-[[concepts/metal|metal]] [[entities/contact|contact]] between moving parts.
- **Pressure Maintenance**: [[concepts/oil-pressure]] is the critical mechanism for [[concepts/engine|engine]] survival; maintaining adequate pressure is the primary factor in preventing catastrophic [[concepts/engine-failure|engine failure]].
- **Monitoring**: System [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] is signaled through [[concepts/oil-pressure|Oil pressure]] warning lights, which indicate a loss of lubrication efficacy.
