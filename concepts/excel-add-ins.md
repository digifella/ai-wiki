## AI-Powered Add-ins
The landscape is shifting toward the integration of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] to automate complex [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], formula generation, and data manipulation.

- **[[entities/claude-ai|Claude AI]] [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]] Add-in**:

    - Demonstrated capability in providing significant value to finance professionals, particularly where native tools like [[entities/microsoft-copilot]] have historically struggled to provide deep utility in Office applications.

- **Automation**: VBA, Office Scripts, and Power Automate.
- **Data Analysis**: Tools for [[concepts/power-query|Power Query]], Power Pivot, and statistical modeling.
- **External Integrations**: API-driven add-ins for Data [[concepts/science|Science]] and real-time financial data feeds.
