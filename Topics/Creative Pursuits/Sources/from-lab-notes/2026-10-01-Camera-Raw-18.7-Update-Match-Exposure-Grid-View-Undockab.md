## Camera Raw 18.7 Update: Match Exposure, Grid View, Undockable Panels
**Clip title:** [[entities/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw]] 18.7 Update – 3 [[concepts/new-features|New Features]] You Need to Know
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] Together

### Summary
This video provides a concise overview of the [[concepts/new-features|new features]] and improvements introduced in [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR) version 18.7. The update focuses on enhancing [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]] and offering greater flexibility for photographers. The presenter highlights three main additions: an improved "Match [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]" function, a new "[[concepts/grid-view|Grid View]]" for browsing and editing, and the ability to undock the Histogram and Vectorscope panels.

The "[[concepts/match-exposure|Match Exposure]]" feature is designed to quickly harmonize exposure levels across multiple photographs. It offers two distinct options: "Match Exposure by [[concepts/camera-settings|Camera Settings]]," which adjusts based on original camera [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] (ISO, [[concepts/aperture|aperture]], shutter [[concepts/speed|speed]]), and "Match Exposure by [[concepts/camera-settings|Camera Settings]] & [[concepts/light|Light]]," which further considers ambient [[concepts/light|light]] conditions. This functionality is particularly useful for series of photos, such as wedding shoots, where it automatically calculates and applies appropriate exposure [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]], thereby saving significant manual editing time.

Another significant improvement is the "Grid View," which allows users to browse and [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|compare]] all their photos in a thumbnail grid. This view facilitates quick selection of single or multiple images for editing, with [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] displayed directly on the thumbnails for real-time comparison. Users can easily switch back to the single-image "[[concepts/loop|Loop]] View" by double-clicking. This feature is presented as a valuable tool for efficiently culling and editing large batches of images directly within ACR.

Finally, the update introduces the capability to undock the Histogram and Vectorscope panels. These crucial analytical tools can now be detached from the main interface and used as resizable, floating [[concepts/microsoft-windows|windows]]. This provides photographers with increased flexibility in their workspace, allowing them to continuously monitor color distribution and exposure levels while making adjustments, without cluttering the primary editing area. The video concludes by mentioning that ACR 18.7 also includes expanded support for new camera models and lenses, further enhancing its utility for a wider range of equipment.
