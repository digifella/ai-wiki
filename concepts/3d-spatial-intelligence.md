# 3D Spatial Intelligence

**3D [[concepts/spatial-intelligence|Spatial Intelligence]]** refers to the computational capability to perceive, understand, and interact with three-dimensional environments. It involves processing spatial data to enable accurate mapping, object recognition, and navigation within physical or virtual spaces.

## Core Concepts

*   **[[concepts/spatial-perception|Spatial Awareness]]:** The ability to determine the position and orientation of objects relative to the observer and each other.
*   **Environmental Mapping:** Constructing digital representations of physical spaces for analysis or [[concepts/simulation|simulation]].
*   **[[concepts/interactive-simulation|Interactive Simulation]]:** Using [[concepts/environment-simulation|simulated environments]] to test [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], train models, or visualize spatial data before real-[[entities/earth|world]] deployment.

## Key Tools & Simulators

### God's Eye View (GEV)

**[[entities/gods-eye-view-gev|God's Eye View (GEV)]]** is a prominent [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] 3D spatial intelligence simulator that has gained significant traction for its ability to provide comprehensive environmental context.
