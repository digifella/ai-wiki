# Aviation Security

**Aviation [[concepts/security|security]]** (AVSEC) refers to the collective measures and protocols designed to protect civil aviation from acts of unlawful interference, including terrorism, sabotage, and cockpit intrusion. It encompasses physical security, [[entities/employees|personnel]] screening, and procedural safeguards to ensure the safety of passengers, crew, and aircraft.

## Key Threat Vectors

*   **Insider Threats:** Malicious actions by crew members or ground staff.
*   **Cockpit Intrusion:** [[concepts/security-exposure|Unauthorized access]] to the flight deck.
*   **Unlawful Interference:** Hijacking, bombing, or cyber-attacks on aviation systems.

## Case Studies & Incidents

### FlyDubai Flight 1073 Incident

A significant recent event highlighting vulnerabilities in cockpit security protocols occurred on September 30, 2026. This incident is documented in detail in [[lab-notes/2026-10-13-FlyDubai-1073-Extremist-First-Officers-Cockpit-Attack-an|FlyDubai 1073: Extremist First Officer's Cockpit Attack and Desert Emergency]].

**Incident Details:**
*   **Flight:** FlyDubai 1073 ([[concepts/boeing-737-max-8|Boeing 737 Max 8]])
*   **Route:** Dubai to Tel Aviv
*   **Date:** September 30, 2026
*   **Perpetrator:** First Officer [[entities/hamam-al-hammami|Hamam Al-Hammami]] (Omani national)
*   **Event:** [[concepts/cockpit-attack|Cockpit attack]] by the First Officer approximately two hours into the flight, leading to a desert [[concepts/emergency-landing|emergency landing]].
*   **Casualties:** 174 passengers and 6 crew members (including two off-duty pilots) were on board.

## Security Protocols

*   **Cockpit Door Reinforcement:** Mandatory hardened doors to prevent forced entry.
*   **Crew Monitoring:** Procedures for monitoring crew behavior and [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]].
*   **Emergency Response:** Protocols for handling in-flight security breaches and emergency landings.
