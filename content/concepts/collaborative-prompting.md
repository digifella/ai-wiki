# Collaborative Prompting

Collaborative [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] refers to a set of techniques for optimizing interactions with [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] to achieve better outputs and deeper [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]. Rather than treating [[concepts/claude|Claude]] as a tool that responds to isolated queries, collaborative prompting frames the interaction as an ongoing [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] where both the user and the AI work together iteratively. This approach emphasizes clarity, specificity, and active refinement of requests to progressively improve results.

The core techniques focus on how users [[concepts/structure|structure]] their prompts to enable Claude to function as a more effective [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] partner. Key strategies include providing sufficient context upfront, breaking complex problems into manageable steps, explicitly stating desired [[concepts/output|output]] formats, and asking Claude to explain its [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. Users can also improve results by requesting that Claude ask [[concepts/clarifying-questions|clarifying questions]] when [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] exists, rather than proceeding with assumptions.

Additional optimization approaches involve using [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] for technical tasks where executable code outputs tangible results, separating distinct problems into distinct conversations to prevent context confusion, and revisiting previous exchanges to build on successful patterns. Users benefit from treating disagreements or unexpected outputs as opportunities to refine their prompts rather than viewing them as failures, creating a [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loop|loop]] that steadily improves the quality of collaboration.

Effective collaborative prompting ultimately treats Claude as a capable but external intelligence that requires clear communication and iterative adjustment, much like working with a skilled colleague on a complex project.
