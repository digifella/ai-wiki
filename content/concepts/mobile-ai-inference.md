# Mobile AI inference

The execution of [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] models, specifically [[concepts/large-language-models]], directly on mobile [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] (e.g., [[entities/iphone|iPhone]], [[entities/ipad]]) rather than via cloud-based [[concepts/application-programming-interfaces-apis|APIs]].

- **Local iOS [[concepts/deployment|Deployment]]**: [[concepts/running|Running]] [[entities/mistral-ai|Mistral]] 7B Instruct natively on iOS devices [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] 04 21 Local [[entities/mistral|Mistral]] LLM Deployment on [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] and iPad.
- **Core Advantages**: Leverages local device [[concepts/compute|compute]] to enhance data [[concepts/privacy-protection|privacy]] and enable offline functionality.
