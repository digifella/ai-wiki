# Daniel Miessler

Daniel Miessler is a security researcher and content creator known for his work on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] infrastructure and information security. He has presented educational content through the [[entities/tldr-sec|Unsupervised Learning Channel]], a platform focused on technical topics in machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] and AI systems.

## Personal AI Infrastructure (Kai)

Miessler presented on the concept of Personal AI Infrastructure, referred to as Kai, discussing approaches to building individual-scale AI systems. This presentation was hosted by [[entities/clint-gibler|Clint Gibler]] on the Unsupervised Learning Channel and explored practical frameworks for personal [[concepts/adoption|AI implementation]], relevant to discussions around post-corporate technology adoption and individual AI tool development.
