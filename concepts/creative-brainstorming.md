# Creative Brainstorming

Creative [[concepts/brainstorming|brainstorming]] is a cognitive process utilized by [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and human teams to generate novel [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] and solutions through collaborative exploration. In the context of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], this technique involves the iterative generation of multiple candidate approaches, often leveraging diverse [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] paths to expand the [[concepts/solution|solution]] space before evaluation and refinement. This method is particularly valuable for addressing open-ended problems that benefit from exploring multiple perspectives rather than pursuing a single predetermined path.

## Implementation in AI Systems

[[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] implement brainstorming by simulating [[concepts/divergent-thinking|divergent thinking]] processes to produce a wide array of potential outputs. These systems may employ ensemble methods or [[concepts/parallel-processing|parallel processing]] to evaluate various hypotheses simultaneously, allowing for a broader exploration of possibilities. The process typically includes stages for [[concepts/creative-idea-generation|idea generation]], [[concepts/critical-thinking|critical analysis]], and synthesis, where the most promising candidates are selected for further development. This structured approach helps mitigate the limitations of linear reasoning and encourages [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in [[concepts/complex-problem-solving|complex problem-solving]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
