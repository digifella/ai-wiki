# Frontline Balance

Frontline Balance represents the critical equilibrium of combat efficacy, attrition sustainability, and operational momentum between belligerents. Shifts result from changes in force [[concepts/density|density]], logistical throughput, [[entities/employees|personnel]] morale, and external support vectors. Assessment requires synthesizing quantitative force data with qualitative [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] of strain and intent.

## Core Dynamics
- **Attrition Thresholds:** Balance tips when loss rates exceed replacement capacity for personnel or complex materiel.
- **Logistical Saturation:** Supply chain [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] dictates operational reach; degradation forces posture contraction.
- **Perceptual Divergence:** Public narratives frequently lag or distort actual battlefield conditions, creating [[concepts/algorithmic-filtering|information asymmetry]].
- **Force Posture:** Redistribution of reserves vs. frontline commitments signals intent and [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]].
