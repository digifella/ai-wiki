# Automation

The use of technology and [[concepts/ai]] to execute repetitive tasks and [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] with minimal human intervention, specifically designed to reduce manual overhead and redirect cognitive resources toward high-leverage activities.

### Key Implementations & Use Cases
- **Personalized [[concepts/second-brain|Second Brain]] Construction**: Utilizing [[entities/claude-code]] and [[entities/obsidian]] to build a [[concepts/secure|secure]], automated [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] system.
	- Focus on automating low-leverage tasks to reclaim significant weekly capacity (e.g., 12+ hours/week).
	- Reference: [[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]] 04 10 Building a [[concepts/secure|Secure]] Personalized AI Seco
- **[[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]] ([[concepts/local-ai-assistants|Clawdbot]])**: A personal [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]] [[concepts/running|running]] on [[concepts/local-infrastructure|local infrastructure]] that executes [[concepts/real-world-tasks|real-world tasks]] by connecting to services including [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], [[entities/asana|Asana]], [[entities/slack|Slack]], and [[entities/telegram|Telegram]].
