# Intel N150

The Intel N150 is a processor from Intel's lineup designed for low-power computing [[concepts/software|applications]]. It targets the segment of users building home servers and network-attached [[entities/storage|storage]] (NAS) devices where [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] and modest performance requirements intersect.

## Applications

The processor is particularly relevant for [[concepts/home-server|home server]] and NAS deployments, where it can provide sufficient [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] while maintaining low power consumption and operating costs. These applications benefit from the N150's efficiency profile, making it suitable for systems that may run continuously or for extended periods.

When evaluating the N150 for these [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], potential builders should consider cost-benefit tradeoffs against alternative solutions.
