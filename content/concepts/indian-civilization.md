# Indian Civilization

Indian civilization refers to the complex societies and cultures that developed across the Indian subcontinent over millennia, beginning with early settlements in the Indus Valley around 3300 BCE. The Indus Valley Civilization, contemporaneous with ancient Egypt and Mesopotamia, demonstrated sophisticated urban planning, standardized [[concepts/weights|weights]] and measures, and an undeciphered script. Following its decline around 1300 BCE, Vedic cultures emerged in northern India, establishing foundational religious and social structures that would shape the subcontinent for centuries.

## Classical and Medieval Periods

The classical period saw the rise of major empires including the [[concepts/mauryan-empire|Mauryan Empire]] (322–185 BCE) under Ashoka, which expanded [[concepts/buddhism|Buddhism]] across Asia, and the Gupta Empire (320–550 CE), often regarded as a golden age of Indian culture, science, and the arts. These periods witnessed significant advances in [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]], literature, and [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]]. Subsequent medieval dynasties sustained regional political structures while maintaining cultural [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] through Sanskrit literature, Hindu temple [[concepts/architecture|architecture]], and the development of diverse artistic traditions.

## Global Trade and Exchange

Indian civilization played a central role in historical global trade networks. The monsoon winds enabled maritime commerce across the Indian Ocean, connecting Indian ports with Southeast Asia, the Middle East, East Africa, and China. Indian textiles, spices, gems, and metalwork were highly valued commodities, while Indian mathematical and astronomical knowledge influenced scientific development across Eurasia. These trade [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] facilitated not only economic exchange but also the transmission of religious ideas, particularly Buddhism and later Islam, which became integral to Indian society.
