This app allows users to access Gemini from any screen on their desktop using a simple keyboard shortcut (Option + Space). The desktop app also integrates creative capabilities, allowing users to generate [[concepts/images|images]], videos, and music directly from their desktop, alongside seamless access to [[concepts/files|files]], [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]], Photos, and [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]]. This [[concepts/integration|integration]] aims to provide live, on-demand AI assistance without switching tabs or applications, anticipating future developments in AI agents and [[concepts/automations|automations]].

Another significant advancement is the [[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]].1 Flash Text-to-Speech (TTS) Preview, available in Google [[entities/ai-studio|AI Studio]]'s Playground. This new model is engineered for powerful, [low-latency speech generation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Low-latency_speech_generation), delivering natural outputs with steerable prompts and expressive [[concepts/audio|audio]] tags for precise narration. It offers a variety of quickstart [[concepts/templates|templates]] for different roles (e.g., everyday assistant, podcast host, master storyteller) and allows extensive [[concepts/customization|customization]] of [[concepts/tone|voice]] [[concepts/style|style]], pace, accent, and [[entities/pitch|pitch]]. Finally, Google is enhancing its [[entities/ai-studio|AI Studio]] for developers and introducing "Skills in Chrome." The AI Studio now offers an improved "vibe coding" experience, where users can describe an app idea in natural language, and Gemini will auto-suggest features and functionalities (e.g., building a CRM for real estate would prompt suggestions for lead management and deal tracking). The platform simplifies the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of various Gemini capabilities, from generating music and converting [[concepts/text|text]] to speech to analyzing images/video and adding database [[concepts/authentication|authentication]]. Concurrently, "Skills in Chrome" will allow users to save and reuse their best AI prompts as one-click workflows directly within the browser, accessible via a simple slash (/) command. This feature, which is rolling out gradually, will streamline tasks in areas like [[concepts/health|health]] and wellness, shopping, and productivity, all while maintaining Chrome's robust security and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] protections.
