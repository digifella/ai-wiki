# Earth Personality

The **Earth** personality is one of the five elemental profiles described by [[entities/tony-robbins|Tony Robbins]] and Dr. [[concepts/stephen-scott-cowan|Stephen Scott Cowan]] in their work on understanding personalities through the ancient Chinese [[concepts/five-elements-theory|Five Elements theory]]. This concept emphasizes traits associated with stability, nurturing, and practicality.

## Key Traits
- Stable and reliable
- Nurturing and caring
- Pragmatic and detail-oriented
