# Dr Know It All

Dr Know It All is a knowledge management system designed to integrate artificial intelligence capabilities with enterprise information infrastructure. The platform operates within distributed organizational environments, aiming to streamline information retrieval and facilitate organizational learning across multiple locations and diverse user groups.

## System Capabilities

The system combines AI technologies with robust knowledge base infrastructure to support core enterprise knowledge management functions. It enables users to access and retrieve information from integrated organizational knowledge sources, ensuring that data is available and actionable for various departments and teams within the organization.
