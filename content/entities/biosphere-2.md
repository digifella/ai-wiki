# Biosphere 2

[[concepts/closed-ecosystem|Biosphere 2]] is a large-scale Earth [[concepts/science|science]] research facility located in [[entities/oracle|Oracle]], Arizona. Constructed in the 1980s and completed in 1991, the [[concepts/structure|structure]] comprises a series of connected domes and enclosed environments designed to simulate and study Earth's ecosystems. The facility covers approximately 3.15 acres and contains distinct biomes including a rainforest, ocean, marsh, savanna, and desert, alongside agricultural and human habitat areas.

## Scientific Purpose and Evolution

The project was originally conceived as a closed ecological system to test whether humans could create self-sustaining artificial environments. The initial high-profile experiment involved sealing eight researchers inside for two years (1991-1993) to study how isolated ecosystems function. While this mission revealed unexpected challenges—including oxygen depletion and food production difficulties—it generated significant data about ecosystem dynamics and the complexity of Earth's natural systems.

Following the initial mission, Biosphere 2 transitioned to a research facility operated by the University of Arizona. Scientists have used the controlled environments to conduct experiments on atmospheric chemistry, plant physiology, aquatic systems, and [[concepts/responses|responses]] to environmental change. The facility's ability to manipulate conditions while maintaining relatively isolated ecosystems has made it valuable for understanding how natural systems respond to various pressures and perturbations.
