# Clean Power

**Clean power** refers to electricity generated from sources that produce little to no greenhouse gas emissions or pollutants. It encompasses renewable energy technologies like solar, wind, and hydroelectricity, as well as low-carbon nuclear options such as nuclear fission and emerging [[concepts/nuclear-fusion]] research. The primary goal is decarbonization while maintaining grid [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].

## Historical Context & Failed Experiments

The [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of clean, [[concepts/limitless-energy|limitless energy]] has been marred by significant scientific frauds that delayed public [[concepts/trust|trust]] and investment in viable technologies. A notable historical case study is detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-06-08-Project-Huemul-Argentinas-Controversial-Nuclear-Fusion-E|Project Huemul: Argentina's Controversial Nuclear Fusion Experiment and Fraud]].

### Key Characteristics of Project Huemul
*   **Nature**: A mid-20th century Argentine [[concepts/scientific-experiment|experiment]] claiming to have achieved controlled [[concepts/fusion-power|nuclear fusion]].
*   **Outcome**: Ultimately exposed as a massive scientific fraud involving fabricated data and deceptive demonstrations.
*   **Impact**: Highlighted the risks of state-sponsored pseudo-[[concepts/science|science]] in the energy sector and the [[concepts/value|importance]] of rigorous [[concepts/document-review|peer review]] in nuclear [[concepts/physics|physics]].
*   **Context**: Often cited in historical analyses of Cold War-era nuclear ambitions and the intersection of political ideology with scientific [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
