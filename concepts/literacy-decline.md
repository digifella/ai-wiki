# Literacy Decline

**Literacy decline** refers to the observed reduction in foundational reading, writing, and critical thinking skills across recent generations, particularly affecting [[entities/gen-alpha|Gen Alpha]]. This phenomenon is driven by a convergence of technological shifts, pedagogical changes, and cognitive adaptations.

## Key Drivers

*   **Technological Saturation:** The ubiquity of short-form video content and algorithmic feeds has altered [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] spans and reduced tolerance for sustained deep reading Deep Work.
*   **Educational Shifts:** A move away from phonics-based instruction and rote memorization toward constructivist methods has left gaps in basic decoding skills.
*   **[[concepts/cognitive-impact|Cognitive Impact]]:** Reliance on digital tools for [[concepts/memory|memory]] and calculation may be eroding Working Memory and [[concepts/problem-solving-skills|Problem Solving]] capabilities.

## Case Study: Gen Alpha

Recent analysis highlights a specific crisis within Gen Alpha (born 2010–2024), characterized by significant drops in [[concepts/numeracy|numeracy]] and literacy.
