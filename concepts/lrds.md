# Galaxy Evolution

[[concepts/galaxy-formation-and-evolution|Galaxy evolution]] describes the physical and structural changes galaxies undergo over [[concepts/cosmic-time|cosmic time]], from their initial assembly in the [[concepts/early-universe]] through periods of peak activity to present-day quiescence. Recent observations have significantly revised models of when and how [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] and black holes formed.

## Early Universe Anomalies: Little Red Dots
**[[concepts/little-red-dots|Little Red Dots]]** ([[concepts/lrd-little-red-dot|LRDs]]) are hyper-luminous, compact astrophysical sources observed via [[concepts/jwst|James Webb Space Telescope]] that challenge conventional structure formation models.

- **Detection & Properties:** Identified since 2022; defined by intense flux and compact morphology distinct from standard galaxy populations.
- **X-Ray Correlation:** [[entities/jwst|JWST]] data links LRD emissions to Pre-Stellar [[concepts/black-holes|Black Hole]] Formation, suggesting these objects are powered by or associated with supermassive black hole progenitors active before star formation.
- **Chronological [[concepts/anomaly|Anomaly]]:** Observations support hypotheses that Black Hole formation preceded the ignition of the [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|first Stars]], revising timelines of cosmic dawn.

## Star Formation History: The Madau Plot and Cosmic Noon
The broad timeline of stellar assembly is mapped by the **[[concepts/madau-plot|Madau Plot]]**, which tracks the Star Formation Rate [[concepts/density|Density]] (SFRD) over redshift. This history is characterized by a rise from the cosmic dawn, a peak known as **[[concepts/cosmic-noon|Cosmic Noon]]** ($z \sim 2$), and a subsequent decline to the present day.

- **Integration with LRDs:** The discovery of high-redshift sources like LRDs suggests earlier activity than previously accounted for in standard Madau Plot extrapolations, potentially indicating rapid early black hole growth alongside or preceding initial starbursts.
