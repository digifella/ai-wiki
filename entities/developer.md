# Developer

A developer in this context is a professional who leverages Claude AI and structured documentation to streamline product development workflows. The core practice involves creating Markdown-based Product Requirements Documents (PRDs) that translate abstract product specifications into concrete, actionable tasks. This approach systematizes the conversion of high-level requirements into executable work items, thereby reducing friction and enhancing clarity in the development process.

## Workflow and Tools

The developer's workflow centers on generating task managers and specialized sub-agents directly from well-structured PRDs. Claude AI processes these documents to identify necessary components and dependencies, automatically producing the digital infrastructure required for product builds. By utilizing this method, developers can rapidly scale their operations, ensuring that every aspect of the product specification is accounted for in the resulting technical architecture.
