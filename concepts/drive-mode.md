# Drive Mode

Drive Mode is a control-locking feature available on Fujifilm digital cameras that prevents accidental adjustments to critical camera settings during shooting. When activated, the feature restricts access to specified parameters, allowing photographers to maintain consistent exposure, focus, and other settings without risk of unintended changes. This is particularly valuable during fast-paced shooting situations where the camera might otherwise be jostled or bumped, potentially altering settings mid-session.

## Practical Applications

The feature proves especially useful in situations where maintaining shooting consistency is important, such as during event photography, documentary work, or when shooting in challenging environments. By locking key settings once they are configured, photographers can focus entirely on composition and timing without worrying about inadvertent parameter shifts. This reduces the need to review and correct settings between shots.

## Scope and Limitations

The extent of Drive Mode's protection depends on the specific Fujifilm camera model, as different cameras offer varying levels of control-locking capability. While it effectively prevents accidental menu navigation and setting changes, it does not physically lock the camera body or lens, nor does it prevent intentional adjustments by the photographer when temporarily disabling the feature.
