## Fixing Document Formatting with Claude

- **Problem**: AI excels at content generation but fails at adhering to specific document formatting requirements (colors, fonts, spacing, tables), requiring manual copy-paste into [[concepts/templates|templates]] and styling [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: Implement a two-tier approach using **[[entities/claude-projects|Claude Projects]]** (for project-specific rules) and **[[concepts/instruction-reuse|Claude Skills]]** (for automated formatting enforcement) to maintain template [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] without manual intervention.
- **Reference**: [D-Squared channel video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DXDn4GCgqos) (see [[concepts/generative-ai]] for related context on AI [[concepts/writing|writing]] limitations).
