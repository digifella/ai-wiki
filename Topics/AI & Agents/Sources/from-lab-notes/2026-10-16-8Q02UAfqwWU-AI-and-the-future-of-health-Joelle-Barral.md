## AI and the future of health | Joelle Barral
**Clip title:** AI and the future of health | Joelle Barral
**Author / channel:** Google DeepMind
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8Q02UAfqwWU

### Summary
In this video, Professor Hannah Fry hosts a podcast episode featuring Joelle Barral, Senior Director of Research at Google DeepMind. The main topic of discussion is the intersection of AI and healthcare, specifically how AI is changing diagnosis and treatment. 

The key points discussed include:
- The history and evolution of AI in healthcare, starting with narrow AI for medical imaging and moving towards more comprehensive tools.
- The use of AI in diagnosing conditions like diabetic retinopathy, and the challenges of establishing "ground truth" when experts disagree.
- The potential for AI to bridge the gap between different scientific instruments and improve our understanding of the human body.
- The importance of data privacy and the challenges of ensuring accountability in AI-driven healthcare.

The conclusion and takeaway of the video is that while AI has the potential to revolutionize healthcare, it is important to approach its integration with caution and care. The goal should be to augment physicians, not replace them, and to ensure that the benefits of AI are distributed fairly and responsibly.

#### Description
In this episode, Professor Hannah Fry interviews Joelle Barral, Senior Director of Research at Google DeepMind, about AI in healthcare. They discuss existing AI applications including image analysis for diabetic retinopathy and the expansion of diagnostic tools as a result of multi-modal models. The conversation highlights AI's potential to improve healthcare delivery, personalize treatment, expand access worldwide, and ultimately, bring back the joy of practicing medicine.
