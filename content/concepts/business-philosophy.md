# Business Philosophy

A business philosophy defines an [[concepts/organization|organization]]'s core values, [[concepts/purpose|purpose]], and guiding principles for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and strategic direction. It transcends transactional goals to establish ethical, cultural, and operational foundations.

## Core Elements
- **[[concepts/motivation|Purpose]]-driven action**: Aligning operations with deeper meaning beyond profit
- **Stakeholder-centric approach**: Prioritizing customer, employee, and [[concepts/public-health|community well-being]]
- **Long-term [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]]**: Balancing immediate results with sustainable growth
- **Ethical boundaries**: Defining non-negotiable principles in all interactions
