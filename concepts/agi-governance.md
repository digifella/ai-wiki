# AGI Governance

**AGI [[concepts/governance|Governance]]** refers to the frameworks, institutions, and regulatory [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] designed to manage the development, deployment, and safety of [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|Artificial General Intelligence]]. Key challenges include aligning [[concepts/frontier-ai]] capabilities with human values, establishing international standards, and mitigating existential risks.

## Key Developments & Actors

### Demis Hassabis & DeepMind
[[entities/demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], CEO of [[entities/deepmind]] and Nobel laureate, has emerged as a central figure in shaping the discourse on AGI Governance. His recent proposals emphasize the need for new institutional structures to manage [[concepts/frontier-ai]] risks.

*   **Proposal:** "A Framework for Frontier AI and the Dawning of a New Age" outlines a roadmap for international cooperation and [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]].
*   **Institutional Focus:** Advocates for building new governance bodies capable of overseeing high-risk [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]], moving beyond voluntary guidelines to enforceable standards.
*   **[[concepts/strategic-pivot|Strategic Shift]]:** Signals a change in the AI debate, moving from theoretical safety discussions to concrete institutional design.

## Core Principles

1.  **International [[concepts/coordination|Coordination]]:** Preventing a race-to-the-bottom by establishing global norms for [[concepts/frontier-ai]] development.
2.  **Safety Standards:** Implementing rigorous testing and evaluation protocols for models approaching [[concepts/agi]] capabilities.
3.  **Institutional [[concepts/innovation|Innovation]]:** Creating dedicated bodies with the technical [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] and authority to regulate complex [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]].
