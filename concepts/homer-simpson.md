# Homer Simpson

**Homer Jay Simpson** is the patriarch of the Simpson family and the central protagonist of the animated series *[[concepts/the-simpsons]]*. He works as a safety inspector at the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant under Mr. Burns.

## Character Profile
- **Role:** Father, husband, employee.
- **Traits:** Gluttonous, lazy, impulsive, yet occasionally displays surprising competence or deep [[concepts/emotional-intelligence|emotional intelligence]].
- **Archetype:** The everyman; a critique of American consumerism and suburban complacency.
