# America

*America* is a documentary series initiated by Destin Sandlin, the creator of the YouTube channel *Smarter Every Day*. The project investigates the feasibility of manufacturing a consumer product entirely within the United States while maintaining cost competitiveness in the global marketplace. It serves as an exploration of domestic industrial capabilities and the economic constraints facing modern American manufacturing.

The documentary examines the practical and economic realities of domestic production at scale. It analyzes key factors influencing production viability, including supply chain logistics, labor costs, and regulatory compliance. By attempting to build a product from raw materials to final assembly on American soil, the series highlights the complexities involved in reshoring manufacturing processes.

Through detailed documentation of the manufacturing journey, the series provides an objective look at the challenges and opportunities inherent in domestic industrial production. It aims to inform viewers about the current state of American manufacturing infrastructure and the economic trade-offs associated with local versus global supply chains.
