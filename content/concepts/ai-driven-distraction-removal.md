# AI-driven distraction removal

AI-driven distraction removal is a feature that utilizes [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] to automatically detect and eliminate unwanted elements from digital [[concepts/images|images]], thereby enhancing the clarity and focus of the primary subject matter. This technology has [[concepts/software|applications]] across various fields including photography, graphic [[concepts/design|design]], [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]], and more.

### Key Features
- **Automatic Detection**: AI algorithms can quickly identify distracting elements such as wires, signs, or any object that detracts from the main subject.
- **Precision Editing**: Once detected, these distractions are carefully removed without affecting the surrounding context of the image. This often involves advanced [[concepts/masking|masking]] techniques to preserve fine details around edges and contours.
- **Efficiency Boost**: By automating what would otherwise be a time-consuming manual process, AI-driven removal significantly speeds up [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] in post-production editing.
