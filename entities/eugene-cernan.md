# Eugene Cernan

**Eugene [[entities/andrew-staibd|Andrew]] Cernan** (born March 14, 1934) is an American former astronaut and naval aviator who was the **last person to walk on the Moon**. He served as the Command Module Pilot for Apollo 10 and Commander for Apollo 17, the final manned mission of the [[concepts/apollo-mission|Apollo program]].

## Lunar Missions & Achievements

*   **Apollo 17 (1972):** Served as Commander; spent over 22 hours on the lunar surface in three Extra-Vehicular Activities (EVAs).
*   **[[concepts/lunar-roving-vehicle|Lunar Roving Vehicle]] (LRV):** Operated the LRV extensively during Apollo 17, facilitating extensive sample collection and terrain mapping.
    *   Set a confirmed [[concepts/speed-record|speed record]] of **17.9 km/h (11.2 mph)** while driving the rover on the Moon. This [[concepts/success|achievement]] is detailed in analyses of the [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] constraints and operational capabilities of the LRV [[lab-notes/2026-06-17-Building-NASAs-Lunar-Roving-Vehicle-Engineering-for-Apol|Building NASA's Lunar Roving Vehicle: Engineering for Apollo Moon Missions]].
*   **Apollo 10:** Conducted a dress rehearsal for a [[concepts/lunar-landing|lunar landing]], descending to within 15,000 feet of the surface before aborting to return to [[entities/earth|Earth]].

## Career Background

*   Trained as a naval aviator; flew over 300 jet sorties in Vietnam.
*   Selected as an astronaut by [[entities/nasa]] in 1963.
*   Retired from [[concepts/nasa|NASA]] in 1976 and the Navy in 1976 with the rank of Commander.
