# Eu Ai Act

The EU AI Act is the [[entities/eu|European Union]]'s comprehensive [[concepts/regulatory-framework|regulatory framework]] governing the development, [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], and use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems. Enacted to ensure [[concepts/safe-ai-use|AI safety]], transparency, and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]], it establishes risk-based classifications for [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] and sets requirements for [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] across member states.

## Multi-Agent Orchestration

One emerging trend in AI system [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] involves multi-agent orchestration, where [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] work together under a coordinating orchestrator layer. These systems typically consist of distinct roles—such as a Planner that designs tasks, Workers that execute them, and a Critic that evaluates results—operating in concert to achieve complex objectives more effectively than single-agent approaches.

## Industry Perspectives

Industry analysts, including IBM, have identified [[concepts/expertise-based-ai-assistants|multi-agent systems]] and orchestration as key trends shaping the near-term future of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]]. These predictions reflect growing recognition that collaborative agent architectures may offer practical advantages for handling increasingly complex real-world problems within regulated environments like those defined by the EU AI Act.
