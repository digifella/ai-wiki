# Blacks Sliders

The [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] slider is a fundamental adjustment tool in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] that targets the darkest tones in an image. Unlike the broader [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]] slider, which affects the entire tonal [[concepts/range|range]], the Blacks slider specifically adjusts shadow regions while leaving midtones and [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] relatively unchanged. This selective [[concepts/power|control]] allows photographers to deepen shadow areas, increase [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], or recover detail in the darkest parts of an image without overexposing brighter areas.

## Function and Effect

Moving the Blacks slider to the right darkens the shadow regions by remapping the [[concepts/black-point-adjustment|black point]] of the image, effectively crushing darker tones and increasing overall contrast. Moving it to the left brightens [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] and can reveal hidden detail in underexposed areas. The adjustment operates on a localized tonal range, making it particularly useful for [[concepts/landscape-photography|landscape photography]] where controlling the relationship between sky and foreground is essential. The slider's effect is most pronounced in areas already in shadow, with minimal impact on midtones and highlights.

## Masking and Targeted Adjustments

Modern versions of Lightroom and Camera Raw include [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that allow the Blacks slider to be applied selectively to specific regions of an image. Photographers can use luminance masks, color range masks, or drawn masks to apply Blacks [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] only to certain areas—such as darkening just the foreground while preserving sky detail, or adjusting individual landscape elements. This targeted approach prevents unwanted side effects and provides greater creative control during the post-processing [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].
