# Video Editing

Video editing is the process of manipulating and rearranging video footage, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and other media elements to create a finished film or video product. It involves selecting clips, arranging them in sequence, adjusting timing and transitions, and integrating [[concepts/audio|audio]], visual effects, and color correction. Video editing can range from basic trimming and concatenation of clips to complex multi-track projects with layered effects and sophisticated post-production techniques.

## AI-Assisted Video Editing
