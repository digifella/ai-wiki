# 3D Modeling

## Overview
[[concepts/3d-reconstruction|3D modeling]] is the process of developing a [[concepts/large-number-management|mathematical representation]] of any three-dimensional surface, object, or volume using specialized software. It is foundational to [[concepts/computer-graphics|computer graphics]], game development, architecture, and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]].

## AI Integration & Open Source Tools
The landscape of 3D modeling is shifting towards democratization through [[concepts/open-source-ai|open-source AI]] projects, enabling [[concepts/local-ai-model|local LLM]] management and AI-native workflows.

*   **Democratization of Tools**: Recent trends highlight the rise of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] projects that make powerful AI capabilities accessible locally, reducing reliance on cloud-based proprietary services [[lab-notes/2026-08-18-Six-Trending-Open-Source-AI-Projects-Local-LLMs-and-AI-A|Six Trending Open-Source AI Projects: Local LLMs and AI Agents]].
*   **[[concepts/ai-native-communication|AI-Native Communication]]**: New frameworks are emerging that integrate [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] directly into the modeling pipeline, facilitating more intuitive interaction with 3D assets.
*   **Local LLM Management**: Tools for running [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] locally are increasingly being adapted to assist with procedural generation, code-based modeling (e.g., OpenSCAD), and asset [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] management.
