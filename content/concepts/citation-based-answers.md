# Citation Based Answers

Citation Based Answers refers to the [[concepts/integration|integration]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] between [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], two [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] that work together to generate [[concepts/responses|responses]] grounded in specific source documents. This approach enables users to receive answers that are directly tied to citations from their uploaded materials, reducing [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] and improving [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] in AI-generated content.

## Workflow Overview

The integration allows users to input [[concepts/notes|notes]], documents, or other reference materials into [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], which then leverages [[entities/google-ai-ultra|Google Gemini]]'s [[concepts/language-capabilities|language capabilities]] to process and understand the content. When queries are submitted, the system generates answers with explicit citations pointing back to the original source material, creating a traceable chain between the response and its supporting evidence.

## Practical Applications

This citation-based approach is particularly valuable for research, [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and [[concepts/automated-synthesis|knowledge synthesis]] tasks where verifiable sources are essential. By combining NotebookLM's [[concepts/document-management|document management]] with [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]'s generative capabilities, users can efficiently extract insights from their materials while maintaining transparency about where information originates. The workflow essentially transforms passive document collections into interactive, cite-able [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]].
