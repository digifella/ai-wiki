# Hugh Wilson

**Hugh Wilson** is a botanist known for his unconventional approach to [[concepts/ecosystem-restoration|ecosystem restoration]], specifically regarding the management of [[concepts/invasive-species|invasive species]] in New Zealand.

## Key Contributions & Methods

- **Gorse-Based Rewilding**: Wilson challenged conventional wisdom by refusing to eradicate [[concepts/gorse]] (*Ulex europaeus*) on [[concepts/degraded-farmland|degraded farmland]]. Instead, he utilized the thorny weed as a foundational component for rewilding efforts.
- **[[entities/banks-peninsula|Banks Peninsula]] Project**: Successfully rewilded a large tract of degraded land on [[concepts/banks-peninsula]]. His method demonstrated that retaining gorse facilitated the return of native [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]], resulting in unexpected [[concepts/habitat-recovery|ecological recovery]].
- **[[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]]**: Advocates for working with existing invasive structures rather than immediate removal, arguing that this approach accelerates the [[concepts/preservation|restoration]] of native ecosystems.
