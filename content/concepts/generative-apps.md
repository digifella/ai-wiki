# Generative Apps

Generative Apps represent [[concepts/software|applications]] built on or enhanced by [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]], particularly [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and multimodal systems. These applications leverage generative capabilities to create content, automate workflows, or provide intelligent assistance across various domains. [[entities/gemini-models|Google Gemini]] serves as a significant platform in this space, offering both API access and integrated tools for developers building generative applications.

## Google Gemini Integration

Google Gemini has become a central component in the generative apps ecosystem, with regular updates expanding its capabilities and integration options. These updates introduce new features and tools designed to improve the functionality and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of generative applications built on the platform. The evolution of [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] reflects broader trends in making generative AI more practical and efficient for production use.

## Market Considerations

The generative apps market involves trade-offs between different platforms and solutions. Cost remains a significant factor for developers and organizations implementing these systems, with various providers offering different [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] models and performance characteristics. As the market matures, multiple competing platforms continue to develop their offerings, each with distinct advantages and limitations for different [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
