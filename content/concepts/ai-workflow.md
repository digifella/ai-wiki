## AI workflow

A systematic sequence of interactions between users, [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]], and [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] designed to transform raw, unstructured inputs into reliable, [[concepts/structured-output]].

### Core Components
- [[entities/prompt-engineering]]: The [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to [[concepts/power|control]] [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] and minimize error.
- [[concepts/structured-output]]: The conversion of "messy" or vague [[concepts/text|text]] into organized, consistent, and actionable formats.
- [[concepts/tool-orchestration|Tool Orchestration]]: The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of multiple AI platforms (e.g., [[entities/notebooklm]], [[entities/gemini]]) to automate multi-step [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and refinement [[concepts/loops|loops]].
