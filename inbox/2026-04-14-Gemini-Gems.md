# [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] Gems

---
---
<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=G72t8gJqKGs>
This video provides a comprehensive guide on leveraging [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]]'s "Gems" feature to create [[concepts/specialized-ai-assistants|specialized AI assistants]], moving beyond basic chat interactions. The presenter, [[entities/alicia|Alicia]], demonstrates how to build and utilize two powerful custom Gems: a [[concepts/meeting-prep-strategist|Meeting Prep Strategist]] and an Automated Weekly Business Report generator, highlighting the significant time and [[concepts/stress|stress]] savings they offer.
**Detailed [[concepts/summary|Summary]]:**
The video begins by pointing out that most users only scratch the surface of [[entities/google|Google]] [[entities/gemini|Gemini]]'s [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] by treating it as a regular chat. Alicia introduces "Gems" as a feature that allows users to build their own specialized AI assistants, pre-loaded with [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]], capable of remembering specific needs and delivering consistent, professional results. She likens these Gems to having personal [[entities/ai-specialists|AI specialists]] or consultants on standby.
Alicia then walks through the exact process of creating two distinct Gems:

1. **Meeting Prep Strategist:**
	**Problem:** Generic AI chats require re-explaining context for every new task. **[[concepts/solution|Solution]]:** A custom Gem can be programmed to act as a world-class strategic advisor for meeting [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. **Building Process:** She names the Gem "Meeting Prep Strategist" and inputs a detailed prompt that defines its persona, initial interaction requirements (asking for user's role, company, other party, meeting goal), desired output format (potential challenges, data-driven talking points, insightful questions, opening statements), and [[concepts/tone|tone]]. The option to upload knowledge [[concepts/files|files]] for reference is also shown. **Demonstration:** Alicia simulates preparing for a meeting between a small-batch coffee company, "Artisan Roast Collective," and a Whole Foods buyer, "Sarah Jenkins," to [[concepts/secure|secure]] a deal. By providing initial details and uploading relevant company and individual profiles, the Gem generates a comprehensive briefing document that includes anticipated challenges (e.g., margin pressure, scalability, competition), strategic talking points, and compelling [[concepts/responses|responses]]. **Benefit:** This Gem eliminates the [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]] and hours of research typically spent on meeting preparation, providing surgical precision in advice.
	
2. **Automated Weekly Business Report:**
	**Problem:** Manual weekly data analysis from various sources (sales, social media, customer [[concepts/feedback|feedback]]) is tedious, time-consuming, and prone to missing key insights. **Solution:** An automated business analyst Gem that synthesizes scattered data into a coherent and insightful weekly report. **Building Process:** She names the Gem "Automated Weekly Business Report" and provides [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] for its core role (elite-level business analyst), process (reading [[concepts/unstructured-text|unstructured text]] data), and required output format (Executive Summary, Key Performance [[concepts/indicators|Indicators]], Analysis of Wins, Opportunities for Improvement, Strategic Recommendations). **Demonstration:** Alicia uploads three messy documents containing sales transactions, social media analytics, and customer feedback for a fictional bakery called "The Gilded Bakery." **Benefit:** The Gem instantly generates a professional business intelligence briefing, providing actionable insights and eliminating hours of Sunday night data analysis and the stress associated with it.
	

Finally, Alicia provides four pro tips for building effective Gems:

1. **One Mission, One Gem:** Focus on [[concepts/specialization|specialization]], not generalization.
2. **Context is Everything:** Use examples for creative tasks and knowledge files for factual tasks.
3. **Test and Iterate:** Refine your Gem through [[concepts/testing|testing]] to improve its performance.
4. **No [[concepts/memory|Memory]] Between Chats:** Each conversation starts fresh, so instructions must be comprehensive.

The video concludes by encouraging viewers to download the detailed instructions for building these Gems from a pinned comment and to support the channel.
