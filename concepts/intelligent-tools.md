# Intelligent Tools

Intelligent Tools refers to a suite of AI-powered applications and features integrated across Google's product ecosystem. These tools leverage generative AI capabilities to enhance productivity, content creation, and information processing across different domains. By embedding AI assistance directly into existing platforms, Google has made these capabilities more accessible to users working within familiar workflows rather than requiring separate applications.

## Key Products

Google Vids is a video creation tool that uses generative AI to automate aspects of video production, including script generation, footage selection, and editing suggestions. NotebookLM enables users to interact with their own documents and research materials through an AI-powered interface, allowing for summarization, question-answering, and synthesis of information. Google AI Studio provides developers and creators with access to generative models and tools for building custom AI applications without requiring extensive machine learning expertise.

## Integration and Accessibility

Rather than presenting AI as a standalone product category, these tools are designed to function within Google's existing applications and services. This approach prioritizes integration with user workflows in productivity, research, and creative work. The tools reflect a broader strategy of making generative AI capabilities available across multiple touchpoints in the Google ecosystem, from workspace applications to development platforms.
