# 2026 04 11 My Korean Mom Said This Dak Bulgogi Recipe Is Perfect Eric Kim Nyt

This is a dak bulgogi recipe published by Eric Kim in the New York Times Cooking section, developed with input and validation from Kim's mother. Dak bulgogi is a Korean marinated chicken dish that features tender, caramelized meat seasoned with a sweet and savory sauce. The recipe represents a family-approved interpretation of this traditional preparation.

## Recipe Context

Eric Kim is a food writer and cookbook author known for recipes that bridge Korean culinary traditions with American home cooking practices. By incorporating his mother's perspective and expertise, the recipe aims to provide an authentic approach to dak bulgogi while remaining accessible to home cooks. The involvement of a Korean mother figure in validating the recipe suggests an emphasis on traditional technique and flavor balance.

## Preparation Method

Dak bulgogi typically involves marinating chicken pieces in a mixture that commonly includes soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, ginger, and other aromatics before cooking. The marinade allows the meat to absorb flavors and develop the characteristic caramelized exterior associated with the dish. The recipe would be suited for grilling, pan-searing, or broiling depending on available cooking equipment.
