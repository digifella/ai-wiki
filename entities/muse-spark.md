# Muse Spark

Muse Spark is a proprietary artificial intelligence model developed by Meta, introduced in April 2026. The model represents a strategic shift in Meta's AI development approach, marking a departure from the company's previous emphasis on open-source systems through its Llama model line. As a proprietary offering, Muse Spark is positioned as a successor to or replacement for Llama in Meta's portfolio.

## Development and Positioning

The development of Muse Spark reflects changes in Meta's broader AI strategy during the mid-2020s. Rather than maintaining the open-source model that characterized earlier Llama releases, the company chose to develop Muse Spark as a closed, proprietary system. This transition was notably discussed by AI researcher Sam Witteveen, who analyzed the implications of Meta's strategic pivot away from open-source AI development.
