# Metal Detectors

Metal detectors are electronic instruments designed to identify metallic objects buried beneath soil and other surfaces. They operate by generating electromagnetic fields that interact with conductive materials, producing audible or visual signals when metal is detected. This technology serves diverse applications across multiple sectors, including security screening, treasure hunting, and artifact recovery.

In the field of archaeology, the device has facilitated a distinctive approach in Denmark, where the government has deputized private metal detectorists to locate and excavate historical artifacts. This policy leverages the widespread use of the technology among hobbyists to uncover items buried in farm fields and other landscapes, integrating civilian efforts into national heritage preservation.
