# Character Generation

Character Generation is a feature within [[entities/vyond-go|Vyond Go]] that enables users to create or select characters prior to initiating AI-assisted video production. This process serves as a foundational step in the [[concepts/script-building|video creation workflow]], allowing creators to establish a distinct visual identity and maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] throughout their generated content. By defining the visual elements early, users ensure that the resulting video aligns with their specific [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] and aesthetic requirements.

Users can choose from a library of pre-designed [[concepts/stock-characters|stock characters]] or create custom characters tailored to their specific needs. Stock characters provide a quick starting point for video production, offering a variety of options without the need for manual design. Conversely, the custom [[concepts/3d-character|character creation]] tool allows for greater flexibility, enabling users to design unique avatars that fit specific brand guidelines or story contexts. This dual approach ensures that both [[concepts/rapid-prototyping|rapid prototyping]] and detailed [[concepts/customization|customization]] are supported within the platform.
