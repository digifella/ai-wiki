# Contextual Prompting

Contextual [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] is a technique for customizing AI language [[concepts/models|models]] to produce [[concepts/output|output]] tailored to specific professional domains and [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. By providing detailed [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] to models like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], users can establish persistent context that shapes how the AI responds to subsequent queries. This approach allows legal professionals and other specialists to optimize [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] for their particular needs without repeatedly explaining requirements in individual prompts.

## Application in Legal Work

Legal professionals use contextual prompting to configure [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] with domain-specific guidelines, [[concepts/terminology|terminology]] preferences, and procedural requirements relevant to their practice. Custom [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] might specify the jurisdiction being addressed, preferred citation formats, required disclaimer language, or the level of formality expected in output. By embedding these [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] into the model's configuration, lawyers can rely on more consistent and professionally appropriate [[concepts/responses|responses]] across multiple sessions and conversations.

## Implementation

Most major AI platforms now offer custom instructions or [[concepts/system-prompt|system prompt]] features that allow users to define how the model should behave. These settings typically persist across conversations, reducing the need to re-establish context with each new query. Effective contextual prompting requires clarity about the specific outputs needed and awareness of the model's [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and limitations within professional contexts.
