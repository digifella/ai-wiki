# Electronic Health Records

**Electronic [[concepts/patient-information|Health Records]] (EHRs)** are digital versions of patients' paper charts, encompassing the comprehensive medical history, diagnoses, medications, treatment plans, [[concepts/vaccination|immunization]] dates, allergies, radiology images, and laboratory test results. They aim to improve patient safety, efficiency, and [[concepts/quality-of-care|quality of care]] through Data Interoperability and [[concepts/secure|secure]] Health [[concepts/data-management|Data Management]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Comprehensiveness**: Includes clinical [[concepts/notes|notes]], lab results, medication lists, and vital signs.
- **Interoperability**: Ability to exchange information between different [[concepts/health|healthcare]] systems using standards like HL7 or FHIR.
