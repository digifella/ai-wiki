Union labor refers to collective worker [[concepts/organization|organization]] through labor unions to negotiate wages, benefits, and working conditions with employers. It has historically driven key labor reforms including the eight-hour workday, workplace [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]], and minimum wage legislation in the [[entities/united-states|United States]].

- **[[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]]'s "[[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]" project** (2026): [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]] host documented a [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] manufacturing a grill brush entirely in [[entities/america|America]] while remaining competitive. The project was motivated by his parents' careers as [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]], demonstrating how Union labor-affiliated manufacturing supports domestic industry [[concepts/resilience|resilience]] and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]].
