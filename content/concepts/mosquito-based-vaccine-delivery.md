# Mosquito-based vaccine delivery

The use of engineered Mosquito vectors to deliver immunogens or therapeutic [[concepts/agents|agents]] to hosts during blood-feeding.

## Key Developments
- [[concepts/malaria-vaccination|Malaria vaccination]] via mosquitos: Experimental observations indicate that animals achieve lifelong protection from reinfection, despite the drug used having an extremely short half-life.
