# Video Generation

Creation of video content using [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], typically involving [[concepts/text-to-video|text-to-video]] synthesis, script generation, and tool [[concepts/integration|integration]]. Key workflows [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] assistants for scripting and specialized video generators for [[concepts/output|output]].

## Key Workflow Integration
- **Content Extraction**: Pull [[concepts/business-philosophy|business philosophy]] or narrative from [[entities/notebooklm]] as foundation
- **Script Development**: Use [[concepts/gemini]]'s "[[entities/canvas|Canvas]]" (Advanced/Pro) for iterative script refinement
- **Video Synthesis**: Generate cinematic 30-second ads via [[entities/google-veo|Google Veo]]
- **[[concepts/asset-management|Asset Management]]**: Store and transfer assets through [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/motivation|Drive]]
- **Step-by-Step Guide**: Turn a [[entities/notebooklm]] [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] into a 30-second [[concepts/video-commercial|video commercial]] using [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]] and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] (Advanced/Pro) (2026 04 14 Building a video from [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] and [[concepts/veo|Google Veo]] Charles Terence [[entities/harper|Harper]] channel)
