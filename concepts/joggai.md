# Joggai

Joggai is an automated software tool designed to convert audio podcasts into video episodes. The platform addresses a workflow challenge common in digital media production, where creators maintain separate distribution channels for audio and video formats of the same content. By automating the technical conversion process, Joggai reduces the manual labor traditionally required to repurpose podcast audio into video-ready content.

## Functionality

The tool streamlines the conversion of audio podcast files into video formats suitable for platforms that prioritize or require visual content. Rather than requiring creators to manually produce accompanying visuals, edit video files, or manage separate production workflows, Joggai automates significant portions of this process. This is particularly relevant for AI-generated podcasts, where the absence of original video footage would otherwise necessitate the creation of supplementary visual materials or static imagery.

## Practical Application

By enabling efficient conversion between audio and video modalities, Joggai helps content creators extend the reach of their podcasts across multiple distribution platforms without proportionally increasing production effort. This automation represents a practical solution to managing content across the diverse format preferences of different media platforms and audience segments.
