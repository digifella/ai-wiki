# Instagram Carousel Design

Instagram carousel designs are multi-slide posts that allow users to swipe through sequential [[concepts/images|images]] or graphics. They are commonly used for [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]], step-by-step tutorials, before-and-after comparisons, and product showcases. The format typically accommodates 2-10 [[entities/google-slides|slides]] per carousel post.

## Creating Carousels with Claude and Canva

[[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] can assist in the carousel design process by generating content concepts, [[concepts/writing|writing]] accompanying captions, and suggesting visual layouts and design approaches. Canva provides the design tools and [[concepts/templates|templates]] needed to create individual carousel slides. The integration between Claude and Canva allows designers to move quickly from concept to finished design—Claude can help outline carousel [[concepts/structure|structure]] and messaging while Canva handles the visual execution.

## Workflow Considerations

An effective [[concepts/instagram-carousel-creation|carousel design workflow]] involves planning the narrative arc across slides, maintaining consistent branding elements throughout, and optimizing text and imagery for mobile viewing. Claude can help organize this planning phase, while Canva's interface manages the actual design production. This combination reduces the time spent on initial ideation and design decisions, making it practical for regular social media [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]].
