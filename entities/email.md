# Email

On 12 February 2026, an email containing allegations regarding governance matters within the Royal Australasian College of Physicians (RACP) was distributed to Fellows and trainees. The communication utilized institutional email addresses for its dissemination without apparent authorization from College leadership.

The College Board expressed concern regarding the unauthorized use of these email addresses. The incident prompted scrutiny of the organization's email security practices and access controls within its communication systems.
