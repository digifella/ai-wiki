# Dreamdojo Ai

Dreamdojo AI is an artificial intelligence system developed by NVIDIA designed to address the simulation-to-reality gap in robotic task execution. This gap represents a fundamental challenge in robotics, where AI models trained in simulated environments often fail to perform reliably when deployed on physical robots due to discrepancies between virtual physics and real-world conditions. The system aims to bridge this divide by enabling robots to execute complex tasks more effectively in real-world conditions despite being trained primarily in simulation.

The technology focuses on enhancing the transferability of learned policies from digital twins to physical hardware. By refining how robots interpret and adapt to the nuances of physical environments, Dreamdojo AI seeks to reduce the reliance on extensive real-world data collection for training. This approach allows for more scalable development of robotic systems capable of handling intricate operations that require high precision and adaptability.

NVIDIA's development of this system underscores the growing emphasis on robust simulation frameworks in the advancement of autonomous robotics. By mitigating the performance drop-off that typically occurs when moving from simulation to reality, the technology supports the deployment of more capable and versatile robotic agents in industrial and commercial applications.
