# Persona Training

Persona Training is a [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] process applied to the [[entities/oss-20b|OSS-20B]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]]. The technique involves adjusting the model's [[concepts/weights|weights]] to customize its behavior and [[concepts/responses|responses]], enabling it to adopt specific personas or communication styles. This approach allows developers and researchers to adapt [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] for particular [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] without retraining from scratch.

## Tutorial and Documentation

A comprehensive tutorial on Persona Training was documented by [[entities/fahd-mirza|Fahd Mirza]], providing step-by-step guidance for practitioners. The tutorial demonstrates practical methods for fine-tuning OSS-20B's weights and is available as a video resource, making the process accessible to those looking to customize open-source language models for their [[concepts/software|applications]].
