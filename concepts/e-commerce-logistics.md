# E-commerce Logistics

## Definition
The end-to-end process of managing the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of goods, information, and finances from suppliers to customers in online retail environments. Key components include [[concepts/inventory-management|inventory management]], order [[concepts/happiness|fulfillment]], last-mile delivery, and reverse logistics.

## Strategic Dimensions
Modern e-commerce logistics has evolved beyond pure commercial efficiency to become a critical component of [[concepts/export-control|national security]] and economic [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], particularly in [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] zones.

### Geopolitical Impact on Supply Chains
Recent developments highlight the intersection of digital commerce and asymmetric warfare:

- **Targeting of Logistics Hubs**: Civilian e-commerce [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] is increasingly viewed as strategic military assets. The targeting of major logistics nodes disrupts both consumer access and potential dual-use [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]].
- **Case Study: Wildberries in [[entities/russia|Russia]]**: The Russian e-commerce giant Wildberries has become a focal point in the ongoing conflict. Its extensive warehouse network serves as a critical [[entities/nodejs|node]] for domestic distribution but also as a [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] in long-range strike campaigns.
- **Ukrainian Counter-Strategy**: [[entities/ukraine|Ukraine]] has implemented a strategy targeting Russian e-commerce logistics to degrade economic stability and logistical capacity. This involves [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] strikes on warehouses and distribution centers, aiming to disrupt the flow of goods and increase [[concepts/operational-costs|operational costs]] for Russian [[concepts/nodes|entities]].
- **[[concepts/economic-warfare|Economic Warfare]]**: Disrupting e-commerce logistics serves as a form of economic warfare, impacting civilian morale and the financial viability of major Russian corporations.

## Key Entities
- Wildberries: Largest online retailer in Russia, heavily impacted by recent logistics disruptions.
- Ukrainian Armed Forces: Conducting long-range strikes against logistical targets.
- Russian Federation: Subject of logistical degradation strategies.
