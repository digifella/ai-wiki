# Alka Kolthari

Alka Kolthari is a researcher whose work examines paternal involvement during childbirth and the broader experiences of fathers within maternity care settings. Her research explores how men experience attending births and the psychological dimensions of their presence during the delivery process.

Kolthari's documented research has given particular attention to cases involving traumatic birth outcomes and their impact on fathers. Her work addresses gaps in understanding the emotional experiences of men during childbirth and how maternity services can be enhanced to better support them.

## Research Areas

Kolthari's work focuses on the psychological impact of traumatic birth events on fathers. She investigates the specific challenges men face when attending births that result in negative outcomes, aiming to improve service delivery and support mechanisms for fathers in these contexts.
