# NotebookLM Update Overview

The video details major updates to [[entities/googles-notebooklm|Google's NotebookLM]], focusing on the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of **[[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|Deep Research]]** and **[[entities/nano-banana-pro|Nano Banana Pro]]** (for visuals), alongside pro tips for getting better results.

## 1\. [[concepts/source-discovery|Source Discovery]] & Deep Research

NotebookLM now offers two ways to find sources within the tool if you don't have your own documents:

* **Fast Research:** The existing feature that quickly finds ~10 sources.
* **Deep Research (New):** An agentic tool integrated from [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]]. It autonomously researches a topic, analyzes findings, adapts its strategy to fill gaps, and generates a comprehensive research report.
	* **Key Feature:** It automatically imports the sources it used (selecting the top ~20 relevant ones) directly into your [[concepts/notebook|notebook]].
	* **QoL Update:** You can now remove all failed/paywalled sources with a single click rather than deleting them individually.

## 2\. Pro Tip: Validating Sources (The 3 Prompts)

To ensure the AI output is balanced and not hallucinating based on biased data, the host suggests asking these three questions to identify blind spots in your sources:

1. _"Identify any areas my sources disagree on, and any contradictions between them."_
2. _"Identify gaps in my sources - what's missing that would be necessary to understanding the topic fully?"_
3. _"Are there any contrarian, alternative, or lesser-known viewpoints that are not covered in these sources?"_

## 3\. The Studio: Visual Updates (Nano Banana Pro)

The Studio panel has been updated with powerful [[concepts/visual-generation|visual generation]] tools powered by Nano Banana Pro.

### **Infographics**

* **Function:** Generates high-quality, dense one-page visuals based on your sources.
* **[[concepts/customization|Customization]]:** You can choose the format (landscape/portrait), level of detail (concise/standard/detailed), and [[concepts/style|style]] (via text prompt).
* **[[concepts/use-cases|Use Cases]]:** Educational summaries, visualizing complex timelines (e.g., [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence|AGI]] predictions), or creative uses like visualizing a resume/LinkedIn profile or D&D lore.

### **[[concepts/slide-decks|Slide Decks]]**

* **Formats:**
	* **Detailed Deck:** Full text and details, suitable for reading alone.
	* **Presenter Slides:** Clean visuals with key talking points, designed for live presentations.
* **Capability:** The AI generates structured slides with accurate graphs, relevant imagery, and coherent narrative flow based entirely on the notebook's sources.

### **Video Overview Update**

* The "Deep Dive" [[concepts/audio-overview|audio overview]] feature now supports **Video**.
* It generates an explainer video with a realistic AI host voice.
* **New Feature:** You can now customize the visual style of the video (e.g., "Retro 80s computer interface," "[[concepts/whiteboard|Whiteboard]]," etc.) to match the topic.

## 4\. Chat & Configuration Updates

* **[[concepts/conversation-history|Chat History]]:** Chats are now **automatically saved**. You no longer lose the conversation if you navigate away. You can also convert a saved chat into a new Source for the notebook.
* **Configure Chat:** A reminder of an often-overlooked feature where users can define the AI's persona and goal (e.g., "Act as a strict editor," "Act as a travel planner," or "Act as a debate partner").
