# Open-Source AI

**[[concepts/open-source|Open-source AI]]** refers to [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] models, tools, and datasets whose source code and [[concepts/parameters|weights]] are publicly available for inspection, modification, and redistribution. This paradigm contrasts with proprietary closed-source models, enabling community-driven [[concepts/innovation|innovation]], [[concepts/opacity|transparency]], and [[concepts/local-control|local deployment]] capabilities.

## Key Developments

*   **[[entities/meta|Meta]]'s Strategic Re-entry:** Meta has re-entered the open models domain with significant releases aimed at [[concepts/access-democratization|democratizing access]] to [[concepts/frontier-ai-capability|advanced AI capabilities]].
*   **[[entities/meta-muse-glimmer-30b|Meta Muse Glimmer 30B]]:** A key [[concepts/deployment|release]] in Meta's open-source strategy, providing [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] models for community use.
*   **[[entities/nous-research|Nous Research]]'s [[concepts/hermes-agent|Hermes Agent]]:** Introduces automated recurring task capabilities via its `/loop` feature, enhancing [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]].
*   **Market [[concepts/consolidation|Consolidation]] Risks:** The potential acquisition of [[entities/hugging-face|Hugging Face]] by [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]] for an estimated $12.9 billion has sparked debate regarding the future of open-source [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and centralization.
