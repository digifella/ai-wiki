# AI Troubleshooting

**AI Troubleshooting** refers to the methodology of leveraging [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to diagnose, analyze, and resolve complex technical issues, particularly those involving persistent software instability or data [[concepts/bribery|corruption]]. This approach often involves granting [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] direct access to system logs, [[concepts/catalog]] structures, or application states to identify patterns invisible to manual inspection.

## Case Study: Adobe Lightroom Classic Instability

A prominent example of this methodology is documented in the analysis of persistent crashes in [[concepts/lightroom-desktop|Adobe Lightroom Classic]].

- **Source:** [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-AI-Driven-Troubleshooting-of-Persistent-Adobe-Lightroom|AI-Driven Troubleshooting of Persistent Adobe Lightroom Classic Crashes]]
- **Context:** The [[concepts/creator|creator]] experienced debilitating, inexplicable freezes and crashes for two years during routine tasks (copying develop settings, rating, module switching).
- **Methodology:** The video documents a journey where AI was granted direct access to the [[concepts/lightroom-catalog]] to diagnose the root cause.
- **Key Insight:** AI-driven analysis can uncover deep-seated catalog corruption or [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] conflicts that standard troubleshooting steps miss.
