# Kevin Patrick Robbins

Kevin Patrick Robbins is a professional photographer specializing in advertising, editorial, and commercial photography. He is known for his work in visual [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]] across these commercial and creative domains.

## Perspective on AI Prompting

Robbins has publicly discussed the limitations of current [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) usage in professional contexts. He argues that many users receive [[concepts/superficial-results|superficial results]] from these tools because they rely on overly simplistic prompts. His commentary suggests that more thoughtful and detailed [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] is necessary for professionals to extract meaningful value from AI systems in [[concepts/business-applications|business applications]].
