# Portable SSDs

Portable solid-state drives (SSDs) are external [[entities/storage|storage]] devices that connect to iPads and other devices via [[concepts/usb-30|USB-C]] ports. These drives offer substantially faster data transfer speeds compared to older external hard drives, making them practical for users who need to move large [[concepts/files|files]] or access data directly from the device. Portable SSDs are typically compact and lightweight, designed to be easily carried alongside tablets and laptops.

## Functionality with iPads

When connected to an [[entities/ipad|iPad]] via USB-C, portable SSDs appear as [[concepts/external-storage|external storage]] within compatible file management [[concepts/software|applications]]. This [[concepts/connection|connection]] allows users to transfer files between the iPad and the [[concepts/ambition|drive]], back up data, or in some cases access files directly from the SSD without copying them to the iPad's internal storage first. The [[concepts/speed|speed]] and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of SSDs [[entities/make|make]] this approach more efficient than using cloud storage for certain workflows, particularly when working with large video files, [[concepts/design|design]] assets, or other bandwidth-intensive projects.

## Productivity Applications

Portable SSDs enhance [[concepts/ipad-productivity-workflows|iPad productivity]] by providing on-device storage expansion without relying on internet connectivity. Content creators, photographers, and professionals working with large datasets benefit from the ability to manage files locally and maintain backups. The combination of iPad's portability and an external SSD creates a flexible [[concepts/setup|setup]] for fieldwork, remote editing, and collaborative projects where offline access to files is necessary.
