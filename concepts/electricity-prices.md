# Electricity Prices

## Overview
Electricity [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] dynamics are increasingly influenced by the integration of [[concepts/renewable-energy|renewable energy]] sources and [[entities/storage|storage]] technologies. In Australia, the proliferation of rooftop solar and grid-scale batteries is fundamentally altering traditional supply-demand curves, leading to structural changes in wholesale and retail pricing models.

## Key Drivers of Price Reduction
Recent developments highlight a shift in how electricity costs are determined, particularly in regions with high solar penetration.

- **Transformation of the Duck Curve**: The traditional "duck curve" phenomenon, where mid-day solar generation suppresses prices and evening peaks [[concepts/ambition|drive]] them up, is being mitigated by [[concepts/battery-storage|battery storage]].
- **Role of Battery Storage**: Grid-scale and household batteries are smoothing out supply fluctuations, effectively "killing" the extreme price volatility associated with the duck curve.
- **Impact on Households**: Most households and small businesses are experiencing lower electricity prices due to this stabilization.
- **Expert Analysis**: Mechanical engineer [[entities/rosie-barnes|Rosie Barnes]] has documented these shifts, attributing the price drops directly to the transformative impact of [[concepts/battery-technology|battery technology]] on the grid.
