# Lighting Control

Lighting [[concepts/power|Control]] is a technique for precise manipulation of lighting elements in AI-generated [[concepts/images|images]] using JSON-structured prompts with [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] model. The method leverages JSON formatting to provide detailed, machine-readable [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] that enable fine-grained control over lighting properties such as intensity, direction, [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]], and shadow placement. This approach represents an evolution in [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] that moves beyond [[concepts/natural-language-descriptions|natural language descriptions]] to [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]] formats.

## Technical Implementation

The technique works by embedding JSON control structures within prompts to [[entities/gemini-app|Gemini]], allowing users to specify lighting [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] with greater precision than traditional [[concepts/natural-language-prompting|text-based prompting]]. This method has been demonstrated through various video tutorials and documented [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]], particularly in the context of [[entities/gemini-nano-banana-2|Gemini Nano Banana 2]] workflows. The [[concepts/json-format|JSON format]] enables systematic control over multiple lighting variables simultaneously, reducing [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] in how the AI interprets lighting requests.

## Practical Applications

Lighting Control is particularly useful for creative professionals working on consistent visual styles across multiple images, product photography mockups, and scenarios requiring specific atmospheric conditions. The technique complements [[concepts/non-destructive-editing|non-destructive editing]] workflows similar to those found in [[concepts/advanced-tools|professional tools]] like [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], offering computational control [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the generation stage rather than post-processing stage.
