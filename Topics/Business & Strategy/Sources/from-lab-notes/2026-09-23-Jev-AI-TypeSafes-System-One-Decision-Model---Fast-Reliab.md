## Jev AI: TypeSafe's System One Decision Model - Fast, Reliable, Cost-Efficient
**Clip title:** Build Anything with Jev, Here’s How
**Author / channel:** David Ondrej
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=f6We53TnkbU

### Summary
The video introduces Jev, a new AI model from [[entities/typesafe-ai|TypeSafe AI]], positioning it as a groundbreaking development that represents a "completely different class of [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]]." The presenter, [[entities/david-ondrej|David Ondrej]], claims Jev is generating significant buzz, likening its current phase to being early to [[entities/gpt-3|GPT-3]]. He asserts his own extensive experience in building AI software that has generated substantial revenue, lending credibility to his claims about Jev's transformative potential. The core message is that Jev offers unparalleled speed, [[concepts/cost-efficiency|cost-efficiency]], and reliability, paving the way for a new era of AI-powered software.

Technically, Jev is described as a "[[concepts/system-one-architecture|System One]] [[concepts/structured-decision|Structured Decision]] Model" that fundamentally differs from traditional [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs). Instead of autoregressively generating text token-by-token, Jev processes information in parallel, returning decisions and probabilities rather than verbose answers. This architecture reportedly makes Jev significantly faster (claimed to be 200x faster) and cheaper (400x cheaper) than existing models. Crucially, the presenter highlights Jev's "zero hallucinations" characteristic and its ability to provide calibrated probabilities, making its outputs highly predictable and reliable for deterministic tasks. Its interface uses three building blocks: Choice, Score, and Noul, which allow it to make real-time decisions within 100-150 milliseconds. Furthermore, a single [[concepts/system-1-classification|Jev model]] can reportedly handle diverse tasks without needing separate training or classifiers for each.

The video showcases several impressive real-world applications that leverage Jev's unique capabilities. These include "predictive spreadsheets" that can classify and rate large datasets, such as customer reviews for urgency, in real-time (around 100ms per row). Another demonstration features a self-driving car simulation where Jev processes basic sensor inputs to make instantaneous driving decisions, highlighting its suitability for latency-critical applications. The presenter also shows Jev automating browser tasks, like booking a flight, with extreme speed and minimal cost, proving its efficiency for intelligent computer use. These examples emphasize Jev's capacity to enable highly responsive, accurate, and cost-effective intelligent software that was previously unfeasible with conventional LLMs.

Concluding, the presenter frames Jev as an opportunity for developers and entrepreneurs to build innovative software and businesses. He encourages viewers to identify existing software that could be disrupted by integrating Jev's speed, cost-efficiency, and reliability. The video outlines a three-step process for building Jev-powered software: conceiving a compelling idea, setting up a Virtual Private Server (VPS) via Hostinger (which is highlighted as a recommended, affordable, and easy-to-use platform), and finally building the application using AI agents like [[entities/astra|Astra]]. The overall takeaway is that Jev is a paradigm shift in AI technology, enabling unprecedented levels of intelligence in software at a fraction of the cost and time, thereby opening up a vast landscape of new possibilities for real-time, deterministic AI applications.
