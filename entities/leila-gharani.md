# Leila Gharani

Leila Gharani is a content creator and educator specializing in Microsoft Excel tutorials and advanced spreadsheet techniques. She operates a YouTube channel dedicated to teaching practical solutions for both common and complex Excel tasks, with a focus on efficiency and automation in data management. Gharani's tutorials cover a range of Excel functions and features tailored to streamline workflows.
