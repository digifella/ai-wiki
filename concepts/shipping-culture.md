# Shipping Culture

**Shipping culture** refers to an organizational ethos prioritizing rapid deployment, iterative release cycles, and "moving fast" over exhaustive perfectionism or prolonged internal review. In the context of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence]] development, it often correlates with aggressive feature rollout and public-facing experimentation.

## Key Dynamics

*   **Speed vs. Safety Trade-off:** High-[[concepts/velocity|velocity]] shipping often reduces time for rigorous safety alignment checks, increasing the risk of deploying unaligned or unstable models.
