# Concert Lighting

Concert lighting presents unique challenges for photographers due to intense [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]], dramatic contrasts, and rapidly changing [[concepts/light|light]] conditions. Stage lighting is typically designed for visual impact rather than accurate color reproduction, often featuring dominant blues, magentas, yellows, or other saturated hues that can overwhelm camera sensors. These environmental factors frequently result in images with extreme color tints that don't accurately represent the actual scene or subject.

## Color Correction Workflow

[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] provides several tools for correcting extreme color tints in concert photography. The White Balance adjustment is typically the first step, allowing photographers to either select a specific color temperature or use the [[concepts/eyedropper-tool|eyedropper tool]] to sample a neutral area in the image. For more [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]], the [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]] panel enables adjustment of specific color channels—particularly useful when different stage lights create competing color casts across the frame.

The Vibrance and Saturation sliders can help manage the oversaturated colors introduced by stage lighting, while the HSL (Hue, Saturation, Luminance) panel allows [[concepts/selective-adjustment|selective adjustment]] of individual [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]]. This targeted approach is particularly valuable in concert settings where isolating and reducing specific dominant colors—such as blue or magenta tints—can restore natural [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] without affecting the overall image mood.

## Balancing Correction and Aesthetics

While correcting color casts is important for accurate representation, concert photography often benefits from retaining some color character from the stage lighting. Rather than pursuing perfect color accuracy, photographers should consider how much correction serves their artistic intent while maintaining the energy and [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]] of the performance.
