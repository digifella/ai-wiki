# DeepSeek R2

A highly anticipated [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/deepseek]] in [[entities/china|China]].

## Current Status
- **Development Delay**: Progress is currently stalled.
- **Key Bottlenecks**:
	- **Performance**: CEO dissatisfaction with the model's current performance levels.
	- **[[concepts/compute|Compute]] [[concepts/hardware|Hardware]]**: Severe shortage of [[entities/nvidia]] server chips (specifically [[entities/h20-chips|H20 chips]]) caused by [[concepts/us-export-controls]].
- **Strategic Context**: The delay [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the growing impact of U.S. trade restrictions on China's long-term AI ambitions.
