# Ai Driven Iteration

Ai Driven Iteration is a design and development methodology that embeds AI-powered tools directly into creative and productive workflows. Rather than treating conception, review, and refinement as separate sequential phases, this approach leverages AI assistance continuously throughout the working process. This integration allows practitioners to generate multiple variations, evaluate alternatives, and implement refinements at each stage, effectively compressing traditional feedback loops.

## Mechanism and Workflow

The core mechanism involves the continuous interaction between the human operator and generative models, enabling real-time adjustments to output. By utilizing platforms such as Google Stitch, users can rapidly prototype concepts and receive immediate structural or stylistic suggestions. This reduces the latency typically associated with manual revision cycles, allowing for a higher volume of exploratory iterations within a fixed timeframe.

## Impact on Productivity

This methodology shifts the focus from linear execution to dynamic exploration, where the boundary between creation and editing becomes fluid. The ability to instantly visualize changes encourages more experimental approaches to problem-solving. Consequently, teams can achieve greater fidelity in their final outputs by testing a wider range of possibilities before committing to a specific direction, optimizing both time efficiency and creative depth.
