# Secure AI Agent

Secure AI Agent is NVIDIA's enterprise-focused framework for building and deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] with built-in security considerations. It is positioned as an alternative to [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], offering organizations a different approach to implementing autonomous AI systems in production environments.

## Positioning and Purpose

The framework is designed to address enterprise requirements where security, [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and [[concepts/governance|governance]] are critical priorities. Rather than replacing existing solutions outright, Secure AI Agent represents NVIDIA's strategic offering in the competitive landscape of [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|agentic AI]] platforms, providing enterprises with options that align with their specific security and operational needs.

## Relationship to NemoClaw

Secure AI Agent is closely associated with NVIDIA's NemoClaw initiative, which explores how newer frameworks can complement or differentiate from established solutions like OpenClaw. The comparison between these approaches highlights ongoing developments in how enterprises can architect [[concepts/ai-productivity-agents|AI agent systems]] with varying trade-offs between flexibility, security, and ease of [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
