# Stock Characters

Stock characters are pre-designed, reusable templates available within video creation platforms such as Vyond Go. These assets allow users to populate animated videos without the need to design characters from scratch, streamlining the production process. They are sourced from platform libraries and offer a variety of appearances, styles, and demographic representations to suit different project requirements.

In the video creation workflow, these characters serve as foundational elements for script-based animation. Users can select specific stock characters and upload scripts before generating the final video, enabling rapid prototyping and consistent visual storytelling. This approach supports efficient content creation by providing standardized options that align with common narrative needs.
