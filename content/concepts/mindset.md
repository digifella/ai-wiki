# Mindset

Mindset refers to an established set of attitudes, beliefs, and assumptions that an individual or group holds about themselves, others, and the world. These mental frameworks shape how people interpret information, respond to challenges, and approach [[concepts/learning|learning]] and development. Mindsets operate largely on an unconscious level but significantly influence behavior, decision-making, and personal outcomes.

## Fixed versus Growth Mindsets

A foundational distinction in mindset research differentiates between fixed and growth mindsets. A fixed mindset assumes that abilities, intelligence, and talents are static and unchangeable traits. In [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], a growth mindset holds that capacities can be developed through effort, practice, and learning. This conceptual framework, developed through psychological research, has [[concepts/software|applications]] across education, organizational development, and [[concepts/personal-development|personal development]] contexts.

## Formation and Change

Mindsets develop through experience, cultural conditioning, social influence, and repeated patterns of thought. While mindsets tend toward stability, they are not immutable; intentional reflection, [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to new perspectives, and deliberate practice can facilitate shifts in fundamental beliefs and attitudes. Understanding one's own mindsets is considered a prerequisite for meaningful personal or organizational change.
