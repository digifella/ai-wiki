# Color Saturation

[[concepts/color-vibrancy|Color Saturation]] is a [[concepts/foundational-color|foundational color]] adjustment parameter located in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]'s [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration Panel]]. It controls the intensity of color information across an image, affecting how vivid or muted colors appear. This adjustment operates globally across the image rather than targeting specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]], making it a primary tool for establishing the overall color mood and intensity of a photograph.

## Application in Skin Tones

Color Saturation plays a particularly important role in [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait photography]] and skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] rendering. Adjusting saturation in the Calibration Panel influences how natural or stylized [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] appear, affecting both the warmth and intensity of flesh tones. Photographers use this parameter to ensure skin appears lifelike or to push colors toward a more artistic interpretation depending on the desired outcome.

## Cinematic Styling

In [[concepts/cinematic-color-grading|cinematic color grading]], Color Saturation serves as a foundational adjustment for establishing [[concepts/style|visual style]]. Many cinematic looks rely on strategic desaturation or selective saturation [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to create mood and [[concepts/hierarchy|visual hierarchy]]. The Calibration Panel's [[concepts/saturation-control|saturation control]] provides a starting point for building these styled looks before applying more granular adjustments to individual color channels.
