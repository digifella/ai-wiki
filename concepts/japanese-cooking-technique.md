# Japanese cooking technique

Core methodologies and principles defining traditional and modern Japanese cuisine. Emphasizes seasonality, ingredient [[concepts/honesty|integrity]], and precise thermal management.

## Key Principles
- **[[concepts/respect|Respect]] for Ingredients:** Minimal processing to highlight natural flavors and textures.
- **Seasonality (Shun):** Utilizing ingredients at their peak freshness and availability.
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]]:** Exact control over cutting, temperature, and timing.

## Techniques
- **Knife [[concepts/skills|Skills]]:** Specific cuts (e.g., Katsuramuki, Hirazukuri) to optimize [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] and cooking [[concepts/speed|speed]].
- **[[concepts/temperature-management|Heat Management]]:** Use of Indirect heat and controlled [[concepts/moisture-control]] to prevent sogginess and ensure proper browning.
- **Broth Making:** Extraction of Umami through Dashi [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] using Kombu and Katsuobushi.

## Related Concepts
- [[concepts/maillard-reaction|Maillard reaction]]: Critical for developing flavor in proteins and vegetables.
- Steaming: Common method for preserving delicate textures.
- Frying: Techniques like Tempura rely on precise batter and oil [[concepts/thermal-regulation|temperature control]].
