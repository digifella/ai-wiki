# Proposal System

The proposal system is a workflow process for managing and reviewing proposals within the organization. It functions as the central intake and evaluation mechanism through which proposals are submitted, assessed, and approved across departments. The system establishes standardized criteria and procedures for proposal handling, enabling consistent review practices organization-wide.

## Current Implementation Challenges

The system currently operates with significant operational friction stemming from its dependence on SharePoint infrastructure. SharePoint's limitations in handling proposal workflows, document management, and review tracking create bottlenecks in the approval process. Additional infrastructure constraints compound these issues, making the system difficult to scale or modify to meet evolving organizational needs.

## Potential AI Integration

There is potential for AI-assisted tools to accelerate the proposal review process. AI could support initial proposal screening, criteria matching, and summarization tasks, reducing manual review effort. However, implementation of such enhancements would require addressing the underlying infrastructure limitations before meaningful gains could be realized.
