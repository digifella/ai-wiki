# Preserve Details 20

Preserve Details 2.0 is an AI-powered upscaling tool in Adobe Photoshop designed to enlarge images while maintaining fine details and texture quality. Part of Photoshop's Super Resolution feature set, it uses generative AI to improve upon traditional interpolation methods, which often produce blurred results or artifacts when scaling images to larger sizes. The tool analyzes image content and intelligently reconstructs detail rather than simply duplicating or blending pixels.

## How it works

The algorithm uses machine learning trained on large image datasets to predict and generate plausible detail at larger scales. When upscaling, it examines surrounding pixels and textural patterns to infer what finer details should exist at the enlarged size, resulting in sharper edges and more natural texture preservation compared to conventional scaling methods.

## Context in Photoshop's upscaling options

Preserve Details 2.0 is one of several generative AI upscaling approaches available in modern Photoshop.
