# Frontier AI Models

Frontier [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] represent the most advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems currently in development or [[concepts/deployment|deployment]], pushing the boundaries of what [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and other AI architectures can achieve. These models are typically characterized by increased scale, improved performance across benchmarks, enhanced safety measures, and novel [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. [[concepts/frontier-models|Frontier models]] serve as the reference point for the state of [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] any given time, though the definition of "frontier" shifts as the field advances.

## Anthropic Claude

[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] family of models exemplifies the [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]] approach, with variants designed to balance advanced capabilities with robust safety and [[concepts/security|security]] measures. [[concepts/claude|Claude]] models have been developed with particular [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to reducing harmful outputs, improving [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]], and maintaining performance across [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]]. The company has publicly discussed both the capabilities and potential risks of its most advanced variants, including decisions about which models to release publicly and which to restrict.

## Characteristics and Considerations

Frontier models typically require substantial [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] to develop and deploy, involve extensive safety [[concepts/testing|testing]] and alignment research, and generate significant discussion about their societal implications. Organizations developing frontier models face decisions about capability disclosure, deployment scope, and how to balance performance improvements with safety considerations. The rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] cycle of [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]] development means that [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] positions in the field can shift relatively quickly.
