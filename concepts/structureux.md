# Structureux

Structureux is a systematic methodology designed for the rigorous critique of responses, outputs, or creative works. It operates on the principle that critique is not merely a final judgment but an essential refinement step intended to enhance quality and reliability. By moving beyond surface-level evaluation, the process encourages a deliberate examination of content against established criteria to identify gaps, errors, inconsistencies, and areas requiring strengthening.

The core practice of Structureux involves assessing work across multiple dimensions simultaneously. This structured approach ensures that factual accuracy, logical coherence, and structural integrity are evaluated concurrently rather than in isolation. The method prioritizes the identification of specific weaknesses and inaccuracies, providing a clear framework for improvement that is applicable to both analytical responses and creative endeavors.

As a tool for quality assurance, Structureux aims to standardize the feedback loop in creative and professional pursuits. It transforms subjective impressions into actionable insights by focusing on objective markers of quality. This systematic rigor helps creators and reviewers maintain high standards, ensuring that the final output is both robust and refined before it is considered complete.
