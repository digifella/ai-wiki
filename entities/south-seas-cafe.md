# South Seas Cafe

The South Seas Cafe is a heritage-listed venue distinguished by its mid-century interior design and architectural features. The establishment represents a significant example of cafe design from its period, with particular attention given to its distinctive flooring scheme and furnishings.

## Interior Design

The cafe's floor comprises a notable mosaic design featuring 1.2-meter tiles, complemented by Dunlop rubber flooring. These flooring elements were characteristic design choices of the mid-twentieth century and remain integral to the venue's historical character. A heritage-preserved board table, associated with Manchester Unity, forms part of the cafe's significant fixtures and contributes to its cultural heritage value.
