# Five Elements Theory
The Five Elements [[concepts/theory|theory]] is an ancient Chinese [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] used to understand and explain the [[entities/nature|nature]] of life, [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and change. It categorizes phenomena into five elements: Wood (木), Fire (火), Earth (土), Gold/Metal (金), and Water (水). Each element corresponds with certain characteristics and behaviors.

- **Wood**: Represents growth, initiative, and assertiveness.
- **Fire**: Symbolizes passion, enthusiasm, and warmth.
- **Earth**: Indicates stability, nurturing, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].
- **Gold/Metal**: Denotes [[concepts/lightroom|clarity]], strength, and [[concepts/organization|organization]].
- **Water**: Signifies adaptability, intelligence, and fluidity.

- **Wood**: Assertive, ambitious, leaders.
- **Fire**: Passionate, expressive, warm-hearted individuals.
- **Earth**: Caring, reliable, nurturing personalities.
- **Gold/Metal**: Precise, analytical, organized types.
- **Water**: Adaptive, intelligent, intuitive characters.
