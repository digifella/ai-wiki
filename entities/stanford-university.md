# Stanford University

[[entities/stanford|Stanford University]] is a private research university located in Stanford, California. Founded in 1885 by Leland Stanford as a memorial to his son, the institution has developed into one of the world's leading centers for higher education and research. The campus spans over 8,000 acres in the [[entities/san-francisco|San Francisco]] Bay Area, making it one of the largest university campuses in the [[entities/united-states|United States]].

The university is particularly recognized for its strength in science, [[entities/national-academies|engineering]], and technology. It has consistently ranked among the top universities globally and is known for its significant contributions to academic and [[concepts/scientific-advancement|scientific advancement]].

## Research & Innovation

Stanford's research ecosystem extends beyond traditional academia into practical AI frameworks. Notably, **[[entities/hazy-research|Hazy Research]]** and **Scaling Intelligence Labs** at Stanford developed **[[concepts/ai-agent|OpenJarvis]]**, a local-first, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/personal-ai-framework|personal AI framework]]. This project emphasizes running powerful AI models directly on personal devices to prioritize privacy and control over cloud reliance.
