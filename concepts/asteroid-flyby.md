# Asteroid Flyby

An **asteroid flyby** is a close approach of a spacecraft to an asteroid, typically for scientific observation, sample collection, or deflection testing. These missions provide critical data on asteroid [[concepts/writing|composition]], structure, and dynamics, contributing to planetary defense strategies and resource utilization research.

## Key Characteristics
- **Trajectory**: Requires precise orbital mechanics to match velocity and proximity without capture.
- **Duration**: Brief interaction window compared to orbital insertion.
- **Objectives**: Imaging, spectroscopy, [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] field mapping, and sometimes sample return.
