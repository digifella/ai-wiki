# Ai Agent Consulting Strategy

Ai Agent Consulting Strategy refers to the different approaches organizations take when providing guidance on implementing [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]. Two prominent models have emerged in the industry: [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] frameworks for agent safety and control, and proprietary [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] services. These represent different philosophies about how [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] technology should be democratized and monetized.

## Nvidia's Open-Source Guardrails Approach

Nvidia has released open-source guardrails designed to help developers implement safety controls and [[concepts/governance|governance]] in AI agents. These tools are made freely available to the broader development community, enabling organizations to build protective mechanisms into their [[concepts/agentic-systems|agent systems]] without requiring external consulting expertise. This approach prioritizes [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] and rapid [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] across the industry.

## OpenAI's Consulting-Based Model

OpenAI has developed a consulting strategy where organizations seeking guidance on implementing AI agents work with consultants rather than relying solely on open-source tools. This model involves direct advisory services for developing and deploying agent systems, positioning consulting expertise as a key value proposition alongside API access and [[concepts/base-models|base models]].

## Strategic Implications

The [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] between these approaches reflects broader questions about technology [[concepts/distribution|distribution]]: whether critical [[concepts/safe-ai-use|AI safety]] infrastructure should be open and freely available, or delivered through professional services. Each strategy has trade-offs regarding accessibility, standardization, and the business models supporting ongoing development and maintenance of agent governance systems.
