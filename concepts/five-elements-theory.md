# Five Elements Theory
The Five Elements [[concepts/theory|theory]] is an ancient Chinese [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] used to understand and explain the nature of life, [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], and change. It categorizes phenomena into five elements: Wood (木), Fire (火), [[entities/earth|Earth]] (土), Gold/Metal (金), and Water (水). Each element corresponds with certain characteristics and behaviors.

- **Wood**: Represents growth, initiative, and assertiveness.
- **Fire**: Symbolizes passion, enthusiasm, and warmth.
- **Earth**: Indicates stability, nurturing, and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]].
- **Gold/Metal**: Denotes [[concepts/lightroom|clarity]], strength, and organization.
- **Water**: Signifies [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]], intelligence, and fluidity.
