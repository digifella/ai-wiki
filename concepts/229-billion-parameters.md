229 billion parameters represents a significant scale within contemporary [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]], positioning such systems in the upper-mid range of modern AI architectures. This [[concepts/parameter-count|parameter count]] substantially exceeds efficient models like [[concepts/mistral-7b|Mistral 7B]] or Llama 2 13B, yet remains considerably smaller than [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier models]] such as GPT-4 or [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] 3. Models at this scale typically demonstrate improved performance across [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], comprehension, and generation tasks compared to smaller variants, though with corresponding increases in computational requirements.

## Computation

The [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] and training costs associated with a 229 billion parameter model are notably higher than those of smaller architectures. Deploying such a model requires substantial [[concepts/storage-bandwidth|memory bandwidth]] and [[entities/storage|storage]] capacity to load [[concepts/parameters|weights]], often necessitating specialized hardware configurations or [[concepts/distributed-computing|distributed computing]] clusters. Consequently, while these models offer enhanced capabilities, they are generally less accessible for real-time or resource-constrained applications compared to smaller, more optimized counterparts.
