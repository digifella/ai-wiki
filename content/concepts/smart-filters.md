# Smart Filters

Smart Filters in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] are non-destructive filtering techniques that allow photographers and image editors to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to portrait [[concepts/images|images]] while maintaining the ability to modify or remove them later. Unlike traditional filters, Smart Filters preserve the original image data, enabling [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of portrait enhancements without quality loss.

## Common Applications

Portrait enhancement with Smart Filters typically involves sharpening techniques to achieve clarity and definition in facial features, [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] for consistent [[concepts/tonal-adjustments|tonal adjustments]] across [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] and backgrounds, and stylistic transformations using [[concepts/neural-filters|neural filters]]. These adjustments can [[concepts/range|range]] from subtle refinements to more dramatic tonal shifts, such as [[concepts/dark-and-moody-photo-processing|dark and moody]] aesthetics or creative [[concepts/style|style]] transfers.

## Related Tools and Methods

Smart Filters work alongside Photoshop's [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] including [[entities/neural-filter|Neural Filters]] for AI-assisted adjustments, the [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]] for comprehensive color and [[concepts/exposure|exposure control]], and various sharpening algorithms designed to enhance detail without introducing artifacts. Similar non-destructive workflows are available in complementary [[concepts/software|software]] like [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]], where [[concepts/adjustment-layers|adjustment layers]] and local editing tools serve comparable functions in portrait post-processing.
