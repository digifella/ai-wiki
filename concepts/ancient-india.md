# Ancient India

Ancient [[entities/india|India]] encompasses the historical period of the Indian subcontinent from approximately 2300 BCE through the 8th century CE, before the establishment of major medieval kingdoms. This era witnessed the development of sophisticated urban civilizations, complex social structures, and significant intellectual achievements that shaped South Asian culture and influenced neighboring regions.

## Scientific and Mathematical Contributions

The period saw substantial advances in [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]], and [[concepts/physics|natural philosophy]]. Indian mathematicians developed the decimal system and the concept of [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] as both a [[concepts/zero|placeholder]] and a number in its own right—innovations that would eventually transform mathematical practice across the [[entities/earth|world]]. Scholars in ancient India made important observations in astronomy, created detailed medical texts such as the Sushruta Samhita, and developed systematic approaches to [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] and epistemology within philosophical traditions.

## Cultural and Literary Development

Ancient India produced enduring literary works including the Rigveda, the earliest known sacred texts of [[concepts/hinduism|Hinduism]], and the great epics the Mahabharata and Ramayana. These texts preserved knowledge of social organization, religious practice, and ethical [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]]. Sanskrit emerged as a language of scholarly and literary expression, with sophisticated grammatical analysis developed by linguists like Panini.

## Political and Economic Structure

The period encompassed various political [[concepts/nodes|entities]] ranging from small kingdoms to larger empires, most notably the [[concepts/mauryan-empire|Mauryan Empire]] (322–185 BCE) and the Gupta Empire (320–550 CE). Trade networks connected ancient India to Central Asia, [[entities/china|China]], and the Mediterranean world, facilitating exchange of goods, [[concepts/ideas|ideas]], and technologies. Urban centers supported craft production, commerce, and administrative functions that sustained complex societies across the subcontinent.
