# Ungoverned AI Solutions

[[concepts/co-pilot-use|Ungoverned AI solutions]] refer to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems and frameworks deployed within organizations without adequate oversight, control mechanisms, or [[concepts/governance|governance]] structures. These systems often emerge from departmental initiatives, experimental projects, or the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of powerful [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic frameworks]] that operate outside formal IT and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] processes. The lack of centralized management creates visibility gaps, making it difficult for organizations to understand what AI systems are in use, how they function, or what data they access.

## Types and Examples

Ungoverned AI solutions typically fall into two categories. Agentic frameworks—[[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]] designed to take actions, make decisions, and interact with other systems with minimal human intervention—are particularly prone to governance gaps when deployed without proper oversight. [[concepts/shadow-ai|Shadow AI]], the informal use of [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] and systems outside official channels, represents another significant vector. Employees may adopt consumer AI services, custom integrations, or internal experiments that bypass standard procurement and security reviews.

## Risks and Implications

The absence of governance introduces material risks to organizations. Cyber security vulnerabilities may go undetected if ungoverned systems lack proper access controls, audit logging, or security [[concepts/testing|testing]]. Data governance becomes compromised when AI systems process sensitive information without clear ownership or compliance frameworks. Additionally, organizations face [[concepts/governance-risk|governance risk]] through uncontrolled decision-making by autonomous systems, potential regulatory non-compliance, and reputational damage if ungoverned AI produces biased or harmful outputs. The distributed [[entities/nature|nature]] of shadow AI and experimental frameworks makes these risks particularly difficult to quantify and manage.
