# Color palette control

The systematic manipulation of specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] within an image to achieve desired aesthetic or technical outcomes during [[concepts/digital-post-processing|Digital Post-Processing]].

### Implementation via HSL/Color Mixer
Using the [[concepts/color-exaggeration|HSL/Color Mixer]] panel in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] allows for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] across three primary vectors:
- **Hue**: Shifting the base color of specific channels.
- **Saturation**: Adjusting the intensity or purity of specific color ranges.
- **Luminance**: Modifying the brightness of individual color channels.
