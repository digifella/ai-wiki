# Integrated model selection

A methodology for selecting and utilizing multiple [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] within a single interface, reducing the need for multiple subscriptions and streamlining workflow.

- **[[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]]'s implementation**: Enables selection of top LLMs (including Perplexity's own Sona) within one interface, eliminating the need for separate subscriptions to [[entities/chatgpt]], [[entities/claude]], and [[entities/gemini]].
