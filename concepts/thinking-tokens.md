# Thinking Tokens

**[[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]] [[concepts/tokens|Tokens]]** refer to the intermediate computational steps or "[[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|chain-of-thought]]" sequences generated by [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) during [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks. These tokens represent the model's internal deliberation process, distinct from the final output. The efficiency of these tokens—measured by the ratio of [[concepts/reasoning-effort|reasoning effort]] to accuracy—is a critical metric for optimizing [[concepts/vllm|model performance]] and cost.

## Key Developments & Evaluations

- **[[concepts/qwen-36-27b|ThinkingCap-Qwen3.6-27B]]**: A fine-tuned variant of the [[entities/qwen]] model developed by [[entities/bottlecap-ai|BottleCap AI]], specifically optimized to reduce reasoning overhead.
	- **[[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|Performance Metrics]]**: Demonstrates identical accuracy to the base [[concepts/qwen3-model|Qwen 3.6-27B]] while significantly lowering computational requirements for [[concepts/local-control|local deployment]].
	- **Efficiency Focus**: Designed to minimize "thinking tokens" (intermediate deliberation steps) without sacrificing logical [[concepts/accuracy|correctness]], enabling [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] local [[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]].
	- **Context**: Detailed analysis of the model's architecture and efficiency gains is available in [[lab-notes/2026-07-31-ThinkingCap-Local-AI-Efficiency-via-Reduced-Reasoning-To|ThinkingCap: Local AI Efficiency via Reduced Reasoning Tokens]].
