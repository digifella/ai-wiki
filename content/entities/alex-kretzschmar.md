# Alex Kretzschmar

Alex Kretzschmar is the Lead [[concepts/developer|Developer]] Advocate at Tailscale, a company focused on networking and VPN technology. In this role, he creates educational content and resources to help developers understand and implement Tailscale's solutions.

## Content and Advocacy

Kretzschmar produces video content covering practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of networking technology, including tutorials on self-hosting and [[concepts/personal-cloud-server|personal cloud server]] setup. His work demonstrates how Tailscale's infrastructure can be applied to real-world [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], making technical concepts accessible to developers and technology enthusiasts.
