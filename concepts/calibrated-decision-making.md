# Calibrated Decision-Making

**Calibrated [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]** refers to the process of aligning AI outputs or human judgments with precise, verifiable ground truth, prioritizing [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]] over probabilistic free-form generation. This approach minimizes [[concepts/data-hallucination|hallucination]] and enhances [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] in [[concepts/integration|software integration]].

## Core Principles
- **[[concepts/structured-outputs|Structured Outputs]]:** Prioritizing JSON, XML, or typed data formats over natural language prose to ensure machine-readability.
- **[[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] over Creativity:** Reducing variance in responses to ensure consistent, repeatable results in [[concepts/automated-content-creation|automated workflows]].
- **Integration Efficiency:** Enabling direct consumption of AI outputs by downstream software without extensive parsing or cleaning.

## Recent Developments: Jev AI
The [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of **[[concepts/weathernext-3|Jev AI]]** highlights a shift toward models designed specifically for structured, calibrated decision-making rather than general-purpose [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]].

- **Focus on Structure:** Jev, developed by TypeSafe AI, emphasizes structured outputs to address the limitations of free-form LLMs in software contexts [[lab-notes/2026-09-18-Jev-AI-Structured-Outputs-for-Software-Integration-and-E|Jev AI: Structured Outputs for Software Integration and Efficiency]].
- **Software Integration:** Designed to reduce the [[concepts/friction|friction]] between [[concepts/ai-inference|AI inference]] and application [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] by providing deterministic, type-safe responses.
- **Efficiency Gains:** By focusing on calibrated decision-making, Jev aims to improve the reliability of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] in critical software pipelines.
