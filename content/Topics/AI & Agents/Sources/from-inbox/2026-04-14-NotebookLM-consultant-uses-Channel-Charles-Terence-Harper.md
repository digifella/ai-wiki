# 🚀 Workflow: Creating a Monetizable AI Consultant

This process allows you to "productize" your brain by synthesizing proprietary knowledge into structured "[[concepts/logic-manuals|Logic Manuals]]" that power a custom [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]].

## Phase 1: [[concepts/knowledge-capture|Knowledge Capture]] & Organization

Before building the app, you must consolidate your raw insights.

1. **Dictate/Note-taking:** Use a note-taking app to record your unique thoughts, insights, and frameworks.
2. **Convert to [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]]:** Transfer these raw notes into structured Google Docs.
3. **Upload to NotebookLM:** Create a new notebook and upload these Google Docs as your primary sources.

* * *

## Phase 2: Synthesis (The "Master Documents")

Use NotebookLM to synthesize your raw data into three critical "Logic Manuals" that [[entities/will|will]] define how your [[concepts/ai-application|AI application]] behaves.

### 1\. The System Glossary

**Role:** Technical Writer **Goal:** Define your unique [[concepts/terminology|terminology]] to ensure the AI uses your specific vocabulary.

* **Prompt Hint:** _"Act as a Technical Writer. Review all attached sources. Extract every specialized term and unique [[concepts/slms|definition]]. Output Requirement: Create a 'System Glossary'. Use only source text for definitions."_
* **Action:** Save the response as a Note in NotebookLM.

### 2\. Standard Operating Procedures (SOP)

**Role:** Process Engineer **Goal:** Turn your frameworks into step-by-step instructions.

* **Prompt Hint:** _"Act as a Process Engineer. Identify specific frameworks or methods in the sources. Output Requirement: Convert each method into a Standard Operating Procedure (SOP) format (Name, Trigger, Steps, Outcome, Constraints)."_
* **Action:** Save the response as a Note in NotebookLM.

### 3\. Diagnostic Logic Tree

**Role:** Senior Consultant **Goal:** Create the "If/Then" logic for [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].

* **Prompt Hint:** _"Act as a Senior Consultant. Review sources for symptoms, problems, and solutions. Output Requirement: Create a 'Diagnostic Logic Tree' formatted as IF \[Symptom\] AND \[Root Cause\] THEN \[Prescribed [[concepts/solution|Solution]]\]."_
* **Action:** Save the response as a Note in NotebookLM.

* * *

## Phase 3: Deployment in Gemini Gems

This is where you build the client-facing interface.

1. **Export Knowledge:** Export the three Master Documents (Glossary, SOP, Logic Tree) from NotebookLM to Google Docs.
2. **Numbering Convention:** Rename them (e.g., `001-Glossary`, `002-SOP`, `003-LogicTree`) so the AI can easily reference them.
3. **Create a New Gem:**
	* Go to Gemini > **Gem Manager** > **New Gem**.
	* **Instructions:** Tell the Gem to act as your consultant. Explicitly instruct it to reference your Master Manuals for every answer.
	* **Knowledge Section:** Attach the Google Docs you just exported.
4. **Validation:** Ask the Gem: _"Are these documents faithful to the sources?"_ to ensure it isn't hallucinating.

* * *

## Phase 4: Monetization & Client Handoff

Once the Gem is built, you can sell access to it as a "digital employee" or consultant.

* **Sharing:** Share the Gem with your client via the "Share" link.
* **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] Control:** When you share a Gem, the client can interact with the AI interface, but you control the underlying "secret sauce" (the source documents) in your [[entities/google-drive|Google Drive]].
* **Client [[concepts/training|Training]]:** Provide a "Quick Start Guide" (using tools like **Tango.ai**) to teach the client how to prompt the Gem effectively.

* * *

### 💡 Key Benefits

* **[[concepts/zero|Zero]] Hallucination:** By grounding the Gem in your Master Documents, the AI only speaks from your expertise.
* **IP Protection:** Clients get the value of your logic without seeing all your raw, proprietary background research.
* **Scalability:** You can sell the same Gem to multiple clients as a subscription-based AI consultant.**!**
