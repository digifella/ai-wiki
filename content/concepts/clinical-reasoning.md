# Clinical Reasoning

**Clinical [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]** is the cognitive process used by [[concepts/health|healthcare]] providers to collect and analyze [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]], generate diagnostic hypotheses, [[entities/make|make]] management decisions, and reflect on outcomes. It synthesizes medical knowledge, patient context, probability assessment, and individualized evidence.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Dual-Process [[concepts/theory|Theory]]**: Dynamic interplay between System 1 [[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]] (intuitive, pattern-recognition, heuristic-based) and System 2 Thinking (analytical, deliberative, [[concepts/hypothesis-driven-experimentation|hypothesis-testing]]).
- **Diagnostic Cycles**: Iterative data gathering, hypothesis generation, and validation; susceptible to Cognitive Bias and Heuristics.
- **Evidence [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Balancing population-level Evidence-Based Medicine with N-of-1 Evidence and patient preferences.
- **Reflection**: Post-decision analysis to mitigate errors and improve future reasoning.

## AI Integration & Recent Developments
- **Role Evolution**: AI systems are expanding beyond passive [[concepts/clinical-assistance|clinical assistance]] toward [[concepts/active-participation|active participation]] in reasoning workflows, raising questions about agency and validation.
- **Medical AI Paradox**: Discussions highlight tensions between AI's potential for accuracy/efficiency and practical [[concepts/adoption|implementation]] barriers, including trust deficits, contextual misalignment, and regulatory [[concepts/friction|friction]].
- **Evidence Infrastructure**: [[entities/openevidence-europe|OpenEvidence]] has announced withdrawal from European markets, impacting regional access to curated evidence repositories and potentially altering evidence retrieval workflows for clinicians.
