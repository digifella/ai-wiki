# Electronic Visa System E Visa

The Electronic Visa System (E Visa) is [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]]'s digital platform for processing visa [[concepts/software|applications]]. It allows eligible foreign nationals to apply for Turkish visas online without visiting a physical embassy or consulate. The system streamlines the application process by enabling applicants to complete forms, submit required documentation, and receive visa decisions through an internet-based interface.

## Application Process

Applicants access the E Visa system through Turkey's official online portal, where they provide personal information, travel details, and supporting documents. The system generates a digital visa upon approval, which applicants can either print or display on mobile devices at border control. Processing times are typically shorter than traditional visa applications, and applicants receive status updates via email throughout the review period.

## Eligibility and Coverage

The E Visa system covers multiple [[concepts/type-of-visa|visa categories]], including tourist and short-term business visas for citizens of specified countries. Eligibility varies by nationality, with Turkey maintaining a list of countries whose citizens can use the electronic system. Certain nationalities or visa types may still require in-person application at an embassy or consulate.
