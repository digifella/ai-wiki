# Seedance 20

Seedance 20 is a structured workflow designed for producing professional commercial videos by integrating Claude AI prompting with the Seedance 2.0 image-to-video model. This approach utilizes Claude’s language processing capabilities to translate creative briefs and concept descriptions into precise, contextually appropriate technical prompts. These optimized prompts serve as the primary input for the Seedance 2.0 engine, ensuring that the generated video content aligns with specific artistic and commercial requirements.

The process emphasizes the synergy between natural language understanding and generative video synthesis. By leveraging Claude to refine and contextualize input data, the workflow reduces ambiguity in the prompt engineering phase. This allows the Seedance 2.0 model to interpret complex visual instructions more accurately, resulting in higher fidelity outputs that meet industry standards for advertising and media production.

Implementation of Seedance 20 typically involves a sequential pipeline where human creatives define the core narrative and visual style. Claude then processes these definitions to generate detailed prompt structures, which are subsequently fed into the Seedance 2.0 interface. This method aims to streamline the production cycle by minimizing the iterative adjustments usually required when translating abstract creative concepts into technical video generation parameters.
