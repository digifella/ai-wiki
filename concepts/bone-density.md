# Bone Density

**Bone [[concepts/density|density]]** refers to the amount of mineral matter per square centimeter of bones. It is a critical metric for assessing [[concepts/osteoporosis]] risk and overall skeletal [[concepts/health|health]].

## Key Factors Influencing Bone Density
*   **Mechanical Loading:** Weight-bearing exercises stimulate bone formation.
*   **Nutritional Intake:** Adequate calcium and vitamin D are essential.
*   **Hormonal Balance:** Estrogen and testosterone play significant roles in bone maintenance.
*   **Environmental Stressors:** Microgravity environments cause rapid bone loss.

## Space Medicine Insights
Research into bone loss in microgravity has yielded significant insights applicable to terrestrial health.

*   Astronauts [[concepts/experience|experience]] severe bone and muscle loss in space due to the lack of gravitational loading.
*   [[entities/nasa|NASA]] has developed [[concepts/countermeasures|countermeasures]] to prevent this degradation, which are now being translated into actionable advice for Earth-based populations.
*   These solutions focus on maintaining bone strength through specific physiological interventions.
