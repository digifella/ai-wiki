# Kimi K2

Kimi K2 is a comprehensive review and testing assessment of the Mistral 3 Large language model, a 675-billion parameter variant developed by Mistral AI. The evaluation documents the model's performance characteristics through systematic testing methodologies across a range of natural language processing tasks.

## Licensing and Availability

The model is distributed under the Apache 2.0 open-source license, which permits broad commercial and non-commercial use with minimal restrictions. This licensing approach makes the model accessible for integration into various applications and research projects.

## Scope of Assessment

The Kimi K2 review provides empirical analysis of the model's capabilities, including performance on standard benchmarks and practical language tasks. The testing framework aims to establish baseline performance metrics and identify areas of strength and limitation across different use cases.
