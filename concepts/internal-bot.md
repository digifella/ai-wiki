# Internal Bot

An Internal Bot is a custom AI agent built on the Microsoft 365 Copilot platform, designed to automate workflows and tasks specific to an organization's needs. Unlike general-purpose chatbots intended for broad public use, internal bots operate within an organization's existing infrastructure, integrating directly with deployed systems, data sources, and established business processes. They function as specialized tools that leverage proprietary data to provide context-aware assistance to employees.

These agents are typically developed to address specific operational requirements, such as HR onboarding, IT support ticketing, or financial reporting. By connecting to enterprise-grade data repositories and Microsoft 365 applications, they can retrieve relevant information and execute actions without requiring users to navigate multiple disparate interfaces. This integration ensures that responses are grounded in verified organizational data, enhancing accuracy and security compared to external large language models.

Development of internal bots often involves configuring custom instructions, defining permissions, and linking specific connectors to Microsoft Graph or other enterprise APIs. This allows the bot to understand the unique terminology and hierarchy of the organization while adhering to strict compliance and data governance policies. Consequently, they serve as efficient extensions of the workforce, reducing manual effort and accelerating decision-making for internal staff.
