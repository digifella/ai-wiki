## OpenAI Dots: Proactive Personal AI Assistant for Workflow Continuity
**Clip title:** I Tested [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s New [[concepts/personal-assistant|Personal Assistant]] Agent: DOTS
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Futurepedia
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=V_1Vn2WfpEY

### Summary
[[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]] has recently unveiled "[[entities/dots|Dots]]," an innovative always-on, proactive [[concepts/personal-ai-assistant|personal AI assistant]] agent, currently in [[concepts/early-access|early access]] for Pro and Enterprise plan users. The video provides a comprehensive overview of Dots' capabilities, positioning it as a [[concepts/orchestrator-model|central orchestrator]] that bridges various components of the user's [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] ecosystem. This includes seamless access to memories, connected tools and [[concepts/skills|skills]], [[concepts/temporary-cron-jobs|scheduled tasks]], and the ability to initiate new chat, work, or [[concepts/codex|Codex]] sessions, even interacting with the user's local computer. The core promise of Dots is to provide a single, centralized point for managing and directing diverse AI-powered activities, thereby offering a significant leap in workflow [[concepts/continuity|continuity]].

Dots offers a range of functionalities designed to streamline [[concepts/productivity|productivity]]. Users can personalize their Dot with a name and avatar, and communicate with it across devices (phone and computer) with persistent chat histories. It can proactively identify and suggest tasks based on its understanding of ongoing projects and stored information. A new "Pages" feature, akin to [[entities/notion|Notion]], allows for the creation of structured [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] and project scratchpads, mitigating [[concepts/context-loss|context loss]] often experienced in extended chat threads. Dots operates from its own persistent [[concepts/cloud-computer|cloud computer]], capable of browsing the web and pulling up multiple tabs, with the user retaining the ability to observe and intervene in its actions.

The video showcases Dots' ability to handle complex, multi-faceted tasks by orchestrating several [[concepts/skills|skills]] across different ChatGPT environments. For instance, it can generate multiple video intros in "Work," use a custom "[[concepts/remotion-plugin|motion graphics]]" [[concepts/skill|skill]] in "[[concepts/codex|Codex]]" to create visual elements, and simultaneously initiate a separate chat to develop a video thumbnail. Its [[concepts/tone|voice]] mode enables natural language interaction, allowing users to converse with it and delegate research tasks, where Dots can find and open relevant links using its integrated browser. While still in its beta [[concepts/phase|phase]] and exhibiting occasional minor bugs (such as temporary [[concepts/cloud-computer|cloud computer]] unavailability or scheduled task access issues), the demonstrated capabilities highlight its immense potential as an integrated project manager.

In conclusion, Dots' primary advantage lies in its capacity for "[[concepts/continuity|continuity]]," [[concepts/acting|acting]] as a centralized hub for delegating and managing a wide array of AI-driven projects and tasks without constant manual oversight. This streamlines workflows by eliminating the need to jump between disparate tools and platforms. OpenAI intends to progressively roll out more features, including support for multiple Dots, personal [[entities/email|email]] integration, and financial transaction capabilities. Despite being a new offering with some initial kinks, Dots represents a significant step toward a future where [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can autonomously manage complex operations, freeing up users to focus on higher-level decisions and creative work.
