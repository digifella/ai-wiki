# Digital Readiness

**[[entities/digital-readiness|Digital Readiness]]** refers to the capacity of individuals, systems, and institutions to effectively access, utilize, and derive value from digital technologies, particularly in [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] contexts. It encompasses [[concepts/technical-infrastructure|technical infrastructure]], digital literacy, and [[concepts/accessibility|inclusive design]] practices that mitigate barriers to [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].

## Key Dimensions

- **Accessibility & Inclusion**: Ensuring tools are usable across diverse demographics.
- **Technical Infrastructure**: [[concepts/software-reliability|Reliability]] of connectivity and device access.
- **Digital Literacy**: Competency in navigating [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] platforms.
- **Sector-Specific Uptake**: Varied adoption rates among [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] professionals versus other providers, influencing system-wide readiness [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-allied-health-digital-readiness-issues-paper|allied-health-digital-readiness-issues-paper]].

## Barriers to Readiness

- **Language and Cultural Limitations**: Many digital [[concepts/health|health]] tools are primarily available in English, effectively excluding non-English speaking populations. This creates significant disparities for culturally and linguistically diverse communities.
	- *Source*: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Module-3-Fleshed-Out-Interactions-V3|Module 3 Fleshed Out Interactions V3]]
	- *Context*: Highlights the exclusionary nature of monolingual interfaces in [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] ecosystems.
- **Provider Access Disparities**: Inconsistent access to national systems like [[concepts/my-health-record|My Health Record]] among [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] practitioners hinders integrated care delivery.
- **Consumer Uptake Gaps**: Low consumer engagement with [[concepts/electronic-health-records|digital health records]] limits [[concepts/data-completeness-checking|data completeness]] and clinical utility, particularly outside primary care settings.
	- *Source*: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-allied-health-digital-readiness-issues-paper|allied-health-digital-readiness-issues-paper]]
