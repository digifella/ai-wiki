# Brain-Computer Interface (BCI)

A **[[concepts/neuralink-technology|Brain-Computer Interface]]** (BCI) is a direct communication pathway between the neural tissue of a brain and an external device. BCIs enable bidirectional data exchange, allowing for control of external systems via neural signals and potentially for sensory input [[concepts/feedback|feedback]].

## Core Components & Mechanics

- **Signal Acquisition**: Detection of neural activity via invasive (intracortical, epidural) or non-invasive (EEG, fNIRS) methods.
- **[[concepts/signal-processing|Signal Processing]]**: Decoding [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] (e.g., [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]]) translate neural firing patterns into digital [[concepts/commands|commands]].
- **Actuation**: External devices (robotic arms, [[concepts/cursor|cursor]] control, [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]]) execute decoded commands.

## Key Developments & Milestones

- **Early Research**: Focus on motor cortex mapping and basic [[entities/cursor|cursor]] control.
- **Invasive BCIs**: High-[[concepts/network-speed|bandwidth]] data transmission via implanted electrodes.
	- **[[entities/neura-pod-neuralink|Neuralink]]**: N1 Implant and Thread technology.
		- **2026 Status**: Significant [[concepts/roles-in-community-settings|patient care]] advancements and expanded trial scopes. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-31-Neuralink-May-2026-Update-Patient-Progress-VOICE-Trial-a|Neuralink May 2026 Update: Patient Progress, VOICE Trial, and BCI Milestones]] for details on the [[concepts/voice-trial|VOICE trial]] and recent [[concepts/patient-progress|patient progress]].
- **Non-invasive BCIs**: Lower spatial [[concepts/solution|resolution]] but higher safety profile; dominant in consumer-grade applications.

## Applications

- **Restorative**: Motor function [[concepts/preservation|restoration]] for paralysis, [[concepts/neural-implants|neuroprosthetics]].
- **Augmentative**: Enhanced cognitive bandwidth, direct digital interaction.
- **Diagnostic**: Monitoring epilepsy, [[concepts/sleep|sleep]] disorders, and neurological [[concepts/health|health]].

## Challenges

- **Biocompatibility**: [[concepts/immune-system-function|Immune response]] to implants, glial scarring.
- **Bandwidth vs. Safety Trade-off**: Invasive methods offer higher [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] but carry surgical risks.
- **Ethical Concerns**: [[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] of neural data, identity alteration, access inequality.
