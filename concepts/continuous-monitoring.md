# Continuous Monitoring

Continuous monitoring is the ongoing collection of health and physiological data using [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]] equipped with [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]]. These devices are worn directly on the body or integrated into clothing and accessories, passively gathering real-time information during daily activities. This approach eliminates the need for frequent visits to [[concepts/health|healthcare]] facilities, allowing for data acquisition in natural environments rather than clinical settings.

By operating continuously throughout the day and night, these systems capture data across multiple contexts, including rest, [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], and routine activities. This persistent observation provides a more comprehensive picture of an individual's health patterns than periodic measurements, enabling the detection of trends and anomalies that might otherwise go unnoticed. The technology supports long-term tracking of vital signs and behavioral metrics, facilitating early intervention and personalized health management.
