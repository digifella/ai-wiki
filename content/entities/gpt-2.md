# Gpt 2

GPT-2 is a transformer-based [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] and released in 2019. It was trained on a large corpus of [[concepts/text|text]] from the internet and can generate coherent text given an initial prompt. The model became notable as one of OpenAI's first significant contributions to [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] machine [[concepts/learning|learning]], allowing researchers and developers to download and run it locally without ongoing costs.

## Availability and Usage

Unlike some of OpenAI's later [[concepts/models|models]], GPT-2 was released in full and made freely available for download. This [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] meant that individuals and organizations could use the model for research, experimentation, and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] without requiring API access or cloud infrastructure fees. The model comes in several size variants, ranging from 124 million to 1.5 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], allowing users to select versions suitable for their computational constraints.

## Impact and Limitations

GPT-2 demonstrated the potential of large-scale language models for [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]] tasks, though by contemporary [[concepts/open-standards|standards]] its outputs are less sophisticated than subsequent models. Its release raised important discussions in the AI community about responsible disclosure and the trade-offs between open access and potential misuse. The model remains useful for education, experimentation, and as a baseline for comparing newer language model architectures.
