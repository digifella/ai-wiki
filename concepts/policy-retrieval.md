# Policy Retrieval

A mechanism for dynamically accessing and delivering policy information in response to user queries, typically through [[concepts/automations|automated systems]].

- **Agent-based implementation**: [[entities/copilot|CoPilot]] Agents (currently in preview) perform [[concepts/policy-lookup|policy lookup]] and [[concepts/response-generation|response generation]] as core functions.
- **Agent design requirements**: Must maintain friendly, helpful [[concepts/tone|tone]] with clear responses; examples include [[entities/georgia-engel|Georgia Engel]]'s [[entities/microsoft-365|Microsoft 365]] training agent for FAQs and task [[concepts/recommendations|guidance]].
- **Current state**: [[concepts/agentic-systems|Autonomous agents]] remain in preview mode with limited deployment capabilities.
