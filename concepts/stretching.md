# Stretching

## Core Principles
*   **Context Matters:** Stretching efficacy depends heavily on the underlying pathology. Blindly stretching painful areas can exacerbate certain conditions.
*   **[[concepts/nerve-irritation|Nerve Irritation]] vs. Muscle Tightness:** Distinguish between muscular [[concepts/tensile-stress|tension]] and neural inflammation. Stretching inflamed nerves often leads to increased irritation rather than relief.

## Sciatica and Nerve De-Irritation
*   **The Stretching Paradox:** Common instinct is to stretch the buttocks and legs for [[concepts/sciatica|sciatica]] pain, but this often backfires by aggravating the [[concepts/sciatic-nerve]].
*   **Mechanism:** Sciatica involves inflammation and irritation of the nerve. Nerves do not respond well to traditional lengthening techniques when inflamed.
*   **Alternative Approach:** [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Sciatica-Relief-Spinal-Extension-Exercise-for-Nerve-De-I|Sciatica Relief: Spinal Extension Exercise for Nerve De-Irritation]] suggests that spinal extension exercises are more effective for [[concepts/nerve-de-irritation|nerve de-irritation]] than direct stretching of the affected limbs.
*   **Key Insight:** Prioritize nerve gliding and centralization (e.g., extension) over peripheral stretching when dealing with radicular pain.
