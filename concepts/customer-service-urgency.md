# Customer Service Urgency

**Customer service urgency** refers to the criticality and time-sensitivity of a customer's issue, often correlated with emotional states such as frustration. In the context of AI-driven support systems, accurately assessing urgency is essential for routing, prioritization, and appropriate [[concepts/response-generation|response generation]].
