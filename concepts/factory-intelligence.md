# Factory Intelligence

**Factory Intelligence** refers to the [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in scientific and mathematical discovery driven by advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]], moving away from traditional, slow, human-centric methods toward scalable, automated, and high-throughput intellectual production.

## Core Concepts

*   **Transition from Artisanal to Factory Models**: The concept marks a departure from "artisanal intelligence" (individual, slow, intuition-based discovery) to "factory intelligence" (rapid, systematic, AI-driven generation of knowledge).
*   **[[concepts/mathematical-breakthroughs|Mathematical Breakthroughs]]**: Recent advancements, particularly by [[entities/openai]], demonstrate AI's capacity to solve complex [[concepts/mathematical-problems|mathematical problems]], redefining the boundaries of what is considered discoverable.
*   **Redefining [[concepts/scientific-discovery|Scientific Discovery]]**: AI is no longer just a tool for analysis but an active agent in generating novel hypotheses and proofs, fundamentally altering the pace and nature of [[concepts/scientific-advancement|scientific progress]].

## Key Developments

*   **[[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s Recent Advances**: Significant strides in AI capabilities have been identified as the catalyst for this shift, enabling the systematization of intellectual labor.
*   **Impact on Research Methodologies**: Traditional research pipelines are being augmented or replaced by automated discovery engines, increasing the [[concepts/velocity|velocity]] of [[concepts/innovation|innovation]].
