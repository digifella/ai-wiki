# Eric Kim

Eric Kim is a food writer and culinary professional recognized for his documentation of Korean cuisine and cooking practices. His career integrates food journalism with hands-on culinary expertise, enabling him to analyze Korean food traditions from both analytical and practitioner perspectives. Through his writing and public engagement, Kim has contributed to broader cultural conversations regarding Korean cooking.

His work focuses substantially on Korean barbecue, where he has documented traditional techniques and the cultural significance of communal dining. By combining his background in professional kitchens with journalistic inquiry, Kim provides detailed accounts of ingredients, preparation methods, and the social dynamics inherent in Korean meal culture.

Kim’s contributions extend beyond recipe instruction to include the preservation of culinary history and the promotion of authentic Korean foodways in international contexts. His efforts help bridge the gap between traditional Korean culinary practices and contemporary global audiences interested in the region's gastronomic heritage.
