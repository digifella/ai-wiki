# iPhone Pro

High-end smartphone series featuring advanced depth-sensing capabilities for spatial computing and augmented reality.

## Hardware & Technology
- **[[concepts/lidar]] (Light Detection and Ranging):** Utilizes laser beams to measure distance and generate [[concepts/3d-point-clouds]].
- **ARKit [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** Enables advanced spatial [[concepts/workflow|workflows]], including the conversion of scanned environmental data into formats compatible with [[concepts/3d-gaussian-splatting]] (3DGS).

## Key Applications
- **3D Scanning:** Generating [[concepts/training-data|datasets]] for [[concepts/3d-gaussian-splatting]] (3DGS) [[concepts/training-process|model training]].
- **Spatial Mapping:** Utilizing laser-based distance measurement for environmental reconstruction.
