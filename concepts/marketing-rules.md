# Marketing Rules

Marketing Rules is a systematic methodology for converting detailed research and knowledge content into compact 30-second video commercials. The approach addresses the practical challenge of transforming long-form written analysis into short-form video content by providing a structured workflow that leverages Google's AI-powered tools.

## Core Process

The methodology centers on using NotebookLM notebooks as a starting point for content repurposing. Users take existing research, analysis, or knowledge documentation and process it through Google Gemini for content condensation and script generation. The resulting scripts are then input into Google Vids, which handles video production, including visual generation, pacing, and formatting to meet the 30-second duration requirement.

## Application and Purpose

Marketing Rules enables content creators, marketers, and researchers to rapidly convert their work into promotional video formats without requiring traditional video production skills or resources. By automating key production steps through AI tools, the methodology reduces the time and technical expertise needed to create marketing materials from existing knowledge bases, making video content creation more accessible to teams working with substantial written research or analysis.
