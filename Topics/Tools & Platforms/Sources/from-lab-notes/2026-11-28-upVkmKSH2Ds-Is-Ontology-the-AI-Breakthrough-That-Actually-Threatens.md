### Summary
The video explores the growing importance of ontology in AI systems, highlighting how major companies are using it to improve AI understanding and performance. The main topic centers on the limitations of large language models (LLMs) regarding company-specific context and how ontology provides a solution.

Key points discussed include the limitations of early LLMs, which lacked specific context about individual businesses. To address this, Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and knowledge graphs were introduced, allowing models to retrieve and connect related information. However, these methods still struggled with the nuanced relationships and shared meanings required for complex organizational workflows. The video introduces the Model Context Protocol (MCP) as a way to standardize how AI connects to external tools, but notes that connectivity alone doesn't guarantee understanding. Ontology is presented as the crucial layer that provides shared meaning, connecting data, models, and actions into a cohesive map of an organization.

The video concludes that as AI transitions from answering questions to taking autonomous actions, understanding context and shared meaning becomes paramount. Ontology acts as the connective tissue that bridges the gap between AI intelligence and real-world operations, making it a critical component for building effective AI agents.
