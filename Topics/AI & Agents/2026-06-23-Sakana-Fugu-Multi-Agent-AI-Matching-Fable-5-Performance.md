## Sakana Fugu: Multi-Agent AI Matching Fable 5 Performance
**Clip title:** Sakana: [[concepts/claude-fable-5|Fable 5]] Intelligence WITHOUT Fable 5!
**Author / channel:** [[entities/julian-goldie|Julian Goldie]] SEO
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZX16o4K40UE

### Summary
The video introduces Sakana Fugu, a groundbreaking [[concepts/desktop-ai-platform|multi-agent orchestration system]] developed by a Japanese AI lab. This system, particularly its Fugu Ultra model, is presented as a significant advancement, claiming to match the performance of leading models like Fable and [[entities/claude-mythos|Mythos]] while offering "frontier capability without the risk of export controls." A core [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] of Sakana Fugu is its ability to access a diverse pool of specialized [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) – both closed and open – through a single, Open AI-compatible API. Instead of relying on predefined workflows, Fugu dynamically coordinates these expert agents to tackle complex, multi-step tasks efficiently, thereby reducing API complexity and improving cost performance.

The presenter showcases several impressive demonstrations of Fugu Ultra's capabilities. These include generating a visually appealing, animated Apple-keynote-style landing page, an interactive first-person raycaster maze, and a "mind-blowing" [[concepts/threejs|Three.js]] spiral galaxy [[concepts/simulation|simulation]]. When comparing Fugu Ultra's outputs side-by-side with other models like GLM 5.2 and [[concepts/opus|Opus 4.8]] on the Goldie Bench, Fugu often produced superior or significantly more polished results, particularly in [[concepts/aesthetic-design|visual design]] and interactive quality. Quantitative benchmarks presented in the video also indicate that Fugu models, including Fugu Ultra, perform shoulder-to-shoulder with Fable 5 and Mythos Preview across various [[concepts/coding|coding]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], scientific, and [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]], although Fable 5 showed stronger performance in [[concepts/swe-bench-verified|SWE-Bench]] Pro.

Beyond performance, Sakana Fugu offers notable advantages in [[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|cost-efficiency]] and flexibility. A direct comparison with [[entities/openrouter|OpenRouter]] Fusion highlights that Fugu Ultra can achieve equivalent output quality at roughly 25% of the cost. Additionally, Sakana AI provides flat-rate subscription plans, offering predictable billing which is beneficial for high-volume [[concepts/agent-deployment|agent operations]], contrasting with Fusion's pay-as-you-go model. The system is also designed to be "vendor-agnostic," allowing users to select or exclude specific models from its pool to manage export concerns, [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] requirements, or avoid vendor lock-in. This design delivers frontier-tier output without single-vendor risk.

In conclusion, Sakana Fugu represents a compelling multi-agent AI [[concepts/solution|solution]] that excels in dynamic orchestration, delivering [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] outputs efficiently and affordably. Its single API access to a diverse LLM pool, competitive performance, and unique [[concepts/pricing-structure|cost structure]] position it as a powerful alternative to existing monolithic AI models. The emphasis on flexibility and vendor independence suggests a future where [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] can adapt more readily to changing needs and regulations, integrating seamlessly into broader "Agent OS" frameworks for comprehensive [[concepts/ai-automation-agents|AI automation]] and management.
