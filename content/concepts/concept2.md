2026 04 14 Fixing document formatting with [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]

- **Problem**: AI excels at [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] but fails at adhering to specific company formatting (colors, fonts, spacing, tables), requiring manual [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] after copying into [[concepts/templates|templates]]
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: Two-tier approach using **[[entities/claude|Claude]] Projects** and **[[concepts/claude-code]]** to automate formatting [[concepts/compliance|compliance]]
- **Key Workflow**:
  - Create [[entities/claude-projects|Claude Projects]] for structured document templates
  - Implement [[concepts/claude-code]] for automated style enforcement
  - Eliminate manual copy-paste formatting steps
- **Outcome**: Maintains AI content quality while enforcing brand-specific formatting [[concepts/open-standards|standards]]
