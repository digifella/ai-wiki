# Question-Asking Approach

[[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s recent thread on managing knowledge has sparked significant interest in how to effectively utilize tools like [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]] and AI for [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]]. However, the essence of his message is often overlooked.

### Key Points:
- Shift from organizing everything yourself to asking questions.
- [[concepts/leverage-ai|Leverage AI]] to read sources, build connections, write articles, etc.
- Utilize tools not just as workflows but as a new relationship with knowledge.

## Integrating New Information
- [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]]'s thread was misinterpreted by many as solely about the technical [[concepts/setup|setup]] of [[entities/obsidian|Obsidian]] and related scripts. (See 2026 04 12 Heres what it actually does how to build it yourself)
- The core message is to approach [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] from a perspective where questions [[concepts/motivation|drive]] the process, with AI handling the bulk of [[concepts/information-synthesis|information synthesis]] and [[concepts/organization|organization]].
