# Robotic Process Automation

**Robotic [[concepts/workflow-automation|Process Automation]] (RPA)** refers to the use of software robots to automate repetitive, rule-based business tasks. While traditional RPA excels at [[concepts/metadata-manipulation|structured data manipulation]] and UI interaction, it historically struggles with [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] and complex [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]].

## Evolution: From Steps to Decisions

Traditional RPA tools primarily automate the "steps" in a workflow (e.g., clicking [[concepts/buttons|buttons]], copying data), represented as rectangles in flowcharts. A significant advancement in the field is the integration of **Image Decision Models**, which enable robots to make direct decisions based on unstructured visual data.

### Key Advancements
- **Unstructured Data Handling:** New models allow RPA to interpret forms, scans, and screenshots directly, moving beyond rigid selector-based automation.
- **Decision-Making Integration:** Automation workflows now incorporate [[concepts/critical-decision-points|critical decision points]] where the bot evaluates visual context rather than just executing pre-defined actions.
- **[[concepts/document-processing|Jev Image Decision Models]]:** Specific implementations focus on direct unstructured data decisions, enhancing the autonomy of robotic processes.
