# Kim Percy

[[concepts/visual-arts|Kim Percy]] is a visual artist whose practice centers on exploring dyslexia and neurodiversity through contemporary [[concepts/art|art]]. Her work addresses how individuals with dyslexia and other neurological differences perceive and process information, translating these experiences into visual exhibitions and installations that challenge conventional perspectives on [[concepts/cognition|cognition]] and [[concepts/learning|learning]].

## Visualising the Invisible Exhibition

Percy's notable project, "[[concepts/visual-art|Visualising the Invisible]]," presents artistic [[concepts/responses|responses]] to dyslexia and its associated cognitive strengths. The exhibition examines visual processing, pattern recognition, and spatial [[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]]—capacities often heightened in dyslexic individuals—through her artistic practice. The work aims to reframe dyslexia beyond deficit [[concepts/models|models]], instead highlighting the distinctive visual and creative abilities that characterize neurodivergent cognition.

## Public Engagement

Beyond gallery-based work, Percy engages in public talks and educational presentations about neurodiversity and artistic practice. Her approach combines personal investigation with broader conversations about representation, [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and the value of neurological diversity in creative fields.
