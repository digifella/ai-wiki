# Data-Enabled Intelligence

**Data-Enabled Intelligence** refers to the systematic extraction of actionable knowledge from large-scale datasets to enhance [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes, particularly within complex domains like [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]]. It bridges raw [[entities/big-data]] accumulation and intelligent application through [[concepts/governance|governance]], interoperability, and analytical frameworks.

## Core Principles
- **Governance**: Structured oversight of [[concepts/data-integrity|data quality]], [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], and [[concepts/security|security]].
- **Interoperability**: Seamless exchange of information across heterogeneous systems.
- **Actionability**: Conversion of insights into operational or clinical decisions.

## Key Research & Applications

### Regional Health Information Networks (RHINs)
The efficacy of Data-Enabled Intelligence in [[concepts/health|healthcare]] is heavily dependent on the underlying infrastructure of [[concepts/rural-and-remote-health|Regional Health]] Information Networks. Proper governance is critical to mitigate data silos and ensure equitable access.

- **Li et al. (2019)**:
  - [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Li---A-Framework-for-Big-Data-Governance-to-Advance-RHIN|Li - A Framework for Big-Data Governance to Advance RHINs A Case Study of China]]
  - Introduces a specific framework for [[concepts/big-data-governance|big data governance]] tailored to [[concepts/rhins|RHINs]] in [[entities/china|China]].
  - Highlights challenges in fast-growing regional health informatization.
  - Emphasizes the role of governance in addressing data fragmentation and ensuring system scalability.
  - Source: *Springer* (DOI: 10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2910838).

### Digital Health Transformation
- Integration of Health Informatization strategies to support [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|predictive analytics]] and real-time monitoring.
- Use of standardized [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] and ontologies to facilitate cross-institutional data sharing.
