# Task Delegation

Transfer of execution [[concepts/power|authority]] for specific objectives to subordinate entities ([[concepts/agents|agents]], systems, humans) to enable parallelization, [[concepts/specialization|specialization]], and scale.

## Autonomous AI Delegation
- **[[entities/hermes-agent]] `/goal`**: Emergent 2026 capability allowing delegation of [[concepts/complex-tasks|Complex Tasks]] and Long-[[concepts/running|Running]] Processes to [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]]. Replaces stepwise [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] with high-level objective setting, enabling agents to plan, iterate, and complete multi-stage workflows independently.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-18-Hermes-Agent-goal-Delegating-Complex-Long-Running-Tasks|Hermes Agent /goal: Delegating Complex, Long-Running Tasks to Autonomous AI]]

## Modal Expansion: Voice & Telephony
- **Vapi [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Extension of [[entities/hermes|Hermes]] [[concepts/ai-agent-autonomy|Agent autonomy]] into synchronous, real-time communication channels via Vapi (AI-first [[concepts/tone|voice]] [[entities/agent|agent]] platform).
- **Autonomous Phone Calls**: Enables fully automated execution of business and personal tasks through voice interfaces, moving beyond text/CLI-based workflows.
