# AI-driven attack technique

## Definition
The use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) and [[concepts/machine-learning]] (ML) models by threat actors to automate, optimize, or conceal malicious activities. This includes Adversarial Machine [[concepts/learning|Learning]], [[concepts/ai-driven-vulnerability-discovery|automated vulnerability discovery]], and the generation of polymorphic malware.

## Key Characteristics
- **Automation:** Scalable generation of phishing content, exploit code, and social [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] narratives.
- **Adaptation:** Dynamic evasion of traditional signature-based detection systems.
- **Targeting:** Enhanced [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] in identifying high-value targets through data aggregation and profiling.
