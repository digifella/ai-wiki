# Sandeep

Sandeep is a collaborator working on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/software|applications]] in [[concepts/health|healthcare]]. He has initiated a partnership with Lemai to develop an [[concepts/aimm-ai-maturity-model|AI maturity model]] specifically designed for health services. This maturity model aims to establish frameworks for evaluating and advancing the [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of AI technologies within healthcare organizations.

## Research Focus

Sandeep's work involves exploring [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]] and their potential to retrieve and validate knowledge across multiple [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs). This research direction reflects broader interests in ensuring AI systems can access expert-level verification and cross-check information across different [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], which has particular relevance for healthcare applications where [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] is critical.

## Collaborations

In addition to the partnership with Lemai, Sandeep's work has involved [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] and input from Prof. [[entities/prof-alex-usher|Alex Usher]], a German academic who has provided expertise on large language models. These collaborative [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] support the development of robust approaches to AI implementation in health services.
