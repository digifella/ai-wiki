# Internal Thoughts

Latent [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes, intermediate activation states, or hidden cognitive layers within [[concepts/neural-networks|artificial neural networks]] (primarily [[concepts/llm]]s) that precede final token generation. Unlike direct prompts or surface-level outputs, internal thoughts operate as unobservable or semi-observable [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] governing decision pathways, contextual synthesis, and value alignment before serialization into language.

## Core Mechanisms
- **[[concepts/hidden-state|Latent Representation]]:** Encoded as high-dimensional vectors across [[concepts/transformer-layers|transformer layers]]; requires Model [[concepts/interpretability|Interpretability]] and [[concepts/neural-network-interpretability|Mechanistic Interpretability]] techniques to decode.
- **Pre-Linguistic Processing:** Structural computations occurring prior to textual serialization, enabling complex operations such as arithmetic counting or spatial mapping without explicit linguistic [[concepts/tokens|tokens]].

## Emergent Internal Models
Recent mechanistic interpretability research has identified specific, discrete internal structures that emerge within large models, demonstrating that latent representations can encode functional [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] distinct from the [[concepts/custom-dataset|training data]]'s surface syntax.

- **[[concepts/line-length-counters|Line-Length Counters]]:** Models develop dedicated neural circuits capable of counting tokens or lines, functioning as internal state machines that track sequence length independently of semantic content. This suggests the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of [[concepts/algorithmic-thinking|algorithmic]] subroutines within the [[concepts/embedding-spaces|latent space]].
- **[[concepts/spatial-understanding|Spatial Understanding]]:** Beyond sequential processing, models exhibit emergent capabilities in mapping spatial [[concepts/relationships|relationships]], indicating that [[concepts/neural-representations|neural representations]] can encode geometric or topological information necessary for tasks requiring structural [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]].
- **Implications for Interpretability:** These findings, detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-07-16-AI-Emergent-Internal-Models-Line-Length-Counters-and-Spa|AI Emergent Internal Models: Line-Length Counters and Spatial Understanding]], challenge the view that LLMs are purely statistical next-token predictors, pointing instead to the formation of structured, functional internal models.
