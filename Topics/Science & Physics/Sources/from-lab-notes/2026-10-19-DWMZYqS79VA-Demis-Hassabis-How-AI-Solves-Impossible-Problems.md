## Demis Hassabis: How AI Solves Impossible Problems

### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

The video features an interview with Demis Hassabis, co-founder of Google DeepMind, and Sebastian Mallaby, author and journalist, discussing the development of artificial intelligence (AI), the merger of Google Brain and DeepMind, and the implications of AI on science and society.

The speakers touch upon the historical context of AI development, mentioning the early language models and the decision not to release them initially. They also discuss the merger of Google Brain and DeepMind, noting the challenges and the success in catching up with rivals. Hassabis highlights the importance of AI in solving complex scientific problems, specifically mentioning AlphaGo and AlphaFold, and envisions a future where AI leads to a second Renaissance in scientific discovery.

In conclusion, the interview underscores the dual purpose of AI as both a powerful tool for science and a technology with potential risks. Hassabis emphasizes the need for international cooperation and coordination around safety topics to ensure the benefits of AI outweigh the risks.

#### Description
What does the future of AI – near or distant – really look like? Can it fulfil its infinite promise and drive breakthroughs in medicine, science and daily life? Or is the danger steadily outweighing the benefits?

Nobel Prize-winning Google DeepMind co-founder Demis Hassabis joins Sebastian Mallaby, author of The Infinity Machine, to discuss the rocky and rapidly accelerating journey towards AGI. They cover why Google held back its own chatbot before ChatGPT, how AI cracks problems too vast for brute force, and whether AGI can ever be built safely.
