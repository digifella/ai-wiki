# Weeknight Dinner

Weeknight dinners are meals prepared and consumed on weekdays, typically characterized by practical constraints on time and effort. These meals prioritize efficiency without necessarily sacrificing quality or nutritional value, making them distinct from weekend cooking where more elaborate [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] is feasible. The concept encompasses various cooking approaches and cuisines adapted for busy schedules.

## Practical Cooking Methods

Weeknight dinner preparation often employs [[concepts/cooking-techniques|cooking techniques]] that minimize active time or total [[concepts/cooking-time|cooking duration]]. Methods such as [[concepts/bbq|grilling]], pan-[[concepts/pan-searing|searing]], and stir-frying allow proteins and vegetables to cook quickly while developing flavor. [[concepts/korean-bbq|Korean BBQ]] approaches, for instance, rely on high-heat cooking at the table or stovetop, enabling diners to prepare individual portions rapidly. Similar efficiency-focused techniques appear across many culinary traditions, from sheet-pan roasting to one-pot meals.
