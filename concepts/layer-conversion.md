# Layer conversion

The process of altering the fundamental type, state, or behavior of a layer within digital imaging software like [[concepts/photoshop]] or [[concepts/lightroom]].

## Core Processes
- Rasterization: The conversion of [[concepts/smart-objects]] or vector data into pixel-based layers, which terminates the ability to perform non-destructive [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].
- **Background Conversion**: Converting a locked Background Layer into a standard, editable layer.

## Relation to Non-Destructive Workflows
- [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] (ACR) utilizes [[concepts/smart-objects]] to allow for non-destructive color and [[concepts/image-quality-enhancement|image quality enhancement]].
- Advanced enhancement techniques (e.g., making colors "pop") are preserved through the ACR workflow as long as the layer is not converted/rasterized.
- ACR-driven [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are accessible directly within [[concepts/photoshop]] or [[concepts/lightroom]] environments prior to any destructive layer conversion.
