# Orion

Orion is a crewed spacecraft system developed by NASA for the Artemis program, designed to transport astronauts to the Moon and facilitate deep space exploration missions. The spacecraft consists of three primary components: a crew module that accommodates up to six astronauts, a service module that provides propulsion and life support systems, and a launch abort system engineered to ensure crew safety during launch and ascent phases.

## Design and Capabilities

The Orion crew module is a cone-shaped capsule capable of operating beyond low Earth orbit, distinguishing it from earlier spacecraft designed primarily for orbital flight. This design allows the vehicle to withstand the thermal and radiation environments of deep space while maintaining life support for extended durations. The service module, built by Airbus, supplies electrical power, thermal control, and propulsion, while the launch abort system provides an emergency escape capability during the initial stages of flight.

## Artemis II Operations

In the context of Artemis II, Orion serves as the primary vehicle for the first crewed mission of the Artemis program. Reports covering this mission focus on crew operations and in-flight systems performance as the spacecraft travels to lunar orbit and returns to Earth. The mission validates the integrated systems required for future lunar landings, ensuring that the spacecraft can safely carry astronauts around the Moon and back without landing on the surface.
