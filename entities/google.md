# Google

## Overview

## Key Developments (2026)

### AI & Machine Learning
*   **Gemma 4**: Released in August 2026 via [[entities/deepmind]] as a unified AI model.
*   **[[concepts/ai-platform-updates|Gemini 4 Argon]]**: Announced in October 2026, featuring a [[concepts/1-million-token-context-window|1 million token context window]] and performance comparable to OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra.
*   **TabFM**: A zero-shot [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation model]] designed for tabular data.
*   **[[entities/embedding-gemma-2|Embedding Gemma 2]]**:
