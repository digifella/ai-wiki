# Germany

Federal Republic of Germany: Central Europe state, largest Economy in [[entities/europe]], NATO/[[entities/eu]] member.

## Current Status & Risks
- Demographic Crisis (2026):
  - [[concepts/fertility-rate|Fertility Rate]] below replacement threshold causing contraction.
  - [[concepts/population-aging|Population Aging]] straining social infrastructure.
  - [[concepts/pension-system|Pension System]] insolvency risk due to dependency ratio inversion.
  - Exacerbated by "Boomer mismanagement" regarding resource allocation [[lab-notes/2026-05-06-Germanys-Demographic-Crisis-Fertility-Decline-Aging-and|Germany's Demographic Crisis: Fertility Decline, Aging, and Pension Insolvency]].
  - Source: Kurzgesagt "GERMANY IS OVER" video analysis.
