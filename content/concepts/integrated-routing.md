# Integrated Routing

Integrated Routing is a feature of Cloudflare's email service, currently in beta, that combines email sending, routing, and [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|AI workflow]] capabilities into a [[concepts/unified-platform|unified platform]]. The service allows users to manage email delivery and processing through Cloudflare's infrastructure while incorporating automated AI-powered workflows for enhanced email handling and intelligence.

The service integrates with Cloudflare's broader ecosystem of [[concepts/developer|developer]] tools and platforms, enabling developers to build email workflows that leverage both traditional routing logic and [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]]. This combination allows for more sophisticated [[concepts/email-management|email management]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], such as intelligent message classification, automated [[concepts/responses|responses]], and context-aware routing decisions based on email content or [[concepts/metadata|metadata]].

By bringing email routing and AI workflows together within a single service, Integrated Routing reduces the complexity of managing separate email and [[concepts/automation-tools|automation tools]]. Developers can configure routing rules and AI processing steps within the same environment, streamlining the setup and maintenance of email-dependent [[concepts/software|applications]] and services.
