# Google Omni

**[[concepts/google-search|Google]] Omni** is a unified [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] architecture developed by [[entities/google|Google]], designed to process and generate content across text, image, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and video modalities within a single model framework. It represents a shift from specialized unimodal models to a cohesive system capable of cross-modal understanding and generation.

## Core Capabilities

- **Unified Architecture**: Consolidates multiple AI capabilities into one model, reducing latency and context switching between different [[concepts/custom-models|specialized models]].
- **[[concepts/multimodal-understanding|Multimodal Understanding]]**: Simultaneously analyzes text, visual, and auditory inputs to derive contextual meaning.
- **Generative Output**: Capable of producing high-fidelity video, [[concepts/audio|audio]], and text responses based on mixed-mode prompts.

## "Nanobanana" Video AI Module

Recent developments have highlighted specific capabilities within the Omni framework, particularly regarding [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]], internally or colloquially referred to as "Nanobanana."

- **Key Findings from Review**:
    - **Unified Video Generation**: The model treats video generation as a native [[concepts/modality|modality]] rather than a post-processing step, ensuring higher coherence in motion and temporal [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
