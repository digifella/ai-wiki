# Sound Choice

**Sound choice** refers to the capacity to make well-reasoned, effective decisions, particularly under conditions of [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] or insufficient data. It is the practical application of **[[concepts/decision-making|good judgment]]** in professional and leadership contexts.

## Core Components

Sound choice is not merely the selection of an option but the synthesis of:
- **Evidence Interpretation**: Deriving direction from incomplete or contradictory [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]].
- **[[concepts/personal-qualities|Personal Qualities]]**: Integrating character, intuition, and ethical standing.
- **Experiential Knowledge**: Leveraging past [[concepts/experience|experience]] to contextualize current options.

## Relation to Leadership

In high-stakes environments where clear evidence is absent, stakeholders look to leadership for **good judgment**—defined as the ability to combine personal qualities with [[concepts/relevant-knowledge|relevant knowledge]] and experience to form [[concepts/opinions|opinions]] and make decisions. This capability is identified as 'the core of [[concepts/exemplary-leadership|exemplary leadership]]' [[concepts/leadership]].
