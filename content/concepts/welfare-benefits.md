# Welfare benefits

Financial assistance provided by Government administration to individuals and households to ensure access to essential resources and mitigate economic [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]].

## Core Domains
- Social [[concepts/secure|security]]
- Public policy
- Economic safety nets

## Administrative Risks & Automation
- The increasing reliance on Algorithmic [[concepts/governance|governance]] and Automated [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] for eligibility [[concepts/verification|verification]] and debt recovery.
- **Case Study: [[concepts/robodebt-scheme|Robodebt Scheme]]: Australia's Unlawful Algorithm Causing Deaths**
	- An automated system introduced in 2016 by the Australian government.
	- Utilized an algorithm to identify and recover alleged overpayments of welfare benefits.
	- Deemed an unlawful process with catastrophic consequences, including documented deaths.
