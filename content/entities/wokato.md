# Wokato

Wokato is [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/lightweight-automation-platforms|ChatGPT Workspace Agents]] platform, designed to automate business tasks and workflows for teams. The system leverages [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]]'s capabilities to function as an [[concepts/ai-agent|autonomous agent]] capable of handling routine operations across workplace [[concepts/software|applications]] and processes.

## Functionality and Purpose

The platform aims to streamline repetitive business tasks by deploying [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] that can operate within team environments. These [[concepts/agents|agents]] are intended to reduce manual work and increase [[concepts/cost|operational efficiency]] by automating workflows that would typically require human intervention.

## Availability

Wokato's ChatGPT Workspace Agents were made available as a feature accessible to teams, with a freemium model component that provides baseline access to users. This approach allows organizations to integrate AI-driven [[concepts/automation|automation]] into their existing workflows without mandatory [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] costs.
