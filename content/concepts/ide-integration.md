# IDE integration

[[concepts/integration|Integration]] of intelligent [[concepts/agents|agents]] within IDEs to enhance professional [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]] without replacing [[entities/developer|developer]] expertise.

- [[entities/junie|Junie]], JetBrains' new [[concepts/smart-coding-agent|smart coding agent]], positions itself as an "[[concepts/anti-vibe-coding|Anti-Vibe Coding]]" tool specifically for professional [[concepts/software|software]] engineers (not for non-coders).
- Avoids "[[concepts/vibe-coding|vibe coding]]" trends by focusing on deeply integrated, context-aware assistance rather than generating large codebases from scratch.
- Designed to complement [[concepts/developer|developer]] [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] within IDE rather than automate entire features.
- Part of JetBrains' strategy to provide professional-grade AI assistance in JetBrains ecosystem.
- [[entities/kombai|Kombai]], the first [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]-built for [[concepts/frontend-development|frontend development]], integrates into IDEs ([[entities/vs-code|VS Code]], [[concepts/cursor|Cursor]], [[entities/windsuf|Windsuf]]) and outperforms general tools (e.g., [[entities/github|GitHub]] [[entities/copilot|Copilot]], [[entities/codepal|CodePal]], [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]]) in frontend tasks (e.g., [[concepts/code|code]] review: 72% success vs. 30-50%).
- [[concepts/bmad-method|BMAD method]] for [[concepts/coding|coding]]: A universal [[concepts/ai-agent-framework|AI agent framework]] for [[concepts/agile-ai-driven-development|Agile AI-driven development]], addressing the lack of proper [[concepts/software|software]] development process in [[concepts/ai-generated-code|AI-generated code]].
