# Italy

The Italy detector is a [[concepts/granular-physics|particle physics]] instrument located in the [[entities/stawell-gold-mine|Stawell gold mine]] in Victoria, Australia. It operates in conjunction with the [[entities/saber-detector|Saber detector]] to search for [[concepts/wimp-particles|weakly interacting massive particles]] ([[concepts/wimps|WIMPs]]), which are theoretical candidates for [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]]. The detector is positioned deep underground to shield sensitive equipment from cosmic radiation interference.

The Italy detector is part of collaborative dark matter research efforts, with the underground location [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] Stawell providing ideal conditions for detecting rare particle interactions. By pairing with the Saber detector, the experiment increases the likelihood of identifying [[concepts/wimp|WIMP]] particles through multiple detection methods and cross-validation of results.
