## Photoshop and Camera Raw September 2026 Update: Top 20 New Features
**Clip title:** Top 20 New Features in 20 Mins: Photoshop & Camera Raw (September 2026 Update)
**Author / channel:** PiXimperfect
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XXumkzBQg80

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive overview of 20 significant new and often-missed features across [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and Camera Raw, highlighting how these updates are streamlining workflows and expanding creative possibilities. The presenter underscores the rapid evolution of these tools, particularly focusing on advancements in [[concepts/ai-powered-editing|AI-powered editing]] and enhanced control over various aspects of image manipulation. From improving foundational editing steps to introducing powerful generative capabilities, the video aims to keep users informed about the latest functionalities that can elevate their photo editing skills.

Several key features enhance image analysis and AI-driven content generation. In Camera Raw, the "Auto-Focus Detection" displays the exact focal points captured by the camera, allowing photographers to quickly verify sharpness. Photoshop's new "AI-Assisted Mode" provides a simplified workspace similar to Adobe Firefly, enabling users to generate and modify image elements through [[concepts/text-prompts|text prompts]], with clear indications of credit usage. Further AI enhancements include "Markup" tools, which allow users to guide generative AI with text annotations or brush strokes directly on the canvas, facilitating complex changes like repainting a wall or altering an outfit. The "Tune (beta)" feature in the AI-Assisted Editor offers controlled lighting and mood adjustments (e.g., transforming a day scene into an evening one), though it currently downscales image resolution.

Beyond generative AI, the updates introduce practical improvements and refined controls. Photoshop now features a non-destructive "[[concepts/light-adjustment-layer|Light Adjustment]]" layer, offering granular control over exposure, contrast, highlights, and shadows as a stackable layer. The "Prompt to Edit" function enables whole-image AI modifications directly from the contextual taskbar without needing prior selections, utilizing specific models optimized for editing rather than complete content generation. Camera Raw also gains a "Trim to Transparent" option, which permanently deletes pixels outside the cropped area, and an expanded "Info Panel" that includes clickable GPS coordinates to instantly locate where a photo was taken. For skin retouching, the AI-powered "Remove Blemishes" tool intelligently cleans up imperfections while meticulously preserving natural skin texture. Additionally, users can now customize Photoshop's "Save Formats" to declutter the save dialogue by hiding rarely used file types.

Advanced color grading and masking capabilities have also seen significant enhancements. Camera Raw's new "The Glow" effect offers various styles—Diffusion, Bloom, and Halation—to add distinct light effects with adjustable warmth and spread. The introduction of "Vectorscope" provides a visual color map, invaluable for precise color correction and achieving accurate skin tones, especially when used in conjunction with masking. Speaking of masks, Camera Raw now supports local "Color Wheels" for targeted color grading on specific masked areas, along with "Feather and Edge" sliders for refining mask selections with greater precision. Another notable feature is "[[concepts/crop-and-expand|Generative Expand]]" in Camera Raw, allowing AI-powered content expansion on raw files while remarkably retaining all original raw information and settings.

The video concludes by emphasizing that while AI tools are undeniably powerful, true mastery in photo editing stems from a deep understanding of core photographic and compositing concepts. Knowing how masks work, analyzing image focus, and grasping the fundamentals of light and color grading will always provide an edge over merely relying on AI prompts. The presenter encourages continuous learning and conceptual understanding to effectively leverage the constantly evolving suite of tools in Photoshop and Camera Raw, thereby fostering genuine creativity rather than just automation.
