# Dog-Friendly Sites

## Overview
Locations and establishments that welcome dogs, including parks, cafes, and accommodations.

## Key Features
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Areas where dogs are allowed without restrictions.
- **Amenities**: Water stations, waste disposal, and shaded areas for dogs.
- **Safety**: [[concepts/secure|Secure]] environments for dogs to roam freely.
