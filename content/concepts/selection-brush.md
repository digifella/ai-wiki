# Selection Brush

The Selection Brush is a tool introduced in [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s July 2025 update, available in both the general release and [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]] versions. Designed to streamline the selection process, it functions as part of [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s broader effort to enhance user [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] and productivity in professional [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]] tasks.

## Functionality

The Selection Brush enables users to [[entities/make|make]] selections by painting directly onto an image, offering an alternative approach to traditional selection methods. This brush-based selection method integrates into Photoshop's existing selection toolkit, allowing for more intuitive and flexible [[concepts/image-masking|image masking]] and editing workflows.

## Release and Availability

The tool was highlighted as one of five significant features in the July 2025 Photoshop update cycle. It represents Adobe's continued investment in refining core editing [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] through both stable releases and experimental features tested in the [[entities/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] program.
