# Below the High Plain

**[[entities/below-the-high-plain|Below the High Plain]]** is a 1959 film by [[entities/nfsa-film-australia|NFSA Film Australia]] that documents the [[concepts/kiewa-hydroelectric-scheme]] in the Victorian Alps. The work explores the intersection of large-scale [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] and the natural landscape, specifically focusing on the area around [[concepts/mount-beauty]].

## Key Themes & Content
- **Engineering Feasibility:** Examines the technical challenges and solutions involved in the hydroelectric [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]].
- **Natural Coexistence:** Analyzes how the scheme interacts with the surrounding alpine environment.
- **Mount Beauty Context:** Highlights the specific role and impact of the scheme on the [[concepts/mount-beauty]] region.
