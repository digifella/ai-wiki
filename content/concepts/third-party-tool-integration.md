# Third Party Tool Integration

Third party tool [[concepts/integration|integration]] refers to the use of [[concepts/third-party-applications|external applications]] and services alongside [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/anthropic-ai|Claude AI]] platform. In April 2026, [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] implemented significant restrictions on certain third-party integrations as part of broader changes to its [[concepts/claude|Claude AI]] subscription model.

## OpenClaw Ban

Anthropic banned [[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]], a third-party tool that had previously enabled extended functionality with Claude AI. The ban was announced on April 8, 2026, marking a shift in the company's policy toward external integrations. This restriction affected users who relied on OpenClaw to expand [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] beyond the native platform.

## Usage Limitations

Concurrent with restrictions on third-party tools, Anthropic introduced new [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] for Claude AI subscribers. These limitations constrained how intensively users could access the platform, affecting both direct Claude usage and interactions through integrated tools. The changes reflected Anthropic's strategic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to its service offerings and subscription tiers.
