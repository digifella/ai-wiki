# OceanGate Titan

The **OceanGate [[entities/titan|Titan]]** was a deep-sea submersible operated by OceanGate Inc., designed for tourist expeditions to the wreck of the RMS Titanic. It imploded on June 18, 2023, during a dive to the Titanic wreck site, resulting in the deaths of all five occupants.

## Incident Overview
- **Date:** June 18, 2023
- **Location:** North Atlantic Ocean, near the Titanic wreck site
- **Outcome:** Catastrophic implosion; loss of all crew and passengers
- **Investigation Status:** Concluded (as of 2026)

## Engineering & Failure Analysis
Initial speculation focused on the novel use of carbon fiber-reinforced polymer (CFRP) for the pressure hull. However, subsequent investigations have shifted the primary cause of failure:

- **Inadequate [[concepts/engineering-process|Engineering Process]]:** The root cause is identified as systemic failures in the [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] validation process rather than an inherent flaw in the carbon fiber material itself.
- **Manufacturing Deficiencies:** Specific manufacturing protocols and [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]] measures were insufficient for the extreme pressures encountered at depth.
- **Source Integration:** Detailed analysis of these failures is documented in [[lab-notes/2026-07-09-OceanGate-Titan-Implosion-Inadequate-Engineering-and-Man|OceanGate Titan Implosion: Inadequate Engineering and Manufacturing Failures]].
