# Generative AI

Generative AI refers to [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] technologies that can produce new and original content autonomously, such as [[concepts/images|images]], [[concepts/text|text]], [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], video, and more. This concept encompasses a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/software|applications]] from creative industries like [[concepts/design|design]], marketing, and entertainment to technical fields including [[concepts/software|software]] development and scientific research.

- **[[concepts/llms|Definition]]**: Generative [[concepts/models|models]] are trained on large [[concepts/training-data|datasets]] to learn patterns and structures that allow them to generate new data similar to the [[concepts/training|training]] set.
- **Applications**:
  - **[[concepts/text-generation|Text Generation]]**: Creating coherent sentences or paragraphs using AI (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt]])
  - **Image Creation**: Generating images from textual descriptions (e.g., DALL-E, Midjourney)
  - **Design**: [[concepts/human-ai-collaboration|AI-assisted design]] [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] and prototyping
  - **Software [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Integration of [[entities/adobe-firefly|Adobe Firefly]] AI into Lightroom Cl
- **Future Architectures**: [[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] proposes [[concepts/jepa|Joint Embedding Predictive Architecture (JEPA)]] as a successor to [[concepts/llms|LLMs]], arguing JEPA [[entities/will|will]] eventually surpass current models in capability and efficiency [[lab-notes/2026-05-05-Yann-LeCuns-JEPA-Proposal-A-Path-Beyond-LLMs|Yann LeCun's JEPA Proposal: A Path Beyond LLMs]].
