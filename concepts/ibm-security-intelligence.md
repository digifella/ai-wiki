# IBM Security Intelligence

**IBM [[concepts/security-intelligence|Security Intelligence]]** is a podcast series produced by [[entities/ibm-technology]] that explores critical topics at the intersection of [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]], [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], and emerging technology risks. The series features discussions on threat landscapes, [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] management, and the evolving [[concepts/security|security]] implications of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]].

## Key Topics & Episodes

### AI Security and Vulnerability Management
Recent discussions have focused on the security implications of [[concepts/private-execution|open-weight AI]] models and shifts in vulnerability prioritization frameworks.

- **[[concepts/ethical-considerations|Open-Weight AI Risks]]**: Analysis of security vulnerabilities inherent in [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] AI models, including potential misuse and [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] risks.
- **CISA Vulnerability Prioritization**: Examination of the [[entities/cisa]] ([[concepts/internet-security|Cybersecurity]] and Infrastructure Security Agency) model for prioritizing vulnerabilities, discussing the potential obsolescence of traditional metrics like [[concepts/cvss]] (Common Vulnerability Scoring System).
- **Emerging Threats**: Coverage of "vibe hunting" techniques and [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] on specific security incidents or [[concepts/nodes|entities]] such as Lightwell.
