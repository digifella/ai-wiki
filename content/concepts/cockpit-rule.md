# Cockpit Rule

A mental model for deciding when to delegate to AI, when to collaborate with AI, and when to avoid AI entirely.

**Context**:
- "Using AI" is no longer a differentiator; it is a baseline expectation (similar to [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]] proficiency).
- To get ahead, higher-level [[concepts/skills|skills]] are required beyond basic [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]].
- [[concepts/the-cockpit-rule|The Cockpit Rule]] is the foundational [[concepts/skill|skill]] among four specific AI framework [[concepts/skills|skills]] outlined by [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]].

**Key Points**:
- **Delegate**: Assign routine, well-defined tasks to AI (e.g., data processing).
- **Collaborate**: Partner with AI for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] requiring human judgment (e.g., strategic planning).
- **Avoid**: Refrain from AI use when inappropriate, unethical, or requiring human expertise (e.g., sensitive client interactions).
