# Feature implementation

The process of translating [[concepts/design|design]] requirements and specifications into functional, production-ready [[concepts/software|software]] components.

## AI-Assisted Development
- Utilization of [[concepts/specialized-sub-agents|specialized agents]] to accelerate the development lifecycle and automate [[concepts/front-end-development|Frontend Development]].
- **[[entities/kombai]]**: An [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] [[concepts/motivation|purpose]]-built for frontend engineering tasks.
	- **[[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Directly integrates into popular IDEs including [[entities/vs-code]], [[entities/cursor]], and [[entities/windsurf|Windsurf]].
	- **[[concepts/specialization|Specialization]]**: Optimized for frontend-specific workflows, outperforming general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|coding agents]] (e.g., [[entities/github|GitHub]] [[entities/copilot|Copilot]], [[entities/codepal|CodePal]], [[entities/gemini]]) in specific metrics.
	- **Performance**: Demonstrates significantly higher [[concepts/success-rates|success rates]] in [[concepts/code|Code]] Review (72% success vs. 30-50% for general [[concepts/models|models]]).
