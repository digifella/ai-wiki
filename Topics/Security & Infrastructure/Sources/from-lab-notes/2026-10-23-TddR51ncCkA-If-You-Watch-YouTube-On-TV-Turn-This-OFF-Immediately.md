## If You Watch YouTube On TV, Turn This OFF Immediately!
**Clip title:** If You Watch YouTube On TV, Turn This OFF Immediately!
**Author / channel:** Spencer Knows!
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TddR51ncCkA

### Summary
Here is a summary of the video:

The video discusses a specific privacy setting within the YouTube TV app on streaming devices or smart TVs. The speaker explains that YouTube TV users should be aware of a setting related to microphone access and voice/audio saving that may be enabled by default, which allows Google to collect and process voice commands and audio recordings.

The speaker demonstrates how to locate and adjust these settings within the YouTube TV app. Users can navigate to Settings, find "Microphone access," and toggle it off if they prefer not to use voice commands or have their audio sent to Google. Additionally, the speaker covers the "Voice and audio saving" setting. Since this setting may not always be visible directly within the TV app, the speaker advises users to visit "myactivity.google.com" on a web browser to manage their YouTube history and uncheck the box for "Include YouTube voice and audio activity."

The speaker recommends that users turn off both microphone access and voice/audio saving across all accounts if they do not require voice search features on their streaming devices. This step helps prevent audio data from being sent to and stored by Google, enhancing user privacy.
