# AI Platforms

AI platforms are software systems and services that provide [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities for use across various industries and applications. These platforms typically offer pre-built tools, [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], and frameworks that enable organizations to integrate [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and AI functionality into their own applications and workflows without requiring in-house development of underlying [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] from scratch. By abstracting complex [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]], these platforms lower technical barriers to [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and reduce implementation timelines.

## Common Capabilities

Most AI platforms provide a range of core functionalities including [[concepts/language-processing|natural language processing]] for text analysis and generation, [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] for image and video analysis, [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-in-healthcare|predictive analytics]], and data processing tools. Many platforms also offer [[concepts/training-process|model training]] and [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] capabilities, allowing organizations to customize AI systems for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]]. These tools are typically accessed through [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]], though some platforms support on-premises or hybrid deployment models.

## Applications and Users

AI platforms serve diverse user [[concepts/number-systems|bases]] from large enterprises to smaller organizations. In professional services, these platforms support tasks such as [[concepts/document-processing|document analysis]], [[concepts/legal-document-review|contract review]], legal research, and due diligence. Other sectors utilize AI platforms for customer service automation, content moderation, [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]], and [[concepts/task-specific-configurations|operational optimization]]. The [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of these platforms has broadened AI adoption beyond organizations with specialized [[concepts/ai-expertise|AI expertise]].

## Market Structure

The [[entities/ai-news-strategy-daily|AI platform]] market includes offerings from major technology companies providing general-purpose AI services, specialized vendors targeting specific industries or [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]], and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] frameworks. [[concepts/pricing|Pricing]] models typically combine subscription fees with usage-based charges, though [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] structures vary significantly across different platforms and vendors.
