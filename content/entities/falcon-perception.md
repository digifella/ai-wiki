# Falcon Perception
Falcon Perception is an advanced framework designed to improve the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and efficiency of visual [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks. It integrates state-of-the-art [[concepts/vision-language-models|vision language models]] ([[concepts/llms|VLMs]]) with other specialized algorithms to address specific challenges in perception.

## Enhancements
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Agentic Visual Reasoning]] Pipeline**: Introduced as a method to enhance VLM capabilities by integrating them with image segmentation techniques, addressing limitations in tasks such as precise [[concepts/object-counting|object counting]] and [[concepts/spatial-understanding|spatial understanding]].
  - New insights from the video "[[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]] Models Can't Count. Here's the Fix." ([https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFYnD1WREdU](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VFYnD1WREdU))
    - Enhancements include integration with image segmentation models to improve object counting and spatial understanding.
- **Integration with [[entities/gemma-4|Gemma 4]]**: Demonstrates how Falcon Perception complements the features of Google's [[entities/gemma|Gemma]] 4, improving its performance on complex visual [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks.
