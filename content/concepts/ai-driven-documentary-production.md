# AI-driven documentary production

The use of [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] technologies to create high-retention, broadcast-quality documentary content. The primary objective is to leverage a free/freemium [[concepts/tech-stack|tech stack]] to achieve high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]] motion and voice-matched narration, specifically avoiding the low-quality "slideshow" aesthetic.

## 🛠️ The "Admin Privables" Workflow
A streamlined production pipeline focused on professional-grade output:

- **Core Goal:** Achieving high-retention, motion-rich, and voice-matched documentary videos.
- **Research & Scripting:** Utilizing [[entities/notebooklm]] for deep [[concepts/information-synthesis|information synthesis]], research, and script drafting.
- **Audio Production:** Utilizing [[entities/google-ai-studio]] for advanced Voiceover generation and voice-matching capabilities.
- **Visual Strategy:** Implementing high-resolution, motion-centric techniques to bypass the "slideshow" look.
