His work focuses on making [[concepts/10x-developer-productivity|AI-assisted coding]] tools accessible to developers who work primarily on smartphones and tablets rather than traditional desktop environments.

## Content Focus

San's videos showcase practical applications of Claude Code, Anthropic's coding assistance tool, with an emphasis on mobile workflows.
