# Core Purpose

Core purpose is defined as the fundamental reason an individual or organization exists, extending beyond the pursuit of profit or the achievement of specific, finite goals. It represents the underlying "why" that drives decision-making, motivates action, and provides long-term direction. In the context of business strategy, understanding this core purpose is considered essential for sustaining motivation and ensuring that daily activities align with deeper organizational values.

The concept is often explored through narrative frameworks, such as the Story Method discussed by High Performance. This approach emphasizes uncovering purpose by constructing a coherent narrative that articulates the organization's identity and mission. By framing purpose within a story, leaders can better communicate their vision and foster a shared sense of meaning among stakeholders.

Identifying core purpose helps distinguish between transient objectives and enduring principles. While goals may change in response to market conditions, the core purpose remains a constant anchor. This stability allows organizations to navigate uncertainty with clarity, ensuring that strategic choices remain consistent with their foundational identity.
