# Dr Robert Ballard

Dr. Robert Ballard is an American oceanographer and underwater explorer best known for discovering the wreck of the RMS Titanic in 1985. Ballard's work advanced deep-sea exploration technology and methodology, establishing techniques that became foundational for subsequent underwater archaeological expeditions.

## The Titanic Discovery

In 1985, Ballard led a joint French-American expedition to locate the Titanic in the North Atlantic Ocean. Operating at depths exceeding 12,000 feet, his team employed remotely operated vehicles (ROVs) and sonar technology to map and document the wreck site. The discovery confirmed the ship's location and condition, providing the first visual evidence of the vessel that sank in 1912 after striking an iceberg.

## Naval Missions and Other Work

Ballard's deep-sea expertise has extended beyond the Titanic to other significant underwater projects. His oceanographic research has included work on hydrothermal vents and other aspects of marine geology. He has also participated in military-related underwater missions, including searches for lost submarines, which informed his technical capabilities and access to advanced exploration equipment.
