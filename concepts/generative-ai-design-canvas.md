# Generative AI Design Canvas

A generative [[entities/google-stitch-20|AI design canvas]] is a class of design tools that embed [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities directly into the core creative workflow. Rather than positioning AI as an optional plugin or secondary feature, these tools treat generative and AI-assisted functions as fundamental to their architecture and operation. This integration allows designers to work iteratively with AI throughout the [[concepts/design-thinking|design process]], using AI-generated suggestions, automated refinements, and contextual assistance as native features of the interface.

## Core Architecture

The defining characteristic of [[concepts/generative-uiux|AI-native design]] canvases is their departure from traditional tool design patterns. Instead of separating AI features into menus or sidebars, these tools incorporate [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]], generative [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], and [[concepts/reasoning|AI reasoning]] into the primary canvas where creative work happens. This allows for real-time [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], suggestion generation, and assisted [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] without requiring designers to leave their working environment or explicitly invoke separate tools.

## Workflow Integration

These platforms enable designers to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] for tasks such as layout suggestions, content generation, style refinement, and design [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|space exploration]]. The AI operates alongside human [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] rather than replacing it, allowing designers to validate, reject, or build upon AI-generated outputs. This [[concepts/hybrid-approach|hybrid approach]] supports [[concepts/design-iteration|iterative design processes]] where AI accelerates exploration while designers maintain directional control and creative judgment.

## Evolution and Examples

The concept has evolved as AI capabilities have matured, with platforms like [[concepts/ai-assisted-prototyping|Google Stitch]] representing developments in embedding [[concepts/generative-ai|generative models]] into [[concepts/collaborative-design|collaborative design]] environments. These tools continue to explore how [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]], image generation, and design reasoning can be integrated into workflows that serve both individual designers and teams.
