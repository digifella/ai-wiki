# Whiteboard

A whiteboard video is an animated format that simulates the real-time process of drawing and writing on a whiteboard. This style combines visual elements with narration to explain concepts, tell stories, or present information in an engaging manner. The format is commonly used for educational content, marketing explanations, and instructional tutorials. Whiteboard videos appeal to viewers by breaking down complex information into digestible visual steps.

The continuous visual progression of drawings helps maintain sustained attention, making it an effective tool for clarifying difficult subjects. By mimicking the natural act of sketching ideas, these videos reduce cognitive load and guide the audience through logical sequences. This approach is particularly valuable for abstract topics where static images might fail to convey dynamic relationships or processes.

Modern production workflows often leverage specific software tools to streamline the creation of these animations. Platforms such as NotebookLM, Nano Banana Pro, and Pictory are utilized to generate the underlying content and automate the animation process. These tools allow creators to focus on narrative structure and visual clarity while reducing the manual effort traditionally required for frame-by-frame animation.
