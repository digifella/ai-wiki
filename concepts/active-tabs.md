# Active Tabs

"Active Tabs" is an educational video produced by Kole Jain that outlines fundamental principles of user interface and user experience design. The content is structured to provide beginners with foundational knowledge while remaining accessible as a reference for experienced designers. Rather than focusing on specialized or advanced techniques, the video emphasizes practical, universally applicable design principles that form the basis of effective digital design.

The presentation covers core concepts such as affordances, visual hierarchy, grid systems, and typography. These topics are presented as essential building blocks for creating intuitive and functional digital products. By explaining how these elements interact, the video aims to establish a common vocabulary and understanding for designers at various stages of their careers.

The material serves as a primer for those entering the field, offering a clear overview of what constitutes good design practice. It avoids niche methodologies in favor of broad, foundational rules that apply across different platforms and mediums. This approach ensures that the information remains relevant and useful for anyone seeking to understand the underlying logic of UI/UX design.
