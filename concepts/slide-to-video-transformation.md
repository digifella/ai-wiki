# Slide To Video Transformation

Slide to Video Transformation is a workflow for converting presentations generated by [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] into video format. [[entities/google|Google]]'s [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] application can automatically generate [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] from source documents such as research papers, articles, or [[concepts/notes|notes]]. Users may wish to convert these presentations into video files for distribution, archival, or presentation purposes across different platforms.

## Process Overview

The transformation process typically involves exporting slides from NotebookLM and using complementary Google tools—such as [[concepts/google-slides|Google Slides]] and [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] video export features—to generate video output. A key consideration for this workflow is avoiding watermarks that may be added during conversion, which can be achieved through careful selection of export settings and tool combinations within the Google ecosystem.

## Use Cases and Applications

Converting slides to video format expands the accessibility and reach of automatically-generated presentations. Videos can be embedded in websites, shared through video platforms, or used in presentations where dynamic playback is preferred over static slide decks. This format is particularly useful for educational content, research dissemination, and creating asynchronous learning materials from document-derived presentations.
