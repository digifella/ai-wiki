# South Seas Cafe

The South Seas Cafe is a heritage-listed venue distinguished by its mid-century interior design and architectural features. The establishment represents a significant example of cafe design from its period, with particular attention given to its distinctive flooring scheme and furnishings.

The cafe's floor comprises a notable mosaic design featuring 1.2-meter tiles, complemented by Dunlop rubber flooring. These flooring elements were characteristic design choices of the mid-twentieth century and remain integral to the venue's historical character.

The interior also includes a heritage-preserved board table, which contributes to the authenticity of the space. Together, these elements highlight the venue's importance as a preserved example of its era's commercial architecture and interior aesthetics.
