In this video, the [[entities/speaker|speaker]] demonstrates how to create professional, high-quality presentation slides that rival those of top [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] firms, using only [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] like ChatGPT-4o and Piktochart, without any prior design experience.
She begins by outlining the core principles of a polished presentation:

* **Framework First:** Start with the story using frameworks like **SCQA** (Situation, Complication, Question, Answer).
* **Lead with the Takeaway:** Present your conclusion upfront and use the slides to support it.
* **Stay Organized:** Use the **MECE** (Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive) principle to ensure your [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] are logical and complete.
* **One Idea Per Slide:** Keep slides focused and use a full-sentence headline that tells the slide's story.
* **Clean Design:** Emphasize clarity over complex design by using simple fonts, minimal color, and consistent spacing.

The speaker then provides a step-by-step [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] using specific prompts in ChatGPT-4o to generate visuals:

1. **Before & After:** A prompt is used to create an image showing a product's transformation from a rough, handmade prototype (a cardboard-like computer mouse) to a sleek, final version.
2. **[[concepts/sketch|Sketch]] to Diagram:** She uploads a simple, hand-drawn sketch of a feedback loop and prompts the AI to recreate it as a clean, professional, slide-ready diagram.
3. **Bullet Points to Visuals:** She transforms a boring bullet-point list into a visually engaging slide. By providing a detailed prompt specifying a minimalist layout, icons, titles, and even the desired "consulting-firm" aesthetic, ChatGPT generates a well-designed slide with five benefit cards.

Finally, she shows how to bring these AI-generated visuals into **Piktochart** to easily assemble them into a complete presentation deck. Using a minimalist template, she demonstrates how to drag and drop the images, edit text, and align elements to create a cohesive and professional final product that can be downloaded in various formats, including PowerPoint.
