# Timbres

Timbres refer to the distinct vocal characteristics and qualities of voices in text-to-speech (TTS) systems. In the context of AI-powered [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]], timbres encompass the acoustic properties that give a voice its unique color, [[concepts/texture|texture]], and personality—such as warmth, brightness, or raspiness. The ability to control and design timbres is an important feature for creating natural and varied synthetic speech.

The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family of models, developed and open-sourced by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]], includes capabilities for [[concepts/voice-design|voice design]] and [[concepts/timbre|timbre]] control. These models allow users to specify or customize vocal characteristics beyond simple pitch and speed [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], enabling more nuanced and expressive speech synthesis. By providing timbre design as a built-in feature, the Qwen3-TTS models extend the flexibility available to developers and researchers working with [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] text-to-speech systems.
