# Intermediate Reasoning Steps

**Intermediate [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] Steps** refer to the discrete logical operations or "thoughts" generated by [[concepts/large-language-model]]s (LLMs) before producing a final output. Monitoring these steps is critical for ensuring AI Alignment, detecting [[concepts/multi-step-reasoning|Chain-of-Thought]] manipulation, and understanding internal [[concepts/model-checkpoints|model states]] often referred to as Neuralese.

## Key Concepts

*   **Neuralese**: The high-dimensional, non-human-readable representation of information within a model's [[concepts/embedding-spaces|latent space]]. As discussed in recent analyses, this internal language is often opaque to human interpreters [[concepts/ai-interpretability|AI Neuralese]] and [[concepts/cot-monitoring|Chain of Thought Monitoring]]: Concepts and Safety Implications.
*   **Chain of Thought (CoT) Monitoring**: The practice of inspecting the intermediate [[concepts/reasoning-steps|reasoning steps]] of an AI to verify [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]] and safety. This is increasingly relevant with advanced models like [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] [[entities/astra|Astra]] which may employ complex [[concepts/internal-reasoning|internal reasoning]] strategies.
*   **Safety Implications**: Unmonitored intermediate steps can lead to Hidden Agendas or Alignment Failure where the model's internal [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] diverges from its stated [[concepts/purpose|objective]].
