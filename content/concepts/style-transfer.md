# Style Transfer

Style Transfer is a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]]-based technique that applies the visual characteristics of one image to another. In [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], this capability is implemented through [[concepts/neural-filters|Neural Filters]], allowing users to analyze the aesthetic properties—such as color palette, brushwork, lighting, and [[concepts/texture|texture]]—of a source photograph and apply those qualities to a different image. The feature automates what would traditionally require manual adjustment across multiple editing [[concepts/parameters|parameters]].

## Technical Implementation

The process relies on [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] trained to isolate and separate style elements from image content. When a user selects a reference image, the [[entities/neural-filter|neural filter]] analyzes its artistic qualities and transfers those attributes to the target image while attempting to preserve the underlying subject matter and composition. This differs from simple filters or effects, as the transformation adapts to the specific characteristics of both the reference and target [[concepts/images|images]] rather than applying a predetermined effect.

## Practical Applications

Style Transfer in Photoshop enables photographers and digital artists to quickly achieve consistent [[concepts/visual-aesthetics|visual aesthetics]] across image series, apply painterly or artistic effects without manual work, or match the mood and tone of reference photography. The feature is particularly useful for batch processing, [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] workflows, and creative experimentation where the [[concepts/style|visual style]] of one photograph needs to inform the treatment of another.
