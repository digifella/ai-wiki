# Slide Deck Repurposing

Slide Deck Repurposing is a workflow technique that converts presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. The process begins with NotebookLM's ability to automatically create structured slide decks from source documents, including articles, PDFs, and other text materials. These slides are then imported into Google Vids, where they serve as the foundation for video production, extending the utility of the original content across multiple formats.

## Workflow and Process

The technique involves uploading NotebookLM-generated slides to Google Vids, where they act as the structural backbone for the final video output. This integration allows users to transform static textual presentations into dynamic video assets without manually recreating the visual layout. By leveraging the existing slide structure, the workflow reduces the time required for content adaptation while maintaining the informational integrity of the original source material.

## Output Characteristics

A key feature of this method is the production of unwatermarked video files, which distinguishes it from standard preview modes or watermarked exports often associated with automated generation tools. This capability ensures that the resulting videos are suitable for professional distribution, internal sharing, or public publishing without additional editing to remove platform-specific branding. The approach effectively bridges the gap between document analysis and multimedia presentation within the Google ecosystem.
