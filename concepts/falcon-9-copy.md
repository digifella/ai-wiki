# Falcon 9 Copy

**Definition**: A term referring to launch vehicles that replicate the [[concepts/minimalist-design|design philosophy]], reusability mechanics, or market positioning of [[entities/spacex]]'s Falcon 9, particularly regarding rapid turnaround and [[concepts/cost-efficient-solutions|cost efficiency]].
