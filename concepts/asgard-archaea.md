# Asgard Archaea

Asgard Archaea are a group of organisms within the Archaet domain central to the [[concepts/endosymbiosis-hypothesis|Endosymbiosis hypothesis]] and the [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of Complex Life.

## Key Insights
- Recent breakthroughs regarding the [[concepts/origins-of-complex-life|origins of Complex Life]] on [[entities/earth|Earth]].
- Focus on the Endosymbiosis hypothesis, which proposes the formation of [[concepts/eukaryotic-cells|Eukaryotes]] through symbiotic interactions.
- Recent experimental recreation of the "first [[entities/contact|contact]]" between organisms that led to the formation of complex life.
- Detailed in: Asgard Archaea: Recreating Endosymbiosis, Origins of Complex Life (Source: [[entities/anton-petrov|Anton Petrov]]).
