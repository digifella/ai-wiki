# Bhutan

**Bhutan**, officially the **Kingdom of Bhutan**, is a landlocked country in the Eastern Himalayas. Known as the "Land of the Thunder Dragon," it is globally recognized for its unique cultural heritage, Buddhist traditions, and national philosophy prioritizing **[[concepts/gross-national-happiness]]** over Gross Domestic Product.

## Key Developments & Initiatives

*   **[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]] (GMC):** A major new economic zone aimed at balancing modern development with mindfulness principles.
    *   Positioned as an "anti-Dubai" model, focusing on sustainable, spiritually grounded urban planning.
    *   Addresses existential crises through [[concepts/mindful-development|mindful development]] strategies.

*   **Environmental Stewardship:** Constitutionally mandated to maintain at least 60% forest cover; a net carbon sink nation.
*   **Foreign Policy:** Maintains strict neutrality and strong diplomatic ties with India and China, while engaging with the global community through its unique cultural identity.
