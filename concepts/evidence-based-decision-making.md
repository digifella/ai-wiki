# Evidence-Based Decision Making

**Evidence-Based [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]** is a structured approach to selecting actions based on the best available evidence from scientific research, data analysis, and professional [[concepts/expertise|expertise]], rather than relying solely on intuition or anecdote. It minimizes bias by systematically integrating Data Analysis, [[concepts/scientific-method]], and stakeholder input.

## Core Principles
- **Systematic Evidence Gathering**: Prioritizes [[concepts/peer-reviewed-research|peer-reviewed literature]] and verified datasets.
- **[[concepts/opacity|Transparency]]**: Clearly documenting sources and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] processes.
- **[[concepts/iterative-refinement|Iterative Refinement]]**: Updating decisions as new evidence emerges.
