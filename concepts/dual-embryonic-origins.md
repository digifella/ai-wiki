# Dual Embryonic Origins

**Dual embryonic origins** refers to the paradigm-shifting discovery that the human brain does not arise from a single, unified pool of [[concepts/neural-progenitor-cells|neural progenitor cells]] ([[concepts/npcs|NPCs]]), but rather from two distinct developmental lineages. This challenges the traditional view of a monolithic brain origin.

## Key Findings
*   **Two Separate Organs:** Recent research indicates the brain functions as two separate [[concepts/nodes|entities]] during development, originating from distinct embryonic sources [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Dual-Embryonic-Origins-of-Human-Brain-Development|Dual Embryonic Origins of Human Brain Development]].
*   **[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]] Discovery:** Groundbreaking work by [[entities/stanford|Stanford]] developmental [[concepts/biology-researchers|biologists]], published in *Nature [[concepts/neuroscience|Neuroscience]]*, fundamentally redefines our understanding of [[concepts/human-brain-development|human brain development]].
*   **Progenitor Heterogeneity:** The brain emerges from heterogeneous pools of progenitors rather than a single unified pool, leading to complex differentiation pathways.
