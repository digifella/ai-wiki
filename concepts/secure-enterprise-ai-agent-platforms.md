# Secure Enterprise AI Agent Platforms

[[concepts/secure|Secure]] enterprise [[concepts/ai-agent-platforms|AI agent platforms]] are specialized systems designed to [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] [[concepts/action-oriented-ai|autonomous AI agents]] in corporate environments while maintaining [[concepts/security|security]], [[concepts/compliance|compliance]], and operational [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]]. These platforms address the core challenge of [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic AI]] across organizations that handle sensitive data and require robust [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks. They bridge the gap between the capabilities of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] and the stringent requirements of enterprise [[concepts/digital-infrastructure|IT infrastructure]].

## Key Characteristics

[[concepts/enterprise-ai|Enterprise AI]] agent platforms typically incorporate multiple security layers including [[concepts/authentication|authentication]], [[concepts/authorization|authorization]], data [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], and audit logging. They provide [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for monitoring agent behavior, controlling resource access, and ensuring that autonomous actions align with organizational [[concepts/policies|policies]]. These platforms often integrate with existing enterprise systems such as identity management, [[concepts/data-management|data governance]], and compliance monitoring tools. They also enable fine-grained control over which data sources and external systems agents can interact with.

## Compliance and Governance

A primary function of these platforms is to support regulatory compliance across industries such as finance, [[concepts/health|healthcare]], and government. They maintain detailed logs of agent decisions and actions for audit purposes, implement role-based access controls, and enforce data residency requirements. Many platforms provide isolation mechanisms that prevent agents from accessing information beyond their intended scope, protecting sensitive customer or proprietary data.

## NVIDIA NemoClaw

[[concepts/agent-toolkit|NVIDIA NemoClaw]] is an example of a [[concepts/enterprise-ai-agent-platform|secure enterprise AI agent platform]] designed to address vulnerabilities identified in earlier systems. It represents an approach to hardening [[concepts/agent-deployment|AI agent deployment]] for organizations that require both the flexibility of [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]] and the security guarantees expected in enterprise environments.
