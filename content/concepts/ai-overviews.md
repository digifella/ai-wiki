# AI Overviews

AI Overviews are synthesized summaries generated by AI systems in response to search queries, displayed prominently at the top of search results pages. Google introduced this feature to consolidate information from multiple sources into direct answers, reducing the need for users to visit individual websites. Rather than ranking web pages, the system generates new text that attempts to answer questions comprehensively by drawing from existing content across the web.

## Impact on Website Traffic

The emergence of AI Overviews has created challenges for content creators and website operators. Because users receive answers directly on the search results page without clicking through to source websites, traffic to individual sites has declined. This shift represents a fundamental change in how search results function, moving away from the traditional model of directing users to ranked web pages toward a model where AI-generated summaries serve as the primary interface between users and information.

## Adaptation Through Generative Engine Optimization

To maintain visibility and relevance in this new environment, websites can employ Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) techniques to structure and present content in ways that AI systems are more likely to draw from when synthesizing overviews. This involves optimizing content clarity, providing comprehensive coverage of topics, and ensuring information is easily extractable by AI systems. Tools like Claude Code can assist in analyzing website content and suggesting structural improvements to increase the likelihood of being sourced in AI-generated summaries.
