# 7 New AI Tools to Replace Your Daily Workflow

The video introduces seven AI tools intended to replace traditional [[concepts/software|software]] like [[entities/microsoft-excel|Excel]], automate job hunting, streamline [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and create autonomous workflows.

## 1\. Shortcut (TryShortcut.ai)

**The "AI Version of Excel"** Shortcut is designed to replace traditional [[entities/google-sheets|Google Sheets]] and Excel workflows by acting as an intelligent data analyst. It operates either as a browser-based tool or an Excel plugin.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Prompt-Based Creation:** You can tell the AI what to build (e.g., "Build a three-statement model for [[entities/tesla|Tesla]]"), and it executes the task.
	* **[[concepts/agentic-research|Autonomous Research]]:** It searches the web and SEC filings to find real-time financial data and extracts it automatically.
	* **Formatting & Formulas:** It builds the spreadsheet, formats the cells, creates assumptions, and writes the complex formulas without user intervention.
	* **Transparency:** Users can click on cells to see exactly which formulas were used to generate the data.

## 2\. AI Apply (AIApply.co)

**Automated Job Application Suite** This tool automates the job search process, aiming to help users get hired faster by tailoring applications to specific roles using AI.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Auto-Apply:** Finds high-match roles and can automatically apply to them.
	* **Document Optimization:** Tailors resumes and generates cover letters specific to the job description.
	* **Interview [[concepts/solution|Answer]] Buddy:** A real-time [[concepts/training|training]] tool where users can practice answering interview questions and receive grading/feedback.
	* **"AI Inception":** The video [[concepts/notes|notes]] that since many companies use AI to filter resumes, using AI to write them increases the chances of passing those filters.

## 3\. Content Buddy (ContentBuddy.ai)

**Viral Content Assistant** A tool designed for creators and businesses to grow on TikTok and Instagram by leveraging data from viral trends.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Trend Discovery:** Finds trending topics within specific niches.
	* **AI Video Remixer:** The standout feature. Users can paste a URL of a viral video (their own or someone else's), and the AI [[entities/will|will]] generate:
		* New [[concepts/hooks|hooks]].
		* A fresh script based on the original concept.
		* Optimized descriptions and hashtags.
	* **Regeneration:** If the output isn't perfect, users can regenerate specific sections like the hook or script.

## 4\. [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] Flows (flows.workspace.google.com)

**Google Ecosystem [[concepts/automation|Automation]]** A native Google tool that uses [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] to create custom [[concepts/agents|agents]] that manage tasks across the Google Workspace suite.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Natural Language Automation:** Users describe a task to Gemini, and it builds the automation flow.
	* **Cross-App [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** Works with [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], [[concepts/motivation|Drive]], Chat, and Calendar, as well as third-party apps like [[entities/asana|Asana]], Jira, [[entities/salesforce|Salesforce]], and QuickBooks.
	* **Example Flows:**
		* Get daily summaries of unread emails.
		* Receive notifications for urgent emails containing specific keywords.
		* Generate post-meeting action items and summaries.
	* **Requirement:** Requires a Google Workspace account (approx. $10/mo).

## 5\. Kortix (Kortix.com)

**Autonomous AI Worker** Described as a "mind-blowing" tool powered by the [[entities/claude-4|Claude]] model, Kortix builds complex automation chains based on simple text prompts.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Task Execution:** Instead of just planning, it executes tasks (e.g., "Create a weekly report on AI news every Monday at 9 AM").
	* **Web Scoping & Document Creation:** It can browse websites, extract information, compile it into a Google Doc, and save it to Drive.
	* **Self-Correction:** It identifies the tools needed (browser, [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]] integration) and asks [[concepts/clarifying-questions|clarifying questions]] to ensure the output matches the user's needs.
	* **Versatility:** Can be used for monitoring [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channels, handling emails, or auto-posting to social media.

## 6\. [[entities/heygen|HeyGen]] (HeyGen.com)

**AI Video Avatars** A "best-in-class" tool for creating realistic digital avatars to automate [[concepts/video-creation|video production]] without a camera or studio.

* **Key Features:**
	* **Video [[entities/agent|Agent]]:** Users can describe a video concept, and the agent generates it.
	* **Digital Clones:** Users can create a [[concepts/realistic-avatar|digital twin]] of themselves that looks, moves, and sounds exactly like them (using voice [[concepts/cloning|cloning]] from ElevenLabs).
	* **Integration:** Can be combined with tools like Content Buddy to take a generated script and immediately produce a video with the [[concepts/ai-avatar|AI avatar]].

## 7\. Pikzels (Pikzels.com)

**Thumbnail Optimization AI** A tool for creating, analyzing, and fixing YouTube thumbnails to improve Click-Through Rates (CTR).

* **Key Features:**
	* **Creation & Recreation:** Generate thumbnails from prompts or recreate existing styles.
	* **AI Analysis:** Users can paste a YouTube video link, and the AI scores the thumbnail on virality, clarity, and curiosity.
	* **One-Click Fix:** If a thumbnail scores low, the tool can automatically "fix" it (e.g., zooming in on the subject, enhancing lighting, boosting [[concepts/color-contrast|color contrast]]) to make it more effective.
