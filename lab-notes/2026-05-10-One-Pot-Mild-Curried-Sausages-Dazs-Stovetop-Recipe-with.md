## One-Pot Mild Curried Sausages: Daz's Stovetop Recipe with Secret Honey
**[[concepts/clip-title|Clip title]]:** [[concepts/dazs-recipe|One Pot Mild Curried Sausages]] with a [[concepts/secret-ingredient|Secret Ingredient]]: Stovetop Cooking for Beginners

### Summary
This video showcases [[entities/daz|Daz]], an Australian chef, preparing his version of a classic "curried sausages" dish. He emphasizes that this is a very [[concepts/mild-curry|mild curry]], making it accessible to a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of palates. The recipe is presented as an economical and hearty one-pot meal, ideal for feeding a family, and traditionally served with [[concepts/white-basmati-rice|white basmati rice]].

Daz begins by browning the sausages thoroughly in a pot before slicing them into smaller, angled pieces. This step is crucial not only for developing flavor but also for helping the sausages maintain their shape and prevent them from disintegrating into a mush during the simmering process. Following this, he sautés finely diced onions until slightly colored, then adds curry powder directly to the hot pan, [[concepts/stirring-technique|stirring]] it for a couple of minutes. He [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] that cooking the curry powder this way extracts significantly more flavor, differentiating it from an Indian hot curry where spices might be "burned." An optional addition of garlic paste is also made at this stage.

A key element Daz introduces as his "[[concepts/secret-ingredient|secret ingredient]]" is honey. He adds honey to the pan, explaining that its simmering action helps to deglaze the pot, lifting any flavorful [[concepts/classical-bits|bits]] stuck to the bottom. More importantly, it contributes a beautiful sweetness that complements the curry spices. Sliced carrots are added next, followed by a sprinkle of turmeric (which he [[concepts/notes|notes]] he usually adds with the curry powder). The cooked sausages are then returned to the pot, and beef stock (initially misspoken as chicken stock) is poured in. The mixture is brought to a boil, then chunky potatoes are added, and the pot is covered to simmer for approximately 10 minutes, allowing the vegetables to cook through.

To achieve a thick, rich gravy, Daz incorporates a cornstarch slurry mixed with cold water, [[concepts/stirring-technique|stirring]] gently to avoid breaking up the potatoes. Finally, green peas are stirred in at the very last moment, cooked for just one minute before the heat is turned off. This precise timing ensures the peas retain their vibrant green color and fresh [[concepts/texture|texture]], preventing them from becoming overcooked and dull. Daz concludes by suggesting that while the stovetop method is effective, cooking this dish in a slow cooker overnight or during the day can yield an even softer, smoother [[concepts/texture|texture]] for the sausages, making it an "absolutely brilliant" meal he highly recommends trying.

📝 *** THE RECIPE *** 📝

This should serve 4-6 adults.
Sausages (plain beef, pork, vegetarian) - 6
Diced Onion (large) - 1
Mild Curry Powder - 2 teaspoons.
Turmeric  - 1 teaspoon.
Garlic  - 1 teaspoon. (optional)
Honey - 1/3 cup
Carrots [[entities/medium|medium]]  - 2.
Potatoes - medium - 5
Frozen Peas (defrosted) - 1 cup.
Beef or Chicken  Stock (hot) - 3 cups (use a stock of your choice).
Corn Starch (corn flour) - 2 teaspoons in ¼ cup of cold water (thicken to your preference).
