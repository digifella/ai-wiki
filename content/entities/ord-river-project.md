# Ord River Project

The [[concepts/large-scale-infrastructure|Ord River Project]] is an irrigation development scheme located in Western Australia's Kimberley region, designed to [[concepts/harness|harness]] water resources from the Ord River for agricultural expansion. The project aimed to transform the region into a productive agricultural area through irrigation infrastructure including dams, channels, and [[concepts/water-management-systems|water management systems]].

## Economic and Implementation Challenges

The project encountered significant economic difficulties throughout its development and operation phases. Initial [[concepts/cost|cost]] estimates were exceeded considerably, and the anticipated returns on investment failed to materialise as planned. The scheme faced unforeseen challenges including engineering complications, environmental factors, and difficulties in attracting and retaining agricultural operators in the remote location. These obstacles resulted in the project underperforming relative to its original economic projections and development goals.

## Legacy

Despite its struggles, the Ord River Project remains a significant case study in large-scale Australian infrastructure planning, illustrating the complexities involved in developing [[concepts/large-scale-irrigation|irrigation systems]] in remote regions and the importance of accurate feasibility assessment in major development initiatives.
