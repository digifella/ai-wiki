## I made this all platform CAD
**Clip title:** I made this all platform CAD
**Author / channel:** Brayden Nilsen
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=78lGpE8UtB8

### Summary
Here is a summary of the video:

The video discusses the creation and features of **PaliCAD**, a 3D CAD software developed by the speaker in response to frustrations with existing CAD software, such as expensive subscriptions, commercial use limitations, storage bloat, and limited platform support. PaliCAD is designed to be affordable, lightweight, intuitive, offline-capable, and available across multiple devices.

The speaker highlights key features of PaliCAD, including its ability to create sketches, convert them into 3D objects, and use various CAD tools like fillets, chamfers, threads, hollowing, and Boolean operations. PaliCAD is built using Flutter and Dart, allowing it to run on macOS, Windows, Linux, iOS, and Android with an interface that adapts to touchscreens and traditional mouse and keyboard controls.

The video concludes with information on how to obtain PaliCAD. It is available as a one-time purchase of $6.99 USD or equivalent in local currency, with no required subscription. It can be purchased on the Apple App Store for iPad and through Patreon for desktop versions (Windows, Mac, Linux) and Android APK.

#### Description
I spent months developing PaliCAD (and years planning it), including building its underlying CAD geometry kernel from scratch. In this video, I explain why I started the project, the challenges I encountered and how it works.

PaliCAD on the Apple App Store (iPad only for now - other devices soon)
https://apps.apple.com/us/app/palicad/id6812409084

PaliCAD is my project to solve issues with the cost, size, bad UI, and platform limitations of existing CAD software. 

PaliCAD website
https://palicad.com

My website
https://braydennilsen.com

PaliCAD and its open-source kernel, Pali Ijo are still actively improving, so expect new features and more robust kernel progress along the way.

Development would not be possible without Cursor and Codex LLM coding agents. Whether you're enthusiastic about AI or completely opposed to it, the reality is that these tools are becoming a major part of software development. They are helping me make the software I have dreamed of for years and I think that's pretty remarkable.
