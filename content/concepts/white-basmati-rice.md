# White Basmati Rice

## Definition
Aromatic long-grain Rice variety native to India, Nepal, and Pakistan. Characterized by elongated slender grains, distinct floral-nutty aroma attributed to 2-acetyl-1-pyrroline, and high elongation ratio upon cooking without clumping.

## Properties
- **Appearance:** Ivory-white, slender grains.
- **[[concepts/texture|Texture]]:** Fluffy, separate grains; lower starch content than short-grain varieties.
- **Flavor:** Subtle nuttiness; high absorbency for spices, oils, and sauces.
- **[[concepts/preparation|Preparation]]:** Benefits from soaking to maximize grain elongation; versatile for Pilaf, Biryani, steaming, and one-pot methods.

## Culinary Applications
- Staple grain in South Asian Cuisine and Middle Eastern dishes.
- Ideal base for Currys and saucy preparations due to flavor retention.
