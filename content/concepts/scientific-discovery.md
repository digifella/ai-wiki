# Scientific Discovery

The systematic process of expanding human knowledge through observation, experimentation, and the validation of new phenomena.

## Core Framework
- Hypothesis generation and [[concepts/testing|testing]]
- [[concepts/empirical-evidence|Empirical evidence]] acquisition
- Peer review and reproducibility
- Falsifiability

### Feynman's Three-Step Method
[[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] introduced a three-step process for [[concepts/scientific-method|scientific inquiry]]:
- **Guess:** Formulate a hypothesis or new law.
- **[[concepts/compute|Compute]]:** Derive the logical consequences of the guess.
- **[[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|Compare]]:** Validate these consequences with empirical observations.
