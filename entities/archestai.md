# Archest.AI

**Archest.AI** is an [[concepts/open-source-enterprise-ai|open-source enterprise AI]] platform designed to provide [[concepts/secure-control|secure control]] and visibility for running [[concepts/ai-agent]]s in production environments. It addresses the challenges of [[concepts/governance|governance]], [[concepts/security|security]], and observability in [[concepts/agentic-ai|autonomous AI systems]].

## Key Features & Capabilities
- **[[concepts/secure|Secure]] Control**: Implements strict boundaries on what [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] can execute, preventing unauthorized actions or data leaks.
- **Production Visibility**: Offers monitoring and logging capabilities similar to traditional DevOps tools, enabling teams to track agent behavior and performance.
- **Open Source**: Built as an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] platform, allowing for community contributions and [[concepts/customization|customization]].
- **Integration**: Compatible with [[concepts/local-llm|local LLM]] runners like [[entities/ollama]] for flexible deployment options.

## Background & Team
- The development team has prior [[concepts/experience|experience]] building **[[concepts/grafana-on-call|Grafana On-Call]]**, bringing [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] in [[concepts/incident-response|incident management]] and high-availability systems to the AI domain.
- Focuses on bridging the gap between experimental AI prototypes and robust, secure production deployments.
