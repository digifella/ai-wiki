## Gemini Skills and Connectors for Construction
**Clip title:** Gemini Skills and Connectors for Construction
**Author / channel:** Tim Fairley
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DjJbAHis1uQ
**Credibility:** Commentary (tier 1 of 5). YouTube video from Tim Fairley: not edited or peer reviewed, so its claims are the speaker's own. Summary written by Gemini.

### Summary
The video covers using "Skills" in Gemini for construction management. Skills are saved instruction sets, reference files, and controls that help define AI tasks like construction takeoffs or estimating. The speaker explains how to set up skills in Gemini through five different methods, including creating them with Gemini, writing them manually, or converting a chat into a skill. The speaker also discusses best practices such as avoiding overfitting and defining clear inputs, methods, and output formats. Finally, the speaker highlights differences in Gemini's connectors and the lack of a built-in plugin feature compared to other tools like ChatGPT and Claude.

### Video Description & Links
#### Description
I show you how to set up and run Gemini skills for construction, including bringing across skills you've already built in Claude or ChatGPT.

Using warehouse drawings in Google Drive, I work through a take-off skill that links quantities to marked drawings, then a pricing skill that uses saved rates to build a draft direct cost estimate. I cover how to review the output and keep unresolved quantities and unpriced work visible.

I also explain the difference between running skills in chat and in tasks, how connectors bring in your Google Workspace files, and where plugins fit into the picture.
