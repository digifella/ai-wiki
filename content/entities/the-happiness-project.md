# The Happiness Project

[[entities/happiness-its-causes|The Happiness Project]] is a video presentation by [[entities/gretchen-rubin|Gretchen Rubin]] focused on practical approaches to increasing personal [[concepts/happiness|happiness]] through deliberate habit formation. The presentation emphasizes actionable strategies that individuals can implement in their daily lives to achieve greater well-being.

## Content and Focus

The video explores the relationship between happiness and [[concepts/daily-routines|daily habits]], presenting Rubin's research and [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] on how structured behavioral changes can lead to sustained improvements in [[concepts/quality-of-life|life satisfaction]]. The presentation was delivered [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the [[entities/happiness--its-causes|Happiness & Its Causes]] event in 2015, positioning it within academic and popular discourse on [[concepts/positive-psychology|positive psychology]] and well-being.

## Gretchen Rubin's Approach

Rubin's framework centers on identifying which [[concepts/habits|habits]] and practices genuinely contribute to happiness for different individuals, rather than prescribing universal solutions. The presentation reflects her broader work on personal productivity, [[concepts/motivation|motivation]], and the [[concepts/science|science]] of habit formation as they relate to quality of life.
