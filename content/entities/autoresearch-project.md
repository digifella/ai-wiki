# Autoresearch Project

The [[concepts/automated-code-modification|Autoresearch]] Project is a research initiative focused on developing and studying [[concepts/test-diagnosis|self-evolving AI systems]] that can autonomously improve their own performance through iterative modification. The core concept involves [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] that modify their own computational processes—referred to as "harnesses"—without external human intervention, enabling a form of [[concepts/ai-recursive-self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]].

## Technical Approach

The project explores mechanisms by which [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and other AI systems can analyze their own outputs, identify inefficiencies, and autonomously generate [[concepts/code|code]] or process modifications to enhance subsequent iterations. This approach leverages the ability of [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]] to reason about and alter their own operational [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], creating [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] loops that [[entities/theoretically-media|theoretically]] accelerate [[concepts/software-performance|performance optimization]] over time.

## Significance

The Autoresearch Project addresses fundamental questions about AI capability development and scalability. By investigating how systems can self-modify and self-improve, the research touches on broader concerns about [[concepts/self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]] dynamics in [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]], including implications for [[concepts/safe-ai-use|AI safety]] and the potential for rapid capability acceleration. The project's work with [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] also contributes to understanding self-improvement mechanisms outside proprietary systems.
