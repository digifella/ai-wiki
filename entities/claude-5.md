# Claude 5

**[[entities/claude|Claude]] 5** refers to the fifth generation of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] developed by Anthropic. As of 2026, the ecosystem includes variants such as [[entities/fable-5]] and [[entities/opus-5]], representing significant advancements in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and context handling capabilities.

## Key Developments

*   **Shift in Prompting Paradigms**: Best practices have moved away from rigid, explicit step-by-step instructions toward defining broad goals and providing rich context [[lab-notes/2026-09-14-Claude-5-Prompting-From-Explicit-Steps-to-Broad-Goals-an|Claude 5 Prompting: From Explicit Steps to Broad Goals and Context]].
*   **Model Landscape**: The generation includes [[entities/fable-5]], [[entities/opus-5]], and implicitly competes with or parallels developments in [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[entities/gpt-6-astra]].
*   **Contextual Understanding**: Enhanced ability to interpret high-level objectives without granular procedural constraints, reducing the need for "[[entities/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]]" in the traditional sense.
