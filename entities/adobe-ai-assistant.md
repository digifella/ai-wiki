# Adobe Ai Assistant

The Adobe Ai Assistant is a feature integrated into Adobe Photoshop that leverages artificial intelligence to streamline design workflows and reduce repetitive tasks. The tool is designed to help designers and image editors work more efficiently by automating common operations across projects of varying complexity.

## Automated Layer Renaming

One of the primary capabilities of the Ai Assistant is automated layer renaming. This function analyzes the content of individual layers and assigns descriptive names based on the visual elements present, such as "Background" or "Text." This automation helps maintain organized file structures without requiring manual input from the user.

## Generative Markup Tools

In addition to layer management, the assistant provides generative markup tools. These features utilize AI to interpret and generate structural data within the design file, further reducing the need for manual tagging and organization. Together, these functions aim to accelerate the pre-production and organization phases of digital design projects.
