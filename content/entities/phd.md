# PhD

A PhD (Doctor of Philosophy) is a terminal research degree awarded by universities across numerous disciplines, including sciences, engineering, humanities, and medicine. The degree typically requires several years of advanced study beyond a bachelor's degree, culmination in original research, and completion of a doctoral dissertation.

In medical and scientific contexts, the PhD is often held alongside professional credentials. For example, individuals may hold both an MD (Medical Doctor) and a PhD, combining clinical practice or medical [[concepts/training|training]] with advanced research qualifications. This dual credential is common among physician-scientists and medical researchers who conduct original research while maintaining clinical responsibilities.

The PhD designation appears in professional contexts to indicate advanced expertise and research credentials. Professionals with this qualification often contribute to academic discourse, research publications, and evidence-based developments in their respective fields.
