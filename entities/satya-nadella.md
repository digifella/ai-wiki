# Satya Nadella

Satya Nadella is an Indian-American business executive who has served as the Chief Executive Officer of Microsoft since February 2014. He joined the company in 1992, spending over two decades in various technical and leadership roles before ascending to the top position. His appointment marked a pivotal moment for the technology giant, initiating a period of significant strategic transformation.

Under Nadella's leadership, Microsoft shifted its focus toward cloud computing, artificial intelligence, and subscription-based services. This repositioning helped revitalize the company's market position and relevance in emerging technology sectors. The strategic pivot away from its traditional desktop-centric model allowed Microsoft to regain momentum in the competitive enterprise software and cloud infrastructure markets.

Nadella's tenure is characterized by a cultural shift within the organization, emphasizing empathy and a growth mindset. He has overseen major acquisitions, including LinkedIn and GitHub, which expanded Microsoft's ecosystem and developer community. His approach has been widely credited with stabilizing the company's financial performance and driving its continued dominance in the global technology industry.
