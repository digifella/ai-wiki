# Battlefield Reality

**Definition:** The objective material state of [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] operations, defined by verifiable force dispositions, attrition metrics, logistical capacity, and terrain [[concepts/power|control]]. Contrasts with Narrative Warfare, propaganda, and speculative forecasting. Ground truth requires correlation of multiple data sources to filter informational noise.

## Core Indicators
- **Material Attrition:** Equipment loss rates vs. production/mobilization [[concepts/output|output]].
- **Operational Tempo:** Changes in [[concepts/exercise|movement]] density and engagement frequency independent of rhetoric.
- **Public Displays:** Correlation between ceremonial force presentation and actual combat readiness.
- **Frontline Stability:** Terrain retention vs. claimed breakthroughs.
