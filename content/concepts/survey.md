# Survey

A survey is a systematic method of collecting data from a sample population to gather information about trends, preferences, or behaviors. In the context of [[concepts/coding|software development]] and [[concepts/cryptography|cryptography]], surveys serve as empirical tools for understanding how developers adopt new tools, technologies, and practices across the industry.

## Developer Surveys on AI Adoption

Recent developer surveys have examined the adoption patterns of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools and their integration into existing technology stacks. These surveys typically gather [[concepts/responses|responses]] from practicing developers about which [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] they use, how they integrate them into their workflows, and how these tools affect their development practices. By comparing multiple surveys conducted around the same period, researchers and industry analysts can identify consistent trends and diverging patterns in technology adoption.

## Methodology and Significance

Developer surveys in this domain rely on self-reported data from respondents across various experience levels and specializations. The comparison of multiple surveys helps validate findings and reveals whether adoption patterns are broad-based or specific to particular developer communities. Understanding [[concepts/tech-stack|tech stack]] adoption through surveys provides valuable insights for tool developers, organizations planning technology investments, and individual developers evaluating which tools to learn.
