# Global Health Network

The **Global [[concepts/health|Health]] Network** refers to the interconnected infrastructure of data, technology, and medical resources designed to improve health outcomes worldwide. This network leverages [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] and remote sensing technologies to enable scalable diagnostics and continuous patient monitoring across geographic boundaries.

## Key Components & Developments

*   **AI-Driven Diagnostics**: Integration of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] for rapid [[concepts/image-analysis|image analysis]] and predictive health analytics.
*   **[[concepts/remote-monitoring|Remote Monitoring]] Infrastructure**: Deployment of accessible hardware for longitudinal data collection in underserved or remote areas.
*   **[[entities/midjourney-medical|Midjourney Medical]] Initiative**: A notable expansion into [[concepts/medical-imaging-technology|medical imaging]] via the development of a global network of [[concepts/full-body-ultrasound|full-body Ultrasound]] scanners. This initiative aims to facilitate continuous, non-invasive health tracking. See detailed analysis in [[lab-notes/2026-06-21-Midjourney-Medical-AI-Ultrasound-Scanners-for-Longitudin|Midjourney Medical: AI Ultrasound Scanners for Longitudinal Health Monitoring]].
