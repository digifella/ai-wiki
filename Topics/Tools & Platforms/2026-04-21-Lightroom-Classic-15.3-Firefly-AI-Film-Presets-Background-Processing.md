## Lightroom Classic 15.3: Firefly AI, Film Presets, Background Processing
**Clip title:** What's NEW in Lightroom Classic ver 15.3 — Background AI Is a GAME CHANGER
**Author / channel:** Anthony Morganti
**URL:** https://youtu.be/797b8VFXIYs

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive overview of the new and enhanced features introduced in [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] Classic version 15.3. The main topics covered include the deeper [[concepts/integration|integration]] with Adobe's Firefly [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] platform for photo and [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]], the addition of new "[[concepts/style|Style]]: Film-inspired" presets, and a significant [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] improvement with AI processing now running in the background. The presenter, [[entities/anthony-morganti|Anthony Morganti]], walks through each feature with practical demonstrations and explanations of their functionality and implications for photographers.

A significant enhancement in this release is the direct [[concepts/integration|integration]] with [[entities/adobe-firefly|Adobe Firefly]], building upon features introduced in version 15.2. Users can now send [[concepts/images|images]] from [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] directly to Firefly for generative AI operations via the "File" menu or a right-click context menu. This includes "Edit Photo," where users can issue [[concepts/text|text]] prompts to add or remove elements (like a seagull in a sky) or expand the [[concepts/canvas|canvas]], with credit costs varying by AI model and resolution. Another option, "Generate Video from Photo," allows for creating short, animated videos (up to 8 seconds) from a still image, also guided by [[concepts/text|text]] prompts and impacting generative credit usage based on duration and chosen AI model. New to version 15.3 is the "Start a Mood Board" feature, designed for collating multiple [[concepts/images|images]], text, and other elements in a creative layout on the Firefly web interface.

Beyond the Firefly integration, [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] 15.3 introduces a new set of built-in presets called "[[concepts/style|Style]]: Film-inspired." These 12 new presets offer various aesthetic looks reminiscent of classic film photography, such as "Warm Gold," "Rich Gold," "Faded Blue," and different [[concepts/black-and-white|black and white]] styles. Users can easily preview these effects by hovering over them in the Presets panel, offering quick creative options without manual [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

Perhaps the most impactful, though less flashy, update for many users is the ability for AI-powered features like Denoise, [Raw Details](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raw_Details), and [Super Resolution](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Super_Resolution) to now run in the background. Previously, activating these features would lock up Lightroom Classic until processing was complete, hindering [[concepts/workflow|workflow]], especially with multiple images or high-resolution [[concepts/files|files]]. With this update, users can initiate these AI processes on single or multiple images and continue working on other tasks within Lightroom Classic, significantly improving productivity and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]].

In conclusion, [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] Classic 15.3 brings a blend of creative AI capabilities and practical workflow enhancements. The Firefly integration opens up new generative editing avenues for photos, videos, and mood boards, albeit with a credit-based system. The addition of film-inspired presets provides more stylistic choices, and the background processing of AI functions is a substantial improvement for efficient [[concepts/photo-management|photo management]] and editing. These updates underscore Adobe's commitment to integrating advanced AI directly into professional photography workflows, offering powerful tools while also refining user experience.
