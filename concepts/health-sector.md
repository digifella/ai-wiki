# Health Sector

The **health sector** comprises the organizations, institutions, and resources dedicated to maintaining, improving, and restoring the health of individuals and populations. It encompasses [[concepts/health|healthcare]] delivery systems, [[concepts/population-health|public health]] initiatives, pharmaceutical industries, and [[concepts/health-information-technology|health information technology]].

## Key Dimensions

- **[[concepts/service-delivery|Service Delivery]]**: Hospitals, clinics, primary care, and specialized medical services.
- **[[concepts/governance|Governance]] & Policy**: Regulatory frameworks, funding [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] (public/private), and [[concepts/healthcare-policy|health policy]] formulation.
- **[[concepts/digital-innovations|Digital Transformation]]**: Integration of IT systems, [[concepts/electronic-health-records|electronic health records]] (EHR), and [[entities/big-data|data analytics]] to improve efficiency and outcomes.
