# Luminance adjustment

The process of manipulating the luminance of brightness ranges or specific color channels to recover detail, manage [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], and [[concepts/power|control]] color-specific brightness.

## Core Tonal Controls
- [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]]: Global brightness adjustment.
- [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]]: Controls the luminance of the darkest areas.
- [[concepts/whites|Whites]]: Sets the brightness of the brightest points.
- [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]]: Sets the darkest point of the image.

## Color-Specific Luminance
- [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]]: Enables targeted luminance [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] within individual color channels, alongside [[concepts/hue|Hue]] and Saturation.

## Workflow & Implementation
- Part of Initial Image [[concepts/preparation|Preparation]], which typically includes [[concepts/straightening|straightening]] and basic panel [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]], [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]], Whites, and [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]].
- Applied via [[concepts/masking|Masking]] presets, such as the "Landscape" feature available in [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]], [[entities/lightroom-cc|Lightroom CC]], and [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw]].
- Targeted color-specific luminance manipulation via the [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]] panel in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]].
