# Starter Plan

Evernote's Starter Plan serves as the entry-level tier within the company's restructured pricing model, which was implemented in 2026. This plan represents a strategic shift toward a more segmented subscription framework, designed to accommodate users with basic note-taking requirements and limited feature needs. By positioning the Starter Plan below mid-tier and premium options, Evernote aims to address a wider spectrum of customer usage patterns and budget constraints.

The introduction of this tier coincided with significant adjustments to the overall pricing structure, resulting in notable user reactions regarding the increase in costs for previously free or lower-cost services. The reorganization of the pricing model was intended to clarify value propositions across different levels of service, allowing the company to better align its offerings with specific user demands while maintaining a clear hierarchy between the entry-level Starter Plan and higher-value tiers.
