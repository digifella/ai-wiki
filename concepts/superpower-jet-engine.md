# SuperPower Jet Engine

The **SuperPower Jet Engine** refers to the advanced propulsion technology being developed to enable [[concepts/commercial-supersonic-flight|commercial supersonic flight]], specifically associated with [[entities/boom-supersonic|Boom Supersonic]]'s [[concepts/overture-airliner]] airliner program. This initiative aims to revive viable supersonic passenger travel through significant advancements in engine efficiency, thrust-to-weight ratio, and noise reduction.

## Key Developments

*   **Symphony Engine Integration**: The core propulsion system for the Overture is the Symphony (engine) engine, developed in partnership with GE Aerospace. This engine is designed to meet the rigorous demands of sustained supersonic cruise while adhering to environmental standards.
*   **Commercial Ambitions**: The program targets the restoration of commercial supersonic passenger flight, leveraging the "SuperPower" capabilities to achieve faster transcontinental travel times.
*   **Technical Validation**: Recent analyses confirm the viability of the engine's design, focusing on the integration of advanced materials and thermodynamic cycles to maximize performance.
