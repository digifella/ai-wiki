# Historical Preservation

**Historical [[concepts/preservation|preservation]]** refers to the practice of protecting and conserving buildings, sites, and objects of [[concepts/historical-significance|historical significance]] to maintain cultural heritage for future generations. It involves balancing the need for modern development with the ethical imperative to retain tangible links to the past.

## Key Principles

*   **Authenticity:** Maintaining the original materials, design, and craftsmanship.
*   **Adaptive Reuse:** Repurposing historic structures for contemporary use while retaining significant features.
*   **Documentation:** Recording the history and physical state of heritage assets.
