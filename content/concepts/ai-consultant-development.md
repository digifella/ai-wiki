# AI Consultant Development

A framework for transforming individual expertise and unstructured personal [[concepts/notes|notes]] into professional, monetizable AI-driven [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] services.

## Core Strategy: Productizing Expertise
The fundamental objective is to "productize" the consultant's cognitive assets by synthesizing proprietary knowledge into structured [[concepts/logic-manuals|Logic Manuals]]. These manuals act as the authoritative intelligence layer used to [[concepts/power|power]] [[concepts/custom-ai-assistants|custom AI assistants]].
