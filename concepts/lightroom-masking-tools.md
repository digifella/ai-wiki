# Lightroom Masking Tools

Part of the [[entities/adobe-lightroom]] suite used for performing precise, non-destructive [[concepts/masking-tools|Local Adjustments]].

## Masking Operations
- [[concepts/add-masking]]: Expands the current selection area.
- [[concepts/subtract-masking]]: Removes specific areas from the existing selection.
- [[concepts/intersect-masking]]: Isolates only the area where two or more masks overlap.
	- Introduced in late 2023.
	- Provides a high-[[concepts/accuracy|precision]] method for complex selections by finding the common area between multiple masks.
