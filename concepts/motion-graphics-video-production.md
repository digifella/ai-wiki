# Motion Graphics Video Production

Systematic creation of animated visual content using digital tools, encompassing keyframe animation, procedural generation, and code-based [[concepts/writing|composition]] for media, advertising, and [[concepts/information-visualization|data visualization]].

## Core Workflows
- **Design & Storyboarding**: Conceptualization, timeline structuring, and asset [[concepts/preparation|preparation]].
- **Animation Engines**: Keyframing, easing curves, and [[concepts/physics|physics]] simulations within DAWs or dedicated [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] software.
- **[[concepts/visual-rendering|Rendering]] & Export**: Rasterization, vector [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]], and codec optimization for distribution.

## Tools & Technologies
- **[[concepts/digital-compositing|Compositing]] Suites**: After Effects, Nuke, DaVinci Resolve Fusion.
- **3D Environments**: Blender, Cinema 4D, Houdini.
- **Code-Based Animation**: [[entities/remotion]], [[concepts/threejs]], p5.js for data-driven and reproducible [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]].
