# Ethical Considerations in AI Applications

[[concepts/ethics|Ethics]] plays a crucial role in the development and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) [[concepts/software|applications]]. It addresses issues ranging from data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] to transparency and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] in [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes.

## Key Concepts

- **Bias and Fairness**: Ensuring that AI systems do not perpetuate or exacerbate existing [[concepts/biases|biases]].
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]**: Protecting user information and ensuring [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with data protection laws.
- **Transparency**: Making the workings of AI algorithms understandable to stakeholders.
- **Accountability**: Holding entities responsible for the actions and decisions made by AI systems.

## Top 10 Security Risks in AI Agents (2026)

### Overview
The [[concepts/owasp|OWASP]] GenAI [[concepts/security|Security]] Project has identified key security risks associated with [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|AI agentic applications]]. These risks are critical to understanding and mitigating vulnerabilities that can arise from deploying intelligent [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] in various environments.

### Key Points

- **Data Poisoning**: Manipulating [[concepts/training-data|training data]] to alter [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]].
- **Model Theft**: Stealing trained [[concepts/models|models]] to exploit [[concepts/intellectual-property-rights|intellectual property]] or misuse [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].
- **Extraction of [[concepts/language-data|Training Data]]**: Reversing engineered AI systems to recover sensitive information used during [[concepts/training|training]].
- **AI Model Manipulation**: Altering the operational environment or inputs to deceive the AI system.
- **Adversarial Attacks**: Injecting malicious input data designed to mislead the model and cause it to [[entities/make|make]] incorrect decisions.
- **[[concepts/data-conceptsintegrityintegrity|Data Integrity]] Violations**: Tampering with [[concepts/dynamic-data-feeds|live data streams]] to disrupt service availability.
- **Model Inversion**: Revealing sensitive attributes about individuals by analyzing model outputs.
- **Unintended Behaviors**: Encouraging [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to perform tasks they were not designed for, potentially harmful or unethical.
- **Lack of [[concepts/power|Control]]**: Failing to implement proper [[concepts/governance|governance]] and oversight mechanisms for deployed AI systems.
- **Insufficient Transparency**: Failing to provide adequate [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] about how decisions are made by [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].
