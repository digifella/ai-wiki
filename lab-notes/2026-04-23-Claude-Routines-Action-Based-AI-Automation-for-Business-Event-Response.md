## Claude Routines: Action-Based AI Automation for Business Event Response
**Clip title:** Claude Routines Just Launched. Here Are 13 You Need.
**Author / channel:** AI Founders
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KpG2yBi5I10

### Summary
The video introduces [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s new [[entities/claude-routines|Claude Routines]], marking a significant evolution in [[concepts/ai-automation|AI automation]]. Traditionally, AI [[concepts/automations|automations]] were time-based, functioning like an alarm clock that triggers at specific intervals regardless of events. [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] Routines, however, are action-based, akin to a doorbell. They respond instantly to real-world events within a business, such as a new lead submitting a form, a negative review being posted, a missed call, an overdue invoice, or a canceled booking. This allows Claude to automatically handle these events autonomously in the cloud, even with a closed laptop, offering a new level of [[concepts/cost|operational efficiency]].

A Claude Routine is essentially a powerful package comprising three core elements. First, "[[concepts/instructions|Instructions]]" serve as a plain English prompt, detailing Claude's designated role (e.g., "my lead follow-up specialist"), the desired outcome, a sequence of steps, the expected output, and any governing rules. This functions like a job description for the AI. Second, a "Workspace" is a [[concepts/secure|secure]] cloud folder where Claude stores essential reference materials such as brand guidelines, [[entities/email|email]] [[concepts/templates|templates]], [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] sheets, or FAQs. The more information provided, and the more it's updated, the "smarter" Claude becomes over time. Third, "App Access" dictates which [[concepts/business-applications|business applications]] (like [[entities/slack|Slack]], [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], [[concepts/google-drive|Google Drive]], [[entities/notion|Notion]], or CRM systems) Claude is permitted to interact with, allowing users to balance capability with desired control.

The video clarifies key distinctions between Routines and existing [[concepts/automation|automation]] methods. Unlike traditional "[[concepts/temporary-cron-jobs|Scheduled Tasks]]," which are rigidly time-based and often rely on a user's local machine being open, Routines are event-driven, cloud-based, and fully autonomous, firing only when a specific event occurs. Furthermore, Routines diverge from "[[concepts/workflow|Workflow]] Builders" like [[entities/n8n|n8n]] or [[entities/zapier|Zapier]]. While these tools require users to meticulously map out every single step, condition, and branching path in a flowchart, Claude Routines only demand a description of the desired *outcome*. Claude then leverages its innate intelligence to dynamically determine the most effective steps, adapting its approach based on the specific context of each event, rather than following a rigid, predefined [[concepts/flow|flow]]. This makes routines superior for tasks requiring nuanced understanding and flexible [[concepts/responses|responses]].

The presenter offers 13 specific routine examples, categorized into event-triggered "doorbells" and scheduled "alarm clocks," demonstrating their immediate business value. Event-triggered routines include a New Lead Follow-Up Writer for instant, personalized [[concepts/responses|responses]]; a Negative Review Response Drafter to swiftly address feedback; a Missed Call Recovery Assistant to prevent lost opportunities; an Overdue Invoice Nudger for gentle payment reminders; a Canceled Booking Win-Back Draft for retaining clients; a [[entities/contact|Contact]] Form Router for efficient inquiry management; a New Customer Onboarding Message for tailored welcomes; a Support Escalation Summarizer for quick issue resolution; an Abandoned Cart Recovery Writer for e-commerce; and a Churn Risk Responder for proactive retention. Scheduled routines encompass a Morning Founder Command-Center Report for daily briefings; a Weekly Founder Product Brief for [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] insights; and a Competitor Launch Watcher for automated [[concepts/market-intelligence|market intelligence]]. The video concludes by encouraging viewers to select one of these routines that addresses an immediate business problem and set it up today, highlighting how these AI-powered [[concepts/automations|automations]] can significantly enhance efficiency, customer satisfaction, and revenue for various businesses.
