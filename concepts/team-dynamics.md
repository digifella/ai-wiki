# Team Dynamics

**[[concepts/group-dynamics|Team dynamics]]** refers to [[concepts/unconscious-mind|the unconscious]], often unspoken [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and interactions that affect the way team members work together and the performance of the team as a whole. Effective dynamics rely on [[concepts/trust|trust]], clear roles, and [[concepts/open-communication|open communication]].

## Key Drivers

### Psychological Safety
A core component of healthy [[concepts/team-management|team dynamics]] is [[concepts/psychological-safety]], the shared belief that the team is safe for interpersonal risk-taking.

- **[[concepts/health|Healthcare]] Context**: Critical in medicine for error reporting and patient safety.
- **Key Insight**: Silence in high-stakes environments often stems from fear of speaking up, not lack of knowledge.
- **Source Analysis**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Amy-Edmondson-Psychological-safety-is-critically-importa| AAMC]] highlights that without psychological safety, teams cannot leverage diverse [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] effectively.
- **Implementation Framework**: [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Four-Steps-to-Building-the-Psychological-Safety-That-Hig| Working Knowledge]] outlines actionable steps to cultivate this environment, emphasizing leader [[concepts/vulnerability|vulnerability]] and structured inquiry to normalize error disclosure and [[concepts/learning|learning]].
