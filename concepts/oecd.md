# OECD

The **Organisation for Economic Co-operation and Development** ([[entities/oecd]]) is an intergovernmental economic organization with 38 member countries, founded in 1960 to stimulate economic progress and [[concepts/global-trade|world trade]]. It serves as a forum for governments to connect, share experiences, and seek solutions to common problems.

## Key Characteristics
- Promotes [[concepts/policies|policies]] that raise living standards and maintain economic stability.
- Monitors global economic trends and provides data-driven analysis.
- Distinct from but often collaborates with the [[entities/european-union]] on regulatory and economic standards.
- Engages in [[concepts/policy-analysis|policy analysis]] regarding emerging technologies, balancing regulatory safety with [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] incentives.
