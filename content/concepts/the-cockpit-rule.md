# The Cockpit Rule

The [[concepts/cockpit-rule|Cockpit Rule]] is a framework for [[concepts/ai-skill-creation|AI skill development]] presented by [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]] in his video "4 [[concepts/skills|Skills]] to Get Ahead (Beyond [[concepts/simple-prompting|Basic Prompts]])." The concept addresses the evolution of AI competency in professional settings, where basic AI usage has shifted from a competitive advantage to a baseline expectation. The rule suggests that to meaningfully differentiate oneself in the job market, professionals must develop skills beyond simple prompt [[concepts/writing|writing]].

The framework identifies four core skills necessary for career advancement in an AI-integrated workplace. These skills represent deeper proficiencies that enable professionals to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] tools more effectively than those relying on elementary techniques. The Cockpit Rule serves as a practical guide for understanding what constitutes advanced AI competency and how to strategically develop expertise in areas that remain genuinely valuable in professional contexts.

By framing [[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI skills]] in this hierarchical way, the rule acknowledges that the baseline has shifted. Just as basic computer literacy was once a differentiator but is now assumed, the same transition has occurred with fundamental AI usage. The Cockpit Rule emphasizes the importance of moving beyond this baseline to develop specialized [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that provide lasting career value.
