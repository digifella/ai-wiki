# Azure AI

Azure AI is Microsoft's comprehensive suite of artificial intelligence services built on the Azure cloud platform. It enables organizations to build, train, and deploy custom AI models and applications at scale. The platform provides managed services and scalable infrastructure designed to integrate with Azure's broader ecosystem of computing and data resources.

## Core Capabilities

The suite consolidates multiple AI-related offerings under a unified brand, including services for machine learning, natural language processing, computer vision, and decision-making applications. Azure AI abstracts much of the complexity involved in AI development, offering pre-built models and tools alongside capabilities for training custom models on proprietary data.

## Integration and Infrastructure

Azure AI leverages Azure's cloud infrastructure to provide the computational resources necessary for training and deploying AI workloads. The platform is designed to integrate with other Azure services, allowing organizations to incorporate AI capabilities into existing cloud-based applications and workflows. This integration supports both development and production deployment scenarios.
