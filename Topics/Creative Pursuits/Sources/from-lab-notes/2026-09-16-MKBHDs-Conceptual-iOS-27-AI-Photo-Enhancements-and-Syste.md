## MKBHD's Conceptual iOS 27: AI Photo Enhancements and System Refinements
**Clip title:** iOS 27 Hands-On: Top 5 New Features!
**Author / channel:** Marques Brownlee
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_oRgdlJUD18

### Summary
This video provides a satirical yet insightful conceptual overview of "[[concepts/ios-27|iOS 27]]" (implying features far into the future, given the year "2027" in the title and the iPhone 14 Pro shown), focusing on a theme of "refinement" rather than groundbreaking changes. The host, [[entities/marques-brownlee|Marques Brownlee]], discusses five key features and several honorable mentions, highlighting how many of these are long-overdue improvements or clever integrations of AI that [[entities/apple|Apple]] has historically withheld. His overall impression of this hypothetical future iOS is that it represents a culmination of highly anticipated tweaks and practical enhancements.

Among the standout AI-powered enhancements are new photo editing tools. Beyond improved "Clean Up" for background object removal, iOS 27 introduces "Extend" and "Reframe." "Extend" uses generative AI to intelligently expand the edges of a photo, allowing users to change aspect ratios without cropping. "Reframe" takes this further by enabling users to virtually "move" the camera's perspective within a photo, generating new pixels to create the illusion of a different angle, though the quality might subtly degrade. Both features rely on an internet connection for cloud processing.

The "Finally" section addresses several long-standing user requests that Android users have enjoyed for years. These include separate volume controls for alarms and system sounds, improved logic for switching from weak Wi-Fi networks to cellular data, and the ability to scrub media playback directly within CarPlay. Furthermore, AirPods with an H2 chip or later finally receive custom three-band equalizer settings, offering users more control over their audio experience. Aesthetic tweaks also enhance the user interface with updated, crisper app icons, a new "Appearance" tab featuring a "Liquid Glass" slider for granular control over transparency effects, and a redesigned Home screen search integrated with Siri.

The most significant update is arguably the "New Siri," reimagined as "Siri AI." Visually, Siri now expands from the Dynamic Island, appearing as a dynamic, semi-transparent orb that reacts to speech. The dedicated Siri app maintains a history of requests, and Siri boasts a more conversational tone, retaining context for follow-up questions, leveraging broader world knowledge, and exhibiting on-screen awareness (e.g., identifying objects in photos). Crucially, Siri gains the ability to access personal, on-device data from Apple's native apps like iMessage, Wallet, Photos, and Calendar, promising deeply personalized assistance. However, third-party app integration is depicted as a future waiting game, dependent on developers pushing updates to allow Siri access to their data.

Finally, the overarching number one feature is a noticeable improvement in system-wide *speed*. iOS 27 is said to deliver significantly faster performance across the board, including 70% faster photo loading, 30% quicker app launches, and 80% speedier AirDrop transfers. This optimization, particularly beneficial for older iPhone models (back to the iPhone 11), promises to make devices feel much newer and smoother. Marques concludes by strongly recommending the hypothetical iOS 27 update, implying it addresses fundamental user experience issues and leverages AI in practical, impactful ways.
