# World AI

**[[entities/world-ai|World AI]]** is an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] company founded by [[entities/fei-fei-li|Fei-Fei Li]], focusing on the development of [[concepts/spatial-intelligence|spatial intelligence]] to bridge the gap between digital models and [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]]. The organization aims to solve fundamental challenges in [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], particularly regarding data [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] and [[concepts/abstraction|generalization]] in unstructured environments.

## Core Concepts

*   **Spatial Intelligence**: A framework for [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to understand and interact with 3D space, enabling robots to navigate and manipulate objects without exhaustive pre-programming for every scenario.
*   **Data Scarcity [[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: Utilizing [[concepts/synthetic-puzzle-generation|synthetic data]] generation and simulation-to-real transfer techniques to overcome the lack of large-scale, [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] real-world robotics datasets.

## Key Developments & Acquisitions

*   **Acquisition of [[entities/scenix|Scenix]] (2026)**: World AI acquired Scenix, co-founded by [[entities/yunzhu-li|Yunzhu Li]], to integrate advanced [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] capabilities with its spatial intelligence models. This move consolidates [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] in creating realistic [[concepts/virtual-environments|virtual environments]] for training robotic agents.
*   **Strategic [[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]**: The combined entity focuses on deploying AI that can generalize across diverse physical tasks, reducing the dependency on massive [[concepts/real-world-data|real-world data]] collection efforts.
