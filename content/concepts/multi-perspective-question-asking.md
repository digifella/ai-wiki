# Multi-perspective question asking

A research technique used to ensure comprehensive topic coverage by generating diverse inquiry angles from various viewpoints, preventing bias and information gaps in automated discovery.

## Applications & Implementations
- **[[entities/stanford-university|Stanford]]'s [[entities/storm|STORM]] AI**: A system designed for [[concepts/agent-based-research]] and Knowledge Curation.
	- **Full Name**: Synthesis of Topic Outlines through Retrieval and Multi-perspective Question Asking.
	- **Function**: Uses multi-perspective inquiry to synthesize detailed topic outlines and perform verifiable research.
	- **Core Framework**: Leverages [[concepts/agentic-ai]] to simulate different perspectives for deep, structured [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]].
