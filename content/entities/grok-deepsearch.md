# Grok Deepsearch

Grok DeepSearch is an [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] agent developed as part of the Grok family of AI systems. It is designed to perform research-oriented tasks and has been evaluated in comparative benchmarks against other major [[concepts/ai-research-agents|AI research agents]].

## Evaluation and Benchmarking

Grok DeepSearch has been assessed alongside competing research [[concepts/agents|agents]] including [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] (specifically [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s o3 model), and Manus in standardized benchmarking tests. These evaluations measure the research capabilities and performance of different AI systems on specific tasks and datasets.
