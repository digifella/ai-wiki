# Time Clarity Interview System

A method for resolving time-related challenges by addressing underlying **clarity problems** (not time [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]]), using AI to conduct structured interviews that audit time usage patterns.

**Core Concept:** Business owners typically face a *[[concepts/clarity-problem|clarity problem]]*—moving rapidly without direction ("tread．effect")—not a time shortage. The system flips standard AI interaction: **AI interviews the user** to uncover time misalignment with strategic goals.
- Addresses the "treadmill" effect: moving fast without heading in a specific direction.
- Reverses standard [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] interaction: the AI conducts [[entities/the-interview|the interview]] rather than being prompted by the user.

**Method: "Flipped" Interaction**
- AI acts as a coach, asking structured questions about current time allocation (e.g., *"What tasks consume 3+ hours daily but don't align with top priorities?"*).
- Reveals hidden time sinks (distractions, low-value activities) and misaligned efforts.
- Generates actionable audit report showing time vs. strategic priorities.

**Implementation**
1. **Prompt AI (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 5.2)** with:
   *"Act as a [[concepts/time-clarity|time clarity]] coach. Interview me to identify misalignment between my time usage and strategic goals. Ask 5 targeted questions..."*
