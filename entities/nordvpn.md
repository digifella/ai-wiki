# Nordvpn

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012. The company operates a global network of servers that allow users to encrypt their internet connections and route traffic through its infrastructure. This process masks the user's IP address and geographic location from internet service providers, network administrators, and other entities that monitor network activity. The service enables users to conceal their online activity and location while browsing the internet.

By connecting to servers in various countries, users can bypass geographic restrictions imposed by content providers or governments. The platform offers applications for multiple operating systems, including Windows, macOS, Linux, iOS, and Android, as well as browser extensions. It also provides specialized servers for specific use cases, such as double VPN for enhanced privacy and obfuscated servers to bypass censorship in restrictive regions.

The company has faced scrutiny regarding its logging policies and past security incidents, including a 2018 breach of one of its no-logs servers. In response, NordVPN has undergone audits to verify its no-logs claim and implemented additional security measures, such as RAM-only servers that wipe data upon reboot. The service is headquartered in Panama, a jurisdiction with no mandatory data retention laws, which influences its privacy stance.
