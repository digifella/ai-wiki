# Flipped Interaction

A technique flipping the standard AI interaction paradigm: the AI initiates structured questioning to guide the user, rather than the user providing prompts. Addresses underlying issues (e.g., clarity) instead of surface-level symptoms (e.g., time management).

**Core Mechanics:**
- **Interaction Shift:** Instead of the user [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] the AI, the **AI interviews the user**.
- **Root Cause Focus:** Targets the *[[concepts/clarity-problem|clarity problem]]* rather than the *time problem*.
- **The "Treadmill" Effect:** Addresses the state of moving fast (high activity) without a specific direction (low clarity).

**Application: AI [[concepts/time-clarity-interview-system|Time Clarity Interview System]]**
- Uses an interview-[[concepts/style|style]] approach to audit time allocation and reveal misalignment with goals.
- Transforms [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 5.2 into a structured coach.
