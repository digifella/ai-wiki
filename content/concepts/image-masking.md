# Image Masking

Image masking is a digital editing technique used to isolate specific areas of an image for selective adjustment or manipulation. In photography and graphic design, [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] allows editors to apply effects, color corrections, or filters to precise regions while leaving the rest of the image untouched. The technique is particularly valuable for complex selections where traditional rectangular or geometric selection tools prove inadequate.

## AI-Powered Masking

Modern image editing [[concepts/software|software]], particularly Adobe's Lightroom and [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], increasingly incorporates AI-driven masking capabilities that automate selection processes. These systems can intelligently identify subjects like hair, skin, or landscape elements and generate masks with minimal user intervention. [[concepts/ai-based-selection|AI masking]] significantly reduces the time required for detailed selection work and has become a standard feature in contemporary [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] workflows, enabling photographers to achieve professional results more efficiently.

## Manual Masking Approaches

Traditional manual masking techniques remain relevant for complex or unusual subject matter where [[concepts/automations|automated systems]] may struggle. These methods typically involve layer masks, brush-based refinement, and channel-based selections that give editors precise control over masked areas. Manual masking requires skill and patience but provides the flexibility to handle edge cases and specialized artistic intentions that AI systems cannot always address.
