# Data Driven Content Iteration

Data Driven Content Iteration is a content creation methodology that applies automated optimization to social media video production. Rather than relying on manual creative workflows, the approach integrates performance metrics with algorithmic systems to continuously refine content outputs based on measurable engagement data. This enables faster feedback loops between content generation and audience response, allowing creators to identify effective patterns and adjust production strategies in real time.

The system utilizes Claude Code and Karpathy's Autoresearch to autonomously generate and optimize social video content. By leveraging these tools, the process shifts from static production cycles to dynamic adaptation, where content is treated as a variable output subject to continuous testing and refinement. This infrastructure supports a cycle of hypothesis, generation, measurement, and adjustment that reduces the latency between creative intent and audience reception.

This methodology operates within the tools-platforms-infrastructure domain, emphasizing the technical stack required to sustain automated content lifecycles. It prioritizes scalability and responsiveness, enabling creators to manage large volumes of content while maintaining alignment with audience preferences. The integration of autonomous agents allows for the rapid identification of high-performing formats and topics, facilitating a more efficient allocation of creative resources.
