# Meat Juiciness

Meat juiciness refers to the perceived moisture and tenderness of cooked meat, determined by how effectively the meat retains its natural liquids during the cooking process. This quality is a critical component of palatability, as juicy meat is generally perceived as more flavorful and tender than dry meat. The degree of juiciness depends on several factors, including the meat's initial moisture content, the specific cooking temperature and duration, and the methods used in preparation prior to cooking.

## Baking Soda and Texture Enhancement

Baking soda (sodium bicarbonate) is commonly used to enhance the texture and juiciness of ground meat. By raising the pH level of the meat's surface, baking soda prevents the proteins from bonding too tightly during cooking. This chemical alteration allows the meat to retain more water, resulting in a softer texture and improved browning. This technique is particularly effective in dishes where ground meat is cooked at high temperatures, helping to counteract the drying effects of heat.
