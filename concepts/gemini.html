Gemini

Google’s ecosystem of multimodal large language models and integrated AI productivity tools.

  • google-workspace

Recent Updates

  • Enhanced integration between gemini and notebooklm to increase tool power and utility.
  • Significant feature updates implemented across google-workspace and Google AI Studio.
  • Introduction of JSON prompting for Gemini, enhancing image control and metadata handling.
  • Integration of NotebookLM with Gemini to generate professional websites without code.
  • Introduction of Image to Video feature in Google Photos, allowing users to turn photos into short video clips.
  • New feature: Generate slide decks in Canvas (Google Workspace) by typing “Please create a slide deck about [topic]” and exporting.
