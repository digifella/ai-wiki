# Qwen 35

Qwen 3.5 is a local [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] model developed by [[entities/alibaba|Alibaba]] that has been evaluated for its performance on [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks. As a locally-deployable model, it represents an alternative approach to cloud-based or paid AI services, allowing users to run [[concepts/inference|inference]] on their own [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] without ongoing subscription costs.

## Coding Capabilities

The model has been specifically assessed for its suitability in software development workflows. Evaluations have compared its coding performance against established paid AI services, examining whether it can serve as a functional replacement for commercial solutions in this domain.

## Local Deployment

A key characteristic of Qwen 3.5 is its ability to run locally, which addresses concerns about data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], latency, and cost associated with [[concepts/cloud-ai|cloud-based AI]] services. This positions it within a broader ecosystem of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] and efficient models designed for [[concepts/edge-deployment|edge deployment]] and [[concepts/local-inference|local inference]].
