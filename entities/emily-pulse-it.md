# Emily Pulse It

Emily Pulse It is an initiative of the Walter and Eliza Hall Institute (WEHI) that explores the intersection of art and science. The project is designed to bridge disciplinary boundaries by fostering dialogue and engagement between artistic and scientific communities. It operates on the premise that collaborative conversation and shared inquiry can enhance understanding within both fields.

The initiative focuses on how artistic masterpieces can be engaged with within scientific contexts, while simultaneously examining how scientific practices inform artistic thinking. By bringing together practitioners from both disciplines, the program aims to create a space for mutual learning and interdisciplinary exchange.
