# Supplement Regimen

A systematic approach to the intake of nutritional supplements, grounded in individual biochemistry, deficiency data, and peer-reviewed efficacy studies. The primary [[concepts/purpose|objective]] is to fill specific nutrient gaps or achieve targeted physiological outcomes while avoiding unnecessary supplementation supplement-interactions.

## Core Principles

- **Individualization**: No universal protocol; regimen must be tailored to bloodwork, [[concepts/diet|diet]], lifestyle, and genetics.
- **Evidence-Based Selection**: Prioritize supplements with robust clinical data over marketing claims. See hierarchy-of-evidence for validation standards.
- **Baseline Assessment**: Always establish current status via testing before initiation to prevent toxicity or redundancy.

## Key Nutrients & Efficacy Reviews

### Omega-3 Fatty Acids
- **Role**: Anti-inflammatory, cardiovascular support, cognitive [[concepts/health|health]].
- **Efficacy Context**: General population benefits are debated; specific indications for high triglycerides are well-supported. Dose and purity (EPA/DHA ratio) matter.
- **Reference**: [[lab-notes/2026-06-10-SciShow-Evaluating-Supplement-Efficacy-for-Omega-3-Folic|SciShow: Evaluating Supplement Efficacy for Omega-3, Folic Acid, Vitamin D]] discusses recent viewer-driven inquiries on efficacy limits.

### Folic Acid / Methylfolate
- **Role**: DNA synthesis, red blood cell formation, neural tube defect [[concepts/preventive-care|prevention]] in pregnancy.
- **Considerations**: Individuals with MTHFR variants may require methylfolate over synthetic [[concepts/folic-acid|folic acid]]. Over-supplementation can mask B12 deficiency.

### Vitamin D
- **Role**: Calcium absorption, immune modulation, bone health.
- **Status**: High prevalence of insufficiency globally. Supplementation is often necessary in low-sunlight regions or for indoor lifestyles. Levels should be monitored via serum 25(OH)D tests.

## Implementation Protocol

1. **Test**: Identify deficiencies (e.g., Vitamin D, B12, Iron).
2. **Prioritize**: Address severe deficiencies first; add performance/cognitive aids only after baseline health is optimized.
3. **Cycle & Monitor**: Re-test every 6–12 months to adjust dosages and prevent accumulation toxicity.
