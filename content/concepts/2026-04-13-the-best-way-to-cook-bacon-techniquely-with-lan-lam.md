# 2026 04 13 The Best Way To Cook Bacon Techniquely With Lan Lam

This concept page documents cooking techniques for bacon, with particular focus on the water method approach. The water method represents an alternative to conventional dry-heat cooking techniques, offering distinct advantages in heat distribution and fat rendering.

## The Water Method

The water method involves starting bacon in a cold skillet with a small amount of water before applying heat. As the water heats, it gradually raises the temperature of the bacon and renders the fat slowly and evenly. Once the water evaporates, the bacon continues cooking in its own rendered fat, resulting in more uniform browning and reduced splattering compared to traditional methods that apply immediate high heat to cold bacon.

## Comparison to Conventional Methods

Traditional bacon cooking typically begins with a hot skillet, which can cause uneven cooking and rapid fat splashing. The water method's staged heating approach produces more consistent results across the entire strip and allows greater control over the final texture, whether one prefers softer or crispier bacon. This technique is particularly useful when cooking larger batches where uniform results are desirable.
