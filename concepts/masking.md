[[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] is a [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] technique that allows photographers and editors to apply [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] selectively to specific areas of an image rather than to the entire photo. By isolating regions through masks, editors can enhance certain elements—such as brightening a subject's face, deepening a sky, or sharpening specific details—without affecting the rest of the [[concepts/writing|composition]]. This selective approach provides greater control and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] than global adjustments.

## Implementation in Software

Modern [[concepts/photo-enhancement|photo editing]] applications like [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] and [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] offer [[concepts/masking-tools|masking tools]] that have evolved significantly with advances in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]. Traditional masks were created manually using brushes, but contemporary software increasingly features automated AI-powered mask presets that can intelligently identify and isolate common subjects like skies, people, or landscapes with minimal user effort.

Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] to [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] further expand these capabilities:

*   **Local [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]]:** [[concepts/new-features|New features]] allowing [[concepts/targeted-color-adjustments|targeted color adjustments]] within masked areas.
*   **Extended White Balance:** Improved white balance controls integrated with masking workflows for more nuanced color correction.
