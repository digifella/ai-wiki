# Extraterrestrial Life

Extraterrestrial life refers to hypothetical life forms that may exist beyond Earth, including organisms ranging from simple microbes to intelligent civilizations. While no confirmed evidence of extraterrestrial life has been documented, the scientific study of this possibility—known as astrobiology or exobiology—has become an established discipline within modern [[concepts/science|science]]. The concept draws on fields including biology, chemistry, [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]], and [[concepts/planetary-science|planetary science]] to assess the conditions under which life might emerge and persist on other worlds.

## Scientific Framework

The search for extraterrestrial life is grounded in the principle that the physical and chemical laws governing life on Earth apply universally. Scientists have identified potentially habitable environments beyond Earth, including subsurface oceans on Jupiter's moon Europa and Saturn's moon Enceladus, as well as exoplanets in their star's habitable zone. Research efforts include telescopic observation of distant planets, analysis of [[concepts/meteorites|meteorites]] for organic compounds, and the study of [[concepts/extremophiles|extremophiles]]—organisms on Earth that survive in harsh conditions that may resemble environments on other worlds.

## Historical and Contemporary Perspectives

Prominent figures like astronomer [[entities/carl-sagan|Carl Sagan]] brought scientific rigor to discussions of extraterrestrial life, emphasizing the statistical likelihood of life elsewhere given the scale of the universe while maintaining skepticism toward unsubstantiated claims. Modern research includes systematic [[concepts/software|programs]] such as the [[concepts/seti|Search for Extraterrestrial Intelligence]] (SETI), which listens for radio signals from space, and robotic missions to Mars and beyond that seek biosignatures—chemical or physical evidence of past or present life. The [[concepts/theoretical-frameworks|theoretical frameworks]] developed to guide this research have also influenced discussions about humanity's place in the cosmos and the [[entities/nature|nature]] of life itself.
