# Blacks Sliders

The [[concepts/blacks|Blacks]] slider is a fundamental adjustment tool in Adobe Lightroom and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] that targets the darkest tones in an image. Unlike the broader [[concepts/exposure|Exposure]] slider, which affects the entire tonal range, the Blacks slider specifically adjusts shadow regions while leaving midtones and highlights relatively unchanged. This selective control allows photographers to deepen shadow areas, increase [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], or recover detail in the darkest parts of an image without overexposing brighter areas.

## Use in Landscape Photography

Landscape photographers frequently employ the Blacks slider as part of their standard processing workflow. The tool proves particularly valuable when working with [[concepts/images|images]] containing deep [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] cast by mountains, forests, or architectural elements. By adjusting blacks, photographers can enhance visual separation between tonal zones, add punch to an image, or preserve shadow detail that might otherwise be lost to pure black areas. The slider's precision makes it possible to maintain the natural appearance of an image while controlling how dark the shadows become.

## Technical Considerations

When using the Blacks slider, photographers typically adjust it in conjunction with other shadow and highlight controls to achieve balanced results. Moving the slider to the right deepens blacks and increases overall contrast, while moving it left lightens shadows. The effect becomes more pronounced in already dark areas of the image, making it predictable for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. Most digital photographers use the Blacks slider after setting exposure, white balance, and highlight recovery, as it functions as a [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] tool rather than a primary correction method.
