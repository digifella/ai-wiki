# AI Selection

AI Selection refers to [[concepts/automated-masking|automated masking]] and selection techniques in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] that use [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to isolate subjects with precision. These tools analyze pixel data and edge detection to create selections that would otherwise require extensive manual refinement. The technology addresses a long-standing challenge in digital image editing: isolating fine details like hair, fur, or translucent materials where traditional selection tools struggle to detect subtle edges and semi-transparent areas.

## AI-Powered Methods

Modern versions of Photoshop include machine [[concepts/learning|learning]]-based selection tools, most notably the "[[concepts/select-subject|Select Subject]]" and "Select and Mask" features with AI refinement. These tools work by analyzing the [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] and characteristics of pixels to identify boundaries between a subject and its background. The AI component learns from [[concepts/training-data|training data]] to recognize common patterns in hair and clothing edges, allowing it to make more intelligent decisions about which pixels belong to a selection than purely algorithmic approaches could achieve. The process is typically non-destructive, enabling users to refine selections iteratively.

## Combining AI with Manual Techniques

While AI selection tools provide a strong foundation, most professional workflows combine automated selection with manual refinement. After [[concepts/running|running]] an AI selection on hair or complex edges, editors typically use the brush-based refinement tools to add or subtract from the selection where the AI has missed details or included unwanted background areas. This hybrid approach leverages the speed of [[concepts/automation|automation]] while maintaining the precision control that detailed work demands. The effectiveness of AI selection depends partly on image quality, contrast, and the complexity of the subject matter.
