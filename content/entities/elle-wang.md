# Elle Wang

Elle Wang's note-taking methodology focuses on structuring AI-generated information using critical frameworks. Core principles:

- Applies [[concepts/qec-framework]] (Question, Evidence, Conclusion) to transform raw information into actionable insights by explicitly framing questions, sourcing evidence, and deriving conclusions.
- Integrates [[concepts/la-approach]] ([[concepts/limitations-and-assumptions|Limitations and Assumptions]]) to critically evaluate AI outputs, identifying hidden [[concepts/biases|biases]] and contextual constraints.
- Optimizes [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] by requiring structured inputs through QEC/LA, reducing hallucinations and increasing note utility.
- Implements [[concepts/whisper-transcription]] for [[concepts/audio-transcription|audio-to-text]] transcription using [[entities/google-colab|Google Colab]] and [[entities/openai|OpenAI]]'s [[concepts/whisper-ai|Whisper AI]], achieving high [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] without downloads (<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ktNeWrkPwmg>).
