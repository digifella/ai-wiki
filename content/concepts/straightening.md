# Straightening

Straightening is a post-processing technique used in Lightroom Classic to correct the alignment of landscape photographs. The method leverages Lightroom's [[concepts/landscape-masking|landscape masking]] feature, which automatically identifies and isolates the horizon and landscape elements within an image. This allows photographers to adjust the rotation and perspective of their [[concepts/images|images]] with precision.

## Application in Lightroom Classic

The landscape mask in Lightroom Classic detects the horizon line and surrounding terrain automatically. Once the mask is applied, users can make rotational [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to correct tilted horizons or misaligned compositions. This approach is more efficient than manual straightening tools, as the [[concepts/software|software]] does the initial detection work rather than requiring the photographer to manually define the area to be adjusted.

The technique is particularly useful for photographs where the horizon line is a key compositional element, as it ensures that adjustments are applied consistently to the landscape portion of the image without affecting other elements like sky areas in unintended ways.
