# Little Red Dots

## Overview
* Compact, extremely luminous infrared sources detected in the high-redshift [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]] ($z > 6$).
* Initial identifications emerged from [[entities/jwst]] observations beginning in 2022.
* Exhibit spectral energy distributions peaking in the red/infrared with flux densities inconsistent with standard [[concepts/galaxy-formation-and-evolution|galaxy evolution]] models.

## Properties & Characteristics
* **Morphology:** Highly compact structures; angular sizes comparable to distant quasars but lacking clear active galactic nucleus signatures in initial optical bands.
* **Luminosity:** Intense emission suggesting high mass-accretion rates or exotic energy sources.
* **Spectral Features:** Red spectral slope indicative of significant dust attenuation or specific emission [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] distinct from [[concepts/first-generation-of-stars|Population III stars]].

## Formation Models & Recent Evidence
* **Pre-Stellar Black Hole Hypothesis:** [[lab-notes/2026-05-15-JWST-X-Ray-Discovery-LRD-Links-to-Pre-Stellar-Black-Hole|JWST X-Ray Discovery: LRD Links to Pre-Stellar Black Hole Formation]] reports X-ray signatures confirming LRD [[concepts/energy-source|power sources]] are linked to black hole accretion preceding stellar formation.
* **Chronology Implications:** Evidence suggests [[concepts/primordial-black-hole]] collapse or direct black hole formation mechanisms occurred before or concurrent with the [[concepts/primordial-stars|first stars]], challenging hierarchical structure formation.
* **Accretion [[concepts/physics|Physics]]:** X-ray data points to efficient accretion flows onto sub-[[concepts/stellar-mass|stellar mass]] objects or rapidly growing seeds, resolving luminosity anomalies without invoking super-Eddington Star Formation [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
