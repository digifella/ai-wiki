# AI Interpretability

**[[concepts/explainable-ai|AI Interpretability]]** refers to the methods and techniques used to understand and explain the internal [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] models, particularly those with complex, opaque architectures often termed "black boxes." It is a foundational component of [[concepts/model-safety|AI Safety]] and Trustworthy AI, ensuring that model behaviors are transparent, auditable, and aligned with human values.

## Key Concepts

*   **[[concepts/mechanistic-interpretability|Mechanistic Interpretability]]**: The study of how [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] implement [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] and representations internally. It involves [[concepts/reverse-engineering|reverse-engineering]] the circuitry of the model to understand specific features and computations.
*   **[[concepts/black-box-models|Black Box]] Problem**: The inability to directly observe or understand the [[concepts/hidden-state|internal state]] of complex models, necessitating interpretability techniques to bridge the gap between input/output and internal [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]].
*   **[[concepts/mathematical-problem-solving|AI Neuralese]]**: A term describing the high-dimensional, non-human-readable internal representations and "language" used by [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] during processing. Understanding this "neuralese" is critical for decoding how models arrive at conclusions.
*   **Chain of Thought (CoT) Monitoring**: The practice of observing and analyzing the intermediate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] steps generated by [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]. This allows for the detection of logical fallacies, bias, or unsafe reasoning paths before a final output is produced.
*   **Safety Implications**: Recent developments, such as those discussed in [[lab-notes/2026-09-11-AI-Neuralese-and-Chain-of-Thought-Monitoring-Concepts-an|AI Neuralese and Chain of Thought Monitoring: Concepts and Safety Implications]], highlight the urgency of monitoring CoT to prevent opaque decision-making in high-stakes environments.
