# Perplexity Ai

[[entities/perplexityai|Perplexity Ai]] is an [[concepts/ai-powered-search|AI-powered search]] engine designed for [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]]. It functions as an alternative to traditional search engines by leveraging [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] to process queries and synthesize information from across the web in a conversational format. Users can ask questions in natural language and receive comprehensive answers with cited sources, making it useful for research, [[concepts/learning|learning]], and fact-checking.

## Usage and Adoption

The platform has gained traction among users seeking a more interactive approach to search. Rather than returning a list of links, Perplexity generates direct answers while maintaining transparency about source material. This approach appeals to those who value efficiency and context in information discovery.

## Integration with Web Content

As [[concepts/ai-search|AI search]] engines have become more prevalent, the practice of [[concepts/ai-overviews|Generative Engine Optimization]] ([[concepts/generative-engine-optimization-geo|GEO]]) has emerged, requiring website creators to adapt their content strategies to be discoverable and accurately represented by AI search tools. This reflects the growing importance of these systems in how information is accessed online.
