# Person-Led Flourishing

**Definition:** A holistic framework for [[concepts/well-being|well-being]] that prioritizes individual agency, dignity, and [[concepts/self-determination-theory|self-determination]] over institutional efficiency or technological optimization. It posits that true flourishing arises when care systems and technologies serve the person's intrinsic goals rather than imposing external structures.

## Core Principles

- **Agency First:** Decisions must originate from the individual’s values, not default protocols.
- **Human-Centricity:** Technology acts as a scaffold for [[concepts/human-connection|human connection]], not a replacement for it.
- **Contextual [[concepts/resilience|Adaptability]]:** Solutions must adapt to the unique [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] of the person, avoiding one-size-fits-all metrics.

## Applications in Aged Care & AI

Recent discourse highlights the tension between technological integration and ethical [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] in elderly care:

- [[lab-notes/2026-05-24-Ethical-AI-in-Aged-Care-Prioritizing-Humanity-and-Person|Ethical AI in Aged Care: Prioritizing Humanity and Person-Led Flourishing]] outlines the necessity of aligning AI implementation with humanistic values.
- **Dr. [[entities/donald-macaskill|Donald Macaskill]]’s Framework:** As Chief Executive of [[entities/scottish-care|Scottish Care]], Macaskill argues that AI must be subordinated to human judgment to prevent dehumanization.
- **Ethical Prioritization:** [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] in [[concepts/aged-care|aged care]] should enhance, not erode, the relational aspects of care. Efficiency gains are secondary to preserving the dignity and autonomy of the care recipient.
- **Risk of [[concepts/algorithmic-decision-making|Automation Bias]]:** Over-reliance on data-driven insights can obscure the nuanced, emotional needs of individuals, contradicting the goals of Person-Led Flourishing.
