# Computational Reasoning

Computational reasoning refers to the ability of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems, particularly [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs), to perform logical [[concepts/inference|inference]] and [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] through [[concepts/internal-working-mechanisms|computational processes]]. This capability is fundamental to how [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] process information, make decisions, and generate responses based on learned patterns and knowledge.

## Efficiency Challenges in LLMs

Traditional LLM approaches face significant inefficiency challenges when [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] over large bodies of knowledge or complex contexts. These systems often require excessive [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] to retrieve and process relevant information, leading to increased latency and higher [[concepts/operational-costs|operational costs]]. The challenge lies in balancing the breadth of knowledge these models can access with the [[concepts/speed|speed]] and efficiency needed for practical applications.

## Context-Aware Knowledge Retrieval

Recent approaches, such as [[concepts/engram|DeepSeek Engram]], address LLM inefficiency through [[concepts/context-aware-knowledge-retrieval|context-aware knowledge retrieval]] [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]]. Rather than processing all available information indiscriminately, these systems intelligently identify and retrieve only the most [[concepts/relevant-knowledge|relevant knowledge]] based on the specific context of a query or task. This targeted approach reduces computational overhead while maintaining reasoning quality.

By improving how LLMs access and utilize stored knowledge, [[concepts/context-aware-retrieval|context-aware retrieval]] systems enhance both the speed and accuracy of computational reasoning tasks, making [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] more practical for real-[[entities/earth|world]] applications where [[concepts/computational-efficiency|computational efficiency]] is a critical constraint.
