# Artificial Intelligence Models

[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial intelligence]] models are computational systems designed to learn patterns from data and perform specific tasks with minimal explicit programming. These models form the foundational intelligence layer of [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]], enabling them to perceive environments, process information, and generate appropriate [[concepts/responses|responses]]. The [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] and capability of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] vary significantly, ranging from classical [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] algorithms to modern deep learning [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]].

## Types and Applications

Modern AI models encompass diverse approaches suited to different tasks. Traditional [[concepts/machine-learning-models|machine learning models]] like decision trees and support vector machines excel at [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]] analysis. [[concepts/deep-learning-models|Deep learning models]], particularly transformer-based architectures, have become dominant for [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]] and complex pattern recognition. [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large language models]] (LLMs) power [[concepts/conversational-ai|conversational agents]] and [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], while [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]] models enable [[concepts/visual-understanding|visual understanding]]. These varied model types are often combined within agent systems to create more capable autonomous systems.

## Integration with Agent Systems

Within autonomous agents, AI models serve multiple critical functions. They process sensory inputs from environments, make decisions about appropriate actions, and generate outputs for interaction with users or systems. [[concepts/custom-models|Specialized models]] are often integrated for specific [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]—for instance, [[concepts/web-crawling|web data extraction]] tools like [[concepts/firecrawl-ai|Firecrawl AI]] combine models for understanding HTML [[concepts/structure|structure]] and content extraction. The quality and appropriateness of an agent's responses depend directly on the underlying model's training, architecture, and capability level.
