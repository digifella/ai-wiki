# Risk Assessment

## Definition
A systematic process of evaluating potential risks and their impacts.

## Key Components
- **Hazard Identification**: Recognizing potential sources of harm.
- **Risk Analysis**: Assessing the likelihood and severity of each hazard.
- **Risk Evaluation**: Comparing estimated risks against benchmark levels.

## Applications
- **[[concepts/health|Healthcare]]**: Patient safety, medical device approvals.
- **Technology**: AI [[concepts/ai-oversight|oversight]], [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]].
- **Business**: Operational [[concepts/continuity|continuity]], [[concepts/compliance|compliance]].
