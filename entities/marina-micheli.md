# Marina Micheli

**Marina Micheli** is a [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] and scholar focusing on the political [[concepts/economic-system|economy]] of data, specifically within the context of platform-society and [[concepts/data-management]]. Her work critiques the dominant corporate models of data infrastructure and explores emerging alternatives.

- **[[concepts/governance|Governance]] Models**: Investigates the shift from corporate-dominated [[concepts/data-sovereignty|data control]] to alternative governance structures in the era of datafication.
