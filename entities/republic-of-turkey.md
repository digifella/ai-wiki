# Republic Of Turkey

The Republic of Turkey is a transcontinental country situated at the crossroads of Europe and Asia. Its capital is Ankara, and the nation covers approximately 783,562 square kilometers. Geographically, Turkey straddles two continents, with roughly 3% of its territory located in Europe and the remaining 97% in Asia Minor (Anatolia). The country shares borders with eight nations: Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq, and Syria. This strategic location has historically positioned Turkey as a significant cultural and political bridge between Eastern and Western civilizations.

## Administrative and Geographic Structure

Turkey is divided into 81 provinces, each governed by a governor appointed by the central government. The country's terrain is diverse, featuring extensive mountain ranges, plateaus, and coastal plains along the Black Sea, Mediterranean, Aegean, and Marmara Seas. The Bosporus and Dardanelles straits are critical maritime chokepoints that connect the Black Sea to the Mediterranean, playing a vital role in global trade and naval strategy.
