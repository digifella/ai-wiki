# Meta FAIR Lab

Fundamental [[concepts/ai-research|AI Research]] laboratory [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] [[entities/meta]], led by [[entities/yann-lecun]]. The lab focuses on developing [[concepts/artificial-general-intelligence]] ([[concepts/agi|AGI]]) through [[concepts/world-models]] and Self-Supervised [[concepts/learning|Learning]].

## Core Research Paradigms
- [[concepts/jepa|JEPA]] (Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]): Moving away from generative pixel/token prediction toward predicting in latent space.
- Self-Supervised Learning: [[concepts/training|Training]] [[concepts/models|models]] on raw data without manual labeling.
- Development of non-generative architectures for [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]].

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/vl-jepa]] (2026-04-14)**: A new [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]]-based approach to AGI that departs from the current [[concepts/llm]]-centric paradigm.
    - **Core Thesis**: "Language is not Intelligence."
    - Represents a [[concepts/strategic-shift|strategic shift]] away from [[concepts/generative-ai]]-driven approaches.
    - Emphasizes vision-based predictive modeling as a foundation for true intelligence, rather than relying on text-only generative processes.
