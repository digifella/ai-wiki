# Company Nordvpn

NordVPN is a virtual private network (VPN) service provider founded in 2012 and headquartered in Panama. The company operates a global network of VPN servers distributed across numerous countries, enabling users to encrypt their internet traffic and obscure their IP addresses. NordVPN provides services to both individual consumers and organizations seeking privacy and security for their online activities.

## Core Operations

The company's primary function is routing user internet connections through encrypted VPN tunnels, allowing customers to browse the web with their actual location and identity concealed. NordVPN maintains server infrastructure across multiple jurisdictions and offers various subscription plans for different user needs. The service operates on a subscription-based business model, with revenue generated through monthly and annual user subscriptions.

## Market Position

NordVPN is among the larger commercial VPN providers globally, competing with other established services in the privacy and security software market. The company markets its services to users concerned about internet privacy, cybersecurity threats, and regional content restrictions. As a Panama-based entity, NordVPN's jurisdiction has implications for its data retention policies and legal obligations.
