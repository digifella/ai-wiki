# Potato Preparation Techniques

This wiki page documents various methods and techniques for preparing potatoes, focusing on maximizing [[concepts/texture|texture]] and flavor.

## Featured Technique: Duck Fat Roast Potatoes

This section details a specific high-impact method for achieving exceptional crispiness and flavor in roast potatoes using rendered fat.

*   **Method Overview:** Preparing roast potatoes utilizing [[concepts/duck-fat|duck fat]] to achieve a superior crispy exterior and fluffy interior.
*   **Key Principle:** Maximizing crispiness and flavor by using duck fat rendered from a previous roast.
*   **Reference Guide:** For a detailed step-by-step guide on this method, consult the resource: [[lab-notes/2026-05-04-Duck-Fat-Roast-Potatoes-Crispy-Preparation-Guide|Duck Fat Roast Potatoes: Crispy Preparation Guide]].
*   **Application [[concepts/notes|Notes]]:**
    *   Focus on achieving high heat for proper crisping.
    *   Rendered duck fat imparts a distinct flavor profile to the potatoes.
    *   This technique is crucial when exploring variations in cooking fats, such as lard or beef fat.
