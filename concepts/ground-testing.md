# Ground Testing

Pre-flight validation procedures performed on aerospace vehicles to verify system [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], propulsion performance, avionics functionality, and structural safety. Includes static fire tests, tank proofs, checkout flows, and integration checks. Critical for [[concepts/risk-mitigation|risk mitigation]] and certification before launch commit.

## Recent Operations & Updates

- **[[entities/spacex]] [[concepts/starship]] Program**:
    - Preparations for Starship Flight 12 are active, involving ground testing and checkout of [[concepts/starship]] V3 and Super Heavy 19.
    - Ground activities encompass stack integration, vehicle readiness reviews, and hardware [[concepts/verification|verification]] sequences leading up to the twelfth flight test.
    - Comprehensive details available in [[lab-notes/2026-05-17-SpaceX-Starship-Flight-12-Preparations-V3-Super-Heavy-19|SpaceX Starship Flight 12 Preparations: V3, Super Heavy 19, and Space Industry Updates]].

## Key Activities
- **Static Fire Tests**: [[concepts/engine|Engine]] ignition on ground to verify [[concepts/engine-thrust|thrust]], telemetry, and shutdown sequences.
- **Tank Proofs**: Pressurization tests to validate structural limits and leak detection.
- **Checkout**: Sequential verification of subsystems via automated flows and manual inspections.
