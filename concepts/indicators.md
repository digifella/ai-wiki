# Indicators

Indicators are measurable variables or observable phenomena utilized in scientific research to quantify, assess, or track conditions, changes, or outcomes within a system. In the context of [[concepts/physics|physics]] and experimental research, they function as proxies for phenomena that are difficult or impossible to measure directly. By translating abstract theoretical quantities into concrete, quantifiable observations, indicators form a critical bridge between conceptual models and empirical measurement.

The primary function of indicators in research is to enable scientists to infer underlying processes or states from observable data. This capability allows for the rigorous assessment of complex systems where direct observation is limited by technological constraints or fundamental physical principles. Consequently, indicators serve as essential tools for validating hypotheses and monitoring dynamic changes in experimental setups.
