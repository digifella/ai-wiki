# Tint Slider

The Tint Slider is a color adjustment control introduced in Photoshop 2025's Color and Vibrance tool. It enables users to shift the overall color temperature of an image along a warm-to-cool spectrum, making it a straightforward method for addressing color casts that result from various lighting conditions. Common applications include correcting the blue tint cast by overcast daylight or the orange tint produced by tungsten bulbs.

## How It Works

The slider operates on a linear axis, with negative values pushing colors toward cooler (blue) tones and positive values shifting toward warmer (yellow/red) tones. This mechanism allows for precise calibration of white balance without the need for complex manual curve adjustments. By targeting the global color temperature, the tool helps neutralize unwanted hues caused by mixed lighting environments or incorrect camera settings.

## Practical Applications

Users typically employ this feature during the initial stages of post-processing to establish a neutral baseline before applying more specific local adjustments. It is particularly effective for correcting white balance errors in RAW files where the original lighting conditions were ambiguous. The tool provides a non-destructive way to restore natural color fidelity, ensuring that skin tones and neutral grays appear accurate within the final composition.
