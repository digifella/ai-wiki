# Reasoning

The process of applying logic to derive conclusions, identify patterns, or perform [[concepts/inference|inference]] based on available premises or data.

## Computational Reasoning
In [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]], reasoning refers to the capacity of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] to execute [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] through multi-step-inference, [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|mathematical-reasoning]], and symbolic-logic.

### Recent Developments
- Advancements in [[concepts/open-weight-models]] are driving the [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of enhanced [[concepts/ai]] capabilities.
    - The launch of Google [[entities/gemma-4|Gemma 4]] introduces a suite of models featuring enhanced [[concepts/ai]] capabilities and a developer-friendly [[concepts/open-source|Apache-2.0 license]], allowing for unrestricted modification, [[concepts/distribution|distribution]], and commercial deployment.
- [[entities/hugging-face|Hugging Face]]'s [[concepts/3-billion-parameter-model|SmolLM3-3B]] (3B [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]) introduces an advanced "[[concepts/thinking-with-3-pro|thinking mode]]" that displays its [[concepts/reasoning-steps|reasoning process]] before generating final output.
