# Concert Lighting

Concert lighting presents unique challenges for photographers due to intense [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]], dramatic contrasts, and rapidly changing [[concepts/light|light]] conditions. These environmental factors often result in [[concepts/images|images]] with extreme color tints—such as dominant blues, magentas, or yellows—that don't accurately represent the scene or subject. Correcting these issues requires specialized post-processing techniques to recover detail and achieve natural-looking colors.

## Color Correction in Adobe Lightroom

[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] provides several tools for addressing color tints in concert photography. The White Balance slider, Color [[concepts/range|Range]] [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], and Luminance controls allow photographers to selectively target and neutralize problematic color casts. By adjusting the temperature and tint values, photographers can compensate for strong single-color lighting while preserving the mood and detail of the original image. The [[concepts/lightroom-calibration-panel|Calibration panel]] offers additional [[concepts/power|control]] by allowing adjustments to individual color channels.

## Practical Approach

Effective color correction in concert lighting typically involves identifying the dominant color cast, applying broad white balance corrections, and then using targeted adjustments for specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]]. This might include desaturating unwanted color channels or using the HSL ([[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, Luminance) panel to refine specific colors. The goal is balancing technical correction with artistic intent, as some concert imagery benefits from retaining stylized lighting while removing distracting or unflattering color tints from subjects.
