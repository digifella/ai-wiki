# User Query

A user query is the input or request submitted by a person to an AI system, agent, or application. It represents the starting point of any interaction, where the user articulates what information they seek, what task they want completed, or what question they need answered. User queries can range from simple factual questions to complex multi-step requests, and they serve as the foundation upon which AI models generate responses.

## Characteristics and Scope

User queries vary significantly in complexity and structure. Simple queries may consist of a single keyword or a direct question, while more advanced interactions involve natural language processing of nuanced instructions. In the context of AI agents, the query often triggers a sequence of internal processes, such as intent recognition and context retrieval, to formulate an appropriate output.

## Role in Retrieval Augmented Generation

In systems utilizing Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), the user query plays a critical role in determining the relevance of retrieved data. The query is used to search external knowledge bases, ensuring that the generated response is grounded in specific, up-to-date information rather than relying solely on the model's pre-trained weights. This mechanism helps reduce hallucinations and improves the accuracy of the final response provided to the user.
