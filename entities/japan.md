# Japan

**Japan** (日本, *Nihon*), officially the **State of Japan**, is a sovereign [[concepts/island-nation|island nation]] in East Asia. It is an archipelago comprising 6,800 islands in the northwestern Pacific Ocean.

## Overview
- **Capital:** Tokyo
- **Government:** Unitary parliamentary constitutional [[concepts/monarchy|monarchy]]
- **Monarch:** Emperor Naruhito
- **[[concepts/economic-system|Economy]]:** [[entities/earth|World]]'s third-largest nominal GDP; heavily export-oriented.
- **Key Industries:** Automotive, electronics, [[concepts/robotics|robotics]], machinery, steel, chemicals.

## Science & Technology Policy
Japan maintains significant investment in nuclear energy and fusion research, though public sentiment and policy shift following historical incidents (e.g., Fukushima Daiichi nuclear disaster).

- **Nuclear Research:** Home to major facilities like the National Institute for Fusion [[concepts/science|Science]] (NIFS) in Toki, Aichi, contributing to international projects like ITER.
- **Recent Assessments (2026):** Critical scrutiny of emerging low-energy nuclear reaction ([[concepts/lenr|LENR]]) claims has gained traction in scientific review cycles.
