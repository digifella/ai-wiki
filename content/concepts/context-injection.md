# Context Injection

Context injection is a technique for optimizing [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) by providing [[concepts/custom-instructions|custom instructions]] that guide their outputs toward specific professional requirements. In the context of [[concepts/legal-work|legal work]], this involves configuring AI assistants like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], and [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] with tailored prompts and [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] that align their [[concepts/responses|responses]] with legal [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], [[concepts/terminology|terminology]], and professional expectations. Rather than using these tools with their default settings, legal professionals can implement context injection to ensure consistent, appropriate, and professionally suitable [[concepts/output|output]].

## Application to Legal Work

The effectiveness of context injection for legal [[concepts/software|applications]] stems from the ability to embed domain-specific constraints and conventions directly into the AI's operating [[concepts/instructions|instructions]]. By establishing custom guidelines before engaging with the tool, legal professionals can reduce the need for extensive post-processing or rewrites of AI-generated content. This approach acknowledges that different professional domains have distinct communication norms, [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] requirements, and formatting conventions that generic AI systems are not optimized for by default.

Context injection represents a practical methodology for bridging the gap between general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] and specialized professional needs, allowing practitioners to adapt these tools more effectively within their existing workflows.
