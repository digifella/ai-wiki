# Muscle Atrophy

**Muscle atrophy** refers to the partial or complete wasting away of a part of the body, often resulting from disuse, disease, or aging. It is closely linked to **[[concepts/bone-density|bone density]] loss**, particularly in contexts of microgravity or prolonged immobilization.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Disuse Atrophy:** Lack of mechanical loading leads to reduced protein synthesis and increased protein degradation in muscle fibers.
- **Bone-Muscle Crosstalk:** Muscle loss often accelerates **[[concepts/osteoporosis|osteoporosis]]** due to reduced mechanical stress on the skeletal system.
- **Systemic Inflammation:** Chronic inflammatory markers can exacerbate both muscle wasting and bone resorption.

## Prevention & Intervention
- **Resistance Training:** Essential for maintaining muscle mass and stimulating bone formation.
- **Nutritional Support:** Adequate protein intake and micronutrients (e.g., Vitamin D, Calcium) are critical.
- **Space Medicine Insights:** Research into [[concepts/astronaut-health|astronaut health]] has yielded significant breakthroughs in preventing bone and muscle loss.
