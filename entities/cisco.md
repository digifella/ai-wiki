# Cisco

Cisco Systems is a multinational technology company headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984, the organization designs and manufactures networking hardware that serves as core infrastructure for enterprise and service provider networks globally. Its primary product lines include routers, switches, and related equipment essential for data transmission and network connectivity across organizations of varying sizes.

## Products and Services

Beyond physical networking hardware, Cisco develops comprehensive software solutions focused on security, collaboration, and network management. These offerings support the operational needs of large-scale enterprises and service providers, enabling secure and efficient data flow. The company’s ecosystem integrates hardware and software to facilitate robust communication and infrastructure management for its global customer base.
