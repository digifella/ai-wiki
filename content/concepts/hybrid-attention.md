# Hybrid Attention

Hybrid [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|Attention]] refers to architectural strategies in [[concepts/transformer-models|Transformer models]] that integrate multiple [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]] (such as Full Attention and [[concepts/sparse-attention-architecture|Sparse Attention]] or Linear Attention) to optimize the trade-off between [[concepts/computational-efficiency]] and long-context modeling performance.
