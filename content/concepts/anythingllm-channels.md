# AnythingLLM Channels

A feature introduced in [[entities/anythingllm]] v1.12 designed to facilitate mobile interaction with Private [[concepts/self-hosted-llms|Self-Hosted LLMs]].

### Key Capabilities
- **Mobile [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Enables users to take [[concepts/local-ai-assistants|local AI assistants]] "on the go" without complex configuration.
- **Seamless [[concepts/integration|Integration]]**: Part of the new "Channels" feature set designed to increase the utility and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of [[concepts/self-hosted-llms|Self-Hosted LLMs]].
- **Simplified [[concepts/setup|Setup]]**: Focuses on providing mobile access to local models without requiring heavy infrastructure management on the mobile side.
