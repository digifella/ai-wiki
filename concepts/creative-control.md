# Creative Control

**Creative control** refers to the degree of authority and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] power a [[concepts/creator|creator]] retains over the artistic, technical, and [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] elements of a media project. In digital production, this concept is increasingly mediated by the capabilities and constraints of software tools, particularly AI-assisted workflows.

## Key Dimensions

- **Authorial Intent**: The alignment between the final output and the [[entities/creator|creator]]’s original [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]].
- **Technical Autonomy**: The ability to manipulate assets without reliance on automated, opaque processes.
- **[[concepts/iterative-refinement|Iterative Refinement]]**: The capacity to make granular [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to [[concepts/writing|composition]], timing, and effects.

## Challenges

- **Black Box [[concepts/algorithms|Algorithms]]**: Difficulty in predicting or modifying AI-generated results.
- **[[concepts/learning|Learning]] Curve**: Maintaining control requires deep knowledge of new feature sets.
- **Standardization**: Industry shifts toward standardized [[concepts/templates|templates]] may limit unique stylistic expression.
