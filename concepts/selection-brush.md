# Selection Brush

The Selection Brush is a tool introduced in Adobe Photoshop's July 2025 update, available in both the standard release and Photoshop Beta versions. It enables users to create selections by painting directly onto images, offering an alternative to traditional selection methods such as marquee and lasso tools. This painting-based approach accommodates different user preferences and working styles within the image editing workflow.

## Functionality

The tool allows users to manually paint selection areas onto their canvas rather than defining boundaries through geometric shapes or traced paths. By using brush strokes to indicate selected regions, the Selection Brush provides a more intuitive method for users who prefer freehand interaction with their images. The painted selections can then be refined and used with standard Photoshop operations like copying, transforming, or applying filters to isolated image areas.

## Workflow Integration

As part of Photoshop's ongoing development focused on productivity enhancements, the Selection Brush complements existing selection tools rather than replacing them. Users can choose between the Selection Brush and conventional selection methods depending on their specific task requirements and personal working preferences. The tool's inclusion in both the main release and beta versions indicates Adobe's commitment to making it a stable feature of the application's core functionality.
