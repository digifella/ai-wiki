# LlamaIndex

## Overview
LlamaIndex is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] framework designed to facilitate the creation and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/agentic-ai|agents]] that leverage [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models (LLMs)]] for a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of tasks. The project aims to bridge the gap between existing [[concepts/document-parsing|document parsing]] solutions and the [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] needed by modern AI-driven [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[entities/agent|Agent]] Framework**: A modular system allowing developers to easily integrate LLMs into their projects, providing tools and utilities to manage [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]].
- **[[concepts/document-parsing|Document Parsing]]**: The process of converting [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] from documents into a structured format that can be processed efficiently by AI systems. This is crucial for tasks such as [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] and [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]].
