# Pandemic supply chain disruption

Disruptions in global [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]] during the pandemic (2020-2022) exposed systemic vulnerabilities in just-in-time manufacturing, causing shortages, cost surges, and shifts toward resilient supply chains. Key [[concepts/causes|drivers]] included:

- Geopolitical tensions and over-reliance on single-source suppliers
- Logistical bottlenecks (port congestion, labor shortages)
- Surge in demand for essential goods

## Case study: Domestic manufacturing viability
- [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] (host of [[entities/smarter-every-day|Smarter Every Day]]) conducted a [[concepts/four-year-experiment|four-year experiment]] to manufacture the [[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]] (a [[concepts/space-jetpacks|safer]] grill brush alternative) entirely within the U.S.
- Motivated by personal ties to union manufacturing (parents were [[entities/union-autoworkers|union autoworkers]]), tested viability amid supply chain challenges
- Documented challenges of [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic manufacturing]] including sourcing, labor costs, and global price [[concepts/market-competitiveness|competitiveness]]
- Demonstrated feasibility of U.S. production but highlighted cost complexity in competing globally
