# Protected Corporate Data

Protected Corporate Data refers to sensitive information within organizations that requires specialized handling and [[concepts/security|security]] measures when integrated with AI systems. In the context of [[concepts/answer-generation|retrieval-augmented generation]] (RAG) and [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]], protecting corporate data involves ensuring that proprietary information, trade secrets, and confidential business records remain [[concepts/secure|secure]] while being accessed by [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] for knowledge retrieval and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks.

## Integration with RAG Systems

Organizations implementing [[concepts/contextualized-language-understanding|RAG systems]] must address the challenge of leveraging their internal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] without exposing sensitive data to unauthorized access or external [[concepts/exposure|exposure]]. [[entities/openrag|OpenRAG]], as presented by [[entities/ibm|IBM]], provides frameworks for building [[concepts/agentic-rag-systems|agentic RAG systems]] that can responsibly access and utilize corporate documents, databases, and knowledge repositories. These systems implement access controls, data [[concepts/governance|governance]] [[concepts/policies|policies]], and security protocols to maintain data protection while enabling [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to perform [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] tasks based on internal information.

## Security Considerations

Protecting corporate data in [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]] requires careful [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to [[concepts/authentication|authentication]], encryption, audit logging, and data segregation. Organizations must establish clear boundaries around which data [[concepts/agentic-systems|agentic systems]] can access, how that access is monitored, and what transformations or outputs are permissible. The goal is to balance the utility of AI systems that can reason over rich internal knowledge with the fundamental requirement that sensitive corporate information remains confidential and compliant with regulatory requirements.
