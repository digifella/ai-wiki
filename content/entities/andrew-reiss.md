# Andrew Reiss

Andrew Reiss is a researcher associated with the [[concepts/closed-ecosystem|Biosphere 2]] project, a large-scale ecological experiment conducted in Arizona. He has contributed to scientific understanding of how [[concepts/self-sustaining-environment|closed ecological systems]] function and interact.

## Connection to Biosphere 2

Reiss's work relates to the [[concepts/controlled-climate-experiment|Biosphere 2 experiment]], an ambitious 1990s project that sealed a group of researchers inside a large constructed ecosystem. The experiment aimed to test whether humans could survive in a self-sustaining artificial environment and to advance understanding of Earth's ecological systems. Though the project faced various challenges, it generated valuable data about atmospheric chemistry, plant growth, and ecosystem dynamics that informed broader environmental [[concepts/science|science]].

The insights gained from [[entities/biosphere-2|Biosphere 2]] and associated research contributed to improved [[concepts/models|models]] of how Earth's biosphere functions as an interconnected system, with implications for understanding climate, [[concepts/biodiversity|biodiversity]], and human environmental impacts.
