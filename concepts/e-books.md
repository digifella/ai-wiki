# E-books

Electronic books (e-books) are digital publications readable on devices like tablets, e-readers, and computers. They offer portability, searchability, and often include interactive features absent in print editions.

## Key Characteristics
- **Formats**: EPUB, PDF, MOBI, AZW
- **Distribution**: Digital stores ([[entities/amazon|Amazon]] Kindle, [[entities/apple|Apple]] Books), institutional libraries
- **Advantages**: Searchable text, adjustable font sizes, reduced physical [[entities/storage|storage]]

## Applications
- **Education**: Interactive textbooks with embedded multimedia
- **Publishing**: Lower production/distribution costs
- **[[concepts/ai-integration|AI Integration]]**: Serving as structured [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge bases]] for RAG ([[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]]) systems

## Related Technologies
- [[concepts/azure-ai]]: Cloud platform for building AI solutions
- RAG ([[concepts/information-provision|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]]): Combines [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] with [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]]
- [[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]]: [[concepts/voice-assistants|Autonomous systems]] that perform tasks using [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]
- E-book formats: Technical standards for digital book delivery
