# Candidate Evaluation

Candidate evaluation is a critical process in human resources that involves assessing prospective [[entities/employees|employees]] to determine their suitability for a position within an organization. With the advent of [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]] (GenAI), traditional methods of evaluating candidates have become increasingly complex, as GenAI can assist candidates in crafting polished and seemingly perfect responses during interviews.
