# Custom Bot

A Custom Bot is a specialized [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] built within the [[concepts/microsoft-365-copilot|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] ecosystem that extends the platform's default capabilities to perform specific tasks. Rather than relying solely on [[concepts/copilot-chat|Copilot]]'s general-purpose functionality, custom bots are configured with tailored [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], data sources, and workflows to address particular organizational or individual [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and business processes.

## Configuration and Capabilities

Custom bots leverage Microsoft 365 Copilot's underlying infrastructure while adding domain-specific configuration. This typically includes defining custom instructions that guide the bot's behavior, connecting relevant data sources such as organizational documents or databases, and establishing workflows that automate or streamline specific business processes. This approach allows organizations to adapt Copilot's generative capabilities to their unique requirements without requiring custom software development.

## Creation and Usage

Building a custom bot involves using Microsoft 365 Copilot's configuration tools to define parameters and integrations. The process, as documented in tutorials like those by Teachers Tech, enables both technical and non-technical users to create bots tailored to specific functions—whether for customer support, internal knowledge management, or process automation. Custom bots can be deployed within teams, departments, or across entire organizations depending on access and sharing settings.
