# James Barton Steel

James Barton Steel is a thought leader in [[concepts/narrative-theory|narrative theory]] and cultural [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]]. His work explores how ancient myths continue to resonate with modern audiences.

### New Content:
- **TEDx Talk** on the reinvention of [[concepts/greek-mythology|Greek mythology]] for contemporary audiences, challenging the idea that stories are fixed by their origin.
  - What’s so ancient about Greek mythology?

    - **Author / channel:** [[entities/tedx-talks|TEDx Talks]]

### Key Points from the Talk:
- Argues that every narrative is unique to its teller and listener, shaped by personal interpretation.
- Greek mythology remains relevant because of its timeless themes and adaptability.
