# Department of Transport

The Department of Transport is the primary government body responsible for planning, regulating, and managing transport [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and services in Victoria, Australia. It oversees major projects including the [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] [[concepts/metro-tunnel|Metro Tunnel]], public transit fare structures, and regional rail networks.

## Key Initiatives & Recent Developments

### Melbourne Metro Tunnel
The completion and operational opening of the Melbourne Metro [[concepts/tunnel|Tunnel]] represent a significant milestone in the department's infrastructure strategy, aimed at increasing network capacity and reducing congestion on the CBD rail [[concepts/loop|loop]].

*   **Ridership Impact Analysis**: Recent assessments indicate substantial changes in [[concepts/train-ridership|train ridership]] patterns following the tunnel's full operational opening.
*   **Fare Policy Implications**: The department is analyzing the correlation between the new infrastructure and subsequent fare policy [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to optimize revenue and [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]].
*   **[[concepts/ai-performance-evaluation|Performance Metrics]]**: Early data suggests a shift in commuter behavior, with increased usage of cross-city routes facilitated by the tunnel.
