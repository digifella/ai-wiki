# Starlink Constellation

The **Starlink Constellation** is a [[concepts/satellite-internet|satellite internet]] constellation deployed by [[entities/spacex]] in [[concepts/low-earth-orbit-leo|Low Earth Orbit (LEO)]]. It aims to provide global broadband internet coverage, particularly in rural and remote areas.

## Overview
- **Operator:** [[entities/spacex]]
- **Orbit:** Low [[entities/earth|Earth]] Orbit (LEO), typically between 340 km and 550 km altitude.
- **Purpose:** Global high-[[concepts/speed|speed]] internet connectivity.
- **Scale:** Thousands of satellites, with plans for tens of thousands.

## Technical Specifications
- **Satellite Design:** Flat-panel design with solar arrays and phased-array antennas.
- **Inter-satellite Links:** Laser links for direct satellite-to-[[concepts/satellite-communication|satellite communication]], reducing reliance on ground stations.
- **User Terminals:** Phased-array antennas for users to connect to the constellation.
