# Bismarck Sea

The Bismarck Sea is a body of water in the southwestern Pacific Ocean, located in Oceania between Papua New Guinea and the Bismarck Archipelago. It is bounded by major islands including New Britain and New Ireland, and connects to the Solomon Sea to the east. The sea is named after German statesman Otto von Bismarck, reflecting the region's colonial history under German administration during the late 19th century.

## 1888 Volcanic Event

On 13 March 1888, Ritter Island, located within the Bismarck Sea, experienced a catastrophic flank collapse. The sudden failure of the island's volcanic structure generated a massive tsunami that propagated across the surrounding waters. The event caused significant damage to nearby settlements and vessels in the region, making it one of the most notable natural disasters recorded in the area during the colonial period.
