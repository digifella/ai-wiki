### Context Language Models (CLMs)
*   **Overview:** A novel approach to managing context in AI agents, developed by Meta and the University of Washington.
*   **Core Problem:** Addresses the inherent bottleneck of fixed-size context windows in traditional [[concepts/llm|LLMs]].
*   **Mechanism:** Replaces traditional summarization/compaction with [[concepts/dynamic-[[concepts/contextual-window|[[concepts/memory-management|memory-management]]]]|[[concepts/memory-constructs|dynamic [[concepts/memory|memory]]]] management]], allowing agents to retain precise context without loss.
*   **Significance:** Enables more accurate and persistent [[concepts/agentic-ai|agentic workflows]] by eliminating the need for lossy [[concepts/context-compaction|context compression]].
