# Aigrid

Aigrid is a YouTube channel that focuses on analyzing and explaining emerging developments in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], particularly those moving beyond [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. The channel specializes in covering novel architectures and approaches to machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], with particular emphasis on [[concepts/multimodal-large-language-models|vision-language models]] and alternative computational paradigms.

## Content Focus

The channel has featured detailed technical breakdowns of Meta's [[concepts/cause-and-effect|VL-JEPA]] ([[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]-Language Joint-Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]), examining how the model represents a departure from generative AI approaches. Aigrid's content typically involves comprehensive [[concepts/text-transcript|transcript]] analysis and comparison of emerging non-[[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM reasoning]] architectures, positioning itself as a resource for understanding the technical details and implications of these newer models. The channel appeals to those seeking in-depth exploration of AI developments beyond the dominant [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] paradigm.
