# People Masking

People Masking is an AI-powered feature in Lightroom Classic that automatically detects and creates masks around human subjects in photographs. Using machine learning algorithms, the feature identifies people within an image and generates precise masks without requiring manual selection work. This allows photographers to apply targeted adjustments—such as exposure, color, or clarity changes—to people independently from the rest of the image.

## Functionality

The feature operates through single-click presets that streamline the masking process. Once activated, it analyzes the photograph to locate human subjects and automatically creates selection masks around them. Photographers can then refine these masks if needed and apply selective edits to enhance specific aspects of the people in their photos, such as skin tone, brightness, or local contrast adjustments.

## Workflow Impact

By automating the detection and initial mask creation, People Masking reduces the time photographers spend on manual selection work. Rather than using brush tools or other selection methods to isolate people, users can apply masks with minimal interaction and proceed directly to making adjustments. This feature is particularly useful for portraits, group photos, and any image where people require separate editing treatment from their surroundings.
