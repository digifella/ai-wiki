# World Building

World building is the discipline of constructing fictional or hypothetical universes characterized by internally consistent rules, geography, history, and social systems. It serves as the foundational framework for narratives across diverse media, including literature, video games, film, and interactive experiences. By establishing a coherent context, effective world building allows characters to act and plots to unfold within a believable environment, thereby enhancing audience immersion and engagement.

The process typically addresses several interconnected dimensions, beginning with physical geography and ecology. This includes the creation of landscapes, climates, and natural resources that influence settlement patterns and cultural development. Alongside the physical environment, builders define the historical timeline, detailing past events, conflicts, and evolutionary changes that shape the current state of the world.

Social and political structures form another critical layer, encompassing governments, economies, religions, and cultural norms. These elements determine how inhabitants interact with one another and with the physical world. The integration of these components ensures that the fictional setting operates with logical consistency, providing a stable backdrop against which stories can be told and explored.
