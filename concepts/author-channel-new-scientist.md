# Author Channel New Scientist

[[entities/new-scientist|New Scientist]] is a weekly science and technology magazine founded in 1956 that functions as a major publishing channel for science communication. The publication translates complex scientific research and technological developments into accessible content for both specialist researchers and general audiences. Since its establishment, New Scientist has become a significant voice in science journalism, reaching millions of readers globally through print editions, digital platforms, and social media channels.

## Content and Coverage

The magazine covers a broad spectrum of scientific fields including physics, biology, chemistry, medicine, space exploration, and emerging technologies. New Scientist distinguishes itself by contextualizing research findings within their broader implications for society and daily life. The publication regularly features news reports, feature articles, opinion pieces, and analysis that help readers understand recent scientific developments and their real-world applications.

## Distribution and Audience

New Scientist operates across multiple platforms to reach its readership. The weekly print edition serves subscribers worldwide, while the digital platform provides online access to articles and multimedia content. The channel also maintains active social media presence, extending its reach beyond traditional publishing formats. The magazine attracts readers ranging from science professionals and academics to curious members of the general public seeking to understand contemporary scientific topics.
