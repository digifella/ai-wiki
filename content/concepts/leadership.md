# Task Delegation

Strategic assignment of tasks to team members based on strengths, capacity, and development goals to optimize performance and foster [[concepts/accountability|accountability]].

## Key Concepts
- **The [[concepts/cockpit-rule|Cockpit Rule]]**: Mental model for deciding when to delegate to AI (e.g., routine data processing), collaborate with AI (e.g., draft refinement), or avoid AI entirely (e.g., nuanced strategic decisions). (Source: AI framework [[concepts/skills|skills]] - [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]] Channel)
- **Baseline Expectation**: Proficiency in [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] is now standard (like [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]]), requiring advanced skills to differentiate (e.g., AI framework application).
