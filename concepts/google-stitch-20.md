# Google Stitch 20

Google Stitch 2.0 is an artificial intelligence design agent developed by Google that automates the conversion of design concepts into visual outputs. The platform accepts multiple input formats, including text descriptions and hand-drawn sketches, allowing designers to rapidly transform initial ideas into concrete design assets. By facilitating this transition, the tool aims to reduce iteration time within standard design workflows.

## Functionality

The system processes user inputs through generative models to produce high-fidelity visual representations. It supports the interpretation of rough sketches and natural language prompts, translating abstract concepts into structured design elements. This capability enables users to bypass manual drafting for early-stage ideation, focusing instead on refining the generated outputs.

## Integration and Workflow

Stitch 2.0 is designed to integrate with existing design infrastructure, allowing for seamless handoff between conceptualization and execution phases. The platform operates as a tool within the broader Google ecosystem, providing a bridge between raw creative input and polished visual assets. Its primary utility lies in accelerating the initial stages of the design process, thereby increasing overall efficiency for design teams.
