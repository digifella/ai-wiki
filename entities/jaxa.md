# JAXA

**[[entities/japan|Japan]] Aerospace Exploration Agency** (JAXA) is Japan's national space agency, responsible for aeronautics, [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|space exploration]], and satellite development. It plays a critical role in international planetary missions, often collaborating with [[entities/esa]] and other global partners.

## Key Missions & Collaborations

*   **[[concepts/project-mercury|BepiColombo Mission]]**: JAXA co-leads this joint mission with [[entities/esa]] to study Mercury. Launched in 2018, the mission involves complex orbital mechanics to navigate [[concepts/solar-gravity]] and extreme heat conditions near the Sun.
    *   The mission includes the Mercury Magnetospheric Orbiter (MMO), developed by JAXA, and the Mercury Planetary Orbiter (MPO), developed by ESA.
