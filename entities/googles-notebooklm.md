# Google's NotebookLM

[[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] is an [[concepts/ai-powered-platform|AI-powered platform]] that converts written [[concepts/notebooklm-sources|source materials]] into [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] podcast content. Users upload documents, [[concepts/notes|notes]], research papers, or other text-based sources, and the platform uses [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] to analyze the content and automatically generate podcast episodes. The [[concepts/audio|audio]] output features [[concepts/ai-generated-voices|AI-generated voices]] in a conversational format, making written material accessible in podcast form.

## Core Functionality

The platform processes uploaded documents through its generative [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] to identify key topics, themes, and arguments. It then synthesizes this information into a scripted podcast [[concepts/communication|dialogue]], complete with natural-sounding narration. This automation eliminates the manual work typically required to produce podcast content from source materials, allowing users to quickly transform large volumes of text into audio format.

## Converting Podcasts to Video

NotebookLM-generated [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]] can be converted into [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] for platforms like [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] or social media. The process involves combining the AI-generated audio with visual elements—typically static images, text overlays, or animated [[concepts/google-slides|slides]] that correspond to the podcast discussion. This workflow enables creators to repurpose podcast content across multiple media formats with minimal additional production work.

## Use Cases

The platform serves researchers, educators, content creators, and professionals who want to make written information more accessible or create supplementary audio content. By automating podcast generation, [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] reduces production barriers and allows users to maintain consistent content output across different media channels.
