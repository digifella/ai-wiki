Prompting refers to the technique of crafting precise inputs to guide AI [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] toward desired outputs. Effective prompting minimizes [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] and maximizes model utility across applications.

## Key Practices
- [[entities/cal-rueb|Cal Rueb]] ([[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/applied-ai-team|Applied AI team]]) emphasizes **clear [[concepts/task-description|task specification]]** in prompts for [[entities/claude-code]], reducing [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] errors
- **[[concepts/iterative-refinement|Iterative refinement]]** is critical: prompt [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] based on model responses yield progressively better outputs
- **Contextual constraints** (e.g., language, [[concepts/style|style]], error handling) must be explicitly defined in prompts
- [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/applied-ai-team|Applied AI team]] focuses on helping partners integrate [[entities/claude]] through expert [[entities/prompt-engineering]]
