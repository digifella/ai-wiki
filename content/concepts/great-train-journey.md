# Great Train Journey

Great Train Journey refers to notable [[concepts/rail-travel|rail journeys]] and train-based holiday experiences available in Australia. These journeys [[concepts/range|range]] from short scenic routes to multi-day expeditions, offering passengers the opportunity to travel through diverse Australian landscapes by rail. Train holidays have become an established tourism category in Australia, combining transportation with leisure and sightseeing.

## Notable Routes and Experiences

Australia offers several recognised train journey experiences that cater to different travel preferences and durations. These routes traverse various regions of the country, including coastal areas, inland regions, and remote [[concepts/australian-outback|outback]] territory. Many journeys are designed to showcase natural scenery, historical landmarks, or regional culture during the travel experience.

## Tourism Appeal

[[concepts/public-transport-revival|Train journeys]] appeal to travellers seeking a more leisurely pace of travel compared to other transport modes. The format allows passengers to observe landscapes while travelling and typically includes accommodation and dining as part of the experience. Rail holidays have become an established segment of the Australian tourism industry, attracting both domestic and international visitors.
