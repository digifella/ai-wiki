# Collaboration Mode

A [[concepts/strategic-approach|strategic approach]] to interacting with AI systems, moving beyond basic [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]] to effective delegation and partnership. As [[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI proficiency]] becomes a baseline expectation (like [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]]), the ability to collaborate effectively with AI is what differentiates high performers.

## AI Framework Skills (Jeff Su's 4 Skills to Get Ahead)

- **The [[concepts/cockpit-rule|Cockpit Rule]]**: A mental model for deciding when to delegate tasks to AI, when to collaborate with it (e.g., co-creating), and when to avoid AI entirely (e.g., for tasks requiring human judgment or creativity).
