# Whiteboard

A whiteboard video is an animated video format that simulates the process of drawing and writing on a whiteboard in real time. This style of video presentation combines visual elements with narration to explain concepts, tell stories, or present information in an engaging manner. The format is commonly used for educational content, marketing explanations, and instructional tutorials. Whiteboard videos appeal to viewers because they break down complex information into digestible visual steps while maintaining sustained attention through the continuous visual progression of drawings and text.

## Creating Whiteboard Videos

Several tools enable the production of whiteboard videos without requiring traditional animation expertise. NotebookLM can assist in scripting and organizing content for whiteboard narratives. Pictory automates the video creation process by converting scripts into animated sequences with synchronized visuals and voiceover. Nano Banana Pro provides specialized features for designing and animating whiteboard-style graphics and illustrations.

The typical workflow involves developing a clear script, creating or generating visual assets, and using software to synchronize drawings with audio narration. The hand-drawn aesthetic—whether created manually or simulated through animation tools—remains central to the whiteboard video's effectiveness as a communication format.
