# Mark Denney

Mark Denney is a landscape photographer and educator known for producing instructional content on photographic techniques. His work focuses on addressing practical technical challenges in landscape photography, particularly methods for achieving sharp, detailed imagery across entire compositions.

Denney is recognized for his detailed explanations of focus stacking, a technique used to enhance overall sharpness in landscape photographs. This method involves capturing multiple images at different focal distances and combining them in post-processing to achieve a greater depth of field than a single exposure allows.
