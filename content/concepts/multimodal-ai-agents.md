# Multimodal AI Agents

Multimodal [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI Agents]] are autonomous or semi-autonomous systems capable of perceiving, [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and interacting across multiple sensory modalities, including [[concepts/text]], image, [[concepts/audio]], and video. Unlike unimodal models, these [[concepts/agents|agents]] utilize integrated architectures to process heterogeneous data streams, enabling [[concepts/complex-task-execution|complex task execution]] in both digital and physical environments.

## Core Capabilities
- **Cross-modal [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]]:** The ability to synthesize and correlate information across disparate data types (e.g., interpreting a visual scene through text-based logic).
- **Sensory [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** Processing continuous streams of [[concepts/audio]] and visual data to maintain situational awareness.
- **[[concepts/agentic-tool|Agentic Tool]] Use:** Executing actions via [[entities/api]] calls, [[concepts/software|software]] interfaces, or robotic control to complete multi-step workflows.

## Recent Developments
- NVIDIA [[entities/nemotron-3-nano-omni|Nemotron 3 Nano Omni]]: Unified Multimodal [[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]] Model Overview:
    - Introduces a transformative, "all-in-one" unified [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] for multimodal AI agents.
    - Integrates multiple modalities—specifically [[concepts/text]], [[concepts/images]], and [[concepts/audio]]—into a single model framework.
    - Positioned as a foundational development for unified agentic modeling.
