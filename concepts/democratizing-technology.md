# Democratizing Technology

Democratizing technology refers to the process of making technological resources and capabilities accessible and usable for everyone, regardless of background or [[concepts/expertise|expertise]]. This concept is crucial in promoting inclusivity and reducing barriers to entry in fields such as [[concepts/coding|software development]], data [[concepts/science|science]], and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].
