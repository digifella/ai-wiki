# LLM integration

## Overview
Integration of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) into persistent, explainable, and reasoning-capable systems. Current trends shift from simple request-response patterns toward [[concepts/neural-symbolic-ai]] frameworks that combine statistical learning with logical rigor.

## Key Developments

### OmegaClaw Framework
*   **Concept:** An open-source [[concepts/neural-symbolic-ai]] [[concepts/agent-harness|agent framework]] developed by SingularityNET.
*   **Core Mechanism:** Utilizes [[concepts/symbolic-logic|symbolic logic]] alongside [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]] to enable persistent and explainable [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], addressing the "[[concepts/black-box-models|black box]]" and stateless limitations of traditional LLM agents.
*   **Significance:** Represents a move toward AI agents that can maintain context and provide auditable reasoning paths rather than just generating probabilistic text.
