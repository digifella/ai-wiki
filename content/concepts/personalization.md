# Personalization

Personalization in technology refers to the [[concepts/ai-workflow|customization]] of user experiences, interfaces, or services based on individual preferences, behaviors, and other data points unique to each user. This can include tailored content recommendations, personalized settings within [[concepts/software|applications]], or customized user profiles across various platforms.
