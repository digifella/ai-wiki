# WIMP

[[concepts/wimp-particles|Weakly Interacting Massive Particles]] ([[concepts/wimps|WIMPs]]) are a leading theoretical candidate for [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]], interacting only via [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] and the weak nuclear force, making them difficult to detect.

## Current Research
- [[entities/katie-mack|Katie Mack]] ([[entities/perimeter-institute|Perimeter Institute]]) leads [[concepts/universe-creation|WIMP detection]] efforts, including the SABRE detector in [[entities/stawell-gold-mine|Stawell gold mine]] (Victoria), coordinated with an Italian detector.
- [[entities/perimeter-institute|Perimeter Institute]]'s collaboration model: researchers invite six collaborators yearly, with two fully funded.
- Mack's work "Creating the Universe" explores cosmological [[concepts/models|models]] relevant to [[concepts/dark-matter|dark matter]].
- [[concepts/particle-detection|WIMP search]] efforts are referenced in Mack's public discussions, including a mention of [[entities/hozier|Hozier]]'s song "No Plan".
