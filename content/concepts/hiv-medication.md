# HIV medication

Therapeutic [[concepts/agents|agents]] used for the treatment and management of HIV infection.

## Key Medications & Clinical Incidents

- Ritonavir
    - Introduced in 1996 as a "miracle drug" for HIV therapy.
    - Experienced a sudden failure in [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]] [[concepts/testing|testing]] in 1998.
    - The crisis was characterized by an unexpected shift in polymorphism (alteration in the drug's crystal [[concepts/structure|structure]]).
    - Detailed study: Ritonavir [[concepts/crystalline-structure|Polymorph Crisis]]: Unraveling the Mystery of a Failing HIV Drug
