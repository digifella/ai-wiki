# SpaceX Starship Flight 14

**[[concepts/starship-flight-14|SpaceX Starship Flight 14]]** represents a critical milestone in the Starship (spacecraft) development program, marking the vehicle's first successful [[concepts/orbital-insertion]] and subsequent Starlink satellite deployment.

## Mission Overview
Flight 14 demonstrated key capabilities required for operational Starlink constellation deployment and reusable launch vehicle recovery.

### Key Achievements
*   **First Orbital Insertion:** Successfully achieved orbit for the first time, validating upper-stage performance and guidance systems.
*   **Starlink Deployment:** Deployed 26 [[concepts/starlink-v3]] satellites, confirming the payload fairing and deployment mechanism functionality.
*   **Controlled Splashdowns:** Executed controlled re-entry and splashdowns for both the Super Heavy booster and Starship upper stage, testing thermal protection and landing precision.
