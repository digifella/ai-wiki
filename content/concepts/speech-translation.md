# Speech Translation

Speech translation is the computational process of converting spoken [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] input into written [[concepts/text|text]] [[concepts/output|output]]. This technology combines [[concepts/automatic-speech-recognition|automatic speech recognition]] (ASR) with [[concepts/nlp|natural language processing]] to transcribe and interpret human speech in real-time or batch processing modes. Modern speech translation systems operate across multiple languages and can handle various acoustic conditions, [[entities/speaker|speaker]] accents, and background noise levels with varying degrees of [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] depending on the model and [[concepts/adoption|implementation]].

## Technical Implementation

Several [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] have been developed to perform speech translation tasks. [[entities/whisper-ai|Whisper]], developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], is a widely-used [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] model that supports multilingual [[concepts/speech-recognition|speech recognition]] and has become a standard tool in the field. These [[concepts/models|models]] are typically implemented on accessible platforms such as [[entities/google-colab|Google Colab]], allowing researchers and developers to experiment with speech translation without requiring specialized [[concepts/hardware|hardware]]. The models use deep [[concepts/learning|learning]] architectures trained on large datasets of audio and corresponding transcriptions.

## Applications and Context

Speech translation finds practical [[concepts/software|applications]] in transcription services, [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] tools for deaf and hard-of-hearing users, meeting documentation, customer service [[concepts/automation|automation]], and [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]. The technology has also been integrated into larger AI systems and [[concepts/notebook|notebook]]-based environments that combine speech processing with other analytical tools, enabling [[concepts/multi-modal-analysis|multi-modal analysis]] of audio content.
