# North Atlantic

The North Atlantic is the northwestern portion of the Atlantic Ocean, bounded by North [[entities/america|America]], [[entities/europe|Europe]], and Africa. It is one of the world's most heavily traveled maritime regions and has been central to numerous historical voyages, naval operations, and scientific discoveries.

## The Titanic Discovery

In 1985, a [[entities/us-navy|U.S. Navy]] research mission originally tasked with locating two lost nuclear submarines—the USS Thresher and USS Scorpion—resulted in an unexpected discovery. While conducting deep-ocean surveys in the North Atlantic using advanced sonar equipment, researcher [[entities/dr-robert-ballard|Robert Ballard]] and his team detected the wreck of the RMS Titanic at a depth of approximately 3,800 meters. The mission's classified [[entities/nature|nature]] meant that the Titanic's discovery was kept confidential for several months before being publicly announced. This accidental find became one of the most significant maritime archaeological discoveries of the twentieth century, providing the first visual evidence of the ship that sank in 1912.
