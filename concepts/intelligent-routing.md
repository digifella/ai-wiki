# Intelligent Routing

**Intelligent Routing** refers to the dynamic allocation of computational tasks and data flows based on context, latency requirements, and resource availability. In the context of modern AI architectures, this concept has evolved to prioritize **[[concepts/local-processing|local processing]]** to enhance [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], reduce latency, and minimize cloud dependency.

## Core Principles

*   **Context-Aware [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]:** Routing decisions are made in real-time based on device capabilities, network status, and data sensitivity.
*   **Hybrid Architecture:** Balances between [[concepts/edge-devices|on-device inference]] and cloud-based heavy lifting to optimize performance and cost.
*   **Privacy [[concepts/preservation|Preservation]]:** Sensitive data is routed to local [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]] to avoid transmission over public networks.

## Microsoft's Strategic Shift

[[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] has significantly advanced the field of intelligent routing through its push for **[[concepts/hybrid-intelligence|Hybrid Intelligence]]** and on-device capabilities. This shift emphasizes empowering users with powerful AI directly on their devices, reducing reliance on cloud-based processing [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-Microsofts-Local-AI-Push-Hybrid-Intelligence-HydraFusion|Microsoft's Local AI Push: Hybrid Intelligence, HydraFusion, and Copilot's On-Device Capabilities]].

Key developments include:

*   **Hybrid Intelligence Framework:** A [[concepts/strategic-approach|strategic approach]] that seamlessly blends [[concepts/local-ai-processing|local AI processing]] with cloud resources, ensuring optimal performance for [[entities/copilot]] and other AI features.
*   **[[concepts/local-execution|On-Device Processing]]:** Enhancing [[concepts/microsoft-windows|Windows]] PCs and [[entities/surface|Surface]] devices to handle AI workloads locally, improving privacy and responsiveness.
*   **HydraFusion:** A specific technology or methodology aimed at optimizing the integration of local and [[concepts/cloud-ai|cloud AI]] capabilities for smoother user experiences.
