# Google Search

[[entities/google-search|Google Search]] is a web search engine developed and operated by Google, one of the most widely used [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] services on the internet. It allows users to search for information across billions of indexed web pages, [[concepts/images|images]], videos, news articles, and other digital content. The service uses algorithmic ranking to present the most relevant results based on search queries, with Google's PageRank algorithm and subsequent refinements forming the foundation of its relevance determination.

## Integration with AI Systems

In recent years, Google Search has been integrated with [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]], particularly through Google's [[entities/gemini-models|Gemini AI]] [[concepts/models|models]] and other [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] systems. These integrations enable more sophisticated query understanding, [[concepts/summarization|summarization]] of results, and direct answers to complex questions. The service has also evolved to support "[[concepts/ai-overviews|Generative Engine Optimization]]," a practice where websites adapt their content strategies for AI-driven search and discovery systems that synthesize information rather than simply listing links.

## Business and Technical Scope

Google Search operates across multiple platforms and formats, including web search, mobile search, image search, and specialized verticals like news and shopping. The service generates significant revenue through targeted advertising and remains central to Google's broader business model. Technical updates and algorithm changes to Google Search have substantial impacts on web traffic [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] and digital marketing strategies globally.
