### Enhancements in Adobe Lightroom Classic v15
- **Refined Color Adjustments:** Advanced algorithms for more precise control over color correction, allowing photographers to achieve nuanced adjustments without compromising image quality.
- **Enhanced [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]] Tools:** Improved [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] for creating masks that accurately define regions of an image for targeted editing. This includes better support for AI-driven selection refinements and smoother edge transitions.
