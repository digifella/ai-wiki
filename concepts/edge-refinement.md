# Edge Refinement

Edge Refinement is a masking technique available in Lightroom and Adobe Camera Raw Filter that improves the precision and natural appearance of mask boundaries during selective editing. When creating masks to adjust specific areas of an image, the edges between masked and unmasked regions often appear harsh or artificial, creating visible silhouettes that betray the editing process. Edge Refinement tools address this problem by feathering, smoothing, and fine-tuning mask perimeters to blend them more seamlessly with surrounding pixels.

## How It Works

The technique typically involves adjusting parameters such as feather amount, contrast, and shift edge to soften harsh transitions. Feathering expands or contracts the mask boundary gradually, while edge shift can move the mask line inward or outward to better align with subject edges. These adjustments create a gradual transition zone rather than an abrupt cutoff, making selective adjustments appear more integrated into the original image.

## Practical Applications

Edge Refinement is particularly useful when isolating subjects for localized exposure, color, or clarity adjustments. It helps prevent telltale halos around portraits, unwanted fringing on product photography, and obvious boundaries when enhancing skies or backgrounds. The technique is essential for maintaining a natural, unedited appearance while applying targeted corrections to specific image regions.
