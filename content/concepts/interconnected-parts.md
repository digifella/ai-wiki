# Interconnected Parts

Interconnected Parts refers to [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/strategic-approach|strategic approach]] to developing [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]], introduced through a series of updates colloquially known as "[[concepts/dom|Christmas Claude]]." This framework emphasizes designing [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] whose capabilities are deeply integrated rather than operating as isolated modules, allowing for more coherent [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and action across multiple domains.

## Core Approach

The strategy involves structuring [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]-based [[concepts/agents|agents]] so that different functional components—perception, reasoning, planning, and execution—work in coordination rather than independently. This interconnected design enables agents to maintain context and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] when handling complex, multi-step tasks that require reasoning across different problem domains simultaneously.

## Implementation Context

Anthropic's rollout of these updates demonstrated the company's focus on practical [[concepts/agent-deployment|agent deployment]] rather than purely theoretical advances. By releasing these capabilities incrementally, Anthropic signaled its commitment to developing [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]] that could handle real-world complexity through integrated architectural choices rather than through simple [[concepts/task-decomposition|task decomposition]].
