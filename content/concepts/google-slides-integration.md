# Google Slides Integration

Google Slides Integration refers to the integration of [[entities/gemini-models|Gemini AI]] capabilities directly within [[entities/google-slides|Google Slides]], enabling users to generate and create [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]] through AI assistance. This integration is part of [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s broader efforts to embed generative AI features across [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] [[concepts/software|applications]], allowing users to [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] for [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] and presentation design without leaving the Google Slides environment.

## Functionality

The integration allows [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] to assist with various slide creation tasks, including generating slide content, suggesting layouts, and organizing information into presentation format. Users can interact with Gemini within Google Slides to accelerate the deck creation process and iterate on presentation designs.

## Context within Google Workspace

Google Slides Integration exists alongside similar Gemini integrations in other Google Workspace applications such as Google Docs and Google Sheets. These integrations represent Google's strategy to distribute [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] across its productivity suite, offering users consistent access to generative [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] within their existing workflows.
