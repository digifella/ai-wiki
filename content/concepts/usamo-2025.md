# Usamo 2025

The USAMO ([[entities/united-states|United States of America]] Mathematical Olympiad) is an annual high school [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]] competition and one of the most prestigious math contests in the United States. The 2025 [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of the competition continues this tradition of identifying and challenging the most talented young mathematicians in the country.

USAMO 2025 maintains the standard format of the competition, which typically consists of two days of [[concepts/testing|testing]] with three proof-based problems each day. Participants have four and a half hours per day to solve these problems, which require deep [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|mathematical reasoning]] and creative [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] approaches rather than computational skill alone.

The competition serves as a crucial pathway for identifying mathematical talent and has historically led to the formation of the United States' team for the [[concepts/international-mathematical-olympiad|International Mathematical Olympiad]] (IMO). Success in USAMO often correlates with exceptional performance in subsequent mathematical careers and academic pursuits.
