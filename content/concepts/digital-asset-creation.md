# Digital Asset Creation

Digital asset creation refers to the process of producing digital content and media files for various purposes, such as marketing, education, or documentation. This encompasses the conversion of generated content into multiple formats and the removal of proprietary watermarks to create standalone, distributable files.

## Workflow with Google Tools

One common workflow involves converting presentation [[entities/google-slides|slides]] generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]]—Google's AI-powered [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] and research tool—into video format using [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]]. This process allows creators to transform text-based or slide-based content into visual media while maintaining quality and removing watermarking that might otherwise appear in the final output. The integration of these Google tools enables a streamlined pipeline from initial content generation through to final video production.

## Practical Applications

This approach is particularly useful for educators, content creators, and organizations that need to produce polished video content from research notes or presentations without spending significant time on manual editing or video production. By leveraging automated tools within the Google ecosystem, creators can focus on content quality rather than technical production challenges.
