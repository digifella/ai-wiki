# Active Participation

**Active Participation** denotes the imperative for human agents to engage critically, verify outputs, and retain agency within [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] [[concepts/loops|loops]], preventing [[concepts/algorithmic-decision-making|automation bias]] and ensuring [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] in [[entities/ai]]-augmented workflows.

## Key Dynamics

- **AI Evolution & Human Agency**: [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] are shifting from [[concepts/clinical-assistance]] toward autonomous [[concepts/clinical-reasoning]], requiring explicit protocols to maintain clinician engagement and prevent passive reliance.
- **Medical Paradox**: [[concepts/frontier-ai-capability|Advanced AI capabilities]] may trigger the [[concepts/medical-paradox]], where increased tool sophistication correlates with degradation of diagnostic intuition and reduced active [[concepts/verification|verification]].
- **Evidence Infrastructure Volatility**: Disruptions in data ecosystems, such as [[entities/openevidence-europe|OpenEvidence]] withdrawing from European operations, underscore risks to knowledge validation and the stability of evidence-dependent participation.
