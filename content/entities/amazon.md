Amazon is a multinational technology company founded in 1994, operating in e-commerce, [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]] (AWS), [[concepts/streaming-services|digital streaming]] (Prime Video), and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] (Alexa).

- **[[concepts/dark-code|Dark Code]] Incident ([[concepts/date-2026-04-13|2026]])**: Following the layoff of 16,000 engineers, Amazon's AI-generated [[concepts/software|software]] created "[[concepts/dark-code|Dark Code]]" — [[concepts/production-software|production software]] fully incomprehensible to all payroll personnel (engineers, teams, CTO). This untraceable risk stems from AI code proliferation and was highlighted in [[entities/ai-news-strategy-daily]]'s video "[I Looked At Amazon After They Fired 16,000 Engineers. Their AI Broke Everything.](https://youtu.be/E1idsrv79tI)" discussing AI's comprehension gap and systemic vulnerabilities.

- **[[concepts/mixture-of-experts|Mixture of Experts]] panel (2026)**: Discusses Amazon blocking [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], alongside debates on [[concepts/ai-scaling-laws|AI scaling laws]] and the implications of the "Fun-cember" model release surge.

- **[[entities/kiro|Kiro]] (2026)**: Amazon's new [[concepts/ai-code-editor|AI code editor]] (a [[concepts/vs-code-fork|VS Code fork]]), analyzed in [[entities/ai-news-strategy-daily]]'s video "[Kiro: Amazon's AI Code Editor](https://youtu.be/73h5Lb_N9r8)" for its strategic positioning in the [[concepts/ai-code-editor|AI code editor]] market.
