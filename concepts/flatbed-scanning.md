# Flatbed Scanning

Flatbed scanning is a method of digitizing film negatives and [[concepts/google-slides|slides]] using a standard [[concepts/flatbed-scanner|flatbed scanner]] equipped with [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] scanning capabilities. While flatbed scanners are primarily designed for documents and reflective materials, many models include transparency adapters or backlit scanning modules that enable them to read transmitted [[concepts/light|light]] through film. This approach allows individuals to convert analog film materials into digital format at home without investing in specialized [[concepts/film-scanning|film scanning]] equipment.

## Equipment and Setup

Most flatbed scanners capable of transparency work require a dedicated transparency unit (TMA) that clips onto the scanner lid to provide uniform backlighting. Users place the film directly on the scanner glass, often using a holder to keep it flat and prevent dust accumulation. The scanner software must be configured to scan in negative mode, which inverts the colors and [[concepts/brightness-levels|brightness levels]] to produce a positive image. Higher [[concepts/solution|resolution]] settings are typically necessary to capture the fine grain and detail inherent in film stock, often exceeding the standard document scanning requirements.

## Image Quality and Limitations

The quality of flatbed scans depends heavily on the scanner’s optical resolution and the [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]] of its light source. While modern flatbeds can produce respectable results for casual use, they generally lack the resolution and color depth of dedicated drum or dedicated film scanners. Issues such as dust, scratches, and static charge are more pronounced because the entire surface of the film is exposed to the scanner’s glass and light source. Additionally, the limited backlight intensity of consumer-grade units can result in lower [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] and less accurate color reproduction compared to professional digitization methods.
