# Source-to-Note Conversion

The process of transforming unstructured Sources into structured, actionable [[concepts/notes]] through automated extraction and synthesis.

### Key Modalities
- **[[concepts/summarization]]**: Condensing long-form content into distilled key points.
- **[[concepts/data-extraction]]**: Identifying specific [[concepts/nodes|entities]], [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], or attributes within a text.
- **[[concepts/data-table-generation]]**:
	- Utilizing tools like [[entities/notebooklm]] to transform [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] videos, Websites, and Uploaded files into structured [[concepts/data-tables]].
	- Ability to define custom columns and specific information types (e.g., extracting rankings, directors, or awards from transcripts).
- **[[concepts/knowledge-productization]]**:
	- Transforming personal [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] and unstructured notes into structured "**[[concepts/logic-manuals|Logic Manuals]]**".
	- Utilizing [[entities/gemini-gems|Gemini Gems]] to power [[concepts/custom-ai-assistants|custom AI assistants]] for monetizable [[concepts/consulting|consulting]] workflows.
