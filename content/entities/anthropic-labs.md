# Anthropic Labs

[[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] [[entities/labs|Labs]] is the division of [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] responsible for developing and demonstrating [[concepts/digital-asset-generation|AI-driven generative design]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]]. The labs leverage [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], Anthropic's [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]], to create tools and platforms that automate and enhance [[concepts/design-workflows|design workflows]] across digital and creative domains.

## Claude Design Platform

The primary offering from Anthropic Labs is a generative design platform built on [[concepts/claude|Claude]]'s capabilities. This platform enables users to generate digital assets and design outputs through AI, streamlining creative processes by allowing designers to specify requirements and receive generated design solutions. The system integrates Claude's language understanding with design generation tasks.
