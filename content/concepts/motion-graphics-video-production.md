# Motion Graphics Video Production

Systematic creation of animated visual content using digital tools, encompassing keyframe animation, procedural generation, and code-based composition for media, advertising, and data visualization.

## Core Workflows
- **[[concepts/design|Design]] & Storyboarding**: Conceptualization, timeline structuring, and asset [[concepts/preparation|preparation]].
- **Animation Engines**: Keyframing, easing curves, and [[concepts/physics|physics]] simulations within DAWs or dedicated compositing [[concepts/software|software]].
- **Rendering & Export**: Rasterization, vector [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]], and codec optimization for [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].

## Tools & Technologies
- **Compositing Suites**: After Effects, Nuke, DaVinci Resolve Fusion.
- **3D Environments**: Blender, Cinema 4D, Houdini.
- **Code-Based Animation**: [[entities/remotion]], [[concepts/threejs]], p5.js for data-driven and reproducible graphics.
