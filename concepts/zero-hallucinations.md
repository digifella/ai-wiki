# Zero Hallucinations

**Zero hallucinations** refers to the architectural or operational guarantee that an AI system produces no fabricated, ungrounded, or factually incorrect outputs. This concept is critical for high-stakes decision-making environments where reliability and truthfulness are paramount.

## Key Developments

### Jev by TypeSafe.ai
Significant progress in this domain is represented by **Jev**, a decision-focused AI model developed by [[concepts/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]].

- **Core Promise:** Explicitly designed for "zero hallucinations" in decision contexts, contrasting with traditional [[concepts/large-language-model]] that prioritize generative fluency over factual grounding.
- **Creator:** Developed by [[entities/diogo-almeida|Diogo Almeida]], a co-inventor of [[entities/chatgpt]].
- **Architecture:** Optimized for speed and reliability in decision-making rather than open-ended text generation.
- **[[concepts/system-one-architecture|System One]] Classification:** Positioned as a "[[concepts/system-one-intelligence|System One]]" [[concepts/decision-model|decision model]], emphasizing fast, reliable, and cost-efficient processing for immediate operational needs [[lab-notes/2026-09-23-Jev-AI-TypeSafes-System-One-Decision-Model---Fast-Reliab|Jev AI: TypeSafe's System One Decision Model - Fast, Reliable, Cost-Efficient]].
- **Differentiation:** Described as a "completely different class of [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]]" compared to standard LLMs, focusing on grounded decision capabilities rather than broad generative fluency.
