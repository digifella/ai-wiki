# Alka Kolthari

Alka Kolthari is a researcher specializing in paternal involvement during childbirth and the broader experiences of fathers within maternity care settings. Her academic work investigates the psychological dimensions of men's presence during the delivery process, aiming to fill existing gaps in the understanding of male emotional responses to birth events.

A significant portion of Kolthari's documented research focuses on cases involving traumatic birth outcomes and their specific impact on fathers. By examining these high-stress scenarios, her work highlights the unique challenges fathers face when witnessing difficult deliveries, contributing to a deeper understanding of how such events affect paternal mental health.

Her studies also address the enhancement of service delivery for fathers, advocating for improved support systems within maternity care. This includes analyzing how healthcare providers can better accommodate the needs of men attending traumatic births, thereby fostering a more inclusive and supportive environment for all parents involved in the delivery process.
