# Controlled Splashdown

**Controlled splashdown** refers to the precise maneuvering of a launch vehicle or spacecraft stage to land in a designated body of water, typically for recovery, safety, or reusability purposes. This technique is critical for [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] Starship operations, where controlled splashdown allows for the [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] of the Super Heavy booster or Starship upper stage after [[concepts/orbital-insertion]] or sub-orbital tests.

## Key Milestones & Events

### SpaceX Starship Flight 14
Significant developments regarding controlled splashdown and mission objectives occurred during the 14th test flight of the [[concepts/starship|Starship vehicle]].

*   **[[concepts/orbital-deployment|Orbital Insertion]]:** Achieved the vehicle's first successful [[concepts/orbital-insertion]], a prerequisite for complex re-entry and splashdown profiles.
*   **[[concepts/starlink-constellation|Starlink]] Deployment:** Successfully deployed 26 [[concepts/starlink-v3]] satellites, demonstrating payload capacity alongside recovery operations.
*   **Controlled Splashdowns:** Executed controlled splashdown maneuvers for vehicle stages, marking a critical step in the reusability pipeline.
