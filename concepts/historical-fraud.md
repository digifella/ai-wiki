# Historical Fraud

**Historical [[concepts/fraud|Fraud]]** encompasses deceptive practices and schemes designed to obtain money, property, or advantage through false pretenses, evolving alongside technological and social changes.

## Key Concepts & Evolution

- **Advance-Fee Fraud**: A scheme where victims are promised large sums of money in return for a small [[concepts/upfront-payment|upfront payment]].
	- **[[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|Lineage]]**: Modern iterations trace back to the **"Spanish Prisoner"** swindle (late 18th century), which evolved into the contemporary **"Nigerian Prince"** scam.
	- **Source Analysis**: See [[lab-notes/2026-07-13-Evolution-of-Advance-Fee-Scams-From-Spanish-Prisoner-to|Evolution of Advance Fee Scams: From Spanish Prisoner to Nigerian Prince]] for detailed [[concepts/historical-context|historical context]] regarding this transition.
- **Social [[entities/national-academies|Engineering]]**: The psychological manipulation of people into performing actions or divulging confidential information, a core component of historical and modern fraud.
- **Impersonation**: Fraudsters often assume false identities (e.g., royalty, officials, prisoners) to establish [[concepts/trust|trust]] or urgency.
