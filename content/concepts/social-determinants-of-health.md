# Social Determinants of Health

[[concepts/public-health|Public health]] focuses on improving [[concepts/health|health]] and [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]] through organized efforts to prevent disease and promote health in communities. The social [[concepts/determinants-of-health|determinants of health]] (SDOH) are the conditions in which people are born, grow, live, work, and age that affect their health outcomes.

## Key Concepts
- **Social Determinants of Health:** These factors include socioeconomic status, education, neighborhood and physical environment, employment, and social support networks. They interact with each other to influence individual health outcomes.
- **[[concepts/life-expectancy|Life Expectancy]] Increase (1900-2000):** [[concepts/population-health|Public health]] efforts contributed significantly to an increase in [[concepts/life-expectancy|life expectancy]] by over 30 years in the [[entities/united-states|United States]], with 25 of those years attributed directly to public health initiatives.

## Introduction to Public Health: Definition and Role

- **Definition:** The field that focuses on preventing disease and promoting health in communities through organized efforts.
- **Role:** To improve population health outcomes, prevent the spread of diseases, and enhance quality of life by addressing both individual behaviors and societal conditions.
