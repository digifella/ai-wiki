# Trainee

A trainee is a person undergoing structured [[concepts/training|training]] or education within an institutional setting, typically working toward professional qualification or skill development. In academic and professional contexts, trainees often work alongside more experienced practitioners while completing formal requirements for their field.

## Role in Institutional Governance

Trainees form part of the broader institutional community and may be included in communications regarding organizational matters. In some cases, trainees' [[entities/contact|contact]] information may be used by institutional leadership to disseminate information about governance issues, [[concepts/policy-changes|policy changes]], or other matters of collective concern. The inclusion of trainees in such communications reflects their status as stakeholders within the institution, though their role in governance decision-making may differ from that of full members or senior staff.
