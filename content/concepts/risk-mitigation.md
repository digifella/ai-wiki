# Risk Mitigation

Risk mitigation involves strategies to reduce or eliminate risks in various contexts, including [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]], project management, and business operations. It focuses on identifying potential hazards and implementing measures to minimize their impact.

## Local AI Privacy Risks and Mitigation Strategies

### Key Points
- [[concepts/ai-ownership|Running AI locally]] does not automatically guarantee [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
- Common misconception: owning a house ([[concepts/local-ai|local AI]]) is inherently more [[concepts/secure|secure]] than renting an apartment ([[concepts/cloud-ai|cloud AI]]).
- [[entities/daniel-jindoo|Daniel Jindoo]]'s video challenges the [[entities/notion|notion]] that local ownership equates to absolute [[concepts/security|security]], highlighting residual risks.
