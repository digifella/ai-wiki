# Nuclear Fusion

The process of combining [[concepts/light|light]] atomic nuclei to release massive amounts of energy, fundamentally mimicking the nucleosynthesis occurring within a [[concepts/star|Star]].

## Core Concepts
- Aims to replicate the colossal energy [[concepts/output|output]] of the [[concepts/sun|Sun]] to create a sustainable [[concepts/power|power]] source for Earth.
- Relies on the fusion of elements such as [[concepts/hydrogen|Hydrogen]] and [[concepts/helium|Helium]].
- Focuses on the scientific quest to [[concepts/harness|harness]] the power of a [[concepts/star|Star]] via terrestrial replication.

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/wendelstein-7-x|Wendelstein 7-X]]**: [[entities/germany|Germany]]'s stellarator reactor has set new world records, demonstrating significant progress in steady-state plasma confinement and validating the non-axisymmetric magnetic field [[concepts/design|design]].
- **Commercial Timeline**: Targeted operational date for a commercial [[concepts/fusion-power|fusion power]] plant in Germany is set for 2045, marking a key milestone in the transition from experimental [[concepts/physics|physics]] to grid-scale energy generation.
- **"Holy Grail" Status**: Fusion remains the primary candidate for limitless, clean energy, with recent breakthroughs reducing the uncertainty surrounding its feasibility.
