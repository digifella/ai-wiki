## AI Masks in Obsidian Wiki

### Overview
AI masks are automated tools designed to enhance workflow efficiency by allowing users to apply precise edits to specific areas of an image without manual selection. These tools leverage [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to detect and isolate elements such as skies, faces, or landscapes with high [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]].
