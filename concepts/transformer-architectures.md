# Transformer Architectures

Transformer architectures are the foundational design pattern for modern [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]], built on the self-[[concepts/self-attention|attention mechanism]] that allows [[concepts/ai-models|neural networks]] to weight [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between different elements in a sequence. This mechanism enables models to dynamically focus on relevant [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] regardless of their position in the input, facilitating both [[concepts/parallel-processing|parallel processing]] and the capture of long-range dependencies in text.

## Core Components

The standard [[concepts/transformer-models|transformer architecture]] consists of an encoder-decoder structure with multiple layers of [[concepts/transformer-attention-mechanism|self-attention]] and feed-forward [[concepts/neural-networks|neural networks]].
