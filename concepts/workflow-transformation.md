# Workflow Transformation

Systematic reconfiguration of processes, decision flows, and execution structures to maximize efficiency, [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and adaptability. Central to modern operational shifts driven by [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and advanced automation frameworks.

## Key Mechanisms
- **Process Reengineering:** Fundamental redesign of workflows to eliminate redundancy, reduce latency, and leverage emerging computational capabilities.
- **Agent Delegation:** Offloading complex, context-dependent sub-tasks to specialized [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]].
- **Cost [[concepts/optimization-guide|Optimization Strategies]]:** Implementing tiered [[concepts/effort-levels|effort levels]] and [[concepts/savings-analysis|savings analysis]] for high-end LLM usage, such as [[entities/anthropic-claude]] [[concepts/claude-fable-5|Fable 5]], to reduce operational overhead while maintaining performance [[lab-notes/2026-07-06-Fable-5-Cost-Optimization-Effort-Levels-and-Savings-Anal|Fable 5 Cost Optimization: Effort Levels and Savings Analysis]].
- **Resource-Constrained Deployment:** Utilizing [[concepts/hardware-heavy-models|local LLMs]], GPU optimization, and [[concepts/ai-model-fine-tuning|parameter-efficient fine-tuning]] (e.g., [[concepts/lora-adapter|LoRA]]) for budget-aware [[concepts/agent-deployment|agent deployment]].
