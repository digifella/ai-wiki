# Personal Qualities

## Definition
Personal qualities are inherent or developed attributes of an individual that influence behavior, decision-making, and interpersonal dynamics. They serve as the foundation for [[concepts/leadership]] and [[concepts/on-the-job-performance|professional effectiveness]].

## Components
*   **Knowledge & [[concepts/experience|Experience]]**: Accumulated data and [[concepts/historical-context|historical context]].
*   **Interpretive Ability**: The capacity to synthesize [[concepts/factual-knowledge|facts]] into [[concepts/actionable-insights|actionable insights]].
*   **Character Traits**: [[concepts/integrity|Integrity]], [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and [[concepts/emotional-intelligence|emotional intelligence]].

## Application in Leadership
*   **Judgment Formation**: Judgment is defined as the ability to combine personal qualities with [[concepts/relevant-knowledge|relevant knowledge]] and experience to form [[concepts/opinions|opinions]] and make decisions.
*   **[[concepts/exemplary-leadership|Exemplary Leadership]]**: According to [[entities/sir-andrew-likierman|Sir Andrew Likierman]], judgment is "the core of exemplary leadership" [[lab-notes/2026-05-28-The-Elements-of-Good-Judgment|The Elements of Good Judgment]].
*   **Decision [[concepts/ambiguity|Ambiguity]]**: In [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where facts are assembled but no clear evidence supports a specific option, leaders rely on personal qualities to interpret evidence and guide choices.
