# Connectome

A comprehensive map of neural connections in the brain, serving as the structural basis for understanding brain function and connectivity.

## Key Developments

- **[[concepts/malecns-v10|MaleCNS v1.0]] [[concepts/deployment|Release]]**: [[entities/google|Google]] and [[entities/janelia|Janelia]] Research Campus have open-sourced the complete [[concepts/neural-wiring-diagram|connectome]] of a male fruit fly's brain and nerve cord.
- **Significance**: Represents a full "wiring diagram" of the organism, enabling detailed computational modeling and analysis of neural circuits.
- **Open Access**: The dataset is publicly available to facilitate broader scientific collaboration and replication.
