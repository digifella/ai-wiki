[[concepts/ethics|Ethics]] plays a crucial role in the development and deployment of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI) applications. It addresses issues ranging from data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] to [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] in [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes.

## Key Concepts

- **Bias and Fairness**: Ensuring that [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] do not perpetuate or exacerbate existing [[concepts/biases|biases]].
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]**: Protecting user information and ensuring [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with data [[concepts/secure|protection]] laws.
- **Transparency**: Making the workings of [[concepts/ai-algorithms|AI algorithms]] understandable to stakeholders.
- **Accountability**: Holding [[concepts/nodes|entities]] responsible for AI outcomes.

## Open-Weight AI Security Risks

Recent analyses highlight specific vulnerabilities associated with [[concepts/open-weight-ai|Open-Weight AI]] models, necessitating updated [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]] frameworks.

- **[[concepts/vulnerability|Vulnerability]] Prioritization**: The [[concepts/cisa|CISA]] has introduced a vulnerability prioritization model to address the unique risks posed by [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]], moving beyond traditional metrics like CVSS which may not adequately capture AI-specific threats.
- **Model-Specific Risks**: Discussions regarding models such as [[concepts/glm-52|GLM-5.2]] indicate that the [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] of [[concepts/open-source-weights|open weights]] introduces distinct [[concepts/security|security]] challenges, including potential misuse and lack of centralized control over model updates or patches.
- **Emerging Threats**: The landscape includes "vibe hunting" and other novel [[concepts/cybersecurity-threats|attack vectors]] that exploit the [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] and modifiability of open-weight architectures.
