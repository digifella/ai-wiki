# Emissions Reduction

Systematic strategies, technologies, and [[entities/public-policy|regulatory measures]] aimed at minimizing the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of pollutants and [[concepts/greenhouse-gases|greenhouse gases]] into the [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|atmosphere]]. Encompasses source reduction, capture technologies, and end-of-pipe treatment across industrial, automotive, and energy sectors.

## Key Mechanisms

- **Catalytic Conversion:** Post-combustion treatment using catalysts to facilitate oxidation and reduction reactions. Converts NOx, Carbon Monoxide, and Hydrocarbons into Nitrogen, Carbon Dioxide, and Water.
    - Central to automotive [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with Emission Standards and mobile source pollution mitigation.
    - [[concepts/chemical-engineering|Chemical engineering]] intervention characterized as high-impact and historically transformative, though often underrated in public discourse.
    - Utilization of precious [[concepts/metal|metals]] ([[concepts/platinum|Platinum]], Palladium, Rhodium) creates economic incentives for theft, introducing [[concepts/security|security]] vulnerabilities in emissions [[concepts/server-administration|infrastructure maintenance]].
- **[[concepts/workflow-enhancements|Process Optimization]]:** Increasing [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] to reduce fuel consumption and associated output.
- **Abatement Technologies:** Scrubbers, Filters, and [[concepts/co2-capture|Carbon Capture]] systems for stationary sources.
