# AI Collaboration

[[concepts/ai-agent-coordination|AI Collaboration]] is the practice of effectively partnering with [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to create synergistic workflows, moving beyond basic [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]] to build structured, intentional teamwork between humans and machines.

- **The Baseline Shift**: "Using AI" is no longer a differentiator on a resume; it is a baseline expectation, similar to [[entities/microsoft-word|Microsoft Word]] [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]].
- **Advanced Differentiation**: True advantage requires building four specific [[concepts/skills|skills]] on top of that baseline ([[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]]).

## Core Skills for AI Collaboration

- **The [[concepts/cockpit-rule|Cockpit Rule]]**: A mental model for deciding when to delegate tasks to AI, when to collaborate with it, and when to avoid it entirely ([[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]]'s "4 Skills to Get Ahead" framework).
- **Context Framing**: Structuring problems to enable AI to operate within precise conceptual boundaries.
- **[[concepts/iterative-refinement|Iterative Refinement]]**: Systematically improving AI outputs through structured [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] [[concepts/loops|loops]].
- **Hybrid [[concepts/decision-making|Decision-Making]]**: Combining AI insights with human judgment for critical outcomes.

> *Note: [[entities/jeff-su|Jeff Su]]'s video details four skills beyond basic AI usage. [[concepts/the-cockpit-rule|The Cockpit Rule]] is the foundational mental model for all others.*
