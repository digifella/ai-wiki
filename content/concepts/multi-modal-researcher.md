# Multi Modal Researcher

A Multi Modal Researcher is an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] built using [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini-25-models|Gemini 2.5]] [[concepts/models|models]] and orchestrated through the [[concepts/langgraph-framework|LangGraph framework]]. This tool is designed to conduct [[concepts/comprehensive-investigations|comprehensive investigations]] by leveraging [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]]—the ability to process and analyze [[concepts/text|text]], [[concepts/images|images]], and other data types simultaneously. The system combines the [[concepts/thinking-and-reasoning-capabilities|advanced reasoning]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] of [[entities/gemini-25|Gemini 2.5]] with [[concepts/langgraph|LangGraph]]'s [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]] to [[concepts/structure|structure]] complex research tasks.

## Architecture and Capabilities

The researcher operates by breaking down investigation tasks into structured workflows managed by LangGraph, which handles [[concepts/multi-agent-orchestration|agent coordination]] and state management. Gemini 2.5 provides the underlying intelligence for analyzing diverse input formats and synthesizing findings across different data modalities. This combination allows the [[entities/agent|agent]] to perform tasks that require understanding [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between text documents, visual content, and [[concepts/contextual-information|contextual information]] in a unified manner.

## Use Cases and Output

The tool is designed to generate [[concepts/various-outputs|various outputs]] from its investigations, ranging from summaries and reports to extracted insights and [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]]. By operating as an agent rather than a simple API call, the Multi Modal Researcher can iterate through research steps, refine queries, and validate findings across multiple sources and formats. This makes it suitable for complex research [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where multimodal analysis and autonomous investigation workflows provide advantages over traditional single-mode analysis.
