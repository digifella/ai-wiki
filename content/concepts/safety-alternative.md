A safety alternative is a product, process, or method that replaces a hazardous or unsafe option with a safer one while maintaining or improving functionality.

- **[[entities/smarter-scrubber|Smarter Scrubber]]**: A safer alternative to wire bristle grill brushes (which shed bristles into food), developed by [[entities/destin-sandlin|Destin Sandlin]] in a four-year U.S. manufacturing experiment. Documented in [Smarter Every Day video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=3ZTGwcHQfLY), the project demonstrated competitive [[concepts/domestic-manufacturing|domestic manufacturing]] while prioritizing safety. 2026 04 14 Destin manufacturing in [[entities/america|America]]
