# Login Credentials Verification

Login credentials verification is the process of authenticating user access to the Company Portal by validating company identification details against official records. The primary verification method uses the Australian Company Number (ACN), a unique identifier assigned to all registered companies in Australia by the Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC). This approach ensures that only authorized representatives of legitimate registered companies can access portal services.

## Verification Process

During the verification process, users provide their company's ACN along with associated credentials. The system cross-references this information against ASIC's official company registry to confirm the company's legitimacy and active registration status. Once the company details are validated, the system grants access to the requesting user, provided their individual credentials also meet security requirements. This two-step validation—verifying both the company and the individual—creates a security layer that protects the portal from unauthorized access while maintaining a streamlined onboarding experience for legitimate users.

## Purpose and Scope

Login credentials verification serves as the foundational security mechanism for the Company Portal, preventing unauthorized access while enabling rapid authentication for registered business representatives. The use of ACN-based verification aligns with Australian regulatory frameworks and ensures that portal access remains restricted to entities with legitimate business registration status.
