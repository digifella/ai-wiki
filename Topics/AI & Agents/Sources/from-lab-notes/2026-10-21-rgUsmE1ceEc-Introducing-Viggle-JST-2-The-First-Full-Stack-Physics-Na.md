## Introducing Viggle JST-2: The First Full-Stack Physics-Native World Model
**Clip title:** Introducing Viggle JST-2: The First Full-Stack Physics-Native World Model
**Author / channel:** Viggle AI
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rgUsmE1ceEc

### Summary
Sure, here is a summary of the video:

The video features Hang Chu, co-founder and CEO of Viggle, discussing the company’s four-year project to answer a single question: If the world we live in could be simulated, what would its architecture look like? While most video models only learn what the world looks like through pixels, Viggle built JST-2 to model what the world actually is using persistent 3D atoms, learned vectors, and physics-native tokens.

JST-2 is presented as the world's first full-stack, physics-native world foundation model capable of generating characters, motion, and scenes in about a second for under a cent. Chu explains that JST-2 worlds are real 3D, generated once and rendered locally in the browser from any angle without needing to re-run the model. 

The video demonstrates how JST-2 can change semi-efficiency in robotics, generating environments, people, and actions for scalable robot training. Chu notes that future versions like JST-3 will replace rule-based physics with learned physics. The video concludes by inviting viewers to try JST-2 for free at viggle.ai/jst2 and read more about its architecture and benchmarks on the company's research blog.
