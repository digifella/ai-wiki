# Kardashev Type II Civilization
A **Kardashev Type II** civilization is a hypothetical stage of technological advancement in which a species has the capability to [[concepts/harness|harness]] all of the energy radiated by its host star. The concept was introduced by Russian astronomer Nikolai Kardashev and serves as a measure for advanced civilizations' [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]].

## Key Aspects
- **Energy Utilization**: Harnesses 100% of the energy [[concepts/output|output]] of their parent star.
- **Technological [[concepts/capabilities|Capabilities]]**: Advanced space technology, including Dyson Spheres or other [[concepts/megastructures|large-scale engineering projects]] to capture stellar radiation.
- **Environmental Impact**: Minimal impact on Earth-like planets; utilizes resources from multiple celestial bodies within a planetary system.
