# Saturation adjustment

The process of increasing or decreasing the intensity and purity of colors within an image.

### Core Principles
- [[concepts/hue]]: The specific color wavelength or type.
- Saturation: The concentration, vividness, or purity of a color.
- Luminance: The perceived brightness or lightness of a color.

### Technical Application
- **Targeted HSL [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]]**: Utilizing the [[concepts/color-mixer]] panel in Adobe [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] to manipulate specific color channels (Hue, Saturation, and Luminance) independently.
- **Selective Saturation**: Implementing precision [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] to specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] rather than applying global saturation changes, allowing for more controlled color [[concepts/integrity|character]].
