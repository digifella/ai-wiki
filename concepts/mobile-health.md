# Mobile Health

**[[entities/mobile-health|Mobile Health]]** ([[concepts/mhealth|mHealth]]) refers to the practice of medicine and [[concepts/population-health|public health]] supported by [[concepts/portable-devices|mobile devices]], including mobile phones, patient monitoring devices, personal digital assistants, and other wireless devices. It encompasses the communication of health information, delivery of [[concepts/health|healthcare]] services, and collection of health data via mobile technologies.

## Core Components & Integration

*   **Definition**: Utilization of wireless technology and [[concepts/portable-computing|portable computing]] devices to deliver and support [[concepts/health-care|health services]].
*   **Integration with Ecosystems**:
	*   Deep integration with [[concepts/electronic-health-records]] (EHRs/EMRs) is critical for data [[concepts/continuity|continuity]].
	*   Synergistic relationship with [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|Remote Patient Monitoring]] (RPM) systems to enable real-time health tracking.
	*   Reliance on [[entities/wearable-devices|Wearable Technology]] for continuous data collection (vitals, activity, [[concepts/sleep|sleep]]).
*   **[[concepts/allied-health|Allied Health]] Perspective**:
	*   Supports [[concepts/ahps|allied health professionals]] in monitoring [[concepts/medical-outcomes|patient outcomes]] remotely.
	*   Facilitates interdisciplinary [[concepts/coordination|care coordination]] through shared data platforms.

## Technical Infrastructure & Data Flow

*   **Data Collection & Transmission**:
	*   Involves [[concepts/secure|secure]] data transmission from [[concepts/edge-devices|edge devices]] to central repositories.
	*   Requires robust [[concepts/technical-infrastructure|technical infrastructure]] to handle high-volume, low-latency data streams.
*   **[[concepts/security|Security]] Considerations**:
	*   Strict adherence to data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] regulations (e.g., [[concepts/hipaa|HIPAA]], [[concepts/gdpr|GDPR]]) is mandatory.
	*   Encryption and secure [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] protocols are essential for protecting [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]] in transit and at rest.

## Current State & Adoption

*   **[[concepts/adoption|Adoption]] Trends**: Increasing integration in [[concepts/chronic-disease-management|chronic disease management]] and post-acute care settings.
*   **[[concepts/data-integrity|Data Quality]]**: Challenges remain in ensuring the clinical validity and accuracy of data sourced from consumer-grade [[concepts/internet-of-things|wearables]].
*   **[[concepts/consulting|Consulting]] Insights**: Industry reports highlight the strategic [[concepts/value|importance]] of [[concepts/digital-health|mHealth]] in modernizing [[concepts/clinical-delivery|healthcare delivery]] [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Remote-Patient-Monitoring-and-Virtual-Care-A-Deep|Remote Patient Monitoring and Virtual Care  A Deep]].
