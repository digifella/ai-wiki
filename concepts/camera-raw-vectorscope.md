# Camera Raw VectorScope

The **[[concepts/mask-refinement|Camera Raw VectorScope]]** is a diagnostic tool within [[entities/adobe-camera-raw]] (ACR) used to analyze color distribution and ensure accurate skin [[concepts/tone|tone]] [[concepts/fat-rendering|rendering]]. It provides a scientific basis for color correction, moving beyond subjective [[concepts/visual-perception|visual perception]].

## Key Features & Updates

- **[[entities/camera-raw-184|ACR 18.4]]+ Integration**: The VectorScope is now a native feature in [[concepts/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] version 18.4 and newer, allowing real-time analysis of RGB data.
- **Skin Tone Accuracy**: Specifically designed to help photographers achieve perfectly accurate [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] by aligning skin data with the standard skin tone line on the scope.
- **Perception vs. Data**: Addresses the unreliability of human color perception by providing [[concepts/purpose|objective]] [[concepts/information-visualization|data visualization]] for [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] decisions.
