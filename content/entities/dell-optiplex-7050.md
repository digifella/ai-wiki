# Dell Optiplex 7050

The Dell Optiplex 7050 is a small form factor desktop computer commonly used in self-hosting and [[concepts/personal-cloud-server|personal cloud server]] setups. Its compact design and reliable performance make it suitable for [[concepts/running|running]] continuous services and [[concepts/software|applications]] from a home or small office environment.

## Use in Self-Hosting

The machine has gained [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] in self-hosting communities as a capable platform for hosting personal [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]], media servers, and network infrastructure. Its [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]] relative to performance makes it practical for [[concepts/247-operation|24/7 operation]], a key requirement for reliable [[concepts/home-server|home server]] deployments. The system can accommodate various Linux distributions and containerized applications typically used in such configurations.

## Hardware Considerations

The Optiplex 7050 offers sufficient processing power and [[concepts/expandability|expandability]] for moderate workloads. Its compact form factor allows for flexible placement in residential settings while maintaining adequate cooling for continuous operation. Users typically integrate such machines into broader personal infrastructure, including networking tools and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]]-focused applications like Tailscale for [[concepts/remote-access|remote access]].
