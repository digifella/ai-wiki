# Video Based Topic Investigation

Video Based Topic Investigation is a multimodal research tool designed to extract and analyze information directly from video content. Built using Google's Gemini 2.5 models and orchestrated through LangGraph, the system processes both visual and textual data simultaneously. This architecture allows for a more comprehensive analysis than traditional text-only research methods by capturing insights from visual context, spoken dialogue, and on-screen text.

The tool leverages the capabilities of Gemini 2.5 to interpret complex video formats, enabling researchers to investigate topics through raw video material. By integrating visual cues with audio transcripts, the system provides a holistic view of the source material. This approach is particularly useful for analyzing content where visual elements are critical to understanding the full context of the information presented.

LangGraph serves as the orchestration layer, managing the workflow and state transitions required to process multimodal inputs efficiently. This setup ensures that the analysis remains structured and scalable, allowing for the systematic extraction of key insights from lengthy or complex video datasets. The result is a research aid that bridges the gap between visual media and structured data analysis.
