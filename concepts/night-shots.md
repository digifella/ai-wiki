# Night Shots

Night Shots is a photography feature integrated into the Adobe Lightroom beta camera app, designed specifically for iPhone 13 Pro and later models. It enhances image capture in low-light environments by utilizing computational photography techniques to process multiple frames. This method aims to produce photographs with greater clarity and detail than standard smartphone night photography modes.

The feature leverages the advanced camera hardware available in supported devices to optimize performance. By combining data from multiple exposures, the app reduces noise and preserves dynamic range, allowing for sharper images in challenging lighting conditions. This approach distinguishes it from traditional single-exposure night modes by relying on software processing to refine the final output.

In addition to low-light capabilities, the beta app includes features for zoom and the removal of reflections from surfaces. These tools complement the Night Shots functionality by providing users with greater control over their composition and image quality. The integration of these features within the Lightroom ecosystem allows for a streamlined workflow from capture to post-processing.
