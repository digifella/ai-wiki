Gemini 2.5 Models

Gemini 2.5 is a multimodal AI model developed by Google that processes and generates text, images, and other content types. As a multimodal system, it accepts diverse input formats and produces outputs across multiple modalities, enabling reasoning and analysis across different data types simultaneously. This capability makes it suitable for complex tasks that require integration of information from various sources.

Application in Research Tools

Gemini 2.5 models serve as the computational foundation for multimodal researcher tools built on LangGraph, a framework for constructing AI agent workflows. In these applications, the model performs comprehensive investigations by analyzing documents, images, and other media to extract insights and support research tasks. The integration of Gemini 2.5 within LangGraph-based systems allows for structured, multi-step reasoning processes that can be orchestrated across different analytical tasks.
