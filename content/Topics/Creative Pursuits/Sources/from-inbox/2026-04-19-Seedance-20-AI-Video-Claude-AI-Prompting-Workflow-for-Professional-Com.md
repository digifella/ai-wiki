## Seedance 2.0 AI Video: [[entities/claude-mythos|Claude AI]] [[concepts/prompting|Prompting]] Workflow for Professional Commercials
**Clip title:** Seedance 2.0 First Impressions | Professional Results
**Author / channel:** Enovair
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZMfz0UI9cag

### [[concepts/summary|Summary]]
This video provides an in-depth review and [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] for Seedance 2.0, an advanced AI video model available on Higgsfield. The presenter demonstrates how to leverage this tool to create professional-looking product videos, sharing her honest first impressions, detailing her specific workflow involving Claude AI, and showcasing four real commercial examples she generated. The core message emphasizes that the quality of the output video is directly tied to the quality and detail of the input prompt.

The presenter's workflow centers around using a custom "Claude [[concepts/skill|Skill]]" she built, specifically designed as a "Seedance 2.0 Video Prompt Builder." Instead of manually crafting complex prompts every time, she describes her video concept and provides reference images to Claude. Claude then utilizes this custom skill, which acts like an instruction manual, to generate highly detailed, time-segmented video generation prompts. These ready-to-use prompts, often incorporating specific camera movements, sounds, and [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]], are then copied and pasted directly into the Seedance 2.0 interface on Higgsfield, alongside the reference images, to produce the final video.

Four commercial examples illustrate Seedance 2.0's capabilities. For a greens supplement powder, the model successfully created a UGC-[[concepts/style|style]] ad with accurate on-screen text, realistic sound effects (like stirring), and natural human movements, although the dialogue delivery was noted as slightly robotic. A premium kitchen knives commercial further impressed by accurately depicting fast movements like chopping and intricate product details, including the logo on the knife blades. The presenter highlighted the significant improvement in handling realistic hand movements, an area where previous AI video [[concepts/models|models]] often struggled.

Two more challenging examples demonstrated the model's advanced features. A luxury watch commercial, with a more intricate [[concepts/storytelling|storytelling]] concept, successfully captured the sophisticated ambiance, [[concepts/integrity|character]] actions (man exiting car, meeting companion), and product close-ups, complete with impressive music and sound [[concepts/design|design]]. However, the video quality was slightly stuttery, and it required three attempts to eliminate an extra hand in one shot. Finally, a track spikes shoe commercial, designed to test realistic [[concepts/physics|physics]] with an athlete exploding from starting blocks, yielded remarkably accurate foot movements, sound, and a precise rendering of the product from the reference image. This particular output was deemed superior to other [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]], despite a minor text error in the company name at the end.

In conclusion, the presenter highly recommends Seedance 2.0, identifying it as the best [[entities/sora|video generation model]] she has tested due to its ability to handle complex storytelling, closely adhere to detailed prompts, accurately interpret reference images, and produce impressive sound design. While the current 720p output [[concepts/solution|resolution]] is a limitation, the model's overall performance and realistic physics handling mark a significant advancement in [[concepts/ai-video-automation|AI video generation]], offering powerful tools for creating engaging product and marketing content.
