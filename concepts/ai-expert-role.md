# AI Expert Role

A strategic position enabling organizations to bridge the **[[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI Skills]] Gap** by translating technical AI capabilities into business value. Focuses on [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], implementation, and [[concepts/organizational-change|change management]]—not just technical [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]].

## Core Responsibilities
- Identify business problems solvable via AI (e.g., [[concepts/workflow-automation|process automation]], [[concepts/scientific-evidence-based-decision-making|data-driven decisions]])
- Translate technical AI outputs into actionable strategies for non-technical teams
- Train and support cross-functional teams in [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-tool|AI tool]] usage (beyond [[concepts/coding|coding]]/data [[concepts/science|science]] roles)
- [[concepts/motivation|Drive]] **AI Adoption Strategy** through collaboration with **Business Transformation** leaders

## Key Mindset Shift
> *"Transition from resisting AI to embracing it as a growth catalyst."*
> (Per 2026 04 14 [[entities/enovair|Enovair]] AI views for 2026)

## Strategic Value
- Addresses critical **AI [[concepts/skills-gap|Skills Gap]]** in companies investing in AI but lacking internal [[concepts/expertise|expertise]]
- Positions role as essential for **Organizational Agility** in AI-driven markets
- Enables scalable AI deployment without requiring full technical [[concepts/specialization|specialization]]
