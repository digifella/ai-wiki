# Virtual Reality

Virtual Reality (VR) refers to the simulated experience that can be similar to or completely different from the real world. It encompasses hardware (headsets, controllers) and software environments that enable immersive spatial interaction.

## Key Developments & Tools

*   **[[entities/gods-eye-view-gev|God's Eye View (GEV)]]:** An open-source [[concepts/3d-spatial-intelligence|3D spatial intelligence]] simulator that has gained significant traction for its ability to model and visualize spatial data.

    *   Demonstrates practical applications for spatial intelligence in VR contexts.
