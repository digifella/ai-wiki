# Marketing Audits

A marketing audit is a systematic, comprehensive examination of an [[concepts/organization|organization]]'s marketing environment, strategies, objectives, and activities. It evaluates how effectively marketing resources are deployed, whether current approaches align with business goals, and identifies gaps or inefficiencies in marketing performance. Marketing audits typically assess brand positioning, customer acquisition and retention, competitive positioning, [[concepts/pricing|pricing]] strategy, promotion effectiveness, and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] channels.

The scope of a marketing audit can [[concepts/range|range]] from examining specific campaigns or channels to conducting a full organizational review. Traditional audits rely on manual data collection, stakeholder interviews, and analyst review of marketing materials and [[concepts/performance-data-gathering|performance metrics]]. This process is labor-intensive and typically requires significant time investment before actionable insights emerge.

## AI-Enabled Audits

Recent developments in [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] have begun automating and accelerating marketing audit workflows. Platforms like [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] enable rapid analysis of marketing materials, performance data, and strategic positioning without requiring extensive manual [[concepts/preparation|preparation]]. These tools can generate audit frameworks, analyze content effectiveness, identify messaging inconsistencies across channels, and produce comprehensive reports. By automating routine analysis tasks, AI-enabled audits [[entities/make|make]] comprehensive marketing assessment more accessible to organizations that previously lacked resources for formal audit processes. This democratization has expanded the use of marketing audits beyond large enterprises to smaller businesses and marketing teams with limited analytical capacity.
