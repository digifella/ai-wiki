# Simple Analogies

Simple analogies are a [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] technique used to help [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] understand and reason about complex concepts by comparing them to more familiar or straightforward ideas. In the context of AI [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], analogies serve as a bridge between abstract problems and concrete examples that the model can more readily process. By mapping unfamiliar domains onto well-understood domains, analogies can improve an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]]'s ability to generate relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]] and follow logical chains of reasoning.

## Application in AI Prompting

When prompting AI agents, simple analogies work by establishing a parallel [[concepts/structure|structure]] between a target concept and a source concept. Rather than explaining a technical process in abstract terms, a user might compare it to an everyday activity—for example, explaining how an algorithm filters data "like a sieve separating sand from pebbles." This approach can lead to more coherent outputs and help [[concepts/agents|agents]] reason through problems by leveraging patterns from the source domain.

## Limitations and Effectiveness

The effectiveness of simple analogies depends on how well the source domain maps onto the target domain. While analogies can accelerate understanding and reasoning, they also risk oversimplifying complex systems or introducing misleading implications if the parallel breaks down. AI agents using analogies must navigate the inherent gaps between source and target domains, which means analogies work best as supplementary reasoning aids rather than as complete [[concepts/explanations|explanations]].
