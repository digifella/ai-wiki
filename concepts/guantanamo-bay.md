# International Law

The system of rules, norms, and standards generally recognized as binding between [[entities/sovereign-state|sovereign states]] and other international actors. It governs interactions in areas such as diplomacy, war, human rights, and trade.

## Core Components
- **Treaties & Conventions:** Written agreements between states, ranging from bilateral pacts to multilateral frameworks like the [[concepts/un-charter|UN Charter]].
- **Customary International Law:** Practices accepted as law through consistent state behavior and *opinio juris* (belief that the action is required by law).
- **General Principles:** Fundamental legal concepts recognized by civilized nations, often used to fill gaps in treaty or customary law.
