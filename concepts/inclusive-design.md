# Equity in Design

[[concepts/accessibility|Inclusive design]] is a creative approach that prioritizes usability across diverse populations. Rather than designing for an idealized "average" user, inclusive design acknowledges that people have varying abilities, backgrounds, cultural contexts, and technological access. This approach treats diversity not as an edge case but as a central consideration throughout the [[concepts/design-thinking|design process]].

## Core Principles

Inclusive design typically emphasizes accessibility, flexibility, and simplicity. Accessible design ensures that people with disabilities—whether visual, hearing, motor, or cognitive—can use products and systems effectively. Flexible design accommodates different preferences and methods of interaction, while straightforward design reduces barriers created by unnecessary complexity.

## Equity, Stakeholders, and Digital Readiness

Equity in [[concepts/digital-health]] requires addressing the structural and contextual factors that create disparities in technology access and usage. Key considerations include:

*   **Stakeholder Analysis**: Identifying all parties affected by [[concepts/digital-interventions|digital interventions]], including patients, clinicians, and [[concepts/allied-health|allied healthcare]] students, to ensure diverse perspectives are integrated into the design process.
*   **[[concepts/digital-readiness|Digital Readiness]]**: Assessing the [[concepts/technical-infrastructure|technical infrastructure]], literacy, and resources available to users to prevent exacerbating the [[concepts/digital-divide]].
*   **Human-Centered Context**: Recognizing that digital [[concepts/health|health]] tools must adapt to the "human side" of care, ensuring that technological solutions do not overlook socioeconomic or [[concepts/cultural-barriers|cultural barriers]] to [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].
*   **Resource Accessibility**: Ensuring that digital tools for [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] are accessible to those with limited technological [[concepts/excellence|proficiency]] or resource constraints.
