# Homer's Enemy

**"Homer's Enemy"** is the twelfth episode of [[entities/the-simpsons]]' ninth season, originally aired on January 7, 1998. Directed by [[entities/david|David]] Silverman and written by [[entities/chef-john|John]] Swartzwelder, the episode is widely regarded as a critical high point of the series, notable for its deconstruction of the show's comedic formula and its exploration of workplace dynamics through the lens of [[concepts/frank-grimes]].

## Overview
The plot centers on [[concepts/frank-grimes]], a competent, hardworking accountant who is transferred to the Springfield Nuclear Power Plant. Grimes becomes increasingly frustrated by the incompetence and luck of [[concepts/homer-simpson]], leading to a psychological breakdown. The episode serves as a meta-commentary on the absurdity of [[concepts/the-simpsons|the Simpsons]] universe, contrasting realistic consequences with the [[concepts/cartoon|cartoon]]'s typical lack of stakes.
