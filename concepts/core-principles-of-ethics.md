# Core Principles of Ethics

Foundational frameworks guiding moral [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and professional conduct, particularly within [[concepts/health-care|Healthcare]] and Digital Systems.

## Key Principles
- **Autonomy**: [[concepts/respect|Respect]] for individual [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] capacity.
- **Beneficence**: Obligation to act in the best interest of the patient/subject.
- **Non-maleficence**: Duty to do no harm.
- **Justice**: [[entities/meta-fair-lab|Fair]] distribution of benefits and risks.
- **Veracity**: Commitment to [[concepts/honesty|truthfulness]] and [[concepts/opacity|transparency]].
