# Gemini Diffusion Model

[[entities/gemini-diffusion|Gemini Diffusion]] is an experimental generative model developed by [[concepts/google-search|Google]] that utilizes a diffusion-based architecture for image generation. It serves as a technical exploration of diffusion methods as a core approach within the broader Gemini family of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], representing Google's investment in this technology as an alternative or complementary method to other generative architectures.

Google has opened access to the model through an experimental access program, making it available to select users and developers. This limited rollout strategy allows for controlled testing and evaluation of the model's capabilities before any potential broader [[concepts/deployment|release]]. The initiative highlights Google's ongoing efforts to integrate and refine diffusion techniques within its larger ecosystem of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools.
