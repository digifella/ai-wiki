# Llama 3

Llama 3 is an open-source large language model developed by Meta, released in 2024 as the third major iteration of the Llama model family. The release includes both base models designed for text generation and instruction-tuned variants optimized for conversational and reasoning tasks. Meta made the model weights publicly available to support research and development within the AI community, aiming to foster innovation in natural language processing.

The model is distributed under Meta's Llama Community License and Llama Research License. These agreements permit both non-commercial research and commercial use, subject to specific conditions regarding safety and responsible deployment. This licensing structure distinguishes it from fully unrestricted open-source models while remaining more permissive than many proprietary alternatives, encouraging broad adoption across academic and industrial sectors.

Technical specifications for Llama 3 include multiple parameter sizes, catering to different computational requirements and use cases. The architecture leverages advancements in training data quality and scale compared to its predecessors, resulting in improved performance on standard benchmarks for language understanding and generation. The model supports a context window that allows for processing longer sequences of text, enhancing its utility for complex tasks requiring extensive context retention.
