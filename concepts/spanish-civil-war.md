# Spanish Civil War

The Spanish Civil War (1936–1939) was a major conflict that pitted the Republican government against the Nationalist rebels, led by General Francisco Franco. The war resulted in the establishment of a dictatorship that lasted until Franco's death in 1975.

## Cultural and Architectural Impact

The conflict had profound effects on Spain's cultural heritage and architectural projects, many of which were interrupted or destroyed during the fighting.

- **Interruption of Major Projects**: The war halted numerous construction efforts, including those initiated during the Second Spanish Republic.
- **Destruction of Heritage**: Significant historical sites and modernist works suffered damage or destruction during the Spanish Civil War.
- **Sagrada Familia Context**: The construction of [[concepts/antoni-gaudí]]'s Sagrada Familia in Barcelona faced severe disruptions due to the war. The workshop was looted, and progress stalled significantly.
- **Long-term Reconstruction**: The cathedral's completion timeline was extended dramatically, spanning over a century. Recent analyses highlight the 144-year journey from Gaudí's initial vision to modern reconstruction efforts.
