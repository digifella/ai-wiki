# Social Media Carousels

Social media carousels are a multi-slide content format available on major platforms including Instagram, LinkedIn, Facebook, and TikTok. Users create carousels by uploading a series of images or slides that viewers navigate sequentially. Each carousel typically contains between 2 and 10 slides, though specific platform limits vary. The format allows creators to present multiple related images or pieces of information within a single post.

## Navigation and Platform Variations

On mobile devices, viewers advance through carousel slides by swiping horizontally, while desktop users click navigation arrows or buttons to progress through the content. Different platforms implement the carousel format with varying design specifications and capabilities. Some platforms emphasize image-based carousels, while others support mixed media types or text overlays on individual slides.

## Common Uses

Carousels are used across multiple contexts including educational content, product showcases, step-by-step tutorials, storytelling, and collection presentations. The format's sequential nature makes it suitable for content that benefits from narrative progression or comparative imagery. Creators often use carousels to increase engagement, as the multi-slide format typically encourages viewers to spend more time interacting with individual posts compared to single-image content.
