# Low Vram Requirements

Low VRAM requirements denote the capacity of AI video generation models to operate efficiently on consumer-grade hardware with significantly reduced video memory consumption. This optimization allows models such as LTX-2 to function on GPUs equipped with 8GB, 12GB, or 16GB of VRAM. This represents a substantial reduction from the 24GB, 40GB, or larger memory capacities historically necessary for running cutting-edge AI systems.

The reduction in computational barriers has made local AI video production accessible to a broader user base. By enabling high-performance generation on standard consumer graphics cards, developers have lowered the financial and technical entry costs for creating AI-generated content. This shift supports the growth of the open-source AI agent ecosystem by allowing individuals to run complex models without relying on expensive cloud infrastructure or enterprise-grade hardware.

Efficiency improvements in model architecture and inference techniques are central to achieving these lower memory footprints. These optimizations ensure that complex video generation tasks remain feasible on widely available hardware, fostering greater adoption of local AI agents. As a result, the barrier to entry for experimenting with and deploying open-source video models has decreased, promoting wider participation in the development and usage of local AI technologies.
