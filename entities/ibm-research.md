# IBM Research

IBM Research is the research division of IBM that conducts work across computing, [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], and related technologies. The division develops both proprietary research and [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tools for use by the broader technology community.

## Docling Toolkit

IBM Research developed [[concepts/docling|Docling]], an open-source toolkit designed to improve [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] workflows in [[entities/ai|artificial intelligence]] applications. The toolkit addresses the challenge of efficiently extracting and processing content from various document formats, enabling better integration of document data into [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]].
