# Cooking Time

Duration and thermal trajectory applied to food substrates to achieve target physicochemical states, including protein denaturation, starch gelatinization, Maillard browning, and leavening expansion.

## Governing Variables
- **Thermal Input:** Intensity, mode (conduction/convection/radiation), and uniformity.
- **Substrate Properties:** Density, [[concepts/moisture-content|moisture content]], surface-area-to-volume ratio, thermal conductivity.
- **Reaction Kinetics:** Activation energy thresholds, leavening gas generation rates, enzymatic degradation.
- **[[concepts/phase|Phase]] Changes:** Ice melting, water evaporation, fat rendering, coagulation points.
