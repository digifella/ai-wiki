### Summary
In this video, Brandon Thomas, CEO of Artcraft, discusses the concept of open-sourcing popular software apps and the internet as a whole. He highlights how the early internet was characterized by open collaboration and accessibility, which has since given way to extraction-based, closed-source models. Artcraft aims to reverse this trend by building 100% open-source alternatives to popular creative software like Photoshop, Illustrator, and Premiere. 

Thomas explains that Artcraft's approach involves leveraging AI to rapidly recreate and improve upon existing applications, making them more accessible and user-friendly. By open-sourcing not just the desktop apps, but also the infrastructure and data models, Artcraft empowers developers and creators to collaborate and build their own tools. This community-driven model has seen rapid growth, with thousands of contributors and millions of downloads.

The video concludes with a call to action for developers and engineers to join Artcraft in its mission to open source the entire tech ecosystem. Thomas emphasizes that AI can be a powerful tool for recreating and decentralizing software, giving users back ownership and control over their digital tools.
