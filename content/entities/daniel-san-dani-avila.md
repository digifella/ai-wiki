# Daniel San Dani Avila

Daniel San, known online as @Dani_Avila, is a content creator focused on demonstrating practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] platform. His work centers on showing how [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s capabilities can be accessed and utilized through mobile devices, particularly smartphones, making the technology more accessible to a broader audience.

## Content and Focus

San's content includes comprehensive updates on Claude Code features and community-developed tools built around the platform. His video demonstrations highlight functional applications and real-world [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] for Claude's [[concepts/coding|coding]] capabilities, with particular emphasis on optimizing API costs and mobile [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]]. This work contributes to the broader ecosystem of Claude-related educational content and tool development.
