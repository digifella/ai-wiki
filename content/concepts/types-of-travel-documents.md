# Types Of Travel Documents

Travel documents are official credentials issued by governments that authorize citizens to cross international borders and establish their identity and citizenship status during travel. These documents serve as fundamental components of [[concepts/security|security]] infrastructure, enabling both individual mobility and state [[concepts/power|control]] over border security. The primary function of travel documents is to verify that a traveler is who they claim to be and has permission to enter or exit a country.

## Turkey eVisas

[[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]] offers electronic visa (eVisa) services as an alternative to traditional visa [[concepts/software|applications]] processed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] embassies or consulates. Turkish eVisas are digital travel authorizations obtained through an online application system, allowing eligible travelers to receive approval electronically without the need for physical documentation submission or in-person interviews. This streamlined process reduces administrative burden for both travelers and Turkish immigration authorities.

The Turkish eVisa system accommodates various categories of travel, including tourism, business, and transit purposes. Applicants must provide biographical information, passport details, and supporting documentation through the online portal. Once approved, the eVisa is electronically linked to the applicant's passport number, and travelers present this digital [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] along with their physical passport upon arrival at Turkish borders. The validity period and permitted duration of stay vary depending on the specific visa category granted.
