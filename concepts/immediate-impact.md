# Immediate Impact

**Definition:** The measurable, short-term consequences of an action, decision, or technology deployment. In strategic contexts, it refers to the visible shift in state or outcome that occurs within the immediate horizon, distinct from long-term Strategic [[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]] or legacy effects.

## Core Principles

- **Visibility:** Immediate impact must be perceptible to stakeholders quickly to maintain momentum and [[concepts/trust|trust]].
- **[[concepts/systems|Feedback Loops]]:** Rapid results enable faster [[concepts/iteration|iteration]]. Delayed impact obscures causality.
- **Resistance Management:** High immediate impact often triggers [[concepts/resistance-to-change]]; mitigating this requires addressing emotional and practical fears simultaneously.

## Contextual Application

### Healthcare & AI Integration
The [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] in clinical settings exemplifies the tension between high potential impact and human resistance.

- **Fear as a Barrier:** As noted in [[lab-notes/2026-05-30-AI-Adoption-in-Healthcare-Understanding-Fear-and-Increme|AI Adoption in Healthcare: Understanding Fear and Incremental Progress]], fear of AI is often rooted in "prehistoric" anxieties about obsolescence and loss of control.
- **[[concepts/incremental-progress|Incremental Progress]]:** Immediate impact in this sector is best achieved through small, non-threatening integrations rather than disruptive overhauls. Dr. [[entities/nick|Nick]] van Terheyden argues that acknowledging fear while demonstrating tangible, immediate utility is key.
- **Trust Building:** Immediate positive [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] from [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] (e.g., diagnostic support) builds the trust necessary for deeper, long-term integration.

### General Strategy
- **Quick Wins:** Prioritize initiatives with high immediate impact to [[concepts/secure|secure]] buy-in for longer-term projects.
- **Metric Selection:** Define KPIs that capture immediate shifts (e.g., user engagement, initial efficiency gains) rather than solely focusing on ROI.
