# Europa subsurface ocean

## Overview
The Europa subsurface ocean is a global layer of [[concepts/liquid-water|liquid water]] beneath the icy crust of Jupiter's moon Europa. It is a primary target in the search for [[concepts/extraterrestrial-life|extraterrestrial life]] due to potential hydrothermal activity and chemical energy sources.

## Exploration Challenges
Accessing the subsurface ocean remains a significant [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] hurdle. Key issues include:
- **Ice [[concepts/cli|Shell]] Penetration:** Drilling through kilometers of ice without contaminating the ocean or losing equipment [[concepts/honesty|integrity]].
- **Thermal Constraints:** Managing [[concepts/heat-dissipation|heat dissipation]] in the extreme cold of the Jovian system.
- **Communication Latency:** Signal delays between [[entities/earth|Earth]] and the Jovian system complicate real-time control of subsurface probes.
