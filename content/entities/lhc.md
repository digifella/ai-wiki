# LHC

The LHC (Large Hadron Collider) is the world's highest-energy particle accelerator, designed to test the predictions of the [[concepts/standard-model]] and explore the fundamental building blocks of [[concepts/particle-physics]].

## Key Research & Experiments
- **[[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]]**: A primary detector used to study high-energy collisions and search for new physical phenomena.
- **[[concepts/quark-substructure|Quark Compositeness]]**: Ongoing investigation into whether Quarks are truly [[concepts/elementary-particles|elementary particles]] or if they possess an internal Substructure.
- [[entities/lhc-cms-experiment|LHC CMS]] Experiment Tests for [[concepts/quark-substructure|Quark Substructure]]: Recent experimental tests analyzing the possibility of quarks having a more complex internal composition.
