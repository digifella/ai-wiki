# Well Being

Well-being refers to a state of physical, mental, and emotional health characterized by life satisfaction, psychological functioning, and overall quality of life. It encompasses both subjective experiences of happiness and contentment, as well as objective measures of health and social functioning. Well-being is increasingly recognized as a multidimensional construct that extends beyond the absence of illness to include positive psychological and social dimensions.

## Dimensions of Well-Being

Well-being typically includes several interconnected components: physical health, mental health, emotional resilience, social relationships, and a sense of purpose or meaning. Different frameworks may emphasize different aspects, but most agree that sustainable well-being requires attention to multiple life domains rather than focusing on any single factor in isolation.

## Well-Being in Adolescents

Research on adolescent well-being has identified specific behavioral habits and practices that contribute to improved outcomes during this developmental period. These include regular physical activity, adequate sleep, social connection, engagement in meaningful activities, and stress management practices. Understanding which habits and practices most effectively support well-being in younger populations helps inform health promotion efforts and interventions tailored to adolescent needs and circumstances.
