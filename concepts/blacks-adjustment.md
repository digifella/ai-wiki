# Blacks Adjustment

The Blacks adjustment is a tonal control slider located within the Tone panel of the Develop module in Adobe Lightroom Classic. It specifically targets the deepest shadow areas of an image, effectively modifying the black point to define the darkest values in a photograph. This tool ensures that pure black is represented accurately while preserving necessary detail in the extreme shadows, preventing the loss of texture in the darkest regions.

Unlike the Shadows slider, which affects a broader range of mid-dark tones, the Blacks adjustment operates on a narrower tonal range. In landscape processing, this distinction allows photographers to deepen the darkest areas for increased contrast without inadvertently brightening the mid-tones or lifting the overall exposure of the shadow regions.

Proper use of this slider helps establish a solid foundation for the image's dynamic range. By setting the black point accurately, editors can prevent images from appearing flat or muddy, ensuring that the darkest elements provide visual weight without clipping into pure black where detail is required.
