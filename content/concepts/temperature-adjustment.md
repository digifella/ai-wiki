# Temperature Adjustment

Temperature adjustment refers to the process of correcting or modifying the [[concepts/color-temperature|color temperature]] of digital [[concepts/images|images]], typically to compensate for lighting conditions or achieve a desired aesthetic. In photography and image editing, temperature is measured on the Kelvin scale, with cooler (bluer) tones at higher values and warmer (more orange/red) tones at lower values. Adjusting temperature is one of the fundamental color correction tasks in post-processing workflows.

## Adobe Photoshop Implementation

Adobe's [[concepts/adjustment-layers|Color and Vibrance tool]] in [[entities/adobe-photoshop-2025|Photoshop 2025]], as covered in [[entities/blake-rudis|Blake Rudis]]'s [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]], provides photographers with refined controls for temperature adjustment alongside saturation and vibrance modifications. This tool allows non-destructive editing of color temperature within Photoshop's native environment, complementing similar capabilities found in Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] and Lightroom Classic. The integration of temperature controls with related color tools enables more sophisticated [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] and correction in a single interface.

## Related Workflows

Temperature adjustment is often used in conjunction with other color correction techniques, including [[concepts/color-cast-correction|white balance correction]] and selective color [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]]. Many modern [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] [[concepts/software|applications]]—including Adobe Lightroom Classic, [[entities/snapseed|Snapseed]], and Adobe Camera Raw—offer automated or AI-assisted temperature adjustment features alongside [[concepts/manual-controls|manual controls]], allowing editors to quickly correct problematic lighting conditions or creatively warm or cool images as needed.
