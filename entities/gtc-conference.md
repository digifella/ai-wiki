# Gtc Conference

The GTC Conference is an annual technology event organized by NVIDIA that convenes industry professionals, researchers, and developers to examine advancements in artificial intelligence, machine learning, and computing infrastructure. The gathering typically features keynote presentations, technical workshops, and panel discussions designed to address the needs of both enterprise and research computing environments.

In recent years, the conference has placed increasing emphasis on the development and deployment of AI agents within enterprise applications. A significant focus of recent programming has been on NVIDIA's NemoClaw and OpenClaw frameworks, which are presented as secure solutions for building and managing AI agents. These sessions highlight the technical architecture and security protocols necessary for integrating such agents into professional workflows.

The event serves as a primary platform for demonstrating NVIDIA's latest tools for secure AI implementation. By showcasing frameworks like NemoClaw and OpenClaw, the conference underscores the company's strategy to support enterprise-grade security standards in the rapidly evolving field of artificial intelligence.
