# Deakin University

Deakin University is an Australian public research university located in the state of Victoria. It operates multiple campuses across the region and functions as a comprehensive institution providing both undergraduate and postgraduate programs across a diverse range of academic disciplines.

The university maintains a significant research focus on digital health and mental health. It has developed particular expertise in examining the intersection of digital technologies with psychological wellbeing and health outcomes.

Prof Martin Held is associated with the institution.
