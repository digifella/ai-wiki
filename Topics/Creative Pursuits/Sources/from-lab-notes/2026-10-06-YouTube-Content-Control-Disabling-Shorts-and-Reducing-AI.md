## YouTube Content Control: Disabling Shorts and Reducing AI Videos
**Clip title:** Disable YouTube Shorts AND AI Videos With This Simple Trick
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Trevor Nace
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=gXtjlubIVgQ

### Summary
This video provides a practical guide for users looking to customize their [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] [[concepts/experience|experience]] by reducing or eliminating two common types of content they might dislike: YouTube Shorts and AI-generated videos. The presenter acknowledges the widespread use of YouTube but highlights these two specific areas as frequent sources of user dissatisfaction.

For YouTube Shorts, the video outlines a straightforward method to disable them. Users can open the YouTube app, navigate to their "You" tab, then access "Settings" via the gear icon. Within "Time management," there's a "Shorts feed limit" option, which can be set to "0 minutes." The presenter demonstrates that this effectively removes Shorts from the main feed and prevents them from playing even when attempting to access the Shorts tab, instead displaying a "You reached your Shorts feed limit" message. This setting offers a direct way to control the presence of Shorts.

Addressing AI-generated videos, the process is slightly more nuanced, as YouTube's flagging of such content is still evolving. For AI Shorts, which sometimes feature a subtle "AI" label, users can tap the three-dot menu, select "Not interested," and choose a [[concepts/purpose|reason]] like "misleading." This action helps signal preferences to YouTube's [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]], reducing similar content. However, for longer-form AI videos, which may use AI voiceovers or [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]], direct "not interested" options are often unavailable.

In these cases, the video suggests an indirect approach: giving a "thumbs down" to AI-suspected videos. While not a direct block, this [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] mechanism still teaches the YouTube [[concepts/algorithm|algorithm]] about your preferences, potentially leading to fewer AI videos being recommended in the future. The presenter [[concepts/notes|notes]] that distinguishing [[concepts/ai-content-creation|AI-generated content]] can be tricky, as some channels fully embrace AI while others might integrate only certain AI elements, and flagging practices on YouTube are constantly changing. Ultimately, the video empowers users to actively shape their content feed on the platform.
