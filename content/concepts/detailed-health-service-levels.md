# Detailed Health Service Levels

The [[concepts/proposal-assembler|Proposal Assembler]] documents a [[concepts/canvas|structured framework]] for categorising [[concepts/health|health]] services [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] different levels of complexity and scope. This framework enables consistent classification of health interventions, from basic [[concepts/preventive-care|preventive care]] through to specialized treatment services. The levels serve as a reference system for planning, budgeting, and resource allocation within health systems.

## Service Level Classification

Health services within the Proposal Assembler are organized hierarchically to reflect both the intensity of care required and the infrastructure needed to deliver services. Each level builds upon foundational elements, with higher levels typically requiring greater [[concepts/specialization|specialization]], technical capability, and trained personnel. This [[concepts/structure|structure]] supports both primary care delivery at community level and advanced interventions in specialized facilities.

## Application in Planning

The detailed service levels function as a planning tool for health authorities and proposal developers. By clearly delineating what constitutes each service level, the framework allows stakeholders to assess service gaps, identify priorities for expansion or improvement, and allocate resources according to [[concepts/population-health|population health]] needs. The classification also facilitates comparison across different health proposals and helps ensure that proposed interventions align with existing service infrastructure.
