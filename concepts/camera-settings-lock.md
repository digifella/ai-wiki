# Camera Settings Lock

Camera Settings Lock is a feature found on [[entities/fujifilm|Fujifilm]] [[concepts/digital-cameras|digital cameras]] that prevents accidental adjustment of specific camera controls during shooting. By selectively locking desired settings, photographers can maintain [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] and avoid unwanted changes that might occur through unintended [[entities/contact|contact]] with [[concepts/buttons|buttons]], dials, or menu controls. This is particularly valuable in dynamic shooting environments where the camera may be jostled or where muscle [[concepts/memory|memory]] might lead to inadvertent [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Functionality

The feature allows users to lock individual controls or groups of controls, such as the [[concepts/aperture|aperture]] ring, [[concepts/shutter-speed|shutter speed]] dial, or [[concepts/iso-settings|ISO sensitivity]]. This [[concepts/customization|customization]] ensures that critical [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] parameters remain fixed while other settings can still be modified as needed. The lock mechanism is typically engaged through the camera’s custom menu, providing a straightforward way to [[concepts/secure|secure]] specific configurations without disabling the entire interface.

## Use Cases

This function is especially useful for photographers who rely on consistent [[concepts/camera-settings|exposure settings]] across a series of shots, such as in landscape or architectural photography. It also benefits those using [[concepts/manual-focus|manual focus]] lenses or specific white balance presets who wish to prevent accidental shifts in focus or color temperature. By securing these settings, the feature reduces the [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] associated with monitoring dial positions, allowing the photographer to focus more on [[concepts/writing|composition]] and timing.
