# Instagram Carousel Design

Instagram carousel designs are multi-slide posts that allow users to swipe through sequential images or graphics. Typically containing between 2 and 10 slides per post, carousels have become a standard format for storytelling, step-by-step tutorials, before-and-after comparisons, and product showcases. The carousel format encourages extended engagement by requiring users to actively interact with content rather than passively scrolling past a single image, which can result in increased time spent viewing the post.

## Design Principles

Effective carousel designs maintain visual consistency across slides while using progression to guide the viewer's experience. Each slide should be legible on mobile devices and include text sparingly to avoid overwhelming viewers. Color schemes, typography, and branding elements should remain cohesive throughout the sequence, while visual hierarchy ensures the most important information is immediately apparent.

## Creating Carousels with AI and Design Tools

Claude AI can assist in planning carousel narratives, generating copy for individual slides, and outlining visual concepts before design work begins. Integration with Canva allows creators to rapidly prototype designs using templates, customize layouts, and export slides in Instagram's required dimensions. This workflow enables iterative refinement of both conceptual and visual elements before posting.
