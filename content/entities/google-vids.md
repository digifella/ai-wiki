# Google Vids

Google Vids is an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]]-powered [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] tool integrated into the [[entities/google-workspace|Google Workspace]] suite of productivity [[concepts/software|applications]]. The product leverages [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] to streamline video production, enabling users to create videos more efficiently than traditional manual methods.

## Features and Integration

As part of Google Workspace, Google Vids is designed to work alongside other [[concepts/google-search|Google]] [[concepts/productivity-tools|productivity tools]], allowing for seamless workflow integration. The tool utilizes generative AI technology to assist with various aspects of video creation, reducing the technical barriers typically associated with video production.

## Context within Google AI Products

Google Vids represents one of several AI product initiatives across Google's ecosystem. It is positioned alongside other AI-enhanced offerings including [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], Google Search enhancements, and [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] as part of Google's broader strategy to integrate artificial intelligence into user-facing products and services.
