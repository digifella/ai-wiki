# Star Ratings

Star ratings in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] are a fundamental organizational tool for managing and prioritizing photos within your [[concepts/catalog|catalog]]. They provide a quick, visual method to mark [[concepts/images|images]] with quality assessments, ranging from one to five [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], allowing photographers to efficiently sort and filter their work during the culling and editing process.

## Implementation in Workflow

Star ratings are typically applied during the initial review [[concepts/phase|phase]] after importing images into your [[concepts/lightroom-catalog|Lightroom catalog]]. By assigning ratings consistently—for example, using five stars for keeper images, three stars for images requiring further consideration, and one star for obvious rejects—you create a structured system for managing large volumes of photos. This approach becomes particularly valuable when working with extensive shoots where manual review of every image would be time-consuming.

## Integration with Catalog Management

Within the broader context of mastering your Lightroom catalog, star ratings work alongside other organizational features such as [[concepts/flags|flags]] and [[concepts/color-labels|color labels]] to create a comprehensive filtering system. When combined with [[concepts/smart-collections|smart collections]] and search functionality, star ratings enable you to quickly locate and access specific subsets of your photos for batch editing, export, or deletion, streamlining your post-production [[concepts/workflow|workflow]].
