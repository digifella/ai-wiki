# Circular Pattern

## Definition
A circular pattern is a sequence that forms a recognizable circular shape when visualized in a specific format or alignment.

## Submission Notes

## Patterns in pi in "Contact"

In [[entities/carl-sagan|Carl Sagan]]'s novel *[[entities/contact|Contact]]*, the main [[concepts/integrity|character]] ([[entities/ellie-arroway|Ellie Arroway]]) is told by an alien that certain [[concepts/megastructures|megastructures]] in the universe were created by an unknown [[concepts/advanced-intelligence|advanced intelligence]] that left messages embedded inside transcendental numbers. To check this, Arroway writes a program that computes the digits of π in several bases, and eventually finds that the base 11 representation of π contains a sequence of ones and zeros that, when properly aligned on a page, produce a circular pattern. She takes this as an indication of intelligent [[concepts/design|design]].

- **Source**: [[entities/carl-sagan|Carl Sagan]]'s novel "Contact"
- **Characters Involved**:
  - Ellie Arroway
  - Alien Communicator

## Examples in Literature
- In *Contact*, the discovery of patterns within π is pivotal to understanding the [[entities/nature|nature]] of the universe and the existence of [[concepts/extraterrestrial-intelligence|extraterrestrial intelligence]].
