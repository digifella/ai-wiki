# Cisco

Cisco Systems is a multinational technology company headquartered in San Jose, California. Founded in 1984, the company designs and manufactures networking hardware that forms core infrastructure for enterprise and service provider networks worldwide. Its primary products include routers, switches, and related equipment that enable data transmission and network connectivity across organizations of all sizes.

## Products and Services

Beyond physical networking equipment, Cisco develops software solutions for security, collaboration, and network management. The company serves enterprise customers, service providers, and government organizations globally. Its portfolio spans both hardware infrastructure and software platforms designed to support modern network operations and cybersecurity requirements.

## Market Position

As a major player in the networking industry, Cisco has maintained significant market presence since its founding. The company continues to adapt its offerings to address evolving demands in cloud computing, security, and enterprise infrastructure management.
