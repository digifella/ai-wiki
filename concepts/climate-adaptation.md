# Climate Adaptation

Climate adaptation refers to the process of adjustment in natural or human systems in response to actual or expected climatic stimuli. This can involve changes in ecological strategies, societal practices, and infrastructure designs that help communities cope with adverse effects of [[concepts/climate-change|climate change]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/resilience|Resilience]]**: The ability of a system to recover from disturbances.
- **Mitigation**: Efforts to reduce the severity of [[concepts/climate-change|climate change]] impacts.
- **Adaptation Measures**: Specific actions taken by individuals or communities to adapt to changing climatic conditions.
- **[[concepts/sustainable-development|Sustainable Development]]**: Practices that meet present needs without compromising future generations' ability to address their own needs.
