# Victorian architecture

**Victorian architecture** refers to the building styles prevalent during the reign of Queen Victoria (1837–1901), characterized by eclecticism, revivalism, and ornate detailing. While often associated with Gothic Revival and Italianate styles in the UK, its influence spread globally, including to Australia.

## Key Characteristics
- **Eclecticism:** Blending of historical styles (Gothic, Renaissance, Baroque) rather than adherence to a single canon.
- **Ornamentation:** Extensive use of decorative elements such as cornices, brackets, and stained glass.
- **Materials:** Use of brick, stone, and later, terracotta and cast iron.
- **Verticality:** Emphasis on height and vertical lines, particularly in urban contexts.

## Regional Variations

### Australian Context
