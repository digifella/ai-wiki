# Cultural Barriers

**Cultural barriers** refer to obstacles in communication, understanding, or access arising from differences in cultural norms, languages, values, or practices. In [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] contexts, these barriers often manifest as exclusions of non-dominant language speakers or culturally diverse populations.

## Key Dimensions

### Language Exclusion
- [[concepts/digital-interventions|Digital health tools]] are predominantly available in English, systematically excluding non-English speaking populations 1715.
- Lack of [[concepts/multi-language-support|localization]] creates a [[concepts/digital-divide]] for linguistically diverse users.
