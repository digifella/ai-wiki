# Multiple Exposure

Multiple exposure is a photographic technique in which a photographer captures two or more images of the same scene and combines them during post-processing. Rather than relying on a single exposure, this method allows photographers to blend multiple frames to achieve results that would be difficult or impossible to capture in a single shot. The technique has applications across various photographic genres, including portrait, still life, and landscape photography.

## Focus Stacking

Focus stacking is a specific application of multiple exposure commonly used in landscape and macro photography to enhance image sharpness. By capturing a series of images at different focus distances and merging them, photographers can extend the depth of field beyond the limits of a single lens aperture. This process ensures that both foreground and background elements remain in sharp focus, overcoming the natural limitations of optical depth of field.
