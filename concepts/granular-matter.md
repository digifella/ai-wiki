# Granular Matter

Granular matter consists of collections of discrete solid particles, such as sand grains, soil, pharmaceutical powders, or industrial pellets, whose bulk properties emerge from mechanical interactions between individual elements. Unlike fluids or gases where particles move freely, granized systems are primarily governed by [[entities/contact|contact]] forces, [[concepts/friction|friction]], and collisions. The defining characteristic of granular matter is that its macroscopic behavior cannot be easily predicted from the properties of individual particles alone; instead, collective phenomena arise from the geometric arrangement and force chains that develop within the packing.

## Physical Characteristics

A key feature of [[concepts/granular-materials|granular materials]] is their ability to support shear [[concepts/stress|stress]] while at rest, behaving like solids, yet flowing like fluids when agitated. This dual nature leads to complex [[concepts/phase|phase]] transitions, such as the transition from a jamed solid state to a flowing liquid state. [[concepts/energy-dissipation|Energy dissipation]] is significant in these systems because collisions are typically inelastic, meaning [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]] is lost as heat or sound during [[concepts/particle-dynamics|particle interactions]]. Consequently, granular materials do not obey the equipartition theorem of statistical mechanics, where energy is evenly distributed among degrees of freedom; instead, energy tends to localize in specific regions or particles.

## Applications and Research

The study of granular matter is critical in various industrial and natural contexts. In geophysics, it helps explain landslides, avalanches, and the stability of planetary regolith. In [[entities/national-academies|engineering]], understanding granular [[concepts/flow|flow]] is essential for the design of hoppers, silos, and conveyor systems to prevent blockages and ensure consistent material delivery. Pharmaceutical research utilizes these principles to control powder flowability for tablet compression, while materials [[concepts/science|science]] investigates granular crystals and jamming transitions to develop new types of programmable matter and adaptive structures.
