# Misinformation Mitigation

Strategies and technologies designed to reduce the spread, impact, and credibility of false or misleading information.

## Key Mechanisms

- **Provenance & Attribution**: Establishing the origin of content to verify authenticity.
- **Detection Systems**: Automated tools to identify synthetic or manipulated media.
- **Public Literacy**: Educational initiatives to help users critically evaluate sources.

## AI Watermarking

A critical sub-domain focusing on embedding detectable signals into AI-generated outputs to distinguish them from human-created or natural content.

- **Scope**: Applies to multimodal content including text, images, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and biological sequences.
- **Core Challenge**: As [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] improves, the boundary between synthetic and natural content blurs, necessitating robust watermarking techniques.
- **Recent Developments**: Research by [[entities/google-deepmind]] highlights the application of watermarking [[concepts/science|science]] to both digital media (deepfakes) and biological data (DNA).
