# Logical Guardrails

**Logical [[concepts/ai-safety|guardrails]]** refer to structured constraints and semantic frameworks that guide the behavior, [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and output of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], ensuring alignment with defined rules, [[concepts/product-safety|safety standards]], and [[concepts/logical-consistency|logical consistency]]. They are critical for preventing hallucinations, maintaining context, and enabling reliable [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] in complex environments.

## Core Mechanisms

- **Semantic Structuring:** Use of formal knowledge representations to define [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between [[concepts/nodes|entities]], reducing [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]] in [[concepts/inference|model inference]].
- **Constraint Enforcement:** Hard-coded or soft-coded rules that limit the search space of possible actions or outputs.
- **[[concepts/verification|Verification]] Layers:** Post-generation checks against predefined logical or factual baselines.

## Ontologies as Guardrails

Ontologies provide the foundational schema for logical guardrails, particularly in neurosymbolic-ai and [[concepts/agentic-systems]]. By defining explicit concepts, properties, and relationships, ontologies allow systems to [[concepts/purpose|reason]] symbolically over neural outputs, enhancing trustworthiness and [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]].

- **Role in [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|Agentic Systems]]:** Ontologies act as the "common sense" layer, enabling agents to understand context and constraints beyond raw data patterns.
- **Neurosymbolic Integration:** Bridges the gap between neural [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] and symbolic [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]], allowing for precise control over agent behavior.
- **Key Resource:** [[lab-notes/2026-07-30-Ontologies-Essential-Guardrails-for-Trustworthy-Agentic|Ontologies: Essential Guardrails for Trustworthy Agentic Systems and Neurosymbolic AI]]

## Implementation Strategies

- **[[concepts/custom-schemas|Schema Definition]]:** Create rigid taxonomies for domain-specific entities (e.g., medical terms, legal codes).
- **Rule Engines:** Integrate logic engines that evaluate agent actions against ontological constraints in real-time.
- **[[concepts/systems|Feedback Loops]]:** Use violations of logical guardrails to retrain or fine-tune underlying [[concepts/neural-networks|neural models]].
