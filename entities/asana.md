# Asana

Asana is a web and mobile-based project management platform that enables teams to organize, track, and manage work collaboratively. The platform allows users to create tasks, set timelines, assign responsibilities, and monitor project progress. It is designed to serve teams of varying sizes, from small groups to large enterprises, helping organizations streamline their workflow management and improve coordination across team members.

## Core Features

Asana provides multiple views and organizational structures for managing projects. Users can structure work through different formats, including lists, boards, timelines, and calendar views, allowing teams to choose visualization methods that suit their workflow. The platform supports task creation and management, progress tracking, and integration with other tools commonly used in workplace environments.

## Use Cases

Organizations use Asana to coordinate work across departments, manage product development cycles, track marketing campaigns, and oversee operational processes. The platform facilitates visibility into project status and individual task ownership, making it applicable across various industries and project types requiring collaborative task management and progress oversight.
