# Cybersecurity Threats

Adversarial actions compromising data confidentiality, [[concepts/integrity|integrity]], and availability across [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]].

## Threat Vectors & Classifications
- Malware, Ransomware, Phishing, Social Engineering, Supply Chain Attacks
- AI-Augmented Attacks: Leveraging generative [[concepts/models|models]] to automate exploit generation, craft polymorphic payloads, and enhance social engineering precision.
- [[concepts/ai-api-key-theft|LLMjacking]]: Targeted exploitation of AI-integrated [[concepts/software|applications]] and prompts to harvest secrets, credentials, and proprietary data.
- API Key Theft: Exfiltration of [[concepts/authentication|authentication]] [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] for AI services, leading to unauthorized resource consumption and direct financial liability for key owners.
- [[concepts/financial-modeling]]: Direct costs from stolen [[concepts/compute|compute]] billing, remediation efforts, and regulatory penalties associated with credential compromise.
- [[entities/ibm|IBM]] [[concepts/security|Security]] Intelligence analysis indicates evolving threat landscapes where [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] amplify both offensive potential and defensive requirements.
