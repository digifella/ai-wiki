# Luminar Neo

**[[entities/luminar-neo|Luminar Neo]]** is an AI-powered [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] software developed by Skylum. It is designed to streamline the post-processing workflow through [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] tools for sky replacement, [[concepts/portrait-photography|portrait]] enhancement, and landscape [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Key Features
- **AI Sky Replacement**: Automatically detects and replaces skies while adjusting lighting and color balance.
- **Portrait Enhancer**: AI-driven tools for skin smoothing, eye enhancement, and facial structure adjustments.
- **Structure & Detail**: Enhances [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] and [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]] without introducing noise.
- **Lens AI**: Corrects lens distortions and [[concepts/vignette-effects|vignetting]] automatically.
- **Integration**: Supports [[concepts/plugins|plugins]] for [[concepts/lightroom]], [[concepts/photoshop]], and Capture One.
