# Dreamdojo Ai

Dreamdojo AI is an artificial intelligence system developed by NVIDIA designed to address the simulation-to-reality gap in robotic task execution. This gap represents a fundamental challenge in robotics, where AI models trained in simulated environments often fail to perform reliably when deployed on physical robots. The system aims to bridge this divide by enabling robots to execute complex tasks more effectively in real-world conditions despite being trained primarily in simulation.

The technical approach involves training AI models within simulated environments while incorporating mechanisms to account for the discrepancies between virtual and physical worlds. By focusing on this specific challenge, Dreamdojo AI seeks to improve the transferability of learned behaviors, allowing robotic systems to maintain performance and accuracy when moving from controlled digital simulations to unpredictable real-world scenarios.
