# Gold

Gold is a precious [[concepts/metal|metal]] and chemical element (Au) significant in [[concepts/geology|geology]], economics, and history. While primarily studied for its occurrence in deposits like the Major Gold Deposit, its historical valuation is often contextualized alongside other platinum-group elements, such as [[concepts/platinum|Platinum]].

## Major Gold Deposit

A Major [[concepts/gold-deposit|Gold Deposit]] is a [[concepts/geological-formation|geological formation]] containing economically viable quantities of gold, typically significant enough to warrant large-scale mining operations. These deposits are characterized by high concentrations of gold within host rock formations.

## Classification and Significance
Major [[concepts/gold-deposits|gold deposits]] are generally classified based on size, grade, and the associated geological structures. They are crucial [[concepts/causes|drivers]] of economic activity in the regions where they are found, often influencing regional development and mining history.
