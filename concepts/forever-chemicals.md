# Forever Chemicals (PFAS)

**Per- and Polyfluoroalkyl Substances ([[concepts/pfas|PFAS]])** are a large, diverse group of man-made chemicals that include PFOA, PFOS, and many others. Known as "forever chemicals" due to their extreme [[concepts/data-persistence|persistence]] in the environment and the human body, they have been in use since the 1940s.

## Key Characteristics
- **Persistence:** Virtually indestructible; do not break down naturally.
- **Ubiquity:** Found in water, soil, air, and consumer products.
- **Bioaccumulation:** Build up in human and animal tissues over time.

## Recent Analysis: Toxic PFAS in Water
*Source: [[lab-notes/2026-09-17-Toxic-PFAS-in-Water-Contamination-Health-Risks-and-Remov|Toxic PFAS in Water: Contamination, Health Risks, and Removal Strategies]]*

- **Origin:** Man-made chemicals originating from the late 1940s.
- **Core Issue:** Pervasive contamination of water supplies due to the inability of standard treatment to remove them.
- **Focus Areas:**
  - [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]] of water contamination.
  - Documented [[concepts/health|health]] risks associated with [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].
  - Emerging strategies for removal and mitigation.

## Health Risks
- Increased cholesterol levels.
- Changes in liver enzymes.
- Decreased vaccine response in children.
- Increased risk of kidney and testicular cancer.
- Pregnancy complications (e.g., high blood [[concepts/pressure|pressure]], preeclampsia).

## Removal Strategies
- **[[concepts/activated-carbon|Activated Carbon]]:** Granular or powdered forms can adsorb some PFAS.
- **Ion Exchange:** Effective for removing specific types of PFAS from drinking water.
- **[[concepts/desalination-technology|Reverse Osmosis]]:** High-pressure filtration that removes a significant portion of PFAS.
- **Advanced Oxidation Processes:** Emerging technologies targeting breakdown of chemical bonds.
