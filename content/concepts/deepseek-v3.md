# Deepseek V3

Deepseek V3 is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] featuring 675 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], released under the [[concepts/apache-2.0|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/license|license]]. The model represents a significant scale increase in the Deepseek series, positioning it as a capable option for organizations seeking openly-licensed [[concepts/foundation-model|foundation models]] with substantial parameter counts.

## Architecture and Capabilities

The model demonstrates [[concepts/multimodal-capabilities|multimodal capabilities]], though specific architectural details remain limited in publicly available documentation. [[concepts/testing|Testing]] conducted on the model shows performance across standard benchmarks and real-world tasks, with reviewers examining both its strengths and limitations in practical [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Availability and Licensing

The [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0 license]] enables free use, modification, and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of the model [[concepts/weights|weights]], making it accessible for both research and commercial applications. This licensing choice distinguishes it from models with more restrictive terms, though users assume responsibility for [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] and safety considerations.
