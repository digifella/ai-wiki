Here's a [[concepts/summary|summary]] of the video by Nick Harris, a Senior [[entities/github-copilot|Copilot]] Cloud [[concepts/solution|Solution]] Architect with [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]], from the "[[entities/copilot|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] Connection" channel:
In this video, Nick Harris demonstrates several recent, actively rolling out updates to Microsoft 365 Copilot across various applications.
**Microsoft Word Updates:**

1. **Enhanced Document Summaries:** The automatic summaries now offer "Brief," "Standard," and "Detailed" options, allowing users to quickly get key points or a more comprehensive overview of a document.
2. **Document Snapshots with New Tabs:** Beyond the "Summary" tab, Word now includes "Insights" and "Discussion" tabs.
	* **Insights:** Extracts key metric information like phone numbers, mailing addresses, and specific intervals (e.g., blade replacement). Crucially, it also provides "Suggested Q&A" derived from the document content, which previously required explicit [[concepts/prompting|prompting]].
	* **Discussion:** Aggregates and [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] conversations and comments within the document, providing a quick overview of who is collaborating and what changes or discussions are taking place.
3. **Image Uploads in Copilot Chat:** Users can now upload images directly into the Copilot chat pane within Word, enabling Copilot to leverage its [[concepts/vision-capabilities|vision capabilities]] to analyze and provide insights based on visual content.

**Microsoft Excel Updates:**

1. **Direct Table Creation from Prompt:** When opening a blank Excel file, Copilot can now generate structured tables with specified rows and columns, including optional example data, conditional formatting, and even basic formulas, directly from a natural language prompt.
2. **Enhanced Prompt Examples:** The Copilot pane in Excel now offers a wider [[concepts/range|range]] of suggested prompts to help users get started, including options for creating charts, pivot tables, or performing advanced data analysis using [[entities/python|Python]].

**Microsoft PowerPoint Updates:**

1. **Brand/Organization Templates:** Copilot now facilitates easier adherence to organizational branding. When creating a new presentation, users can select brand templates uploaded by IT administrators to SharePoint Online, making it simple to maintain a consistent corporate identity.
2. **[[concepts/visual-slide-design|Slide Design]] Suggestions & Imagery:** Copilot helps create visually appealing presentations by suggesting design layouts and incorporating contextual imagery that aligns with the presentation's content.
3. **Presentation Translations:** A powerful new feature allows users to translate an entire presentation into a different language (e.g., Spanish). Copilot creates a new copy of the presentation with all text translated. (Note: Users are advised to review the translated content for linguistic nuances).

**Microsoft 365 Copilot (Business Chat) Update:**

1. **Scoping to SharePoint Locations:** In the core Microsoft 365 Copilot chat experience (when the "Work" toggle is selected), users can now provide a hyperlink to a specific SharePoint site, document library, or folder. Copilot [[entities/will|will]] then intelligently retrieve information _only_ from that specified location, enhancing data [[concepts/governance|governance]] and ensuring more focused, relevant [[concepts/responses|responses]] from organizational content. (Note: There are limitations to how much data Copilot can consume from very large locations, so it might provide a subset of [[concepts/files|files]]).

Nick concludes by thanking viewers for their support and encourages them to like, share, and subscribe, emphasizing the continuous evolution of AI to enhance work.
