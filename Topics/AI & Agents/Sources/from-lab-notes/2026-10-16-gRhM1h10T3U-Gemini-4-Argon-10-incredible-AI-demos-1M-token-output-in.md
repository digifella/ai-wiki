## Gemini 4 Argon: 10 incredible AI demos (1M token output in action)

**Author / channel:** Google Cloud Tech

### Summary
Sure! Here is a summary of the video content:

The video presented by Smitha Kolan and Shubham Saboo showcases Google Cloud's new frontier model, Gemini 4 Argon. Unlike previous models that focused primarily on data input capacity, Argon's major shift is its ability to output up to 1 million tokens in a single run. This allows the AI agent to hold complex problems in its working memory, solve them from start to finish without interruption, and process vast amounts of information.

The video details ten impressive demos built using Gemini 4 Argon, highlighting its capabilities in various domains. These demos include 1960s code translation (converting legacy Fortran code to modern C++ and Rust), Python runtime optimization (profiling and fixing bottlenecks in the agent development kit), autonomous Android UI construction (rebuilding a 2D map into a 3D spatial experience), desk racer (turning desk photos into a playable 3D racing game), a pagoda 3D model (generating a detailed Japanese pagoda in 3D), fur and hair simulation (rendering realistic hair and fur in a web browser), 3D DEXA body composition viewer (turning clinical scans into an interactive 4-layer 3D digital twin), NASA Artemis II 3D (visualizing the Artemis II mission telemetry), agent gadgets (creating physical hardware from a drawing), and Argon tennis (combining hardware sensors, web tech, and AI models for a 3D tennis game).

The video concludes by emphasizing Gemini 4 Argon's capabilities as a knowledge worker capable of multimodal reasoning and transforming unstructured real-time data into structured, polished deliverables. The speakers express excitement about the potential of these tools, which are open-sourced and available for developers to explore.
