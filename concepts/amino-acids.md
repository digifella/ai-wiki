# Amino Acids

**Amino acids** are organic compounds that serve as the fundamental building blocks of proteins. They consist of an amino group, a carboxyl group, and a side chain (R group) specific to each amino acid. The sequence of amino acids determines a protein's unique three-dimensional structure and function.

## Key Concepts

*   **[[concepts/protein-folding|Protein Folding]]**: The physical process by which a polypeptide chain folds into its functional 3D structure. The specific arrangement is dictated by interactions between amino acid side chains.
*   **Structure-Function Relationship**: The 3D conformation of a protein, derived from its amino acid sequence, directly determines its biological activity.
*   **AlphaFold Breakthrough**: [[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]]'s AlphaFold [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] revolutionized the [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] of protein structures from amino acid sequences, solving a 50-year-old grand challenge in biology.

## DeepMind AlphaFold Integration

*   **AI Prediction**: AlphaFold accurately predicts the 3D structure of proteins based on their amino acid sequences, drastically reducing the time and cost associated with experimental methods like X-ray crystallography.
*   **Scientific Impact**: The system's ability to model complex folding patterns aids in understanding [[concepts/disease-mechanisms|disease mechanisms]] and accelerating [[concepts/drug-discovery|drug discovery]].
