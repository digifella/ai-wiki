# LLM

A [[concepts/large-language-model|Large Language Model]] (LLM) is a type of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] model trained on extensive [[concepts/text|text]] data to understand and generate human-like language.

## Key Insights
- 2026 04 14 [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude code]] [[entities/yifan|Yifan]] [[entities/beyond-the-hype|Beyond the Hype]] channel: [[concepts/reverse-engineering|Reverse-engineering]] of [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] reveals its superior performance (over other [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|coding agents]] using identical [[concepts/deep-learning-models|LLM models]]) stems primarily from sophisticated [[entities/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]] rather than model [[concepts/architecture|architecture]].
- 2026 04 14 Improving RAG [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] for retrieval: Using LLMs for advanced [[concepts/data-indexing|data indexing]] and [[concepts/structured-query-generation|structured query generation]] improved RAG [[concepts/recall|recall]] from 50-60% to over 95% in a customer service chatbot project.
- 2026 04 14 [[concepts/external-knowledge|Context engineering]] by [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] channel: [[concepts/context-engineering|Context Engineering]] (defined by [[entities/tobi-lütke|Tobi Lütke]] and [[entities/andrej-karpathy|Andrej Karpathy]] as "the art/science of providing all necessary context") is distinguished from [[concepts/prompt-engineering|Prompt Engineering]] as the practice of structuring contextual input for LLMs.
