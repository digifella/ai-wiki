# Tesla Fsd Supervised

Tesla Full Self-Driving Supervised (FSD Supervised) is an advanced driver assistance system classified as Level 2 automation. The technology automates steering, acceleration, and braking functions while requiring the human operator to maintain continuous monitoring of the driving environment. Unlike higher levels of autonomy, the system does not assume responsibility for safety; the driver must remain actively engaged and prepared to intervene immediately if the system encounters limitations or hazards.

In April 2026, the system received regulatory approval in the Netherlands, marking a significant milestone for its deployment in Europe. This approval allows Tesla to offer the service on public roads within the country under specific legal frameworks. The decision is viewed as a potential precedent for broader adoption across the European Union, as it establishes a regulatory pathway for high-level driver assistance systems in a major European market.

The approval process involved rigorous evaluation of the system's safety protocols and driver monitoring mechanisms. Regulators focused on the effectiveness of the in-cabin sensors in ensuring driver attentiveness and the system's ability to safely hand over control to the human operator. This regulatory clearance distinguishes FSD Supervised from fully autonomous prototypes, reinforcing its status as a supervised tool rather than a replacement for human driving.
