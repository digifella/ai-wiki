# PDP-11/73

The [[PDP-11/73]] is a [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] variant of the Digital Equipment Corporation [[concepts/pdp-11]] series, featuring a 32-bit backplane and dual [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]]. It represents a critical [[entities/nodejs|node]] in the evolution of [[concepts/unix|Unix]] operating systems and vintage [[concepts/computing-infrastructure|computing infrastructure]].

## Modernization & Restoration

Recent efforts have focused on integrating the PDP-11/73 into modern networks, specifically to host a public website. This process involves overcoming significant hardware and software barriers inherent to mid-1970s technology.

- **Performance Upgrades**: Recent modifications have reportedly increased [[concepts/computational-speed|processing speed]] by 4×, though such aggressive hardware changes can introduce stability issues during boot sequences [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-PDP-1173-Modernization-Overcoming-HardwareSoftware-Chall|PDP-11/73 Modernization: Overcoming Hardware/Software Challenges to Host Public Website]].
- **Operating System**: The system runs [[concepts/211bsd|2.11BSD]], a version of [[entities/uc-berkeley|Berkeley]] Software Distribution Unix tailored for the [[concepts/pdp-11-architecture|PDP-11 architecture]].
- **Integration Challenges**: The primary hurdles involve adapting legacy hardware interfaces to modern networking standards and ensuring the stability of the OS after hardware modifications.
- **Documentation**: A detailed chronicle of the troubleshooting, upgrading, and integration journey is available in recent lab [[concepts/notes|notes]].
