# Vignette Application

Vignette application is a post-processing technique that darkens or lightens the edges of a photograph while maintaining a brighter center, thereby directing viewer attention toward the main subject. This effect is commonly used in portrait, landscape, and product photography to create compositional emphasis and add subtle depth. The technique can be applied either during shooting through lens characteristics or, more commonly, during post-processing where it offers greater control and flexibility.

## Implementation in Lightroom Classic

In Lightroom Classic, vignette effects are primarily managed through the Effects panel within the Develop module. Users can adjust the Amount slider to control the intensity of the darkening or lightening, while the Midpoint slider determines how far the effect extends toward the center of the image. The Style dropdown allows for the selection between Paint Overlay, which affects pixel values directly, or Color Grading, which influences the hue and saturation of the vignette area.

Additionally, the post-crop vignette feature enables photographers to apply the effect specifically after cropping, ensuring the vignette aligns correctly with the final composition. This method provides precise control over the shape and feathering of the vignette, allowing for natural-looking results that enhance the photograph without appearing artificial. The technique is particularly effective for isolating subjects and guiding the viewer’s eye through the frame.
