# NemoClaw Knowledge Wiki

A living AI knowledge base, automatically maintained by NemoClaw. Voice routing, vault Q&A, and the Karpathy-style concept/entity conversion all run locally on qwen3.6:27b via Ollama (with llama3.2:3b for lightweight extraction).

New notes are ingested from voice memos, YouTube summaries, and market intelligence. Concepts and entities are extracted, cross-linked, and published here automatically.
