# Video Editing & AI Decision Models

## Overview
The landscape of video editing is undergoing a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] driven by [[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]]. Traditional workflows relying on manual timeline manipulation and complex software stacks are increasingly being augmented or replaced by prompt-driven generative capabilities. Concurrently, the broader [[concepts/ai-ecosystem|AI ecosystem]] is evolving with specialized decision models for operational tasks such as customer service assessment.

## AI-Driven Workflow Integration
Recent advancements highlight the convergence of photo and video editing through [[concepts/unified-ai|unified AI]] interfaces. Key developments include:

*   **Prompt-Driven Generation**: Tools like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and [[entities/gemini]] allow for rapid content synthesis.
*   **Workspace Integration**: [[lab-notes/2026-10-04-Google-Vids-AI-Video-Creation-and-Editing-Tool-Summary-R|Google Vids AI Video Creation and Editing Tool Summary Report]] details [[concepts/google-vids|Google Vids]]' evolution into a "mind-blowing" AI editor within [[concepts/google-workspace|Google Workspace]]. It functions similarly to [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]] but for video, enabling seamless creation and editing for general users.
*   **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]] & Lowering Barriers**: The shift toward unified interfaces and doc-like video tools significantly lowers the technical barrier to entry, making professional-grade editing accessible to non-experts.

## AI Decision Models
Parallel to creative workflows, advancements in [[concepts/ai-decision-models|AI decision models]] address [[concepts/efficient-operation|operational efficiency]]:

*   **Customer Service Assessment**: Models such as CLM, Laya, [[concepts/openjev|OpenJev]], Kev, and Jev are deployed to assess urgency and frustration in customer interactions.
*   **Operational Urgency**: These models help streamline response times and improve [[concepts/solution|resolution]] accuracy in high-volume service environments.
