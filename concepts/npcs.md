# NPCs

**Non-Player Characters** (NPCs) are [[concepts/nodes|entities]] in interactive systems, such as video games, virtual worlds, or simulations, that are not controlled by human players. In modern AI contexts, NPCs are evolving from scripted state machines into dynamic, [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]] powered by [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/unified-multimodal-models|multimodal models]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Autonomy:** Ability to act without direct player input.
- **Interactivity:** Capacity to respond to player actions and environment changes.
- **[[concepts/memory|Memory]]:** [[concepts/storing|Retention]] of past interactions to inform future behavior.

## Evolution & AI Integration
- **Traditional:** Scripted [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] trees and finite state machines.
- **Modern AI-Driven:**
  - Use of LLMs for natural language generation and [[concepts/contextual-awareness|contextual awareness]].
  - Integration with [[concepts/robotics]] for physical embodiment in [[concepts/physical-ai-robotics|embodied AI]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
  - Enhanced via Multimodal inputs ([[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]]) for richer environmental perception.
  - Recent advancements highlight [[lab-notes/2026-05-25-AI-Progress-Co-Scientists-DNA-NPCs-Robotics-Multimodal-V|AI Progress: Co-Scientists, DNA, NPCs, Robotics, Multimodal, Video Editing]] detailing new [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] and [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] capabilities that streamline NPC [[concepts/digital-asset-creation|asset creation]] and behavioral tuning.
