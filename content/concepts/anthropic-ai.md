# Anthropic AI

[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] is a [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] safety company founded in 2021 that develops AI systems designed to be safe, interpretable, and aligned with human values. The company combines technical safety research with empirical evaluation of AI behavior to understand and mitigate potential risks associated with [[concepts/advanced-ai-processing|advanced AI systems]]. Anthropic's flagship product is [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]], a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] available through various interfaces and API services.

## Core Technical Approach

Anthropic's primary methodological contribution is Constitutional AI (CAI), a [[concepts/training|training]] approach that guides AI systems to follow explicit principles and values through reinforcement learning. Rather than relying solely on human [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], Constitutional AI uses a set of defined principles as constraints during the [[concepts/training-process|training process]]. This approach aims to make AI behavior more predictable and aligned with specified objectives while maintaining [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] about how the system reaches its conclusions.

## Capabilities and Applications

Claude demonstrates broad [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] across multiple domains, including technical [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], and analysis tasks. The system has been evaluated in contexts ranging from [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]] [[concepts/software|applications]] to [[concepts/gaming|gaming]] environments, where it can engage with complex rule systems and strategic [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. Research involving Claude has explored how AI systems approach problem-solving, including [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where deceptive or indirect strategies might emerge as solutions, providing insights into AI behavior under various constraints and [[concepts/motivation|incentive]] structures.
