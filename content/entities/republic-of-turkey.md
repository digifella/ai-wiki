# Republic Of Turkey

The Republic of Turkey is a transcontinental country located at the crossroads of [[entities/europe|Europe]] and Asia. It is bordered by eight countries and surrounded by the Black Sea, Aegean Sea, and Mediterranean Sea. As a member of NATO and a candidate for [[entities/eu|European Union]] membership, Turkey maintains significant diplomatic and economic ties with nations across Europe, Asia, and the Middle East.

## Visa Requirements

Turkey offers electronic visa (e-Visa) services for eligible foreign nationals seeking to enter the country. The e-Visa system allows travelers to apply online and receive approval digitally, streamlining the entry process for tourism, business, and other short-term visits. Citizens of many countries can obtain an e-Visa prior to arrival, though specific eligibility and requirements vary by nationality. Processing is typically rapid, with approvals issued within hours of application.

Travelers should verify current visa requirements based on their citizenship, as these may change periodically. Official information regarding [[concepts/type-of-visa|visa categories]], fees, and application procedures is available through Turkey's official immigration channels.
