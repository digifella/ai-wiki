# Egg Salting Techniques and Scrambled Eggs

**Definition**: The process of adding salt to eggs before cooking them as a method for enhancing flavor and [[concepts/texture|texture]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/salting-eggs|Egg Salting]] Benefits**
    - Enhances umami flavors
    - Aids in protein coagulation
- **Techniques for Tender Results**
    - Proper salting timing (before whisking)
    - Use of the right liquid ratios (e.g., milk, cream)
