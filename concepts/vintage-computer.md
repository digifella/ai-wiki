# Vintage Computer
A term referring to older computing systems that predate modern digital computers but still retain operational significance and interest in the context of technological history. Key features include limited [[concepts/compute-capacity|processing power]] and [[concepts/memory|memory]] compared to contemporary standards.

## Demystifying AI Transformer Training on a 1979 PDP-11
The video, presented by [[entities/dave|Dave]], aims to demystify the [[concepts/training-process|training process]] of a [[concepts/neural-network|neural network]] by running a transformer on a vintage 1979 [[concepts/pdp-1144|PDP-11/44]] computer. This system operates with a single 6MHz CPU and initially has only 64KB of RAM (later upgraded to 4MB). The core idea, [[entities/dave-plummer|Dave]] argues, is not magical or new; rather it highlights the fundamental principles of AI training regardless of [[concepts/hardware-limitations|hardware limitations]].
