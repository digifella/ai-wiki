# Youtube

YouTube is a video-sharing platform founded in 2005 and acquired by Google in 2006. It functions as one of the internet's largest repositories of video content, allowing users to upload, view, share, and comment on videos. The service hosts a diverse range of material, including user-generated content, professional productions, educational resources, and entertainment media.

## Core Features

Users create accounts to upload and manage videos through a creator dashboard. The platform provides tools for video editing, monetization, and audience analytics. Viewers can search for content, subscribe to channels, and interact with videos through likes and comments. The interface supports various playback speeds and subtitle options to enhance accessibility and user control.

## Business Model

YouTube generates revenue primarily through advertising, offering various ad formats such as pre-roll, mid-roll, and display ads. The platform also operates a subscription service called YouTube Premium, which removes advertisements and provides access to exclusive content and background playback. Additionally, creators can earn money through the YouTube Partner Program, which allows them to share ad revenue and receive channel memberships or super chats from viewers.
