# Kimi K2

[[entities/kimi-k2|Kimi K2]] is a review and [[concepts/testing|testing]] assessment of the [[entities/mistral-3-large|Mistral 3 Large]] [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]], a 675-billion parameter variant released under the [[concepts/apache-2.0|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/license|license]]. The review evaluates the model's [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and performance characteristics through practical testing.

## Model Specifications

Mistral 3 Large represents a significant scale increase in the [[entities/mistral|Mistral]] model family, with 675 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. The [[concepts/apache-2.0-license|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] allows for both commercial and non-commercial use with appropriate attribution, positioning it within the open-source [[concepts/ai-ecosystem|AI ecosystem]].

## Review Content

The assessment was documented as a video review covering model performance across various testing [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. The evaluation focuses on practical capabilities and real-world application testing rather than theoretical benchmarks alone, providing empirical data on the model's actual behavior and limitations.
