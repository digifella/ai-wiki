# Intismeran Autogene

**[[entities/v940|Intismeran Autogene]]** is a personalized [[concepts/mrna-cancer-vaccine|mRNA cancer vaccine]] developed by [[entities/moderna]] and [[entities/merck|Merck]] & Co. for the treatment of [[concepts/resected-melanoma|resected melanoma]]. It functions as an [[concepts/adjuvant-therapy|adjuvant therapy]], designed to stimulate the immune system to recognize and attack tumor-specific neoantigens following surgical removal of the primary tumor.

## Key Developments

- **Clinical Trial Results:** Positive [[concepts/phase-3-trial|Phase 3 trial]] results were announced in 2026, demonstrating efficacy in preventing recurrence in patients with resected melanoma.
- **Combination Therapy:** The vaccine is administered in conjunction with Keytruda ([[concepts/pembrolizumab|pembrolizumab]]), a PD-1 inhibitor, to enhance anti-tumor immune response.
- **Personalization:** As an mRNA vaccine, Intismeran Autogene is tailored to the specific mutational profile of each patient's tumor.
