# Allied Health

[[concepts/allied-health|Allied health]] encompasses [[concepts/health|healthcare]] services provided by professionals other than physicians and nurses, including physiotherapists, occupational therapists, dietitians, and other specialized practitioners. These professionals work alongside medical doctors to provide comprehensive patient care and rehabilitation services.

## Wearable Technology in Allied Health

[[concepts/wearable-devices|Wearable devices]] and [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] have become increasingly significant tools in allied health practice. These devices, worn directly on the body, continuously monitor patient vital signs, [[concepts/exercise|movement]] patterns, and activity levels. The data collected enables allied health professionals to track patient progress, adjust treatment protocols in real time, and provide more [[concepts/personalised-care|personalized care]]. [[concepts/software|Applications]] include monitoring rehabilitation exercises, tracking mobility improvements, and detecting changes in patient condition between clinical sessions.

## Clinical Applications

The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[entities/wearable-devices|wearable technology]] allows allied health practitioners to extend monitoring beyond clinical settings into patients' daily lives. This continuous data collection provides objective metrics that complement traditional assessment methods and help identify patterns that might not be apparent during scheduled appointments. The technology supports evidence-based practice and enables more informed [[concepts/clinical-reasoning|clinical decision-making]] across various allied health disciplines.
