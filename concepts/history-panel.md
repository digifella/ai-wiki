# History Panel

A vital component of the [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] [[concepts/develop-module]] used to facilitate [[concepts/non-destructive-editing]] workflows.

### Key Functionalities
- **History Tracking**: The History Panel maintains a chronological record of all [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] applied to an image, allowing users to revert to specific previous states.
- **Visual Comparison**: The **[[concepts/before-after-view|Before & After View]]** enables a direct visual comparison between the original unedited image and the current state of edits.
