# Affect Theory

Affect theory is a branch of interdisciplinary studies that explores emotional [[concepts/responses|responses]] and their impact on human behavior, [[concepts/cognition|cognition]], and social interactions. It draws from various fields including [[concepts/psychology|psychology]], sociology, and [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] to understand the dynamics of feelings and affects.
