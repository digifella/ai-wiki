# Cane Toad

The **Cane Toad** (*Rhinella marina*) is a large, terrestrial amphibian native to South and Central [[entities/america|America]], notorious for its disastrous introduction as an [[concepts/invasive-species|invasive species]] in Australia.

## History & Introduction
*   Originally introduced to Queensland in 1935 from Hawaii (which had sourced them from Puerto Rico) in an attempt to control [[concepts/sugar|sugar]] cane beetles.
*   The biological control effort failed as toads did not prey on the beetles but instead proliferated rapidly, causing significant ecological damage to native Australian fauna.

## Control Strategies: Intraspecific Cannibalism
Recent scientific efforts focus on exploiting the toad's unique biology to curb its [[concepts/population-growth|population growth]].

*   **Mechanism:** Leveraging [[concepts/intraspecific-cannibalism|intraspecific cannibalism]] where larger toads prey upon smaller ones.
*   **[[concepts/purpose|Objective]]:** To accelerate population decline by encouraging natural predation within the species.
