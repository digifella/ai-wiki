# Few-shot examples

A [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]] technique where a model is provided with a small number of input-output examples to demonstrate desired behavior before completing a new task. Enables generalization to unseen inputs by leveraging contextual patterns.

**Relation to [[concepts/context-engineering|Context Engineering]]**:
- [[concepts/external-knowledge|Context Engineering]] (a 2026 concept) is the art/science of strategically providing context to LLMs, distinct from traditional [[entities/prompt-engineering|Prompt Engineering]] (see Context engineering by [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] channel).
- Few-shot examples serve as foundational context in Context Engineering, demonstrating how to [[concepts/structure|structure]] input-output [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] for model guidance.
