# Skill Gaps

Skill gaps refer to the discrepancies between the competencies currently possessed by individuals or organizations and those required to meet present or future objectives. In organizational contexts, identifying and addressing these gaps is essential for maintaining competitiveness and achieving strategic goals. Skill gap analysis typically involves assessing current workforce capabilities against role requirements, market demands, or emerging technologies to inform training and hiring strategies.

In the context of AI agents and large language models (LLMs), skill gaps represent limitations in what the system can effectively execute or understand. These gaps may arise from insufficient training data, architectural constraints, or a lack of specific tooling integration. Evaluating these deficiencies is critical for ensuring that AI agents perform reliably in complex, real-world scenarios where precision and domain-specific knowledge are required.

Recent approaches utilize tools like Claude Code to analyze external data sources, such as LinkedIn profiles, to quantify and visualize these gaps. By processing professional data, such systems can map existing skill distributions against desired competency frameworks. This automated analysis helps organizations identify specific areas where their current AI capabilities or human workforce fall short of strategic needs, facilitating more targeted development and resource allocation.
