# Chomsky's Universal Grammar

**Universal [[concepts/grammar|Grammar]] (UG)** is the [[concepts/theory|theory]] within [[concepts/generative-grammar]] positing that the ability to acquire grammar is hard-wired into the brain. Chomsky argues that humans are biologically equipped with a specific cognitive module (Language Acquisition Device) that allows children to deduce complex [[concepts/language-grammar|grammatical rules]] from limited input, solving the "poverty of the stimulus" problem.

## Core Tenets
- **Innateness Hypothesis**: Linguistic competence is largely innate rather than learned through general cognitive [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
- **Universal Structures**: All human languages share a common underlying structure (deep structure) despite surface variations.
- **Critical Period**: There is a biologically determined window for language acquisition, after which [[concepts/learning|learning]] becomes significantly more difficult and less native-like.
- **Autonomy of Syntax**: Syntax is an autonomous system with its own principles, independent of semantics or pragmatics.

## Critiques and Challenges
The dominance of UG in [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] [[concepts/linguistics|linguistics]] has faced significant scrutiny regarding empirical validity and institutional rigidity.

- **Empirical Refutations**:
  - Research indicates that language acquisition may rely on general [[concepts/cognitive-abilities|cognitive abilities]] and statistical learning rather than a specialized innate module.
  - Cross-linguistic data often reveals variations that challenge the proposed universals of UG.
- **[[concepts/academic-dogmatism|Academic Dogmatism]]**:
  - Critics argue that the field suffers from insularity, where dissenting views are marginalized, leading to a stagnation of theoretical alternatives.
  - Personal accounts from linguists suggest that early education in the field often presents UG as an undisputed fact rather than a contested hypothesis, creating intellectual barriers for researchers exploring alternative frameworks [[lab-notes/2026-05-31-Critiquing-Chomskys-Generative-Grammar-Empirical-Challen|Critiquing Chomsky's Generative Grammar: Empirical Challenges and Academic Dogmatism]].
