# Issuance Of Visas

The [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Republic of Turkey]] provides an electronic visa ([[concepts/electronic-visa-system-e-visa|e-visa]]) system designed for eligible foreign nationals seeking entry for tourism, business, or other short-term purposes. This digital [[concepts/authorization|authorization]] serves as a valid travel document, allowing visitors to bypass the traditional requirement of obtaining a paper visa from a [[entities/turkish-embassy|Turkish embassy]] or consulate. The system was implemented to streamline the application process and eliminate the need for in-person visits to diplomatic missions.

Eligibility for the e-visa is determined by the applicant's nationality, with specific countries designated as eligible for this streamlined entry method. Applicants must complete the online application form, provide necessary personal and travel details, and pay the required fees through the official government portal. The system verifies the information against international databases to ensure [[concepts/security|security]] and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with entry regulations before approval.

Once approved, the e-visa is electronically linked to the applicant's passport. Travelers are required to carry a printed copy or have digital access to the visa document when presenting themselves at Turkish border control. The validity period and duration of stay permitted under the e-visa vary based on the applicant's country of origin and the specific [[concepts/type-of-visa|type of visa]] issued, ranging from single-entry short stays to multiple-entry options over longer periods.
