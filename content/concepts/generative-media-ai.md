# Generative Media AI

Generative media AI refers to systems trained to create or modify visual, audio, and textual content based on user prompts or [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. These tools have become integrated into both [[concepts/desktop-apps|standalone applications]] and existing creative [[concepts/software|software]] platforms. Common applications include image generation, video synthesis, text composition, and audio creation. The capability landscape varies significantly across models, with different systems optimized for different tasks—some excel at photorealistic imagery while others prioritize artistic style or rapid [[concepts/iteration|iteration]].

## Capabilities and Applications

Leading [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]] differ in their core strengths and architectural approaches. Image generation models can create artwork from text descriptions, while language models assist with copywriting, ideation, and content planning. Integration into existing professional software—such as [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], Lightroom, and Canva—has made these tools accessible to photographers, designers, and content creators without requiring specialized technical knowledge. Tools like [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] demonstrate how generative AI can transform research workflows by synthesizing information from multiple sources.

## Pricing and Business Models

Generative AI services operate under various pricing structures, including free tiers with limitations, [[concepts/subscription-models|subscription models]] for regular users, and pay-per-use systems for enterprise applications. Pricing often [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]] with usage frequency, output quality, or advanced features. Understanding the cost-benefit relationship between different platforms is essential for creators managing production budgets, particularly when selecting tools for commercial versus personal projects.
