[[concepts/automated-code-modification|Autoresearch]] refers to the practice of leveraging [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] to conduct research and development autonomously, without direct human intervention. This includes tasks such as [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] training processes, managing [[concepts/ai-model-deployment|model deployment]], and diagnosing issues in test results.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/automated-diagnostic-analysis|Self-evolving AI]]:** The ability for an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] to improve its own performance through [[concepts/iterative-learning|iterative learning]] and [[concepts/ai-workflow|optimization]].
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Autonomous Optimization]]:** Processes by which AI systems can optimize their own parameters or configurations without human input.
