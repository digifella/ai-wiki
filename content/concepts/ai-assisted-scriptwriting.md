# AI-assisted scriptwriting

2026 04 14 [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] advanced prompts [[entities/tool-drop-channel|Tool Drop Channel]]

The use of advanced AI [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] to automate research, drafting, and production for high-retention media.

## The "Admin Privileges" Workflow
A specific workflow designed to create broadcast-quality documentary videos (high-[[concepts/solution|resolution]], motion-heavy, voice-matched) while avoiding the low-quality "slideshow" aesthetic.

### Implementation Details
* **Goal**: Produce high-retention, professional-grade video content using a free/freemium [[concepts/tech-stack|tech stack]].
* **Research & Scripting**: Utilizing [[entities/notebooklm]] for [[entities/chatgpt-deep-research|deep research]] synthesis and advanced prompt-based script drafting.
* **Voiceover**: Leveraging [[entities/google-ai-studio]] to generate high-fidelity, voice-matched [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]].
* **Visual Strategy**: Focusing on high-resolution motion to complement the scripted content.

## Core Tech Stack
* [[entities/notebooklm]]: Primary engine for research and script development.
* [[entities/google-ai-studio]]: Primary tool for advanced voiceover generation.
