# Nasa Accident Report

A formal investigative document produced by [[entities/nasa]] or its contractors following a mission [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]], failure, or [[concepts/critical-incident|safety incident]]. These reports analyze root [[concepts/causes|causes]], sequence of events, and contributing factors to prevent recurrence.

## Key Components
- **Executive Summary**: High-level overview of the incident and primary findings.
- **Chronology**: Detailed timeline of events leading up to the failure.
- **Root Cause Analysis**: Identification of technical, procedural, or human-factor origins.
- **[[concepts/recommendations|Recommendations]]**: Actionable steps for design modification, procedure [[concepts/software-updates|updates]], or training improvements.
- **Technical Data**: Telemetry, telemetry analysis, and [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] schematics.
