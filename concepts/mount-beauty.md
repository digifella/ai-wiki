# Mount Beauty

**Mount Beauty** is a locality in the Victorian Alps of Australia, situated within the region impacted by the [[concepts/kiewa-hydroelectric-scheme]]. It serves as a key reference point for understanding the intersection of large-scale engineering projects and natural landscapes in Victoria.

## Kiewa Hydroelectric Scheme Context

Mount Beauty is closely associated with the [[concepts/kiewa-hydroelectric-scheme]], a major water storage and power generation project in the Australian Alps. The area provides critical geographical and historical context for the scheme's development and operation.
