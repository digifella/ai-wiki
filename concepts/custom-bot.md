# Custom Bot

A Custom Bot is a [[concepts/specialized-ai-assistant|specialized AI agent]] built within the [[concepts/microsoft-applications|Microsoft 365 Copilot]] ecosystem that extends the platform's default capabilities to perform specific tasks. Rather than relying solely on Copilot's general-purpose functionality, custom bots are configured with tailored [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], data sources, and workflows to address particular organizational or individual [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]] and business processes.

These agents leverage Microsoft's underlying [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]] to interpret natural language requests and execute actions across connected applications. By defining specific parameters and connecting to relevant data repositories, users can create bots that automate complex sequences, retrieve precise information, or generate content in a consistent manner. This configuration allows the bot to act as a dedicated assistant for defined roles, such as customer support, data analysis, or internal [[concepts/knowledge-bases|knowledge retrieval]].

The development of these bots typically involves defining the agent's persona, setting up [[concepts/security|security]] permissions, and [[concepts/linking|linking]] it to [[concepts/third-party-apis|external APIs]] or internal databases. This ensures that the AI operates within the organization's [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] boundaries while providing accurate, context-aware responses. As demonstrated in tutorials by experts like [[entities/jamie|Jamie from Teachers Tech]], building a custom bot requires careful alignment of the AI's capabilities with the specific requirements of the intended workflow to ensure effective and reliable performance.
