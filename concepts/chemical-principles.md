# Chemical Principles

**Chemical Principles** encompass the fundamental laws and theories governing the [[concepts/writing|composition]], structure, properties, and reaction of matter. This domain bridges Thermodynamics, Kinetics, and [[concepts/quantum-mechanics]] to explain molecular behavior.

## Core Domains

- **Thermodynamics & Equilibrium**: Governs energy transfer, spontaneity (Gibbs [[concepts/limitless-energy|Free Energy]]), and the direction of chemical reactions.
- **Kinetics & [[concepts/causes|Mechanisms]]**: Describes reaction rates, activation energy, and the step-by-step pathways of molecular transformations.
- **Structure & [[concepts/link-aggregation|Bonding]]**: Explains atomic interactions via Covalent Bonding, Ionic Bonding, and Intermolecular Forces.
- **Solutions & Colligative Properties**: Analyzes solute-solvent interactions, concentration effects, and [[concepts/phase|phase]] behavior.

## Recent Developments in Synthetic Chemistry

Advancements in bottom-up assembly are redefining the boundaries between non-living matter and biological systems.

- **[[concepts/artificial-cell|Artificial Cell]] Construction**: Recent breakthroughs demonstrate the creation of self-sustaining synthetic [[concepts/nodes|entities]] capable of metabolic-like functions.
