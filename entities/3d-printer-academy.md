# 3D Printer Academy

**3D Printer Academy** is a content creator and educational resource focused on [[concepts/3d-printing]], mechanical design, and technical fabrication. The channel produces detailed breakdowns of complex mechanisms, often providing 3D-printable models or deep dives into their engineering principles.
