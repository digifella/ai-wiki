# RHIC

## Overview
The Relativistic Heavy Ion Collider (RHIC) is a high-energy particle accelerator located at [[entities/brookhaven-lab|Brookhaven National Laboratory]] designed to study the fundamental properties of Quantum Chromodynamics (QCD) and the behavior of matter under extreme conditions.

## Core Research
- Generation and characterization of Quark-Gluon Plasma (QGP).
- Probing the Strong Nuclear Force through heavy-[[concepts/heavy-ion-collisions|ion collisions]].
- Investigation of [[concepts/quantum-field-theory]] (QFT) phenomena, including:
    - [[concepts/experimental-validation|Experimental confirmation]] of the [[concepts/physical-reality|physical reality]] of [[concepts/virtual-particles]] 2026 04 24 Experimental Confirmation of Virtual Particle Reality.
    - Analysis of particles appearing and disappearing within vacuum/empty space.
