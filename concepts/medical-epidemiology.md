# Medical Epidemiology

## Core Concepts
- Study of disease distribution and determinants in populations
- Key methodologies: observational studies, clinical trials, surveillance
- Interdisciplinary field combining medicine, statistics, and [[concepts/public-health|public health]]
- Integration of [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] technologies to enhance trial design and execution [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Jiang---Key-Trends-in-Digital-Health-and-the-Future-of-C|Jiang - Key Trends in Digital Health and the Future of Clinical Trials in the US]]

- Infectious [[concepts/epidemiology|Disease Epidemiology]]
- Chronic Disease Epidemiology
- [[concepts/disease-ecology|Environmental Epidemiology]]
- Molecular Epidemiology
- Digital [[concepts/health|Health]] Epidemiology (emerging focus on data from 2016-2020 studies)
