# Elizabeth Lobdell

Elizabeth Lobdell is a writer and journalist associated with [[entities/bbc-future|BBC Future]], where she has contributed articles examining scientific experiments and their impact on environmental understanding. Her work focuses on translating complex scientific concepts for general audiences.

Lobdell is known for her coverage of the [[concepts/controlled-climate-experiment|Biosphere 2 experiment]], a landmark ecological research project conducted in Arizona. Her reporting has explored how this ambitious closed-system experiment advanced scientific knowledge of Earth's ecosystems and the interconnections between different environmental components.
