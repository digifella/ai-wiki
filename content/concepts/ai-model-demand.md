# AI model demand

The demand for [[concepts/ai-models]] is a measure of the computational load and user-driven [[concepts/scaling|scaling]] requirements placed on [[concepts/llm]] infrastructure.

## Key Drivers and Risks
- **[[concepts/compute-scarcity|Compute Scarcity]]:** Rapidly increasing demand can trigger a "[[concepts/compute-crunch|compute crunch]]" if [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] availability is misaligned with usage.
- **Competitive Dynamics:** Infrastructure shortages in one provider (e.g., [[entities/anthropic]]) can be actively exploited by competitors (e.g., [[entities/openai]]) to shift market share.
- **Operational Failure:** Miscalculating the relationship between demand and available [[concepts/compute]] leads to significant public relations nightmares.
