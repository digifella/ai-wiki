## Genie 4: A new generation of world simulation
**Clip title:** Genie 4: A new generation of world simulation
**Author / channel:** Google DeepMind
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sFfPkUJd_Jk

### Summary
The video introduces Genie 4, presented as a realistic and interactive world-building model. According to the speaker, Genie 4 allows users to generate and simulate environments and worlds on command using prompts. The system can predict subsequent events in real-time, enabling users to explore and reshape environments dynamically.

The video showcases several applications of Genie 4. It demonstrates how a prompt like "a crowned crane walks across the snow" can be modified with additional elements, such as a shooting star or a white piano, showing the model's ability to adapt scenes on the fly. Another example shows the transformation of a painting—Van Gogh’s *Starry Night*—into an immersive, dynamic environment complete with ambient sound. The speaker also highlights Genie 4's interactive capabilities, showing how users can control characters within created scenes, such as riding a dragon or navigating a motorcycle chase that responds to prompts.

Additionally, the video suggests practical uses for Genie 4, such as simulating scenarios for robotics, allowing robots to practice tasks virtually before attempting them in the physical world. The speaker concludes by emphasizing that Genie 4 transforms ideas into interactive, sight, sound, and shape experiences, positioning the model as a powerful tool for storytelling and simulation.

### Video Description & Links
#### Description
Meet Genie 4, Google DeepMind’s most realistic and interactive world model yet. Built on our Gemini models, Genie 4 lets you step inside dynamic worlds in real time, steer scenes on the fly with natural language, and watch what happens next: how the world responds, how it sounds, and how the people in it move, react, and speak.

All Genie 4 recordings in this video show real-time interactions from Genie 4, where users use standard keyboard controls for navigation and camera control, and text prompts to steer the scene as it evolves.
