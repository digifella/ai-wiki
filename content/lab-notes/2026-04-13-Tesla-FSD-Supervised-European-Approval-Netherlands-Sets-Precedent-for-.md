## Tesla FSD Supervised European Approval: Netherlands Sets Precedent for EU Rollout
**Clip title:** Tesla FSD Approved in Europe--This Changes Everything
**Author / channel:** [[entities/dr-know-it-all|Dr. Know-it-all Knows it all]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=TEPbcUddNNw

### Summary
The video provides a comprehensive overview of recent developments concerning Tesla's [[concepts/full-self-driving-fsd|Full Self-Driving (FSD)]] system, focusing on its expansion into new markets, [[concepts/technical-enhancements|technical enhancements]], and future prospects. The main topic revolves around the progression of Tesla's [[concepts/autonomous-driving-technology|autonomous driving technology]], particularly the significant milestone of FSD receiving its first supervised approval in Europe.

A key highlight is the approval of FSD Supervised in the Netherlands, announced by [[entities/elon-musk|Elon Musk]] and subsequently confirmed by Tesla Europe, Middle East & Africa. This marks a crucial step for Tesla's FSD rollout across the European continent. The Dutch regulatory authority (RDW) conducted an "extremely rigorous" review, spanning over a year and a half, involving extensive research and [[concepts/testing|testing]] on both test tracks and public roads. Their official statement concluded that the FSD system, due to continuous strict driver monitoring, is safer than other [driver assistance systems](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Driver_assistance_systems). It was also clarified that FSD Supervised is a driver assistance system, not autonomous self-driving, emphasizing that the driver remains fully responsible and must maintain control at all times. This initial approval in the Netherlands is anticipated to serve as a template, paving the way for FSD to gain approval in other [[entities/eu|European Union]] member states.

Beyond regulatory approvals, the video touches upon technical advancements, specifically improved auto wipers. Tesla has implemented a new software-based auto-wiper system that utilizes the wiper motor itself as a tactile sensor. This [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] allows the system to estimate the physical [[concepts/friction|friction]] between the wiper blades and the windshield, enabling it to detect dry-wipe conditions more accurately than relying solely on front cameras, thus preventing unnecessary operation. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] also shared a positive personal anecdote about FSD's impressive performance in handling numerous emergency vehicles during a recent [[concepts/ambition|drive]], demonstrating its ability to safely pull over and proceed when necessary.

Looking ahead, the discussion highlights the anticipated Full Self-Driving version 15 (V15), referred to by Elon Musk as the "large model." Musk expects V15 to "far exceed human levels of safety, even in completely unsupervised and complex situations." The speaker explains that the perceived incremental improvements in current FSD versions, and the delay in V15, are likely due to a significant underlying rewrite of the compiler and runtime using MLIR, resulting in a 20% faster reaction time. This foundational work, while not immediately visible, is crucial for enabling the substantial "step change" in capability and safety that V15 is expected to deliver, moving beyond mere point releases.

In conclusion, Tesla's FSD is experiencing pivotal advancements across multiple fronts. The European regulatory approval is a major market expansion, underscoring the system's validated safety benefits under supervised conditions. Concurrent technical innovations like the improved auto wipers demonstrate ongoing refinement. Most significantly, the foundational rewrite for V15 signals a forthcoming leap in FSD's capabilities, promising a future of increasingly safer and more sophisticated autonomous driving experiences.
