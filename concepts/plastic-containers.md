# Plastic Containers

## Overview
Plastic [[concepts/containerization-technology|containers]] are ubiquitous [[entities/storage|storage]] vessels used for food, household chemicals, and personal care products. Proper maintenance, including the removal of labels and adhesive residue, is critical for reuse, recycling, and aesthetic [[concepts/preservation|restoration]].

## Label and Residue Removal
Effective cleaning of Plastic containers and Glass jars often requires specific techniques to remove sticky adhesives without damaging the material.

### Key Methods
*   **[[concepts/cooking-time|Heat Application]]**: Using heat to soften adhesives before [[concepts/scraping|scraping]] or peeling.
*   **Solvent Use**: Applying oils or solvents to dissolve remaining residue.
*   **Mechanical Removal**: Gentle scraping to [[entities/lift|lift]] labels intact.
