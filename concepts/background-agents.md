# Background Agents

**Background Agents** are autonomous software processes or AI-driven [[concepts/nodes|entities]] that operate independently of direct user interaction, executing tasks, [[concepts/monitoring-systems|monitoring systems]], or managing data in the background. They are critical for maintaining system state, automating workflows, and enabling persistent intelligence in [[concepts/ai-models|AI Systems]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Autonomy**: Operate without continuous human input.
- **[[concepts/data-persistence|Persistence]]**: Maintain state and context over time.
- **Integration**: Interface with various [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], [[concepts/communication|messaging]] systems, and engines.
