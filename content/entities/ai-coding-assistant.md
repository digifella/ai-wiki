# Ai Coding Assistant

An AI coding assistant is a [[concepts/software|software]] tool or service that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to aid developers in [[concepts/writing|writing]], [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and managing code. These assistants can automate routine [[concepts/coding|coding]] tasks, suggest code completions, and help identify errors, potentially increasing development productivity.

## Cost and Resource Considerations

[[concepts/terminal-based-ai-coding-agents|AI coding assistants]] can introduce significant infrastructure and operational costs, particularly when deployed at scale or integrated with [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]]. A notable case study involves the expenses incurred during the [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[entities/journey-kits|Journey Kits]], where bills from services like [[entities/vercel|Vercel]] accumulated unexpectedly. [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]] documented these cost overruns in a video discussing the practical financial implications of relying heavily on [[concepts/development-speed|AI-assisted development]] workflows.

## Vibe Coding and Its Implications

The term "vibe coding" refers to a development approach that prioritizes intuitive, rapid prototyping using [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] without rigorous planning or architectural forethought. While this approach can accelerate initial development, it may lead to inefficient code, unexpected costs, and technical debt. Berman's analysis highlights the potential drawbacks of this methodology, particularly regarding long-term sustainability and budget management in AI-assisted projects.
