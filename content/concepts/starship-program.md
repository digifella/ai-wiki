# Starship Program

Fully reusable super heavy-lift launch vehicle developed by [[entities/spacex]] for Orbital Launch, Mars Colonization, and Point-to-Point Transport. System [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] comprises two stages: Super Heavy booster and [[concepts/starship]] spacecraft. Engineered for rapid reusability, high [[concepts/engine-thrust|thrust]] via Raptor Engine array, and in-orbit propellant transfer capability.
