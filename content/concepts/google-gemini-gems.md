# Google Gemini Gems

Customizable versions of [[entities/gemini]] designed to function as specialized experts for specific, repeatable tasks.

### Workflow Optimization
- **The Problem**: Standard interaction with [[concepts/llms]] (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt]], [[entities/claude]]) often involves a costly "back-and-forth" chat [[concepts/loop|loop]] to refine outputs, leading to inefficiency for solopreneurs and small teams.
- **The [[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: [[entities/google-gemini-gems|Google Gemini Gems]] streamline workflows by pre-defining [[concepts/instructions|instructions]], reducing the need for manual iterative [[concepts/prompting|prompting]].
