# Engineered Solutions

Engineered solutions refer to advanced and innovative approaches designed to solve complex problems in various fields such as [[concepts/agriculture|agriculture]], infrastructure, and environmental management. These solutions often integrate traditional practices with modern technology to optimize resource use and sustainability.

### Key Examples:
- **[[concepts/qanats|Qanats]]**: Traditional underground water channels used extensively in arid regions like [[concepts/iran|Iran]] for irrigation and drinking purposes.
  - WikiLink Qanats

### Case Studies:
- **[[entities/iran|Iran]]'s Water [[concepts/crisis|Crisis]]: Ancient [[concepts/qanat|Qanat]] Management and 20th Century Decline**
  - The country [[concepts/faces|faces]] a severe and long-standing water crisis due to its [[concepts/arid-geography|arid geography]] and low average annual precipitation (ranking 158th globally).
  - Historically, central lands were uninhabitable without sophisticated [[concepts/water-management|water management]] techniques like qanats.
  - 75% of Iran's limited rainfall occurs on only 25% of the country.
  - WikiLink Water Crisis
