# Embedding Capabilities

Dense [[concepts/vector-representations|vector representations]] mapping [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] to continuous vector spaces for similarity computation, clustering, and retrieval.

## Core Properties
- Preserves semantic [[concepts/structure|structure]]; proximity correlates with meaning.
- Enables cross-modal alignment ([[concepts/text|text]], image, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]]) in unified latent spaces.
- Critical component for [[concepts/vector-databases]], [[concepts/rag]] pipelines, and [[concepts/anomaly|anomaly]] detection.

## Integrations & Updates
- [[entities/ibm|IBM]] [[entities/granite|Granite]] 4.1 suite expands embedding [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] across language, [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], and speech modalities [[lab-notes/2026-05-08-IBM-Granite-Speech-4.1-ASR-Models-Features-Accuracy-and|IBM Granite Speech 4.1 ASR Models: Features, Accuracy, and Enterprise Applications]].
- Enterprise embeddings emphasize low-latency [[concepts/inference|inference]], [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]] support, and sovereign [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
