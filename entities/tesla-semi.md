# Tesla Semi

The Tesla Semi is an electric semi-truck developed by Tesla, Inc. for commercial freight transportation. Announced in 2017, the vehicle marks Tesla's entry into the heavy-duty trucking market, a sector historically dominated by diesel-powered engines. The truck is engineered for both long-haul and regional hauling operations, utilizing an all-electric powertrain designed to eliminate tailpipe emissions and reduce operational costs through lower energy expenses and decreased maintenance requirements compared to conventional internal combustion engine trucks.

## Technical Specifications and Performance

The Semi features a distinctive aerodynamic cab design intended to maximize range efficiency. It is powered by a triple-motor drivetrain that provides significant torque for heavy loads. Tesla has stated that the vehicle can accelerate from 0 to 60 mph in 5 seconds while fully loaded, a performance metric that surpasses many diesel competitors. The truck is capable of traveling up to 500 miles on a single charge, depending on the payload and driving conditions, with fast-charging capabilities allowing for rapid replenishment during stops.

## Production and Deployment

Following years of development and pre-order accumulation, Tesla began limited production of the Tesla Semi in 2022. Early deliveries were made to major logistics partners, including PepsiCo and Walmart, who have integrated the trucks into their supply chains to test real-world performance and durability. The company continues to refine manufacturing processes and engineering aspects to scale production volume and meet the growing demand from commercial fleet operators seeking to decarbonize their operations.
