# Privacy Law

**[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] Law** encompasses the regulatory frameworks and ethical guidelines governing the collection, use, [[entities/storage|storage]], and dissemination of personal data and [[concepts/health|health]] information. It intersects critically with data [[concepts/secure|protection]], digital ethics, and professional [[concepts/governance|governance]] in [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] and technology sectors.

## Core Concepts
- **Regulatory [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]]**: Adherence to statutory requirements (e.g., [[concepts/hipaa|HIPAA]], [[concepts/gdpr|GDPR]]) regarding information [[concepts/security|security]] and user consent.
- **Ethical Governance**: Frameworks ensuring that [[entities/ai]] and digital tools [[concepts/respect|respect]] individual rights and minimize harm.
- **Professional [[concepts/accountability|Responsibility]]**: Mandates for [[concepts/ahps|allied health professionals]] and tech practitioners to maintain confidentiality and [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] in digital interactions.
