# SES Family

SES Family is a digital [[concepts/health|health]] intervention developed [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the University of Copenhagen that addresses [[concepts/conflict|conflict]] resolution and family dynamics through an interactive mobile application. The platform provides structured modules designed to help users navigate interpersonal conflicts and develop practical resolution strategies. The application features a simple, accessible layout intended to support engagement with [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] content.

## Development and Approach

The intervention draws on psychological frameworks including Jungian grief [[concepts/models|models]] to inform its approach to family conflict. SES Family operates with approximately 20 employees and operates through the online platform [[entities/sesfamilycom|SESFamily.com]]. The development team has worked to create an evidence-based tool that makes mental health support more accessible to users seeking to improve family [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] and communication.

## The Digital Health Context

SES Family exists within a broader tension in [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] sometimes referred to as the [[concepts/digital-paradox|digital paradox]]—while there is significant demand for digital mental health solutions, actual uptake and consistent use of these tools remains challenging. The platform represents an attempt to bridge this gap through [[concepts/design|design]] focused on usability and practical utility rather than complexity.
