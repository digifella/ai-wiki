# Video Editing

Video editing is the process of manipulating and rearranging video footage, [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], and other media elements to create a finished film or video product. It involves selecting clips, arranging them in sequence, adjusting timing and transitions, and integrating audio, visual effects, and color correction. Video editing can [[concepts/range|range]] from basic trimming and concatenation of clips to complex multi-track projects with layered effects and sophisticated post-production techniques.

## AI-Assisted Video Editing

Recent developments in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] have introduced new [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to video editing workflows. Tools like [[entities/google-vids|Google Vids]] [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] to automate aspects of [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] and editing, including intelligent clip selection, automatic transitions, and content [[concepts/organization|organization]]. These AI-powered approaches aim to streamline the editing process, particularly for users without extensive technical experience, by handling routine editorial decisions and suggesting compositional improvements.

## Applications and Tools

Video editing is used across diverse contexts including film and television production, social media [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], corporate communications, and educational materials. Both professional-grade software and consumer-accessible [[concepts/software|applications]] support video editing, with options ranging from [[concepts/specialized-tools|specialized tools]] designed for specific workflows to all-in-one platforms that integrate editing with other creative functions like [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] and audio mixing.
