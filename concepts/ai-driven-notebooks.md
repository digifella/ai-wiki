# AI Driven Notebooks

AI Driven Notebooks refers to Google's integrated notebook feature within Gemini, created following the merger of NotebookLM into the main Gemini platform. This consolidation combined NotebookLM's document analysis capabilities with Gemini's conversational AI interface, enabling users to organize research materials and source documents while leveraging AI assistance for synthesis and exploration.

## Core Functionality

The notebooks feature allows users to upload and reference documents, PDFs, and other source materials within a chat-based environment. Gemini can analyze these uploaded sources, answer questions about their content, and help synthesize information across multiple documents. Users can maintain organized collections of research materials alongside their conversations, creating a unified workspace for document-based research and writing tasks.

## Integration with Gemini

By integrating NotebookLM directly into Gemini rather than maintaining it as a separate product, Google streamlined its AI assistant offerings. This approach gives Gemini users access to notebook functionality without switching between applications, while leveraging the same underlying language model capabilities for document analysis and conversation. The merged platform represents Google's strategy of consolidating specialized AI tools into a single, more comprehensive interface.
