## Core Definition
[[concepts/demystifying-llms|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are foundational AI systems where performance depends on both the surrounding infrastructure (harness) and the underlying [[concepts/generative-prediction|generative prediction]] mechanisms.

## Infrastructure & Performance
*   **Harness & Architecture**: Performance is contingent on the "harness" surrounding the model. Emerging architectures like [[entities/pathway]]'s [[concepts/bdh-architecture]] challenge current LLM limitations by focusing on data flow, continuous learning, and real-time processing, moving beyond static Post-Transformer paradigms.
*   **Efficiency & Access**: A surge in [[concepts/open-source]] projects is democratizing [[concepts/local-llm]] and [[concepts/agent-capabilities|agent capabilities]]. Tools like [[entities/unsloth]] and [[entities/llamacpp]] emphasize VRAM-Efficiency, enabling local deployment.
*   **Benchmarking**: Critical focus on [[concepts/ai-benchmark-integrity|Benchmark-Integrity]] and avoiding [[concepts/glitch-tokens]] that produce Anomalous-Responses.
