# Synthetic Data

**Synthetic Data** refers to the automated creation of logical, spatial, or pattern-based problems designed to evaluate or train [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], particularly in the context of **Fluid Intelligence** and **General [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AGI)** benchmarks. Unlike static datasets, synthetic puzzles allow for infinite variation and specific testing of [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] rather than memorization.

## Key Applications

*   **[[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] Evaluation & AGI Benchmarks**: Synthetic puzzles serve as dynamic probes for [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]], moving beyond static evaluation to test fluid intelligence and [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]] in real-time.
*   **Robotics & [[concepts/spatial-intelligence|Spatial Intelligence]]**: Synthetic environments address data [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] by enabling spatial intelligence training without [[concepts/hardware|physical hardware]] constraints, allowing for safe and scalable [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] of complex physical interactions.
*   **[[concepts/local-ai-model|Local LLM]] [[concepts/performance-benchmarking|Performance Benchmarking]]**: Recent efforts focus on evaluating [[concepts/model-performance|model performance]] on constrained local hardware. For instance, the [[lab-notes/2026-10-06-Swift-1.5-Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-IQ3_S-16GB-LLM-Performance|Swift 1.5 Qwen3.8-27B GSQ-RCO IQ3_S 16GB LLM Performance Benchmark]] details the performance of the UkisAI Swift-1.5-Qwen3.8-27B-GSQ-RCO-GGUF model with [[concepts/iq3-s-quantization|IQ3_S quantization]] on an [[concepts/rtx-2000-ada|RTX 2000 Ada]] (16GB VRAM) setup. These benchmarks are critical for assessing viability in resource-constrained environments.
