# Rocket Landing

**Rocket landing** refers to the controlled descent and touchdown of a launch vehicle or its stages after completing its mission in space. This capability is central to Reusability in aerospace, significantly reducing launch costs by allowing hardware recovery and refurbishment.

## Technical Overview

*   **Vertical Landing**: The most common method for first-stage recovery, involving precise [[concepts/engine-thrust|thrust]] vectoring and grid fins for attitude control.
*   **Horizontal Landing**: Emerging techniques for upper stages or specific vehicle designs, often involving runway touchdowns.
*   **Propulsive Descent**: The use of rocket engines to slow the vehicle during atmospheric re-entry and final approach.
