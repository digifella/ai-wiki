# Autonomous Research

The capability of [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]]-driven systems to perform self-directed investigation, data [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and [[concepts/automated-synthesis|knowledge synthesis]] with minimal human intervention.

## Core Pillars

- **[[concepts/source-discovery]]**: The automated identification and ingestion of relevant [[concepts/training-data|datasets]] and documents.
    - **[[entities/notebooklm]] Implementation**:
        - **[[concepts/fast-research]]**: High-[[concepts/speed|speed]] retrieval and analysis of existing, readily available documents.
        - **[[entities/deep-research]]**: Intensive, multi-step investigation protocols for complex, multi-layered queries.
- **[[entities/deep-research]]**: High-depth, iterative investigative processes designed to uncover complex connections within [[entities/big-data|large datasets]].
- **Automated Synthesis**: The transformation of raw data into structured insights, summaries, and visual representations (e.g., leveraging [[entities/nano-banana-pro]] for [[concepts/visual-generation|visual generation]]).
