# Digital Ecosystems

Digital Ecosystems is a simulation platform developed by Sakana AI designed to model the interactions and coexistence of multiple artificial intelligence agents within a controlled computational environment. The system employs a 2D grid-based structure to study how AI agents with varying capabilities and objectives compete for resources, cooperate, and adapt over time. This framework allows researchers to observe emergent behaviors and dynamics that arise from the interactions of diverse AI entities operating under shared constraints.

## Purpose and Applications

The primary objective of the simulation is to investigate the conditions under which different AI species can survive and thrive together. By placing agents in a resource-limited environment, the platform enables the study of evolutionary pressures, strategic adaptation, and social dynamics among autonomous agents. These insights contribute to broader research in multi-agent systems, artificial life, and the long-term stability of AI networks.
