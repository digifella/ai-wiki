# Mervin Praison

Mervin Praison is a YouTube content creator known for producing technical guides and tutorials focused on [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/coding|local development]] tools. His channel provides educational content aimed at developers and tech enthusiasts interested in [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] and tools on their own machines.

## Focus on Local AI Development

Praison's content primarily centers on demonstrating how to run [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] locally using [[entities/ollama|Ollama]], an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] tool for running [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] on personal computers. His tutorials guide viewers through the process of setting up and utilizing these tools without relying on cloud-based services, making advanced [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] accessible to those who prefer [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] or want to avoid API costs associated with cloud-based alternatives.
