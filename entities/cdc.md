# Cdc

The Centers for Disease Control and Prevention (CDC) is a United States federal health agency operating under the Department of Health and Human Services. Established in 1946, it serves as the primary national public health institute, responsible for protecting the country from health, safety, and security threats. The agency’s core mission involves disease prevention, health promotion, and emergency response, supported by extensive scientific research and data analysis.

## Climate Change and Health

In recent years, the CDC has expanded its focus to address the intersection of environmental factors and public health. The agency produces educational and scientific content detailing the impacts of climate change on community health, with specific attention to vulnerable populations. This work aims to inform healthcare providers, policymakers, and the public about the growing health risks associated with changing environmental conditions.
