## Strategic AI Model Routing for Software Development Cost Optimization
**Clip title:** Cut your AI cost IN HALF (EASY)
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1KKB_UiW6ls

### Summary
The video delves into the often-overlooked high costs associated with utilizing [[concepts/advanced-ai-models|advanced AI models]], particularly for [[concepts/coding|software development]]. It introduces "[[concepts/model-mixing|model routing]]" as a powerful and straightforward method to achieve significant savings, potentially upwards of 90%, on AI-related expenditures. The core principle of [[concepts/model-mixing|model routing]] is to intelligently select the right AI model for the right task, rather than indiscriminately using the most capable (and expensive) models for every stage of a project.

The central thesis differentiates between the "planning" and "execution" phases of a development task. During the planning [[concepts/phase|phase]], which involves conceptualization, research, [[concepts/architecture|architectural design]], and defining the "how-to," it is beneficial to use a [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]], "frontier" model (like [[entities/fable|Fable]], as a hypothetical example). These models [[entities/excel|excel]] at [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]], understanding intricate requirements, and generating comprehensive specifications. This stage typically involves more input [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] (processing information) than output. Conversely, the "execution" [[concepts/phase|phase]], where actual code is generated based on the established plan, can be efficiently handled by a less expensive, but still highly capable, "good enough" model (such as [[entities/gpt-35|GPT-3.5]] or Composer). Here, the model's primary task is to write code according to a clear spec, leading to a higher volume of output [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] (generated code).

An illustrative example of [[concepts/writing|writing]] an [[concepts/user-authentication-system|authentication system]] demonstrates this principle in action. The research and specification writing would be handled by the premium [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]]. However, subsequent tasks like actual [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]], creating pull requests, editing code based on [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], and deployment can be delegated to the cheaper, yet competent, execution-focused models. A detailed cost analysis in the video shows a significant reduction from $9.50 (if the [[concepts/frontier-model|frontier model]] did everything) down to $3.02 when employing this strategy – a remarkable 68.2% saving. This underscores that by segmenting tasks and aligning them with appropriate model capabilities, organizations can drastically optimize their AI spending.

The video also explores practical implementation methods, ranging from manually copy-pasting between different AI [[concepts/chat-interfaces|chat interfaces]] (e.g., using Fable for planning, then [[concepts/codex|Codex]] or [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]] for coding) to leveraging more automated, model-agnostic tools. Platforms like [[concepts/cursor|Cursor]], Factory, and Not Diamond (which the presenter is a minor investor in) offer built-in model routing capabilities, intelligently directing tasks to the most suitable (and cost-effective) models. These tools often allow users to specify "[[concepts/effort-levels|effort levels]]," further [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] how much "[[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]]" a model performs for a task, which directly impacts token usage and cost. The ultimate takeaway is to develop a deep understanding of various AI models' strengths and weaknesses, making informed choices for each specific task rather than relying on default settings. This [[concepts/strategic-approach|strategic approach]] to AI utilization, as exemplified by companies like Coinbase who have maintained flat AI spending despite increasing token usage, is crucial for [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] AI effectively and efficiently.
