# Conversational AI

[[concepts/ai-chatbots|Conversational AI]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems designed to engage in natural language [[concepts/communication|dialogue]] with users. These systems process user input, understand intent and context, and generate relevant responses in a conversational manner. Conversational AI powers various applications including [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]], [[concepts/voice-assistants|virtual assistants]], and web-based interfaces that enable human-computer interaction through text or [[concepts/tone|voice]].

## Google Gemini Web App

The [[concepts/google-ai|Google Gemini]] [[concepts/web-application|web app]] is a general-purpose [[concepts/conversational-interface|conversational interface]] accessible through a web browser. It provides a straightforward chat [[concepts/experience|experience]] suitable for casual users and those seeking quick information or assistance. The interface prioritizes ease of use, making it accessible for non-technical users while still offering robust capabilities for [[concepts/power-users]].
