# Tree Sap

**Tree sap** refers to the fluid transported in xylem cells (wood) or phloem vessels (bark) of vascular plants. It is distinct from [[concepts/plant-resin]], though both are often colloquially grouped under "sap" in popular culture.

## Composition and Types

*   **Xylem Sap:** Primarily water and dissolved minerals, transported from roots to leaves.
*   **Phloem Sap:** Contains sugars ([[concepts/sucrose|sucrose]]), amino acids, and hormones, transported from sources (leaves) to sinks (roots, fruits).
*   **Plant Resin:** A viscous mixture of terpenes and other organic compounds produced by plants, often for defense. It hardens over time and is the precursor to [[concepts/amber]].

## Evolution and Defense Mechanisms

Recent paleontological findings have shifted understanding of when and why plants began producing resinous defenses.

*   **Oldest Amber Discovery:** Evidence suggests plant resin defense [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] evolved earlier than previously thought.
*   **Redefining Plant Evolution:** The discovery of the oldest known amber indicates that early plants utilized resin not just for sealing wounds, but as a sophisticated defense against pathogens and herbivores.
*   **Key Insight:** This challenges the timeline of plant evolution, showing that complex chemical defenses were present in ancient flora.
*   **Source Analysis:** For detailed breakdown of this discovery, see [[lab-notes/2026-07-24-Oldest-Amber-Discovery-Redefines-Plant-Resin-Defense-Evo|Oldest Amber Discovery Redefines Plant Resin Defense Evolution]].

## Amber Formation

*   **Process:** Tree sap (specifically resin) is buried under sediment, subjected to heat and pressure, and undergoes polymerization over millions of years.
*   **[[concepts/fossilization|Fossilization]]:** The resulting [[concepts/amber]] preserves organic material, providing a window into ancient ecosystems.
*   **Cultural Impact:** Often featured in media such as *[[concepts/jurassic-park|Jurassic Park]]*, amber is scientifically valuable for extracting ancient DNA and studying extinct species.
