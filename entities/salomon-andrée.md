# Salomon Andrée

**Salomon August Andrée** (1834–1897) was a Swedish ballooning and Arctic explorer. He is best known for organizing the ill-fated Andrée's Arctic balloon expedition, which aimed to cross the Arctic Ocean using a hydrogen-filled balloon launched from Svalbard.

## Key Events & Legacy

- **The 1897 Expedition**: Led by Andrée alongside Nils Strindberg and Knut Fränkel, the attempt ended in disaster. The balloon crashed shortly after launch on the Kronprins Karls Forland, where the crew perished from starvation and [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] while attempting to reach the mainland by sledge.
- **Discovery of Remains**: The bodies and the crew's journals were not discovered until 1930 by Norwegian explorers Hjalmar Riiser-Larsen and Jon Lindström.
- **[[concepts/historical-context|Historical Context]]**: Andrée’s [[concepts/ambition|ambition]] was part of a broader, often tragic era of early Arctic air exploration, sharing thematic parallels with later figures like [[entities/roald-amundsen]] and [[entities/umberto-nobile]] regarding the risks of pioneering aviation in extreme environments.
