# Renewable Energy Storage

**Renewable Energy [[entities/storage|Storage]]** refers to technologies and systems that capture energy generated from renewable sources (e.g., [[concepts/solar-energy|Solar Energy]], Wind Energy) for later use, addressing the intermittency of supply. Key categories include electrochemical (Battery Storage), mechanical (Pumped Hydro Storage, Compressed Air Energy Storage), and thermal storage.

## Key Technologies & Developments

### Thermochemical Storage
Thermochemical storage utilizes reversible chemical reactions to store energy as chemical bonds, offering high energy [[concepts/density|density]] and negligible self-discharge over long periods.

*   **[[entities/season|Season]]'s Technology**: A Swiss-developed [[concepts/solution|solution]] focusing on long-term storage of surplus renewable energy for months with minimal loss.
    *   **Mechanism**: Uses thermochemical reactions to store heat energy.
    *   **Advantage**: Addresses the "seasonal" gap in renewable supply, potentially outperforming traditional Heat Pump systems in long-duration [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
    *   **Source Analysis**: See [[lab-notes/2026-07-16-Seasons-Thermochemical-Heat-Storage-Long-term-Renewable|Season's Thermochemical Heat Storage: Long-term Renewable Energy Solution]] for detailed breakdown.

### Electrochemical Storage
*   Dominated by [[concepts/lithium-ion-battery]] technology for short-to-[[entities/medium|medium]] term storage.
*   Emerging alternatives include [[concepts/flow|Flow]] Battery and solid-state batteries for grid-scale stability.

### Mechanical Storage
*   Pumped Hydro Storage remains the largest capacity form of grid storage globally.
*   Flywheel Energy Storage provides rapid response for frequency [[concepts/regulation|regulation]].

## Challenges
*   **Round-trip Efficiency**: Energy lost during conversion and storage processes.
*   **Cost**: Capital expenditure for [[concepts/large-scale-computing|large-scale infrastructure]].
*   **Material [[concepts/limited-resources|Scarcity]]**: Dependency on critical minerals for battery production.
