# Cartilage Preservation
Cartilage [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] refers to methods of maintaining or studying cartilaginous structures such as those found in sharks and other fish. This is crucial for understanding evolutionary history and the biological mechanisms behind cartilage formation and degradation.

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/evolutionary-biology|Evolutionary Biology]]**: Study of how species, including ancient and modern sharks, have adapted over time.
- **Cartilage [[concepts/structure|Structure]]**: The unique composition and function of cartilage in skeletal support and flexibility.
- **[[concepts/fossilization|Fossilization]] Processes**: How cartilaginous remains are preserved for scientific study.
