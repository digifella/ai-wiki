# UK
Sovereign state comprising England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland. Constitutional [[concepts/monarchy|monarchy]], parliamentary democracy, major economic/military [[concepts/power|power]], former colonial hegemon.

## Core Attributes
- Capital: London
- Government: Unitary parliamentary constitutional monarchy
- Currency: Pound sterling
- Population: ~67M
- Economic Blocs: G7, NATO, Commonwealth of Nations (post-Brexit realignment)

## Integrated External Notes
- Regional Caribbean economic shifts and post-colonial development trajectories intersect with historical UK trade and diplomatic frameworks: [[lab-notes/2026-05-12-Havanas-Transformation-Prosperity-Revolution-and-Economi|Havana's Transformation: Prosperity, Revolution, and Economic Decline]]
- Pre-1959 Havana operated as a prosperous, modern Atlantic trading hub ("Paris of the Caribbean"), reflecting broader Western European and [[entities/united-states]] capital inflows that historically paralleled British Empire commercial networks in the Caribbean.
- Post-[[concepts/revolution|revolution]] economic decline and nationalization in Cuba created a long-term diplomatic [[concepts/counterpoint|counterpoint]] to UK post-colonial foreign policy, which prioritized selective engagement, Commonwealth ties, and regulatory stability over ideological alignment.
- UK maintains active consular presence in [[entities/havana]], facilitating trade, [[concepts/health|healthcare]] cooperation, and [[concepts/cultural-exchange|cultural exchange]] amid Cuba's ongoing structural economic challenges.
