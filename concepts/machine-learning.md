### HIL: Hybrid AI for Adaptive Human-like Dynamic Athletic Control
- **Source:** [[entities/two-minute-papers|Two Minute Papers]], "[[concepts/unsloth-optimization|NVIDIA]]'s AI Learns Why Copying Humans Isn't Enough"
- **Core Concept:** Introduces a novel approach to training [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] for dynamic athletic control, specifically focusing on parkour.
- **Problem Addressed:** Highlights the limitations of existing AI methods that rely solely on copying humans (imitation learning), which often fail in complex, dynamic physical environments.
- **Integration:** Represents a shift towards [[concepts/hybrid-ai|Hybrid AI]] (HIL) models that combine imitation with adaptive [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]] to achieve robust human-like performance in high-stakes physical tasks.
