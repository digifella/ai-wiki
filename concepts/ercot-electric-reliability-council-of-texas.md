# Ercot Electric Reliability Council Of Texas

ERCOT ([[entities/ercot|Electric Reliability Council of Texas]]) is the independent system operator (ISO) responsible for managing electricity transmission and ensuring reliable power distribution across most of Texas. Established in 1971, ERCOT operates as a non-profit organization overseeing the interconnected transmission grid that serves approximately 26 million customers in central, eastern, and northern portions of the state. The organization does not own generation facilities, transmission lines, or distribution [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]]; instead, it coordinates the operation of the grid, balancing supply and demand in real-time to maintain reliability.

## Scope and Jurisdiction

ERCOT manages the [[concepts/flow|flow]] of electric power to over 90% of Texas's electric load. Its jurisdiction covers the majority of the state, excluding areas served by municipal utilities, electric cooperatives, and a few small regions near the borders with Louisiana and New Mexico, which are connected to other interconnections. The grid is largely isolated from the two other major North American power [[concepts/grids|grids]], the Eastern Interconnection and the Western Interconnection, to avoid federal [[concepts/regulation|regulation]] under the Federal Energy Regulatory Commission (FERC).

## Operational Structure

As a non-profit entity, ERCOT functions as a neutral coordinator rather than a utility. It does not own the physical assets of the [[concepts/electricity-grid|power system]] but relies on market [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and operational protocols to manage the flow of electricity. The organization oversees a competitive wholesale electricity market where generators sell power and retail providers purchase it. ERCOT's primary mandate is to ensure the reliability of the grid, particularly during periods of high demand or [[concepts/extreme-weather-events|extreme weather events]], by monitoring system conditions and issuing alerts or [[concepts/recommendations|directives]] to market participants.
