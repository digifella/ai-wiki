# Naive But Good Natured AI

Naive But Good Natured AI is a [[concepts/coding|software development]] technique for [[concepts/mcps|AI coding agents]] named after the character [[entities/ralph-wiggum|Ralph Wiggum]] from the animated series *The Simpsons*. The technique, popularized by the YouTube channel [[entities/gary-explains|Gary Explains]], applies Ralph's characteristic approach to [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]]—straightforward, well-intentioned, but lacking sophistication—as a deliberate design pattern for AI systems.

## Core Approach

The technique involves building [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] that operate with simple, direct logic rather than complex optimization strategies. Like Ralph Wiggum's innocent and earnest attempts at tasks, Naive But Good Natured AI systems prioritize transparency and benevolence in their decision-making processes over computational elegance. This approach can be valuable in [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] and alignment with human intentions matter more than raw efficiency.

## Application

Developers using this technique design AI systems that follow straightforward behavioral loops and rules, making their actions predictable and easy to understand. The method trades algorithmic sophistication for clarity of [[concepts/motivation|purpose]], which can reduce unexpected behaviors and make systems easier to debug and maintain. This approach is particularly relevant for [[concepts/safe-ai-use|AI safety]] considerations, where well-understood and benign agent behavior is preferable to opaque optimization.
