# Diagnostic Accuracy

**Diagnostic accuracy** refers to the ability of a diagnostic test or clinical process to correctly identify the presence or absence of a condition. In the context of emerging technologies, maintaining high accuracy requires rigorous oversight to mitigate risks associated with automation and algorithmic bias.

## Key Considerations

*   **Human-in-the-[[concepts/loop|Loop]] Necessity**: Despite debates regarding [[concepts/doctor-replacement]], human oversight remains critical to validate AI-generated data and prevent catastrophic errors.
*   **Scribe Error Mitigation**: AI scribes may hallucinate or invent clinical details (e.g., drug histories), directly compromising the [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of the diagnostic record.
*   **Oversight Protocols**: [[concepts/continuous-monitoring|Continuous monitoring]] of AI outputs is required to ensure they align with clinical reality and do not introduce false positives or negatives.
