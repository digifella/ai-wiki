# Evernote Pricing

Evernote's recent shift in its [[concepts/pricing-model|pricing model]] has prompted significant discussion and concern among users. This page provides a comprehensive overview of the new [[concepts/pricing-structure|pricing structure]] and insights into user reactions.

## New Tiered Pricing Structure
- Introduction of premium tiers with enhanced features.
- [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]] to [[entities/storage|storage]] limits based on subscription level.
- Emphasis on business solutions for teams.

### User Reactions
- Outrage over perceived increase in costs.
- Confusion regarding changes to feature sets.
- [[concepts/anxiety|Anxiety]] about the impact on personal and [[concepts/professional-use|professional use]].

## Migration Guidance
- Options for users considering a switch to alternative [[concepts/note-taking-software|note-taking apps]].
- Steps for [[concepts/data-management|data export]] from Evernote, including:
  - Use of third-party tools for streamlined migration.
  - Manual transfer process with potential file size limitations.
- Strategies for maintaining functionality without the need for premium subscriptions.
