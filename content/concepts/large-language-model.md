# Large Language Model

[[concepts/large-language-models|Large language models (LLMs)]] are advanced AI systems designed to understand and generate human-like text based on vast amounts of [[concepts/training-data|training data]]. They have revolutionized fields such as [[concepts/natural-language-processing|natural language processing]], conversational [[concepts/agents|agents]], [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], and more.

### Key Features
- **Versatility:** Capable of handling a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of tasks from [[concepts/summarization|summarization]] to translation.
- **[[concepts/contextual-understanding|Contextual Understanding]]:** Ability to comprehend context in long-form text due to their deep understanding of language patterns.
- **Scalability:** Can be fine-tuned for specific [[concepts/software|applications]] or scaled up for broader [[concepts/use-cases|use cases]].
- **[[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval Augmented Generation]] (RAG):** A technique that augments an LLM's generation process by providing it with relevant, up-to-date information from external sources, enhancing [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and context.
- **Data Visualization Backend:** Utilized as the core engine in applications like Microsoft's [[entities/data-formulator|Data Formulator]] to automate the creation of rich, complex data visualizations.
