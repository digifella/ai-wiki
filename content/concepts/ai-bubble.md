# AI Bubble

## Definition
Speculative expansion of asset valuations within the [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] sector, characterized by capital inflows disproportionate to fundamental revenue or utility, sustained by hype narratives, and vulnerable to rapid correction upon liquidity shocks, valuation repricing, or structural market failures.

## Key Events & Indicators
- **2026-05-13**: Sector-wide correction signals intensify. [[entities/anthropic]] [[concepts/faces|faces]] abrupt private valuation collapse, with [[entities/openai]] and [[entities/xai]] exhibiting parallel depreciation trends.
- [[lab-notes/2026-05-13-Anthropic-Stock-Legal-Voiding-of-Unauthorized-Secondary|Anthropic Stock: Legal Voiding of Unauthorized Secondary Market Transfers]] documents legal enforcement voiding unauthorized secondary transfers, disrupting price discovery and revealing fragility in private market structures.
- Analyst [[entities/matthew-berman]] interprets these valuation drops and liquidity constraints as definitive evidence that the "AI bubble has finally popped."

## Mechanisms & Risks
- **Secondary Market Fragility**: Reliance on unauthorized or loosely regulated secondary transfers for liquidity; legal challenges can instantly invalidate valuations and block exits.
- **Contagion Risk**: Valuation corrections in top-tier [[entities/labs|labs]] ([[entities/anthropic]], [[entities/openai]], [[entities/xai]]) propagate to broader VC ecosystems and infrastructure plays.
- **Liquidity Shocks**: Inability to trade or exit positions due to legal voiding or transfer restrictions amplifies downward pressure on paper wealth.
