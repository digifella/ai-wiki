# Media Management

**Media management** refers to the systematic organization, storage, [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]], and [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] of digital assets (images, video, audio, documents). Effective strategies ensure [[concepts/data-integrity|data integrity]], [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]], and efficient workflow across devices.

## Core Principles
- **Centralization:** Consolidating assets into unified libraries to prevent fragmentation.
- **[[concepts/metadata|Metadata]] Standardization:** Utilizing consistent tagging, [[concepts/file-naming-conventions|naming conventions]], and EXIF/IPTC data for searchability.
- **Redundancy:** Implementing the 3-2-1 backup rule (3 copies, 2 media types, 1 offsite).
- **Lifecycle Management:** Regular archiving of inactive assets and purging of duplicates.
