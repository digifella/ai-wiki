# Patient Needs

**Patient needs** represent the comprehensive set of clinical, emotional, informational, and logistical requirements essential for optimal health outcomes and care experiences. Effective [[concepts/health|healthcare]] delivery requires the systematic identification, prioritization, and [[concepts/happiness|fulfillment]] of these needs through [[concepts/personalised-care|Patient-Centered Care]] frameworks and clinical workflows.

## Core Dimensions
- **Clinical Needs**: Medical diagnoses, treatments, medication management, and symptom control.
- **Informational Needs**: Access to understandable health information, treatment options, and prognosis details.
- **Psychosocial Needs**: Emotional support, [[concepts/anxiety|anxiety]] reduction, and [[concepts/human-connection|social connection]].
- **Systemic Needs**: Navigation assistance, scheduling ease, and financial [[concepts/opacity|transparency]].

## Assessment and Identification
Identifying patient needs relies on direct communication, standardized surveys, and [[concepts/empirical-evidence|observational data]]. Failure to accurately assess these needs leads to poor adherence, dissatisfaction, and suboptimal health outcomes. Tools for assessment include Shared [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]] and [[concepts/digital-health|digital health]] monitoring.

## Digital Health and Visualization
Modern [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] systems increasingly utilize digital tools to map and address patient needs. Visualization dashboards play a critical role in translating complex [[concepts/patient-data|patient data]] into [[concepts/actionable-insights|actionable insights]] for providers.

- **Rapid Development & Telehealth**: Recent initiatives highlight the [[concepts/value|importance]] of rapid development of visualization dashboards to support telehealth and [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|remote patient monitoring]] [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Dixit---Rapid-development-of-visualization-dashboards|Dixit - Rapid development of visualization dashboards]].
- **[[concepts/conscious-thought|Awareness]] and Process**: Integrating visualization tools enhances provider awareness of patient status, streamlining the development of care processes that respond dynamically to changing patient needs.
- **Multihospital Systems**: Large-scale implementations, such as those documented in Multihospital healthcare systems, demonstrate how [[concepts/information-visualization|data visualization]] can bridge gaps in communication and [[concepts/coordination|care coordination]] across dispersed facilities.
