### Summary
Here's a summary of the video content:

The video provides a Photoshop tutorial by Colin Smith, founder of PhotoshopCAFE, on changing the color of an object in a difficult image. Smith demonstrates how to achieve results that surpass AI and even challenges AI at the end to see who can do it fastest. The technique works on any image, allowing for the color to be changed to any desired color, including black or white.

Smith covers three different levels of difficulty for changing object colors in Photoshop: Level 1 (easy), Level 2 (intermediate), and Level 3 (difficult). He explains that Level 3 involves images where colors in the object overlap with similar colors in the background, skin tones, or hair, requiring the use of masks and color range adjustments for precision.

The tutorial demonstrates using Hue/Saturation adjustments, eyedropper tools, and color range sliders to modify hues, saturation, and lightness. Smith also compares manual editing with AI-generated color changes, highlighting the flexibility, control, and lack of credit consumption or resolution loss associated with manual editing in Photoshop.

In conclusion, Smith emphasizes that manual editing in Photoshop offers greater control, flexibility, and resolution preservation compared to AI-generated color changes, making it a preferred method for complex image editing tasks.
