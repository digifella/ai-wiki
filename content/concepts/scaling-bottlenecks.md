# Scaling Bottlenecks

Constraints that impede the expansion of system performance, capacity, or intelligence in large-scale computational models.

## Primary Vectors
- [[concepts/compute|Compute]]-availability: [[concepts/hardware|Hardware]] shortages or inaccuracies in provisioning GPU/TPU clusters.
- Data-[[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]]: The "data wall" caused by the exhaustion of high-quality, human-generated [[concepts/training|training]] [[concepts/training-data|datasets]].
- [[concepts/memory|Memory]]-bandwidth: The physical limitation of data transfer speeds between [[concepts/memory|memory]] and [[concepts/central-processing-units|processors]].
- Infrastructure-limits: Constraints involving [[concepts/electricity-grid|power grid]] capacity, cooling, and network latency.
