### Summary
The video features a discussion between host Professor Hannah Fry and guests Maria Eckstein and Kim Stachenfeld, senior research scientists at Google DeepMind. The conversation focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence and neuroscience, specifically how AI can be used to model and understand the human brain.

The speakers discuss the historical context of AI and neuroscience, noting that while AI was once in its infancy and looked to the brain for inspiration, the relationship has now reversed. Today, researchers use AI as a tool to help figure out the algorithms of the human mind. The conversation explores the challenges of using AI to model complex systems like the brain, the limitations of traditional models, and the potential of hybrid modeling approaches.

The speakers also touch on the concept of reward learning and how it applies to both AI and neuroscience. They discuss the differences between simple models and complex neural networks, highlighting the trade-off between predictive power and interpretability. The discussion concludes with a reflection on the future of neuroscience and AI, emphasizing the potential for AI to uncover new insights into the human brain and behavior.

#### Description
The human brain remains one of the greatest unsolved mysteries in science. We have roughly 86 billion neurons firing in a way that somehow produces intelligence, and yet the brain is still largely a black box. In the early days of AI, researchers looked to the brain for clues about how to build smarter machines. But today, that relationship has been flipped on its head.
