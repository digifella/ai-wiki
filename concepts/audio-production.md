# Audio Production

## Overview
**[[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]] production** encompasses the creation, recording, mixing, and mastering of sound. Modern workflows increasingly integrate **[[concepts/generative-ai|Generative AI]]** to accelerate [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]], particularly in **[[concepts/text-to-speech]] (TTS)** and [[concepts/multilingual-speech-synthesis|voice synthesis]].

## AI-Driven Voice Synthesis
The integration of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] and specialized audio models allows for high-fidelity, customizable [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|voice generation]] without traditional studio recording.

### Gemini Audio Capabilities
Recent advancements in **[[entities/google-deepmind]]**'s ecosystem have introduced **[[entities/google-gemini|Gemini]] Audio**, a model focused on [[concepts/custom-voice-generation|custom voice generation]] and control. Key features include:

*   **Custom Voice Generation:** Ability to create unique voice profiles from input data.
*   **Expressive Control:** [[concepts/granular-control|Granular control]] over tone, emotion, and pacing in generated speech.
*   **Diverse Output:** Generation of varied and natural-sounding voice content suitable for production needs.
*   **[[concepts/api-integration|API Integration]]:** Accessible via the **[[concepts/gemini-25-flash|Gemini 2.5 Flash]]** API for scalable implementation.
