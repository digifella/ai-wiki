# Trainee

A trainee is an individual engaged in structured training or education within an institutional framework, typically working toward professional qualification or skill development. This role occupies an intermediate position between students and fully qualified practitioners, combining theoretical learning with practical experience under supervision. In academic and professional contexts, trainees work alongside more experienced practitioners while completing the formal requirements for their chosen field.

Trainees are generally required to contribute meaningfully to the operational activities of their host institution while receiving guidance. The position serves as a transitional phase where theoretical knowledge is applied to real-world scenarios, allowing for the assessment of competency and readiness for independent practice.

In the context of the College, trainees are recognized as a distinct stakeholder group within the governance structure. A communication regarding allegations about governance matters was sent to both Fellows and trainees, indicating their inclusion in institutional discourse and oversight processes. This reflects the broader trend of integrating trainees into the professional community's administrative and ethical frameworks.
