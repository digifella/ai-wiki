# Cognizant AI Lab

Cognizant [[concepts/ai-lab|AI Lab]] is a research division of Cognizant that focuses on advancing [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] capabilities. The lab conducts research and development in areas related to [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) and [[concepts/ai-automation-agents|AI automation]].

## Research Focus

In November 2025, Cognizant AI Lab published a research paper titled "[[concepts/zero-errors|Solving a Million-Step LLM Task with Zero Errors]]." This work addressed the challenge of enabling language models to successfully complete extended task sequences without errors, a significant technical problem in deploying LLMs for complex, multi-step operations. The research demonstrated approaches for improving [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] and accuracy in long-running LLM tasks.

The lab's work has included exploration of [[concepts/ai-automation-workflows|AI automation]] frameworks, including investigations into [[concepts/event-based-automation|action-based automation]] systems for [[concepts/business-applications|business applications]] and event response [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. This research contributes to the broader development of practical [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] capable of executing complex, extended workflows with high reliability.
