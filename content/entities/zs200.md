# ZS200

The ZS200 is a superzoom [[concepts/point-and-shoot-camera|compact camera]] from Panasonic's ZS series. It belongs to a line of [[concepts/point-and-shoot-cameras|point-and-shoot cameras]] designed to offer extended [[concepts/optical-zoom|optical zoom]] capabilities in a portable form factor.

## Context and Market Position

The ZS200 has been succeeded by later models in the ZS series, including the ZS300. According to a PetaPixel video review, the progression from earlier ZS models to the ZS300 represents an extended development cycle with relatively modest technical upgrades relative to the time elapsed and cost to consumers. The superzoom category itself has remained a niche segment in the compact camera market.
