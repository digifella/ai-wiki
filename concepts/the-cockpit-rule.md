# The Cockpit Rule

The Cockpit Rule is a framework for skill development in artificial intelligence presented by Jeff Su in his video "4 Skills to Get Ahead (Beyond Basic Prompts)." The concept addresses a fundamental shift in how AI competency functions within professional environments. As basic AI usage—such as simple prompting—has become widely accessible and expected, it no longer serves as a meaningful differentiator in the job market. The rule suggests that proficiency with foundational AI tools is now a baseline requirement rather than a competitive advantage.

## Core Principle

The metaphor draws from aviation: just as modern pilots spend most flight time on autopilot rather than manual control, AI users should focus on automating routine tasks rather than performing them manually. This allows professionals to concentrate their efforts on higher-level decision-making and strategic work. The rule implies that true advancement comes not from mastering basic AI capabilities, but from developing skills that complement and leverage AI effectively within professional workflows.

## Career Implications

For career advancement, the framework suggests that professionals need to develop competencies beyond simple prompt engineering. This includes understanding when and how to apply AI tools appropriately, integrating AI into existing processes, and maintaining critical judgment over AI-generated outputs. The ability to work effectively alongside AI systems—knowing their limitations and appropriate use cases—becomes the distinguishing factor in competitive job markets where basic AI literacy is already assumed.
