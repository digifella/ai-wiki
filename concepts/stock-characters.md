# Stock Characters

Stock characters are pre-designed, reusable templates available within video creation platforms such as Vyond Go. These assets allow users to populate animated videos without the need to design characters from scratch, thereby streamlining the production process. They are sourced from platform libraries and offer a variety of appearances, styles, and demographic representations to suit different project requirements.

In the video creation workflow, these characters serve as foundational elements for script-based animation. Users can select specific stock characters and upload scripts before generating a video. This integration enables the automatic synchronization of character actions and dialogue with the provided audio or text input, reducing manual animation time.

The utility of stock characters lies in their ability to provide consistent visual identity across multiple projects. By utilizing standardized assets, creators can maintain brand coherence while accelerating the initial stages of video development. This approach is particularly effective for educational content, corporate training, and marketing materials where rapid turnaround and professional quality are prioritized.
