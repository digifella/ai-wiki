# Digital Twin Cloning

Creating a digital replica of a person using AI for realistic interaction, combining visual and [[concepts/tone|voice]] mimicry to avoid the [[concepts/uncanny-valley]].

## Key Tools & Workflow
- **[[entities/heygen|HeyGen]]**: Generates photorealistic [[concepts/ai-avatars|AI avatars]] with facial animation and lip-syncing from video input
- **[[entities/eleven-labs|ElevenLabs]]**: Clones voice patterns from [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] samples for natural-sounding synthetic speech
- **Filming Protocol**: Requires 30-60 seconds of [[concepts/excellence|high-quality]] video (even lighting, neutral background) to train models
- **Synchronization**: Merges [[concepts/heygen|HeyGen]]'s visual output with ElevenLabs' [[concepts/audio|audio]] to create cohesive digital twins

## Primary Use Case
- **Content Efficiency**: Replaces repetitive human recording for scalable [[concepts/video-resource|video content]] (training, marketing, personal [[concepts/branding|branding]])
- **Resource Savings**: Eliminates need for physical presence in [[concepts/video-creation|video production]] while maintaining personal brand authenticity
