# Servant Leadership

**Servant [[concepts/leadership|Leadership]]** is a leadership [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] in which the main goal of the leader is to serve. This is in direct [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] with traditional leadership roles where the leader's main focus is the profit of the company. The servant leader shares power, puts the needs of [[entities/employees|employees]] first, and helps people develop and perform as highly as possible.

## Core Principles
- [[concepts/empathy]] and [[concepts/active-listening|active listening]]
- Stewardship and commitment to the growth of people
- Community building and [[concepts/conscious-thought|awareness]]
- Foresight and healing
