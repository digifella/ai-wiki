# Imaging USA 2026

## Overview
Imaging USA is an annual event hosted by Professional Photographers of [[entities/america|America]] (PPA), bringing together photographers and imaging professionals from around the world to share knowledge, trends, and new technologies in photography. The 2026 edition continued this tradition with a focus on leveraging [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] within industry-leading [[concepts/software|software]] such as [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]] and [[entities/adobe-lightroom|Lightroom]].

## Key Highlights
- **Adobe's [[entities/julieanne-kost|Julieanne Kost]]** presented on ways photographers can boost their productivity using AI features.
- Live sessions covered innovative techniques for [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], post-processing, and workflow management in digital photography.
