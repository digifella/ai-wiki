# Matthew B

Clawdbot, also known as OpenClaw, is a customizable AI assistant framework designed for individual users seeking greater control over their assistant's behavior and configuration. Unlike commercial AI services that operate with fixed parameters and standardized responses, Clawdbot prioritizes user customization, enabling individuals to define how the assistant processes information, generates responses, and adapts to specific workflows or preferences.

The system is built around a modular architecture that allows users to configure multiple aspects of the assistant's operation. This structure supports the setup and configuration processes necessary for tailoring the AI to specific needs, providing a flexible alternative to rigid, pre-defined commercial solutions.
