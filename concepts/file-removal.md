# File Removal

File removal refers to the process of deleting or exporting files stored within Evernote. This became a widespread concern in April 2026 following Evernote's announcement of significant changes to its pricing structure. As the platform modified its pricing model, many users reassessed their storage usage and account configurations to determine whether to maintain their subscriptions or reduce their data footprint.

## Deletion Methods

Users can remove files from Evernote through several approaches. Direct deletion involves selecting individual notes or attachments and permanently removing them from the account. Evernote also provides export functionality that allows users to download their data in standard formats before deletion, enabling them to preserve important information while reducing their stored footprint on the platform. The choice between deletion and export depends on whether users wish to retain local copies of their data.

## Context and User Response

The interest in file removal coincided with broader changes in Evernote's service offerings and cost structures. Users with large data libraries faced decisions about storage tier downgrades or subscription cancellations, making file management an important practical consideration. Understanding removal options became relevant for users seeking to align their account usage with revised pricing tiers or those transitioning away from the platform entirely.
