# The Simpsons

**[[concepts/the-simpsons|The Simpsons]]** is an American animated sitcom created by Matt Groening for the Fox Broadcasting Company. It is the longest-running American primetime television series and the longest-running animated series in American television history. The show is a satirical depiction of working-class life epitomized by the Simpson family, which consists of Homer, Marge, Bart, Lisa, and Maggie.

## Overview
Set in the fictional town of Springfield, the series critiques American culture, society, the media, and the human condition. It has been widely acclaimed for its longevity, cultural impact, and ability to balance humor with social commentary.
