# Master Of Editing

[[concepts/master-of-editing|Master Of Editing]] is a course taught by [[entities/will|Will]] that focuses on the [[concepts/catalog|Lightroom catalog]] system and [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] techniques. The course covers practical approaches to organizing and managing photo libraries within [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]'s catalog framework, alongside editing workflows for processing [[concepts/images|images]].

## Course Content

The curriculum includes instruction on [[concepts/dark-and-moody-photo-processing|dark and moody photo processing]] methods, with emphasis on techniques for dramatic [[concepts/photo-enhancement|photo enhancement]]. The course teaches how to use Lightroom's tools and systems to achieve specific aesthetic outcomes in post-production work.
