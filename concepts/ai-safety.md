# Guardrails
**Guardrails** refer to [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and [[concepts/policies|policies]] designed to ensure the safe and ethical use of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] (AI) technologies. This concept has gained prominence as AI's capabilities expand, leading tech companies like [[concepts/unsloth-optimization|Nvidia]] to release open-source frameworks and [[entities/openai|OpenAI]] to provide consulting services. The release of [[concepts/model-customization|open-weight models]] like [[concepts/gpt-oss|GPT-OSS]] further democratizes access while raising safety questions.

## Recent Developments in Governance and Monitoring
- **[[concepts/ai-oversight|Anthropic Observer Agents]]:** Implementation of real-time [[concepts/monitoring-systems|monitoring systems]] to track AI behavior and ensure [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with safety protocols [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]].
- **Demis Hassabis's AGI Framework:** [[lab-notes/2026-07-18-Demis-Hassabis-Building-New-Institutions-for-AGI-Governa|Demis Hassabis: Building New Institutions for AGI Governance and Frontier AI Standards]] proposes the creation of new institutions to establish standards for [[concepts/frontier-ai|frontier AI]] and AGI. This initiative aims to address the [[concepts/governance|governance]] challenges associated with the dawning of a new age in AI capabilities.
- **Regulatory Impact:** Ongoing discourse emphasizes the tension between necessary safety guardrails and the potential negative impacts of [[concepts/government-regulation|government regulation]] on [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] and economic growth.
