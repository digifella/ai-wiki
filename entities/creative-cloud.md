# Creative Cloud

[[concepts/adobe|Creative Cloud]] is [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s subscription-based platform launched to transition the company from perpetual [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] to cloud-based [[concepts/service-delivery|service delivery]]. The platform provides access to a comprehensive suite of creative applications and services designed for professional designers, photographers, video editors, and other creative [[entities/employees|workers]]. Rather than purchasing individual software licenses, users pay a recurring subscription fee to access the full range of tools and [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud services]].

## Core Applications

The platform includes Adobe's flagship applications across multiple disciplines, such as [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] for [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], Illustrator for vector [[concepts/webgpu|graphics]], and [[entities/premiere-pro|Premiere Pro]] for video production. These tools are integrated with cloud [[entities/storage|storage]] and collaboration features, allowing users to sync files across devices and share projects with teams. Recent [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] have introduced beta features like the [[concepts/harmonize-feature|Harmonize tool]], which aims to streamline [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]] within the ecosystem.
