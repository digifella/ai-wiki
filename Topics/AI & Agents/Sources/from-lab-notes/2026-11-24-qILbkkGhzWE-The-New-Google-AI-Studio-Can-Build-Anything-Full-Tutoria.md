#### Description
✉️ Collaborations → antonina@youraiworkflow.co

Google AI Studio is a whole new tool now: every model is new, it builds Android apps with login and a database, and it plugs straight into Google Sheets, Maps and Tasks. In this hands-on Google AI Studio tutorial, I vibe code real work apps with Gemini 4 Neon and Hydrogen, one prompt per app.

1️⃣ Every model is new: Gemini 4 Neon, Hydrogen, Argon and Carbon

2️⃣ Live invoice cards from Google Sheets (Google Sheets preset)
"Turn my Google Sheet of client invoices into a beautiful page of invoice cards, focused on how much money is still left to be paid. Each card shows the client, the amount and the due date, and late ones turn red. When I click a card, give it a big PAID stamp and drop the total. Keep the app and the sheet in sync both ways, so a change in one shows up in the other."
Pick a mode before you send: Plan to brainstorm, Default, or Max for the most effort. The app makes you sign in with Google, so your data stays private when you publish. Share it with your team from Share.

3️⃣ An Android time clock app for your team (Build an Android app preset)
"Build a time clock app for my small team. Everyone signs in with Google and gets one big button: they tap Start when their shift begins and Stop when it ends, and while they're on the clock there's a big ticking timer. As the admin, I can set everyone's hourly rate and see exactly what I owe each person."
Login switches on Firebase Firestore with one click. Install the app on your phone with a USB cable: AI Studio guides you through every step. Team members don't see the manager's Team tab.

4️⃣ A trip planner on Google Maps (Google Maps preset)
"Build a trip planner where I describe how long my trip is and what I like and don't like, and it gives me a complete day-by-day itinerary on a map, with every stop in order and the travel time between them."
Choose full access: it needs billing, but small apps won't be charged. Link a paid Gemini API key so the app runs fast (about 4¢ a trip). If it then asks for a Maps API key, that's a bug: Code → .env.example → delete the Google Maps API key line → save.

5️⃣ Gemini 4 Neon vs Hydrogen: a 3D city from Google Tasks (Google Tasks preset)
"Build a 3D city that grows as I get work done in Google Tasks. Every task is an empty plot with its name on a little sign. When I tick a task off, a building rises there, bigger for the tasks I've had the longest, and overdue tasks get a small storm cloud above them."
Hydrogen finished first and everything worked. Neon looked more polished, with rain and smoother movement. Upgrade only if you care about design.

6️⃣ The Android app 14 hours later + how to share it
The clock kept running the whole time. As the manager, I clocked Michael out, set $40 an hour, and the app worked out what I owe him. Share it with your team without the Play Store: Files by Google → Apps → ⋮ → Share. To publish on Google Play, click Publish in AI Studio (you'll need a Google Play developer account, $25 one time). Comment "Android" for a full publishing tutorial.
