# Itinerary Planning

[[concepts/travel-planning|Itinerary planning]] is the process of organizing and documenting the key details of a trip, including transportation, accommodation, and scheduling. It serves as a centralized reference for travelers to track bookings, confirm arrangements, and maintain a clear timeline of their journey.

## Core Elements

An itinerary typically includes flight bookings with reference numbers and departure times, arrival details, and pre-arranged accommodation. For a trip to Istanbul in September 2025, this would encompass the outbound flight from [[entities/tk1849-airline|Melbourne to Bangkok]] on 4 September, arrival in Istanbul on 5 September [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] 5:15 AM, and hotel accommodation at a selected property.

## Practical Purpose

By consolidating booking confirmations, flight references, and hotel details in one location, an itinerary reduces the likelihood of missed connections or forgotten reservations. It provides a quick reference during travel and can be shared with relevant parties such as accommodation providers or travel companions. Regular updates to the itinerary help track changes and ensure all arrangements remain current.
