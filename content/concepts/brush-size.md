# Brush Size

The diameter of a Brush Tool in digital [[concepts/art|art]] [[concepts/software|applications]], controlling stroke width and detail level. [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]] are critical for precision work and broad strokes.

## Key Adjustment Methods in Adobe Photoshop
- **Bracket Keys:** Tap `[` or `]` to decrease/increase size
- **Shift + Bracket Keys:** Hold **Shift** + `[`/`]` to adjust edge **hardness**
- **[[concepts/hud-heads-up-display|Heads Up Display]] ([[concepts/hud|HUD]]):**
  - *[[entities/mac|Mac]]:* Hold **[[concepts/power|Control]] + Option** and drag
  - *[[entities/windows|Windows]]:* Hold **Control + Alt + Right-click** and drag
