# Mask Refinement

Mask refinement is the iterative process of improving and adjusting [[concepts/layer-masks|layer masks]] in digital [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], particularly within [[concepts/photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]. After creating an initial mask through selection tools, brush painting, or automated methods, refinement involves modifying the mask's properties—such as feathering, density, and edge definition—to achieve greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] and control over which areas of a layer are visible or hidden.

## Refinement Techniques

Common refinement approaches include adjusting mask edge contrast using the Refine Edge dialog, applying feathering to soften transitions, and selectively painting on the mask with varying brush opacity to create gradual falloffs. Users may also employ threshold adjustments, level corrections applied directly to the mask channel, or automated edge detection features to improve accuracy. The process typically involves zooming in to inspect problem areas, making incremental adjustments, and testing the results against the underlying image.

## Workflow Integration

Mask refinement typically occurs after initial mask creation but before final output, forming part of the broader [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] cycle common to digital image editing. This approach acknowledges that first-pass masks rarely achieve optimal results and that systematic adjustment—testing visibility at different zoom levels and layer opacities—often yields more polished outcomes than attempting perfect masks in a single pass.
