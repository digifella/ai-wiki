# AI video automation

The use of AI-generated video tools and automated programming workflows to streamline the end-to-end production, editing, and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of video content at scale.

### Key Workflows & Technologies
- **Automated Generation**: Utilizing [[entities/claude-code]] and [[entities/blotato]] to transform scripts into Shorts and viral-style content in minutes.
- **[[concepts/distribution|Distribution]] Systems**: Implementing [[concepts/seo]] to automate content delivery across YouTube, Instagram, and other social ecosystems.
- **Scale**: Leveraging [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] to manage high-volume AI-generated video pipelines for maximum viewership and engagement.
