# Claude Ai

[[concepts/2026-04-08-anthropic|Claude AI]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by Anthropic, an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] safety-focused company based in [[entities/san-francisco|San Francisco]]. The model processes natural language inputs to generate text, code, and other outputs across a wide range of language understanding and generation tasks. Claude operates through various interfaces including [[concepts/saas|web applications]], [[concepts/open-standard-protocols|APIs]], and integrations with third-party platforms, making it accessible for both direct user interaction and programmatic use.

## Core Capabilities

Claude is designed to perform tasks including [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], [[concepts/summarization|summarization]], analysis, and [[concepts/coding|coding]] assistance. In the context of creative workflows, it is utilized for generating presentations and managing [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] workflows for video production. These capabilities allow users to leverage the model for complex [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] and automation tasks within professional environments.
