# Member Checking

**Member checking** (also known as *respondent validation*) is a technique in [[concepts/qualitative-research]] where findings, interpretations, or conclusions are returned to participants for [[concepts/verification|verification]]. This process enhances Credibility and Trustworthiness by ensuring the [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]]'s interpretation aligns with the participants' lived experiences.

## Key Functions
- **Validity**: Confirms accuracy of data interpretation.
- **Participant Empowerment**: Gives [[concepts/tone|voice]] to participants in the [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] construction.
- **[[concepts/bug-fixing|Error Correction]]**: Identifies misunderstandings or misrepresentations early in the analysis [[concepts/phase|phase]].
