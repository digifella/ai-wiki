## The Process

The typical [[concepts/zero|zero]] [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]] involves feeding an existing or AI-generated [[concepts/audio|audio]] podcast into specialized software that automatically generates accompanying visuals. The AI analyzes the audio content and produces corresponding video elements, arranges them in sequence, and synchronizes them with the audio track. This eliminates the need for manual editing, clip selection, or timeline arrangement that characterizes traditional video production.
