# Pi Series

The concept of a "Pi series" refers to any mathematical sequence that converges to the value of Pi (π). Historically, various mathematicians and scientists have contributed to the development of numerous formulas for calculating π. These include infinite series expansions, continued fractions, and other [[concepts/numerical-analysis|numerical methods]].

## Historical Development

The study of Pi has a long history dating back to ancient civilizations such as the Babylonians and Egyptians. Over time, mathematicians have developed increasingly sophisticated methods for estimating π with greater precision.
