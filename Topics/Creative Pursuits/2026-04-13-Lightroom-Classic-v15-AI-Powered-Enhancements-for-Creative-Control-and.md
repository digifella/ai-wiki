## Lightroom Classic v15: AI-Powered Enhancements for Creative Control and Workflow
**Clip title:** 15 New Features in [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] Classic (v15)
**Author / channel:** Julieanne Kost
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dKXqg50v1sA

### Summary
[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]] Classic version 15 introduces a suite of new features and significant enhancements designed to streamline workflows, expand creative control, and improve overall performance for photographers. The updates focus heavily on AI-powered content removal, refined [[concepts/color-and-masking-adjustments|color and masking adjustments]], and better [[concepts/integration|integration]] with other Adobe products and camera systems.

Key enhancements begin with [[concepts/ai-driven-distraction-removal|AI-driven distraction removal]]. The new [[concepts/automatic-dust-removal|Automatic Dust Removal]] feature intelligently identifies and removes dust spots from [[concepts/images|images]], while the "Visualize Spots" tool aids in pre-analysis. For more complex elements, an improved [Reflections Removal](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Reflections_Removal) model leverages advanced AI to better detect and eliminate glare from surfaces like plate glass, even reconstructing blown-out areas and preserving intricate textures more effectively than previous iterations. Furthermore, the "Remove" tool's "Detect Objects" capability now intelligently includes [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] and reflections when selecting and removing elements, leading to cleaner and more natural results in content-aware fills.

Creative [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] see new precision with the Point Color tool's "Variance" slider, allowing users to consolidate similar [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] for a smoother appearance or increase [[concepts/color-contrast|color contrast]] within a selected [[concepts/range|range]]. Landscape [[concepts/masking|masking]] also receives an upgrade, now capable of isolating and adjusting "Snow" with dedicated masks, in addition to existing Sky and Water selections. This version also includes eight new Adaptive Landscape presets for quick, intelligent edits. For workflow efficiency, users can now zoom and pan within the Crop tool using the Navigator panel and dedicated shortcuts, even while using the straighten tool. Additionally, precise [HDR Headroom control](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HDR_Headroom_control) offers finer adjustments in 1/10 stop increments for high [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]] displays.

Beyond [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], several backend and compatibility improvements have been implemented. When exporting [[concepts/files|files]] for external editing in [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]], [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] now supports new SDR and HDR [[concepts/color-spaces|color spaces]], including [Rec 2020](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Rec_2020) and [HDR P3](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/HDR_P3)/Rec 2020, and allows for custom display profiles. Managing image files is made easier with a new alert system that quickly identifies and helps relink missing sub-folders in the [[concepts/library-module|Library module]]. Photographers requiring exact timing can now refine photo capture times down to the millisecond, crucial for syncing files from multiple cameras. [[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR) Sidecar support has also been expanded; proprietary raw file adjustments are now stored in a separate .ACR file alongside a smaller XMP, facilitating easier transfer of edits. Library and [[concepts/smart-collections|Smart Collections]] benefit from new filtering options, including shared collection engagement (likes and comments) and specific manual or AI-driven distraction removal types. Finally, video and slideshow exports can now be rendered in higher resolutions, up to 4K, significantly improving output quality.

Overall, Lightroom Classic 15 prioritizes a faster, more intuitive, and more powerful editing experience. [[concepts/speed-enhancements|Performance enhancements]] ensure smoother operation across various functions, including hovering over presets, cropping, using removal tools, adjusting sliders, improved [[concepts/memory-management|memory management]] on [[entities/windows|Windows]], and faster image loading in the [[concepts/develop-module|Develop module]]'s filmstrip. These updates collectively enhance both the creative possibilities and the practical efficiency of managing and processing images.
