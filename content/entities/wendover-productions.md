# Wendover Productions

Wendover Productions is a YouTube channel dedicated to producing educational video content that analyzes the economic and geopolitical implications of global events. The channel examines complex topics by breaking down how specific incidents, [[concepts/policies|policies]], or geographic factors influence markets, trade, and international relations.

## Content Focus

The channel has produced notable analysis on topics including the economic impact of potential disruptions to critical global infrastructure. One example is a video examining how conflicts involving Iran could affect oil markets through the [[entities/strait-of-hormuz|Strait of Hormuz]], a critical chokepoint through which a significant portion of global oil trade passes. The channel's approach typically involves tracing the chain of economic consequences that flow from geopolitical events.

## Approach

Wendover Productions is characterized by its methodical examination of cause-and-effect [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] in global economics and [[concepts/geopolitics|geopolitics]]. Rather than focusing on breaking news or immediate commentary, the channel produces in-depth [[concepts/explanations|explanations]] of systemic issues and their broader implications for markets and [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]].
