# Lubrication system

A fundamental subsystem within an [[concepts/internal-combustion-engine]] designed to reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]], minimize wear, and assist in [[concepts/heat-dissipation|heat dissipation]] by maintaining a hydrodynamic film between moving components.

### Critical Importance
- **Engine Survival:** Maintaining consistent [[concepts/oil-pressure|Oil Pressure]] is the primary factor in preventing catastrophic mechanical failure.
- **Failure Detection:** Warning Lights serve as the critical interface for detecting pressure drops that threaten engine [[concepts/integrity|integrity]].
