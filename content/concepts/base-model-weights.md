# Base model weights

The learned [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] (numerical tensors) within a [[concepts/neural-network]] or [[concepts/large-language-model]] (LLM) that are optimized during the Pre-[[concepts/training-process|training process]]. These [[concepts/weights|weights]] encode the patterns, linguistic structures, and factual information captured from the [[concepts/training|training]] corpus.

## Core Principles
- **Knowledge [[entities/storage|Storage]]**: The [[concepts/weights|weights]] act as the foundational repository of learned information within the Model [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]].
- **[[concepts/inference-engine|Inference Engine]]**: During [[concepts/inference]], input vectors are transformed through matrix multiplications with these weights to produce outputs.
- **Adaptability**: Base weights serve as the starting point for [[concepts/fine-tuning]], [[concepts/instruction-tuning]], and Parameter-Efficient [[concepts/fine-tuning|Fine-Tuning]] (PEFT) techniques.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] [[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: The availability of high-quality weights, such as those in the [[entities/deepseek|DeepSeek]] V4 suite, drives [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in the [[concepts/open-source]] ecosystem.

## Recent Developments
- [[entities/deepseek-v4|DeepSeek V4]] represents a significant advancement in the release of [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]], [[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]] weights designed for [[concepts/algorithm-efficiency|computational efficiency]] and scale.
