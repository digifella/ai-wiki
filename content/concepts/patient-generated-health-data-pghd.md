# Patient Generated Health Data (PGHD)

Patient Generated [[concepts/health|Health]] Data refers to health and wellness information collected directly by patients or gathered through devices they use, rather than through traditional clinical settings. This data is typically captured continuously or at regular intervals using [[concepts/wearable-devices|wearable devices]] and [[concepts/embedded-sensors|embedded sensors]] that monitor various physiological [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] such as heart rate, activity levels, sleep patterns, and other vital signs relevant to individual health conditions.

## Collection and Technology

PGHD is primarily collected through wearable devices—electronic instruments worn on the body that incorporate embedded sensors to perform real-time monitoring. These devices range from smartwatches and fitness trackers to specialized medical [[concepts/internet-of-things|wearables]] designed for specific health conditions. The continuous [[entities/nature|nature]] of data collection provides a more comprehensive picture of a patient's health status compared to periodic clinical assessments, particularly valuable in [[concepts/allied-health|allied health]] settings where ongoing monitoring supports treatment and rehabilitation.

## Clinical Applications

In allied health contexts, PGHD enables healthcare providers to access objective, longitudinal data about patient behavior and physiological [[concepts/responses|responses]] outside clinical environments. This information supports more informed clinical decision-making, allows for earlier detection of changes in patient status, and facilitates more personalized treatment approaches. The data can also increase patient engagement by providing individuals with direct [[concepts/feedback|feedback]] about their [[concepts/health-metrics|health metrics]].

## Considerations

Effective implementation of PGHD systems requires careful [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to data security, [[concepts/privacy-protection|privacy protection]], and interoperability with existing health information systems. The [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of data collected by consumer-grade devices may vary, and integration of PGHD into clinical workflows requires clear protocols and validation [[concepts/open-standards|standards]].
