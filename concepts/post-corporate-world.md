# Post Corporate World

Post Corporate World is a conceptual framework describing a shift toward individual autonomy in digital infrastructure. It proposes that people manage their own data, communication, and digital services independently, rather than relying on centralized corporate platforms such as Google, Meta, or Microsoft. This approach emphasizes personal control over digital assets and interactions, marking a fundamental change in how individuals engage with technology.

The concept was detailed in a presentation on Personal AI Infrastructure, specifically focusing on the Kai agent, hosted by Clint Gibler and presented by Daniel Miessler. The discussion highlights the transition from platform-dependent ecosystems to self-hosted or personally managed digital environments. This model aims to reduce dependency on large tech corporations by empowering users to maintain ownership of their digital identity and data.

Central to this framework is the role of personal AI agents, such as Kai, which act as intermediaries between the user and external services. By managing these interactions locally or through trusted, decentralized channels, individuals can preserve privacy and agency. The framework suggests that as AI capabilities mature, the technical barriers to maintaining personal infrastructure will lower, making this shift increasingly viable for non-technical users.
