# Bhutan's Gelephu Mindfulness City & Australia's High-Speed Rail

**[[concepts/gelephu-mindfulness-city|Gelephu Mindfulness City]] (GMC)** is a special administrative region and new urban development project in southern [[entities/bhutan|Bhutan]]. It represents a shift from traditional [[concepts/gross-national-happiness|Gross National Happiness]] (GNH) metrics toward a model of "[[concepts/mindful-development|mindful development]]," aiming to balance economic growth with spiritual and environmental well-being.

## Key Characteristics
*   **Philosophy:** Addresses the modern existential crisis by integrating mindfulness into urban planning, contrasting with hyper-capitalist models like Dubai.
*   **Location:** Situated in the Gelephu region, bordering India, designed to boost cross-border trade and tourism.
*   **Core Pillars:**
    *   **Mindfulness:** Mental well-being and spiritual health are central to the city's [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] and community life.
    *   **Sustainability:** Strict environmental protections and carbon-neutral goals.
    *   **Economic Hub:** Aims to become a regional center for finance, healthcare, and education.
