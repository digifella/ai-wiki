# Sesfamilycom

SES Family is a digital health application developed at the University of Copenhagen that provides interactive modules focused on conflict resolution and interpersonal communication. The platform delivers evidence-based training through guided, interactive lessons designed to help users develop practical skills for managing disputes and relationship challenges in personal and family contexts.

## Purpose and Function

The application serves as an educational tool aimed at improving communication strategies and conflict resolution abilities among its users. By breaking down complex interpersonal dynamics into structured modules, SES Family enables individuals to work through scenarios and learn techniques applicable to their everyday relationships. The platform's modular design allows users to progress through content at their own pace while engaging with interactive learning materials.

## Development Context

As a product of the University of Copenhagen, SES Family represents an academic approach to digital health intervention, combining research-informed practices with accessible technology. The application reflects institutional commitment to translating psychological and communication research into practical tools for public use.
