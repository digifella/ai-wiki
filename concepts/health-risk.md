# Health Risk

**[[concepts/health|Health]] risk** refers to the [[concepts/probability|probability]] of an adverse health outcome occurring within a defined population or individual over a specified period. In [[concepts/population-health|public health]] contexts, it often involves balancing biological hazards against intervention costs, including [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] implications.

## Key Dimensions

- **Biological Hazard**: Pathogen transmissibility, severity, and mortality rates (e.g., [[concepts/covid-19]]).
- **Intervention Risk**: Potential harms or trade-offs introduced by [[concepts/mitigation-strategies|mitigation strategies]].
- **Privacy vs. Health Tradeoff**: The tension between data collection for disease control and individual privacy rights.

## The Risk-Risk Tradeoff in Digital Contact Tracing

Recent [[entities/tomasz-janowski|academic]] literature highlights the complexity of managing Health Risk through technological interventions, particularly [[concepts/contact-tracing|contact-tracing]] [[concepts/apps|apps]].
