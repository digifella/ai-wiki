## Gemini's New Notebooks Feature: Integrated AI Research and Chat Organization
**Clip title:** [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] and [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] Just Merged (Massive Update)
**Author / channel:** [[entities/paul-j-lipsky|Paul J Lipsky]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Y-LTxr1bv9M

### Summary
The video details a significant update to [[entities/google-gemini|Google Gemini]]: the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of Google's [[concepts/ai-research|AI research]] tool, [[concepts/notebooklm|NotebookLM]], directly into the [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] interface, now simply called "Notebooks." This update fundamentally transforms how users interact with and organize their AI conversations and research. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] highlights this as one of the biggest changes to Gemini, providing a much-needed folder and organization system that many users have been requesting.

The "Notebooks" feature, accessible from Gemini's left [[concepts/sidebar|sidebar]], displays all existing [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] projects and allows for the creation of new ones. Previously, [[entities/notebooklm|NotebookLM]] functioned as a standalone AI research tool where users could create topic-specific notebooks, add various sources ([[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], websites, YouTube videos, Google Docs, copied [[concepts/text|text]]), chat with the aggregated information, and generate different assets like [[concepts/audio-overviews|audio overviews]], [[concepts/slide-decks|slide decks]], [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]], reports, quizzes, and [[concepts/infographics|infographics]]. Now, all these functionalities are seamlessly integrated within Gemini, making it a centralized hub for AI-powered research and conversational tasks.

A key aspect of this integration is the ability to organize Gemini chats within these notebooks. Users can open any [[concepts/notebook|notebook]] and initiate a chat directly within it, with all subsequent conversations being saved to that specific notebook. This provides an organized, project-based approach to using Gemini, replacing the previous chronological list of chats. Additionally, each notebook can have "notebook [[concepts/memory|memory]]" toggled on, allowing Gemini to consider all past chats within that specific notebook when generating new [[concepts/responses|responses]], making the AI more contextually aware and creating an "evolving workspace." Users can also provide specific [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to customize Gemini's response [[concepts/tone|tone]] and [[concepts/style|style]] within each notebook, further personalizing the experience.

For practical application, the speaker suggests using [[entities/notebook-lm|NotebookLM]] for extensive research and generating various output assets, as it's optimized for those tasks. However, for ongoing, evolving conversations where context and organization are paramount, using the integrated "Notebooks" within Gemini is preferred due to its superior [[concepts/chat-organization|chat organization]] and "notebook memory" feature. While the feature is currently rolling out to Ultra and Pro users, with eventual availability for free users, it is not yet accessible on mobile devices. This merge is seen as a game-changer, solving a major pain point for Gemini users by offering a robust, intelligent project and folder system that enhances productivity and personalized interaction with the AI.
