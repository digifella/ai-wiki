# Bootcamp Sessions

Vyond Bootcamp Sessions are guided [[concepts/learning|learning]] experiences within the Vyond platform designed to help users create animated videos. These sessions provide structured tutorials and hands-on practice for both new and experienced users working with Vyond's animation tools.

## AI Assistance

Bootcamp Sessions include AI assistance features that help users throughout the [[concepts/video-creation|video creation]] process. This AI support provides guidance and suggestions to streamline the creation workflow and improve the quality of animated content.

## Stock Characters in Vyond Go

Within Bootcamp Sessions on Vyond Go, users have the ability to select and customize stock characters before generating their videos. This allows creators to choose character assets that align with their project needs and creative [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] early in the production process, rather than making character selections after [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
