## Saturn's Hexagonal North Pole: Mystery and Explanations

Saturn's North Pole features a persistent, massive hexagonal cloud pattern, a unique atmospheric phenomenon first observed by [[concepts/voyager-2]] in 1981 and later detailed by the Cassini-Huygens mission.

### Key Findings
- The structure is a stable, geometric jet stream located at the planet's polar region.
- Recent analysis suggests [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|fluid dynamics]] and wave interactions may explain its perfect hexagonal shape.
