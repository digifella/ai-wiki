LLM [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] refers to the [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]] within [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] that enable logical step-by-step [[concepts/output|output]] generation, [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]], and [[concepts/inference|inference]]. It encompasses both the observable output patterns and the underlying mechanisms that produce them.

**Key Insights:**
- [[entities/anthropic|Anthropic]] Discussion about how LLM think: [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]] researchers challenge the "glorified [[concepts/auto-complete|auto-complete]]" metaphor, exploring LLMs as [[concepts/complex-reasoning|complex reasoning]] systems through [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] research (video: [Tracing Thoughts: Language Model Interpretability](https://youtu.be/fGKNUvivvnc), article: [Tracing Thoughts: Language Model Interpretability](https://www.anthropic.com/news/tracing-thoughts-language-model)).
- [[concepts/reasoning|Reasoning]] involves internal token-level "[[concepts/thought-tracing|thought tracing]]" rather than single-step prediction.
- [[concepts/interpretability|Interpretability]] work reveals LLMs construct intermediate representations before final outputs.
