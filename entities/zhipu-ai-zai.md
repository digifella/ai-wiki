# Zhipu Ai Zai

Zhipu AI, also known as Z.Ai, is a Chinese artificial intelligence company specializing in the development of large language models and AI systems. The organization focuses on creating practical AI solutions tailored for business automation, software development, and complex reasoning tasks.

The company's primary product is the GLM-4.7 open-source large language model. This model is designed to support coding, business automation, and complex reasoning capabilities, providing developers and enterprises with accessible tools for advanced computational tasks.
