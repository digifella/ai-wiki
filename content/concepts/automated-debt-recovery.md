# Automated debt recovery

The application of [[concepts/automations|automated systems]], algorithms, and [[concepts/software|software]] [[concepts/workflow|workflows]] to identify, notify, and collect outstanding debts from individuals or entities.

## Critical Risks and Case Studies

- **Algorithmic [[concepts/accountability|accountability]]**: The systemic danger posed when automated [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] processes lack human oversight, [[concepts/verification|verification]], or error-correction mechanisms.
- **[[concepts/robodebt-scheme]] (Australia)**
    - An implementation of Automated debt recovery introduced in 2016 by the Australian Government.
    - Utilized an unlawful algorithm to identify and recover alleged overpayments of [[concepts/welfare-benefits|welfare benefits]].
    - Resulted in catastrophic failures, significant humanitarian harm, and documented fatalities.
    - *Reference: Into the [[concepts/shadows|Shadows]] (Video: "Robodebt: The Government Algorithm That Wound Up Killing People")*
