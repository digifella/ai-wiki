# AI Video Cloning

AI video cloning refers to technology that uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to create [[concepts/digital-avatars|digital avatars]] capable of replicating a person's appearance and voice in video form. The technology combines [[concepts/computer-vision|computer vision]], facial recognition, and [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|speech synthesis]] to generate realistic video performances of individuals without requiring their physical presence during filming. These synthetic videos can depict a person delivering speeches, performances, or other content they did not originally record.

## Technical Foundation

The underlying process relies on [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] trained on large datasets of video and audio recordings. These models learn to map facial movements, expressions, and voice characteristics from source material, then apply this learned information to generate new video sequences. The technology typically requires a collection of source footage from which to extract biometric and vocal patterns, though some approaches require relatively modest amounts of [[concepts/training-data|training data]]. The output quality depends on the sophistication of the models, the quantity and quality of [[concepts/training|training]] material, and the [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] available for rendering.

## Applications and Considerations

Potential applications include creating content for actors or public figures, dubbing films into different languages while preserving lip-sync, and generating educational or training videos. However, the technology raises significant concerns around consent, authenticity, and misuse. The ability to create convincing synthetic videos of real people has prompted discussion about verification methods, legal frameworks, and the distinction between consensual and non-consensual use. Media literacy and detection tools have become increasingly important as the technology advances.
