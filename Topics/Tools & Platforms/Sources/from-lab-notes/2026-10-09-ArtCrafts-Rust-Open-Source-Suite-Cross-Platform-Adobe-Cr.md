## ArtCraft's Rust Open-Source Suite: Cross-Platform Adobe Creative Cloud Alternatives
**Clip title:** Somebody Vibe Coded EVERY SINGLE ADOBE App
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** FUTC
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=eFB79TYI-Vw

### Summary
The video provides an enthusiastic and detailed overview of the [[concepts/version-numbers|ArtCraft]] team's new suite of [[concepts/open-source-system|open-source software]], which are presented as direct, from-scratch alternatives to [[entities/adobe|Adobe]]'s [[concepts/adobe|Creative Cloud]] applications. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] expresses profound surprise and excitement, labeling this development as "breaking news" in the photography [[entities/earth|world]] and noting that many have awaited such tools "since the birth of AI." These applications are entirely written in [[concepts/rust-programming-language|Rust]], a modern and [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] programming language, and are specifically highlighted as "clean-room implementations," meaning they were developed independently without relying on any of Adobe's proprietary source code.

The [[concepts/version-numbers|ArtCraft]] lineup is comprehensive, including PhotoCraft (a [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] alternative), VectorCraft (Illustrator), FilmCraft ([[entities/premiere-pro|Premiere Pro]]), LightCraft ([[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]]), PrintCraft, EffectCraft (After Effects), and DesignCraft. The [[entities/speaker|speaker]] emphasizes Rust's modern design and [[concepts/speed|speed]], drawing a [[concepts/connection|connection]] to the theoretical "SaaS-pocalypse" where AI could generate custom software on demand, making traditional [[concepts/subscription-models|subscription models]] obsolete. A significant advantage underscored is the [[concepts/operating-system-compatibility|cross-platform compatibility]] of these tools, with native versions available for [[entities/macos|macOS]], [[concepts/microsoft-windows|Windows]], and [[entities/linux|Linux]], addressing a long-standing frustration for [[entities/linux|Linux]] users in particular, who are often excluded from Adobe's ecosystem.

The video then delves into practical demonstrations of PhotoCraft, LightCraft, and FilmCraft. PhotoCraft successfully opens layered PSD files, allows for basic text and vector editing, and features real-time curve [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] with dynamic previews, akin to professional applications. LightCraft showcases impressive RAW file import capabilities, including DNGs, and remarkably can import and apply existing [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] presets. While some [[concepts/advanced-features|advanced features]] like AI-driven [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] and realistic [[concepts/film-grain|film grain]] in LightCraft, or smooth 4K/5K video playback in FilmCraft without proxies, still exhibit areas for improvement, the core functionality and user interfaces are surprisingly robust and familiar to Adobe users.

In conclusion, the speaker asserts that the [[concepts/structured-naming|ArtCraft suite]] "completely exceeds my expectations," calling them "legit" and surprisingly usable given their nascent stage of development. The ability to import Lightroom presets directly and the broad multi-platform support, especially for Linux, are hailed as game-changers. Despite acknowledging some current limitations and the potential for legal challenges from Adobe, the speaker views this open-source initiative as a powerful and promising step towards more accessible and versatile creative tools, marking a significant shift in the digital creative landscape.
