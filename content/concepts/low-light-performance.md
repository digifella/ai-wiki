# Low light performance

Ability of a camera system to capture clear, noise-reduced [[concepts/images|images]] in dim lighting conditions, determined by [[concepts/sensor-size|sensor size]], aperture, pixel technology, and computational processing.

- [[entities/colin-smith]], founder of [[entities/photoshopcafe]], introduces Adobe Project Indigo (a new mobile [[concepts/camera-app|camera app]]) on April 14, 2026, highlighting that while traditional cameras (DSLRs, mirrorless) excel in low light, smartphones have surpassed point-and-shoots through convenience, larger viewfinders, and integrated on-device editing/sharing.
