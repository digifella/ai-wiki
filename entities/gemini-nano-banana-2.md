Gemini Nano Banana 2 is a specialized variant within Google's Gemini model family, engineered specifically for AI-powered image editing tasks. As part of the broader Gemini lineup, which includes models optimized for various computational environments and use cases, this iteration focuses on delivering efficient image manipulation capabilities.
