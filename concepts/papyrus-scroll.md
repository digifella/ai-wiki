# Papyrus Scroll

A rolled document made from papyrus sheets, historically used for [[concepts/writing|writing]] and record-keeping in [[concepts/urban-planning|ancient civilizations]], particularly Egypt and the Mediterranean.

## Key Developments

- **AI-Driven Decipherment (2026):** Recent breakthroughs in [[concepts/virtual-unrolling]] techniques have allowed researchers to decipher previously inaccessible [[concepts/herculaneum-papyri|Herculaneum papyri]] without physical damage.
- **Herculaneum Papyri:** A collection of over 1,800 [[concepts/carbonized-papyrus|carbonized papyrus]] scrolls discovered in the 18th century at the Villa dei Papiri.
- **Technological Shift:** Traditional physical unrolling often destroyed the fragile scrolls; modern [[entities/ai]] models now use X-ray and CT scan data to virtually unroll and read text.
