# Nematron 3

[[entities/ai-assistant|Nemotron]]-3 is a family of [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] released by [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]. The family includes the [[concepts/nemotron-3-nano-model|Nemotron-3 Nano]] variant, a 30-billion-parameter model designed for efficient [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] across various [[concepts/software|applications]].

## Model Specifications

[[entities/nemotron-3-nano|Nemotron-3 Nano]] operates with 30 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], positioning it within the mid-[[concepts/range|range]] of [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. As an open-source release, the model is available for public use and modification, distinguishing it from proprietary alternatives.

## Release and Context

NVIDIA introduced the [[concepts/nemotron-3-family|Nemotron-3 family]] as part of its broader strategy to provide accessible AI [[concepts/models|models]] to developers and researchers. The release reflects the industry trend toward making capable language models openly available rather than restricting them to commercial platforms.
