# Halos

**Halos** refer to the visible artifacts or fringing that often appear around the edges of a subject during digital [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] or [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]]. They typically manifest as bright or dark outlines where the foreground and background colors [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] sharply, resulting from imperfect edge detection or color spill.

## Context: AI-Driven Edge Refinement

Recent advancements in [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] have targeted the reduction of halos in complex masking [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], particularly for fine details like hair and fur.

- **[[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]]'s [[concepts/enhance-edge|Enhance Edge]] AI**: [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] introduced an AI-powered "Enhance Edge" feature in **[[concepts/photoshop-beta|Photoshop Beta]] 27.11.0** to address persistent issues with complex selections.
- **Hair Masking**: The primary use case for this feature is improving the accuracy of hair masking, a traditionally difficult task due to the semi-transparent and intricate nature of hair strands.
- **Artifact Reduction**: The AI specifically targets the elimination of halos and other edge artifacts that occur during standard selection processes.
- **[[concepts/generative-credits|Generative Credits]]**: The implementation utilizes generative AI capabilities, which consume Generative Credits within the [[concepts/adobe-ecosystem-integration|Adobe ecosystem]].
