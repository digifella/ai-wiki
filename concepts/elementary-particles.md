# Elementary particles

Fundamental constituents of matter that, according to the [[concepts/standard-model]], possess no internal structure.

## Classification
- **Fermions** (Matter particles):
    - Quarks: Particles that [[concepts/experience|experience]] the strong interaction.
    - Leptons: Including electrons and neutrinos.
- **Bosons** (Force carriers):
    - Gauge bosons: Mediators of fundamental forces, such as Gluons and [[concepts/light|Photons]].

## Substructure Research
- **Compositeness**: Ongoing investigation into whether particles currently classified as elementary actually possess internal constituents.
- LHC [[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] Tests for [[concepts/quark-substructure|Quark Substructure]]: [[concepts/experimental-verification|Experimental testing]] via the [[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] at the LHC to search for evidence of quark internal structure.
