# Non Residence Visa

A Non Residence Visa is an electronic visa category administered by the Republic of Turkey that permits foreign nationals to enter and remain in the country for specified purposes without establishing formal residency. Processed entirely through Turkey's online e-visa system, this visa type accommodates short-to-medium term visits and is intended for individuals who do not plan to become permanent residents.

## Purpose and Duration

The Non Residence Visa is designed for temporary visits, covering activities such as tourism, business meetings, and medical treatment. It allows holders to stay in Turkey for a limited period, typically ranging from 30 to 90 days depending on the specific visa type and the applicant's nationality. This category is distinct from long-term residence permits, which are required for those intending to live in Turkey for extended periods or for work and study purposes.

## Application Process

Applications are submitted exclusively through the official Turkish e-Visa portal. Applicants must provide valid passport information, travel details, and payment for the visa fee. The system generally processes applications quickly, often issuing the visa electronically within minutes or a few days. Travelers must ensure their passport remains valid for at least six months beyond their entry date into Turkey.

## Entry Requirements

Holders of a Non Residence Visa must enter Turkey within the validity period specified on their visa. Overstaying the permitted duration can result in fines, deportation, or future entry bans. While this visa facilitates easy access for short stays, it does not grant the right to work or enroll in educational institutions. Individuals planning to reside in Turkey for longer than the visa allows must apply for a separate residence permit through the General Directorate of Migration Management.
