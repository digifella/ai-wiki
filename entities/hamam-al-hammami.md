# Hamam Al-Hammami

*   **Route:** Dubai to Tel Aviv
*   **Role:** First Officer
*   **Actions:** Initiated a cockpit attack approximately two hours into the flight, leading to a desert [[concepts/emergency-landing|emergency landing]].
