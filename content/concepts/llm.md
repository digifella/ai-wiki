# LLM

[[concepts/large-language-model-llm|Large Language Models]] (LLMs) are AI systems trained on vast amounts of [[concepts/text|text]] data to generate human-like [[concepts/responses|responses]] and perform a wide [[concepts/range|range]] of language tasks. They form the computational foundation for many modern [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]], from chatbots to [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] tools.

## Role in AI Automation

LLMs have become central to [[concepts/automation|automation]] workflows because they can process natural language [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] and integrate with other systems to execute [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]]. When combined with orchestration frameworks like [[concepts/langgraph-framework|Langgraph]], LLMs can be chained together with tools and logic to create multi-step automated workflows that handle [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], data processing, and external [[entities/api-calls|API calls]].

## Integration and Context Engineering

[[concepts/effective-use-of-llms|Effective use of LLMs]] in automation requires careful [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to how information is presented to the model, a practice known as [[concepts/context-engineering|context engineering]]. By structuring prompts and providing relevant context, developers can improve the [[concepts/accuracy|accuracy]] and [[concepts/software-reliability|reliability]] of LLM outputs within [[concepts/automations|automated systems]]. This becomes especially important when LLMs are part of larger agent-based architectures where their outputs directly affect [[concepts/downstream-processes|downstream processes]].
