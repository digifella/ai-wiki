# Docling

[[concepts/docx|Docling]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] toolkit developed by [[entities/ibm-research|IBM Research]]. It is designed to prepare documents for use in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] workflows by converting various document formats into structured, machine-readable outputs. The toolkit supports multiple document types, including PDFs, Word documents, and other structured files, extracting content in ways that language models and [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]] can efficiently process.

The toolkit automates several technical challenges inherent in document processing. It performs layout analysis and text extraction to preserve the semantic structure of the source material. By standardizing the output format, Docling enables downstream [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to interpret complex documents with greater accuracy and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].

As part of the broader tools and platforms [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]], Docling serves as a bridge between [[concepts/unstructured-data|unstructured data]] sources and AI-driven applications. Its open-source nature allows developers to integrate robust [[concepts/content-extraction|document parsing]] capabilities into their own pipelines without relying on proprietary solutions. This facilitates more scalable and reproducible workflows for organizations leveraging [[concepts/demystifying-llms|large language models]].
