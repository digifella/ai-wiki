# AI Methodology

AI Methodology refers to systematic approaches for integrating [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) and autonomous [[concepts/agents|agents]] into [[concepts/coding|software development]], [[concepts/website-building|website building]], and creative workflows. These methodologies establish structured frameworks for applying [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] to accelerate project delivery while maintaining quality and [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]]. Rather than replacing traditional development practices, AI methodologies typically augment existing processes by automating routine tasks, generating code scaffolding, and assisting with design and documentation work.

## Core Applications

In software development, AI methodologies facilitate code generation, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]] assistance, and [[concepts/automated-software-testing|automated testing]] support. Developers use language models to generate boilerplate code, translate between programming languages, and identify potential bugs in existing code. Website building applications [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] to generate layouts, copy, and interactive elements from [[concepts/natural-language-descriptions|natural language descriptions]] or design specifications. Creative workflows benefit from AI assistance in drafting content, [[concepts/brainstorming|brainstorming]] variations, and iterating on designs more rapidly than traditional methods.

## Key Considerations

Implementing AI methodologies requires [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to output quality, consistency with organizational [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], and appropriate human oversight. Organizations typically establish guidelines for when AI-generated outputs require review, how to integrate AI-assisted work into existing version control and [[concepts/quality-assurance|quality assurance]] processes, and how to maintain security and intellectual property standards. The effectiveness of AI methodologies depends heavily on clear [[concepts/prompting|prompting]] practices, [[concepts/iterative-refinement|iterative refinement]] of AI tool usage, and understanding the limitations of current language models.
