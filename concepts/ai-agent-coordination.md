# AI Agent Coordination

**AI [[concepts/multi-agent-orchestration|Agent Coordination]]** refers to the [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] and protocols enabling multiple [[concepts/ai-agent]]s to collaborate, share context, and execute [[concepts/complex-workflows|complex workflows]]. Effective [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] is critical for moving beyond single-agent limitations to handle tasks requiring diverse capabilities or [[concepts/persistent-state|persistent state]] management across long-horizon operations.

## Core Mechanisms

*   **[[concepts/latent-state-transfer|Latent State Transfer]]:** A method where agents exchange compressed representations of their internal [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] or context rather than raw data. This facilitates faster synchronization and reduces computational overhead in [[concepts/recursive-multi-agent-systems|recursive multi-agent systems]].
*   **Recursive [[concepts/expertise-based-ai-assistants|Multi-Agent Systems]]:** Architectures where agents are organized in hierarchical or looped structures, allowing for self-correction, specialized sub-task delegation, and emergent [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] capabilities.
*   **[[concepts/asana-integration|Task Automation]]:** Coordination enables the automation of complex, multi-step processes such as travel booking, schedule management, and insurance claim submission by decomposing high-level goals into agent-specific actions.
