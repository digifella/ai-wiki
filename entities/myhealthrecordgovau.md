# MyHealthRecord.gov.au

## Overview
The official Australian government platform for the **[[entities/my-health-record|My Health Record]]** (MHR) system. It provides a [[concepts/secure|secure]], online summary of an individual’s health information, accessible by authorized health practitioners and the record holder.

## Key Functions
- Centralized [[entities/storage|storage]] of clinical summaries, pathology reports, and medication histories.
- Facilitates data exchange between [[concepts/health|healthcare]] providers across the public and private sectors.
- Ensures [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] with national data standards and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] regulations.

## Recent Developments & Research
- [[concepts/secondary-use|Secondary use]] of MHR data is being leveraged for [[concepts/public-health]] monitoring and clinical research to improve [[concepts/healthcare-improvement]] outcomes.
- Frameworks are in place to balance data utility with [[concepts/ai-security]] and [[concepts/ethics-approval]] requirements.
