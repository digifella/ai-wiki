# Potential Solutions

Potential Solutions refers to the identification and evaluation of candidate approaches to address specific problems or challenges within business and organizational contexts. In strategy development, potential solutions emerge from problem analysis and represent the range of viable options available before final decisions are made. This concept is particularly relevant in domains requiring systematic evaluation of alternatives, such as process improvement, technology adoption, and operational optimization.

## Identification and Evaluation

The process of surfacing potential solutions typically follows structured problem definition. Organizations may generate candidates through internal brainstorming, benchmarking against industry practices, consulting external expertise, or adapting solutions from adjacent domains. Evaluation criteria often include feasibility, cost, implementation timeline, risk profile, and alignment with organizational capabilities and strategic objectives. Different solutions may trade off against these dimensions in ways that require explicit comparison.

## Strategic Context

Potential solutions operate as an intermediate stage between problem recognition and commitment to a specific course of action. Maintaining a portfolio of plausible alternatives supports more informed decision-making and reduces the risk of premature convergence on suboptimal approaches. The robustness of this evaluation phase often determines whether implemented solutions address root causes or merely symptoms, and whether chosen approaches generate unintended consequences in other areas of the organization.
