# Google Ai Ultra

[[concepts/ai-ultra-subscription|Google Ai Ultra]] is the latest [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] platform, representing an advancement in the company's [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] and integration strategy. The platform features a [[concepts/deep-think-mode|deep think mode]], which enables more sophisticated [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and analysis capabilities for [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]]. Ai Ultra is designed to work seamlessly across multiple Google products and services, creating a unified AI experience across the company's ecosystem.

## Core Features and Integration

The platform integrates deeply with [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], Google's primary [[concepts/ai-chatbots|conversational AI]] system, enabling enhanced functionality across various [[concepts/software|applications]]. Ai Ultra is also integrated with Google Search, allowing AI-powered features to enhance search results and [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]]. Additionally, the platform works with [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]], Google's research and note-taking tool, facilitating advanced content synthesis and [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] capabilities.

## Product Ecosystem

The integration with NotebookLM includes enhanced features for creating [[concepts/mind-maps|mind maps]] and interactive visual content from research materials and documents. The platform supports multi-format content synthesis, allowing users to transform information across different formats. Ai Ultra also encompasses related tools such as [[entities/google-nano-banana-pro|Google Nano Banana 2]], an image generation system designed for rapid professional-quality outputs with enhanced control over generation [[concepts/parameters|parameters]].
