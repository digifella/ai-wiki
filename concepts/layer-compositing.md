# Layer Compositing

Layer compositing is a fundamental technique in digital image editing that involves combining multiple image layers to create a final composite. This process typically includes arranging layers in a specific order, adjusting their opacity, and applying blending modes to control how pixels from different layers interact. The core advantage of layer-based compositing is its non-destructive workflow, allowing editors to preserve original image data while experimenting with different combinations and adjustments.

## Blending and Color Adjustment

Modern compositing software like Adobe Photoshop provides specialized tools for harmonizing layers during the blending process. The Harmonize feature enables editors to blend layers while simultaneously applying color and tonal adjustments, facilitating smoother integration between disparate image elements. This approach reduces the need for manual correction of color mismatches or exposure inconsistencies that often occur when combining photographs or design elements from different sources.

## Workflow Benefits

Layer-based compositing offers significant practical advantages over traditional flat-image editing. By maintaining separate layers throughout the creative process, editors can isolate specific elements, make targeted adjustments, and easily revise decisions without affecting other parts of the composition. This flexibility makes layer compositing essential for professional image editing, photo retouching, graphic design, and digital art production.
