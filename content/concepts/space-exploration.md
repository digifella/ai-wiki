# Space Exploration

Space exploration is a broad field encompassing the use of spacecraft to explore outer space, with particular emphasis on human and robotic missions beyond Earth. This page outlines key developments in recent space exploration initiatives.

### Recent Developments

- **[[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis Program]]**: [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s Artemis program aims to return humans to the Moon by 2025 and establish a sustainable presence there.
- **[[concepts/starship|Starship]] Mission**: [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] is developing Starship as an advanced spacecraft designed for deep space missions, including trips to Mars.
