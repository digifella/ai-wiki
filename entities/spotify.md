# Spotify

Spotify is a digital music streaming service that operates on a subscription-based model, offering users access to millions of songs, [[entities/podcasts|podcasts]], and other [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] content. The platform has become a primary target for users seeking to reduce or eliminate recurring subscription costs through alternative solutions.

## Self-Hosted Alternatives

There is growing interest in building personal media servers as alternatives to subscription services like Spotify. These projects typically run on affordable hardware, such as [[entities/ubuntu|Ubuntu]]-based systems, and can be configured for under $100. Such servers allow users to host and stream their own media collections without ongoing subscription fees, though they require [[concepts/installation|initial setup]] and maintenance.
