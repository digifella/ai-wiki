# Mentour Pilot

His content bridges the gap between layperson curiosity and professional aviation knowledge, focusing heavily on Human Factors, CRM, and Systems [[concepts/human-cognition|Thinking]].

*   **Industry Critique:** Commentary on airline operational pressures, regulatory gaps, and the intersection of economics and safety.
