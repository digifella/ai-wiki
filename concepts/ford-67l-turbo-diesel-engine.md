# Ford 6.7L Turbo Diesel Engine

The Ford 6.7L Power Stroke is a heavy-duty turbocharged diesel V8 [[concepts/engine|engine]] produced by Ford. It is primarily used in Ford Super Duty trucks and E-Series vans.

- **Induction:** Twin-scroll turbocharger
- **Fuel System:** High-[[concepts/pressure|pressure]] common rail direct injection
