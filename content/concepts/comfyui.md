# Comfyui

ComfyUI is a [[concepts/local-installation|local installation]] platform designed for [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]], particularly [[concepts/text-to-video|text-to-video]] and image-to-video systems. It enables users to operate models like [[concepts/14b-parameter-model|Wan 2.2]] on their own [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] rather than relying on cloud-based services. The platform provides a node-based interface for configuring and executing complex [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] workflows.

## Use Cases

ComfyUI is primarily used for [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] of advanced video generation models. Users can leverage it to create videos from text prompts or convert existing [[concepts/images|images]] into video sequences using models such as Wan 2.2. This local-first approach offers advantages in terms of [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], cost, and [[concepts/customization|customization]] compared to cloud alternatives.

## Technical Integration

The platform integrates with various state-of-the-art generative models and allows users to take advantage of [[concepts/architectural-improvements|architectural improvements]] in newer [[concepts/model-releases|model releases]]. ComfyUI's node-based workflow system facilitates both simple and complex generation tasks, making it accessible to users with varying levels of technical expertise while remaining flexible for advanced customization.
