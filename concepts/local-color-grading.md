# Local Color Grading

**Local [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|Color Grading]]** refers to the selective application of color [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]], tonal shifts, and [[concepts/contrast|contrast]] modifications to specific regions or objects within an image, rather than applying global changes to the entire frame. This technique allows for precise [[concepts/creative-control|artistic control]], enhancing depth, directing viewer [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]], and correcting localized [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]].

## Core Mechanics
- **Selection [[concepts/disconnection|Isolation]]:** Utilizes masks (luma, range, or AI-based subject masks) to isolate targets.
- **[[concepts/training-process|Parameter Adjustment]]:** Modifies hue, saturation, luminance, temperature, and tint within the masked area.
- **[[concepts/layer-styles|Blend Modes]]:** Often employed to interact with underlying pixels (e.g., Overlay, Soft [[concepts/light|Light]]) for non-destructive integration.
- **Depth Enhancement via Brushes:** Techniques such as those detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-05-29-Lightrooms-Three-Brushes-Dodging-Burning-and-Enhancing-P|Lightroom's Three Brushes: Dodging, Burning, and Enhancing Photo Depth]] utilize localized [[concepts/dodging|dodging]] and [[concepts/burning|burning]] to create three-dimensional depth and make subjects "pop" without affecting global [[concepts/exposure|exposure]].
