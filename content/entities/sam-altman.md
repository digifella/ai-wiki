# Sam Altman

Sam Altman is the Chief Executive Officer of [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research company. He has led the organization through significant developments in [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] technology and public [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of AI systems.

## Recent Developments

Under Altman's [[concepts/leadership|leadership]], OpenAI launched the [[entities/chatgpt-images|ChatGPT Images]] model in April 2026, adding image generation [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] to the ChatGPT platform. This release expanded ChatGPT's functionality beyond text-based interactions. The new [[concepts/image-generation-model|image generation model]] was subsequently benchmarked against competing systems, including [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[entities/google-nano-banana-pro|Nano Banana Pro]], to evaluate its performance for professional [[concepts/software|applications]].
