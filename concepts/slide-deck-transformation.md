# Slide Deck Transformation

Slide Deck Transformation is the process of converting presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video format using Google Vids. NotebookLM, Google's AI-powered notebook application, can automatically generate structured slide decks from source documents. These slides can then be imported into Google Vids, Google's AI video creation platform, to produce finished video content. This workflow allows users to repurpose text-based research and documentation into multimedia presentations.

## Basic Workflow

The transformation begins in NotebookLM, where users upload or reference source documents. The application analyzes the content and generates a corresponding slide deck with structured layouts and text. Users can then export these slides and import them into Google Vids. Within Google Vids, the slides serve as the visual foundation for video creation, and the platform's AI tools can add narration, transitions, and other video elements to produce a complete video file.

## Practical Considerations

A key advantage of this approach is the ability to generate videos without watermarks, as the output from Google Vids can be downloaded as unwatermarked files suitable for distribution or publication. This makes the workflow practical for professional, educational, or content creation purposes where branding or platform attribution is not desired. The combination of NotebookLM's content analysis capabilities with Google Vids' video generation tools creates an efficient pipeline for converting document-heavy materials into visual media.
