# AI Ethics Principles

Core principles guiding the ethical development and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]].

## Key Principles
- **Transparency**: AI systems should be understandable and explainable.
- **Fairness**: AI should avoid bias and ensure equitable outcomes.
- **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]]**: Clear responsibility for AI decisions and impacts.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]]**: Protection of user data and respect for [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] rights.
- **Safety**: Ensuring AI systems are [[concepts/secure|secure]] and reliable.
- **Beneficence**: AI should promote well-being and minimize harm.

## Applications
- **[[concepts/health|Healthcare]]**: [[concepts/ethical-considerations|Ethical considerations]] in AI-driven diagnostics and treatment.
- **Business**: Fairness in AI hiring and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] tools.
- **Government**: Transparency in AI-powered public services.

## Challenges
- Balancing [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] with ethical constraints.
- Ensuring cross-cultural applicability of principles.
- Regulatory gaps in emerging [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]].
