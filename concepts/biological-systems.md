# Biological Systems

**Biological systems** refer to complex, interconnected networks of living organisms and their components, ranging from molecular pathways to entire ecosystems. In modern research, these systems are increasingly analyzed through the lens of computational biology and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to understand their dynamics, predict behaviors, and intervene in pathological processes.

## Key Developments in Computational Biology

The integration of AI into the study of biological systems has accelerated the pace of discovery, particularly in [[concepts/protein-folding]] and drug design.

*   **AI-Driven [[concepts/drug-discovery|Drug Discovery]]:** Significant advancements are being made by Isomorphic [[entities/labs|Labs]], a spin-off from [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]], which leverages AI to revolutionize how biological targets are understood and targeted.
*   **AlphaFold Integration:** The application of AlphaFold models allows for precise [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|prediction]] of protein structures, a critical component in understanding biological [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] at the molecular level.
*   **Medicinal Chemistry:** [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] are now central to the workflow of [[concepts/medicinal-drug-design|medicinal drug design]], enabling faster [[concepts/iteration|iteration]] of candidate molecules.

For detailed [[concepts/notes|notes]] on this specific intersection of AI and biology, see [[lab-notes/2026-10-05-Isomorphic-Labs-AlphaFolds-AI-Revolutionizing-Drug-Disco|Isomorphic Labs: AlphaFold's AI Revolutionizing Drug Discovery and Design]].
