# Bec

Bec conducted a comprehensive review of the organization's AI readiness and implementation strategy. The assessment evaluated critical infrastructure capacity, technical resources, and governance frameworks necessary for successful AI system deployment across the organization.

## Key Findings

The review identified significant infrastructure limitations that constrain the performance and scalability of AI systems. These limitations encompassed hardware capacity, network bandwidth, and storage systems that would need enhancement to support expanded AI operations. Additionally, the assessment highlighted specific tool issues affecting implementation efficiency and data pipeline reliability.

## Governance and Risk Assessment

A central concern of the review was the identification of risks posed by ungoverned AI solutions and Shadow AI—informal or unauthorized AI implementations operating outside established oversight frameworks. These uncontrolled deployments create security, compliance, and operational risks that undermine organizational coherence. Bec's findings emphasized the necessity of establishing clear governance structures and policies to bring all AI initiatives under proper monitoring and control.
