# Desktop AI Platform

A localized [[concepts/software|software]] environment designed for the orchestration, management, and automated execution of [[concepts/agentic-ai]] and [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs).

### Core Functionalities
- **[[concepts/multi-agent-orchestration|Multi-Agent Orchestration]]:** Centralized management of diverse [[concepts/agentic-ai]] (including [[entities/hermes]], [[entities/claude-code]], OpenCode, and [[entities/gemini]]) to perform complex, automated tasks.
- **Local [[concepts/automation|Automation]]:** Utilizing desktop-based platforms to run and coordinate [[concepts/autonomous-workflows|autonomous workflows]].
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] [[concepts/integration|Integration]]:** Leveraging free, community-driven tools for scalable AI management.

### Notable Implementations
- [[entities/aionui|AionUI]]: Free Desktop Platform for Multi-Agent AI Management and [[concepts/automation|Automation]]: A free, [[concepts/open-source|open-source]], desktop-based platform specifically engineered for the management and automation of various [[concepts/ai-connectors|AI agents]].
