# Anita Woolley

**Anita Woolley** is a psychologist and [[entities/tomasz-janowski|researcher]] known for her work on [[concepts/swarm-intelligence|Collective Intelligence]] and [[concepts/social-dynamics|social dynamics]] in groups. Her research focuses on how groups function, the impact of social sensitivity on [[concepts/team-performance|team performance]], and the concept of "[[concepts/learning|learning]] on the edge."

## Key Concepts & Research
- **Collective Intelligence**: Investigates the properties that make certain groups smarter than the sum of their individual parts.
- **Social Sensitivity**: Identifies the ability of group members to read each other's emotional states as a primary predictor of group intelligence.
- **Learning on the Edge**: Explores [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] for adaptive group learning and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] under pressure or uncertainty.
