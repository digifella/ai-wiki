# Local Deployment
**[[concepts/llms|Definition]]:** Local deployment refers to setting up and [[concepts/running|running]] [[concepts/software|software]] or services on a personal computer or server within one's own network rather than relying on [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud-based solutions]]. For [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]], this involves downloading, installing, configuring, and running the model locally.

## Key Considerations
- **Resource Requirements**: High [[concepts/inference-optimization|computational power]] and [[entities/storage|storage]] space are essential.
- **[[concepts/privacy|Privacy]] & [[concepts/security|Security]]**: Data remains under personal [[concepts/power|control]] without needing to transmit it over potentially insecure networks.
- **[[concepts/ai-workflow|Customization]]**: Ability to tailor [[concepts/model-behavior|model behavior]] and [[concepts/integration|integration]] with other tools is enhanced.

## Recent Developments
- **[[entities/openai|OpenAI]] [[entities/gpt-oss|GPT-OSS]]**: Release of new [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/models|models]] under the **[[entities/apache-20|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/license|license]]**, significantly increasing [[concepts/accessibility|accessibility]] for local deployment and custom integration.
