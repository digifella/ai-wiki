# Emergency Landing

An **emergency landing** is the forced landing of an aircraft due to in-flight emergencies, technical failures, or [[concepts/security|security]] threats. It differs from a forced landing (where the pilot has no choice but to land immediately due to imminent danger) and a precautionary landing (where the pilot chooses to land due to potential issues).

## Key Causes
*   Mechanical failure
*   Medical emergencies
*   Security threats (e.g., cockpit intrusion)
*   Weather conditions

## Notable Incidents

### FlyDubai Flight 1073
On September 30, 2026, FlyDubai Flight 1073 ([[concepts/boeing-737-max-8|Boeing 737 Max 8]]) experienced a critical [[concepts/cybersecurity-incident|security breach]] during a flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv.

*   **Incident:** First Officer [[entities/hamam-al-hammami|Hamam Al-Hammami]] launched a [[concepts/cockpit-attack|cockpit attack]] against the Captain.
*   **Outcome:** The aircraft executed an emergency landing in the desert.
*   **Casualties:** 174 passengers and 6 crew members were on board; two off-duty pilots were present.
