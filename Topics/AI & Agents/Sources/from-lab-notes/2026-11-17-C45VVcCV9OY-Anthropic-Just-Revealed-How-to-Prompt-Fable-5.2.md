### Summary
Anthropic's newly released Claude Fable 5.2 model represents a significant step up in power, speed, and capability for complex tasks. In this video, the speaker shares insights and practical rules on when and how to leverage this new model effectively, comparing its performance with Opus 5.5 across real-world use cases.

The speaker highlights several key points about Claude Fable 5.2:

* **Performance and Cost:** Fable 5.2 is up to 30% faster than Fable 5.1 and scores significantly higher in real-world tests compared to Opus 5.5. However, it is priced higher (double Opus 5.5) and consumes usage limits much faster.
* **Use Cases:** Fable 5.2 excels in demanding reasoning, long-horizon agentic work, coding, and tasks requiring high precision, such as security audits and financial modeling. For everyday writing and quick tasks, Opus 5.5 remains a better choice due to its speed and cost-effectiveness.
* **Prompting Strategies:** To get the best out of Fable 5.2, users need to adapt their prompting techniques. Key rules include avoiding mid-chat model switches, setting the right effort level (defaulting to 'high'), giving the entire task upfront with clear context, and asking for proof rather than reasoning.

The video concludes that while Fable 5.2 requires more careful management and higher cost, its superior performance in complex and visual tasks makes it a valuable tool for high-value work. The speaker offers free resources, including prompts and mods, to help users optimize their interaction with the new model.
