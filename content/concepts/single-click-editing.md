# Single Click Editing

Single Click Editing is a workflow feature in [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] that allows photographers to apply multiple automated [[concepts/ai-masks|AI masks]] to an image with a single click. Rather than manually creating and adjusting individual masks for different parts of a photograph, users can save and apply preset combinations that automatically isolate specific elements like skies, subjects, or backgrounds.

## How It Works

The feature leverages Lightroom Classic's [[concepts/ai-based-selection|AI masking]] capabilities combined with custom presets. Photographers create preset configurations containing multiple masks tailored to common photographic [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]—such as landscape compositions requiring separate [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] for sky and foreground, or portrait work needing distinct masks for skin, eyes, and background. Once saved, these presets can be applied to new [[concepts/images|images]] in a single action, with the AI masks automatically detecting and isolating the relevant elements.

## Applications

Single Click Editing is particularly useful for landscape and people photography, where consistent editing approaches across similar image types can save significant time. Rather than repeating the same manual [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] steps on each image, photographers can establish their preferred masking [[concepts/structure|structure]] once and reuse it across entire shoots or projects.
