# Psychological Science

Psychological [[concepts/science|science]] is an empirical study of the [[concepts/human-cognition|human mind]] and behavior encompassing a broad range of sub-disciplines including cognitive, social, developmental, and clinical psychology. The field seeks to understand how people perceive, think, act, learn, grow, and interact with their environment.

- cognitive psychology studies [[concepts/cognition|mental processes]] such as [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]], [[concepts/memory|memory]], perception, [[concepts/problem-solving|problem solving]], creativity, and language.
- social psychology examines the impact of social influences on individuals' behaviors, attitudes, feelings, and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]].
- developmental psychology investigates how people change from conception to death, particularly focusing on growth in childhood.
