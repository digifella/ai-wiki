# Jonathan Huberman

Jonathan Huberman is a [[entities/video-creator|content creator]] and educator who operates a [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel focused on demonstrating practical [[concepts/software|applications]] of emerging technologies. His channel explores tools and workflows that help creators automate and enhance their content production processes.

## Video Podcast Generation

Huberman has created instructional content demonstrating how to convert audio-based content into video podcast episodes. His tutorials show the process of taking conversations generated by [[concepts/ai-integrated-notebooks|NotebookLM]] or existing podcast [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]] [[concepts/files|files]] and transforming them into formatted video content suitable for podcast [[concepts/distribution|distribution]]. The [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] utilizes tools including JellyPod and [[concepts/joggai|JoggAi]] to automate the [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] process.

His work addresses a practical need for content creators seeking to repurpose audio content across different platforms and formats without manual video production work.
