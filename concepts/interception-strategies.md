# Interception Strategies

Interception strategies represent a [[concepts/mindset-shift|paradigm shift]] in oncology from treating established tumors to preventing malignant transformation by targeting pre-cancerous states or risk factors. This approach is particularly critical for high-mortality cancers like Lung Cancer.

## Core Principles

*   **Pre-emptive Intervention**: Targeting molecular or cellular changes before clinical cancer manifests.
*   **Risk Stratification**: Identifying individuals with high risk profiles (e.g., [[concepts/systemic-inflammation|chronic inflammation]], genetic predisposition) for targeted prophylaxis.
*   **Mechanism Focus**: Disrupting pathways such as Inflammation, oxidative [[concepts/stress|stress]], or DNA repair defects that [[concepts/ambition|drive]] carcinogenesis.

### Musculoskeletal & Nerve Health: Sciatica Management
*   **[[concepts/nerve-de-irritation|Nerve De-Irritation]] vs. [[concepts/stretching|Stretching]]**: Effective management of [[concepts/sciatica]] requires targeting [[concepts/nerve-irritation|nerve irritation]] rather than stretching tight muscles, which can exacerbate symptoms.
*   **Mechanism**: Sciatica is characterized by inflammation and irritation of the [[concepts/sciatic-nerve|sciatic nerve]]; nerves do not respond well to traditional stretching of the buttocks or legs.
*   **Intervention**: Spinal extension exercises are recommended to alleviate pain by reducing nerve irritation.
