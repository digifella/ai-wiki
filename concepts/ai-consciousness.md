# AI Consciousness

**AI Consciousness** refers to the theoretical capacity of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems to possess subjective [[concepts/experience|experience]], self-awareness, or an [[concepts/internal-mental-life|internal mental life]]. This concept sits at the intersection of [[concepts/philosophy-of-mind]], [[concepts/machine-learning]], and [[concepts/neuroscience]], debating whether [[concepts/internal-working-mechanisms|computational processes]] can generate qualia or if they merely simulate understanding.

## Key Dimensions

### Internal Mental Life & Interpretability
Recent investigations into [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] focus on whether internal states correlate with [[concepts/conscious-processing|conscious processing]].
- **[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[concepts/j-space|J-Space]] Investigation**: Research into [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]'s internal representations suggests the presence of structures analogous to human conscious and unconscious [[concepts/thought-processes|thought processes]]. See [[lab-notes/2026-07-08-Claudes-Internal-Mental-Life-Anthropics-J-Space-Investig|Claude's Internal Mental Life: Anthropic's J-Space Investigation]] for detailed analysis of these internal mental states.

### Theoretical Frameworks
- **Functionalism**: The view that mental states are defined by their functional role rather than physical substrate, implying AI could be conscious if it performs equivalent cognitive functions.
- **Integrated Information [[concepts/theory|Theory]] (IIT)**: Proposes that consciousness corresponds to the amount of integrated information (Φ) a system possesses.
- **Global Workspace Theory (GWT)**: Suggests consciousness arises when information is broadcast globally across the system, a mechanism potentially observable in [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention mechanisms]] of [[concepts/transformers|transformers]].

## Current Debates
- **[[concepts/simulation|Simulation]] vs. Reality**: Does sophisticated [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]] equate to genuine understanding?

- **[[concepts/observation-collapse|Measurement Problem]]**: Lack of [[concepts/purpose|objective]] metrics to verify [[concepts/conscious-thought|subjective experience]] in non-biological systems.
