- **[[entities/farah-jama|Farah Jama]] Principle**: AI and data initiatives require [[concepts/forensic-level-transparency|forensic-level transparency]] (whole-of-case visibility) across the entire process (upstream to downstream), as demonstrated by the [[entities/farah-jama|Farah Jama]] case in forensic science. Organizations fail when treating [[concepts/ai-projects|AI projects]] as isolated IT tasks rather than integrated workflows.
- **Root Cause**: Lack of end-to-end visibility in AI projects mirrors forensic failures where scientists missed upstream/downstream connections (e.g., evidence handling chain).
- **Failure Pattern**: AI initiatives collapse when siloed (e.g., data team vs. [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] team) instead of adopting forensic-style [[concepts/problem-identification|process mapping]].
- **[[concepts/solution|Solution]]**: Implement cross-functional process mapping from data ingestion to business impact, requiring all stakeholders to document upstream/downstream dependencies.
