# Temperature Slider

The Temperature Slider is a control within the Color and Vibrance tool introduced in Adobe Photoshop 2025. It enables users to adjust the color temperature of images along a spectrum from cool (blue) tones to warm (yellow/orange) tones. This adjustment affects the overall thermal cast of an image, making it useful for correcting white balance issues or applying intentional color grading to achieve a desired visual aesthetic.

## How It Works

The slider operates as a linear control that shifts the color balance of an image across the temperature spectrum. Moving the slider toward the blue end reduces warmth, effectively cooling the image, while moving it toward the yellow end increases warmth. This mechanism allows for precise manipulation of the image's tonal characteristics without altering the underlying luminance data directly, providing a straightforward method for balancing exposure and mood.

## Application

Users typically employ this tool to correct color casts caused by different lighting conditions, such as fluorescent or daylight environments. It also serves as a creative instrument for establishing a specific atmosphere, where cooler tones might convey a sense of distance or night, and warmer tones suggest intimacy or sunlight. The integration of this slider into the Color and Vibrance tool streamlines the workflow by centralizing color adjustments in a single interface.
