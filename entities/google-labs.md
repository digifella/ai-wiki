# Google Labs

[[entities/google-labs|Google Labs]] is an experimental space within [[entities/google|Google]] where innovative [[concepts/ideas|ideas]] and technologies are developed and tested before being launched as full-scale products or services. The lab serves as a sandbox for early-stage research, allowing Google to explore new concepts without the constraints of immediate commercial pressures.
