# Sony Alpha A7R IV

The Sony Alpha A7R IV is a full-frame mirrorless camera released by Sony in 2019. It features a 61-megapixel Exmor R CMOS sensor and is designed for high-resolution photography. The camera is capable of capturing detailed [[concepts/images|images]] with extensive [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]], making it suitable for professional and advanced amateur photographers.

## Film Scanning Applications

The A7R IV has become popular among photographers for digitizing film [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] home. Its high resolution and macro [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] allow users to photograph film negatives or [[entities/google-slides|slides]] directly, creating digital scans without dedicated [[concepts/film-scanning|film scanning]] equipment. This method, sometimes called "scanning by photography," offers a cost-effective alternative to traditional flatbed or dedicated film scanners. The camera's sensor resolution and lens options enable reasonably detailed captures of film originals when proper lighting and macro focusing techniques are employed.
