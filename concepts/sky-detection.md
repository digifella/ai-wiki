# Sky Detection

Sky detection is an automated masking technique employed in modern photo editing software to isolate sky regions within landscape and outdoor photographs. Primarily available in Adobe Lightroom and Adobe Camera Raw, the feature utilizes artificial intelligence to identify and select sky areas based on their tonal, color, and textural characteristics. This automation allows photographers to apply targeted adjustments to the sky without manually using brushes or lasso tools, ensuring that edits do not inadvertently affect the foreground or other elements of the composition.

## Technical Implementation

The algorithm analyzes the image to distinguish the sky from terrestrial elements by recognizing common atmospheric gradients and horizon lines. Users can activate the feature via a dedicated button in the Masking panel, which generates a precise selection mask. The software allows for refinement of the selection edge, enabling adjustments to the radius and feathering to ensure a natural transition between the edited sky and the rest of the image.

## Workflow Integration

Once the sky mask is generated, it functions as a non-destructive adjustment layer. Photographers can modify exposure, contrast, temperature, and saturation specifically for the sky region. This targeted approach is particularly useful for correcting overexposed horizons or enhancing cloud detail without altering the exposure of the ground elements. The mask can be saved as a custom mask or modified further using the brush or gradient tools for more complex compositions.
