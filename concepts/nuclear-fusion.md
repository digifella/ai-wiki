# Nuclear Fusion

The process of combining [[concepts/light|light]] [[concepts/atomic-nuclei|atomic nuclei]] to [[concepts/deployment|release]] massive amounts of energy, fundamentally mimicking the [[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]] occurring within a Star.

## Core Concepts
- Aims to replicate the colossal energy output of the Sun to create a sustainable power source for [[entities/earth|Earth]].
- Relies on the fusion of elements such as Hydrogen and Helium.
- Focuses on the scientific quest to [[concepts/harness|harness]] the power of a Star via terrestrial replication.

## Recent Developments
- **[[concepts/wendelstein-7-x|Wendelstein 7-X]]**: [[entities/germany|Germany]]'s stellarator reactor has set [[concepts/new-world|new]] records in plasma confinement stability, validating key stellarator [[concepts/design|design principles]].
- **Private Sector Critique**: Despite significant private investment in fusion startups, technical feasibility remains contested. Physicists have raised substantial concerns regarding the [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] viability of Helion Energy's proposed $15 billion machine, citing fundamental physical challenges that may prevent successful operation.
- **Timeline Pressures**: Fusion startups continue to push back completion dates, highlighting the gap between commercial promises and scientific reality.

## Critical Analysis & Skepticism
- **Helion Energy Critique**: Detailed analysis of the technical barriers facing Helion Energy's approach is documented in [[lab-notes/2026-10-01-Physicists-Critique-Helion-Energys-15-Billion-Nuclear-Fu|Physicists' Critique: Helion Energy's $15 Billion Nuclear Fusion Machine Cannot Work]].
