# Lens Correction

Lens Correction is a feature introduced in Adobe Camera Raw version 18.3 that addresses optical distortions and aberrations inherent to camera lenses. When light passes through a lens, it naturally experiences various optical imperfections that can degrade image quality. This feature provides photographers with tools to systematically correct these issues and restore images to a more optically neutral state.

## Common Optical Issues

Camera lenses commonly produce several types of distortion. Barrel distortion causes straight lines to bow outward, particularly noticeable at the edges of the frame, while pincushion distortion pulls lines inward toward the center. These geometric anomalies are often exacerbated by wide-angle focal lengths or specific lens designs.

## Correction Mechanisms

The tool utilizes profile-based corrections that automatically detect the specific lens model and apply predefined mathematical adjustments to reverse these distortions. Users can also manually fine-tune parameters such as amount, midpoint, and focal length to address residual aberrations that automated profiles may not fully resolve. This process helps ensure that architectural lines remain straight and that color fringing along high-contrast edges is minimized.
