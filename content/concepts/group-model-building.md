# Group Model Building

Group Model Building (GMB) is a participatory research methodology employed in focus group settings, particularly within [[concepts/clearing-houses|clearing houses]] and institutional contexts focused on [[concepts/children-and-family-violence|children and family violence]]. The approach facilitates structured [[concepts/dialogue|dialogue]] among stakeholders, allowing researchers to gather qualitative data and develop shared understanding of complex social issues through collaborative modeling and discussion.

## Application in Policy and Research

Group Model Building has been utilized by organizations including the [[entities/nsw-dept-justice|NSW Department of Justice]] in Australia as part of their approach to understanding and addressing children and family violence. As a research space, GMB operates within clearing houses—institutional frameworks designed to synthesize, evaluate, and disseminate [[concepts/flow-management|research findings]] to practitioners and policymakers. This positioning allows the methodology to bridge research evidence and practical application in sensitive domains.

## Contemporary Development

As an evolving research practice, Group Model Building continues to adapt to contemporary contexts and emerging technologies. Recent interest has focused on the [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools within the GMB framework, exploring how computational approaches might enhance data analysis, pattern recognition, and stakeholder engagement in focus group settings. This extension reflects broader trends toward hybrid methodologies combining [[concepts/qualitative-research|qualitative research]] with technological [[concepts/innovation|innovation]].
