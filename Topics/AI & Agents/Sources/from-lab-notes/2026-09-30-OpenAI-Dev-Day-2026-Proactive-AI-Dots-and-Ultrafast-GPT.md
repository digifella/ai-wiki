## OpenAI Dev Day 2026: Proactive AI "Dots" and Ultrafast GPT-6 Astra Advancements
**Clip title:** OpenAI COOKED
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Matthew Berman
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Xc6ERvZM1NY

### Summary
[[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s [[entities/dev-day-2026|Dev Day 2026]] showcased a suite of advancements and new products, emphasizing a future where AI is more deeply integrated into daily personal and professional workflows. The overarching theme revolved around making powerful AI more accessible and proactive, while also pushing the boundaries of its capabilities and [[concepts/speed|speed]]. The event featured several significant announcements, including a new personal [[concepts/ai-assistant|AI assistant]], a [[concepts/premium-speed-tier|premium speed tier]], a cost-efficient new model, and enhancements for developers and [[concepts/collaborative-workspaces|collaborative workspaces]].

The most prominent new offering is "[[concepts/dots-agent|Dots]]," OpenAI's always-on, proactive personal AI assistant, powered by the advanced [[concepts/gpt-6-astra|GPT-6 Astra]] model. Designed to anticipate user needs rather than just respond to prompts, Dots integrates seamlessly with platforms like [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]], [[entities/slack|Slack]], Teams, [[entities/gmail|Gmail]], and [[entities/google-docs|Google Docs]]. This personalized agent learns user preferences and aims to handle tasks autonomously, enhancing [[concepts/productivity|productivity]]. While inspired by similar emerging AI assistants, Dots positions itself as a [[concepts/frontier-intelligence|frontier intelligence]] that brings proactive assistance to a wider audience, with conversations not counting towards standard ChatGPT [[concepts/rate-limits|usage limits]] unless controlling threads in [[concepts/codex|Codex]] or [[concepts/value-creation|ChatGPT Work]].

For users demanding ultimate speed, OpenAI introduced "Ultrafast," a premium speed tier for GPT-6 Astra, leveraging Cerebrus chips to offer up to 8x faster token generation in Codex and 6x faster in the API. This significant speed boost, however, comes at a substantially higher cost, highlighting a strategic [[concepts/tiered-pricing|tiered pricing]] model where cutting-edge performance demands a premium. To accommodate this, a new "Pro 500" plan was unveiled at $500 per month, offering 25 times the usage limits compared to the previous Plus plan, demonstrating that while base intelligence becomes cheaper, the absolute frontier remains an investment.

Balancing the premium speed, OpenAI also announced [[concepts/gpt-61|GPT-6.1 Sol]], a groundbreaking model offering near-Astra intelligence at approximately a fifth of the price. This highly cost-efficient model performs remarkably well on various benchmarks, including [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] (DeepSWE), PDF processing, and [[concepts/computer-use|computer use]] (OSWorld), making [[concepts/frontier-ai-capability|advanced AI capabilities]] far more accessible without sacrificing significant quality. This [[concepts/deployment|release]], alongside an even lighter model named Luna, underscores OpenAI's dual strategy of pushing [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] boundaries with Astra while simultaneously democratizing powerful, [[concepts/ai-specialization|efficient AI]] for broader [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

Further expanding its ecosystem, OpenAI rolled out several tools for developers. The "Codex [[concepts/security|Security]] Cloud" integrates cyber-capable models (Daybreak Blue) to continuously scan [[entities/github|GitHub]] repositories, identify vulnerabilities, and suggest fixes. "Codex in the Cloud" transforms the development [[concepts/experience|experience]], allowing developers to run Codex environments remotely from any device. A refreshed "[[concepts/codex-cli|Codex CLI]]" now includes [[concepts/instruction-control|voice control]] and enhanced workflow management. Additionally, a new "Code Review" feature in the ChatGPT desktop app simplifies project changes, and the "Decisions API" leverages Luna's intelligence for real-time, finite [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]]. Finally, "ChatGPT Space" offers a collaborative workspace akin to [[entities/notion|Notion]], allowing teams to work with [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] across various document types and projects, and "Plugin Extensions" have been relaunched, enabling deeper integration of [[concepts/third-party-applications|third-party applications]] within ChatGPT with token portability. These initiatives reflect OpenAI's commitment to creating a comprehensive, collaborative, and increasingly automated environment for both consumers and developers.
