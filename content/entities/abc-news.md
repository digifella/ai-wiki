# ABC News

ABC News is the news division of the [[concepts/organization|Australian Broadcasting Corporation]] (ABC), Australia's national public broadcaster. It provides comprehensive news coverage across multiple platforms including television, radio, and digital media through its website [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] abc.net.au/news and mobile [[concepts/software|applications]].

The [[concepts/organization|organization]] delivers news content spanning national and international events, current affairs, and investigative reporting. ABC News serves as a primary source of information for Australian audiences and operates under the ABC's charter to provide impartial, factual reporting in the public interest.
