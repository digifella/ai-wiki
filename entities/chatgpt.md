# ChatGPT

**ChatGPT** is an advanced [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by [[entities/openai]], capable of generating text, code, and multimodal content. It serves as a central interface for accessing various AI capabilities, including generative image and video creation, as well as direct control of external creative software.

## Capabilities & Evolution

*   **Multimodal Generation:** Generates text, code, and multimodal content, expanding beyond pure text interfaces.
*   **RLCD & [[concepts/decision-making|Decision Making]]:** Research by co-inventor [[entities/diogo-almeida]] indicates a [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] toward [[concepts/calibrated-decision-making|calibrated decision-making]] via [[concepts/rlcd]], moving away from purely [[concepts/human-preferred-text|human-preferred text]] generation.
