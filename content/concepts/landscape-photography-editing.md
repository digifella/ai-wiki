# Landscape Photography Editing

[[concepts/landscape-photography|Landscape photography]] editing involves a [[concepts/range|range]] of techniques designed to enhance natural scenes while maintaining their authenticity. The editing process typically focuses on adjusting [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color balance, [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], and clarity to bring out the details and mood of the landscape. Common [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] include correcting white balance to handle different lighting conditions, recovering shadow and highlight details, and refining saturation and vibrance to [[entities/make|make]] colors more compelling without appearing artificial.

## Selective Editing and Masking

Modern landscape editing often requires selective adjustments to different parts of an image. Rather than applying edits uniformly across a photograph, editors use masks to isolate specific areas—such as the sky, foreground, or individual elements—and apply targeted adjustments to each. This approach allows for precise [[concepts/power|control]], such as darkening an overexposed sky without affecting the landscape below, or enhancing the contrast of distant mountains independently from the foreground.

## AI-Assisted Masking in Lightroom Classic

Recent developments in editing [[concepts/software|software]] have introduced [[concepts/ai-detection|automated AI masking]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] that streamline the selective [[concepts/photography-workflow|editing workflow]]. [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] now offers single-click preset masks that use [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automatically identify and select common landscape elements, such as skies and foliage. These presets allow photographers to apply multiple masks simultaneously with minimal manual effort, significantly reducing the time required for complex editing tasks while maintaining creative control over individual adjustments.
