# Nursing Roles

[[concepts/community-health-nursing|Community health nursing]] encompasses several distinct professional roles, each contributing to population-level [[concepts/health|health]] outcomes and individual care. These roles define the scope of practice, responsibilities, and focus areas for nurses working in community settings rather than hospital environments. Understanding these roles is essential for clarifying professional boundaries and ensuring appropriate allocation of nursing expertise within health systems.

## Core Nursing Roles in Community Health

[[concepts/community-health|Community health]] nurses serve as direct care providers, educators, advocates, and coordinators within their populations. The nurse as caregiver provides assessment, treatment, and [[concepts/health-promotion|health promotion]] services to individuals and families. The educator role involves teaching clients about [[concepts/primary-prevention-interventions-designed-to-prevent-disease-before-it-occurs|disease prevention]], health maintenance, and self-management strategies. Nurses also function as case managers, coordinating services and resources across multiple providers to ensure [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] of care. Additionally, community health nurses advocate for clients' rights and needs, particularly among vulnerable or underserved populations.

Broader professional responsibilities include research and evaluation, where nurses contribute to evidence-based practice by documenting outcomes and participating in quality improvement initiatives. Community health nurses may also take [[concepts/leadership|leadership]] roles in policy development and community planning, influencing health systems and [[concepts/public-health|public health]] decisions [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] organisational and population levels. These varied roles reflect the complexity of modern nursing practice and the diverse [[concepts/skill|skill]] set required to address health inequities and promote wellness across communities.
