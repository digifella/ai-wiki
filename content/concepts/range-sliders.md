# Range Sliders

Range sliders are interface controls used in Adobe [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] that allow users to define the scope of color [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] within the [[concepts/point-color|Point Color]] feature. These sliders enable precise selection of which tones or [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] will be affected by editing operations, functioning as a gating mechanism that constrains adjustments to specific portions of an image's tonal or chromatic spectrum.

## Application in Adobe Camera Raw

Within Adobe Camera Raw's Point Color feature, range sliders work in conjunction with [[concepts/color-variance-control|color variance control]] to target adjustments to particular areas of an image. When editing landscapes with diverse color information—such as scenes containing reflections—range sliders help isolate adjustments to prevent unintended modifications to other parts of the image. This targeted approach allows for more controlled and nuanced [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|color grading]] without affecting the entire image uniformly.

The practical workflow involves setting slider positions to define upper and lower boundaries of the color range to be modified. This methodology is particularly useful when working with complex [[concepts/images|images]] where multiple color regions need different treatment, enabling editors to work efficiently without requiring extensive [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] or layering techniques.
