# Sakana Ai

[[entities/sakana|Sakana]] AI is an [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research company focused on developing [[concepts/2d-grid-simulation|digital ecosystems]] that simulate the survival and coexistence of multiple AI species. The company's research explores how different [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] interact, compete, and cooperate within constrained [[concepts/virtual-environments|virtual environments]].

## Research Focus

The company's core work involves creating simulations where AI [[concepts/nodes|entities]] operate under resource constraints and environmental pressures similar to biological ecosystems. These digital environments are designed to study emergent behaviors, evolutionary dynamics, and the conditions under which different [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] can coexist sustainably. The research aims to understand fundamental principles about [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] interaction that could inform the design of more robust and cooperative AI systems.

### Automated Research Paradigms

Beyond ecosystem [[concepts/simulation|simulation]], Sakana AI develops automated research frameworks, notably the [[concepts/ai-scientist|AI Scientist]], which automates the [[concepts/scientific-discovery|scientific discovery]] process.

Recent advancements include the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of **[[entities/fugu|Fugu]]** and **[[concepts/fugu-ultra|Fugu Ultra]]**, multi-agent AI systems designed for [[concepts/complex-orchestration|complex orchestration]] tasks. Key aspects include:
