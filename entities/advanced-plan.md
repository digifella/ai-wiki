# Advanced Plan

The Advanced Plan is a mid-tier subscription offering from Evernote designed for users with substantial note-taking and organizational requirements. It provides enhanced features and storage capacity beyond Evernote's basic free and starter tiers, positioning it as a bridge between entry-level users and enterprise customers. This tier is part of Evernote's broader pricing structure, catering to individuals who need more robust tools than the free version offers but do not yet require the full suite of enterprise-grade solutions.

The plan typically includes increased monthly upload limits, offline access to notebooks, and advanced search capabilities. These features support power users who manage large volumes of documents and require reliable synchronization across multiple devices. By offering a middle ground in Evernote's pricing model, the Advanced Plan addresses the needs of professionals and students who find the free tier too restrictive but do not need the collaborative tools found in higher tiers.

User reactions to Evernote's pricing adjustments have frequently centered on the value proposition of this specific tier. As the company shifted its focus toward enterprise solutions and increased prices for premium plans, the Advanced Plan became a focal point for discussions regarding cost versus utility. Many users have evaluated whether the incremental benefits justify the cost, leading to broader conversations about migration to alternative note-taking applications that offer more competitive pricing structures for similar feature sets.
