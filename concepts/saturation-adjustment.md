# Saturation Adjustment

**Saturation Adjustment** refers to the process of modifying the intensity or purity of colors within an image. In professional workflows, precise control is required to avoid unnatural artifacts, particularly when dealing with complex subjects like human skin.

## Key Techniques & Tools

*   **Vectorscope Analysis**: Utilizing the Vectorscope allows for objective measurement of color balance and saturation levels, ensuring skin tones remain within the designated "skin tone line" or vector path.
*   **AI-Assisted Masking**: Modern workflows integrate [[concepts/ai-masking]] to isolate specific regions (e.g., skin) for targeted saturation adjustments, preventing [[concepts/halos|color spill]] into hair or backgrounds.
*   **[[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]]**: [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s [[entities/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] Filter provides a robust Vectorscope interface for real-time monitoring during global or local adjustments.
