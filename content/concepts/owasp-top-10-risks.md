# OWASP Top 10 Risks

The [[concepts/owasp-top-10-for-ai-agents|OWASP Top 10 for AI]] [[concepts/agentic-applications|Agentic Applications]] is a framework developed by the [[entities/owasp|Open Worldwide Application Security Project]] to identify and address the most critical security vulnerabilities specific to [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]] and autonomous systems. Unlike traditional OWASP Top 10 lists focused on [[concepts/web-applications|web applications]], this variant addresses unique risks introduced by AI agents—systems designed to perform tasks, make decisions, and take actions with varying degrees of autonomy. The framework helps organizations prioritize security efforts when developing, deploying, and maintaining [[concepts/ai-agentic-applications|agentic AI systems]].

## Scope and Application

This framework applies to AI systems that operate with some level of autonomy, including [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]]-based [[concepts/agents|agents]], robotic process [[concepts/automation|automation]], and decision-making systems. The risks encompass both technical vulnerabilities and broader systemic concerns, such as prompt injection attacks, insecure [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agent design patterns]], and inadequate monitoring of agent behavior. Organizations developing agentic applications use this framework to assess their security posture and implement appropriate controls before and after [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].

## Purpose and Use

The OWASP Top 10 for AI Agentic Applications serves as a reference guide for security practitioners, developers, and risk managers involved in [[concepts/ai-projects|AI projects]]. By establishing a shared taxonomy of risks, it enables cross-organizational communication about AI security threats and supports the development of defensive measures, [[concepts/testing|testing]] methodologies, and [[concepts/mitigation-strategies|mitigation strategies]] tailored to [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|agentic systems]].
