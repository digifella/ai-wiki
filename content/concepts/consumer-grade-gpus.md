# Consumer-grade GPUs

[[concepts/hardware|Hardware]] designed for end-users, characterized by lower [[concepts/vram]] capacities compared to enterprise-grade accelerators, but increasingly capable of [[concepts/running|running]] complex [[concepts/reasoning|AI Inference]] workloads.

## Key Capabilities & Developments
- **Local [[concepts/generative-ai-models|Generative AI]]**: Recent advancements in [[concepts/open-source|open-source]]/open-[[concepts/weights|weights]] models, such as [[entities/ltx-2]], enable high-fidelity [[concepts/ai-generated-videos|AI Video Generation]] directly on consumer [[concepts/hardware|hardware]].
- **Multimodal Processing**: Emerging architectures allow for the simultaneous generation of video and [[concepts/synchronized-audio]] locally.
- **Efficiency Optimizations**: Development of models optimized for Low VRAM environments allows for sophisticated generative tasks without reliance on cloud-based computing.
