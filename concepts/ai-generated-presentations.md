# AI Generated Presentations

AI Generated Presentations refer to the automated creation of presentation slides using artificial intelligence systems. Rather than manually designing each slide, users provide text input—such as prompts, outlines, or structured data—which AI systems process to generate both content and formatting. This approach combines large language models for content generation with presentation formatting tools to produce complete, ready-to-use slideshows.

## Workflow and Implementation

The typical process involves a user providing high-level input to an AI system, which then generates presentation content in a structured format. The Marp system exemplifies this approach by using AI to create presentations through Markdown, a lightweight markup language. Users describe their presentation topic or provide an outline, and the AI generates appropriately formatted Markdown that Marp can convert into slides. This separates content generation from visual presentation, allowing flexibility in how the final slides are rendered.

## Applications and Use Cases

AI-generated presentations accelerate workflows where speed is valued, such as quick business briefings, educational materials, or iterative design processes. The technology can handle both content creation and structural decisions about slide organization, though the quality of output typically depends on the clarity of user input and the underlying AI model's capabilities.
