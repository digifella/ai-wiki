# Vercel

Vercel is a cloud platform for deploying and hosting [[concepts/web-applications|web applications]], particularly those built with modern JavaScript frameworks. The platform provides serverless functions, [[concepts/edge-computing|edge computing]], and integrated [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] pipelines that enable developers to quickly move [[concepts/software|applications]] from development to production environments.

## Billing and Cost Considerations

Vercel's [[concepts/pricing-model|pricing model]] has been the subject of discussion within development communities regarding unexpected cost overruns. In a notable case documented by [[entities/matthew-berman|Matthew Berman]], significant billing charges were incurred during the deployment of [[entities/journey-kits|Journey Kits]], an [[concepts/ai-coding-assistance|AI-assisted coding]] project. This experience highlighted potential challenges with cost management when deploying applications that utilize intensive [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] or experience high traffic volumes, particularly in the context of [[concepts/programmatic-interaction|AI-enhanced development]] workflows.
