# Training Pauses

**Training pauses** refer to the deliberate suspension of [[concepts/training-process|model training]] or deployment pipelines triggered by the detection of anomalous, unsafe, or unaligned behaviors in [[concepts/frontier-ai]]. These interventions are critical [[concepts/risk-mitigation-strategies|risk mitigation strategies]] employed by AI laboratories to prevent [[concepts/containment-breaches]] or irreversible loss of control.
