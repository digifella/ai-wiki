# Cargo Return Vehicle

A **cargo return vehicle** (CRV) is a spacecraft or aerodynamic capsule designed to transport payload from orbit, lunar surface, or other celestial bodies back to [[entities/earth|Earth]]. Unlike disposable capsules, modern CRVs prioritize reusability, rapid turnaround, and high-volume logistics to support sustained off-world infrastructure.

## Key Characteristics
- **Re-entry Capability**: Utilizes heat shields, lifting-body [[concepts/fluid-dynamics|aerodynamics]], or powered descent for atmospheric interface.
- **Payload Efficiency**: Optimized volume-to-mass ratio for freight rather than human life-support systems.
- **Autonomy**: Capable of uncrewed re-entry and landing/recovery operations.

## Development & Examples
### Historical Precedents
- Space Shuttle: Early attempt at reusable cargo/crew transport, largely phased out due to cost.
- Dragon (spacecraft): Current primary CRV for [[entities/nasa]] and commercial payloads; partial reusability.
