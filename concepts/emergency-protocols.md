# Emergency Protocols

Emergency protocols are detailed procedures established to ensure a coordinated response in the event of a disaster or [[concepts/crisis|crisis]]. These plans help mitigate risks and provide guidelines for [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] during emergencies.

## Key Components
- **Incident Command System (ICS)**: A standardized approach to command, control, and coordinate emergency response.
- **Communication Plans**: Ensuring clear lines of communication between all stakeholders involved in the emergency response.
- **Resource Management**: Identifying, mobilizing, allocating, and managing resources effectively during emergencies.
