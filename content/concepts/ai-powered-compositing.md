The [[concepts/integration|integration]] of [[concepts/generative-ai]]-driven features with traditional Digital Imaging manipulation techniques to streamline complex image assembly, element synthesis, and [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]].

* **Asset Pipeline**: Initial image selection and importing via [[concepts/lightroom]].
