# Agentic Frameworks

Agentic frameworks are software tools and libraries designed to facilitate the development of autonomous AI agents. These systems are capable of perceiving their environment, making decisions, and executing actions with minimal human intervention. By providing standardized abstractions for agent architecture, these frameworks simplify the complex process of creating intelligent systems that can operate independently.

Key components typically included in these frameworks are decision-making loops, planning mechanisms, and tool integration protocols. They also establish standards for inter-agent communication, allowing multiple agents to coordinate and share information effectively. This structured approach enables developers to focus on specific agent behaviors and goals rather than reinventing foundational architectural patterns for each new application.

The rise of agentic frameworks has introduced new governance and cybersecurity challenges for organizations. The autonomous nature of these agents can lead to ungoverned implementations that operate outside established security boundaries. Consequently, organizations are increasingly concerned about the potential for these systems to introduce vulnerabilities or act in ways that conflict with corporate policies and regulatory requirements.
