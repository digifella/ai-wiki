# Transport Efficiency

**Transport efficiency** refers to the optimization of movement of people and goods, minimizing time, cost, and energy consumption per unit of output. It is a critical metric for evaluating [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] projects, particularly in geographically dispersed nations.

## Key Drivers
*   **Modal Shift:** Transitioning from low-efficiency modes (private cars, short-haul flights) to high-efficiency modes ([[concepts/high-speed-rail|high-speed rail]], mass transit).
*   **Network Density:** Increasing connectivity reduces marginal costs and improves overall system throughput.
*   **Technological Integration:** Use of smart-grids and autonomous-vehicles to optimize routing and energy use.

## Case Study: Australia's High-Speed Rail
Australia's vast geography and concentrated coastal population create significant inefficiencies in current [[concepts/intercity-travel|inter-city travel]], dominated by road and air transport. The proposed high-speed rail network aims to rectify this by offering a time-competitive alternative to domestic flights.

### Albanese Administration's Vision
Prime Minister [[entities/anthony-albanese|Anthony Albanese]] has championed a high-speed rail network as a transformative infrastructure project. Key aspects include:
*   **Economic Integration:** Reducing travel time between major economic hubs (e.g., [[entities/sydney|Sydney]], Melbourne, Brisbane) to foster labor mobility and business integration.
*   **Environmental Impact:** Shifting passengers from carbon-intensive air travel to electric rail, significantly lowering the carbon-footprint of domestic transport.
*   **Urban Decongestion:** Alleviating [[concepts/pressure|pressure]] on airports and highways by providing a reliable, high-capacity alternative.

### Transformative Potential
*   **Time Efficiency:** High-speed rail can match or exceed door-to-door travel times for distances under 1,000 km, where airport security and transit times often negate flight speed advantages.
*   **Productivity:** On-board work capabilities and reliability make rail superior to air travel for business efficiency.
*   **Regional Development:** Potential to stimulate growth in intermediate regions along the corridor.
