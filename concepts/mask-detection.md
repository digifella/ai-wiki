# Mask Detection

Mask Detection is an automated feature in Lightroom Classic that utilizes artificial intelligence to generate selection masks with a single click. Instead of requiring users to manually draw or paint precise selections, the tool applies pre-configured AI presets to automatically identify and isolate specific elements within an image. This functionality streamlines the editing workflow by significantly reducing the time traditionally spent on manual masking tasks.

The feature includes specialized presets tailored for landscape and people photography. In landscape workflows, the algorithm can automatically detect and isolate the sky, allowing for targeted adjustments to atmospheric elements without affecting the foreground. For portrait and people photography, the AI identifies human subjects to facilitate precise edits on skin, hair, or clothing, ensuring that adjustments remain confined to the intended areas.
