# Personal Computer

A **Personal Computer** (PC) is a multi-purpose computer designed for individual use, characterized by its ability to run a variety of [[concepts/software]] and Operating System. Modern PCs serve as central hubs for [[concepts/productivity|productivity]], entertainment, and increasingly, local computational tasks such as [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] [[concepts/inference|inference]].

## Key Characteristics
- **General Purpose**: Capable of executing diverse tasks from word processing to complex data analysis.
- **User-Centric**: Designed for direct interaction by a single user at a time.
- **[[concepts/hardware|Hardware Components]]**: Typically includes a Central Processing Unit, [[concepts/ram|Random Access Memory]], [[entities/storage]], and [[concepts/webgpu|Graphics]] Processing Unit.

## Evolution and Capabilities
Historically limited to basic computing, modern PCs have evolved to support [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] tasks. The integration of powerful GPU has transformed the PC into a viable platform for [[concepts/on-device-ai|local machine learning]] workloads, reducing reliance on [[concepts/cloud-based-services|cloud-based services]] for tasks like image and [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]].
