# Firecrawl
## Overview
[[concepts/firecrawl-ai|Firecrawl]] is a tool designed to enable [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|artificial intelligence models]] to interact with the internet by allowing them to "see" and gather data from websites autonomously. This capability bridges the gap between AI's analytical prowess and its current inability to natively browse or engage with web content, positioning it as essential in the emerging era of [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]].

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/ai-agent|AI Agent]] Era**: A period marked by the increasing autonomy and capabilities of [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems.
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Autonomous AI Agents]]**: Intelligent entities that can independently navigate and interact with digital environments without direct human intervention.
