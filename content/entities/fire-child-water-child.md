# Fire Child Water Child

**[[concepts/stephen-scott-cowan|Fire Child]]** and **Water Child**: Core concepts from Dr. Stephen Scott Cowan's work exploring personality types through the ancient Chinese [[concepts/five-elements-theory|Five Elements theory]]: Wood, Fire, Earth, Gold (Metal), and Water.

## Tony Robbins' Five Elements: Understanding Personalities to Enhance Influence
### Summary
- The video delves into understanding personalities through the lens of the Five Elements.
- It aims to enhance influence in various [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] by recognizing elemental profiles.
- Draws insights from Dr. Stephen Scott Cowan's "Fire Child, Water Child" work.

### Key Points
- **Wood**: Assertive and forward-moving individuals.
- **Fire**: Warm, passionate people who lead with emotion.
- **Earth**: Grounded, nurturing personalities focused on stability.
- **Gold (Metal)**: Structured, organized types who value order.
- **Water**: Adaptable, intuitive people who flow around obstacles.
