# Asic Login Portal

The ASIC Login Portal is a web-based [[concepts/user-authentication-system|authentication system]] operated by the [[entities/asic-company|Australian Securities and Investments Commission]] (ASIC), the national corporate regulator. It serves as the primary digital access point for users to interact with ASIC’s services, providing [[concepts/secure|secure]] entry to company information, regulatory filings, and official business records maintained in ASIC’s national registers.

The portal facilitates the [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] of documentation related to incorporated companies, registered associations, and managed investment schemes. By requiring [[concepts/user-authentication|user authentication]], it ensures that sensitive regulatory data and proprietary business records are only accessible to authorized parties, thereby maintaining the [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] and confidentiality of the national registers.

Through this interface, businesses and professionals can manage their [[concepts/compliance|compliance]] obligations, submit necessary documents, and access historical data. The system supports the operational requirements of the corporate sector by streamlining the process of verifying company status and obtaining official extracts from ASIC’s databases.
