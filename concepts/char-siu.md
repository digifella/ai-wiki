# Char Siu

[[concepts/bbq-pork-preparation|Char Siu]] (Chinese: 叉燒; pinyin: chāshāo) is a [[concepts/cantonese-cuisine]] [[concepts/bbq|Barbecue]] Pork [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] defined by a sweet-savory [[concepts/glazing|glaze]] and caramelized crust. Commonly translated as "Honey Glazed Pork" or referred to as **[[entities/tarsiu|Tarsiu]]** in Cantonese dialect. See also: [[lab-notes/2026-05-28-Homemade-Char-Siu-Recipe-The-Woks-of-Life-Family-Method|Homemade Char Siu Recipe: The Woks of Life Family Method Summary]].

## Profile
- **Protein**: Pork Belly (fat-rich, tender) or Pork Leg (leaner).
- **Marinade Base**: [[concepts/light|Light]] Soy Sauce, Dark Soy Sauce, Hoisin Sauce, Five Spice Powder, Rock [[concepts/sugar|Sugar]] or Honey, Shaoxing Wine, minced garlic/ginger.
- **Color/Depth**: Red Fermented Bean Curd (fermented bean paste) provides signature hue and umami; red food coloring optional.
- **Cooking**: Roasting (charcoal or oven) with periodic basting; high heat finish for glaze char; internal temp ~65°C (149°F) before rest.

## Usage
- **[[concepts/cantonese-cha-siu|Char Siu]] Rice**: Sliced over steamed rice with sauce drizzle.
- **Dim Sum**: `Baozi` filling component.

## Key Insights: The Woks of Life Family Method
- **[[concepts/accessibility|Accessibility]]**: Homemade preparation is cost-effective and replicable without specialized rotisserie equipment.
- **Dialect Note**: In Cantonese, the dish is phonetically rendered as "Tarsiu."
- **Methodology**: Comprehensive home preparation focuses on replicating the traditional roast pork profile through controlled [[concepts/marinating|marination]] and roasting techniques.
