# Cultural Exchange

**Cultural exchange** refers to the sharing and mixing of ideas, values, traditions, beliefs, and artifacts between different communities or societies. This can occur through various means such as trade, conquest, missionary work, tourism, media, and education.

## Key Aspects
- **Trade Routes**: Ancient Silk Road, Spice Route, Trans-Saharan Trade Routes
- **Religion**: Spread of Buddhism from India to East Asia, Christianity from the Middle East to [[entities/europe|Europe]]
- **Mathematics**: Development and spread of algebra and zero by Islamic scholars
- **[[concepts/philosophy|Philosophy]] and Literature**: Exchange between Greek philosophy and Indian thought
