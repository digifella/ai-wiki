# Diogo Almeida

**Diogo Almeida** is a co-inventor of the techniques underlying [[entities/chatgpt]] and a key figure in the evolution of [[concepts/large-language-models|Large Language Models]] (LLMs). He is currently associated with [[concepts/rlcd]] (Reinforcement Learning from [[concepts/calibrated-decisions|Calibrated Decisions]]), a framework proposing a shift away from traditional [[concepts/human-preferred-text|human-preferred text]] generation toward [[concepts/calibrated-decision-making|calibrated decision-making]].

## Key Contributions & Concepts

*   **Co-inventor of ChatGPT's Core:** Played a pivotal role in developing the foundational techniques behind ChatGPT [[entities/chatgpt]].
*   **Advocate for RLCD:** Proposes that the industry is shifting from "human-preferred text" to "calibrated decisions" via the RLCD methodology [[concepts/rlcd]].
*   **Critique of Current LLM Paradigms:** Argues that the core idea of ChatGPT is being superseded by new training approaches that prioritize decision calibration over text preference [[concepts/rlcd]].
*   **Founder of [[concepts/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]]:** Established [[entities/typesafeai|TypeSafe.ai]] to develop Jev, an AI model explicitly designed for reliable decision-making with [[concepts/zero-hallucinations|zero hallucinations]], contrasting with traditional LLM text generation.
*   **Development of Jev:** Created Jev to address the limitations of current LLMs by focusing on fast, reliable, and hallucination-free decision outputs [[lab-notes/2026-09-19-Jev-TypeSafe.ais-Fast-Reliable-Decision-AI-with-Zero-Hal|Jev: TypeSafe.ai's Fast, Reliable Decision AI with Zero Hallucinations]].
