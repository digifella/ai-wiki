# Multimodal AI

[[concepts/multimodal-ai]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] models capable of ingesting and/or generating data across various [[concepts/data-management|Data-Modalities]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/modality|Modality]]**: A specific data type or format used as input or output.
- **Common Modalities**: Includes Text, Images, [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]], [[concepts/lidar|Lidar]], and [[concepts/thermal-imaging|Thermal-Imaging]].
- **Processing Capabilities**: Models are distinguished by their ability to integrate and [[concepts/purpose|reason]] across these different data sources.

### Recent Developments & Models
- **[[concepts/open-source-weights|Open-Weights]] & Agents**: Includes [[concepts/ai-licensing|open-weights models]] like Inkling and vision-only [[concepts/computer-use|Computer Use]] Agents like [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] [[entities/fara-15-27b|Fara 1.5-27B]].
- **[[concepts/structured-decision-models|Structured Decision]] Making**: [[concepts/emergent-behavior|Emergence]] of models designed for rapid, structured [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] rather than generative text output.
  - **[[lab-notes/2026-10-03-Clef-27B-Multimodal-AI-Decision-Model-for-Structured-Inp|Clef 27B: Multimodal AI Decision Model for Structured Input Analysis]]**: Cloudflare's 27 billion-parameter model designed for rapid decision-making.
    - **Input**: Text, images, video, or JSON data.
    - **Output**: Calibrated probabilities for specific questions rather than generated text.
    - **Focus**: [[concepts/chat-prompt|Structured input]] analysis and decision logic.
