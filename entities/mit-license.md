# MIT License

The [[concepts/mit-license|MIT License]] is a permissive [[concepts/photo-editing|free software]] [[concepts/license|license]] originating at the [[concepts/mit|Massachusetts Institute of Technology]] (MIT). It grants permissions for redistribution and use in source and binary forms, with conditions regarding copyright notices.

### Key Points
- **Permissive License**: Allows usage, modification, distribution, and even inclusion in proprietary software.
- **Simplicity**: The license text is short and easy to understand.
- **No Warranty**: Provides no warranties or guarantees about the quality of the software.
- **Common Usage**: Widely used for [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] projects.
