# Cellular Replication

**Cellular replication** is the biological process by which a cell divides to produce two or more daughter cells. It is the fundamental mechanism of growth, repair, and reproduction in living organisms. The process involves precise [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] of DNA Replication, Cytokinesis, and Cell Cycle [[concepts/regulation|Regulation]].

## Key Mechanisms
- **Binary Fission**: Primary method in prokaryotes.
- **Mitosis**: Nuclear division in eukaryotic somatic cells.
- **Meiosis**: Reduction division for gamete production.

## Synthetic Biology Advances
Recent developments in [[concepts/digital-gene-technology|Synthetic Biology]] aim to construct minimal artificial cells capable of autonomous life processes.

- **[[concepts/plant-animal-hybridization|Spud Cell]]**: A landmark bottom-up [[concepts/artificial-cell|artificial cell]] constructed in 2026. Unlike previous models, Spud Cell demonstrates full autonomy in eating, growing, and evolving.
