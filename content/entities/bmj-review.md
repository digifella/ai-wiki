# Bmj Review

The BMJ Review addresses a critical gap in [[concepts/health|healthcare]]'s approach to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/adoption|adoption]]. While AI technologies are being rapidly integrated into clinical practice and healthcare systems, the organisational frameworks necessary to govern their implementation have not kept pace. This mismatch creates significant risks around patient safety, data security, and the responsible use of these powerful tools.

## Governance and Ethics Challenges

Existing organisational governance structures in healthcare institutions often lack the specific processes and oversight mechanisms required to ensure AI systems are deployed safely and ethically. Previous attempts at establishing [[concepts/ai-governance|AI governance frameworks]] have primarily focused on high-level ethical principles rather than practical implementation guidance. The BMJ Review identifies this disconnect as a pressing concern that requires urgent [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] from healthcare administrators, clinicians, and policymakers.

The review emphasises that [[concepts/healthcare-organisations|healthcare organisations]] need to develop comprehensive governance systems that address not only the ethical dimensions of AI use but also operational safeguards, transparency mechanisms, and [[concepts/accountability|accountability]] structures. Without such frameworks in place, healthcare institutions risk deploying [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] without adequate scrutiny of their clinical validity, potential biases, or unintended consequences for patient care.
