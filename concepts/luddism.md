# Luddism

Early 19th-century English Labor [[concepts/exercise|Movement]] protesting the introduction of machinery during the [[concepts/industrial-revolution]]. Characterized by organized machine-breaking targeting specific technologies that de-skilled [[entities/employees|workers]] and depressed wages. Named after the legendary Ned Ludd. Modern usage often mischaracterizes the movement as anti-technology; historical evidence points to specific [[concepts/socio-economic-grievances|socio-economic grievances]] regarding labor standards and economic displacement.

## Core Themes
- Defense of skilled craftsmanship and quality standards against mechanization.
- Resistance to exploitation by factory owners and unregulated mechanization.
- Socio-economic motivations prioritized over technological opposition; demands focused on wages, working conditions, and [[concepts/regulation|regulation]].
