# Shrimp
Edible Crustacean species utilized globally as a staple culinary ingredient and Protein source. Characterized by a segmented exoskeleton, rapid thermal denaturation, and high nutrient density.

## Culinary Applications
- Primary protein in Stir-fry and noodle-based Asian Cuisine; prized for quick cook times and high sauce absorption.
- Central to [[concepts/singapore-noodles]], a stir-fried Rice Noodle dish traditionally paired with shrimp, eggs, and curry powder. Key [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] insights:
  - Chef [[entities/bill-leung|Bill Leung]] ([[entities/the-woks-of-life|The Woks of Life]]) demonstrates techniques for achieving restaurant-quality results that surpass standard takeout.
  - Emphasizes precise wok [[concepts/temperature-management|heat management]], ingredient sequencing, and balancing aromatic spices with fresh Shrimp to prevent rubbery [[concepts/texture|texture]].
  - Full methodology and ingredient breakdown documented in [[lab-notes/2026-05-16-Woks-of-Lifes-Bill-Leung-Mastering-Superior-Homemade-Sin|Woks of Life's Bill Leung: Mastering Superior Homemade Singapore Noodles]].
- Requires careful handling; turns opaque and firm [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] optimal doneness. Common preparations include Wok Cooking, steaming, [[concepts/bbq|grilling]], and curing for [[concepts/software|applications]] like Sushi or Ceviche.

## Nutritional & Biological Profile
- Rich in Omega-3 Fatty Acids, Selenium, Choline, and Vitamin B12; naturally low in Saturated Fat.
- Ecological impact varies by sourcing; sustainable Aquaculture and wild-caught certifications recommended to mitigate habitat strain.
- Taxonomically grouped with Prawn, Crab, and Lobster; distinguished by size, aquatic habitat, and regional culinary classification conventions.
- Processing often involves deveining, shelling, and quick blanching or marinating to preserve moisture and enhance umami.
