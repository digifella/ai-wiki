# European Union

The [[entities/eu|European Union]] is a political and economic union of 27 member states located primarily in [[entities/europe|Europe]]. Established through the Maastricht Treaty in 1992, it evolved from the earlier European Economic Community founded in 1957. The EU operates through a complex system of institutions including the European Commission, the Council of the European Union, and the European Parliament, with the Court of Justice serving as its highest judicial authority.

## Visa and Travel

Citizens of non-EU countries requiring entry to EU member states must typically obtain appropriate visas depending on their nationality and [[concepts/motivation|purpose]] of visit. [[entities/republic-of-turkey|Turkey]], while geographically straddling Europe and Asia, is not an EU member state but maintains candidate status. Turkish citizens and visitors to Turkey may require electronic visas or other travel documentation depending on bilateral agreements and their country of origin.

## Economic and Political Function

The EU maintains a single market allowing free [[concepts/exercise|movement]] of goods, services, capital, and people among member states. It also operates the eurozone, a monetary union of 20 member states that use the euro as their common currency. Member states coordinate [[concepts/policies|policies]] on trade, environmental [[concepts/open-standards|standards]], and regulatory frameworks while maintaining individual [[concepts/governance|governance]] over most domestic affairs.
