# SEO

Search Engine [[concepts/ai-workflow|Optimization]] (SEO) is the practice of increasing the organic visibility of web content within search engine results pages.

### Emerging Trends
- [[concepts/generative-engine-optimization]] ([[concepts/geo|GEO]]) is emerging as the successor to [[concepts/seo|traditional SEO]], focusing on adapting websites for AI-powered search engines.
- [[concepts/traditional-seo|Traditional SEO]] methodologies are facing obsolescence due to the rise of generative platforms such as Google [[concepts/ai-overviews|AI Overviews]], [[entities/chatgpt]], [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], and [[entities/gemini]].
- The shift moves optimization focus from ranking in link lists to ensuring visibility within [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] [[concepts/responses|responses]] and summaries.
