# Elementary particles

Fundamental constituents of matter that, according to the [[concepts/standard-model]], possess no internal [[concepts/structure|structure]].

## Classification
- **Fermions** (Matter particles):
    - Quarks: Particles that experience the strong interaction.
    - Leptons: Including electrons and neutrinos.
- **Bosons** (Force carriers):
    - Gauge bosons: Mediators of fundamental forces, such as Gluons and Photons.

## Substructure Research
- **Compositeness**: Ongoing investigation into whether particles currently classified as elementary actually possess internal constituents.
- [[entities/lhc|LHC]] [[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] Tests for [[concepts/quark-substructure|Quark Substructure]]: Experimental [[concepts/testing|testing]] via the [[entities/cms-experiment|CMS Experiment]] at the LHC to search for evidence of quark internal [[concepts/structure|structure]].
