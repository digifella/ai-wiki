# PDP-11/44

The PDP-11/44 is a computer from Digital Equipment Corporation's PDP-11 series, released in 1979. It was a mid-range 16-bit minicomputer intended for scientific, educational, and commercial [[concepts/software|applications]]. The system featured improved [[concepts/memory|memory]] management and floating-point capabilities compared to earlier PDP-11 models.

## Notable Demonstration

A PDP-11/44 was used in a video presentation by Dave to demonstrate transformer AI [[concepts/training|training]] concepts. The presentation, titled "EXPOSED: The Dirty Little Secret of AI (On a 1979 PDP-11)," explored how fundamental machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] principles could be illustrated using [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] from that era. The demonstration highlighted the conceptual basis of [[concepts/transformer-models|transformer architecture]] independent of modern specialized hardware.
