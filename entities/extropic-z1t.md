# Extropic Z1T

**Extropic Z1T** is a novel AI [[concepts/hardware-architecture|hardware architecture]] developed by Extropic that utilizes probabilistic [[concepts/p-bits|P-bits]] to achieve significant improvements in energy efficiency compared to traditional GPU-based [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Core Technology:** Utilizes probabilistic P-bits (Pseudo-random bits) for [[concepts/computation|computation]], diverging from deterministic logic gates used in standard silicon.
- **Efficiency Gains:** Claims to deliver [[concepts/weathernext-3|AI models]] that are **100x more energy-efficient** than current GPU implementations.
- **Primary Use Case:** Addressing the escalating energy consumption and thermal constraints associated with large-scale AI model training and [[concepts/model-inference|inference]].
- **Architecture:** Designed to handle stochastic processes natively, making it particularly suitable for [[concepts/probabilistic-ai-models|probabilistic AI models]] and sampling-heavy workloads.
