# Product Safety

Product Safety is a documentary series created by Destin Sandlin that investigates the manufacturing processes and safety protocols governing consumer goods in the United States. Produced over a four-year period, the project aims to demystify how everyday items are designed, tested, and regulated to ensure they meet established safety standards. The series serves as an educational resource for understanding the complex intersection of engineering, regulatory compliance, and industrial manufacturing.

The production involves on-site examinations of factories and testing facilities to observe the rigorous procedures required for product certification. By focusing on specific consumer items, the series highlights the technical challenges and regulatory hurdles involved in bringing safe products to market. This approach provides viewers with a detailed look at the behind-the-scenes mechanisms that prevent defects and ensure public safety.

The project underscores the importance of standardized testing and quality control in modern manufacturing. It explores how different industries adapt to federal regulations and international standards to maintain compliance. Through this lens, the series offers a factual examination of the systems that protect consumers from hazardous products while facilitating industrial innovation.
