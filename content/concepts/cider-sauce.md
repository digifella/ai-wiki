# Cider sauce

A culinary sauce base utilizing cider to provide acidity and fruit-forward notes, often enriched with cream to create a rich, velvety [[concepts/texture|texture]].

### Culinary Applications
- **Poaching [[entities/medium|Medium]]:** Used as a liquid base for poaching fish, specifically Salmon, following Normandy culinary traditions.
- **Flavor Profile:** Integrates the sweetness of apple-based liquids with [[concepts/cream-sauce|cream sauce]] to balance acidity and richness.
