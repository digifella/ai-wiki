# 2d Image Rotation

2D image rotation is a geometric transformation that reorients a flat image around a central point or axis while preserving its two-dimensional character. Traditional implementations rely on mathematical algorithms to reposition pixels, altering the image's orientation without introducing depth information or changing intrinsic properties. This process has historically been limited to fixed angular increments, such as 90°, 180°, or 270°, or free rotation within the original plane, serving as a fundamental operation in digital image editing for decades.

The advent of generative AI has expanded the capabilities of this concept beyond simple planar manipulation. Tools such as the AI Rotate Object feature in Photoshop Beta allow for the 3D manipulation of 2D images. This technology enables users to simulate perspective changes and depth, effectively breaking the constraints of the original flat plane while maintaining the visual integrity of the subject.

This evolution marks a shift from rigid geometric transformations to dynamic spatial adjustments. By integrating 3D context into 2D workflows, modern software provides greater flexibility for creative pursuits, allowing for more realistic compositing and object placement without requiring manual reconstruction of the scene geometry.
