# Boeing 737 Max 8

The Boeing 737 Max 8 is a narrow-body, twin-[[concepts/engine|engine]] [[concepts/jet-transport|jet airliner]] manufactured by Boeing Commercial Airplanes. It is part of the 737 Next Generation family and the 737 MAX family, featuring CFM International LEAP-1B engines and advanced aerodynamic features.

## Notable Incidents

### FlyDubai Flight 1073
On September 30, 2026, a Boeing 737 Max 8 operated by FlyDubai as Flight 1073 experienced a severe [[concepts/cybersecurity-incident|security breach]] during a flight from Dubai to Tel Aviv.

*   **Incident Overview:** Approximately two hours into the flight, First Officer [[entities/hamam-al-hammami|Hamam Al-Hammami]] (Omani national) launched a [[concepts/cockpit-attack|cockpit attack]].
*   **Casualties/Status:** The aircraft carried 174 passengers and 6 crew members (including two off-duty pilots). The situation was resolved in a desert [[concepts/emergency-landing|emergency landing]].
*   **Investigation:** The First Officer had a documented history of extremist ideologies.
