# Chickpea Salad

Chickpea salad is a cold dish that combines cooked chickpeas, also known as garbanzo beans, with fresh vegetables and a light dressing. Typically served as a side dish or a light main course, the preparation involves mixing the legumes with ingredients such as tomatoes, cucumbers, and onions. The mixture is usually dressed with olive oil, lemon juice, or vinegar, and seasoned with herbs and spices like parsley, cumin, or garlic. Chickpeas are legumes native to the Mediterranean and Middle East regions, though they are now cultivated globally.

## Nutritional Profile and Health

The dish is characterized by a high fiber and protein content, which contributes to prolonged satiety and makes it a suitable option for weight management diets. Chickpeas provide complex carbohydrates that release energy slowly, helping to stabilize blood sugar levels compared to refined grain-based salads. The inclusion of healthy fats from olive oil aids in the absorption of fat-soluble vitamins found in the accompanying vegetables.

From a micronutrient perspective, chickpeas are a significant source of folate, iron, and magnesium. When combined with vitamin C-rich ingredients like tomatoes or lemon juice, the bioavailability of non-heme iron is enhanced. The low energy density of the salad, relative to its volume and nutrient content, supports caloric control without compromising essential nutrient intake.
