## Policy Changes in AI Subscriptions

**[[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]'s [[entities/claude|Claude]] AI Subscription Changes:** Recent policy modifications have significantly impacted users of third-party tools like [[concepts/openclaw|OpenClaw]]. As of April 4th, 2026:

- **[[concepts/automated-information-pipelines|OpenClaw]] Ban:** Usage through [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]] such as [[concepts/conversational-chatbots|OpenClaw]] is no longer covered by existing subscriptions.
- **[[concepts/usage-limits|Usage Limits]] and Financials:** Users are required to either "turn on extra usage" (implying additional costs) or cancel their subscriptions entirely.
