# Pressure

**Pressure** is defined as the force applied perpendicular to the surface of an object per unit area. In [[concepts/fluid-motion|fluid-dynamics]], pressure is a scalar quantity that plays a central role in the Navier-[[entities/stokes|Stokes]] equations, which describe the motion of viscous fluid substances.

## Key Concepts

*   **[[entities/navier|Navier-Stokes Equations]]**: The fundamental partial differential equations governing [[concepts/fluid-flow|fluid flow]]. The pressure term in these equations represents the force per unit area exerted by the fluid.
*   **[[concepts/existence-and-smoothness|Existence and Smoothness]]**: One of the seven Clay Mathematics Institute asks whether smooth solutions to the Navier-Stokes equations always exist in three dimensions.
*   **Fluid Flow [[concepts/blow-up|Blow-Up]]**: Refers to scenarios where the solution to the Navier-Stokes equations becomes infinite or undefined in finite time, challenging the smoothness conjecture.
