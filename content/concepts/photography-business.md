# Photography Business

A photography business encompasses the commercial practice of creating [[concepts/images|images]] for clients across advertising, editorial, and commercial contexts. Like other creative businesses, modern photography practitioners increasingly incorporate [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] tools into their workflows to improve efficiency and creative output.

## Effective Use of AI in Photography Work

[[entities/kevin-patrick-robbins|Kevin Patrick Robbins]], an advertising, editorial, and commercial photographer, has noted that many photography business practitioners achieve only [[concepts/superficial-results|superficial results]] when using [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) in their work. He attributes this limitation primarily to the use of overly simplistic prompts that fail to provide sufficient detail or context to the AI system. More effective results require photographers to invest effort in crafting detailed, specific prompts that clearly communicate their creative [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] and technical requirements.

The distinction between superficial and substantive AI-assisted work in photography reflects a broader principle: the quality of AI output directly correlates with the quality of input provided by the user. Photography businesses seeking to leverage these tools effectively must move beyond basic queries and develop [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] [[concepts/skills|skills]] tailored to their specific creative and commercial needs.
