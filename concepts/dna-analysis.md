# DNA Analysis

## Overview
**DNA Analysis** refers to the scientific techniques used to determine the Nucleotide sequence of DNA molecules. It is fundamental to Genomics, Forensic [[concepts/science|Science]], and [[concepts/personalized-medicine|Personalized Medicine]]. Recent advancements have shifted towards high-throughput sequencing and computational interpretation driven by [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]].

## Recent Developments (2026)
- **[[concepts/ai-integration|AI Integration]] in Genomics**: Emerging [[concepts/ai-models|AI Models]] are being deployed as "co-scientists" to accelerate DNA sequence interpretation and variant calling. See [[lab-notes/2026-05-25-AI-Progress-Co-Scientists-DNA-NPCs-Robotics-Multimodal-V|AI Progress: Co-Scientists, DNA, NPCs, Robotics, Multimodal, Video Editing]] for specific model [[concepts/software-updates|updates]].
- **Automated Lab Workflow**: Integration with [[concepts/robotics]] for sample [[concepts/preparation|preparation]] reduces human error and increases throughput.

## Key Techniques
- **Sequencing**:
  - Next-Generation Sequencing (NGS)
  - Single-Nucleotide Polymorphism (SNP) Analysis
- **Amplification**:
  - Polymerase [[concepts/domino-effect|Chain Reaction]] (PCR)
- **[[concepts/numerical-analysis|Computational Analysis]]**:
  - Alignment [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] (e.g., BWA, Bowtie)
  - Variant annotation

## Applications
- **Medical Diagnostics**: Identifying [[concepts/genetic-disorders|genetic disorders]] and cancer markers.
- **Forensics**: Individual identification via Short Tandem Repeat (STR) profiling.
- **Ancestry**: Tracing [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|lineage]] and population genetics.
