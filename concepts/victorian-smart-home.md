# Victorian smart home

The concept of automated domestic systems predates the digital age, with Cragside serving as the primary historical precedent for modern smart home technology.

## Historical Precedent: Cragside

[[lab-notes/2026-08-05-Cragside-William-Armstrongs-World-First-Victorian-Smart|Cragside: William Armstrong's World-First Victorian Smart Home Innovations]]

*   **Location:** Northumberland, [[entities/united-kingdom|England]]
*   **Key Figure:** [[concepts/william-george-armstrong]], often referred to as "the magician of the North"
*   **Significance:** Recognized as the [[entities/earth|world]]'s first smart home due to its early integration of automated utility systems.
*   **Core Innovations:**
    *   Implementation of [[concepts/hydroelectric-power|hydroelectric power]] generation for domestic use.
    *   Automated control of lighting, heating, and [[concepts/water-management-systems|water systems]].
    *   Early examples of [[concepts/remote-monitoring|remote monitoring]] and control within a residential setting.
