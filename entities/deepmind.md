# DeepMind
## Overview
[[entities/google-deepmind|DeepMind]] Technologies Limited is a British [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] research company founded in September 2010 by [[entities/demis-hassabis|Demis Hassabis]], Shubho Sengupta, and Mustafa Suleyman with the goal of solving intelligence and then using it to solve everything else. In January 2014, [[concepts/google-search|Google]] acquired DeepMind for approximately £400 million.

### Achievements
- AlphaGo: A program that learns to play the board game Go better than any software or human before.
- AlphaFold: An [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] designed to predict protein structure with unprecedented accuracy.
