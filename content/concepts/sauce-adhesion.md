# Sauce Adhesion

The physical ability of a sauce to cling to the surface of food, specifically within the context of Pasta [[concepts/cooking-methods|cooking methods]].

## Key Determinants
- **Surface [[concepts/texture|Texture]]:** The starch profile and physical roughness of the pasta.
- Texture optimization: Managing the structural [[concepts/integrity|integrity]] of the pasta to provide a suitable substrate for coating.
- Flavor [[concepts/integration|integration]]: The efficacy of the sauce in coating and penetrating the pasta surface.
- **Viscosity Matching:** The relationship between sauce density and the surface topography of the pasta.
