# AI Avatars

AI avatars are digital characters powered by artificial intelligence that serve as visual interfaces within applications and software platforms. Rather than interacting solely through text or traditional UI elements, users engage with animated or rendered representations that respond to input, deliver content, and perform specific tasks. These avatars function as intermediaries between users and underlying AI systems, combining visual presentation with computational capabilities to create more direct and intuitive interactions.

## Integration in Google Products

Google has incorporated AI avatars into several of its products to enhance user engagement and content creation. Google Vids uses avatars to generate video content, allowing users to create presentations and videos with AI-generated presenters. NotebookLM similarly integrates avatar functionality to provide visual representation of AI-generated content, such as audio overviews and explanations. These implementations demonstrate how avatars can extend the utility of AI tools beyond text-based interactions into multimedia formats.

## Video Generation with VEO3

VEO3 represents Google's generative model for video creation, enabling the production of dynamic visual content. When integrated with avatar technology, VEO3 facilitates the generation of video content featuring AI-powered characters that can deliver information, present ideas, or engage users in interactive scenarios. This combination allows for the automated creation of video materials that would otherwise require significant manual production effort, making avatar-driven video generation more accessible for various applications.
