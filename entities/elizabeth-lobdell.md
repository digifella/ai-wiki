# Elizabeth Lobdell

Elizabeth Lobdell is a writer and journalist who contributes to BBC Future, a digital publication specializing in science communication and long-form explanatory journalism. Her work focuses on making complex scientific research accessible to general audiences, with a particular emphasis on environmental topics and large-scale scientific experiments.

## Biosphere 2 Reporting

Lobdell has produced reporting on the Biosphere 2 experiment, a major ecological research project conducted in Arizona. Her coverage examines the experiment's significance for understanding Earth's ecosystems and the broader implications for environmental science.
