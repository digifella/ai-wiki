# AI Videos

AI Videos is a content generation feature within [[entities/gartner|Google NotebookLM]] that automatically produces video presentations from source materials. Users can upload documents, notes, PDFs, and other text-based content, which the system processes to generate corresponding video output. This feature eliminates the need for manual video production, scripting, or editing, streamlining the conversion of written materials into visual format.

## Integration with NotebookLM

The feature functions as part of NotebookLM's suite of generative tools, which includes related capabilities such as interactive study guides, [[concepts/audio-overviews|audio overviews]], and visual materials like [[concepts/infographic-design|infographics]]. AI Videos extends the platform's functionality beyond text-based outputs, allowing users to repurpose source materials in multiple formats. This integration positions [[concepts/video-generation|video generation]] as one of several ways users can transform and present their research or study materials.

## Functionality

When generating videos, the system extracts key information and concepts from uploaded source materials and structures them into a video narrative format. The resulting videos include visual elements and are designed to present information in an accessible, engaging manner without requiring users to have video production expertise or [[concepts/software|software]]. The feature is intended to save time for researchers, students, and professionals who need to create visual content from existing textual resources.
