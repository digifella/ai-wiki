# Melbourne Cbd

[[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]]'s Central Business District (CBD) is the commercial and administrative heart of Victoria's capital city. Located in the Hoddle Grid area of central Melbourne, the CBD contains significant Victorian and modern [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] alongside contemporary office and retail developments. The district has evolved considerably since its establishment during Melbourne's gold rush expansion in the 19th century.

## Historic Architecture

The CBD preserves numerous examples of 19th and early 20th-century banking and commercial architecture. Notable among these structures is [[concepts/melbourne-cbd-architecture|333 Collins Street]], which features a historic [[concepts/banking-chamber|banking chamber]] that represents an important example of Melbourne's financial [[concepts/cultural-heritage|heritage]]. The [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] of such chambers and the broader architectural fabric of the CBD reflects ongoing efforts to maintain the district's historical character while adapting to contemporary urban needs.

## Modern Development

The CBD continues to function as Melbourne's primary business precinct, hosting major corporations, financial institutions, government offices, and cultural institutions. The district has been subject to ongoing urban renewal and [[concepts/infrastructure-development|infrastructure development]], balancing the retention of heritage structures with modern commercial requirements.
