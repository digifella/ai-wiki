# Finding Purpose

Finding purpose refers to the process of identifying personal meaning and direction in life. Purpose functions as a foundational element of [[concepts/health|wellbeing]], providing [[concepts/motivation|motivation]], [[concepts/resilience|resilience]], and a sense of alignment between values and actions. The concept extends beyond career or [[concepts/success|achievement]] to encompass broader life direction and what individuals consider [[concepts/value|worth]] dedicating their effort toward. Research in [[concepts/psychology|psychology]] and health studies consistently links a [[concepts/meaning-in-life|sense of purpose]] to improved [[concepts/mental-health|mental health]] outcomes, longevity, and overall [[concepts/quality-of-life|life satisfaction]].

## The "Why" Framework

[[entities/simon-sinek|Simon Sinek]]’s approach to identifying one’s "why" utilizes a story-based method to uncover core motivations. This framework suggests that understanding the underlying [[concepts/purpose|reason]] for one's actions helps clarify personal direction. By articulating a clear [[concepts/storytelling|narrative]] for why they do what they do, individuals can better align their daily behaviors with their deeper values. This method serves as a practical tool for translating abstract concepts of meaning into actionable life goals.
