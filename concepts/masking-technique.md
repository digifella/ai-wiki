# Masking Technique

A method for isolating specific regions of an image to apply localized adjustments without affecting the rest of the composition.

## Core Concepts
- **Selective Editing**: Applying adjustments (exposure, color, clarity) to defined areas.
- **[[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|Non-Destructive Workflow]]**: Masks preserve original pixel data, allowing for reversible edits.
- **Automation vs. Manual**: Ranges from AI-driven subject/sky detection to manual brush or gradient masks.

## Applications in Lightroom
- **Sky & Reflection Isolation**: Crucial for balancing high-[[concepts/contrast|contrast]] scenes like sunrises/sunsets over water.
- **[[concepts/dreamy-edit|Dreamy Aesthetic]] Creation**: Softening reflections or enhancing mist through localized clarity/texture adjustments.
- **Subject Emphasis**: Drawing viewer [[concepts/attention-mechanism|attention]] by darkening backgrounds or brightening subjects.
