# Starlink V3

**Starlink V3** refers to the third generation of [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]]'s satellite constellation, designed to significantly increase global broadband capacity and reduce latency. This generation is characterized by the deployment of next-generation satellites via the fully reusable SpaceX Starship launch vehicle, enabling higher throughput per satellite and more efficient mass deployment compared to previous Falcon 9 missions.

## Key Developments

*   **Starship Flight 14 Integration**: The deployment of Starlink V3 satellites is tied to the milestones of SpaceX Starship, specifically targeting its first successful orbital mission.
*   **Flight 14 Details**: On 2026-09-17, significant updates were released regarding [[concepts/starship-flight-14|SpaceX Starship Flight 14]]: First Orbital Mission, Starlink V3 Deployment, which aims to deploy the initial batch of V3 satellites.
