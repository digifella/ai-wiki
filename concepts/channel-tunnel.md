# Channel Tunnel

The **Channel [[concepts/tunnel|Tunnel]]** (also known as the **Chunnel**) is a 50.5 km rail tunnel beneath the English Channel connecting Folkestone in the [[entities/uk|United Kingdom]] with Coquelles in France. It is the longest undersea section of any tunnel in the [[entities/earth|world]].

## History and Construction
- The concept dates back to the early 19th century, but political and military concerns delayed implementation for over a century.
- Construction began in 1988 and was completed in 1994, involving significant [[entities/national-academies|engineering]] challenges due to [[concepts/geology|geology]] and hydrology.
- The project faced substantial cost overruns and political controversy during its development [[concepts/phase|phase]].

## Operational Challenges
- Initial projections for passenger and freight volumes were significantly overestimated, leading to financial difficulties for the operating consortium.
- [[concepts/security-concersns|Security concerns]], particularly regarding smuggling and terrorism, have necessitated rigorous border control measures within the tunnel.
- Technical failures, including fires and signal issues, have occasionally disrupted service, highlighting the complexity of maintaining such a critical [[concepts/infrastructure|infrastructure]] link.
