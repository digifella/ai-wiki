# Capability Renewal

**Capability Renewal** refers to the organizational process of updating, replacing, or significantly enhancing existing core competencies to adapt to external shifts in technology, market dynamics, or regulatory environments. It is a subset of [[concepts/organizational-structure]] and often necessitates robust Change [[concepts/pmi-frameworks|Management frameworks]] to mitigate resistance and ensure [[concepts/adoption|adoption]].

## Core Dimensions

*   **Technological Integration**: The alignment of new digital tools with [[concepts/vintage-computing|legacy systems]] to enhance [[concepts/efficient-operation|operational efficiency]].
*   **Human Capital Adaptation**: Retraining and upskilling [[entities/employees|personnel]] to leverage new capabilities effectively.
*   **Process Restructuring**: Redefining workflows to accommodate new technological [[concepts/affordances|affordances]].

## Healthcare Context

The [[concepts/health-care|healthcare]] sector exemplifies the challenges of capability renewal due to high-stakes environments and complex stakeholder networks.

*   **[[concepts/digital-innovations|Digital Innovations]] in Hospitals**: Recent studies highlight that [[concepts/health|healthcare]] facilities are undergoing constant change, which presents significant challenges for all stakeholders [[lab-notes/2026-05-26-Hospodkova---Change-Management-and-Digital-Innovations-i|Hospodkova - Change Management and Digital Innovations in Hospitals of]].
*   **Key Findings (Hospodkova et al., 2021)**:
    *   Research across five European countries indicates that successful implementation of digital innovations requires tailored [[concepts/organizational-change|change management]] strategies.
    *   Stakeholder engagement is critical; changes are often perceived as challenging without adequate support structures.
    *   The integration of digital tools is not merely technical but deeply cultural, requiring shifts in professional norms and practices.
