# Republic Of Turkey

The Republic of Turkey is a transcontinental country situated at the crossroads of Europe and Asia. Its capital is Ankara, and the nation covers approximately 783,562 square kilometers. Geographically, Turkey straddles two continents, with roughly 3% of its territory located in Europe and the remaining 97% in Asia Minor (Anatolia).

The country shares borders with eight nations: Greece, Bulgaria, Georgia, Russia, Azerbaijan, Iran, Iraq, and Syria. This strategic location has historically positioned Turkey as a significant cultural and political bridge between Eastern and Western civilizations.
