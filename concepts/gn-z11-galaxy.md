# GN-z11 Galaxy

[[entities/gn-z11|GN-z11]] is a high-redshift galaxy that has been observed to exist approximately 400 million years after the [[concepts/big-bang|Big Bang]]. Located in the constellation Ursa Major, it is one of the most distant known galaxies and provides valuable insights into the [[concepts/early-universe|early universe]].

- Discovered through observations by Hubble Space Telescope.
- Holds significant interest for scientists studying cosmic reionization era.
- [[concepts/distant-galaxies|GN-z11]]'s brightness indicates a high star formation rate compared to other young galaxies in its epoch.
- Evidence suggests it contains over 1 billion solar masses of [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]] packed into a diameter that is one-sixth the size of [[concepts/milky-way|Milky Way]].
