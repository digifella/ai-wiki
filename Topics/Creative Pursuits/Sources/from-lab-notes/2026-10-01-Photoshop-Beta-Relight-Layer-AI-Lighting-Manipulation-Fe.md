## Photoshop Beta: Relight Layer AI Lighting Manipulation Feature Overview
**Clip title:** NEW Feature RELIGHT LAYER in Photoshop
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** [[entities/colin-smith-channel|photoshopCAFE]]
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=P0OsXyEeuvM

### Summary
This video introduces [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]]'s new AI-powered "[[concepts/relight-layer|Relight Layer]]" feature, currently available in [[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]], which aims to replace the older, removed "Lighting Effects" tool. The presenter, [[entities/colin-smith|Colin Smith]] from [[entities/colin-smith-channel|PhotoshopCAFE]], demonstrates how this non-destructive tool allows users to easily manipulate lighting in an image by generating new [[concepts/light|light]] sources and adjusting their properties. Unlike its predecessor, the Relight Layer is AI-driven, offering a more intuitive and visually responsive [[concepts/experience|experience]].

To access the feature, users navigate to the Layer menu and select "New Relight Layer." This action creates a new layer and automatically [[entities/places|places]] a movable white dot on the [[concepts/canvas|canvas]], representing the primary [[concepts/light|light]] source. Dragging this dot interactively changes the direction of the light on the image. Beyond simple positioning, a properties panel offers comprehensive controls: a "Close/Far" slider adjusts the light's depth (allowing for front-lighting or back-lighting effects), while "Left/Right" and "Low/High" sliders offer precise directional control. Users can also modify the light's color (using Kelvin temperatures or a custom color picker), intensity, and diffusion (to control the hardness or softness of the light). A crucial "Original Lighting" slider allows blending the newly generated light with the image's existing ambient light, offering subtle or dramatic [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

The "Relight Layer" feature supports adding up to three distinct light sources, each with its own customizable properties. This capability allows for complex lighting setups, such as combining a cool blue "moonlight" effect with a warm red light emanating from a corner, as demonstrated with a scene of wooden barrels. A significant application highlighted is its power in [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]]: users can seamlessly integrate a subject from one image into a new background by matching the lighting conditions. The video illustrates this by placing a woman with a camera against a luminous blue screen, then using the Relight Layer to simulate the blue light reflecting onto her, adjusting its position, color, and diffusion to create a believable blend.

While incredibly powerful and user-friendly, the "Relight Layer" does consume 30 [[concepts/generative-credits|generative credits]] when the "Done" button is clicked, as it renders the [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] into a higher [[concepts/solution|resolution]]. The presenter concludes by emphasizing the tool's effectiveness for intricate lighting adjustments and [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] tasks, offering a much easier workflow than previous methods. This AI-driven approach significantly simplifies the process of achieving realistic and customized lighting within Photoshop, opening up new creative possibilities for image manipulation.
