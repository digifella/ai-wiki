# Webinar Creation

Webinar creation is a video production method that converts presentation slides into video content using a combination of AI-assisted tools. The workflow begins with NotebookLM, which generates presentation slides from source material, then transitions to Google Vids for video production. This integrated approach reduces manual steps in converting static presentations into dynamic video format.

The process involves taking slides created by NotebookLM and importing them into Google Vids. This transition allows for the automated generation of video assets directly from the structured slide deck. The resulting output is an unwatermarked video file, distinguishing it from standard export options that may include platform branding.

By leveraging this specific toolchain, users can streamline the production of webinar materials. The method prioritizes efficiency by minimizing the need for manual slide design and video editing, relying instead on the interoperability between the notebook-based slide generator and the video creation platform.
