# Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite

[[concepts/gemini-2.5-flash-lite|Gemini 2.5 Flash Lite]] is a lightweight variant of [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini-25-models|Gemini 2.5]] [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]]. It is designed to provide faster [[concepts/inference|inference]] speeds and reduced computational requirements compared to the full [[entities/gemini-25|Gemini 2.5]] model, making it suitable for [[concepts/software|applications]] with latency or resource constraints.

## Purpose and Use Cases

The model is intended for [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where efficiency is prioritized, such as mobile applications, edge devices, or high-volume inference services. Flash Lite maintains core language understanding [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] while optimizing for [[concepts/speed|speed]] and cost-effectiveness in production environments.

## Technical Context

As part of [[entities/google|Google]]'s [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] family of [[concepts/models|models]], Flash Lite represents the company's approach to [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] language models across different computational tiers. It continues Google's strategy of offering multiple model sizes to serve diverse user needs and infrastructure requirements.
