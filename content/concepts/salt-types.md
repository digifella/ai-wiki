# Salt types

## Overview
Salt varieties differ by [[concepts/film-grain|grain]] size, density, and mineral composition, all of which dictate how they interact with food during cooking and seasoning.

## Application Principles
* Effective cooking requires a comprehensive understanding of how to use various salt types.
* Mastery of [[concepts/salt-density|salt density]] and specific application techniques is essential to avoid common seasoning errors.
* Reference: [[entities/americas-test-kitchen|America's Test Kitchen]] via 2026 04 26 [[concepts/seasoning-timing|Mastering Salt]] for Home Cooks Types Densities and Applic

## Common Varieties
* **Kosher Salt**: Large, irregular grains; preferred for controlled seasoning and brining.
* **Table Salt**: Fine, uniform crystals; high density; requires precise measurement to prevent over-salting.
* **Sea Salt**: Produced through evaporation; contains trace minerals; [[concepts/texture|texture]] varies by source.
* **Flaky Salt**: Large, thin crystals; used primarily as a finishing salt for [[concepts/texture|texture]] and immediate flavor impact.
