# Winter Rainfall
Winter rainfall is a crucial aspect of many climate zones around the world, playing a significant role in agricultural productivity and overall ecosystem [[concepts/health|health]]. In regions such as California's Central Valley or parts of Mediterranean climates, winter rain provides essential moisture for dormant plants to prepare for spring growth.

### Summary
[[concepts/iran|Iran]] [[concepts/faces|faces]] a severe and long-standing water crisis, stemming from its inherently [[concepts/arid-geography|arid geography]] and low average annual precipitation. Only 25% of the nation's limited rainfall occurs in winter, exacerbating issues for agricultural and urban water needs.

- Historically, Iran relied on ancient [[concepts/qanat|qanat]] systems to manage scarce resources efficiently.
- [[concepts/qanats|Qanats]] are underground tunnels that tap into aquifers and channel water across large distances without losing much through evaporation.
- The 20th century saw a decline in traditional [[concepts/qanat-management|qanat management]] as modern techniques were introduced but often mismanaged, leading to over-extraction of groundwater.
