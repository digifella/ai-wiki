# Photoshop Beta: Enhanced Before/After View, AI Dynamic Layers, and Relight Layer

**Source:** [[lab-notes/2026-09-09-Photoshop-Beta-Enhanced-BeforeAfter-View-and-AI-Dynamic|Photoshop Beta: Enhanced Before/After View and AI Dynamic Layers]]

## Overview
[[concepts/software-updates|Updates]] to the [[concepts/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] Beta application focusing on [[concepts/efficiency-principles|workflow efficiency]], [[concepts/ai-driven-layer-management|AI-driven layer management]], advanced selection [[concepts/accuracy|precision]], and non-destructive [[concepts/lighting-manipulation|lighting manipulation]].

## Key Features
- **Enhanced [[concepts/beforeafter-view|Before/After View]]:** Improved comparison tool for evaluating edits against the original state.
- **[[concepts/ai-dynamic-layers|AI Dynamic Layers]]:** Introduction of dynamic layer capabilities driven by [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]].
- **[[concepts/enhance-edge|Enhance Edge]] AI:** New capability in [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] Beta 27.11.0 designed to improve selections for complex elements.
  - **[[concepts/hair-masking|Hair Masking]]:** Specialized precision for fine details like hair strands.
  - **[[concepts/edge-cleanup|Halo Reduction]]:** Algorithmic reduction of edge artifacts during complex selections.
- **[[concepts/relight-layer|Relight Layer]] AI Lighting:** A new non-destructive tool allowing users to manipulate lighting by generating new [[concepts/light|light]] sources on specific layers, replacing the older "Lighting Effects" tool.

## Relight Layer Details
- **Functionality:** Enables AI-powered lighting manipulation on individual layers without altering the original [[concepts/digital-images|pixel data]].
- **Workflow:** Users can add, remove, or adjust light sources to change the mood and depth of an image post-capture.
- **Context:** Demonstrated as a significant upgrade for [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] and retouching workflows in the Beta channel.
