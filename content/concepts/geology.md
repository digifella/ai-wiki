# Geology

Geology is a branch of Earth sciences that studies the solid features of Earth and other terrestrial planets and moons. It encompasses processes such as [[concepts/tectonic-plate-shifts|plate tectonics]], rock formation, volcanic activity, sedimentation, and erosion, which shape planetary surfaces over time.

## Mars Geological Findings
- [[concepts/ancient-rainforests|Ancient rainforests]] on Mars: Evidence suggesting past [[concepts/habitability|habitability]] through fossilized remains or isotopic signatures.
- [[concepts/signs-of-life|Signs of life]]: Potential biosignatures indicating microbial activity in the Martian subsurface.
- Lightning phenomena: Recent observations indicate electrical discharges similar to those occurring on Earth, impacting atmospheric composition.
