# Nano

The [[concepts/nemotron-3-nano-model|Nemotron-3 Nano]] is a 30-billion-parameter [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] AI model released by [[entities/nvidia]]. It employs a [[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] with 3 billion active [[concepts/parameters|parameters]].

## Nemotron-3 Family Overview
- **Nano**: 30B [[concepts/total-parameters|total parameters]] (3B active)
- **Super**: 100B total [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] (10B active)
- **Ultra**: 500B total parameters (50B active)
