# Tk1849 Airline

Tk1849 is a scheduled flight service operating between [[concepts/melbourne-region|Melbourne]] and Bangkok with a stopover in Istanbul. The route connects Australia's second-largest city to Thailand's capital, with the Istanbul leg serving as an overnight rest point rather than a final destination.

## Route and Schedule

The flight departs Melbourne at 13:20 and arrives in Istanbul at 05:15 the following morning. Passengers remain in Istanbul overnight before continuing to Bangkok. The service is operated by [[entities/jq29-airline|JQ29 Airline]] under flight designation Tk1849.

## Booking Details

As of September 2025, the flight was booked on 4 September with an overnight hotel stay at Old Alessia City in Istanbul on 5 September. The booking reference is U4PKH2.
