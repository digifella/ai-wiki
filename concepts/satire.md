# Satire

**Satire** is a genre of literature, and sometimes graphic arts and dramatic performances, in which vices, follies, abuses, and shortcomings are held up to ridicule, ideally with the intent of shaming individuals, corporations, government, or society itself into improvement. It employs wit, irony, sarcasm, and exaggeration to expose and criticize human stupidity or vices.

## Core Mechanisms
- **Irony**: Discrepancy between expectation and reality.
- **Sarcasm**: Mocking or insulting [[concepts/tone|tone]].
- **Parody**: Imitation of a [[concepts/style|style]] for comic effect or ridicule.
- **Hyperbole**: Exaggeration for emphasis.
- **Inversion**: Reversing roles or values to highlight absurdity.

## Functions
- **Social Critique**: Targets political corruption, social norms, and cultural trends.
- **Catharsis**: Provides relief through laughter at shared frustrations.
- **[[concepts/conscious-thought|Awareness]]**: Highlights contradictions in [[concepts/open-source-philosophy|logic]] or morality.
