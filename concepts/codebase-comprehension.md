# Codebase comprehension

Understanding the structure, functionality, and interdependencies of a software [[concepts/code|codebase]]. Critical for maintenance, [[concepts/debugging|debugging]], and [[concepts/feature-development|feature development]].

**Key aspects**:
- Architecture and design [[concepts/thematic-analysis|pattern recognition]]
- File/module navigation and dependency mapping
- Data [[concepts/flow|flow]] and control path tracing
- Bottleneck identification and refactoring planning
