# AI Expert Role

A strategic position enabling organizations to bridge the **[[concepts/ai-proficiency|AI Skills]] Gap** by translating technical [[concepts/capabilities|AI capabilities]] into business value. Focuses on [[concepts/adoption|adoption]], implementation, and change management—not just technical proficiency.

## Core Responsibilities
- Identify business problems solvable via AI (e.g., process [[concepts/automation|automation]], data-driven decisions)
- Translate technical AI outputs into actionable strategies for non-technical teams
- Train and support cross-functional teams in AI tool usage (beyond [[concepts/coding|coding]]/data science roles)
- [[concepts/motivation|Drive]] **AI Adoption Strategy** through collaboration with **Business Transformation** leaders

## Key Mindset Shift
> *"Transition from resisting AI to embracing it as a growth catalyst."*
> (Per 2026 04 14 [[entities/enovair|Enovair]] AI views for 2026)

## Strategic Value
- Addresses critical **AI Skills Gap** in companies investing in AI but lacking internal expertise
- Positions role as essential for **Organizational Agility** in AI-driven markets
- Enables scalable AI [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] without requiring full technical [[concepts/specialization|specialization]]
