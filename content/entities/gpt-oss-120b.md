# Gpt Oss 120b

Gpt Oss 120b is an open-weight [[concepts/statistical-language-modeling|language model]] developed by [[entities/openai|OpenAI]], featuring 120 billion [[concepts/parameters|parameters]]. As an [[concepts/open-weight-model|open-weight model]], its [[concepts/weights|weights]] are publicly available, allowing researchers and developers to download and run it locally on their own infrastructure rather than relying solely on API access or [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud services]].

## Capabilities and Use Cases

The model is designed to perform general-[[concepts/motivation|purpose]] natural language tasks including [[concepts/text-generation|text generation]], [[concepts/fact-based-queries|question answering]], and [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. With 120 billion parameters, it occupies a middle tier in terms of model scale, balancing capability with computational requirements. The [[concepts/local-deployment|local deployment]] option enables use cases where data [[concepts/privacy|privacy]], latency, or cost considerations make [[concepts/cloud-based-solutions|cloud-based solutions]] impractical.

## Technical Requirements

[[concepts/running|Running]] Gpt Oss 120b locally requires substantial [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]]. The model's size means users typically need high-end GPUs or specialized [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] to achieve reasonable [[concepts/inference|inference]] speeds, though [[concepts/parameter-reduction|quantization]] techniques can reduce [[concepts/memory|memory]] requirements at the cost of some performance.
