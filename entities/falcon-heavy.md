# Falcon Heavy

## Overview
A heavy-lift launch vehicle developed by [[entities/spacex]]. Known for its ability to reuse boosters and its high payload capacity to Geostationary transfer orbit and Heliocentric orbit.

## Key Missions & Payloads
- **STP-2 (2019):** First operational mission; deployed six [[entities/nasa]] payloads.
- **Arabsat-6A (2019):** First commercial mission.
- **GOES-U (2024):** Deployed the latest generation of Geostationary Operational Environmental Satellites.
- **Nancy Grace [[concepts/falcon-heavy|Roman Space Telescope]] (2027):** Scheduled launch vehicle for the [[concepts/nancy-grace-roman-space-telescope]].

## Technical Specifications
- **Configuration:** Three Merlin engine cores (Core + two Side booster).
- **Reusability:** All three boosters can land simultaneously (two on Landing pad, one on a Drone ship).
- **Payload to LEO:** ~63,800 kg (reusable) / ~64,000 kg (non-reusable).
- **Payload to GTO:** ~26,700 kg.
