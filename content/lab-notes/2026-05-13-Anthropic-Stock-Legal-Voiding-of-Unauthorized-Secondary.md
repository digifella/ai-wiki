The core issue highlighted by Anthropic's legal notice is that any sale or transfer of its stock not explicitly approved by its Board of Directors is void and [[entities/will|will]] not be recognized. While acknowledging the rationale for safeguarding against fraud, the speaker argues that current regulations foster a "rich get richer" dynamic.
