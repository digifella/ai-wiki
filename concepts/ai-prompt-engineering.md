# AI Prompt Engineering

AI [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]] refers to the practice of designing and refining text inputs—prompts—to guide AI language models toward producing desired outputs. Rather than issuing generic requests, prompt engineers craft carefully structured [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] that specify desired [[concepts/tone|tone]], format, context, and constraints. This practice has emerged as essential in professional and legal settings where output quality directly impacts [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] and [[concepts/compliance|compliance]].

## Core Techniques

Effective [[concepts/prompt-engineering|prompt engineering]] involves several established approaches. Specificity—providing detailed context and clear parameters—significantly improves output relevance. Structural techniques such as [[concepts/few-shot-examples|few-shot prompting]] (providing examples) and role-based framing (e.g., "act as a legal [[entities/zed-francis|analyst]]") help models understand task requirements. [[concepts/iterative-learning|Iterative refinement]] through testing and adjustment allows practitioners to optimize responses for their particular [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].

## Professional Applications

In professional environments, [[concepts/prompting|prompt engineering]] addresses practical constraints of [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]]. [[concepts/legal-professionals|Legal professionals]] use engineered prompts to standardize [[concepts/legal-document-review|contract review]] processes or ensure compliance-focused analysis. Business analysts refine prompts to generate reports in specific formats or with particular analytical frameworks. The technique bridges the gap between general-purpose AI capabilities and domain-specific requirements without requiring [[concepts/model-retraining|model retraining]].

## Limitations and Considerations

Prompt engineering's effectiveness depends on the underlying model's capabilities and knowledge. Well-engineered prompts cannot extract information a model does not possess or override fundamental limitations in [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. Additionally, over-reliance on specific prompt structures can create fragile systems vulnerable to minor variations in input. Organizations implementing prompt engineering should treat it as one tool among several for [[concepts/ai-integration|AI integration]], not as a substitute for proper model selection and human oversight.
