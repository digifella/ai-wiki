# Artisanal Intelligence

**Artisanal Intelligence** refers to the traditional, human-centric model of scientific and intellectual discovery. It is characterized by:
- Slow, iterative processes driven by individual [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] and intuition.
- High [[concepts/cognitive-load|cognitive load]] per unit of output.
- Reliance on manual [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and "crafting" of solutions.

## Evolution of Intelligence Paradigms

The concept is currently undergoing a significant shift due to rapid advancements in [[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]], particularly regarding [[concepts/mathematical-reasoning|mathematical reasoning]] and automated discovery.

### Key Developments (2026-10-09)
Recent analysis suggests a transition from artisanal methods to scalable, [[concepts/automations|automated systems]]:
- **[[concepts/mathematical-breakthroughs|Mathematical Breakthroughs]]:** [[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]]'s latest models have demonstrated capabilities that redefine the boundaries of automated mathematical discovery [[lab-notes/2026-10-09-OpenAI-AI-Redefines-Scientific-Discovery-Mathematical-Br|OpenAI AI Redefines Scientific Discovery: Mathematical Breakthroughs and Factory Intelligence]].
- **Factory Intelligence:** The [[concepts/emergent-behavior|emergence]] of "Factory Intelligence" implies a move toward high-throughput, standardized generation of scientific insights, contrasting sharply with the bespoke nature of artisanal work.
- **Redefining Discovery:** The [[concepts/speed|speed]] and scale of AI-driven discovery are challenging the traditional timeline and methodology of [[concepts/scientific-advancement|scientific progress]].
