# Map Based Itinerary Creation

Map based itinerary creation is a [[concepts/travel-planning|travel planning]] method that combines [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] with [[entities/google-my-maps|Google My Maps]] to automate the organization of travel destinations and routes. Rather than manually planning each stop on a journey, this approach uses AI to suggest locations, determine optimal ordering, and integrate them into a visual map interface. The method leverages the mapping platform's ability to display multiple points of interest while AI algorithms handle the computational aspects of itinerary optimization.

## Process and Integration

The [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] typically involves providing AI with travel [[concepts/parameters|parameters]] such as destination, duration, interests, and preferences. The AI then generates a list of recommended attractions, restaurants, accommodations, or activities relevant to the trip. These suggestions are subsequently added to a Google My Maps project, which organizes them spatially and can calculate routes between locations. This combination allows travelers to visualize their itinerary geographically while benefiting from AI-driven recommendations tailored to their specific needs.

## Practical Applications

This approach is particularly useful for travelers seeking to optimize their time across multiple destinations without manually researching and plotting each location. The visual component of mapping helps identify logical routes and clustering of nearby points of interest, reducing travel time between stops. The method bridges the gap between algorithmic travel recommendations and practical spatial planning, making complex multi-destination itineraries easier to conceptualize and execute.
