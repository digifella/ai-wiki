# AI Masking

**[[concepts/ai-based-selection|AI Masking]]** refers to the use of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] to automatically generate precise selection masks for [[concepts/image-editing|image editing]], object [[concepts/disconnection|isolation]], and color correction. It reduces manual labor by leveraging [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|machine learning models]] to identify edges, textures, and semantic content.

## Core Concepts

- **Semantic Segmentation**: AI identifies specific objects or regions (e.g., skin, sky, hair) to create masks without manual tracing.
- **[[concepts/remove-background|Edge Refinement]]**: Advanced models handle complex boundaries like hair strands or translucent materials better than traditional threshold-based masks.
- **Integration with Color Tools**: [[concepts/ai-masks|AI masks]] are frequently paired with [[concepts/color-analysis|color analysis]] tools (e.g., Vectorscope) to apply targeted corrections.
- **3D Spatial Context**: Emerging tools like [[lab-notes/2026-09-12-Gods-Eye-View-GEV-3D-Spatial-Intelligence-Simulator-Walk|God's Eye View (GEV) 3D Spatial Intelligence Simulator Walkthrough]] leverage [[concepts/3d-spatial-intelligence|3D spatial intelligence]] to enhance [[concepts/depth-estimation|depth perception]] and object isolation, offering a "[[concepts/vision-model|God's Eye View]]" for more accurate spatial masking.

## Applications in Photo Editing

- **Selective Color Correction**: Applying corrections to specific masked regions (e.g., sky, skin) without affecting the rest of the image.
- **Object Isolation**: Removing or replacing backgrounds with high [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].
- **[[concepts/automated-content-creation|Automated Workflows]]**: Integrating AI masks into [[concepts/batch-processing|batch processing]] pipelines for consistent results.
