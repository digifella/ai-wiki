# Ai Selection

Ai Selection in Adobe Photoshop utilizes artificial intelligence to automate the isolation of subjects from their backgrounds. By analyzing pixel data and edge information, these tools generate precise masks that traditionally required extensive manual refinement. This technology is particularly effective for complex edges, such as hair, fur, or translucent materials, where standard selection methods often fail to capture fine details accurately. The process typically begins with the user making a rough selection or allowing the software to auto-detect the subject.

The AI engine then processes the image to distinguish between the foreground and background, focusing on subtle transitions in color and luminance. For hair masking, this involves identifying individual strands and semi-transparent areas that blend with the background. Users can refine these initial results using the Select and Mask workspace, which offers specialized tools like the Refine Edge Brush to enhance the accuracy of fine details without losing the natural texture of the hair.

While AI-driven selection significantly reduces the time required for complex tasks, it is not infallible. In cases where the background shares similar colors or textures with the subject, manual adjustments may still be necessary. Combining AI-generated masks with manual layer masks allows for greater control, ensuring that the final composite maintains realistic edges and avoids the haloing or jagged artifacts common in less sophisticated selection methods.
