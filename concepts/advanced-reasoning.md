# Advanced Reasoning

Advanced reasoning involves complex cognitive processes such as multi-step deduction, causal [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]], and physical simulation. It is often tested through novel, non-standard benchmarks that require models to integrate domain-specific knowledge with logical planning.
