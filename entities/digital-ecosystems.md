# Digital Ecosystems

Digital Ecosystems is a simulation platform developed by Sakana AI that models the interactions and coexistence of multiple artificial intelligence agents within a controlled computational environment. The system employs a 2D grid-based structure to study how AI agents with varying capabilities and objectives compete for resources, cooperate, and adapt over time. This framework allows researchers to observe emergent behaviors and dynamics that arise from the interactions of diverse AI entities operating under shared constraints.

## Purpose and Applications

The simulation serves as a research tool for understanding how artificial intelligence systems can coexist and interact in shared spaces. By creating virtual environments where AI agents must navigate resource scarcity, achieve objectives, and respond to other agents' behaviors, Digital Ecosystems enables the study of competition, cooperation, and evolutionary dynamics at the computational level. This approach provides insights relevant to multi-agent systems, AI alignment, and the design of robust AI architectures that must function alongside other intelligent systems.
