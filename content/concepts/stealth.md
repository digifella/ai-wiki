# Stealth

**Stealth** is the practice of operating or moving without detection, minimizing signature across sensory domains (visual, acoustic, thermal, electromagnetic, informational). Core objective: denial of awareness to adversaries while maintaining operational capability.

## Mechanisms
- **Crypsis & Camouflage:** Disruption of outline, background matching, counter-shading, masquerade. Camouflage
- **Signature Reduction:** Suppression of noise, heat, radar cross-section, [[concepts/light|light]] emission. Radar Absorption
- **Misdirection & Deception:** Feints, decoys, false [[concepts/flags|flags]], social engineering. Deception
- **Information [[concepts/security|Security]]:** OPSEC, signal discipline, compartmentalization. OPSEC

## Applications
- **Military:** Infiltration, reconnaissance, sabotage, asymmetric warfare. Espionage Guerrilla Warfare
- **Biological:** Predation strategies, anti-predator [[concepts/adaptations|adaptations]], cryptic coloration. [[concepts/evolutionary-biology]]
- **Digital:** Low-observable network traffic, anonymous routing, adversarial ML. [[concepts/cybersecurity]]
- **Social:** Blending into crowds, behavioral camouflage, covert [[concepts/power|influence]].

## Historical Context & Case Studies
- **Japanese [[concepts/shinobi|Shinobi]] vs. Popular Mythology:** Historical shinobi were specialized intelligence operatives focused on pragmatic espionage, infiltration, and sabotage, contrasting sharply with supernatural "ninja" tropes.
