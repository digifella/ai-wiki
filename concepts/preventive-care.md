# Preventive Care

[[concepts/preventive-healthcare|Preventive care]] encompasses healthcare practices and interventions designed to prevent illness and disease before they occur, rather than treating conditions after they develop. It represents a proactive approach to health management that contrasts with reactive, treatment-focused medicine. By addressing risk factors and identifying health problems in their earliest stages, preventive care aims to reduce disease burden, improve [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality of life]], and lower overall healthcare costs.

## Levels of Prevention

Preventive care is typically organized into three levels. [[concepts/primary-prevention|Primary prevention]] aims to prevent disease from occurring in the first place through measures such as vaccinations, [[concepts/health-promotion|health education]], and lifestyle modifications. [[concepts/secondary-prevention|Secondary prevention]] involves screening and early detection of disease in asymptomatic individuals, allowing for intervention before symptoms appear. [[concepts/tertiary-prevention|Tertiary prevention]] focuses on managing existing conditions to prevent complications and slow disease progression.

## Common Preventive Practices

Preventive care includes routine health screenings such as blood pressure monitoring, cholesterol testing, and cancer screenings; vaccinations against infectious diseases; [[concepts/offering-counseling-services|counseling]] on [[concepts/nutrition|nutrition]], [[concepts/exercise|exercise]], and smoking cessation; and regular check-ups with healthcare providers. The specific preventive services recommended vary based on age, sex, family history, and individual risk factors.

## Impact and Outcomes

Effective preventive care can reduce the incidence and severity of [[concepts/chronic-diseases|chronic diseases]], improve [[concepts/population-health|population health]] outcomes, and decrease healthcare expenditures by avoiding costly emergency interventions and hospitalizations. However, the [[concepts/success|success]] of preventive care depends on adequate [[concepts/healthcare-access|access to healthcare]] services, patient engagement, and [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] among healthcare providers.
