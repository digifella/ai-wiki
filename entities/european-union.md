# European Union

The European Union is a political and economic union comprising 27 member states, primarily located in Europe. Established through the Maastricht Treaty in 1992, the EU evolved from the European Economic Community, which was founded in 1957. The union operates as both a single market and a political entity, with the primary objective of eliminating internal trade barriers and facilitating the free movement of goods, services, capital, and people among its member states.

## Institutions and Governance

The EU is governed through a complex institutional framework consisting of several key bodies. The European Parliament represents EU citizens and is directly elected, while the Council of the European Union represents member states' governments. The European Commission acts as the executive branch, proposing legislation and implementing decisions, and the Court of Justice of the European Union ensures that EU law is interpreted and applied consistently across all member states.

## Economic and Monetary Union

A significant aspect of the EU is the Economic and Monetary Union, which includes the Eurozone. This group of member states has adopted the euro as their common currency, managed by the European Central Bank. The union maintains a customs union, meaning that goods can move freely between member states without tariffs, and it negotiates trade agreements on behalf of its members as a single bloc in international forums.

## Visa and Travel Policies

For non-EU nationals, travel within the Schengen Area is governed by specific visa regulations. The Schengen Agreement allows for passport-free travel between participating countries, which includes most EU member states. Turkey is not currently a full member of the EU, and Turkish citizens require a visa to enter the Schengen Area for short stays. The European Union has implemented an Electronic Travel Authorization (ETIAS) system for visa-exempt travelers, which will eventually apply to Turkish citizens once the system is fully operational, requiring pre-travel registration for entry into Schengen countries.
