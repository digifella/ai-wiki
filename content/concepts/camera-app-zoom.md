# Camera App Zoom

[[entities/adobe-lightroom|Adobe Lightroom]]'s beta camera application, available for [[entities/iphone-13-pro|iPhone 13 Pro]] and later, introduces computational photography features integrated directly into the camera interface. Rather than requiring post-processing [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] in editing [[concepts/software|software]], the app processes [[concepts/images|images]] [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] the moment of capture, taking advantage of the processing [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] built into recent iPhone [[concepts/models|models]].

## Core Capabilities

The application provides zoom functionality that combines optical and computational methods suited to the [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] capabilities of iPhone 13 Pro devices. Beyond zoom, the [[concepts/camera-app|camera app]] includes night shot functionality for improved low-[[concepts/light|light]] photography and [[concepts/reflection-removal|reflection removal]] tools that can eliminate unwanted reflections from glass or other reflective surfaces during shooting.

These features represent a shift toward real-time computational photography, where [[concepts/photo-enhancement|image enhancement]] occurs during capture rather than exclusively through post-processing workflows. The [[concepts/integration|integration]] into Lightroom's camera interface means users can apply these adjustments before saving their photos to the device.
