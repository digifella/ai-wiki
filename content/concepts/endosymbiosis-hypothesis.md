# Endosymbiosis hypothesis

The Endosymbiosis hypothesis proposes that complex Eukaryote organelles originated from free-living Bacteria that were internalized by a host cell.

## Recent Breakthroughs
- Recent research into the [[concepts/origins-of-complex-life|origins of complex life]] on Earth focuses on the mechanisms within the Endosymbiosis hypothesis.
- Experimental recreations of the "first [[entities/contact|contact]]" event provide insights into the evolutionary transition that formed complex life.
