# Docx

[[concepts/docling|Docling]] is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] toolkit developed by [[entities/ibm-research|IBM Research]] designed to streamline [[concepts/document-processing|document processing]] for [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] workflows. It addresses challenges in extracting [[concepts/json-structuring|structured data]] from documents in formats such as PDFs, Word files, and [[concepts/images|images]], converting them into formats suitable for use with [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] and other AI systems.

## Core Functionality

The toolkit automates the conversion of documents into machine-readable formats, preserving document [[concepts/structure|structure]] and content [[concepts/relationships|relationships]]. This includes handling text extraction, layout analysis, and [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] [[concepts/preservation|preservation]] across various document types. By standardizing how documents are ingested into AI pipelines, Docling reduces manual [[concepts/data-preprocessing|preprocessing]] work and improves [[concepts/data-conceptsintegrityintegrity|data quality]] for downstream [[concepts/ai-powered-applications|AI applications]].

## Development and Availability

As an open-source project, Docling is publicly available for developers and organizations to use, modify, and integrate into their own systems. The toolkit is maintained by IBM Research and reflects their work in document understanding and processing technology. Its open [[entities/nature|nature]] allows the community to contribute improvements and adapt the tool for specific [[concepts/scenarios|use cases]].
