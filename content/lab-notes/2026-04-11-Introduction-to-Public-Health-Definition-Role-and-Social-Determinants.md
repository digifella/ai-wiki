## Introduction to Public Health: Definition, Role, and Social Determinants
**Clip title:** What is Public Health??
**Author / channel:** Let's Learn Public Health
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=t_eWESXTnic

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive introduction to the field of [[concepts/public-health|Public Health]], highlighting its significant role in improving population health and saving lives. It begins by illustrating public health's impact, noting that between 1900 and 1999, [[concepts/life-expectancy|life expectancy]] in the [[entities/united-states|United States]] increased by over 30 years, with 25 of those years attributed to public health interventions. The video then defines "health" according to the World Health [[concepts/organization|Organization]] as a complete state of physical, mental, and social [[concepts/well-being|well-being]], extending beyond the mere absence of disease. Public health is subsequently defined as "the science and [[concepts/art|art]] of preventing disease, prolonging life, and promoting health through the organized efforts of society."

A key distinction is drawn between [clinical medicine](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Clinical_Medicine) and public health using the example of a car accident resulting in a broken leg. While a clinician focuses on treating the individual's immediate injury, a public health approach investigates the underlying factors that led to the accident to prevent future occurrences for the entire population. These "social [[concepts/determinants-of-health|determinants of health]]" include individual behaviors ([[concepts/computer-vision|vision]], driving skills, substance use), and broader societal and economic environments (laws, enforcement, community attitudes, social support, road safety, vehicle maintenance, access to education, job security, and healthcare services). The video emphasizes that health is a complex interaction of these numerous factors, and public health aims to address these upstream causes to benefit society as a whole.

The responsibility for public health is presented as a coordinated effort involving all sectors of society, not just health departments. This includes various levels of government, the private sector, non-governmental organizations, international bodies, and the community, all united by the shared goal of improving public health. The video concludes by outlining how public health operates through three core service areas: Protection (controlling infectious diseases, managing environmental hazards, ensuring healthy workplaces, and handling health emergencies), Promotion (encouraging [[concepts/healthy-behaviors|healthy behaviors]] and improving social determinants), and [[concepts/preventive-care|Prevention]] (vaccination and screening). These core areas are supported by crucial "enablers" such as good [[concepts/governance|governance]], advocacy for supportive [[concepts/policies|policies]], building capacity (a well-trained workforce), and reliable information (research, surveillance, monitoring, and evaluation).
