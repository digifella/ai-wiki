# Deepseek V3

[[entities/deepseek-v3|Deepseek V3]] is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] comprising 675 billion parameters, released under the [[concepts/apache-2-license|Apache 2.0]] [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/license|license]]. The model was developed by [[concepts/deepseek-ai|Deepseek]] and represents a significant [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] effort within the [[entities/deepseek-ai|Deepseek]] model series. Its size and permissive [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] make it accessible for both research and commercial applications without restrictive usage constraints.

## Architecture and Scale

With 675 billion parameters, Deepseek V3 operates at a scale comparable to other frontier-class language models. The architecture employs transformer-based [[concepts/design|design principles]] standard to contemporary [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]]. The model's scale allows it to handle complex language understanding and generation tasks across diverse domains and languages.

## Licensing and Availability

The [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache 2.0 license]] under which Deepseek V3 is released permits free use, modification, and distribution, including for commercial purposes, provided that license terms are preserved and any modifications are documented. This open-source positioning distinguishes it from closed-model alternatives and enables organizations to [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] the model within their own infrastructure without licensing fees.
