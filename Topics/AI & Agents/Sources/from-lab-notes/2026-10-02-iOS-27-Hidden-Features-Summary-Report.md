### Summary
The video "[[concepts/ios-27|iOS 27]] Hidden or Hard To Find Features" by ZolloTech delves into a variety of subtle yet impactful functionalities introduced in the latest iOS update, designed to enhance [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]] and control. The primary focus is on uncovering features that might not be immediately apparent, spanning across [[concepts/customization|customization]] options, [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] settings, [[concepts/media-management|media management]], and system utilities. The presenter guides viewers through various settings and applications, highlighting how these new additions can streamline daily [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] usage.

Several core system and [[concepts/privacy|privacy]] features have received notable [[concepts/software-updates|updates]]. Users can now set their time zone independently of the device's actual time. Within the [[concepts/software-update|Software Update]] section, the exact [[concepts/installation|installation]] date and build number of previous iOS versions are now displayed. For location privacy, the "Find My" app allows users to temporarily hide their location from specific contacts for custom durations, ranging from 15 minutes to up to 30 days. The Photos app introduces a [[concepts/star-ratings|rating system]] for individual pictures, a new "Captured by Me" folder to easily view user-created content, and the convenient ability to save any frame from a video as a standalone photo. Additionally, an option for immediate [[entities/icloud|iCloud]] photo syncing is available for situations requiring urgent uploads.

Further enhancements are found in communication, browsing, and camera functionalities. In Messages, the microphone icon in the text input field can be customized to either initiate dictation, record [[concepts/audio-modality|audio]], or be removed entirely based on user preference. Safari gains a powerful "Notify Me" feature, which alerts users when content on a specific website changes, with customizable check frequencies (hourly, daily, weekly, or monthly) – perfect for tracking availability or price changes. FaceTime, on newer [[entities/iphone|iPhone]] models (iPhone 17 Pro and 18 Pro Max as per the video's hypothetical naming), now supports a dual-camera mode that records simultaneously from both the front and rear cameras during a call. The [[concepts/camera-app|Camera app]] offers expanded customization of photo and video controls, accessible by long-pressing the icons at the top of the viewfinder, enabling users to tailor their shooting [[concepts/experience|experience]].

Beyond these, system-wide controls, [[concepts/gaming|gaming]], and device recovery have been refined. The Control Center now permits resizing of the Focus mode tile and integrates a timer widget directly into the Dynamic Island on compatible devices. Game enthusiasts can enjoy greater control over external game controllers, with the ability to customize the Home button's function to open the App Library, Games folder, or a specific app, alongside options to override in-game button behaviors. A new safety feature, "Impersonation Risk Detection," appears under Privacy & [[concepts/security|Security]], allowing [[concepts/apps|apps]] to receive a risk level from the iPhone, though its widespread implementation is still developing. Lastly, an enhanced recovery mode, triggered by continuously holding the power button during boot, offers multiple options, from an intelligent recovery assistant to [[concepts/app-updates|software updates]] and full data erasure, providing more granular troubleshooting capabilities. Wallpapers can also leverage [[entities/apple|Apple]] Intelligence to seamlessly extend and fill the screen more naturally, enhancing visual appeal.

In conclusion, iOS 27 brings a wealth of "hidden" features that collectively offer users more robust control, improved privacy, enhanced media management, and intelligent assistance across their iPhone experience. These updates, ranging from detailed [[concepts/time-zone-settings|time zone settings]] and personalized photo ratings to smart Safari notifications and customizable camera controls, emphasize [[entities/apple|Apple]]'s commitment to user-centric design and efficiency. While some [[concepts/advanced-features|advanced features]] are contingent on newer hardware, the overall takeaway is a more refined and adaptable operating system that empowers users with deeper [[concepts/personalization|personalization]] and more accessible troubleshooting tools.
