# Meituan Technology

Meituan Technology is a Chinese technology company that has expanded its operations into the field of artificial intelligence model development. The organization has prioritized research into alternative methodologies for training large-scale AI models, specifically focusing on hardware architectures and training techniques that reduce dependency on industry-standard Nvidia processors.

## LongCat 2.0

A key initiative under this strategic direction is the development of LongCat 2.0, a large language model comprising 1.6 trillion parameters. The training of this model was conducted without the use of Nvidia hardware, serving as a practical exploration of alternative computational approaches for scaling AI systems. This effort highlights the company's focus on diversifying the infrastructure required for advanced artificial intelligence research.
