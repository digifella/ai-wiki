# PRD

A **[[concepts/product-requirements-document|Product Requirements Document]] (PRD)** formally defines a product's requirements, features, and functionality for development teams and stakeholders. It serves as the primary reference for aligning [[entities/national-academies|engineering]], design, and business goals.

* **Core components**: Product overview, target audience, functional/non-functional requirements, user stories, technical constraints, timeline.
* **Benchmark example**: [[concepts/feynmans-three-step-scientific-method|Compare]] of [[entities/claude-opus|Claude Opus]] 4.5 vs [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] 5.2 [[entities/matt-maher|Matt Maher]] tested [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] ([[entities/claude|Claude]] [[entities/opus-45|Opus 4.5]] vs [[concepts/gpt-5|GPT-5]].2) using a complex "[[concepts/one-shot-build|One-Shot Build]]" PRD for a movie/TV [[concepts/companion|companion]] app ("[[entities/showbiz|Showbiz]]"). The PRD included [[concepts/technical-specs|technical specs]], design [[concepts/tokens|tokens]], and personality guidelines as input, evaluating model capabilities through a real-[[entities/earth|world]] documentation challenge.
