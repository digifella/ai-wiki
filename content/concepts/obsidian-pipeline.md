# Obsidian Pipeline

An [[concepts/automated-pipeline|Obsidian Pipeline]] is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] or processing system designed to automate and organize the handling of [[concepts/notes|notes]] within [[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]], a popular [[concepts/knowledge-management|knowledge management]] application. Pipelines enable users to transform, filter, route, and [[concepts/structure|structure]] note content according to predefined rules or sequences, reducing manual data entry and improving [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]] across a vault.

## Implementation and Scope

Obsidian Pipelines can operate [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] various levels of complexity, from simple template-based workflows to more sophisticated [[concepts/automation|automation]] using [[concepts/plugins|plugins]], scripts, or [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]. Common [[concepts/software|applications]] include automating [[concepts/metadata|metadata]] tagging, routing notes to specific folders based on content criteria, transforming raw input into standardized formats, and triggering actions based on note properties or [[concepts/relationships|relationships]].

## Relationship to Note Processing

The pipeline concept reflects broader note management practices within Obsidian, where users often need to process incoming information—such as clipped web articles, research materials, or meeting notes—into a consistent structure for later retrieval and synthesis. A well-designed pipeline can reduce [[concepts/friction|friction]] in this intake process and help maintain vault hygiene over time.
