# CRISPR-like system

A class of biological [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]] that utilize RNA-guided nucleases or associated enzymes for targeted genomic modification, distinct from or analogous to the canonical CRISPR-Cas systems. Recent advancements highlight the role of [[concepts/artificial-intelligence|artificial intelligence]] in identifying novel variants within these systems.

## Key Developments

- **Discovery of [[concepts/array-associated-reverse-transcriptase|Array-associated Reverse Transcriptase (ART)]]:** In September 2026, [[entities/claude-ai]] identified a novel CRISPR-like system involving array-associated [[concepts/reverse-transcriptase|reverse transcriptase]] activity, expanding the known repertoire of RNA-guided editing tools [[lab-notes/2026-09-26-Claude-AI-Discovers-Novel-CRISPR-like-System-Array-assoc|Claude AI Discovers Novel CRISPR-like System, Array-associated Reverse Transcriptase (ART)]].
- **AI-Driven Discovery:** The integration of [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models]] in biological discovery allows for the rapid identification of non-canonical systems from [[concepts/genomic-data|genomic data]], as demonstrated by the recent ART finding.
