# Misty Sunrise

A photographic condition characterized by low-lying water vapor diffusing sunlight during dawn, creating soft, ethereal lighting and reduced [[concepts/contrast|contrast]]. Often associated with dreamy lake edits and atmospheric depth.

## Key Characteristics
- **Diffused Light:** Soft [[concepts/shadows|shadows]] and gradual tonal transitions.
- **Atmospheric Perspective:** Enhanced sense of depth due to light scattering.
- **Color Palette:** Typically cool blues, purples, and warm golden/orange highlights.

## Editing Techniques
- **Masking:** Use of [[concepts/skin-tone-correction|Sky Reflection Masking]] Trick for Dreamy Lake Edits to isolate and enhance reflections in calm water bodies.
- **Tone Curve:** Adjusting [[concepts/blacks|blacks]] and [[concepts/whites|whites]] to maintain detail in misty areas.
- **Color Grading:** Emphasizing warm highlights against cool shadows to mimic sunrise warmth.
