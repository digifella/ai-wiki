# Brush Size

The diameter of a Brush Tool in digital art applications, controlling stroke width and detail level. [[concepts/adjustments|Adjustments]] are critical for [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] work and broad strokes.

## Key Adjustment Methods in Adobe Photoshop
- **Bracket Keys:** Tap `[` or `]` to decrease/increase size
- **Shift + Bracket Keys:** Hold **Shift** + `[`/`]` to adjust edge **hardness**
- **[[concepts/hud-heads-up-display|Heads Up Display]] (HUD):**
  - *Mac:* Hold **Control + Option** and drag
  - *[[entities/windows|Windows]]:* Hold **Control + Alt + Right-click** and drag
