# Autonomous Operations

[[concepts/automated-business-operations|Autonomous operations]] refer to systems that can operate independently without continuous human intervention or oversight. In the context of AI and [[concepts/development-workflows|development workflows]], this involves leveraging advanced [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] to automate [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]], optimize processes, and even make decisions autonomously.

## Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|AI Agents]]:** Intelligent [[concepts/software|software]] entities capable of performing tasks on behalf of humans.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open Source Projects]]:** Collaborative efforts that enable wide [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] and rapid [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] in AI technology.
- **[[concepts/development-workflows|Development Workflows]]:** Processes used by developers to manage code changes and deployments, which can be significantly enhanced through autonomous operations.
