# Mirrorless photography

A camera [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] that allows [[concepts/light|light]] to hit the imaging sensor directly, eliminating the mechanical mirror and pentaprism used in [[concepts/dslr-photography|DSLR]] cameras.

- **Core Strengths**: [[entities/high-performance|High performance]] in video, low-light environments, and maintaining high [[concepts/dynamic-range|dynamic range]].
- **Market Position**: While smartphones have surpassed [[concepts/point-and-shoot-cameras|point-and-shoot cameras]] in convenience, mirrorless systems remain the standard for professional-grade imaging.
