# Adaptive Human-like Dynamic Athletic Control

**Adaptive Human-like [[concepts/dynamic-athletic-control|Dynamic Athletic Control]]** refers to the capability of [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to perform complex, high-dynamic physical tasks (such as parkour) by moving beyond simple imitation [[concepts/learning|learning]]. This approach emphasizes understanding the *why* behind human movements to achieve [[concepts/robustness|robustness]] and [[concepts/resilience|adaptability]] in unstructured environments.

## Core Concepts

*   **Limitations of Imitation:** Traditional methods often fail because copying human data does not account for the underlying [[concepts/physics|physics]] or adaptive [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] required in dynamic [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] [[lab-notes/2026-08-03-HIL-Hybrid-AI-for-Adaptive-Human-like-Dynamic-Athletic-C|HIL: Hybrid AI for Adaptive Human-like Dynamic Athletic Control]].
*   **[[concepts/hybrid-model|Hybrid AI Architecture]]:** Utilizes a combination of simulation-based [[concepts/reinforcement-learning|reinforcement learning]] and real-[[entities/earth|world]] constraints to train agents that can generalize across varied terrains and obstacles.
*   **Dynamic Athleticism:** Focuses on maintaining balance, momentum, and [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] during rapid state changes, mimicking elite human athletic performance.
