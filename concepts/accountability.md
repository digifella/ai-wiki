# Accountability

Accountability is the quality of being responsible for one's actions and decisions. It encompasses taking ownership of mistakes, delivering on commitments, and maintaining [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] in interactions.

### Key Aspects

- **Taking Responsibility:** Owning up to both successes and failures.
- **Transparency:** Being open about processes, intentions, and outcomes.
- **[[concepts/integrity|Integrity]]:** [[concepts/acting|Acting]] according to one's values and principles.
- **Continuous Improvement:** Reflecting on performance and seeking ways to enhance effectiveness.
