# VL-JEPA

**Backlinks:**
- 2026 04 14 New paper for a [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]] approach to AGI not LLM

## Overview
**[[concepts/cause-and-effect|VL-JEPA]]** (Vision-Language Joint Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]) is a research-driven approach to AGI that prioritizes vision-based modeling over purely linguistic prediction.

## Key Principles
- **Research Origin:** Developed by [[entities/yann-lecun|Yann LeCun]] and the [[entities/meta-ai|Meta]] FAIR Lab.
- **Architectural Shift:** Represents a strategic departure from current [[concepts/generative-ai]] paradigms and [[concepts/llm]]-centric architectures (e.g., [[entities/chatgpt]]).
- **Core Thesis:** Operates on the philosophical and technical premise that "**Language is not Intelligence**," suggesting that intelligence requires a model of the world that transcends text-based generation.
