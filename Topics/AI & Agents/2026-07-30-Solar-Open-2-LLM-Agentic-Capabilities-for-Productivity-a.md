## Solar Open 2 LLM: Agentic Capabilities for Productivity and Coding Demonstrations
**Clip title:** Solar Open 2 - An Agentic Model for Office [[concepts/productivity|Productivity]]
**[[entities/tasia-custode|Author]] / channel:** Fahd Mirza

### Summary
This video introduces and extensively tests [[entities/solar-open-2|Solar Open 2]], an [[concepts/open-weight|open-weight]] [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language model (LLM)]] developed in Korea by [[entities/upstage|Upstage]]. The model is highlighted for its impressive capabilities in [[concepts/agentic-tasks|agentic tasks]], [[concepts/coding|coding]], [[concepts/office-productivity|office productivity]], and document-intensive work, boasting a 250 billion parameter [[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] (MoE) architecture with only 15 billion parameters actively used per token. A key [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] lies in its Hybrid-[[concepts/self-attention|Attention mechanism]], which utilizes three [[concepts/optimized-attention|linear attention]] layers interleaved with one softmax [[concepts/transformer-layers|attention layer]], allowing it to handle up to 1 million [[concepts/tokens|tokens]] of context with significantly reduced [[concepts/inference|inference]] costs by effectively eliminating positional [[concepts/encoding|encoding]] (dubbed "NoPE").

The presenter demonstrates Solar Open 2's prowess through two challenging real-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]]. First, the model is tasked with [[concepts/acting|acting]] as an AWS Technical Account Manager (TAM) and Cloud Architect to perform a cost and architecture review based on a detailed, messy AWS report. The prompt required identifying top cost [[concepts/causes|drivers]], root-cause analysis, specific fixes with estimated dollar impact, [[concepts/risk-assessment|risk assessment]], and a two-quarter rollout plan. Remarkably, Solar Open 2 delivered a comprehensive and nuanced response, quantifying [[concepts/leftover-utilization|cost savings]], ranking solutions by impact and risk, and even intelligently pushing back on certain "money-saving" moves that could compromise Service Level Agreements (SLAs) or introduce operational risks—a feat typically requiring human [[concepts/expertise|expertise]].

The second demonstration involves a complex coding task: building a self-contained HTML file (with [[concepts/inline-css|inline CSS]] and [[concepts/javascript|JavaScript]]) for an interactive "Earth-Moon travel comparison chart." This prompt demanded the model to act as a [[concepts/full-stack-developer|full-stack developer]], designer, mathematician, and UX engineer simultaneously, incorporating dark space themes, animated vehicles, editable inputs for distance and [[concepts/speed|speed]], live calculations, and [[concepts/responsive-design|responsive design]], all with [[concepts/concept-of-nothingness|zero]] external dependencies. The model successfully generated a clean, fully functional, and interactive web page that accurately performed all calculations and met the [[concepts/aesthetic-design|visual design]] requirements, showcasing its exceptional ability to handle intricate, multi-faceted coding challenges.

Finally, the video touches upon the model's multilingual capabilities and [[concepts/licensing|licensing]]. When asked to generate unique, culturally appropriate short quotes in numerous languages, the results were mixed; while many translations seemed correct, some appeared overly literal or lacked profound naturalness, suggesting that multilingual generation might not be its absolute strongest suit compared to its agentic and coding performance. Regarding [[concepts/licensing|licensing]], Solar Open 2 operates under an Upstage Solar License, essentially [[concepts/apache-2-0|Apache License 2.0]] with a crucial additional condition: any derivative AI model must start with "Solar" and publicly display "Built with Solar," though commercial use is fully permitted. Overall, Solar Open 2 distinguishes itself as a highly efficient and capable model for complex, real-world applications, particularly excelling in long-context [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] and [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]].
