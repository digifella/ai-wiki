# Vignette Application

Vignette application is a post-processing technique that darkens or lightens the edges of a photograph while keeping the center brighter, drawing viewer [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] toward the subject. In Lightroom Classic, this effect is applied through the Effects panel, where users can control the intensity, feathering, and roundness of the vignette to achieve subtle or pronounced results depending on the image and artistic intent.

## Controls and Parameters

Lightroom Classic provides several adjustable parameters for [[concepts/vignette-effects|vignette effects]]. The Amount slider determines the strength of darkening or lightening at the edges, while the Midpoint slider controls how far from the edges the effect extends toward the center. The Roundness parameter shapes the vignette from rectangular to circular, and the Feather slider determines the softness of the transition between the vignetted and unvignetted areas.

## Combined with Grain Effects

Vignettes are often applied alongside grain effects in Lightroom Classic to enhance the vintage or film-like quality of photographs. While vignetting darkens edges, grain adds [[concepts/texture|texture]] across the image. These two effects complement each other in stylizing photographs, though both should be applied with restraint to avoid overwhelming the image or appearing artificially processed.
