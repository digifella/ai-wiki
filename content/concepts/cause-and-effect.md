# Cause And Effect

[[concepts/vl-jepa|VL-JEPA]] ([[concepts/computer-vision|Vision]]-Language Joint Embedding Predictive [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]) is a vision-based architecture developed by [[entities/meta|Meta]] that represents an alternative approach to [[concepts/large-language-model-llm|large language models]] (LLMs) in the [[entities/pursuitunimelbeduau|pursuit]] of [[concepts/agi|artificial general intelligence]]. Rather than relying primarily on text-based [[concepts/transformer-architectures|transformer models]], VL-JEPA emphasizes [[concepts/visual-understanding|visual understanding]] and prediction as foundational components of intelligent [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]]. The architecture uses joint embeddings of vision and language to learn abstract representations of the world through [[concepts/predictive-modeling|predictive modeling]], allowing the system to anticipate future states based on visual input.

## Distinction from LLM-Based Reasoning

The key distinction between VL-JEPA and conventional [[concepts/llm-reasoning|LLM reasoning]] architectures lies in their primary [[concepts/modality|modality]] and [[concepts/learning|learning]] mechanism. LLM-based systems build [[concepts/reasoning-capabilities|reasoning capabilities]] atop language understanding and generation, whereas VL-JEPA positions visual prediction as a primary learning signal. This approach reflects a hypothesis that general intelligence may benefit from grounding in visual causality—understanding how actions cause changes in the observable environment—rather than purely linguistic [[concepts/pattern-matching|pattern matching]].

## Implications for AGI Development

VL-JEPA's focus on cause-and-effect [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] through visual prediction suggests that robust AGI systems might require deep understanding of physical and causal principles. By [[concepts/training|training]] on visual prediction tasks, the architecture potentially develops generalizable knowledge about how the world works, which could transfer to reasoning tasks beyond vision. This contrasts with non-vision-based reasoning architectures that may lack this grounded causal foundation, though comparative [[concepts/empirical-evidence|empirical evidence]] remains limited as of 2026.
