# Telehealth Systems

[[concepts/telehealth|Telehealth]] systems encompass the use of electronic information and telecommunication technologies to provide [[concepts/health-care|health care]] from a distance. These systems facilitate remote diagnosis, treatment, and monitoring, bridging geographical gaps between patients and providers. Key components include [[concepts/remote-health-monitoring|Remote Patient Monitoring]], Teleconsultation, and increasingly, autonomous or semi-autonomous robotic assistance.

## Key Components & Integrations

*   **Clinical Support Agents**: Integration of [[concepts/multi-agent-systems]] to assist physicians in [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]], scheduling, and data analysis.
*   **Robotic Assistance & Spatial Intelligence**: Medical [[concepts/robotics|robotics]] are evolving beyond pre-programmed tasks through the application of [[concepts/spatial-intelligence]]. As highlighted in [[lab-notes/2026-08-02-Fei-Fei-Li-Spatial-Intelligence-Solves-Robotics-Data-Sca|Fei-Fei Li: Spatial Intelligence Solves Robotics Data Scarcity]], spatial intelligence addresses the critical challenge of data [[concepts/limited-resources|scarcity]] in robotics, enabling machines to better understand and navigate complex physical environments. This advancement is pivotal for enhancing the autonomy of robots in telehealth settings, allowing for more intuitive [[concepts/human-robot-interaction]] and precise assistance in clinical procedures without extensive manual retraining for every new scenario.
