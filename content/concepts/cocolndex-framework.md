# Cocolndex Framework

A [[concepts/data-transformation|data transformation]] framework for building [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|knowledge graphs]] from documents to enhance [[concepts/llm-rag]] systems.

- Processes [[concepts/markdown|markdown]] documents to extract entities and [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] using LLMs
- Builds structured knowledge graphs in [[entities/neo4j|Neo4j]] for efficient [[concepts/llm-rag]] querying
- Enables [[concepts/real-time-knowledge-graph|real-time knowledge graph]] construction from document collections
