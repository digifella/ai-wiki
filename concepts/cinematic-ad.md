# Cinematic Ad

A cinematic ad is a 30-second video advertisement produced through a structured workflow that transforms written content into polished visual media. This format merges professional production aesthetics with automated generation techniques, facilitating the efficient creation of marketing assets for digital platforms, product launches, and brand messaging. By bridging traditional video production with artificial intelligence, the process allows creators to achieve broadcast-quality results without the resource intensity of conventional filming.

## Workflow and Technology

The creation process begins with the development of a detailed script or narrative structure within NotebookLM. This written content serves as the foundational source material, leveraging the platform's ability to organize and synthesize information. The structured notes are then exported and imported into Google Vids, which utilizes the generative capabilities of Gemini to interpret the text and generate corresponding visual elements.

Google Vids processes the input by automatically selecting stock footage, images, and audio tracks that align with the semantic meaning of the script. Gemini assists in refining the narrative flow and ensuring that the visual transitions match the tone of the written content. The final output is a cohesive 30-second video that maintains a cinematic style through careful editing, color grading, and pacing, all generated through an automated pipeline rather than manual filming.
