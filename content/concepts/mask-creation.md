# Mask Creation

Mask creation is a fundamental technique in digital [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] that involves isolating specific areas of an image for selective [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. In Lightroom and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], masks allow photographers to apply edits to particular regions while leaving the rest of the image unaffected, enabling precise control over [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, clarity, and other [[concepts/parameters|parameters]].

## Fixing Mask Edges

A key challenge in mask creation is achieving clean, natural-looking edges where the masked area meets the rest of the image. [[entities/colin-smith-channel|Colin Smith]] of PhotoshopCAFE has demonstrated techniques for refining mask edges in both Lightroom and [[concepts/camera-raw-filter|Camera Raw Filter]], focusing on reducing harsh transitions and creating more polished results. These methods help photographers achieve seamless [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] that appears intentional rather than obviously artificial.

## Practical Applications

Masks are commonly used to selectively brighten or darken specific parts of an image, enhance subject separation from backgrounds, or apply localized color corrections. Mastering [[concepts/edge-refinement|edge refinement]] techniques ensures that these edits blend naturally with the surrounding image, maintaining the overall [[concepts/cohesion|cohesion]] and professional appearance of the final photograph.
