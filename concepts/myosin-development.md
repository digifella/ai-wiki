# Myosin Development

**[[concepts/uniform-crispness|Myosin development]]** is the process of extracting and aligning myosin proteins from muscle tissue through [[concepts/mechanical-agitation|mechanical agitation]] (mixing, pounding, or grinding) and salt. This creates a viscous, sticky matrix that binds meat particles together, determining the final [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]], juiciness, and structural [[concepts/honesty|integrity]] of [[concepts/ground-meat|ground meat]] preparations.

## Mechanism
- **Protein Extraction:** Salt dissolves myosin from [[concepts/muscle-fibers|muscle fibers]].
- **Viscosity:** Mechanical work aligns these proteins into a gel-like network.
- **Binding:** This network traps water and fat, preventing separation during cooking.

## Applications & Texture Control

### Burgers
- **Goal:** Tender, distinct [[concepts/texture|texture]]; minimal binding.
- **Technique:** Minimal handling. Avoid over-mixing to prevent excessive [[concepts/universal-technique|myosin development]], which leads to a dense, rubbery texture.
- **Reference:** See [[lab-notes/2026-06-22-Myosin-Development-Mastering-Burger-Sausage-Meatball-Tex|Myosin Development: Mastering Burger, Sausage, Meatball Textures]] for specific handling protocols.

### Sausages
- **Goal:** Snappy, cohesive bite.
- **Technique:** High myosin development required. Vigorous mixing or pounding is necessary to create a strong protein matrix that holds the emulsion of fat and meat.
- **Key Factor:** [[concepts/thermal-regulation|Temperature control]] is critical; heat denatures proteins before the matrix forms.

### Meatballs
- **Goal:** Balanced structure; tender but not crumbly.
- **Technique:** Moderate mixing. Enough agitation to bind the meat without creating the dense elasticity found in sausages.

## Key Variables
- **Salt Concentration:** Essential for solubilizing myosin.
- **Temperature:** Keep meat cold (<40°F/4°C) to prevent fat smearing and premature [[concepts/food-chemistry|protein denaturation]].
- **Agitation Time:** Directly correlates with texture [[concepts/density|density]] (Low = Burger, High = Sausage).
