## Orion Systems

**[[concepts/spacecraft-systems|Orion systems]]** are a crucial component of [[entities/nasa|NASA]]'s [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]] aimed at returning humans to the Moon and eventually to [[concepts/mars|Mars]]. These spacecraft are designed for deep [[concepts/space-exploration|space exploration]], leveraging advanced technology to ensure crew safety and mission [[concepts/success|success]].

### Key Features
- **Life Support**: Advanced environmental control and life support system (ECLSS) to maintain habitable conditions during long-duration missions.
- **Propulsion**: [[entities/high-performance|High-performance]] engines capable of both [[entities/earth|Earth]] orbit and lunar transfers.
- **Navigation and [[concepts/recommendations|Guidance]]**: [[concepts/accuracy|Precision]] navigation systems for accurate trajectory management in [[concepts/deep-space-exploration|deep space]].
