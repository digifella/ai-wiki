# Gyro Tourbillon

A complex [[concepts/watch-mechanism|watch mechanism]] designed to maintain accurate timekeeping by counteracting the effects of [[concepts/gravitational-pull|gravity]] on the escapement and balance wheel. Often described as the "heart of a watch," it integrates the principles of the Tourbillon Watch Mechanism: Purpose, [[concepts/gravity-compensation|Gravity Compensation]], and 3D-Printed Model with gyroscopic stabilization.

## Core Concepts

- **Gravity Compensation**: The primary purpose of the tourbillon is to neutralize positional errors caused by gravity, which affects the rate of the balance wheel in different orientations.
- **Gyroscopic Stabilization**: Unlike traditional tourbillons that rotate on a single axis, the gyro tourbillon utilizes gyroscopic forces to maintain orientation and stability, enhancing [[concepts/accuracy|precision]].
- **Mechanical Complexity**: Represents a high level of horological [[entities/national-academies|engineering]], often involving intricate gear trains and rotating cages.
