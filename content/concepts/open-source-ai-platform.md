# Open Source AI Platform

An open source AI platform is a [[concepts/software|software]] system for developing, [[concepts/training|training]], and deploying [[concepts/artificial-intelligence-models|artificial intelligence models]] built on [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] principles. These platforms provide publicly accessible source code, enabling developers and organizations to inspect, modify, and redistribute the underlying tools and frameworks. This approach contrasts with [[concepts/proprietary-ai|proprietary AI]] systems where the underlying code remains closed and controlled by a single vendor.

[[concepts/open-source-machine-learning|Open source AI platforms]] typically offer core components including machine [[concepts/learning|learning]] frameworks, [[concepts/pre-trained-model|pre-trained model]] libraries, and development tools. Examples include platforms like Hugging Face, which provides access to [[concepts/transformer-architectures|transformer models]] and model hosting infrastructure, and various initiatives from major technology companies that release [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] under open licenses. These platforms often benefit from community contributions and peer review, which can accelerate development and improve security.

The [[concepts/adoption|adoption]] of open source AI reflects broader industry trends toward democratizing [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] and reducing dependency on proprietary solutions. Organizations use these platforms to customize AI systems for their specific needs, integrate with existing infrastructure, and maintain greater control over their AI implementations. However, open source AI platforms also raise considerations around model [[concepts/governance|governance]], liability, and the [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] required for training and [[concepts/deployment|deployment]].
