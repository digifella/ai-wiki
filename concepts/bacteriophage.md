# Bacteriophage

**Bacteriophage** (or **phage**) is a virus that infects and replicates within bacteria and archaea. They are the most abundant biological [[concepts/nodes|entities]] on [[entities/earth|Earth]] and play a critical role in horizontal gene transfer and bacterial population control.

## Key Concepts

- **CRISPR-Cas Systems**: Originally discovered as an adaptive immune system in bacteria and archaea against phage infection.
- **[[concepts/reverse-transcriptase|Reverse Transcriptase]]**: Enzymes that synthesize DNA from an RNA template, commonly found in retroviruses and retrotransposons.
- **[[concepts/array-associated-reverse-transcriptase|Array-associated Reverse Transcriptase (ART)]]**: A novel [[concepts/crispr-like-system|CRISPR-like system]] recently identified via AI analysis, expanding the known repertoire of phage-defense [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
