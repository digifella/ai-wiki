# DIY

**DIY** (Do It Yourself) refers to the practice of building, modifying, or repairing items without relying on paid labor. In technical contexts, it often involves leveraging [[concepts/open-source-hardware|open-source hardware]], Electrochemistry, and resourceful material sourcing to achieve professional-grade results at a hobbyist scale.

## Core Principles
- **Resourcefulness**: Maximizing utility from accessible materials (e.g., repurposing e-waste for components).
- **[[concepts/iterative-learning|Iterative Learning]]**: Prototyping via failure; documentation is key (see Zettelkasten or personal wikis).
- **Safety First**: Critical when dealing with hazardous chemicals, high voltages, or pressurized systems.

## Key Domains

### Electrochemistry and Material Refinement
A significant sub-field of DIY chemistry involves using electrical currents to manipulate chemical states, often bypassing industrial-grade equipment requirements through clever design.
- **Ion-Exchange Membranes**: DIY construction allows for controlled separation of ions, crucial for purifying [[concepts/metal|metals]] or generating specific chemical solutions without commercial membranes.
- **[[concepts/metal-refining|Metal Refining]]**: Using Electrolysis to extract or refine metals (e.g., copper, silver) from ores or scrap.
- **[[concepts/energy-applications|Energy Applications]]**: Building custom batteries or fuel cells using ion-exchange principles.

> **Note**: Detailed experiments and methodologies regarding these specific chemical processes are documented in [[lab-notes/2026-06-09-Electrolysis-and-DIY-Ion-Exchange-Metal-Refining-and-Ene|Electrolysis and DIY Ion-Exchange: Metal Refining and Energy Applications]].

### Electronics and Hardware
- **PCB Fabrication**: Milling, etching, or [[concepts/3d-printing|3D printing]] circuits.
- **Microcontrollers**: Programming Arduino or [[entities/raspberry-pi]] for automation and sensing.

## Safety & Ethics
- **Chemical Handling**: Always use proper PPE (gloves, goggles) when dealing with acids, [[concepts/number-systems|bases]], or electrolytes.
- **Legal [[concepts/compliance|Compliance]]**: Ensure refined materials or generated energy systems comply with local regulations regarding waste disposal and electrical [[concepts/grids|grids]].
