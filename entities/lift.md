# Lift

**Lift** is an AI model developed by [[entities/datalab|Datalab]] designed for schema-constrained local structured [[concepts/data-extraction|data extraction]]. It specializes in converting unstructured documents, specifically [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]] and images, into structured formats like [[concepts/json]].

## Key Features
- **[[concepts/structured-data-extraction|Schema-Constrained Extraction]]**: Enforces specific data structures during extraction to ensure [[concepts/logical-consistency|consistency]].
- **[[concepts/local-execution|Local Execution]]**: Runs locally, preserving [[concepts/privacy|data privacy]] and reducing latency.
- **[[concepts/multilingual-support|Multilingual Support]]**: Tested and validated across 10 different languages.
- **Input Versatility**: Handles both PDF documents and image-based inputs.
