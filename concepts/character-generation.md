# Character Generation

Character Generation is a feature in Vyond Go that enables users to create or select characters before initiating AI-assisted video production. This process serves as a foundational step in the video creation workflow, allowing creators to establish visual identity and maintain consistency throughout their generated content.

## Stock Characters and Customization

Users can choose from a library of pre-designed stock characters or create custom characters tailored to their specific needs. Stock characters offer a quick starting point for video production, while custom character creation allows for greater control over appearance, presentation, and branding alignment. This flexibility accommodates both users seeking rapid content generation and those requiring distinctive visual elements.

## Role in Video Production

Character Generation functions as an early phase in the overall script-building and video creation process. By establishing characters before generating video content, users can ensure that their selected or created characters appear consistently across scenes and maintain logical continuity throughout the final output. This preliminary step helps streamline the subsequent stages of AI-assisted video generation.
