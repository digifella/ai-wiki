# Hard Takeoff Phase

The [[concepts/guiding-role|Hard Takeoff]] [[concepts/phase|Phase]] refers to a theoretical scenario in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] development characterized by rapid, self-sustaining acceleration of an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]]'s capabilities. In this phase, an advanced AI reaches a threshold where it can autonomously modify its own code, [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]], and training processes at a [[concepts/speed|speed]] exceeding human oversight. This self-directed improvement creates a [[concepts/performance-feedback|feedback loop]], where each enhancement enables faster subsequent improvements, potentially leading to a dramatic and swift transition in intelligence levels.

The concept is often discussed in the context of specific industrial strategies, such as the acceleration of [[concepts/self-developing-ai|self-developing AI]] through [[concepts/nodes|entities]] like xAI and [[entities/anthropic-institute|Anthropic]]. Proponents of this view suggest that leveraging an "[[concepts/ai-model-factory|AI Model Factory]]" approach could facilitate the conditions necessary for such a takeoff. This involves [[concepts/computational-scaling|scaling]] [[concepts/computational-resources|computational resources]] and data processing to allow [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]] to iterate on their own architectures with minimal human intervention, thereby compressing the timeline for capability gains.

Critics and researchers debate the feasibility and safety of such a scenario, noting that current AI systems still rely heavily on human-guided training and infrastructure. The Hard Takeoff Phase remains a hypothetical construct used to analyze potential risks and timelines in [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]], rather than an observed phenomenon. It serves as a framework for discussing the implications of [[concepts/ai-recursive-self-improvement|recursive self-improvement]] and the potential for AI systems to outpace human control [[concepts/causes|mechanisms]].
