# Multimodal AI

[[concepts/multimodal-ai]] refers to [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] models capable of ingesting and/or generating data across various [[concepts/data-management|Data-Modalities]].

### Key Concepts
- **[[concepts/modality|Modality]]**: A specific data type or format used as input or output.
- **Common Modalities**: Includes Text, Images, [[concepts/audio-modality|Audio]], [[concepts/lidar|Lidar]], and [[concepts/thermal-imaging|Thermal-Imaging]].
- **Processing Capabilities**: Models increasingly bridge the gap between digital perception and physical action, enabling complex [[concepts/robotics|Robotics]] applications.

### Recent Developments & Integrations
- **[[concepts/physical-ai|Physical AI]] & Robotics**: Significant strides are being made in integrating [[concepts/unified-multimodal-models|multimodal models]] into physical agents. [[concepts/2026-04-29-google-deepmind|Google DeepMind]] is actively deploying [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] AI to create adaptable, [[concepts/general-purpose-robots|general-purpose robots]] capable of navigating unstructured environments.
- **[[concepts/open-source|Open-Source]] & [[concepts/custom-models|Specialized Models]]**: The landscape includes [[concepts/ai-licensing|open-weights models]] like [[concepts/openclaw|Inkling]], vision-only [[concepts/computer-use|Computer Use]] Agents like [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] [[entities/fara-15-27b|Fara 1.5-27B]], and [[concepts/structured-decision-models|structured decision models]] like Cloudflare's [[concepts/decision-model|Clef 27B]].
- **Document Comprehension**: Advanced [[concepts/rag|RAG]] techniques now support [[concepts/complex-document-comprehension|complex document understanding]] via [[concepts/page-screenshots|screenshot-based retrieval]].
