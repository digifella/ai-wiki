# Voice Design

[[concepts/tone|Voice]] design refers to the creation and [[concepts/customization|customization]] of synthetic voices for [[concepts/text-to-speech-model|text-to-speech]] applications. The [[concepts/17b-parameter-model|Qwen3-TTS]] family of models, released as [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] software by the [[entities/qwen-team|Qwen team]], provides tools and capabilities for voice design alongside related functionalities. These models enable developers and creators to generate natural-sounding speech from text while maintaining control over vocal characteristics.

## Key Capabilities

The [[concepts/instruction-control|Qwen3-TTS]] models support three primary features: voice design, [[concepts/ai-clone|voice cloning]], and [[concepts/text-to-speech-generation|text-to-speech generation]].
