# Muse Spark

[[concepts/ai-model-performance|Muse Spark]] is a proprietary [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] model developed by [[entities/meta|Meta]], introduced in April 2026. It has been positioned as a successor to Meta's previously [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[entities/llama|Llama]] model line, representing a [[concepts/strategic-pivot|strategic shift]] in the company's approach to [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] [[concepts/deployment|deployment]] and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]].

## Strategic Context

The development of Muse Spark reflects Meta's transition toward [[concepts/proprietary-ai|proprietary AI]] systems, moving away from the open-source [[concepts/licensing|licensing]] model that characterized earlier versions of Llama. This shift has been discussed in technology circles, including analysis by AI commentator [[entities/sam-witteveen|Sam Witteveen]], who covered the model's features and performance characteristics alongside Meta's broader strategic repositioning in the [[concepts/generative-ai|generative AI]] sector.
