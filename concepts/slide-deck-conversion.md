# Slide Deck Conversion

Slide Deck Conversion is the process of transforming presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video content using Google Vids. NotebookLM automatically structures source documents into organized slide presentations, which serve as the foundation for video production. Google Vids then converts these slides into formatted videos, streamlining the transition from document analysis to multimedia output within Google's integrated toolset.

## Workflow and Process

The conversion workflow typically begins with document upload to NotebookLM, which generates thematic slides based on the provided sources. These slides are then exported or linked to Google Vids, where they are used as the structural basis for a video. Google Vids processes the slide content to create a narrated or visual presentation, allowing users to produce video assets directly from the analyzed document data without manual slide design.
