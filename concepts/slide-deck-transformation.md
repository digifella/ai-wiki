# Slide Deck Transformation

Slide Deck Transformation refers to the workflow of converting presentation slides generated by NotebookLM into video format using Google Vids. NotebookLM, an AI-powered notebook application, automatically structures source documents into slide decks. These slides serve as the foundational content for subsequent video production, allowing users to repurpose text-based research and documentation into multimedia presentations.

The process begins within NotebookLM, where users upload source materials to generate a structured slide deck. Once the slides are finalized, they are imported into Google Vids, Google's AI video creation platform. This integration enables the automatic assembly of the slides into a cohesive video, streamlining the transition from static presentation content to dynamic visual media.
