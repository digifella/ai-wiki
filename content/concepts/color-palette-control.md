# Color palette control

The systematic manipulation of specific [[concepts/color-ranges|color ranges]] within an image to achieve desired aesthetic or technical outcomes during Digital Post-Processing.

### Implementation via HSL/Color Mixer
Using the HSL/Color Mixer panel in Adobe [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] allows for targeted [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] across three primary vectors:
- **[[concepts/hue|Hue]]**: Shifting the base color of specific channels.
- **Saturation**: Adjusting the intensity or purity of specific color ranges.
- **Luminance**: Modifying the brightness of individual color channels.
