# Mask Creation

Mask creation is a fundamental technique in digital [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] that involves isolating specific areas of an image for [[concepts/color-and-masking-adjustments|selective adjustments]]. In [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] and [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]], masks allow photographers to apply edits to particular regions while leaving the rest of the image unaffected, enabling precise control over [[concepts/exposure|exposure]], color, [[concepts/clarity-slider|clarity]], and other parameters. This selective editing approach is essential for refining images with localized adjustments that would be difficult or impossible to achieve through global edits alone.

## Edge Refinement

One of the most critical aspects of effective mask creation is managing mask edges. Poorly defined edges can result in visible transitions between masked and unmasked areas, creating an artificial or unpolished appearance in the final image. Adobe's Lightroom and Camera Raw offer tools for refining mask boundaries, including feathering options to create smooth transitions and edge adjustment controls to reduce halos or unwanted artifacts along mask perimeters.

## Practical Application

Photographers commonly use masks to selectively enhance specific elements such as skies, skin tones, or background elements without affecting the rest of the composition. Well-executed masks are invisible to the viewer, appearing as natural enhancements that strengthen the overall image. Mastery of mask creation techniques, including proper edge handling, is therefore essential for producing professional-quality edited photographs.
