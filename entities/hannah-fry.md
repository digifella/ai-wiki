# Hannah Fry

**Hannah Fry** is a British mathematician, [[entities/tasia-custode|author]], and broadcaster known for her work in [[concepts/practical-mathematics|Applied Mathematics]] and public engagement with [[concepts/science|science]]. She serves as a host for the [[entities/google-deepmind]] podcast series, focusing on the intersection of [[concepts/mathematics|mathematics]], technology, and society.
