### Nvidia CUDA: GPU Parallel Computing for AI Advancement

**Clip title:** Nvidia CUDA in 100 Seconds
**Author / channel:** Fireship
**URL:** https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pPStdjuYzSI

- Launched by Nvidia in 2007, CUDA ([[concepts/compute|Compute]] Unified Device [[concepts/architecture|Architecture]]) enables GPUs to perform general-[[concepts/purpose|purpose]] computing tasks beyond traditional graphics processing.
- Developed based on early work by [[entities/ian-buck|Ian Buck]] and [[entities/john-nicholls|John Nicholls]], CUDA provides a [[concepts/general-purpose-computing|parallel computing]] platform that significantly boosts the efficiency of [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]] [[concepts/training|training]] and [[concepts/inference|inference]] processes.
- By utilizing the massive parallel [[concepts/architecture|architecture]] of GPUs, CUDA accelerates computations in machine learning frameworks like TensorFlow, PyTorch, and others, facilitating faster development cycles and more robust model performance.
