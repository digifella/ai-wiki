# Thought Tracing

Thought tracing is a technique in AI [[concepts/interpretability|interpretability]] that reconstructs and analyzes the intermediate [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] steps (or "thoughts") generated by a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] (LLM) during [[concepts/workflow-automation|task execution]], revealing its internal [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process rather than treating it as a black box. Recent advancements include the identification of emergent internal structures, such as the "[[concepts/j-space|J-space]]," which functions as a [[concepts/conscious-processing|global workspace]] within the model's architecture.
