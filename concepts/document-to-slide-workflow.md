# Document To Slide Workflow

The Document To Slide Workflow is a process for converting written notes and documents into presentation slides using AI-powered tools, primarily Google NotebookLM and Gemini. This workflow leverages automated analysis and generation capabilities to transform source material into formatted presentations, reducing the manual effort required for slide creation and enabling faster iteration on content organization and design.

The process typically begins by uploading source documents, such as PDFs or web pages, into Google NotebookLM. The platform processes these inputs to create a grounded knowledge base, allowing the AI to reference specific details accurately. Users can then prompt the system to summarize key points, structure arguments, or extract actionable insights from the provided text.

Gemini is often integrated to handle the actual slide generation or to refine the output from NotebookLM. By combining NotebookLM’s contextual understanding with Gemini’s generative capabilities, the workflow automates the drafting of slide content, including titles, bullet points, and speaker notes. This integration ensures that the resulting presentation remains faithful to the source material while adhering to standard presentation structures.

The final output is a draft slide deck that serves as a starting point for further refinement. While the workflow significantly accelerates the initial creation phase, it requires human review to ensure accuracy, appropriate tone, and visual coherence. This approach is particularly useful for rapid prototyping of presentations, educational material preparation, and summarizing complex reports into digestible formats.
