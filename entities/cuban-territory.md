# Cuban territory

Sovereign territory comprising the Republic of [[entities/cuba|Cuba]], the Isle of Youth, and associated archipelagos. Jurisdiction includes areas with contested or shared administrative control due to historical treaties and foreign military presence.

## Guantanamo Bay

- **Sovereignty Status:** [[concepts/guantanamo-bay]] operates under a 1903 lease formalized in the 1934 Treaty, granting the [[entities/united-states]] perpetual control pending mutual agreement or abandonment; [[entities/havana|Cuba]] disputes the treaty's validity and rejects rent payments.
- **Acquisition and Base:** US acquisition followed the Spanish–American War, establishing a naval station that evolved into a key strategic [[concepts/military-base|military base]] while nominal Cuban sovereignty remains legally contested.
- **[[concepts/detention-center|Detention Center]]:** Site repurposed in 2002 to host the Guantanamo Bay detention camp, facilitating extraordinary rendition, indefinite military detention, and interrogation of terrorism suspects without civilian due process.
- **Controversies:** Ongoing debates center on human rights violations, international legal challenges, diplomatic [[concepts/friction|friction]], and global pressure for closure; the site remains a focal point in US counterterrorism policy and Cuba-US relations.
