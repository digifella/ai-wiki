# Early Access

**Early access** refers to a software distribution model where a product is made available to a limited group of users before its general public [[concepts/deployment|release]]. This [[concepts/phase|phase]] allows developers to gather [[concepts/feedback|feedback]], identify bugs, and refine features while generating initial revenue or user engagement.

## Key Characteristics
- **Limited Availability:** Restricted to specific user segments (e.g., paid subscribers, beta testers).
- **[[concepts/iterative-design|Iterative Development]]:** Features are often rolled out incrementally based on real-[[entities/earth|world]] usage data.
- **[[concepts/performance-feedback|Feedback Loop]]:** Users provide direct input that influences the final product roadmap.
- **Risk/Reward:** Users may encounter instability but gain access to cutting-edge capabilities.

## Notable Examples

### OpenAI Dots
[[concepts/whisper-transcription|OpenAI]] has introduced **[[concepts/dots-agent|Dots]]**, an always-on, proactive [[concepts/personal-ai-assistant|personal AI assistant]] agent designed for [[concepts/session-resumption|workflow continuity]]. It acts as a [[concepts/orchestrator-model|central orchestrator]] within the [[entities/chatgpt]] ecosystem, bridging various components to enhance user [[concepts/productivity|productivity]].

- **Status:** Currently in early access.
- **Eligibility:** Available exclusively to [[entities/chatgpt]] Pro and Enterprise plan users.
- **Core Functionality:**
  - Proactive assistance rather than reactive command processing.
  - [[concepts/hidden-engineering|Seamless integration]] with existing ChatGPT tools and data.
  - Designed to maintain context across different workflow stages.
