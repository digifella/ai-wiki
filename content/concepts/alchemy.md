# Alchemy

Medieval practice of transforming base metals into gold, seeking the philosopher's stone, and exploring the fundamental [[entities/nature|nature]] of matter. Evolved into modern Chemistry and influenced Hermeticism.

## Key Concepts
- Philosopher's Stone: Legendary substance enabling transmutation and immortality.
- Emerald Tablet: Foundational [[concepts/text|text]] of Hermetic [[concepts/philosophy|philosophy]] attributed to [[entities/hermes|Hermes]] Trismegistus.
- Magnum [[concepts/opus|Opus]]: The alchemical process of spiritual and material transformation.

## Modern Context
- Metaphorical use in fields like data [[concepts/science|science]] ("data alchemy").
- Historical precursor to Chemistry and Pharmacology.
- Alchemy's symbolic framework still influences Jungian [[concepts/psychology|psychology]].
