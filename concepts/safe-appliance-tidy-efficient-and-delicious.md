# SATED (Safe Appliance Tidy Efficient and Delicious)

**[[concepts/sated|SATED]]** is an acronym for **Safe Appliance Tidy Efficient and Delicious**, referring to a specialized cooking device engineered for microgravity environments.

## Overview
The device addresses critical challenges in Zero-[[concepts/gravitational-pull|Gravity]] [[concepts/water-bath-method|food preparation]], specifically focusing on safety, cleanliness, [[concepts/energy-efficiency|energy efficiency]], and culinary quality for astronauts.

## Operational Context
- **Environment:** [[concepts/international-space-station]] (ISS) and other zero-gravity habitats.
- **Design Goals:**
  - **Safe:** Mitigates fire and heat hazards in pressurized cabins.
  - **Tidy:** Contains crumbs and liquids that would otherwise float freely.
  - **Efficient:** Optimizes power usage in limited energy systems.
  - **Delicious:** Improves meal quality over traditional rehydrated options.
