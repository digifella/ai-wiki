# Second Brain

A [[concepts/personal-knowledge-management-pkm|Personal Knowledge Management (PKM)]] system designed to capture, organize, and distill information for long-term [[concepts/storing|retention]] and creative output.

## Core Principles
- **Capture:** Collect information from various sources without immediate judgment.
- **Organize:** Structure [[concepts/notes|notes]] using a logical [[concepts/hierarchy|hierarchy]] or networked graph.
- **Distill:** Reduce information to its essence for easy [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]].
- **Express:** Use the stored knowledge to create new content or solve problems.

## Tools & Ecosystem
- **[[concepts/obsidian|Obsidian]]:** A local-first, markdown-based note-taking app that supports Second Brain workflows through [[concepts/plugins|plugins]] and graph visualization.
- **[[concepts/ai-integration|AI Integration]]:** Modern PKM systems increasingly [[concepts/leverage-ai|leverage AI]] for [[concepts/summarization|summarization]], tagging, and [[concepts/natural-language-search|semantic search]].
