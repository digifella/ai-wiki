# Adobe Camera Raw

Raw image processing module in [[entities/adobe]] [[concepts/lightroom]] and [[concepts/photoshop|Photoshop]].

## New Features (2026)

- [[entities/julieanne-kost]] demonstrates the **[[concepts/color-variance-control|Color Variance Control]]** in the **[[concepts/point-color|Point Color]]** feature ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FSk--Gi-PeA)):
  - Uses the [[concepts/eyedropper-tool|eyedropper tool]] to select a color (e.g., green from trees).
  - Adjusts [[concepts/hue|Hue]], Saturation, and Luminance to target the selected color.
- [[entities/glyn-dewis]] [[concepts/highlights|highlights]] the "early access" **Variance** slider within the **[[concepts/point-color|Point Color]]** section of the [[concepts/color-mixer|Color Mixer]] ([video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mEgFQC430k0)):
  - Controls the [[concepts/range|range]] of colors affected by a [[concepts/point-color|Point Color]] adjustment after sampling a specific color.
