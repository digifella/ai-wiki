# Marketing Rules

Marketing Rules is a systematic methodology designed to convert detailed research and knowledge content into compact 30-second video commercials. The approach addresses the practical challenge of transforming long-form written analysis into short-form video content by providing a structured workflow that leverages Google's AI-powered tools. This process enables users to repurpose existing intellectual assets efficiently without requiring extensive manual editing or production resources.

The core process centers on using NotebookLM notebooks as the primary source material for content repurposing. Users begin with existing research, analysis, or knowledge documentation stored within the platform. This foundational content is then processed through Google Gemini to condense the information, identifying key narratives and essential points suitable for brief visual storytelling.

The workflow integrates Google Vids to finalize the production phase. By combining the condensed script generated by Gemini with the visual capabilities of Google Vids, the methodology facilitates the creation of professional-grade video commercials. This integration allows for the rapid generation of marketing assets that maintain the accuracy and depth of the original source material while adhering to the constraints of short-form video formats.
