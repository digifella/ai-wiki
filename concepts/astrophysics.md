# Astrophysics

Astrophysics is the branch of [[concepts/astronomy|astronomy]] that applies the principles of [[concepts/physics|physics]] to understand celestial objects and cosmic phenomena. It combines [[concepts/empirical-evidence|observational data]] from telescopes and satellites with theoretical models grounded in mechanics, thermodynamics, [[concepts/superheavy-nuclei-stability|nuclear physics]], and [[concepts/electromagnetic-interaction|electromagnetism]]. By analyzing the [[concepts/light|light]], radiation, and particles emitted by [[concepts/stellar-objects|stars]], galaxies, and other cosmic [[concepts/nodes|entities]], astrophysicists determine their [[concepts/material-properties|physical properties]], chemical [[concepts/writing|composition]], and [[concepts/evolutionary-lineage|evolutionary history]].

## Stellar Astrophysics

This subfield focuses on the life cycles of stars, from their formation in nebulae to their eventual death as white dwarfs, [[concepts/neutron-stars|neutron stars]], or [[concepts/black-holes|black holes]]. It examines the internal processes that power stars, such as [[concepts/fusion-power|nuclear fusion]], and how [[concepts/stellar-mass|stellar mass]] influences their lifespan and final state. The study of stellar spectra allows researchers to classify stars and understand the [[concepts/element-synthesis|nucleosynthesis]] processes that create the elements found in the universe.

## Galactic and Extragalactic Astrophysics

This area investigates the structure, dynamics, and evolution of galaxies and the large-scale structure of the universe. It explores the distribution of [[concepts/the-composition-of-the-universe-extends-beyond-what-is-directly-observable|dark matter and dark energy]], which govern the motion of galaxies and the expansion of space. Researchers also study [[concepts/active-galactic-nuclei|active galactic nuclei]], quasars, and the interactions between galaxies, such as mergers and collisions, to reconstruct the history of cosmic evolution.

## High-Energy Astrophysics

[[concepts/high-energy-particles|High-energy astrophysics]] deals with the most energetic processes in the universe, including those involving extreme gravitational fields and high-temperature plasmas. It studies phenomena such as supernovae, gamma-ray bursts, and the emission of X-[[concepts/rays|rays]] and [[concepts/gamma-rays|gamma rays]] from black holes and neutron stars. This field relies heavily on space-based observatories, as [[concepts/earths-atmosphere|Earth's atmosphere]] blocks most high-energy radiation, to detect and analyze these powerful cosmic events.
