# Enormous Numbers

Enormous numbers play a significant role in the field of [[concepts/physics|physics]] and other scientific disciplines. They are used to describe phenomena that span vast [[concepts/musical-scales|scales]], from subatomic particles to cosmological structures.

### Key Points
- [[concepts/mathematics|Mathematics]] is often employed as a tool in physics without necessarily providing true [[concepts/conceptual-understanding|conceptual understanding]] of the underlying principles.
- [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|Procedural knowledge]], such as mathematical operations and formulas, can be used to manipulate numbers effectively but may not convey the deeper insights required for genuine comprehension.

### Feynman's Perspective
[[entities/richard-feynman|Richard Feynman]] discusses the limitations of mathematics as merely a tool in his address "[[concepts/mathematics-as-a-tool|Mathematics as a Tool]], Not Understanding," where he contrasts [[concepts/procedural-knowledge|procedural knowledge]] with true [[concepts/conceptual-insight|conceptual insight]].
- He uses historical examples, including that of the Mayans, to illustrate how advanced mathematical systems can exist without deep understanding of their significance.
