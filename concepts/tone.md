# Tone

Tone refers to the stylistic and emotional quality of communication, particularly when prompting AI models like Claude. In the context of interacting with Claude, tone encompasses the manner in which instructions are conveyed, the formality level employed, and the implicit expectations set through language choice. The tone of a prompt influences how the model interprets the request, the depth of its response, and the style in which it presents information.

## Impact on Model Responses

The tone established in a prompt functions as a contextual frame that shapes model behavior. A formal, technical tone signals that the user expects precise, specialized language and detailed analysis. Conversely, a casual or conversational tone may elicit more accessible explanations or creative approaches. Claude responds to tonal cues by adjusting vocabulary complexity, explanation depth, and the overall register of its output.

## Practical Applications

Effective prompt engineering considers tone as a deliberate choice rather than an afterthought. Specifying desired tone explicitly—such as requesting "a professional report tone" or "an encouraging, supportive tone"—increases the likelihood that Claude will match expectations. This technique applies across various use cases, from technical documentation and creative writing to customer service responses and educational content.
