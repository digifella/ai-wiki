# B&H Photo

B&H Photo Video is an educational video channel by B&H Photo Video, a major photography and videography retailer. The channel produces [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] content covering various aspects of photography and film processing, including instruction on [[concepts/film-scanning|film scanning]] techniques.

## Film Scanning Tutorials

B&H Photo has published tutorial content on [[concepts/high-quality-images|scanning film at home]], addressing the practical process of digitizing analog film without professional laboratory services. These tutorials provide step-by-step guidance for photographers seeking to develop film scanning as a skill, demonstrating methods that can make the process accessible to hobbyists and professionals alike. The tutorials feature instructors such as [[entities/lotta-milde|Lotta Milde]] and cover essential aspects of the home scanning workflow.
