# Custom GPTs

Specialized configurations of [[concepts/llm]]s (such as [[entities/chatgpt]], [[entities/claude]], and [[entities/gemini]]) that utilize pre-defined [[concepts/instructions|instructions]] to execute specific tasks, reducing the need for iterative [[concepts/prompting|prompting]].

### Related Implementations
- **[[entities/google|Google]] [[entities/google-gemini-gems|Gems]]**
	- Targeted [[concepts/solution|solution]] to eliminate the inefficient "back-and-forth" chat [[concepts/loop|loop]] common in standard [[concepts/llm]] usage.
	- Essential for [[concepts/workflow-optimization|optimizing workflows]] for solopreneurs and small teams by minimizing time spent on [[concepts/iterative-prompt-engineering|prompt refinement]].
	- Reference: [Tim Peakman Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Pwu0c5dUU0)
- **JSON-driven Image Generation Workflow**
	- Uses custom GPTs (e.g., "JSON Image	Image [[concepts/creator|Creator]] V.3") to orchestrate [[entities/gemini|Gemini]] and [[entities/dall-e-3|DALL-E 3]] for consistent storyboard and image generation.
	- Leverages JSON to standardize outputs and maintain visual [[concepts/continuity|continuity]] across generations.
	- Reference: [Craig Does AI Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qXUww5tnLHs)
