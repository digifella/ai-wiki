# Wood Personality

**Definition:** The [[concepts/wood-element|Wood element]] represents growth and expansion. People with a strong Wood personality are characterized by their forward-[[concepts/human-cognition|thinking]] nature, [[concepts/ambition|ambition]], decisiveness, and a propensity for initiating projects and change.

### Related Concepts
- [[concepts/fire-personality|Fire Personality]]
- [[concepts/earth-personality|Earth Personality]]
- [[concepts/major-gold-deposit|Gold]] ([[concepts/metal|Metal]]) Personality
- [[concepts/water-personality|Water Personality]]

### Key Characteristics
- Ambitious and goal-oriented.
- Creative and innovative thinkers.
- Strong sense of direction and purpose.
- Tendency to lead and initiate new ventures.

### Challenges
- Can be impatient or overly critical when obstacles arise.
- May struggle with overthinking or excessive planning at the expense of action.
