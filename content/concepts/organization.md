# Organization

Organization is the practice of arranging and maintaining order in physical or digital spaces to reduce clutter and improve functionality. It involves establishing systems and [[concepts/habits|habits]] that prevent disorder from accumulating over time, making spaces more efficient and easier to navigate.

## The One-Minute Rule

A practical approach to maintaining organization is [[entities/gretchen-rubin|Gretchen Rubin]]'s one-minute rule, which suggests that if a task takes less than one minute to complete, it should be done immediately rather than deferred. Applied to organization, this means addressing small items of clutter or tidying tasks right away—such as putting something away or filing a document—rather than allowing them to accumulate into larger organizational problems. This preventative strategy reduces the effort required for major organizing projects by addressing disorder at its source.
