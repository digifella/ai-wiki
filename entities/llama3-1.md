# Llama 3.1

Llama 3.1 is a large language model developed by Meta, released in July 2024. It represents an incremental update to the Llama 3 model family, building upon the previous generation's architecture and training methodology. The model is available in three parameter sizes: 8 billion (8B), 70 billion (70B), and 405 billion (405B) parameters, enabling deployment across different computational constraints and use cases.

## Model Scale and Capabilities

The 405B variant represents Meta's largest language model to date and marks a significant scaling effort in the model family. Each variant is designed to balance performance with practical deployment considerations, allowing organizations to select appropriate model sizes based on their infrastructure and latency requirements. The range of sizes reflects the trend in the industry toward offering multiple variants of the same model architecture to serve diverse applications.

## Availability and Distribution

Llama 3.1 was made available to the research and development community, continuing Meta's approach of releasing large language models with relatively open access compared to some competitors. The model weights and supporting documentation were released to facilitate research, commercial deployment, and fine-tuning by external organizations.
