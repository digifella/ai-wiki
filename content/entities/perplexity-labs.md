# Perplexity Labs

[[concepts/ai-copywriting|Perplexity Labs]] is an [[concepts/ai-agent|AI agent]] product developed by [[concepts/perplexity-ai|Perplexity]], the search engine company known for its AI-powered search capabilities. The product is designed to automate tasks in marketing and video production workflows, leveraging AI to assist with [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] and campaign development.

## Capabilities and Testing

Perplexity Labs has been evaluated against other prominent [[entities/ai-tools|AI tools]] including [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] and [[concepts/claude-ai|Claude]]. Reviews have focused on its performance in practical [[concepts/software|applications]] such as marketing content generation and video production tasks, positioning it as a tool for professionals seeking to streamline creative and marketing workflows.
