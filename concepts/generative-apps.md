# Generative Apps

Generative [[concepts/apps|apps]] are applications that integrate [[concepts/generative-ai-models|generative AI models]]—particularly [[concepts/large-language-models|large language models (LLMs)]] and multimodal systems—to produce new content, automate processes, or deliver intelligent assistance. These applications extend beyond simple [[entities/api-calls|API calls]] to embed generative capabilities directly into user-facing tools and workflows. The defining characteristic is their ability to generate outputs such as text, code, images, or other media in response to user input or [[concepts/coding-instructions|system prompts]].

## Key Characteristics

Generative apps differ from traditional software by leveraging [[concepts/neural-networks|neural models]] trained on [[entities/big-data|large datasets]] to perform tasks without explicit programming for each scenario. They can handle open-ended requests, adapt to context, and produce varied outputs from the same input. Common applications include [[concepts/writing|writing]] assistants, [[concepts/code-generation|code generation]] tools, [[concepts/ai-bots|chatbots]], [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]] platforms, and search interfaces that synthesize information rather than retrieve it.

## Google Gemini Integration

[[concepts/google-ai|Google Gemini]], the company's [[concepts/multimodal-ai|multimodal AI]] model family, powers various generative applications across [[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s product ecosystem. [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] is available through different access tiers—including [[concepts/gemini-api|Gemini API]], consumer applications like [[entities/google|Google]]'s chatbot interface, and enterprise deployments—allowing developers and organizations to build or integrate generative capabilities into their own applications.

## Development Considerations

Building effective generative apps requires [[concepts/attention-mechanisms|attention]] to model selection, [[concepts/prompt-based-modeling|prompt engineering]], output [[concepts/quality-control|quality control]], and [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience design]]. Developers must balance capability with cost, latency, and safety considerations. Many generative apps combine multiple models, [[concepts/document-retrieval|retrieval]] systems, and traditional software components to deliver reliable performance while managing the inherent variability of generative outputs.
