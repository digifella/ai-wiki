# Philosophical Debates

## Introduction to Wellbeing Discourse
[[entities/kate-laffan|Kate Laffan]]'s [[entities/tedxlse|TEDxLSE]] talk, "What is [[concepts/health|wellbeing]]?" delves into the complexity of defining this crucial concept. She points out that while everyone recognizes its importance both personally and in policy-making, there’s little agreement on what exactly constitutes wellbeing.

- **[[entities/speaker|Speaker]]:** [[entities/kate-laffan|Kate Laffan]]
- **Date:** 2026-04-11
- **Resource:** [TEDx Talks](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=vv4cW-NKjWI)

### Summary of Key Points
Kate references [[concepts/derek-parfit|Derek Parfit]]'s work from 1984, which simplifies the philosophical debates about [[concepts/wellbeing|wellbeing]] into three broad categories:

- **Subjective Wellbeing:** Focuses on personal experiences and feelings.
- **Objective List Theories:** Emphasizes a list of objectively desirable states or conditions.
- **Desire Satisfaction Theories:** Argues that wellbeing is tied to the satisfaction of one's desires.
