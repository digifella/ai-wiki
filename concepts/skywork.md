# Skywork

Skywork is an artificial intelligence-powered application designed to automate the creation of business presentations. As a generative AI tool, it leverages large language models to generate textual content, structure slide layouts, and apply design elements, thereby reducing the manual effort traditionally required for producing professional visual materials.

The platform functions within the broader category of generative applications that assist users in creating structured documents. By automating both content generation and slide composition, Skywork aims to increase efficiency in the presentation creation workflow, allowing users to produce finished decks more rapidly than through manual methods.

In the context of current AI agent evaluations, Skywork is frequently compared against other prominent tools such as ChatGPT and Manus. These head-to-head assessments focus on the tool's capability to handle complex presentation tasks and its performance relative to general-purpose language models or specialized multi-agent systems in the domain of automated content creation.
