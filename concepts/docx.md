# Docx

Docx, formally known as [[entities/docling|Docling]], is an [[concepts/open-source|open-source]] [[concepts/docling|document processing toolkit]] developed by [[entities/ibm-research|IBM Research]]. It is designed to convert documents in various formats, including [[concepts/pdfs|PDFs]], Word documents, and images, into structured, machine-readable formats that can be processed programmatically. This conversion capability makes the toolkit particularly useful in [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] and [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] workflows, where unstructured document data must be transformed into formats suitable for algorithmic analysis.

The core functionality of Docx involves extracting and structuring content from diverse document types. By standardizing the output, it enables downstream applications to ingest and analyze document data more efficiently. This structural transformation is critical for integrating document processing into automated pipelines, ensuring that data remains consistent and accessible for further computational tasks.
