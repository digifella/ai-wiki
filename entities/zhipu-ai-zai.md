# Zhipu Ai Zai

Zhipu AI (also known as Z.Ai) is a Chinese artificial intelligence company that develops large language models and AI systems. The company focuses on creating practical AI solutions for business automation, software development, and complex reasoning tasks.

## GLM-4.7 Model

GLM-4.7 is Zhipu AI's open-source large language model designed to address specific use cases in coding, business automation, and complex reasoning. The model represents the company's effort to provide accessible AI tools for developers and organizations seeking alternatives to proprietary language models.
