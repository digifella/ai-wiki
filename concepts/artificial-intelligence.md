# Artificial Intelligence

**[[concepts/ai-technologies|Artificial Intelligence]] (AI)** refers to the [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] of [[concepts/human-intelligence|human intelligence]] processes by computer systems, including [[concepts/learning|learning]], [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], and self-correction. In materials [[concepts/science|science]] and energy [[entities/storage|storage]], AI is accelerating discovery through high-throughput screening and [[concepts/machine-learning|predictive modeling]].

## Applications in Energy Storage

AI is currently revolutionizing [[concepts/battery-technology]] and [[concepts/materials-design]] by enabling the invention of materials that do not yet exist in nature. Key developments include:

- **Accelerated Discovery:** Utilizing [[entities/gemini-25-flash]] and other advanced models to predict [[concepts/material-properties|material properties]] and stability, significantly reducing the time from hypothesis to validation.
- **[[concepts/ai-in-design|Generative Design]]:** Employing AI to propose novel molecular structures for energy storage components that optimize for capacity and longevity.

## Core Concepts and Analogies

To understand the mechanics of modern [[concepts/ai-models|AI systems]], it is useful to view them through the lens of human [[concepts/human-cognition|cognitive processes]]. As detailed in [[lab-notes/2026-09-15-Modern-AI-Six-Concepts-Explained-Through-Human-Analogies|Modern AI: Six Concepts Explained Through Human Analogies]], six essential concepts demystify how these systems operate:

- **Perception:** AI systems process raw data (images, text, sensor readings) similarly to how human senses interpret the environment.
- **Reasoning:** [[concepts/algorithms|Algorithms]] apply logical rules to draw conclusions, mirroring human deductive and inductive reasoning.
- **Learning:** Through [[concepts/exposure|exposure]] to vast datasets, models adjust their internal parameters to improve performance, akin to human experience-based learning.
- **[[concepts/memory|Memory]]:** AI utilizes short-term [[concepts/context-windows|context windows]] and long-term [[concepts/vector-databases|vector databases]] to retain and retrieve information, paralleling human working and [[concepts/knowledge-retention|long-term memory]].
- **Action:** Systems execute decisions or generate outputs (text, code, control signals) based on processed information, similar to human motor responses.
- **[[concepts/feedback|Feedback]]:** Continuous evaluation against ground truth or reward signals allows for iterative improvement, comparable to human [[concepts/error-correction|error correction]].
