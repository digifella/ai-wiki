# Deep Research Automation

Deep Research Automation is a workflow process that converts outputs from NotebookLM's Deep Research feature into formatted, professional websites without requiring coding knowledge. NotebookLM's Deep Research tool generates comprehensive research documents and structured information based on source materials provided by users. This automation streamlines the subsequent step by taking these generated outputs and transforming them into accessible web formats.

The process typically involves extracting key insights, citations, and structural data from the AI-generated research documents. These elements are then mapped to predefined website templates or content management systems. By automating the translation of unstructured or semi-structured research data into HTML or CMS-compatible formats, the workflow reduces the manual effort usually required for web publishing.

This approach enables users to rapidly deploy informational sites, knowledge bases, or reports directly from their research findings. It bridges the gap between deep analytical output and public-facing presentation, allowing for the quick dissemination of verified information. The system ensures that the integrity of citations and the logical flow of the original research are preserved in the final web structure.
