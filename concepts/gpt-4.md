# Gpt 4

GPT-4 is a [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] developed by OpenAI, officially released in March 2023. It serves as the successor to GPT-3.5 and marks a significant step forward in technical capabilities, particularly regarding [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]], [[concepts/contextual-understanding|contextual understanding]], and performance across a wide variety of tasks. The architecture supports multimodal interactions, accepting both text and image inputs to facilitate more complex user interactions.

The model features a [[concepts/context-length|context window]] of up to 32,000 tokens, enabling it to process longer documents and maintain [[concepts/coherence|coherence]] over extended conversations compared to its predecessors. This increased capacity allows for more detailed analysis and [[concepts/storing|retention]] of information within a single interaction. GPT-4 has demonstrated improved performance on standardized benchmarks and [[concepts/complex-problem-solving|complex problem-solving]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]], establishing it as a robust tool for diverse applications.

While GPT-4 itself is a proprietary model hosted by OpenAI, the broader landscape of [[concepts/democratization-of-ai|accessible AI]] has evolved with the [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[concepts/open-source-alternatives|open-source alternatives]]. Notably, OpenAI has introduced [[concepts/openai-models|gpt-oss]], an [[concepts/open-source-model|open-source model]] designed to run locally. This development allows users to deploy capable [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] on their own hardware, providing an alternative to cloud-based [[concepts/ai-inference|inference]] for those prioritizing data privacy or [[concepts/local-execution|local execution]] environments.
