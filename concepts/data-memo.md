# Data Memo

A **data memo** is a reflective document created during the data collection and analysis phases of [[concepts/qualitative-research]] to capture initial impressions, analytical insights, methodological decisions, and emerging patterns. It serves as a bridge between raw data and final analysis, ensuring [[concepts/opacity|transparency]] and reducing [[concepts/memory|memory]] bias.

## Purpose and Function
- **Real-time Reflection**: Records immediate thoughts while data is fresh, preventing loss of nuance.
- **Analytical Development**: Transforms observations into theoretical concepts or [[concepts/coding|coding]] schemes.
- **Audit Trail**: Provides evidence of the [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] process for [[concepts/document-review|peer review]] or replication.
- **[[concepts/problem-solving|Problem Solving]]**: Documents methodological challenges and [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] made during fieldwork.

## Key Components
- **Descriptive [[concepts/notes|Notes]]**: Contextual details about the setting, participants, or artifacts.
- **Methodological Notes**: Decisions regarding sampling, [[concepts/interview-techniques|interview techniques]], or observation protocols.
- **Theoretical Notes**: Links between data and existing literature or emerging [[concepts/theory|theory]].
- **Personal/Reflective Notes**: [[entities/tomasz-janowski|Researcher]]'s emotional responses, [[concepts/biases|biases]], and positionality impacts.

## Integration with Methodologies
Data memos are central to [[concepts/grounded-theory]], Ethnography, and Phenomenology. They differ from field notes in their focus on analysis rather than pure description. Effective [[concepts/memoing|memoing]] supports Triangulation by providing context for interpretative choices.
