# Google Vids Automation

Google Vids Automation is a two-stage workflow that converts text-based source material into finished video files by combining NotebookLM's slide generation with Google Vids' video production capabilities. The process begins with NotebookLM, an AI-integrated notebook tool that analyzes raw text input and automatically generates presentation slides in Google Slides format. These generated slides serve as the structural foundation for the subsequent video creation phase, ensuring that the visual narrative aligns with the underlying source data.

The second stage involves importing the generated Google Slides deck into Google Vids. This platform utilizes the slide structure to automatically assemble a video timeline, applying stock footage, images, and audio narration to match the content of each slide. Users can customize the visual style, select voiceover options, and adjust the pacing to refine the final output.

This automation reduces the manual effort typically required for video production by handling the transition from static text to dynamic visual media. The resulting video files are exported without watermarks, allowing for direct use in professional or personal creative projects. The workflow leverages existing Google Workspace integrations to streamline the creation process from initial text input to final video export.
