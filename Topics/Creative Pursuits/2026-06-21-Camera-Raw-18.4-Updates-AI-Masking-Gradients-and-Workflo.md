## Camera Raw 18.4 Updates: AI Masking, Gradients, and Workflow Enhancements
**Clip title:** Improved Subject [[concepts/layer-masks|Masking]], Bidirectional Linear Gradients, Vectorscope and More in [[concepts/camera-raw|Camera Raw]] v18.4!

### Summary
This video provides a comprehensive overview of the [[concepts/new-features|new features]] and improvements introduced in [[entities/camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR) version 18.4, focusing on enhancements that empower photographers with more precise control and efficient workflows. The core theme revolves around advanced AI-driven [[concepts/layer-masks|masking]] capabilities, sophisticated [[concepts/color-analysis|color analysis]] tools, and several [[concepts/quality-of-life|quality-of-life]] workflow optimizations.

A significant portion of the update centers on improved masking, particularly the "[[concepts/select-subject|Select Subject]]" feature. The video demonstrates how ACR 18.4 now generates notably more accurate and detailed masks for complex subjects like cacti, birds with fine feathers, and trees with intricate foliage, surpassing the quality of previous versions. This enhanced subject detection is crucial for applying [[concepts/color-and-masking-adjustments|selective adjustments]], including the "[[concepts/lens-blur|Lens Blur]]" feature, with greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]]. It's noted that while existing legacy masks won't automatically update, they can be manually updated by triggering any AI-driven adjustment, which [[entities/will|will]] prompt a warning about potential visible changes due to the mask recalculation. Additionally, [[concepts/mask-refinement|mask refinement]] sliders (Feather and Edge) are now available for all AI-generated object masks, offering [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over mask edges, and these refinements can be copied and pasted.

Beyond masking, ACR 18.4 introduces powerful tools for color and image manipulation. The new bidirectional linear gradient allows for simultaneous adjustments from two opposing sides of an image, fading towards the center, providing [[concepts/creative-control|creative control]] for tasks like darkening both foreground and background while leaving the subject unaffected. Furthermore, the introduction of a Vectorscope offers an objective and visual way to evaluate color hue and saturation in an image. This tool is invaluable for identifying unwanted [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]], achieving intentional color harmonies, and ensuring natural and consistent [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] across subjects, as it displays color distribution live as adjustments are made.

The update also includes several smaller, yet impactful, [[concepts/workflow-enhancements|workflow enhancements]]. The crop tool now maintains the current zoom level when entering or exiting, streamlining the cropping process. The generative AI-powered "[[concepts/remove-tool|Remove tool]]" gains the ability to intelligently fill transparent areas in an image, a useful feature for correcting perspective distortions that create empty regions or stitching panoramas with misaligned edges. Lastly, for users on [[entities/apple|Apple]] [[concepts/silicon|Silicon]] machines, the Denoise feature has been re-enabled to leverage the [[entities/apple|Apple]] [[concepts/neural-engine|Neural Engine]], resulting in significantly faster processing times for Bayer and X-Trans raw files, further optimizing post-processing efficiency.
