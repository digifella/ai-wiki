# Methodical Science

This article documents a [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] by [[entities/david|David]] on color correction techniques for concert photography, specifically addressing the challenges posed by extreme and variable stage lighting. The tutorial uses [[entities/adobe|Adobe]] [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]] as the primary tool for post-processing [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]].

## Scope and Context

Concert photography presents unique color correction challenges due to intense, rapidly changing colored lights that often cast unnatural [[concepts/color-casts|color casts]] across subjects and scenes. Standard white balance and [[concepts/exposure-settings|exposure settings]] frequently prove insufficient for recovering accurate [[concepts/skin-tones|skin tones]] and natural colors in these conditions. This tutorial focuses on systematic approaches to correcting these issues after capture rather than in-camera solutions.

## Methodology

The tutorial demonstrates practical workflows within Lightroom Classic for identifying and correcting color shifts introduced by [[concepts/concert-lighting|concert lighting]]. This includes techniques for selectively adjusting white balance, managing individual color channels, and using localized corrections to restore natural appearance to specific image elements while preserving intentional lighting aesthetics where appropriate.
