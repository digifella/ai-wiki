# AI Algorithms

AI [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] in [[concepts/photo-editing|photo editing]] apply [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] and computational techniques to automate and refine [[concepts/image-adjustments|image adjustments]] with greater [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] than [[concepts/manual-controls|manual controls]] alone. These algorithms analyze image data to identify specific features—such as facial characteristics, color distributions, and [[concepts/texture|texture]] patterns—and apply targeted modifications based on learned patterns from [[concepts/language-data|training data]]. By processing visual information intelligently, AI-driven tools can execute complex [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] that would be time-consuming or inconsistent if performed manually.

## Skin Smoothing and Retouching

Skin smoothing algorithms use AI to distinguish between skin [[concepts/texture-slider|texture]] and important facial details like pores and subtle blemishes, applying smoothing selectively to avoid the over-processed [[concepts/presence|appearance]] common with basic filters. These systems typically employ [[concepts/deep-learning-models|deep learning models]] trained on large image datasets to recognize skin regions and determine appropriate levels of smoothing. The result is more natural-looking retouching that preserves skin texture while reducing unwanted imperfections.

## Color Correction and Tone Mapping

AI algorithms assist with color correction by analyzing an image's overall color balance, lighting conditions, and subject matter to suggest or automatically apply [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|tonal adjustments]]. Rather than requiring manual adjustment of individual color channels or curves, these systems can intelligently redistribute colors to achieve better [[concepts/contrast|contrast]], saturation, and white balance. Some algorithms learn from user preferences or reference images to develop personalized correction profiles.

## Control and User Agency

While AI algorithms handle [[concepts/complexity-classes|computational complexity]], effective [[concepts/photo-enhancement|photo editing]] tools maintain [[concepts/user-control|user control]] through adjustable parameters and preview systems. Users can typically modulate the intensity of AI-applied adjustments, mask specific regions, or layer multiple algorithms to achieve desired results. This balance between automation and manual override allows editors to leverage algorithmic sophistication while retaining creative [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]].
