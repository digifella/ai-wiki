# World Foundation Models

[[entities/earth|World]] [[concepts/foundation-model|Foundation Models]] (WFMs) are large-scale [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] systems designed to enable [[concepts/coordination|coordination]] and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] across multiple [[concepts/agentic-systems|autonomous agents]]. Unlike traditional [[concepts/general-purpose-llms|foundation models]] optimized for individual tasks, WFMs maintain shared representations of environmental state, constraints, and objectives that allow distributed agents to operate cohesively toward common goals. These models function as a central knowledge substrate that agents can query, update, and [[concepts/purpose|reason]] about during collaborative [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]].

## Core Capabilities

WFMs typically incorporate the following capabilities and emerging paradigms:

*   **[[concepts/ai-agent-coordination|Multi-Agent Orchestration]]:** Centralized reasoning substrate for distributed [[concepts/background-agents|autonomous agents]].
*   **Proactive Automation:** Support for [[concepts/proactive-agents|proactive agents]] that anticipate needs rather than reacting solely to prompts.
*   **[[concepts/cost-optimization|Cost Optimization]]:** Patterns for managing high-tier [[concepts/llm-cost|LLM costs]] through efficient orchestration.
*   **Tabular Data [[concepts/general-purpose-models|Foundation Models]]:** Application of zero-shot foundation models to structured data, challenging the dominance of classical [[concepts/machine-learning|machine learning]] in this domain. See [[lab-notes/2026-10-12-Google-TabFM-Zero-Shot-Foundation-Model-for-Tabular-Data|Google TabFM: Zero-Shot Foundation Model for Tabular Data]] for details on Google's approach to treating databases and spreadsheets with a "one model for everything" paradigm.
