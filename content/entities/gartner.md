# Gartner

Gartner is a leading research and advisory company that provides insights, data, and analysis across technology, business, and organizational topics. The company is widely recognized for its market research, competitive positioning analysis, and technology recommendations across enterprises and institutions globally.

## Research and Advisory Services

Gartner delivers research through multiple formats including reports, briefings, and ongoing advisory services. The company is particularly known for its Magic Quadrant assessments, which evaluate and position vendors in specific technology markets based on their ability to execute and completeness of [[concepts/computer-vision|vision]]. Organizations across industries rely on Gartner research to inform technology purchasing decisions, competitive strategy, and digital transformation initiatives.

## Market Intelligence and Trends

The company tracks emerging technologies, market trends, and organizational [[concepts/best-practices|best practices]], publishing findings through its research library and live events. Gartner's analysis spans [[concepts/cloud-computing|cloud computing]], [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]], [[concepts/cybersecurity|cybersecurity]], enterprise [[concepts/software|software]], and numerous other domains, helping clients understand market dynamics and prepare for future developments in their sectors.
