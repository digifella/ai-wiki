# Gemini App

Gemini App is an AI-powered application designed to assist users in generating content and creating presentations. The application uses artificial intelligence technologies to streamline content creation workflows, making it easier for users to produce structured outputs without requiring extensive manual effort or specialized technical skills.

## Canvas Workflow

The application employs a canvas-based workflow as its primary interface for content generation and presentation creation. This approach allows users to organize and develop their content in a visual, flexible workspace where AI assistance can be applied to various stages of the creative process.

## Functionality

Gemini App helps users move from initial ideas to finished presentations by leveraging AI to handle repetitive aspects of content creation. The system is intended to reduce barriers to entry for users who may lack expertise in design, writing, or technical production, enabling broader access to professional-quality content generation tools.
