# Responsible AI Use

## Core Principles
- Alignment with human values and societal benefits
- Transparency and explainability of AI systems
- Fairness and mitigation of bias
- [[concepts/accountability|Accountability]] and oversight mechanisms

## Key Challenges
- Balancing [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] with [[concepts/ethical-considerations|ethical considerations]]
- Ensuring cross-cultural applicability of AI [[concepts/ethics|ethics]]
- Addressing the "black box" problem in complex [[concepts/ai-models|AI models]]
- Establishing clear legal frameworks for AI [[concepts/accountability|accountability]]

## Implementation Strategies
- Developing sector-specific [[concepts/governance|governance]] frameworks
- Integrating [[concepts/ethics|ethics]] into [[concepts/ai-development|AI development]] lifecycle
- Creating interdisciplinary oversight bodies
- Promoting public awareness and literacy about AI

## Healthcare Applications
- New [[concepts/ai-technologies|AI technologies]] are being rapidly adopted by healthcare professionals
- Existing [[concepts/organisational-governance|organisational governance]] often lacks processes for safe and responsible use
- Previous frameworks focused on high-level ethics principles without practical implementation
- Need to translate principles into practice, assess risk, and embed [[concepts/ai-delegation|AI oversight]]
- Study aimed to develop and validate a practice-oriented [[concepts/ai-governance-framework|AI governance framework]]
