# SciShow

[[entities/scishow-channel|SciShow]] is an educational [[concepts/research-communications|science communication]] channel and video series that explores scientific topics for general audiences. The channel produces content ranging from standalone educational videos to special field trip episodes that feature scientists and research institutions.

## SciShow Field Trip: Feng Zhang and Gene Editing

One notable SciShow Field Trip episode features [[entities/feng-zhang|Dr. Feng Zhang]] at the [[entities/broad-institute|Broad Institute]], where the discussion centers on [[concepts/crispr|CRISPR technology]] and the evolution of gene editing techniques. The episode explores how CRISPR has developed as a gene-editing tool and examines ongoing research into new [[concepts/software|applications]] and improvements to the technology. Dr. Zhang, a key figure in CRISPR research, discusses the scientific advances and potential future directions for gene editing.
