Agentic tasks require AI systems to autonomously plan, [[entities/make|make]] decisions, and execute sequential actions toward a goal (e.g., [[concepts/coding|coding]], research, tool usage), rather than providing static [[concepts/responses|responses]].

## Key Characteristics
- **Autonomy**: Requires iterative [[concepts/problem-solving|problem-solving]] and step-based execution
- **Goal-oriented**: Focuses on achieving specific outcomes through multiple actions
- **Tool-aware**: Often involves integrating [[concepts/external-tools|external tools]]/APIs

## Model Performance in Agentic Tasks
- **[[entities/gemini-3|Gemini 3]] Flash** ([[concepts/google-search|Google]]'s lightweight "workhorse" model) significantly outperforms [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Flash and rivals larger [[concepts/models|models]] ([[entities/gemini|Gemini]] 2.5 Pro, 3 Pro) in agentic task benchmarks, particularly for coding workflows
- **[[concepts/kimi-k2|Kimi K2]]** ([[entities/moonshot-ai|Moonshot AI]]'s [[entities/mixture-of-experts|Mixture-of-Experts]] model with 32B [[concepts/activated-parameters|activated parameters]]) achieves state-of-the-[[concepts/art|art]] performance in research [[entities/agent|agent]] benchmarks, outperforming [[concepts/gemini|Gemini]], [[entities/chatgpt|ChatGPT]] (o3), [[entities/grok|Grok]] DeepSearch, and [[entities/manus|Manus]] on a specific research task
- Positioned as a [[entities/high-performance|high-performance]] daily driver for developers handling complex [[concepts/agentic-patterns|agentic workflows]]
