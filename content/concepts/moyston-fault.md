# Moyston Fault: The Tectonic Collision That Created Victoria

---
## Overview
The Moyston Fault is an invisible yet profoundly significant fracture located beneath [[concepts/western-victoria|Western Victoria]], Australia. It acts as a crucial boundary that divides two vastly different geological regions.

This information is sourced from: [[lab-notes/2026-05-02-Moyston-Fault-The-Tectonic-Collision-That-Created-Victoria|Moyston Fault: The Tectonic Collision That Created Victoria]].

### Key Facts
*   **Location:** Beneath Western Victoria, Australia.
*   **Significance:** Functions as a major tectonic boundary.
*   **Role:** Defines the collision and geological [[concepts/structure|structure]] of the region.
*   **Context:** The fault represents a critical juncture in the tectonic history of Victoria.
