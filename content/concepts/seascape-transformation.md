# Seascape Transformation

The process of modifying the atmospheric, lighting, and color characteristics of seascape photography—such as converting daytime imagery to a sunset aesthetic—using AI-driven tools.

## Implementation via adobe photoshop
- Application of [[concepts/neural-filters]], specifically the [[concepts/landscape-mixer]], to perform [[concepts/style|Style]] Transfer.
- Technique allows for transferring the stylistic elements of a source photograph onto a target seascape.
- Enables the ability to "copy and paste" the complete visual look (color, lighting, and atmosphere) from one image to another.
