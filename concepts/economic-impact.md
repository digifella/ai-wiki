# Economic Impact

Assessment of quantitative and qualitative changes to economic [[concepts/indicators|indicators]] resulting from interventions, shocks, or projects. Encompasses GDP shifts, Employment generation, Income Distribution effects, and Welfare [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]]. Analysis distinguishes Direct Effects, Indirect Effects, and Induced Effects, utilizing Input-Output Models, CGE Models, or [[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis]].

## Evaluation Frameworks
- **[[concepts/cost-benefit-analysis]]**: Requires Shadow [[concepts/pricing|Pricing]] of Externalities; vulnerable to Discount Rate sensitivity and Valuation [[concepts/biases|biases]].
- **Fiscal Multiplier**: Measures expenditure propagation; efficacy varies by Economic Cycle and Leakage.
- **Distributional Impact**: Assesses regressive or progressive effects across income quintiles and demographic groups.

## Emerging Contexts: Open-Source AI & Geopolitics
The proliferation of frontier-level [[concepts/reasoning-models|open-source models]] introduces new dimensions to economic impact assessment, particularly regarding technological sovereignty and market disruption.
- **Open-Source Disruption**: Models like [[lab-notes/2026-07-22-Kimi-K3-Geopolitical-and-Economic-Impact-of-Open-Source|Kimi K3: Geopolitical and Economic Impact of Open-Source Chinese AI]] demonstrate capability parity with leading closed-source alternatives, challenging traditional IP-based economic moats.
- **Geopolitical Economic Shifts**: The [[concepts/deployment|release]] of [[concepts/kimi-k3|Kimi K3]] highlights the strategic economic impact of Chinese [[concepts/ai-innovation|AI innovation]], potentially altering global [[concepts/supply-chains|supply chains]] for [[concepts/computational-resources|compute]] and talent.
- **Regulatory Implications**: Assessing the economic impact of such models requires evaluating [[concepts/ai-safety]] frameworks, including export controls, [[concepts/data-sovereignty|data sovereignty]], and the potential for rapid diffusion of capabilities across emerging markets.
