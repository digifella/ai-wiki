# Roles and Responsibilities

## Definition
The explicit or implicit expectations, duties, and authorities assigned to individuals or positions within a [[concepts/team-composition|Team Structure]] or organization. Clear definition is critical for minimizing [[concepts/ambiguity|ambiguity]], reducing [[concepts/friction|friction]], and enhancing [[concepts/team-effectiveness]].

## Key Principles
- **[[concepts/clarity-slider|Clarity]] over Completeness**: Better to have clearly defined core responsibilities than exhaustive but vague job descriptions.
- **[[concepts/accountability|Accountability]] vs. Ownership**: Distinguish between those who are accountable for outcomes and those who own the day-to-day execution.
- **Dynamic Nature**: Roles evolve with project phases; static definitions can create bottlenecks.

## Impact on Team Dynamics
Ambiguity in roles leads to:
- Overlap and redundancy in effort.
- Gaps in critical task coverage.
- [[concepts/conflict|Conflict]] regarding authority and [[concepts/decision-making|decision-making]] rights.

## Best Practices for Definition
1. **Outcomes-Based Definition**: Define roles by the value they deliver, not just tasks they perform.
2. **Regular Calibration**: Revisit role definitions during retrospectives or project transitions.
3. **Explicit Decision Rights**: Clearly map who has final say in specific domains to prevent decision paralysis.
