# Demetrious Cocktail Mixing

Demetrious Cocktail Mixing is a bartending operation based in Heraklion, Crete, specializing in cocktail preparation and craft beverage development. The business provides mixology services and custom drink creation within the local food and beverage sector, serving clients who require professional cocktail preparation for events and establishments.

## Founder Background

The venture was founded by Helen, a former pharmacist who transitioned into cocktail mixing and mixology. Her background in pharmacy informed her approach to beverage formulation, emphasizing precision and scientific accuracy in the creation of drinks. This professional history distinguishes the operation's methodology within the local industry.
