# Terafab

Terafab is a [[concepts/vertical-integration|vertical integration]] initiative involving [[entities/tesla|Tesla]] and [[entities/spacex|SpaceX]] aimed at [[concepts/domestic-production|domestic production]] of advanced 2nm [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] chips. The project represents an effort to consolidate semiconductor manufacturing capabilities across the two companies, combining their respective [[concepts/expertise|expertise]] in electric vehicles, energy [[entities/storage|storage]], and aerospace technologies with [[concepts/nanotechnology|chip fabrication]].

The strategy centers on achieving greater control over the [[concepts/semiconductor-supply-chain|semiconductor supply chain]], which has become critical for both companies' [[concepts/computing-architecture|AI infrastructure]] needs. Rather than relying solely on external chip manufacturers, the vertical integration approach would allow Tesla and SpaceX to design, manufacture, and [[concepts/deployment|deploy]] custom [[concepts/ai-chips|AI chips]] for their operations, potentially reducing dependency on third-party suppliers and improving production timelines.

## Manufacturing and Scale

The 2nm process [[entities/nodejs|node]] targeted by Terafab represents cutting-edge semiconductor manufacturing technology. Achieving this scale of fabrication requires significant capital investment, specialized equipment, and manufacturing expertise. The project's feasibility depends on securing necessary resources and technical capabilities across both organizations.

## Strategic Implications

The initiative addresses broader concerns about semiconductor supply chain [[concepts/security|security]] and domestic manufacturing capacity. By pursuing internal chip production, Tesla and SpaceX aim to support their expansion in AI-driven applications while potentially contributing to national technological independence in advanced semiconductor manufacturing.
