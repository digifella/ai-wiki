# Orion

Orion is a spacecraft system developed for NASA's [[concepts/artemis-program|Artemis program]], serving as the primary crew vehicle for lunar missions. The spacecraft is designed to carry astronauts beyond low Earth orbit and support extended operations in deep space, including lunar orbit and surface missions.

## Artemis II Mission

Orion played a central role in the Artemis II mission, a crewed [[concepts/lunar-flyby|lunar flyby]] that demonstrated key [[concepts/spacecraft-systems|spacecraft systems]] and [[concepts/crew-operations|crew operations]] in the cislunar environment. The mission provided critical data on in-flight performance, thermal management, and crew interface with onboard systems during extended spaceflight duration.

## System Performance

The Artemis II mission validated various Orion subsystems operating in the deep [[concepts/vacuum-of-space|space environment]], including propulsion, power, communications, and life support. In-flight [[concepts/testing|testing]] and operational procedures conducted during the mission informed ongoing refinement of spacecraft systems for subsequent [[concepts/lunar-exploration|Artemis missions]] targeting [[concepts/lunar-landing|lunar landing]].
