# Vizio

**Vizio** is a prominent manufacturer of [[concepts/smart-tv]]s and home entertainment electronics. The company has faced significant scrutiny regarding its data collection practices and the underlying economic models that enable its competitive pricing.

## Business Model & Data Collection

Vizio's strategy relies heavily on a data-driven [[concepts/business-model|revenue model]], shifting from traditional hardware margins to digital advertising and data monetization.

- **Affordability via Data:** The low cost of Vizio smart TVs is attributed to a fundamental shift in business models, where hardware is subsidized by data collection rather than hardware innovation [[lab-notes/2026-09-13-Smart-TV-Data-Collection-and-Shifting-Business-Models|Smart TV Data Collection and Shifting Business Models]].
- **Surveillance Capitalism:** Like other major TV manufacturers, Vizio collects extensive user viewing data to build profiles for targeted advertising.
- **Industry Context:** This practice is not unique to Vizio; competitors such as [[entities/lg]] also employ similar data collection strategies, raising broader industry concerns about consumer [[concepts/privacy|privacy]].
