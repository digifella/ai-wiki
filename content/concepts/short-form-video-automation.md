# Short Form Video Automation

Short Form Video Automation is a [[concepts/workflow|workflow]] that combines [[concepts/ai-assisted-coding|Claude Code]] with [[entities/blotato|Blotato]] to streamline the creation and [[concepts/distribution|distribution]] of short-form video content across multiple social media platforms. The system reduces manual intervention in video production by automating key stages of the creative pipeline, from concept development through final posting. This approach is particularly suited to creators seeking to maintain consistent [[concepts/output|output]] across platforms like [[entities/tiktok|TikTok]], Instagram Reels, and [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] Shorts without proportional increases in production time.

## Technical Approach

The [[concepts/automation|automation]] relies on [[concepts/claude-code|Claude Code]] to handle scriptwriting, shot planning, and video structuring tasks, while Blotato manages the technical aspects of video assembly, formatting, and multi-platform distribution. [[concepts/json-based-prompting|JSON-based prompting]] allows for [[concepts/granular-control|granular control]] over output [[concepts/parameters|parameters]], enabling creators to specify [[concepts/style|style]], [[concepts/tone|tone]], duration, and platform-specific requirements within a single workflow. This separation of creative and technical tasks allows both components to operate efficiently within their respective domains.

## Practical Application

The system enables creators to generate complete video concepts and execute them within minutes rather than hours or days. By abstracting repetitive technical work, the workflow allows human creators to focus on conceptual decisions and strategic planning rather than the mechanics of editing and posting. The multi-platform dimension addresses a common challenge in [[concepts/content-creation|content creation]]: adapting the same core idea to different platform specifications and audience expectations.
