# Selective blending

A technique for merging elements from multiple images or layers into a single cohesive [[concepts/writing|composition]] by selectively revealing or hiding specific parts of each source. Commonly used to enhance or correct photographs without visible seams.

**Core applications:**
- Fixing imperfections (e.g., closed eyes in group photos)
- Combining optimal elements from similar shots (e.g., best lighting, expressions)
- Creating seamless composites from non-tripod-stabilized images

**Key implementation method ([[concepts/photoshop]]):**
- Stack source images and [[concepts/image-straightening|Image alignment]]
- Use [[concepts/layer-masks|Layer masks]] to selectively reveal desired elements from each layer
- Blend transitions to maintain natural [[concepts/presence|appearance]]

**AI-powered advancements ([[concepts/beta-version|Photoshop Beta]]):**
- [[concepts/contextual-task-bar|Harmonize Feature]]: Automatically blends color, lighting, and shadow across layers
