# Vaccine Production

## Overview
The process of manufacturing biological preparations that improve [[concepts/immune-system-function|immunity]] to a particular disease. Modern production relies heavily on rapid genomic sequencing, strain selection, and scalable biomanufacturing.

## Strain Selection & Mutation Prediction
Traditional methods for predicting future pathogen strains rely on **[[concepts/phylogenetic-tree-walking|phylogenetic tree-walking]]** along evolutionary branches. This approach has limitations:
- Fails to mimic real-[[entities/earth|world]] [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] where mutations are shared across different branches.
- Lacks [[concepts/accuracy|precision]] in identifying [[concepts/independent-evolution|convergent evolution]] events.

**AI-Enhanced [[concepts/user-attention-prediction|Prediction]]**
Recent advancements in [[concepts/agentic-frameworks|Agentic Systems]] in Infectious Disease Research & Genomics address these gaps:
- **[[concepts/high-dimensional-mapping|High-Dimensional Mapping]]**: AI maps genomic data into higher-dimensional spaces to better represent mutational "fingerprints."
- **Key Contributor**: [[entities/yatish-jain|Yatish Jain]] (CSIRO Bioinformatics Products [[concepts/team-lead|Team Lead]]).
- **Impact**: Enables more accurate prediction of antigenic drift, allowing for timely [[concepts/software-updates|updates]] in vaccine formulations before outbreaks occur.
