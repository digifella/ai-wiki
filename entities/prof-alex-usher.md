# Prof Alex Usher

Prof Alex Usher is a German academic specializing in the evaluation and assessment of large language models (LLMs). His research focuses on understanding the capabilities and performance characteristics of these systems through rigorous technical analysis.

## Work on LLM Evaluation

Usher has participated in collaborative research initiatives aimed at advancing knowledge of how LLMs function and perform across different contexts. He provided feedback on LLMs to Lemai as part of broader efforts to evaluate and improve these systems. His work emphasizes technical rigor in assessing model performance and identifying areas for refinement.
