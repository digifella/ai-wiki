# Policy Transfer

Policy transfer is a technique in reinforcement [[concepts/learning|learning]] (RL) and [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] where learned [[concepts/policies|policies]] or strategies are transferred from one environment to another. This method aims to enhance the efficiency of learning by leveraging pre-existing knowledge gained in a different, but related task or setting.

## Challenges and Applications
- **[[concepts/robotics|Robotics]]:** In robotics, policy transfer can bridge the gap between simulated environments and real-world [[concepts/software|applications]], helping robots learn [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] more efficiently.
- **[[concepts/agentic-ai|Autonomous Systems]]:** Policy transfer is crucial for autonomous systems to adapt quickly to new situations without extensive retraining.

## New Insights (Updated: 2026-04-12)
- The [[concepts/sim2real|Sim2Real gap]] in robotics poses a significant challenge, as robots struggle to perform [[concepts/complex-tasks|complex tasks]] due to discrepancies between [[concepts/simulation|simulation]] and reality.
- [[entities/nvidia|NVIDIA]]'s recent AI advancements demonstrate unexpected success in bridging this gap, offering new hope for more efficient policy transfer from simulated environments to real-world [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]].
