# FLUX.1

[[concepts/ai-image-generation|AI image generation]] model developed by [[entities/black-forest-labs]], designed for high-fidelity text-to-image synthesis with efficient [[concepts/training|training]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]].

## Key Features
- Supports diverse [[concepts/text|text]] prompts and complex subject generation
- Optimized for low [[concepts/vram|VRAM]] [[concepts/inference|inference]] and [[concepts/training|training]]
- Compatible with [[concepts/lora-adapter|LoRA adapter]] [[concepts/fine-tuning|fine-tuning]] for domain-specific adaptation

## Training Applications
- Demonstrated face recreation training via [[concepts/lora-adapter|LoRA adapter]] to consistently generate specific person [[concepts/images|images]] across varied [[concepts/scenarios|scenarios]] ([[entities/adam-lucek|Adam Lucek]]'s [[concepts/adoption|implementation]])
- Achieved reliable results with [[concepts/low-vram-requirements|low VRAM requirements]] (demonstrated in [2026-04-14 video](https://youtu.be/Drw6tnvtA5I))
- Video title: "Training [[entities/flux1|FLUX.1]] ([[entities/black-forest-labs|Black Forest Labs]]) [[concepts/lora-adapter|LoRA Adapter]] on [[concepts/faces|Faces]] | Low VRAM Image Generation"
