# Heygen

Heygen is an [[concepts/ai-assisted-video-creation|AI-powered video creation]] platform that generates synthetic video content. The platform uses [[concepts/ai-technologies|artificial intelligence]] to automate aspects of video production, including [[concepts/character-generation|avatar generation]] and [[concepts/video-editing|video editing]] tasks.

## Video Agent Tool

Heygen released a Video Agent Tool that automates video editing and production workflows. According to a YouTube [[concepts/tutorial|tutorial]] published in April 2026, the tool is designed to handle tasks traditionally performed by human video editors, making video production more accessible to users without specialized editing [[concepts/skills|skills]].

The tool represents Heygen's expansion into automated video workflows, enabling users to generate complete video content with minimal manual intervention. The platform targets content creators and businesses seeking to streamline their video production processes.
