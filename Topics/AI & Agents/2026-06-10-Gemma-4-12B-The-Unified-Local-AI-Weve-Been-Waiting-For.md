#### Description
Gemma 4 12B answers the rumor about a new intermediate model between their mobile ([[concepts/e2b|E2B]], E4B) and more [[concepts/hardware|hardware]] heavy models (26B MoE, 31B) but really stepped up the game with QAT ([[concepts/parameter-reduction|Quantization]] Aware Training). 

This is *on top* of the [[concepts/multi-token-prediction-mtp|MTP]]  (Multi-Token Processing) support for these models! Gemma 4 is a serious step in capability and performance for local models across the board. 

Nice to see at least some level of competition from other [[entities/labs|labs]] since Qwen has been backpacking the entire industry for local Ai recently!
