# Character Generation

Character Generation is a feature within Vyond Go that enables users to create or select characters prior to initiating AI-assisted video production. This process serves as a foundational step in the video creation workflow, allowing creators to establish a distinct visual identity and maintain consistency throughout their generated content. By defining the visual elements early, users ensure that the resulting video aligns with their specific narrative and aesthetic requirements.

The feature supports two primary methods for character acquisition: custom creation and selection from a library of stock characters. Custom creation allows users to design unique avatars tailored to specific needs, while the stock library provides pre-made options for rapid deployment. Both approaches integrate seamlessly with the subsequent AI video generation tools, ensuring that the chosen visual style is preserved in the final output.

This capability is essential for maintaining brand coherence and narrative clarity in AI-generated videos. By standardizing character appearance across scenes, the feature helps prevent visual discontinuities that can distract viewers. Consequently, it streamlines the production process by reducing the need for post-production adjustments to character consistency.
