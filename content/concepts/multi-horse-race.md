# Multi Horse Race

"Multi Horse Race" refers to an evaluation of the [[concepts/large-language-model|large language model]] landscape as of mid-2025, presented by [[entities/dave-plummer|Dave Plummer]], a retired [[entities/microsoft|Microsoft]] [[concepts/software|software]] engineer. The assessment examines the competitive state of leading LLM systems during this period and their relative [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and characteristics based on hands-on usage.

## Methodology and Scope

Plummer's evaluation is based on direct experience with the top four LLM offerings available [[concepts/assistive-technology|at]] that time. Rather than theoretical analysis, his assessment comes from actually subscribing to and extensively using these [[concepts/models|models]], allowing for practical observations about their performance, [[concepts/user-experience-design|user experience]], and suitability for different tasks. This ground-level perspective grounds the evaluation in real-world usage patterns rather than marketing claims or benchmark results alone.

## Context

The video was published on Plummer's [[entities/daves-garage|Dave's Garage]] [[entities/youtube|YouTube]] channel, where he regularly discusses technology topics from a practical engineering perspective. The mid-2025 timeframe [[entities/places|places]] this evaluation at a moment when the LLM market had matured beyond its initial novelty [[concepts/phase|phase]] but was still characterized by rapid development and competition between multiple capable systems.
