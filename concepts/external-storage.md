# external storage

Hardware used to expand the capacity or functional utility of a host device.

## Integration & Connectivity
*   Utilizes physical interfaces, such as [[concepts/usb-30|USB-C]], to attach peripherals and expand device capabilities.
*   On mobile hardware like the [[entities/ipad|iPad]], the [[entities/usb-c-port|USB-C port]] serves as a critical [[concepts/gateway|gateway]] for connecting external [[entities/storage|storage]] to facilitate advanced [[concepts/productivity|productivity]] and creative workflows beyond standard charging functions.
