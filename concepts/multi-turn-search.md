# multi-turn search

A technique in [[concepts/knowledge-bases|information retrieval]] where an [[concepts/ai-system|AI system]] engages in multiple iterative exchanges with a user to refine queries, gather context, and progressively narrow down relevant information. This approach significantly improves [[concepts/search-precision|search precision]] compared to single-query methods.
