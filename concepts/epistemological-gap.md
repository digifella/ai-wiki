The **epistemological gap** refers to a philosophical and practical divide in understanding or knowing something from different perspectives or levels of analysis. In the context of [[concepts/health|healthcare]] systems, it can highlight discrepancies between what medical professionals know versus what patients [[concepts/experience|experience]] over time.
