# Large Hadron Collider

The **[[concepts/large-hadron-collider|Large Hadron Collider]] (LHC)** is the world's largest and most powerful particle accelerator, located at [[entities/cern]]. It accelerates beams of protons or heavy ions to near light speed and collides them to study fundamental particles and forces.

## Key Discoveries & Research
- **[[entities/higgs-boson|Higgs Boson]] Discovery**: Confirmed the existence of the Higgs boson, validating the mechanism by which elementary particles acquire mass.
- **Mass from Energy**: Investigates the inverse process of nuclear fission/fusion, demonstrating how high-energy collisions convert [[concepts/kinetic-energy|kinetic energy]] into new mass ($E=mc^2$).
- **Standard Model Validation**: Continues to test predictions of the Standard Model of particle physics under extreme conditions.
