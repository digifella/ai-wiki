# 2026 04 08 Anthropic

Anthropic is an AI safety company founded in 2021 that develops large language models and AI systems designed with a focus on safety and interpretability. The company is known for creating Claude, a conversational AI assistant available through various interfaces including web applications and APIs.

## Claude and Product Development

Claude represents Anthropic's primary product offering, positioned as an alternative to other large language models in the market. The system is built using constitutional AI methods, an approach Anthropic developed to align AI behavior with human values through training techniques that emphasize helpfulness, harmlessness, and honesty.

## Research and Safety Focus

Anthropic maintains an active research program centered on AI safety, interpretability, and understanding how large language models function. The company publishes research on topics including mechanistic interpretability, scaling laws, and methods for making AI systems more transparent and controllable. This focus on foundational safety research distinguishes Anthropic's approach within the broader AI development landscape.
