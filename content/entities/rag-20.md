# Rag 20

[[concepts/rag-20|Rag 2.0]] represents an advanced approach to building [[concepts/answer-generation|Retrieval-Augmented Generation]] (RAG) systems designed specifically for [[concepts/agentic-ai|AI agents]]. Rather than treating RAG as a simple [[concepts/document-retrieval|document retrieval]] mechanism, this framework integrates agentic behavior—where the system can [[entities/make|make]] autonomous decisions about what information to retrieve and how to use it—with structured knowledge representations.

## Agentic RAG and Knowledge Graphs

The core [[concepts/innovation|innovation]] of Rag 2.0 lies in combining [[concepts/custom-knowledge-bases|agentic RAG with knowledge graphs]]. [[concepts/knowledge-graphs|Knowledge graphs]] provide structured, interconnected data that allows [[concepts/agents|agents]] to understand [[concepts/relationships|relationships]] between concepts and entities, moving beyond keyword matching to semantic understanding. This combination enables [[concepts/ai-agents|AI agents]] to reason about information more effectively, follow complex retrieval chains, and retrieve contextually relevant information that simple vector similarity might miss.

## Practical Application

This approach improves upon earlier RAG implementations by giving systems greater autonomy in retrieval decisions. Instead of executing a single retrieval pass based on a query, agents can iteratively refine their information needs, traverse [[concepts/knowledge-graph|knowledge graph]] relationships, and synthesize answers from multiple connected data sources. This results in more sophisticated [[concepts/fact-based-queries|question-answering]] [[concepts/capabilities|capabilities]] and better handling of multi-step [[concepts/reasoning|reasoning]] tasks.
