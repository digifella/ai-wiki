# Adobe Camera Raw

**[[entities/adobe-camera-raw|Adobe Camera Raw]] (ACR)** is a raw [[concepts/image-processing|image processing]] plugin for [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Adobe Photoshop]] and standalone application. It serves as the [[concepts/engine|engine]] for [[entities/lightroom-classic|Lightroom Classic]]'s [[concepts/adobe-lightroom-develop-module|Develop module]], allowing [[concepts/non-destructive-workflow|non-destructive editing]] of RAW files.

## Recent Feature Updates

### Bidirectional Gradient
ACR has introduced a new **[[concepts/bidirectional-gradient|bidirectional gradient]]** tool, enhancing control over [[concepts/photo-tonal-adjustments|tonal adjustments]] across an image.

- **Cross-Platform Parity**: Users of [[concepts/lightroom|Lightroom]] Classic can replicate this specific effect using alternative [[concepts/gradient-tool|gradient tools]] or by leveraging strategic color grading techniques.

### Strategic Color Grading for Mood
Color grading is essential for evoking emotion and adding depth, moving beyond generic presets to consciously decide how a photo should "feel".

- **Technique**: Utilize the **Two-Color Grading** method to shift the overall mood of an image effectively.
- **Reference**: See [[lab-notes/2026-10-11-Changing-Photo-Mood-Strategic-Two-Color-Grading-Techniqu|Changing Photo Mood: Strategic Two-Color Grading Techniques]] for a detailed breakdown of this approach.
- **Source**: [Changing Photo Mood: Strategic Two-Color Grading Techniques](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=tKQieyX7-E0) by Lou imag'in.

## Key Capabilities

- **[[concepts/non-destructive-editing|Non-Destructive Editing]]**: All [[concepts/adjustments|adjustments]] are stored as [[concepts/metadata|metadata]], preserving the original RAW data.
- **[[concepts/reflection-removal|Reflection Removal]]**: Advanced [[concepts/algorithms|algorithms]] help mitigate unwanted reflections in glass or water.
- **[[concepts/generative-fill|Generative Fill]]**: Integrated [[concepts/ai-tools|AI tools]] for content-aware expansion and [[concepts/object-removal|object removal]].
- **Plugin Integration**: Functions seamlessly as a plugin within [[entities/adobe-photoshop|Photoshop]] for complex [[concepts/digital-compositing|compositing]] workflows.
